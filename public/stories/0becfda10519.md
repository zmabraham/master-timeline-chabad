# Passing of R. Michoel Bliner, mashpia of Tomchei Temimim

*1911*

On Yom Kippur 5672 R. Michoel had asked to be brought into the Great Hall where the Rebbe Rashab prayed, with his bed placed against the wall between the hall and the second room. The Rebbe Rashab spoke with him on Yom Kippur, discussing whether he was permitted to forgo fasting, though he chose to fast anyway. The Rebbe Rashab attended the funeral that Motzaei Shabbat through deep mud, and the Rebbe Rayatz carried the bier, losing a galosh in the mire en route to the cemetery.
