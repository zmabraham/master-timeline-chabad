# Rebbe summarizes library's growth 5688–5694

*1934*

Reviewing six years of acquisitions, the Rebbe reported that according to Liberman the collection had grown nearly twofold, with paper and binding alone now worth more than what had been spent, and roughly seven hundred new sefarim added, many donated by authors and publishers. He noted that if Heaven helped him retrieve the Moscow library as well, the combined holdings would constitute an enormous treasure.
