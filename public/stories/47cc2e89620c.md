# Fatal car accident kills yeshiva secretary

*1991*

On the night of 20-21 Tammuz 5751, a car struck the vehicle carrying Shenetsis and the secretary of the Moscow yeshiva. The secretary was killed and Shenetsis lay in hospital with multiple broken bones. The author learned of the tragedy the next morning, 21 Tammuz, when he came to Marina Roshcha en route to camp.
