# R' Yosef Kogel writes RA"Ch about R' Maharash's succession

*1866*

Soon after the Tzemach Tzedek's passing, Chassidim in Pleshchenitz had accepted the Maharash's leadership. On 8 Av 5626 R' Yosef Kogel wrote to RA"Ch relaying news from R' Meir Amtsizer in Orsha, who had seen copies of tzettlach written by the Tzemach Tzedek to his son R' Shmuel — 'open your mouth and your words will illuminate hidden things' — confirming his succession. Kogel adds that most of the world is now drawn after the Maharash, telling 'wonders' about him.
