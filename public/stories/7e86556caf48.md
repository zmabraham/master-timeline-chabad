# Yeshiva relocation from Lubavitch begins

*1918*

Following the Rebbe Rashab's departure from Lubavitch in early 5676 and worsening conditions during World War I, the yeshiva students remained until 5678 (1918). The shiurim moved first under R' Yehoshua Arsh to Oryol for about a year and then to Kremenchug, while the Great Hall (Zal) remained until students gradually relocated to Kremenchug. The last farbrengen in Lubavitch took place on Shabbat Mevarchim Elul.
