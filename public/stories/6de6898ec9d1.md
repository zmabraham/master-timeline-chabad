# Alter Rebbe sends full doctrinal reply to R' Avraham of Kalisk

*1800*

Only in 5560, after his release from imprisonment, did the Alter Rebbe finally answer R' Avraham of Kalisk's doctrinal challenges with what he called 'a proper response.' That foundational explanation of his approach has not survived. R' Avraham accepted the answer fully in his letters of 5561.
