# Tzemach Tzedek falls seriously ill in Lyubavichi

*1859*

Only weeks after moving back into the rebuilt buildings in Lyubavichi at the start of 5620, the Tzemach Tzedek fell seriously ill in the month of Kislev. The illness is noted as a major episode in his later years, recorded in the family chronicle Beis Rebbi (part 3, chapter 6).
