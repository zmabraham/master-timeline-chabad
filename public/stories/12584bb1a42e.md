# Death of R' Avraham Chaim, chassid in New York

*1914*

R' Avraham Chaim ben Dov Ber passed away on 25 Kislev 5675 (first night of Chanukah). He left a detailed will specifying no eulogies, no newspaper announcements, and burial directly touching the earth per the Zohar. Decades later in 5760, his grave was located not at the Achuzas Olam plot but at Mount Zion cemetery in Brooklyn, correcting an earlier transcription error that had given his death year as 5674.
