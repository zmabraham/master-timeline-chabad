# First inspection of Schneersohn books at Khimki warehouse

*1999*

On 6 Tevet 5760, the emissary traveled with librarian Oleg to the Russian State Library's Khimki branch. After being required to obtain a special permit on site, he was allowed onto the 10th floor for the first time, where 6,000 unsorted Polyakov-collection volumes were kept. He recorded about 250 volumes and identified roughly 70 as bearing clear Lubavitch markings.
