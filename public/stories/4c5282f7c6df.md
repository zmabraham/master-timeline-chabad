# Rebbe Rayatz uproots from Rostov to Leningrad

*1924*

Mounting Soviet pressure forced the Rebbe Rayatz to uproot his household from Rostov and move to Leningrad (formerly Petrograd) at the start of summer 5684. By that point the central Tomchei Tmimim yeshiva was situated in Kharkov, with R' Yechezkel Feigin serving as mashgiach and director.
