# Rebbe Rayatz urges revival of Torah study among Hebron Chabad remnant

*1929*

Writing to R' Shmuel Yehuda Chaim on 4 Shevat 5689, the Rebbe Rayatz lamented how abandoned the Hebron settlement had become and called for fixed shiurim in Tanya and Likkutei Torah. He named the dedicated chassid R' Moshe Goldsmid as the one who would gladly take on this teaching, if only listeners could be assembled.
