# Kazarnovsky becomes rabbi of Bensonhurst congregations

*1929*

In winter 5689, R' Shlomo Aharon Kazarnovsky was appointed rabbi of both Bnei Yitzchak and Tiferes Yisrael synagogues in Bensonhurst, Brooklyn, where he served for the rest of his life. On 21 Tevet 5689, Rayatz wrote congratulating him and praying he would succeed in providing proper Torah teaching.
