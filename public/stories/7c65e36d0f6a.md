# Rabbi Alexander Yudasin and Tel Aviv shul gabbaim petition the Rebbe

*1953*

Rabbi Alexander Yudasin, former head of the Krivoy Rog beis din, joined with gabbaim of the Chabad shul, Beis Hamedrash Perushim in Neve Tzedek, the Shas chevra, Kahal Chassidim in Neve Shalom and other Tel Aviv synagogues in signing a petition to the Rebbe on 9 Iyar 5713. They pleaded for him to visit the Holy Land to illuminate the present era, invoking the Talmudic blessing 'blessed is your coming in peace.'
