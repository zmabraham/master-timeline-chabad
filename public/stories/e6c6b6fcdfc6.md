# RA"Ch immigrates with his family to the United States

*1898*

According to R' Dovid Shifrin's account, in 5658 RA"Ch arrived with his family in the United States and established himself as a dealer in diamonds and pearls at 444 Grand Street, 2nd floor, New York. His home became a gathering place for Torah scholars, and he maintained an ongoing correspondence with the Rebbe Rashab back in Russia.
