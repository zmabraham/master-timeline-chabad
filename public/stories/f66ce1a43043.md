# Third edition of Siddur with Da"Ch printed in Königsberg

*1852*

Following the תקצ"ז decree against Hebrew presses in Russia, presses in Prussian border towns — Königsberg, Johannisberg, Memel, Danzig — produced sefarim for the Russian market. Books destined for smuggling bore disguised title pages omitting the printer and bearing a falsely earlier date. Most copies of this siddur read 'printed in Berditchev' with the year encoded as תקצ"א, but a small batch made for Königsberg's local Jews received a second title page openly declaring 'Königsberg... at the press of A. Zamter, in the year ועבדתם את ה' בכל לבבכם.'
