# Rebbe Rayatz requests detailed roster of Tzemach Tzedek students

*1938*

The Rebbe Rayatz wrote requesting names, ages, family situation, intellectual capacities, duration of study in revealed Torah and Chassidus, and how long each student had been at Yeshivat Tzemach Tzedek. The letter reflects his close attention to the Jerusalem institution and ongoing concern for the spiritual development of its students.
