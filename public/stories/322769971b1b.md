# Rebbe permanently positions the new amud in the upstairs beis medrash

*1963*

After the new amud spent more than half a year in the Rebbe's room, the downstairs courtyard shul at 770 was already serving for Kabbalas Shabbos, Shacharis, and Mussaf, while Minchah on Erev Shabbos and Shabbos remained upstairs — requiring staff to carry the third amud up and down. On Pesach 5723 the Rebbe told R' Chadakov to leave the older amud downstairs permanently and bring the new amud up to the small beis medrash as its fixed amud.
