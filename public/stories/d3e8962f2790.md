# Schneersohn library confiscated by Soviet government

*1921*

In 1921 the Russian government confiscated the Schneersohn library, which would later become the focus of decades-long Chabad efforts to recover the texts. Culture Minister Gubenko cited this 1921 nationalization in his December 1990 letter refusing to return the collection to Agudas Chassidei Chabad. The confiscation set the stage for the Didan Notzach campaign decades later.
