# Worldwide Shabbat Mevarchim sermons on Moshiach in Moscow

*1992*

About eighty women and girls attended the motzaei-Shabbat gathering at the shul, with Rabbi Kogan translating for the visiting rabbis. The diarist himself spent motzaei-Shabbat teaching Gemara and Mishnayos classes to the yeshiva students, content to delegate the women's program while ensuring the worldwide Moshiach focus reached every group in Moscow.
