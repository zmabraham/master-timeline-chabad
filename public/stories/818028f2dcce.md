# R' Avraham Shimon Engel of Zelichov appointed rosh yeshiva in Warsaw

*1923*

Engel was renowned for encyclopedic mastery of Talmud and skill in explaining Rishonim; his opening shiur in Bava Metzia reportedly analyzed twenty-eight different Rishonic approaches. He had previously taught in yeshivot in Tzanz, Krakow, Krzanov and Stolin before joining Tomchei Tmimim. R' Shmuel Zalmanov reported to the Rebbe both his brilliance and his weakness in giving over the deeper analytical reasoning that students needed.
