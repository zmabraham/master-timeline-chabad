# R' Yitzchak Zalman Posner murdered by anti-Bolshevik bands

*1924*

R' Yitzchak Zalman Posner — father of R' Shalom Posner — was a regular guest of the author in Homel, traveling there roughly every two weeks to purchase wares for his store in Karop in Chernigov province. After refusing the author's invitation to stay for Shabbos so he could return for an unusual second trip the following week, he set out by train to Makoshin and then by riverboat. On Friday morning anti-Bolshevik bands boarded the boat and murdered thirteen Jews on board, claiming they were Bolsheviks, then dumped the bodies into the river. His mother in America was never informed of his death.
