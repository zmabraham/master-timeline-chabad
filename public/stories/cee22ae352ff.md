# R' Zalmanov reports to R' Slonim on Rayatz's Warsaw visit

*1931*

On 7 Menachem Av 5691, R' Shmuel Zalmanov wrote to R' Azriel Zelig Slonim about the Rebbe Rayatz's recent visit to Warsaw on his way to Marienbad. The Rebbe stayed two weeks, delivered three maamarim and many sichos, celebrated Chag HaGeula at the yeshiva, took interest in its state, and met privately with nearly every student.
