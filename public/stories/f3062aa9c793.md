# US visas granted to future Rebbe and Rebbetzin in Marseille

*1941*

The State Department had cabled the Marseille consulate in mid-March that the Schneersohns qualified for visas under the quota. The Rebbetzin wrote her father on 26 Adar II that the consul had promised the documents. The visas were finally issued on April 17, 1941, paving the way for travel through Lisbon to New York.
