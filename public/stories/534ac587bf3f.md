# Birthday of the Rebbe Rashab recalled in 1944 diary

*1860*

In his 5705 diary the Rebbe Rayatz noted that 20 Marcheshvan was the birthday of his father, the Rebbe Rashab, who was born on Monday of parashat Chaye Sarah, 20 Marcheshvan 5621 (5 November 1860), at the ninth hour of the morning. The Rayatz recorded a dream that night in which his father appeared in Shabbat garments on the 84th anniversary of his soul's descent.
