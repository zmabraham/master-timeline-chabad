# R' Avraham of Kalisk reconciles with the Alter Rebbe

*1801*

Two letters from R' Avraham of Kalisk in 5561 survive. One, addressed to the Alter Rebbe, asks that 'former matters not be remembered, what has passed is past.' The other, to the entire Chabad community in Russia, declares that no real difference exists between him and the Alter Rebbe — 'not a hairsbreadth' — especially regarding the practice of hitbonenut (contemplative meditation).
