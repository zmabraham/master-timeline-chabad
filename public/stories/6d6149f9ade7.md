# Hebron Joint Vaad publicly announced in Chabatzelet

*1887*

Following the arbitration ruling, the new joint Vaad published an announcement in Chabatzelet on 3 Marcheshvan 5648 declaring that all disputes between Chabad chasidim and the other Ashkenazi kollelim of Hebron were ended. The three elected leaders — R' Baruch Mordechai Halevi Gloiberman, R' Binyamin Rivlin, and R' Meir Tzvi Hershler — would receive all donations for the Hebron Ashkenazi community.
