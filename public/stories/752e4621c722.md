# Heirs of R. Binyamin Navarro grant mechilah to Avraham Tzarfati

*1697*

On 13 Elul 5457 (1697) in Fez, Yosef Piso ben R. Moshe, Yitzchak Navarro, and Avraham ibn Lachsin (acting under power of attorney for Chana, widow of R. Binyamin Navarro) executed a sweeping mechilah to Avraham Tzarfati ben R. Meir, waiving every claim and grievance from the dawn of creation, even up to 1,000 ouquiyot. Only their shared ownership in a known parcel of land inherited from R. Binyamin remained between them. R. Yehuda ibn Atar and R. Avraham ibn Masud ben Atia signed the deed.
