# Volume 7 of Tzemach Tzedek responsa bound

*1825*

Manuscript 233 is one of only three early bound volumes of the Tzemach Tzedek's responsa that survives. It collects question-and-answer exchanges from 5582 to 5585. Together with manuscripts 1019 and 1050 it provides our chief evidence for the structure of the lost volumes 1–14.
