# Rebbe Maharayatz visits Eretz Yisrael and inspects Kollel Chabad

*1929*

The Rebbe reported being pleased with the kollel's administration and orderly procedures, and witnessed the scope of its activity in supporting Torah scholars, the holy yishuv, widows, orphans and the very poor. He saw the institution as continuing the legacy of his ancestors, and the visit set the stage for his formal acceptance of the kollel's nesius days later. The visit also fell during the summer that included the 1929 Arab riots in Hebron.
