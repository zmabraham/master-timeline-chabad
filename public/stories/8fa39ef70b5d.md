# Yehoshua Hutner Rabbi Avraham Yehoshua Hutner 17 Kislev 5671

*2009*

Yehoshua Hutner Rabbi Avraham Yehoshua Hutner 17 Kislev 5671, December 18, 1910 - 26 Nissan 5769, April 20, 2009) was the founder and director of the Yad HaRav Herzog institutions, director of the Talmudic Encyclopedia project and the Complete Israeli Talmud Institute
