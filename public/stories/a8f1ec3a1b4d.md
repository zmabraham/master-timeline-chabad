# Rebbe Rashab begins effort to exempt rabbis from Russian army service

*1916*

From Sivan 5676 (mid-1916) through Tevet 5677, the Rebbe Rashab worked from Rostov to coordinate efforts through his agents in Petrograd — primarily R. Sh. M. Trainin, later joined by R. Eliezer Ginzburg of Moscow — to obtain draft exemptions for rabbis and ritual slaughterers (shochtim). Letters were written to government ministers, and attorney Oscar (Israel) Grosenberg was engaged. The effort was complicated by distinctions between holders of blue and white identity cards.
