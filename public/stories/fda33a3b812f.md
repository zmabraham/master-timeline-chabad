# R. Shmuel Segal of Rohatchov travels to the Land of Israel

*1824*

R. Shmuel Segal, moreh tzedek of Rohatchov, prepared for nearly a year before traveling to Eretz Yisrael in 5584 (1824). He spent several weeks in Mohilev obtaining a passport, leaving R. Yissachar Ber Horowitz and other local rabbis to handle pressing halachic questions in his absence. His departure precipitated R. Yissachar Ber's increased involvement in Rohatchov's rabbinical affairs.
