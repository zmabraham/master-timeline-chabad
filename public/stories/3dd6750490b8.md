# Pesach shiurim across Petersburg shuls and yeshiva

*1992*

Based in Petersburg ahead of Pesach 5752, the author records giving shiurim before Kabbalat Shabbat in the small shul, before Musaf in the great shul, and again in the small shul; speaking at a post-tefillah farbrengen about yetzias mitzrayim in our times; teaching a parents' group of day-school children on Sunday afternoons; and learning with yeshiva students from 5 to 8 pm. Alongside the classes he handled chametz sales using forms specially printed by Kuritz and fielded live halacha questions throughout the day, noting deep satisfaction with the output of the learning.
