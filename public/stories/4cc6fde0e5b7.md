# American students hear Maamar Shoftim and have first yechidus with Rebbe Rayatz

*1939*

Two days after their arrival, on Friday 3 Elul (Parshas Shoftim), the Rebbe Rayatz delivered the maamar 'Shoftim v'shotrim titen lecha b'chol she'arecha,' which was then repeated by the chozer Rabbi Mordechai Mentlik before his own departure to America. That night a farbrengen was held in Shlomo Zalman Hecht's home led by Rabbi Yechezkel Feigin.
