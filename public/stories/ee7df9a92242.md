# Maamar Mai Chanukah written for Kriah V'Kedushah Kislev issue

*1944*

Because of the Rebbe Rayatz's illness, the monthly Yiddish journal Kriah V'Kedushah had not appeared during Kislev. On Sunday 15 Tevet 5705 he wrote the maamar 'Mai Chanukah' for the Kislev issue and sent it to Mr. L. The Tevet issue maamar, 'Ki Ner Mitzvah V'Torah Or,' followed the next day, 16 Tevet.
