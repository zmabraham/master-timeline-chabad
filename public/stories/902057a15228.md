# Third denunciation letter by Simcha Keysin against Rabbi DovBer of Lubavitch

*1825*

In July 1825, the mitnaged Simcha Keysin of Shklov submitted a third denunciation to the governor of Vitebsk, alleging that the Maamad funds collected from chassidim by Rabbi DovBer Schneuri were being used for personal pleasures rather than communal needs. The letter attached a document from 1813 detailing distribution of these funds, supposedly written by the Rebbe himself. This denunciation, along with earlier ones, triggered the criminal investigation of the Mittler Rebbe.
