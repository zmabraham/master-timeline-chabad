# Facsimile of Moshe ben Natan HaKohen's Haggadah for the Chatam Sofer published in Berlin-Leipzig

*1924*

In 5684 (1924), B. Cohen published in Berlin-Leipzig a precise facsimile reproduction of an illustrated Haggadah manuscript originally created in 1816 by Moshe ben Natan HaKohen for the renowned halakhic authority Moshe Sofer, the Chatam Sofer. The 19-leaf quarto reproduction featured colorful illustrations alongside the Yiddish translation in Tzene-Rene script. The work was printed by B.G. Teubner.
