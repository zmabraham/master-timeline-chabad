# R' Gershon Chen leaves Lubavitch for Alexandria

*1911*

In 5671 (1911), R' Gershon Chen's father-in-law R' Chaim Leib Atkin, rabbi of Alexandria, passed away, and Chen left Lubavitch to fill the rabbinical position there. In 5679 he opened a branch of Tomchei Tmimim in Alexandria, as recorded in Toldot Chabad b'Russia haSovietit.
