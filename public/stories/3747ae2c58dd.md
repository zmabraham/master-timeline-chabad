# Uvarov instructs Governor-General to summon the Tzemach Tzedek

*1843*

On 14 March 1843 (24 Adar II 5603), Count Uvarov wrote to Pyotr Diakov, Governor-General of Vitebsk, Smolensk, and Mogilev provinces. He advised that one of the Chassidic rabbis must be included on the commission, given the Chassidim's prominence in Belorussia and Lithuania. He requested Diakov to notify Mendel Schneersohn to travel to Petersburg, calling him a man of moral merit and sound mind.
