# Hamelitz reports Baron Ginzburg secured one million francs from JCA

*1900*

The young Baron David Ginzburg traveled to Paris and persuaded the Jewish Colonization Association (JCA), founded by Baron Maurice de Hirsch, to allocate a million francs for schools run by the Society for Promotion of Enlightenment in Russia. When the Rebbe Rashab read of this in Hamelitz, he launched a sustained campaign to redirect the funds away from Maskilic schools and toward economic relief.
