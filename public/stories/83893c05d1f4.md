# Hebron census taken for Montefiore

*1839*

On Sunday during Montefiore's visit to Hebron in Tammuz 5599, the community delivered to him a census of the entire Jewish population, with Sephardim and Ashkenazi-Chabad counted separately. The census, preserved in the Montefiore Archive in London, became a primary source for the genealogy of the Hebron Chabad community. It records details of residents including R' Shmuel Halevi (age 64) and his son R' Menachem Aryeh (age 40).
