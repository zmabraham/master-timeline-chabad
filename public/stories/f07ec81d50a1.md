# Passing of the Maggid of Mezeritch in Anipoli

*1772*

After moving from Rovno to Anipoli, the Maggid passed on 19 Kislev. The Alter Rebbe is recorded as having been present, and the lot fell on him to perform the taharah and burial. The date later took on iconic significance in Chabad after the Alter Rebbe's release from imprisonment on the same date in 1798.
