# Rashab general letter on Jerusalem yeshiva leadership

*1903*

After deliberating throughout the summer, the Rebbe Rashab decided to prioritize the Jerusalem yeshiva over the Yafo rabbinate. In his general letter to chasidim, he announced that R' Shneur Zalman would oversee both the nigleh and chassidus learning. For unknown reasons, however, the plan never came to fruition.
