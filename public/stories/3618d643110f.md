# Rebbe Rayatz writes thank-you letters to those who secured his release

*1927*

Although exhausted from his journey from Russia and not yet settled in housing, the Rebbe Rayatz immediately sat down to write thanks to the people who had advocated for his release. He had no secretaries at this time, as R. Chaim Lieberman was still in exile in Tambov and R. Yechezkel Feigin had not yet managed to leave Russia.
