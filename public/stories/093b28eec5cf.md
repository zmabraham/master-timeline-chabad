# Riga community petitions Soviets to release Rebbe and books

*1927*

On 23 Menachem-Av 5687 the Riga Jewish community council notified the Soviet regime of the Frierdiker Rebbe's appointment as Chief Rabbi of Riga and pleaded that he be allowed to immigrate with all his family and bring along the writings of his holy forefathers and his own manuscripts. The letter was part of the diplomatic pressure that eventually persuaded Moscow to authorize the export of the library.
