# Ten thousand dollars raised in Kremenchug for Tomchei Temimim

*1918*

Following the narrator's return from Rostov, the Kremenchug chassidim raised the 10,000 dollars the Rebbe Rashab had mandated to sustain Tomchei Temimim. The daughters of R' Nosson Gurary — Perla and Leah — personally whitewashed the walls and scrubbed the floors of the new yeshiva quarters in preparation for the students.
