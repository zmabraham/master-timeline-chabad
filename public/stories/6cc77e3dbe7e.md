# R' Yitzchak Dov Oshpal passes away in New York

*1983*

R' Yitzchak Dov Oshpal, born in Svintzian in 5671, was among the earliest students of the Vilna Tomchei Tmimim, later its Rosh Yeshiva, and eventually a longtime Rosh Mesivta at Tomchei Tmimim in New York. He served as rabbi of Anshei Lubavitch of Boro Park and director of the Agudas Chabad Chevra Kadisha until his passing on 12 Adar 5743.
