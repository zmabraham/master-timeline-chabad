# Maxwell raises seforim with Gorbachev

*1991*

On 14 Tammuz 5751, Maxwell pressed Gorbachev for the seforim at the end of a productive 40-minute meeting. Gorbachev replied that the books belong to the Chassidim and his aides were already preparing the paperwork to return them through the emissaries in Moscow. He pointedly added that Russia must not sell sacred objects, even in exchange for Maxwell's many favors. Chernyaev then urgently summoned Tumarkin to prepare return documents.
