# Tomchei Tmimim yeshiva leaves Lubavitch

*1918*

The Tomchei Tmimim yeshiva departed Lubavitch in the summer of 5678 (1918). After the departure, the entire Rebbeim's courtyard burned down following the histalkus of the Rebbe Rashab, as recounted in the Frierdiker Rebbe's letters; the surviving wooden buildings on the property all postdate that fire. By 1991 a new brick post-office building was being constructed in the middle of the old courtyard.
