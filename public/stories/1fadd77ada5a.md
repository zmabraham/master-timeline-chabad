# First printing of Kitzurim v'Hearos al Tanya at Gertz Brothers press

*1948*

The Rebbe (then Ramash) handed the memoirist successive manuscript fascicles of the Tzemach Tzedek's Kitzurim v'Hearos al Tanya to typeset, until a small kuntres grew into a full book. When the Gertz print shop's vacation ended before the book was finished, the brothers gave him a key so he could work alone until midnight to complete it. The first edition's colophon reads 'Printed at the Gertz Brothers Press.'
