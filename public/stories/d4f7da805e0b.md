# R. Israel of Polotzk's group arrives in Eretz Yisrael

*1777*

After a long and difficult journey, the group of Chassidim led by R. Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk and including R. Israel of Polotzk reached Eretz Yisrael on 5 Elul 5537. Hundreds of Constantinople Jews joined them on the way to visit ancestral graves. The new settlers established themselves in Tzfat, where they began building a synagogue and faced immediate financial hardship from the many poor who attached themselves to the group.
