# Rebbe Rayatz announces plan to visit Warsaw yeshiva

*1927*

Following the visit of R. Yisrael Pinchas to Riga, the Rebbe Rayatz wrote on the first night of Chanukah 5688 that he planned to visit Tomchei Tmimim of Warsaw. He stated his intention to travel no earlier than the upcoming month of Shevat, beginning preparations for the journey from Riga.
