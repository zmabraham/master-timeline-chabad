# Rabbi Dudi Kaplin speaking at the international Shluchim Convention banquet 5775

*1987*

Rabbi Dudi Kaplin speaking at the international Shluchim Convention banquet 5775. Rabbi Dudi Kaplin at a mezuzah-affixing ceremony. Rabbi David (Dudi) Nechemiah HaKohen Kaplin (born 5748, 1987) is a shliach of the Rebbe and director of the Chabad House on the island of Cozumel in Mexico, and the rabbi.
