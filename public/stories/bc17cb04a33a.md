# Arbitrage court rules to return seforim to Moscow Chabad community

*1991*

Following the Thursday hearing, the judges informed the parties that the formal verdict on the next Tuesday would be to return the books to the Moscow Chabad community. On the intervening Friday the court also received a letter from Moscow City Hall signed by Sevastyanov recommending the return, along with a legal opinion by attorney Kabalkin commissioned by the court itself.
