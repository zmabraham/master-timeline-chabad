# Chaim Ginzburg lobbies for the Tzemach Tzedek to attend Petersburg commission

*1843*

On 10 February 1843 (22 Adar I 5603), the activist R. Chaim Ginzburg wrote to Dr. Lilienthal that the entire Chassidic community wanted the Tzemach Tzedek to travel to Petersburg as their representative. Although the Chassidim had initially selected R. Yitzchak Aizik of Orsha as their delegate, opinion shifted toward the Rebbe himself. The letter helped shape the government's eventual invitation.
