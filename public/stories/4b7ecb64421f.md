# Search begins for the Mitteler Rebbe's arrest file in Soviet archives

*1988*

In winter 5748 (early 1988), Rabbi Shalom DovBer Levin was dispatched to Moscow to search for the seforim collection of the Rebbeim. Before the trip he wrote to the Rebbe suggesting that R. Nissan Mindel also check the Tsarist government archives for documents related to the Mitteler Rebbe's arrest. The Rebbe replied that the search should extend to documents concerning the Alter Rebbe and the Tzemach Tzedek as well. On Erev Rosh Chodesh Shevat the team visited Hendel Deutsch in Petersburg, who suggested the Khovansky archive might be in Minsk.
