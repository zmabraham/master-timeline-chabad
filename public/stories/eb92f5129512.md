# Cornerstone laid for Beis Menachem second floor

*1879*

Binyamin ben Shlomo Yechezkel, a Sasson family relative, set the first stone. The community elder Shneur Zalman Mendelovitz prayed for the donor's late wife Leah bat Parchi and blessed the entire Sasson family in Bombay, London and Calcutta. The plaque on the upper floor names the building Knesset Eliyahu.
