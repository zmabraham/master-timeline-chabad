# R. Moshe Yisrael dies in Alexandria

*1740*

Around 1737 R. Moshe Yisrael moved from Rhodes to Alexandria, Egypt, and died there in 1740. His third responsa volume was printed posthumously by his son R. Avraham in Constantinople in 1742, while his collected sermons appeared as Apei Moshe in Livorno in 1828.
