# Rostov delegation brings 12 students to Tomchei Tmimim

*1914*

In 5674 a delegation came from the Rostov-on-Don kehilla to evaluate the yeshiva. Since Rostov was beyond the Pale of Settlement, the community lacked shochtim, rabbanim, mohalim and other religious functionaries, so they decided to send their children to be trained as future klei kodesh. After visiting several yeshivos, they were impressed by Lubavitch and enrolled twelve students, hiring R. Moshe Rosenblum as their melamed.
