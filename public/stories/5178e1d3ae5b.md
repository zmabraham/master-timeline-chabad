# Seventh Rebbe inaugurates worldwide tefillin campaign on eve of Six-Day War

*1967*

The campaign echoed the Sixth Rebbe's wartime call for repentance as a means to invoke divine deliverance, and reflected Chabad's view — rooted in Kabbalah and Chasidism — that spiritual transformation precipitates physical salvation. It became one of the Seventh Rebbe's signature 'mivtzoim' (campaigns).
