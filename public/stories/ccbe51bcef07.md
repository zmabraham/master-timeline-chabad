# Post-WWI Kollel elections install three administrators and four-man council

*1920*

At the end of Tevet 5680 (Jan 1920), after most Anash had returned from wartime exile and one of the administrators (R. Mordechai Dovber Slonim) had passed away, the surviving members of Kollel Chabad held elections by secret ballot. They selected three administrators and four council members to jointly manage the kollel, explicitly declaring the kollel under the authority of the Rebbe of Lubavitch.
