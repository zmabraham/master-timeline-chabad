# Alter Rebbe arrives at the Maggid of Mezeritch around age 20

*1765*

After exhausting available sources of knowledge in revealed Torah, the future Alter Rebbe sought a teacher of inner Torah. Around age twenty he left his father-in-law's home in Vitebsk to study under the Maggid, where he found 'rest for his soul' and was eventually entrusted with composing a new Shulchan Aruch.
