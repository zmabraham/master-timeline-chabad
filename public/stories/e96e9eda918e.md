# Rebbe Rayatz delivers maamar 'Hashkifa' on first Shabbos in America

*1929*

Just three days after disembarking in New York, the Rebbe Rayatz delivered the maamar 'Hashkifa Mim'on Kodshecha' on Shabbos Parshas Ki Tavo 5689. The maamar was later printed. It was given at R. Eliyahu Simpson's Boro Park shul, located below the apartment where the Rebbe was lodged during his first week in the United States.
