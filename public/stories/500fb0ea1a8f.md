# Ketubah d'irkesa written in Vienna on 1 Elul 5706

*1946*

Because halacha forbids living without a ketubah even for an hour, once the family reached the relative safety of Vienna the narrator commissioned a replacement ketubah arranged by R. Avraham Eliyahu Plotkin. The witnesses were R. Moshe Binyamin Kaplan and R. Menachem Mendel Raskin. The document confirms the family was already in Austria by Rosh Chodesh Elul 5706.
