# Rebbe Rashab dispatches secret emissary to Vitebsk and Lubavitch

*1919*

By Rosh Chodesh Adar Sheni 5679 the emissary had still not returned. The Rebbe described in letter 1103 how he had heard nothing from his sister and brother-in-law in Vitebsk for nine months, that the Lubavitch courtyard and homes had been confiscated, libraries and furniture seized or smashed, and that the synagogue had been turned into a clubhouse 'out of vengeance.'
