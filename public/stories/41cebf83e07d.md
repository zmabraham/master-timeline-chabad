# SILA's sixth Russia journey to advance Chevron Chabad projects

*1910*

SILA's sixth Russia trip pursued three major Chevron Chabad initiatives: registering a Chevron property in the Rebbe Rashab's name, renewing the minyan at the historic Avraham Avinu synagogue, and founding the Toras Emes yeshiva. He remained in Lubavitch for most of 5671 and much of 5672 working on these projects. The Toras Emes yeshiva would become a landmark institution of Chabad education in Eretz Yisrael.
