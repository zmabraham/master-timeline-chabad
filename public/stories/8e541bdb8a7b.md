# Rebbe returns to Leningrad from Kostroma exile

*1927*

Word of the liberation spread quickly across the Soviet Union; in Kharkiv, Rabbi Hillel Levin received a telegram and Chasidim gathered at Rabbi Aharon Tomarkin's home to celebrate. In Leningrad, Stanislav Messing had opposed the release and threatened to arrest the Rebbe again. The Rebbe recited HaGomel that first Shabbat before relocating to Malakhovka outside Moscow.
