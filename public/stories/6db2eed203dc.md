# Death of R. Moshe ben Asher, leader of Vilna mitnagdim

*1801*

With his death, the bitter public agitation in the streets of Vilna died down. Subsequent kahal elections in 5562 and 5565 included both chassidim and mitnagdim, and chassidic minyanim continued openly, eventually gaining a permanent space near the Old Kloyz.
