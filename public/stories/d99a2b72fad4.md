# Author arrives at Tomchei Tmimim Vitebsk on Rosh Chodesh Iyar 5688

*1928*

Together with Avrahmaleh Kvash and Dovid Green of Kaminetz, the author defied warnings and boarded a train to Vitebsk, the major rail hub serving the Tomchei Tmimim network in Polotsk and Nevel. He arrived on the eve of Rosh Chodesh Iyar and was hosted at the home of R' Berel Yaffe, where he saw for the first time large numbers of religious boys with payos and Lubavitcher tzitzis, writing home that he had reached 'Eretz Yisrael'.
