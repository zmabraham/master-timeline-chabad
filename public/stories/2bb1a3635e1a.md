# Future Rebbe and Rebbetzin flee Paris to Vichy

*1940*

With the Paris train station overwhelmed by Jews fleeing the Wehrmacht, the future Rebbe's enlistment in the French army eased his passage out of the city. The couple reached Vichy on Erev Shavuot and would remain there about two and a half months before continuing south. The Rebbetzin later related these events to the chronicler.
