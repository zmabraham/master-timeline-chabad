# Rabbi Weitman with the Chief Rabbi of Israel Rabbi Yisrael Meir Lau

*1954*

Rabbi Weitman with the Chief Rabbi of Israel Rabbi Yisrael Meir Lau during his visit to the Chabad institutions in the city. Rabbi Yosef David Weitman (born 5714, 1954) is one of the Rebbe's shluchim to São Paulo, Brazil, rabbi of the great "Beit Yaakov - Safra" synagogue in the city
