# Khovansky orders treasury to reimburse travel costs of escort to Lubavitch

*1825*

On 7 October 1825, Governor Khovansky wrote to the Vitebsk Provincial Treasury ordering it to reimburse Lieutenant Chevkin, district police officer Korvitsky, and another officer for the costs of the round-trip carriage journey from Vitebsk to Lubavitch and on to Shklov to fetch Rabbi DovBer and witnesses. They had used 6 horses on the way out and 9 on the return, since two more chassidim were added in Shklov.
