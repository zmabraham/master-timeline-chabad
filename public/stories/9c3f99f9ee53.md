# Shabbat Slichot 5706: refugees arrive at Pocking DP camp

*1946*

Pocking, a former wartime airbase converted into a vast refugee camp, housed many factions — Hungarian, Polish, right-wing, left-wing, Betar, Hashomer Hatzair — each in separate barracks. Anash received five or six barracks, with one set aside as a Tomchei Temimim yeshiva and another as a beit knesset. R. Nissan Nemenov organized a full daily seder of Tehillim, tefillah, and learning from dawn through ma'ariv, and arranged a group to study sh'chita; the narrator received his sh'chita certification later in Paris.
