# Nazi murder of the Jewish men of Shumyachi and surrounding shtetlach

*1942*

A day after killing the women, the Germans murdered the men of the gathered community. R' Zusman, the Shumyachi shochet and bodek who had also served as acting rav after R' Tzvi Edelstein left for Eretz Yisrael, had refused earlier suggestions to move to Moscow, saying that without him the townspeople would be forced to eat treif. He, his two young sons and the rest of the men were murdered on 29 Adar 5702.
