# Rebbe Rayatz instructs founding of Chmielnik yeshiva

*1929*

When Yosef Goldstein traveled to the Rebbe Rayatz in Riga for the High Holidays of 5690, the Rebbe ordered him to found a branch of Tomchei Tmimim in his hometown of Chmielnik under the name 'Toras Emes.' Goldstein returned home, partnered with Avraham Zonstein of the Tmimim association, and the yeshiva was operational within weeks. The name 'Toras Emes' was retained at the Rebbe's insistence even though local authorities preferred the older 'Beis Yosef' designation.
