# Wedding of Golda Schusterman and Nechemia Nodel in Samarkand

*1946*

R' Tzemach HaLevi Horowitz proposed the match between Golda and Nechemia Nodel, a young man recently returned wounded from the front. He was described as 'a very refined bachelor,' and the wedding took place in good fortune. The match later kept the memoirist's parents from emigrating with him, since Nechemia's elderly parents were staying behind.
