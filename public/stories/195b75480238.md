# R' Yaakov Landa, rabbi of Bnei Brak, passes away

*1986*

After serving Bnei Brak as its rabbi for a full jubilee — fifty years — R' Yaakov Landa passed away on 26 Shevat 5746 (February 5, 1986). His earlier career had taken him from Tomchei Tmimim Lubavitch to Kornitz, the Rebbe Rashab's court in Rostov, Moscow's Tiferes Bachurim, and the Libau rabbinate before his aliyah.
