# R. Yechezkel Feigin letter on Vilna-region towns

*1932*

Reporting to R. Yisrael Jacobson, the Rebbe's secretary R. Yechezkel Feigin described plans for a tour of the Vilna province around Adar, mentioning that the region contained nearly forty small towns, most populated by anash. The letter offers one of the clearest contemporary snapshots of Chabad's geographic reach in interwar Lithuania.
