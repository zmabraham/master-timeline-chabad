# Vilna Temimim send coded letter to Rebbe about arrested students

*1940*

Disguising names and events as a commercial correspondence about 'merchants' and 'merchandise,' the students reported that Shmuel Arbis, Avraham Rayevski, and Shraga of Lodz had been imprisoned, while Yisrael Ratzer was in hiding. They feared the prisoners would be deported to Siberia and described how R. Yehuda Eber, then in Riga, had authorized resuming costly smuggling attempts despite tighter Soviet border controls.
