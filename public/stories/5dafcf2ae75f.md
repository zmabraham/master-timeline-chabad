# Alter Rebbe travels to Mezeritch to study Kabbalah

*1772*

The young Rabbi Shneur Zalman of Liadi, then about twenty years old, traveled from Vitebsk to Mezeritch to learn the foundations of Chassidic teachings and conduct. As he later wrote in his own hand, he began studying Kabbalah there under Rabbi Dov Ber. He also studied halacha with the Maggid during this period.
