# Rebbetzin Shterna Sara's dream of three Rebbes

*1879*

On Simchat Torah 1879, Rebbetzin Shterna Sara was distressed after her father-in-law Rabbi Shmuel overlooked her when bestowing blessings on the family. That night, she dreamed of a Jew who promised her a son if she would give eighteen rubles to charity. Rabbi Shmuel later identified the dream visitors as the first three Lubavitcher Rebbes: the Tzemach Tzedek, the Mitteler Rebbe, and the Alter Rebbe. Ten months later, she gave birth to Yosef Yitzchak.
