# Extraordinary 19 Kislev farbrengen in Malachovka

*1933*

On 19 Kislev 5694 (December 7, 1933), an extraordinary farbrengen took place in the Malachovka shul. Many anash and elder Chassidim gathered there because the area was quiet in winter — Moscow officials had returned to the city and vacated their dachas. The farbrengen lasted several nights; at one point Rabbi Shmuel Levitin stood on the table with his long peyot loose over his face and sang 'Az der Rebbe iz do, iz doch hoderes vehaemunah uday do' — a moment that pierced the author's heart given his personal and the broader circumstances.
