# Riga committee meets at Wolshonok's home to plan citywide Chassidus gathering

*1930*

Because the Rebbe Rayatz's instruction to hold the gathering during Chol HaMoed Pesach only reached Riga on the third day of the intermediate days, the committee had to postpone. On 29 Nisan 5690 twelve committee members gathered at R. Wolshonok's home and decided in protocol number 9 to convene the general assembly on Rosh Chodesh Iyar at the middle minyan of the Dreyzin beis midrash.
