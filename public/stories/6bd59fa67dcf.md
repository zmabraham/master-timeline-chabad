# Germans capture Lubavitch region just before Pesach

*1918*

Following the Brest-Litovsk treaty, German forces advanced into the area encompassing Lubavitch right before Pesach 5678. Civilian rail traffic was halted — only military trains ran — preventing the narrator from returning from Kremenchug. The occupation persisted through the summer, with German authorities installing local Ukrainian rulers under their suzerainty.
