# Wandsbeck Haggadah printed by Yisrael ben Avraham

*1733*

In 5493 (1733), Yisrael ben Avraham produced a small-format Haggadah in Wandsbeck (Germany), bearing the Yiddish title noting this 'great mitzvah of telling'. The 24-leaf miniature edition included the Akdamut at its conclusion. It served the Yiddish-speaking Ashkenazic communities of Northern Germany.
