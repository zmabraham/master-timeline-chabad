# Rebbe Rashab's 5666 maamarim circulated

*1906*

Two editions of Rebbe Rashab's 1906 (5666) discourses are exhibited—one mimeographed, the other handwritten by the well-known Chassid R. Zalman Idel Zislin. Tsarist restrictions prevented formal publication, so discourses circulated in multiple copies among Chassidim.
