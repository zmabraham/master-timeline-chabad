# Ramash and Chaya Mushka arrive in the United States

*1941*

The Sixth Rebbe greeted his son-in-law warmly and tasked him with chairing three new pillars of Chabad: Merkos L'Inyonei Chinuch, Kehot Publication Society, and Machne Israel. Yitzchok Groner recalled the Ramash personally greeting each of the forty yeshivah students who waited at the dock.
