# Postponed Lag BaOmer rabbinical assembly in Vilna

*1908*

Acting with great urgency, R. Eliezer Gordon scheduled a general rabbinical assembly for Lag BaOmer 5668 in Vilna, conflating it with the affairs of the newly authorized Knesset Yisrael. Rabbis across the region had already begun gathering in district capitals to consult, but it became clear that a general assembly required the Minister of the Interior's permission rather than the local governor's. The organizers were forced to publicly announce the postponement via newspapers and telegrams.
