# Inyurkollegia issues second legal opinion on library ownership

*1991*

After all the steps taken since the initial legal opinion, the delegation sought a fresh evaluation. Mozheisky heard the details and said he would consult with the chairman, asking them to call back the next morning at 11. The resulting strong letter affirmed Agudas Chasidei Chabad's ownership and challenged Gubenko's silence on the issue.
