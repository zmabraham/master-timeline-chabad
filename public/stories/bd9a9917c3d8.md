# Benckendorff reports on Mendel Schneersohn to Education Minister Uvarov

*1841*

On 25 June 1841 (18 Tammuz 5601), Count Benckendorff, head of the Third Section (secret police), forwarded a gendarmerie report on the Tzemach Tzedek to Education Minister Uvarov. The report described Schneersohn as the head of the Chassidic sect in Vitebsk and Mogilev, allegedly opposing the government's plans for Jewish education. Benckendorff requested the document be returned when no longer needed.
