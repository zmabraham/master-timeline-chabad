# Death of R' Gershon Dov of Driben

*1911*

In 5671 (1911), the rosh yeshiva of Tomchei Tmimim in Shchedrin, the gaon R' Zalman Dov 'der Dribiner', passed away. The yeshiva placed advertisements in newspapers seeking a replacement, and various candidates came to deliver test shiurim before the staff and senior students, but none were chosen.
