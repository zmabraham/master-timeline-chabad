# R' Isser Yanovsky appointed rabbi of Krivoy Rog

*1894*

Around 5654/5, R' Isser Yanovsky was appointed rabbi of the town of Krivoy Rog in Kherson province, not far from Nikolayev. Rashab's letter of 23 Marcheshvan 5655 references his receipt of letters from both Kremenchug and Krivoy Rog, apparently related to this appointment.
