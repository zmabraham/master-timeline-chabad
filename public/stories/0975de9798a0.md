# Purim 5695 farbrengen at R. Zalman Kornitzer's Malakhovka home

*1935*

The Purim 5695 farbrengen at R. Zalman Kornitzer's home was led by R. Elchonon Morozov and joined by R. Yonah Cohen, a fervent singer who drank heavily and led niggunim while R. Elchonon spoke. R. Elchonon expounded that the Rebbe Rayatz is 'Avinu Malkenu'—just as the Alter Rebbe was Atzmus, so too the present Rebbe—and that, unlike the Alter Rebbe's chassidim, the Rayatz's chassidim include those who never saw him yet bind themselves to him with mesirus nefesh. The drasha left a deep impression on the bochurim present.
