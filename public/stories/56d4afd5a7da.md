# R. Yisrael of Polotsk writes from Jassy to Vitebsk-Polotsk leaders

*1778*

On 9 Iyar 5538 (May 1778), R. Yisrael of Polotsk wrote from Jassy, while traveling as a shadar (emissary), to the leaders of the Vitebsk and Polotsk communities. He reported the arrival of the caravan in Tzfat on 5 Elul tarki''z, described the Black Sea shipwreck and the miraculous survival of about 30 souls, and detailed the spiritual aims of the new community.
