# Rebbe weeps over Chasidim murdered in the Holocaust

*1945*

With the war in Europe ended and the scope of the Nazi genocide becoming clear, the sixth Rebbe sat in his office gazing at pictures of devoted followers like Rabbis Yechezkel 'Chatche' Feigin and Yitzchak 'Itche der Masmid' Horowitz. The fate of his daughter Sheina remained unknown. Rabbi Shmuel Levitin could not console him, and only the upbeat Rabbi Moshe Chaim Yehoshua Schneersohn-Twerski was finally able to lift his mood.
