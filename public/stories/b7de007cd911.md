# Printing of Amudei Gola in Ladi with the Alter Rebbe's approbation

*1805*

The Ladi printing house, operated in part by R' Baruch ben Eliyahu, produced Amudei Gola in 5565 (1805) carrying the Alter Rebbe's haskama. The same printer would issue Tanya the following year in Shklov, and his role has often been confused in the literature with that of a grandson of the Alter Rebbe.
