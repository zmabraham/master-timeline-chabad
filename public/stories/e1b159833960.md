# Russian actor Stas warns Chabad library return has been blocked

*1991*

On 27 Tevet R' Cunin and R' Levin spent hours with Stas, a prominent young Russian actor, who said his contacts confirmed a group around Gubinko had decided to block the library to avoid setting a restitution precedent. He proposed a Russian press campaign in Ogoniok and Literaturnaya Gazeta, including a piece by Lichachev, to shape opinion before approaching President Bush. He offered to introduce the delegation to the Gorby Club, where the real decision sat.
