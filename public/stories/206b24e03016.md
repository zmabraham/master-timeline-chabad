# Fifth edition of Likkutei Torah printed in Vilna

*1904*

After two years of intensive proofing — by R' Shmuel Sofer (Rabinowitz) and the Rashab personally, drawing on original manuscripts and reliable copies — the fifth edition appeared in Vilna in תרס"ד. A 'mode'ah rabbah' was inserted noting newly added hagahot that had been omitted from earlier editions due to copyist errors, and a corrections list (luach hatikun) was added at the end of part two.
