# Jacobson moves into apartment at 204 Christopher Street

*1926*

Around Passover, Jacobson moved into an apartment at 204 Christopher Street near the Anshei Bobruisk synagogue, and later relocated to 276 Christopher Street. His father warned him that questions of kashrus on poultry — especially 'pakin' blemishes in the intestines — would arise before Pesach, particularly in Brownsville, which was then called 'the Jerusalem of America.' His father came frequently in those days to support him through the first such she'eilos.
