# Separate Chabad fund for Eretz Yisrael is founded

*1805*

Whereas the Alter Rebbe had previously served as nasi over Eretz Yisrael funds in general, sent through the chief gabbai R' Yaakov of Smilyan and distributed in the Land by R' Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk and then R' Avraham of Kalisk, in 5565 he founded a dedicated kollel fund for Anshei Chabad. This created the institutional framework that would evolve into Kollel Chabad of Eretz Yisrael.
