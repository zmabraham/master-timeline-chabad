# Wedding of Rachel Leah of Kopust

*1893*

On Friday 9 Tamuz 5653 in the town of Kopust, R' Yaakov ben Avraham Yeshaya Halevi Vishelitzky married Rachel Leah, daughter of the Admor Maharshaz of Kopust — the author of Magen Avos and the senior leader of the Kopust branch of Chabad. The surviving ketubah was witnessed by Shneur Zalman David ben Moshe and Binyamin ben Shimon.
