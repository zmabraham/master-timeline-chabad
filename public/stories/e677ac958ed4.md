# Lubavitch becomes the center of Chabad

*1813*

In 5574 (1813/14) the Mitteler Rebbe established his court in Lubavitch, and the town began its 102-year run as the headquarters of the worldwide Chabad movement. Four successive Rebbes would reside there: the Mitteler Rebbe, the Tzemach Tzedek, the Rebbe Maharash, and the Rebbe Rashab. Alongside the Rebbe the town always had its own moreh tzedek (community rabbi) for everyday halachic questions.
