# Narkompros authorizes return of Schneersohn library (permit 670)

*1921*

Following an appeal from the Committee of Jewish Synagogues, the scientific libraries department of Narkompros formally resolved to return the books. The authorization carried the number 670, but Rayatz lacked the means to physically remove the books from storage and had nowhere to house them. The crates remained in Moscow and the original permit document was subsequently lost, becoming the centerpiece of a multi-year struggle as later officials disclaimed knowledge of it.
