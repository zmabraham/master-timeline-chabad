# Rashab writes ethical will for his son

*1888*

In 1888, when he was just twenty-seven and his son Yosef Yitzchak was eight, the Rashab was deeply concerned about his health and decided to write an ethical will. The will outlined how he wished his wife to educate their son if he passed away. Based on the verse 'Educate a child in accordance to his way,' the document was eventually published in 1943 under the title Chanokh LaNaar.
