# R' Chaim Kavash receives rabbinic appointment in Yochavitch

*1926*

After studying in Tomchei Tmimim in Lubavitch, R' Chaim Kavash was appointed rabbi of Yochavitch in the Polotsk district in 5686. As Soviet pressure on rabbis in small towns mounted, he fled first to Nevel and, when the Germans invaded, deeper into Russia to Samarkand.
