# First denunciation against Chabad by Shimcha Kissin

*1825*

Kissin's first letter to Governor Khovansky targeted the Chassidic movement broadly without yet naming the Mitteler Rebbe personally for embezzlement. He alleged the sect ("Karliner") manipulated young men, controlled charity coffers, monopolized shechita, and ran printing presses producing books of miracle tales. The letter demanded the authorities suppress Chassidism, setting in motion the events that led to the Rebbe's arrest.
