# Eshelon No. 3 crosses Dnieper at Darnitsa, route to Poland confirmed

*1946*

After a long halt near Darnitsa the train began moving across the Dnieper, and the passengers realized for certain they were on the road to Poland rather than Siberia. The narrator silently thanked God for being granted to see the journey 'from narrowness to expanse.' Further west they were shown mounds where Germans and Ukrainians had buried murdered Jews alive.
