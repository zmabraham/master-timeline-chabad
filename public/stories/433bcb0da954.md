# Schneersohn elected as delegate from shtetl Nevel

*1909*

During preparations for the Petersburg Rabbinical Conference, electors in the shtetl of Nevel (Vitebsk gubernia) unanimously voted to send the Rebbe Rashab (Rabbi Sholom DovBer Schneersohn) as their delegate. Secular activists tried to introduce a break in the voting and lobby against his candidacy, but failed. Nevel was the locality from which he was officially chosen.
