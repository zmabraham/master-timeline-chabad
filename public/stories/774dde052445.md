# Rayatz visits Riga from Warsaw, 12-20 Adar

*1933*

Shortly after relocating to Warsaw, the Rebbe traveled back to Riga for a nine-day visit covering Purim. He recited the maamar 'Zachor Es Asher Asah Lecha Amalek' on the night of his arrival, delivered a talk to the women later published in Likkutei Dibburim vol. 3, and farbrenged at length with the temimim and anash on Shabbos.
