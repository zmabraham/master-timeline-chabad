# Exhibition on the Tzemach Tzedek

*2015*

In the winter of 5776 (2015–16), the Lubavitch Library presented its fourteenth themed exhibit, devoted to the third Chabad Rebbe, Rabbi Menachem Mendel Schneersohn, the Tzemach Tzedek. The display drew on his manuscripts, his Tanach (used for reading the Haftarah), his siddur and the first edition of his responsa work, Tzemach Tzedek (Vilna 5631).
