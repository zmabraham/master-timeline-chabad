# Petrovsky deflects Chabad team at Moscow human rights conference

*1991*

Petrovsky, who nine months earlier had told the delegation they should have come to the foreign ministry from the start, was now chairing the human rights conference. From the gallery the Chabad team could only speak with a deputy; when Secretary of State Baker rose to speak they waved and he nodded back. A staffer named Babilkov later told them the books issue could not be raised at the conference without consent of the 35 participating states.
