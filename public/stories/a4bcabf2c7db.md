# R' Yisrael Dover Yaffe dies of plague in Tzfat

*1875*

After several years supporting his son-in-law R' Asher Yechezkel Horowitz at his table, R' Yisrael Dover Yaffe died from the plague. His death left R' Asher Yechezkel as head of the family and the emerging Chabad community in Tzfat, a leadership role he would hold for decades.
