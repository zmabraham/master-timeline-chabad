# Shulsinger Brothers facsimile of Gershom Cohen's Prague Haggadah

*1964*

In 5724 (1964), the Shulsinger Brothers issued in New York a facsimile reproduction of the Haggadah printed at the press of Gershom Cohen in Prague in 1527. The 42-leaf folio edition revived one of the most influential early printed Haggadot for modern audiences. It was a key contribution to mid-20th-century Hebrew bibliophilic publishing.
