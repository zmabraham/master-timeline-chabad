# 13 Nissan farbrengen at Reb Zalman Moshe's home in Moscow

*1934*

On the night of 13 Nissan 5694 (March 29, 1934), the yahrtzeit of the Tzemach Tzedek, the author overcame his exhaustion and went late at night to Reb Zalman Moshe's home. Soon afterwards, Rabbi Berel (Garfinkel) Kornitzer — the pride of the Rostov tmimim — arrived on a mission from Rabbi Zalman Schneersohn regarding communal finances, followed by Reb Yaakov Maskalik (Yaakov Zhuravitzer) who had come specifically to farbreng. The three sat down to farbreng and the author listened from the side.
