# All-Soviet Jewish Vaad convention opens in Moscow

*1991*

The Vaad, an umbrella body recently formed to speak for Soviet Jewry, convened in Moscow with roughly a thousand delegates from across Russia. Chabad leadership viewed its agenda warily, since Reform Jewish representatives were included and the body claimed to speak for Chabad organizations as well. After deliberation, the Chabad delegation decided neither to participate formally nor to issue public protests, but to respond that they were still studying the Vaad's platform.
