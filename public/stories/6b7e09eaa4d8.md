# Kollel Chabad reorganized with Va'ad HaMoetza after WWI

*1920*

With the Hebron Chabad community dissolved by war and Russian income cut off after the Revolution, only the local administrators remained of the old kollel structure. By secret ballot, anash chose three memunim and a four-member Va'ad HaMoetza, explicitly declaring the kollel under the patronage of the Lubavitcher Rebbe and stipulating that all decisions were temporary until contact with the Rebbe could be restored. This arrangement lasted nine years until the Rayatz formally accepted the kollel's presidency.
