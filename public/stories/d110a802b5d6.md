# Rebbe sends Zelig Slonim on first mission to England

*1926*

While locked in spiritual combat with the Yevesektzia, the Rebbe still attended to overseas Chasidim. Slonim's mission included finding positions for Chabad rabbis and shochtim. He worked to install Rabbi Mordechai Gutnick at London's Nusach Ari synagogue despite congregational opposition, and the Rebbe coached him to bypass recalcitrant leaders by appealing directly to members.
