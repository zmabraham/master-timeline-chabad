# Lag BaOmer dinner in Kharkiv

*1886*

In May 1886, returning from Yalta to Lubavitch, the Rashab and his family stopped in Kharkiv on Lag BaOmer. Local Chasidim organized a large dinner to welcome them, with rabbis, prominent Chasidim, and community members crowding the room to the rafters. When the Rashab taught a maamar, Chasidim crowded around to listen. During the event, young Yosef Yitzchak found his mother crying privately and conveyed her concerns about his father's health back to the Rashab.
