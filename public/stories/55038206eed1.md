# R' Aharon's confessional letter to R' Shneur Zalman

*1787*

After returning to Eretz Yisrael, R' Aharon the Great was rebuked by R' Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk for having spoken against R' Shneur Zalman during his shadar mission. From Tiberias he wrote a tearful confession asking R' Shneur Zalman to forgive him so that he could be readmitted to R' Menachem Mendel's presence.
