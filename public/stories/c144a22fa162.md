# Chabad refugees settle in Lod and in newly named Kfar Chabad

*1949*

Mainly in the year 5709 (1949), Chabad refugees arriving in the Land of Israel settled in two groups. One took over abandoned houses near the Lod train station; the other moved into the abandoned village of Safriya, renamed Kfar Chabad. Agudat Chabad of the Holy Land also received a dedicated building in the Lod compound to house a new Chabad yeshiva.
