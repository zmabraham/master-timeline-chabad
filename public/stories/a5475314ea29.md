# Farewell farbrengen at R' Shmuel Gronem on Shabbos Mevorchim Elul

*1918*

Before evacuating Lubavitch the remaining bochurim and senior figures held a final farbrengen at the Rashag's home on Shabbos Mevorchim Elul 5678. They drew bottles of wine from the cellar of the Rebbe Rayatz's father-in-law R' Avraham. The atmosphere of taking leave of Lubavitch — even with hope of return — was overwhelming, despite reassuring news from Kremenchug and Rostov.
