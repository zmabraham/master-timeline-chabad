# Letter to Rebbe Rayatz describing founding of Tzemach Tzedek yeshiva

*1922*

Writing on 18 Sivan 5682, Rabbi Nae reported he had returned to his post at the kollel in Tevet 5680 and secured a 15 pound sterling monthly budget. The funds enabled avreichim and bachurim from various Jerusalem yeshivot to gather twice daily at Beit Menachem Tzemach Tzedek for structured Chassidus study. The yeshiva was thereby named Yeshivat Tzemach Tzedek.
