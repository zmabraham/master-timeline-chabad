# First printing of Tanya completed in Slavuta

*1796*

The first printing of the Tanya — comprising Likutei Amarim and Sha'ar HaYichud VeHa'emunah — was completed on 20 Kislev 5557 (December 1796) in Slavuta. The book was eagerly received by chassidim and reprinted at least six times in its first decade.
