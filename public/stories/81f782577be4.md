# Pekuam refuses to redirect Hebron mikveh donation without donor consent

*1847*

Heads of the Pekidim and Amarkalim responded to Hebron Chabad's request to redirect the stalled mikveh donation toward a free-loan fund or bikur cholim. R' Asher had insisted any unused sum be returned to him to capitalize a different kollel, with interest funding synagogue lamp oil. The Pekuam declined to act without his decision.
