# Rebbe announces Eber's arrival in Riga to Chicago

*1940*

On 3 Nissan 5700, the Frierdiker Rebbe wrote to Agudas Chabad in Chicago that a telegram had arrived from the chief Rosh Yeshiva R. Yehuda Eber confirming his safe arrival in Riga. This was part of efforts to coordinate continued rescue funding from America.
