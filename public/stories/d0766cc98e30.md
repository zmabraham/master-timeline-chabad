# RA"Ch hears a 'Tze'enah Ure'enah' maamar from the Maharash on Shavuos

*1879*

RA"Ch wrote to his nephew R' Shmuel Rosenbaum that on Shavuos 5639, during the Maharash's lifetime, he was in Lubavitch and heard an important maamar beginning 'Tze'enah Ure'enah'. He entered yechidus to ask about it, received the Rebbe's holy reply, and noted that another Chassid had also asked a question on the same maamar — material RA"Ch promised to relate in a later letter.
