# WWI expulsion order ends the Pale of Settlement in practice

*1915*

Most Pale provinces were declared front-zone areas in which Jewish residence was forbidden, and deportees were assigned to five interior provinces: Voronezh, Tambov, Penza, Crimea, and Yekaterinoslav. The decree was later rescinded, but by then German forces had occupied the original Jewish areas, leaving nowhere to return. The formal abolition came with the fall of the tsarist regime in 1917.
