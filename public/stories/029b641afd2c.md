# Rebbe Rayatz returns to Riga after five-week journey

*1928*

In letters dated 6 and 8 Nissan 5688, the Rebbe Rayatz reported having returned on 28 Adar exhausted but spiritually nourished by his trip. He described the lasting positive impression made on him by Tomchei Tmimim of Warsaw and the yeshiva in Vilna. He resolved upon returning home to personally take on the project of expanding the Warsaw yeshiva.
