# Pope orders burning of the Talmud across Italy

*1553*

In 5313 (1553), apostates denounced the Talmud to the Pope, who decreed that all copies of the Talmud in every Italian state be burned, with severe penalties for anyone retaining tractates. The decree halted the Sabbioneta Talmud project and forced Italian Jews to substitute study of the Rif — which presents the Talmud's halachic conclusions — in place of the Talmud itself.
