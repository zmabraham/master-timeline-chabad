# Uncles emigrate to America after Shavuos

*1907*

After Shavuos of 5667 (1907), uncles Yisrael Yeshaya and Yosef Hillel left Zhuravichi for America, partly to escape Russian army conscription which required a 300-ruble fine for those who emigrated without reporting. They had corresponded with their uncles already in America, who sent them travel tickets and described American prosperity. The family escorted them out of town on the Motzaei Yom Tov following the holiday.
