# R' David Teiblum crowned as Admur by Kotzk-Pilov chassidim

*1939*

In Nisan 5699 (April 1939) R' David Teiblum — known as R' David Riker after his hometown Reika — was crowned Admur by the chassidim of Kotzk-Pilov following the death of R' Yosef Morgenstern. He had served as maggid shiur of the lowest class at Tomchei Tmimim Warsaw since 5686. Before accepting, he sought and received the affirmation of the Rebbe Rayatz; he was later murdered in the Holocaust.
