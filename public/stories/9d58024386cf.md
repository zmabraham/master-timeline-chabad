# Aussiedlung of Radom ghetto and murder of R' Baruch Friedman

*1942*

Yosef Goldstein, who survived because of his work permit, walked beside R' Baruch Friedman to the selection and described how the mashpia stood with eyes raised heavenward, hands clasped, completely detached from his surroundings. Friedman had refused to seek a work permit, certain that he would not be taken to labor; he had once said at a Simchat Torah farbrengen in Warsaw that the Rebbe had blessed him that he would not die without teshuvah. He was deported to Treblinka along with most of the ghetto's inhabitants.
