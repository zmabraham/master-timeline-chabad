# London-printed booklet on Schneersohn library arrives in Moscow

*1991*

Pross had spent weeks editing the booklet based on long phone conversations with R. Y.Y. Aharonov and the diarist's running journal. After final corrections were faxed in, the booklet was printed in London on 21 Adar. Fifty copies reached Moscow the next evening, ready to be handed to officials, journalists and public figures as a propaganda counterweight to Soviet stonewalling.
