# R' Shlomo of Karlin relocates to Ludmir

*1784*

R' Shlomo of Karlin was driven from Karlin by the mitnagdim and resettled in Ludmir in Volhynia. The precise date of relocation is undocumented, but by 5544 (1784) he was already established in Ludmir, having earlier been denied the Alter Rebbe's full agreement to settle in Beshenkovich because of a doctrinal disagreement about the role of the tzaddik.
