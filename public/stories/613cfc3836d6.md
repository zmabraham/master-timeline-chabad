# Rabbi Yekutiel Schneersohn (5778) Rabbi Yekutiel Schneersohn

*1938*

Rabbi Yekutiel Schneersohn (5778) Rabbi Yekutiel Schneersohn (known as: Kuti) (born on 30 Av 5698, August 27, 1938) is an educator, one of the elder Chabad Chassidim of Kfar Chabad. Biography: Born to Rabbi Yisrael Yosef Schnei
