# Communal fundraising campaign for Petersburg shtadlanim

*1824*

Representatives of Mohilev and Vitebsk Jews gathered in the town of Lubavitch to coordinate a special levy for delegates traveling to Petersburg on behalf of the community. They assigned 1,030 rubles to Mohilev province (60%) and 690 rubles to Vitebsk (40%). Khovansky later used this episode as evidence that 'voluntary' donations under the Mitteler Rebbe's leadership were in fact compulsory levies imposed by a few leaders.
