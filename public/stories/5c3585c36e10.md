# Yeshivah in Nevel closed by Soviet authorities

*1928*

On November 2, 1928, the JTA reported that the yeshivah in Nevel — a vibrant Chabad town about 120 miles from Lubavitch — had been shut down by Soviet authorities a year after the Rebbe's arrest. Nevel had been a center of chasidic life with a Jewish population of nearly 5,900 in 1897. The local Jewish community was later annihilated by the Nazis during the Holocaust.
