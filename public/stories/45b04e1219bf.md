# Culture Minister Gubenko refuses to sign book-return order

*1990*

On 16 Kislev 5751 the Chabad delegation, with Veronika as their advocate, brought a prepared memo to Culture Minister Nikolai Gubenko for signature. Gubenko refused to sign, claiming the matter required the Supreme Soviet, and at first refused even to meet them. After pressure he met the delegation with deputies Zolotov and Babushkin and ultimately agreed to instruct the Lenin Library director to draft a fresh memo distinguishing duplicates so a final approval could be issued.
