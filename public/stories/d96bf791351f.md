# Nicholas I orders special ministerial committee on Jewish affairs

*1840*

The committee included Minister of Interior Stroganov, Minister of Education Uvarov, Minister of Finance Vrontchenko, and Bludov of the legislative ministry, chaired by Count Kiselev; the head of the Third Section, General Dubelt, was later added. Uvarov took on the mission of 'enlightening' the Jews and allied with the Maskilim to compel the opening of state-style Jewish schools. He appointed Dr. Max Lilienthal of Riga as his intermediary with the Jewish population.
