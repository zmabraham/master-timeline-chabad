# Rayatz directs preparation of Tomchei Tmimim alumni list

*1931*

On 22 Menachem Av 5691, the Rebbe Rayatz wrote to R' Shmuel Zalmanov asking the hanhalla to compile a list of all students who had studied at Tomchei Tmimim over its first ten years, including where they were now and what they were doing. The aim, the Rebbe wrote to talmid R' Yitzchak Mandelbaum, was to identify alumni who could be placed as roshei yeshiva, mashpiim, and madrichim to spread Torah.
