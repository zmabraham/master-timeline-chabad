# Azharot of ibn Gabirol first printed in Constantinople

*1515*

R. Shlomo ibn Gabirol of Spain (c.1020-1057) composed the Azharot, a poetic enumeration of the commandments. R. Shimon b. Tzemach Duran (Rashbatz, Algeria 1361-1444) wrote on it the commentary Zohar HaRakia, and the two were issued together in their first edition in Constantinople in 5275 (1515).
