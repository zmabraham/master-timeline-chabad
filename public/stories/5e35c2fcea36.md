# Donation for Beis Avraham veRachel building

*1906*

Around 16 Adar 5666 (1906), the wealthy R' Avraham Rosenzweig donated 8,000 rubles to build a stone building in Lubavitch called 'Beis Avraham veRachel', housing the chadarim and a dining hall. Construction was delayed by pogroms and the revolutionary upheaval but resumed and was completed around 5671.
