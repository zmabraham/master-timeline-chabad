# Rebbe Rayatz visits Hebron during his 1929 Holy Land trip

*1929*

The Rebbe Rayatz visited Hebron in Tammuz 5689, just weeks before the massacre that would destroy the community. He visited the home of R' Yaakov Yosef Slonim, toured Chabad's Chatzer Romano property, repeated a maamar in the synagogue, and called on the Slabodka yeshiva. The visit is described in Sefer HaToldos vol. 4 and in Sefer Chevron.
