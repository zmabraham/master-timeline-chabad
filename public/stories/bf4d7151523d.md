# R' Yitzchok Matmid leads Lag B'Omer 5688 farbrengen in Vitebsk

*1928*

During his stay in Vitebsk's Kleiner Zeit, R' Yitzchok Matmid told R' Avraham Mayorer mid-shiur that the lesson must be shortened so a farbrengen could be held. The gathering took place at the home of R' Chaim Shmuel of Lubavitch, the shamash of the shul where the senior 'par zich' bochurim also took their meals.
