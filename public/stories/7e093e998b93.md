# R' Yaakov Landa marries Mina Rabinowitz

*1929*

In the winter of 5690 (1929-1930), R' Yaakov Landa, rabbi of the Libau Chassidim, married Mrs. Mina Rabinowitz. A 9 Kislev 5690 article in the Frimorgn newspaper announced the wedding and noted that the Rogatchover Gaon would serve as the mesader kiddushin.
