# Yeshivah Tomchei Temimim relocates to Rostov

*1920*

After fleeing Lubavitch during the war, the yeshivah had landed in Kremenchuk, where students rationed bread to once every two days. The new Rebbe brought the yeshivah to Rostov, saying he wanted Tomchei Temimim next to him. Conditions remained dire, but Rabbi Shmuel Gurary borrowed funds and sold personal property to sustain it.
