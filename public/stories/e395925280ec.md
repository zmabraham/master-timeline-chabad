# Author's bar mitzvah in Lubavitch

*1908*

On 3 Kislev 5669 the author turned thirteen. His relative R. Mordechai Leib Rabinowitz learned with him the maamar 'Ein HaKadosh Baruch Hu Ba BiTrunya' from 5648, which he reviewed thoroughly. The bar mitzvah came soon after a difficult period in which his protests over class placement nearly resulted in expulsion from Tomchei Tmimim.
