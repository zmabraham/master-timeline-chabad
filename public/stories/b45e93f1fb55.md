# Wedding of Israel Yehuda and Itta Devorah Gurkov on 5 Nissan

*1938*

The wedding took place under the shadow of constant NKVD danger. Around the same time, R' Yonah HaKohen of Poltava sent flour for shemurah matzah for the entire Passover holiday from Hadiach, and a particularly fine talit gadol made by the righteous converts of Voronezh as a wedding gift, which Israel Yehuda treasured and used for decades.
