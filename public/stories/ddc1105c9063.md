# RSM Chaikin publishes Chabad kollel proclamation

*1888*

In 5648 (1888), R' Shmuel Menachem Chaikin continued his active role in Hebron after Levin's appointment, publishing a kruz regarding matters of the Chabad kollel. R' Yehuda Leib Shaul Levin co-signed the proclamation, marking the new dual rabbinic leadership. The proclamation is preserved in Migdal Oz.
