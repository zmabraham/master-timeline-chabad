# First Chabad-Sephardi agreement in Hebron

*1830*

In 5590 the two Jewish communities of Hebron, Chabad and Sephardi, formalized an inter-communal agreement signed by R' Shmuel Halevi among others. The agreement documents the exorbitant 'protection' payments demanded by Sheikh Abdul Rahman, the local ruler known as 'the Black Rabbi.' This was the first of several such accords as the communities tried to share the burden imposed by the local strongman.
