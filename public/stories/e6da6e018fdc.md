# Mr. Palmer reports on visit to Paris and Eretz Yisrael refugee camps

*1949*

On 17–18 Shevat 5709, Mr. Palmer (R.Ch.S.) presented Rebbe Rayatz with a lengthy report (six to nine hours over two days) on his visit to Chabad communities in Paris and refugee camps in Eretz Yisrael. He expressed amazement at the state of hiskashrus among anash, and Mr. Broida marveled at the closeness shown to children and at the diligent learning of the Tomchei Tmimim students.
