# R. Yissachar Ber Horowitz becomes moreh tzedek of Bychov Chadash

*1827*

After several years of teaching and engaging in halachic rulings in Rohatchov, R. Yissachar Ber Horowitz formally took up the moreh tzedek post in Bychov Chadash in 5587 (1827). By 27 Sivan 5587 he was writing responsa from his new post on questions such as tefillin parchment, sending them to the Tzemach Tzedek and other leading rabbis. He would remain there until his next post in Lubavitch.
