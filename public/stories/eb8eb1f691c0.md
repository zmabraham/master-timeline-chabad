# Rebbe endorses melamdus as Levin's path in Paris

*1948*

Replying to Levin's report on the elders' counsel, the Frierdiker Rebbe wrote on 21 Av 5708 strongly endorsing his becoming a melamed. He blessed him with the gift of teaching, influence over students in conduct and chassidic ways, and ample parnasa. Levin then began traveling from Bezancourt into Paris, using addresses given by R. Zalman Serebryanski, to teach Sephardi 'patrons' who had never before encountered a Torah tutor.
