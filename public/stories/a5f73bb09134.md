# First lithograph of the Tzemach Tzedek portrait approved by Vilna censor

*1886*

In the winter of 5647, N. Metz of Vilna issued the first widely distributed lithograph of the Tzemach Tzedek, captioned in Hebrew 'Tzaddik for an everlasting memory: portrait of the holy gaon Rabbi Menachem Mendel of Lubavitch' and in Russian as 'Portrait of the Lubavitcher rabbi Mendel Schneersohn.' Censor approval was granted in Vilna on 30 October 1886. It became the basis for many subsequent reproductions of the Tzemach Tzedek's likeness.
