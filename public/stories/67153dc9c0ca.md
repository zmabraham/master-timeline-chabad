# Governor Khovansky orders Rabbi DovBer brought to Vitebsk for interrogation

*1825*

In September 1825 (Tishrei 5586), Governor Khovansky of Vitebsk ordered the regional police to detain Rabbi DovBer of Lubavitch and bring him to Vitebsk to face investigation arising from Simcha Keysin's denunciations. The escort traveled from Vitebsk to Lubavitch and detoured through Shklov so it could pick up two chassidic witnesses to bring along for questioning.
