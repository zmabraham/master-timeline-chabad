# Rebbe's cable calling for 'visible and revealed joy' during the war

*1991*

As Iraqi Scud missiles struck Israel during the Gulf War, the Lubavitcher Rebbe dispatched a telegram declaring that joy must be made visible and revealed. The cable was read aloud by Rabbi Moshe Chaim Levin in the Marina Roscha synagogue in Moscow on Shabbat Parashat Bo, before the Torah reading. The community responded with extensive dancing and a heightened spirit during the prayers and farbrengen that followed.
