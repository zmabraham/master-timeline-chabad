# The Rebbe writes R' Yosef Chaim Rosenfeld accepting the Bezpaloff cane

*1959*

R' Shneur Zalman Bezpaloff had originally brought two silver-handled canes to Rebbe Rayatz, who kept one; Bezpaloff later moved to San Francisco. When the shliach R' Yosef Chaim Hakohen Rosenfeld visited him there, Bezpaloff gifted him the second cane, which Rosenfeld then forwarded to the Rebbe. The Rebbe's reply (Igros Kodesh vol. 18 #6612) noted his habitual avoidance of gifts and enclosed a check in exchange.
