# Bolsheviks requisition rooms in the Rashab's Rostov home

*1920*

Writing on the eve of 3 Kislev 5681, the Rashab reported that the previous week a 'rekvizitsia' had been carried out on his home — the two upper and lower wings, plus the room beside his hall and the corner room facing his bedroom. With great effort he managed to retain his own room, but his son's household lost their granddaughter Chana's room. 'We are now surrounded by neighbors,' he wrote; intervention had been useless.
