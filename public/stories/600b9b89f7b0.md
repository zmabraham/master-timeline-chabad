# Tzemach Tzedek begins recording hanachot

*1805*

At about age fifteen, the future Tzemach Tzedek began writing down hanachot of the discourses he heard from his grandfather the Alter Rebbe. Manuscript 1116 in the library archive contains his hanachot of an Alter Rebbe maamar 'Shema Yisrael' delivered in 5566 (1805-1806), preserved in his youthful handwriting.
