# Tzemach Tzedek issues Likutei Torah in Zhitomir

*1848*

A decade after Russian censorship had blocked the planned sequel to Torah Or, the Tzemach Tzedek brought the Vayikra–Devarim and Shir Hashirim volume to the new Zhitomir chassidic press. Censorship constraints required a name change—Likutei Torah—and the two volumes thereafter circulated as a paired set.
