# Chabad community settles in Jerusalem

*1847*

Several leaders of the Chabad community in Jerusalem, including R' Eliyahu Yosef Rivlin, R' Ephraim Yitzchak Epstein, R' Uri Ornstein, R' Yosef Yehuda Leib Mishyov, and R' Tzvi Hirsch Fundaminsky, settled in the holy city in 5607. They later joined signatories on community petitions addressed to Sir Moses Montefiore.
