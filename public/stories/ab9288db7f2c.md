# R' Yissachar Ber Melamed writes a veksel in Lubavitch

*1854*

Open promissory notes (vekslach) were widely used in shtetl commerce: merchants could pay for goods with them, and sellers could pass them on as cash. The bearer would collect from the original signer on the appointed day. This veksel, written in Lubavitch by R' Yissachar Ber son of Meir Melamed, is one of three surviving shtarot from the town between תרט"ו-תרח"י.
