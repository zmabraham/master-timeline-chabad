# Rabbi Granstadt speaks at the annual conference of the Bnei Noach Brazil organization

*1970*

Rabbi Granstadt speaks at the annual conference of the Bnei Noach Brazil organization. Rabbi Granstadt. Rabbi Yaakov HaLevi Granstadt (born 5730, 1970) is the Rebbe's shliach, director of Ohr Menachem institutions and Chabad House in the Brooklyn neighborhood of São Paulo, Brazil, and heads the Bnei Noach organization.
