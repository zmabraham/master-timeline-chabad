# Opportunity to purchase yeshiva building arises

*1937*

At the beginning of Tishrei 5698 (autumn 1937), an opportunity arose to purchase the entire building housing the Tomchei Tmimim yeshiva in Otwock. The building cost more than the Rebbe Rayatz's available means, and he decided to organize a special fundraising campaign among Chabad chassidim in the United States and the Land of Israel.
