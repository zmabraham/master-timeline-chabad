# Soviet arbitrage court rules to return Schneersohn library to Chabad

*1991*

On Monday afternoon the three judges summoned the Chabad delegation, a US embassy representative, and CNN, and at 5:30 PM handed over the signed ruling. The court found that claims of nationalization or ownerless property were baseless, that the collection belonged to the Chabad movement as a whole rather than personally to the Rebbe, and that it must be returned to Chabad within one month. The delegation faxed the ruling to the Rebbe through Rabbi Groner, to the Kfar Chabad newspaper and Algemeiner, to Rabbi Krinsky for the general press, and arranged English translations sent to governments in the UK, Italy, and Germany.
