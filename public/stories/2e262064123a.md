# Mitteler Rebbe's case presented to Governor-General Khovansky

*1827*

On 12 February 1827, the Mogilev High Court's decision in favor of the Mitteler Rebbe was forwarded to Governor-General Khovansky. Khovansky considered the acquittal unjust and prepared an appeal, with his own legislative proposals concerning Jewish rabbis, to be submitted to the Ruling Senate.
