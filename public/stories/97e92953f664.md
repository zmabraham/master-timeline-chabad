# MaHaRiL of Kopust sends Rashbatz funds and instructions for printing

*1866*

Among the last letters of the Maharil of Kopust — written about two and a half months before his passing — was this note to R' Shmuel Betzalel Sheftil enclosing his share of the printing costs of the second edition of Likkutei Torah, plus a 'long memo' detailing the printing specifics. The letter highlights the close working partnership between the rebbe and his publishing manager.
