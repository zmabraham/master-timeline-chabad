# Passing of Rabbi Yaakov ibn Tzur (the Yaavetz) in Fez

*1752*

Rabbi Yaakov ibn Tzur — the Yaavetz, author of Mishpat u'Tzedakah B'Yaakov and one of Morocco's foremost halachic authorities — passed away on the night of Shabbat, 2 Tevet 5513 (December 1752) in Fez, just under five months before what would have been his eightieth birthday. He was buried on Sunday, 3 Tevet. His marriage-records pinkas, in continuous use since 5466, passed to his descendants who maintained it until 5617.
