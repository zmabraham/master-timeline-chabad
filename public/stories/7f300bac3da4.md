# Marriage of Miriam bat Yaakov ibn Attar

*1718*

On 20 Tevet 5478 (Jan 12, 1718), Miriam bat Yaakov ben Yosef ibn Attar was married. The match was recorded in the Ya'abetz's marriage register. Yaakov was a son of Rabbi Yehuda ibn Attar's brother Yosef, making Miriam a grandniece of the head rabbi of Fez.
