# International matzah campaign delivers 140,000 kilos to Soviet Jews

*1929*

Mordechai Dubin secured Soviet approval through diplomatic channels in Riga while production ran at full force in Riga, Dvinsk, and Berlin. Another 5,689 packages were mailed directly to families in four hundred communities. The campaign cost $88,000 (some $1.3 million today); the JDC contributed $10,000 thanks to Dr. Rosen's urging in Moscow.
