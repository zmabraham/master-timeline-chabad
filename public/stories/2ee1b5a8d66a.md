# Vilna Gaon issues first cherem against chasidim

*1777*

The Vilna Gaon, one of the most influential Jewish scholars of the eighteenth century, formally opposed the nascent chasidic movement with a cherem. He issued the ban again in 1781, and after his passing his students orchestrated accusations against Rabbi Schneur Zalman that led to the Chabad founder's imprisonment by the czar.
