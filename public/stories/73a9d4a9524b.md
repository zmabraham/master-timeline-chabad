# Levin davens before the amud on Shabbat Shuva 5723

*1962*

On 8 Tishrei 5723, the yahrzeit of his mother (Hashem yikom damah), Levin davened all the Shabbat tefillot before the amud in the Rebbe's beit midrash, with the gabbai R' Yochanan Gordon pushing him toward the amud for Musaf. He stood beside the Rebbe for maftir. Before the Shabbat, an Anash printer had given him a galley of the Tzemach Tzedek's maamar 'V'chol Adam' brought from Russia, which the Rebbe delivered at the farbrengen that day.
