# Rebbe Rashab letter to Jerusalem trustees on schools and chalukah

*1902*

Reacting with anguish to news that some Kollel Chabad members were sending their children to modern schools, the Rebbe Rashab insisted that the kollel was founded for those engaged in Torah and avodah in Eretz Yisrael, not for supporting those undermining traditional Jewish life. He ruled that beard-shavers and parents enrolling children in such schools must be cut from the chalukah lists, a position the Jerusalem trustees only partially implemented after a year of dispute.
