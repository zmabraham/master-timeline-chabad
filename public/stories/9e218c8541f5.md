# Elchonon Morozov arrested in Leningrad

*1927*

Before being taken away, Morozov handed the list of underground yeshivot and schools to his eleven-year-old son. Jailed with hardened criminals, in May he was indicted for helping Jews cross the border illegally, sentenced to three years' exile, and deported to Krasnoyarsk. Rabbi Feigin called the arrest 'terrifying and ominous' for the network.
