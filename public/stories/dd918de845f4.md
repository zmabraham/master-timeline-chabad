# Louis Lamm acquires R. Akiva Eiger oil portrait

*1913*

Louis Lamm, an Amsterdam book dealer and publisher, acquired the oil portrait of R. Akiva Eiger — said to originate from Russia and from the Eiger family inheritance — in 1913 for 25 Reichsmark. He kept it for over two decades before it was sold to the Rayatz in 1936, eventually entering the Chabad library archive.
