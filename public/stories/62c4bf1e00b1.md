# Library books transferred to storage in Moscow

*1915*

During Cheshvan 5676 (late 1915), all the printed books were packed into crates and sent for safekeeping, ultimately to Moscow rather than Nizhny as originally planned. The 5,430 numbered items (approximately 12,000 volumes) were placed in a secure storage facility protected against fire, water, and theft. The books were stored in the warehouses of the chassid R' Zalkind Persitz in the Kukurov district of Moscow.
