# Rayatz sends follow-up telegram to Barcelona on Schneersohn file

*1941*

Continuing his frantic efforts to free the Rebbe from Nazi-occupied Europe, the Rayatz sent another telegram from Brooklyn to Barcelona in early Adar 5701. The cable simply requested the current status of the Schneersohn rescue file. These telegrams reflect the extraordinary length of the bureaucratic process that ultimately led to the Rebbe's escape.
