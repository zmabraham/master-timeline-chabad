# Shimon Goldman studies in Kaloszyn yeshiva

*1939*

In summer 5699 the 13-year-old Shimon Goldman from Siedlce began learning at the Lubavitch branch yeshiva in Kaloszyn, recalling Leib Kremer as menahel and the dayan's son as maggid shiur. The boys studied Masechet Pesachim and ate 'days' arrangements with local families. He later recorded his memories in Kfar Chabad magazine.
