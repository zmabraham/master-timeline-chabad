# Rebbe responds to invitations to visit Israel and France

*1953*

Responding to the petitions of Anash in Israel and France, the Rebbe wrote on 13 Elul 5713 acknowledging their invitation to visit. He explained that material and spiritual concealments and obstacles prevented him from fulfilling the desired visit, and prayed that God would remove them so they could meet in abundance of joy. He enclosed the newly published Kuntres for Chai Elul and offered blessings for the new year. The response was published in Igrot Kodesh vol. 7.
