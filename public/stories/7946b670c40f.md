# Family travels to Yalta for Rashab's health

*1885*

In fall 1885, the Rashab and his family traveled almost one thousand miles south from Lubavitch to Yalta for six months, where the warm climate would benefit his health. Five-year-old Yosef Yitzchak accompanied them and studied under Rabbi Schneur Zalman Slonim from Hebron, who had come to Lubavitch for an extended stay. The family took daily walks together and the Rayatz later described it as an idyllic period spent with his parents.
