# Rashab visits ancestral gravesites before founding yeshivah

*1896*

Pained by the spiritual condition of Russian Jewry, the Rashab visited the gravesites in Mezibuzh, Anipoli, Hadyach, and Nizhyn. Upon returning to Lubavitch he also prayed at the gravesites of his grandfather the Tzemach Tzedek and his father the Maharash. With their blessing, he established the yeshivah the following year.
