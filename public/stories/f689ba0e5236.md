# Brooklyn Chassidim wire $4,000 to Riga for the Rebbe's resettlement

*1927*

Gathered in the Agudas Chassidei Chabad office in the Kramer brothers' factory, the author and R' A. Simpson — with Rabbi Lookstein's qualified support — pressed for an immediate $4,000 wire to R' Mordechai Dubin in Riga. The Kramer brothers initially balked at so large a sum and proposed delay, offering only $1,000, then $1,500. The author refused to leave the factory until the full sum was sent; by day's end the money was wired, and by Monday word arrived that the Rebbe and his family had reached Riga.
