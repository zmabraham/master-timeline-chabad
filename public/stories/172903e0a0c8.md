# Yeshivah named Tomchei Temimim on Simchat Torah

*1898*

On Simchat Torah 1898, as the seventh hakafah began around 4 a.m., the Rashab paused the dancing and announced that the new yeshivah would be named Tomchei Temimim, derived from the hakafot verse 'Tomekh temimim, hoshiya na.' Its students would forever be known as temimim.
