# Volume 3 of Tzemach Tzedek responsa bound

*1821*

Volume 3, containing responsa such as Even HaEzer 2 §145 (explicitly written in 5581), was bound around that year. Like most of the early numbered volumes, the original is lost; we know its contents only through summaries the Tzemach Tzedek later copied into other manuscripts when preparing a mehadura batra reorganized by the sections of the Shulchan Aruch.
