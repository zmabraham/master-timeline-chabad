# Rebbe appeals to R. Kahana-Shapira of Kovno for Levitin

*1929*

In Nissan 5689 the Rebbe wrote (Igros Kodesh vol. 16, letter 6201) to the Kovno rabbi, detailing Levitin's biography and recent imprisonment in Velikie Luki. He asked Kahana-Shapira to intervene through high government officials so that Levitin would be permitted to relocate to Lithuania, specifically to his former rabbinate in Rakishok.
