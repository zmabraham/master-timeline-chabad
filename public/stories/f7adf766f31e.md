# Rayatz's only return visit to Lubavitch as Rebbe

*1922*

On Erev Rosh Chodesh Elul 5682 (1922), the Rayatz traveled to Lubavitch to prostrate himself at the Ohel of his predecessors. While there he delivered a Chassidic discourse in the synagogue that had been relocated to the stone house. This was the only time he visited Lubavitch after assuming the leadership of Chabad.
