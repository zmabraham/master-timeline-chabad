# Lania Odnopozov delivers Rabbi Levi Yitzchak's prison photo to mission

*1991*

On 13 Nisan 5751, the yahrtzeit of the Tzemach Tzedek, Mr. Lania Odnopozov of Yekaterinoslav arrived in Moscow with a photograph of Rabbi Levi Yitzchak Schneerson taken at his 1939 arrest, together with a written survey of the archive's more than one thousand documents. The materials confirmed many known details of his self-sacrifice under interrogation and added new ones. The mission's emissary, Rabbi Shalom Dovber Levin, wrote to the Rebbe asking whether to pursue the archive; the Rebbe's reply emphatically encouraged him to begin.
