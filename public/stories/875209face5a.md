# Tzar's decree closes Jewish printing presses in Russia

*1836*

The decree, recounted by the Rebbe Rayatz, ordered a sweeping inspection of Jewish books throughout Russia, with suspicious volumes to be burned. By Tevet 5597 all Hebrew presses in Russia were shuttered, leaving only the Romm partnership press in Vilna operating. This crippled Chasidic publishing for decades and is cited as a major reason why so few of the Tzemach Tzedek's writings were printed in his lifetime.
