# Tomchei Temimim relocates to Otwock; network reaches 1,440 students

*1939*

From a single Warsaw branch with modest enrollment in 1930, the yeshivah grew into an educational empire spanning Poland and Lithuania. Otwock held 450 students, Warsaw 250, and Lodz 150, with the rest distributed among other branches. Many branches also spawned Chabad chadarim. The relocation forced by the war later led to new branches in Japan, China, and elsewhere.
