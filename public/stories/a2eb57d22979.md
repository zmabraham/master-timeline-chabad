# Tosafot Chagigah copied by hand in Egypt

*1589*

In 5349 (1589), a rabbi in Egypt who owned a Spanish-edition Talmud — which had been printed with Rashi but without Tosafot — copied a portion of the Tosafot on Tractate Chagigah by hand into a blank leaf at the start of his copy. The page is preserved in the Chabad Library archive.
