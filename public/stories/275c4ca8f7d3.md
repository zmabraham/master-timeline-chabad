# Yaavetz begins serving in a Fez synagogue as shaliach

*1688*

From 5448 (1688), the young Rabbi Yaakov ibn Tzur served as 'shaliach for every matter of holiness' in a Fez synagogue, including Torah reading and weekly sermons on halacha, aggadah, and ethics. His later rabbinic appointment letter (1697), preserved in the Ibn Tzur archive at the Agudat Chasidei Chabad library, lavishly praises his cantillation and preaching during these years.
