# Fifth Rabbinical Commission convenes in Petersburg

*1894*

Chaired by Rabbi Tzvi Hirsch Rabinowitz of Kovno, son of Rabbi Yitzchak Elchanan Spektor, the commission accepted a proposal that religious rabbis also be required to obtain secular education certification (tzenz) to manage civil registers. The decision was made without consulting other leading rabbis of the generation, and the Rebbe Rashab only learned of it about three years later, prompting his sustained opposition.
