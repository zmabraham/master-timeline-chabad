# Author arrested as draft evader in Chernigov

*1916*

On the Rebbe Rashab's advice the author traveled to Chernigov to seek help from the Rabbi Dovid Tzvi Chen rather than register as a rabbi near Zhuravichi. A surprise mobilization led the local police to demand his documents, which were not in order, and he was held with army deserters for four days. Through the intervention of communal leader R' Mordechai Polai he was released on Shabbos afternoon and brought before a fresh medical board which granted him a white discharge ticket.
