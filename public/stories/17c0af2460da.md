# Prosecutor General transfers oversight of the Mitteler Rebbe's case to Babinovich

*1826*

On 9 January 1826 (13 Shevat 5586), Mogilev Prosecutor General Smolaninov reported to Governor-General Khovansky that he had instructed the inspector of the Babinovich district court to examine the file on Rabbi Berke Schneur (Mitteler Rebbe) of Lubavitch and reach a swift, lawful decision. This formally moved the case from Khovansky's officials into the regular court system.
