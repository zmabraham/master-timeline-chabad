# Rayatz fasts and farbrengs on Purim despite illness

*1921*

The doctor permitted the Rebbe a fifteen-minute maamar; he instead recited one for two and a half hours and sat with Chasidim for three more. Many yeshivah students were also ill, and the financial strain of medical bills was mounting. Reb Shmuel Gurary's death exacerbated the crisis.
