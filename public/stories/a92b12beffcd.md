# Birth of Rabbi Hillel of Paritch

*1795*

The great chossid, mekubal and author of Pelach HaRimon was born in 5555 (1795) to Rabbi Meir HaLevi of Alisov. He would become one of the most prominent figures of early Chabad chassidus and a senior mashpia, later serving as mara d'asra of Babruysk after Rabbi Baruch Mordechai departed for Eretz Yisrael.
