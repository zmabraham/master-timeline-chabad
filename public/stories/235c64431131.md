# Rebbe Rayatz rescued from Warsaw to Riga

*1939*

Having been trapped in Warsaw at the outbreak of World War II, the Rebbe Rayatz was extracted to Riga on 2 Tevet 5700. Once safe in Latvia he immediately turned to organizing the rescue of his Tomchei Tmimim students from Vilna to the United States, opening the long campaign that occupies this chapter.
