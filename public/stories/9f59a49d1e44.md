# R' Aharon the Great departs as shadar to chu"l

*1785*

According to Sheluchei Eretz Yisrael, R' Aharon the Great departed for the diaspora as an emissary in 1785 (תקמ"ה), carrying a letter of mission from R' Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk and R' Avraham of Kalisk. He remained abroad for three years collecting funds and strengthening ties with the Chassidic communities.
