# Pereshel-Weintraub facsimile of the Hamburg 1728 Abarbanel Haggadah

*1980*

In 5740 (1980), Tuvia Pereshel and Munish Weintraub published in New York a facsimile of an illustrated manuscript Haggadah by Yaakov Sofer of Berlin, who had copied it from the 1695 Amsterdam edition. The 26-leaf octavo edition included Abarbanel's commentary and bilingual introductions. It continued their annual Haggadah facsimile project.
