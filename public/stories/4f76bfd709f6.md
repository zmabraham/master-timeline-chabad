# Push to enlist G7 leaders on Chabad seforim

*1991*

On the evening of 27 Tammuz 5751, Veronika urged that the leaders of the seven major nations ask Gorbachev directly when the seforim would be returned. She drafted a letter from the delegation to be co-signed by their seven ambassadors. R. Y.Y. Aharonov phoned R. Garlick (who had earlier moved Andreotti to act) and R. Pinchas Wagle (for British PM John Major), requesting renewed letters and meetings.
