# Toras Levi Yitzchak Tehillim and Purim supplement published

*1983*

In 5743 (1983), Kehot published the final supplement of Toras Levi Yitzchak, comprising notes on Tehillim and on Purim from Rabbi Levi Yitzchak Schneerson's writings. This concluded the publishing of his Soviet-exile glosses that the Rebbe had been preparing since 5730.
