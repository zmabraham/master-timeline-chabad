# Students cable Rashag on first night of Chanukah

*1945*

On the first night of Chanukah 5706, Rabbi Meir Ashkenazi and the Tmimim cabled the Rashag (R. Shmaryahu Gourary) in English, reporting that the visa department had scheduled their appointments with the consul between 20 and 31 December. They warned that all necessary papers had to arrive in Shanghai by the 20th, or the matter would be delayed by months.
