# JCA halts Mefitzei Haskalah funding mid-grant

*1903*

After three years of sustained protest, JCA stopped releasing funds, retaining 600,000 of the original million francs allocated for the Society for Promotion of Enlightenment's schools. The Rebbe speculated in a follow-up letter that the long campaign of rabbinic protests against Mefitzei Haskalah may finally have moved the committee.
