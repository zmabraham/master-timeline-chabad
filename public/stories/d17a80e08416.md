# Wedding of Rebbe Rayatz and founding of Tomchei Temimim

*1897*

At the end of 5657 (1897), the wedding of the Rebbe Rayatz took place in the great hall of the Rebbes' courtyard in Lubavitch. Immediately after the wedding, the Tomchei Temimim yeshiva was founded, and he was appointed as its director. After the wedding, an additional room was built onto his father's house for his residence.
