# Rebbe Rayatz visits Lodmir in Volhynia

*1932*

Traveling from Druskininkai through Brisk and Kovel, the Rebbe arrived in Lodmir to be greeted by massive crowds requiring police escort. He delivered the maamar 'Re'eh Anochi Notein' before Shabbat at the great synagogue holding two thousand people, addressed an audience of hundreds in Yiddish on the nature of Chassidus, visited the gravesite of Rabbi Shlomo of Karlin, and discussed strengthening the mikveh and establishing a yeshiva with community leaders.
