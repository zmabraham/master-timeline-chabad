# R' Avraham Dovid Lavut moves to Nikolayev as rabbi

*1856*

In 5616, Rabbi Avraham Dovid Lavut, the grandfather of the Rebbe (Ramash) and author of Kav Naki and Shaar HaKollel, moved from his rabbinic post in Romanovka to become rabbi of Nikolayev. He would serve as rabbi of Nikolayev until his passing in 5650.
