# R. Dovber Moshe Shmotkin passes away in Eretz Yisrael

*1929*

R. Dovber Moshe Shmotkin had relocated from Warsaw to Eretz Yisrael, returning periodically to visit Warsaw. He died on 12 Adar 5689 (Feb 22, 1929) and was buried on the Mount of Olives. On 29 Adar Sheni the Rebbe Rayatz wrote a letter of condolence to his sons and sons-in-law.
