# Hebron Chabad locates Cave of Othniel for mikveh site

*1848*

After receiving reports from Hebron Chabad dated 2 Kislev and 9 Iyar that a suitable mikveh site had been found, the Pekuam relayed the news to R' Asher ben Shimshon in London. He responded with an additional 550 grush to cover the increased cost. The Pekuam praised the recovery of the Cave of Othniel ben Kenaz from strangers but declined to solicit further donations due to widespread economic distress among former donors.
