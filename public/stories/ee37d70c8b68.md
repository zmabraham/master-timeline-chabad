# Rebbe Rashab purchases Hebron building for future yeshiva

*1908*

In Av 5668 the Rebbe Rashab of Lubavitch purchased a large multi-story stone building in Hebron containing eighteen dwellings, three large water cisterns, surrounding land suitable for additional construction, and a khan rented for two hundred rubles annually. The building would later house the Toras Emes yeshiva established in 5672.
