# Attached letter to Avraham Zalman Ginzburg of Vitebsk for tea from Moscow

*1870*

On 27 Tammuz 5630 (26 July 1870), in Ovruch, the Rebbe of Ovruch wrote to the philanthropist R' Avraham Zalman Ginzburg of Vitebsk asking him to arrange that four pounds of Gulubkov tea be shipped from Moscow to Ovruch. He explained that although tea was available in nearby Kiev, he could not drink that tea. Ginzburg would later become a mechutan of Maharash when his son married Devorah Leah.
