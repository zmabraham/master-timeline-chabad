# Aliyah of Rabbi Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk; Alter Rebbe turns back at Mohilev

*1777*

In summer 5537, Rabbi Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk and Rabbi Avraham of Kalisk led an aliyah of Chassidic disciples to Eretz Yisrael. The Alter Rebbe joined them as far as Mohilev in Podolia, where Chassidim and his fellow rebbes pleaded with him to remain in Russia to oversee their spiritual and material welfare. He stayed in Mohilev about a year and a half before returning to Vitebsk in 5539.
