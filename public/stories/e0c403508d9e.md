# Temimim assembly resolves to send students from Russia to America

*1924*

Following Rosh Hashanah, the Temimim convened and resolved by majority vote that they must campaign to leave Russia, sending one or two emissaries to foreign countries who would then help bring additional students from Russia to safety. The author was the only one at the assembly preparing to travel to America, and the responsibility for the American mission was placed on him. The decisions were submitted to the Rebbe Rayatz.
