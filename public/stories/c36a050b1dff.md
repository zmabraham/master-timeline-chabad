# Rabbi Chaim Hillel Springer

*1981*

Rabbi Chaim Hillel Springer (born 5741, 1981) is one of the Rebbe's shluchim to the city of Tzfat, deputy director of the Beis Chana Tzfat institutions, one of the rabbis of the Chach"l Yeshiva in Tzfat, and director of the worldwide ATAH Toldos Chayim.
