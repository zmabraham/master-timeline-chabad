# Maggid of Mezeritch grants approbation to 'Halakhah Pesukah' (Turka)

*1765*

The Maggid, who normally declined to give approbations, made an exception for 'Halakhah Pesukah' because of its value in clarifying practical halachic rulings. The approbation testifies to his deep concern for clear and accessible psak halacha among his circle and is the only such endorsement of his preserved to us.
