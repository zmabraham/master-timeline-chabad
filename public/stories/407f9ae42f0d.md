# Wedding date set for Rebbe Menachem Mendel and Chaya Mushka

*1928*

After waiting in hopes that the groom's father Rabbi Levi Yitzchak Schneersohn would be granted Soviet permission to attend, the date was finalized for 14 Kislev in Warsaw, with the chuppah at the courtyard of Tomchei Tmimim yeshiva. The father was ultimately denied travel papers. Mass invitations were sent to Chasidim, activists, rabbis, and Rebbes.
