# Document on distribution of Maamad funds written by Rabbi DovBer

*1813*

In 1813, Rabbi DovBer Schneuri composed a detailed accounting document about the distribution of Maamad funds collected from chassidim. The document was later entrusted to R. Pinchas Schick of Shklov, the treasurer, and after his death fell into the hands of his nephew. It eventually reached the mitnaged Simcha Keysin, who used it as the central piece of evidence in his 1825 denunciation against the Mittler Rebbe.
