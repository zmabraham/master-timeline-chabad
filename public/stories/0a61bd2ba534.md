# Daily Rambam study program instituted on Acharon shel Pesach

*1984*

In a special sicha on Acharon shel Pesach 5744 (1984), the Rebbe instituted the daily study of Rambam's Mishneh Torah in three tracks — three chapters per day, one chapter per day, and Sefer HaMitzvos (Likkutei Sichos vol. 27 p. 229).
