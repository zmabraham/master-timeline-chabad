# Levitin sent to Georgia as chief shliach

*1915*

During World War I, in 5675 (1915), Levitin was forced to leave Rakishok. The Rebbe Rashab then sent him as chief shliach to Georgia, where he brought additional Tomchei Tmimim students and established a network of educational institutions, eventually building a major yeshiva in Kutaisi over roughly a decade.
