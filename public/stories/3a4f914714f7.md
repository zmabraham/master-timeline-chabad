# Rebbe Rayatz visits Glubokoe for two weeks

*1934*

Traveling from Warsaw via a brief stop in Vilna, the Rebbe was received at the Glubokoe station by Rabbi Menachem Mendel Kupershtok and a large crowd. Over the two-week visit he delivered the maamar 'Az Yibaka' at the Lubavitch shul, received Anash for yechidus throughout each day, and prayed at the Starashele and Lubavitch synagogues. On 5 Tammuz community leaders informed him of their decision to open a branch of Tomchei Tmimim in the town.
