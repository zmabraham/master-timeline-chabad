# Shemtov arrested after Korosten conference work

*1927*

The secret police had discovered Shemtov's activities and his role at the Rabbinical Conference. After release at Passover, he was detained again in June on the same day the Rebbe was arrested in Leningrad. He was held for another thirty days and grilled about the clandestine Chabad system before being released pending trial.
