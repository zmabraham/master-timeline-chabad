# Shluchim banquet at Hotel Kosmos in Moscow

*1991*

The banquet opened with Tehillim chapter 90 read by R' B. Cunin. Speakers included Kogan on behalf of Agudat Chabad, Shmuel Levin, Yaakov Weisselbach of Kishinev, Shaievich, and Pidrovsky, with R' Kotlarsky as MC and R' M. Levin speaking in Russian. Important Soviet figures and journalists attended this milestone marking Chabad's public emergence in Russia.
