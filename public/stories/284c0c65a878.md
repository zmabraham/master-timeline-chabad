# Liadi destroyed by fire as Alter Rebbe flees Napoleon's advance

*1813*

After the Alter Rebbe's passing in late 1812, news arrived that the family's homes in Liadi had been destroyed by fire — described as the 'returning flame' of war. The Mittler Rebbe, then in Kremenchug, decided he could not return to Liadi. Many of the Alter Rebbe's manuscripts were lost in this fire.
