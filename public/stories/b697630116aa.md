# Author's first Shabbos at 770 Eastern Parkway

*1948*

The sefer Torah was carried up from the ground-floor shul to the Rebbe's second-floor study, where the leining took place on his writing desk before a tiny minyan. As a newly-arrived refugee the author was let in, wearing a heavy hand-spun woollen tallis from Russia that looked like a winter blanket. The gabbai R' Yochanan Gordon later told him the Rebbe could not stop looking at the tallis throughout the reading; the author kept it as a memento of 'what anash wore in the land of Egypt.'
