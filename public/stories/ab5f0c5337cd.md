# Rayatz instructs R' Yisrael Yehuda Levin to travel to Eretz Yisrael

*1949*

Levin had written asking the Rebbe Rayatz where to go after leaving Europe. The Rebbe's secretariat replied on 19 Shevat 5709 directing him to Eretz Yisrael, where he should contact Rabbi R. S. D. Zislin and R. M. Gurary of the Vaad HaMesader of Anash and Tmimim in Tel Aviv. The letter set the direction for Levin's emigration after the war.
