# Rebbe Rayatz returns from Otwock to Riga

*1931*

After his Tishrei stay in Otwock, the Rebbe Rayatz returned to Riga around the middle of Marcheshvan 5692. He remained there for two more years before the final relocation to Warsaw, and during that period a branch of the Tomchei Temimim yeshiva was also opened in Riga.
