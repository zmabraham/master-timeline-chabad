# Rebbe Rayatz acquires Shmuel Wiener's collection

*1924*

In Kislev 5685 (December 1924), the Rebbe Rayatz acquired the collection of bibliographer-collector Shmuel Wiener. The acquisition of approximately 5,000 rare books — including some 400 Haggadot — formed a cornerstone of the Lubavitch library's renowned holdings.
