# Merkos L'Inyonei Chinuch founded by Rayatz

*1943*

While other postwar Orthodox leaders focused on yeshivas as citadels of scholarship for a small nucleus, the Rebbe campaigned for fundamental change in general Jewish life worldwide. Merkos became the engine for that mission, predating other national Jewish educational organizations such as Torah Umesorah. The work would later expand into the global shluchim network.
