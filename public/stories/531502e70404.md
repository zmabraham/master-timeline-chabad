# Gubernia prosecutor approves Mitteler Rebbe acquittal verdict

*1827*

On February 1, 1827, corresponding to 16 Shevat 5587, the Mogilev gubernia prosecutor general endorsed the High Court verdict that acquitted Rabbi DovBer of all charges in the illegal-fundraising case. Eight days later the court submitted the approved judgment to Governor-General Khovansky for final ratification. This prosecutorial concurrence removed the last administrative obstacle within the gubernia to formal recognition of the Rebbe's innocence.
