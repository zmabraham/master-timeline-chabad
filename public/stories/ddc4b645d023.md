# Father Eliyahu Shusterman dies in Samarkand

*1951*

On 18 Elul 5711 (September 19, 1951), the author received a telegram from his sister Golda in Samarkand reporting their father Eliyahu Shusterman's death after a prolonged illness. The author immediately sat shiva in New York. Eliyahu is buried in the Samarkand cemetery alongside chassidim R. Eliyahu Levin (Pechicer), R. Yerachmiel Chadash, and R. Bentzion Geisinsky.
