# Letter from Maharchash"z of Lyady on 24 Nisan 5622

*1862*

This letter, one of seven from Rabbi Chaim Schneur Zalman of Lyady — son of the Tzemach Tzedek — was written while his father was still alive but in great weakness. It asks longtime supporters in Krasłowa, through Reb Dan and Rabbi Shneur Zalman, to repeat their previous year's fundraising effort on his behalf.
