# Tmimim emigration pact made on Rosh Hashana 5685

*1924*

Gathered for Rosh Hashana 5685, the chevrah of Tomchei Tmimim alumni committed that anyone who succeeded in leaving Soviet Russia for another country must labor on behalf of fellow Tmimim trying to follow. This compact shaped the rescue work of Agudas HaTmimim through the next decade. The author later operated under this mandate to secure visa affidavits and travel funds for many comrades — work which became sharply harder after the Rebbe Rayatz's 1929 American visit.
