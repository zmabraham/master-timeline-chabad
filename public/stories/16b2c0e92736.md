# Tzemach Tzedek released from secret-police surveillance

*1847*

During the years surrounding the 1845 clothing decree the Tzemach Tzedek lived under the watch of the Tsarist secret police and feared writing letters in his own name on sensitive halachic matters, leaving such correspondence to his sons. The surveillance ended in Nisan 5607, after which he could resume more open conduct of his nesius.
