# Pereshel facsimile of Meshulam Zimel's 1719 Vienna manuscript Haggadah

*1988*

In 5748 (1988), Tuvia Pereshel published in New York a facsimile of an illustrated manuscript Haggadah written by Meshulam Zimel ben Moshe in Vienna in 5479 (1719) for Natan Oppenheim. The 28-leaf octavo edition included a bilingual Hebrew-English introduction by Pereshel. It continued his annual project of reissuing rare Haggadah manuscripts.
