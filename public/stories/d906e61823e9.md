# In Cusco, Peru, with the emissary Rabbi Ofer Kripor (5777). Rabbi Yaakov Tzvi (Jonathan Henry) Sacks

*2020*

In Cusco, Peru, with the emissary Rabbi Ofer Kripor (5777). Rabbi Yaakov Tzvi (Jonathan Henry) Sacks (27 Adar I 5708, March 8, 1948 – 20 Cheshvan 5781, November 7, 2020) was the Chief Rabbi of England, a leader, philosopher, and author.
