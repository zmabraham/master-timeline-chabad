# R' Meir of Bychov's earliest known signature, from Tiberias

*1786*

The earliest documentary trace of R' Meir of Bychov is his signature on a Tiberias letter from winter 5546, signed 'Meir son of R' Yaakov of Bychov.' This places him in the Chasidic yishuv of Tiberias by that date. He would later become one of the leading shadarim of the community.
