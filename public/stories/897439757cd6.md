# Rashab publishes kuntres defending Hebron's primacy in Kollel Chabad

*1906*

The Rashab argued that although Jerusalem possesses greater inherent holiness, the present spiritual quality of Hebron was higher because Jerusalem had become contaminated by sinners while Hebron remained pure under vigilant rabbinic leadership. He cited the Mitteler Rebbe and Tzemach Tzedek's deliberate choice of Hebron and urged Torah scholars to settle there. He also announced plans for a new society called Chevronim to strengthen Hebron, though the previous year's troubles had delayed its launch.
