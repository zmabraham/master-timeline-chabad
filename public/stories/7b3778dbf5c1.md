# Rebbe Rayatz visits Lubavitch to pray at ancestral graves

*1922*

On Erev Rosh Chodesh Elul 5682 (August 24, 1922), the Rebbe Rayatz traveled to Lubavitch to pray at the holy graves of his ancestors. During his stay, he delivered a chassidic discourse in the synagogue located in the brick house. This was the only time he visited Lubavitch after assuming the leadership of Chabad.
