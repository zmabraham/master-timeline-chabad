# Kuperstock visits Globoke for Shabbos Yitro

*1930*

From America, the Rebbe Rayatz directed R. Menachem Mendel Kuperstock, the rabbi of nearby Fastov, to visit Globoke. He arrived for Shabbos Parshas Yisro, spoke publicly, repeated chassidic discourses, and left a positive impression on the chassidic community there.
