# R. Moshe Yisrael prints Mishat Moshe in Constantinople

*1734*

Between 1734 and 1735, R. Moshe Yisrael printed the first two parts of his responsa collection She'elot U'Teshuvot Mishat Moshe in Constantinople. The third volume was later printed posthumously by his son R. Avraham in 1742, and his sermons were collected and published as Apei Moshe in Livorno in 1828.
