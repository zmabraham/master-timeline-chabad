# Rebbe encourages founding a Tomchei Temimim branch in Braslau

*1934*

To Weitz the Rebbe suggested that a Tomchei Temimim student would be a more suitable rosh mesivta than the candidate Weitz had proposed, and that the cost would be lower. To R' Shneur Aron the Rebbe described Braslau as urgently needing a yeshiva in the spirit of Tomchei Temimim and asked him to rally local Anash to bring the plan to fruition without delay, coordinating logistics through Weitz in Vilna.
