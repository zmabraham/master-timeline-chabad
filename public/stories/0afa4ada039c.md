# Author begins importing Calabria esrogim to North America

*1927*

After Shavuos 5687 the author wrote to Tomchei Tmimim in Warsaw and to R' Yechezkel Feigin in Riga, asking them to ship ten Calabria esrogim to America in time for Sukkos 5688. Six were taken by named recipients, four remained unsold because of cosmetic flaws. By 5689 he was importing twenty in individual cardboard boxes, and the operation grew from there — he later credited himself as the first to bring Calabria esrogim to the Western Hemisphere.
