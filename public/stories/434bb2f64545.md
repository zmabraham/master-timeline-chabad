# Rebbe Rashab rebukes Warsaw chassidim over splitting the minyan

*1903*

When R. Yosef Yozik Horowitz established a second Chabad minyan in Warsaw apart from the existing one, the Rebbe Rashab disapproved. On 13 Tevet 5663 he wrote to him, expressing how heavily the split weighed on him because he sensed it would lead to division of hearts and machloket. The two minyanim would later become the well-known shuls at Franciscan 30 and Dzika 8.
