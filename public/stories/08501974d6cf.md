# Mariasha Putrefas writes Pleshchenitsy memoir

*1956*

In 5716 (1956), Mariasha Badana Putrefas, a native of Pleshchenitsy, wrote a Yiddish memoir recounting the beginnings of Chassidut in the town. Her account—covering R' Shlomo Kugel of Kiskoriene, his sons Hendel, Yosef and Gershon, and the arrival of Chassidut in the mitnagdic town—was later translated into Hebrew by R' Y. Mondshine and published in Kfar Chabad issue 883.
