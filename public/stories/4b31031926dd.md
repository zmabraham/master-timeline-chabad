# Matzah campaign delivers Passover supplies to Jewish soldiers in Far East

*1905*

Facing obstacles of an anti-Semitic regime, the need for a broad Jewish coalition, logistical nightmares of shipping thousands of miles, and securing funding, the Rashab and Rayatz intensified the effort. After much work, czarist permission was obtained and Passover supplies were distributed to soldiers dispersed across a vast geographical area.
