# Germany invades Poland; bombs fall on Otwock and the yeshivah

*1939*

At 4:45 a.m. a German battleship shelled Gdańsk, and panzer columns crossed into Poland. By 8:00 a.m., as students prepared for morning prayers in Otwock, German planes were bombing the town. Ten children in the Jewish orphanage were killed, and the new yeshivah building was struck though no students died. The Rebbe later admitted to the American students, 'I did not foresee it.'
