# Rebbe tours all floors of Schneersohn library at 770

*1992*

Doctors had encouraged the Rebbe to go up to the roof terrace to enjoy the warm weather. Before reaching the terrace the Rebbe toured every floor and department of the library — pushed in his wheelchair by his three secretaries Yitzchok Wilhelm, Moshe Klein, and Yengi Bistritzky. On the terrace he sat for tea while Rabbi Leibel Groner read aloud the contents of submitted questions; the Rebbe responded with nods of his head. The library visit lasted roughly half an hour, and the terrace visit another half hour.
