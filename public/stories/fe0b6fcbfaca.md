# Mitteler Rebbe petitions Governor Khovansky for release

*1825*

Having endured three rounds of interrogation and roughly a month of confinement, Rabbi DovBer formally petitioned Governor Khovansky for release. Six days later the investigators delivered their summary of the inquiry to Khovansky. The Rebbe's release on 10 Kislev followed shortly thereafter, marking the first of what would become three stages of liberation.
