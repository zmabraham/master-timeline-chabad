# Sixth Rebbe suffers heart attack

*1944*

Already weakened after the High Holidays and ordered to rest by his cardiologist, the Rebbe received the report describing how Reb Itche der Masmid, Rabbi Mordechai Cheifetz, Rabbi Yechezkel Feigin and others had been burned to death in a Riga synagogue. He slowly recovered over the following weeks but his health remained fragile.
