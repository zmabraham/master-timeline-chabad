# Magen Avos yeshiva absorbed into Toras Emes

*1912*

In a Lag B'Omer letter to his brother-in-law R. Menachem Mendel Nae, Shilo specified that the 40 ruble donation from Meir Vozlinski for Magen Avos in fact belonged to Toras Emes. He further warned not to hand over funds from Russia to the Magen Avos gabbaim without his knowledge. This documents how Magen Avos was administratively folded into the newly established Toras Emes.
