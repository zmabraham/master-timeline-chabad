# Announcement of Rebbe Rayatz's return from Kostroma exile

*1927*

On 15 Tammuz 5687, secretary Shneur Zalman Schneersohn wrote with the long-awaited news that 'today is the day we hoped for — our holy father returned home in peace and success.' This announced Rebbe Yosef Yitzchak's release from his Kostroma exile, following his arrest by Soviet authorities, death sentence, and dramatic redemption on 12-13 Tammuz. The release would soon be commemorated annually as Chag HaGeulah.
