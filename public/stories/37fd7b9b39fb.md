# Wedding of the Lubavitcher Rebbe and Rebbetzin Chaya Mushka in Warsaw

*1928*

On Tuesday 14 Kislev 5689 in Warsaw, the future seventh Lubavitcher Rebbe Menachem Mendel Schneerson married Chaya Mushka, daughter of the Rebbe Rayatz. R' Yochanan Gordon recalled in a later letter that the Rebbe Rayatz had urged guests to attend by saying, 'Imagine such a wedding where the Rebbe my father will surely be present, and the Maharash and the Tzemach Tzedek and the Mitteler Rebbe and the Alter Rebbe — and perhaps the Baal Shem Tov.'
