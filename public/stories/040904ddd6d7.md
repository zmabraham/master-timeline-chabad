# Germans close Chmielnik yeshiva

*1939*

With the German invasion on Rosh Hashanah 5700, study at the Chmielnik yeshiva was officially banned. Despite the prohibition, many students continued learning clandestinely in the women's section above the shul, and lessons persisted until the end of winter 5701. The clandestine yeshiva represents one of the documented cases of continued Torah study under Nazi occupation in occupied Poland.
