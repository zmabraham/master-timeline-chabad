# Rabbi Moshe Chaim Yehoshua Schneersohn-Twerski immigrates to US

*1924*

Born in 1867, he settled on Eastern Parkway near what would become the sixth Rebbe's shul. A distant relative and close confidant of the sixth Rebbe, his naturally joyous disposition made him the one who could console the Rebbe after the Holocaust photographs incident of May 1945.
