# Montefiore departs Jerusalem for Hebron

*1839*

On Friday morning, two days after the Hebron delegation reached him, Montefiore and his entourage departed Jerusalem for Hebron. After a brief stop at Rachel's Tomb they continued on, arriving near Hebron toward evening. Hundreds of Hebron residents came out to meet them, dancing and singing Psalms in their honor, and escorted them to an olive grove near the city where they pitched their tents.
