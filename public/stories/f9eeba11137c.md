# Roosevelt elected to a fourth term as US President

*1944*

Writing on 22 Marcheshvan 5705 (8 November 1944), the Rebbe Rayatz recorded that it had become known that Mr. F. D. Roosevelt had been elected President of the United States for the fourth time. The previous day's diary entry — written on US election day, 21 Marcheshvan / 7 November 1944 — had noted the day's significance.
