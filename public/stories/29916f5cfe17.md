# R. Yissachar Ber Horowitz asks halakhic question of MaHaRIL

*1823*

In the summer of 1823, R. Yissachar Ber Horowitz of Rogatchov — who would later serve as moreh tzedek in Lubavitch — sent a halakhic question to the MaHaRIL. The question mentioned that R. Shmuel Segal had already been away two weeks in Mohilev attempting to obtain a passport for travel to the Land of Israel and had been preparing for nearly a year for the journey.
