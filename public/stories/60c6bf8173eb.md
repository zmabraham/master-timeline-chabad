# Rebbe Rayatz thanks R. Yehoshua Isaac Baruch for reports

*1934*

In Igros Kodesh vol. 17, no. 6369, the Rebbe Rayatz thanked R. Yehoshua Isaac Baruch of Kovno for reports about anash holding public shiurim in Chassidus. The Rebbe wrote that such news literally sustained his physical strength, and blessed all who concerned themselves with this work.
