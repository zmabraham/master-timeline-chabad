# Di Yiddishe Heim issue 64 publishes author's Otwock memoir

*1975*

In the summer 5735 issue of Di Yiddishe Heim, on page 5 of the English section, the author published his own account of leading six students from Achei Tmimim to the Frierdiker Rebbe in Otwock on the eve of World War II. The piece appeared only days before his passing on 17 Sivan 5735. It preserved both the practical narrative of the journey and the Rebbe's words during their yechidus about his future plans for America.
