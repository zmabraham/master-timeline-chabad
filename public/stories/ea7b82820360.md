# Yevsektzia stages show trial against Lubavitch yeshivah

*1921*

The Yevsektzia 'testified with lies' and forcefully demanded that the yeshivah be closed and that its students be added to the army of workers. During this period they also forced their way into the Rebbe's home, seizing household goods, clothing, and silver inherited from his father. Three students and the Rebbe's future son-in-law Rabbi Shmaryahu Gurary were imprisoned for five days.
