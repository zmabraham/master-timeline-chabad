# Passing of the Alter Rebbe on 24 Tevet 5573

*1813*

The Alter Rebbe died on 24 Tevet 5573 while in flight from the Napoleonic war, leaving his Shulchan Aruch still unprinted. Leadership of Chabad passed to his son, the Mitteler Rebbe, who would oversee posthumous publication of his father's halachic and chassidic works.
