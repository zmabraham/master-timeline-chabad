# Author's father Ephraim Shmuel passes away on 15 Shevat 5693

*1933*

After being put on a transport to Ukraine, the author escaped at Sharafan station by hiding inside an iron staircase and Georgian Jews smuggled him back to Sachkhere. Back in Kutaisi a telegram arrived informing him that his father Ephraim Shmuel had passed away on 15 Shevat, and he sat shiva in the Kutaisi shul.
