# Second Berditchev–Liadi marriage links the dynasties

*1808*

Months after the grand Zhlobin wedding, a second match further tied the two dynasties: Rabbi Yekutiel Zalman, son of Rabbi Yosef Bunem and grandson of Rabbi Levi Yitzchak, married Rebbetzin Rivka, daughter of the Mitteler Rebbe. The Tzemach Tzedek—their future brother-in-law—noted in a manuscript a discourse delivered by the Alter Rebbe on the second night of Parshat Yitro 5568, identifying it with this winter wedding.
