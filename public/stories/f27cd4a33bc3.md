# Rayatz writes condolence letter on the passing of Rabbi Shalom Shlomo Schneersohn

*1926*

Upon learning of the death of his relative Rabbi Shalom Shlomo Schneersohn, the Rayatz wrote a condolence letter on 4 Iyar 5686 to the widow Rebbetzin Rachel. He noted that he had not shared the news with his mother Rebbetzin Shterna Sarah to spare her grief, and offered prayers and blessings for the family's wellbeing.
