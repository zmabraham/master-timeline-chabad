# Passing of the Raza, elder brother of the Rebbe Rashab

*1908*

The Raza, who lived in Vitebsk, passed away during the week of Parshas Vayera 5669. The family withheld the news from the elderly Rebbetzin Rivka, who was still alive. When she insisted on traveling to Vitebsk after a week without letters from her son, an elder of the town, R. Dovber 'Bere Avremkes,' gently told her there was no longer reason to go, and the horses were returned to the stable.
