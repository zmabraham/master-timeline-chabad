# Alter Rebbe's Hilchos Talmud Torah reissued in Lvov

*1799*

Unlike the anonymous 1794 Shklov first edition, the Lvov 5559 (1799) printing identifies the work as having been authored by "the author of the Tanya." The 34-folio duodecimo carried new approbations and helped circulate the Alter Rebbe's Torah-study halachot in Galicia.
