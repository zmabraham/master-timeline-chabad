# R' Avraham Dovid Lavut moves from Romanovka to Nikolaev

*1856*

Lavut, author of Kav Naki, headed a yeshiva and talmud Torah in Romanovka and was its mora d'asra. A surviving letter of support signed by the Tzemach Tzedek's sons R' Chaim Shneur Zalman, R' Yehuda Leib and R' Yisrael Noach urging anash to back this yeshiva must therefore date between 5605 and 5615. In 5616 (1856) Lavut relocated to Nikolaev, a move that brackets the latest possible date of the letter.
