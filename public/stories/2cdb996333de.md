# Passing of R. Chaim Yeshaya Schneersohn, rabbi of Romen

*1927*

R. Chaim Yeshaya Schneersohn passed away on 15 Kislev 5688 after more than a quarter-century as rabbi of Romen. A direct descendant of the Tzemach Tzedek through the Kapuster and Retchitsa branches of the dynasty, he had been a leading anash figure in the city during the late Tsarist period. His signature stands at the foot of the 1910 Romen petition to the St. Petersburg rabbinical conference preserved in the Rebbe Rashab's archive.
