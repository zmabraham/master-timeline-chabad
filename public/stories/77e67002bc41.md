# Rabbi Eliyahu Wilhelm. Rabbi Wilhelm at a Sefer Torah dedication

*1962*

Rabbi Eliyahu Wilhelm. Rabbi Wilhelm at a Sefer Torah dedication. Rabbi Eliyahu Wilhelm (born 5722, 1962) is the director of Ohelei Tmimim Yeshiva in Kfar Chabad, director of the Chabad publishing institute Chazak Educational Initiatives, founder of the Chassidus library
