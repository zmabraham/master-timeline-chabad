# Mogilev High Court reports Mitteler Rebbe acquittal to Khovansky

*1827*

On February 9, 1827, the First Department of the Mogilev High Court — judges Felix Marzhevsky, Porfiry Karpilovich, and Matvei Dekterev — submitted their verdict in the case of the Mitteler Rebbe to Khovansky for approval. They reported that no objection had been raised by the gubernia prosecutor and that 'Mr. Berke Schneur remains at his liberty.' Khovansky would subsequently request the full case file with a summary, which the court forwarded on March 22, 1827.
