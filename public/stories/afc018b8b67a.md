# Publication of edited Jacobson memoirs

*1996*

On 15 Shevat 5756 (February 1996), Rabbi Shalom DovBer Levin in Brooklyn, NY, signed off on the foreword to Zichron LiBnei Yisrael, the edited volume of R. Yisrael Jacobson's memoirs. The volume covered Jacobson's years 5667-5699 in Lubavitch, Shchedrin, Kremenchug, Kherson, Homel, and New York. Levin organized the fragmentary original writings into chapters with annotations, photographs, and indexes.
