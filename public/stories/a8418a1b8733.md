# Lubavitcher Rebbe publishes Haggadah for children with English translation

*1945*

In 5705 (1945), a Haggadah following the Chabad Alter Rebbe's nusach was published in New York with laws and Seder signs in English at its end. The 32-page octavo edition was designed for children. It launched the Lubavitcher Rebbe's project of producing Haggadot specifically suited for educating Jewish youth.
