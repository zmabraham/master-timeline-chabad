# Vaad Magen UMekhaseh founded to protect Russian Jewry

*1927*

The first meeting included Rabbi Yosef Rosen (the Rogotchover Gaon), Berlin chief rabbi Dr. Meir Hildesheimer, and Rabbi Mendel Zak. The committee laid out a plan to enlist major Jewish groups like the JDC and Alliance Israélite Universelle. Renowned rabbis including the Chafetz Chaim and Rabbi Chaim Ozer Grodzinski joined the effort.
