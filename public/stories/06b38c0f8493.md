# Hidden Rostov mikvah uncovered intact on Lag B'Omer

*1998*

Guided by the testimony of elder chassidim who had studied in Rostov decades earlier, workers on Lag B'Omer 5758 began excavating the suspected location of the Rashab's hidden mikvah. Beneath the basement floor they found the mikvah whole, and continuing to dig they exposed the lower reservoir — still holding the rainwater the Rebbeim had used to fill it, sealed there since 1924.
