# Alter Rebbe begins composing the Shulchan Aruch under the Maggid

*1770*

According to the preface written by his sons, the Alter Rebbe began the Shulchan Aruch while "sitting under the wing of Tachkemoni" with the Maggid in Mezeritch, around 5530 (1770). He started with Orach Chaim, completing that section within about two years, and continued reworking the code for over forty years.
