# Russian Jewish statute of 1804 permits chassidim to build separate synagogues

*1804*

Following years of informing against the chassidim that culminated in the Alter Rebbe's arrest, the imperial Jewish statute of 5564 (1804) codified the right of a divided community to split. Chassidim used §53 to found their own minyanim across many towns; the same paragraph was later cited in denunciations against the Alter Rebbe. In Pleshchenitz, for example, chassidim long blocked from a separate shul finally invoked this right to build a chassidisher shtibl.
