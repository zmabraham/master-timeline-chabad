# Chabad Haggadah for Kfar Chabad youth published

*1961*

In 5721 (1961), Tzeirei Agudat Chabad in the Holy Land published a Haggadah for youth visiting Kfar Chabad. The 32-page sixteenmo edition was based on the Chabad Alter Rebbe's nusach. A second printing followed in 5732 (1972) for the next generation of Israeli Chabad youth.
