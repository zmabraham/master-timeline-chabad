# Dr. Eliezer HaLevi sees Asher ben Shimshon's silver menorah in Hebron

*1838*

Writing for 'Mevaseret Tzion' (Tobolsk 1847), Dr. Eliezer HaLevi described arriving for the afternoon prayer in Hebron and being shown an elegant silver menorah hanging before the holy ark. The inscription identified its donor as R' Asher Shimshon of London, described as a long-standing benefactor of Eretz Israel residents.
