# Maharashab teaches Chassidus on Parshat Re'eh

*1914*

On the Friday of Parshat Re'eh 5674, Maharashab delivered a discourse beginning 'Re'eh Anochi Notein Lifneichem... et HaBrachah Asher Tishmeun' as part of his ongoing series on the union of light and vessels, including the exchange of their natures whereby the speaker draws downward and the vessel becomes elevated and attached.
