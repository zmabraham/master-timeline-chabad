# Eber's pidyon nefesh from Riga

*1941*

On Rosh Chodesh Adar 5701, R. Yehuda Eber sent the Frierdiker Rebbe a pidyon nefesh — the final extant correspondence from him. He asked the Rebbe to arouse mercy for his family, naming each by their mother's name, that the officials should mercifully grant the documents needed to reach America.
