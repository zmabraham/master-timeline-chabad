# Rebbe writes JDC head Morris Troper urging mass emigration of Polish Jewry

*1940*

Calling it his 'sacred duty to give firsthand information,' the Rebbe lauded the JDC as the only Jewish institution still working in Warsaw. He pressed Troper to extend relief into emigration assistance — first for the most urgent cases and ultimately for 'the wholesale immigration of Polish Jewry to a quiet place under the sun, where they can live a normal Jewish life.'
