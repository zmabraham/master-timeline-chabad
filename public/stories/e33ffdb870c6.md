# Tzemach Tzedek's good-conduct resolutions

*1841*

Manuscript 1124 preserves a list of 'good practices' the Tzemach Tzedek set for himself, addressed in the style of a man speaking to himself. Internal evidence places it in 5601-5603 (1841-1843), a period during which he was subjected to surveillance by the Russian secret police, which deeply affected his health.
