# Chabad Spanish-translation Haggadah by Natan Greenblatt

*1992*

In 5752 (1992), a Chabad Haggadah with Spanish translation by Natan Greenblatt and illustrations by Zalman Kleiman was published in New York. The landscape-format 17-page-plus-50-leaf edition served the growing Spanish-speaking Chabad communities in Latin America and the United States. It expanded the Chabad Haggadah project into the Spanish-speaking Jewish world.
