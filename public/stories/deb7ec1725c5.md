# Rebbe Rashab flees Lubavitch as German army advances

*1915*

After several weeks of travel they reached Rostov-on-Don, deep in the Russian interior far from the front. The Frierdiker Rebbe later recorded their hope to return to Lubavitch the following summer once the war ended. They never returned, and the departure proved to be the effective end of Lubavitch's century as Chabad's center.
