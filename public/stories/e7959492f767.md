# Yehoshua (Shaya) Yuzbitz, director of Toras Emes institutions, Jerusalem

*1938*

Yehoshua (Shaya) Yuzbitz, director of Toras Emes institutions, Jerusalem. Rabbi Yehoshua (Shaya) Yuzbitz (born in 5698, 1938) is one of the senior Chabad activists in Jerusalem, an activist in the local Tzeirei Agudat Chabad branch since its founding, director of the Tzemach Tzedek Synagogue (Jerusalem)
