# Final letter of R. Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk to chassidim in Russia

*1788*

The letter describes how the Alter Rebbe wore down his feet traveling 'to seek God' and was 'anointed' to be moreh tzedek for the Russian community. Printed as the 22nd letter of Likutei Amarim/Tanya, it formally transferred leadership of the Russian chassidim to the Alter Rebbe.
