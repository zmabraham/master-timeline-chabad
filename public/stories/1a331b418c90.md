# Levin replies to Aharonov on commission letter, eve of Rosh Hashanah 5760

*1999*

Library Eastern department head Mira Trifonenko proposed an international commission to identify Polyakov collection ownership, with Chabad covering all expenses. Levin suspected this would lead to hundreds of thousands of dollars in costs for hostile commission members while never actually allowing real inspection. Still, he offered to travel personally if needed, but only with a precise agenda.
