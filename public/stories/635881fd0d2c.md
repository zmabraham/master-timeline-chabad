# Rabbi Zelig Slonim sent to Australia as Rebbe's emissary

*1939*

Slonim had previously served as the Rebbe's emissary in England, Scandinavia, and South Africa. The Sydney Jewish News announced his arrival as a distinguished scholar endorsed by the chief rabbis of Palestine, the UK, and South Africa. He worked on projects including building a mikveh in Brisbane before departing Perth in March 1940 on a dangerous journey back to Eretz Yisrael.
