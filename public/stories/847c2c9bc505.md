# Moshe Wittenberg makes aliyah to Jerusalem from Vitebsk

*1882*

Wittenberg was a known upright and reliable man, a Torah scholar and prominent Chabad chassid. After settling in Jerusalem, his stature led the Rebbeim and senior charity overseers abroad to propose elevating him to manage Kollel Chabad funds for Eretz Yisrael. His arrival from Vitebsk would prove foundational for the kollel's modern financial structure.
