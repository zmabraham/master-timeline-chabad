# Wedding of Rebbe Rashab in Ovruch

*1875*

Although the tnaim of 5625 had set the wedding for Jerusalem in summer 5635, the actual wedding of the future Rebbe Rashab to Rebbetzin Sterna Sara took place on Motzaei Shabbos 11 Elul 5635 in the city of Ovruch. The Rebbe Maharash did not attend, for various reasons; he was known to have wanted the wedding held specifically in Lubavitch, arguing that if not Jerusalem as stipulated, then Lubavitch.
