# Ibrahim Pasha's army sacks Hebron's Jewish community

*1834*

On Monday 28 Tammuz the deputy of the king (Ibrahim Pasha) entered Hebron with his army, having defeated the Arab rebels. The soldiers were given license to destroy: Jews were killed by sword and arrow, women were raped, Torah scrolls were torn apart and trampled, tefillin batim were smashed open in search of hidden treasure, and synagogue vessels and curtains were looted. Survivors were forced to carry the plunder on their shoulders to the army camp two hours' journey from the city.
