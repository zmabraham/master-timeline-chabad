# Lag BaOmer farbrengen held at Tomchei Tmimim Vilna

*1940*

Mashpia R. Yehoshua Isaac Baruch arranged that the Kerzner minyan announce the farbrengen, and over a minyan from each of the Kerzner and Apatow chassidim joined. At the gathering he repeated the maamar 'Ein HaKadosh Baruch Hu Ba B'Trunya' of the Rebbe Rashab.
