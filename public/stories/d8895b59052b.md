# Rebbe celebrates last Simchat Torah with Russian Chasidim

*1927*

Despite the risk of arrest, Chasidim flocked from across the country. The room, with a normal capacity of six hundred, swelled with hundreds more. The Rebbe urged each Chasid to influence their community, declaring 'I will be with you.' At the train station thousands lined the platform, and one Chasid, Rabbi Meir Simcha Chein, wept, 'The Rebbe is going, and we are left here.'
