# James Baker asks Shevardnadze to return Chabad library

*1990*

During Chanukah 5751, through Baruch Hecht and producer Jerry Weintraub, U.S. Secretary of State James Baker submitted a request to Soviet Foreign Minister Eduard Shevardnadze that Chabad's request for the rebbes' library be granted. The intervention was part of broader pressure from American officials, senators, and press as the delegation pushed through the Soviet Foreign and Culture ministries.
