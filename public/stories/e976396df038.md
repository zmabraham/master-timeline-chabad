# Yakovlev meeting with Chabad delegation on the Schneersohn library

*1991*

A writer named Lanya from Dnepropetrovsk had spent days arranging a meeting with Yakovlev. That morning his office called urgently for R. Kogan, and Veronika, R. Aharonov, R. Kogan and Lanya went to Yakovlev's office at 12:30. The author remained at the Lenin Library with the yeshiva and school children to await results. Lenin Library director Volik then unexpectedly invited him upstairs for the first time, but he declined to come alone without the rest of the delegation.
