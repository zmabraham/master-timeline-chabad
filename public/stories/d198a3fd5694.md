# Rebbe inquires about ohel restoration in Lubavitch

*1948*

On 5 Marcheshvan 5709 (November 7, 1948), while living in 770 in New York, the Rebbe Rayatz wrote to R' Yonah Eidelkop inquiring about the ohel in Lubavitch. He asked about arrangements for repairs and indicated that he would cover all related expenses. He requested information on what repairs were needed.
