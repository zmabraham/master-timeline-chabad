# Krasov ketubah enters the Rebbe Rayatz's archive

*1925*

In 5685, the bibliographer R' Shmuel Wiener — known for his scholarly catalogues of Jewish manuscripts — transmitted an extraordinary ketubah from 5440 Crimea to the Rebbe Rayatz. It eventually became part of the Lubavitcher library's collection of more than fifty antique ketubot of over a century in age.
