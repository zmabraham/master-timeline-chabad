# Pinchas Klein details Schneersohn library remnant found in Warsaw

*1946*

On 19 Adar I, Pinchas Klein wrote his friend R' A.L. Kramer that, having been in Lodz at the end of the week, he had learned that a small fraction of the Rebbe's library — including many manuscripts and printed sefarim — had been found in Warsaw and was being held by the Historical Department of the Yiddish Central Committee.
