# Tomchei Tmimim bochurim arrested in Kursk

*1940*

Coming to daven at the Marina-Roshcha shul, the author was told by R' Zusya that the entire Kursk yeshiva had been seized. During the search of Gershon's lodgings the NKVD had found a postcard addressed to the author's Cherbinov apartment and taken it with them. The author's mother-in-law rushed to Kursk to plead for Gershon, his wife went to Shumyachi to nurse her ailing father, and the author himself fled to his parents in Zlobin to wait out the Yamim Noraim.
