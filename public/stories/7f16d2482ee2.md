# Ramash leads Sukkot farbrengen in Riga

*1929*

Rabbi Eliyahu Chaim Althaus wrote that the Ramash spoke for hours saying words of Chasidut with teachings from the Sages: 'The critics and cynics have been silenced.' News spread so that on Simchat Torah, a huge crowd gathered, and Althaus reported people leaving the farbrengen saying they had never seen or heard anything like it.
