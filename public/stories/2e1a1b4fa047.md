# Kiev yeshiva students arrested by NKVD agents after Simchat Torah 5694

*1933*

Two clean-shaven, fashionably dressed agents quietly entered the upstairs ezras nashim where the yeshiva learned, tested the two youngest bochurim on their gemara, then announced they were under arrest with police already at the door. The author escaped through the women's gallery windows while others were taken to NKVD headquarters; a sympathetic Jewish interrogator later returned most papers but denied them Kiev passports and warned them to flee.
