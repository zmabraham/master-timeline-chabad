# Diarist records somber assessment of Schneersohn library mission

*1991*

On Rosh Chodesh Shevat 5751 R' Shalom Dovber Levin wrote that the statements from Stas and Osadchuk made the situation appear hopeless and that the delegation's efforts felt like water wearing away stone. His colleagues disagreed with his dark mood and urged continued effort, but he felt duty-bound to report an accurate situation to Lubavitch headquarters. He resolved to keep the diary going regardless of his unsettled state.
