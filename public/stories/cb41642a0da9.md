# Wedding of Rebbetzin Chaya Mushka and Rebbe Menachem Mendel in Warsaw

*1928*

The kabbalat panim was held in the Tomchei Temimim hall and the chuppah in the yeshiva's courtyard. Tomchei Temimim students joined the wedding meal in a separate hall alongside the mashpia R' Alter, whom the Rebbe Rayatz instructed to distribute mashke to the talmidim for l'chaim. The Rebbe then addressed them with a sicha on the principle that 'Torah is preserved only in one who puts himself to death over it,' and afterward danced with the yeshiva's director and supervisors.
