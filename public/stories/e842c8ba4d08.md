# Rabbi Aryeh Zev Lipsker - (known as Arik Lipsker; born

*1983*

Rabbi Aryeh Zev Lipsker - (known as Arik Lipsker; born on the 22nd of Nissan 5743, 1983) is a public activist, one of the directors of Tzeirei Agudat Chabad in the Holy Land, representative of the Chabad community on the Rehovot municipality and deputy mayor on the Rehovot city council. Biography
