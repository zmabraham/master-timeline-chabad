# Berditchev publication of Magen Avot drashot by Rebbe Mahara'sh of Kapust

*1902*

In 5662 (1902), 'Magen Avot' — drashot of the Chabad-Kapust Rebbe Shlomo Zalman (Mahara'sh of Kapust) on Sefer Vayikra — was published in Berditchev. The 50-leaf folio edition preserved the teachings of the Kapust branch of Chabad, which split from the main Lubavitch line. It served the Kapust-Chabad community in the Russian Empire.
