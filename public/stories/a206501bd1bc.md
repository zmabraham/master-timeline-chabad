# Tomchei Tmimim yeshiva founded in Lubavitch

*1897*

Established by the Rebbe Rashab, Tomchei Tmimim quickly gathered cohorts of select bachurim and provided their full upkeep, including clothing and footwear for poor students. A later department for outstanding avreichim — men close to receiving semicha for hora'ah — was added, with the institution also covering their families' needs. Letters of support from the rabbis of Nikolaev, including the Rebbe's maternal grandfather R' Meir Shlomo Yanovsky, were solicited as enrollment grew and resources tightened.
