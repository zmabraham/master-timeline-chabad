# R. Shimon Noach Nimoitin appointed mashpia of Vilna

*1924*

The Rebbe wrote on 2 Elul 5684 to R. Shimon Noach Nimoitin directing him to repeat maamarei Chassidus before anash of Vilna and to travel periodically to nearby towns to encourage fixed Torah study and chavrusa learning. On the same day he wrote to the anash of Vilna asking them to receive R. Shimon Noach warmly and set up a venue for him to deliver Chassidus.
