# Lubavitch fair assembly raises funds for Jews expelled from villages

*1824*

On Wednesday evening, 20 Adar I 5584 (20 February 1824), Jewish leaders from towns across the Mogilev and Vitebsk provinces convened at the annual Lubavitch fair to address the plight of fellow Jews recently expelled from village settlements and left without means of livelihood. They resolved to send delegates to St. Petersburg to lobby imperial officials, allocating 60% of the funding burden to Mogilev province and 40% to Vitebsk. Trustees were elected — Alexander Ziskind of Dubrovna and Moshe Yehuda Leibowitz Poponia of Shklov for Mogilev (acting in consultation with Rabbi DovBer of Lubavitch), and Binyamin Beinish Nachumowitz of Vitebsk and Binyamin Beinish Zussamowitz of Polotsk for Vitebsk.
