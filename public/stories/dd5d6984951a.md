# R. Avraham Chaim Rosenbaum arrested in Plescenice

*1888*

As elected elder of Plescenice, Rosenbaum delegated the local administrative office (upravа) to his assistant R. Chaikel Rapaport, entrusting him with his official seal. Rapaport used the seal to issue forged passports and 'false benefits' to save Jewish boys from military conscription; when the authorities discovered this, both men were arrested. Rosenbaum was first held in Borisov for most of 5648 (1888) before being transferred to Smolensk.
