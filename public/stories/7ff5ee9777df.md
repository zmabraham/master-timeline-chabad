# Sheinin appointed rabbi of Dokshytsy

*1921*

In Tishrei 5682 (autumn 1921), the Rebbe Rayatz dispatched Sheinin to take up the rabbinate of the chassidic community in Dokshytsy. R. Yochanan Gordon, the town's shochet, later wrote of Sheinin's profound personal qualities and devotion to the Rebbe.
