# Wedding of R' M. Blizinski in Warsaw

*1926*

On 2 Nisan 5686 (around mid-March 1926) R' M. Blizinski was married in Warsaw. He later recalled the date precisely in a 1977 letter to his grandson, noting that the wedding took place just before the yeshiva left Franciscan St. 8 and moved to its new building. The detail helped establish the exact timing of the yeshiva's relocation.
