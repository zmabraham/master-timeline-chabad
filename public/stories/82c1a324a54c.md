# Rabbi Mordechai Dubin establishes Derech Eretz School in Riga

*1920*

The school laid the foundation for educational change in Riga. Its founding dean was an eighteen-year-old Rabbi Chaim Mordechai Aizek Hodakov. The opportunity to fulfill the Rebbe's later mission of transforming Jewish education in Latvia came in 1934, when Hodakov was appointed chief inspector of all Jewish schools.
