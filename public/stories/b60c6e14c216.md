# Rebbe writes Feigin about Soviet rescue caution

*1940*

On 15 Kislev 5701, the Frierdiker Rebbe wrote to R. Yechezkel Feigin describing the painful constraints in maneuvering for Soviet exit permits. 'Everyone knows how hard it is to move people whose word is empty,' he wrote, expressing fear that any pressure on the contact might prove counterproductive.
