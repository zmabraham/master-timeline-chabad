# R' Shlomo Zalman Havlin departs Rostov for the Holy Land

*1921*

Sent by the Rebbe Rayatz to renew Toras Emes, R' Shlomo Zalman Havlin departed Rostov with his family in mid-Elul 5681. The Rebbe wrote on 16 Elul 5681 to Shila on Havlin's behalf and on 19 Cheshvan 5682 reported having received word that Havlin was in Tiflis awaiting passage from Batum. The journey ended up taking nearly four months.
