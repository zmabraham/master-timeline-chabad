# Wedding of R' Moshe Ashkenazi and Devora Krasik

*1942*

On 5 Elul תש"ב the chassid R' Moshe Ashkenazi married Devora Krasik in Tel Aviv. As a wedding gift, R' Nachum Yehoshua Ershler, shochet u-bodek of Königsberg, presented the couple with his rare copy of the third edition of the Siddur with Da"Ch — the small Königsberg-printed batch with the openly-acknowledged title page — inscribed with blessings for them to celebrate their golden jubilee.
