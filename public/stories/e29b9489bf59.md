# Rivkah bat Shem Tov Cohen waives inheritance upon marriage to R' Shmuel Elbaz

*1717*

Rivkah's mother provided her with cash and goods worth approximately 1,000 ounces, far more than her legal portion of the estate. Rivkah and, on 1 Shevat, her betrothed R' Shmuel Elbaz (later author of Vayomer Shmuel) renounced all further claims against the estate. The dayanim R' Yehuda ibn Attar, R' Yaakov ibn Attar, R' Yaakov ibn Tzur and R' Avraham Tzarfati ratified the waiver.
