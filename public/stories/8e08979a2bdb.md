# Yosef Yitzchak's bar mitzvah

*1893*

On Monday, Tamuz 5653 (June 26, 1893), Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn celebrated his bar mitzvah in Lubavitch. He woke at 6:00 a.m., immersed in the mikveh, and prayed at the gravesites of his ancestors before reciting two of three maamarim he had memorized. The weeklong celebration drew Chasidim from throughout Russia, with the third maamar revealed only privately on the following Shabbat to a select group. His father blessed him that 'You should never be fearful of anything.'
