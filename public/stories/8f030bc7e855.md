# The Rebbe and Rebbetzin arrive in New York

*1941*

After fleeing Paris in June 1940, spending months in Vichy, Nice and Marseille obtaining papers, and crossing through Lisbon, the Rebbe and Rebbetzin Chaya Mushka arrived safely in New York on 28 Sivan 5701. Their arrival concluded a perilous year-long escape from Europe coordinated by the Rayatz through American diplomats and rescue networks. The date is commemorated annually in Chabad as a day of gratitude.
