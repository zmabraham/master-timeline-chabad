# Igud Talmidei HaTmimim alumni organization founded

*1934*

In 5694 (1934), three years after the Rebbe Rayatz had directed R' Zalmanov to compile a list of all Tomchei Tmimim alumni, the Igud Talmidei HaTmimim was founded. Its purpose was to unite the graduates of the yeshiva for joint efforts in spreading Torah, yiras shamayim, and the study of chassidus.
