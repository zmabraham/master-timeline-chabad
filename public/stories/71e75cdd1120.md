# Didan Notzach farbrengen at Moscow's Agudas Chabad synagogue

*1999*

On the night of 5 Tevet 5760, the diarist joined a Didan Notzach gathering at the Agudas Chabad synagogue in Moscow with roughly fifty Anash members and yeshiva students. He spoke in Hebrew while Rabbi M. Hochberg translated to Russian. He also joined a live broadcast to a parallel farbrengen at Beis Rivka in New York to report progress in identifying Schneersohn books that day.
