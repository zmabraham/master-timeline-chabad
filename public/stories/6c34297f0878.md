# Hebron Vaad in financial crisis as R' Baruch Mordechai resigns

*1889*

By late Kislev 5650 the Hebron Ashkenazi Vaad faced severe financial distress: R' Baruch Mordechai Halevi resigned, doctors and shochtim went unpaid, and the pharmacy ran out of medicine. The community's rabbis and notables wrote to the two remaining members, R' Binyamin Rivlin and R' Meir Tzvi Hershler, urging them to continue managing the Vaad's affairs until new elections on Rosh Chodesh Elul.
