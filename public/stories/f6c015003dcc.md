# Mittler Rebbe prints Siddur with Chassidus and Biurei HaZohar in Kapust

*1816*

Drawing on his own manuscript collection and copies circulating among chassidim, the Mittler Rebbe assembled the Alter Rebbe's discourses on the siddur and on the Zohar. Both volumes were printed in Kapust in 1816. These remain core published texts of the Alter Rebbe's Chassidus.
