# Havlin reports over 100 students at the Jaffa cheder

*1924*

In a letter to R' Yehuda Leib Horowitz, Havlin described how he was compelled to fulfill the Rebbe Rayyatz's instruction to establish the cheder after the chassidim of Jaffa pleaded with the Rebbe to provide schooling for their children. He reported that more than one hundred children of immigrants, who otherwise had no place for traditional Torah education, were now learning in the cheder.
