# Tel Aviv Haggadah illustrated by Y. Zimberknopf (Zim)

*1952*

In 5712 (1952), an illustrated and decorated Haggadah by Y. Zimberknopf, known as Zim, was published in Tel Aviv. The 88-page folio edition reflected the burgeoning Israeli Haggadah tradition in the early years of the state. It demonstrated the visual style that emerged in the new Hebrew-speaking artistic culture.
