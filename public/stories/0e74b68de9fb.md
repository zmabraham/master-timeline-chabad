# Berlin Haggadah illustrated by Y. Liberman published by Gunzer and Levin

*1925*

Around 5685 (1925), the book firm of Gunzer and Levin issued an illustrated Haggadah in Berlin with art by Y. Liberman. Printed by Rudolf Grätz, the 48-page folio included a special German title page but no German translation. The Yiddish translation occupied a separate 23-page section.
