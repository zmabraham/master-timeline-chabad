# Tu B'Shevat prison letter to Rabbi Yehuda Leib of Pleshchenitsy

*1890*

On 15 Shevat 5650, R' Avraham Chaim wrote once more to Rabbi Yehuda Leib of Pleshchenitsy. He thanked God for life and health, saying his days now seemed long but only until they passed, and likened his situation to the riddle that 'today' is yesterday's tomorrow and tomorrow's yesterday.
