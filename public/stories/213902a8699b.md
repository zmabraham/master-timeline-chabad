# Rabbi Avraham Menachem Danziger, 3 Tammuz 5681, July 9, 1921

*1973*

Rabbi Avraham Menachem Danziger, 3 Tammuz 5681, July 9, 1921 – 17 Adar II 5765, March 28, 2005, was the sixth Rebbe in the Alexander Chassidic dynasty between the years 5733-5765 (1973-2005), and a member of the Council of Torah Sages of Agudath Israel.
