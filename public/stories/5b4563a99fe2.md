# Pekidim VeAmarkalim letter volumes 1826-1829

*1826*

The Pekidim VeAmarkalim corresponded extensively with the kollel heads and gabbaim in every city, keeping copies in special letter ledgers. Fifteen large volumes are preserved at the Yad Ben Zvi library. The first three volumes covering 5586-5589 were printed between 5725 and 5739 in the series 'Igrot HaPekidim VeHaAmarkalim MeAmsterdam.'
