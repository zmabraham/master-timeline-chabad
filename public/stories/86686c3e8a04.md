# Release of Rabbi DovBer on Yud Kislev (Chag HaGeula of the Mittler Rebbe)

*1825*

On 10 Kislev 5586 (late November 1825), Rabbi DovBer Schneuri was released from his detention in Vitebsk. The date became established in Chabad tradition as Yud Kislev, the Chag HaGeula (Festival of Liberation) of the Mittler Rebbe, observed annually as a day of celebration, farbrengens, and study of his teachings.
