# Bris milah of R. Shilem Kuratin's son on 19 Kislev 5674

*1913*

A son was born to the mashgiach R. Shilem Kuratin, with the bris milah held on 19 Kislev 5674. The child was named Shneur Zalman, and the Rayatz was honored with sandekaus. The Rebbe Rashab was abroad that year, so the 19 Kislev celebrations were led by mashpi'im and anash.
