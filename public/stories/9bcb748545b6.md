# R' Shlomo Zalman HaKohen letter mentions passing of R' Menachem Mendel

*1788*

In summer 1788, R' Shlomo Zalman HaKohen of Vilna addressed a letter to the diaspora referencing the passing of R' Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk. The letter was also addressed to 'R' Aharon the Small,' confirming the latter's role 'attending the Rebbe' at that time.
