# Komsomolskaya Pravda editor agrees to publicize Chabad library case

*1991*

On the seventh day of Pesach 5751, Rabbi Yitzchak Kogan met with Mr. Filinov, editor of Komsomolskaya Pravda, delivered the mission's documents and brochures, and explained Chabad's case for the library. Filinov entered into the issue enthusiastically, promising not only to publish a series in his own paper but to draft a press release and circulate it to other Soviet outlets. The meeting marked the start of Chabad's coordinated press push in the Soviet Union.
