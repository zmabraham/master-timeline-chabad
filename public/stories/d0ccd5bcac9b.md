# Soviet law signed returning religious books and objects to faith bodies

*1991*

On 20 Tevet morning the delegation learned from the local press that a law had been signed Friday evening to return to religious institutions books and objects nationalized under Soviet rule. R' Levin assessed that the law did not cover the Schneersohn case, since Chabad sought the books for its headquarters abroad rather than for a Russian institution. The delegation continued pressing for an exception, as Lichachev had previously conceded the Chabad case warranted unique treatment.
