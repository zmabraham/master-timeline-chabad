# Rebbe Rashab discusses division of father's books with son Rayatz

*1910*

In letter 282 the Rebbe Rashab described how the books from the Rebbe Maharash's private study could be identified by their bindings, and referenced an old list of how the books were divided among the brothers, which R. Nissan Skvolla still held. The autograph manuscripts had gone entirely to him as successor.
