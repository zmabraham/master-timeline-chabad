# Letter on Chabad Chassidus translated and published in Yiddish

*1938*

Members B. Gadin and Ch. Mikhailover of Achos Tmimim translated the Hebrew letter (vol. 3, no. 837) into Yiddish and printed it as a standalone kuntres in Riga. It was titled 'Di Moralishe un Dertzierishe Badaytung fun Chassidus Chabad' and presented as an answer to an inquiry from Germany.
