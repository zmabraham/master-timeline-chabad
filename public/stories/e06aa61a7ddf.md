# Publication of HaTamim journal first issue

*1935*

The first issue of the HaTamim journal was published in Warsaw in 5695 (1935). It contained the first printing of a handwritten letter of the Baal Shem Tov addressed to his disciple Rabbi Yaakov Yosef of Polnoye, alongside notes on its provenance in the handwriting of the Rebbe Rayatz.
