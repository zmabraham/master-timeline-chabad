# Death of the Alter Rebbe

*1812*

In 1812, Rabbi Schneur Zalman of Liadi, the founder of Chabad and first Lubavitcher Rebbe, passed away while fleeing from Napoleon's invading army through Russia. His son Rabbi Dovber succeeded him and the following year settled in Lubavitch, establishing the town as Chabad's capital. The Alter Rebbe's flight reflected his strong opposition to Napoleon's potential influence on Jewish life.
