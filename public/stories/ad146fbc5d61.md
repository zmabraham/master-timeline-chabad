# Rebbe Rayatz reaches Riga from besieged Warsaw

*1939*

Trapped in besieged Warsaw for four months, the Rebbe Rayatz, his family, and his secretaries were finally permitted to leave following an enormous rescue effort mounted by Agudath Chassidei Chabad in America. On 5 Teves 5700 (December 17, 1939) they reached Riga, where they would remain about two and a half months while preparations were made for the journey to the United States.
