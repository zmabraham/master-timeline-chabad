# Rebbe Rayatz appoints Yehuda Eber as spiritual director of Tomchei Tmimim Vilna

*1930*

Eber, brother of the well-known R' Eliyahu of Krasnoluki, had himself been a senior figure inside the Soviet Tomchei Tmimim network before escaping with his family. The Rayatz's letter (Igrot Kodesh vol. 2, letter 498) introduces him to the Vilna leadership as a great talmid chacham and gifted young man. He served in Vilna until 5693, when he was reassigned as spiritual director of the central yeshiva in Warsaw.
