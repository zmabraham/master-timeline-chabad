# Future Rebbe boards Serpa Pinta in Lisbon

*1941*

The passenger manifest lists Mendel Schneersohn (46, rabbi, Russian refugee, born Nikolaev) and Chaya Mussia Schneersohn (40, Russian refugee, born Smolensk), both with visas issued in Marseille on April 17, 1941, and tickets paid by her father, the Rebbe Rayatz, with destination 770 Eastern Parkway, Brooklyn.
