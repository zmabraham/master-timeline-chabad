# Agudas Chasidei Chabad founded in the United States

*1924*

In summer 5684 Rabbi Mendel Leib Lukshin of Krivoyrog reached America carrying lengthy guidance from the Rebbe Rayatz, with whom he had spoken at length in Rostov about establishing the new society. The office was set up behind a partition in the back of the Kremer brothers' wholesale trouser shop at 687 Broadway. R' Moshe Eliezer Kremer accepted the presidency at R' Dovid Shifrin's invitation, while Shifrin served as organizer and secretary.
