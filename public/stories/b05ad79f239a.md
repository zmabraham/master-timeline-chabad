# Lieberman appeals to American Aguda to rescue the Otwock library

*1939*

Immediately upon reaching Riga, librarian Chaim Lieberman wrote to Rabbi Yisrael Jacobson of Agudath Chassidei Chabad in America. He reported that when they fled Otwock for Warsaw only the manuscripts were taken; the entire library — valued at perhaps thirty thousand zloty — and many household effects remained in Otwock. He warned that with Otwock falling under foreign control all could be lost, urging strong efforts to obtain export permission though prioritizing rescue of lives.
