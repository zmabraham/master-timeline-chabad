# Pinchas Klein sends list of surviving Tomchei Temimim talmidim

*1946*

Klein asked that his name and that of his wife be brought before the Rebbe for blessings. He listed surviving talmidim including Yosef Goldstein, a shochet in Lodz; Heshel Reizman in Częstochowa; Feivel Griner; Yehoshua Wolf Abramtchak from Koszwin; the Zilberberg brothers; Fishel Gitelis from Kowel; Yaakov Katz from Chelm; and Motl Bumelgrin from Zwolen.
