# Hebron Yeshiva Magen Avot renewed

*1903*

Upon the renewal of yeshiva Magen Avot in Hebron in 5663, R' Yaakov Yosef Slonim of Hebron raised with the future Rebbe Rayatz the possibility of affiliating it with the Tomchei Tmimim yeshiva network. The Rayatz, then practical director of Tomchei Tmimim, initially deferred the idea due to upheavals in Russia, though the affiliation was implemented several years later.
