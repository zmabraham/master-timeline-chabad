# First 19 Kislev farbrengen in Samarkand and birth of Miriam Reizel

*1944*

For the first time since arriving in wartime exile, the Chabad chassidim of Samarkand secured a large room and organized a major 19 Kislev farbrengen for 5705. The author hauled his big number-30 kerosene retouching lamp to the venue to illuminate it. Returning home, he ran to fetch a nurse for his wife's labor, but by the time he came back she had given birth on her own to their daughter Miriam Reizel — 'a chassidishe daughter' — at the very hour of the farbrengen.
