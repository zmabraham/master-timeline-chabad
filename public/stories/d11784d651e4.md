# R. Shraga Feivish Zalmanov marries and settles in Warsaw

*1897*

After studying for several years in Lubavitch, R. Shraga Feivish Zalmanov married Tzirla, daughter of R. Yosef Yozik Horowitz, around 5657. The couple settled with the bride's father in Warsaw, where R. Shraga Feivish would later become one of the central leaders of the Chabad community.
