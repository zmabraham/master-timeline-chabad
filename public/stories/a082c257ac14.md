# GPU closes Vitebsk Tomchei Tmimim before Purim 5691

*1931*

Soviet agents entered the Kleiner Zeit shul where the bochurim were learning and arrested them along with two adult Jews present. A GPU investigator examined the prisoners' headcoverings and tzitzit: the older balei-batim whose flat yarmulkes and short, thick tzitzit looked different were released, while the boys with round yarmulkes and long thin tzitzit were identified as Lubavitchers. They were released that night but the yeshiva had effectively been broken.
