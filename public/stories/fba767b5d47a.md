# Kfar Chabad Haggadah with 'V'Higadta L'Vincha' practical guide by Yekutiel Green

*1986*

In 5746 (1986), Yekutiel Green's practical Chabad Haggadah guide 'V'Higadta L'Vincha' was published in Kfar Chabad following the Alter Rebbe's nusach. The 127-page octavo edition included practical guidance and Chabad anthological 'pearls'. A revised expanded edition followed in 5754 (1994).
