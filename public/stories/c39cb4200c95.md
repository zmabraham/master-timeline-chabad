# Maciów community committee formally proposes hosting a Chabad yeshiva

*1931*

Signed by Rabbi Shalom Shachna Cherniak, his deputy Rabbi Yosef Lashtshiver, and the seven committee members, the letter described how they had deliberately left the upper story of the building unfinished to prevent secularists from establishing schools there. They offered the Rebbe immediate use of a large, ornate hall sufficient for thirty students, and invited the Rebbe to take an active interest in establishing a major yeshiva in Volhynia at this site.
