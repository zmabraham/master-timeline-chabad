# Anonymous donor begins funding Achei Tmimim children's class

*1940*

The funds enabled hiring R. Refael Nachman HaKohen to teach the small children ages 6-9; the yeshiva then had about 25 students. The anonymous donor, revealed later, was Baruch Eliyahu Schneersohn, son of the holy R. Mendel of Babruysk, a truly God-fearing man and great philanthropist. He insisted his identity remain unpublicized.
