# Mogilev High Court's First Chamber upholds the Mitteler Rebbe's acquittal

*1826*

On 29 November 1826, the First Chamber of the Mogilev High Court ruled in the case of Rabbi Berke Schneur. It found that the donations he received were given voluntarily by his followers and that no criminal offense had occurred, agreeing with the earlier Babinovich verdict. The decision was later presented to Governor-General Khovansky, who disagreed and appealed it to the Ruling Senate.
