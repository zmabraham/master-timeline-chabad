# R' Yehuda Leib Zalkind appointed rabbi of Dinaburg

*1818*

R' Yehuda Leib Zalkind, author of 'Zecher Yehuda,' was a leading disciple of the Alter Rebbe and study-partner of the Tzemach Tzedek. After serving in Wilkomir (Vilna district), he was called around 5578 (1818) to the rabbinate of Dinaburg, first in the old quarter and later in the central new district.
