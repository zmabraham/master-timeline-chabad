# Otwock yeshiva opens after Pesach with three classes

*1926*

R. Meir Blizinski enlisted a local bachur named Naftali Tzuker, who already had a small study group, and added two additional classes. Blizinski personally taught Tanya nightly to the upper class while shouldering most of the budget from his dowry savings and the Warsaw Tomchei Tmimim leadership.
