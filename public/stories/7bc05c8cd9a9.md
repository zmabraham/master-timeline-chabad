# R. Yissachar Ber Horowitz becomes moreh tzedek of Konotop

*1843*

After ten years as moreh tzedek of Lubavitch, R. Yissachar Ber Horowitz accepted the post of moreh tzedek in Konotop in 5603 (1843). He continued his halachic correspondence from there, including extensive teshuvot reflecting on his earlier years. He became known posthumously as 'the Rav of Konotop,' and the Tzemach Tzedek referred to him as such in marginal notes on his old responsa.
