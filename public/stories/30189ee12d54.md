# Mikhail Nachmanson arrested for counterrevolutionary activity

*1936*

On September 9, 1936, his birthday, former GPU/NKVD officer Mikhail Nachmanson was arrested for counterrevolutionary activity and transferred to Lubyanka Prison in Leningrad. Without trial, he received an extrajudicial five-year sentence in Kolyma. He was released in 1941, rearrested in 1950, freed after Stalin's death in 1953, and given amnesty in 1956, yet remained a committed Communist until the end of his life.
