# Rebbe Rashab publishes 'Cheshbon Katzar' on Petersburg Conference

*1910*

At the conclusion of the conference, the Rebbe Rashab printed a booklet titled 'Cheshbon Katzar from the Petersburg Rabbinical Conference at the Rabbis' Assembly, 5670.' In the introduction he wrote that he wished to present to his brethren a short accounting of the conference's actions and clarify the various decisions and their reasoning. The pamphlet was published over the course of the summer of 5670.
