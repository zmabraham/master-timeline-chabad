# R' Avraham Naftali Hirtzka Goldberg joins as ram in Warsaw

*1923*

Known as 'der Klobutzker' for the small town near Lodz where he grew up and studied, Goldberg later lived in Wielun before being brought to Warsaw. He was a classic Polish lamdan who loved pilpul; R' Shmuel Zalmanov reported to the Rebbe that his style was not quite the Chabad approach but that students were inspired by the vitality of his teaching.
