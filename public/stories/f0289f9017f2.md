# Gubenko letter formally refuses Schneersohn library return

*1990*

On 23 Kislev 5751 a reply from Culture Minister Gubenko to Dr. Armand Hammer's appeal arrived at Hammer's office, announcing the decision not to return the Schneersohn-Agudas Chassidei Chabad collection because it had been nationalized in 1921. Veronika dismissed it as a personal opinion lacking response to Hammer's letter to Gorbachev and continued working through the Foreign Ministry. Copies of the letter were taken in Russian and English and delivered to the Foreign Ministry the same afternoon.
