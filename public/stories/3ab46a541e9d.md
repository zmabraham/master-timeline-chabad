# Renewed Chabad-Sephardi agreement in Hebron

*1842*

Following the Ottoman return to power and Abdul Rahman's renewed exactions, the Chabad and Sephardi communities of Hebron updated their inter-communal agreement in 5602. The new accord, signed by R' Shmuel Halevi and R' Menachem Aryeh among others, formalized the separate 'ransom for souls' that the sheikh demanded from each community. This necessitated allocating him a fixed share in the Chabad kollel's distributions abroad.
