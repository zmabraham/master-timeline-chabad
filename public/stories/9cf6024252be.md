# Branch of Torat Emet opens in Rishon LeZion

*1925*

Building on the precedent set in Jaffa, a further branch was opened in Rishon LeZion. The Rebbe Rayyatz referred to both branches in his letter of 2 Shvat 5686 to R' Shlomo Zalman Havlin, asking for more detailed reports from Tel Aviv and Rishon LeZion.
