# Sheinin tours Vilna-region towns to spread chassidus

*1924*

After Sheinin reported in Shevat 5684 on his travels, the Rebbe responded (Igros Kodesh vol. 1, letter 171) urging more correspondence with chassidim, dedicated trips, and Tanya recitation in Donilovich. The same day the Rebbe also wrote to R. Yeshaya Friedman, shochet in Donilovich, and to R. Shraga Faivish Bronstein of Kurnits, both of whom had begun teaching Tanya publicly thanks to Sheinin.
