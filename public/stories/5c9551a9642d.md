# Death of R' Asher Grossman of Nikolayev

*1919*

R' Asher Grossman, shochet of Nikolayev and one of the leading chassidim of Rashab and Maharash, passed away on 15 Marcheshvan 5680. He had served as shochet of Nikolayev until at least 5678 and edited Rashab's corrected Tanya edition. Before his death he left dowry funds with Rashab for his still-unmarried daughter Chaya Frieda, which transferred to Rayatz after Rashab's passing months later.
