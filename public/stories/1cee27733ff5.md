# New York Haggadah with 'Divrei Binah' and 'Toldot Adam' Biala commentaries

*1961*

Around 5721 (1961), a Haggadah was issued in New York featuring the 'Divrei Binah' commentary by Rabbi Yitzchak Yaakov of Biala and the 'Toldot Adam' commentary by Rabbi Yehoshua of Ostrova. The 91-page octavo edition included a preface by the Admor of Biala. It represented the publishing efforts of the Biala Chassidic dynasty in postwar America.
