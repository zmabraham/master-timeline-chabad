# Rayatz at Tomchei Tmimim annual celebration holding London cane

*1943*

A photograph from the annual fundraising celebration for the Tomchei Tmimim Lubavitch yeshivos, held on 7 Adar II 5703, shows Rebbe Rayatz holding the cane catalogued as library item 5188, whose metal handle is engraved 'LONDON SOLID S K.' Several other canes from his estate, including one purported to belong to R' Levi Yitzchak of Berditchev, are preserved in the library archive.
