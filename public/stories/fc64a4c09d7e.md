# Favorable Schneersohn-library article in Nyezavisimaya Gazeta

*1991*

An article prepared by Filinov, former editor of Komsomolskaya Pravda and then head of an information agency, appeared on 2 Iyar 5751 (April 16, 1991) in Nyezavisimaya Gazeta, supporting the campaign to return the Schneersohn library to Chabad. Filinov had picked up the story through his agency rather than his former paper. He subsequently said he had no time to follow up and transferred the topic to a colleague, Detshkov, who set up a follow-up interview with the Chabad delegation.
