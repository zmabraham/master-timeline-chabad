# Alter Rebbe relocates from Liozna to Liadi

*1801*

Following the conclusion of his summer 5561 stay in St. Petersburg, the Alter Rebbe did not return to Liozna but established his court in Liadi. From this point onward he was the rebbe of an identifiable Chabad community rather than the universal leader of Russian chassidim, and the special Liozna takanos limiting yechidut were not reinstated.
