# Chabad yeshiva holds first day of learning at Moscow AGUH shul

*1991*

After a day delay caused by a missing bus on the planned transfer date, the yeshiva held its first day of seder at the AGUH shul. The author delivered a general introductory shiur to the upper class. The arrangement followed an internal division of responsibilities in which R. Kogan took over running the AGUH shul, the author took on building Agudas Chasidei Chabad activity and public relations, and R. Aharonov took the connection with Veronika Irina, food and clothing imports, and Russian-language publishing.
