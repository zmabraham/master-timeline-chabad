# Frierdiker Rebbe's family arrives in Riga

*1939*

After remaining in Warsaw through the early months of the German occupation, the Frierdiker Rebbe, his family, and son-in-law Rashag traveled together to Riga at the start of Teves 5700 (December 1939). From Riga they would continue to the United States, while the future Rebbe and Rebbetzin remained behind in Paris awaiting their own visas.
