# Publication of Maamarei Maharash on Likutei Torah in New York

*1945*

In 5705 (1945), 'Torat Shmuel - Likutei Torah - Matzah Zu - 5640' was published in New York, containing the Chassidic discourses of the Maharash Rebbe Shmuel Schneersohn of Lubavitch. The 94-page edition focused on the Pesach-related discourse 'Matzah Zu' delivered in 5640 (1880). It served the Chabad community's tradition of studying the Rebbes' annual maamarim.
