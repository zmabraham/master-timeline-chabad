# Arrests of Rabbi Meshulam Zalman Neimark and Starodub Jews

*1881*

In 5641 (1881), the Russian authorities discovered the conscription-evasion efforts of Rabbi Meshulam Zalman Neimark along with many Starodub Jews. They were arrested and held in custody for two full years, an episode documented in the Igrot Kodesh of the Maharash.
