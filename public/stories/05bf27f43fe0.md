# R' Tzvi Hirsch Harker continues to Tiberias on Alter Rebbe's instruction

*1789*

By summer 5549 R' Tzvi Hirsch Harker was already on his way back to Tiberias when he saw that Russian chassidic support was dwindling. He wanted to return to Russia to rally support, but the Alter Rebbe instructed him to continue his journey, promising to personally take responsibility for increasing Russian chassidic support. From Zaslov mid-journey he wrote to anash describing the Alter Rebbe's great love and dedication.
