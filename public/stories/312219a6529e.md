# Rabbi Kunin burns Rebbe's 'Lenin dollar' near Lenin's tomb

*1991*

On 26 Nisan 5751, after meetings with the U.S. embassy and Soviet officials, the Chabad delegation walked to Red Square to fulfill the Rebbe's instruction concerning the dollar he had given 'for Lenin.' The mausoleum was closed to visitors until the following week, so Rabbi Bentzion Kunin burned the dollar adjacent to Lenin's tomb. The Rebbe had told him to use it to elevate the side of 'sitra achra' and bring good news.
