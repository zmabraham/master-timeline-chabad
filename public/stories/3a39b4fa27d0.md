# Beilis blood libel trial in Kiev

*1913*

Beilis was accused of killing a 12-year-old boy whose body was found near the brick factory he managed. Prosecutors tried to link him and ritual murder to the Chabad community through his friendship with a man named Feivel Schneersohn. The Rashab and Rayatz played a major behind-the-scenes role, with the Rayatz enlisting attorney Oscar Gruzenberg to spearhead the masterful defense.
