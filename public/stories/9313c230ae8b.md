# Founding members of Dvinsk Achos Tmimim documented

*1940*

The girls were Abigail Golda, Sara Sheskin, Mina Schneersohn, Roza Kagan, Zelda Westerman, and Chaya Zayer - all from chassidic families, several with fathers who had learned in Lubavitch or Rostov. R. Peletz spoke to them about the Baal Shem Tov and explained that the Rebbe wished such a society be founded in their city.
