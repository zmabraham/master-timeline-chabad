# Russian internal passport issued to Maharashab

*1903*

On 2 January 1903 (16 Tevet 5663), the Orsha district police of Mogilev Gubernia issued Sholom Berke ben Shmuel Schneersohn an internal Russian passport. The document recorded him as a hereditary honorary citizen and second-guild merchant of Orsha, aged 48, residing permanently in the town of Lubavitch. The passport's validity was restricted to the Pale of Settlement and would document his subsequent travels through 1917.
