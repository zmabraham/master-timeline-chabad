# Feigin writes Jacobson revealing Peretz plot against Simchat Torah

*1930*

Feigin disclosed that members of the local left-wing Peretz youth club had infiltrated the crowd intending to cut the electricity and violently break up the hakafot. 'When the Peretz leaders heard the Rebbe's strident words, they instructed their followers to leave.' When Feigin later informed the Rebbe, he replied, 'Now I know that I was justified in speaking that way.'
