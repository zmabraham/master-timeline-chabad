# Death of Schusterman's sister Shayna in scarlet fever epidemic

*1927*

Scarlet fever swept Zlobin, with virtually no household spared. Shayna left a two-year-old son named Achiezer, who was later reported lost in the war. Rabbi Binyaminson's adopted son Alterke also died in the epidemic, as did Chaim, son of the melamed R' Shalom Horowitz, on his thirteenth birthday on Rosh Hashanah.
