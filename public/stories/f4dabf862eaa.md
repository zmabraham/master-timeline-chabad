# Dutch translation of Sephardic Haggadah 'Kol Yaakov' printed in Amsterdam

*1859*

In 5619 (1859), Jacob ben David Lopes Cardozo published 'Sefer Kol Yaakov' in Amsterdam, a Sephardic-custom Haggadah newly translated into Dutch with annotations. The 53-page edition served the Dutch-speaking Sephardic community of the Netherlands. It represented a fresh translation effort aimed at making the Seder accessible in the local vernacular.
