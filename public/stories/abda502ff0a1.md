# Author travels to Leningrad for Rosh Hashanah 5685

*1924*

The author's financial situation was strained, so he borrowed funds collected for ma'amad after R. Yosef the temimid's departure and bought a train ticket to Leningrad. His wife baked challahs and roasted two chickens for the journey. He stopped in Neveil to visit his brother-in-law R. Meir Simcha Chen and to see the famous chassidic town for the first time, lodging with relatives near the Rebbe.
