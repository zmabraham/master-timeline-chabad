# French translation of the Haggadah by David Drach printed in Metz

*1819*

In 5579 (1819), David Drach's French translation of the Haggadah was issued in Metz, following both Ashkenazic and Sephardic customs. The Hebrew original and French translation were printed page-by-page, with a separate French title page. The 127-page work served the French-speaking Jewish community emerging in post-revolutionary France.
