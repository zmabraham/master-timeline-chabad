# Shluchim of the Rebbe in the traditional photo (5785 / 2024), the seventh generation

*2024*

Shluchim of the Rebbe in the traditional photo (5785 / 2024). In the seventh generation, under the leadership of the Rebbe, the concept of shlichus became a central demand of the Rebbe — from Chabad chassidim in particular, and from every Jew in general — to leave one's place and devote oneself to spreading Judaism
