# Chabad delegation visits Yeltsin's White House on day of coup's collapse

*1991*

The delegation walked past the barricades of metal drums, stones and planks that had stopped the tanks and entered the main hall where Yeltsin was seated; Yeltsin noticed them and watched them at length. They met Ryzhkov, who said the new political moment brought the books closer. Elie Wiesel, also present, promised to raise the books with Gorbachev. The delegation later spoke at length in aide Sukhanov's office, who said he would try to arrange a Yeltsin meeting.
