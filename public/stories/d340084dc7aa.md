# Letter from Rebbe Maharaitz of Ovruch to his brother Maharash

*1870*

In the summer of 5630 (1870), Yosef Yitzchak of Ovruch sent a letter to his brother Maharash of Lubavitch inquiring about his health, his milk-drinking and gymnastics regimen, the status of the shadarim collecting funds for Eretz Yisrael and Maamad, and the progress of printing the Tzemach Tzedek responsa. He also asked Maharash to deliver, on his behalf, a gift he had bought in Petersburg to his son Maharashab (then nine years old), and to send a gift to his betrothed Sterna Sarah.
