# Famine strikes Eretz Yisrael

*1779*

In 5539 a devastating famine struck Eretz Yisrael, described in chassidic letters as 'a famine in the land such as never was before.' A summer 5540 letter referred back to 'two years of famine in the land,' confirming the hardship spanned multiple years. This famine deepened the financial crisis of the new chassidic settlement and intensified the need for shadar fundraising.
