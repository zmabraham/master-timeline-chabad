# New York Times article on Chabad and the Schneersohn library

*1991*

An article on Rabbi Berel Lazar, Chabad activity in Russia, and the disputed Schneersohn library, written by Francis Kleins, was published in the New York Times on 6 Iyar 5751 (April 20, 1991). The article was arranged after 'Stas' connected the team with the photographer Avakian. It notably omitted any mention of the official Chabad delegation in Moscow and portrayed Rabbi Lazar as the one handling the books issue.
