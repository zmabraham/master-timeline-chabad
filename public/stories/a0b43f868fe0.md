# Rebbe launches Maamarei Admur HaZaken editing project on Shavuos 5738

*1978*

The Rebbe's Shavuos announcement gave rise to a long-running editorial enterprise whose first major output was the multi-volume series Maamarei Admur HaZaken. Over the following years more than twenty volumes appeared, mostly arranged by the year each maamar was originally said.
