# Rabbi David Tzvi Chen, one of the great chassidim of the Maharash, the Rashab, the Rayatz

*1980*

Rabbi David Tzvi Chen, one of the great chassidim of the Maharash, the Rashab, the Rayatz, passed away and is buried in the Chabad section on the Mount of Olives in Jerusalem, 28 Kislev 5741 (06/12/1980) - Rabbi Avraham Yifrach, one of the great rabbis of Morocco
