# Rebbe's first edition of Haggadah 'Likutei Minhagim u'Ta'amim' in New York

*1946*

In 5706 (1946), the Lubavitcher Rebbe Menachem Mendel Schneerson published for the first time his Haggadah 'Likutei Minhagim u'Ta'amim' (Anthology of Customs and Reasons) in New York. The 47-page octavo edition presented a systematic anthology of Chabad customs and explanations for the Seder. It established the foundational Lubavitch Haggadah for subsequent decades and was reprinted dozens of times with additions over the following half-century.
