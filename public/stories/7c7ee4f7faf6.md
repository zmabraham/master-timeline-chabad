# RMM of Vitebsk and chassidic caravan arrive in Tzfat

*1777*

On 5 Elul 5537 (September 1777), the chassidic caravan led by R. Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk arrived safely in Tzfat. The six-month journey from Russia had cost many lives in a Black Sea shipwreck, but the remaining group of about 300 souls reached the Holy Land, an event documented in letters by R. Yisrael of Polotsk and R. Avraham of Kalisk.
