# R' Yehoshua Kostral writes RA"Ch a letter of consolation

*1904*

On 7 Shevat 5664, from San Remo, Italy, R' Yehoshua Kostral wrote to RA"Ch to console him on the death of his wife, lamenting that he hardly knew who shared RA"Ch's home in America — perhaps his younger son R' Yosef who had married there. He gently raised the question of remarriage, noting RA"Ch was not yet old enough to live alone.
