# Chevrat Mishmeret HaBoker veHaErev founded in Mantua

*1702*

On Rosh Chodesh Nisan 5462 (March 1702) the 'Chevrat Mishmeret HaBoker veHaErev' was established in Mantua for those who would rise early and remain late on Shabbat for Torah study. Thirteen years later the society celebrated its founding.
