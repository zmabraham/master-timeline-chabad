# R. Moshe Eliyahu Gerlitzky sent to teach in Łódź

*1936*

Having studied in Otwock until 5697, R. Moshe Eliyahu Gerlitzky was instructed by the Rebbe to return to Łódź — this time as a teacher. He found a group of devoted chassidish bochurim and taught them nigleh and chassidus through 5699, ultimately managing the division during the final two years of the yeshiva's existence.
