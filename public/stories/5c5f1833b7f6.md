# R' Moshe Yaffe travels as Tiberias chassidim emissary

*1846*

R' Moshe ben David Yaffe, listed in the 5609 census as a talmid chacham and emissary, undertook a second mission in 5606. This later mission was on behalf of the chassidic community of Tiberias rather than Hebron, as documented in 'Shluchei Eretz Yisrael' p. 788.
