# Rebbe Rashab arrives in Orel

*1915*

On Monday evening, 17 Marcheshvan 5676, the Rebbe Rashab's party arrived in Orel at 7:00 p.m. after a tightly packed journey of roughly twenty-nine hours. Unable to find suitable lodgings, they stayed in three rooms at the home of R. David Fradkin while sharing a common dining room. Despite efforts by R. Yaakov the shochet, no permanent residence in Orel could be secured.
