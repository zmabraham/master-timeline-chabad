# Hebron Chabad rabbis issue 5649 kruz

*1889*

In 5649 (1889), R' Shmuel Menachem Chaikin and R' Yehuda Leib Shaul Levin co-signed another kruz related to the Hebron Chabad kollel. By this point Levin was consistently signing with the title 'rav and moreh in Hebron,' while Chaikin signed without titles. The signing order — Chaikin first, then Levin — reflected respect for the elder while acknowledging Levin's official rabbinic role.
