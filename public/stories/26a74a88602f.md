# Institute for preparing Rebbeim's maamarim founded on Shavuos 5738

*1978*

In a special sicha on Shavuos 5738 (1978), the Rebbe announced the opening of an institute dedicated to preparing the chassidic discourses of the previous Rebbeim for publication. The institute produced the series of Maamarei Admur HaZaken, Maamarei Admur HaEmtzaei, Ohr HaTorah of the Tzemach Tzedek, Toras Shmuel of the Rebbe Maharash, and Sefer HaMaamarim of the Rashab and Rayatz — about two hundred new volumes prepared from manuscript.
