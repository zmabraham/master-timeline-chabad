# Summer 1772: Volyn tzaddikim convene at Rovno around the Maggid

*1772*

The Alter Rebbe and R. Avraham of Kalisk arrived together; R. Avraham, fearful of the Maggid's reproach, waited at the outskirts while R. Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk interceded on his behalf. Only then did the Alter Rebbe bring him into the Maggid's chamber. The gathering attempted to coordinate the chassidic response to the herem.
