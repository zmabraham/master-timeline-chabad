# Hoshana Rabbah 5723 hakafot mission and 770 farbrengen

*1962*

On Erev Shabbat Hoshana Rabbah 5723, Levin and others were sent according to a list to far-away shuls to gladden Jews with hakafot. They later gathered at Rabbi Yaakov Hecht's shul for kiddush and walked together with song and dance back to 770. At the farbrengen the Rebbe asked all those who had gone to gladden others to say l'chaim and recite Atah Hareita and the hakafot — a peak of joy as the Rebbe danced with the Rashag during the first and seventh hakafot.
