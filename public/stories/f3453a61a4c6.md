# Chabad demonstration at Russian State Library over book transfer

*1991*

Despite a fresh court order compelling the library to release the books, the directors refused to comply, and the lobby filled with prayers, communal singing, lectures on bibliography by the diarist, and arguments with opponents. Press, television and a US embassy representative were present. The library leadership lobbied Yeltsin's government and new-old foreign minister Shevardnadze, while Chabad activists pushed Yeltsin's deputy Primokin and the justice minister to force compliance.
