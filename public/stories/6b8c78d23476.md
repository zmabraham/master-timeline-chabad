# Leningrad rabbis arrested including Shimon Lazaroff

*1930*

The indictment named Lazaroff as head of the religious association Tzemach Tzedek, accusing him of organizing unlawful yeshivas and chadarim funded by foreign capitalists. Sentenced to ten years, he died in a prison camp in 1933. Despite arrests the underground system showed remarkable resilience, with yeshivas closing in one town and reopening in another.
