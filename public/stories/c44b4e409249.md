# Rebbe Rayatz departs Riga for the United States

*1940*

After roughly two and a half months in Riga, the Rebbe Rayatz set out for the United States on 24 Adar I 5700 (March 4, 1940). The relocation marked the transfer of the headquarters of the Chabad movement from war-torn Europe to America, consistent with planning begun in winter 5699.
