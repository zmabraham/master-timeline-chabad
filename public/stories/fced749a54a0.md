# American talmidim arrive in Riga and cable Agudas Chabad

*1939*

After spending Shabbos in Warsaw with help from the Joint and the American consulate, the talmidim continued to Riga, where they sent a cable to Agudas Chabad in New York reading: 'Arrived safely Riga out of danger. Rabbi well Otwock.' The signatories were the three Hechts, Barnetzky, Levy, Kalodny, Gordon, Greenberg, Fisher, and Altein.
