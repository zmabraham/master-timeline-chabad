# Rebbetzin Sterna Sara's letter from Riga about the impending wedding

*1928*

On Rosh Chodesh Kislev 5689 from Riga, Rebbetzin Sterna Sara wrote in Yiddish to her relative Rabbi Shlomo Yehuda Leib Eliezerov, describing the hardships the family had endured in Soviet Russia and the joyous transition to her granddaughter Chaya Mushka's upcoming wedding. She told him she had recently had the opportunity to meet his nephew the chosson Menachem Mendel and found him 'a quite distinguished young man,' inviting Eliezerov to share in the simcha even from afar.
