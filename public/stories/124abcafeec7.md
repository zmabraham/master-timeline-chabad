# Cunin and Koegan present menorah request at Gorbachev's office

*1991*

On 30 Shevat 5751 / Rosh Chodesh Adar, Rabbis Cunin and Koegan went to Gorbachev's office to seek a thank-you audience in connection with the menorah they had presented earlier and the order to return the seforim. The secretary who had received the menorah recognized them, and after a two-hour wait — during which Rabbi Cunin recited Tehillim and davened Minchah in the waiting room — they were ushered in to chief secretary Malokoyedov, who took down their details and promised a reply within a day or two.
