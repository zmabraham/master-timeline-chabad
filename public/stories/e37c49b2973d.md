# Release of the Mitteler Rebbe on 10 Kislev 5586

*1825*

On 10 Kislev 5586 (approximately November 8, 1825) the Mitteler Rebbe was released from imprisonment, a date that became the annual chassidic festival of Yud Kislev. The summary report by investigators Loginov and Markov, compiling all the interrogations and seized documents, was written three days after this release. Although the Rebbe was freed in body, the case formally continued through the Russian courts for another fifteen months until his final acquittal.
