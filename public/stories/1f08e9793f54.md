# R' Chaim Kavash born in Vyetka

*1889*

Born in Vyetka in 5649, Chaim Kavash went on to study in the Tomchei Tmimim yeshiva in Lubavitch and to serve as a rabbi in Yochavitch and a refugee community leader in Samarkand. His estate preserved several artifacts of disputed but reputed Baal Shem Tov and early-chassidic provenance.
