# Rebbe receives Slavita Sefer Torah

*1954*

In late 5714 (1954) the Rebbe received the small Sefer Torah of the Slavita brothers — the holy printers arrested by the Tzarist government, who read from this scroll during their many years of imprisonment. From then on the Rebbe used it for his aliyos on Shabbos and Monday/Thursday, and held it during Hakafos. A wall-mounted Aron Kodesh was built to house it and later transferred to the Library.
