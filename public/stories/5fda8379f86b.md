# Rabbi Yisrael Shalom Yosef of Mezhibuzh appoints Asher Zelig Muterperel as rabbi of Michelfaliye

*1898*

Dated Parashat Acharei 5658 in Mezhibuzh, the letter approves Asher Zelig Muterperel — son of Rabbi Shalom of Ba'ar — to succeed his late father-in-law as rabbi of Michelfaliye. The rebbe instructs the community to support him generously so 'no stranger shall sit on his throne' and so he can devote himself to study.
