# Rebbe escapes German-occupied Poland to New York via Riga

*1940*

After escaping German-occupied Poland, the Rebbe made few public appearances during a short second stay in Riga before departing to the US. One of these was a rare public talk to Achot Hatemimim. The wandering ended in New York, where the Rebbe established Chabad's permanent base at 770 Eastern Parkway.
