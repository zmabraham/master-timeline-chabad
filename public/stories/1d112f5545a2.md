# Rabbis' March on Washington

*1943*

Organized by Peter Bergson with Vaad Hatzalah and Agudas Harabonim, the march was the only Jewish demonstration at the Capitol during the war. The Sixth Rebbe, unable to travel, sent a delegation of four — Mindel, Quint, Gurary, and Binyamin Levitin. President Roosevelt left the White House by a back exit on the advice of Wise and Roseman.
