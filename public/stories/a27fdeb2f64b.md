# Sefer Ha-Agur printed in Rimini by Gershom Soncino

*1526*

In 5286 (1526), Gershom Soncino printed Sefer Ha-Agur of Rabbi Yaakov Landau at his Rimini press. Landau, a German halachist who had earlier served as proofreader at the Naples press, had first issued the book in Naples; the Rimini edition followed.
