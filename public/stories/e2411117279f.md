# R. Levin receives Rebbe's letter dated 27 Nissan 5707

*1947*

Through the Paris office Levin received a letter from the Frierdiker Rebbe dated 27 Nissan 5707, blessing his health and that of his household along with success in their material and spiritual needs. This was part of a series of letters now flowing between Pocking and the Rebbe via the Paris lishka.
