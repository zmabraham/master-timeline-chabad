# Passing of R. Shmuel HaLevi Levin in Jerusalem

*1913*

R. Shmuel b. Shlomo Tzvi HaLevi Levin, the former Lubavitch moreh tzedek, died on 18 Marcheshvan 5674 (November 1913) in his Jerusalem home, having purchased his burial plot three years earlier. He was buried in the Old Cemetery (har hazeitim). The Kolel Chabad chevra kadisha register identifies him as 'the elder, who was called the Rav of Lubavitch.'
