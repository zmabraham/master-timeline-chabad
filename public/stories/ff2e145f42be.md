# Tiberias plague quarantine ends as city gates reopen

*1791*

After the Adar–Tammuz 5551 plague quarantine, Tiberias's gates were reopened in Tammuz. The Chasidic community wrote letters of thanksgiving to its overseas supporters describing the lifting of the closure. The timely arrival of the shadar R' Meir of Bychov soon after was seen as a divine deliverance.
