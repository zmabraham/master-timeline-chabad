# Levin and Teitelbaum smuggle a letter to the Rebbe

*1943*

After years cut off from the Rebbe, an opening to write through Eretz Yisrael appeared in 5703. On the first day of the week of Bamidbar, Levin and Michael Teitelbaum jointly wrote a Yiddish letter — Teitelbaum opening, Levin closing — and gave it to R' Leib Cohen for transmission. Cohen, who did not know the writers, forwarded it with a note that it had come from Samarkand via Mrs. A. Dvoskin (Teitelbaum's wife).
