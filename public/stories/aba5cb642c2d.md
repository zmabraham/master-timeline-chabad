# Author remains in Lubavitch for Pesach 5668

*1908*

As Pesach 5668 approached, the author wrote home asking for travel funds, but the mashgiach R. Yechezkel Himmelstein denied him leave. After Pesach his father informed him that supporting him was a hardship, and he should seek yeshiva stipends. The Rebbetzin Shterna Sarah's 'krupnik' meals, lodging arrangements, and modest cash from the yeshiva and bachurim's fund began his life as an exile-student.
