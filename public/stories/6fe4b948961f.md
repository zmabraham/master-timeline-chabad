# Rebbe Rashab begins nesius in Lubavitch

*1882*

Upon the passing of the Maharash in 5643 (1882), his son Rabbi Sholom Dovber Schneersohn (Rashab) assumed leadership of Chabad. He remained in Lubavitch for the next three decades, founding the Tomchei Tmimim yeshiva and corresponding extensively with the town's moreh tzedek R. Dovid Yakobson.
