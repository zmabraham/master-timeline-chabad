# Senator Wagner appeals to Secretary of State Hull regarding the Rebbe's fate

*1939*

On the eve of Yom Kippur, Wagner relayed appeals from leading New York Jews seeking the Rebbe's whereabouts. Days later, House Foreign Affairs chairman Sol Bloom replied that communication with Poland had been disrupted but the department would advise when contact was reestablished. The exchange marked the entry of US government channels into the rescue effort.
