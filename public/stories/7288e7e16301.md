# Tu BiShvat farbrengen launches Samarkand Tomchei Tmimim

*1942*

On Tu BiShvat 5702 the chassidim gathered at R' Yehoshua Karf's home, where Michael Teitelbaum urged arousing mercy for Anash and called for starting a Tomchei Tmimim in Samarkand. They began with three boys — Sholom Levertov, Zelig Katzman, and Heishke Dobrawski — each chassid pledging to feed and teach them on assigned days. The yeshiva soon expanded greatly once R' Mendel Futerfas and R' Abba Pliskin arrived to hire teachers and run it.
