# 30 crates of sefarim still held by Schenker in Warsaw, Rebbe seeks transit route

*1941*

On 9 Cheshvan 5702 the Rebbe wrote that the New York shipment was being unpacked. He noted that among the 100 boxes received were seven of household items of little value, and that some 30 more crates of sefarim remained in Warsaw; he asked Chasdan to find any forwarding firm with a Warsaw connection to retrieve them.
