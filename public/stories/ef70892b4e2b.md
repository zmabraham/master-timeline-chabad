# Petersburg rabbinical conference debates rabbinic credentials and chinuch

*1910*

In Adar 5670, leading rabbis of the Russian Empire assembled in St. Petersburg for a rabbinical conference whose agenda included the proposed requirement that rabbis hold a secular-education certificate, the future of the kazyonny (crown) rabbinate, and the place of secular studies in the cheder system. The Rebbe Rashab invested years of preparation in shaping the lineup of attendees and their positions, urging opposition to mandatory secular credentials and to the modernist reforms championed by the maskilic attorney Henry Slosberg. Communities such as Vitebsk and Romen submitted petitions backing the Rashab's positions, several of which were preserved in his archive.
