# Sixth Rebbe disembarks at Pier 97 in Manhattan

*1940*

Thousands welcomed the Rebbe as he came down the gangway in a wheelchair, bedecked in a shtreimel. When a reporter expressed skepticism, the Rebbe insisted 'America iz nisht aderesh — America is no different. It will become a center of Torah.' Later that day he addressed crowds at the Greystone Hotel, calling for rescue of Polish Jewry and the founding of a yeshivah.
