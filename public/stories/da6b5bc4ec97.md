# Frierdiker Rebbe writes about Senator Borah's library intervention

*1932*

In August 1932, the Frierdiker Rebbe (Rayatz) wrote a letter discussing efforts by US Senator William Borah to recover the Chabad library held by Soviet authorities. The letter appears in his published correspondence (vol. 11, letter 3881). Borah was known for raising religious freedom issues regarding the USSR.
