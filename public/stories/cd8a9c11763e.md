# Dvinsk committee letter to Rabbi Goldfein on printing Rogatchover writings

*1936*

On 19 Adar 5696, immediately after the shiva, a member of the committee organized by Rebbetzin Rivka wrote on her behalf to her son-in-law Rabbi Abba David Goldfein in his name, asking him to come to Dvinsk for several months to edit and prepare the Rogatchover's manuscripts for publication.
