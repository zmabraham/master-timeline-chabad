# Rosenbaum counts months to release in Rosh Hashanah-period letter

*1889*

From the Smolensk jail on 25 Tishrei 5650, Reb Avraham Chaim told the young R' Zev Wolf Kugel that he could not yet share substantive news, but in about eight months he hoped to bring glad tidings. He interpreted the 240 days remaining of his sentence as the gematria of 'mar' (bitterness) that would be transformed into 'dror' (freedom), invoking the blessing 'matir asurim.'
