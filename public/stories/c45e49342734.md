# Rebbe Rayatz exhibition closes on jubilee yahrzeit, 10 Shvat 5761

*2001*

The 10 Shvat 5761 close date matched the half-century since the Rebbe Rayatz's 1950 passing and the seventh Rebbe's acceptance of the Chabad nesius. The yom hilula remains the central Chabad date for the seventh Rebbe's leadership.
