# Rashab and family flee Lubavitch for Rostov

*1916*

At the start of 5676 (late 1915), with World War I raging and the German army advancing toward the Lubavitch region, Rashab and his household were forced to flee Lubavitch. After spending several days in Orel, they settled in Rostov-on-Don, deep within the Russian interior. The Tomchei Tmimim yeshiva also relocated.
