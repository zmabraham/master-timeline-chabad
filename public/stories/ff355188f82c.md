# R' Yehuda Leib Slonim makes his last trip to Lubavitch

*1885*

R' Yehuda Leib Slonim, who along with his brothers managed Kollel Chabad after R' Shimon Menashe took on rabbinic duties, made numerous trips to Lubavitch to coordinate the kollel's affairs over decades. His last trip in 5645 is documented in a letter from Rebbe Rashab (vol. 1, letter 4). He died eight years later in 5653.
