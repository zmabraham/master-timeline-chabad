# Hebron Vaad letter signed by four gabbaim

*1898*

By 5 Tammuz 5658 the Vaad of the Ashkenazi community in Hebron was led by four signatories: Mordechai Dovber Slonim, Binyamin Rivlin, Yaakov Slonim, and Levi Eliezer Klansky. The same first two signed Vaad documents in 5663 and 5669, when Chaim Avraham Fundaminsky was added, all stamping with the seal 'Zechus Avos LeBanim Tizkor — Seal of the Vaad HaKlali of the Ashkenazi Communities of Hebron.'
