# Yosef Weinberg reports on the Radom yeshiva to the Rebbe

*1941*

Weinberg reported receiving a postcard from Meir Hendel announcing that he had founded a yeshiva in Radom with roughly 150 students, with Hendel as rosh mesivta and Yaakov Yehuda Goldberg delivering a shiur to one class. The letter conveyed news of the underground yeshiva functioning in Nazi-occupied Poland to the Rebbe in North America.
