# Munkacs Haggadah with 'Sha'ar Yissachar' commentary by Chaim Elazar Shapira

*1938*

In 5698 (1938), a Haggadah with a commentary on the Pesach Aggadah drawn from the manuscript of Rebbe Chaim Elazar Shapira of Munkacs's work 'Sha'ar Yissachar' was published in Munkacs by Grafia Press. The 54-leaf octavo edition preserved the teachings of the famed Munkacher Rebbe on the Passover Seder. It appeared just before the Holocaust devastated the Munkacs Chassidic community.
