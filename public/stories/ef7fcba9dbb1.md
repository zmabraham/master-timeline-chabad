# Hebron Kollel leadership recorded in 5609 census

*1849*

These five men were registered as 'sages, rabbis, leaders, appointees and overseers' of the Hebron Chabad community. The same individuals signed the power-of-attorney document to Montefiore that year. R' Yosef Yehuda Leib Shmerling was listed separately as 'engaged in kollel matters.'
