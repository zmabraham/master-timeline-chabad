# Russian police search Alter Rebbe's home and seize 103 books

*1800*

On 24 Kislev 5561 (November 29, 1800 Old Style), Russian police searched Rabbi Schneur Zalman's home in Lozna in search of writings against the monarchy. They removed 103 books and 14 manuscript collections to Petersburg as evidence. The remaining volumes were sealed in the house under a special seal, with the leaders of the Lozna community signing a written undertaking to keep them locked and untouched.
