# R. Avraham David Lavut, Rabbi of Nikolayev, passes away

*1890*

In 5650 (1890), Rabbi Avraham David Lavut, who had served as Rabbi of Nikolayev, passed away. Before his passing he instructed the heads of the community that his grandson Rabbi Meir Shlomo Yanovsky should fill his place. Yanovsky thereupon assumed the Nikolayev rabbinate, a post he would hold until his own passing in 5693.
