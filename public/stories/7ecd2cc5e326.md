# Rebbe Rayatz arrives in Warsaw for his first visit

*1928*

Leaving Riga on Sunday morning 28 Shvat 5688, the Rebbe Rayatz traveled to Warsaw via Dvinsk, the border stations, and Vilna, where chassidim greeted him at each stop. He reached Warsaw on the morning of erev Rosh Chodesh Adar (Feb 20, 1928), lodging in the home of R. Shraga Feivish Zalmanov. Over the following weeks he received thousands of visitors, met with rabbanim and admorim, said maamarim, and traveled briefly to Otwock to visit R. Moshe Hornstein.
