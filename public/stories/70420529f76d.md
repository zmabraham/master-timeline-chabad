# Toldot Yaakov Yosef printed in Koretz

*1780*

Rabbi Yaakov Yosef of Polonne, one of the Baal Shem Tov's foremost students, published Toldot Yaakov Yosef in Koretz in 1780. The book quotes hundreds of teachings he heard directly from the Baal Shem Tov. It was the very first Chassidic book to appear in print.
