# Shluchim convention of the Soviet Union opens in Moscow

*1991*

The convention's main sessions were held at the Molodyozhna Hotel, with Rabbis Krasik, Moskowitz, Levin, Avelsky, Slonim, Lazar, Korobkin and Brook on the dais. Talmidim shluchim came in from Rostov and other cities, and the special delegation from Eretz Yisrael addressed the gathering, including a talk on synagogue libraries throughout Russia. A public banquet that evening drew a wide audience.
