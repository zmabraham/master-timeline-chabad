# Rebbe cables Rabbi Abramsky and Dr. Schwarzbart in London for Canadian visas

*1941*

On 6 Elul 5701 (August 29, 1941), the Rebbe sent telegrams to Rabbi Abramsky and to Dr. Schwarzbart in London. He explained that 39 of his finest Tomchei Tmimim students were trapped in Kobe under the Jewish committee's supervision, all Polish citizens, and asked them to leverage their contacts to obtain Canadian visas — at least ten.
