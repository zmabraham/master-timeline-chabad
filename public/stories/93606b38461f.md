# Rayatz becomes engaged in Bolivke

*1896*

On 28 Sivan 5656 (June 9, 1896), the tena'im were signed in Bolivke, formally engaging the Rayatz to Nechama Dina. The Rebbe Rashab took on all wedding expenses despite his family's limited means, having chosen Nechama Dina over wealthier matches with large dowries.
