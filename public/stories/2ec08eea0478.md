# List of 43 students at Tomchei Tmimim in Vilna compiled

*1940*

Secretary R. Moshe Leib Rothstein compiled a detailed register of the bochurim who had reached Vilna, recording their origins, ages, and arrival dates, plus those still stuck at the border. A photograph of the 31 January 1940 list of 43 students survives in the archive. Most of those listed were later saved via Russia-Japan-China, though not all.
