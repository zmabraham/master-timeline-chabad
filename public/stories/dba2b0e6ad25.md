# Editing of Likkutei Dibburim from 5700 begun

*1945*

On 17 Tevet 5705 (2 January 1945) the Rebbe Rayatz began going through transcripts of sichot from 5700, starting from the day of his arrival in the United States, in order to prepare them for publication. The project — eventually published as part of Likkutei Dibburim — was undertaken at the urging of his son-in-law Ramash (the future Rebbe).
