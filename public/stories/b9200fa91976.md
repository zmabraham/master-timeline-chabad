# Ohel of the Rebbes in Lubavitch rebuilt

*1989*

In 5749 (1989), Rabbi Dovid Nachshon and Rabbi Avi Taub rebuilt the Ohel of the Rebbes in Lubavitch over the graves of the Tzemach Tzedek and Maharash. From that point onward, Anash began traveling regularly to Lubavitch to visit the courtyard of the Rebbes and pray at the Ohel. The restoration coincided with the opening of the Soviet Union.
