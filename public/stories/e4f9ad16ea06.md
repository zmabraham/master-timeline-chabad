# Acting court inspector reports on Mitteler Rebbe case progress

*1826*

Following Khovansky's order while passing through Babinovich, the acting court inspector took charge of expediting the case against Rabbi DovBer Schneuri of Lubavitch. The inspector found that the case had reached the peace court on December 23, 1825, but progress had been slow. Inquiries sent to the Rebbe were lost in transit, forcing a second messenger to be dispatched. The court then resolved to take sworn testimony from Jewish communities in Orsha, Kopust and Shklov regarding their alleged 1813 invitation to the Rebbe to succeed his father.
