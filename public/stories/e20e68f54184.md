# Rebbe Rayatz appoints R' Yehuda Eber Rosh Yeshiva in Vilna

*1930*

Having rescued R' Yehuda Eber from Russia to Riga, the Rebbe Rayatz sent him to head the Vilna yeshiva. In his letter introducing the appointment, the Rebbe described R' Eber as one of the directors of Tomchei Tmimim in Russia, an outstanding talmid chacham of exceptional talent, still young in years.
