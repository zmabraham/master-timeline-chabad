# Rabbi Mendel Horenstein killed at Treblinka

*1942*

His adopted nephew Yaakov Yosef Liss was also killed by the Nazis in 1942. The couple had adopted Yaakov Yosef when his mother (Mendel's sister Sara) died a few days after giving birth to her second child. In 1950 the seventh Rebbe received details of their fate from a Treblinka survivor.
