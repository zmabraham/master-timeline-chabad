# Rebbe Rayatz appeals to NY librarians for State Department intervention

*1946*

On 24 Adar I 5706, after the war's end, the Rebbe Rayatz wrote to Alexander Marx of the JTS library and Joshua Bloch of the NY Public Library. He explained that three crates of ancient manuscripts — spanning 150 years of Chabad nesius — together with thousands of rare sefarim from the Shmuel Wiener collection, had been seized by the Nazis from his library in Otwock. The crates had been registered under Rabbis Jacobson and Hecht (US citizens) and were the property of Agudas Chasidei Chabad; he asked the librarians to write the State Department vouching for their scholarly and national-Jewish importance.
