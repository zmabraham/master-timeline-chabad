# Final pre-war letter from Rebbe to Chadakov in Riga

*1939*

The Riga correspondence covered farbrengens of anash, rabbinical appointments, taharas hamishpacha, mikvaos, tefillin campaigns, kosher food for Jewish soldiers, summer camps, education, refugee aid for Ashkenaz refugees in Riga, separation of boys' and girls' schools in Lvov, and plans for an Achos Tmimim monthly journal. War cut short the journal and many other plans.
