# R' Chaim Aba Veil begins shul attendance in Dvinsk

*1905*

In 5665 (1905) the young Chaim Aba Veil started accompanying adults to the 'Farbershe Minyan' — the Painters' Synagogue on the Zhitomir Street in the Pletzer district of Dvinsk. Founded originally by the city's painters, it had become by then a leading Chabad shul, and existed until the Nazi destruction.
