# R' Shlomo Kugel lives in Kiskoriene near Pleshchenitsy

*1836*

According to a 5716 memoir by Mariasha Badana Putrefas, in 1836 (5596) a Jew named R' Shlomo Kugel lived in the village of Kiskoriene near Pleshchenitsy in the Minsk gubernia. Known as R' Shlomo Kiskoriener, his sons Hendel, Yosef and Gershon later spread Chassidut from the surrounding villages into the strongly mitnagdic town of Pleshchenitsy.
