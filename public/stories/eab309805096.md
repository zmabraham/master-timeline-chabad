# Rebbe and family depart Warsaw for Berlin on the last day of Chanukah

*1939*

The central station had been bombed, so Bloch arranged transport to the city's outskirts. German soldiers along the route challenged the obvious group of religious Jews, and at one checkpoint rifles were aimed at the Rebbe; Bloch invoked the authority of Admiral Canaris and threatened arrest if they interfered. After a full-day journey, the group reached Berlin, staying at the Jewish Community Center and then a hotel for Shabbat.
