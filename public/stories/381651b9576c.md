# Bar mitzvah of Rabbi Shlomo Sternberg's only son in Lipovets

*1923*

The author's brother Yosef had befriended Mordechai, son of Rabbi Sternberg, and a private melamed was teaching the two boys Gemara with Rashi and Tosafot. At the bar mitzvah seudah the brother sat beside the bar mitzvah boy together with their teacher, a rare moment of communal Jewish celebration in the increasingly hostile shtetl.
