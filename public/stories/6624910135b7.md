# Rebbe dispatches Zalman Gurary to open yeshivah in Volhynia

*1931*

Locals laughed at Gurary, telling him Novardok had already failed there. The Rebbe had written ahead introducing his "outstanding student" to local Chasidim and synagogues. Gurary persevered, overcoming initial skepticism by emphasizing the goal of educating local students. The success reflected the Rebbe's broader strategy of empowering students as spiritual entrepreneurs.
