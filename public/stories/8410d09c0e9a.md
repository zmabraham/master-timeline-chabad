# R' Aharon Eliezer Tzeitlin arrested on first night of Sukkos 5698

*1937*

Serving as shochet in Mozhaysk and operating a clandestine synagogue from his home, R' Aharon Eliezer Tzeitlin was seized by Soviet authorities on the first night of Sukkos 5698. He was deported to Siberia, where, weak in health, he died in his place of exile. The author had earlier exhausted himself attempting to bring Tzeitlin and his family to America; in 1929 the Rebbe Rayatz had told the author that only supernatural intervention could bring them out.
