# Prof. Khavkin writes detailed survey of the Warsaw yeshiva building

*1927*

On 5 Tevet 5688 (29 December 1927) Prof. Mordechai Zev (Vladimir) Khavkin penned a detailed report on the Warsaw yeshiva branch, describing its layout — kitchen and dining hall on the ground floor, five spacious classrooms above, a sleeping attic, and an old single-story wing converted to a prayer hall for the public. He noted that the equipment, of the simplest possible kind, was funded by a Joint Distribution Committee reconstruction grant from Mr. Bernhard Cohen. The report was later translated from English and printed in Beton Chabad issues 34–35.
