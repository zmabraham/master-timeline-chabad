# Mother Rachel Shusterman dies in Tashkent

*1953*

On 4 Tevet 5714 (December 10, 1953), the author's mother Rachel bat Aharon Tzvi died in Tashkent. Because of the climate of fear in the Soviet Union, the family did not inform the author for a year and a half; he therefore sat no shiva and recited no kaddish. His brother Yosef said kaddish in Tashkent and arranged a minyan there with great mesirat nefesh.
