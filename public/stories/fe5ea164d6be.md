# R' Avraham Eliyahu Ashurov sent to help establish Yeshivas Toras Emes in Hebron

*1912*

In 5672 (1912), R' Avraham Eliyahu Ashurov of Surazh, who had studied at Tomchei Tmimim in Lubavitch, was part of the group sent to establish Yeshivas Toras Emes in Hebron. He later returned to Russia during World War I, eventually serving as rabbi of Drivin and then emigrating to Riga.
