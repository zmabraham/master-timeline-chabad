# Rayatz leaves Soviet Russia for Riga

*1927*

Forced out at the beginning of 5688 after his imprisonment and exile, Rayatz immediately resumed efforts to recover the library — now from outside Russia. On Rosh Chodesh Marcheshvan he wrote to the Soviet embassy in Latvia formally requesting their support in returning the books to him. The Soviet ambassador in Riga promised to intercede, but no concrete result followed in the short term.
