# Death of Rabbi Yosef Leib Beriya

*1936*

In 1936, Rabbi Yosef Leib Beriya, a scholar respected by both Jews and non-Jews alike, passed away in Lubavitch. His death directly preceded the Soviet authorities' closure of the town's last synagogue, which he had maintained until his passing. His death marked the practical end of Lubavitch's organized Jewish life.
