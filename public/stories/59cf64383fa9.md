# Rabbi Yitzchak Yehoshua Heschel Friedman with the book "Dvar Malchus — Chiddushim"

*1996*

Rabbi Yitzchak Yehoshua Heschel Friedman with the book "Dvar Malchus — Chiddushim u'Biurim in Hilchos Melachim." Born 24 Iyar 5756, May 13, 1996, London. Resides in Bnei Brak. Position: 8th Sadigura Rebbe. Spouse: Malka. Father: Rabbi Yisrael Moshe Friedman.
