# Rebbe Rashab appoints R. Menachem Mendel Nae as Kollel Chabad bookkeeper

*1905*

The Rebbe instructed Rabbi Nae to request his predecessor R. Leib Menuchin to transfer all accounting documents addressed to the Rebbe's son. Nae was directed to send subsequent financial reports to designated central overseers including R. Chaim Bagin. The appointment came at a time of reorganization of the kollel's senior administration.
