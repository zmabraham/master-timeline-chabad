# Rabbis face conscription crisis over registration deadlines

*1916*

As the summer of 5676 progressed, the conscription appearance date arrived for rabbis. Most rabbis assumed they were not required to report, since provincial governors had already been instructed to submit lists of practicing rabbis. Military officials in Yekaterinoslav and apparently Minsk concurred, issuing exemption certificates pending clarification. However, other officials ruled that rabbis who failed to appear at the original call-up had forfeited their exemption. The Rebbe Rashab enlisted the prominent Petrograd lawyer Oskar Gruzenberg, who learned from Deputy Interior Minister Baltz that such rabbis would face trial for non-appearance but retained their underlying exemption rights.
