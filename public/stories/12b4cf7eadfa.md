# Beis Chabad expansion campaign declared on 5th candle of Chanukah

*1985*

In a special sicha on the 5th candle of Chanukah 5746 (December 1985), the Rebbe established expansion of Beis Chabad as a special campaign (Likkutei Sichos vol. 25 p. 400). He pledged to contribute matching funds to anyone who would found or expand a Beis Chabad in their place of shlichus.
