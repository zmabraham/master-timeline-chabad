# Passing of the Mitteler Rebbe

*1828*

The Mitteler Rebbe, R' Dovber Schneuri, passed away in 5588. He had instituted yearly support quotas for every chassid - rich and poor alike - for Eretz Yisrael through tzedakah boxes in every home. This obviated the need for special emissaries from Hebron to come rally Russian support.
