# New Russian statute on the Jews published

*1835*

In 5595 (1835), the Russian government published a new statute on the Jews, the second comprehensive code of its kind after the original statute of 5565 (1804/5). The legislation governed key aspects of Jewish communal life, the role of rabbis, residence rights, and other matters that had been at the center of Khovansky's earlier proposals and of the legal pressures on the chassidic movement throughout the 1820s and 1830s.
