# Rebbe Rashab letter to trustees defining Kollel Chabad's purpose

*1906*

The Rebbe articulated his understanding that the holy fathers had founded the Eretz Yisrael fund specifically because the land was uniquely suited to Torah and avodah, and not for the sake of artisans and businessmen lacking the yoke of Torah. He returned to this theme in many later letters, in ongoing tension with the Jerusalem trustees who maintained a broader yishuv-support mission.
