# Passing of Esther Miriam, daughter of the Mitteler Rebbe

*1819*

In Tammuz 5579 (July 1819), Esther Miriam, a daughter of the Mitteler Rebbe, passed away. Her matzeva, identified only relatively recently, describes her as 'the modest and honored woman,' a phrasing suggesting she was already married at the time of her death. According to one reconstruction she may have been a first wife of Rabbi Aharon of Kremenchug, before he later married Chaya, daughter of Rebbe Nachman of Breslov.
