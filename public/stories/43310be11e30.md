# Final agreement signed for Polyakov synagogue handover in Moscow

*1991*

Veronika, Natalya from the Mossoviet, the building's former director Galina, R' Y. Kogan and the diarist agreed that two women's section rooms and three ground floor rooms would transfer immediately, a phone line at 202-7696 would be installed at once, and electricity billing would shift to Chabad after paperwork. The question of whether Chabad would have to finance the new building for the displaced tenants was deferred, with Chabad arguing that other religious communities had not been required to pay.
