# Ibn Tzur family relocates from Meknes to Fez

*1683*

Rabbi Reuven ibn Tzur, father of the Yaavetz, relocated his family from Meknes to Fez during the Yaavetz's youth. A note in a 1559 Riva di Trento Mishnayot volume — now preserved in the Library of Agudat Chasidei Chabad — records his father purchasing it in Fez in 5443 (1683), suggesting the move took place when Yaakov was roughly ten years old. The Yaavetz would spend most of his life in Fez.
