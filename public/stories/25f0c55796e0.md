# Passing of Rabbi Avraham Avli Posveler of Vilna

*1836*

Rabbi Avraham Avli Posveler, who served as av beit din of Vilna, passed away in 5596 (1836). He had been born in 5524 (1764) in Posval. Several volumes of his responsa and novellae written from Vilna in 5593–5594 survived in the Chabad library archives.
