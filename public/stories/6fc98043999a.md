# Avigdor files additional denunciation citing Tanya

*1800*

On 24 Tevet 5561 (28 December 1800), Avigdor of Pinsk submitted yet another denunciation, copying out passages from the Tanya concerning the souls of the nations and arguing that the chassidic movement was harmful to the state. He proposed that the leaders of the chassidim be arrested and interrogated.
