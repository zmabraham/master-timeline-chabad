# Maharil of Kopust writes to R' Aharon of Belnitz about Bobruisk rabbinate

*1851*

Dated 24 Sivan 5611, this letter from the son of the Tzemach Tzedek to R' Aharon Lipschitz indicates that Bobruisk's leaders had come to receive him as rav. The appointment ultimately did not materialize, and R' Hillel of Paritch was chosen instead.
