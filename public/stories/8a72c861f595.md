# Major donation received for Tomchei Tmimim expansion

*1906*

The donation enabled construction over subsequent years of two brick buildings in the eastern portion of the courtyard. These housed a study hall for the lower shiurim, a special dining hall for Passover, and a tea station for the yeshiva students. The expansion accommodated both the growing enrollment of Tomchei Tmimim and the cheder students who studied alongside them.
