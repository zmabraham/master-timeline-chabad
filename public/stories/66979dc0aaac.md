# Massacre of Lubavitch's Jews

*1941*

On November 4, 1941, Nazi forces murdered 483 Jews and destroyed the ghetto in Lubavitch, effectively ending the Jewish presence in the historic town. Only one Jewish resident remained in Lubavitch until the early 1960s. The Hebrew Standard of Australia later reported that the brutality of the extermination in Lubavitch 'supersedes anything the Red Army committee investigating Nazi atrocities have thus far come across.'
