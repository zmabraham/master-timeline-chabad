# Rebbe Rayatz writes Toras Emes students upon R' Alter's return

*1928*

After roughly two months in Warsaw, R' Alter began the return trip to Jerusalem rather than relocating his family. On 5 Tevet 5689 the Rebbe wrote to the students of Toras Emes urging them to give themselves fully to R' Alter's program of study and avodah, signaling that he would now remain permanently in Eretz Yisrael.
