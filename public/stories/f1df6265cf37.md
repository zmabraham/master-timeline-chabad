# Iraqi Scud missiles first strike Israel during Gulf War

*1991*

Soon after the U.S.-led coalition began its air campaign against Iraq, Saddam Hussein retaliated by firing Scud missiles at Israeli cities. Word reached the Chabad delegation in Moscow from Kfar Chabad that there were no fatalities, only a few wounded, with two homes destroyed. The unfolding war became an obstacle to their library efforts, as Soviet officials cited the Gulf crisis as a reason to delay decisions on the Schneerson collection.
