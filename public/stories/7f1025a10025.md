# Simchat Torah farbrengen with the Rebbe Rashab in Rostov

*1916*

Wartime conditions had been declared: no public gathering was to take place at the Rebbe's home, and infractions risked being shot on sight. After the hakafot the gabbai R' Elchanan Dov Morozov quietly announced that everyone should go home and that there would be no farbrengen. Seven bochurim — including the author, R' Yisrael Feigin, and Zalman Dovid Michelin — stayed behind to chazer the maamar. When they later climbed to the upper floor, they found the Rebbe Rashab seated with chassidim; he had a table brought for them, waited for each to wash and make hamotzi, and invited each one to say l'chaim — an honor previously unheard of for students.
