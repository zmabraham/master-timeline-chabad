# R. Shmuel Segal departs Hebron on emissary mission

*1826*

Following his 1824 aliyah to Hebron, R. Shmuel Segal was sent abroad on an emissary mission in 1826-1827 on behalf of the Chabad community. He resumed his communal leadership in Hebron upon his return, where he served for decades and signed nearly all communal documents from 1830 onward.
