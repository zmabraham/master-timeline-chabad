# Warsaw yeshiva expands to additional apartment on Franciszkanska Street

*1924*

Having moved to 8 Franciszkanska Street at the start of 5683, the Warsaw yeshiva outgrew the space by the beginning of 5685 and rented an additional nearby apartment at 6 Franciszkanska. The shiurim and main gathering remained at number 8, while the shiur lectures and Chassidus learning that couldn't fit took place in the shul at number 6.
