# Arrest of Rebbe Rayatz with house search in Petersburg

*1927*

During the search, officials did not open the cabinets containing the Rebbeim's manuscripts but inquired whose handwriting they were. They showed particular interest in one cabinet of antique manuscripts including Livnat HaSapir by R' Avraham Saba and the Shemoneh Shearim by R' Shmuel Vital, plus first-edition printed books like the 1476 Ralbag and 1489 Ramban. The interest, however, was only superficial.
