# Rebbe's Shemot farbrengen frames mission as pidyon shevuyim

*1988*

Without naming the delegation, the Rebbe told his Shabbat farbrengen that one must redeem the books, manuscripts and possessions of righteous leaders from captivity with self-sacrifice. He invoked the Tzemach Tzedek's principle that an emissary is empowered to make on-the-spot decisions, and afterward instructed the three delegation members to say l'chaim on a full cup.
