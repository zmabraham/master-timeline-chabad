# Rotstein meets Rebbe in Riga to report on Vilna yeshiva

*1939*

At the start of Tevet 5700, the Frierdiker Rebbe arrived in Riga. R. Moshe Leib Rotstein traveled there together with R. Shmuel Zalmanov to brief the Rebbe on how the Tomchei Temimim yeshiva had been reconstituted in Vilna after fleeing Poland.
