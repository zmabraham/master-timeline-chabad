# Rebbe Rashab purchases Romano courtyard in Hebron

*1908*

At the end of summer 5668 the Rebbe Rashab acquired the famous Romano courtyard in Hebron from Avraham, son of the late Markado Romano. The grand mansion had been built outside the Jewish ghetto in the 1870s and had hosted prominent visitors, including R' Chizkiyah Medini, the Sdei Chemed, who lived and died there. After the purchase the building would become a central Chabad property in Hebron.
