# Author moves to Shchedrin branch after Shavuot 5670

*1910*

At Shchedrin he heard a shiur in nigleh from R. Moshe Mordechai Amrami (Poharelsky) of Pochep on tractate Bava Batra. The two classes were combined under R. Shaul Dov Zislin's direction, and the mashgiach for nigleh was R. Yaakov Baruch Krasik.
