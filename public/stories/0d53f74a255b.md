# Dyakov reports to Benckendorff on Tzemach Tzedek's character and influence

*1841*

Dyakov identified the man in question as Mendel Schneersohn, registered as a third-guild merchant in Babinovichi but residing permanently in Lyubavichi since 1813. He described the Rebbe as approximately fifty years old, of weak health and rarely leaving his home, possessing good character and conduct with no opinions contrary to the laws. Dyakov noted that Jews — Chassidim and even Misnagdim — turned to him from many places to resolve religious questions, and that he was honored as a descendant of great rabbis, including his grandfather of Liadi who 'was known in Petersburg due to some matter.'
