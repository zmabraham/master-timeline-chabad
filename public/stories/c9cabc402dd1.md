# Underground Tomchei Temimim yeshiva founded in Radom ghetto

*1940*

Funding for the yeshiva came largely from the father of bochur Motl Bumelgrin, a wealthy and respected community member who took on its principal expenses. The roshei yeshiva and mashpi'im R' Berl Kornitzer and R' Baruch Friedman were brought in from Otwock, leaving their families behind to teach and inspire the students. Despite the dangers of underground life under Nazi occupation, the yeshiva continued operating until the end of summer 5702.
