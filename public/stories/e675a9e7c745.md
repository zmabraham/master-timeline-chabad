# Rebbe Rashab begins Rosh Hashanah discourse on divine unity

*1914*

At the start of 5675 the Rebbe Rashab began expounding the concept of divine unity in both directions during the Tishrei maamarim. When chozrim asked for elucidation he replied enigmatically that 'the ends and the edges do not yet come together.' Only mid-winter, in the course of further chazara, did he indicate that the matter had now been clarified.
