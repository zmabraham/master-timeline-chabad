# Rebbe Rayatz launches Pidyon Shvuyim rescue fund

*1940*

Immediately upon his arrival in the U.S. in 5700 (1940), the Rebbe Rayatz established a dedicated fund called Pidyon Shvuyim aimed at rescuing Tmimim and Anash trapped in Nazi-occupied Europe. A group of more than thirty students was eventually routed through Russia, Japan, and Shanghai to safety.
