# R. Chaim Asher brings Rebbe's letters to Pocking on Lag B'Omer

*1947*

On Lag B'Omer 5707 R. Chaim Asher came from Paris bearing further letters from the Rebbe in response to those sent after the first 'baruchim haba'im' batch. Levin then wrote again pleading for mercy on his sister Frayde Meni Sosonkin, her husband Asher (son of R. Shmaryahu Sosonkin of Batum) and their daughter Chava, who remained stranded in Lvov.
