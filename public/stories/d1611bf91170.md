# Yosef David Ayash sent as shadar to Italian cities for Hebron

*1826*

Starting in 5586 (1826), Yosef David Ayash traveled through the cities of Italy as a shadar on behalf of Hebron, continuing until 5590. His shlichut was conducted independently because the Pekuam committee did not handle fundraising in Italy. Ayash later left the Jewish people, but his shlichut is described in detail in Sheluchei Eretz Yisrael.
