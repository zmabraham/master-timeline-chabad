# Founding convention of Agudas Talmidei HaTmimim in Warsaw

*1934*

The convention drew Tmimim from Poland and abroad to the beit midrash at Moranowski 7/9. The assembly elected R' Shmaryahu Gourary as chairman and R' Schneur Zalman Gourary as director, with R' Moshe P. Katz as secretary. The agenda included Chassidic outreach, member assistance with placements, and a charter for the Agudah.
