# MiBeis HaGenazim exhibition and album

*2010*

In the winter of 5770 (2009–10), the Lubavitch Library presented its eleventh exhibition, titled 'MiBeis HaGenazim,' surveying treasures held in the archive. A printed album of the same name accompanied the exhibit, documenting 154 special items preserved in the library's collection. The volume later served as a model for a successor work compiled in 5778.
