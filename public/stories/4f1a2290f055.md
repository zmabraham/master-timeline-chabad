# Hillel Horwitz marries Rivka Cohen

*1940*

The Rebbe had blessed the engagement on 20 Iyar 5700 and worked to secure a rabbinical position for Hillel in the town of Livenhof (Livani), writing to R. Mordechai Dubin on 29 Nissan to advocate for the appointment. R. Yitzchak Matmid sent the Rebbe a wedding invitation with a pidyon nefesh attached.
