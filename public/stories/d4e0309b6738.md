# R' Meir Tzvi Hershler immigrates from Hungary to Eretz Israel

*1865*

R' Meir Tzvi Hershler arrived from Hungary in 5625 at age 10 and later married Sarah, daughter of R' Yonatan Zeev Wolf Mendelsohn of Hebron. He settled permanently in Hebron between 5630-5634. Decades later he would represent the non-Chabad communities during the 5647 din torah between the kollels and serve as their representative on Hebron's Vaad HaKlali.
