# First responsa collection of the Alter Rebbe issued in Zhitomir

*1847*

For the Zhitomir 5607 (1847) Shulchan Aruch edition, all the Alter Rebbe's known responsa—previously printed in their respective halachic contexts—were recollected and issued as a standalone 44-folio supplement. The collection was reprinted with every subsequent Shulchan Aruch edition.
