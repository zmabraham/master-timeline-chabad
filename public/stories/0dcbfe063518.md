# British Consul Finn reports on Abdul Rahman's extortion

*1854*

In late summer 5614, British Consul James Finn in Jerusalem wrote to Lord Stratford de Redcliffe describing the Hebron Jews' suffering at the hands of Abdul Rahman. He reported a unique feature of the sheikh's extortion: collecting a fixed share of the seasonal halukkah donations from European Jewry on which the Hebron Jews depended. His name was entered in the kollel ledgers as a stipend recipient, and he would send representatives to collect three days before the scheduled distribution.
