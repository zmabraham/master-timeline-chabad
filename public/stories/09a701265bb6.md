# Meeting with prospective State Library director Filipov

*1992*

Filipov asked for a month's delay before any meeting with the librarians, demanded that Chabad first prepare secure stacks before transfer, and floated 'compromises' such as having Chabad finance photocopies of the entire collection — moves the diarist read as new excuses. The diarist returned convinced the road to recovering the books in Moscow was hopeless and that the effort should pivot to bringing the collection to the Chabad library in New York.
