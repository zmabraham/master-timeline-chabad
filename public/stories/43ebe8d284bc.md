# Wedding of Rayatz's daughter Chaya Mushka in Warsaw

*1928*

The Rebbe Rayatz's second visit to Warsaw took place on 14 Kislev 5689 (November 27, 1928), for the wedding of his daughter to his son-in-law, the future Lubavitcher Rebbe. After the wedding, on the fourth night of Chanukah, the Rayatz wrote a public letter of thanks to all of Polish anash and the temimim for their efforts on behalf of the wedding arrangements.
