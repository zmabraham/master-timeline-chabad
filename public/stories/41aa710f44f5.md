# Petersburg Rabbinical Conference convenes

*1910*

The Conference lasted approximately one month from 4 Adar Sheni to 6 Nissan 5670. The Rebbe Rashab dominated as the true leader, setting the tone and effectively directing the assembly despite formally remaining in the background. Because sessions had to be conducted in Russian and his fluency was limited, his views were articulated by Rabbi Mendel Chen of Nezhin, who excelled in Russian oratory, Talmudic learning, and piety. Henrich Sliozberg later wrote in his memoirs that the young Rebbe rarely spoke much, but every word demonstrated mastery over the masses; he was as immovable as stone on religious matters.
