# Author becomes shamash at Yeshivat R. Meir Simcha HaKohen shul in Brooklyn

*1949*

In Nissan 5709 (April 1949), the author began a four-year tenure as shamash at the Yeshivat R. Meir Simcha HaKohen shul in East Flatbush. He worked alongside Rabbi J.J. Hecht, the shul's rav, in friendship and harmony. The shul's contract had to be renewed annually around Pesach.
