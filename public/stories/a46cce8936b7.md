# Rabbi Schneur Zalman establishes Colel Chabad

*1788*

After Rabbi Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk's passing, Rabbi Schneur Zalman established the fund to ensure the continuity of the chasidic community in Eretz Yisrael. Today, Colel Chabad remains the oldest continuously operating charity in Israel and is the country's largest provider of food security.
