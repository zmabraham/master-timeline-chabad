# Rayatz recites maamar publicly on first day of Passover

*1920*

On the first day of Passover, the Rayatz again told Rivkin he wished to recite a maamar privately. Rivkin arranged for Chasidim to gather outside the door of the Rashab's study; they cracked open the doors and listened. A week later, at the end of Passover, the Chasidim entered the room itself while he spoke.
