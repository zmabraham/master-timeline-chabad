# Passing of R' Moshe Ashkenazi

*2008*

R' Moshe Ashkenazi, who had received the unusual Königsberg-imprint Siddur with Da"Ch at his 1942 wedding, passed away on 23 Tevet תשס"ח. The siddur — one of only a handful of copies printed openly for Königsberg's own Jewish residents rather than smuggled into Russia — was given by his son-in-law R' Nachman Yosef Tversky to the Lubavitch library, where it provided definitive evidence that the disputed third edition had in fact been printed in Königsberg in תרי"ב.
