# Sukkot tension at shul exposes author's split focus

*1952*

On Sukkot 5713 (October 1952), at a kiddush in the shul sukkah in East Flatbush, the author told the balebatim that he had not come to the shul to 'make a living' but to accomplish something in Yiddishkeit. The gabbaim later quoted him as saying he could manage without them. Combined with their discovery of his work at the print shop, this set the stage for his departure.
