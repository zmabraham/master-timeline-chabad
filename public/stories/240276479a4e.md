# Vilna Tomchei Tmimim management issues first preserved letter

*1929*

The signed letter, on yeshiva letterhead and stamped with the institution's seal, documents the working committee a few years into operations. Eliyahu Weitz served as director, with a multi-member board overseeing the branch from the Levin company offices on Kolejowa Street. The branch was already a recognized communal institution, supported by monthly donations from Vilna and the region and even by an allocation from R' Chaim Ozer Grodzinski's Vaad HaYeshivos.
