# Rotstein, Zalmanov, and Ushpal arrive in San Francisco

*1940*

At the end of Tishrei 5701, the three Lubavitch rabbis arrived in San Francisco after their long journey through Russia, Siberia, and Japan. On 29 Tishrei, the Frierdiker Rebbe wrote to R. Yaakov Katz in Chicago that the three rabbis had safely arrived in America and were traveling on to New York.
