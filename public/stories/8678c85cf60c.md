# Shmuel Wiener invited to Lubavitch to help organize the library

*1913*

Around 5673 (1913), the famous bibliographer Shmuel Wiener was brought to Lubavitch specifically to help arrange the library. According to R' Chaim Liberman's account, Wiener was given two weeks off from his work, and the Rebbe Rayatz asked him to come for a short time to view the books. He ended up staying only about a week.
