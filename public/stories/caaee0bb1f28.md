# Rebbe relocates from Riga to Poland

*1933*

Throughout his Riga years the Rebbe's stationery read 'Riga, temporary.' Permanence remained elusive: 'The Rebbe resided in Riga for six years and during that extended time, no decision was made on his permanent place of residence,' wrote chasidic historian Rabbi Sholom Ber Levin. Poland too would only provide a home for a few years.
