# Morgen Zhurnal article on R. Gorodetzky's Paris refugee work

*1949*

On 27 Sivan 5709, the Morgen Zhurnal carried an article about the work of Rabbi Benjamin Gorodetzky in Paris arranging temporary housing for refugees in special homes around Paris and organizing their emigration to Eretz Yisrael and other countries. His operation included partnership with the Joint Distribution Committee for refugee resettlement.
