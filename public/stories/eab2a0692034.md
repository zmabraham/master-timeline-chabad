# Decision to open Tomchei Tmimim branch in Glubokoe

*1934*

On Monday 5 Tammuz during the Rebbe Rayatz's visit to Glubokoe, he met at the home of Rabbi Kupershtok with gabbaim of the local batei midrash and town notables. There they informed him of their decision to open a branch of Tomchei Tmimim Yeshiva in the town.
