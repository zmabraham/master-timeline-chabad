# Mitteler Rebbe's petition to Governor Khovansky requesting release

*1825*

After the conclusion of his interrogations and shortly before his release, the Mitteler Rebbe wrote a brief memorandum to Governor Khovansky. He expressed confidence that justice would prevail and that the false denunciation against him would be exposed, but appealed to the governor's sense of fairness to expedite a decision because his fragile health was steadily worsening. A longer follow-up petition was later printed in the kuntres 'Bad Kodesh.'
