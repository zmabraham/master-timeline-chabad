# Speaking at the inauguration of Chabad House 770 Pisgat Ze'ev. Rabbi Chaim Levi Yitzchak Kurtz

*1970*

Speaking at the inauguration of Chabad House 770 Pisgat Ze'ev. Rabbi Chaim Levi Yitzchak Kurtz (born 5731, 1970), is a shliach of the Rebbe in the Pisgat Ze'ev neighborhood in Jerusalem. Biography: Born in Kfar Chabad on 12 Kislev 5731 to his parents
