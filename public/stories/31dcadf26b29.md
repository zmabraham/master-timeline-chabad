# Rebbe encourages Avraham Tzvi Segal to join Tomchei Tmimim

*1940*

Responding to the Pinsk student's longing for a chassidic yeshiva, the Rebbe told him to thank Hashem for awakening such a desire and assured him that Hashem had arranged the opportunity to realize it at Tomchei Tmimim. Segal eventually became a student at the Vilna yeshiva and its union secretary.
