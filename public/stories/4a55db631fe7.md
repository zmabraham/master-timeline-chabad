# Investigation continues with sworn testimony from Jewish communities

*1826*

Inspector Kilikovski reported that on February 8 the policeman sent to question the Rebbe had returned admitting he had lost the Rebbe's responses on the road from Lubavitch to Babinovich. A second messenger was dispatched and brought back responses on February 10. The court then resolved to obtain sworn testimony from communities in Orsha and Kopust, and from chasidim in Shklov, to verify whether they had written to the Rebbe in Kremenchug in 1813 promising to compensate his losses from the French invasion and to support his family if he succeeded his father as their rabbi.
