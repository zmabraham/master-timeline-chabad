# Lazaniya brings Rayatz arrest file from Dnepropetrovsk

*1991*

On 14 Tammuz 5751, Lazaniya was expected in Moscow from Dnepropetrovsk together with his liaison and a KGB official, carrying the full file on the arrest of R. Levi Yitzchak Schneerson, the Rebbe's father. He had confirmed by phone on multiple recent days that he would deliver the materials. The diary lists this as one of four major expected events of the day.
