# Decision to found Tomchei Tmimim branch in Homel

*1921*

Travel to the central yeshiva in Lubavitch had become unsafe under the new Soviet regime, leaving students wandering with no stable place to study. At a Simchat Torah gathering in the home of R' Shmuel Biale 'der zeiger macher,' the chassidim of Homel decided to open their own branch of Tomchei Tmimim. They wrote to the Rebbe Rayatz requesting permission and a rosh yeshiva, and he approved, granting the new institution the name 'Tomchei Tmimim of Lubavitch in Homel.'
