# Author first views original Tzemach Tzedek portrait in Moscow

*1991*

On Issru Chag Pesach 5751 (7 April 1991), the librarian visited the Moscow home of Yehuda Leib Ginzburg — a great-grandson of R. Shlomo Zalman of Kapust (the Magen Avot), himself a grandson of the Tzemach Tzedek — and saw on the wall a vivid colored portrait of the Tzemach Tzedek. Years of attempts to obtain the painting or a photo for the Chabad library followed; eventually R. Baruch Gorin succeeded in securing a copy of this rare and beautiful image.
