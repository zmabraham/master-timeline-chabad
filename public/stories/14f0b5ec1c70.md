# Lubavitch Library exhibition hall opens

*1994*

On Chai Elul 5754 (August 1994) the Lubavitch Library inaugurated its exhibition hall in Brooklyn, opening selected treasures from the archive to the broader public. The library had previously been intended only for researchers, rabbis and authors, but the Rebbe had earlier approved opening an exhibition once a suitable space became available. The first exhibit, which ran until Erev Rosh Chodesh Kislev 5755, featured items connected to each of the Chabad Rebbes and to every area of Chabad activity.
