# Six-member delegation arrives in Jerusalem to audit Kollel Chabad accounts

*1914*

Lubavitch sent R. Eliezer Moshe Modyevsky of Kharol, R. Yitzchak Yoel Rapaporowitz and R. Nosson Gurary of Kremenchug; Bobruisk sent R. Menachem Mendel (son of the Bobruisker Rebbe), R. Yitzchak Shmuel Luria and R. Shemarya Mekler. The gabbaim Bichowsky and Bagin also came to face beis-din proceedings with the Eretz Yisrael officials. The visit triggered roughly a month of intensive ledger review and public testimony in Jerusalem.
