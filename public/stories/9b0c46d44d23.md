# Last maamar of Hemshech Tarav: Shabbos Vayera 5676 "Cholas Ahava"

*1915*

On Shabbos Parshas Vayera 5676 the Rebbe Rashab delivered the maamar on the inyan of "cholas ahava," the final maamar of the Hemshech that had begun on Shavuos Tarav. The next day, Sunday Parshas Chayei Sara, the Rebbe and his entire household relocated from Lubavitch.
