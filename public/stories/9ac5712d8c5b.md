# Second Chabad–Sephardic Hebron agreement lowers payment

*1830*

Chabad argued that since they also paid Sheikh Abdul Rahman one riyal every Rosh Chodesh and additional gifts at weddings and at the arrival of emissaries, those sums should count toward shared protection payments. The 5590 agreement reduced their annual obligation to 900 grush, paid each year upon the ship's arrival to the head pakid R' Chaim HaLevi. The agreement explicitly named Sheikh Abdul Rahman as 'sar ha'ir' (ruler of the city).
