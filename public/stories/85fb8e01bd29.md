# Sefer Ohr Sameach presented to the Rebbe at his wedding

*1928*

Among the books the Rebbe received at his wedding on 14 Kislev 5689 was the Ohr Sameach commentary on the Rambam by R' Meir Simcha HaKohen of Dvinsk. The Rebbe himself inscribed the title page: 'From the books of Menachem Schneersohn, 14 Kislev 5689.'
