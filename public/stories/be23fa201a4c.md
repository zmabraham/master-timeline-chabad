# Wedding of Miriam Reizel to Moshe Nemenov

*1963*

The wedding took place on 23 Kislev 5724 (December 9, 1963) at the Grand-Paradise Hall in Williamsburg, joining the families of two Zlobin natives. On the Rebbe's instruction, sheva brachos were held in Cleveland — first at the shul of Reb Zalman Kaizen and then at the home of Reb Chaim Stein, mashgiach of Telshe Yeshiva, where the Telshe roshei yeshiva attended.
