# Mohilev government fines Babinovich court over investigation conduct

*1826*

On 3 March 1826 (6 Adar II 5586), the government of Mohilev province imposed a fine on the Babinovich peace court, citing illegal leniency in how the investigation was being run. At the same time, it ordered the courts in Kapust, Orsha, Shklov and Mohilev to accelerate gathering chassidic testimony in the case against Rabbi DovBer.
