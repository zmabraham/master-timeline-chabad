# Investigator Korbitsky files court report on Mittler Rebbe's court and library

*1825*

Korbitsky's report catalogued the synagogue's dimensions, Torah scrolls, menorahs from Petersburg, candlesticks, curtains, and a library of 611 books in four cabinets available for loan to townspeople. The report is a primary source for understanding the Mittler Rebbe's court at Lubavitch in this period. It was later cited in the published account of the Mittler Rebbe's arrest and release.
