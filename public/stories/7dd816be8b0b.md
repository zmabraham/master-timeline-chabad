# Rebbe Rayatz makes first return visit to Lubavitch

*1922*

After travel restrictions were lifted, the Rebbe Rayatz traveled to Lubavitch on Erev Rosh Chodesh Elul, delivering a maamar in the brick shul — the other synagogues having burned — and praying at the Ohel before departing for Moscow with R' Shmuel Aba Dulitzky. A delegation from Homel led by the author met him on the platform at the Krasna station, and the author received a yechidus on the train. The Rebbe likened his renewed journey to Lubavitch to a horse finally breaking loose from its tether, signifying the renewal of the bond with the town since he had assumed the nesius.
