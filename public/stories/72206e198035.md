# Reshag becomes chief mashpia in Lubavitch

*1905*

Following the passing of the Rashbatz on 15 Sivan 5665, R' Shmuel Gronem (Reshag) Esterman was appointed chief mashpia of Tomchei Tmimim in Lubavitch in summer 5665 (1905). The Rebbe Rashab wrote on 15 Menachem-Av 5665 that with Reshag's arrival in Lubavitch, the study of chassidut rose to new heights. He served until his passing in 5681.
