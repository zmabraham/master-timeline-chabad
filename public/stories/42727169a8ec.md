# US Acting Secretary of State Phillips declines formal book intervention

*1933*

Following efforts by R. Avraham Eliyahu Axelrod to mobilize American officials around Litvinov's Washington visit (negotiating US recognition of the Soviet Union), Acting Secretary Phillips writes that 'in view of the fact that Rabbi Schneerson is not a citizen of the United States, this Government is not in a position to take any formal steps to induce the Soviet Government to return to him the books and documents in question.'
