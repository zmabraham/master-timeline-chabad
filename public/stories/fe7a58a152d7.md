# Rebbe Rayatz arrives in Warsaw for three-week visit

*1928*

The Rebbe Rayatz arrived in Warsaw on the eve of Rosh Chodesh Adar I. He spent three weeks in the city and nearly a week in Otwock, visiting the Tomchei Tmimim yeshiva and meeting with Chasidim. He also held several meetings related to aid for Soviet Jewry, returning via a week-long stop in Vilna.
