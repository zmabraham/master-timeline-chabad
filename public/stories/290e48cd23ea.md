# Rebbe Rayatz instructs R' S. Z. Gurarie to study Mishnayot by heart

*1926*

On 28 Tishrei 5687 (October 1926) the Rebbe Rayatz wrote again to R' Shneur Zalman Gurarie, instructing him to learn Mishnayot by heart starting from Seder Taharot. The pace was to be no more than one chapter every two days, with thorough memorization and calm review. The letter is found in Igrot Kodesh vol. 11, letter 3800.
