# Polotsk yeshiva shut down and R' Shlomo Chaim Kesselman exiled to Poltava

*1929*

Soon after the Nevel raid, the GPU moved against the Polotsk branch of Tomchei Tmimim, arresting its students and administrators. R' Shlomo Chaim Kesselman was singled out and exiled to Poltava, while the rest of the bochurim fled, most routing through Vitebsk because of its central rail position.
