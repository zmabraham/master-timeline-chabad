# Alter Rebbe sends two shadarim to Volhynia to mediate kollel dispute

*1796*

In winter 5556 (1796), the Alter Rebbe wrote about the 'great noise' that had erupted over the Eretz Yisrael funds from Volhynia and Ukraine, where new voices were challenging the long-standing arrangement of channeling all monies through R' Avraham of Kalisk. He dispatched two shadarim, R' Dovber of Dubrovna and R' Refael, to Volhynia to negotiate a compromise. The talks eventually produced an agreement that Volhynia's funds would be transported separately by a Polish shadar from Eretz Yisrael.
