# Sons of Alter Rebbe publish expanded Tanya

*1814*

In 5574 (1814), one year after the passing of the Alter Rebbe on 24 Tevet 5573, his holy sons published a new edition of the Tanya. They added two additional sections: Iggeret HaKodesh and Kuntres Acharon. From this edition onward, all printings of the Tanya have contained these five parts.
