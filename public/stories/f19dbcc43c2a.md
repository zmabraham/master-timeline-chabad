# First printing of the Tanya

*1797*

During the winter of 5557 the Alter Rebbe published the Tanya, formalizing the Chabad approach to chassidic teachings in print. Until this point no doctrinal disputes had surfaced between him and R' Avraham of Kalisk, but the printing immediately drew a sharp critical response from R' Avraham, who argued that disseminating intellectual chassidic doctrine broadly contradicted the path of the Maggid of Mezritch.
