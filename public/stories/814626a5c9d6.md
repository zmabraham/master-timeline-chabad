# Mitteler Rebbe settles in Lubavitch

*1813*

In the summer of 1813, Rabbi Dovber, the second Chabad Rebbe, settled in the town of Lubavitch. He was the first of four Chabad Rebbes to reside there, establishing the town as the capital of the Chabad chasidic movement for the next 102 years. His arrival followed the death of his father, Rabbi Schneur Zalman, who had passed away the previous year while fleeing Napoleon's invading army.
