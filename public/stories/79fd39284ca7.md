# Yeshiva refuses new students for summer 5696

*1936*

The Rosh Yeshiva reported it was the first time since Tomchei Temimims founding that they advertised in the press that no new talmidim would be accepted. Dozens of students from across Poland and abroad still came begging entry. The Rebbe Rayatz suffered greatly at having to refuse so many promising bochurim while other major yeshivos shrank to mere dozens of students.
