# Founding of Agudat HaTmimim

*1909*

Agudat HaTmimim was founded at the start of 5670 (1909) as a support network for married alumni of Tomchei Tmimim who had taken positions as rabbis, shochtim, merchants and activists. About 45 alumni attended the founding meeting in Lubavitch with the Rebbe Rayatz as chairman. By 14 Tevet 5670, a pamphlet listed about 200 members.
