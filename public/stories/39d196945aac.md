# The shliach Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Lerer — Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak HaKohen Lerer (born

*1961*

The shliach Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Lerer. Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak HaKohen Lerer (born in 5722, 1961) is an emissary of the Rebbe and director of Chabad institutions in Yavne, and the rabbi of the Chabad community in the city.
