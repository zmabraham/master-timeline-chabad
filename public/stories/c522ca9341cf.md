# Uvarov requests Third Section investigation of Tzemach Tzedek

*1841*

Two days after his letter to Stroganov, Uvarov turned to the head of the secret police with the same request. He cited the Israelitische Annalen article and its respected editor Dr. Jost as warranting confidence in the report. Uvarov asked Benckendorff to collect detailed information on the Tzemach Tzedek's character, conduct, opinions, contacts and influence over the Jews, formally initiating the Third Section's investigation.
