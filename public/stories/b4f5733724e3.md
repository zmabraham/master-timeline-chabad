# Chabad press conference on the Schneersohn library at Hotel Orlonok

*1991*

Attendance was small. R. Kogan recounted the full history of the library struggle for about half an hour and then took questions; the author answered questions on the history of the collection in Yiddish, with Kogan translating. Earlier the same day Kogan and Aharonov had met with the wife of Yeltsin's deputy Rutskoi, herself politically active, to press the books issue.
