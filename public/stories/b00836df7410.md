# Tel Aviv Haggadah illustrated by Gad Ullman from Massada Press

*1964*

In 5724 (1964), Massada Press in Tel Aviv issued a Haggadah whose calligraphy and illustration were both executed by Gad Ullman. The 20-leaf landscape edition (12x34 cm) showcased Ullman's artistic approach to the Haggadah text. It reflected the developing Israeli book arts tradition of the mid-1960s.
