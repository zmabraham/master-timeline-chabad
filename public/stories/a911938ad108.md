# Interrogation of R. Aharon of Strashelye

*1825*

Rabbi Aharon HaLevi of Strashelye, the Alter Rebbe's senior disciple turned rival rebbe, was drawn into the inquiry for over a week. His earlier letters with Rabbi DovBer were among the materials Kissin had appended to his denunciation. Despite their well-known dispute, R. Aharon's testimony did not yield material that strengthened the case against Rabbi DovBer.
