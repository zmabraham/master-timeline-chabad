# Shadar R' Mordechai Chefetz arrives in America

*1938*

Stationed at the author's home at 494 Stone Ave for about twelve weeks, his farbrengens drew students and bochurim from major New York yeshivos and sometimes lasted until just before dawn. R' Yisroel Jacobson Feigin's letter of 21 Shevat 5698 conveyed the Rebbe's praise of Chefetz as a 'penimi who remembers the past with fine inner sensitivity.' His visit substantially elevated the reputation of Lubavitch among yeshiva students.
