# Death of R. Moshe Wittenberg

*1899*

HaChavatzelet reported that on the third day of Chol HaMoed Pesach 5659 the renowned philanthropist Moshe ben Reuven Wittenberg, born and raised in Vitebsk, passed away childless. He had loved Jerusalem and made it his home for about eighteen years. After his death R. Manish Verovlanski and R. Mordechai Shlonk were jointly appointed to the address role.
