# Wedding of the author at the Marina-Roshcha synagogue, Moscow

*1940*

The principal mechutanim from the bride's side were her mother Feigl, her brother Gershon, and her uncle R' Zusya the shamash with his daughter. R' Yakov 'Yana' Goray of Romen and R' Shmarya Sasonkin (Batumer), who had fled Leningrad, were among the chassidim who attended. Because of the Soviet climate of fear, no invitations were sent and no photographs were taken.
