# Hebron Chabad-Sephardic agreement of 5628

*1868*

This marked the formal entry of R' Levi Yitzchak Slonim into kollel leadership. He would soon dominate Hebron Chabad affairs, with later agreements stating that 'no significant matter could be concluded without R' Levi Yitzchak as long as he was the chosen representative of Chabad.'
