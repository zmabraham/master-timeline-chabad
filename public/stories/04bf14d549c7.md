# R' Yechiel Cohen sent again to USA from Beit El kabbalists

*1849*

In 5609 (1849), R' Yechiel Cohen was sent again to America, this time on behalf of the Beit El kabbalists' beit midrash in Jerusalem. Despite the change of sender, the Chabad kollel of Hebron remained connected to the mission, as seen in subsequent Pekuam correspondence. The journey extended for several years.
