# R. Chaim Yeshaya Schneersohn becomes rabbi of Romen

*1901*

R. Chaim Yeshaya Schneersohn — son of the rebbe of Retchitsa, grandson of the Kapuster rebbe R. Yehuda Leib, and great-grandson of the Tzemach Tzedek — assumed the rabbinate of Romen in 5661 (1900/1901). He would serve the town for over twenty-five years and was among the senior anash figures in the city who endorsed the local petition to the 1910 St. Petersburg rabbinical conference. He held the position until his passing in late 1927.
