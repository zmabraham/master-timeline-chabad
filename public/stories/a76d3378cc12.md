# Closure of Tomchei Tmimim branch in Romen

*1929*

Schusterman had traveled to Romen to study at the yeshiva but had to return when it closed. The rosh yeshiva, Rabbi Yaakov ('Yana') Gurary, a local of Romen, was left without a position. Some townspeople proposed he assume the vacant rabbinate previously held by Rabbi Chaim Shneerson (grandson of the Tzemach Tzedek), but Rabbi Binyaminson — when offered the post — withdrew upon seeing factions form.
