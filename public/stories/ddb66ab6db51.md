# Rebbe responds to Agudat Yisrael attack on Lubavitch

*1967*

Around the time of the annual Agudat Yisrael leadership convention, one of the speakers publicly attacked the Rebbe and the Lubavitch movement, drawing a comparison to Yitzchak Avinu who loved Esav 'because there was trapping in his mouth.' The Rebbe responded at the Shabbat Toldot 5728 farbrengen (Dec 2, 1967) and at subsequent gatherings, as later published in Sichot Kodesh p. 179ff.
