# Rebbe Rashab founds Tomchei Temimim Yeshiva

*1897*

In the year 5657 (1897), Rabbi Sholom Dovber Schneersohn (the Rebbe Rashab) founded the yeshiva 'Tomchei Temimim Lubavitch.' Over subsequent years it expanded to dozens of branches across Russia, operating openly at first and then underground after Soviet persecution intensified. The yeshiva became the backbone of Chabad's educational network in Russia.
