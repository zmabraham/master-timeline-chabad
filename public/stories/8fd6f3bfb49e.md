# Elections to the All-Jewish General Congress

*1918*

Chabad's bloc 'Achdut' — number 3 on the ballot — was formed by merging with the religious lists 'Netzach Yisrael' and 'HaMasoret VeHaCheirut,' with the Rebbe's approval. In some places the Orthodox ran separately as 'Adas Yisrael' with a platform he composed. Despite electoral success, the subsequent consolidation of Bolshevik rule prevented the Congress from ever meeting.
