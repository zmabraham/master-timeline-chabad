# R' Shmuel Vital begins writing Shemoneh Shearim in Damascus

*1649*

R' Shmuel Vital completed the eight-volume autograph of the Shaarim between 5409 and 5413 (1649-1653), noting his location and date in each volume. The set was acquired for the Lubavitch library and catalogued as numbers 1942-1949. It was one of the manuscript treasures that drew Soviet interest during the 1927 search.
