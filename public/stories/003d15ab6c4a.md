# Rayatz establishes Rabbinical Committee for Russian Jewry

*1922*

By 1922 the Rebbe was in contact with communities across Russia about schools, mikvaot, rabbis, and shochtim. The Rabbinical Committee leveraged the inconsistency between formally permissive Soviet law and the aggressive Yevesektzia, lobbied the Soviet Bar Association for clarification on religious schooling, and defended teachers in court.
