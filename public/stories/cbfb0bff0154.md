# Conference of Chabad rabbanim and wealthy chassidim in Lubavitch

*1908*

Around Parshas Vayera in Marcheshvan 5669, a notable assembly of senior Chabad rabbanim and wealthy chassidim convened in Lubavitch — alluded to by the Rayatz in a later sicha of Pesach 5694. The author saw R. David Tzvi Chen of Chernigov and R. Yaakov Mordechai Bezpalov of Poltava for the first time. The aliyos in shul that Shabbos required special direction from the Rebbe Rashab himself.
