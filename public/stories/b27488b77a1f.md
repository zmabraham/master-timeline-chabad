# Rebbe Rayatz instructs Rosh Yeshiva to organize Warsaw move

*1933*

With the move to Warsaw finalized, the Rebbe instructed R. Yehuda Eber to seek a suitable courtyard with multiple apartments for the yeshiva and its officials. He emphasized that the relocation was specifically to spread Torah 'in the spirit of Tomchei Temimim of Lubavitch' — not a yeshiva that merely bore the name.
