# Wedding of the Lubavitcher Rebbe and Rebbetzin Chaya Mushka

*1928*

The wedding on 14 Kislev 5689 became one of the most observed dates on the Chabad calendar — the day the Rebbe later called 'the day that bound me to you.' In this diary it is mentioned in passing alongside a 14 Kislev entry as the Rebbe's wedding anniversary, set beside the diarist's own 14 Kislev wedding decades later.
