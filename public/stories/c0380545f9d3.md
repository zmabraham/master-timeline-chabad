# Rayatz launches family purity campaign on first US visit

*1930*

On his first visit to the United States in 5690 (1930), the sixth Lubavitcher Rebbe (Rayatz) confronted widespread ignorance of family purity and founded leagues of women in many towns to strengthen observance. This work is described in Toldos Chabad B'Artzos HaBris chapter 11.
