# Likkutei Levi Yitzchak printed by Kehot

*1970*

The marginal notes that Rabbi Levi Yitzchak Schneerson wrote on his few books during his Soviet exile of 5699-5704 (1939-1944) miraculously reached his son the Rebbe, who from 5730-5743 (1970-1983) prepared them for print. The first volumes — Likkutei Levi Yitzchak on Zohar and on Tanya — were published by Kehot in 5730 (1970).
