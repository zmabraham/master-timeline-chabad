# Agudas Chassidei Chabad founded in the United States

*1924*

Still in its infancy at the time of the Rebbe Rayatz's 1929 visit, the organization connected with roughly forty Chabad synagogues in New York City and many more across the country. Yehuda Lokshin was its director when the Rebbe wrote to him in January 1929 about plans to visit America.
