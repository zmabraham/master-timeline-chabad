# R' Yitzchak Dov Oshpal appointed Rosh Yeshiva in Vilna

*1935*

Following the Days of Awe in 5695, R' Yitzchak Dov Oshpal received instructions from the Rebbe Rayatz's secretariat to travel immediately to Vilna to take up the position of Rosh Yeshiva and director. Despite his youth, his scholarship was already renowned. He found roughly sixty students and worked to restore the discipline that had loosened since R' Spitz had departed. The third (top) class was led by Rabbi Israel Gustman.
