# Russian Culture Minister presents first returned Tanya to Vice President Gore

*1993*

Sidorov showed Gore twelve books from the Schneerson collection and gave him one: a Tanya printed in 5660 by the Rebbe Rashab through Tomchei Tmimim. A special leather-bound copy inscribed as a gift 'to the head of Tomchei Tmimim, the Rebbe Yosef Yitzchak' was the actual volume transferred. Vice President Gore handed it to Rabbi Boruch Shlomo Cunin to deliver to the Lubavitcher Rebbe.
