# Chassidic caravan ship sinks in Black Sea; some 30 of 80 survive

*1777*

On the journey from Mohilov to Constantinople in 1777, a severe storm sank one of the caravan's ships near Crimea. About 80 Jews were aboard; only some 30 survived, landing destitute in Gozlov (Eupatoria). With help from Jews of Krasov and Keilah and the Karaite community of Kafa, some survivors made their way back to Russia while others continued on to Eretz Yisrael.
