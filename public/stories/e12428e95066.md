# Rabbi Aryeh Leib Kaplan arrested and exiled to Uzbekistan

*1939*

On the train into exile he met Rabbi Levi Yitzchak Schneerson, father of the seventh Rebbe, who was also being banished. They were placed in villages thirty miles apart and stayed in contact. Kaplan was killed by anti-Semitic thugs on Yom Kippur 1943; Rabbi Levi Yitzchak died in 1944 and is buried in Alma Ata.
