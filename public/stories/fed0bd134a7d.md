# R' Eliyahu Nochum Shklyar reaches America from Eretz Yisroel

*1929*

After leaving Russia for Eretz Yisroel and marrying there, R' Eliyahu Nochum Shklyar wished to join his father in America; the author persuaded the family to sponsor him by promising he would find a livelihood. Shklyar arrived between Rosh Hashana and Yom Kippur 5690 with his wife and eldest daughter Yehudis. A few days after Sukkos, the author placed him as shochet at the slaughterhouse where his uncle R' Yeshaya Jacobson was head shochet — a post he held for many years.
