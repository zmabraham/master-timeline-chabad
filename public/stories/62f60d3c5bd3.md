# Rebbe Rayatz holds chassan mohl for Tomchei Temimim students

*1928*

On 13 Kislev 5689, the day before his daughter Chaya Mushka's wedding in Warsaw, the Rebbe Rayatz held a chassan mohl gathering specifically for the talmidim of Tomchei Temimim. He delivered a maamar of chassidus, niggunim of Chabad were sung, and after Birkat HaMazon the Rebbe danced with the students of the yeshiva.
