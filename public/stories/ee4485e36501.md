# Simchat Torah 5691 sicha and 'Pratz Klub' disruption attempt

*1930*

The printed sicha records the Rebbe's pointed remarks to the assembled, some of whom were freethinkers. During the gathering the electric lights were briefly extinguished and reignited; the disruptors had actually intended to cut the electric wires. The Rebbe later remarked that at the moment of speaking he did not understand what was forcing the content out of him.
