# Tzemach Tzedek's Even HaEzer §41 written between Chanukah and Sivan 5619

*1859*

Internal evidence in Even HaEzer §41 — its opening refers to 'Sivan 5618 past... Chanukah 5619 past' — locates the responsum between Chanukah 5619 and Sivan 5619, late 1858 to spring 1859. This and Yoreh Deah §42 establish that the entire manuscript 2143 ('Book 5619') was written in that year, the latest extant Tzemach Tzedek responsa volume.
