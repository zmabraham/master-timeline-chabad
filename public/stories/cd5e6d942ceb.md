# Pereshel facsimile of Netanel ben Aharon Segal's 1772 Haggadah manuscript

*1972*

In 5732 (1972), Tuvia Pereshel published in New York a facsimile of a manuscript Haggadah by the soferet Netanel ben Aharon Segal dated 5532 (1772). The 32-leaf octavo edition came with an introduction by Pereshel. It continued his annual series of rare Haggadah reproductions.
