# R' Yitzchok Goldin in Poland searches Warsaw cellars for lost manuscripts

*1947*

While R' Yitzchok Goldin was in Poland in summer 5707, the Rebbe Rayatz wrote secretary R' Moshe Leib Rodstein asking that his son-in-law the Ramash write Goldin about searching specific Warsaw cellars and other sites where many manuscripts had been gathered. The search produced no results at that time.
