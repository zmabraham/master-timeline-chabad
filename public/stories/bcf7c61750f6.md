# R' Zev Wolf Lewitin appointed mashgiach

*1900*

At the start of the month of Adar Rishon 5660, by call of the menahel, R' Zev Wolf Lewitin, son-in-law of R' Gershon Dov, came to Lubavitch and was appointed mashgiach over the bachurim's study of nigleh. He served as mashgiach until his passing in 5670. R' Yehuda Leib Bodnevitz was simultaneously appointed mashgiach for chassidut studies and R' Michoel Bliner as additional mashpia.
