# Anash of Jerusalem petition the Rebbe to visit Israel

*1953*

In Iyar 5713 the community of Anash in Jerusalem composed and signed a heartfelt petition imploring the Rebbe to make a visit to the Land of Israel. Citing the historic bond between Rebbe and chassid and noting that only a select few could afford to travel to Brooklyn, they begged him to come strengthen the community and its institutions in Jerusalem, Tel Aviv, Lod, Safed and elsewhere. Roughly three hundred signatures from across Israel and France were collected in total.
