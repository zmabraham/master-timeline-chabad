# Rosh Hashana 5677 with the Rebbe Rashab in Slavyansk

*1916*

The Rebbe Rashab had summered in Slavyansk in 1916 and remained there through Rosh Hashana. The Rayatz, then yeshiva director, had instructed that no student travel for the chag, but nearly all the older bochurim came anyway. The students lodged in vacated summer cottages and subsisted largely on stale black bread and tea, while important guests were hosted at the Rebbe's table. A newly built local mikveh cracked overnight, forcing the Rebbe to walk to the town mikveh on Rosh Hashana morning.
