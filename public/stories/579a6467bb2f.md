# Rebbe writes Rabbi Munk requesting German translation of maamar

*1929*

This was the first time a chasidic discourse would be published in a European language other than Hebrew or Yiddish. The Rebbe wanted to engage German Jews, many of whom lacked a strong Jewish academic background, and give them the opportunity to appreciate concepts of chasidic philosophy. The Ramash later arranged for another translation in 1936.
