# Rabbi Yehuda Leib Donin – Rabbi Yehuda Leib Donin (born in

*1972*

Rabbi Yehuda Leib Donin – Rabbi Yehuda Leib Donin (born in 5732, 1972) is one of the Rebbe's shluchim in Haifa and director of the Chabad House in the Ahuza neighborhood of the city, and a member of the Committee for Matters of Geulah and Moshiach of Tzeirei Agudas Chabad in the Holy Land.
