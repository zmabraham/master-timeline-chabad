# Group of 232 Chasidim cross Soviet border on December 2, 1946

*1946*

Russian guards removed all passengers and inspected papers; Rabbi Berel Gurewicz was arrested while the rest were allowed to reboard. The Ramash (later the seventh Rebbe) later told Chasidim in Paris that during those exact two hours, the sixth Rebbe in Brooklyn sat reciting the Az Yashir prayer and finally exclaimed, 'They got through, they got through,' moments before a telegram arrived from Przemysl.
