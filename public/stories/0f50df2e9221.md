# Alter Rebbe's first arrest – Tzok HaItim

*1798*

In 5559 the Alter Rebbe was arrested during what he later called 'tzok haitim' – the pressing times. As a result, although R' Avraham of Kalisk's letter had reached him in 5558, he was unable to compose a proper reply until 5560. This first imprisonment in Petersburg is the famous episode that Chabad commemorates each Yud-Tes Kislev as the day of his miraculous liberation.
