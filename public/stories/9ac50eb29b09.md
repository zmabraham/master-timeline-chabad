# Decision to convene Chabad Shluchim and Rabbinical Conference in Moscow

*1991*

The schedule placed the women's gathering on the night of 22 Shevat, the shluchim and shluchos conferences on Monday, and the rabbinical conference on Tuesday, with rabbis traveling in from outside the FSU. The Rebbe later replied that 'the time itself contributes to great success,' and added a blessing for 'great and abundant' success.
