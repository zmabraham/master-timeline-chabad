# Eliyahu Sasson of Bombay donates 200 pounds for Jerusalem synagogue expansion

*1879*

Overcrowding had forced worshipers into the courtyard, especially on the High Holidays, with reports of illness from the summer sun. Sasson's gift, secured through intermediary Shneur Zalman Mendelovitz, would fund a full second floor named Knesset Eliyahu in honor of the donor.
