# Last known appointment of Hebron Joint Vaad

*1909*

The last known appointment document of the Hebron joint Vaad is dated 15 Kislev 5670 and bears the signatures of seventy-five heads of family in Hebron. The names of the three elected members were not specified in the document, which simply addressed them as 'three who walk uprightly chosen by majority to manage the Vaad HaKlali.'
