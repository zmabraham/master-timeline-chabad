# R' Avraham Chaim's arrest and imprisonment in Smolensk

*1888*

R' Avraham Chaim Rosenbaum spent two years imprisoned in Smolensk, Russia, after trying to help a Jewish youth avoid Tsarist military conscription. Despite the harsh conditions, he maintained an extensive correspondence on Torah, Kabbalah, Chassidus, grammar, and divine service. His letters from prison reveal both the difficulty of his confinement and his continued spiritual life.
