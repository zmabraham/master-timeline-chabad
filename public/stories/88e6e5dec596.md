# Together with close ones at an evening benefiting Chabad institutions in the city at a celebration

*1969*

Together with close ones at an evening benefiting Chabad institutions in the city at a bar mitzvah celebration for a family of terror victims at the Western Wall. Rabbi Eliyahu Yochanan Butman (born 20 Elul 5728, 1969) is a shliach of the Rebbe, chairman of Chabad institutions in the city and director of the Chabad House
