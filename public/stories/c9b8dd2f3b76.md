# Fastov chassidim plead to keep Kuperstock

*1930*

On 14 Tishrei 5691, the chassidim of Fastov sent a signed letter to the Rebbe Rayatz protesting the planned transfer of Kuperstock to Globoke. They asked why their community should be deprived of a rabbi who guided them in the ways of their forebears, given that they too held a longstanding right to his leadership.
