# Rayatz follow-up letter to Althoyz pressing for chasidic memoirs

*1938*

Roughly two years after his first request, Rayatz wrote to Althoyz on 3 Shevat 5698 (February 1938) reiterating that he should write at least short notes — names, nicknames, appearances, and occupations — of the elder chasidim he had seen in childhood, youth, and adulthood, asking especially about 'the bears' (di berelech).
