# Mitnagdic denunciation submitted to Russian authorities against the Alter Rebbe

*1798*

On 4 Sivan 5558 the Mitnagdim submitted their formal denunciation against the Alter Rebbe and the Chassidim of Lithuania. The text of this denunciation was preserved and published in Kerem Chabad vol. 4. It led directly to the Alter Rebbe's arrest along with thirty Chassidim from Vilna, Vidz, Hamadora, Pandel and Vysokoye-Litovsk at the start of 5559.
