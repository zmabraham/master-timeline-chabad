# Uvarov requests intelligence on the Tzemach Tzedek from Stroganov

*1841*

Acting on the German-Jewish article and Maskilic agitation, Uvarov asked Stroganov whether the Ministry of Interior held any information on Mendel of Lyubavichi, his personality and the extent of his influence over the Jews and especially the Chassidim. This launched a year-long secret investigation involving the Third Section, the governor-general of Vitebsk-Mogilev, and gendarmerie officers across three provinces. The correspondence became the documentary backbone for the build-up to the 1843 Rabbinical Conference.
