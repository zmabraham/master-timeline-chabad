# R. Shmuel Zalmanov appointed chief supervisor of Tomchei Tmimim Warsaw

*1926*

R. Shmuel Zalmanov, then one of the senior students, was appointed by the Rebbe Rayatz to supervise and centralize the study of Nigleh in every division of the yeshiva and to test the students. Soon after, he was also given responsibility for administrative management, including organizing funding sources. He remained in this role until his marriage in 5693 (1933).
