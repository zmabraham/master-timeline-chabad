# Pereshel facsimile of the 1782 Kitse manuscript Haggadah

*1975*

In 5735 (1975), Tuvia Pereshel published in New York a facsimile of a manuscript Haggadah produced in the community of Kitse in 5542 (1782). The 28-page octavo edition included an English introduction by Pereshel. It was part of his ongoing series of rare Haggadah reproductions for the Passover season.
