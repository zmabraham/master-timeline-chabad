# Founding of Gemach Shomrei Shabbos in New York

*1910*

The Shomrei Shabbos gemach (free-loan society) was established in 5670 and based at the Tzemach Tzedek synagogue at 184 Henry Street, a three-story stone building in a Jewish neighborhood of New York. Although little is known of its earliest years, the institution's location at the Chabad shul suggests it may have been founded from the outset as a Chabad chassidic gemach. R' Gershon Simson served as its first chairman.
