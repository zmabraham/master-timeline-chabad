# Maharil of Yanovitch passes away

*1825*

Maharil of Yanovitch had served as the primary halachic respondent for the Chabad community after the Alter Rebbe's passing in 5573. With his death at the start of 5586, the responsibility shifted increasingly to his great-nephew the Tzemach Tzedek, who had already been building a responsa practice for roughly eight years.
