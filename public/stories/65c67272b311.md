# Tsar orders Alter Rebbe's case transferred to the Senate

*1800*

On 22 Kislev 5561 (November 27, 1800 Old Style), an order was issued in the Tsar's name directing that the proceedings against Rabbi Schneur Zalman be handed to the Senate. The Senate in turn assigned the investigation to its third department, the political secret police of Tsarist Russia, where the broader inquiry into the Chassidic movement and the future legal status of the kahal communities was being examined.
