# Agudas HaTmimim founded in Manhattan on Motzei Shabbos Shemini

*1926*

On the night of Saturday parshas Shemini 5686, the Tomchei Tmimim alumni then in America assembled at R' A. Simpson's residence at 137 W. 116th Street in New York and formally established Agudas HaTmimim. R' Yitzchak Sneiderman and the shochet R' Avraham Ram joined the society. Its early meetings focused on supporting Maamad Beis Chayeinu (sending one thousand dollars in one campaign), securing affidavits to bring Tmimim from Russia, and founding a properly observant cheder.
