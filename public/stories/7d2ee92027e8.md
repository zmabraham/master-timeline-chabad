# Passing of the Mittler Rebbe in Nezhin

*1827*

After visiting his father's grave in Haditch, the Mittler Rebbe fell ill on the way home. He passed in Nezhin on 9 (or 10) Kislev 5588, ending fifteen years of nesius. His resting place in Nezhin became, with Haditch and Rostov, one of the key Chabad pilgrimage sites in the former Russian Empire.
