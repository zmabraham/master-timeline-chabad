# Wartime shortage of Calabrian etrogim in Lubavitch

*1914*

The outbreak of World War I cut off the usual supply of Calabrian etrogim. Three presumed non-grafted etrogim were brought from the Holy Land by R' Shneur Zalman Havlin, who had returned to Lubavitch that summer and was stranded by the war. The Rebbe Rashab was so distressed by the lack of Calabrian etrogim that he developed fever and facial swelling and did not attend shul on the first days of the festival.
