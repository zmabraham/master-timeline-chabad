# US Vaad publishes Tomchei Tmimim branch list on 16 Elul 5698

*1938*

The American Vaad listed branches in Warsaw, Otwock, Vilna, Lodz, Chmielnik, Patzanow, Raciaz, Globoke, Stopnica and Chelm. The letter was part of fundraising efforts to support a network the founders rightly sensed was facing existential threats from the deteriorating European political situation.
