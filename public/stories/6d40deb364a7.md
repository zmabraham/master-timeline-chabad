# Library rerouted via Stettin-Stockholm to evade Soviet-occupied Latvia

*1940*

By late July the Soviets had annexed Latvia and installed a Communist regime. R' Chefetz — citing his poor health and unable to receive the books in Riga — instructed Schenker shipping to redirect the cargo through Stettin and on to Stockholm, to wait there for transit to New York. The reroute spared the library from Soviet confiscation.
