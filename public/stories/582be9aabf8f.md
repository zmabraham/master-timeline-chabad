# Alter Rebbe's letter (51) defending fund management

*1805*

In late summer 5565 the Alter Rebbe wrote a detailed letter explaining the events: the winter 5563 demands by emissaries, the gabbai R' Yaakov Smilyaner's intervention, the letter he sent to R' Avraham of Kalisk offering to transfer all collected funds, and how 5563-5564 funds remained undelivered because Kalisker's own emissaries had said he would refuse them.
