# Founding of separate 'Kollel Chabad HaMeyuchad'

*1913*

In the summer of 5673 (1913), the three new administrators – Lapshitz, Kazarnovsky and Levin – decided to formally constitute a separate kollel, named 'Kollel Chabad HaMeyuchad'. They explained their decision in two kuntreisim, 'Tzedek u-Mishpat u-Meisharim' and 'Divrei Shalom v'Emes'. This separate kollel persisted for roughly ten years before reunification.
