# Beginning of opposition against the Alter Rebbe

*1803*

In 5563 (1802/1803), the dispute against the Alter Rebbe – originating from R. Avraham of Kalisk in Eretz Yisrael – formally erupted. The Alter Rebbe later noted that twenty-two years of supportive correspondence (beginning in 5542) had ended with this conflict, marking a significant rupture in the chassidic leadership of the time.
