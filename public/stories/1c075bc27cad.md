# Passing of the Alter Rebbe of Lubavitch

*1812*

On 24 Tevet 5573 the Alter Rebbe, founder of Chabad chassidut, passed away during the flight from Napoleon's invading armies. His son Rabbi Dovber Schneuri (the Mittler Rebbe) succeeded him as leader of Chabad. The Mittler Rebbe continued his father's practice of collecting and sending funds to the Chabad community in Tzfat, in coordination with R' Yisrael of Pikov, son of R' Levi Yitzchak of Berditchev.
