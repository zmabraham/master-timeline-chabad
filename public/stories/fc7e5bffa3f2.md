# Founding meeting of Yeshivas Tomchei Temimim

*1897*

On 15 Elul 5657 (September 12, 1897), the day of the wedding reception, the Rashab unveiled his plan for a new yeshivah integrating Talmud with chasidic philosophy. Despite skepticism from many of the assembled rabbis and philanthropists, the Rebbe pressed forward, and this date became the official founding of Tomchei Temimim.
