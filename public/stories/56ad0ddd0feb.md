# Stopnica branch of Tomchei Temimim active

*1936*

The Stopnica branch existed from 5696 until 5699 and is referenced in the yeshiva calendar for 5697, in a 16 Elul 5698 letter from the US Vaad LeHachzakas Tomchei Temimim listing active yeshivos, and in the 5699 yeshiva calendar. Beyond these mentions no further details are known.
