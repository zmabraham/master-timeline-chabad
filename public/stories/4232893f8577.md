# Rabbi Guy Malka. Rabbi Guy Malka (born 26 Iyar 5731,

*1971*

Rabbi Guy Malka. Rabbi Guy Malka (born 26 Iyar 5731, May 21, 1971) is an educator working with the younger groups, mashpia at Chabad Yeshiva Ra'anana and at Nachalat Har Chabad, and occasionally appears as a guest lecturer and as an entertainer at children's gatherings
