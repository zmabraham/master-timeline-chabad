# Special farbrengen for charter-flight guests on eve of Rosh Hashana 5723

*1962*

At the special farbrengen held on the eve of Rosh Hashana 5723 (September 1962) for the chassidim who had come on the charter flight, the Rebbe spoke about the maaleh of the amud they had presented. He related the famous story in which the Mitteler Rebbe asked his father the Alter Rebbe what he had prayed with on Rosh Hashana, and was answered 'with the shtender' — for all is G-dliness and 'He alone is, and there is none besides Him.'
