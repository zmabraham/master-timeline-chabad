# R. Yosef Kugel of Plescenice passes away

*1876*

R. Shmuel Dovber of Borisov, writing to R. Avraham Chaim Rosenbaum on 26 Adar 5636 (1876), reacts with shock to the sudden news of the passing of 'our friend, the God-fearing R. Yosef.' Earlier letters from 5 and 18 Adar of that year had still sent regards to R. Yosef and his nephew R. Gershon Leib, showing how unexpected the loss was for the chassidic circle of Plescenice.
