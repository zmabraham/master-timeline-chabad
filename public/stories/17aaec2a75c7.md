# Rabbi Rafael Mordechai Barshansky arrested in Homel

*1922*

When Soviet authorities convened a public assembly to attack religion, Rabbi Rafael Mordechai Barshansky — a powerful preacher — rose to refute them. Unable to defeat him in open debate, the authorities trumped up charges, with one of his former servants among the judges, and imprisoned him. After great efforts he was freed and subsequently emigrated to America.
