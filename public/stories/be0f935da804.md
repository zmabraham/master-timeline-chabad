# Rabbis Aronov and Cunin fly to New York with R' Levi Yitzchak file

*1991*

Veronika asked them to also carry a letter requesting the Rebbe's blessing for the library after Gorbachev had told Chernyaev he wished to defer signing the prepared release papers. In a brief late-night yechidus the Rebbe accepted the file with words about the merit of pidyon shevuyim and 'every moment one hastens,' and when R' Cunin reported that only the Rebbe's blessing remained for the library the Rebbe replied 'do you suspect me?' He added that the book matter could yet be finished before R' Cunin's son's wedding on 18 Elul, and that bringing 'one more file' would mean Moshiach has come.
