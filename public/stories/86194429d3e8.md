# Rabbi Haber. Rabbi Chaim Eliezer Haber (born 5742, 1982

*1982*

Rabbi Haber. Rabbi Chaim Eliezer Haber (born 5742, 1982) is among the Rebbe's shluchim in Beer Sheva, engaged in writing lesson plans and sermons for all the Rebbe's shluchim in Israel and the world, and a partner in nationwide projects to make Torah study accessible to the source
