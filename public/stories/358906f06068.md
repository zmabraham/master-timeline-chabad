# Mitteler Rebbe undertakes fundraising journey through Ukraine and Lithuania

*1813*

After his father's passing and the family's wartime ruin, in 1813 Rabbi DovBer travelled through the Volhynia, Ukraine, Little Russia, and Lithuania regions to gather support for rebuilding homes, a large synagogue for gatherings and discourses, and household necessities. Communities along the route — both Chassidic and non-Chassidic — convened assemblies and contributed substantial sums, and Belarus communities followed through on the pledges they had sent to him in Kremenchug. In total roughly 35,000 rubles were collected and distributed among family members for housing, clothing, books, and about six months of living expenses.
