# Beis Lubavitch established in London

*1958*

In 5718 (1958), a building was purchased in London to serve as a regional Chabad headquarters. The Rebbe instructed it be named 'Beis Lubavitch' (Igros Kodesh vols. 17-18). When construction was completed, the building's key was sent to the Rebbe, who responded with a special letter expressing great satisfaction at the milestone.
