# Marozov writes to Axelrod in Baltimore

*1925*

On 2 Iyar 5685, secretary Elchonon Dov Marozov acknowledged receipt of three shekalim from a donor through Axelrod, who had by then emigrated to Baltimore. Marozov described how despite difficult material conditions in Soviet Russia they had celebrated Pesach with joy, lamenting that no acquaintance had bread or peace of mind. He praised Axelrod for having escaped 'the darkness here where the air is full of heresy and apostasy, enforced with a strong hand.'
