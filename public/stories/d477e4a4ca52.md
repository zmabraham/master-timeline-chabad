# Chabad library shipped from Russia to Latvia

*1927*

A Soviet investigator who understood the value of Jewish books urged the government to block the export. Mordechai Dubin arranged a second investigator who lacked such knowledge and authorized the license. The library, augmented in 1925 by a 5,000-volume collection from Shmuel Weiner, traveled with the Rebbe to Latvia, separate from the bulk stored in Moscow that the Soviets had refused to return.
