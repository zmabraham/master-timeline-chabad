# Likkutei Biurim on Kuntres HaHispaalus by Rabbi Hillel of Paritch published

*1868*

Rabbi Hillel of Paritch, a foremost chassid of the Mitteler Rebbe and Tzemach Tzedek, wrote extensive commentaries on the Rebbeim's chassidic discourses. His Likkutei Biurim on Kuntres HaHispaalus was first printed in Warsaw in 5628 (1868).
