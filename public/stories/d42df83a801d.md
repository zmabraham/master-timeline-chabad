# Wedding of the Frierdiker Rebbe in Lubavitch

*1897*

This wedding coincided with the founding of Tomchei Tmimim yeshiva in the same summer. The great hall again served as the venue for one of the dynasty's central family events. Together, the founding of the yeshiva and the marriage of the heir-apparent made the summer of 5657 a pivotal moment in Lubavitch history.
