# Meeting with former Soviet Foreign Minister Shevardnadze

*1991*

After several failed attempts, the delegation reached Shevardnadze in his new ministerial-oversight role. He recounted that two months earlier he had passed their matter to President Gorbachev, who had referred it to Culture Minister Gubenko. He accepted their memoranda and documents and promised to study what he could do, while advising them to also approach the ministers of culture and justice again.
