# Haggadat Am published by Adamah with English translation by Mordechai HaCohen

*1987*

In 5747 (1987), Adamah Press in the Holy Land issued 'Haggadat Am', translated and commented in English by Mordechai HaCohen and illustrated by Chaim Ron. The 184-page folio edition served the modern Israeli-American Jewish audience. It represented contemporary Israeli liturgical publishing aimed at a bilingual market.
