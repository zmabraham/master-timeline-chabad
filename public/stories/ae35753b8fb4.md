# R' Menachem Mendel Naeh appointed Kollel Chabad trustee

*1912*

The Rebbe Rashab wrote to Naeh asking him to undertake reforms in the kollel concerning prayer and Torah study, so that it would at least partially correspond to the holy intent of its founders. The appointment came amid a broader period of transition in the kollel leadership, including the death of R' Dovber Efrat on Erev Rosh Chodesh Tammuz that same summer.
