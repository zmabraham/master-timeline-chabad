# Simcha Keysin's letter to authorities denouncing the Chabad chassidim of Shklov

*1825*

In June 1825, Simcha Keysin submitted a denunciation to a high Russian official, complaining that the chassidic movement led by the late Rabbi Shneur Zalman and now his son Rabbi DovBer was expanding its influence, seizing parts of the main Shklov synagogue, controlling printing presses for chassidic-rite siddurim, and forcing 'true Jews' to use their kosher meat. He framed the chassidim as an illegitimate sect harming the Jewish community.
