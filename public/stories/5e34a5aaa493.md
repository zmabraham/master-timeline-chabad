# Polyakov Synagogue returned to Chabad possession in Moscow

*1991*

Despite the current tenants' refusal to vacate on the grounds that the city had not yet provided alternate premises, large groups of Chabad children and adults pushed through the doors after davening at camp, broke into the sanctuary, turned on the electricity and began davening. Police observed and left while officials negotiated at the Mossoviet; Veronika intervened forcefully and returned with a decision that the building would transfer to Chabad that very night, with militia stationed to protect it.
