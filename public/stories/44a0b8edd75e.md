# Rebbe Rayatz gives 50th anniversary gift to Rebbetzin Nechama Dina

*1947*

On 13 Elul 5707, marking fifty years since their wedding, Rebbe Yosef Yitzchak wrote a brief Yiddish note to his wife wishing many long, healthy, content years, nachas from their children, and all good both materially and spiritually. He gifted her a new coat funded by $500 from 'our joint fund,' to be worn in health and with a joyful heart. The future Rebbe (then Ramash) gifted Rebbetzin Nechama Dina a printed copy of Rebbe Rayatz's Sefer HaZichronot, which he had brought to press that same year.
