# Investigators submit summary of Vitebsk interrogations to Khovansky

*1825*

On 12 Kislev 5586 the Vitebsk investigators compiled and submitted their formal review of the inquiry into the Mitteler Rebbe to the governor-general, Prince Nikolai Khovansky. The document incorporated the findings of Korbitsky's on-site investigation in Lubavitch concerning the synagogue and the Rebbe's library, alongside the responses given by the Rebbe and witnesses such as R. Aharon of Strashelye during the Vitebsk interrogations.
