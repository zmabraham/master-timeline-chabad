# Tzemach Tzedek answers responsum to R' Aharon of Kremenchug

*1838*

On 22 Sivan 5598 (June 1838), Rabbi Menachem Mendel Schneersohn — the Tzemach Tzedek — wrote a responsum on Orach Chaim, printed as siman 102 in his Shu't Tzemach Tzedek, in reply to a question from his relative Rabbi Aharon of Kremenchug. R' Aharon was a grandson of the Alter Rebbe through his daughter Freida, and was a son-in-law of both the Mitteler Rebbe and of Rebbe Nachman of Breslov.
