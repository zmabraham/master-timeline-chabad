# Mitteler Rebbe writes petition to Governor Khovansky

*1826*

While held in Vitebsk between Cheshvan and the beginning of Kislev 5587 (October-November 1826), the Mitteler Rebbe composed a written petition to General-Governor Khovansky. He requested that the governor personally adjudicate his case, drawing on kabbalistic and chassidic principles the Alter Rebbe had earlier presented before Czar Paul. The petition built on the framework relating fear of Heaven to fear of an earthly king and argued that a king's vision penetrates more truthfully than that of any subordinate judge.
