# Rabbi Yaakov Yehuda Schwartz, Rabbi Yaakov Yehuda (Yanky) Schwartz 

*1989*

Rabbi Yaakov Yehuda Schwartz, Rabbi Yaakov Yehuda (Yanky) Schwartz (born 5749, 1989) is a shliach of the Rebbe and director of the Chabad House and 'Beit Menachem' center in the Chefzibah neighborhood in Beit Shemesh, and founder of the 'HaParsha B'Chayai' institute and the 'Nishmata' telephone line for Chassidus classes
