# Recovered Warsaw sefarim shipped via Paris to New York

*1948*

In winter 5708, R' Shmuel Zalmanov reported that his relative R' Shneur Zalman Gurevitch had finally shipped, together with his own possessions, all the recovered Chabad books obtained from Dr. Kahane at the Warsaw kehilla. On 17 Shevat the Rebbe Rayatz wrote Gurevitch directly to thank him. Whether any of these were among the missing 17 crates remained unverified.
