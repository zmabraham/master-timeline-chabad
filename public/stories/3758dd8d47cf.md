# R. Meir Shlomo Yanovsky writes pre-Rosh Hashanah letter to grandchildren

*1930*

On 24 Elul 5690 (Sept. 17, 1930), Rabbi Meir Shlomo Yanovsky and Rebbetzin Rachel wrote from Nikolayev to their grandchildren — the future Rebbe and Rebbetzin Chaya Mushka, and Rebbetzin's brother Yisrael Aryeh Leib. They expressed longing to see them, asked for letters in return, and gave their Rosh Hashanah blessings; this was one of the last letters before Yanovsky's passing three years later.
