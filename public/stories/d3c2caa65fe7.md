# Rabbi Yechezkel Feigin killed in Warsaw synagogue fire

*1941*

Passport issues had prevented Feigin from leaving Riga with the Rebbe in 1939 for the United States. In December 1941, Nazis brought most of the city's Jews into a synagogue and set it ablaze, forcing other Jews to watch. According to some reports, Rabbi Yitzchak Gurevitz removed the Torah scroll from the ark and danced with Feigin as they burned alive; when news reached the Rebbe in 1945, he fainted from grief.
