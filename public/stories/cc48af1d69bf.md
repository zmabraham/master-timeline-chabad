# Rebbe declares Mivtza Chinuch and Ahavat Yisrael; year of education

*1975*

In 5736 (1975–76), the Rebbe declared the campaigns of Chinuch (Jewish education) and Ahavat Yisrael (love of fellow Jew), and proclaimed the year a 'Year of Education.' He spoke about education at numerous farbrengens that summer and instructed children to memorize the Twelve Pesukim and sayings of Chazal. Special children's rallies were held at which the Rebbe addressed the children and listened to their recitation.
