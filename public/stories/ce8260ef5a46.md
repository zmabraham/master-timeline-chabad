# Lubavitcher Rebbe sends delegation to Russia for redemption of books

*1991*

The Rebbe appointed the four delegates to lead the newly formed Agudas Chabad in Russia and later in the Commonwealth of Independent States. The delegation met with the Russian government to discuss the books still held in the Moscow public library. The government subsequently sent a recommendation to the leadership of the Smolensk region, which includes Lubavitch, to assist with related plans including the future museum project.
