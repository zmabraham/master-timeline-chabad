# Rabbi David Yosef the Rishon LeTzion. Born 14 Av 5717 (1957)

*1957*

Rabbi David Yosef the Rishon LeTzion. Born 14 Av 5717 (1957) Jerusalem. Place of activity Jerusalem. Affiliation Sephardic rabbis. Additional roles head of the Yechave Daat beit midrash. His teachers his father Rabbi Ovadia Yosef. His writings the Halacha Brura book series
