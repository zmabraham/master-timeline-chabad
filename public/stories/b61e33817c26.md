# Fez beit din pesharah on the shop of Yitzchak's heirs

*1698*

In the last third of Kislev 5459 (late 1698) in Fez, the beit din finalized a ruling concerning a shop linked to one Yitzchak, ruling that his relatives who inherited would pay in full to rescue the shop from claimants and that R. Maimon would also be empowered to collect an additional sum at the time of redemption. After a clerical error in the amount, '30' was corrected to '20' between the lines before the remaining dayanim signed. The document was signed by R. Menachem Sirero, R. Vidal Hatzarfati, R. Yehuda ibn Atar, R. Shmuel Hatzarfati, and endorsed by R. Moshe ibn Hamu.
