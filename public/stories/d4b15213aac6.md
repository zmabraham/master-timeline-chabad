# R' Avraham of Kalisk critiques the Tanya in writing

*1797*

Following the appearance of the Tanya in 5557, R' Avraham of Kalisk wrote to the Alter Rebbe that he found no value in 'the Book of the Beinonim' for saving souls, since common chassidim need only faith in their teachers, not intellectual analysis. He simultaneously addressed the Russian chassidic community, urging that chassidic texts be gathered and kept hidden among the spiritually refined rather than studied broadly. He grounded his position on the rabbinic teaching 'the righteous shall live by his faith.'
