# Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Meir Teichtel. Rabbi Teichtel arranging a wedding

*1962*

Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Meir Teichtel. Rabbi Teichtel arranging a wedding. Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Meir Teichtel (born 5722, 1962) is the rabbi of the Chabad community of Aubervilliers and a member of the Lubavitch Rabbinical Council of France, serving as head of the 'Yad Mordechai' kollel in Paris.
