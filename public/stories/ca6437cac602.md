# Letter to Tzemach Tzedek signed by R' Shimon Shmerling

*1831*

R' Shimon Shmerling, one of the founders of the Chabad community in Hebron, signed the 5591 letter from Hebron to the Tzemach Tzedek. This was part of regular correspondence between the Hebron community and the Lubavitcher leadership in Russia, reporting on the situation and requesting continued support.
