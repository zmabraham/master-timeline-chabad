# Library opens Rebbe Rayatz exhibition on 12 Tammuz 5760

*2000*

Marking the 120th anniversary of the Rebbe Rayatz's birth and 73 years since his Soviet liberation, the exhibition displayed his shtreimel, kittel, walking staff, passport, diaries, and chassidic manuscripts. It ran through the jubilee 50th yahrzeit period ending 10 Shvat 5761.
