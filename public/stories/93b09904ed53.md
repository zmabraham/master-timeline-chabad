# Rebbe Rayatz visits Glubokoe and Postov

*1932*

Responding to requests from Anash in the roughly forty small towns of the Vilna region who could not travel to see him, the Rebbe spent about ten days touring the area until Purim. He delivered three maamarim in Postov and was accompanied by Rabbi Menachem Mendel Kupershtok of Postov and Rabbi Mordechai Chefetz. The train broke down en route, requiring a four-hour wait during which he received about fifty people in yechidus.
