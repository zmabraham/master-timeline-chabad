# R' Yaakov Landa leaves Russia and settles in Libau

*1927*

At the beginning of 5688 (1927), R' Yaakov Landa exited Soviet Russia, where he had served as rabbi in the Rebbe Rashab's court in Rostov and headed Tiferes Bachurim in Moscow. He settled with relatives in Libau (Liepaja), Latvia, with the Rebbe Rayatz personally directing him to assess the local community's needs.
