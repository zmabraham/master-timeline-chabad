# Rebbetzin Chana Schneersohn passes away

*1964*

Rebbetzin Chana Schneersohn, mother of the Lubavitcher Rebbe and widow of Rabbi Levi Yitzchak, passed away on 6 Tishrei 5725. With her death ended the twelve-year concealment of her younger son Israel Aryeh Leib's earlier passing, which had been hidden from her to protect her health.
