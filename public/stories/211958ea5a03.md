# Publication of Klalei HaTorah V'HaMitzvot volume 3

*1986*

In 5746 (1986), Rabbi Pinchas Teitz edited and published volume 3 of the Rogatchover's Tzofnat Paneach — Klalei HaTorah V'HaMitzvot, picking up where Rabbi Kasher had left off. Teitz also began preparations for volume 4. The Rebbe responded with thanks and encouragement to expedite the fourth volume.
