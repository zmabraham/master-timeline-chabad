# Imrei Noam by Rabbi Hillel of Paritch published in Vilna

*1876*

Rabbi Hillel of Paritch wrote his own chassidic discourses and commentaries on those of the Rebbeim. Imrei Noam was first printed in Vilna in 5636 (1876) and is displayed at the Lubavitch Library exhibition of the great Chabad chassidim.
