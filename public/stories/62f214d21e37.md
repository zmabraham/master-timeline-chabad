# Rabbi Karniel (5784, left). Rabbi Aharon Karniel (right)

*1970*

Rabbi Karniel (5784, left). Rabbi Aharon Karniel (right, next to Rabbi Ephraim Fishel Demichovsky (Kfar Chabad)). Rabbi Aharon Yehoshua (Arke) Karniel (born 20 Iyar 5730, 1970) is the Rebbe's shliach and founder and director of the Chabad House in Bnei Ayish.
