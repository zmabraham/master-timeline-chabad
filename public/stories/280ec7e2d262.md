# Aryeh Ehrlich at a 19 Kislev farbrengen at the Chabad shul in Meah Shearim 5771

*1987*

Aryeh Ehrlich at a 19 Kislev farbrengen at the Chabad shul in Meah Shearim 5771. R. Pinchas Aryeh Leib Ehrlich-Weber, known as 'Aryeh Ehrlich' (born 5747, 1987), is a Chabad journalist and author, who serves as editor of the chareidi weekly magazine 'Mishpacha.'
