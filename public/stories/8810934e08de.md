# Chabad settlement in Tzfat renewed

*1870*

R' Yisrael Dover Yaffe, son of one of the Hebron Kollel Chabad administrators, relocated from Hebron to Tzfat to live near his father-in-law R' David Yehuda Leib. Around the same time, the widow Mrs. Bila, niece of the Tzemach Tzedek, immigrated to Tzfat with her five orphaned sons. The two families merged through marriage and became the nucleus of the renewed Chabad community in Tzfat.
