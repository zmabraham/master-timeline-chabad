# Warsaw Haggadah with 'Shem MiShmuel' commentary by Shmuel Bornstein of Sochatchov

*1928*

In 5688 (1928), the Haggadah with the 'Shem MiShmuel' commentary by Rebbe Shmuel ben Avraham Bornstein of Sochatchov was published in Warsaw, edited by Chanoch Heinich Falman. Printed by Wegmeister, the 64-leaf octavo edition became one of the central Chassidic Haggadah commentaries of the interwar era. It distilled the Sochatchov Chassidic teachings on Passover.
