# R. Yechezkel Banet of Neitra writes to Maharia Asad on monitoring reformers

*1844*

In a letter dated the 6th day of Chanukah 5605 from Neitra, R. Yechezkel Banet expressed pain over the continuing troubles being caused to Torah scholars by the reformers. He informed R. Yehuda Asad that he had stationed informants to alert him the moment any modernist proposal was placed before the government committee, at which point he would dispatch a special messenger so that a coordinated response could be prepared. The letter reflects the underground intelligence network the Hungarian rabbinate maintained in the wake of the Paks assembly.
