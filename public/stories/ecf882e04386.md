# R' Menachem Mendel formally appoints Alter Rebbe as leader of Russia

*1786*

In 5546 (1786), responding to the Alter Rebbe's hesitation, R' Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk wrote from Tiveria urging him to 'be strong and courageous… for Hashem has given you as a shepherd' and to lead all the chassidim of Russia. R' Avraham of Kalisk wrote in parallel echoing the appointment.
