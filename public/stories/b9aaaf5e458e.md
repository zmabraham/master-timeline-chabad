# Rabbi Hutner sends apology letter after Agudat Yisrael convention reports

*1968*

On Yom Rishon of Parshat Vayechi 5728 (Jan 7, 1968), Rabbi Hutner wrote a second letter to the Rebbe after hearing from Rabbi Zalman Gurary about the recent Agudat Yisrael convention and its attacks on the Rebbe. He stressed that he had never attended any such convention, that his earlier halachic letter was strictly private and personal, and asked forgiveness if it had caused any pain — while noting that the Torah content itself was not subject to retraction.
