# Rabbi A.D. Lavut re-edits and prints the Siddur Torah Or in Vilna

*1887*

By 5647 (1887), various siddur reprintings had accumulated textual corruptions. Rabbi Lavut's Vilna edition restored the nusach to its early Shklov-Kapust form and added Shaarei Tefillah, a commentary explaining the precise wording. The edition was reprinted multiple times in 1889, 1890, and 1892.
