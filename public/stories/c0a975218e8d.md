# Frierdiker Rebbe delivers 19 Kislev 5691 maamar on divine influx

*1930*

R. Yechezkel Feigin reported the content of the maamar to the author in a letter from Kislev 5691. The Rebbe taught that the spiritual influence to neshamot Yisrael is drawn from the name Havayah via meavir ha-teva — overriding the natural order.
