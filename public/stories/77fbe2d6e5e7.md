# R. Moshe ben Asher petitions to overturn new Vilna kahal

*1799*

Three days after the new chassidic-led kahal was sworn in, R. Moshe ben Asher, a leader of the mitnagdim, lodged a formal complaint demanding the old board be restored. He soon followed with a second petition urging the authorities to suppress the chassidic movement in Vilna entirely.
