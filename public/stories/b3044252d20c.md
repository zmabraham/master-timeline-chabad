# Pereshel-Weintraub facsimile of the Amsterdam 1662 Haggadah

*1981*

In 5741 (1981), Tuvia Pereshel and Munish Weintraub published in New York a facsimile reproduction of the 1662 Amsterdam-printed Haggadah. The 24-leaf octavo edition included bilingual English-Hebrew introductions by the publishers. It was part of their annual program of reissuing rare Haggadot in the period before Passover.
