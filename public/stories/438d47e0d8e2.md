# Russian decree against Hebrew presses

*1837*

The תקצ"ז decree (gezeirat hadefusim) shuttered most Hebrew presses inside Russia. In response, Hebrew presses sprang up in Prussian towns near the Russian border — Königsberg, Johannisberg, Memel, Danzig — producing sefarim aimed primarily at the Russian Jewish market. Books were smuggled in with disguised title pages, omitting place and printer or even falsifying the year, so as to claim if seized to be old volumes legally predating the decree.
