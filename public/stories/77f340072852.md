# Bragadin and Justinian Rambam controversy in Venice

*1550*

In 5310 (1550), the rival Venetian printers Bragadin and Justinian both issued editions of Rambam's Mishneh Torah. Bragadin's edition included new glosses by Maharam of Padua; Justinian pirated those glosses, reprinted them, and undersold his competitor. The dispute convulsed the Jewish world, and the Rema (R. Moshe Isserles) issued a cherem against anyone who purchased the Justinian edition.
