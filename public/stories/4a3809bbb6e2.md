# R. Yechezkel Feigin writes about plans to expand Warsaw yeshiva

*1928*

Feigin reported that the Rebbe Rayatz was deeply moved by the conduct and condition of the Warsaw yeshiva, which had approximately 250 students and was the largest Chabad yeshiva. He noted that there was great financial hardship despite the achievement, and that the Rebbe was determined to take responsibility for substantially expanding the institution materially and spiritually. The director of Agudas Chassidei Chabad in America was being informed in preparation for fundraising efforts.
