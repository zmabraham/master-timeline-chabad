# Rosenbaum released from Smolensk prison around Parshat Korach 5650

*1890*

After almost two years of imprisonment in Smolensk, Reb Avraham Chaim was released around the Monday of Parshat Korach 5650. In his last prison letter he announced the date of his expected freedom and his intention to journey at once to Lubavitch to see the Rebbe Maharash's son and successor circle.
