# Achos Tmimim founded in Riga

*1936*

Beginning the previous winter, daughters of Riga chassidim had organized informal gatherings. By Yud-Tes Kislev 5697 they were structured enough to send a collective letter to the Rebbe describing their celebration. Over the course of 5697 the framework crystallized into the named organization Achos Tmimim, with three appointed 'shepherds': R. Avraham Eliyahu Asherov, R. Mordechai Chefetz, and R. Eliyahu Chaim Altheuse.
