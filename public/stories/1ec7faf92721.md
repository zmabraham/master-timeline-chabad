# Liberation of the Mitteler Rebbe on Yud Kislev

*1825*

The first stage of the Mitteler Rebbe's liberation came when Khovansky freed him to return home after the initial interrogations concluded. This date became Yud Kislev, the Chassidic festival of redemption commemorating his release. The legal case, however, was far from over: Khovansky immediately referred the file to the Mogilev provincial authorities for full trial proceedings.
