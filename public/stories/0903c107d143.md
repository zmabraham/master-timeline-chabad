# Letter to Tzemach Tzedek from R' Shmuel of Tzfat

*1835*

In the winter of 5595 R' Shemayah wrote to the Tzemach Tzedek explaining that after Pesach, once the rains stopped, he intended to settle in Hebron among his Chabad brethren. He noted that R' Shmuel Halevi specifically advised him to join the Hebron community. The letter is preserved in Migdal Oz and indirectly confirms R' Shmuel's role as the de facto rabbi of Hebron.
