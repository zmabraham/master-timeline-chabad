# Łódź yeshiva moves to Pomorska A41 building

*1932*

Beginning in Shevat 5692, the Łódź yeshiva relocated from Paludniowa 25 to spacious modern quarters at Pomorska A41. The new building featured central heating, hot water and modern facilities, and housed roughly fifty families including many chassidim. With the move, the yeshiva expanded to three divisions and grew to about one hundred students.
