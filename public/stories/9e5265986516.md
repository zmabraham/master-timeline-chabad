# Tzemach Tzedek instructs family members to settle in Hebron

*1843*

Around 5604 the Tzemach Tzedek instructed several members of his family to immigrate to Eretz Yisrael and settle in Hebron. Three granddaughters of the Alter Rebbe came from Lubavitch with their families: Menucha Rachel (daughter of the Mitteler Rebbe) with R' Yaakov Slonim, Sarah Rivka with R' Nachum Yosef Schneersohn, and Rachel with R' Moshe Tzvi Pundeminsky. They became the cornerstone of the Chabad settlement in Hebron.
