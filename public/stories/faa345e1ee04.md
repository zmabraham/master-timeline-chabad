# Rebbe Rashab approves Eliezerov as Rabbi of Hebron

*1903*

The Rashab had earlier received a petition from Hebron residents demanding a rabbi from outside the city; though he disagreed, he had stepped back rather than impose his choice. When the community itself converged on Eliezerov, he sent an immediate telegram of approval and a long letter the next day to Eliezerov himself.
