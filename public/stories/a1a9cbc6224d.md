# R. Yochanan Gordon immigrates to America from Dokshitz

*1931*

On arrival R. Yochanan replaced his sons' Russian-style caps with American hats. When Sholom Dovber was embarrassed to enter the author's home wearing the new hat during Chol Hamoed Pesach, the author explained how garments of honor change with time and place, citing teachings from both the Rebbe Rashab and the Rebbe Rayatz on the matter.
