# Shanghai students receive visa application forms

*1946*

On the same 7 Shevat 5706, the students wrote to the Frierdiker Rebbe that they had received the visa application blanks, but since they had not yet received the affidavits, they could not return them to the consulate — the applications required the names and addresses of those sending the affidavits, which they did not yet know. They asked for the Rebbe's blessing that they obtain the visas quickly and easily.
