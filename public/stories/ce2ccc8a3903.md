# Shmuel Tzvi Fuchs enters Otwock yeshiva after bar mitzva

*1935*

Fuchs entered the first preparatory class, where nigleh was studied by day and Tanya at night. He would learn there until the outbreak of WWII at the end of 5699, and counted his Tishrei 5696 with the Rebbe as a central experience of his life.
