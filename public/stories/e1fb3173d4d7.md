# Vilna 'Siddur with Likutei Torah' of Chabad's Alter Rebbe

*1912*

In 5672 (1912), a Siddur was published in Vilna containing Likutei Torah—Chassidic discourses drawn from the writings of the Chabad Alter Rebbe Schneur Zalman of Liadi, arranged according to the order of the prayers. The 360-leaf octavo edition made the Alter Rebbe's Chassidic teachings accessible alongside the daily liturgy. It served the Chabad community of Lithuania and beyond in the years before the Russian Revolution.
