# Rad'tz Chen quietly dissolves Chernigov Jewish party assembly

*1917*

Under the Provisional Government, plans were underway for a national Jewish assembly in Kharkov to elect a council overseeing religious and educational affairs, and parties of every stripe were sending emissaries to local towns. A visiting rabbi came to Chernigov on behalf of a religious party — the author recalls it being called Netzach Yisrael or similar — and the Rad'tz himself attended the public meeting. Sensing during the speeches that the gathering did not in fact reflect truly observant priorities, the famously otherworldly Rad'tz acted with quiet practical wisdom and dissolved the assembly, and no delegates were chosen for that faction.
