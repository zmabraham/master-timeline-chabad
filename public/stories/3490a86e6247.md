# R. Pinchas Horowitz stays in Brod en route to the Frankfurt rabbinate

*1771*

Traveling from Volhynia to Germany after taking leave of the Maggid, R. Pinchas Horowitz stopped in Brod for Shabbos. His approbation for the Zolkva 5532 edition of Totza'os Chaim was dated there on Wednesday 15 Cheshvan 5532, fixing the timing of his journey precisely. From Brod he continued westward toward Frankfurt.
