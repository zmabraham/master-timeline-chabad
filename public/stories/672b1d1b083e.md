# R' Yechiel Heilprin among yoshvim during mourning year

*1883*

R' Yechiel Heilprin was among the yoshvim in Lubavitch during 5643 (1883), which was the year of mourning of the Rebbe Rashab for his father, the Rebbe Maharash. From 5660 onwards, R' Yechiel served as a shadar (emissary) for Tomchei Tmimim, raising funds for the yeshiva.
