# Yeshivat Torat Emet founded in Hebron

*1911*

The Rebbe Rashab established Yeshivat Torat Emet in Hebron at the beginning of 5672. To launch it he dispatched a dedicated mashpia along with seven of the finest students from Yeshivat Tomchei Temimim in Lubavitch. In a general letter to chassidim in winter 5672 he explained that the lack of a proper yeshiva in their kollel had left even those drawn to Torah study without support, and announced the new institution as the realization of the founders' true vision.
