# Maggid Devarav LeYaakov first edition published in Koretz

*1781*

In 5541 (1781), the first edition of Maggid Devarav LeYaakov by Rabbi Dovber, the Maggid of Mezeritch, was published in Koretz. The work transmits Chassidic teachings of the Maggid, successor to the Baal Shem Tov. A copy is preserved in the Lubavitch Library.
