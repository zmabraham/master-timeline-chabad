# Secretariat letter recounts collapse of Moscow library negotiations

*1927*

The letter recounts that Rayatz had again obtained a Narkompros authorization through the prominent attorney I. S. Orinson, but when emissary R. Chaim Manesson came to collect the books, library staff confiscated the permit and stalled, eventually claiming the document was lost. When Orinson tried again, no new permit was forthcoming. After more than three years, the library board declared the books were now their property. Eisenstadt — described as acting from personal vengeance against the Rebbe's known anti-Zionist stance — was identified as the driving force.
