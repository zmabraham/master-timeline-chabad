# Rayatz appoints five trusted men to oversee Kollel matters

*1924*

On 7 Iyar 5684 (May 1924), after two more years of inconclusive correspondence, Rebbe Rayatz appointed a committee of five trusted associates: R. Shlomo Zalman Havlin, R. Shiloh, R. Yitzchak Shmuel Luria, R. Menachem Mendel Naeh and R. Moshe Dov Rivkin. He asked them to examine the kollel's true situation so he could direct its restoration.
