# Passing of Rebbetzin Rivka, wife of the Rebbe Maharash

*1914*

The Rebbetzin's yahrzeit on 10 Shevat is observed by the Frierdiker Rebbe leading prayers before the amud. The date is referenced in the narrative of the Frierdiker Rebbe's 10 Shevat 5690 prayers in Crown Heights, when Michael Feder traveled from Philadelphia to be present.
