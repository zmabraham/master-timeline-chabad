# Jacobson becomes baal tokea at Anshei Bobruisk

*1926*

Before Elul 5686, the congregants asked Jacobson to serve as their baal tokea, despite his protest that he had almost never blown the shofar before. After practicing and finding the task within his ability, he purchased a new shofar. He went on to serve as the shul's baal tokea for twenty-four years, until he eventually left the position.
