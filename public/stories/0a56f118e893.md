# HoD residents protest EY fund distribution

*1836*

In Adar 5596 the HoD residents (Holland and Deutschland), having established a separate fifth kollel in Eretz Yisrael, contested the distribution arrangement. Though their immigrants were few, they argued that since most Pekoam funds came from their home countries, they deserved a larger share than per-capita allocation would yield. The dispute would persist until 5608.
