# Lilienthal urges harsh suppression of Chassidic opposition

*1841*

On 11 March 1841 (Rosh Chodesh Nissan 5601), shortly after being appointed to oversee Jewish education, Dr. Max Lilienthal wrote to official Dukst-Dushinsky in the Education Ministry. He reported that his negotiations with the Chassidim of Minsk and Vitebsk revealed they would mount strong and consistent opposition. He urged the government to intervene and crush them in the harshest manner possible.
