# Rakeffet visits Riga as covert Mossad agent and finds living Chabad legacy

*1985*

After services, older Jews ushered him upstairs once government agents left, then 'broke out vodka and cake' for a farbrengen with stories of the Rebbe. Rakeffet attests the richness absorbed during the Rebbe's six-year stay was 'still pulsating half a century later.'
