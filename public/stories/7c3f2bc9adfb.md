# Rabbi Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk names three Russian Chassidic leaders

*1782*

On 4 Adar 5542, Rabbi Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk wrote from Eretz Yisrael instructing his Russian Chassidim not to travel to Volhynia for Chassidic guidance but to bind themselves only to three named leaders in Russia: Rabbi Yisrael of Polotsk, Rabbi Yissachar Ber of Lubavitch, and Rabbi Schneur Zalman. The letter notably omitted other Maggid disciples then in Russia—Rabbi Levi Yitzchak (then in Pinsk), Rabbi Shlomo of Karlin, and Rabbi Chaim Chaikel of Amdur—marking the beginning of what would become the proto-Chabad line of leadership.
