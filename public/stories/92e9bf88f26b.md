# Wedding of Chana Schneerson and Rabbi Shmaryahu Gurary in Rostov

*1921*

At the time, Rostov was suffering from typhus and famine, and the Yevsektzia had forcibly closed the Lubavitch yeshivah. The secret police broke into the Rebbe's home before the wedding and seized property including the wedding dress. With limited finances, only light refreshments of cake and drinks were served to guests.
