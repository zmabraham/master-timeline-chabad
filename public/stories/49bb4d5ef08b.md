# Author meets Rebbe Rayatz at Perchtoldsdorf near Vienna

*1937*

The Rebbe took deep interest in the students; when asked which maamarim to teach them, he answered 'whatever you learn with them — the main thing is the embroidery (ushibatzta).' The Rebbe also remarked on R' D.M. Rabinowitz's unsanctioned trip to Eretz Yisrael saying 'Grandfather would command, Father would say, and I say as it comes out — but one must obey.' Before parting, the Rebbe instructed the author to write down everything he saw and heard on the journey.
