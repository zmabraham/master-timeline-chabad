# R. Yehuda Eber appointed rosh yeshiva in Warsaw

*1931*

Early in 5692 (autumn 1931) the Rebbe Rayatz appointed R. Yehuda Eber rosh yeshiva of Tomchei Tmimim Warsaw. Together with the position came the assignment to organize Tanya classes in batei midrash across the city, a task R. Yehuda delegated in part to a young avreich named Moshe Chaim.
