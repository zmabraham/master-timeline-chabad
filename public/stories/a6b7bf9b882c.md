# Morozov killed by firing squad

*1938*

Having hidden since 1930 under the false identity Berke Pesner, Morozov was finally caught in 1938 along with his son Shmuel. Police tortured Shmuel to confirm his father's identity; Morozov cried out, 'Shmuel, tell them that I am your father!' Police told Morozov his crime was being 'a representative of the Lubavitcher Rebbe, who is G-d's representative in Russia.'
