# Warsaw students meet R' Alter at Acharon shel Pesach farbrengen

*1922*

The gathering, hosted at the home of R' Zalman Shmotkin, continued nonstop into the next day, and Alter delivered the maamar 'V'henif yado al hanahar.' With his arrival the yeshiva's level of study in nigleh and chassidus rose markedly and an elevated atmosphere took hold among the students.
