# R. Yehuda Eber appointed Rosh Yeshiva of Tomchei Temimim Warsaw

*1931*

On 26 Tishrei 5692, R. Yehuda Eber wrote to R. Yisrael Jacobson reporting his new appointment by the Rebbe Rayatz as r"m and mashgiach for nigleh studies at the Warsaw yeshiva. He also noted his expectation that the Rebbe himself would settle permanently in Warsaw.
