# Lenin Library workers stage protest against Schneerson book return

*1992*

On 25 Shevat, the author arrived at the library to find a protest led by director Dyragin and his deputies chanting 'Down with Levinson.' Police restricted him to the waiting hall and helped push him out. He returned that evening with a KGB escort and a government delegation, was shown about 700 already-segregated Schneerson books and another 1,500 awaiting verification, but was told nothing would be released until a 'commission' completed its review.
