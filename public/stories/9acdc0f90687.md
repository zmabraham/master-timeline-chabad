# Agreement among Ashkenazi Kollelim in Eretz Yisrael over Siberian shlichim revenue

*1868*

On 19 Tammuz 5628, leaders of the Perushim, Volyn Chassidim, and Chabad kollelim in Jerusalem, Hebron, Tzfat and Tiberias formalized in writing that revenue from Chita, Verkhneudinsk, Petrovsky Zavod, Barguzin and Achinsk would belong solely to Kollel Reisin. Chabad signatories Shimon Menashe Chaikin, Levi Yitzchak Slonim and Shneur Zalman Pundaminsky signed on behalf of Kollel Chabad of Hebron. The text was preserved in the Kollel Chabad letter register and copied again in 5662 when the kollel debated dispatching dedicated shlichim to Siberia.
