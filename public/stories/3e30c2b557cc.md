# Main yeshiva moves from Warsaw to Otwock; Mechina remains in Warsaw

*1935*

The Olam and two upper classes moved to Otwock, leaving roughly 60 Mechina talmidim in Warsaw under R' Schneur Zalman Gourary and R' Moshe Pinchas Katz. The Mechina initially stayed at the Agudas Chabad shul at Zamenhof 8, then later relocated to the Shmotkin family compound at Moranowska 32 when a third class was added.
