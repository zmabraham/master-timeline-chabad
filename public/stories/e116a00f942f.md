# Simchat Torah 5679 farbrengen with the Rebbe Rashab in Rostov

*1918*

The Rebbe spoke about Moshiach coming through wealthy supporters and about the founding of Tomchei Tmimim, declaring 'I am a sichli' and that he would not be moved by externalities. When he discussed connection to the House of David he turned to the Rayatz and said 'Yitzchak, hear this — it concerns you.' Hakafot were not held that night, and the Rebbe Rashab uncharacteristically danced with the assembled chassidim, something he had previously done only with his son.
