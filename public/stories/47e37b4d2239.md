# Rebbe writes letters of thanks for US entry permits

*1940*

Upon arriving in Riga in early 5700, the Frierdiker Rebbe wrote several letters of gratitude for US entry permits obtained for himself, his family, and his secretaries Feigin, Liberman, Rotstein, and Rosh Yeshiva Eber. Ultimately only Liberman, Chadakov, and Mindel would accompany the Rebbe and his family to America.
