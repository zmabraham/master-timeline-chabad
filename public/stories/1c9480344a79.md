# Frierdiker Rebbe writes Pidyon Nefesh for daughter trapped in Paris

*1940*

The prayer asked Heaven to protect Chaya Mushka and Menachem Mendel — the latter not a French citizen and registered for national defense service — and to soften the heart of the American consul to grant their visa. The text was written in the days surrounding the German advance on France, capturing the family's acute fear for the couple stranded in Paris.
