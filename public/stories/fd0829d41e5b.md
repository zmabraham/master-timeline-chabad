# Birth 20th of Cheshvan 5621, Lubavitch; passing 2nd of Nissan 5680, Rostov

*1860*

Birth 20th of Cheshvan 5621, Lubavitch; passing 2nd of Nissan 5680, Rostov; place of activity Lubavitch. Rabbi Shalom DovBer Schneersohn — the Rashab. 20th of Cheshvan 5621 (November 5, 1860) — 2nd of Nissan 5680 (March 21, 1920) is the Chabad rebbe
