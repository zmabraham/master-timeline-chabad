# Speaking at a gathering on behalf of the Society for the True and Complete Redemption

*1955*

Speaking at a gathering on behalf of the Society for the True and Complete Redemption. Rabbi Tuvia Doron (5715 – 5 Elul 5785; 1955 – 29/08/2025) was one of the Rebbe's emissaries in Bat Yam, served as rabbi and mashpia of the Chabad synagogue in the Ramat HaNasi neighborhood of the city, and was an author.
