# R' Avraham of Kalisk appoints R' Mordechai of Lechovitch over Russian EY funds

*1805*

After two years of non-receipt of Russian funds, R' Avraham of Kalisk wrote general letters in winter 5565 complaining that he had received nothing from Russia and announcing the appointment of R' Mordechai of Lechovitch to manage the EY funds collected in Russia. R' Mordechai in turn appointed R' Aharon and R' Yisrael of Ostravna as general gabbaim. The split was triggered by malicious emissaries who had spread false rumors.
