# Alter Rebbe writes to Vilna scholars on polished shechita knives

*1811*

In 5571 the Alter Rebbe addressed a letter to the rabbis of Vilna detailing the reasons Chassidim insisted on polished shechita knives. In a handwritten note in the margin he asked R' Yehuda Leib of Kraz and the other learned Anash of Vilna to study the relevant gemara and poskim carefully so they could respond to objections. The letter shows that an active Chabad scholarly group remained in Vilna into the second decade of the 19th century.
