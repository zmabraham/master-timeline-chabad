# Rosenbaum writes Rosh Chodesh Nissan letter from jail to R' Chaim Gershon Kugel

*1889*

From his cell in Smolensk on 1 Nissan 5649, Reb Avraham Chaim wrote to R' Chaim Gershon Kugel acknowledging the warmth of his letter. He noted that an imprisoned man's spirit is consoled when even distant friends share in his pain. The exchange preserved one of several friendships Rosenbaum maintained from prison.
