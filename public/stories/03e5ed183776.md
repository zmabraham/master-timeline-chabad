# Rebbe Maharash advises chassid Shmuel Dovber of Borisov on Pleshchenitz trade

*1872*

The Reshadam had written to ask whether to travel to Pleshchenitz to buy goods at a favorable price. The Rebbe's reply, sent through Levi Yitzchak Idelevitz on the 7th day of Chanukah 5633 (December 1872), told him to set the merchant's letter aside and make the trip. The exchange shows how the Maharash gave concrete commercial guidance to chassidim engaged in trade.
