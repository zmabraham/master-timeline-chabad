# Rebbe Rayatz spends Tishrei in Otwock near Warsaw

*1931*

Although the move from Riga to Warsaw had been postponed, the Rebbe Rayatz spent the month of Tishrei 5692 in Otwock near Warsaw, where the Tomchei Temimim yeshiva was located. He had earlier hoped to relocate the senior division of the yeshiva from Warsaw to Riga, but when that plan fell through he decided to take up residence near the yeshiva instead.
