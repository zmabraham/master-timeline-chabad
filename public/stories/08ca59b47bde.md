# R' Yechezkel Himelstein perishes in Holocaust

*1933*

R' Yechezkel Himelstein, who had served as general manager and mashgiach over the shiurim and chadarim in Tomchei Tmimim Lubavitch from 5671, continued in this role through Kremenchug and managed branches in Polotsk, Homel and Cherson. In 5693 (1933) he emigrated from Russia to Riga and headed the Tomchei Tmimim branch in Dankra, Latvia, where he perished in the Shoah.
