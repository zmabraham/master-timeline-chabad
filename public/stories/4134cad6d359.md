# R' Shmuel Schneiderman, R' Shlomo Aharon Kazarnovsky, and Chazzan Shmuel Kantroff arrive in USA

*1927*

During the period 5686–5688, a small wave of Tmimim reached the United States. R' Schneiderman settled in Philadelphia and remained there until his passing in 5715. Chazzan Kantroff moved between several towns before settling in Brooklyn, and R' Shlomo Aharon Kazarnovsky took a rabbinic position in Rochester before eventually being recruited as secretary and director of Agudas Chassidei Chabad.
