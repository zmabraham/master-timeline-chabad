# Funeral of R' Moshe Eliezer Kremer; presidency passes to his sons

*1925*

At the cemetery on 6 Iyar 5685, before R' Moshe Eliezer Kremer was lowered into the grave, R' Mendel Leib Lukshin halted the burial to extract a handshake commitment from the sons that they would continue their father's work as leaders of Agudas Chasidei Chabad. As eldest, R' Chaim Zalman Kremer (CHZK) thereby inherited the presidency. The agudah's office remained behind the family's wholesale trouser shop on Broadway.
