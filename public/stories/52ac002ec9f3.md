# Rashab sends Tzemach Tzedek's citizenship certificates to attorney Gruzenberg

*1913*

On 3-4 Marcheshvan 5674 (late October/early November 1913), the Rashab wrote to Oscar Gruzenberg, lead defense counsel in the Mendel Beilis blood libel trial. Having read in the newspapers Gruzenberg's reference to the Tzemach Tzedek, he sent him the Hereditary Honorary Citizen certificate issued by Tsar Nicholas I, along with the earlier Honorary Citizen certificate. The Rashab believed these documents would help demonstrate the integrity of Russian Jewish leadership in the Tsar's eyes.
