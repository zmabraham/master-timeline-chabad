# Seventh Rebbe's 25th wedding anniversary farbrengen

*1953*

He noted that 'generally speaking, even regarding a private person, his wedding constitutes an all-encompassing event. For me, however, my wedding day brought me into more general and communal affairs.' He told those gathered that the wedding 'connected me to you, and you to me,' framing his marriage as a bond with the Chasidim.
