# Publication of Toldot Chabad B'Russia HaTza'arit

*2010*

The fourth volume of the Toldot Chabad series, covering Tsarist Russia in selected chapters across the first five generations of Chabad leadership, was completed in Brooklyn on 12 Tammuz 5770 (June 24, 2010) and published by Otzar HaChassidim at 770 Eastern Parkway. The preface acknowledges R' Yitzchak Wilhelm and R' Dovid Moshe Olidort for their editorial review.
