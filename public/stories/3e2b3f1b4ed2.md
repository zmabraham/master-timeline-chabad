# Rebbe's 'I have done all I can' sicha on bringing Moshiach

*1991*

On the night leading to Thursday, 27 Nissan 5751, the Rebbe delivered a sicha stating that he had done everything in his ability to bring Moshiach and it had not helped, and now one, two, or three people should determine what to do to bring him. The Rebbe pointed to the special days around Tiferes shebeTiferes. The sicha sparked worldwide awakening: at the Shabbos farbrengen and dollar distribution chassidim called for the Rebbe to reveal himself and lead them to Eretz Yisrael, but the Rebbe responded that they should not give him advice, indicating instead that the practical response was to add in Torah and mitzvos with hiddur.
