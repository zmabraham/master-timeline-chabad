# Yakovshvili family escapes Russia as 'Polish' refugees

*1947*

Yakovshvili was part of the audacious escape of about one thousand Chasidim organized by the Chabad underground in late 1946 and early 1947. The Shemtov family escaped on the same route. In 1952 the Jacobsons settled in Toronto, where Simon served briefly as rabbi of the local Chabad congregation before his death in June 1953.
