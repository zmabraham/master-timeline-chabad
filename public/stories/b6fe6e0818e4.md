# Alter Rebbe delivers the 'Frumer Vaera' discourse

*1794*

The discourse known as 'Der Frumer Vaera,' given on Shabbat Parshat Vaera 5554 (January 1794), survived in MaharI"L's transcript with handwritten glosses by the Alter Rebbe himself — one of the very few such doubly-authenticated manuscripts to reach later generations. It became a touchstone for understanding how the Alter Rebbe edited the discourses his brother recorded for him.
