# Rebbe writes Iyar 5688 letters on building the library

*1928*

From Riga the Rebbe wrote to bookseller David Frankel of Husiatyn requesting catalogues from booksellers in Yemen, Australia, Africa, France and Italy, and to R. Chaim Hillel Bloch in the Holy Land instructing him to ask authors and Jerusalem institutions to send their publications, forms, calendars and circulars. The strategy aimed to gather books and ephemera cheaply, paying only postage, in order to rebuild the collection lost in Russia.
