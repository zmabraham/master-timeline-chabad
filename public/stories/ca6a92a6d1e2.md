# Rebbe directs further travels for R. Refael Stein

*1932*

Following up on the Poretz mikveh project, the Rebbe wrote on 22 Menachem Av 5692 (Igros Kodesh vol. 11, letter 3883) that he expected the mikveh to be made fit soon. He decided that Stein should now travel further, with instructions to be communicated by R. Moshe Leib Rothstein. The Rebbe also addressed a letter to all anash in the Vilna region (letter 3884) introducing Stein as his emissary.
