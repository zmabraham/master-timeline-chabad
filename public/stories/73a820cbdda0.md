# Summer 1772: 'Zemir Aritzim v'Charvot Tzurim' published in Brody

*1772*

Following the Shklov debate, the sages of Shklov wrote to the Vilna Gaon, who in turn coordinated with Brody. The resulting pamphlet attacked chassidic practices — including the upside-down acrobatics ('koliyen zich') of R. Avraham of Kalisk's circle — and triggered widespread persecution of the tzaddikim of Volyn.
