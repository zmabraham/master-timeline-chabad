# RMM of Vitebsk endorses three tzaddikim's leadership of Russian chassidim

*1782*

On 4 Adar 5542 (Feb 1782), R. Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk wrote that Russian chassidim should not travel long distances to other leaders in Volhynia but should consult the three great tzaddikim already among them. He named R. Yisrael of Polotsk, R. Yissachar Ber of Lubavitch and the Alter Rebbe (R. Shneur Zalman), affirming their authority over the Eretz Yisrael fund.
