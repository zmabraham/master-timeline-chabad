# Assembly of Polish rabbis convenes

*1908*

Russian-ruled Poland organized its own rabbinical assembly on Rosh Chodesh Kislev to respond to the preparatory questions for the Petersburg Rabbinical Conference. Two Chabad representatives, R. Menachem Mendel Chein of Nezhin and R. Shmaryahu Leib Medalia of Vitebsk, attended at the invitation of the Gerrer Rebbe. They reported the assembly's discussions and decisions back to the Rebbe Rashab, who immediately relayed the details to his son, the Rayatz, who was overseeing preparations for the Vilna assembly.
