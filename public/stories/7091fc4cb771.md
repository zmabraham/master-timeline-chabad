# Move of Shchedrin students delayed; learning in Great Hall

*1910*

Although the students were supposed to leave for Shchedrin after Pesach, the host town was not ready to receive them. They remained in Lubavitch, studying in the Great Hall under supervision of R. Yosef Friedman of Rogachov for nigleh and R. Moshe of Zembin for chassidus.
