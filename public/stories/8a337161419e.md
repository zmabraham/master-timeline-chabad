# Yonah Wolf Mendelsohn sent from Hebron to America as shadar

*1878*

HaChavatzelet of 11 Cheshvan 5639 reported the shedarut as undertaken 'by the community of Israel residents of Hebron from the Sephardic and Ashkenazi congregations.' No indication ties it to the broader Kollel Chabad system. The article noted that Hebron's communities had never authorized the Jerusalem rabbinate to send emissaries on their behalf.
