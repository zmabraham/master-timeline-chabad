# Frierdiker Rebbe plans to relocate from Riga to Warsaw, 12 Tammuz 5693

*1933*

R. Shmuel Levitin read aloud a letter from R. Yehuda Eber reporting that the Frierdiker Rebbe planned to relocate his residence from Riga to Warsaw and required $1,500 for the move. The Rebbe requested that the relocation expenses be covered by the Moscow-area Anash, which they apparently quietly fulfilled.
