# First complete Rif edition with commentaries printed in Constantinople

*1509*

In 5269, the first complete printed edition of Hilchot Rav Alfas (Rif) — Rabbi Yitzchak Alfasi's foundational digest of Talmudic law — was issued in Constantinople, accompanied by its commentaries and the Mordechai appended at the end. Several portions of this edition are preserved in the Chabad Library archive.
