# Rebbe Rashab convenes Moscow rabbinic assembly

*1917*

Following the collapse of the Tsarist regime and establishment of the Provisional Government in the winter of 5677, elections were planned for Jewish community councils and a general Jewish Congress to organize Jewish communal life. The Rebbe Rashab fought to organize a religious front against anti-religious factions. He pushed for an assembly of Torah giants in Russia, which Rabbi Chaim Ozer wanted to instead achieve through founding Agudas Yisrael branches. The assembly was ultimately convened in early Elul in Moscow, where the proposal to found a permanent rabbinical association was rejected by the participants.
