# Gershon Fradkin released from solitary confinement in Kursk

*1940*

Gershon had been the last bochur to arrive in Kursk before the mass arrest and had 'not yet had time to sin much,' so the NKVD released him alone. He spent all of Tishrei — Rosh Hashanah, Yom Kippur and Simchas Torah — in the 'Kamera Adinochka' solitary cell, and told the author he had even worked out without pencil or paper on what date Yom Kippur would fall the following year. The imprisonment damaged his lungs and he travelled back to Shumyachi to recuperate.
