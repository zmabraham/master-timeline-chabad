# Ignatiev's 'Temporary Regulations' tighten the Pale of Settlement

*1882*

The regulations prohibited Jews from living outside cities and towns within the fifteen Pale provinces and outlawed Jewish-held sales, mortgages and leases on rural real estate beyond the Pale. Although labeled 'temporary,' they remained in force for 33 years until the middle of World War I, devastating Jewish livelihood and packing Jews into overcrowded urban districts.
