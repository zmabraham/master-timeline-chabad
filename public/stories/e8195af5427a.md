# Rebbe sends R' Meir Glazman to survey yeshivas in Lvov and surroundings

*1931*

Responding to Glazman's first report from Lvov, the Rebbe expressed pleasure that he had grasped the broader purpose of the visit. The Rebbe asked him to identify every yeshiva, study circle, and shtiebel where Torah was learned in the city, to record names of contacts for future correspondence, and to extend his survey to the surrounding towns of the Lemberg region for another week.
