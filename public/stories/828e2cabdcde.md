# Rabbi Yehuda Eber leaves Russia for Riga

*1930*

After serving as a leading rosh yeshiva in the underground Tomchei Tmimim network in Poltava, Rostov, Nevel, and Vitebsk, Rabbi Yehuda Eber received permission to leave Soviet Russia in the winter of 5690. He arrived in Riga intending to continue on to the United States, but was unable to obtain entry papers despite efforts by Rabbi Yisrael Jacobson of Agudas Chabad. He remained stranded with his family in Riga while pursuing the American visa.
