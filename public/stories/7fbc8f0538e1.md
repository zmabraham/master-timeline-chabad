# Secret covenant of nine Chasidim in Moscow

*1924*

Building on a 19 Kislev 5684 address in Rostov calling for emissaries to every city, the Rebbe formalized a clandestine covenant the next winter. The nine became generals of an underground network of schools, yeshivot, synagogues, and mikvaot that lasted until the Soviet collapse. Among the known members were Elchonon Morozov, Bentzion Shemtov, Simon Yakovshvili, and Yerachmiel Binyaminson.
