# Maciów yeshiva proposal revived by R' Yitzchak Aryeh Mandelbaum

*1933*

Mandelbaum warned that the largest cheder class in Maciów and surrounding towns was only beginning Gemara, and that students leaving without further Torah education risked drifting from religious life entirely. He committed to personally establishing a small yeshiva on his return home, teaching the local boys Gemara and Chassidus daily. The plan, however, did not materialize.
