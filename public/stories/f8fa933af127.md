# Mitteler Rebbe's arrest file located in Minsk

*1994*

On 2 Nissan 5754 (14 March 1994), R. Boruch Schiff received notification from Minsk that the long-sought file had been located and photographed — some 120 pages of documents from the Khovansky chancellery covering the entire 1825-1828 affair. The find ended a search that had begun in 1976 with the Rebbe's instructions to Hendel Deutsch and intensified in winter 1988. The translated dossier became the basis for the published volume of the case.
