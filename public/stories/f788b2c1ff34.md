# Rogatchover Gaon passes away in Dvinsk

*1936*

The death of one of the great halachic luminaries of the era prompted commemorations across the Jewish world. R. Rafael Cohen, rabbi of the Riga chassidic community, delivered a eulogy at Erev Rosh Chodesh Nissan in the beis medrash named for the Rogatchover, later published as the booklet 'Emunah V'Zechirah'.
