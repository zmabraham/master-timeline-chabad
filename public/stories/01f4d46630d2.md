# Rebbe Rayatz writes to R' Dov Nisan Bezpalov about yeshiva property

*1928*

On 3 Tevet 5689 (December 1928) the Rebbe Rayatz wrote a confidential letter to R' Dov Nisan Bezpalov, asking him to identify properties around Warsaw suitable for purchase on long-term bank financing, with a preference for towns near Warsaw such as Otwock. He stressed that the matter be kept entirely private and not discussed even with close associates. The letter is found in Igrot Kodesh vol. 16, letter 6016.
