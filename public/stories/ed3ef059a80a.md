# Rebbe Rashab encourages New York anash via letter through Rosenbaum

*1902*

Receiving Rosenbaum's report on the New York group together with 100 rubles, on 24 Adar I 5662 the Rebbe Rashab wrote a long letter (Igrot Kodesh vol. 1 #114). He praised the men of the Rebbe Maharash's circle for retaining their spiritual life in a distant land, urged them to meet on Thursday nights, Shabbat and motzaei Shabbat to learn Chassidut together, and to correct shortcomings in their tefillah and observance.
