# Three Lubavitch rabbis arrive in New York

*1940*

On Friday Parshat Noach, 1 Rosh Chodesh Cheshvan 5701, R. Moshe Leib Rotstein, R. Shmuel Zalmanov, and R. Yitzchak Dov Ushpal arrived in New York with their families. The Lubavitch periodical HaKeriah VeHakedusha (issue 2) reported on their rescue from the 'European inferno' through the Pidyon Shvuyim Fund established by the Lubavitcher Rebbe.
