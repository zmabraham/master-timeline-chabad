# Sunday school for Jewish children opens at Moscow Agudat Chabad synagogue

*1991*

Rabbi D.B. Lazar opened the day with a brief address, after which the children prayed together with explanations given in Russian. Rabbi Kogan also addressed the children before they were divided into classes and learned for several hours in the synagogue hall, while the yeshiva students moved to the dining room and some helped teach. The opening took place while the formal struggle to recover the Schneersohn library was at its peak.
