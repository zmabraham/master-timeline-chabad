# Rebbe Rayatz travels to Berlin to meet Joint executives

*1928*

The Joint had funded the Rebbe's Soviet activities since 1922, but signaled they would not continue funding Torah work in Europe for 5689. In Berlin the Rebbe met with R. Aharon Teitelbaum, head of Central Relief, along with Hildesheimer, Mr. Cohen, Mr. Rosen, and Hildesheimer to argue for continued support specifically for Russia.
