# Mogilev provincial government transfers the rebbe's case to Babinavichi court

*1825*

On 16 December 1825, the Mogilev provincial government (signed by Civil Governor Maksimov and Counselors Butovich, Kishkovich, and Chitozhribsky) reported to Khovansky on action taken in response to his 17 November order (no. 5849). The report summarized findings against Rabbi Dovber Schneuri — including the 35,000-ruble post-1812 collection, ongoing annual collections of 10,000-14,000 rubles, and his serving as arbitrator between Jews and Christians — all allegedly contrary to §§51-52 of the 1804 Jewish Statute. Per §110 of provincial law, the file (no. 872) was forwarded to the magistrate's court in Babinavichi district, which contained Lubavitch, for prosecution.
