# Author and Mikhal Rapoport arrested at Gori, Stalin's birthplace

*1931*

Although they had safely passed major cities like Rostov, Baku, and Tbilisi, the GPU seized them in the small town of Gori because the local secret police were unusually aggressive in Stalin's hometown. They were locked up with a violent Georgian prisoner and demanded their tefillin from the guards in the morning. They were eventually released in Tbilisi after intervention.
