# US recognizes Soviet Union; Rebbe's religious-freedom campaign fails

*1933*

Russian Foreign Minister Maxim Litvinov traveled to Washington to negotiate diplomatic recognition. The Rebbe drafted seven demands and mobilized leaders including Chaim Ozer Grodzinski, Nahum Sokolow, Rabbi Eliezer Silver, and US Senators Borah and King. The US declined to make recognition contingent on Jewish religious freedom, securing only a concession for visiting American citizens.
