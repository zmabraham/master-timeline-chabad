# Petersburg Rabbinical Conference elections in Russian provinces

*1909*

After the holidays in Tishrei 5670, the government ordered elections for delegates to the Petersburg Rabbinical Conference, with two representatives to be chosen per province through a multi-stage process. The Rebbe Rashab immediately issued a kol koreh, dispatched R. Moshe Rosenblum to coordinate with the gedolim, and obtained additional kol koreh letters from R. David Friedman of Karlin and R. Chaim Ozer Grodzinski. Thousands of copies were distributed to synagogues. Dr. Krebs at the Ministry of Interior actively lobbied against electing Orthodox rabbis, citing precedents where ministers had selected only one or two religious rabbis to past commissions.
