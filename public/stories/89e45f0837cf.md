# Passing of the Alter Rebbe (24 Tevet)

*1813*

After abandoning his home in Liadi during Napoleon's 1812 invasion, the Alter Rebbe died in flight, leaving his household possessions consumed by fire. His son and successor, the Mitteler Rebbe, remained in Kremenchug for a period before being persuaded to return to White Russia. The destitute state of the Rebbe's family after the war later became central to the Mitteler Rebbe's collection of Ma'amad funds.
