# Second informer letter submitted against the Mitteler Rebbe

*1825*

In late Sivan to Tammuz 5585 (June 1825), Simcha Kisin filed his second informer letter portraying the chassidic movement as greedy exploiters of naive young Jews who seized control of communal funds, charity boxes, shechita, and printing houses. He attached a 1814 distribution letter regarding the ma'amad bais harav as supposed evidence that the Mitteler Rebbe was amassing vast personal wealth. The denunciation demanded the persecution and destruction of the chassidic movement.
