# Delegation prepares memorandum sets for Soviet officials

*1991*

On 20 Shevat 5751, Rabbi Aharonov and the diarist traveled to the Armand Hammer office in Moscow to prepare twelve sets of all the main documents in their possession, totaling some fifty pages. They faxed a copy to Yona Pruss in London to arrange Russian-to-English translation and recruit someone to compose a general historical and documentary overview, work that continued through the night.
