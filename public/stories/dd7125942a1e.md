# Vaad Chevroni established in Lubavitch

*1905*

In Tammuz 5665 (summer 1905), the Vaad Chevroni was finally established in Lubavitch under R' Eleazar Moshe Madiyevsky, with the Rashab's encouragement. R' Sh.Y.L. Eliezerov had spent Shavuot in Lubavitch and remained to help organize the committee, whose purpose was to attend continuously to improving Hebron's material and spiritual conditions.
