# R' Natan Amram departs Hebron as shadar

*1835*

On the eve of Shavuot 1835 (ערב חג השבועות תקצ"ה), R' Natan Amram departed Hebron as a joint Chabad-Sephardic shadar. Although a request had been made to also include Germany, Holland, France, and England, those territories remained off-limits per the Pekidim u'Amarkalim agreement.
