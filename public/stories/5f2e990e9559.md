# Birth of Rabbi Levi Yitzchak Schneerson

*1878*

Rabbi Levi Yitzchak Schneerson, son of Baruch Schneur Schneersohn and father of the Rebbe Menachem Mendel (the seventh Chabad Rebbe), was born in 5638 (1877/78). He descended from Rabbi Baruch Shalom, eldest son of the Tzemach Tzedek, through Baruch Schneur — a lineage Maharash's family tradition noted explicitly.
