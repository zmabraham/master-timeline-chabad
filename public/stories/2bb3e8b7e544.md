# World War I devastates Hebron's Jewish community

*1914*

When World War I broke out in 5674/1914, Hebron's Jewish community was severely shaken. Fears of Turkish military conscription, the cessation of chalukah funds reaching the yeshivot, and a collapse of incoming support from emissaries (shadarim) forced many Jews to flee. Hunger and disease drove residents to seek refuge wherever they could find it.
