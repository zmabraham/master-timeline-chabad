# Fire at Beshen destroys over forty manuscripts of the Alter Rebbe

*1824*

According to the Rebbe Rayatz citing his grandfather the Rebbe Maharash, the Alter Rebbe would entrust his manuscripts to the chassid R' Yisrael Yaffe of Kapust, who circulated them among chassidim for study and copying. A vast cache held at R' Beshen's home was lost in the 1824 fire. Only two volumes from 5566-5567 that were on loan to the Mittler Rebbe survived intact.
