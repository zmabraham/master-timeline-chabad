# Chabad center moves from Liozna to Liadi

*1801*

After about eighteen years in Liozna, the Alter Rebbe relocated to Liadi in 1801. Liadi would remain the Chabad center until his passing in 1812. The towns of Liozna, Liadi, and Lubavitch all sat near one another on the Russian-Belarusian frontier.
