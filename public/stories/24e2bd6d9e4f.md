# District court inspector reports delays in Mitteler Rebbe investigation

*1826*

Inspector Kilikovsky of the Babinovich district court informed Governor-General Khovansky that the case against Rabbi Berke Schneur of Lubavitch was being held up because the courts in Orsha and Mohilev had not delivered the inquiry results requested on February 12. The Mohilev provincial administration had issued an urgent reminder on March 3, and Kilikovsky himself had pressed the inspectors of those districts on February 24 and again on March 17.
