# Rebbe appears publicly in wheelchair after Shavuot tahalucha

*1992*

On the evening following the Shavuot tahalucha of 5752 the Rebbe was brought to the 770 entrance in his wheelchair to see the assembled crowd. He stayed outside for roughly four minutes; when asked if he wished to go back inside he nodded yes. The author, standing in the corridor as the Rebbe emerged from his room, noted that the Rebbe's face appeared almost as usual but that he looked only straight ahead at the crowd. Some reported the Rebbe slightly inclined his head toward Rabbi Yoel Kahan.
