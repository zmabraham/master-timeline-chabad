# Frierdiker Rebbe writes celebratory letter to Warsaw yeshiva

*1921*

Word of the Warsaw founding had reached the Rebbe only at the beginning of Tammuz, smuggled across the closed Soviet border by special messenger. He spent days speaking of his joy and called the students his sons, praying that the yeshiva would grow and spread its light. The next day he wrote a follow-up letter to R. Shraga Feivish Zalmanov.
