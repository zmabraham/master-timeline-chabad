# Rebbe Rayatz replies to Yisrael Yehuda Levin's photograph

*1935*

On 16 Sivan 5695 (around June 18, 1935), the Rebbe Rayatz replied to the photograph and letter the author had sent the previous month. The Rebbe wrote that he had enjoyed seeing the photograph, asked the author to convey greetings to his friends and relatives, and blessed him with Hashem's help. In the darkness of those Soviet years, the letter and blessing gave the author considerable joy and encouragement.
