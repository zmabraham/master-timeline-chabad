# Vilna chassidim describe 19 Kislev farbrengens to Frierdiker Rebbe

*1936*

The letter was signed by Yitzchak Weiman, Anshil Aronovich, Menachem Mendel Levin and Chaim Yehoshua Baruch Kapelovich. It documents the active chassidic life in multiple Vilna minyanim on the eve of the Holocaust, including Apatov, Kirzner, Shnipishok and Novogrod.
