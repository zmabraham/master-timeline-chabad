# World Agudath Israel founded in Katowice, Poland

*1912*

The Agudah unified many chasidic groups with the Lithuanian yeshivah community, though Chabad did not join. In Latvia, Agudath Israel became a major influential party, winning two Saeima seats in 1922 and 1925, falling to one in 1928, and regaining two in 1931. After the 1934 Latvian coup, multi-party elections did not resume until 1990.
