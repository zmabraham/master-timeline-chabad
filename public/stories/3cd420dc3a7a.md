# Maharashab and Maharaitz travel from Zaalshe to Lubavitch

*1914*

On 24 Menachem Av 5674, Maharaitz set out from Zaalshe for Lubavitch to attend to yeshiva matters; Maharashab unexpectedly decided to accompany him. They arrived at 9:30 at night. Early the next morning Maharashab visited the great hall where the temimim slept and was deeply moved by their condition. He prayed for hours, visited the ohel of his ancestors, and returned to Zaalshe that afternoon, exhausted from the visit.
