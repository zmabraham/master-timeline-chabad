# Avraham Meir Pinchas Rodes founds evening kollel for working avreichim

*1988*

Starting in 5748, Avraham Meir Pinchas Rodes established an evening kollel for avreichim whose Torah study is not their primary occupation. The participants gather at Rabbi Frenkel's shul from Sunday through Thursday evenings until late. From an initial group of ten regular avreichim, additional neighborhood participants joined, growing the program into a 'torch of light.'
