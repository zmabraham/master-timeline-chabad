# Yeshivat Torat Emet founded in Hebron

*1912*

Following years of efforts to build a Chabad yeshiva presence in Eretz Israel—including the affiliation of Magen Avot with Tomchei Tmimim—Yeshivat Torat Emet was founded in Hebron at the beginning of 5672. The yeshiva would become a major center for Chabad learning in the Land of Israel and was supervised by Rebbe Rashab from Lubavitch.
