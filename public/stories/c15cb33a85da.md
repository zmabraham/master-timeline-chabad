# Vaad formed to bring the Frierdiker Rebbe to settle in America

*1930*

An awakening grew among anash in summer 5690 that the Rebbe should immigrate to the United States. A vaad was chosen with Max Halbreich, brother and son-in-law of Chaim Halbreich, as chairman and R. Nissan Telushkin among its energetic activists, and funds were raised. The Rebbe would later write on 18 Cheshvan 5691 that he did not intend to settle in America.
