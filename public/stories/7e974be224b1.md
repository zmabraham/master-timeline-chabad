# Chiddushei HaRim of Gur certifies a shochet's sharpened knife

*1862*

Rabbi Yitzchak Meir Alter, the first Gerrer Rebbe and author of Chiddushei HaRim, wrote to his chossid Rabbi Meir Shachna Reich on Sunday, 7 Adar Sheni 5622. He confirmed having tested the shochet's blade himself—finding the man sensitive to even the finest pgima and capable of restoring the knife to proper condition.
