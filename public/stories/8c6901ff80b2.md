# Night train from Kiev to Dnepropetrovsk to continue library search

*1990*

On Motzaei Shabbos Beshalach, 11 Shevat 5750 (February 10, 1990), having exhausted leads in Kiev, Rabbi Shlomo Cunin's delegation boarded an overnight sleeper train to Dnepropetrovsk. Their Soviet escort Kasianenko had secured the tickets after multiple ticket offices initially refused. They arrived the next morning at 9:45 AM and immediately surveyed three Dnepropetrovsk libraries — the university library, the public library, and the historical museum — though most were closed for the day.
