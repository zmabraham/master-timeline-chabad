# Kaloszyn yeshiva dispersed at outbreak of WWII

*1939*

During the first week of the war the Kaloszyn yeshiva kept its learning schedule, but as the Wehrmacht's rapid advance became clear, Leib Kremer and the hanhalah declared an emergency. Unable to take responsibility for boys from other towns, they dispersed the yeshiva and sent students such as Shimon Goldman back to families like his in Siedlce.
