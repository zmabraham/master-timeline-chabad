# Frierdiker Rebbe writes about Tomchei Temimim students in Poland

*1941*

In Iggeret 1640 the Rebbe described how his students who remained in Poland were giving themselves over literally to Torah study and teaching others. He noted that they were keeping the Torah not merely amid poverty but amid hunger and cold, and that in one place a single student had gathered roughly 150 talmidim — a clear reference to Hendel's yeshiva in Radom.
