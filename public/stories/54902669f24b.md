# R. Shimon Levi Belastotzky reports from Disna

*1934*

Writing to the Rebbe on 13 Nissan 5694, R. Shimon Levi b'R. Shneur Zalman Belastotzky of Disna reported that the chavura studying chassidus was growing and gaining strength. However, his repeated attempts to establish a 'Tiferes Bachurim' group had not yet succeeded in taking firm hold.
