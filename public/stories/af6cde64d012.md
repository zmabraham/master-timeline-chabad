# Mother of Shmuel Kadish Bartnovsky dies before Pesach

*1917*

While the author was studying in the Kherson yeshiva, his close friend Shmuel Kadish Bartnovsky lost his mother in the weeks leading up to Pesach 5677. The author traveled with him to Nikolaev to accompany him in his grief, then returned to Kherson and made his way home for Pesach. He returned to Lubavitch after the chag with his younger brother Shlomo Tzvi, who was to study there.
