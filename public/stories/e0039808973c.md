# Chabad Persian-translation Haggadah by Yehoshua Netaneli

*1986*

In 5746 (1986), Yehoshua Netaneli's Persian-language translation of the Chabad Haggadah was published in New York. The 68-page octavo edition served the Persian-speaking Jewish community of Iranian background. It supported the Chabad project of reaching Persian Jews displaced by the 1979 Iranian Revolution.
