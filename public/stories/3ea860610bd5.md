# Frierdiker Rebbe arrives in Riga, Latvia

*1927*

On the Monday following the $4,000 wire transfer, news came that the Rebbe and his household had reached Riga. He was accompanied by his secretary and gabbai R' Yechezkel Feigin, who from Riga maintained an active correspondence with the author about day-to-day matters of the chatzer. Riga would serve as the Rebbe's primary residence for the next several years.
