# Alter Rebbe's seized writings handed to Senate's third department

*1800*

On 18 Tevet 5561 (December 22, 1800 Old Style), General Procurator Obolininov transferred the confiscated books and manuscripts of the Alter Rebbe to Olenin, prosecutor of the Senate's third department. The materials were accompanied by a Russian-language report prepared by the Jewish translators Leib ben Noach and Yehuda ben Feivish, who certified that they had found nothing in the writings opposed to Jewish law, the monarchy, or the welfare of the country.
