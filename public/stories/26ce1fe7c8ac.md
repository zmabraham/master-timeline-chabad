# Kfar Chabad delegation presents Rebbe with the village's etrog

*1962*

Members of Anash from Kfar Chabad presented the Rebbe with the etrog grown in the village. The Rebbe blessed them that just as the etrog has both taste and fragrance — symbolizing all qualities — so should all Israel, Anash, and Kfar Chabad in particular be granted matters of taam and reach throughout the entire year.
