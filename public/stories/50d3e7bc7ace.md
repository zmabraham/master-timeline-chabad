# Author's wedding on 8th day of Chanukah 5680 in Belitsa

*1919*

The original tenaim date had been advanced once his future brother-in-law saw the father-in-law's failing health and the spreading famine. The author's mother brought three or four pounds of meat, an uncle obtained a small amount of wheat flour for challos, and a brother-in-law procured a pound of sugar. About ten chassidim joined for sheva brachos on Shabbat, where the author began to chazer a maamer but stopped midway, overwhelmed by the surrounding conditions.
