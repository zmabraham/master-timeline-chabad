# Library worker reveals discarded Shut Maharik in trash

*1991*

About fifty Chabadniks visited the library for dancing and singing while a TV crew interviewed Rabbi Kogan and library director Valik. Kogan displayed a copy of Shut Maharik that had been found discarded; Valik bristled but Kogan refused to say who provided it. The librarian who had found the volume three years earlier in the Khimki storage rooms then gave testimony — filmed so his face and voice could not be identified — and the segment aired before Yom Tov.
