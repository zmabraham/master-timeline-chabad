# WWII ends in summer 5705

*1945*

In the summer of 5705 (1945), World War II ended with the victory of the United States and its allies over Germany and Japan. In Shanghai, the Japanese forces who had controlled the city for several years and confined Jews to a ghetto-like area were replaced by American troops. Among the American soldiers were many Yiddish-speaking Jews from New York's East Side and Manhattan, whose presence cheered the Chabad yeshiva students stranded there.
