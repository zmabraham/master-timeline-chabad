# Founding of Kupat Bachurim student welfare fund in Warsaw

*1932*

On 15 Av 5692, a dedicated fund called Kupat Bachurim was founded to support the bachurim of Tomchei Temimim Warsaw. Its honorary chairman was R. Menachem Mendel Schneerson (the Rebbe's son-in-law), with R. Yechezkel Feigin as chairman.
