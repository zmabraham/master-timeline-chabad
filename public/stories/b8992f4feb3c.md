# R' Shneur Zalman of Liadi reconciles with R' Aharon

*1783*

In an undated letter (likely from 1783), R' Shneur Zalman of Liadi described investigating a dispute and finding that his beloved companions, including R' Tzvi b"R Mendel Segal and R' Aharon the Great, remained loyal to him. After two weeks together, the misunderstanding was resolved and their mutual love was renewed.
