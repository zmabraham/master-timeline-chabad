# First Haggadah printed with literal German translation in Cologne

*1838*

In 5598 (1838), a Haggadah with a German translation rendered word-for-word was printed in Cologne. The 91-page volume marked a milestone as the first appearance of the Haggadah with this style of vernacular German translation. Musical notation for the Seder songs was appended at the end of the work.
