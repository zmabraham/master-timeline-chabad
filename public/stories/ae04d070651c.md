# Rebbe Rayatz falls ill with heart disease

*1944*

Already at the end of Tishrei 5705 (October 1944), Rebbe Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn began to feel fatigue, agitation and pains around the heart. Dr. Wilder visited him almost daily, several times together with the cardiologist Dr. Dressler. They prescribed medication and ordered him to rest, though the Rebbe continued his writing, correspondence, and reception of visitors as much as possible.
