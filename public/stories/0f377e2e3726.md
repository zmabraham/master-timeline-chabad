# Initial editing of Shulchan Aruch Orach Chaim concludes

*1773*

According to his sons' introduction, the foundational editing of Orach Chaim took roughly two years. Beginning in summer 5531 with Tzitzis and Pesach, the Alter Rebbe continued through Vilna, Shklov, Rovno and Anipoli, completing the section in proximity to the Maggid's histalkus on 19 Kislev 5533. He continued to revise and add to the work in the following years until about 5537.
