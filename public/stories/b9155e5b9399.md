# Zerach Gordon and Hecht couple arrive in Stockholm en route to New York

*1939*

After a difficult ship journey, the small group reached Stockholm on the second day of Chol HaMoed Sukkos. From there they planned to continue to New York, separate from the remaining seven talmidim who left Riga later. In Stockholm they were assisted by Rabbi Yisrael Zuber, who arranged food, lodging, and a place to learn in the synagogue.
