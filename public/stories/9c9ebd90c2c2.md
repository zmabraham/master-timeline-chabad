# Passing of the Maggid of Mezritch

*1772*

R. Dov Ber, successor to the Baal Shem Tov and teacher of the founders of the major chassidic dynasties including the Alter Rebbe, passed away on 19 Kislev 5533. His passing marked the close of the period during which the Alter Rebbe consulted him on the editing of the Shulchan Aruch. Decades later 19 Kislev would also become the date of the Alter Rebbe's own redemption from imprisonment.
