# Eli Segal purchases first portion of the Lubavitch courtyard

*1996*

In 5756 (1996), the philanthropist Mr. Eli Segal joined the long-running effort to build a museum on the site of the courtyard of the Rebbes in Lubavitch, purchasing the first portion of the courtyard. The initiative built on years of negotiation by Rabbi Natan Gurary with the Russian government following the opening of the Iron Curtain, in response to repeated encouragement from the Rebbe.
