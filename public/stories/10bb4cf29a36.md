# Lubavitch Museum opens in original Chabad courtyard

*2001*

The opening commemorated 80 years since the 5681 fire that destroyed the courtyard and 81 years since the passing of the Rashab in Rostov. The museum building had been constructed at the center of the courtyard reassembled through purchases in 5756, 5758, and 5760. It is administered by Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Aharonov, Rabbi Shalom Dovber Levin, and Rabbi Nosson Gourary.
