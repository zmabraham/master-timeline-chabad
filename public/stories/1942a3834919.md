# Shluchim move to new Moscow apartment near synagogue

*1992*

After staying at a hotel, the family moved Thursday afternoon into a new apartment close to the synagogue, rented for two months. The first Shabbat there featured the whole family eating together and hosting guests: the chassan and kallah Chaim and Talia Tolochinsky at night and school children for shul on Shabbat morning. The author expressed gratitude for finding the apartment so easily.
