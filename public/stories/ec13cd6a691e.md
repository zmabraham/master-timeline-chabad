# Rebbe Rashab confirms broader rabbinic consensus around Kollel Chabad reform

*1907*

Writing to the future Rebbe Rayyatz, the Rashab reported that R' Yeshaya Berlin had received word from the Rebbe of Lyadi confirming consensus around Frumkin, with the rebbes of Bobruisk and Retzitza also agreeing. He hoped this would finally settle the matter so that the land might be quiet, though as he wrote a week later he could only hope it was truly so.
