# Rebbe Rayatz relocates from Warsaw to Otwock

*1935*

Otwock's pine-scented air and quieter atmosphere benefited the ailing Rebbe, and the town boasted many tzaddikim and a chasidic ambiance. The Tomchei Temimim yeshivah soon followed the Rebbe there, and Otwock became one of Europe's great centers of Jewish learning until the Nazi invasion in 1939.
