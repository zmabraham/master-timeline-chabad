# Passing of Rabbi Meir, son of Rabbi Levi Yitzchak of Berditchev

*1805*

Rabbi Meir, son of Rabbi Levi Yitzchak of Berditchev, passed away in Kislev 5566. The Alter Rebbe wrote a letter of consolation to his in-law that was later included as Iggeret HaKodesh chapter 28 in Tanya. His death is thought to have delayed the major Berditchev–Liadi marriage of his son, eventually held in summer 1807 after the year of mourning.
