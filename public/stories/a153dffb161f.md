# Talmid Shmuel Avraham Chanovitz dies in exile in Shanghai

*1943*

Of the 38 students who reached Japan and then Shanghai, talmid Shmuel Avraham Chanovitz did not survive the exile. He passed away in Shanghai on 9 Adar Sheini 5703 (March 16, 1943). A memorial matzeivah was erected in his honor; the remaining 27 students reached the United States only in the summer of 1946.
