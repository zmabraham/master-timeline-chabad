# Bet Chorin Haggadah with commentaries printed in Metz

*1767*

In 5527 (1767), the 'Bet Chorin' Haggadah was issued in Metz, France, with commentaries by Moshe ben Chaim Alshich, the Maharal's 'Gevurot Hashem', and 'Olelot Efraim'. The 30-leaf quarto edition included beautiful illustrations and concluded with a map of the Land of Israel in vernacular language. It exemplified the French-printed Haggadah tradition of the late 18th century.
