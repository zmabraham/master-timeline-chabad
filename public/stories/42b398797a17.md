# Rebbe approves vocational training for Vilna students

*1940*

Writing to R. Baruch, the Rebbe endorsed Yosef Weinberg and Gershon Chanovitz's shechita studies and Menachem Zev Greenglass and Chaim Leib's stam studies. He suggested that if other students were similarly inspired, an 'artel' of craftsmen should be organized among the students, given the conditions of the time.
