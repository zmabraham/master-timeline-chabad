# Rebbe Rayatz received Maharash's kippah from R' Shneur Tzvi Schneersohn

*1928*

A handwritten note by Rebbe Rayatz, later discovered in a Russian archive, recorded that the mitznefet (kippah) of his grandfather the Maharash was given to him as a gift by his relative R' Shneur Tzvi Schneersohn, grandson of R' Chaim Avraham, on 3 Elul 5688. The kippah, woven with silver threads and bearing a small tail, eventually entered the Chabad library's artifact archive after the 'didan notzach' books trial of 5746–47.
