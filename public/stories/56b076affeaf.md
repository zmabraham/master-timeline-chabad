# Volomin branch founded

*1912*

In 5672 (1912), R' Boruch Sholom HaKohen moved to Volomin near Warsaw, where he committed to fund half the expenses of the local yeshiva if a manager from Lubavitch was sent. The Rebbe Rashab sent R' Mordechai Perlov to lead the yeshiva. Students began preparing to travel to Lubavitch, but the outbreak of World War I in summer 5674 ended the project.
