# Rebbe Rayyatz writes to R' David Patash about the Jaffa cheder

*1923*

Having received a letter from Chabad chassidim in Jaffa describing the spiritual ruin of their community, the Rebbe explained that Torat Emet of Jerusalem lacked resources to open a branch. Since Patash, recently arrived from Russia, was known for his devotion to Torah causes, the Rebbe asked him to take up the project. The letter set the stage for Patash to become a central supporter of the new institution.
