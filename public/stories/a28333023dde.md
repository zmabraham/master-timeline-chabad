# Meeting at Stas's mother's home with Time photographer

*1991*

On the night of 4 Adar 5751 the delegation met at the home of Stas's mother together with a reporter and the Time magazine photographer they had met previously at Stas's office. They walked the journalists through every step of the campaign and showed them the support letters; Rabbi Cunin compared Stas's mother to Queen Esther bringing about a Purim-style miracle through her closeness to power, and she committed herself to the cause.
