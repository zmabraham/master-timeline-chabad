# HaOlam reports on the Rebbe's library in Riga

*1928*

On 22 Elul 5688, HaOlam printed an account by a Yiddishe Shtime correspondent who had visited the library in Riga. The article enumerated such treasures as a 440-year-old manuscript of R. Avraham Saba, a 1475 Venice siddur, 950 Haggadah editions in many languages, 900 selichos volumes, and manuscripts of the Baal Shem Tov and Maggid of Mezeritch, painting a vivid picture of the collection's scope just months after its arrival.
