# Pavel Papov reports on the Schneersohn book collection in Moscow

*1920*

Papov reports that the Schneersohn crates are intact except for one damaged by customs officials, that each crate weighs roughly 15 pud, and that at least 12 wagons would be needed to move the collection. The books are on the third floor accessible only by a manual hoist, and lacking funds to operate it, Papov seals the rooms. The report sets the stage for transfer of the collection from the nationalized Kokorev warehouses to the Rumyantsev Library (later the Lenin Library).
