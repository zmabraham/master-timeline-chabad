# Levin marries in Kharkov

*1938*

After running an underground yeshiva branch in Krivoy Rog for over a year, Levin married and settled in Kharkov. His army exemption obtained through the eyeglasses miracle remained valid until World War II, when the Soviets stopped honoring such exemptions and drafted virtually every young man with hands and legs. Many of his friends were taken to the front and never returned.
