# Golda Meir attends Rosh HaShanah at Moscow's Choral Synagogue

*1949*

Meir had been disappointed by the small attendance on the first day, but the second day's enormous crowd — which she later told Italian reporter Oriana Fallaci had been orchestrated by Chabad — moved her deeply. She said it was 'the one time in my life that I considered becoming religious,' and recounted the moment to soldiers during the 1973 Yom Kippur War.
