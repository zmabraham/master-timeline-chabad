# Rayatz urges American Tmimim to mobilize scholarly petition for library

*1926*

Rayatz reports that his books are currently housed in a former church attached to the Rumyantsev Museum (renamed for Lenin) and that the Yevsektsiya in Belarus has been falsely claiming the library was given to them in perpetuity. He outlines a plan to involve bibliophile associations, prominent scholars in the world of Jewish studies, and synagogue leadership writing to influential figures — particularly to a person whose intervention with Soviet authorities would carry weight — to formally request access to his library for scholarly work.
