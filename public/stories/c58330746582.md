# Rabbi Yechezkel Feigin killed by Nazis in Warsaw

*1941*

The Nazis brought most of the city's Jews into a synagogue and set it on fire, forcing other Jews to watch. By some accounts, Rabbi Yitzchak Gurevitz removed a Torah scroll from the ark and danced with Feigin as they burned. When the Rebbe received word of these deaths in 1945, he fainted from grief.
