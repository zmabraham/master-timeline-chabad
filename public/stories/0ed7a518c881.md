# Yevesektzia stages show trial against Lubavitch yeshivah

*1921*

Following a similar staged trial against a Vitebsk cheder in January, the Yevesektzia targeted Lubavitch in Rostov. They forced their way into the Rebbe's home, seized inherited household goods and silver, and imprisoned three students plus the Rebbe's future son-in-law Shmaryahu Gurary for five days before he negotiated their release.
