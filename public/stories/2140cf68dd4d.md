# Rayatz settles in Otwock at Shapira hotel for the month of Tishrei

*1931*

In Elul 5691, the Rebbe Rayatz arrived in Otwock from Riga, renting the Shapira hotel as a spacious residence and beis midrash for himself and his family. He instructed that the students of the Lodz yeshiva join him in Otwock for the entire month of Tishrei.
