# Passing of R' Moshe of Zembin in Lubavitch

*1915*

On the morning after the all-night Horowitz farbrengen, R' Moshe of Zembin went to the mikveh and felt unwell. He was brought into the nearby Binyamin's Shtibel, where R' Dovber Shmotkin obtained medicine from Yehoshua's pharmacy that briefly revived him. He died there before Shabbos; the Rebbe Rashab cancelled the planned chassidus and asked whether any further attempts at resuscitation could be made, and the levaya was held on Motzaei Shabbos.
