# Rebbe Rayatz stops in Vilna returning from Lodmir

*1932*

Accompanied by his son-in-law Rashag, the Rebbe stayed at the Briston Hotel and prayed at the Apatov Chabad synagogue. He held a third Shabbat meal with senior Chabad rabbis including those of Kornitz, Postov, Doksitz, Dolhinov and Radoshkovitz, received many for yechidus, and visited Rabbi Chaim Ozer Grodzinski and the wealthy chassid Shneur Zalman Zeligson.
