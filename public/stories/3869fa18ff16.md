# Rabbi Lerer receives certificate of appreciation from mayor of Ashkelon for his work

*1970*

Rabbi Lerer receives certificate of appreciation from mayor of Ashkelon for his work in the city. R' Avraham (Avi) Lerer (born 5730, 1970) is a member of the Chabad community in Ashkelon, founder and director of the 'V'Samachta' association which works for the welfare of those at 'Barzilai' Hospital in the city.
