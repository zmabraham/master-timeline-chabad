# Jerusalem census records trustees of Kollel Chabad

*1875*

Avraham Statzeres, born in Navaradok in 5565, had made aliyah in 5621. Aharon Lipkin, born in Tolzin in 5593, had made aliyah in 5607. Yeshaya Orenstein, born in Eretz Yisrael in 5595, served alongside them as the scribe of the trusteeship.
