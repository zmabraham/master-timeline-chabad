# R' DovBer Efrat appointed Chabad administrator in Jerusalem

*1902*

On 21 Cheshvan 1903 (כ"א מ"ח תרס"ג), R' Manish Wirowlianski, the administrator of the Chabad kollel in Jerusalem, passed away. The Jerusalem Chassidim appointed R' DovBer Efrat in his place, a role he held for nearly a decade until his death.
