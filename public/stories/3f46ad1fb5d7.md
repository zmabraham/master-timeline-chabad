# Wedding of Chava, daughter of Rabbi Y. Jacobson

*1948*

On 5 Kislev 5709, the daughter of Rabbi Yaakov Yehuda Jacobson was married. The Rebbe Rayatz's daughter Chana attended and described it as a genuine first-class chassidic simcha, with men and women dancing in two separate halls and the Tomchei Tmimim students enlivening the celebration with love and friendship.
