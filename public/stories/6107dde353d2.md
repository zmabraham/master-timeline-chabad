# Rebbe offers his uncle R' Moshe Hornstein the Warsaw yeshiva directorship

*1928*

Writing from Riga the day after his appointment letters for R' Alter, the Rebbe addressed his uncle R' Moshe Hornstein, who lived in Otwock near Warsaw. He asked him to accept overall management and financial stewardship of Tomchei Temimim, explaining that the institution had to be rebuilt from scratch and that he could now coordinate more easily than before. R' Alter Simchowitz was to convey the practical details, and Hornstein could leave his own business to his wife and son in order to focus on the yeshiva.
