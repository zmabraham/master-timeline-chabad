# Approval to publish Jacobson's memoirs

*1991*

In late 1991 / early 5752, family members proposed editing and publishing R. Yisrael Jacobson's memoirs. They sought the Rebbe's approval, and he responded with not only consent but with strong encouragement, noting that many would learn from the reading, especially regarding authentic Chabad communal activism. Work began first on a separate volume covering events of the winter of 5700 (1939-40) and the rescue of the Rebbe Rayatz from Nazi-occupied Warsaw.
