# Burning of R. Yechezkel Feigin in synagogue

*1941*

On 10 Kislev 5702, the Nazis burned R. Yechezkel Feigin alive in a synagogue together with the chasidim R. Yitzchak Horowitz (Hamasmid) and R. Eliyahu Chaim Althaus. They were among the many Lubavitch chassidim murdered al kiddush Hashem in occupied Riga.
