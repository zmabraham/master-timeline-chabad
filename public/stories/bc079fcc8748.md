# Babinovichi peace court issues decision on Mitteler Rebbe

*1826*

On July 20, 1826, after roughly half a year of deliberation, the Babinovichi peace court concluded its work on the case concerning Rabbi DovBer's alleged illegal collection of funds from his chassidim. The decision was then presented for re-examination by the First Department of the Mogilev High Court. The High Court's inspector, Kilikovsky, reported the transfer to Prince Khovansky on July 31, 1826.
