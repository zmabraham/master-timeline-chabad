# Rabbi Yitzchak Hutner writes Rebbe questioning Mivtza Tefillin halacha

*1967*

After reading the Rebbe's Shabbat Bereishit kuntres, Rabbi Yitzchak Hutner — Rosh Yeshiva of Chaim Berlin and author of Pachad Yitzchak — sent his first letter to the Rebbe on Or L'Yom Bet of Parshat Vayeshev 5728. Citing the Ra'ah on one who eats matzah without knowing it is matzah, he argued that many 'tinokot shenishbu' approached by mivtza volunteers had no idea anything was inside the batim, and that without such knowledge the act constituted mit'asek rather than fulfillment of the mitzvah.
