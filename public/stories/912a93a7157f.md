# Letter from R. Shmuel Dovber of Borisov conveying the Maharash's compromise ruling

*1877*

After spending three Shabbosos in Lubavitch alongside R. Avraham Chaim and his brother, R. Shmuel Dovber of Borisov was directly entrusted by the Rebbe Maharash to relay his ruling in the partners' dispute. The psak gave R. Hendel a binary choice: retain the krechma and let R. Avraham Chaim sell his mill to anyone (even a non-Jewish license holder), or relinquish the krechma in exchange for 300 rubles. The Rebbe also advised R. Hendel to consult three trusted men if he could not decide on his own.
