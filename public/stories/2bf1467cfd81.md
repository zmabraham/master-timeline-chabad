# Letter on drought and tax oppression in Fez community

*1725*

In the year 5485 (1725), Rabbi Yehuda ibn Attar wrote anguished letters to Rabbi Yaakov ibn Tzur, then serving as rabbi in Meknes. He described the dire situation in Fez: severe drought, Torah scholars imprisoned by the local governor for three days over debts, and a punishing communal tax of 500 ounces per seventy households in the Mellah. He wrote in tears, fearing violence between debtors and oppressors.
