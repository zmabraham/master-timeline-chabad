# Death of Rabbi Yitzchak Yosef Twersky of Ovrutch

*1876*

Rabbi Yitzchak Yosef Twersky of Ovrutch, son-in-law of the Tzemach Tzedek of Lubavitch, died in 5636. He was succeeded by his son Nachum Dov ('Maharanad'), who led the Ovrutch chasidic court until his own death in 5654 and was also the brother-in-law of the Rebbe Rashab.
