# Globoke chassidim ask Rebbe for new rabbi

*1934*

Sixteen members of the Globoke chassidic community signed a heartfelt Yiddish letter on 4 Kislev 5695, telling the Rebbe Rayatz how Kuperstock had elevated the chassidic spirit not only locally but throughout the wider Vilna region. They mourned his impending departure to Eretz Yisrael and asked for a successor.
