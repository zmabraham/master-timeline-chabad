# Letter from Shainin in Dokshitz during wartime

*1940*

From within Nazi-shadowed Dokshitz at the height of winter 5700, Rabbi Aryeh Leib Shainin sent a disguised letter to his former townsman R' Yochanan Gordon in New York. Writing as 'Uncle Yaakov Leib Glaz,' Shainin reported that he and his wife were severely ill and begged for material support. Shainin, who had been sent to serve as rabbi of Dokshitz by the Rebbe Rayatz in 5683, would later perish in the Holocaust.
