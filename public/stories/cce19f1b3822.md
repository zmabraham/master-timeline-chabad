# Sefer HaMitzvot first printed in Constantinople

*1510*

Maimonides composed Sefer HaMitzvot in his youth in Arabic, and it was translated into Hebrew during his lifetime by R. Avraham ibn Chasdai, R. Shlomo ibn Ayyub, and R. Moshe ibn Tibbon. The work catalogs the 613 commandments and serves as a preface to his Mishneh Torah. Its first printing appeared in Constantinople in 1510.
