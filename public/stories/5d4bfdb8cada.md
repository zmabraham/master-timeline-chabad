# R. Meir Blizinski recounts Otwock founding in letter of 13 Shevat 5737

*1977*

The detailed memoir letter recounted his marriage on Rosh Chodesh Nisan 5686, his decision to establish a yeshiva in Otwock, the recruitment of Naftali Tzuker and R. Shlomo Tziley, and the eventual collapse of the branch in spring 5687. The letter became the primary first-person source for understanding the Otwock branch's brief existence.
