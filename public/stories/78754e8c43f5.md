# Winter devoted to rescuing Rebbe Rayatz from Nazi-occupied Warsaw

*1939*

After the German invasion of Poland, the winter of 5700 (1939–1940) was consumed by coordinated efforts to extract Rebbe Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn, his family, and his entourage from occupied Warsaw. A dedicated book documenting the events and testimony of those days is being prepared for publication. The rescue effort drew on diplomatic, logistical, and financial mobilization across the Chabad network.
