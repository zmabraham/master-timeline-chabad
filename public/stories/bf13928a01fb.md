# Tzemach Tzedek travels to Bobruisk, Paritch and Rogachev

*1831*

Reconstructed from the headings of his Chassidic discourses, this winter tour brought the Tzemach Tzedek to important Chassidic centers in White Russia. Such trips combined public maamarim, private yechidus, and the strengthening of local Chassidic communities. The journey is documented in the chronological registry of his maamarim.
