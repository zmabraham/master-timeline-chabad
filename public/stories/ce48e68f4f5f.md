# Yaavetz moves from Fez to Meknes to head the community

*1718*

Between Iyar and Menachem Av of 5478 (spring–summer 1718), Rabbi Yaakov ibn Tzur left Fez and settled in Meknes, where the community received him as 'head and dignitary.' A 13 Menachem Av letter from Jerusalem emissary Rabbi Yom Tov Crispo expresses joy at the news of his installation. He would remain in Meknes for roughly eleven years.
