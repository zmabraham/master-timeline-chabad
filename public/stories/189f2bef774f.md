# Achei Tmimim yeshiva founded in Newark

*1942*

In 5702 (1941-1942), R. Mordechai Mentlik and R. Moshe Pinchas Katz — both living in Newark and commuting daily to their yeshiva work in New York — founded the Achei Tmimik yeshiva in Newark, New Jersey. They continued to reside in Newark until 5706, when they moved to New York.
