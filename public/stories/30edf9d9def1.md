# R. Baruch Mordechai Itinga settles in Jerusalem

*1851*

Around 1851, more than thirty-five years after the Mitteler Rebbe had advised against his aliyah, R. Baruch Mordechai Itinga finally ascended to the Land of Israel and settled in Jerusalem. He had served for an extended period as rabbi of Bobruisk in the intervening years. He died in Jerusalem just over a year later, on 14 Elul 1852.
