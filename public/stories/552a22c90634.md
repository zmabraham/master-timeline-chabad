# Two R' Aharons emigrate to Eretz Yisrael; ship sinks near Crimea

*1777*

In summer 1777 (תקל"ז), R' Aharon b"R Yitzchak Aizik (Aharon the Great) and R' Aharon b"R Meir of Smilyan (Aharon the Small) joined R' Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk's caravan to Eretz Yisrael. Their ship sank near Crimea, but both survived. They settled first in Tzfat and later moved to Tiberias.
