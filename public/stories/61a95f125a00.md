# Lubavitcher refugees arrive in Łódź

*1946*

At Wrocław German and Polish locals warned that many Jews had survived, and the refugees grasped that danger still lay ahead. Bribed Polish officials let them disembark, and Anash who had arrived earlier hired a truck to bring them to 42 Zachodnia Street, where the Lubavitch refugees from Russia lodged together. A va'ad of Anash in Łódź coordinated relief, likely with Joint Distribution Committee aid, and began arranging onward travel out of Poland.
