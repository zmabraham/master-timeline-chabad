# Assembly on reviving Chassidic communities in America

*1944*

On the evening of 29 Tishrei 5705 the Rebbe Rayatz convened an assembly of Anash that he had summoned regarding the revival of Chassidic communities in the present generation. The discussion was framed around three categories: regular congregants of Anash shuls, individual Anash who pray in other shuls, and descendants of Chassidic families — with a coordinating committee to be selected.
