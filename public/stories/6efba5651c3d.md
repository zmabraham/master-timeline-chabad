# Arrest of Cherson Tomchei Tmimim faculty and students

*1929*

The Chabad yeshiva in Cherson was opened by R' Yosef Himelstein and the mashgiach R' Tzemach HaLevi Gurevitz for the winter term. Before the new arrivals from Zlobin reached the city, the entire faculty and student body had been arrested. They were eventually released under condition not to leave Cherson — and immediately fled the city the moment they were freed.
