# Rebbe Rayatz writes Jacobson about sons-in-law visas

*1938*

On 18 Av 5698 (August 15, 1938), the Rebbe Rayatz wrote to R. Israel Jacobson, director of Aguch in America, urging him out of inner love to help obtain US entry visas for both of R. David Stockhamer's new sons-in-law — R. Mordechai Mentlik and R. Moshe Pinchas Katz — so they could settle near him in Newark, 'a good place for a ben Torah.'
