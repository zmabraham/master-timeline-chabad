# Alter Rebbe writes summer 1805 letter recounting Maggid's regard for him

*1805*

The letter provides the most detailed first-person account of the 1772 Rovno gathering: R. Avraham waiting at the outskirts, R. Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk interceding, and the Alter Rebbe escorting him into the Maggid's chamber. It also defends the Alter Rebbe's status as a beloved disciple of the Maggid.
