# Passing of Rebbetzin Sterna Sarah, 13 Shevat 5702, in the United States

*1942*

Rebbetzin Sterna Sarah, wife of the Rebbe Rashab and mother of the Rayatz, passed away on Shabbos morning 13 Shevat 5702 in the United States. As her mother-in-law Rebbetzin Rivka had decades earlier, she asked her attendant for a siddur, davened, and handed it back; when the attendant turned to put it away she had already passed. The Rayatz was in Chicago on klal matters at the time, mirroring the Rebbe Rashab's absence at the petira of his own mother.
