# First Achos Tmimim Dvinsk farbrengen with Riga delegation

*1940*

Mrs. Basya Chefetz, Mrs. Mina Skobla, and Mrs. Rivka Kramnikov traveled from Riga to attend. About fourteen girls came from Dvinsk. R. Peletz opened the gathering on the purpose of Chassidus, and three girls - Mrs. Kayder, Mrs. Zelikman, and Mrs. Kaufman - addressed the group. The Rebbe responded with a 200-lat gift to seed a gemach for Achos Tmimim Dvinsk.
