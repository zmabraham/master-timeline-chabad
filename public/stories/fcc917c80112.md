# Yaavetz appointed scribe of the Fez beit din at age 20

*1693*

In 5453 (1693), at age twenty, Rabbi Yaakov ibn Tzur was appointed scribe (sofer beit din) of the Fez rabbinical court led by Rabbis Vidal Tzarfati and Menachem Serero. He later recorded that during this period he visited the court rabbis two or three times daily, presenting his halachic questions and observing their adjudication. An archive document from 5459 still bears his signature with the title 'sofer.'
