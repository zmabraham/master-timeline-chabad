# RA"Ch writes from Smolensk prison on his contact with the Rebbetzin

*1889*

On 20 Iyar 5649 RA"Ch wrote from prison in Smolensk to the mashpia R' Hendel, reporting that R' Avraham Yitzchak Zarchi had visited him and told him of a letter the Rebbetzin (wife of the Maharash, Rebbetzin Rivkah) had sent before Pesach to R' Leib Berlin on RA"Ch's behalf. RA"Ch expressed his distress at troubling her and admiration at her humility.
