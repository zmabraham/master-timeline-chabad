# Rebbe Rayatz's first visit to Vilna after leaving Russia

*1928*

A few months after leaving Soviet Russia, the Rebbe traveled to Warsaw and on his return to Riga stopped in Vilna at the urgent request of local chassidim and the Tomchei Tmimim students there. Originally planned for two days, he extended his stay to a full week after a further appeal from chassidim in Dolhinov. He delivered a maamar at the Apatov shul where the yeshiva was housed, prayed there on Shabbat, and met with Rabbi Chaim Ozer Grodzinski regarding aid to Russian Jewry.
