# Yisrael Azriel Feldman arrives in the United States

*1949*

Feldman wrote to the Rebbe describing his background as a native of Różan, Poland and son of a shochet from the chassidim of Uman, his studies in Tomchei Temimim Warsaw in 5694-95, and his sojourn in Russia before reaching the Steyer camp. After his arrival in America he was accepted as rabbi of Beth Knesses Agudath Achim in Milwaukee.
