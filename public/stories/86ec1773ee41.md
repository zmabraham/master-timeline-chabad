# Rebbe's response to concerns over Moscow Jewish library plan

*1992*

The diarist had written that the delegation had never received explicit instructions to consent to the Moscow academy/university/library plan, had refused to sign any document that could be read as such consent, and feared the books could become locked into a Russian Jewish library. The Rebbe's reply pushed the decision back onto senior askanim while withholding personal endorsement of either course.
