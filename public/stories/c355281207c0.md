# Rebbe asks Levin to consult elder chassidim about employment

*1948*

Idle in Bezancourt with no parnasa, Levin asked the Rebbe what work to pursue. The reply, dated 29 Sivan 5708, instructed him to consult senior anash and report back their advice. R. Yisrael Noach and other elders subsequently advised him to take up melamdus.
