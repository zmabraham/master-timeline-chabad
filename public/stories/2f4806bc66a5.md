# Arrest and release of the Frierdiker Rebbe

*1927*

Schusterman recalls that the arrest and release of the Rebbe occurred during the summer of 5687, while he was studying in Suraz. The Suraz community was unaware of events at the time; Rabbi Axelrod may have known but feared to publicize it, as the Yevsektzia had begun operating in Suraz as well.
