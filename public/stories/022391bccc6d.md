# Death of R' Isser Yanovsky, rabbi of Krivoy Rog

*1908*

In winter 5668, R' Isser Yanovsky passed away. He had served as rabbi of Krivoy Rog for about fifteen years and had traveled to Carlsbad for medical treatment in 5666 with Rashab's support. On 21 Adar I 5668, Rashab wrote his father-in-law R' Asher requesting that any valuable items, especially writings from Rebbeim, found in R' Isser's estate be sent to him.
