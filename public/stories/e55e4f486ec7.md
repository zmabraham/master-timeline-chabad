# Detroit Jewish Chronicle praises Rebbe's impact on Latvian Jewry

*1928*

The paper wrote that 'the Lubavitcher Rebbe had brought a great deal of activity into the life of Orthodox Jewry in Latvia.' It concluded that 'Soviet Russia has thus, by expelling the Lubavitcher Rebbe, done a good turn to the Jews of Latvia.' The story was an early sign that the Rebbe's influence transcended his immediate community.
