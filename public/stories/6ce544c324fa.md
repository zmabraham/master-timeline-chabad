# Rabbinical assembly in Paks to strengthen Orthodox observance

*1844*

On 3 Elul 5604, prominent Hungarian rabbis gathered in Paks to strengthen Orthodox observance against the inroads of the Reform movement. Among the issues debated was the proposed founding of a modernist rabbinical seminary promoted by the reformers, which the Ksav Sofer later reported had been successfully blocked. In follow-up correspondence, the Ksav Sofer counseled that ongoing resistance to the reformers should be conducted discreetly rather than through further public assemblies.
