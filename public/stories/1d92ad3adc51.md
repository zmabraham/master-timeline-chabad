# Rebbe's 'Song in every interpretation' response on the library mission

*1991*

After Rabbi Kunin wrote to the Lubavitcher Rebbe describing the delegation's frustrations in Moscow and asking for a blessing, the answer came in the morning: 'We stand in immediate proximity to Parashat Beshalach — song in every interpretation. I will mention it at the gravesite.' The cryptic, encouraging response reframed the mission's hardships as a prelude to triumph and song. It was received as the Gulf War was breaking out and the delegation faced repeated rebuffs from Soviet officials over the Schneerson collection.
