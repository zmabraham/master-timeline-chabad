# R' Yosef Friedman appointed mashgiach

*1911*

After the passing of R' Zev Wolf Lewitin, R' Yosef Friedman of Rogachov was appointed mashgiach over nigleh at Tomchei Tmimim, as mentioned in a letter dated 15 Shevat 5671 (1911). He later became rabbi of Ostrovna. R' Yisrael Jacobson recalled him as filling the role of the departed R' Zev Wolf Halevi.
