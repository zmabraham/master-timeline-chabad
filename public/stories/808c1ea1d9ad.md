# Rebbe Rayatz writes on global anash correspondence

*1929*

The Rebbe instructed his correspondents in Kovno to gather scattered descendants of chassidim, organize letter-writing campaigns to anash who had emigrated to Brazil and South Africa, and engage in carefully worded correspondence with relatives still trapped in Soviet Russia. The letter (Igros Kodesh vol. 16, no. 6154) emphasized the duty to awaken in such Jews their ancestral connection to Chassidus.
