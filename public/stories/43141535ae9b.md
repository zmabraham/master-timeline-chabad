# Hendel Deutsch emigrates and is sent by ChaMa to search Russian archives

*1991*

In 5751 (1991), R. Hendel Deutsch emigrated from the Soviet Union to the United States. That year the ChaMa organization sent him back to continue searching Russian archives for documents related to Chabad history. He returned with many documents but, despite further guidance from R. Shalom DovBer Levin, did not locate the Mitteler Rebbe's arrest file.
