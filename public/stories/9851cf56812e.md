# Rabbi Avraham Litvin receiving a certificate of appreciation from the mayor of Decatur

*1959*

Rabbi Avraham Litvin receiving a certificate of appreciation from the mayor of Decatur. Rabbi Avraham Litvin (born in 5720, 1959) is the head shliach of the Rebbe in the state of Kentucky in the United States, and director of the Chabad House in the large central city in the state, Louisville
