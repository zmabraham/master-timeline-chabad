# Lvov Haggadah 'Gevurot Yisrael' (Maggid of Kozhnitz) and 'Mateh Moshe'

*1864*

Around 5624 (1864), a Haggadah was published in Lvov featuring two commentaries on the Pardes pattern: 'Gevurot Yisrael' by the Maggid Rabbi Yisrael of Kozhnitz, and 'Mateh Moshe' by his son Moshe Eliyakum Briya. Additional drashot on Pesach, the four cups, and 'Kadesh U'Rechatz', plus drashot on Sefirat HaOmer and a commentary on Chad Gadya by Rebbe Mordechai of Chernobyl, were included. The 34-leaf quarto edition was printed by Sh.L. Flecker.
