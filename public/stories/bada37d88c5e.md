# Maharashab receives permanent exemption from Russian military service

*1879*

On 25 July 1879, during the summer of 5639, the Vitebsk Gubernia conscription office issued certificate no. 1645 to 'the Vitebsk merchant's son Sholom Berke Shmuilov Schneersohn', recording that he had presented himself for service under the 1877 draft. Following medical examination he was declared wholly unfit for military service and granted a permanent exemption. He was registered as a Vitebsk resident — likely to ease obtaining the exemption — reflecting his father's property holdings in that city.
