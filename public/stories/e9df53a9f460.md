# The Rebbe and Rebbetzin flee Paris on erev Shavuot

*1940*

With Nazi forces overrunning France, the Rebbe and Rebbetzin Chaya Mushka escaped from Paris on erev Shavuot 5700. They traveled to Vichy where they remained for several months attempting to secure American immigration papers. The flight began a year of wandering across France that would not end until their arrival in New York in Sivan 5701.
