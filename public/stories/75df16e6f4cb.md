# Document confirming Mitteler Rebbe's standing issued in Vitebsk

*1816*

On March 3, 1816, the former military governor of White Russia, Duke Alexander of Württemberg, gave Rabbi Dovber Schneuri a formal document parallel to the one his father had received in 1801. It described him as an honest man enjoying the full respect of the Jewish community, traveling to teach ethics and beneficial conduct—though, as Khovansky later noted, it conferred no right to collect money.
