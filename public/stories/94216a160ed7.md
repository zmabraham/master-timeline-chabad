# Yechidus with the Rebbe and instruction to write memoirs

*1957*

On 2 Marcheshvan 5718 (October 1957), R. Yisrael Jacobson entered the Rebbe's chamber for a yechidus, marking the 50th anniversary of his first entry to the Rashab's chamber. During that audience, the Rebbe instructed him to record his memoirs of his time in Lubavitch. He continued writing these memoirs through 5718-5719 and later published selections in the quarterly Di Yiddishe Heim from 5724 until his passing on 17 Sivan 5735.
