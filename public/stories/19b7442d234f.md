# Chabad airlifts ~130 Chernobyl rescue children from Moscow to Israel

*1991*

On 1 Tammuz 5751 (June 13, 1991), the first flight of Chernobyl children rescued by Chabad reached Israel despite severe complications. Visas were granted only at the last minute and Aeroflot initially refused to board children without them. After takeoff a mechanical fault forced an emergency return to Moscow; the children boarded a replacement aircraft and arrived in Israel that evening, greeted by Chabad with Shehecheyanu, singing, and dancing.
