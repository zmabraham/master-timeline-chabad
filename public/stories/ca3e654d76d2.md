# Passing of Rebbe Rayatz

*1950*

Rebbe Rayatz's histalkus on 10 Shevat 5710 ended his nesius and the era in which the second-floor room at 770 served as his yechidus room. According to his secretary R' Nissan Mindel, Rayatz used a single mechanical pencil to answer thousands of letters in special numbered notebooks for several consecutive years until his passing on that date.
