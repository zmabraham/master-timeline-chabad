# Threat to nationalize the Rashab's Rostov home in summer 1923

*1923*

In a letter dated Rosh Chodesh Elul 5683, Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn relayed his mother's news that the authorities intended to nationalize the Rostov home. In the event, no further confiscation occurred that summer, and the Rebbe's household remained in the building another half year. The episode underscored the constant precariousness of Chabad's headquarters under Soviet rule.
