# First refugee group including Tomchei Tmimim arrives in Shanghai from Kobe

*1941*

Erev Shabbos Parshas Shoftim, 7 Elul 5701 (August 30, 1941), 350 Polish-Jewish refugees arrived in Shanghai from Kobe. Among them were the Mir Yeshiva headed by mashgiach Rabbi Yechezkel Levenstein and Rosh Yeshiva Rabbi Chaim Shmuelevitz, students of Chachmei Lublin, the Lubavitcher Yeshiva, and talmidim from Telz, Kamenitz, Kletzk, and Radin. A special reception committee met them at the port and transported them to prepared lodgings in shuls and batei medrash.
