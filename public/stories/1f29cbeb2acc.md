# Jerusalem Jews tell Montefiore agricultural plan is no longer viable

*1849*

On Montefiore's return visit in 5609, the Jerusalem community informed him that conditions had reversed: with the end of Egyptian rule over Eretz Yisrael, weapons had returned to local hands and the countryside had become too dangerous for Jewish agricultural settlement. The letter was signed by several Chabad leaders in Jerusalem including R' Eliyahu Yosef Rivlin, R' Ephraim Yitzchak Epstein, and R' Uri Ornstein.
