# Domostroy newspaper publishes article attacking Chabad library claim

*1991*

Shortly before Passover 5751, the newspaper Domostroy printed an article attacking the Chabad mission and its claim to the Schneerson library held in Moscow. The piece's contents made clear it had been commissioned by the Ministry of Culture, raising the question of whether it had been ordered before or after recent talks with Gubenko. The mission concluded that the time had come to begin its own press campaign and prepared a written response.
