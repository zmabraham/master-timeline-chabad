Rabbi Yaakov Kruglyak. Rabbi Avraham Yaakov Kruglyak (born on 13 Tishrei 5748, 1987) is the secretary of the Nachalat Har Chabad council, rabbi of the 'Achvat Menachem' Chabad synagogue in the 'HaMachane' neighborhood, and chief kashrut supervisor of the 'Nachalat HaKashrut' organization.
