# Yossi Rosenstein illustrated Haggadah with English translation published in Jerusalem

*1986*

In 5746 (1986), an illustrated Haggadah by Yossi Rosenstein was printed in Jerusalem with an English translation. The 142-page duodecimo edition served both Hebrew- and English-speaking audiences. It reflected the late 20th-century Israeli tradition of producing bilingual illustrated Haggadot.
