# Rebbe Rayatz writes to Aryeh Leib Kremer about visas

*1945*

On 24 Tammuz 5705 (Igrot Kodesh vol. 8, letter 2736), the Frierdiker Rebbe wrote to R. Aryeh Leib Kremer in response to his letter about visas for the Tomchei Tmimim students stranded in Shanghai. The Rebbe instructed him to take stronger and more reliable measures, to immediately send the list of students to R. Yerachmiel Binyaminson, and announced that he himself would write to Binyaminson asking him to intervene with those who had influence in such matters.
