# Wedding of Rebbe's daughter; donor offers building for yeshiva

*1928*

On 14 Kislev 5689 (27 November 1928) — the date of a wedding in the Rebbe's family — R' Avraham HaKohen, a Warsaw philanthropist, expressed his desire to donate a new building for the Tomchei Tmimim yeshiva. The Rebbe Rayatz alluded to the offer days later in a Chanukah letter to him, and pursued the matter through R' Dov Nisan Bezpalov, hoping to acquire property in Warsaw or in nearby Otwock for the yeshiva. The plan ultimately did not come to fruition at that time.
