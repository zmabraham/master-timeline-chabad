# Rebbe blesses founding of Society of the Sisters of the Temimim in US

*1938*

He wrote, 'In reply to your letter advising me of your excellent resolution to interest yourselves in the study of Chasidism… I was greatly gratified with your good decision.' He appointed a committee of three prominent American rabbis — Yisrael Jacobson, Yisrael Kazarnovsky, and Yochanan Gordon — to teach the group and advise on format and curriculum, instructing them to set up weekly classes.
