# Mittler Rebbe settles in Lubavitch, establishing it as Chabad center

*1813*

Following the close of the Napoleonic War, the Mittler Rebbe returned to White Russia and chose Lubavitch as his residence. He purchased a wooden house from the estate owner Krouyer and began building a large wooden synagogue in the courtyard. This move opened the 102-year period during which Lubavitch served as the movement's worldwide center.
