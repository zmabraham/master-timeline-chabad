# Passing of R. Pinchas Horowitz, the Baal Haflaah, in Frankfurt

*1805*

The Baal Haflaah had taken his Frankfurt seat at the start of 5532, after passing through Brod. His son R. Zvi Hirsch Halevi Horowitz eulogized him in Sefer Lachmei Todah, noting that he had taught Torah in Frankfurt 'more than thirty-three years.' His earlier praise of the Alter Rebbe's Shulchan Aruch is one of the key indicators dating the Alter Rebbe's halachic work.
