# Wedding of the Rayatz and Nechama Dina in Lubavitch

*1897*

Chasidim streamed to Lubavitch for the celebration. The Rayatz donned a shtreimel and recited Shehechiyanu before the ceremony. The chuppah was held Friday afternoon (13 Elul 5657) in the courtyard of the main synagogue, after which the crowd escorted the couple through town with singing and dancing.
