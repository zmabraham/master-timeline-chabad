# Mikhail Nachmanson arrested by Soviet authorities

*1936*

Nachmanson, a Nevel-born Chabad-family Chasid turned Bolshevik who had risen to head regional NKVD departments, was transferred to Lubyanka Prison and given an extrajudicial five-year sentence. He was released in 1941, re-arrested in 1950, freed in 1953 after Stalin's death, and amnestied in 1956 — yet remained a committed Communist until the Soviet Union's collapse.
