# Confrontation with Lenin Library commission over Polyakov collection

*1992*

On 3 Adar I the commission tried instead to enlist him in cataloguing damaged pages of an early Bologna print. Director Philipov backed commission chair Livachkin against the author, denying he had ever been invited to prove ownership. Later that day the Rebbe reiterated through R. Leibel Groner that the delegation must follow lawyer Veronika's lead.
