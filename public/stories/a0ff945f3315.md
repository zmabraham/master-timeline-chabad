# Poland returns Chabad manuscripts to the movement

*1977*

Veronika and the delegation cited the 1977 Polish return of Chabad writings as proof that Eastern European governments could and did restore confiscated Jewish religious texts. The precedent was used to argue that Poland, Britain, Israel and the United States should pressure Gorbachev to do the same with the Schneersohn library. The Polish return was invoked repeatedly in 1991 as the delegation built its diplomatic case.
