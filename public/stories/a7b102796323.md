# R' Natan Amram appointed joint shadar to Italy and Europe

*1834*

In Nissan 5594 (1834), R' Natan Amram — later rabbi of Alexandria, Egypt — was designated to travel as a joint shadar from the Sephardic and Chabad communities of Hebron through Italy and surrounding European countries. His departure was delayed by the pogroms and earthquake, and his mission was expanded. The expanded shlichut letter, signed primarily by Chabad leaders, was preserved in Sarid V'Palit.
