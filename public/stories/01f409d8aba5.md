# Yud-Beis Tammuz farbrengen organized by Kobe Tomchei Tmimim students

*1941*

For Yud-Beis and Yud-Gimmel Tammuz 5701 (July 7-8, 1941), the Kobe students organized a public farbrengen in addition to their internal seudah. They invited the refugee Vaad of leading rabbanim, who filled three rooms of the yeshiva to capacity. Talmidim explained the meaning of the day and reviewed Chassidus, and the Amshinover Rebbe participated and spoke several times.
