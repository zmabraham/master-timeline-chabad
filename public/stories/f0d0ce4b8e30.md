# R' Meir Refael's of Vilna draws close to Chassidus

*1789*

Around 5549, the wealthy Vilna chassid R' Meir Refael's together with his sons Shlomo and Yaakov traveled to the Alter Rebbe in Liozna to study Chassidic conduct. He later became the head of the Chabad kehilla in Vilna and a close disciple of the Rebbe. He testified to this date during his interrogation in 5559.
