# American Chassidim lobby for Rebbe Rayatz's release

*1927*

Once word of the arrest arrived in the U.S., the chevra gathered to plan a response. Attorney Kramer engaged Senator Borah, an influential American politician whose voice carried weight even abroad. The American community's organized strength was weak, however, and the fundraising effort fell short of the urgency — Avraham Dov Levin der Malach even denied the whole story, reasoning that anything printed in the newspapers must be false.
