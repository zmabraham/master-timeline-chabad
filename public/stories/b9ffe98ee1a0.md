# Kitzee illuminated Haggadah manuscript completed

*1760*

In 5520 (1760), the calligrapher Chaim ben Asher Anshil of Kitzee, near Pressburg, completed his ornate manuscript Haggadah on parchment. Twenty-two leaves of square Hebrew text and Yiddish ritual instructions in tzena u'rena script were enhanced with elaborate illustrations.
