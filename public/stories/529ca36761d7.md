# Permanent students' minyan established at Toras Mitzion in Brownsville

*1934*

Initial Shabbatos sometimes lacked a full minyan and R' Yochanan Gordon complained about the difficulty. Over time the minyan grew and moved to the Anshe Ozaritz shul at 885 Hopkinson Ave. Eventually the minyan flourished with multiple minyanim attending Shabbos services and influence spread further into the yeshiva world.
