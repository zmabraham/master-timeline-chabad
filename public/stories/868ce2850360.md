# Catalog of Rebbe Rashab's book collection compiled

*1915*

During 5675 (1915), a catalog was apparently prepared of the books in the Rebbe Rashab's library, listing 5,642 numbered entries representing approximately 12,000 volumes. The work began in early 5673 but the surviving list, which includes books printed up to 5670, was likely completed during 5675 since the books were placed in storage at the start of 5676. The catalog counted multi-volume sets like Shas as single entries.
