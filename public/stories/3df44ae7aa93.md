# Rabbi Shila Eliezerov notifies Rabbi Zevin of Moshiach Torah scroll

*1941*

Shortly after Simchat Torah 5702, Rabbi Shlomo Yehuda Leib Eliezerov (Shila), the Rebbe's representative in the Land of Israel, wrote to Rabbi Shlomo Yosef Zevin notifying him of a telegram received from the Frierdiker Rebbe announcing his decision to write a Torah scroll to greet Moshiach. Anash in Eretz Yisrael immediately began purchasing letters in the scroll, and the Kollel secretary recorded 156 buyers in a special ledger. Among the registrants were Rabbi A.C. Naeh, Rabbi Yaakov Landau of Bnei Brak, Rabbi Yaakov Yisrael Kanievsky, and Rabbi Zevin himself.
