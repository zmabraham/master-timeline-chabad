# Lt. Col. Ernst Bloch killed at the front in the final days of the war

*1945*

After leading the Rebbe's rescue, Bloch was promoted to colonel and later appointed a battalion commander in Russia in 1943. In February 1945 the SS forced his dismissal from the army due to his Jewish background. When the Red Army reached Berlin in April 1945, he returned to the front and was killed days before the German surrender.
