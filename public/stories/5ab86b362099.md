# Cremona printing of the Zohar

*1558*

Concurrent with the Mantua edition, the Zohar was printed at the Cremona house of Vincenzo Conti in a larger format with textual variants from the Mantua text, known as the Zohar Gadol. This Cremona text was reprinted twice more, in Lublin (1623-4) and Sulzbach (1684).
