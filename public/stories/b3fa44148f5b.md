# Bill of sale of four amot in Hebron for Chabad settlement

*1822*

The 5582 bill of sale of four amot in Hebron marked a foundational step in establishing the permanent Chabad community in the city. R' Shimon Shmerling, one of the founders, was a signatory. In the same year, EY support funds from Russia were sent through R' Yitzchak of Radvil, son of R' Yechiel Michel of Zlotchov.
