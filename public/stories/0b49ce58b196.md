# Rayatz sends blessing letter to Shanghai refugees bound for Israel

*1949*

On Rosh Chodesh Adar 5709, Rebbe Rayatz dispatched a letter of blessing to Chabad refugees traveling from Shanghai to Eretz Yisrael via America. The letter was drafted on the suggestion of his son-in-law Rabbi M.M. Schneerson and delivered through Rabbi Ashkenazi and Rabbi Chadakov. Rabbi Chadakov later reported that the emissaries and letter generated great emotional impact and brought hearts closer.
