# Rabbi David Meir Rabinowitz visits yeshiva in Otwock

*1936*

Rabinowitz, en route to Eretz Yisrael, stopped at Otwock with his son Peretz and other American travelers. Upon entering the hall where about 70 senior students were learning, he was so overcome with emotion that he sat speechless against the wall for some time. At his farewell gathering he cried out that he had never imagined Tomchei Temimim could be so elevated.
