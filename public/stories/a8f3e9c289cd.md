# R. Shneur Zalman Slonim arrives at Lubavitch

*1885*

R. Shneur Zalman, son of R. Mordechai Dov Slonim of Hebron, came to Lubavitch and remained a substantial time. He later accompanied the Rebbe Rashab to Yalta for the winter and, on returning to Eretz Yisrael, was tasked with locating Chassidic manuscripts there.
