# Grodner Moment publishes feature on the Rebbe's activity in Riga hotel

*1928*

The paper described the Rebbe sitting 'day and night in a room designated for prayer and learning,' meeting all types of Jews in the afternoons. On Shabbat, large crowds flooded the hotel to hear the Rebbe recite a maamar, with his voice 'becoming stronger and more flowing, words streaming from his mouth, quotes from the Bible, Talmud, and Zohar woven and seasoned with parables.'
