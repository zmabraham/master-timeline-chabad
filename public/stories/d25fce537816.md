# Death of R' Yisrael ben Yitzchak Yaffe of Kapust

*1829*

R' Yisrael ben Yitzchak Yaffe of Kapust, one of the founding settlers of the Chabad community in Hebron, passed away before Shvat 5589 (January 1829). He had served as a shadar and signatory on key documents of the community. His son R' Ephraim later succeeded him as memuneh (administrator) of the Chabad kollel in Hebron.
