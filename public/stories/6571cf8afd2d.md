# Nazi murder of the Jewish women of Shumyachi and surrounding shtetlach

*1942*

After the war the author learned that the Germans had concentrated the Jews of the surrounding shtetlach in a single killing site, murdering the women on 28 Adar 5702. The author's wife Henya was the sole survivor of the entire Fradkin–Paz family; the children, grandchildren and great-grandchildren they raised are credited to the merit of these kedoshim.
