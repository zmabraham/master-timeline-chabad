# Rayatz writes to Yaakov Yosef Slonim about buying books in Eretz Yisrael

*1904*

On the Tuesday of Parashas Pinchas 5664, Rayatz sends Slonim a list of 47 books he is willing to buy from a Jerusalem dealer for 92 rubles and 40 kopeks, asking him to negotiate the prices down. He emphasizes interest specifically in old editions and requests that the seller send lists of antique seforim, not new ones. He also asks Slonim to obtain addresses of dealers in Algiers, where he had heard cheap acquisitions were possible.
