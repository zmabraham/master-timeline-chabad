# Author's first pilgrimage to parents' graves in Samarkand and Tashkent

*1993*

In Elul 5753 (August/September 1993), the author traveled for the first time to Samarkand to visit his father Eliyahu's grave and to Tashkent to visit his mother Rachel's grave. He left funds in Samarkand for restoration of the matzevah and arranged for a new matzevah in Tashkent. For decades earlier he had relied on shluchim traveling to the Soviet Union to bring back photographs of the gravestones.
