# Preliminary rabbinical conference in Baranovich

*1909*

Following the Rebbe Rashab's longstanding insistence that any major rabbinical assembly be preceded by a smaller preparatory conference of selected senior figures, a three-day gathering was held in Baranovich. This allowed the leading Orthodox rabbis to clarify positions, narrow disagreements, and agree on what would be proposed publicly before the broader twenty-five-rabbi convention in Vilna began two days later.
