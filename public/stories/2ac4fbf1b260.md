# Rayatz arrested in Leningrad; manuscripts hidden by chassidim

*1927*

When Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn was arrested by Soviet authorities in 1927, he entrusted manuscript bundles (bichlach) to several chassidim to conceal until the danger passed. The Seventh Rebbe later clarified that the manuscripts were returned almost immediately and that authorities had not, in fact, formally confiscated them.
