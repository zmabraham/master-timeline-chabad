# R. Yisrael Noach Chatzkevitch appointed mashpia for Lithuania

*1928*

R. Yisrael Noach Chatzkevitch, who served as rav of Kopishok, was commissioned by the Rebbe Rayatz in summer 5688 to travel among the towns of the Kovno, Vilna, and Dvinsk regions, establishing regular shiurim in Chassidus among anash.
