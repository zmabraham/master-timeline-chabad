# First chasidic aliyah led by Rabbi Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk

*1777*

His protege Rabbi Schneur Zalman, the future founder of Chabad, escorted him until Mogilev-Podolski in southern Ukraine and considered continuing on himself, but his mentor instructed him to remain in Europe to spread chasidic teachings. This aliyah established a chasidic presence in the Holy Land that endured for generations.
