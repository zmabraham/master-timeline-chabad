# Rebbe Rashab pushes for rabbinical assembly on JCA funding crisis

*1901*

The JCA (Jewish Colonization Association) had begun channeling funds to schools of the Mefitzei Haskalah society, threatening traditional Jewish education in Russia. Rebbe Rashab considered the matter so urgent that he sought to organize a rabbinical assembly to formulate a response. He worked behind the scenes through R. Avraham Avli Perlson, asking the great rabbis (such as the Telshe Rav) to take ownership of the cause publicly while he supported them from the background.
