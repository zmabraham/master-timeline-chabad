# Ramash describes parental-love teaching to Edelman and Fogelman

*1943*

The Ramash had been chastised by elder Chasidim for warmly welcoming non-observant Jews rather than criticizing them. He asked the Rebbe if he was acting correctly. The Rebbe replied that just as a parent feels deeper love for a child missing a limb, G-d loves every Jew completely, but loves those who are missing observance even more. This philosophy clearly infused the Ramash's later leadership as the seventh Rebbe.
