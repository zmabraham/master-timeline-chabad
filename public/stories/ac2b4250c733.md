# Author makes pilgrimage to Rostov Ohel for 2 Nisan yahrzeit

*1925*

Arriving in Rostov the author felt the city's emptiness, no longer the holy place that had drawn him in earlier years. He prayed Maariv with the local Temimim in the Rebbe's former home, where the absence of the tzaddik cried out from every corner. On Friday, the yahrzeit, he went to the Ohel and carried out the Rebbe Rayatz's instructions precisely, remaining in Rostov for Shabbos before returning home.
