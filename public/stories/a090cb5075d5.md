# Alter Rebbe arrested and taken to St. Petersburg

*1798*

At the beginning of 5559 (autumn 1798) the Alter Rebbe was arrested following the mitnagdim's denunciation and immediately sent to the capital for questioning, while interrogations of the Vilna chassidim arrested with him began in Vilna itself. Some were later sent toward St. Petersburg but recalled to Vilna en route.
