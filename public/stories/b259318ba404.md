# Soviet OVIR refuses to extend Chabad delegation's visas

*1991*

Their attorney Lada and patron Veronika (Deborah Rachel) consulted with Soviet officials Yegorov, Ivanov, Babushkin, and Tolstikov, but found no solution. The delegation decided to pay the fine, retrieve their passports, and seek extensions piecemeal — a setback that came as the visa office itself faced impending closure.
