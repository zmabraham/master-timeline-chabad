# R' Yisrael Poizner brings Baal Shem Tov kettle to the Rebbe

*1970*

R' Yisrael Poizner had purchased the small kettle in Samarkand in 5726 from a member of Anash, who said it was among the vessels left by R' Chaim Kavash and was used by the Baal Shem Tov. In 5729 the kettle was smuggled across the Soviet border to Eretz Yisrael via R' Avraham Leib Godin of Riga, and around 5730 Poizner presented it to the Rebbe with an accompanying letter of provenance.
