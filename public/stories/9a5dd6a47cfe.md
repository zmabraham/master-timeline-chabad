# R. Mushulam Zalman Neimark moves from Horki to Starodub rabbinate

*1862*

After serving in Horki from approximately 5610, R. Mushulam Zalman Neimark transferred to Starodub around 5622, where he served until about 5646. He was succeeded in Horki by the Chasid R. Chaim Shimon Dov Zivov, author of Shivim Temarim. He later also served in Vitebsk and Nevel.
