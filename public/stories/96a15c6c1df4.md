# Rebbe Rashab writes to R. Shabsai Shaftil seeking Alter Rebbe manuscripts

*1886*

In letter 555 the Rebbe expressed his intense longing for the writings of the Alter Rebbe and asked Shaftil to update him on their status. The fate of these particular manuscripts remained unknown; a year later Shaftil sent him a manuscript of the Mitteler Rebbe instead.
