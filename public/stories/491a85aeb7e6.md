# Levin's sister Freida traced through Soviet bureau

*1943*

Hearing in summer 5703 that a Soviet office had opened to trace people lost since the war began, Levin's wife inquired about his family and learned that his sister Freida was in a camp near Stalingrad. Sharing the surname Levin, she presented herself as Freida's sister-in-law and obtained her travel papers. Freida joined them in Samarkand and helped run the household weaving operation.
