# Imperial provincial decision permits Chassidim of Vilna to pray as they wish

*1798*

Following the complaint submitted by R' Yehuda ben Eliyahu against the elected committee of five, the cherem, and the looting of his home, the provincial authorities ruled in the Chassidim's favor. On 26 April 1798 the government publicly announced that the Chassidim of Vilna were free to pray as they pleased and that no fines or physical punishments were to be levied against them. The ruling briefly checked the wave of persecution.
