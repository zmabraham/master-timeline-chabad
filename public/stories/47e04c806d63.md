# Jerusalem Spinka Haggadah with Imrei Yosef and Chakal Yitzchak commentaries

*1964*

In 5724 (1964), a Haggadah following the Spinka rite was published in Jerusalem featuring the 'Imrei Yosef' commentary by Rebbe Yosef Meir Weiss of Spinka and the 'Chakal Yitzchak' commentary by Rebbe Yitzchak Aizik Weiss of Spinka. The 239-page octavo edition served the Spinka Chassidic community. It transmitted the teachings of the Carpathian dynasty to the Israeli Spinka community.
