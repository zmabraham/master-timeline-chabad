# Pekuam letter names new Hebron Kollel leadership

*1865*

This is the latest surviving Pekuam letter to the Hebron leadership. The three named men headed the community administration; R' Dov Ber ben Naftali Hirsch may have been R' Dover of Lobki, registered in the 5626 census as a member of R' Shimon Menashe's beit din.
