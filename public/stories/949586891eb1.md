# Death of R' Chaim ben R' Moshe m'Shklov, first Ashkenazi shochet of Jerusalem

*1839*

The chevra kadisha records of Jerusalem give the death date of R' Chaim ben R' Moshe m'Shklov as Rosh Chodesh Tammuz 5599 (not 5591, as one secondary source has it). He served as the first shochet for the Ashkenazi community of Jerusalem. The author uses this correct date to demonstrate that he could not have been the father of R' Yosef Yehuda Leib m'Shyuf, who had already signed as 'son of the late' two days before this date.
