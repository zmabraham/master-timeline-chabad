# Soviet Jewish agricultural-colony resettlement project launched

*1924*

Yevsektzia leader Semyon Dimenstein had advocated this in conferences as early as 1918. Authorities relocated Jewish families, sometimes forcibly, in the hope of 'productizing' Jews into farmers. Before they ended their support in 1938, the JDC invested $16 million ($238 million today) in helping the Communists move Jews to agricultural settlements.
