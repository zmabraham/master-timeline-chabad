# Spektor acknowledges donations from Zekharia Lifshitz for fire victims

*1887*

On 24 Elul 5647, days before the new year, Spektor wrote from Kovno acknowledging the money Lifshitz contributed and gathered for those left destitute by fire. He invoked the blessing of the perishing upon Lifshitz and offered an effusive year-end wish for him and his family to be inscribed and sealed for good life and abundant divine favor.
