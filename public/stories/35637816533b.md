# English-language 'V'Higadta L'Vincha' Chabad Haggadah translated by Moshe Miller

*1995*

In 5755 (1995), Moshe Miller's English translation of Yekutiel Green's Chabad practical guide Haggadah 'V'Higadta L'Vincha' was published. The 334-page octavo edition expanded access to Chabad Pesach teaching for English-speaking Jews worldwide. It served as a definitive English Chabad Pesach resource.
