# Alter Rebbe arrested and sent from Lozna to Petersburg

*1800*

At the beginning of winter 5561, the Alter Rebbe was arrested for the second time on charges connected to his leadership of the Chassidic movement. On 4 Kislev 5561 (November 21, 1800) he was transferred from Lozna to the imperial capital of Saint Petersburg. His arrival there was confirmed roughly a week later in correspondence between the Russian general procurator and the governor of Belarus.
