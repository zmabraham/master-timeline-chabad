# Yechezkel Feigin reports first-year progress of Vilna yeshiva

*1928*

In a letter to R' Israel Jacobson dated 3 Iyar 5688, R' Yechezkel Feigin (secretary to the Rebbe Rayatz) reported that the Vilna Tomchei Tmimim had reached 28 students in its first year and that more were expected to join in the upcoming summer term.
