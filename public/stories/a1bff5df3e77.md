# Death of R. Manish Verovlanski; Dov Ber Efrat appointed as ba'al ha'adres

*1902*

Following Verovlanski's death the kollel's leadership convened unanimously on Friday and chose R. Dov Ber Efrat as his successor. By tradition the appointment required approval from the network of overseas overseers, so confirmation by telegram was sought before printing new envelopes. R. Yehuda Leib Menuchin, the kollel bookkeeper, wrote that the appointment had been by full consensus.
