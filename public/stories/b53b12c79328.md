# Vitebsk cheder show trial halted by 5,000 protesters

*1921*

Five thousand local Jews came out shouting to stop the trial and threatening to close the cinema where it was being held, while thousands more assembled in local synagogues. Uneasy Yevsektzia members called off the trial after physical threats, but they regrouped soon after. A second trial was held in which the cheder was sentenced to be liquidated.
