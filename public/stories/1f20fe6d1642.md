# Shabbat Teshuvah 5693 spent stranded at Rioni train station

*1932*

Rumors of an imminent crackdown caused the mashpia to send the author and Shlomo Matusov to Baku, but they could not stay there. Returning on a delayed train they arrived at the Rioni junction just before sunset on erev Shabbat Teshuvah and could not travel the last quarter-hour leg to Kutaisi. They guarded their belongings, davened, and made kiddush on borrowed bread at the station until motzaei Shabbat.
