# Second wave of Jewish expulsions from Russian villages begins

*1822*

In 5582 (1822) the second wave of expulsions of Jews from villages began, displacing families across the Mogilev and Vitebsk provinces and leaving them without homes or means of livelihood. The crisis prompted the Lubavitch fair assembly of 1824, in which Chassidic leaders coordinated a province-wide relief fund and Petersburg lobbying effort under the guidance of the Mitteler Rebbe.
