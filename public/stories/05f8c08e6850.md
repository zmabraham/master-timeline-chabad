# Zhitomir 'Seder HaTefilah' with Chabad Chassidic discourses by Alter Rebbe

*1863*

In 5623-5624 (1863-1864), a Seder HaTefilah containing all annual prayers according to the Arizal nusach was printed in Zhitomir, accompanied by Divrei Elokim Chayim Chassidic discourses by the Chabad Alter Rebbe Schneur Zalman. The two-volume quarto edition came to a total of 310 leaves. It became a foundational printed Siddur for the Chabad community.
