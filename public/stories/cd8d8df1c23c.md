# Approbation of Maharil printed in Tehillim, Shklov

*1816*

Rabbi Yehuda Leib of Yanovich (Maharil), the Alter Rebbe's brother, was author of She'erit Yehudah. Among his approbations is one to a Tehillim with anthologized commentaries and Zohar selections, printed in Shklov around 1816. He wrote that he was lending his name 'to strengthen those who hold fast to G-d's Torah.'
