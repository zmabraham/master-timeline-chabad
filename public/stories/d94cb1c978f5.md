# Yisrael Azriel Feldman aids Anash refugees in Steyer DP camp

*1947*

Feldman, a former Tomchei Temimim Warsaw student who had fled to Russia and then to the Steyer DP camp, was serving as the camp's rav and av beit din. He built a mikvah and arranged an oven for baking matzot at the highest standard, helping Anash including R' Moshe Zalman Kaminetzky, R' Elimelech Perman, R' Zalman Teibel and R' Menachem Mendel Seitz. He noted that he and Pinchas Klein were the only Tomchei Temimim talmidim in the camp.
