# Rebbe Rayatz replies with welcome blessing to Reitchik in Vilna

*1940*

Replying from Riga on 25 Adar I 5700 (Igros Kodesh vol. 14, letter 5261), the Rebbe Rayatz acknowledged Reitchik's safe arrival in Tomchei Tmimim, blessed him, and reminded him to follow the directives in the Rebbe's general letter to the students - to succeed in learning and become a G-d-fearing chossid and lamdan.
