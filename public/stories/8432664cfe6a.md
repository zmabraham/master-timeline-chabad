# Yosef Yitzchak begins formal education

*1884*

In 1884, when Yosef Yitzchak was four years old, he began his formal education in cheder. On his first day, his father and uncle Rabbi Zalman Aharon celebrated by tossing candies that they said came from the angel Mikhael, continuing a family tradition from the Tzemach Tzedek. His teacher, Rabbi Yekusiel Aizik, was a master educator descended from Chasidim of the Alter Rebbe, known for his enthusiastic storytelling about the Baal Shem Tov.
