# Attorney Kremer petitions for future Rebbe's US visa

*1940*

Kremer, a member of the Agudas Chabad executive, formally requested Butler's assistance in securing a US visa for Rabbi Mendel Schneerson 'as a member of the leadership of the movement.' This began over a year of intensive correspondence between Chabad's New York lawyers and the State Department to extract the future Rebbe and Rebbetzin from Nazi-controlled Europe.
