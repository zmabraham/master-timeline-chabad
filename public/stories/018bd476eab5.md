# Rebbe Rashab summarizes failed JCA Paris campaign

*1901*

The Rebbe described how Baron Ginzburg had retained the JCA committee's full confidence, dismissing the rabbinic protest as 'Chasidism,' and how JCA president Leven had broken his promise to consult Polyakov independently. Although the committee acknowledged the schools' teachers were harmful, they refused to act on the rabbis' protest and merely forwarded the complaints to Petersburg. The Rebbe resolved to convene an assembly of Russian rabbis to escalate the effort.
