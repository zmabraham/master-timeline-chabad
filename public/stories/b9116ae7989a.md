# Rabbi Feigin appeals to US Chasidim for fundraising help

*1929*

Feigin explained that historic sources of income in Russia were nonexistent in Riga. He described the Rebbe's spiritual suffering: 'There are few anash here. When the Rebbe says a maamar, there are not many who appreciate it.' He noted the Rebbe was burdened by many debts and was unable to establish a yeshivah due to lack of resources.
