# Reports on 24 Tevet (Alter Rebbe yahrzeit) observances

*1938*

R. Yehoshua Isaac Baruch wrote to the Rebbe Rayatz that he had dispatched 26 letters to 24 communities urging hisva'adus and limud Chassidus on 24 Tevet, including double letters to Ponevezh (for both shtiblach) and Skopishok. He cited responses describing observances in Anikst, Traskun, Salok, Yanove, Mazheik, Vizun, and other towns.
