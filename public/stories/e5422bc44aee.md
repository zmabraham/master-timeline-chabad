# Tishrei 5696 holiday minyanim in Moscow under Soviet surveillance

*1935*

On Hoshana Rabbah and Simchat Torah, Israel Yehuda traveled to the Marina Roshcha synagogue, where the sharp-minded R' Mendel Leib Yaakobson unexpectedly joined the hakafot. Fearing arrest, on Shmini Atzeret the group gathered at the home of a friendly Lithuanian Jew situated directly next to the Butyrka prison, holding a heartfelt farbrengen with R' Melech Kaplan while their imprisoned brethren sat just meters away.
