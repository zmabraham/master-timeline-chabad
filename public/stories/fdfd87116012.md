# Count Orlov writes to Governor Golitsyn about lifting Tzemach Tzedek surveillance

*1847*

On 20 January 1847 (15 Shvat 5607), Count Orlov, who had succeeded Benckendorff as head of the Third Section, wrote to Prince A.M. Golitsyn, Governor-General of Vitebsk, Mogilev, and Smolensk. He noted that Schneersohn lived quietly, was uninvolved in conflicts, and had received the title of Honorary Citizen. Orlov asked Golitsyn for his opinion on whether the surveillance imposed in 1841 could now be lifted.
