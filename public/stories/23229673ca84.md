# Library staff identify 3,000 books from Schneersohn collection

*1992*

In 5752 (1992), librarians at the Russian State Library sorted through the Polyakov collection and identified clear markings on 3,000 volumes as belonging to the Schneersohn library — 700 in the main building and 2,300 in the Khimki warehouse. These were consolidated on the 11th floor of the main building. The Chabad emissary was not permitted to participate, leaving the identification incomplete.
