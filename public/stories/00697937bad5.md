# Author smuggles shemurah flour by train from Georgia to Kiev

*1933*

He boarded the train carrying flour that was shemurah from the time of cutting, a stricter standard than what was available in Kiev. The flour bundle was searched multiple times during the multi-day journey but he reached Kiev safely. The matzot baked from his flour were divided among all ten bochurim of the Kiev yeshiva for the sedarim.
