# Rebbe outlines master plan for an open scholarly library

*1928*

After Liberman's own emigration from Russia in early summer 5688, the Rebbe shared his vision: rather than a closed private collection, the library would be opened to researchers like the seminary or British Museum libraries. He envisioned modest annual book purchases over about fifteen years, eventual cataloging, and a dedicated building — perhaps named for a wealthy donor — to house the future thousands of volumes.
