# Rayatz's final visit to Lubavitch

*1922*

In 1922, Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn made his final pilgrimage to the town of Lubavitch to pray at the graves of the Lubavitcher Rebbes interred there. The visit came years after his father had left during World War I, and the town was no longer the spiritual capital it had once been. This was the last visit by a Lubavitcher Rebbe to the historic town.
