# Simchat Torah farbrengen of Rebbe Rashab in Small Hall, 5670

*1909*

Because the Great Hall was closed, the Simchat Torah daytime farbrengen took place in the crowded Small Hall, with the Rebbe Rashab entering through a door from the Maharash's adjoining room. He spoke at length and wept while recalling his father's Simchat Torah and 19 Kislev. Crushing pressure broke tables and benches; his elderly mother Rebbetzin Rivkah and his wife Rebbetzin Sterna Sarah came to plead with him not to overexert himself.
