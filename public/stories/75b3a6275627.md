# Rafael Cohen elected rabbi of Riga chassidic community

*1935*

On Motzaei Shabbos Parshas Pekudei, R. Faigin had written to the Rashag (Chadakov) conveying the Rebbe's instruction that anash should work and campaign for Cohen's appointment. He was confirmed in the position in Adar Sheni 5695, with Chief Rabbi R. Menachem Mendel Zak requesting his official authorization to perform marriages.
