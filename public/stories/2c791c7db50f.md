# Rebbe uses the third amud at 770 by Tishrei 5712

*1951*

In the early years of his nesius the Rebbe refused a special chair and amud, sitting on the regular bench at the regular table for tefillah on weekdays. By Tishrei 5712 a low slanted amud had been built in the southeast corner for Shabbos Shemoneh Esreh. R' Moshe Herlig recalled that on Yom Kippur of that year the Rebbe brought his bundle of pidyonos and asked Herlig to guard the amud's lid against anyone opening it during the break.
