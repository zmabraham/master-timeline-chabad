# First negotiating day at the Lenin Library

*1988*

Through Armand Hammer's Moscow office head Nina Vlasova and Deputy Culture Minister Litvinov, the delegation gained a 90-minute meeting at the library. Diakonova rejected their request to search alongside library staff, insisting outsiders had never been permitted into the holdings, and asked them to hand over their lists for internal review. The delegation supplied 12 representative titles and pressed for a reversal.
