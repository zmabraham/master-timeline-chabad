# Tzemach Tzedek portrait on the Remigalski Riga calendar

*1928*

In 5689 (1928-29), the Remigalski publishing house in Riga produced a yearly calendar with the Tzemach Tzedek's portrait on the title page; a copy is in the Chabad library. The same image was distributed as a New Year greeting card, reflecting how widely circulated the Tzemach Tzedek's image had become by the interwar period.
