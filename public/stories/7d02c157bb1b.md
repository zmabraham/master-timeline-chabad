# HaChavatzelet supplement names Kollel Chabad trustees

*1889*

Issue 19's supplement carried the signature 'Yitzchak Yaakov Shabtai and B.M. Reizes, trustees of Kollel Chabad in the Holy City.' Although Sharion had not remembered the names of the trustees of his era, this published notice preserved them. Soon thereafter Michel Baruch Reizes would emerge as senior trustee.
