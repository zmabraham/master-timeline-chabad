# Chabad delegation meets US Ambassador Strauss in Moscow over library

*1991*

Strauss recounted that the library had been raised in every meeting Secretary of State Baker held with Soviet officials, and that he himself had pressed the Soviet Foreign Minister at a Shabbat luncheon by telling him the matter stood at the top of President Bush's priorities. The Foreign Minister promised to study the issue within 24 hours, and inquiries were already coming in to the court from the Soviet Foreign Ministry. Rabbi Kunin urged Strauss to push Gorbachev and Yeltsin for a definitive presidential order to return the books immediately.
