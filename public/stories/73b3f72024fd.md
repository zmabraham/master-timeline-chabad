# Rebbe Rayatz in New York describes plight of European yeshivot

*1940*

Writing in Yiddish (Igrot Kodesh vol. 12, no. 4362), the Rebbe described how part of his students were already in Vilna while others remained in Poland and had to be brought across the border. The letter served as a discreet appeal for help in continuing the rescue effort from the safety of America.
