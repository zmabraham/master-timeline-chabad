# Rebbe moves from Warsaw to Otwock

*1935*

The sixth Lubavitcher Rebbe, Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn, had lived in Warsaw from Elul 5693 (1933). In early Tammuz 5695 (July 1935) he moved with his household and library to the smaller town of Otwock outside Warsaw, where he would remain for about four years until the end of 5699 (1939). The library was relocated under his oral direction since he was personally present in Warsaw at the time.
