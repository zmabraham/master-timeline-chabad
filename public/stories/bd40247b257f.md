# Haggadah 'Yismach Yisrael' of Alexander Chassidut reprinted in Bnei Brak

*1993*

In 5753 (1993), the Haggadah with the 'Yismach Yisrael' commentary by Rabbi Yerachmiel Yisrael Yitzchak of Alexander was reissued in Bnei Brak with many added materials. The 176-page octavo edition served the Alexander Chassidic community and admirers of their teachings. It demonstrated the continued vitality of classical Polish Chassidic Haggadah commentary.
