# Rebbe Rayatz asks U.S. government to allow plane travel

*1946*

On 3 Nissan 5706 (Igrot Kodesh vol. 9, letter 2883), the Frierdiker Rebbe wrote to R. Yosef Fleischer, a Chabad askan in Chicago, that he had written to the U.S. government as president of Chabad chassidim, requesting the Secretaries of the Interior and War to grant the students permission to travel on a government airplane, since they were needed for teaching and ruling for the Chabad organization. He noted that the matter had to remain confidential.
