# Rebbe responds to R' Alter on resuming Toras Emes leadership

*1929*

Replying to R' Alter's 14 Tevet letter, the Rebbe gave thanks for his safe arrival and prayed that Hashem strengthen his health so he could diligently lead the Toras Emes yeshiva in Jerusalem. He assumed by then that R' Alter had already re-entered his duties at the yeshiva.
