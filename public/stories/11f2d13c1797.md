# Yeltsin publicly demands Gorbachev's resignation on Russian television

*1991*

On Tuesday, in a major escalation of tensions between the USSR and Russian Federation governments, Yeltsin publicly called for Gorbachev to step down. The demand came in an all-Russian television interview ahead of a referendum on the balance of powers between the union and federation governments. The political turbulence created uncertainty for delegations working with Soviet officials.
