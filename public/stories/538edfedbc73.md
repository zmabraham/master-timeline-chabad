# Author arrives in the United States aboard the SS Sobieski

*1948*

Knowing he would be at sea for Purim, the author bought a kosher Megillas Esther in Paris and read it aboard the ship. The American visa that brought him over came not from Tomchei Tmimim but from Telz Yeshiva of Cleveland — one of the six 'professor' visas allotted to Telz was redirected to the 'Lubavitcher,' thanks to the intervention of Freidel Stein (née Sacks), niece of his wife and wife of Telz mashgiach R' Chaim Stein.
