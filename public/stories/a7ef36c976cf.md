# VAAD conference convenes; rabbis press Soviet officials on Schneersohn library

*1991*

On 26 Adar 5751, the Chabad delegation joined Chief Rabbi Eliyahu and other rabbinic guests at the VAAD conference luncheon, seated beside religious-affairs chairman Kristopanov and his deputy Moldaviewicz. The rabbis raised the urgent question of returning the Schneersohn library while Rabbi Kogan continued the case in Russian. Soviet librarian Kozlova later told the delegation she had still received no instruction to show them the collection at Khimki.
