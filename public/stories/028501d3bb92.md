# Shalom Dovber Levine appointed director of the Chabad Library

*1977*

In 5737 (1977) Rabbi Shalom Dovber Levine was appointed director of the library of Agudas Chasidei Chabad at 770. From that year onward he began publishing serialized articles drawn from the library's collections — first in Yagdil Torah and later in Kfar Chabad, Heoros U'Biurim, Heichal HaBesht and other journals. These articles later formed the basis of his book MiBeit HaGenazim.
