# Tomchei Tmimim funds Magen Avot students in Hebron

*1911*

The accompanying receipt template had each student in Hebron acknowledge that his stipend came 'from the association Tomchei Tmimim of Lubavitch, under the leadership of the Rebbe.' Each student signed individually. The disbursements covered Tammuz and Menachem Av 5670, marking a concrete step in establishing Lubavitch's institutional presence in the Land of Israel before the founding of Torat Emet.
