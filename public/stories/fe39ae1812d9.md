# 19-20 Kislev farbrengens across Riga's Chabad minyanim

*1936*

R. Eliyahu Chaim Altheuse reported the festivities in detail: a newly received account of the arrest and liberation in colloquial language was read aloud in each minyan, the Shas was divided for the year, and chazaras maamarim and divrei hisorerus extended deep into the night. The Rebbe's minyan at Kurmanava 20/01 functioned as the central chassidic hub.
