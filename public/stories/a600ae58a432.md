# Russian Federation approves Poliakov Synagogue for Chabad

*1990*

On 20 Kislev 5751 the Council of Ministers of the Russian Federation approved giving Chabad the Poliakov Synagogue, a handsome two-story building in central Moscow. The delegation toured the building and was deeply impressed, although it remained temporarily occupied by another office. Local leaders planned to relocate the yeshiva and Tzeirei Agudas Chabad activities there from the Marina Roshcha synagogue.
