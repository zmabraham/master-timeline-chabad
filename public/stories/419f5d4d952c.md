# 'Emet MeEretz Titzmach' clears Jerusalem officials and invites public claims

*1914*

After nearly a month of daily review, the delegates announced they had examined every ledger of income from every place and individual, and found all properly recorded with matching orders, talons and receipts. They had posted notices inviting anyone with a complaint to come forward — even offering a reward to whoever could prove a missing share — and received only generic complaints about small allowances rather than evidence of misappropriation. They concluded with full confidence in the integrity of the kollel officials Slonim, Schlank and Naeh.
