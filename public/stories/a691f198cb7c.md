# Birth of the Tzemach Tzedek

*1789*

The Tzemach Tzedek, third Chabad Rebbe and author of monumental halachic and Chassidic works, was born on Erev Rosh Hashanah 5550 (September 19, 1789) to R' Shalom Shachna and Devorah Leah (daughter of the Alter Rebbe). He was raised by his grandfather the Alter Rebbe after his mother's early death.
