# Chasidim arrive in Eretz Yisrael, settle in Tzfat

*1777*

When the early Chasidic aliyah reached the Holy Land in Elul 5537, the group settled initially in Tzfat, not Tiberias. This is corrected by the author against a folk tradition that placed the arrival in Tiberias. The Tiberias community was only established several years later.
