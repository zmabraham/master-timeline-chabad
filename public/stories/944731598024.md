# Rebbe Rayatz announces opening of Tomchei Tmimim Warsaw

*1922*

The letter (Igrot Kodesh vol. 14, letter 5136) explained that the Warsaw branch had been founded on the suggestion of the Rebbe's son-in-law. R' Alter Simchowitz, who had previously directed the Shchedrin branch, was being dispatched to run it. The opening marked the formal re-establishment of Lubavitch yeshiva infrastructure outside Soviet Russia.
