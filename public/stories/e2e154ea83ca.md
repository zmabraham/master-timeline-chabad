# Khovansky orders Mohilev court to send case file to the Senate with his appeal

*1827*

On 31 May 1827 (Rosh Chodesh Sivan 5587), writing from Kaluga, Khovansky directed the first department of the Mohilev higher court to ship the 16-page case file (no. 553) along with his official opinion to the ruling Senate. This formally placed the acquittal of Rabbi DovBer in the hands of the highest judicial body of the Russian Empire.
