# Levin's group reaches UNRRA camp at Rehau

*1946*

At dawn the guide ordered them onto the road; Itta Devorah ran ahead and reached the UNRRA gate first on 7 Elul 5706 (Parshat Ki Seitzei). Once inside they were protected from being returned to Czechoslovakia by UNRRA jurisdiction. An MP jeep — driven by the same officers from the earlier turn-back — passed moments later but could not enter UNRRA territory. The next day a Jewish woman ran in reporting that 150 refugees just behind them had been seized by MPs.
