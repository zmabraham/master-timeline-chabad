# Moscow officials decide to return Polyakov synagogue to Chabad

*1991*

After friendly meetings with Mr. Svastianov of the Moscow Soviet and behind-the-scenes work by Natalia and Veronika, the Moscow city authorities resolved to return the Polyakov synagogue to Chabad. Rabbi Shloime Kunin reported on erev Shabbos 28 Nissan that the decision had been signed. The decision came despite intense competing pressure from the Reform community, who had brought press and television hoping to take possession of the building, and despite a subsequent court challenge by the tenant organization claiming it was not obligated to vacate.
