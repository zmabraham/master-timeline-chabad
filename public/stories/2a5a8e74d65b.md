# Rebbe Rashab appoints R. Menachem Mendel Naeh as Kollel administrator

*1912*

On 16 Elul 5672 (Aug/Sept 1912), the Rebbe Rashab wrote letter 341 (vol. II) asking R. Menachem Mendel Naeh, an experienced accountant who had managed Kollel finances for years, to formally accept the title of administrator. Naeh had previously refused the title in 5669 but agreed to participate informally. He now accepted out of deference to the Rebbe.
