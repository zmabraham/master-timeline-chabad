# Tzemach Tzedek responsa and Piskei Dinim series completed

*1884*

After 13 years of editorial work and printing, the series was completed in 5644. Newly discovered teshuvos and chiddushim were appended to the Piskei Dinim volume. The publishers gave the entire series the name 'Tzemach Tzedek' (gematria of Menachem Mendel), and from this point the Rebbe himself was named after his book. Choshen Mishpat responsa were blocked by Russian censors who opposed rabbinic adjudication.
