# First edition of Tanya printed in Slavita

*1796*

Tradition suggests that the partners R' Shalom Shachna (the Alter Rebbe's son-in-law) and R' Mordechai HaLevi Horowitz jointly printed the first edition of Tanya in Slavita. They remained occupied with that print shop throughout their lives. This edition is the foundational text of Chabad Chasidic teaching.
