# R' Shlomo Zalman HaKohen begins third shadar mission

*1789*

At the end of summer 5549 R' Shlomo Zalman HaKohen left the Land of Israel as shadar for the third time. His authorization letter was signed by 21 chassidim from Tiberias, Tzfat, and Pekiin. This mission lasted three full years before he returned home.
