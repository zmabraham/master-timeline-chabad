# Rebbe's first publication: Luach 5703 in English

*1942*

After settling in New York, the Rebbe was appointed by his father-in-law to head Machne Israel, Merkos L'Inyonei Chinuch, and Kehot. His first published work, in 1942/43, was an English-language Luach for 5703 with laws, customs and information for young students.
