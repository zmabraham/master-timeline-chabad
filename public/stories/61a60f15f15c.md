# Tzemach Tzedek tours Borisov, Minsk, Korenitz, Vilna and Polotsk

*1835*

This summer journey was unusually broad, taking the Tzemach Tzedek into Lithuania proper, including Minsk, Vilna, and Polotsk. According to the report of Captain Yurkovsky six years later, during this stay in Minsk the Rebbe delivered public sermons and explained passages from Kabbalah and the Talmud. The trip is reconstructed from the headings of his maamarim.
