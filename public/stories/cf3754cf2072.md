# Birth of R' Yochanan Gordon in Dokshitz

*1894*

R' Yochanan Gordon, future longtime gabbai of 770 Eastern Parkway, was born in the town of Dokshitz to his father R' Yisrael and mother Esther. He recorded his birthdate of 23 Iyar 5654 himself on a 'Sefer HaChassidim' registration card filled out 17 Tevet 5723. He would go on to study at Tomchei Tmimim in Lubavitch and become a central figure in Chabad communal life in America.
