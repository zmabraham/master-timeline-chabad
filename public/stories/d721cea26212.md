# Author's first entry into the Rashab's chamber

*1907*

On Tuesday, 3 Cheshvan 5668 (1907), the young Yisrael Jacobson entered the holy chamber of the Rebbe Rashab in Lubavitch for the first time. Fifty years later, on the corresponding date in 5718, he marked the anniversary by entering the chamber of the Rebbe (Rayatz's successor). It was at that 5718 yechidus that he was directed to begin writing his memoirs.
