# Rebbe Rashab visits the graves of his ancestors before departing Lubavitch

*1915*

On Thursday, 13 Marcheshvan 5676, the Rebbe Rashab traveled to the resting places of the previous Chabad Rebbes in Lubavitch. He remained there for roughly four hours and returned with eyes red from weeping, traces of which were still visible the following day. That night he told the Rayatz of his hope that the German army would not reach Lubavitch.
