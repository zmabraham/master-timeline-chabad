# Russian government certification of Lubavitch rabbinic appointment

*1916*

More than a year after the original 23 Av 5675 Hebrew rabbinical appointment letter for R. Elchanan Yakubovitz was drafted, the document received Russian civil certification on 2 September 1916. The Lubavitch starosta endorsed it with his seal alongside R. Dovid Yakobson's signature attesting that the 40 Hebrew signatures were genuine. The delay points to the dislocations of the wartime evacuation period.
