# Death of Rabbi Yehuda ibn Attar of Fez

*1733*

Rabbi Yehuda ibn Attar passed away in the Hebrew year 5493 (1733), having served as the leading rabbinical authority of Fez and one of the most revered figures of Moroccan Jewry. Honored by Moroccan Jews as 'Arbi Alkabir' (Our Great Rabbi), he left behind a rich corpus of responsa and a network of correspondence with Rabbi Yaakov ibn Tzur of Meknes.
