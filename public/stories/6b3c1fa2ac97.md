# Ramash delivers first maamar, accepting the Chabad leadership

*1951*

A year after the Rayatz's passing, Chasidim packed 770 in Brooklyn for the yahrzeit farbrengen, eager to see whether the Ramash would accept leadership. After he spoke on ahavat Yisrael, the elder chasid Rabbi Sender Nemtzov rose and called out for chasidut. The Ramash then delivered his first maamar, 'Basi Legani,' built on his father-in-law's final teaching, marking the start of his tenure as seventh Rebbe.
