# Simchas Torah 5673 Rayatz farbrengen with the Tzarske familye remark

*1912*

On Simchas Torah the Rayatz farbrenged in the same apartment, in the room of R. Nachman the meshares (then occupied by R. Shlomo Leib Eliezerov). R. Elchanan Dov Morozov made a witty barb at R. Shilem, who quoted "sent forth his hand against him." The Rayatz responded by calling them "tzarske familye" (royal family), and someone added in Russian: "He will lie many klaftern deep, but tzarske familye — don't touch the royal family."
