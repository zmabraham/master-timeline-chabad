# Feigin weighs Norwegian vs Swedish ship options

*1940*

On 3 Adar Sheni 5700, Feigin wrote again about logistics. A Norwegian ship would sail from Bergen April 5–8, while a Swedish ship would depart Gothenburg April 13. The Swedish option would mean spending the start of Pesach aboard ship, which he hoped to avoid, especially given his wife's frail health.
