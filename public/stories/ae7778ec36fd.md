# Tzemach Tzedek travels to Shklov and Tzarei

*1833*

Shklov was historically a stronghold of the Misnagdic camp, making the Rebbe's visit there significant for Chabad–Misnagdic relations. The trip is documented through the dated headings of maamarim he delivered in those towns. It forms part of the chronological registry of his discourses reconstructed from manuscript sources.
