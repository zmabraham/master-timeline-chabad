# Beit Din ruling on Ibn Saadon vs. Ibn Danan debt in Fez

*1722*

The Ibn Saadon brothers had sold ostrich feathers to R' Shlomo ibn Danan on credit and were never fully paid before his death. His son R' Shmuel claimed his father had paid more than recorded and that non-Jewish creditors had seized all the inherited land. R' Yehuda ibn Attar ruled that the creditors could collect from the 'almasriya' still in the heir's courtyard, subject to mutual oaths.
