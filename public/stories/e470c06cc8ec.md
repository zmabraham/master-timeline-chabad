# Ephraim Shmuel opens large cheder in Lipovets

*1920*

The new apartment was rented from the widow of the melamed Reb Gedalyahu Shmuel. Ephraim Shmuel taught Chumash with Rashi to a large group of children, and Leah Beila assisted by teaching them Hebrew writing and arithmetic. This was the largest cheder in Lipovets.
