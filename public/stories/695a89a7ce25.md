# Grigory Lulov executed by Supreme Soviet verdict

*1940*

Born in Riga in 1899, Lulov had joined the Communist party in 1916 and the Cheka in 1919. He received the Order of Lenin and an 'Honorary Worker of the Cheka-GPU' badge in 1937. Arrested at the end of 1938, he was executed on January 21, 1940, during Stalin's purges of the security organs.
