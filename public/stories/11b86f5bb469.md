# Rebbe Rayatz responds to R. Nissan Horowitz on Tzemach Tzedek learning

*1941*

Responding to a detailed letter from mashpia R. Nissan Horowitz dated 22 Sivan, the Rebbe expressed great pleasure at the description of Chassidus study at the yeshiva. He blessed the students that their learning should bear fruit in heartfelt service through prayer. The exchange shows the close ongoing supervision the Rebbe Rayatz maintained over the Jerusalem yeshiva.
