# R. Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk writes from Tzfat to chassidim of Vilna

*1778*

The letter calls on the Vilna chassidim as witnesses to his earlier attempts to reconcile and explain the chassidic position. It is among the foundational documents of the chassidic side of the early conflict, paralleling the Alter Rebbe's later epistles on the same theme.
