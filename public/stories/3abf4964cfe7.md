# Molotov radio broadcast announces Nazi invasion of USSR

*1941*

On Sunday at noon, while the author was sitting at home in Moscow doing portrait retouching work, Foreign Minister Vyacheslav Molotov broadcast news that Hitler had begun invading the Soviet Union and had already captured many cities. The announcement upended life for Soviet Jewry instantly — portrait commissions evaporated and within days the Luftwaffe began nightly raids over Moscow, forcing residents into trench shelters dug in their courtyards. Refugees soon poured eastward in masses, and the author would shortly join them.
