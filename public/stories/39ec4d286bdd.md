# Rebbe Rashab stays at Slavyansk healing waters

*1918*

Amid the Russian Civil War, when travel was nearly impossible, the Rebbe made the one known journey of the period to the Slavyansk spa, arriving the eve of 9 Tammuz 5678 and remaining through 10 Elul. He wrote of overcrowded train cars, the unavailability of seat reservations, and dread about the return trip.
