# Rebbe Rayatz writes coordinated letters blessing the Latvia Chassidus committee

*1930*

On 4 Nisan 5690 the Rebbe Rayatz wrote a coordinated set of letters: to R. Chaim Mordechai Aizik Chodakov (later his personal secretary) who chaired the Riga committee, to R. Shneur Zalman Yitzchak Wolshonok, to Anash of Riga, and to the General Committee for Expanding Chassidus Study in Latvia. He thanked them for the news that additional batei midrash had opened Chassidus shiurim and proposed that during Chol HaMoed Pesach all of the city's study groups convene in one shul to review a discourse together, strengthen mutual love, and inspire the younger generation.
