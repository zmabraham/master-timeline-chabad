# Levitin sent as shadar to America

*1938*

In Elul 5698 (1938), R. Shmuel Levitin was sent by the Rebbe Rayatz to America as a shadar. When his primary mission concluded in the spring of 5699, he asked whether to return home to Rakishok, but the Rebbe instructed him to remain in America - which ultimately spared him from the Holocaust.
