# Rayatz writes to Agudath Israel about home schooling

*1923*

After the Rabbinical Committee blocked a 1923 proposal by the People's Commiserate of Justice to close loopholes permitting religious home instruction, the Rebbe acknowledged the partial victory. Despite the formal ruling allowing private religious instruction for up to six children, local authorities continued to harass teachers. The letter is preserved in Igrot Kodesh.
