# Asara b'Tevet diplomacy day for Chabad mission in Moscow

*1990*

On 10 Tevet 5751 the delegation met Moscow deputy mayor Muzykantsky regarding restitution of the Polyakov Synagogue (still partly occupied by a choir); the US ambassador Maxwell at his residence; American philanthropist Tom Ferris on medical aid; and Israeli consular officials to finalize the next-morning Chernobyl children's airlift. News came that Likhachov had spoken with Gorbachev and Supreme Soviet chairman Lukyanov to recommend return of the books and that Lushchenkov was preparing to issue a binding decision.
