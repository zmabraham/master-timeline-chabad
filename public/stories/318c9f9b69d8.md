# US Embassy in Marseille confirms passage from Lisbon to New York

*1941*

After months of effort, the American Embassy in Marseille notified the Schneersohns on March 20, 1941 (21 Adar 5701) that its Lisbon office had arranged their passage from Lisbon to New York no later than July 15, 1941. An advance payment of 840 US dollars was required and the cable was transmitted from Lisbon four days later on March 24.
