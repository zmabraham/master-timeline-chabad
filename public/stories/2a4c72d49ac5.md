# Rebbe Rayatz dates Tzemach Tzedek manuscript

*1909*

In Kuntres Etz HaChaim the Rashab had cited the inscription with uncertainty about which Rebbe had written it. In his Teves 5670 letter he reconsidered and concluded it was more likely the Tzemach Tzedek's hand. The manuscript was later catalogued as item 29 in the Rayatz's collection but was lost during World War II along with many other bichlach.
