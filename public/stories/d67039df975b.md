# R' Meshulam Zalman Neumark receives recommendation as rabbi of Horki

*1860*

Dated 15 Teves 5620, the joint letter from Maharil of Kopust, R' Chaim Shneur Zalman of Liadi, and R' Yisroel Noach of Niezhin praised R' Meshulam Zalman as 'a renowned moreh tzedek both in psak and in chassidus' and urged the community to retain him with a strengthened salary.
