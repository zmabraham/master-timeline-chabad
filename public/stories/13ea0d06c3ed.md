# Assassination of Soviet ambassador Pyotr Voykov in Warsaw

*1927*

In early June 1927, a Russian monarchist assassinated Soviet ambassador to Poland Pyotr Voykov at the Warsaw train station, citing Voykov's role in killing Czar Nicholas. Stalin responded with mass executions of former nobility and broader extrajudicial GPU powers. The Yevesektzia seized this atmosphere of repression to move against the Rebbe.
