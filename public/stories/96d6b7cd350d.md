# Illustrated Abarbanel Haggadah printed in Amsterdam by Shlomo Proops

*1712*

In 5472 (1712), Shlomo Proops issued a richly illustrated Haggadah in Amsterdam with commentaries by Don Isaac Abarbanel, Ma'aseh Hashem, Mateh Aharon, and Chevel Bnei Yehudah, alongside abridgments of other commentators. The work included an engraved map showing all the Israelite wanderings in the desert, etched on copper plates. The 31-leaf folio became an iconic illustrated edition of the era.
