# Rebbe Rayatz visits Vilna yeshiva late at night

*1932*

At the conclusion of his Vilna visit in early Elul 5692, the Rebbe Rayatz expressed his desire to visit the yeshiva at a late hour. Finding the students still learning at long tables with open gemaros, the Rebbe declared 'Hakol kol Yaakov - I wish to hear the sound of Torah.' The bochurim resumed learning aloud as the Rebbe walked among the tables gazing at each student before bestowing his blessing.
