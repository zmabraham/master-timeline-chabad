# Soviet August Coup against Gorbachev begins in Moscow

*1991*

Walking out into the streets the diarist found people debating on every corner along Tverskaya, a demonstration of one to two thousand at Manezhnaya Square, and Red Square sealed off by guards. A friendly policeman assured Chabad's new shul was being protected and that all was calm, but reports were coming in that tanks were on the main thoroughfares and rumors circulated that Gorbachev had been killed. The diarist sat writing calmly in the Lubavitch Synagogue office as the August Coup unfolded around him.
