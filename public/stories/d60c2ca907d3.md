# Three Shanghai students arrive by plane

*1946*

As reported in HaPardes (Tammuz 5706), three students arrived by plane from Shanghai a few weeks earlier: Avraham Yitzchak Garfinkel, Nachum Preger, and Mordechai Meir Brziski of Chmielnik. The Frierdiker Rebbe's efforts to bring all the students by airplane had not succeeded; only two or three made the trip by air while the rest traveled by U.S. military ship.
