# Second arrest at Malakhovka shul with Shimshon Charitonov

*1935*

After Pesach 5695 the author resumed sleeping in the Malakhovka shul together with Shimshon Charitonov. In a midnight raid, detectives shone flashlights and burst through the door, taking both bochurim to the Lyubertsy police. At dawn the author quickly donned tefillin and prayed, then persuaded the police chief—through humor and a misfiled prior pledge—to release them. R. Yaakov Maskalik later comforted him by recounting the Tzemach Tzedek's blessing of 'mice' that ate incriminating court papers.
