# First edition of Likkutei Torah printed in Zhitomir

*1848*

The Likkutei Torah collected the Alter Rebbe's chassidic discourses on the latter three Chumashim and Shir HaShirim. Two of the Tzemach Tzedek's sons — the future Maharil of Kopust and R' Chaim Shneur Zalman — funded and oversaw the first printing in Zhitomir in תר"ח, with their names appearing on the title page and in the haskamos.
