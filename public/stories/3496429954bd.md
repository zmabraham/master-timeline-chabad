# Ladino Haggadah 'Hamon Chogeg' by Elisha Chabilyo printed in Livorno

*1794*

In 5554 (1794), Elisha Chabilyo authored 'Sefer Hamon Chogeg' which was printed in Livorno, Italy. The work presented the text of the Haggadah alongside a Ladino translation, with two Hebrew commentaries — one based on the plain meaning and the other on ethical instruction. The Hebrew original and Ladino translation were printed in vocalized square letters, passage by passage.
