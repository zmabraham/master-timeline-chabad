# Altheuse reports on Yud-Tes Kislev to Faigin

*1936*

Altheuse described the Lubavitcher Rebbe's minyan as 'der Lubavitcher Rebbe's minyan' - the central chassidic gathering place where shiurim in Chassidus were learned daily, where one could obtain a kuntres, kosher tefillin straps, or Lubavitcher tzitzis, and where the name 'Lubavitch' was constantly on every mispallel's lips.
