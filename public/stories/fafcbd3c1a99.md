# Rebbe Rashab letter on plans to send books to Nizhny

*1915*

On 27 Tishrei 5676 (October 5, 1915), the Rebbe Rashab wrote to R' Shmuel Michel Trainin that he might travel from Lubavitch within a few weeks with his entire household. He planned to send the books, which were numerous, for safekeeping and could not delay until the last moment. His initial plan was to send them to Nizhny, though they ultimately went to Moscow.
