# Passing of R' Yochanan Gordon

*1969*

R' Yochanan Gordon, the longtime gabbai of 770 Eastern Parkway and a fixture of Chabad communal institutions in New York, passed away on Erev Rosh Chodesh Elul 5729. After his death, leadership of the Shomrei Shabbos gemach he had directed alone since 5714 passed to his son-in-law R' Shimon Goldman, who continued its work for many years thereafter.
