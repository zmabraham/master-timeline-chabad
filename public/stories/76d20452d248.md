# R' Menachem Aryeh arrives in Hebron from Rohatchov

*1832*

According to the 1839 Hebron census, R' Menachem Aryeh (also called Nachum Aryeh Leib) made aliyah from Rohatchov in 5592 (1832) at age 40. He came together with his already-married 16-year-old son R' Yisrael Yosef. He later served as administrator of the Chabad kollel, dayan, and head of yeshiva, signing the 1834 Tzemach Tzedek correspondence and other communal documents.
