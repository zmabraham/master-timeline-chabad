# Rabbi Jacobson arrives back in New York to launch the rescue campaign

*1939*

Jacobson had missed his original passage on the Queen Mary and crossed the Atlantic in a converted swimming-pool locker aboard an overcrowded ship, leading Rosh HaShanah services on deck. Back in New York he enlisted attorney Samuel Kramer and called Asher Rabinowitz late at night, dispatching him to Washington to find a way to contact the Rebbe — refusing to let Shabbat or Yom Kippur deter the mission.
