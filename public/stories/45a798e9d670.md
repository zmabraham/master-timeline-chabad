# Rebbe instructs R' Alter to train students to speak chassidus publicly

*1923*

The Rebbe wrote that even shy students must be pushed to overcome their reluctance to speak, since training them this way would yield great benefit over time. He closed with strong encouragement, invoking his late father's spirit and reminding Alter that the work of spreading Torah is done 'not by might and not by power, but by knowing Him.'
