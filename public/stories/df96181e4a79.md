# Rashag writes Tomchei Tmimim students about affidavits sent for US visas

*1941*

Rashag (Rabbi Shmaryahu Gourary) wrote on 17 Sivan 5701 (June 12, 1941) instructing the Kobe students to tell the consul that Agudas Chassidei Chabad guaranteed responsibility for them and that the affidavits were secondary. He listed by name the affidavits already sent for individual students and elder refugees, including telegraphed affidavits for Bornstein and Feder.
