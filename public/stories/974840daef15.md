# Sefer HaTmimim first edition published

*1989*

At the beginning of 5750 (late 1989), the first edition of Sefer HaTmimim was published, recording students of the Lubavitch yeshiva Tomchei Tmimim. Shortly afterward additional names were uncovered from a 5670 Vaad Agudas HaTmimim membership roster, leading to a second expanded edition published in 5751 that included the previously missing names.
