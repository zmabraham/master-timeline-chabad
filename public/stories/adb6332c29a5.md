# Rashab visits gravesites of chasidic forebears

*1896*

In the summer of 1896, troubled by the spiritual state of Russian Jewry, the Rashab traveled to Mezibuzh, Anipoli, Hadyach, and Nizhyn to pray at the graves of the Baal Shem Tov, the Maggid of Mezeritch, the Alter Rebbe, and the Mitteler Rebbe. Upon his return he prayed at the graves of his grandfather and father before establishing Tomchei Temimim.
