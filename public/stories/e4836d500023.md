# Rashab seeks medical care in Moscow

*1896*

In 1896, the Rashab was gravely ill and traveled with his wife to Moscow for medical care. Doctors gave him a bleak prognosis of just months to live. He suggested to his wife they travel to Eretz Yisrael to spend his last days there, saying their sixteen-year-old son could lead Chabad in his place. Rebbetzin Shterna Sara prevailed on him to remain in Lubavitch, and he ultimately recovered, living another twenty-four years.
