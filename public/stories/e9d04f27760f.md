# Rayatz's St. Petersburg arrest and death sentence

*1927*

In 1927, Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn was arrested in St. Petersburg and held at the notorious Shpalerka Prison, where Soviet authorities sentenced him to death for his religious activities. World Jewry mounted significant international pressure on the Soviet government, ultimately securing his release. This seventh and final arrest marked the climax of his confrontations with anti-religious authorities in Russia.
