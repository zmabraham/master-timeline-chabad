# R. Berchen reports founding Livani branch

*1940*

In an Erev Shavuos 5700 letter R. Berchen described how, after toil and travail, they finally succeeded in founding the small yeshiva in Livani, staffed by Tzvi Cohen and Shalom Ber Asherov who had relocated there. A planned Preili branch failed for lack of a teacher willing to relocate, and the Riga vaad had functionally collapsed. He confirmed receipt of $50 toward Livani.
