# Death of R' Chanoch Hendel Kugel

*1900*

R' Chanoch Hendel Kugel, who served as mashpia for the Lubavitch branch of Tomchei Tmimim from its founding, passed away on 21 Tevet 5660 (1900). His role was filled in the summer of 5660 by R' Shmuel Betzalel (Rashbatz) Sheftel as chief mashpia. The Rebbe Rashab was at the medicinal springs in Kharkov at the time and corresponded with his son about the appointment.
