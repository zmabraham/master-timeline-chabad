# Rebbe sends R. Refael Stein to Vilna region

*1932*

Sent on a mission to strengthen Torah and Yiddishkeit in the Vilna region, Stein traveled first to Poretz to address the mikveh there. The Rebbe wrote to him on 8 Menachem Av 5692 (Igros Kodesh vol. 11, letter 3882) expressing great delight at the success of the mikveh effort in Poretz.
