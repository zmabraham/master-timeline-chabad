# R' Mordechai Mentlik dispatched to Krakow for outreach work

*1933*

On 14 Menachem Av 5693 the Rebbe wrote to Rosh Yeshiva R' Yehuda Eber proposing such a mission, offering to fund up to 100 zloty, and noting that the local circle led by R' Michael Zeligson studied Chassidus twice weekly. By 23 Av the Rebbe had settled on Mentlik and authorized an expanded budget of 125 zloty. The students later recalled this mission in a 18 Elul 5701 letter from Shanghai, when one of the avreichim from that Krakow circle resurfaced as a refugee in the Far East.
