# German military unit locates the Rebbe and delivers exit visas

*1939*

After weeks of searching ruined Warsaw, Bloch's soldiers — several of Jewish background — entered the apartment as the Rebbe's grandson Berke and others braced for execution. Instead, the soldiers handed out exit visas and travel papers. The mission had been authorized at the highest levels of the Abwehr under Admiral Wilhelm Canaris, working through Helmuth Wohlthat in Goering's office.
