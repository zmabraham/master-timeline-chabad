# Shavuot celebrated joyfully at Ponevezh Chabad minyan

*1940*

R. Shmuel Tzvi Lisson reported to the Frierdiker Rebbe that the chassidim learned the maamar 'Va'ehyeh Etzlo Amun' from Likutei Torah on the first day and held a festive meal with dancing on the second. The Soviet occupation had not yet extinguished chassidic joy.
