# Koegan and Cunin travel to Leningrad to meet Ligachev

*1991*

Because Gubenko claimed Ligachev had retracted his support for returning the Chabad library, the delegation decided over Shabbat to send Rabbis Koegan and Cunin by overnight train to Leningrad. They met Ligachev at his vacation site about ninety minutes from Leningrad, received a strong new letter of support, and prepared to return that night on the train.
