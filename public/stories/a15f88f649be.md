# Birth of Yisrael Yehuda Levin in Zhivotov

*1912*

Levin was born to Ephraim Shmuel ben Yosef and Leah Beila (daughter of the Dashov shochet Yisrael Bilenki) in the small town of Zhivotov in Vinnitsa province. His memoir, written across 35 years beginning around 1960, would document his life under Soviet rule and his eventual escape. The town was a religious community where his father served as a melamed.
