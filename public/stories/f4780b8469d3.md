# Bnei Brak Haggadah 'Birkat Moshe' of Lelov Chassidut

*1991*

In 5751 (1991), Simcha Krakowski compiled and published in Bnei Brak the Haggadah 'Birkat Moshe' according to the customs and teachings of the Lelov Rebbes, with material from Rabbi Moshe Mordechai Biderman of Lelov and Rabbi Shimon Natan Nata Biderman. The 148-page octavo edition combined teachings, Pesach customs, and conduct guides. It served the Lelov community and admirers of their distinct Chassidic path.
