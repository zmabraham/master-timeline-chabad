# Vilna yeshiva committee appeals to Vaad HaYeshivot for aid

*1929*

A letter from the Vilna Tomchei Tmimim committee, preserved in the Vaad HaYeshivot file at the YIVO archives, described the yeshiva's dire situation in the Apatow Shul where many poor children were being supported. The committee emphasized that despite the yeshiva's chassidic name, students of all orientations learned there, appealing for funds.
