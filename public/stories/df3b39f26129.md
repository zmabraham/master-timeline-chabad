# Rashab purchases courtyard property at Bratsky Pereulok 44 in Rostov

*1918*

After two years in the Pushkinsky apartment, in 5678 the Rashab moved to buy a substantial building — roughly 16 by 32 meters, two stories with a basement — at Bratsky Pereulok 44. The purchase fell during the chaotic onset of the Russian Civil War and Bolshevik nationalization of property; the down payment of 50,000 was made in January, and the Rashab wrote on 28 Adar that confirmation of the deed and the renovations were uncertain. The deal was nonetheless completed, and by the end of that summer the family moved in, with public prayers held there from Tishrei 5679 onward.
