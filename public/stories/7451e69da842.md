# Tzemach Tzedek travels to Bayov, Rogachev, Dubrovna and Smiljan

*1837*

This summer journey began with a wedding in Bayov, continued through Rogachev to Dubrovna and Smiljan, and ended with a return to Dubrovna. The route is reconstructed from the dated headings of maamarim that the Rebbe delivered along the way. It exemplifies how the registry of his discourses preserves not only Torah content but also family events and biographical detail.
