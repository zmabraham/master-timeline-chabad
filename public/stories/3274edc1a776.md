# Final Soviet approval to ship 66 book crates to Riga

*1927*

Just before the Rebbe's departure, on 5 October 1927, customs and foreign-trade representatives issued the conclusive export authorization covering 66 crates of books and household items destined for Riga. The document noted that the books had been examined and approved for shipment by foreign-ministry officials, completing the long bureaucratic battle to bring the library out of the Soviet Union.
