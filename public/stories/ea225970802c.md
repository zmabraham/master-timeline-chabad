# Hemshech 5666 typesetting begins by Rebbe's instruction

*1965*

On the night of 2 Nisan 5725 (April 4, 1965) Rabbi Chadakov phoned to relay that the Rebbe, on returning from the Ohel, ordered typesetting of the Hemshech Yom Tov shel Rosh Hashanah Tarsa"v to begin immediately and that the most corrected manuscript be sought. Reb Baruch Sholom Shvei went to the press that very night and began setting the type. On the following motzaei Acharon Shel Pesach the Rebbe gave the author a bottle of wine 'for the Samach-Vov.'
