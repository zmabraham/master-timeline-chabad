# ken united. The Association of Descendants published an impressive calendar. M

*1892*

ken united. The Association of Descendants published an impressive calendar. Hundreds of Chabad chassidim ascended to the Rebbetzin's resting place in Hebron. The Association of Descendants of the Alter Rebbe calls to restore the guard. Continued article in issue 1892, and in issue 1893 in the 'Letters to the Editor' section page 23
