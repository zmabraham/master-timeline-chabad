# Rayatz and R' Chaim Liberman arrested in Leningrad

*1927*

On the eve of 15 Sivan 5687 (14-15 June 1927), the Sixth Lubavitcher Rebbe (Rayatz) and his secretary R' Chaim Liberman were both arrested in Leningrad. After some time in the Leningrad prison, they were sent into exile separately—the Rebbe to Kostroma and Liberman to Tambov.
