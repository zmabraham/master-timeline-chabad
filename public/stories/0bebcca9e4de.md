# Lubavitch's last synagogue closed by Soviets

*1936*

In 1936, Communist authorities shuttered the last operating synagogue in the town of Lubavitch, ending centuries of organized Jewish religious life there. The closure followed the passing of Rabbi Yosef Leib Beriya, a scholar respected by Jews and non-Jews alike, who had maintained the synagogue. This marked the end of Lubavitch as a functioning Jewish community under Soviet rule.
