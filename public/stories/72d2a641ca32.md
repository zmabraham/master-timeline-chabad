# R' Yissachar Ber Horowitz studies with MaharI"L in Yanovich

*1812*

R' Yissachar Ber Horowitz, who later served ten years as rabbi of Lubavitch, recorded that he heard a particular teaching from MaharI"L 'while studying with him in 5572.' This testimony fixes the earliest documented year for MaharI"L's residence in Yanovich, where he served as moreh tzedek.
