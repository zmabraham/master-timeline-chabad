# Booklet 'Kiyum Nefesh M'Yisrael' printed in Riga

*1939*

The Rebbe noted to his son-in-law on 7 Tevet 5699 that 'the Achos Tmimim institution in Riga requested permission to print the letter that begins 'all who sustain one Jewish soul' - I have surely sent him a copy - and I granted them permission.' The original letter had been addressed to Mr. Elchanan Cohen of New York.
