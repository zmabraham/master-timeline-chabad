# Rabbi Gidi Sharon - Rabbi Gideon (Gidi) Sharon (born 5711

*1950*

Rabbi Gidi Sharon - Rabbi Gideon (Gidi) Sharon (born 5711, 1950) is a Chabad chassid who operates the website Machon HaRebbe - Otzaro Shel Moshiach, which was the first to make broad portions of the Rebbe's teachings accessible to the public via the internet
