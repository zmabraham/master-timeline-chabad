# Wedding of Maharil of Kapust, son of the Tzemach Tzedek

*1824*

The Mitteler Rebbe recorded delivering a discourse 'at the wedding of my grandson Yehuda Leib, 14 Cheshvan 5585.' Rabbi Shlomo Freides, a major chronicler of the Alter Rebbe's teachings, presented forty volumes of his transcripts as the dowry. The Tzemach Tzedek subsequently annotated and expanded these manuscripts, though most were later lost in the great Lubavitch fire.
