# American students bid farewell to the Rebbe and flee Otwock for Latvia

*1939*

After the American Consul urged them to leave, the boys stopped at the Rebbe's home before departing. The Rebbe, already in silk Shabbat garments, told them to head to neutral Latvia and asked Meir Greenberg to tell Mordechai Dubin to have the Latvian government request a permit for the Rebbe and family. When asked about possibly desecrating Shabbat, the Rebbe replied that the merit of the Baal Shem Tov and Rabbi Schneur Zalman — both born on 18 Elul — would suffice that they would not need to transgress.
