# Wedding of Chaim Meir Yechiel in Sadigura

*1880*

Rabbi Avraham Yaakov of Sadigura, son of the Ruzhiner, sent a circular letter from Sadigura in 5640 announcing the wedding of his grandson Chaim Meir Yechiel — son of his son-in-law Rabbi Aviezri Zelig — to Bat-Sheva, daughter of his nephew Rabbi Yitzchak of Bohush. The letter was carried by emissaries Reb Gedalia the shochet and Reb Chaim Kwashis of Kamenetz, who invited chasidim to attend.
