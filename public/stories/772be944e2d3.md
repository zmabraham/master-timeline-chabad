# Rabbi Menachem Sirero, son-in-law of the Yaavetz, dies young

*1718*

Rabbi Menachem Sirero, son-in-law of the Yaavetz, passed away in Cheshvan 5479 (autumn 1718). Born in 5453, he was only about twenty-six. The Yaavetz had inscribed Sirero's marriage to his daughter Azi Bueno in his own hand on 8 Adar in the family marriage-records register.
