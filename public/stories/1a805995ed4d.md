# Benjamin Cohen and Robert Pell strategize the Rebbe's rescue

*1939*

Cohen impressed on Pell the Rebbe's standing as 'one of the leading Jewish scholars of the world' and suggested approaching Helmuth Wohlthat, a German diplomat Pell had befriended at the 1938 Evian Conference. The same day Pell telegrammed US consul Raymond Geist in Berlin, asking him to discreetly enlist Wohlthat in arranging safe passage for the Rebbe to Riga.
