# Rashab founds Torat Emet yeshiva as independent Chabad institution

*1912*

Until then Kollel Chabad had served as the overarching organization for all Chabad activity in the Holy Land, with private institutions operating under its supervision. The Rashab broke this pattern by founding Torat Emet as an independent yeshiva, marking the beginning of a new institutional model for Chabad in Eretz Yisrael.
