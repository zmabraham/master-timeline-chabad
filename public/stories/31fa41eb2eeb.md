# Informer denounces Alter Rebbe to Russian authorities

*1798*

On 4 Sivan 5558 (8 May 1798), an informer from Vilna submitted a denunciation to the Russian authorities accusing Rabbi Shneur Zalman of Liadi of organizing a conspiracy, gathering rebellious youth, supporting the French Revolution, and recommending exile to Siberia. This denunciation initiated the legal case against the Alter Rebbe.
