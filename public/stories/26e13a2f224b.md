# Maharash sells chametz to R. Avraham Zeligsohn

*1874*

A document from 5634 (1874) preserves the Rebbe Maharash's sale of chametz to R. Avraham b. Yosef Zeligsohn, who served as moreh tzedek of Lubavitch throughout the Maharash era. The document is reproduced in Tikunei Mikvaot Le-Takanat Rabbeinu, p. 185.
