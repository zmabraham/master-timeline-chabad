# R. Yechiel Tzvi Hirsch Gurary settles in Warsaw

*1925*

R. Yechiel Tzvi Hirsch Gurary, son of R. Natan Gurary and son-in-law of the Radzyn Rebbe R. Mordechai Yosef Eliezer Leiner, left Soviet Russia in 5685 (1925). Together with his mother Esther and his younger brother Shneur Zalman, he settled in Warsaw. Immediately upon arrival he was appointed to the management board of the yeshiva, joining R. Shraga Feivish Zalmanov and R. Shneur Zalman Shmotkin at the head of Warsaw Chabad.
