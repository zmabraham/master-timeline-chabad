# Rashag meets Chief Rabbi of France in Paris

*1928*

Rashag's Paris mission included a meeting with French Chief Rabbi Israel Levi and his secretary Moshe Lieber. The following day he met Mr. Warnser, personal secretary to Baron Edmond Rothschild, as well as Mr. Bigart of the Alliance Israélite Universelle and Mr. Unger of JCA, who pledged participation in the work.
