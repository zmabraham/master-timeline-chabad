# Author's first yechidus with the Rebbe Rashab

*1913*

In the winter of 5673 the author entered his first yechidus with the Rebbe Rashab, marking his entry among the bochurim devoted to inner avodah. He had prepared with intense Krias Shema al hamita and committed to at least six hours of Chassidus study daily plus extended tefillah. The Rebbe instructed him to daven b'peirush hamilos and to connect the themes of Pesukei D'Zimra and Birchos Yotzer, which would open the heart.
