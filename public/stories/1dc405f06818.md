*1966*

Rabbi Binyamin Lerer Rabbi Binyamin Lerer (born 5727, 1966) is one of the rabbis of the Chabad community in Beitar Illit, rabbi of the Chabad community in the Hadekel neighborhood and rabbi of the 'Zvehil' synagogue in the city, and rabbi of the 'Africa Israel' investment group
