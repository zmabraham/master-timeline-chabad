# Moshe Leib Rotstein marries Kayla Chanovich in Globoke

*1924*

In 5684 (1924), R. Moshe Leib Rotstein — born Zusya Shifrin in Russia around 5660 — married Kayla, daughter of R. Yisrael Chanovich, the shochet of Globoke. Rotstein had fled Russia after WWI and the Revolution, adopting a forged identity. His new father-in-law passed away the following year.
