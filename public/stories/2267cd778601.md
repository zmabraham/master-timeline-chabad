# R. Manish Verovlanski appointed ba'al ha'adres by the Rebbe Rashab

*1900*

The Rebbe Rashab wrote to R. Mendel Slonim on 3 Menachem Av 5660 expressing his preference that funds be sent to Verovlanski because this would establish proper order. The Rebbe Rashab later wrote that Verovlanski's appointment came by his own selection. Verovlanski served only three years before his death.
