# Levin's group reaches Prague; Rebbe sends R. Yisroel Jacobson

*1946*

After a polite border check by Czech police, the Warsaw–Prague express reached Prague on Wednesday, 17 Av 5706. R. Yehuda Chitrik told Levin the verse 'I bore you on eagles' wings' was being fulfilled. The Frierdiker Rebbe had dispatched R. Yisroel Jacobson from America specifically to receive them and arrange their onward journey.
