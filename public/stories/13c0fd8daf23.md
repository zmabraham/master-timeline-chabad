# Mandelbaum appointed rabbi of Globoke

*1935*

Following Kuperstock's aliyah and the appeal of the Globoke chassidim on 4 Kislev 5695, the Rebbe Rayatz sent Mandelbaum - a Tomchei Tmimim alumnus and head of the Lodz branch - to take over the Globoke rabbinate. He led weekly chassidus farbrengens, Tanya lessons, and oral discourses for the community.
