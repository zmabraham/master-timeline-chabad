# Wedding of Rebbe Menachem Mendel Schneerson and Chaya Mushka in Warsaw

*1928*

The chuppah was held in the courtyard of the Tomchei Tmimim yeshiva in Warsaw. The groom's father R. Levi Yitzchak Schneersohn, in Soviet Russia, was denied a travel permit and could not attend. The Rebbe's uncle R. Moshe Hornstein and aunt served as unterfirer on the groom's side in place of the father.
