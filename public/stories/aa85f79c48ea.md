# Rabbinic Vaad on Jewish education convenes in Russia

*1843*

The maskilim had proposed the conference to pressure rabbinic leaders into agreeing to open Haskalah schools for Jewish youth. Initially the Tzemach Tzedek declined to attend due to ill health following the 5601 search, but it was ultimately decided that his presence was crucial to prevent harmful resolutions. Following the conference the Russian government issued him a certificate of honored citizenship for his descendants.
