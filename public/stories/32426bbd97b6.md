# Judeo-Arabic Haggadah 'Chukat HaPesach' printed in Livorno

*1859*

In 5619 (1859), 'Chukat HaPesach' was published in Livorno by Agudat Ezov, organized into three sections containing legal novellae, the Haggadah with Seder laws in Egyptian-dialect Judeo-Arabic, and a new commentary on Pirkei Avot called 'Avot u'Gevurot'. The 202-page volume also included readings for Rosh Chodesh Nissan, the offerings of the tribal princes, and the burning of leaven. It served the Arabic-speaking Sephardic communities of North Africa.
