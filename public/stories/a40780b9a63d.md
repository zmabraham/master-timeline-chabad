# WWI forces Torat Emet shluchim back to Russia

*1914*

When World War I began in 5674 (1914), the mashpia and the student shluchim who had been sent from Lubavitch to staff Yeshivat Torat Emet in Hebron were forced to return to Russia. The yeshiva's original Hebron operation effectively shut down. The institution would later be reopened in Jerusalem, where it continues to operate today.
