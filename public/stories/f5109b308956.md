# R. Mordechai Leizer Lapatovsky visits Kiev yeshiva for Sukkot 5694

*1933*

R. Mordechai Leizer Lapatovsky, brother-in-law of R. Yona Cohen and R. Eli Chaim Roitblat, traveled from Zhitomir to Kiev to spend Sukkot with the underground yeshiva. He farbrenged with the bochurim in a spirit of mesirus nefesh and shared eyewitness stories of avodah from Lubavitch.
