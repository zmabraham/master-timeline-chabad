# Passing of R' Shlomo Zalman HaKohen in Tiberias

*1799*

On 10 Adar II 5559 R' Shlomo Zalman HaKohen passed away in Tiberias. He had served the chassidic communities of the Holy Land on three lengthy shadar missions over more than two decades. His son R' Yitzchak later took up emissary duty in 5571.
