# Luach Birkat HaNehenin published in Shklov

*1800*

This first printing of the Luach Birkat HaNehenin appeared in 5560 (1800) in Shklov, well before the full Shulchan Aruch reached press. It transmitted an early recension of the Alter Rebbe's halachot of blessings on food and remained a separate booklet alongside later expanded versions.
