# Librarian Chaim Lieberman escapes Warsaw to Riga

*1939*

On 8 Tishrei 5700 (September 21, 1939), librarian R. Chaim Lieberman left Warsaw for Riga with only a summer coat. He later recounted that all his possessions remained behind, including manuscripts of the Rebbe Maharash he had hoped to organize and effects of the Rebbe Rayatz entrusted to him. A suitcase he tried to forward via Zalmanov demanded an exorbitant ransom and was abandoned.
