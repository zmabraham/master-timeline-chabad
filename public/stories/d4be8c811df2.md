# New opportunity emerges to ship Schneersohn library to USA

*1940*

On 3 Sivan, R' Chefetz reported to the Rebbe Rayatz that R' Yisroel Yaakov Ever had received word from Vilna that Aharon Blum needed an additional 500 sefarim of Rashi (i.e., dollars) to renew the shipment. The Rebbe authorized payment, and R' Ever wrote urgently to Blum to dispatch the library at once.
