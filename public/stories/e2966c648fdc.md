# Poalei Tzion attacks Tomchei Temimim

*1906*

On March 22, 1906, Marxist Zionist Poalei Tzion members marched into Tomchei Temimim demanding the release of teenage student Berel Davzik. Three days later they returned with weapons, threw stones, and fired bullets at the yeshivah, narrowly missing Rabbi Moshe Klatzkin of Romanov. Students drove them off, but the Rayatz forbade reprisal.
