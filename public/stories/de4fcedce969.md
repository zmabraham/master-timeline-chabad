# Starodub Jews remain imprisoned for two years

*1881*

The arrested Starodub Jews remained imprisoned for two years (5641-5643). The Rebbe Maharash sent a short letter of encouragement to Neimark in prison, dispatched his secretary R. Levi Yitzchak Idelevitz to Starodub to assess the situation, and corresponded in code with the prisoners. Activists in Riga, Velizh, Starodub and Kiev coordinated to retain top defense attorneys in Kiev.
