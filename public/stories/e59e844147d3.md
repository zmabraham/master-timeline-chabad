# R' Meir Refael's elected to the Vilna kahal

*1790*

Despite his Chassidic affiliation, R' Meir Refael's was elected to the Vilna kahal in 5550. In this early period there was no strong opposition in Vilna to the Chassidic kehilla, and the Chassidic minyan operated with the approval of the kahal head R' Moshe ben Asher.
