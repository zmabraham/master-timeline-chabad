# Rebbe's blessing for the international Chabad Shluchim Conference

*1992*

The brief reply confirmed both the date and the structure that Lazar, Aharonov and the diarist had been drafting through Tevet — a women's evening on the night of 22 Shevat, then back-to-back shluchim, shluchos and rabbinical conferences. With the Rebbe's go-ahead, organizers moved into invitations and logistics.
