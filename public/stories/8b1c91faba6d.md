# Levin family flees Zhivotov for Lipovets

*1920*

The roads were thick with mud, forcing the family to repeatedly dismount and help push the wagon. They traveled several days, sleeping at the wagon-driver's village home along the way. All six members of the family arrived safely in Lipovets days before Passover 1920, where Rabbi Shlomo Sternberg and shochet Reb Levi helped them settle.
