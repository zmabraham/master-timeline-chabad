# Pogroms during the Peasants' Revolt against Hebron's Jewish community

*1834*

On 9 Iyar 5594 (May 17, 1834), Arabs in Hebron rebelled against the regional ruler, and for many days threatened the Jewish community with cruel deaths. R' Chaim Halevi, the pakid, used his standing with local nobles and significant payments to secure their protection. The pogroms were followed by an earthquake, both of which were described in a fundraising letter signed by the Chabad kollel leaders sent with shadar R' Natan Amram.
