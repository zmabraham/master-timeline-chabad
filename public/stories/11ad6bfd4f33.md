# Jaffa branch of Torat Emet officially opens

*1924*

Following months of planning and the Rebbe Rayyatz's letters from late 5683 and early 5684, the cheder finally opened in Jaffa during the summer of 1924. By Elul of that year the Rebbe was already referring to it as an established branch in a fundraising letter for Torat Emet. R' Shlomo Zalman Havlin oversaw the institution from Jerusalem, with local support led by Patash, R' Yaakov Malkov, and R' Zalman Aharon Menevitch.
