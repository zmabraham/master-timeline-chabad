# Kollel Chabad in Hebron formally established with first leadership

*1827*

These three men signed as appointees on the shadarut documents of 5587, the founding year of Kollel Chabad as a formal entity. Alongside them, R' Leib Chitrik of Sena was appointed ne'eman (treasurer/bookkeeper). They styled themselves the 'appointees of the community of Ashkenazim called Chabad in Hebron.'
