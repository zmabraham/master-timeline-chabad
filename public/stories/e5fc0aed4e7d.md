# Bichawski publishes circular defending the adres system

*1908*

Bichawski urged donors not to wait for the arrival of traveling emissaries before remitting R'Meir Baal HaNess funds. Communities should send their contributions directly several times per year, and to avoid depriving the emissary of his livelihood, pay him a percentage of the sums collected and forwarded. The circular reflected the gradual shift away from purely shadar-based fundraising.
