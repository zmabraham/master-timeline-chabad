# Rebbe Rayatz flees Otwock for Warsaw as war breaks out

*1939*

With the outbreak of World War II in Poland, on 20 or 21 Elul 5699 (around September 4, 1939) the Rebbe Rayatz evacuated Otwock together with his household and secretariat, traveling to Warsaw with the intent of continuing to Riga. Only manuscripts were taken; the entire library of roughly 120 crates and many household effects had to be left behind in Otwock. The party became trapped in the Warsaw siege for four months.
