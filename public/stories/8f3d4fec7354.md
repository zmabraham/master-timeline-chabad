# American bochurim travel to Tomchei Tmimim Otwock to study

*1938*

R' Dovber Levy, an American-born great-nephew of the Malach, had transferred to the author's circle and wanted to study in Europe; the author personally covered the shortfall in his fare. R' Aryeh Leib Gellman of Mizrachi, who sailed with them, later returned praising the bochurim's stringent yiras shomayim aboard ship, including their refusal to daven in a shul without a mechitza. The departure boosted morale among the remaining students in New York.
