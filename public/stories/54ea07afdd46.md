# Letter to Rabbi Avraham Chaim Gagin

*1846*

In 5606 R' Shmuel Halevi, maggid of Hebron, signed an official letter addressed to R' Avraham Chaim Gagin (the AGaN), Sephardi chief rabbi of Jerusalem. The letter is preserved in Migdal Oz and is one of the last documents bearing his signature before his death sometime between 5609 and 5615. It illustrates the ongoing institutional relationship between the Hebron Chabad community and the Sephardi rabbinic establishment of Jerusalem.
