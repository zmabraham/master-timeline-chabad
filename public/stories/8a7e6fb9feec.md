# Maharayatz formally accepts nesius of Kollel Chabad; appoints Eliezerov as bakoach

*1929*

The Rebbe accepted the nesius on condition that an institutional 'bakoach hanesius' — distinct from the regular officials — be installed to oversee all kollel affairs, sign in the name of the nesius, and handle all correspondence with him. He named his relative R. Shlomo Yehuda Leib Eliezerov to that position. The appointment restored the traditional structure of Chabad rebbes serving as nesi'im of the kollel that had been founded in the time of the Alter Rebbe.
