# Tomchei Tmimim branch founded in Babinovitch

*1909*

In 5669 the yeshiva opened a branch in Babinovitch to bridge the gap between the cheder-level shiurim in Lubavitch and the senior bachurim of the large zal. R. Zev Wolf Levitin supervised the nigleh studies and R. Shlomo b. David Risin oversaw Chassidus. A separate group of bachurim continued learning in Horodishtch under R. Shlomo Zalman Havlin.
