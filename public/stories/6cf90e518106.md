# Rabbi Levi Yitzchak Schneerson arrested by Soviet authorities

*1939*

Rabbi Levi Yitzchak Schneerson, kabbalist and rabbi of Yekaterinoslav and father of the seventh Rebbe, was arrested by the Soviet regime in 5699 (1939) as punishment for promoting Jewish observance. A photograph of him taken on the day of his arrest is preserved in the Lubavitch Library.
