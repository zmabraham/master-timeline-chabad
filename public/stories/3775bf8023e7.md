# Yehonatan Gershon Balter forced to leave Kobe yeshiva for Eretz Yisroel

*1941*

On 3 Sivan 5701 (May 29, 1941), Yehonatan Gershon Balter was compelled to depart Kobe by sea. The yeshiva students escorted him to the ship with brotherly farewells, and he later reported reviewing Chassidus aloud aboard the vessel. He eventually reached Tel Aviv where he joined Yeshivas Achei Tmimim.
