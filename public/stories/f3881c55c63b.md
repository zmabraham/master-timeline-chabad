# Future Rebbe and Rebbetzin reach Nice under Italian zone

*1940*

After roughly two and a half months in Vichy, it became clear that the German-subordinate Vichy regime was equally dangerous for Jews. The couple moved to Nice, where they would spend 8–9 months while the Frierdiker Rebbe coordinated intensive efforts from New York to secure their visas, travel papers, and basic provisions including matzah and an esrog for Sukkos.
