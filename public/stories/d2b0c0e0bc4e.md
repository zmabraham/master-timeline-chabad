# Rebbe Rashab advises Rosenbaum to invest lottery winnings in a house

*1906*

Continuing the conversation about Rosenbaum's lottery winnings, the Rebbe Rashab wrote from Lubavitch on 18 Cheshvan 5667 that 'in my opinion it is correct that he buy a stone house from which he will have income according to his needs.' He blessed him that God should send him a good and successful property and prosperity in body and soul.
