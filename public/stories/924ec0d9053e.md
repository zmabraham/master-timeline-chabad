# Open letter 'To Our Brethren of Israel' appeals for Polish Jewry via the Joint

*1939*

The Rebbe described soup kitchens, hospitals, old-age homes and orphanages running on Joint funding inside Warsaw, and asked Jews of every party affiliation to contribute. A parallel circular addressed to anash and Tomchei Tmimim students worldwide called the health and economic situation 'terrifying' and demanded rapid practical help.
