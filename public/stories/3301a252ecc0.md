# Letter to R. Yaakov Schechter on dire pidyon shvuyim cash crunch

*1941*

He related that the previous week he had been forced to send 5,040 dollars beyond earlier remittances, and that a fresh cable required two more ship tickets at 400 dollars. The pidyon shvuyim fund was empty, and the rescue depended on free-loan funding sourced from the Ladies Auxiliary and other supporters.
