# R' Yitzchak ben Aharon of Königsberg, donor of Ohel Yitzchak, dies

*1905*

R' Yitzchak ben Aharon, a shochet from Königsberg, together with his wife Breina, donated the funds to build the magnificent Ohel Yitzchak beit medrash in Meah Shearim, named for him. He also funded four kollel houses adjacent to it that were allotted by lottery to needy Chabad families every three years. He passed away on 15 Kislev 5666 as recorded in 'Avnei Zikaron' vol. 12.
