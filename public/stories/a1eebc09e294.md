# Recovery of Mitteler Rebbe manuscripts via Yisrael Yaffe descendants

*1888*

Tasked by the Rebbe Rashab in 1886 to find descendants of R. Yisrael Yaffe, R. Shneur Zalman Slonim located them in Eretz Yisrael after over two years of investigation. Among the recovered manuscripts were large portions of the Mitteler Rebbe's Pirush HaMilos and unpublished sections related to Amrei Binah and Shaar HaTefillin, which the Rebbe Rashab acquired.
