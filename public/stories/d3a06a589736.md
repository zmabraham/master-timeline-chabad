# Chabad delegation arrives in Moscow

*1988*

The plane arrived at 4:30 PM and the team reached the Hotel Intourist by 7. Realizing the library housing the Schneersohn collection was only a few blocks away, they walked over to inspect its three buildings—the Rumyantsev wing, the Muzei Knigi where the first books had been located, and the high-rise stacks—so they would recognize them in negotiations to come.
