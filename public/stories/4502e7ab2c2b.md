# Rayatz visits Chasid R. Chaim Dover Vilensky in Lubavitch

*1893*

The Rayatz later recalled having seen the noted Chasid R. Chaim Dover Vilensky of Kremenchug two or three times, the last being in Lubavitch in the summer of 5653 (1893). Decades later he sought to commemorate this Chasid by gathering material from Vilensky's son for the Hatamim journal.
