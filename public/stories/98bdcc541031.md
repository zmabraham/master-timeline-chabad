# Rabbi Koptchik speaking in the hall of Tomchei Tmimim Yeshiva Beit Shemesh.

*1982*

Rabbi Koptchik speaking in the hall of Tomchei Tmimim Yeshiva Beit Shemesh. Rabbi Yechiel Kopchik farbrenging in the sukkah of 770. Rabbi Yechiel Yehuda (Chilik) Kopchik (born 5742, 1982) is the director of Talmud Torah Chabad in Beit Shemesh, general mashpia at Tomchei Tmimim Yeshiva
