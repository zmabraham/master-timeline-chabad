# Rebbe Rayatz orders comprehensive alumni list

*1924*

On 18 Shevat 5684 (January 24, 1924), the Rebbe Rayatz wrote to R. Sholom Dovber Halevin requesting a comprehensive list of all yeshiva students from Tomchei Tmimim and its branches — Lubavitch, Zembin, Kovlitz, Horodishtz, Shchedrin, Romny, Cherson, Hebron, Kremenchug, Poltava, Warsaw, Kutaisi, Nevel, and Jerusalem — from 5657 to 5684. A mimeographed list was reportedly prepared in Rostov-Leningrad, though no copies have been found.
