# Vilna edition of Tanya printed by Tomchei Temimim

*1900*

The print run included 3,400 standard copies and 1,400–1,600 quality 'regal' paper copies. The cover declared the edition was issued by Tomchei Temimim, and the proceeds beyond expenses were dedicated to supporting the yeshiva's students. This was the first major scholarly edition of Tanya.
