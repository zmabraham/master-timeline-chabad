# Hebron yeshiva officially renamed 'Toras Emes'

*1911*

Following the Rashab's decision to send a contingent of avreichim to Hebron, the mashpia R. Shlomo Zalman Havlin and his group arrived in the city. The Rebbe Rayatz then wrote to him on Rosh Chodesh Kislev 5672 instructing that the yeshiva be called 'Toras Emes'. This name change reflected not only renaming but a reorganization absorbing the existing Magen Avos yeshiva.
