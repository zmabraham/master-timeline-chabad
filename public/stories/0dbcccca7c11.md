# Rashbatz appointed chief mashpia

*1900*

In the summer of 5660 (1900), following the passing of R' Chanoch Hendel Kugel, R' Shmuel Betzalel (Rashbatz) Sheftel was appointed chief mashpia at Tomchei Tmimim in Lubavitch. He had previously taught the Rebbe Rashab's son in Lubavitch from 5654 onwards. Rashbatz served until his passing on 15 Sivan 5665.
