# Rebbe Maharash travels to Switzerland for medical treatment

*1879*

On the instruction of his physicians, the Maharash departed Lubavitch in Tishrei 5640 (October 1879) and traveled westward through Europe. He stayed in Vevey until 19 Cheshvan, then moved to Montreux on Lake Geneva. In a letter to the chasid Shmuel Dovber of Borisov dated 27 Cheshvan, he reported that the climate was helping him and that he planned to continue to Biarritz before returning home via Paris.
