# Wedding of the Rebbe Rayatz to Rebbetzin Nechama Dina

*1897*

Rayatz married Rebbetzin Nechama Dina in Elul 5657 (August/September 1897) in the Large Hall of his grandfather Maharash's Lubavitch courtyard. This was the third major family simcha hosted in that hall, after Maharash's daughter Chaya Mushka's wedding and Rayatz's own bar mitzvah in 5653.
