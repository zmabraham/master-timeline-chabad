# Czernowitz Conference declares Yiddish 'glue' of Jewish life

*1908*

The Yiddish movement believed using Yiddish would be a barrier to assimilation, rejecting classic Jewish religious beliefs and observance. Many of its early schools were strongly anti-Zionist, though most later became supportive of Zionism. Yiddish schools spread across Russia, Europe, the US, and Canada, but most had closed by the early 1970s.
