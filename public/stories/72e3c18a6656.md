# Death of Rebbetzin Nechama Dina Schneersohn

*1971*

Born in Kishinev in 1881 to Rabbi Avraham Schneersohn, she had married the Rayatz in Lubavitch in 1897. As the rebbetzin of the sixth Lubavitcher Rebbe, she shared his lifetime of communal work through czarist persecution, Soviet exile, and eventual relocation to America. She passed away on the same Hebrew date as her husband, 10 Shevat.
