# Rabbi Tal Dekel. Rabbi Tal Dekel (5715, 1955 – 11 Sivan 5785,

*1955*

Rabbi Tal Dekel (5715, 1955 – 11 Sivan 5785, June 8, 2025) was active in the Rebbe's mivtzaim in Haifa, Yokneam Illit, and Migdal HaEmek, and in his later years was the Rebbe's emissary at the Goldn rehabilitation and geriatric hospital.
