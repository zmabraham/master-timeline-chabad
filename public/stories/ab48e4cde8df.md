# Reprint of Kitzurim v'Hearos al Tanya for Tzemach Tzedek bicentennial

*1989*

For the bicentennial of the Tzemach Tzedek's birth, the work was reprinted in an enlarged edition, though without the 'Gertz Press' attribution of the original. When the memoirist passed by during the Rebbe's distribution of the volume, he told the Rebbe that this was the first book he had ever printed, and the Rebbe responded with a smiling face.
