# Feigin briefs Rebbe on stalled rescue while at sea

*1940*

On Rosh Chodesh Adar Sheni 5700, with the Rebbe aboard ship bound for America, R. Yechezkel Feigin wrote detailing the obstacles to his family's travel. HIAS New York would not raise the $1,200 ceiling, and confusion arose over whether the Joint had given monetary aid or only endorsement, delaying the matter further.
