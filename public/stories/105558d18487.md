# R' Leib Menuchin letter on Tzfat yeshiva proposal

*1904*

R' Asher Yechezkel hoped to open a Chabad yeshiva in Tzfat headed by his son R' Yeshaya. R' Leib Menuchin advised the kollel's overseas administrators that the kollel's funds should not be used for this venture. The yeshiva ultimately did not come to fruition.
