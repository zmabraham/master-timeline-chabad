# Mitteler Rebbe case transferred to Babinovichi peace court

*1825*

On December 19, 1825, the case against Rabbi DovBer of Lubavitch — concerning allegedly illegal fundraising from his chassidim — was transmitted to the peace court of the town of Babinovichi for adjudication. The court would begin substantive work on the file in mid-January 1826. This routing through a local peace court was the standard procedure before the matter could rise to the Mogilev High Court for review.
