# Khovansky forwards Mitteler Rebbe case to Mogilev gubernia

*1825*

On November 17, 1825, after reviewing the investigators' report, Prince Khovansky forwarded the case against Rabbi DovBer of Lubavitch to the Mogilev gubernia administration under reference numbers 5849 and 5850. This transfer began the lengthy judicial phase, in which the matter would be routed first to the Babinovichi peace court and ultimately to the Mogilev High Court. Khovansky later cited this directive when pressing officials for updates on the case.
