# Simchat Torah 5693 farbrengen at the Liebov home in Kutaisi

*1932*

On Simchat Torah 5693 a farbrengen was held in the Liebov family home, and the weekly Shabbat minyan and learning were hosted by Rebbetzin Bas-Sheva Lipsker, mother of the Lipsker family. The hidden community continued to function despite the rising danger and persistent fears of arrest.
