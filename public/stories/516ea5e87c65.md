# Rebbe Rayatz writes Ramash about renting Gelbfish building

*1939*

On 15 Iyar 5699 (May 4, 1939), the Rebbe Rayatz wrote to his son-in-law (Ramash) about a strong possibility of renting a spacious residence in the courtyard adjacent to his own home — the Gelbfish pension with about forty rooms and large halls. The plan required a two-year-plus lease at 8,000 zloty per year paid in advance, totaling nearly 18,000 zloty, to be financed from funds collected by R. M.D. — but a few months later the war erupted and all plans came to a halt.
