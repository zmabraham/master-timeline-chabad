# Rabbi Herzog addresses refugees at Maharal's Prague synagogue

*1946*

On Sunday Parshat Re'eh, 21 Menachem Av 5706, the Chabad group returned to the Altneuschul. Rabbi Yitzchak Halevi Herzog, Chief Rabbi of Eretz Yisrael, spoke on Parshat Re'eh and called on all to immigrate to Eretz Yisrael. Litvish yeshiva talmidim Levin knew from Samarkand and other Polish refugees were also present.
