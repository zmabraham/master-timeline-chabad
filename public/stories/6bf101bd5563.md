# Agudas Chassidei Chabad founded in Poland, Lithuania, and Latvia

*1930*

For the High Holidays of 1930, Chasidim from Poland and Lithuania came to celebrate with the Rebbe in Riga. A group of twenty-one Chasidim convened in the home of the Rashag, who suggested the new organization. With the Rebbe's approval, Agudas Chassidei Chabad took on the mandate of strengthening the Chabad community across the three-country region.
