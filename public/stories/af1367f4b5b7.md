# Rebbe Rashab reorganizes Yeshivat Magen Avot

*1903*

Distressed by the near-total absence of Chassidut study in the Holy Land, particularly among younger married men, the Rebbe Rashab in 5663 personally undertook to reorganize Yeshivat Magen Avot in Hebron. He hoped this would address the spiritual gap he had observed among otherwise pious students. In a letter from Elul 5663 he explained that even ardent Torah scholars in the Land knew almost nothing of Chassidut and were utterly distant from it.
