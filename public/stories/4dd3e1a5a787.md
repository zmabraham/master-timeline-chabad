# Levin suspects librarians of forging collection markings on the books

*1993*

Levin noted that on several books the 'P' mark for Polyakov coexisted suspiciously with an 'S' for Schneerson freshly written on the card. He suspected the librarians were systematically erasing 'P' marks and adding 'S' marks to hide the books' true provenance. He urged Ashkenazi to keep checking discreetly since the Russians would block Levin himself.
