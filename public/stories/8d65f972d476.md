# R. Elchanan Yakubovitz publishes Even Shimon

*1929*

Following his evacuation from Lubavitch and a brief tenure in Russia, R. Elchanan Yakubovitz returned to Latvia in 5678 (1918). He resumed teaching as a rosh mesivta in several yeshivot and in 5689 (1929) printed the first volume of his halachic work Even Shimon, named after his father R. Avraham Shimon.
