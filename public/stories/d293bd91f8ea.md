# Tomchei Tmimim students travel to Rostov for Rosh Hashanah 5679

*1918*

The chassidim prayed in the Shaalash, a wooden structure built in the courtyard of the Rebbe Rashab's home on Bradsky Pereulok 44 in Rostov, which he had purchased after the move from Lubavitch. R' Yechiel Halpern served as chazzan. This was the first Rosh Hashanah of the Tomchei Tmimim student body following the relocation from Kremenchug for the chag.
