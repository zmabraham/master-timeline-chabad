# Mittler Rebbe urges Mordechai and Yehuda to help build a chassidic shul in their town

*1804*

Anash in one town had loaded the entire burden of building their new chassidic synagogue and managing communal affairs onto R' Yehuda Leib, leaving him no time for learning. The Mittler Rebbe asked R' Mordechai and R' Yehuda to take a share of the work, promising that the merit of the public would stand them in good stead. The undated letter belongs to the years after the 1804 statute opened the way for separate chassidic shuls.
