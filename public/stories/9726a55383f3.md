# Rebbe Rayatz farbrengs with Tomchei Tmimim students on Purim

*1928*

After the Megillah reading the night of Purim, a select group of Anash dined with the Rebbe Rayatz, who spoke of Chassidus and avodah until past midnight. The following afternoon at 3:30 he came to the yeshiva hall for the Purim seuda, with Anash seated at the tables and students standing in rows singing Chabad melodies. The farbrengen continued until 2 a.m., after which the Rebbe told a chassid in the office that he had not imagined the yeshiva in such an exalted state, calling it 'a piece of my father's work'.
