# Rebbe's response: 'Together they went, together they will return'

*1991*

Feeling sidelined from the library negotiations and immersed instead in teaching at the Moscow yeshiva, the author asked the Rebbe via Rabbi Leibel Groner whether he should return to 770. The Rebbe's reply came that night: 'Let him not separate from his fellows on this mission — together they went, together they will return when the time comes. I will mention it at the gravesite.' The answer confirmed the delegation as an indivisible unit and effectively defined the author's role as combining the library mission with sustained Torah teaching in Moscow.
