# R' Shmuel of Amdura first travels to the Alter Rebbe

*1790*

According to his own 1799 interrogation testimony, R' Shmuel of Amdura traveled to the Alter Rebbe to study from him three times: in 5550, 5554, and 5558. His extant letters also cite teachings received from the Alter Rebbe, evidencing the latter's growing role as teacher across Lithuanian chassidic communities.
