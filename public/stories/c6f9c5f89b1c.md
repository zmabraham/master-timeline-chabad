# Chabad Russian-translation Haggadah issued by FREE in New York

*1991*

In 5751 (1991), the FREE organization (Friends of Refugees of Eastern Europe) published in New York the 15th edition of a Russian-translation Haggadah. The 36-page quarto edition served the wave of Russian-speaking Jewish immigrants arriving from the collapsing Soviet Union. It exemplified the Chabad-FREE initiative to make Jewish observance accessible to Soviet Jewry.
