# Publication of Vayomer Shmuel by R' Shmuel Elbaz in Casablanca

*1929*

R' Shmuel Elbaz, son of R' Yitzchak, was the same figure who in 1717 had married Rivkah bat Shem Tov Cohen of Fes and consented to her inheritance waiver. His subsequent work Vayomer Shmuel appeared in print in Casablanca centuries later, in 1929, cementing his reputation among the rabbinic authorities of Morocco.
