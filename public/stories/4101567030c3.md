# Chabad ships Ezras Achim food packages to eight Russian cities

*1990*

On 14 Tevet R' Yosef Kogan and R' Shlomo Cunin packed Ezras Achim food parcels at Marina Roshcha for distribution to eight Russian towns. The delegation held an internal meeting with R' Moshe Chaim Levin and R' Dovber Lazar on Chabad work in Moscow and Aguch oversight. After Maariv the parcels were sent by train.
