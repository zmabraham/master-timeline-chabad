# Rebbe publishes index of Rayatz's maamarim and sichos

*1968*

Titled 'Maftech Sifrei Maamarei v'Drushei Admur Mohorayatz,' the booklet built on the registry tradition of organizing maamarim by year of delivery. The Rebbe personally edited the material and gave it to print. The work serves as an essential research tool for tracing the Rayatz's Chassidic discourses across his nesi'us.
