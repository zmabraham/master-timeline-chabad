# Rebbe issues twin encyclicals on Polish Jewry's plight from Riga

*1939*

Writing on 5 Tevet, the Rebbe described Jews 'burned alive' and homes turned to graves, urged worldwide fundraising channeled through the JDC 'irrespective of political party or personal philosophy,' and called on Chabad communities globally to treat aid to Polish Chasidim as 'saving lives and redeeming captives.'
