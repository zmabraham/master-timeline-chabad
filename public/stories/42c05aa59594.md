# Yeshiva relocates from Warsaw to Otwock

*1935*

In Tishrei 5696 (autumn 1935) the Warsaw Tomchei Tmimim — having outgrown the building at Mlawska 7 / Sapuzinski 17 where it had operated for nearly ten years — relocated to the nearby town of Otwock, taking up new quarters at Slovatzkenya Street 1. The move responded to overcrowding that had developed even before the expansion noted in earlier internal reports.
