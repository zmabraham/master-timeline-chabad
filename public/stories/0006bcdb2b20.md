# Rabbi Raichik dispatched as shliach to central locations

*1949*

On 23 Shevat 5709, Rebbe Rayatz blessed Rabbi Raichik and his wife for their journey on shlichus. The terms of his work were arranged by the Rebbe's son-in-law Rabbi Menachem Mendel Schneerson (the future Rebbe), who organized his deployment to central locations.
