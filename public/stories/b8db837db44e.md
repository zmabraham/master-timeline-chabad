# Chabad manuscript collection returned from Warsaw to New York

*1977*

When the Nazis occupied Warsaw at the start of 5700, the precious manuscript collection of the Chabad Rebbes was lost for thirty-eight years. It was eventually located in a library in Warsaw. At the beginning of 5738 (late 1977) the collection was returned to the central Chabad library at 770 in Brooklyn, rejoining the corpus from which scholars continue to edit and publish the Chasidic teachings of the Rebbes.
