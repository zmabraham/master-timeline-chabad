# 19 Kislev 5679 farbrengen in Rostov

*1918*

Chassidim of Kremenchug including R' Shmuel Kuratin tried to travel to Rostov for the chag but could not obtain train tickets and had to return home as the rail network was in disarray. Witnesses recalled that as R' Shaul Dov Zislin chazered the maamer 'honey and milk flowed from his mouth.'
