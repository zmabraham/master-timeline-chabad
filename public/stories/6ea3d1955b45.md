# Rebbe directs diarist to remain on Schneersohn library work

*1991*

The diarist had submitted a written question on 4 Elul presenting offers to serve as av beit din of Leningrad and as general supervisor of Tomchei Temimim branches across Russia under Ezrat Achim. The Rebbe's reply read 'one engaged with the library here and connected to it — this requires continuation, etc. I will mention at the tziyun,' effectively keeping him in Moscow until the library matter was resolved.
