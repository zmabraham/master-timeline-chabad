# R' Alter writes Rebbe about ill health and yeshiva difficulties

*1928*

R' Alter wrote that he was 'very weak' and that his daily seder was unsustainable: he ate only mezonot before tefilla, taught chassidus until nine, but often could not hold out for davening to conclude after noon. He still hoped R' Moshe Hornstein would accept the directorship within a week and asked the Rebbe for guidance and a berachah for strength to serve b'mesirah amitis.
