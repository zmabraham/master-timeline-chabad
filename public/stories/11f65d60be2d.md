# Rebbe Rashab forced to flee Lubavitch as German army approaches in WWI

*1915*

World War I brought the front lines near Lubavitch, the longtime seat of Chabad. The Rebbe Rashab departed with his family, expecting the displacement to be temporary. In fact, this marked the end of Lubavitch's era as the center of the Chabad movement; the town's chassidic infrastructure would never be restored.
