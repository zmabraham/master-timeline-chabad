# Rebbe Maharash sends business and shidduch advice via secretary Levi Yitzchak Idelevitz

*1879*

The Rebbe Maharash often communicated with chassidim through his secretary R' Levi Yitzchak Idelevitz. In this letter, written on Wednesday 17 Menachem Av 5639 (1879), the Rebbe advises Yaakov Ayerov — likely on a match for one of his sons — that the proposal from Kalk seems good and that he should look into it carefully. The note also reports that the Rebbe's health has improved, helping fix the dating to 5639.
