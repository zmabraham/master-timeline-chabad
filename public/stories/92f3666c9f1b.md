# R' D.M. Rabinowitz arrives in USA on Chai Elul

*1937*

Delayed a week by a maritime workers' strike, he understood the Rebbe Rayatz's earlier letter — which had said his date of arrival would be propitious — as a hint to the spiritual significance of Chai Elul. He convened a farbrengen with Anash that day at the Broadway Central Hotel to fulfill the Rebbe's allusion. The letter cited tradition that on Chai Elul the Baal Shem Tov spoke Torah publicly before his students.
