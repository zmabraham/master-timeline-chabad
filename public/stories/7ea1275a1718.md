# Vilna yeshiva reports examination by R' Aryeh Leib Sheinin

*1930*

R' Aryeh Leib Sheinin, rabbi of Dokshitz, visited the Vilna yeshiva and examined the students in their Torah, avodah, and conduct. So impressed was he that he urged the administration to expand beyond their current five classes by adding a higher division for advanced bochurim who would otherwise need to travel elsewhere.
