# Eighteen thousand rubles raised in Moscow for Tomchei Temimim on Purim

*1918*

After arriving in Moscow on Purim eve and being hosted at R' Baruch Shalom Kahan's home, the fundraisers were taken the next morning to the office of the magnate R' Zalka Persitz. Through phone calls throughout the day they collected 18,000 rubles. The emissaries sewed 9,000 rubles into each of their garments and returned to Lubavitch.
