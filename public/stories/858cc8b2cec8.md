# Six Tel Aviv yeshiva students granted permission to study in New York

*1949*

Six older students from the Tel Aviv branch of Tomchei Tmimim received approval from the Rebbe Rayatz to relocate for advanced study at the central yeshiva in New York. The approval letters were later printed in Igrot Kodesh vol. 10 (letters 3640-3641). They departed in Tevet 5710 and arrived in New York.
