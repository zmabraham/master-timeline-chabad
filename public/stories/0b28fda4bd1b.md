# Investigator Morozov reassigned from Mitteler Rebbe investigation

*1825*

About two weeks into the month-long investigation that ran from early Cheshvan to early Kislev, Major Morozov was reassigned to a separate criminal case involving misuse of treasury funds. Khovansky formally released Morozov from the joint inquiry and instructed Loginov and Markov to complete the entire investigation without him, urging them to make every effort to bring out the truth. The two remaining investigators carried the case forward together.
