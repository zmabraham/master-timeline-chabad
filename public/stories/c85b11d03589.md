# Freigang submits combined gendarmerie report on Tzemach Tzedek

*1841*

Freigang summarized the parallel reports of Colonel Kutsinsky from Vitebsk, Colonel Wojciech from Mogilev and Captain Yurkovsky from Minsk. The reports broadly agreed that the Tzemach Tzedek lived simply and modestly, was regarded by his followers as a great scholar and held strong influence over them, while showing no signs of disloyalty. Yurkovsky's Minsk report uniquely recounted the Rebbe's 1835 visit to Minsk and noted that 'Zalman, the grandfather of Mendel of Lyubavichi' had been summoned to Petersburg around 1802 on suspicion of belonging to a harmful sect but was cleared and returned home shortly afterwards.
