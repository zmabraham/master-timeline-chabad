# R' Levi Yitzchak of Berditchev gives haskamah for Slavita Shas and Tur

*1800*

R' Shalom Shachna (son-in-law of the Alter Rebbe) and R' Mordechai of Orsha (the Alter Rebbe's brother) traveled to obtain this approbation. R' Levi Yitzchak praised them as 'two men of Ephraim' bringing the Shas and Turim to press. This signaled the formal start of the large Slavita printing project.
