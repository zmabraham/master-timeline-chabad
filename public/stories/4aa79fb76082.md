# 'Pidyon Shvuyim' rescue fund established in New York

*1940*

From his quarters at the Greystone Hotel, the Rebbe entrusted Rabbi Eliyahu Simpson with the work of registering European families in urgent need. The first list, written on hotel stationery, recorded nearly one hundred families totaling roughly 500 souls across nineteen Lithuanian towns, with parallel lists soon prepared for occupied Poland.
