# Tzemach Tzedek travels to Ula, Polotsk, Ushatch and Chashniki

*1832*

This northern circuit of the Tzemach Tzedek is recorded in the maamarim he delivered along the way, which were dated and inscribed with their location of delivery. The trip extended his presence into Vitebsk-region towns and helped consolidate Chabad influence in the area. The journey appears in the chronological registry of his discourses.
