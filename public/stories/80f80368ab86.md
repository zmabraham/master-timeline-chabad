# Turkish government expels Russian-citizen Jews from Hebron

*1914*

R' Avraham Chaim Naeh recorded in the preface to his Shnos Chaim that the expulsion took place in Tevet 5675 (December 1914). Because much of the Chabad community in Hebron held Russian citizenship, they were forced out by Turkey as enemy nationals. Many of the exiles concentrated in Alexandria (No-Amon), Egypt.
