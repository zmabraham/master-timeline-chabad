# Death of R' Moshe Rosenblatt of Zembin

*1915*

R' Moshe Rosenblatt of Zembin was accepted at Tomchei Tmimim in 5669 as a mashpia for younger students. He served until his passing in Shevat 5675 (1915) in Lubavitch. He was known as 'Moshe der Chozer' for his role repeating the Rebbe Rashab's discourses at guest lodgings, and he typically opened each farbrengen with a niggun.
