# Birth of Rabbi Refael Oved ibn Tzur, son and future successor

*1706*

Rabbi Refael Oved ibn Tzur was born on Monday, 3 Shevat 5466 (January 1706) in Fez. He was one of seventeen children born to the Yaavetz, of whom only he and his brother Reuven survived their father. He would eventually inherit his father's rabbinic mantle and continue the family's marriage-registration pinkas, now preserved as MS 1605 in the Library of Agudat Chasidei Chabad.
