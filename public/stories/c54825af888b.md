# Alter Rebbe and thirty Chassidim arrested

*1798*

Following the Mitnagdic denunciation of summer 5558, the Alter Rebbe was arrested at the start of 5559 along with thirty Chassidim from Vilna, Vidz, Hamadora, Pandel and Vysokoye-Litovsk. Heads of the Vilna Chassidic kehilla, including R' Meir Refael's, R' Aryeh Leib, R' Shlomo Zalman, R' Nachum ben Yitzchak, R' Yehuda Leib of Kraz and Rabbi Baruch Mordechai Ettinger, were interrogated. A separate arrest warrant was also issued against R' Moshe Meizlish, who managed to evade capture.
