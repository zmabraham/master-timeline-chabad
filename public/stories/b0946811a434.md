# Author arrives in Samarkand and joins an underground minyan

*1941*

R' Yeshaya Hofin helped him rent an upstairs room in a tupik near the Mahalla market and warned that Lubavitcher chassidim feared to daven in the Bukharan synagogue. The author was let into the underground minyan in time for the last Shabbos of Elul. Because his landlady also possessed a sefer Torah, he and R' Aizik agreed to alternate Rosh Hashanah and Yom Kippur services between their homes, with the strict condition that only ten men attend.
