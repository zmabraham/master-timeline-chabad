# Sefer Ha-Tania (Rabbati) printed in Mantua

*1514*

In 5274 (1514), Rabbi Yechiel ben Yekutiel ben Binyamin Harofeh's halachic compendium Sefer Ha-Tania — later called Tania Rabbati to distinguish it from the Alter Rebbe's Tanya — was printed in Mantua. The work collects rulings and customs for the entire year.
