# Rebbe Rayatz sends R' Aryeh Leib Shainin to Dokshitz

*1923*

In 5683 the Rebbe Rayatz sent R' Aryeh Leib Shainin to the town of Dokshitz to serve as its rabbi and mashpia. Shainin had been one of the most outstanding talmidim of Tomchei Tmimim in Lubavitch — the Rebbe Rashab reportedly said that the entire founding of Tomchei Tmimim had been worthwhile for him alone. Shainin lived in Dokshitz until the Second World War and was murdered there in the Holocaust.
