# Branch opened in Nevel

*1901*

In early summer 5661 (1901), a new branch of Tomchei Tmimim was opened in the town of Nevel. About twenty students were selected and sent with the mashgiach R' Yehuda Leib (Tzites) Bodnevitz, who hailed from Nevel and thus was suited to lead this branch. The Nevel branch lasted about a year and a half before closing in late summer 5662.
