# Rebbe's death sentence commuted to Solovki exile

*1927*

On Wednesday June 29, an official informed Rabbi Eliyahu Chaim Althaus that the firing-squad verdict had been commuted to exile in the remote Solovki Islands prison camp. The family was told to bring food and say goodbye. A crowd gathered outside the prison as Lulov tried one last time to extort a public retraction from the Rebbe, who refused.
