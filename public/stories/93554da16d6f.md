# R. Shmelka and R. Pinchas Horowitz visit the Maggid in Rovno and praise Alter Rebbe's work

*1771*

After being chosen as rabbis of Nikolsburg and Frankfurt-am-Main respectively, the two brothers came to take counsel from the Maggid before assuming their posts. At his court they saw the Alter Rebbe's draft of the Shulchan Aruch and praised it greatly, telling him to be strong and complete the work. The Alter Rebbe later recalled this meeting in his correspondence with R. Pinchas, the Baal Haflaah.
