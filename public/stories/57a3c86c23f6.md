# Rebbe Rayatz meets Justice Louis Brandeis

*1930*

Rabbi Kook had given the Rebbe a letter of recommendation. The Rebbe proposed an alliance with the JDC coordinating colonies while others facilitated emigration and supported religious life, with Brandeis as chair. 'Rabbi Schneersohn impressed me so much,' Brandeis later wrote, but he limited himself to behind-the-scenes work, and the broader coalition floundered.
