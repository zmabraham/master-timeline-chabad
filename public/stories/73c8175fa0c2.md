# Khovansky orders investigation of Mitteler Rebbe's fundraising

*1825*

On October 2, 1825, Prince Khovansky issued an order to Colonel Loginov and Court Counselor Markov to conduct a formal inquiry into the accusations brought by Simcha Kisin against the Mitteler Rebbe. They were instructed to determine the source of the sums Rabbi Schneur had distributed among his relatives, whether they came from communal taxes, who collected them, under what legal authority, and whether accountings had been rendered. The investigators summoned the Rebbe along with Lubavitch resident Lavut and the Shklov townsmen Zalman Reizin and Shimon Freidin.
