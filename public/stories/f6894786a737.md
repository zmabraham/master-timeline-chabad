# Shmuel Horowitz's Yiddish children's Haggadah 'V'Higadta L'Vincha' in New York

*1983*

In 5743 (1983), Shmuel Horowitz published in New York 'V'Higadta L'Vincha', a Yiddish-language children's guide containing Mah Nishtanah, Seder laws and customs for cheder-aged children, with explanations and illustrations. The 31-page octavo edition served the Yiddish-speaking Chabad community in America. It maintained the Yiddish heritage in Chabad children's Pesach education.
