# Passing of the Vilna Gaon (Gra)

*1797*

The Vilna Gaon, leader of the Mitnagdim, died on the third day of Chol HaMoed Sukkos 5558. His death emboldened R' Moshe ben Asher and other opponents in Vilna to intensify their campaign against the Chassidim. The day after his passing they declared a cherem against the Chabad kehilla.
