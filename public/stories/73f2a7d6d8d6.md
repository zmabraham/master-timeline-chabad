# Royal Wedding of Chaya Mushka and the Ramash in Warsaw

*1928*

The Rebbe wore his grandfather's shtreimel publicly for the first time since assuming leadership, symbolizing a new era of Chabad's rebirth in Western Europe. The Rebbe taught a maamar starting at 11:00 p.m. and again at 4:00 a.m. The wedding introduced the relatively unknown Ramash to the broader Jewish community, including Rabbi Meir Shapiro, who afterward told the Rebbe, 'You have taken a son-in-law who is a gaon.'
