# Zionist Office census records 757 Jews in Hebron

*1918*

After the British conquest, the Palestine Office of the Zionist Organization conducted a census in 5678 (1918). It tallied 256 Ashkenazim, 444 Sephardim, and 57 Maghrebi and Yemenite Jews — a total of 757 souls. This was roughly half the Jewish population recorded a decade earlier.
