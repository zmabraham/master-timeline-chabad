# NYC Mayor Abraham Beame presents Rebbe certificate for 25 years of leadership

*1975*

On 10 Shevat 5735 (January 22, 1975), New York City Mayor Abraham Beame presented the Lubavitcher Rebbe with a certificate of honor commemorating the 25th anniversary of his assumption of the Chabad leadership. The certificate is displayed in the library exhibition's New York case.
