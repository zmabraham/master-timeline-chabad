# Rebbe Rayatz writes encouragement letter to the new Vilna yeshiva branch

*1927*

Published in Igrot Kodesh of the Rayatz (vol. 1, letter 323), the letter responded to the spring 5687 effort by Vilna anash to launch the yeshiva at the Apatov shul. It set the institution on its formal footing under the central Tomchei Tmimim network. The branch's office was eventually housed not in the shul itself but in the office of the I. V. Levin company at 15 Kolejowa Street.
