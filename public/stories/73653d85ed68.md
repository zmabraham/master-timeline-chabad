# Rebbe Rashab letter on cataloging library books by subject

*1914*

On 21 Tevet 5674 (January 19, 1914), the Rebbe Rashab wrote to his son expressing his long-standing desire for a subject-based catalog of the library. He wanted a list showing which general topics appeared in which books, such as the category of Torah. He hoped Moshe Chaim could create such a list, and had originally wanted Wiener to do it.
