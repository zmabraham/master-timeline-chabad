# Rayatz writes Vilna students after Warsaw and Polish branches flee to Vilna

*1940*

After the German invasion of Poland, students of the Warsaw yeshiva and other Polish branches fled to Vilna, joining the existing branch there. In his letter (Igrot Kodesh vol. 5, letter 1141), the Rayatz acknowledged a report relayed by R' Shmuel Zalmanov and R' M. L. Rodshtein on the temporary arrangements. He told the talmidim to internalize that the yeshiva was in a state of relocation, not stable settlement.
