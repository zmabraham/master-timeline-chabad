# Avraham Chaim permitted to build a sukkah in prison courtyard

*1889*

In a letter written between Yom Kippur and Sukkot 5650 Avraham Chaim lamented that he would be cut off from the community for the first nights of the festival, unable to eat even a kezayit of bread in a sukkah. To his relief the warden then granted permission to construct a sukkah in the prison courtyard, as recorded in a letter by Rabbi Shmuel Rosenbaum. The concession reflected the warden's growing regard for him and was one of several leniencies that followed Rabbi Zalman Aharon's earlier intervention.
