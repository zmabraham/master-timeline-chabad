# R' Yaakov Landa serves as shadar in Latvia

*1932*

At the start of 5692 (1932), the Rebbe Rayatz dispatched R' Yaakov Landa as a Kollel Chabad shadar (fundraising emissary) to the Jewish communities of Latvia. That same year, his appointment as general rabbi of Libau was also under consideration, prompting correspondence from the Rogatchover.
