# Second Hebron inter-community agreement expanding shdarus reach

*1842*

In 5602 (1842), the Hebron Sephardic and Chabad communities signed another agreement renewing the joint shdarus arrangements. The terms largely matched the 5590 agreement, but added Turkey and the Maghreb (North Africa, inner and outer) to the list of joint shlichut territories alongside Italy, Persia, and Sephardic lands. The expansion reflected growing fundraising operations across the Mediterranean and Ottoman lands.
