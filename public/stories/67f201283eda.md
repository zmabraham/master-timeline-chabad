# Pereshel facsimile of Netanel ben Aharon Segal's 1771 Haggadah manuscript

*1971*

In 5731 (1971), Tuvia Pereshel published in New York a facsimile of a manuscript Haggadah by Netanel ben Aharon Segal from the year 5531 (1771). The 24-leaf octavo edition included an introduction by Pereshel. It inaugurated his annual project of publishing facsimiles of rare Haggadot before each Passover.
