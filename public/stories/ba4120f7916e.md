# First wall calendar of Chabad customs by R. A.C. Naeh

*1938*

R. Avraham Chaim Naeh authored a calendar of synagogue laws and Chabad customs, initially as a booklet and later as a wall calendar. The oldest wall-calendar copy preserved in the library is from 5699 (1938-1939), published by Kollel Chabad — beginning an annual tradition that continued under his son R. Baruch Naeh and beyond.
