# Avraham Padabranker studied at the Lithuanian yeshiva in Kiev

*1927*

In 5687 (1926/27), Avraham Padabranker studied in Kiev at the Litvish yeshiva. The Rosh Mesivta was Rabbi Morgenstern, who served under the leadership of Rabbi Mattasov, the chief rabbi of Podol. Years later he was encountered in Chernigov, having held onto some connection to Yiddishkeit unlike many of his peers who broke under Soviet pressure.
