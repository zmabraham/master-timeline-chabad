# R' Yaakov of Smilyan becomes chief gabbai in chu"l

*1781*

R' Yaakov of Smilyan started his work as the chief gabbai abroad collecting funds for the Chassidic settlement in Eretz Yisrael in 1781 (תקמ"א). This role was foundational for the organized fundraising system that supported Chabad and other Chassidic groups in the Holy Land.
