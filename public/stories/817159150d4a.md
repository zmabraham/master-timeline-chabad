# First documented use of the name 'Chabad' for the Alter Rebbe's chassidim

*1803*

The earliest known use of 'Chabad' as the name of the Alter Rebbe's chassidim is in the demand brought by R' Avraham of Kalisk's emissaries in winter 5563 (1803) that maamadot emissaries 'not called by the name Chabad' replace the existing ones. This implies that by that point the designation had already become familiar in the chassidic world.
