# Tnaim signed between Rebbe Rashab and Rebbetzin Sterna Sara

*1865*

On 10 Sivan 5625, while the future Rebbe Rashab was just four and a half years old, his tnaim were signed with his cousin Sterna Sara, daughter of the Admor Maharin of Ovruch. The Tzemach Tzedek — grandfather of both bride and groom and just ten months before his own passing — personally inscribed the opening blessing on both copies and added on the reverse a pledge to give the couple 50 rubles every year, totaling 500 rubles. The contract stipulated that the wedding itself would take place in Jerusalem in the summer of 5635.
