# Bentzion Shemtov weds Esther Golda in Siberian exile

*1930*

Esther Golda made the long trek from Moscow that summer. Without a mikveh, she immersed in an icy river; without a ring, Shemtov fashioned one from a silver fork. Exiled rabbi Shmuel Levitin officiated. Reb Mendel Futerfus, a legendary underground figure, had suggested the match.
