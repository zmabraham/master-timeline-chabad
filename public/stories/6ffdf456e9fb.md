# Marriage of Rashab and Shterna Sara

*1875*

In 1875, Rabbi Shalom Dovber (the Rashab) married his cousin Rebbetzin Shterna Sara, fulfilling an engagement contract drawn up in 1865 when both were young children. Shterna Sara moved four hundred miles from her hometown of Ovruch, Ukraine, to Lubavitch following the marriage. The match had been suggested by Rabbi Menachem Mendel, who saw the two grandchildren together and declared them 'Bride and groom!'
