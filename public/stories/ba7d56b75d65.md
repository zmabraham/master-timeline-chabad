# R' Yehoshua Heshel Reizman appointed maggid shiur in Warsaw Mechina

*1937*

After three years in the Olam, R' Heshel Reizman was called in spring 5697 to teach the junior Mechina class alongside R' Schneur Zalman Gourary, who led the upper class. When Gourary later left the yeshiva, Reizman took over the upper class and R' Yitzchak Hendel filled the junior position. Reizman remained in his hometown of Suchedniow when WWII broke out and survived the war.
