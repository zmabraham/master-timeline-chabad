# Around 25 yeshiva students arrive in Moscow as shluchim to Russia

*1991*

On 22 Shevat 5751, approximately twenty-five bochurim shluchim arrived in Moscow to travel in groups to various Russian cities for three-month assignments. A welcome reception was held at Marina Roscha at 2:00 p.m. and the groups departed that evening for their destinations; among those assigned to the Moscow yeshiva were Yaakov Goldschmid and Shlomo Wilhelm.
