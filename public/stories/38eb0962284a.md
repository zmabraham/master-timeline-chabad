# Chabad emissaries spend Shabbat with rescued Chernobyl children at camp

*1991*

On Shabbat 26 Sivan 5751 (June 8, 1991), R' Yosef Yitzchak Aharonov, R' Shlomo Cunin, and R' M.M. Shemtov stayed at the preparation camp for roughly 140 Chernobyl rescue children awaiting evacuation to Israel. For most of the children it was their first experience of communal prayer, Kiddush, Shabbat meals, and Havdalah. Several girls received Jewish names during the Torah reading, and many boys said they would receive Hebrew names at brit milah upon arriving in Israel.
