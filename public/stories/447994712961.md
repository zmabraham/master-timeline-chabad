# Gorby Club director offers paid US loan of Schneersohn collection

*1991*

On 28 Tevet the delegation, brought by Stas, met Ilya Osadchuk, director of the Gorby Club at the Mossoviet. Osadchuk said no one in power intended to return the books and proposed instead a US fund to develop the Moscow library in exchange for an exhibition loan of the collection. The delegation declined to respond on the spot and decided to wait for Veronika's return before deciding.
