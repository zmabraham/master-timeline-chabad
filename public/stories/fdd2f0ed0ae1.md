# Went to be tested at Tomchei Tmimim Yeshiva in the town of Lubavitch,

*1916*

Went to be tested at Tomchei Tmimim Yeshiva in the town of Lubavitch, where he was accepted as a student in the cheders and afterwards in the hall of Tomchei Tmimim Yeshiva in the town of Lubavitch, where he remained until the year 5677 (1916), when the yeshiva relocated to the city of Kremenchug on the bank of the Dnieper River
