# Rayatz writes to Katzenellenbogen urging caution

*1926*

Gurewicz had been traveling Russia falsely claiming the Rebbe's endorsement for a national congress. In a personal handwritten letter to Rabbi David Tevel Katzenellenbogen dated 23 Cheshvan 5687, the Rayatz wrote that the idea required further consideration and urged consultation with other rabbinic leaders. The chief rabbi was being misled by LERO's leadership.
