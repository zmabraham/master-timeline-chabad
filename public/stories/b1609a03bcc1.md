# Rebbe Rayatz arrested, exiled, and forced to leave Soviet Russia

*1927*

In 5687 (1927) the sixth Lubavitcher Rebbe, Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn, was arrested by the Soviet GPU for his work to preserve Jewish life under communism. After being sentenced, his sentence was commuted and he was eventually freed and ordered out of the country. He refused to leave without his library, and only after firm insistence was he permitted to take his collection with him as he relocated the center of Chabad to Riga.
