# R' Mordechai Dovber Slonim, head of Kollel Chabad Hebron, passes away

*1915*

While the chief rabbi Shila and the yeshiva director R' Shlomo Zalman Havlin had fled to Russia at the start of the war, R' Mordechai Dovber Slonim remained behind in Hebron. Unable to withstand the suffering of the war years, he passed away in good name on 5 Tevet 5676. With his death, only a remnant of Chabad's Hebron leadership survived.
