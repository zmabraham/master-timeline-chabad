# Tzemach Tzedek portrait printed in Keneset Yisrael (Warsaw)

*1888*

In 5648 (1888), the Warsaw journal Keneset Yisrael reproduced a portrait of the Tzemach Tzedek in its third volume (cols. 215-216), signed by the artist Z. Schatz. This version differs noticeably from the original painting and from the Metz lithograph, and the author of the present study judges it an unsuccessful attempt unworthy of further analysis.
