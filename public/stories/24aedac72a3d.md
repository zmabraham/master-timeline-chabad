# Summer 5677 fire devastates Lubavitch

*1917*

The alarm bell rang while the author was at the mikveh together with R' Avraham Dovid Poizner of Klimovich. They misjudged the direction of the fire and ran first to Poizner's home to rescue his Vilna Shas, hauling the seforim to dry ground at the riverbed. When the author reached his own lodgings at the home of R' Michael Katzenelenbogen, the roof was already alight; his brother Shlomo Tzvi was guarding their bedding. Several bochurim had tried to salvage furnishings but most was lost, including the author's books and writings and two large cabinets of seforim belonging to R' Elchanan Dov Morozov in the neighboring house.
