# Rebbe convenes senior students on Polish yeshivah challenges

*1936*

Some students wanted to expand Talmud study and minimize chasidic philosophy, concerning the Rebbe who wished the Polish yeshivah to mirror the original Lubavitch model. He asked seniors to win over peers to the integration of nigleh and nistar. He confided his personal anguish: "In Russia I experienced self-sacrifice. Now I am experiencing it again with the move from Riga to Poland."
