# Knesses Yisrael yeshivah founded in Slabodka

*1877*

In 1877, Rabbi Nosson Tzvi Finkel established Knesses Yisrael in Slabodka as a kollel for married men. It soon became a full yeshivah and introduced systematic study of musar, sparking the controversy that would lead opponents to open the rival Knesses Beis Yitzchak in 1897.
