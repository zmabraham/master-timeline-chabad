# Shabbat farbrengen at Polyakov Synagogue with shluchim kinus

*1991*

After Friday-night seudah at Molodyozhnaya Hall, the kinus participants marched in procession to the Polyakov Synagogue under a militia honor guard. Following the Torah reading, R' Berel Lazar and R' B. Cunin spoke and led dancing into the courtyard. The featured speaker at the multi-hour farbrengen was R' Shmuel Motusov, who described being arrested decades earlier for learning Torah in this same synagogue.
