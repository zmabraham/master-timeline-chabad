# R. Yosef Yozik Horowitz passes away in Warsaw

*1914*

R. Yosef Yozik Horowitz, who had built up Chabad life in Warsaw from the 1890s onward, died at the start of 5675 (autumn 1914). Leadership of the Warsaw Chabad community then passed to his son-in-law R. Shraga Feivish Zalmanov, his brother-in-law R. Dovber Moshe Shmotkin, and his nephew R. Shneur Zalman Shmotkin.
