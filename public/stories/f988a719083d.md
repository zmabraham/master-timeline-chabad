# Khasbulatov letter refers Chabad library claim to arbitrage court

*1991*

Disclosed by Judge Gribenikov at the press conference defending the court's later ruling, Khasbulatov's February 6 1991 letter was the mechanism that put the case before the arbitrage court. Gribenikov stressed it was a routine policy referral rather than political pressure, and that the court took seven months of independent study before reaching its unanimous decision.
