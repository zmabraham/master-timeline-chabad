# Shaar HaYichud printed in Kapust as part of Ner Mitzvah V'Torah Or

*1820*

Although written in 1814, the third letter was the first of the three to reach print. It appeared in 1820 under the title Shaar HaYichud as part of the published Ner Mitzvah V'Torah Or in Kapust. Kuntres HaHispaalus and the introductory letter would only follow in print decades later.
