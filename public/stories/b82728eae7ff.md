# Rashab writes from Lubavitch that Rayatz's books are intact

*1907*

Both the Rashab and the Rayatz spent most of 5667 abroad. The Rashab returned to Lubavitch for Pesach and wrote on 11 Nissan that 'your books are intact and stand in order as they were.' He repeated the reassurance about Rayatz's separate personal library on 24 Nissan, underlining that father and son maintained distinct collections in the Lubavitch library.
