# Jacobson writes Rebbe about two students preparing to travel to Otwock

*1938*

Jacobson described both students positively: Barnetzky as a deep, hard-working chossid, and the 17-year-old Levy as exceptionally gifted, a nephew of the chassid 'Reb Avraham Dov the Malach.' Levy had only joined Achei Tmimim months earlier but Jacobson believed he could become 'a beautiful vessel.'
