# Wedding invitation from the Chiddushei HaRim

*1864*

From Gur on Rosh Chodesh Adar II 5624, the Chiddushei HaRim wrote a wedding invitation for the union of his grandson Pinchas Menachem Mendel of Pilcz to his granddaughter Hendel Leah, sister of the future Sfas Emes. The invitation was addressed to R' Meshulam Reich, who was a son-in-law of the Rebbe Rashab the elder (son of the Tzemach Tzedek) and later served as a shadar of Kolel Chabad — through whom the letter eventually reached the archive of the Lubavitch rebbeim.
