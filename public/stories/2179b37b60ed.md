# Rabbi Chaim Ashkenazi speaks at an 'Awakening Gathering' in Kfar Chabad

*2010*

Rabbi Chaim Ashkenazi speaks at an 'Awakening Gathering' in Kfar Chabad, Nissan 5768. Rabbi Chaim Ashkenazi, 23 Iyar 5709 – 13 Nissan 5770 (2010), was rabbi of the Chabad community of Tel Aviv, mashpia of the Chabad yeshivas of Lod and Or Yehuda, and a member of Agudat Chassidei
