# Mohilev provincial government orders continued interrogation of the Mitteler Rebbe

*1826*

Civil Governor Maksimov and his councillors reported that the Babinovich magistrate court had bypassed proper procedure by sending a policeman with questions to the Rebbe instead of summoning him to a hearing — an evident leniency. A fine of 10 rubles was imposed on the responsible officials, and the court was ordered to investigate the Rebbe thoroughly on the charge of collecting large sums from his followers and to punish the accused with full severity. Oversight was placed in the hands of the district court inspector and the provincial prosecutor.
