# Passing of Rabbi Hillel of Paritch in Cherson

*1864*

During one of his travels, Rabbi Hillel of Paritch—author of Pelach HaRimon and longtime av beis din of Babruysk—passed away in Cherson on 11 Menachem Av 5624. An ohel was built over his grave, and many chassidim journeyed there to prostrate themselves at his resting place.
