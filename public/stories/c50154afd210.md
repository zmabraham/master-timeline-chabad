# Fire destroys the Lubavitch courtyard

*1868*

Just two years into the Maharash's leadership, the wooden buildings of the Rebbe's courtyard were destroyed. The disaster forced major rebuilding decisions, including whether the Maharash could lawfully build his residence on the site of his father's home given the Tzemach Tzedek's will against such use. The rebuilding produced the large hall and new family residences that would characterize the courtyard for the remaining decades.
