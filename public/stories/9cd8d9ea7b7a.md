# Frierdiker Rebbe departs America aboard the ship Bremen

*1930*

Before departure the tmimim gathered at the Rebbe's hotel expecting a farbrengen. The Rebbe prolonged his prayers until 5pm and then delivered over an hour of sharp rebuke. A large crowd including delegations from many cities came to the port to see him off, where the Rebbe addressed them with words of farewell and encouragement.
