# Mass arrest of Anash and yeshiva students on Erev Pesach 5693 in Kutaisi

*1933*

The Soviet authorities swept through Kutaisi just before Pesach 5693, seizing many chassidim and bochurim along with the yeshiva's mashpia, director, and mentor R. Zalman Levitin. The older bochurim were already imprisoned by the NKVD in Batumi. Any students who remained free had no choice but to flee.
