# Passing of the Maggid of Mezeritch

*1772*

On 19 Kislev 5533, the Maggid of Mezeritch—chief disciple of the Baal Shem Tov—passed away. His three Russian-Lithuanian disciples, Rabbi Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk, Rabbi Avraham of Kalisk, and Rabbi Schneur Zalman of Liadi, returned to Russia to spread Chassidic teaching. Decades later, this date acquired additional weight when the Alter Rebbe was released from Tsarist imprisonment on the same day in 1798.
