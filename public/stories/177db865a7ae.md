# R' Chaim Shmuel Schmerling migrates from Hebron to Jaffa

*1866*

The Chabad settlement in Jaffa, and to a significant degree the broader Ashkenazi community there, originated with R' Chaim Shmuel Schmerling's relocation from Hebron in 5626 (1866). He founded the public institutions of the Ashkenazi community and lived in Jaffa for nearly thirty years.
