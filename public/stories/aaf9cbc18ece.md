# Alter Rebbe flees Lyadi with family ahead of Napoleon's invasion

*1812*

On Friday, Erev Rosh Chodesh Elul 5572 (around August 7, 1812), as Napoleon's army advanced on White Russia, Rabbi Schneur Zalman traveled with his entire household to Krasna. The following Friday they had to flee further from the French. The Mitteler Rebbe recorded that they escaped with only two wagons carrying twenty-eight souls — leaving all heavy possessions behind, despite the elderly Rebbe's frailty and the harshness of the journey.
