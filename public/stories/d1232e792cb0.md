# Rayatz decides to transfer Warsaw advanced students to Riga

*1931*

In response to the hanhalla's questions in late Nisan 5691, the Rebbe Rayatz informed Warsaw that he had decided to establish the main Tomchei Tmimim near his residence in Riga. Around 30-35 advanced 'Ulam' students from Warsaw would be selected to move there, while the Warsaw branch would continue only as a preparatory mechina. R' Yehuda Eber was being dispatched to examine and select students.
