# One of the Tmimim students in Lubavitch, mashpia in Tomchei Tmimim Yeshiva in Orsha

*1987*

One of the Tmimim students in Lubavitch, mashpia in Tomchei Tmimim Yeshiva in Orsha. After the war he served as shochet and rabbi in Alma Ata during the years of wrath, and did not abandon his post with mesirus nefesh until his last day.
