# Anash in Vilna write to Rebbe Rayatz proposing Tomchei Tmimim yeshiva

*1927*

In early Nisan 5687, Anash of Vilna were inspired to found a branch of Tomchei Tmimim Lubavitch in their Apatow synagogue, modeled on the Warsaw yeshiva. A letter signed by the founding committee was sent to the Rebbe Rayatz in Leningrad, requesting his approval, guidance on selecting students, structuring nigleh and chassidus studies, and finding a suitable mashpia. The shaliach R' Lemil from Warsaw also assisted during a visit.
