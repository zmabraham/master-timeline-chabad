# Vidz kahal disavows R' Hirsh ben Yosef's complaint against Vilna

*1798*

After Chassidim in Vidz submitted a complaint against the Vilna kahal's authority over them, the Vidz kahal moved to distance itself. On 19 Av 5558 they wrote a formal disavowal, asserting the action was taken without their knowledge by 'people belonging to the Karliner sect.' The episode is referenced by the Alter Rebbe in a 5571 letter as an example of denunciation he opposed.
