# Rebbe instructs delivery of 25 books to Rotstein

*1940*

On 14 Iyar 5700, the Frierdiker Rebbe wrote to R. Mordechai Chefetz instructing that 25 copies of his sefer be given to R. Moshe Leib Rotstein. The directive was part of ongoing arrangements connected to Rotstein's anticipated departure for the United States.
