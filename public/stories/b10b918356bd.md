# Third denunciation by Shimcha Kissin

*1825*

The third installment of Kissin's denunciations reached Khovansky in July 1825. Together with the two earlier letters, it supplied Governor Khovansky of Vitebsk with the pretext to summon the Rebbe for interrogation in early autumn. Kissin's central exhibit remained the 1813 letter on Maamad distribution that he had obtained through Pinchas Schick of Shklov.
