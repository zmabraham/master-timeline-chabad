# Diarist travels to New York for Yud Shevat 5752

*1992*

Seven months of phone and letter appeals had barely dented the shlichus expenses despite tight economizing. The Rebbe's reply, 'agreement and blessing — I will mention at the Ohel,' authorized the trip. The diarist traveled with Rabbi S. Heber and hoped to land in time to daven Maariv with the Rebbe, who was leading the amud that evening.
