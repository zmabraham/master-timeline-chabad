# Mordechai Shlonk appointed as Kollel Chabad trustee

*1887*

In his Tumat Yesharim pamphlet of 1913, Shlonk wrote that he had served as a kollel trustee for 26 years. His appointment in 5647 came by directive of the Kapuster Rebbe alongside Michel Baruch Reizes. Shlonk's long tenure made him a key continuity figure during the leadership transitions of the late 19th and early 20th centuries.
