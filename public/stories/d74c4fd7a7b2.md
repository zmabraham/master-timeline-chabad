# Frierdiker Rebbe spends Shavuot in the Bronx

*1930*

On Shavuot the Rebbe prayed in the synagogue attached to the mikveh at 172 East and delivered a maamar. During his US visit he also delivered chassidus on weekdays, including a Tuesday parshat Toldot maamar at Beis Knesses Tzemach Tzedek on Marcy Street in Brooklyn that drew an estimated five thousand people.
