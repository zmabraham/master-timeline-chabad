# R' Reuven Liberman, last of the Pleshchenitsy Chassidim, dies

*1927*

R' Reuven Liberman, who had served as shochet and leader of Chassidic life in Pleshchenitsy since R' Avraham Chaim Rosenbaum's emigration, died on Shabbat 28 Nisan 5687 (30 April 1927) at age seventy. His son R' Chaim Liberman, then secretary to the Rayatz, traveled from Leningrad immediately after Pesach to attend the funeral, writing afterward that 'such men are gone from us now in every respect.'
