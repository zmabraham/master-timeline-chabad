# Arrest of R' Eliyahu Far, shochet Tzvi Zarchi, and Vitebsk Zal bochurim

*1931*

In Zarutchei the GPU arrested the older bochurim of the Zal, including Michael Teitelbaum, Herzl Minkowitz, and Avraham Aharon Chen. Soon after, they took the chassid R' Eliyahu Far and the shochet R' Tzvi Zarchi, father of Meir Zarchi. Most of the remaining rabbis and prominent chassidim fled, leaving only the suspected collaborator Goldberg as the Russian rav.
