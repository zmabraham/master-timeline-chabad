# Death of printing partner Reb Shalom Dov Ber Pevzner

*1987*

In Iyar 5747 (May 1987), while the author was attending a wedding in Cleveland, his longtime printing partner Reb Shalom Dov Ber Pevzner — who had suffered for years from ulcers — went to the press despite feeling ill, collapsed, and died before an ambulance could help. The author completed all outstanding contracts alone, sharing the profits with the widow and with Merkos, which had been a one-third partner in the business.
