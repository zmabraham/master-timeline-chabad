# Wedding of Rebbe Rayatz and Rebbetzin Nechama Dina

*1897*

On 13 Elul 5657, Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn, son of Rebbe Rashab and the future sixth Rebbe of Lubavitch, married Nechama Dina, daughter of Rabbi Avraham Schneersohn of Kishinev. The date would later be commemorated annually with gifts and blessings exchanged between the couple. The anniversary became significant in Chabad lore and was marked publicly fifty years later in 5707.
