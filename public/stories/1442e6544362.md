# Pawn-sale deed of courtyard property from Saadia to Yeshua in Fez

*1715*

Saadia obligated himself to repay 150 ounces by the end of Nisan, failing which the mortgaged house would pass to Yeshua. The deed extensively voided any prior moda'a (secret protest) by Saadia and provided that scribes and any future court could repeatedly write cancellations of such protests. Witnessed in Fez by Avraham Serero and Shaul.
