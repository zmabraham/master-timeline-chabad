# Shlomo of Layev sends 1000 rubles to the Mitteler Rebbe

*1825*

The transfer documented a large single donation to the Rebbe's treasury earlier in 1825, months before Shimcha Kissin filed his denunciations. Such gifts later figured in Khovansky's calculations of the Rebbe's annual income, which the governor claimed reached 10,000-14,000 rubles per year.
