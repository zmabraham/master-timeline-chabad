# Yarmarka market and Yosef Yitzchak's arrest in Lubavitch

*1891*

During the two-day yarmarka market fair in Lubavitch on June 29-30, 1891, young Yosef Yitzchak encountered a police sergeant beating Reb Dovid the butcher with false accusations of theft. After defending the butcher, Yosef Yitzchak was arrested and locked in a dark cell, where he reviewed Mishnah by heart and prayed Minchah. He was released when officers realized he was the nephew of Rabbi Zalman Aharon Schneersohn, and the butcher was exonerated when the sergeant's actual theft was uncovered.
