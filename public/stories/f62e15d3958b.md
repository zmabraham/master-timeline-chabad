# R' Avraham Dahan R' Avraham Dahan (born 5727, 1967)

*1967*

R' Avraham Dahan R' Avraham Dahan (born 5727, 1967) is a Chabad chassid, owner and founder of the 'Plant Talmud' orchard at the Mitzpe HaYamim farm in the Galilee, an ethno-botanical researcher, and a lecturer in the 'Jewish Plant Medicine' course at the 'Bri'ah' school
