# Rebbe Rayatz rescued from Warsaw to Riga

*1939*

After four months in Warsaw under relentless German bombing, an elaborate international rescue effort brought the Rebbe Rayatz, his family, and close attendants safely to Riga on 5 Tevet 5700. From Riga he immediately turned to mobilizing American Chabad and the Joint Distribution Committee to fund the evacuation of his remaining students.
