# Yiddishes Tageblatt predicts end of Chabad

*1920*

Following the Rashab's passing, Jewish newspapers worldwide predicted the end of Chabad. In the Yiddishes Tageblatt, Jonathan Mark opined that the Rashab's son lacked the aptitude to follow in his place. Seven years later, Mark reversed course and praised the new Rebbe in his book Gedolim Fun Unzer Tzeit.
