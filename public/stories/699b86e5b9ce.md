# Wiener buys 1490 Soncino Rambam in Yekaterinoslav

*1887*

In the summer of 5647 (1887), Shmuel Wiener acquired a precious Soncino 1490 incunabulum edition of the Rambam in Yekaterinoslav (now Dnipro). His inscription documented the purchase. The book was located by Chabad emissaries in 1991 in the Asiatic Institute's collection, which holds roughly 60 incunabula.
