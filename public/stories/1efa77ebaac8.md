# R. Avraham Chaim decides to sell his share of the liquor business

*1877*

R. Avraham Chaim Rosenbaum had been a partner with the mashpia R. Hendel in a whiskey and tavern business in Pleshchenitz. In the summer of 1877 he decided to liquidate his share, but he and R. Hendel could not agree on terms of the split. Both wrote their arguments to the Rebbe Maharash, who ultimately issued a binding compromise.
