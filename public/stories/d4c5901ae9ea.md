# Mendel Beilis blood libel trial

*1913*

In 1913, Kiev Jew Mendel Beilis was accused of murdering twelve-year-old Andrei Yushchinksy for ritual purposes. The Rayatz, dispatched by the Rashab, enlisted leading attorney Oscar Gruzenberg, who orchestrated a masterful defense culminating in a Shema declaration. Beilis was acquitted, but the trial drew worldwide attention to Russian anti-Semitism.
