# Rashab inquires after Rayatz's manuscript and book purchases

*1907*

In addition to repeating that Rayatz's books in Lubavitch are still in their place, Rashab inquires about the status of manuscripts being sought from Husiatyn and asks whether the list sent to Eretz Yisrael has produced any response. This pattern of detailed book-purchase oversight runs through many of the Rashab-Rayatz letters of this period.
