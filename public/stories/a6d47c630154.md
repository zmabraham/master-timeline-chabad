# Rebbe publishes first maamar since leaving Russia, in Yiddish

*1928*

It was the first maamar printed not in Hebrew but in Yiddish since the eighteenth century, making chasidic teachings accessible to a broader public. In its introduction, the Rebbe addressed those who had drifted from chasidic roots: 'Remember your historical roots, your parents and grandparents.' He urged parents to 'instill these teachings in your children.'
