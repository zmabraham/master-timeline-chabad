# Rebbe inquires about Livenhof and Kreuzburg activity

*1931*

The Frierdiker Rebbe wrote on 27 Av 5691 (Igrot vol. 17, no. 6299) complaining that since leaving Riga at the start of Tammuz he had only heard fragmented news 'from afar' about Livenhof and Kreuzburg, and asked Pinchas Mordechai Teitz to send a detailed report on developments there.
