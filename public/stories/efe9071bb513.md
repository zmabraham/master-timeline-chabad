# Hungarian Hoizman brothers settle in Hebron from Liska

*1865*

R' Alexander Zisha Hoizman, born in Hungary in 5578, emigrated in 5625 with his wife Bat-Sheva and three children—Shimon, Menachem Manes, and Tzvi Hersh—via Trieste, where he met his brother R' Aryeh Leib also bound for Eretz Israel. The two Karlin Chassidim families initially worshipped with the Chabad community but eventually founded 'Kahal Ungarit' with its own synagogue as their families grew and intermarried with other non-Chabad families.
