# GPU officer Grigory Lulov executed

*1940*

On January 21, 1940, GPU officer Grigory Nikolaevich Lulov was executed following his arrest at the end of 1938. Born in Riga in 1899, Lulov had joined the Communist Party in 1916 and the Cheka in 1919, becoming an 'Honorary Worker of the Cheka-GPU' decorated with the Order of Lenin in 1937 before falling victim to the purges himself.
