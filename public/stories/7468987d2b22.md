# Rayatz becomes director of Tomchei Temimim

*1898*

After Chasidim led by Rabbi Asher Grossman of Nikolayev urged the Rayatz to lead the yeshivah and the Rashab endorsed the idea, the Rayatz assumed the directorship on 24 Elul 5658 (September 11, 1898). He immediately took on fundraising, faculty management, and expansion of the institution.
