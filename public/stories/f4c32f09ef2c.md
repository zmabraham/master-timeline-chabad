# Pekidim VeAmarkalim begin uniform EY fund distribution

*1825*

Starting in 5585 the Chevrat Terumat HaKodesh in Amsterdam (Pekidim VeAmarkalim) systematically distributed all donations among the four kollels - Sephardim, Perushim, Chassidim, and Chabad. Funds were sent four times yearly, usually via Beirut. This system continued until disputes arose with the later-established HoD kollel.
