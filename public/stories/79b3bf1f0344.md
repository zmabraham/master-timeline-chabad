# Yitzchak Shamir addresses Tel Aviv memorial for seventh Rebbe

*1994*

Speaking at a Tel Aviv memorial for the seventh Lubavitcher Rebbe, Shamir said that on his early-1950s mission to Russia, 'we were surprised to discover that in every city across Russia, there was an existing clandestine Jewish network being directed by the Rebbe in New York.' The discovery led to a long partnership between the Mossad and Chabad's underground.
