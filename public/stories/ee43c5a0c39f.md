# Kollel Chabad publishes detailed fundraising accounts

*1910*

The supplement to HaModia presented a detailed list of remittances from hundreds of towns abroad for fiscal year 5670. Most communities were marked as having sent funds directly to Jerusalem rather than through shadarim. About one hundred towns were listed as collected by R. Ber Reich and roughly forty by R. Hirsch Tomerkin, indicating the surviving network of itinerant emissaries.
