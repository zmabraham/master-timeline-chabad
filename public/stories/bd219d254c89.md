# R' Dan Tomarkin of Rogachov serves as chief gabbai of Kollel Chabad

*1855*

Joining the existing gabbaim R' A. of Rogachov and R' Yitzchok Aizik of Bichov, Tomarkin became the dominant figure in administering Kollel Chabad's overseas fundraising. The Tzemach Tzedek himself prepared the lists of distribution recipients, and after the Rebbe's passing in 5626 Tomarkin continued under the leadership of the Tzemach Tzedek's sons. He led the Rogachover Vaad that prepared the distribution lists in the decades that followed.
