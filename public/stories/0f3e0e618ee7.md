# Rebbe describes how Rebbetzin Chana made ink in exile

*1963*

On 20 Menachem-Av 5723 (10 August 1963), the yahrzeit of his father R. Levi Yitzchak Schneerson, the Rebbe told that when his father was in Soviet exile, he eventually received some books — including a Zohar — but no paper, so he wrote his Kabbalistic glosses in the margins. He also lacked ink, so Rebbetzin Chana learned to manufacture ink from grasses; lacking expertise, her batches came out red, green or black, accounting for the varied colors visible in the manuscript glosses later published as Likutei Levi Yitzchak.
