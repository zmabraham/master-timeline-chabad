# R' Yitzchak Mandelbaum proposes opening a yeshiva in Maciów

*1931*

Mandelbaum forwarded a letter from a young man in Maciów (Volhynia) describing a magnificent stone building, recently completed by a wealthy New York native in memory of his deceased daughter. The donor had built dormitory space, a dining hall, and apartments for the heads of the yeshiva, and pledged ongoing support. The local community urged that a Chabad yeshiva be founded there.
