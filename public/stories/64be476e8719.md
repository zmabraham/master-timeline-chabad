# Publication of Rashimat Maamarei DACh of Maharash and Rashab

*1981*

R. Naftali Greenberg compiled supplements to the existing registries based on manuscripts in the Rebbe's library, identifying more than two hundred Maharash maamarim missing from the 1947 list. The Rebbe directed that all material be published quickly together with his own subsequent annotations. The volume was edited together with R. David Moshe Olidort and appeared in Iyar 5741.
