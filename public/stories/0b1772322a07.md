# Rebbe Rashab delivers maamar 'Hachodesh Hazeh' in Rostov

*1918*

Following his fundraising trip to Moscow, the narrator traveled to Rostov to ask the Rebbe Rashab's guidance on the future of Tomchei Temimim. He arrived before Shabbos Parshas Hachodesh 5678 and heard the Rebbe deliver the maamar 'Hachodesh Hazeh' that Shabbos. Motzaei Shabbos he had a forty-minute yechidus in which the Rebbe gave his ruling that the students should remain in Lubavitch.
