# Rayatz forwards Nachum Etrog's Jerusalem book list to Slonim

*1904*

Rayatz writes that he has obtained an extensive list from Nachum Etrog in Jerusalem and is sending it to Slonim with prices marked according to his own knowledge and his various catalogs. He explains his priorities: the chief interest is rare books from first printings, with Kabbalah works first, then responsa, then poskim. He also urges Slonim to search the genizah of Jerusalem — and especially Chevron — for manuscripts of the Chabad Rebbes, particularly the Mitteler Rebbe.
