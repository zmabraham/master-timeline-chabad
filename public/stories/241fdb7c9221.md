# 19 Kislev farbrengen at Chelm yeshiva

*1936*

On 19 Kislev 5697 the Chelm branch hosted a celebration of the Chag HaGeulah starting at 7 PM, including students, hanhalah members and visiting avreichim. They read the Frierdiker Rebbe's klali letter and the parsha of the Chag HaGeulah, then learned the maamar 'Asarah Sheyoshvim' from 12 Tammuz 5688 during the seudah.
