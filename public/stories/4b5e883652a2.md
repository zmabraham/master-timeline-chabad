# Death of R' Yosef Garfinkel in wartime exile

*1943*

On 28 Nissan 5703, during the World War II evacuation of Chabad families from Russia, R' Yosef Shmuel Garfinkel passed away in exile in Ferghana, Uzbekistan. His widow Chaya Mushka, along with their son Nechemia Sholom Rephael and daughter Rivka, later left Russia during the 5706-7 mass emigration of Chabad chassidim.
