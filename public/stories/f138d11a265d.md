# Tomchei Tmimim yeshiva opens in Romny under R' Yani Gurarie

*1925*

Having earlier lived in Kremenchug, R' Yani Gurarie came to Romny in 5685 to establish an underground branch of Tomchei Tmimim during the Soviet anti-religious crackdown. The yeshiva operated for three years amid escalating harassment from the Yevsektsiya.
