# R' Leib Baal HaYisurin passes away in Tzfat

*1836*

R' Leib Baal HaYisurin, one of the early Chabad chassidim in the Holy Land, relocated to Tzfat in his final years. He passed away there on 15 Cheshvan 5597, only weeks before the great earthquake claimed the lives of others of the small Chabad community in the city.
