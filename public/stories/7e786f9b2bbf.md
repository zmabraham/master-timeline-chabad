# Rebbe and family relocate from Otwock to Warsaw under Latvian diplomatic cover

*1939*

With trains halted and roads under bombardment, the Latvian Embassy dispatched its diplomatic vehicle twice. Chaim Lieberman left with part of the family on Monday, and the Rebbe followed the next day, taking valuable historic manuscripts but leaving the bulk of his library behind in Otwock. A tearful crowd bade him farewell as he blessed them, saying 'A king guards his subjects.'
