# Rabbi Havlin. Rabbi Yosef Yerachmiel Havlin (born 5736, 1975)

*1975*

Rabbi Havlin. Rabbi Yosef Yerachmiel Havlin (born 5736, 1975) is the Rebbe's shliach in Frankfurt, Germany, and also serves as rabbi of the city and av beit din of the community, and head of Tomchei Tmimim Yeshiva Frankfurt. Biography: edit source.
