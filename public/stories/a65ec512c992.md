# Rabbi Chaim Yitzchak Cohen blesses the Rebbe with the priestly blessing. Rabbi Chaim

*1952*

Rabbi Chaim Yitzchak Cohen blesses the Rebbe with the priestly blessing. Rabbi Chaim Yitzchak Cohen (born 5712, 1952) is the director of the Beis Moshiach Center London and the initiator of the 'European Moshiach Congress'
