# Esther bat Yitzchak Adhan renounces inheritance for her dowry

*1708*

On 25 Iyar 5468 (1708) in Fez, Esther bat the late R. Yitzchak ben Mordechai Adhan, confirmed to be of full age, formally relinquished every claim and right she had against her mother Alaz and her brothers in exchange for the dowry they were providing for her marriage. She additionally granted them by way of an open, public, healthy-person's gift all the documents, notes, and remaining property from her father's estate, on land and movables alike. The deed was signed in Fez by R. Shlomo Amiel and R. Moshe ben Makhluf ben Maman, written up on 19 Sivan 5468 at the request of Yaakov ben Atar, who had a stake in the underlying mechilah.
