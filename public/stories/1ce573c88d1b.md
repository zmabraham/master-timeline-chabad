# Chabad library rescued from occupied Poland to New York

*1941*

After the German invasion of Poland and the Rayatz's own escape, his library and manuscript collection were extracted from occupied territory in 5701 (1940-1941) and shipped to New York. From that point forward the books rested in the Rebbe's quarters at 770 Eastern Parkway, where they have remained the heart of the Chabad library.
