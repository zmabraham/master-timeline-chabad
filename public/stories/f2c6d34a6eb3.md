# 39th wedding anniversary of Maharashab and Rebbetzin Sterna Sarah

*1914*

On 12 Elul 5674, the 39th anniversary of the wedding of Maharashab and Sterna Sarah, Rebbe Yosef Yitzchak recorded a heartfelt blessing in his diary that they should be granted length of days and years in pleasantness, true health, and great nachas from their children.
