# Yezhovshchina terror disrupts underground yeshiva networks

*1937*

During the height of Stalin's Great Terror in 5697, the NKVD arrested people directly off the streets and deported them to Siberia, creating a 'terrible fear' Israel Yehuda could no longer endure. He fled to Moscow before Purim to consult R' Yonah Cohen, returning to Voronezh only for Passover, where the group baked matzot with utmost stringency under R' Betzalel Wilshansky's supervision.
