# Rebbe Rashab delivers maamar in the small zal, Shabbos Noach

*1907*

After Kabbalas Shabbos on Parshas Noach, the Rebbe Rashab entered the kleiner zal where he then customarily said Chassidus. The Rayatz arrived first in Shabbos garb and shtreimel, and bachurim sang niggunim including the famed Yedid Nefesh of Lubavitch. Sudden silence fell as the Rebbe entered and delivered the maamar — the author's first such experience.
