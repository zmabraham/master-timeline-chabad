# Rebbe writes to Zevin urging protest against Leningrad Jewish conference

*1927*

Despite the suffering he endured in Russia for opposing the Leningrad Jewish conference, the Rebbe dispatched a detailed action plan from Riga to disrupt it. The conference, organized by the Leningrad Jewish community and surreptitiously supported by the Yevsektzia, targeted nine key cities including Moscow and Kutaisi. The plan succeeded and the conference was never convened.
