# Passing of grandfather R. Avraham Shlomo, shochet of Zhuravichi

*1901*

R. Avraham Shlomo, who had been a chasid of the Tzemach Tzedek, the Maharash, and the Rashab, passed away on 25 Elul 5661. He had served as shochet in Paritch before being pressed by his family to return to Zhuravichi after his father's death. He left behind his widow Bracha and three sons: Yaakov Leib, Yisrael Yeshaya, and Yosef Hillel.
