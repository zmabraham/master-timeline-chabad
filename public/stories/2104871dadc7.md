# Rabbinical Conference convenes in Petersburg

*1843*

After the Third Section's 1841 investigation and Dr. Max Lilienthal's personal visit to Lyubavichi, the government insisted on the Tzemach Tzedek's attendance to neutralize his anticipated opposition to its educational reforms. The Rebbe brought along one of his sons and a Chassid, and packed some thirty seforim with paper to record chiddushim during his stay. The conference's deliberations were kept secret — Beis Rebbi records that the Tzemach Tzedek refused to disclose what transpired, saying he 'had not been given permission to reveal' them.
