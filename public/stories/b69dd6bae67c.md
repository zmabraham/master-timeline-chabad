# Mitteler Rebbe prints Siddur Im Da"Ch and Biurei HaZohar in Kapust

*1816*

Following his father's passing, the Mitteler Rebbe brought to press in Kapust the Siddur Im Da"Ch—pairing the Alter Rebbe's siddur with related chassidic discourses—and the 139-folio first edition of Biurei HaZohar. These two volumes anchored the early posthumous publication of the Alter Rebbe's chassidic teachings.
