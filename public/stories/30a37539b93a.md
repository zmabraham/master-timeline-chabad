# Rebbe Rashab arrives in Rostov-on-Don

*1915*

Leaving Lubavitch on 16 Marcheshvan, the Rashab's party traveled some 29 hours to Orel, remained there until 25 Marcheshvan, then took the express train and arrived in Rostov at 10 a.m. on Wednesday, 26 Marcheshvan 5676. His internal Russian passport was stamped two days later, on 23 October 1915 (Julian) — 28 Marcheshvan. Rostov would remain the seat of Chabad leadership for the rest of his life.
