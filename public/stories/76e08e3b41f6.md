# Kulashi students arrested by drunk Communist officials at night

*1933*

Walking home after a drinking party in Shevat 5693, the local officials noticed the elevated lamp glowing in the closed shul and burst in to arrest the students. They were held overnight in freezing police cells where local women came in the morning to weep at seeing them through the cracks, then transferred to the district prison in Samtredia where the warden slapped the tall author across the face on arrival.
