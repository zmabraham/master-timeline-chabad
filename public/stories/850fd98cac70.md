# Rabbi Mendel Futerfas arrested ending the Great Escape

*1947*

When remaining Chasidim attempted to cross on a regular passenger train, agents circulated onboard and arrested Futerfas at the border. The arrests began a roundup that ensnared other organizers including Sara Katzenelenbogen, forcing the Lvov network to scatter deep into Russia to evade the secret police. Futerfas would be sentenced to nine years in prison.
