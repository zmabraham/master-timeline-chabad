# Manuscript of Darkei Moshe and Maharshal's glosses copied in Szydłów

*1641*

In 5401 (1641), a scribe in Szydłów, near Krakow, completed copying a manuscript volume containing two works of glosses on the Tur and Beit Yosef: the long recension of Rema's Darkei Moshe, and Maharshal's glosses on the Tur (which were never independently printed but were later incorporated into the Derisha, Perisha, and Bach).
