# Mailufim volume of Tzemach Tzedek edited by the Rebbe

*1946*

Eighty years after the Tzemach Tzedek's passing, the previously blocked Choshen Mishpat responsa finally appeared in print. The Milu'im volume edited by the future Rebbe included Choshen Mishpat teshuvos, supplements across all four chelkei Shulchan Aruch, additions to the chiddushim on Shas, and further omissions discovered during typesetting.
