# Death of R' Shilem Kuratin

*1919*

R' Shilem Kuratin, mashgiach for chassidut studies at Tomchei Tmimim from winter 5672, continued in his post when the yeshiva moved to Kremenchug. He passed away on 5 Elul 5679 (1919) in Kremenchug at a relatively young age. The Rebbe Rashab was not told of his passing. R' Alter Simchowitz was later appointed in his place.
