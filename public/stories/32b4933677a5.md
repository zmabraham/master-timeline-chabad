# Tomchei Temimim relocates to Poltava

*1922*

Unable to obtain authorization to travel by train, the students made the long trip by wagon. The Rebbe appointed Rabbi Yechezkel (Chatche) Feigin as administrator of the central yeshivah in Poltava. The local Chabad community of some twenty thousand Jews was diverse and better organized, and the Yevsektzia had less influence there.
