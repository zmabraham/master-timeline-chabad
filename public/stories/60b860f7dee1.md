# Rashab departs Lubavitch ahead of German advance

*1915*

On October 25, 1915 (17 Cheshvan 5676), the Rashab and his family departed Lubavitch in the face of the advancing German army during World War I. With his departure, Lubavitch's 102-year run as the capital of the Chabad chasidic movement came to an end. The Rashab never returned to Lubavitch, though the yeshivah remained in the town for another two years.
