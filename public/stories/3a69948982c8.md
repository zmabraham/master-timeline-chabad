# Haggadah with Yiddish Kabbalistic commentary printed in Frankfurt an der Oder

*1789*

In 5549 (1789), Itzik son of Aharon Zelig, typesetter and printer, produced a Haggadah in Frankfurt an der Oder that featured the original Hebrew alongside a Yiddish translation in parallel columns. Beneath these, a Kabbalistic commentary was printed in square Hebrew letters. The final page additionally contained a Yiddish commentary on Chad Gadya in Tzene-Rene script.
