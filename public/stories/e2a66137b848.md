# German conquest of Riga and murder of R. Yehuda Eber and family

*1941*

The Chassidic scholar R. Yehuda Eber had fled Nazi-occupied Warsaw to Riga, where in early 1941 he served as the conduit for R. Zalman Butman's coded reports to the Rebbe Rayatz in New York. Eber and his household were unable to obtain travel permits from Riga to the United States before the German army overran the city later in 1941. He and his family perished along with the rest of Riga's Jews in the subsequent Nazi massacres.
