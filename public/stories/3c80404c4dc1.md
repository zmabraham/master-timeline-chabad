# R' Yehoshua Kostral writes from San Remo consoling Rosenbaum

*1904*

From San Remo on 7 Shevat 5664, R' Yehoshua Kostral acknowledged Rosenbaum's letter informing him of Yetta's passing. He admitted he scarcely knew the deceased or Rosenbaum's American household and so could hardly comfort him, yet he urged that 'you are not yet old' and that sitting alone is impossible—he should seek a second match.
