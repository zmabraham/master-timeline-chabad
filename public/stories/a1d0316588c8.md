# R' Yehuda Eber reports founding of Mechina to the Rebbe Rayatz

*1931*

R' Yehuda Eber's report described 10–11 boys learning in the new Mechina, attributing low initial numbers to short notice. He predicted growth in subsequent terms. By 20 Kislev there were 13 talmidim, and from 3 Tevet R' Moshe Chaim was teaching them Tanya daily.
