# Mohilev provincial government sends case to Babinovich peace court

*1825*

On 19 December 1825 (21 Tevet 5586), Mohilev authorities determined that the pre-trial inquiry was not yet complete and sent the file to the peace court in Babinovich, the jurisdiction to which Lubavitch belonged. The Babinovich court was tasked with finishing the questioning of witnesses before the case could go to formal trial.
