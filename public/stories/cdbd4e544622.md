# Special delegation brings the first returned Tanya to the Lubavitcher Rebbe

*1993*

At 3:20 PM the entire delegation, with attorney Devorah Rachel Kogan and supporter Albi Rozhouse, approached the Rebbe at the entrance to his room. Cunin placed the Tanya on the table; the Rebbe raised his eyes and gazed at each person passing before him. The delegation then sang and danced in the Gan Eden HaTachton for about an hour.
