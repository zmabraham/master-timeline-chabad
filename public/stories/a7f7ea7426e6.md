# Chabad delegation visits Lubavitch town graves

*1990*

Late on 21 Kislev 5751 the delegation left Moscow by car with Veronika's driver, arriving in Lubavitch at 8 AM on 22 Kislev. They prayed at the ohel for the success of their mission, visited the Chabad house and the site of the courtyard, and observed that only a well — and perhaps the surrounding trees — remained as physical markers of the rebbes' era. They departed at 10 AM and returned to Moscow that night.
