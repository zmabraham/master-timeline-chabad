# Yisrael Yehuda Levin in Pereyaslav after fleeing Vitebsk

*1931*

In 5691 (1931), after escaping from Vitebsk, the author settled for a time in Pereyaslav. There he became close with the rav demata, Rabbi Yehoshua Zelig Diskin, a great scholar of noble character, with whom he later corresponded. Rabbi Diskin wrote on his behalf to Chief Rabbi Avraham Yitzchok Kook in Eretz Yisrael, helping to set in motion paperwork for emigration to the Holy Land.
