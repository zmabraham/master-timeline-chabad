# Rebbe Rayatz cables Jacobson to extract library from Warsaw

*1940*

In early Adar Sheni 5700 (early March 1940), still in Riga, the Rebbe Rayatz sent a telegram to Rabbi Yisrael Jacobson urgently instructing him to do whatever possible to extract his library and personal effects from Warsaw. He asked Jacobson to also enlist the assistance of Kremer and Kleinfeld in the rescue effort.
