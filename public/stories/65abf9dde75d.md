# Rebbe cables Kobe committee to delay students' departure to Shanghai

*1941*

On 23 Menachem Av 5701 (August 16, 1941), the Frierdiker Rebbe sent a telegram to the Jewish committee in Kobe asking them to delay the transfer of his Tomchei Tmimim students to Shanghai, as he had obtained Paraguayan and Canadian visas for them that were expected within days. The following day he cabled the Polish ambassador in Japan asking for Canadian visas for 38 students.
