# Jerusalem trustees report partial implementation of Rashab's school ruling

*1903*

After a year of correspondence about how strictly to apply the Rebbe Rashab's ruling, the Jerusalem trustees reported that those violating the standards had now received only a third of their normal portion. They pushed back on the gabbaim's complaint that these recipients had been paid before God-fearing poor families, asserting that the God-fearing poor had in fact been paid first.
