# Martyrdom of Moshe Yehoshua Bzhilianski (Alter of Teplik), Breslov Chassid

*1919*

In 5679 (1919), during the turbulent post-revolutionary period in Ukraine marked by widespread pogroms, the Breslov Chassid Moshe Yehoshua Bzhilianski — known as Alter of Teplik — was killed in sanctification of God's name. He had been working on a Breslov Haggadah called 'Or Zoreach' based on the writings of Rabbi Nachman of Breslov. The Haggadah was eventually published posthumously in Jerusalem in 1927 by his Breslov colleagues.
