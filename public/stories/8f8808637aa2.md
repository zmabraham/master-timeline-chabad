# Exhibition on the Rebbe Rayatz

*2000*

In the summer of 5760 (2000), the Lubavitch Library held an exhibition dedicated to the sixth Rebbe, Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn (Rayatz). Exhibits drew on his manuscripts, the spodik he wore, his writing implements and items from his secretariat. It was the eighth in the library's series of themed exhibits.
