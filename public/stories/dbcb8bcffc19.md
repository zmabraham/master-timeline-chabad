# Rabbi Eli Beitelman - Rabbi Eliyahu (Eli) Beitelman, 5743, 1983

*2023*

Rabbi Eli Beitelman - Rabbi Eliyahu (Eli) Beitelman, 5743, 1983 - 16 Iyar 5783, 2023) was a Chabad chassid from Los Angeles, California, who served as the Rebbe's emissary in Pacific Palisades in western California, and had a tremendous influence on hundreds of young people
