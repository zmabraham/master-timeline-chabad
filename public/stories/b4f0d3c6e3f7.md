# Veronika returns; Gorbachev delays seforim decision

*1991*

On 26 Tammuz 5751, Veronika returned from Eretz Yisrael through Cyprus expecting to meet Chernyaev with news on returning the seforim. Instead she only reached Tumarkin, who admitted the matter was unresolved and that Gorbachev had told Chernyaev, 'when we return to Moscow we'll talk.' Alarmed, she directed the delegation to escalate pressure from abroad.
