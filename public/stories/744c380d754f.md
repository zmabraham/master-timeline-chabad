# Wedding of Yaakov Weisblach of Kishinev at Moscow AGUH shul

*1991*

The groom led Chabad activity in Kishinev; the bride came from Machon Chaya Mushka. Reception and chuppah were held at the AGUH synagogue and the seudah at Hotel Kosmos, with visiting bochurim and seminary girls (the summer-camp counselors) leading the dancing. The author and his wife served as shushvinim and the chosson declared in his speech that it was the most beautiful wedding he had ever seen.
