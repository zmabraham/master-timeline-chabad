# Rashab names yeshivah 'Tomchei Temimim' on Simchat Torah

*1898*

After dancing intensely around the bimah, the Rashab paused the singing and declared: 'The yeshivah that was established by the grace of G-d doesn't have a name. Now I am naming it Tomchei Temimim.' The name derived from the verses recited during the seventh hakafah. Students and alumni would henceforth be called 'temimim.'
