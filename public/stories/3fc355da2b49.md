# District court inspector reports completion of Mitteler Rebbe inquiries

*1826*

In a brief dispatch dated 17 Iyar 5586, Kilikovsky, inspector of the Babinovich district court, informed the governor-general that all the depositions and inquiries needed for the case concerning Rabbi Berke Schneur of Lubavitch's alleged unlawful activities had been received from the various localities. The materials had been added to the case file and surveyed, advancing the proceedings toward a decision.
