# 1837 Galilee earthquake kills Chabad chassidim in Tzfat

*1837*

Even after most Chabad chassidim had relocated to Hebron, a handful remained in Tzfat. The catastrophic Galilee earthquake of 24 Tevet 5597 (January 1, 1837) killed several of them, including R' Shemaya and R' Lapidot Chalavna and his son Shalom Aharon. After the earthquake only a few Chabad families lingered in Tzfat for another two years.
