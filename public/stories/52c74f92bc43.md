# Death of R' Shmuel Halevi, first Chabad rabbi of Hebron

*1849*

R' Shmuel Halevi Segal, who had served as maggid and de facto rabbi of the Hebron Chabad community since his arrival in 5584, died at some point between 5609 and 5615. In the 5609 census he is still listed among the community appointees, but in the 5615 census only his widow appears. His son R' Menachem Aryeh succeeded him as a leading figure of the community and served as a dayan.
