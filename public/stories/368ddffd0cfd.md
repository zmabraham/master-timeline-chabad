# Passing of the author on 17 Sivan 5735

*1975*

Across the 35 years between the Frierdiker Rebbe's arrival in America and his own passing, the author served as rav in the Brownsville community, member of the Agudas Chassidei Chabad leadership, and dealer in Calabrian etrogim. After 5700 he took on additional roles as mashpia at central Tomchei Tmimim, head of Beis Rivkah, general gabbai of the Chevra Kadisha, rav of the Lubavitch kehilla in New York, organizer of Tomchei Tmimim in Newark, and founder and mashpia of Hadar HaTorah. He maintained this vigorous, multi-front work until his final day.
