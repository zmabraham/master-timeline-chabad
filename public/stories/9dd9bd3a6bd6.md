# Senate in Petersburg upholds acquittal of the Mitteler Rebbe

*1827*

On 28 November 1827, the Governing Senate in Petersburg issued its decision on Khovansky's 25 May appeal of the Mogilev high court ruling acquitting Rabbi Dovber Schneuri of charges of illegal fundraising. Reviewing testimony from the Babinavichi community, 5 other communities, and 160 sworn witnesses — all confirming that the 30,000-ruble post-1812 collection and ongoing support were given voluntarily, and that the rebbe served as a preacher rather than as a salaried, tax-collecting rabbi — the Senate found Kisin's denunciation legally unfounded. It ordered the rebbe fully acquitted and referred Khovansky's broader proposals on Russian rabbis to its First Department. The decree was received in Mogilev on 12 December 1827.
