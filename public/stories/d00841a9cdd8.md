# Prison letter from R' Avraham Chaim to R' Zev Wolf Kugel

*1889*

Writing from the Smolensk prison on 25 Tishrei 5650, R' Avraham Chaim told the grandson of R' Hendel that he could not yet describe his physical state, but that he expected good news within about eight months. He played on the gematria of the Hebrew month-name letters and the phrase mattir asurim, encoding his redemption hope into the date itself.
