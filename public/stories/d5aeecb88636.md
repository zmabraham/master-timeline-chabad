# Feigin and Eber appeal for Petsamo route via Rashag

*1940*

On Erev Rosh Chodesh Sivan 5700, with the Italian route closed, R. Yechezkel Feigin and R. Yehuda Eber wrote a joint letter to the Rashag describing their plight. They had been ready to travel five times already, all foiled. The only remaining option was via Petsamo in Finland, but Finland had refused entry permits; they urged the Rashag to leverage US senators or the Finnish consul to intercede.
