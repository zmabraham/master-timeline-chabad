# Scarlet fever epidemic strikes Lubavitch

*1916*

During the winter of 5676 scarlet fever spread through Lubavitch and children died daily, a calamity locals linked to the homemade liquor production prompted by the government ban. The town rav, R' David Yaakovson, declared a ban on the illicit distilling. One yeshiva student from Homel named Aharon died; his fellow students sat with him around the clock and buried him in Lubavitch.
