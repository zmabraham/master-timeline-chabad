# Rebbe contrasts Chabad and Polish chasidism in letter on identity

*1938*

He wrote, 'One of the fundamental principles of Chabad is that it teaches its followers to be deeply engaged in the avodah of Chasidism. In contrast, for the Polish Chasidim, the main point is the connection to the Rebbe.' The letter framed Chabad's focus on internalizing Torah ideals through one's own effort, contrasting the Polish emphasis on the bond with the tzaddik.
