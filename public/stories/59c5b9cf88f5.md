# Leah Beila Levin writes Rebbe Rayatz after being injured in food line

*1935*

On Tuesday of Parshas Yisro 5695 (around January 22, 1935), Leah Beila Levin—widow of R. Ephraim Shmuel and mother of the author—wrote a letter to the Rebbe Rayatz, now preserved in the archives. She described being shoved by a non-Jew while waiting on a sidewalk in a Soviet food queue, falling and gashing her head open and requiring stitches. She wrote of her hope for redemption and ascribed her survival to the merit of her orphaned children.
