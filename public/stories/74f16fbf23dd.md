# Shemtov marries Esther Golda in Siberian exile

*1930*

Esther Golda Futerfus made the long trek to Siberia in summer 1930 to join her fiancé. The wedding was held in Yanivskiek, about fifty miles from the railroad; lacking a mikveh, she hacked through an icy river to immerse. With no ring, Shemtov fashioned one from a silver fork, and exiled chasidic rabbi Shmuel Levitin officiated.
