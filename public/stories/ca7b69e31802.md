# Rebbe rebuffs general rabbi's opposition in Globoke

*1930*

The general kehilla's rabbi opposed Kuperstock's appointment, wanting to reserve the Chabad rabbinate for his son-in-law. The Rebbe Rayatz, in a letter dated 8 Tishrei 5691 (Igros Kodesh vol. 17, letter 6276), wrote that he had not expected such agitation and stressed that the chassidic community must not surrender its longstanding chazakah over its own rabbinate.
