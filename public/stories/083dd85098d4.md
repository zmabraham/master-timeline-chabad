# Rosenbaum writes his will mentioning the Tzemach Tzedek's tallis

*1911*

Writing his will in 5671 (1911), Rosenbaum noted that he still possessed the tallis of the Tzemach Tzedek, acquired in full payment from the meshulach R. Madil after he personally identified it as the one in which the Rebbe had prayed. He admitted uncertainty whether the tzitzis on it were the originals or replacements made by R. Madil, and explained that out of awe he could not bring himself to wear another tzaddik's tallis in this world.
