# SILA writes to Rebbe Rashab about Bukhara shlichut

*1907*

Less than a year after returning to Chevron, R' Shneur Zalman Ladier ('SILA') wrote to the Rebbe Rashab on 14 Tevet 5667 explaining that disputes over Kolel Chabad leadership and personal pressures made permanent settlement in Eretz Yisrael impossible. He asked permission to undertake another shlichut, this time for the Sephardi community to Bukhara. He estimated the mission would take eight months.
