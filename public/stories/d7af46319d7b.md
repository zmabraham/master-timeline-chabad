# Yeshaya Chanoch Berchen becomes rabbi of Gostini

*1935*

According to the volume 'Latvia: Synagogues and Rabbis' (Riga 2005), Yeshaya Chanoch Berchen was installed as rabbi of Gostini on 11 January 1935 (26 Tevet 5695). The town had two Chassidic shuls and two for the misnagdim. He would soon lead the Chabad yeshiva there and serve as a key node in the Latvia branch network.
