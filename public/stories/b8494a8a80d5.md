# Chabad chassidim relocate from Tiberias to Tzfat

*1800*

Chabad chassidim relocated from Tiberias to Tzfat in the years 5560-5565, establishing Tzfat as their principal center until the Mitteler Rebbe later redirected them to Hebron. The move set the stage for two decades of Chabad presence in Tzfat before the 1837 earthquake.
