# Bar mitzvah of Shalom Dovber Gourary, grandson of the Rebbe Rayatz

*1936*

The celebration was held in the yeshiva hall at Otwock, drawing guests from Poland, Riga, Vienna and elsewhere. Sichos delivered at the farbrengen were later printed in Likkutei Dibburim vol. 2. A surviving photograph shows the Rebbe Rayatz at the head table with the bar mitzvah boy, his father R' Shmaryahu Gourary, and the Rebbe's secretary R' Yechezkel Feigin.
