# Rebbe declares 'U'fartzta' expansion on 12 Tammuz

*1958*

In a sicha on 12 Tammuz 5718 (summer 1958), the Rebbe announced the campaign of 'U'fartzta' — bursting through all barriers in spreading chassidus and Judaism. This call, repeated throughout 5718-5719, marked the beginning of a major expansion in the shlichus enterprise (Igros Kodesh vol. 18 letter 6541).
