# Future Rebbe makes inventory of Rayatz's manuscript holdings

*1930*

The list, in the future Rebbe's own hand, notes that pages 1 and 2 are copied from the general index while page 3 records bichlach not found in the index but seen personally — possibly added after the index was compiled. The numbering on the right matches the order arranged by Rashab himself. This list aligns with the 265 bichlach catalogued in Rayatz's general index, suggesting the future Rebbe compiled it after Rayatz entrusted the manuscripts to him.
