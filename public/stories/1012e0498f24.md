# R' Moshe Meisels asks Mitteler Rebbe about aliyah

*1814*

Between 1814 and 1815, R' Moshe Meisels asked the Mitteler Rebbe about settling in Eretz Yisrael. The Rebbe advised against the move at the time and said he would not allocate Chassidic charity funds beyond fifty gold coins for travel expenses for him and R' Baruch Mordechai, rabbi of Bobruisk.
