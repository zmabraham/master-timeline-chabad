# Second marriage of R. Oved ibn Attar, son of Rabbi Yehuda

*1734*

On the night of Tuesday, 18 Av 5494 (~July 19, 1734), Rabbi Oved ibn Attar — son of the late Rabbi Yehuda ibn Attar — was married for the second time. The wedding entry refers to Rabbi Yehuda with the honorific 'savah de-mishpatim' (elder of judgments) and as a 'hasid,' 'light of Israel,' indicating Rabbi Yehuda had already passed away (he died in 5493).
