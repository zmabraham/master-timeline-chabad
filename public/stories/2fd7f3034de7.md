# Kfar Chabad Haggadah with 'Pesach BiTzva'im' children's booklet

*1987*

In 5747 (1987), Menachem Mendel Brod edited a 40-page Haggadah printed in blue letters in Kfar Chabad, accompanied by a 22-leaf 'Pesach BiTzva'im' (Pesach in Color) booklet with photographs and illustrations for the Seder night. The duodecimo edition served Chabad children visually and pedagogically. It exemplified Chabad's emphasis on visual learning for Pesach.
