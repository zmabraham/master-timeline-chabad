# Rashab abandons Lubavitch and relocates family to Rostov-on-Don

*1915*

After 102 years as home to four Chabad Rebbes, the sun set on Lubavitch. The historic Chabad library was packed into thirty-five crates and placed in storage in Moscow, while the Rashab took valuable manuscripts with him. The Rashab told the Chasidim 'We hope to return... before Passover' but they never returned. They settled in Rostov-on-Don, 700 miles south of Moscow.
