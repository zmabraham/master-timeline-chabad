# R. Israel Jacobson receives Soviet exit papers in Homel

*1925*

Jacobson's parents in America had sent him a rabbinic invitation from a synagogue in their Brooklyn neighborhood, which the U.S. consul in Riga needed to verify before issuing a visa. With the passport in hand, he boarded a carriage to the train station with his wife and daughters Chaya Sara and Rachel Dvasha, and was seen off at the station by Anash including his brother-in-law R. Aharon Eliezer and R. Zalman Chasdan. He traveled with French Line, hoping the company would extend him credit toward Riga since he still lacked the full ticket fare.
