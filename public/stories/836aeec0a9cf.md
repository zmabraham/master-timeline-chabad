# Khovansky orders police to bring the Mitteler Rebbe for interrogation

*1825*

Order No. 4767 instructed Korbicki, together with Chevkin and another officer, to travel via Lubavitch, Babinovichi, and Shklov to bring the Rebbe to Vitebsk. Khovansky framed the summons as a private meeting on "several matters" rather than an arrest warrant. According to Beis Rebbi, the Rebbe was actually taken from his home in Lubavitch immediately after Sukkot 5586.
