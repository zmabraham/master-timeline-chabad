# Rebbe Rayatz's third visit to Warsaw

*1931*

On 9 Tamuz 5691 (June 24, 1931), the Rebbe Rayatz left Riga on his third trip to Warsaw, this time to inspect the three Tomchei Tmimim yeshivot under his leadership in Warsaw, Vilna, and Lodz. He summarized the journey in a letter dated 5 Menachem Av of that year.
