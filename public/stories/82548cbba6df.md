# Rabbi Hutner sends third letter defending his position to Rebbe

*1968*

On 13 Shevat 5728 (Feb 12, 1968), Rabbi Hutner sent his third reply, opening with thanks for 'a second utterance' from the Rebbe while reiterating his prior reservations. He elaborated his mit'asek argument with proofs from Rabbi Akiva Eiger §8, the Chavat Daat, the Alter Rebbe's Kuntres Acharon on safek p'sik reisha, and Tosafot Shabbat 11. In a postscript he relayed Rabbi Zalmanov's recent report of seeing tinokot shenishbu offering both hands for tefillin — illustrating, in his view, how complete their unfamiliarity could be.
