# Transfer of Fez mortgage deed from Yeshua ibn Zazon to R' Yaakov Halevi

*1717*

On 25 Kislev 5478, Yeshua transferred all his rights in the pawn deed to R' Yaakov Halevi, swearing he had not collected on it and accepting responsibility for any future challengers including R' Shlomo Cabalero. Witnessed by Yaakov ibn Attar and Yaakov HaTzarfati in Fez.
