# Dispute between R. Shimon Zarchi and R. Yosef Ashkenazi of Yakobstadt

*1836*

R. Shimon Zarchi, author of Nachalas Shimon and rabbi of Yakobstadt, and R. Yosef Ashkenazi, author of Ohel Yosef and then a rosh metivta in Yakobstadt, clashed in 5596 over several halachic matters. They submitted their reasoning to the Tzemach Tzedek, who responded with multiple rulings adjudicating between them (Orach Chaim §§43, 75; Yoreh Deah §217; Choshen Mishpat §85).
