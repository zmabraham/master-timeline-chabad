# Rashab's letter on building a Jewish library in Riga

*1902*

The Rashab urged that since maskilim maintained great libraries for non-religious purposes, a God-fearing Jew should fund this acquisition. He proposed that R. Yeshaya Berlin dedicate 3-4 rooms of his large house in Riga for the library and a study hall, so observant Jews could come to learn. The purchase ultimately did not materialize.
