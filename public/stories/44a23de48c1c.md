# R' Shneur Zalman Rosenbaum's American shliach mission for Tomchei Tmimim

*1905*

R' Avraham Chaim wrote on Rosh Chodesh Adar Sheni 5665 (1905) that his brother R' Shneur Zalman had arrived in New York as an emissary of the Lubavitcher Rebbe to raise funds for the yeshivos. R' Shneur Zalman traveled through New York, Philadelphia, New Haven, Hartford, Springfield, and Boston, and R' Avraham Chaim asked whether he should also visit Rochester. The mission supported the four-city yeshiva network that had been founded six years earlier.
