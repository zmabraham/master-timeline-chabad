# Rebbe Rashab appoints R' Yisrael Dovber Frumkin as Kollel Chabad trustee

*1906*

Hoping that Frumkin's character and standing would help reform the kollel's distribution practices in line with the policy he had set out the same day in a parallel letter to Frumkin, the Rebbe instructed the existing trustees to admit Frumkin to the vaad and make no decisions without him. The Jerusalem trustees and the rebbes of Lyadi and Bobruisk resisted the appointment, and after months of dispute it was unclear whether Frumkin ever effectively served.
