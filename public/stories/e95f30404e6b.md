# Jacobson family crosses Soviet–Latvian border at Sebezh

*1925*

The crossing came at sunset, and only when Latvian officials and railway staff boarded the train did the family begin to grasp that they were physically out of the Soviet Union. Even then the dread of being seized back on some pretext lingered—it could not yet be felt in the heart that the escape from Bolshevik rule was real.
