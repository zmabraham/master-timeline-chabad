# Jerusalem facsimile reissue of the Vienna 1813 Alshich Haggadah

*1975*

In 5735 (1975), a facsimile reproduction of the 1813 Vienna Haggadah was issued in Jerusalem with commentaries by Moshe Alshich, Yehuda Loew (Maharal) of Prague's 'Gevurot Hashem', and 'Olelot Efraim'. The 52-leaf folio came with an accompanying introduction. It made classical Ashkenazic exegesis on the Haggadah more accessible to modern readers.
