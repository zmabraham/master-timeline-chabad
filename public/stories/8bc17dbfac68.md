# Death of Rabbi Yeshaya Meshulam Zusya Twersky

*1881*

Rabbi Yeshaya Meshulam Zusya of the Chernobyl dynasty died in 5641. Letters he co-signed with his brother Baruch Asher and his nephew Mordechai of Loyev are therefore dateable to the window 5636–5641 — after Menachem Nachum of Loyev's death but before his own.
