# HaKohen Kook, initiated and founded together with his brother-in-law Rabbi Tzvi Yehuda Kook,

*1942*

HaKohen Kook, initiated and founded together with his brother-in-law Rabbi Tzvi Yehuda Kook, Rabbi Meir Berlin, and others, the association for publishing the writings of HaRaayah Kook, in which he served as director and as one of the editors of the writings
