# Przemyśl Haggadah 'Bet HaYayin' by Meir Yehuda Libush Langerman of Turka

*1898*

Around 5658 (1898), the Haggadah 'Sefer Bet HaYayin' by Meir Yehuda Libush Langerman, head of the rabbinical court of Turka, was issued in Przemyśl by Zupnik, Knoller, and Wolf. The 52-leaf quarto featured 'Likkutim Tilpiot' built largely on the teachings of Rebbe Avraham Yaakov of Sadigura. It represented the Sadigura-Ruzhin Chassidic tradition's interpretation of the Passover liturgy.
