# Rebbe Rayatz moves to Warsaw

*1933*

The Rebbe had delayed the move until obtaining a Latvian passport, which would later prove lifesaving during the Nazi invasion. He spent the summer at Marienbad, holidays at Otwock, and then settled in Warsaw to be near the flagship Tomchei Temimim yeshivah. Feigin wrote that the Rebbe "returned to his old self," delivering long maamarim again.
