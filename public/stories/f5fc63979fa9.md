# R. Elchanan Dov Marozov appointed mashgiach of Chassidus

*1908*

Earlier in the year R. Shlomo Risin of Rogachov had served as mashgiach of Chassidus in the great hall. Mid-winter 5668 saw R. Elchanan Dov Marozov appointed in his stead. R. Eliezer Kaplan, head of the yeshiva office, came to the large zal to read aloud a letter from the Rebbe Rashab announcing the appointment.
