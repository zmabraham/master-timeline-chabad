# Rebbe welcomes new Vilna arrivals and urges them to summon others

*1940*

Receiving Greenglas's report that the three bochurim had reached Vilna, the Rebbe replied on 8 Shevat 5700 with effusive blessing and a pressing instruction: write at once to every remaining talmid scattered across Poland to come to Vilna without delay. He demanded a list of names contacted, warning that 'the time is exceedingly precious.'
