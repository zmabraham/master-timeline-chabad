# Rosenbaum imprisoned in Smolensk; permitted religious articles in jail

*1888*

In the summer of 5648 (1888), the Chabad chassid Reb Avraham Chaim Rosenbaum was sent to prison in Smolensk. From his arrival the authorities permitted him to bring religious articles into the cell. He would remain there nearly two years, conducting an extensive correspondence with chassidim and family members.
