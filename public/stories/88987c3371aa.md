# Yechidus in Rostov on emigration to America

*1923*

The author traveled to the Rebbe Rayatz in Rostov for Shavuot 5683, where the Rebbe delivered chassidus and R' Yechezkel Feigin served as chozer at the chazarah following Shacharis. In a yechidus after the holiday, after noting that his parents and siblings had already emigrated to the United States, the author received the Rebbe's answer that going to America was 'very right, one must travel, do not be ashamed.'
