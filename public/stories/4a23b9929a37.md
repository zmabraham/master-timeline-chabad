# R' David Mondshine. Rabbi David Mondshine (born 5730)

*1970*

R' David Mondshine. Rabbi David Mondshine (born 5730, 1970) is one of the Rebbe's shluchim in Moscow, Russia, CEO of the Or Avner global education organization, member of the leadership of the Federation of Jewish Communities of Russia, and heads the 'Leviev Fund.'
