# 'Igud Talmidei HaTemimim' association founded

*1934*

In 5694 (1933/34) the Igud Talmidei HaTemimim — Union of Tomchei Tmimim Students — was established, with R' Shneur Zalman Gurarie at its head. The association coordinated alumni and student activities and would, the following year, launch the Chabad periodical HaTamim. It also took responsibility for printing the Rebbe Rayatz's discourses and addresses.
