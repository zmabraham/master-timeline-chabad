# Alter Rebbe writes Tishrei 1811 epistle on polished shechitah knives

*1811*

The letter (Iggeret 61) reports R. Chaim of Volozhin's 1803 disclosure and lists the many regions and communities that had by then accepted the polished knives — including Ukraine, parts of Poland, Volyn, and the Holy Land. The enactment, instituted at the Maggid's beit midrash, eventually became universal Jewish practice.
