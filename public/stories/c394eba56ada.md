# Yagdil Torah institute founded on 20 Av

*1976*

At the 20 Menachem Av 5736 (1976) farbrengen, the Rebbe announced the founding of a special institute — with centers in both Jerusalem and New York — to collect novellae of the Lubavitcher Rebbeim and publish them in a special compilation. The publication was later named 'Yagdil Torah.'
