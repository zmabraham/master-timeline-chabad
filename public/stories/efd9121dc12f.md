# Rashab summons Rayatz to discuss future under Communist rule

*1920*

On Friday 8 Adar, with Purim approaching, the Rashab summoned his son. Studying Pri Etz Chaim on Mordechai and Esther, the Rashab made extraordinary predictions about Lenin's strange death, Trotsky's exile and execution, Stalin adapting to the times, and the eventual fall of the Soviet regime. He charged the Rayatz with mesirat nefesh to spread Torah, which proved prophetic when the Rayatz's father passed weeks later.
