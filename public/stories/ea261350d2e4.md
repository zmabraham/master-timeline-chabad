# Chief Rabbis visit Marina Roshcha shul and Chabad yeshiva in Moscow

*1991*

On the morning of 28 Adar 5751, after a brit milah arranged by Rabbi Avraham Genin, the chief rabbis toured Marina Roshcha synagogue with Rabbi Berel Lazar. They examined students Baruch Gorin and Eliyahu Kazatzkov on tractate Gittin and marveled at their progress after less than a year of returning to Jewish learning. The rabbis then ruled, per Halacha, that the Schneersohn library must be returned to Chabad immediately, and recited Psalms 140 and 144 at the Lenin Library to hasten the redemption of the books.
