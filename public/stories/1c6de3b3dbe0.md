# Yeltsin's adviser proposes raising books at the Congress

*1992*

Senator Gore reported that the new Russian ambassador to Washington had called him saying Yeltsin would return the books after the April 6 Congress and asked Chabad not to make noise. Gore was skeptical that promises tied to upcoming summits would be honored. Meanwhile Shakhrai's aide told Pirtsova the only way to push the issue through was via the April Congress, preferably through Ambassador Strauss raising it with Shakhrai.
