# R. Avraham of Kalisk writes to Smilian about the aliyah journey

*1778*

In the winter of 5538 (1777-1778), R. Avraham of Kalisk wrote a letter (Likkutei Amarim 29) to the community of Smilian recounting the chassidic caravan's journey from Russia in Adar 5537 and its eventual arrival in Eretz Yisrael. This is one of the earliest documentary sources for that aliyah.
