# R. Menachem Nachum Preger passes away

*1981*

R. Menachem Nachum Preger passed away in 5741 (1980-1981). Originally from Brisk, he had been accepted to Tomchei Tmimim in Otwock in 5698, fled to Vilna at the war's outbreak, and was saved with the group of students who traveled to Japan, Shanghai, and eventually New York.
