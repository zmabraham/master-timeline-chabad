# Nine Tomchei Tmimim students sail from Shanghai toward Canada

*1941*

On Erev Yom Kippur 5702 (September 30, 1941), nine of the senior Tomchei Tmimim students who had received Canadian visas sailed from Shanghai. The ship traveled to San Francisco rather than directly to Canada; from there the students would travel by train across the United States to Montreal under special supervision to prevent them from disembarking on US soil.
