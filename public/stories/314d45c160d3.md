# Delegation delivers mishloach manot to Soviet Foreign Ministry

*1991*

After receiving permission to enter the building, they were told Bessmertnykh was not present; a staff member recorded their names and purpose. Veronika later reported that Deputy Minister Ivanov had quietly come down to identify the delegation in person and afterward arranged a fresh inquiry into the library status. However, the Supreme Council was in session appointing new ministers (with Gubenko expected to be replaced), delaying any new information.
