# Mitteler Rebbe submits written answers to the interrogators

*1825*

On 9 October 1825 (9 Marcheshvan 5586), the Mitteler Rebbe submitted detailed written answers explaining that the 35,000 rubles in question were one-time donations gathered after the family's 60,000-ruble loss when the French burned their property in Liadi during the 1812 invasion. He described fleeing with his father to inner Russia, his father's death in Haditch, and the appeal of Belarus Jews who reached him in Kremenchug asking that he succeed his father as their rabbi. He insisted Chassidim's gifts are voluntary and never fixed taxes, protested that a translator had inflated the sum to 140,000 rubles, and asked to see the original Hebrew document to identify the error. He signed in Hebrew, with Yehuda Yaffe of Kopust signing the Russian version on his behalf.
