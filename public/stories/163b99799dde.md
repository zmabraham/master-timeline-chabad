# Wiener's bibliography of Haggadot published in St. Petersburg

*1901*

In 5661 (1901), the bibliographer Shmuel Wiener published in Petersburg his Reshimat Haggadot-Pesach Shenidpesu B'Khol Artzot Tevel — a list of 909 editions of the Haggadah printed worldwide between 1500 and 1900. It became the foundational bibliographic reference for the Haggadah.
