# 19 Kislev celebrations across Lithuania reported

*1936*

R. Yehoshua Isaac Baruch detailed gatherings in Kovno (with over 200 attendees including students of the local yeshiva), Kelm, Telz, Ponevezh (both shtiblach), Vilkomir, Anikst, and other towns. R. Yisrael Noach Chatzkevitch had come from Kopishok to spend the chag in Kovno; the Rebbe's klali letter for 19 Kislev was relayed by mail to each community.
