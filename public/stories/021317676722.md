# Rebbe writes about Levin's ailing daughter

*1947*

In early winter 5708 Levin's infant daughter took ill and he wrote to the Rebbe. The reply, dated 5 Kislev 5708 and sent in the Rebbe's name, conveyed mazal tov on her birth and a blessing to raise her to Torah, chuppah and good deeds amid material and spiritual ease.
