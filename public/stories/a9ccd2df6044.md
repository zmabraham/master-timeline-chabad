# Acquittal verdict forwarded to Khovansky for confirmation

*1827*

On 9 February 1827 (24 Shevat 5587), the verdict acquitting Rabbi DovBer, now approved by the Mohilev chief prosecutor, was forwarded to Governor Khovansky in Vitebsk. Khovansky was expected to confirm the ruling, but instead would choose to appeal to the Senate in St. Petersburg.
