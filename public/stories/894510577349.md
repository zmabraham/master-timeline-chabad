# Chabad delegation meets Soviet political adviser Yuli Gelman

*1990*

On the evening of 6 Tevet 5751 the delegation moved into Hotel Ukraina and traveled to Yuli Gelman, who advised that the decisive figure was now Dmitry Likhachov, the Russian Federation's chief authority on rare collections. Gelman, then in Moscow for the Congress of People's Deputies, said Likhachov was open to meeting before returning to Leningrad. He also pledged to lobby Jewish deputies at the Congress and to seek a meeting with Gorbachev after the Congress closed.
