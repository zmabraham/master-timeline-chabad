# R' Shneur Zalman Schneersohn (Shazbani) passes away

*1882*

R' Shneur Zalman ben Nachum Yosef Schneersohn (known by his initials Shazbani), author of Nimukei Shazbani and other works, died in the winter of 5642 in Hebron. He left behind several adult daughters and minor orphan sons. The beit din, together with his brothers Chaim Tzvi and Pinchas, appointed two trustees over his estate to act on behalf of the orphans, including reaching settlements with his daughters.
