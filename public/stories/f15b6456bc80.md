# R. Moshe Pinchas Katz writes about visa difficulties

*1939*

On 11 Adar 5699 (March 2, 1939), R. Moshe Pinchas Katz wrote to R. Yosef Avraham Kagan about the difficulty of obtaining a US visa. He had visited the consulate the previous week with an American yeshiva student, hoping that the citizen sponsor might receive favorable consideration, and the consul had promised a quick response, but the waiting was agonizing.
