# Tzemach Tzedek's sons recommend R. M.Z. Neimark to Horki community

*1860*

The sons of the Tzemach Tzedek sent a letter to the leaders of the Horki community asking them to strengthen the salary of R. Mushulam Zalman Neimark, their moreh tzedek, lest he look elsewhere. They emphasized that competent halachic authorities expert in Chasidus were rare in those times.
