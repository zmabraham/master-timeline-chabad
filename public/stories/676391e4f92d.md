# Severe financial deterioration at Tomchei Temimim Warsaw in summer 1938

*1938*

By summer 5698, the yeshiva's secretariat was barely functioning. The chief secretary R. Yosef Avraham Kagan was preparing to leave for London, and his colleague R. Moshe Pinchas Katz had married and was no longer working. R. Yehuda Eber lamented to the Rebbe that income had collapsed and no suitable replacements were available.
