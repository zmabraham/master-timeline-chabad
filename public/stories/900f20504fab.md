# Babinovichi peace court begins hearing Mitteler Rebbe case

*1826*

On January 13, 1826, the Babinovichi peace court opened its examination of the case against Rabbi DovBer of Lubavitch. The court would labor over the file for more than six months before issuing its decision in July 1826. Its conclusions were then forwarded to the Mogilev High Court for review, eventually leading to the Rebbe's complete acquittal.
