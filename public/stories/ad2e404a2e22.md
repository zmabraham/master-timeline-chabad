# Rebbe Rayatz letter on visiting Lubavitch graves

*1928*

On 5 Cheshvan 5689 (September 19, 1928), the Rebbe Rayatz wrote to his uncle Rabbi Moshe Leib Ginsburg about restoration efforts at the ohel of the Tzemach Tzedek and the Rebbe Maharash. R' Yechiel Chazan had written that the ohel needed major repairs. The Rebbe asked how much the repairs would cost so he could help arrange them.
