# Rebbe Rashab returns to Lubavitch after Pesach 5674

*1914*

The Rebbe Rashab returned to Lubavitch after Pesach 5674, ending the absence that had begun the previous Kislev. On Shavuos he resumed delivering the Hemshech that he had begun on Shavuos Tarav ("BeSha'a SheHikdimu"), continuing without break until Shabbos Parshas Vayera 5676.
