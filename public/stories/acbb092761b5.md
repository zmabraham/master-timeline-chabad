# Mass arrests of Lubavitcher activists in Leningrad

*1938*

On the Thursday night that seven 'shul-Jews' were arrested in Zlobin, the NKVD in Leningrad simultaneously arrested R' Elchanan Dov Morozov, R' Yitzchak Raskin, R' Shmuel Nimaytin, and others. When the memoirist himself fled to Leningrad days later, he learned of the parallel arrests.
