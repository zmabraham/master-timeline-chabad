# Rebbe Rayatz blesses founding of Tomchei Tmimim yeshiva in Vilna

*1927*

Responding to the founders' letter, the Rebbe Rayatz expressed great joy and confidence that students from surrounding regions would soon gather. He stressed that the director must be a former Tomchei Tmimim student deeply familiar with the institution's spirit, fully dedicated to admissions and supervising students with sharp insight and a firm hand.
