# Author's contract as shamash not renewed

*1953*

In Nissan 5713 (April 1953), the East Flatbush shul did not renew the author's contract as shamash, ending his four-year tenure. Rabbi Avigdor Miller, mashgiach of Yeshivat Rabbi Chaim Berlin, banged the table in protest, ceased davening there, and gathered neighborhood signatures protesting to the absent president in Florida. When the balabatim later invited the author back for lesser duties, the Rebbe responded 'avekgegangen iz avekgegangen' — once gone, gone. The chevra mishnayos he had led gave him their entire $500 treasury and presented silver candlesticks his wife still uses.
