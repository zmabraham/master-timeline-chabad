# Gubenko televised speech prompts new Chabad appeal

*1990*

On the evening preceding 13 Tevet 5751 Gubenko appeared on Soviet television describing the Ministry of Culture as 'a ministry of kindness' whose central mission was 'to open the soul'. Veronika proposed turning his own words against him; R. Kogan drafted the letter overnight at Armand Hammer's Moscow office and presented it to the Ministry of Culture before 9am on 13 Tevet. The maneuver came as Gorbachev and Lukyanov's decision was being slowly implemented over Gubenko's continued resistance.
