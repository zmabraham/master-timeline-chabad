# Polyakov-Schneersohn collection survey at Moscow Lenin Library

*1990*

On Sunday, 5 Shevat 5750 (January 31, 1990), Rabbi Shlomo Cunin and his colleagues spent two and a half hours in the rare books reading room of the Moscow library identifying books from the Polyakov collection that matched the Lubavitch inventory. The Polyakov collection contained over 8,000 books numbered up to 8,577, while the Lubavitch list compiled around 1906 recorded 5,642 books. The discrepancy reflected both multi-volume works counted separately and post-1906 acquisitions never catalogued in the original Lubavitch list.
