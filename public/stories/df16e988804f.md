# Shanghai yeshivah students send report to Rashag exposing Vaad Hatzalah policy

*1945*

The letter described how funds were cut off in December 1942 and only small humiliating amounts dispensed thereafter. It also noted that Vaad Hatzalah had initially refused funding to the Amshinover Rebbe. The revelations prompted New York rabbis to seek intervention from Chabad leaders in Israel.
