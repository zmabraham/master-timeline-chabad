# Yechidus on Alter Rebbe's 150th yahrzeit and bochurim's roles

*1962*

On Erev Rosh Chodesh Elul 5722 (August 30, 1962) the author entered yechidus for his birthday. The Rebbe noted that this was the 150th year since the Alter Rebbe's passing and asked for a list of unpublished manuscripts at the print shop to release that year. He also pressed the author to resume the Torah-reading dikduk shiur for bochurim and to have bochurim daven before the amud on weekday Mincha.
