# Tzemach Tzedek visits Vilna

*1835*

News of the visit reached the chassidim of Vilna at the end of Sivan or beginning of Tammuz. When the Rebbe arrived in Av, he delivered a public maamar in the great Ashkenazi beis medrash that drew leading mitnagdim, and he held learned discussions with R. Avraham Avli. The young maskil Shmuel Yosef Fuenn, then sixteen, attended the maamar and described it in print years later.
