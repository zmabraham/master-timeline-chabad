# Yeshiva relocated from Zembin to Lubavitch

*1898*

On the night of Simchas Torah 5659 (1898), during the hakafot, the Rebbe Rashab publicly announced that the yeshiva, which had until then been based in Zembin in Minsk province, would henceforth be established in Lubavitch. In its first year, the main yeshiva had been in Zembin under R' Shmuel Gronem, while a smaller group of students remained in Lubavitch.
