# Author returns to Chernigov to seek draft exemption

*1917*

As the war ground on, the Russian government broadened conscription to include men previously released with white tickets. The author's father, fearing he would be called up despite serving as a shochet, sent his son to Nezhin to consult R' Menachem Mendel Chen, son of the Rad'tz, who had connections that might secure an exemption. The author spent a day there and was deeply impressed when the rav sat down at eleven at night to learn a sugya from mishna through Tosfos, explaining each word as if teaching a small child. The author was ultimately exempted again 'through the upper channels.'
