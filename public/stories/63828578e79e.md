# Group of lax students expelled from Tomchei Tmimim, most readmitted

*1913*

During 5673 a group of students grew lax with seder, rushing through tefillah in 35–40 minutes after Chassidus shiur to get to nigleh learning. R. Shilem Kuratin spoke with the Rebbe Rashab, who said he preferred dozens of proper students to hundreds of indifferent ones. The students were expelled, and after a week or more of pleading and promises most were readmitted, though one left Lubavitch entirely.
