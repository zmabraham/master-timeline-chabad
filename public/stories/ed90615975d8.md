# Levin blesses on the Rebbe's etrog on Sukkot 5723

*1962*

After the first day of Sukkot fell on Shabbat, Levin waited in line on the second day to enter the Rebbe's sukkah and bless on his arba minim. The Rebbe stood in a corner holding a sefer and watched Levin throughout, who silently prayed not to fumble the brachah from the intensity of the moment.
