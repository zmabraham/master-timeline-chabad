# Maamarei Admur HaZaken HaKtzarim editing project begins

*1981*

The Rebbe directed the editor to arrange the discourses by the order of Torah verses, then by Sages' sayings. The volume included a 'General Survey of the Short Maamarim' and a tentative chronological list through 5561 (1801). The Rebbe expressed reservation about exact dating, given that scribes had not been precise.
