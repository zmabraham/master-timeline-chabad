# Pereshel facsimile of the Offenbach 1722 Avodat HaGefen Haggadah

*1983*

In 5743 (1983), Tuvia Pereshel published in New York a facsimile of the 1722 Offenbach Haggadah with the 'Avodat HaGefen' commentary by Yehuda Leib ben Eliyahu and his son Ze'ev Wolf. The 20-page octavo edition came with illustrations and an introduction by Pereshel. It continued his series of resurrecting noteworthy early modern Haggadot.
