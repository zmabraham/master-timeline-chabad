# Rebbe travels from Riga to Warsaw with R. Teitz

*1931*

In Tammuz 5691 the Frierdiker Rebbe left Riga for Warsaw, riding the train together with Pinchas Mordechai Teitz. During the journey the Rebbe urged Teitz to expand the Yavneh learning groups and try to open a full yeshiva in Livenhof itself.
