# R' Shimon Shmerling's shlichus to Lubavitch for Hebron settlement

*1821*

R' Shimon Shmerling, founder of the Chabad community in Hebron and its first emissary, traveled from Hebron to Lubavitch in summer 5581 specifically to coordinate the establishment of the Chabad settlement in Hebron. He is recorded in the Montefiore census as having immigrated in 5581, suggesting he and his family came to Eretz Yisrael around this time.
