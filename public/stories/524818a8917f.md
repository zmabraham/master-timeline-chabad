# Three Tomchei Tmimim bochurim reach Vilna after border crossing

*1940*

After zig-zagging through Slonim, Lida, Oshmany, Globoka, Postov and Sventzian, dodging border guards and changing currency twice, the group of bochurim crossed the second border on the night of 28 Tevet and reached Vilna the next night. They found Tomchei Tmimim already operating in the Apatow Kloiz with dozens of bochurim from Otwock.
