# Chabad community established in Vidz, Lithuania

*1792*

Records of donations to the Chassidim settled in Tiberias, collected by R' Moshe ben Yitzchak of Vidz, prove that a Chabad kehilla functioned in Vidz from 5552 onward. Dozens of donors from Vidz are listed in the receipts covering the years 5552–5557 (1792–1797). The community grew under his leadership during this period.
