# Mohilev higher court acquits Rabbi DovBer

*1826*

On 29 November 1826 (11 Kislev 5587), after completing its deliberations, the Mohilev higher court issued a verdict acquitting Rabbi DovBer of the charges that had been brought against him. The case file together with the ruling was passed to the chief prosecutor of Mohilev province for the legally required review and confirmation.
