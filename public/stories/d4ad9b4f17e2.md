# Fez beit din awards 1.5 shops to heirs of R. Refael Moshe ibn Tzur

*1706*

On 29 Iyar 5466 (1706) in Fez, R. Yitzchak ben R. Refael Moshe ibn Tzur sued the community treasurers, R. Yitzchak Hatzarfati and R. Saadia Aflalo, over three shops in the New Vegetable Market that had belonged to his father. The shops had been sealed when R. Refael Moshe departed for Tetouan years earlier, after which the kahal opened them and used them in his prolonged absence. Witness testimony, a former attempt to claim them by R. Moshe HaKohen on behalf of the heirs, and the surviving doorpost markings led the beit din — R. Yehuda ibn Atar, R. Shmuel Hatzarfati, and R. Yaakov ben R. Reuven ibn Tzur — to award one and a half shops to the heirs and one and a half to the poor of the kahal. The treasurers and parnasim of the community signed their agreement.
