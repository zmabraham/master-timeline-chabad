# Rabbi Yehoshua Isaac Baruch perishes in the Kovno Shoah

*1941*

Before the Rebbe Rayatz left for America in winter 5700, R. Yehoshua Isaac Baruch had traveled from Vilna to Riga to take leave of him. The Rebbe's parting blessing — 'May Hashem help that we merit to meet again with Moshiach' — deeply troubled Baruch, who took it as a prophecy that they would not meet again before the redemption. He was murdered in the Kovno Holocaust on 9 Cheshvan 5702.
