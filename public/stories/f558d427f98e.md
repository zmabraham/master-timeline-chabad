# Rebbe Rayatz arrives in New York and founds Agudas Chasidei Chabad

*1940*

The Rebbe Rayatz reached New York in 5700 after his escape from Nazi-occupied Europe, and shortly thereafter founded Agudas Chasidei Chabad to organize the chassidic community in America. R' Yochanan Gordon was appointed as one of the members of its founding management. This event marked the formal beginning of organized Chabad institutional life in the United States.
