# Feigin urges Jacobson to expedite Agudath Chabad statute for Otwock purchase

*1938*

On 9 Tammuz 5698 (July 8, 1938), Rabbi Yechezkel Feigin wrote to Rabbi Yisrael Jacobson, head of Agudath Chassidei Chabad in America, urgently requesting completion of the organization's official statute. The Rebbe Rayatz had decided that a building in Otwock — to house his residence, library, and the yeshiva — must be purchased in the American organization's name, since the security of Jews and their property in Poland was no longer assured. The lawyer required the statute to explicitly authorize the Aguda to acquire real estate abroad.
