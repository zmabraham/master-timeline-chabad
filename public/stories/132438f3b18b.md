# Rashab delivers 19 Kislev 1903 discourse (later printed in Torat Shalom)

*1903*

The Rashab cited the letter and noted its unusual phrasing — 'I did not hear' rather than 'I do not know' — calling it a wonder. The discourse was later printed in 'Torat Shalom' and is the source of an enduring puzzle about R. Levi Yitzchok's wording.
