# Helicopter trip to Lubavitch with Jerry Levin film crew

*1992*

On 28 Shevat the author accompanied Levin's crew as guide, while a busload of visiting yeshiva students travelled in parallel with Rabbis Eisenbach and Yaroslavsky. Aerial shots of the historic courtyard of the Rebbeim were captured for the 11 Nissan film. The party returned to Moscow that evening so the author could resume Schneerson-library negotiations the next morning.
