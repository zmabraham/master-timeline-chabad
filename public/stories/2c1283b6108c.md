# Maamar on Kabbalas Shabbos of 10 Teves 5673

*1912*

10 Teves 5673 fell on Friday erev Shabbos. The Rebbe Rashab davened Kabbalas Shabbos at home, and announced that students could eat something and then he would deliver a maamar. The doors were closed by the time the author and others returned; they stood outside the window in the bitter cold and snow listening to the maamar delivered b'kol ram, and R. Shimshon Vitebsker was let in mid-maamar through a chain of doors opened by the Rayatz's daughter Chana.
