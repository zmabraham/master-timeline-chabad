# Hebron delegates arrive at Montefiore's camp on Mount of Olives

*1839*

The Hebron delegation arrived at Montefiore's camp on the Mount of Olives on the night of erev Rosh Chodesh Tammuz 5599. Lady Judith Montefiore notes in her diary that the messengers came to thank them for coming to the Land of Israel and stayed overnight. Dr. Eliezer Halevi's memoirs likewise mention these Hebron visitors who came to greet them.
