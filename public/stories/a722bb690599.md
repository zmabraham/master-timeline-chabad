# Rabbi Avraham Lison with the Rebbe at the dollar distribution at the farbrengen

*1922*

Rabbi Avraham Lison with the Rebbe at the dollar distribution at the farbrengen. Rabbi Avraham Lison (12 Cheshvan 5683, 1922 – 15 Shevat 5779), was the eldest resident of Kfar Chabad, mashpia of the Beis Nachum Yitzchak synagogue in Kfar Chabad.
