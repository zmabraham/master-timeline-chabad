# Passing of Rabbi Baruch Shalom Schneersohn

*1869*

Rabbi Baruch Shalom — 'safek bechor' (a doubtful firstborn) per a note by his brother Maharash — passed away on 16 Shevat 5629 (late January 1869), four years after his father the Tzemach Tzedek. He was interred about 4.5 cubits from his father's grave inside the ohel built in Lubavitch. The dating of his birth and death is used in halachic literature to discuss Ashkenazi customs regarding naming a child after a living relative.
