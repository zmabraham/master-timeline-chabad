# Rebbe asks Rabbi Ashkenazi in Shanghai to obtain visas for Vilna students

*1941*

Writing to Rabbi Meir Ashkenazi in Shanghai, the Frierdiker Rebbe explained that he could not bring all his students to America and therefore needed Ashkenazi's help to obtain Shanghai transit visas for talmidim in Vilna, in newly Soviet-occupied territories, and from Riga, where the large yeshiva in Dvinsk had operated. He pleaded that the effort be undertaken with maximum urgency.
