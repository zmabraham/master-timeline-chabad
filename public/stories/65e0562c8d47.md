# Synagogue arson attempt in Moscow

*1992*

Intruders entered the Chabad synagogue at night, doused Rabbi Yitzchak Kogan's bedroom with gasoline and set it ablaze. People heard the commotion and rushed in to put out the fire. Rabbi Kogan was in Petersburg at the time. The shluchim believed it was intimidation by opponents of their work over the books campaign; Veronika tried to link it to her recent efforts to recover the synagogue's upper floor.
