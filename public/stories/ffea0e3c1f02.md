# Tzemach Tzedek's Shulchan Aruch printed in Zhitomir

*1856*

The Lubavitch moreh tzedek R. Avraham b. Avigdor haKohen joined the approbations for the 5616 (1856) Zhitomir edition of the Alter Rebbe's Shulchan Aruch. He served as moreh tzedek of Lubavitch throughout most of the later years of the Tzemach Tzedek, occasionally co-signing responsa or signing in the Rebbe's stead.
