# Soviet seizure of Rashab's library transferred to Russian National Library

*1924*

After the Rebbe Rashab evacuated Lubavitch in 1915, the bulk of the combined libraries was stored in Moscow while priceless manuscripts were taken to Rostov. With the fall of the czarist government the Moscow books were seized. Authorities agreed to repatriate the collection in 1921 but reneged, again in 1925. The Rebbe Rayatz pursued the library's return throughout his life.
