# Address to women in Moscow on Rebbetzin Chaya Mushka

*1991*

On Sunday 25 Shevat 5751, while other delegation members met with Veronika and prepared to host her party that evening, the diarist traveled to a women's gathering in Moscow marking 22 Shevat. He spoke for about three-quarters of an hour in Hebrew about his personal acquaintance with Rebbetzin Chaya Mushka, with Rabbi Z. Korobsky translating into Russian.
