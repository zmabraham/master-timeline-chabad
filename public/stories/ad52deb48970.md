# Letter to Pekuam regarding Magen Avot yeshiva in Hebron

*1890*

In 5650 (1890), R' Shmuel Menachem Chaikin wrote to the Pekuam Amsterdam committee on behalf of the Magen Avot yeshiva in Hebron, with R' Yehuda Leib Shaul Levin co-signing as rabbi. The dual signature pattern continued: Chaikin signed first, Levin added the title 'rav and moreh in Hebron.' The yeshiva was a Chabad institution requiring external support.
