# American students Berel Levy and Hecht enroll in Otwock yeshivah

*1938*

The Rebbe worried Americans accustomed to comfort would struggle with Otwock's austere conditions, but Levy wrote home that they felt little strangeness. Levy described hundreds of students from across Europe and hundreds of Chasidim arriving for yechidut. The Rebbe took personal interest, advising Levy on his English reading habits and suggesting he read New York Times briefs.
