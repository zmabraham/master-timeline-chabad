# Ramash begins his Reshimot diary

*1928*

The diary included a wealth of chasidic lore, history, and customs, with anecdotes of all the Rebbes back to the Baal Shem Tov. Most of the Reshimot was written in Europe before WWII, with small sections added in Brooklyn in the early 1940s. The notations were discovered in his desk shortly after his passing and published posthumously by Kehot.
