# Rebbe calls Israeli army veterans up at Motzaei Simchat Torah farbrengen

*1962*

Toward the end of the Motzaei Simchat Torah farbrengen the Rebbe unexpectedly invited all those from Eretz Yisrael who had served in the army or miluim to come up on the table and say l'chaim, then led the singing of 'V'haya Machanecha Kadosh' and 'Shalom Al Yisrael.' He said cryptically 'one should fulfill it with the fear,' which Chassidim later interpreted in light of Soviet missiles approaching Cuba. Levin received kos shel bracha and was called back to receive a bottle of wine to distribute in Eretz Yisrael.
