# Rebbe Rayatz arranges welcome receptions on 2 Tammuz

*1946*

On 2 Tammuz 5706 (Igrot Kodesh vol. 9, letter 2923), as soon as the Shanghai students set out from China, the Frierdiker Rebbe wrote to several Chabad askanim asking them to arrange a fine reception for the Tomchei Tmimim students along their travel route, with special emissaries to organize welcome events in as many towns as possible.
