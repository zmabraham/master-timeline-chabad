# Prokuror Beklashov orders chassidim be tolerated

*1800*

After a second mitnagdic denunciation at the start of 5560, Prokuror General Alexander Beklashov issued an order on 8 Shevat 5560 (Feb 4, 1800) to the Vilna municipal police instructing them to summon the kahal heads and inform them that 'by the will of His Imperial Majesty the Karliner sect must be tolerated.' The order would later be cited in the Alter Rebbe's second interrogation.
