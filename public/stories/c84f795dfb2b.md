# Tsar Alexander issues full release certificate for Alter Rebbe

*1801*

By order of Tsar Alexander I, 'the Jew Zalman ben Baruch' was declared free to return home and enjoy full liberty in his former place of residence. A copy of the release was delivered to the Alter Rebbe through the Vitebsk governor's office on 15 Av 5561 (July 25, 1801), accompanied by a certificate from Pyotr Tarabeev confirming the Alter Rebbe's right to exercise his liberties throughout White Russia.
