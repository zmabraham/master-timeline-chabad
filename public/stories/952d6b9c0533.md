# Passing of Rebbetzin Rivkah in Lubavitch

*1914*

Her resting place was placed adjacent to the Maharash's grave alongside the ohel originally built for the Tzemach Tzedek. The Lubavitch cemetery also contained a separate ohel for the Chabad rebbetzins, including the wives of the Alter Rebbe, the Mittler Rebbe, and the Tzemach Tzedek. Her passing came on the eve of the upheavals that would soon end Lubavitch's role as Chabad's center.
