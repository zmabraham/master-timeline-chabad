# Arranged for her to enter yechidus with the Rebbe of Lubavitch, among

*1919*

Arranged for her to enter yechidus with the Rebbe of Lubavitch, among other things the Rebbe told her: 'I knew your father-in-law well; we served together in the Jewish self-defense forces in the city of Yekaterinoslav in 1919 when the "Whites" captured our city'"
