# R' Dan Tomarkin appointed gabbai of Kollel Chabad

*1855*

In 5615 the Tzemach Tzedek appointed the wealthy chasid R' Dan Tomarkin of Rogachov as a gabbai of Kollel Chabad, a position he held for over thirty years. He initially served alongside others on the committee and later became the general gabbai, transferring funds to the Tzemach Tzedek for distribution. Because R' Dan lived in Rogachov, the committee that became known as the 'Rogachov Vaad' was established in that city.
