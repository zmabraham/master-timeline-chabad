# Rebbe urges Vilna students to greater diligence

*1940*

The Rebbe addressed Yosef Hakohen Bornstein (Portowicz), Menachem Zev Greenglass and Avraham Yitzchak Garfinkel, noting that while the yeshiva was reorganizing well, the spiritual impact expected of the students was not yet evident. He pressed them to strengthen both their study and their avodah shebalev.
