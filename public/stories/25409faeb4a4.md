# Mogilev high court forwards Senate acquittal decree to Khovansky

*1828*

On 11 January 1828, the First Department of the Mogilev high court (signed by Chief Judge Felix Marzhevsky and Counselor Porfiry Karpilavich) forwarded Khovansky a copy of the Senate's 28 November 1827 decree, which had reached the department on 2 January 1828. Because the Senate had sent its ruling directly to the Mogilev provincial government rather than to Khovansky (who had filed the appeal), the high court provided him with the official copy. The transmittal (no. 54) closed Khovansky's involvement in the case begun by his 18 September 1825 proposal (no. 4425).
