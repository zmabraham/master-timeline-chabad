# Kobe students summary letter on US visa cancellations dated 8 Tammuz

*1941*

A document preserved in the secretariat archive, dated 8 Tammuz 5701 (July 3, 1941), summarized the students' situation: the consul in Yokohama would grant visas to those who denied their relatives, but a July 2 telegram from the State Department appeared to nullify this. The document also discussed students changing their surnames (e.g., Rodal to Bodal) and securing new affidavits under those names.
