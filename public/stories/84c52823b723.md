# Orlov asks Uvarov to consent to lifting Tzemach Tzedek surveillance

*1847*

On 14 March 1847 (9 Nissan 5607), Count Orlov wrote to Count Sergey Uvarov, the Education Minister. He summarized Golitsyn's report and asked Uvarov, whose 1841 letter had triggered the surveillance, to formally consent to its removal. He noted that the local government's report seemed sufficient grounds to end the watch.
