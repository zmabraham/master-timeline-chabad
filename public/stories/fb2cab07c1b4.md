# Tel Aviv facsimile of the Copenhagen Haggadah by Uri Feivush ben Yitzchak

*1986*

In 5746 (1986), Nahar and Steimatzky publishers in Tel Aviv issued a facsimile of the Copenhagen Haggadah, a manuscript written by Uri Feivush ben Yitzchak Segal in Altona-Hamburg in 1739. The 34-leaf folio facsimile featured the Abarbanel commentary, Kabbalistic notes, Seder laws in Hebrew, Yiddish, and Ladino, and full illustrations. It included a six-leaf bilingual Hebrew-English introduction by Chaya Benjamin.
