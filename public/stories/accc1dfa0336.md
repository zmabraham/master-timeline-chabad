# Maharash of Lubavitch begins his nesi'ut

*1866*

Rabbi Shmuel Schneersohn, son of the Tzemach Tzedek, began leading as Rebbe of Lubavitch in 5626 and continued until his passing in 5643. His tenure is referenced in a recommendation letter from Rabbi Mordechai of Loyev that mentions a 'great rabbi of Lubavitch' (either the Maharash or his son the Rashab) who interceded on behalf of a chasid seeking to settle in the Land of Israel.
