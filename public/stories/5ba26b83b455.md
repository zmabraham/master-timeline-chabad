# Maamar HaShiflus VeHaSimcha by Rabbi Yitzchak Aizik of Homil published

*1868*

Rabbi Yitzchak Aizik of Homil was a leading chassid of the Alter Rebbe, Mitteler Rebbe, and Tzemach Tzedek. His Maamar HaShiflus VeHaSimcha, an explication of chassidic teachings on lowliness and joy, was first printed in 5628 (1868) and is now exhibited at the Lubavitch Library.
