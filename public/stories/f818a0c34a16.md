# Rabbi Shmuel Levitin arrives in US as Rebbe's emissary

*1939*

Levitin, born in 1883, had been imprisoned by the Soviets for religious activities in Georgia before escaping Russia in 1937. He visited communities across America until war broke out. His wife was trapped in Europe and killed by the Nazis. Levitin emerged as a chasidic elder playing a major role in Chabad's American development.
