# Gurarie family relocates to Rostov, near the Rebbe's home

*1919*

At the start of 5680 (autumn 1919) the Gurarie family relocated to Rostov, settling close to the residence of the Rebbeim. R' Shneur Zalman was then about eight years old. The proximity allowed close contact with the Rebbes' court during a formative period of his life.
