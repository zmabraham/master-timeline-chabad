# Council in Lubavitch over whether to leave the war zone

*1915*

On 10 Marcheshvan 5676, R. Shmuel Michel Trainin and R. Shmuel Gourary arrived from Petrograd and met in the Rebbe Rashab's room with him, the Rebbetzin Shterna Sarah, and the Rayatz. They debated whether to remain in Lubavitch, move to a major city, or travel to the Caucasus health resorts. After about five hours it was resolved that the whole family would travel together, though the destination was still undecided.
