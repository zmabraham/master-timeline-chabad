# Encouraging election results reported in Tevet

*1909*

In Tevet, the Rebbe Rashab wrote to Rabbi Shilo (Hareshil"a) that the conflict between the parties was very intense, but that — God willing — the observant camp would prevail in the elections with a majority of kosher delegates. He noted that even the few elected were accomplishing much, and that opponents were also working hard from outside, requiring great effort on their part to rouse and act.
