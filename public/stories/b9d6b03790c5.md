# R' Yisrael Yaffe of Kapust prints Shulchan Aruch HaRav

*1816*

In 5576 (1816), the brothers R' Yehuda and R' Yisrael Yaffe, sons of the late R' Alexander Sender Feivish Yaffe, printed volumes 1-3 of the Alter Rebbe's Shulchan Aruch, Orach Chaim, at their press in Kapust. This R' Yisrael Yaffe is distinct from R' Yisrael ben Yitzchak Yaffe, also a printer in Kapust who later settled in Hebron. Both Yaffes eventually made aliyah and lived in Hebron.
