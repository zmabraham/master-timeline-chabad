# Montefiore census documents Chabad synagogues in Jerusalem and Hebron

*1855*

The 5615 Montefiore census, published as 'Luach Shemos HaBatei Knesios D'Chabad,' documented Chabad's synagogues and study halls in Jerusalem and Hebron. It described a Jerusalem beis midrash named 'Dat Moshe ViYehudis' (after Montefiore) where twelve bachurim studied gemara, poskim and chassidus under R' Eliyahu Yosef Rivlin.
