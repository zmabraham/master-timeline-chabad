# Mottel Altein sent to Pittsburgh's Nusach Ari Congregation

*1941*

The night before, the Rebbe had summoned Altein and refused to delay the trip. Altein heard news of the Japanese attack en route. He arrived to find his telegram unopened at the synagogue and the president debating whether to relocate the shul and join the Conservative movement. The Rebbe's intervention kept it Orthodox.
