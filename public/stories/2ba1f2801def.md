# Heirs of R' Yitzchak ben David ve-Chayun add further building repairs to mortgage

*1715*

Witnesses Yaakov ben Mordechai Azulai (mason) and Yaakov Punitz (carpenter) testified before the Fes beit din that the orphans had paid for essential repairs to the bathroom wall, the roof, the courtyard floor, doors, and beams of the mortgaged property. These sums of roughly 5.25 and 12 ounces were added to the existing mortgage, to be repaid in full at redemption.
