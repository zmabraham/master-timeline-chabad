# Chabad chasidim move from Chevron to Jerusalem

*1847*

An 5609 letter from the Chevron Chabad community to Montefiore explicitly says 'two years ago we settled in Jerusalem,' dating their move to 5607. This established the Chabad presence in Jerusalem alongside Chevron. R' Uri of Slonim, who had been an emissary in Chevron, also relocated to Jerusalem around this time.
