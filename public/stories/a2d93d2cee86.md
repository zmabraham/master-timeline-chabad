# Meeting with Ukrainian Academy of Sciences library in Kiev

*1990*

On 9 Shevat 5750 (February 4, 1990), Rabbi Berel Levine and Rabbi Shlomo Cunin met with the scholarly secretary of the Kiev Academy of Sciences library. Levine explained how the books of the Institute for Jewish Culture had been seized by the Yevsektsiya from synagogues, confiscated by the Soviet government in 1949, and stored in two cellars. They located two Hebrew incunabula (Abudraham, Lisbon 1491, and a Shorashim from Naples 1491) bearing the stamp of the Institute, suggesting that the rest of the lost collection might also have ended up at the Academy.
