# R. Avraham Chaim writes essay on sadness vs. bitterness to R. Chanoch Hendel

*1889*

Responding to news about R. Hendel's grandson, who was engaged in avodas haLev based on Tanya, R. Avraham Chaim explained that the same effort to fulfill 've'ahavta lerei'acha' protects from excessive bodily attachments. He taught that sadness over the state of Klal Yisrael can be transformed into bitterness drawn from gevuros dikedushah — which carries vitality and pleasure — whereas plain atzvus comes from the cold, dry element of earth without any moisture of kedushah. He warned that tears that overflow such bounds must be examined to see whether they are truly an offering to G-d or merely the yetzer's pretext to rob a person of joy.
