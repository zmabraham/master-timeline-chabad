# Rebbe directs reprinting Chabad works in square script

*1989*

Older Chabad works had long been reproduced via photo-offset of original printings in small Rashi script, hindering study. At the Rosh Chodesh Iyar 5749 farbrengen the Rebbe instructed that these works be reset in square letters. This launched the project to prepare a new edition of the entire Tzemach Tzedek series, integrating all the supplements, omissions, and milu'im accumulated over the previous century.
