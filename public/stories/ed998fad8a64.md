# SMaK parchment manuscript purchased

*1545*

The library preserves an early Ashkenazi parchment copy of R. Yitzchak of Corbeil's SMaK (Amudei Golah) together with the glosses of his student R. Peretz of Corbeil. An owner inscription records its purchase on 26 Shevat 5305 (February 1545). It is catalogued as archive manuscript 1956.
