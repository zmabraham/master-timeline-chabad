# Branch in Dokshitz opened

*1902*

On 17 Sivan 5662 (1902), founders of the yeshiva society met with the Rebbe Rashab at the country house and decided to open a small branch of Tomchei Tmimim in Dokshitz at the urging of its residents. The avreich R' Yehoshua Lein was appointed as teacher. The branch is mentioned in correspondence through Erev Tisha B'Av 5666.
