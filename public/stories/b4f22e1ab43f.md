# Yeshivat Tzemach Tzedek founded in Jerusalem

*1920*

After WWI, exiles of Eretz Yisrael returned home, with Hebron's community largely resettling in Jerusalem. Upon his return from Egypt, Rabbi Menachem Mendel Nae launched the yeshiva as part of his repair work for Kollel Chabad. The yeshiva convened in the Beit Menachem Tzemach Tzedek synagogue, fulfilling the Rebbe Rashab's wish to bind kollel scholars to regular Chassidus study.
