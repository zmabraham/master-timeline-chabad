# Bar Mitzvah of the Rebbe Rayatz in the Large Hall

*1893*

According to Rayatz's own memoirs, the Large Hall built by his grandfather Maharash hosted his bar mitzvah celebration in 5653 (1893). The same hall had hosted the wedding of his aunt Chaya Mushka to Rabbi Moshe HaKohen Hornstein, and would also host his own wedding four years later.
