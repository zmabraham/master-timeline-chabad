# Rashbatz appointed teacher of Yosef Yitzchak

*1893*

A few days after Rosh HaShanah 1893, the Rashab appointed Rabbi Shmuel Betzalel Sheftel, known as the Rashbatz, to teach his son Yosef Yitzchak. The Rashbatz had previously taught the Rashab himself at the behest of Rabbi Shmuel. He remained Yosef Yitzchak's teacher until 1900, and was renowned for his ability to clarify chasidic concepts through parables and his deep knowledge of chasidic teachings from previous generations.
