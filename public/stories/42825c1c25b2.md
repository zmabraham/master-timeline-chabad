# R' Aharon of Belinitz formally agrees to serve as MaHaRiL's borer

*1856*

The negotiation dragged on for nearly a year. Only at the start of summer תרט"ז did R' Aharon respond affirmatively, his reply delivered by his son. MaHaRiL responded warmly on yom beis parshas Naso, promising to share with him the responsa from the Tzemach Tzedek, R' Yitzchak Aizik of Homel, and R' Shneur Zalman of Vielizh.
