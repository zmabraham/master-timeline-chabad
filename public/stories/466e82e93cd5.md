# Rabbi Chaim Lieberman flees Warsaw

*1939*

On 8 Tishrei 5700 (Sept 21, 1939), Rabbi Chaim Lieberman left Warsaw separately, having only about twenty minutes to prepare. The Frierdiker Rebbe's son-in-law Rashag had planned to join him but stayed behind because his wife and son refused to leave the Rebbe. Lieberman departed alone with nothing, while the rest of the family would remain in Warsaw until early Teves before traveling together to Riga.
