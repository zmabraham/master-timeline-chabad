# Rebbe Rayatz sends Rabbi Shmuel Levitin to the United States

*1938*

Levitin was unable to return to Europe due to the outbreak of war and remained in the US, where he played a key leadership role in the US Chabad community until his passing in 1974. He was part of a wider mission of Rebbe-sent emissaries who acted as a living bridge between the Rebbe in Europe and his followers in America.
