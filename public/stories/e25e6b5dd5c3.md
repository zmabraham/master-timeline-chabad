# Passing of Rebbe Maharash in Lubavitch

*1882*

Maharash died in Lubavitch on 13 Tishrei 5643 (late September 1882), having served continuously since 1866 despite chronic weakness in his final years. He was laid to rest in the ohel he had constructed years earlier for his father the Tzemach Tzedek. His yahrzeit remains observed as a major day in the Chabad calendar.
