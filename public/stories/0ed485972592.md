# 200+ Chabad members in Eretz Yisrael publicly remove gabbaim Bichowsky and Bagin

*1914*

Responding to accusations and a unilateral decision by the gabbaim abroad to dismiss the Jerusalem-based officials, 204 signatories from Jerusalem, Hebron, Tzfat and Tiberias issued a 'Moda'a Rabba' from the Kollel Chabad etrog office. They declared the two gabbaim and their faction removed, and asked that all funds be sent only to the established address of R. Mordechai Dov Slonim and R. M. Schlank. Bichowsky soon published 'HaTaynos VeHa'ashomos' detailing his charges, and the officials responded in the kuntres 'Tumas Yesharim'.
