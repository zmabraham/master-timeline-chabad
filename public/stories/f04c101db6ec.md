# Tzemach Tzedek winter 5624 responsum on Orach Chaim

*1864*

Orach Chaim §114 was composed in the winter of 5624, after more than four years of the Tzemach Tzedek's illness. Such late responsa appear only sporadically; the vast majority of halachic work during this period was answered by his son Maharin, with the Tzemach Tzedek consulted only on the most difficult cases.
