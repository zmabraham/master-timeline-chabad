# Passing of the Rogatchover Gaon

*1936*

Rabbi Joseph Rosen, the renowned Rogatchover Gaon of Dvinsk, passed away on 11 Adar 5696 (March 5, 1936). Immediately after the shiva, his widow Rebbetzin Rivka organized a committee to print his manuscripts. His death set in motion decades of efforts to publish his extensive Torah writings.
