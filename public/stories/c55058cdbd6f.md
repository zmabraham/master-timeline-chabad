# Israel Aryeh Leib Schneersohn passes away

*1952*

Israel Aryeh Leib Schneersohn, the Rebbe's younger brother, passed away on 13 Iyar 5712. His death was concealed from his mother Rebbetzin Chana out of concern that the news would harm her health. For the next twelve years the Rebbe personally added a few lines signed 'Leib' to the bottom of letters his sister-in-law Gania sent to Rebbetzin Chana to maintain the disguise.
