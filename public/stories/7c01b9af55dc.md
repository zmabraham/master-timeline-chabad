# Rabbi Naki receives the book Peninei Talmidei HaTmimim. Rabbi Naki

*1967*

Rabbi Naki receives the book Peninei Talmidei HaTmimim. Rabbi Naki (right) at the dedication of a new mikveh of purity in Herzliya, 5786. Rabbi Yosef Yaakov Naki (born 28 Adar II 5727; 04/08/1967) is a shliach of the Rebbe, rabbi of the Yad HaTisha neighborhood, member of the rabbinical chamber
