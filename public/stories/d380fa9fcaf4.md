# R' Mordechai Chefetz reports library shipment from Otwock unexpectedly delayed

*1940*

Before the Rebbe Rayatz left Warsaw he had arranged with attorney Aharon Blum to handle export of his library and manuscripts. Under the original plan the books were to leave Otwock through Italy in Adar II 5700. In a letter of 6 Nissan, R' Mordechai Chefetz of Riga — who served as the conduit for correspondence with the Hornstein family in Otwock — reported that shipment had inexplicably stalled.
