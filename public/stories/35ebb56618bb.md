# Chabad delegation buys 22,000 Shabbat candles for distribution in Russia

*1991*

On 22 Shevat 5751, the yahrzeit of Rebbetzin Chaya Mushka, the delegation in Moscow resolved to buy twenty-two thousand Shabbat candles to distribute as part of Mivtza Neshek across Russia. They began the purchase and distribution that day and wrote to the Rebbe asking permission to appoint a local person to manage the project; the Rebbe replied with a blessing to continue spreading good tidings.
