# Lilienthal visits the Tzemach Tzedek in Lubavitch

*1842*

In 5602, Lilienthal reported to the Education Ministry that he had sent a warm letter to the Rebbe and then traveled to Lubavitch to meet him. He described the Rebbe as about forty-five years old, not tall, and of utterly simple bearing. Lilienthal proposed that the Tzemach Tzedek join the planned rabbinic commission in Petersburg, but the Rebbe initially refused, citing his fragile health since the search and interrogation he had endured.
