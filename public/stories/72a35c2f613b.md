# R' Avraham Chaim sends Tzemach Tzedek manuscript to the Rebbe Rashab

*1907*

Writing from Würzburg on 11 Elul 5667 (1907), the Rebbe Rashab thanked R' Avraham Chaim for a 100-mark donation and discussed two books to be sent. Most precious, the Rebbe wrote, was the handwriting of the Tzemach Tzedek, which he requested be sent by registered mail to Lubavitch. The Rebbe Rayatz later recounted this gift in a letter dated 12 Tishrei 5696.
