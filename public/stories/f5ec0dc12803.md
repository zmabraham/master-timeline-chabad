# Tzemach Tzedek acquires the Sederin estate as Hereditary Citizen

*1852*

In 5612 (1852), Bet Rebbi records that the Tzemach Tzedek secured from the government the inherited citizenship title (Potomstvennoe) for himself and his descendants. As part of this status he purchased the Sederin estate in the Bobruisk area of Minsk province, registered in his name. Reports linked the elevation to his role in encouraging Jewish agricultural settlement.
