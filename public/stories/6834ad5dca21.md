# Soviet authorities close Tomchei Tmimim yeshiva in Rostov

*1921*

With the central yeshiva shut down by Soviet authorities, the Rebbe Rayatz opened a new branch of Tomchei Tmimim in Warsaw and dispatched R' Alter Simchowitz from Russia to direct its spiritual program. This effectively shifted the institutional center of gravity for Lubavitch yeshiva education out of Russia.
