# Vaad Hatzalah cuts off funding to Chabad students in Shanghai

*1942*

While the Mir Yeshivah and other yeshivos received generous stipends for food and clothing, the Chabad students were left to fend for themselves. Rabbi Chaim Shmuelevitz quietly tried to assist them. After the war, the policy became a lasting wound; in 1962 the Seventh Rebbe told Rabbi Gutnick that it had caused his father-in-law to weep.
