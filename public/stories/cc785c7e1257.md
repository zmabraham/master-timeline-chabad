# Rayatz spends Chag HaGeula 12-13 Tammuz with yeshiva in Warsaw

*1931*

On 12-13 Tammuz 5691, the Rebbe Rayatz celebrated his liberation festival with the Tomchei Tmimim community at the yeshiva in Warsaw. He delivered three maamarei chassidus and many sichos during his approximately two-week stay, and met privately in yechidus with virtually every student of the yeshiva.
