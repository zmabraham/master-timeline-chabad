# Author's final yechidus with the Rebbe Rashab

*1918*

He raised concerns about premature deaths in the family of the proposed bride suggested by his future brother-in-law R' Aharon Eliezer Zeitlin. The Rebbe Rashab repeated three times 'nito vos tzu choshesh zein' — there is nothing to fear — and closed with 'Hashem should protect you from everything,' the last words the author would ever hear from him in person.
