# KGB returns arrest file of R' Levi Yitzchak Schneersohn in Kiev

*1991*

Following months of negotiation through Lanya Odnopozov, the diarist and R' Yosef Kogan took a night train to Kiev and met with the KGB deputy chairman for all of Ukraine, who delivered a Russian-language speech blaming the original arrests on 'our elders' and presenting the file as a gesture of esteem for Chabad. The file included the original interrogation documents, the sentence of exile without trial, R' Levi Yitzchak's unanswered appeal to KGB chief Beria, and a recent rehabilitation ruling clearing him of all charges. Officials also offered to search for files of four Chabad rabbis arrested the same day in Kharkov — Pevzner, Gorkov, Pinson and Gurewitz — and to help retrieve the file of the Rayatz.
