# Tzemach Tzedek summer 5623 responsum on Yoreh Deah

*1863*

Yoreh Deah §337, written in summer 5623, is one of the few responsa preserved from the years after the Tzemach Tzedek became gravely ill. Most halachic work had passed to his son Maharin by this point; this responsum is among the small set still authored personally.
