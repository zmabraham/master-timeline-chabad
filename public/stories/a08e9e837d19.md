# Dinaburg adopts dual-rabbi structure

*1865*

From 5625 (1865) onward, the Jewish community of Dinaburg was served by two rabbis at the same time, one chosen by the Chassidim and one by the Misnagdim. The Misnagdic community first brought R' Aharon Shaul Zelig HaKohen of Pruzhana to fill their post.
