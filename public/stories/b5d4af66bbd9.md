# Marriage of R' Shlomo Aharon Kazarnovsky to Chaya Frieda Grossman

*1921*

In 5681, R' Shlomo Aharon Kazarnovsky (born Mogilev 5657, studied in Lubavitch 5671-5677) married Chaya Frieda Grossman, youngest daughter of R' Asher of Nikolayev. He inquired about the shidduch from Rayatz, who instructed him to travel to the Ohel to ask Rashab. After the wedding, he received the dowry funds R' Asher had entrusted before his death.
