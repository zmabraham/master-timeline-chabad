# Frierdiker Rebbe urges talmidim to relocate to Vilna on 3 Adar I 5700

*1940*

The letter (Vol. 5, no. 1153) instructed the recipients to hurry along talmidim who had learned in the smaller branches in Chelm, Lodz and elsewhere to make their way to Vilna. From there, a group of 38 talmidim was ultimately rescued and brought to safety.
