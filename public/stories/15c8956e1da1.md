# Passing of the Mittler Rebbe in Nezhin

*1827*

At the start of 5588 the Mittler Rebbe traveled to prostrate himself at the grave of his father the Alter Rebbe in distant Haditch. On the return journey he stopped in Nezhin, where he passed away on the tenth of Kislev. He is buried there, and his son-in-law the Tzemach Tzedek subsequently assumed the leadership of Chabad.
