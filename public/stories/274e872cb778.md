# Marriage of Dona bat Oved ibn Attar to Avraham

*1731*

On 20 Elul 5491 (~September 22, 1731), Dona bat Oved ibn Attar — a granddaughter of Rabbi Yehuda ibn Attar — was married to Avraham, son of Chaim Shem Tov ben Avraham ibn Attar. Like her aunt Shi'acha a generation earlier, Dona married within the extended ibn Attar family. The marriage was inscribed in the Ya'abetz's register.
