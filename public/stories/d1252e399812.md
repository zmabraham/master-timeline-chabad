# Din torah opens before Jerusalem Beis Din Tzedek between Kollel officials and gabbaim

*1914*

After Bagin arrived, the parties presented their claims before the Beis Din Tzedek. The gabbaim ultimately refused to continue litigating before that beis din, and the dispute dragged on for two months. The gabbaim returned abroad at the start of Elul, and on 16 Elul the beis din published its ruling 'Emet UMishpat Kasuv' siding with the Jerusalem officials.
