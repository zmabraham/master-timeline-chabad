# Baker raises Chabad library return with Gorbachev in Moscow

*1991*

During Baker's March 1991 Moscow visit, the Chabad delegation worked through Dennis Ross and Ambassador Matlock to ensure the library question reached Gorbachev directly. Baker first pressed Bessmertnykh, then raised the matter with Gorbachev himself, telling them the US considered the books American property. A Chabad delegation including Rabbi Shlomo Cunin intercepted Baker at his hotel late Friday night to thank him for his intervention.
