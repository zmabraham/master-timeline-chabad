# Alter Rebbe settles in Liozna

*1783*

After roughly two years in Horodok, the Alter Rebbe relocated to Liozna in the summer of 5543 (1783). In his later 1798 interrogation he himself testified that he had lived in Liozna 'about 15 years and more.' Liozna became the seat of his leadership of Belorussian and Lithuanian chassidim until 1801.
