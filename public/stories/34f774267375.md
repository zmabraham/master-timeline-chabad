# Twelve Tomchei Temimim students arrested as suspected spies in Poltava

*1918*

Rumors reached Kremenchug on a Friday that twelve Tomchei Temimim students had been killed at Poltava station. In fact they had been detained as suspected spies — triggered by Zalman Birnbaum's Polish-style cap and rapid movements. R' Tzvi Gurary used his military contacts to telephone the Poltava war chief, and R' Mordechai Aharon Friedman traveled by military train to obtain rabbinic guarantees from R' Duber Greenpress and R' Eliyahu Akiva Rabinowitz that freed them.
