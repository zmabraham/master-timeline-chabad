# Bolshevik October Revolution begins, sparking Russian civil war

*1917*

The Bolshevik Revolution at the start of 5678 unleashed widespread shortages of food and goods across Russia and ignited civil war. With the Russians ceasing combat, German forces advanced rapidly into the Jewish heartland of the western empire. The crisis directly threatened Tomchei Temimim in Lubavitch, prompting plans to relocate the yeshiva.
