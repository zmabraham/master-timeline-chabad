# Third interrogation of the Mitteler Rebbe in Vitebsk

*1825*

Investigators presented the Mitteler Rebbe (Rabbi DovBer Schneuri of Lubavitch) with a list of yearly donations pledged by various people for the support of a rebbe, found among his papers. He testified that he had never seen the list and did not know how it had come into his home. It is presumed the list was actually seized at the home of R. Aharon of Strashelye, who was arrested around the same time, and was mistakenly attributed to the Rebbe.
