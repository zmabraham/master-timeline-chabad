# Lost Maharash letter rediscovered as photo in geniza

*1999*

After nearly forty years of unsuccessful searches, R. Shaul Vilhelm called from Detroit on 27 Elul 5759 to report that Yosef Levin had found a photocopy of the lost buch in a public library's geniza. The photocopy contained the long-sought Maharash letter to R. Chaim Yaakov Widrewitz, in which the Rebbe acknowledged receiving Widrewitz's kuntress and promised to set aside time to study it despite poor health and many demands.
