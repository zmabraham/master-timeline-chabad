# Russian translation of the Haggadah by Moshe Goldstein printed in Vilna

*1870*

Around 5630 (1870), Moshe ben Yaakov Goldstein produced a Russian translation of the Haggadah, accompanied by annotations in Russian, printed in Vilna. The 70-page work featured the original Hebrew and Russian translation page-by-page. It marked an early example of a Russian-language Haggadah for the Russian Empire's Jews.
