# Romanian translation of the Haggadah by Avraham Shlomo Gold printed in Craiova

*1902*

Around 5662 (1902), Avraham Shlomo Gold issued the third revised edition of his Romanian translation of the Haggadah, with many additions, in Craiova. The 83-page work included annotations and musical notation. It served the Romanian-speaking Jewish community of the Balkans.
