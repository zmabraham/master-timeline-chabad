# Rabbi Teitz of Livenhof requests teacher for the youth

*1930*

In winter 5691 Rabbi Avraham Binyamin Teitz of Livenhof asked the Frierdiker Rebbe to send a teacher for the town's young men. The Rebbe wrote to Yaakov Landa on 6 Tevet 5691 (Igrot vol. 2, no. 516) saying that Livenhof was unusual: even the local balebatim wanted to study, and Rabbi Teitz, while distinguished, was not an outgoing public figure.
