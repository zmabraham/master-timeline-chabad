# Kehot publishes Sefer HaToldot Admur Maharash

*1947*

The biography described how the Maharash, on doctors' orders for physical activity, produced craftworks including a man-height menorah, mosaic tables, sifrei Torah, megillos, and mezuzos. While the household vessels were lost in transit out of Russia, the megillah and one of the tables survived; Kehot's 5707 edition reproduced one of its pages, and a clearer reproduction appeared in Igros Kodesh Admur Maharash in 5753.
