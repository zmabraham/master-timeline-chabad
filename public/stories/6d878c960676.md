# Announcement of Yeshivas Tomchei Temimim Lubavitch in America

*1940*

Speaking at the Greystone Hotel, the Rebbe announced his vision to fortify Jewish education in the US and told the crowd the new yeshivah would launch at once. He pressed Rabbi Yisrael Jacobson that he would not sleep until Jacobson promised to open it. Jacobson began enlisting students the next morning.
