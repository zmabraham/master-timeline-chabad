# Matusof son of the director of Chabad institutions in Morocco Rabbi Shlomo Matusof

*1966*

Matusof son of the director of Chabad institutions in Morocco Rabbi Shlomo Matusof Rabbi Saadia Dahan spoke with him many times during their studies at the Chabad yeshiva about the Rebbe his mother passed away in 5725 Adar 5726 (1966) he immigrated to Israel with his father and the family settled in Ashdod
