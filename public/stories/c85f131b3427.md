# Citywide farbrengen of Riga Chassidus study groups at Dreyzin shul

*1930*

Fulfilling the Rebbe Rayatz's proposal, the Riga committee assembled a large gathering of Anash at the middle minyan of the Dreyzin beis midrash for a citywide farbrengen of all the Chassidus study groups. The event ran from 9 pm until 2 am, and Chodakov reported the same day that it would both strengthen existing learners and attract new ones to fixed Chassidus study.
