# Aliyah of R. Yaakov Mordechai Twer and family to Eretz Yisrael

*1832*

Aged 38 at the time of aliyah, R. Yaakov Mordechai descended from MaHaRIL, the brother of the Alter Rebbe. His family became the root of the Brimat, Saulson, Singer, and Grossman families in the Holy Land; his eldest son R. Schneur Zalman remained behind in Mogilev and later emigrated separately.
