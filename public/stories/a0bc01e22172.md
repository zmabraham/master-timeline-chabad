# R' Levin's letter on the modest salary of the Hebron rabbinate

*1888*

On 19 Tevet 5648 (January 3, 1888), R' Yehuda Leib Shaul Levin wrote home that his total salary in Hebron was only 12 rubles a month, with no wedding fees, mechirat chametz revenue, or Purim funds as in the diaspora. He noted that prior to him, the Hebron rabbi had not taken a salary at all. The letter is preserved in Sinai journal.
