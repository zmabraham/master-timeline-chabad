# Yakovlev drafts letter for Gorbachev to return Chabad library

*1991*

On the eve of Rosh Hashana 5752, a Chabad delegation met Yakovlev in his Kremlin office. He told them he had repeatedly pushed for a positive ruling on the books and was now going directly to Gorbachev. Veronika drafted the letter, Yakovlev revised it in pen, and his secretary typed the corrected version before he carried it to Gorbachev.
