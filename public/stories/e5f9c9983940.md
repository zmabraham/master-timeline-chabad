# Jacobson sails for Poland with six Achei Tmimim students on Île de France

*1939*

The group included Mordechai Dov Altein, Meir Greenberg, Avraham Dovber Hecht, Yitzchak Kalodny, Zerach Gordon, and Mordechai Fisher, intending to study in Poland for two years. They traveled by train through France, Belgium, and Germany after disembarking, reaching Otwock at the start of Elul.
