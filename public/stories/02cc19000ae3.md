# Bochur Shlomo Zilberberg reports library found in Otwock

*1946*

On Rosh Hashanah La'ilanot (Tu BiShvat) 5706, Shlomo Zilberberg — who as a young man had packed the library in Otwock in 1939 — wrote that the books had been located, promising precise details to follow. It was the first postwar word that any of the lost crates might be recoverable.
