# Shiur at the Moscow yeshiva on eruvin

*1991*

On Monday 19 Tammuz 5751, with delegation activity slow, the author traveled to the Moscow yeshiva and taught a class in the halachos of eruvin — covering eruvei tavshilin, chatzeiros and techumin. Afterwards he went to the delegation office, where R. Yitzchak Kogan and Ziva Mikieva of the Joint Venture magazine were preparing a feature article on the Polyakov synagogue.
