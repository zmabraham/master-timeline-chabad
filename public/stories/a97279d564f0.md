# Rebbe Rayatz describes Pesach with talmidim in Otwock

*1938*

Though he had not said Chassidus on the first days, the Rebbe wrote that the Sedarim and Yom Tov meals passed pleasantly and unhurriedly, with talmidim of Tomchei Temimim and their teachers joining. They spent long hours delighting in the goodness of Hashem and in memories of earlier days.
