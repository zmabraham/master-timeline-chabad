# Jacobson accepted as rabbi of Anshei Bobruisk in Brownsville

*1926*

Through the advocacy of fellow Zhuravichi townspeople including R' Zev Chaikin, and after R' Memel Lukshin spoke with R' Shneur Zalman Chavkin (deputy chair of the Brownsville branch), Jacobson was given a two-week trial period and then hired as rabbi. The parent society in Manhattan opposed hiring a rabbi due to one maskil member's objection, so his salary was paid directly by individual congregants and the Mishnayos study society rather than the official society. Members who initially opposed him gradually came to embrace him, attending his home and shul on Yud-Tes Kislev, Purim, and other festive days.
