# Publication of Toldot Chabad in Soviet Russia

*1989*

Shalom Dovber Levin, son of Yisrael Yehuda Levin and longtime librarian of the Central Lubavitch Library, used excerpts from his father's accumulating memoir notes in this historical work. The full memoir Zichronotai was prepared for publication separately and signed by the author in Elul 5755.
