# Death of R' Chizkiyah Medini (Sdei Chemed)

*1904*

R' Chizkiyah Medini died on 24 Kislev 5665 in the Romano mansion in Hebron, where he had lived and taught since his arrival in 5661. He passed away while still engaged in his Torah study and writing. His monumental halachic encyclopedia, the Sdei Chemed, was completed during his years in that house, and the yeshiva later established there was named in his honor.
