# Passing of Rabbi Menachem Mendel Kasher

*1983*

Rabbi Menachem Mendel Kasher, who edited and published the Rogatchover's Tzofnat Paneach series including the Klalei HaTorah V'HaMitzvot volumes, passed away on 27 Marcheshvan 5744 (November 3, 1983). The continuation of his publication project passed to others.
