# Sixth Lubavitcher Rebbe Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn passes away

*1950*

He had started from scratch three times: after his father's passing in 1920, in Riga in 1927, and in America in 1940. His passing set the stage for his son-in-law, the seventh Rebbe, to assume leadership and expand the initiatives launched in Riga.
