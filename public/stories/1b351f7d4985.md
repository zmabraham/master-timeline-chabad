# Levin recovers from illness in Sarafand

*1951*

Worn down by hard labor and poverty, Levin became gravely ill in summer 5711. R' Shlomo Kesselman wrote to the Rebbe about him, and the reply urged Levin to strengthen his observance of the three daily shiurim and the takanot concerning Tehillim, expressing confidence that he would soon report improving health.
