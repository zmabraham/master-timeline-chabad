# Russian deportation of Jews from border provinces

*1915*

During WWI, as the front line approached Jewish population centers in Russia, the government decreed that Jews must abandon their homes within 24 hours. This was carried out fully during the summers of 5675 and 5676, with Jews stripped of property and expelled from their towns. The refugees were assigned to five interior gubernias: Voronezh, Tambov, Penza, Crimea, and Yekaterinoslav. Although the decree was later rescinded, the Germans had by then captured the original Jewish areas, leaving the refugees stranded.
