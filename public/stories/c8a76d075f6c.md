# Murder of Rachamim Turgeman in Hebron

*1877*

Rachamim Turgeman, a notable of the Hebron Jewish community and a British protégé, was severely beaten by a local Turk and died seven days later. The Hebron community alerted the governor, and because he was under British protection a commission including the British consul's representative and Dr. Chaplin came from Jerusalem, performed an autopsy confirming the cause of death, and arrested the assailant for trial.
