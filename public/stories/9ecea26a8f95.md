# Rosenbaum recounts losses and widowhood in Rosh Chodesh Adar II letter

*1905*

On Rosh Chodesh Adar II 5665 Reb Avraham Chaim summarized the previous year: his wife of his youth had died after a four-month illness costing over 1,000 dollars, his diamond business had lost nearly 2,000 more, and he had been visited by his brother R' Shneur Zalman Rosenbaum, dispatched by the Rebbe Rashab as an emissary for the Tomchei Tmimim yeshivas. He noted with pride his brother's success in raising funds in New York and Philadelphia.
