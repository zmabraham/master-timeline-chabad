# Passing of R. Elchanan Yakubovitz in Jerusalem

*1941*

R. Elchanan Yakubovitz, the last man to bear the title 'rabbi of Lubavitch' before the dissolution of the community, died in Jerusalem on 24 Elul 5701 (September 1941). With his death the direct rabbinical line of the historic Chabad town came to an end. His biographical notes were preserved by his son R. Aharon Yakubovitz.
