# Third Section orders Mogilev gendarmes to spy on the Tzemach Tzedek

*1841*

On 21 July 1841 (15 Av 5601), the head of the Third Section formally instructed Colonel Voytsekh of the gendarmerie in Mogilev province to secretly monitor Mendel Schneersohn's conduct and views. The order cited suspicions that he and his followers opposed the government's plans for the Jews. This launched what would become a six-year covert surveillance operation.
