# Sefer Ha-Mitzvot of Rambam printed in Constantinople

*1510*

In 5270 (1510), Rambam's Sefer Ha-Mitzvot — his enumeration and explanation of the 613 commandments — was printed in Constantinople. It joined a small but growing body of pre-Shulchan Aruch halachic literature being issued by the Ottoman Sephardic presses.
