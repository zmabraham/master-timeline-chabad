# Yeshiva administration asks Rebbe to appoint R. Yehuda Eber as Rosh Yeshiva in Warsaw

*1931*

On 26 Nisan 5691, the leadership of Tomchei Temimim in Warsaw petitioned the Rebbe Rayatz to bring R. Yehuda Eber on as a leader of the nigleh studies. They believed he would greatly benefit the depth and quality of Talmud study at the yeshiva.
