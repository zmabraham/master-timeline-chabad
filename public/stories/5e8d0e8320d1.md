# Mitteler Rebbe denounced and brought to Vitebsk

*1826*

At the end of Tishrei 5587 (October 1826), informers' charges against the Mitteler Rebbe escalated and he was promptly brought to Vitebsk for investigation. There the case fell under the jurisdiction of General-Governor Khovansky of Vitebsk province. The arrest opened months of investigation into the ma'amad collection and the chassidic movement before the Rebbe's eventual full acquittal.
