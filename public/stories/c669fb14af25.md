# Rebbe's 13 Nisan sicha alludes to Kremlin library mission

*1991*

In his 13 Nisan 5751 sicha at 770, the Lubavitcher Rebbe discussed the Tzemach Tzedek, the day's baal hilula, noting how he had remained in Lubavitch while arranging affairs in the capital, Petersburg. Hasidim understood this as an allusion to the Rebbe himself, sitting in New York while directing the mission to recover the Schneerson library from the Kremlin. The sicha was delivered while the special delegation was actively pressing the Soviet government on the books.
