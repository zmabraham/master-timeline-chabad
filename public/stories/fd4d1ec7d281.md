# Illustrated Haggadah with hand-written text printed in New York

*1949*

Around 5709 (1949), a 58-page Haggadah was printed in New York featuring colorful illustrations and the text itself hand-lettered by the artist. The quarto edition exemplified the mid-20th-century trend of artist-designed liturgical works. It served the American Jewish community of the post-war period.
