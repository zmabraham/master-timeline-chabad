# Tzemach Tzedek's covert surveillance is officially lifted

*1847*

On 19 March 1847 (14 Nissan 5607), the Education Ministry issued document number 2643. Count Uvarov informed Count Orlov that he fully agreed to terminate the gendarmerie's covert surveillance on Honorary Citizen Rabbi Mendel Schneersohn. The six-year covert watch that had begun on 21 July 1841 was thus officially closed.
