# Romano courtyard registered to R' Rachamim Yosef Franko

*1888*

After Markado Romano's death in Damascus while returning from visiting his ill son in Istanbul, his Istanbul heirs decided to register the Hebron mansion temporarily in the name of R' Rachamim Yosef Franko. A deed from approximately 1888 records the transfer from 'Markado Effendi son of Ibrahim Romano the Istanbuli' to 'Rachamim Effendi Franko.' The arrangement was not a final sale, since the heirs themselves did not live in the Holy Land.
