# Rashab evacuates Lubavitch as the war front approaches

*1915*

As World War I drew nearer to Lubavitch, the Rashab departed the town with his family in Marcheshvan 5676 (October/November 1915), ending the 102-year period in which Lubavitch served as the worldwide capital of Chabad. The newly installed second rabbi R. Elchanan Yakubovitz also left at this time, his tenure as Lubavitch's rabbi having lasted only a few months.
