# American Tomchei Tmimim students instructed to flee Poland

*1939*

Once war began, the Rebbe Rayatz told the American bochurim at Tomchei Tmimim in Otwock to seek passage out of Poland alongside foreign diplomats and citizens. The mashpia R. Yisroel Eber distributed travel money. They escaped via Warsaw, Riga, and Stockholm, eventually returning to the United States.
