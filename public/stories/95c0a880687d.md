# Rebbe Rayatz enters Me'arat Hamakhpelah in Hebron

*1929*

Eliezer Don Slonim, whose unique relationship with local Arab leaders made the visit possible, escorted the Rebbe with six rabbis. The anti-Semitic mufti of Jerusalem telegrammed local authorities demanding the visit be blocked, but his message arrived after the Rebbe had finished. The Rebbe also dispensed charity to the local Arab poor as he left.
