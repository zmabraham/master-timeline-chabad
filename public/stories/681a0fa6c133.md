# Vilna yeshiva students begin learning trades

*1940*

R. Baruch reported that Yosef Weinberg, Gershon Chanovitz and Moshe Eliyahu Gerlitzky began learning poultry shechita (about six weeks of training, two hours daily). Menachem Zev Greenglass and Chaim Leib Praffs took up stam writing for four weeks, three hours daily, while Tzvi Fuchs began learning milah. Baruch approved these vocational studies given wartime conditions.
