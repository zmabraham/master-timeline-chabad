# R. Moshe Yisrael sent on Safed mission to North Africa

*1710*

Born in Jerusalem around 1680 and son-in-law of R. Moshe ibn Habib, R. Moshe Yisrael was dispatched as an emissary (shadar) of Safed to North Africa in 1710. He remained on this mission until 1713, traveling through Moroccan communities including Meknes and Fez. During this period he corresponded with fellow emissary R. Nissim Avraham Hakohen regarding the collection of pledged donations.
