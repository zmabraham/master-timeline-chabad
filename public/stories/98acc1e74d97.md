# R' Dovber Rivkin travels to Eretz Yisrael as Rosh Mesivta of Toras Emes

*1924*

Around the same time that R' Alter Simchowitz left for Eretz Yisrael, R' Dovber Rivkin also traveled there. He was appointed Rosh Mesivta (R"M) at Yeshivas Toras Emes, while R' Alter Simchowitz served as mashpia at the same institution.
