# Rebbe Rashab declines to move Slonim from Yafo

*1903*

The Rebbe Rashab had wanted to appoint R' Shneur Zalman to oversee a new Chabad yeshiva in Jerusalem, but worried that leaving Yafo after three years of intense work there would unravel everything he had built. He concluded that pulling him out would undo the essential reforms he had instituted.
