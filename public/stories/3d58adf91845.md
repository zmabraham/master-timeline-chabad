# Rabbi Aryeh Leib Kaplan killed on Yom Kippur

*1943*

Kaplan had been planning to immigrate to Palestine in 1922 when the Rayatz advised him to stay and help Russian Jews: 'If you protect the children of G-d, G-d will watch over your children.' He served as a rabbi near Kiev until his 1939 arrest and exile to Uzbekistan, where on the train he met Rabbi Levi Yitzchak Schneerson. His children all later escaped to Israel and the United States.
