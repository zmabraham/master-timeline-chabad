# First Chabad–Sephardic agreement in Hebron

*1825*

The first agreement obligated the Chabad community in Hebron to pay 1,000 grush annually to the Sephardic kollel to share city expenses, including protection payments to the tyrant Sheikh Abdul Rahman who held Hebron under his patronage. Two subsequent agreements would lower this amount as Chabad argued that its own direct payments to the sheikh deserved credit.
