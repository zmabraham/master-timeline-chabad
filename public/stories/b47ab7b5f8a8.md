# Vitebsk treasury asks who should reimburse adjutant's travel costs

*1828*

On 17 March 1828, following the Senate's exoneration of the Mitteler Rebbe, the Vitebsk treasury chamber wrote to Governor-General Khovansky asking how the 119 rubles 52 kopeks paid for Adjutant Vlasenko's 1825 trip to Starashele should be recovered. Since the Senate had found no one guilty, the funds would eventually have to be written off as a state expense. The exchange continued through September 1828.
