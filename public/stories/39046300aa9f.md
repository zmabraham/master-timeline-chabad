# Mazkir R' Reuven Liberman writes letter on state of Pleshchenitsy

*1910*

R' Reuven Liberman, leader of the Pleshchenitsy Chassidim after R' Avraham Chaim Rosenbaum's emigration, wrote on the eve of Shabbat Parshat Chukat 5670 (1910) describing how the town's glory had vanished. He contrasted the flourishing time when Rosenbaum lived there with its present decline, and longed to see him again even for a moment.
