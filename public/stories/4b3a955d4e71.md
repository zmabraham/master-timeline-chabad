# R. Mordechai Mentlik passes away

*1987*

R. Mordechai Mentlik passed away in New York on 25 Tishrei 5748 (October 18, 1987). He was the author of the work Amrei Mordechai and served as a member of the executive of Agudas Chassidei Chabad. Beginning when the Rebbe Rayatz arrived in New York and opened Tomchei Tmimim there, R. Mentlik served as the senior rosh yeshiva.
