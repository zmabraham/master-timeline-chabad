# Election of two delegates from Chernigov gubernia

*1909*

The city of Chernigov elected two delegates to the rabbinical conference: Rabbi David Tzvi (the Rad"tz) Chen, the religious rabbi of the city, and Mr. Yitzchak Schneersohn, the government-appointed rabbi (Rabbiner). The Rebbe Rashab wrote on the night of 25 Cheshvan that the government rabbi of Chernigov was not aligned with their camp, while the Rad"tz had only been chosen from Chernigov itself, with further district elections still pending.
