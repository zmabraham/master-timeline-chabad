# Zembin branch moves to Kovlitz after fire

*1900*

About two weeks before Shavuot 5660 (1900), a terrible fire destroyed the entire town of Zembin where the yeshiva branch was located. All houses, study halls and lodgings burned, and students fled to Lubavitch. After requests from several towns to host the yeshiva, lots were drawn and Kovlitz was chosen. Immediately after Shavuot the students relocated to Kovlitz with R' Shmuel Gronem.
