# Inauguration of yeshiva's large hall

*1901*

On the third day, 8 Tammuz 5661 (1901), the Great Hall (30 amos by 30 amos, originally built in 5632 as a prayer space) was renovated and inaugurated as the study hall of Tomchei Tmimim. The funds were donated by R' Yeshaya Berlin, one of the yeshiva's main supporters. The dedication ceremony was held with great splendor.
