# Dedication poem in Vilna Tanya for Rebbe Rashab

*1899*

The poem's first letters spelled out 'Shalom DovBer ni' and the closing letters 'b'Rabi Shmuel ziya'. When the first copy reached Rebbe Rashab on 22 Tevet, he was displeased: he had not approved the poem and instructed that copies not be sold until R' Asher signed the dedication openly. The page was later torn from most copies.
