# Chabad community in Jerusalem acquires Beit Menachem synagogue

*1858*

After being 'wanderers without a synagogue,' as the 5626 Montefiore report put it, the Chabad community in Jerusalem acquired the Beit Menachem synagogue starting in 5618 (1858). They added a study room on its roof, where the Dat Moshe ViYehudis beis midrash later hosted 20 bachurim and 15 baalei batim.
