# R' Shlomo Zalman Havlin and R' Moshe Dovber Rivkin arrive in USA from Yeshivas Toras Emes

*1928*

Stopping in Riga en route to receive the Frierdiker Rebbe's guidance, the two arrived in Hoboken at the end of Iyar to set up an independent office for Toras Emes — separate from Agudas Chassidei Chabad. Tension arose with Agudas Chassidei Chabad's chairman R' Chaim Zalman Kramer, who lobbied them to channel all fundraising through the existing umbrella. After weeks of negotiation and a marathon overnight meeting that briefly produced a tentative agreement, Havlin instead rented his own offices on Blake Street and later on Henry Street, installing R' Mordechai Gruner to manage it.
