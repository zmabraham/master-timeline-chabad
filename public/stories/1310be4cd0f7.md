# Library books trial: artifact collection transferred to library archive

*1987*

Rebbe Rayatz's yechidus room had contained a wall cabinet holding inherited holy objects, several without provenance markings. After the conclusion of the books-trial saga in 5746–47, this collection — including a much-used black silk sleeve attributed to the Tzemach Tzedek and a silver atarah likely traced to the Chernobyl–Cherkas families — was moved into the library's artifact archive for cataloguing and preservation.
