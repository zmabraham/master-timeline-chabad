# Daughter Feigel born to narrator on last day of Pesach 5709

*1949*

The narrator had begun serving as shamash at his East Flatbush shul at the start of Pesach and walked home on the final yom tov evening to discover the news. Feigel was the first child they named after his murdered mother-in-law, once they had confirmed she did not survive. Their son Gershon was similarly named after the wife's brother Gershon, hy'd.
