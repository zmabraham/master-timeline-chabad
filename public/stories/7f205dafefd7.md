# Wedding poem printed for R. Yitzchak Marini in Mantua

*1732*

The 5492 (1732) wedding of R. Yitzchak Marini and his bride Yudita in Mantua was honored with a printed poem of praise by R. Yeshayahu Bassan, author of Lechmei Todah. A companion sheet preserved in the library archive bears a poem by his son R. Yisrael Binyamin Bassan for the wedding of Ben-Tzion Ashkenazi and his bride Brachah.
