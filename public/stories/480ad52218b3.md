# R. Naeh responds to Rayatz's letter on Kollel's mission

*1922*

On 18 Sivan 5682 (June 1922), R. Naeh wrote a substantive reply to Rebbe Rayatz's recently-received letter. He agreed with the Rebbe's vision that the original purpose of the RMBN (Rabbi Meir Baal HaNes) fund was to support scholars in Eretz Yisrael, while acknowledging that since the era of the Tzemach Tzedek, simple working families had also been supported.
