# Toras Emes yeshivah founded in Hebron

*1911*

In 1911, the Rashab dispatched a group of outstanding Tomchei Temimim students led by Rabbi Zalman Havlin to Hebron to establish Toras Emes. As a branch of Tomchei Temimim, it was intended to invigorate chasidic study in Eretz Yisrael and produce a new cadre of Jewish leaders for the land.
