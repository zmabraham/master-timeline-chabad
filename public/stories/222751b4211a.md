# Bar mitzvah of M. Schusterman in Suraz

*1927*

After Shacharit at the 'rabbi's minyan' a bottle of liquor was placed on the table. Rabbi Axelrod called on the boy to deliver a drasha on tefillin from the verse 'V'shamarta es ha-chukah ha-zos l'mo'adah.' There were no invitations, no dinner, and no chair-lifting — only the minyan's congregants saying 'l'chaim.'
