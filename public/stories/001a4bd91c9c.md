# Passing of Rabbi DovBer, the Mittler Rebbe

*1827*

On 9 Kislev 5588 (late November / early December 1827), Rabbi DovBer Schneuri, the Mittler Rebbe, passed away. His passing came mid-way through the Senate's appeal proceedings concerning the Khovansky case; the Senate did not yet know of his death when it issued its ruling on the case days later. The date 9 Kislev is now marked annually as his yahrzeit, just one day before his Chag HaGeula on 10 Kislev.
