# R. Avraham Chaim forbids his wife to visit him in Smolensk

*1889*

R. Avraham Chaim's wife had written that she wished to come to Smolensk for public appearance's sake. He replied that he did not consent: it would mean abandoning the home and shop and placing a week's burden on their son Zalman, with little real benefit. The decision is recorded in his letter to R. Hendel from the week of Parshas Masei 5649.
