# Rebbe Rashab passes away in Rostov

*1920*

On Motzaei Shabbos Vayikra, the early morning of Sunday 2 Nissan 5680 (March 21, 1920) at 3:30 AM, the Rebbe Rashab passed away in Rostov-on-Don. In his final years he had occupied himself extensively with arranging the manuscript collection of his ancestors. Shortly before his passing he told the surrounding chassidim 'I am going to heaven, the writings I leave to you.'
