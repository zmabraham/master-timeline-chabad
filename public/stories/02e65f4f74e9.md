# Rabbi Lipsch farbrenging at the 28 Nissan 5783 gathering, Rabbi Yosef Yehuda

*1988*

Rabbi Lipsch farbrenging at the 28 Nissan 5783 gathering. Rabbi Yosef Yehuda Lipsch (born 5748, 1988) is a shliach of the Rebbe and director of the Chabad House in the Ramat Aviv Gimmel neighborhood, and a sought-after lecturer on topics of redemption and Moshiach.
