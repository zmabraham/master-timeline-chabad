# City authorities seize Lubavitch courtyard buildings

*1918*

At the end of 5678 (1918), after the last Tomchei Temimim students left Lubavitch, the municipality seized the courtyard. The great hall was converted into the city theater, the Rashab's house became the court building, the Maharash's rooms became the city council, the Rayatz's rooms became municipal offices, and the brick house became the city school.
