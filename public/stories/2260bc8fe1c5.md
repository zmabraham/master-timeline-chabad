# Three-judge din Torah in Lubavitch

*1817*

In 5577 (1816/17) a rabbinical court sat in Lubavitch composed of MaharI"L of Yanovich, the Mitteler Rebbe, and his brother R' Chaim Avraham. The proceedings, preserved in the genizah, illustrate how MaharI"L continued to anchor halachic decision-making in the Chabad center even after the Alter Rebbe's death.
