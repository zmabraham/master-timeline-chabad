# Rebbe arrives in Riga with library

*1927*

After protracted negotiations and intercession by Dr. Cohen of Berlin and Mordechai Dubin, the Soviet government permitted Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak to emigrate at the beginning of 5688 together with all his printed books and manuscripts. He settled in Riga and began rebuilding his life in exile, still burdened by emigration debts and without permanent quarters.
