# Jerusalem Chabad community petitions Montefiore for a synagogue

*1849*

The early Chabad settlers in Jerusalem, numbering some 30 baalei batim, sent a letter to Sir Moses Montefiore in 5609. They described their forced migration from Hebron two years earlier, the absence of a fixed beis midrash and mikveh, and asked for support to acquire a property they had found, available on a 12,000-grush mashkanta. Twelve heads of the community, led by R' Eliyahu Yosef Rivlin, signed the appeal.
