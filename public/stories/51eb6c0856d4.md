# Petersburg Rabbinical Conference on Jewish education

*1843*

The conference, proposed by the maskilim and convened by the Minister of Education, aimed to pressure leading rabbis to consent to government 'enlightenment' schools for Jewish youth. The Rebbe initially pleaded that his post-interrogation illness made travel impossible, but ultimately agreed that his attendance was essential to block undesirable resolutions. Following the conference he was granted the imperial title of Honorary Citizen for life.
