# Avigdor of Pinsk denounces Alter Rebbe to Tsar

*1800*

On 10 Iyar 5560 (23 April 1800), Avigdor, formerly rabbi of Pinsk and then residing in Vilna, submitted to the Tsar a lengthy denunciation against Rabbi Shneur Zalman of Liadi and the chassidic movement. He attacked Tzava'at HaRivash, argued the movement was a continuation of Sabbatianism, and complained that chassidim had caused his dismissal from the Pinsk rabbinate.
