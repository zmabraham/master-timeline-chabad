# R' DovBer Efrat leaves Hebron rabbinate for Jerusalem

*1902*

After nine years as rabbi of Hebron's Ashkenazi community, R' DovBer Efrat left in summer 1902 (קיץ תרס"ב) and settled in Jerusalem in Elul of that year after taking a new wife there. He explained in HaChavatzelet that the heavy burden of communal duties had become too much for him in his advancing age.
