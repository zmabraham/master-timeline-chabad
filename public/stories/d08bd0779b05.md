# Narrator reads Torah for Rebbe Rayatz on Shabbat HaChodesh 5709 at 770

*1949*

R. Yitzchak Dzikobson brought the narrator to 770 hoping merely to observe the Rebbe's maftir aliyah on this last Shabbat before Beis Nisan, the Rashab's yahrtzeit. Because the regular ba'al korei R. Yochanan Gordon was occupied downstairs at the aufruf of his future son-in-law Shimon Goldman, Dzikobson sent the narrator upstairs to lein for the Rebbe. The Rebbe Rayatz, seated in his wheelchair, signaled with his head the moment the reader accidentally voiced a stray 'beit' from 'bnei Yisrael' that does not appear in the verse — a final demonstration of his exacting attention to leining. This was the Rebbe's last Beis Nisan in this world.
