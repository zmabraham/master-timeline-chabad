# Gulf War ends with US-led coalition victory

*1991*

On Purim morning the delegation heard the news that the war in Iraq had ended in victory for the US-led coalition. Iraqi forces were entirely driven out of Kuwait, significant portions of Iraq were taken, and Saddam Hussein was given 48 hours to comply with UN resolutions or face continued advance. The diary frames this as a redemption parallel to the ongoing struggle to free the Schneerson library.
