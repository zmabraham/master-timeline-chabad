# Kollel Chabad writes Rebbe Rashab on Siberian shlichim, citing 1868 agreement

*1902*

When the Kollel Chabad officials in Jerusalem debated dispatching their own emissaries to the cities of Siberia, they wrote to Rebbe Sholom DovBer of Lubavitch explaining that they were bound by an earlier pesher with the Perushim Vaad HaKlali. They attached a copy of the 1868 agreement which had been preserved in the kollel's letter register. The letter is the earliest known reference to that agreement in Chabad correspondence.
