# Secretary R. Yechezkel Feigin reports Rebbe's recovery in Warsaw

*1933*

Feigin reported that during the Yamim Noraim and Sukkos the Rebbe delivered deep Hemshech maamarim of nearly two hours, though it was beyond his strength. Students repeated the maamarim and selected ones entered the Rebbe to chazer them, evoking the atmosphere of Lubavitch twenty years earlier.
