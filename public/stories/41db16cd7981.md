# Third interrogation of Rabbi DovBer; investigators sent to Lubavitch and Strashelye

*1825*

On 19 October 1825 (19 Marcheshvan 5586), Rabbi DovBer faced his third interrogation in Vitebsk. The same day, the authorities dispatched a special investigator to Lubavitch to verify the Rebbe's testimony against local realities, and sent a separate officer to Strashelye to bring Rabbi Aharon Halevi of Staroshele for questioning in the same broader inquiry into chassidic fundraising.
