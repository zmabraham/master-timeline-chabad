# Hebron Chabad sends delegation to greet Montefiore in Jerusalem

*1839*

When Montefiore wrote from Jerusalem to the heads of the Hebron community about the purpose of his visit, the letter arrived in Hebron on 28 Sivan. That same day the Chabad community wrote a letter of invitation asking him to visit their city, sent through a special delegation of two community leaders who traveled to Jerusalem to escort him personally to Hebron.
