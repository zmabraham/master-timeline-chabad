# Agudas Chassidei Chabad established in America

*1924*

Many Jews had emigrated from the chasidic heartland in Russia to new communities worldwide. Some were ardent Chasidim, others merely nostalgic. While America formalized Chabad in 1924, in many other countries the connections remained informal. The Rebbe encouraged his Chasidim to expand their vision and take responsibility for all Jews.
