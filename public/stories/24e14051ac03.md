# Passing of the seventh Lubavitcher Rebbe in Brooklyn

*1994*

After 42 years of leadership during which he transformed Chabad into the largest Jewish movement in the world, the seventh Rebbe passed away on 3 Tamuz 5754. He was buried in Montefiore Cemetery, New York, next to the Rayatz. The Ohel has since become a pilgrimage site visited by close to a million Jews and non-Jews seeking prayer and inspiration.
