# Rebbe Rayatz writes to Tzemach Tzedek yeshiva students

*1941*

The Rebbe asked about their fixed Chassidus learning schedule on weekdays and Shabbat and instructed them to study his recently delivered maamarim. He emphasized that each student of Tzemach Tzedek must create an atmosphere of Torah study around himself. He blessed them and their families with health, livelihood, and protection from trouble.
