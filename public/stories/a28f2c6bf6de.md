# Enlightenment activist Mordechai Ben Hillel scouts Lubavitch for a school

*1897*

Ben Hillel reported that Lubavitch was visited by thousands annually and that establishing a school there would influence all of Russian Jewry. With the support of Baron Gunzburg, the school opened with 130 students despite the Rashab's strong opposition. The Rashab would later lament Gunzburg's support at a major farbrengen.
