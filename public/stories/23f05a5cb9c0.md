# Hebron Romano land purchase deed

*1871*

In 1871 a deed in Arabic was executed recording the purchase of a Hebron parcel for 'Markado the Jew of Constantinople residing in al-Khalil' from Khalil, son of the late Ibrahim Abu-Amar Siyaj, for 4,000 grush. The Romano family used such purchases to begin building outside the walls of the Jewish quarter, breaking the ghetto pattern. The grand Romano mansion that resulted was constructed over the following years and completed in 5638.
