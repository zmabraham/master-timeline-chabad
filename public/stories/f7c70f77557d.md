# Rebbe Rashab and Rabbi Chaim Soloveitchik return to Petersburg

*1910*

During the month of Iyar 5670, the Rebbe Rashab and Rabbi Chaim Soloveitchik of Brisk returned together to Petersburg. The state of communal affairs required them to remain in the capital through the end of Sivan. On the second day of Shavuot, Rabbi Chaim joined the Rebbe Rashab for the festive holiday meal, where they discussed words of Torah.
