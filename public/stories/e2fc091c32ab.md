# WWII breaks out and Vilna Tomchei Tmimim temporarily closes

*1939*

When World War II broke out in Elul 5699, the Vilna Tomchei Tmimim shut down and dispersed its students to their homes. R' Yitzchak Dov Oshpal returned to his family in Koretz. Shortly afterward, the yeshiva reopened as a refuge for displaced Warsaw Tomchei Tmimim students, with R' Oshpal returning to head it.
