# Rashab founds Va'ad HaChevroni to protect Hebron Chabad community

*1905*

After the kollel's center shifted from Hebron to Jerusalem, the Hebron Chabad community declined economically as Jerusalem-based administrators favored their own city. The Rashab established the Va'ad HaChevroni to advocate for Hebron's rights abroad and demanded that a Hebron representative be included among the official signatories receiving kollel funds. He saw protecting Hebron as essential to maintaining the legacy of his predecessors who had specifically chosen Hebron.
