# R' Yaakos Pinchas Meyuchas (born 5744, 1984) is a lawyer

*1984*

R' Yaakos Pinchas Meyuchas (born 5744, 1984) is a lawyer by profession, a politician and public activist, one of the members of the Chabad community in Beitar Illit, and director of the chareidi headquarters of the Religious Zionism party (formerly - the National Union)
