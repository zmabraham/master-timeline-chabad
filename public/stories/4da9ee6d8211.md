# Gorbachev defers seforim decision again

*1991*

Returning at dawn on Friday 15 Av 5751 from the Lubavitch trip, the author spoke with R. Y.Y. Aharonov, who reported Chernyaev's latest update: no decision today, possibly Monday, possibly only when Bush arrived in Moscow. Veronika was deeply disappointed. The repeated postponements, despite the commission vote and high-level pressure, left the delegation again in suspense.
