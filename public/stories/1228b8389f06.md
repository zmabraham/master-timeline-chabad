# Chernobyl children airlift departs Moscow for Israel

*1990*

On Friday 11 Tevet 5751 the delegation accompanied Chernobyl children and their families to the Moscow airport for the Chabad-coordinated airlift to Eretz Yisrael, which lifted off before Shabbos and landed in Israel on schedule. The same day Veronika met with the Justice Minister, who confirmed that a special government commission under Lukyanov had been convened to decide the library question. The successful airlift removed a parallel concern that the library campaign might endanger the children's evacuation.
