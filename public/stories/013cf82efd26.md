# Delegation sends document sets to Gorbachev and Gubenko aides

*1991*

On 21 Shevat 5751 the delegation finalized memoranda received from Yona Pruss in London and from Singer regarding Bronfman's conversations with Yakovlev, and made copies at the Armand Hammer office. Rabbi Koegan delivered four sets to Veronika and then a set each to Chernyaev, Gorbachev's aide, and Babushkin, Gubenko's aide, while Rabbi Aharonov and the diarist brought four sets to Rabbi Cunin at the U.S. Consulate.
