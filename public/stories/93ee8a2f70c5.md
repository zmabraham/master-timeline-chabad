# Lieberman cables the Rebbe's Warsaw address from Riga

*1939*

Lieberman, having escaped Warsaw to Riga, transmitted the Rebbe's location and pleaded for accelerated immigration. Asher Rabinowitz wired Justice Brandeis with the address, who passed it to Cohen in the White House, then to Geist in Berlin, and finally to Lt. Col. Ernst Bloch leading the search in Warsaw.
