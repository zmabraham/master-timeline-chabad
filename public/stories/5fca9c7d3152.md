# Rebbe instructs loans for rescue travel expenses

*1940*

On 25 Adar Sheni 5700, the Frierdiker Rebbe wrote to R. Mordechai Chefetz in Riga instructing that gemach loans be given: 2,500 to R. Yehuda Eber, 1,500 to R. Moshe Leib Rotstein, and up to 500 lats to R. Shmuel Zalmanov, all on the Rebbe's account.
