# Kiryat Gat mayor sends Rebbe key of the city — first such gift

*1962*

In Elul 5722 (August–September 1962), the mayor of Kiryat Gat in Israel became the first municipal head to express appreciation for Chabad by sending the Rebbe the key of the city. He entrusted the key to Rabbi Yaakov Peles, who later delivered it. This precedent led many other mayors to follow suit through their local shluchim.
