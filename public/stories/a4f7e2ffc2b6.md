Rabbi Isser Zalman Weisberg. Rabbi Isser Zalman Weisberg (born 5714, 1954) is a Chabad chassid serving as a Torah editor and consultant for askanim and organizations. He previously served as rabbi in several communities and as an educator in high school and post-high school institutions. He lived in Toronto
