# Rebbe gives extra dollar for tzedakah before family's trip to Israel

*1991*

Before traveling to Israel on Sunday, 12 Marcheshvan 5752, the family received dollars from the Rebbe, who added an extra dollar for tzedakah in Eretz Yisrael. With a smile he remarked that surely they had arranged who would read from the Torah, to which the writer's wife replied 'certainly!' — and the Rebbe blessed them with great success.
