# Wedding of Shlomo Zalman Hecht, son-in-law of Rabbi Jacobson

*1938*

Rabbi Israel Jacobson, head of Achei Tmimim in America, arranged that immediately after his daughter's wedding on 3 Elul 5698, the young couple would travel to Poland so the groom could study in Tomchei Tmimim in Otwock. This had been approved in advance by the Rebbe Rayatz, and the trip was made with great fanfare.
