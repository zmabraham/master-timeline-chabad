# R' Shneur Zalman Gurarie heads Tomchei Tmimim Warsaw mechina

*1933*

At the start of 5693 (autumn 1932) R' Shneur Zalman Gurarie was appointed head of the mechina — the preparatory division of Tomchei Tmimim Warsaw, then located at Maranow 9 with two classes. The appointment marked his transition from senior student to leadership within the yeshiva system. He would soon take on broader organizational roles.
