# Colonel Wojciech reports on his investigation of the Tzemach Tzedek

*1841*

Earlier, during the February fair in Lyubavichi, Wojciech had entered the Rebbe's home on the pretext of inquiring about the synagogue, finding him studying from a book and conversing in halting Russian. He described the Tzemach Tzedek as over fifty, physically weak, living in poverty with six sons and two daughters, registered as a third-guild merchant but leaving trade to his family. Wojciech concluded the Rebbe was a 'simple man with limited intellect,' morally above reproach, who declined the title of rabbi inherited from his ancestors and wished only to live quietly with his books.
