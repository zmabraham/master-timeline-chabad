# Rayatz petitions Rumyantsev Library director for return of books

*1922*

Lodging at the home of R. Meir Yaakovson during a stay focused on klal work, Rayatz petitions the library director, recounting the 1921 Narkompros decision in his favor and explaining that the collection had migrated from the nationalized Kokorev warehouses to the Rumyantsev Library only because he had lacked storage. The petition was not honored, and the books remained in the library's possession.
