# Passing of R' Chaim Shmuel Shmerling in Jaffa

*1894*

R' Chaim Shmuel Shmerling passed away in Jaffa on 20 Adar I 5654 and was buried in Jerusalem. Twenty-eight years after founding the Jaffa Chabad community in 5626, his death marked the end of his role in extending the family's pioneering legacy across the Chabad settlements of Eretz Yisrael.
