# Rebbe directs founding of libraries on 6 Tishrei

*1972*

At the 6 Tishrei 5733 (1972) farbrengen, the Rebbe directed his chassidim to found libraries of sacred books across the world. Dozens of new libraries opened at Chabad Houses in various countries, including one founded by Tzeirei Agudas Chabad of New York which the Rebbe named 'Sifriyat Levi Yitzchak' in honor of his father.
