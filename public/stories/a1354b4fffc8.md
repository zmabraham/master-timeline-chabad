# Summer 1772: Alter Rebbe's halachic deliberation with the Maggid in Rovno

*1772*

The Alter Rebbe's responsum on the leniency of R. Levi Yitzchok of Berditchev in checking lungs records this earlier discussion at Rovno. The Maggid demonstrated from the Talmud that residual filaments on the lung are not themselves a sirkha, illustrating how halacha was taught alongside chassidut in Mezeritch/Rovno.
