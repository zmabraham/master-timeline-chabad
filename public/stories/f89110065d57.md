# Rebbe celebrates last Simchat Torah with Russian Chasidim in Leningrad

*1927*

In October 1927, the Rebbe celebrated his last holiday in Russia, Simchat Torah, with his Chasidim in Leningrad. Despite the risk of arrest, Chasidim flocked from across the country; the synagogue swelled with people packed into every corner. The Rebbe urged them, 'Don't fear anyone,' and consoled them, 'I will be with you,' while one Chasid wept, 'The Rebbe is going, and we are left here.'
