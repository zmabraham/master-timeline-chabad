# R' Meir of Bychov departs on his first shlichut

*1789*

The author corrects the standard reference work 'Shluchei Eretz Yisrael,' showing that R' Meir of Bychov's first shlichut began in summer 5549 (not 5551 as commonly cited). Evidence includes a letter of Erev Rosh Chodesh Tammuz 5549 from R' Shmuel Gronem introducing R' Meir as the bearer. R' Meir returned to Eretz Yisrael in Menachem Av 5551.
