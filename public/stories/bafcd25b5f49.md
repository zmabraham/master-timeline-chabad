# Rebbe endorses the Hassidic-lineage students' union

*1940*

Responding to a letter dated 4 Adar I, the Rebbe approved the founding of an union of students of Hassidic lineage who were studying in the Lithuanian yeshivot of Kamenitz, Kletzk, Pinsk, Bialystok, Lomza, Ostrow-Brisk, Chachmei Lublin, Tomchei Tmimim and Slonim. He praised the new bylaws, the spiritual committee and the executive board, and emphasized that success would depend on the spiritual committee's commitment.
