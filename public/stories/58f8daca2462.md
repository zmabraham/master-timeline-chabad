# Breslov Haggadah 'Or Zoreach' arranged by Moshe Yehoshua Bzhilianski, killed in 1919

*1927*

In 5687 (1927), the Breslov community in Jerusalem published the Haggadah 'Or Zoreach', based on the writings of Rabbi Nachman of Breslov and arranged by the late Moshe Yehoshua Bzhilianski known as Alter of Teplik, who was killed sanctifying God's name in 5679 (1919). The 53-page octavo edition brought Rebbe Nachman's distinctive interpretation of the Haggadah to a wide audience. It served as a foundational text for the Breslov Chassidic community.
