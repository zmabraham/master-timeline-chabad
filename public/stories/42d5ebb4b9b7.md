# Matzah operation for Soviet Jewry begins

*1928*

Reports from across the USSR indicated that Passover flour could not be obtained anywhere. The Rebbe launched an enormous operation to ship 28 train cars of matzah to communities in 17 Soviet towns and 5,689 packages of matzah flour to private addresses in 404 towns. Negotiations with the Soviet embassy in Riga and Berlin over export permits and customs duties dominated the next three months.
