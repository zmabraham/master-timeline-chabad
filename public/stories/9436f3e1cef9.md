# Shabbos Beha'aloscha 5674 at the Zaalshe dacha

*1914*

On Shabbos Parshas Beha'aloscha 5674 the author was at the Rebbe Rashab's summer dacha at Zaalshe. The Rebbe spoke about the root of the middos that transcends the intellect. The author would visit Zaalshe again that summer for Parshas Pinchas and Parshas Ki Setze.
