# Aguda Tmimim membership flyer arrives at the Library

*1989*

On Rosh Chodesh Kislev 5750, a flyer was brought to the Lubavitch Library showing that already in 5670 a printed list of Tmimim had been prepared. The list contained only members of the Vaad Aguda Tmimim — alumni of Tomchei Tmimim from 5657 to 5670 who had married before 14 Teves 5670 — and notably included the Rebbe's father Rabbi Levi Yitzchak Schneerson and his two brothers, prompting the Rebbe's later acknowledgment 'I have seen and thank you.'
