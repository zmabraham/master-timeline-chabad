# Rabbi Levi Yitzchak Schneerson passes away in exile

*1944*

After Soviet exile in Chiili (Kazakhstan), Rabbi Levi Yitzchak Schneerson was eventually permitted to move to Alma-Ata, where he passed away on 20 Av 5704 (Aug 9, 1944). His wife Rabbanit Chana had sent him the Tehillim he recited tearfully in his final years; she later brought it out of Russia.
