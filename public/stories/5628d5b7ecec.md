# Rabbi Dov Landau shakes hands with Rabbi Yehuda Leib Shapira

*1930*

Rabbi Dov Landau shakes hands with Rabbi Yehuda Leib Shapira. Rabbi Ephraim Dov Landau (born 7 Nisan 5690, April 5, 1930) is the central leader of the Lithuanian-Charedi community, and the supreme authority on community matters in the general Charedi public for his rabbi
