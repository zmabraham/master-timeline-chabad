# Tzemach Tzedek begins nesius in Lubavitch

*1828*

Following the Mitteler Rebbe's passing in late 5588, the Tzemach Tzedek eventually took up the mantle of Chabad leadership in Lubavitch. His nesius would extend until 5626 (1866). Under him Lubavitch became a major center of halachic responsa and chassidic teaching.
