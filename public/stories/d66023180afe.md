# Tzeirei Agudas Chabad founded in Eretz Yisrael

*1951*

Soon after assuming the nesius, the Rebbe founded the Tzeirei Agudas Chabad youth organization in Eretz Yisrael in the summer of 5711 (1951), as described in the introduction to Igros Kodesh vol. 5. The organization was tasked with spreading Yiddishkeit and chassidus among broad circles of Jewish youth.
