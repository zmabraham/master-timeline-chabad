# Rayatz writes Dr. A. Freimann about lost library

*1928*

Writing from exile in Riga, Rayatz tells the noted Judaica scholar Freimann that the library, internationally known as 'the book treasury of the Lubavitcher Rebbe Schneersohn,' was unlawfully seized by the Soviet government during his Rostov exile, and that he draws strength from the hope that with G-d's help it will eventually be returned.
