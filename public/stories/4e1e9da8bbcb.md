# Letter from Hebron migrants to Montefiore

*1849*

Two years after relocating to Jerusalem, leaders of the migrant families wrote to Sir Moses Montefiore in 5609. They described the crushing oppression of the local rulers in Hebron and the influx of new arrivals which only intensified the burdens. They explained that the special share allocated to Abdul Rahman in the Chabad kollel's distributions consumed funds intended for their own livelihood, forcing them to thin out the Hebron community.
