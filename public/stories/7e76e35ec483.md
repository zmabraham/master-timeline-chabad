# Chatzkevich becomes rabbi of Ponimonek

*1911*

In 5671 (1911) Chatzkevich succeeded his father-in-law R. Yitzchak Moshe Weiler as rabbi of Ponimonek. The Tomchei Tmimim journal 'HaAch' (29 Sivan 5671) congratulated him, calling him a beloved chossid and yarei shomayim and wishing him further elevation.
