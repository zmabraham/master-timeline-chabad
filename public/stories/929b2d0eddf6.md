# Publication of Sefer HaZichronot Divrei HaYamim

*1976*

Gurkov was the father-in-law of Yisrael Yehuda Levin and one of the first Anash to comply with the Rebbe Rayatz's 1946-1949 directive to record memoirs of Soviet-era religious work. His volume became a foundational primary source for the history of Chabad in the Soviet Union.
