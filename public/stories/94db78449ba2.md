# Tomchei Tmimim Homel adds assistant rosh yeshiva

*1922*

Students gradually streamed into the Homel branch of Tomchei Tmimim, and by the start of Adar a second teacher was needed. Rabbi Yosef Baruch was hired as assistant to R' Yechezkel Feigin, and the student Meir Shor of Padobranka was appointed deputy mashgiach. Although other shuls now offered space, the chassidim remained at the Babushkin shul on grounds of hazakah and were granted permission to set up long study tables throughout the building.
