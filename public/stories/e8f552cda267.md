# Rayatz organizes Rashab's manuscripts during the year of aveilus

*1920*

Rayatz arranged the maamarim chronologically, one binder per year, adding a handwritten label to each binder and compiling a detailed index of openings. These bound binders are now numbered 2541-2585 in the Library archive. He likewise organized Rashab's letters, and on 19 Marcheshvan 5681 recorded in his diary the count of maamarim and letters then in his possession.
