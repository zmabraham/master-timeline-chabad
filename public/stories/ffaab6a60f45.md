# R. Avraham Ziskind passes away

*1967*

R. Avraham Zushe Ziskind passed away in New York on Rosh Chodesh Nisan 5727 (April 11, 1967). After his marriage he had served as a shochet in New York, engaging extensively in halacha and chassidus, and in his final years headed the avreichim kollel attached to the Rebbe's secretariat.
