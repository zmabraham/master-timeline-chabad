# Tzarist conscription of yeshiva students intensifies

*1914*

Following Sukkot 5675 the standard call-up of 21-year-olds was followed by ever-younger classes as the war intensified. Most Tomchei Tmimim students were drafted, and many of those discharged could not return to Lubavitch. The campaign reshaped the yeshiva's enrollment for the rest of the war.
