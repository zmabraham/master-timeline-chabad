# R' Shneur Zalman Zeligson, Vilna yeshiva co-founder, passes away

*1930*

R' Shneur Zalman Zeligson prayed in the building that became the Vilna yeshiva before its founding and was among its founders. He later served as an examiner of the students and a supporter of the institution until his passing on 25 Shevat 5690, as recounted by his student R' Moshe Dreyzin.
