# R' Shmuel Zalmanov sends Rayatz overview of Tomchei Tmimim Warsaw

*1930*

On 20 Cheshvan 5691, R' Shmuel Zalmanov wrote to the Rebbe Rayatz with a general overview of Tomchei Tmimim Warsaw at the start of the new term. He reported 29 new students admitted and 110 students in total studying across three divisions, detailing who was teaching nigleh and chassidus in each class and stressing the continuing need for an inner mashpia.
