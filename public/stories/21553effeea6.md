# Telegram reports US consul has obtained nothing from Washington for refugees

*1941*

On 27 May 1941 (Rosh Chodesh Sivan 5701), students wired Rashag that the American consul in Yokohama had visited but had received nothing new from Washington and that the situation remained as before. Student Goldman had denied his Polish relatives on the application form and would receive his visa, while the Canadian visas had not yet arrived.
