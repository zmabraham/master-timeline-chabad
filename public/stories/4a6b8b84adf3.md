# The Rebbe in Brooklyn Heights and prolific author in English - see Aharon Leib Raskin (Brooklyn Heights)

*1960*

The Rebbe in Brooklyn Heights and a prolific author in the English language, see Aharon Leib Raskin (Brooklyn Heights). Rabbi Raskin farbrenging at 770. Rabbi Aharon Leib (Eric) Raskin (born 5720, 1960) is a member of the editorial board of Otzar HaChassidim - editor of the book series.
