# Rebbe and Tomchei Tmimim relocate from Warsaw to Otwock

*1935*

Otwock's clean air suited the Rebbe's health, and the resort town also proved a better setting for the yeshiva than Warsaw. The move fulfilled a plan the Rebbe had already harbored in 5691 but which had been deferred. Otwock would remain the seat of the court and the yeshiva until the outbreak of World War II.
