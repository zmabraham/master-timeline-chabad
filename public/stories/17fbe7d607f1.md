# Departure from Zhuravichi toward yeshiva

*1907*

On the night of 28 Tishrei 5668, the author and his father set out from Zhuravichi by wagon, first arriving in Rogachev on Sunday morning. That evening they continued by covered wagon some 60 versts to Bobruisk, arriving Monday morning, to consult the father's uncle R. Tuvia the shochet about whether to send the boy to the Lida yeshiva or to Lubavitch.
