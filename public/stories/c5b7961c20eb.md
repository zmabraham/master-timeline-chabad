# Ramash leads Lag BaOmer farbrengen in Paris

*1947*

The future seventh Rebbe traveled to France in 1947 to reunite with his mother Rebbetzin Chana after twenty years of separation. At the Lag BaOmer gathering he told the story of how, on December 2, 1946, the sixth Rebbe had recited Az Yashir and proclaimed 'They got through' at the exact hour the refugees were detained at the border.
