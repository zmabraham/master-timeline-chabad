# Rebbe Rayatz arrives in America and opens Tomchei Tmimim in New York

*1940*

Upon the Rebbe Rayatz's arrival in America at the end of winter 5700, he established the Tomchei Tmimim yeshiva in New York. R' Yitzchak Dov Oshpal, who had escaped to America during the war, was appointed Rosh Mesivta and educator, a position he held the rest of his life.
