# New executive committee elected at Anash gathering in Warsaw

*1931*

Following a general meeting of Anash held on Motzaei Shabbos Shemos and continued on Motzaei Shabbos Vaera, an expanded executive committee was elected to manage Tomchei Tmimim Warsaw. The committee was reported in a letter dated 2 Shevat 5691 to the Rebbe's son-in-law Rashag, who had been placed in charge of the yeshiva's treasury. Members included R' Mendel Rotstein as honorary chairman and R' Shraga Feivish Zalmanov as chairman.
