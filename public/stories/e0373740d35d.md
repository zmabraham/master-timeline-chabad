# Levin's second yechidus on Rosh Chodesh Cheshvan 5723

*1962*

On his second yechidus, just before 10pm on the night of Rosh Chodesh Cheshvan 5723, Levin handed in a lengthy tzettel and mentioned the kadosh R' Yona Cohen. The Rebbe asked for his mother's name and his date of death and instructed Levin to fix a yahrzeit, say kaddish, and learn mishnayot. Levin later confirmed with R' Elya Chaim Roitblat (R' Yona's brother-in-law) that the date was 22 Shevat and the mother's name was Frade, and conveyed the information to R' Chadakov.
