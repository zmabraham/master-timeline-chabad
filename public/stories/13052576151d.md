# Haaretz reports the Rebbe has perished

*1939*

With the Rebbe's whereabouts unknown to Chasidim worldwide, false reports of his death circulated in the press. Two days later Haaretz relayed a Vilna dispatch saying he was alive, and on November 16 reported that Tel Aviv Chasidim had received a telegram from Rabbi Chaim Ozer Grodzinski confirming the Rebbe was alive in Warsaw.
