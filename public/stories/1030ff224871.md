# Chai Elul farbrengen at Moscow AGUH shul

*1991*

Following his regular Shulchan Aruch shiur in the yeshiva, the author drove to the AGUH shul for the farbrengen. He spoke on the ahavas Yisrael of the Baal Shem Tov and of the Rebbe, who sends shluchim to care for every Jew in Russia, with Korbsky translating. Speakers also included R. Kogan, the shochet R. Mottel Lifshitz, and Rappoport.
