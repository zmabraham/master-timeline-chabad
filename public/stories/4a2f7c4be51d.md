# R' Shimon Zelichover deported from Krakow to the death camps

*1943*

After years of teaching in Tzanz, Krzanov, Stolin, Tomchei Tmimim Warsaw, Yeshivat Chochmei Lublin and finally Krakow, Engel's life ended in the Shoah. His Torah was later collected in the volume Naharei Eish, printed in New York in 5753.
