# Passing of the Rebbe Rashab in Rostov-on-Don

*1920*

Diagnosed with typhoid on Sunday after a Friday onset of illness, the Rashab's condition deteriorated through the week. Late at night he asked to be moved to his study, telling Chasidim 'I am going to heaven, and I am leaving you my manuscripts.' After blessing each of his daughters and his son, he passed at dawn while whispering 'with all your heart and all your soul' from the Shema.
