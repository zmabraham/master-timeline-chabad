# Visits to Chabad summer camps in beis hamikdash halachos

*1991*

On Monday morning 5 Menachem Av 5751, the author traveled with his wife to the boys' camp and stayed through Tuesday afternoon, teaching the upper grades in hilchos beis hamikdash and continuing the nightly group. On Tuesday afternoon he visited the girls' camp, spoke during dinner on beis hamikdash, and reviewed the next day's shiur with a counselor. On Wednesday 7 Menachem Av he taught the fourth-year class in the Moscow yeshiva on the same topic.
