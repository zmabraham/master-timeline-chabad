# Construction begins on the Mittler Rebbe's great synagogue in Lubavitch

*1814*

After purchasing a wooden house from the local nobleman Krauer, the Mittler Rebbe built a sizable synagogue in the courtyard. According to the tsarist investigator Korbitsky's later report, it measured roughly 21 by 13 meters; the Rebbe Rayatz recorded much larger dimensions. The site reportedly stood where the Alter Rebbe had studied with R' Yissachar Ber decades earlier.
