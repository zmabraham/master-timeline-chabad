# Russian decree on conscription of displaced rabbis

*1916*

While the initial conscription circular addressed only resident rabbis, a follow-up order extended the registration requirements to rabbis who had been displaced in summer 5675 from the border districts. These cases had to be processed through officials at the rabbis' original places of residence, from which they had been deported, severely impeding orderly clarification. Throughout summer 5676, the Rebbe Rashab continued to advocate for their status.
