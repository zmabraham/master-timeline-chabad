# Hadoar publishes 'The New Book of Lamentations' on Soviet Jewish colonies

*1928*

The letter, addressed to JDC Moscow head Dr. Rosen, denounced the Soviet plan to relocate Jews to communal farms as a Yevsektzia scheme 'to break down and destroy the Jewish spirit.' It documented hunger, cold, and religious oppression in the colonies. 'We plow and plant with tears and harvest with despair,' the writer warned.
