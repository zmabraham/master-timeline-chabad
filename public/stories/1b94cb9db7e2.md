# Rayatz asks Eliyahu Simpson for weekly written summaries of Rashab's maamarim

*1913*

While traveling and unable to hear Rashab's discourses himself, Rayatz writes Simpson to send him each week a brief outline of the Shabbos maamar — the opening, the conclusion, and the connective content, rather than a full hanachah. He asks Simpson to coordinate with fellow chozrim Alter and Yerucham to ensure the reports arrive consistently at the address provided.
