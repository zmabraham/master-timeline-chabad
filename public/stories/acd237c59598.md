# Hebron Chabad families relocate to Jerusalem

*1847*

Unable to bear the burden of Sheikh Abdul Rahman's exactions and post-conquest hardship, several Chabad families left Hebron for Jerusalem starting in 5607. They settled in the northern part of the Old City on a street the Arabs called 'Harat al-Wad,' which became known among Ashkenazim as 'Rechov HaChevronim' — the Hebronites' Street — for the very families who settled there. In 5609 they described their motives in a moving letter to Montefiore.
