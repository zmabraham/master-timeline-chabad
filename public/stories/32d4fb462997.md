# Rebbe praises Shimon Bliner for establishing Mishnah and chasidic classes in Moscow

*1927*

The Rebbe wrote, 'I enjoyed reading that you established classes in Mishnah and chasidic philosophy. I read the names of those involved; please share with them my blessing.' This was one of many letters showing the Rebbe's intimate involvement in underground Russian Jewish activity from his Riga base.
