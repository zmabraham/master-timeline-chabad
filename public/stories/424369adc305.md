# Levin arrives in Moscow at Hotel Minsk to begin library inspection

*1999*

His original plan had been to start work the same day, but Mira had warned by fax that a Russian national holiday closed the library. He spent the day at the Agudas Chabad center conversing with Rabbi Yisroel Kogan and Boruch Gorin, then returned to his hotel to rest and pray. He scheduled the first inspection day for the morning of 5 Tevet, 'didan natzach' day.
