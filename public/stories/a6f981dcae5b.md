# Yahrzeit of Rebbetzin Yocheved bat Leah

*1919*

The 5705 diary records that 16 Marcheshvan was the yahrzeit of the Rebbe Rayatz's mother-in-law, Rebbetzin Yocheved bat Leah, who passed away on 16 Marcheshvan 5680 (9 November 1919). The Rebbe marked the day by preparing a Yiddish maamar on 'Hayta li dim'ati' for the Kriah V'Kedushah journal.
