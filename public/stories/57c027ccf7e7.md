# R' Yehuda Leib Slonim joins Hebron Kollel leadership

*1872*

He joined the existing trio of R' Shimon Menashe Chaikin, R' Levi Yitzchak Slonim, and R' Shneur Zalman Pundaminski. The four men together appeared as overseers in the 5635 census, alongside R' Yeshaya Ornstein, who served as the kollel's scribe while living in Jerusalem.
