# Polish gentile delivers Maharik with Alter Rebbe's glosses

*1986*

In the summer of 5746 (1986), a Polish gentile came to the United States carrying a copy of Responsa Maharik (Lvov 5558) that contained marginal annotations in the handwriting of the Alter Rebbe of Chabad. He claimed it had come to him by inheritance from his grandfather. A Chabad chassid purchased the volume and returned it to the Chabad library.
