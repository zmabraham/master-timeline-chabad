# Rebbe Rayatz sends letter of encouragement to Shomrei Shabbos gemach

*1942*

The earliest documented contact between the Rebbe Rayatz and the Chabad Shomrei Shabbos gemach is a 5702 letter in which the Rebbe sent encouragement together with a check, asking 'to participate with them in their good actions.' This was during the Rebbe Rayatz's early American years and the height of World War II.
