# Tzemach Tzedek pens responsum seeking to release R' Hillel's vow

*1855*

When R' Hillel cited his old vow as blocking his service as borer, the Tzemach Tzedek personally researched whether the vow could be released. He drafted a responsum on the question, joined by responsa from R' Yitzchak Aizik of Homel (author of Chana Ariel) and R' Shneur Zalman of Vielizh. The Tzemach Tzedek's reply was eventually published in Shu"t Tzemach Tzedek, Yoreh Deah siman 181.
