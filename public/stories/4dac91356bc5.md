# Khovansky orders Adjutant Vlasenko sent to fetch R. Aharon of Starashele

*1825*

On 19 October 1825, Governor-General Khovansky directed the Vitebsk treasury chamber to pay Adjutant Captain Vlasenko's travel expenses for a mission, accompanied by a policeman of the Mogilev province, to the town of Starashele. The trip was to bring R. Aharon Halevi of Starashele to Vitebsk to be questioned in connection with the Kissin informer affair. Five days later the treasury reported the cost at 119 rubles 52 kopeks.
