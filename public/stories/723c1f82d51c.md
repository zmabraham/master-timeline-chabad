# Partial rabbinic exemption granted by Russian government

*1916*

After persistent lobbying, the government granted exemption or extended deferments for approximately fifty rabbis on the conscription lists. This represented only a small fraction of those awaiting decisions, and it remained unclear whether the exemption came by direct Imperial decree or only by ministerial decision. The Rebbe Rashab immediately wrote to Reb Shmuel Michel Trainin to clarify the details and try to bring the matter before the Tsar.
