# Rebbe Rayatz arrives in America at New York Harbor

*1929*

Through the intervention of attorney Yekusiel Kremer, the Rebbe and his entourage were transferred at sea onto a small government vessel at South Ferry, sparing him the ordinary immigration disembarkation. A welcoming delegation that included R. Mordechai Lipa Lokshin, the Kremer brothers, Judge Philip Kleinfeld, and R. Shmuel Eliezer Nelson boarded the cutter to greet him. Despite a torrential downpour and an under-organized reception, the Rebbe was escorted through Manhattan by motorcycle police to R. Eliyahu Simpson's home in Boro Park, where he gave brief opening remarks ("Sadna de'ar'a chad hu") from the balcony.
