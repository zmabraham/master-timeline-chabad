# Rebbe Rashab inquires after SILA in Bukhara

*1908*

Still in Bukhara nearly a year after his arrival, SILA received a letter from the Rebbe Rashab on 7 Adar II 5668 expressing his strong desire that SILA return home. The Rebbe asked for a detailed report on his welfare and the duration of his remaining stay. SILA would not return to Chevron until Shevat 5669.
