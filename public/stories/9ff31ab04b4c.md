# Delegation flies from Moscow to Kiev to search for the lost library

*1990*

On Thursday, 9 Shevat 5750 (February 4, 1990), the eve of the Frierdiker Rebbe's yahrzeit, Rabbi Shlomo Cunin and Rabbi Berel Levine flew from Moscow to Kiev together with their assigned Soviet librarian-escort Andrei Kasianenko. Their visas, hotel bookings and tickets had been obtained only after days of bureaucratic struggle, including a night spent crowded into Rabbi Mindel's single hotel room. They arrived in Kiev around 12:40 PM and immediately began arranging meetings with library officials to locate the confiscated Yiddish Cultural Institute library.
