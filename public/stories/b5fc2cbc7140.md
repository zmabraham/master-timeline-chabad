# Mohilev Supreme Court acquits the Mitteler Rebbe

*1826*

The court concluded that the Mitteler Rebbe had been freely chosen by the Chasidim as their religious teacher, that the donations he received were voluntary and unforced, and that even Jews of other provinces sent contributions without compulsion. It held that no one could be forbidden from giving a legal donation or disposing of private property, and that the Rebbe's arbitration work did not amount to exercising judicial authority. The ruling cleared him of all wrongdoing.
