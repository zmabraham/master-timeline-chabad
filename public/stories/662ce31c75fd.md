# Double wedding held in Moscow Chabad shul during the August 1991 coup

*1991*

With tanks in central Moscow and Shaievich unable to appear, the community moved the chuppah into the AGUH synagogue and added a second couple — Igor and Asya Gerkovsky of Moscow and Yura and Palina Kreimer of Kishinev. About forty guests and visiting bochurim attended. Before the chuppah, word arrived from the Rebbe that no one should change their travel or wedding plans, lifting morale; Rabbi B.S. Kunin arrived from the airport at the last minute and was honored with mesader kiddushin.
