# Likutei Torah printed in Zhitomir

*1848*

After the Zhitomir press received government approval in 5607 (1847), publishers turned to printing the long-delayed second volume of Torah Ohr. It appeared in 5608 (1848) under the new title Likutei Torah, becoming one of the foundational works of Chabad chasidic literature. The Tzemach Tzedek had spent decades collecting, editing, and annotating the manuscripts that made up the volume.
