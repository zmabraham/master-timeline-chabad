# Rayatz travels abroad to console his father after Rebbetzin Rivka's passing

*1914*

On Motzei Shabbos following the levaya, the Rayatz traveled to Germany to comfort his father the Rebbe Rashab and to report on the funeral arrangements. When someone pointed out that his hat was not properly placed, he replied that he never paid attention to such things, certainly not now. From abroad the Rebbe Rashab sent a telegram instructing that ten bochurim study around the clock for the entire shloshim in his mother's room.
