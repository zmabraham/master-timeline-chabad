# Sukkot 5677 at the Rebbe Rashab's court in Rostov

*1916*

The Tomchei Tmimim students received return tickets after Rosh Hashana but were warned not to go on to Rostov; the author and several others went anyway. Calabrian etrogim, the chassidic standard, were in short supply because of the war. The students were hosted by the family of Mordechai Gertzulin and his mother Chana, where they also encountered Novardok mussar students for the first time and debated their approach to prayer. Lacking means to set up a sukkah at their first lodging, they built one at a Rostov house used by Tomchei Tmimim students from that city.
