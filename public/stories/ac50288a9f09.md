# Rayatz arrives in New York from Europe

*1940*

On 9 Adar Sheni 5700, Rebbe Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn (Rayatz) arrived safely in New York after being rescued from Nazi-occupied Europe. R' Shlomo Aharon Kazarnovsky immediately began searching for a building to serve as the Chabad headquarters and the Rebbe's residence. On 13 Nissan, Rayatz wrote thanking him for these efforts.
