# Rebbe approves Livenhof yeshiva and reports 27 students

*1932*

In a Chol HaMoed Pesach letter dated 25 Nissan 5692 the Frierdiker Rebbe told Pinchas Mordechai Teitz that he had discussed strengthening Tiferes Bachurim/Yavneh with R. Chadakov and was thrilled at the possibility of founding a small yeshiva in Livenhof. A subsequent letter celebrated that, with mesirus nefesh, the yeshiva had already enrolled 27 students.
