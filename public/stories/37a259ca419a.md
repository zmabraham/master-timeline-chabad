# Levi Yitzchak Schneerson arrested in Dnepropetrovsk

*1939*

Rabbi Levi Yitzchak Schneerson—chief rabbi of Dnepropetrovsk and father of the seventh Lubavitcher Rebbe—was arrested by Soviet authorities in 1939. The Seventh Rebbe later denied that his father's writings had been confiscated at the time of arrest, but still directed the 1988 mission to inquire about related papers held in Dnepropetrovsk.
