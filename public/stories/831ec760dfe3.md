# Khovansky reports Babinovich court mismanagement to Mohilev government

*1826*

Khovansky relayed the district court inspector's findings that the Babinovich peace court had failed to bring the Rebbe in for sworn interrogation as the law required, instead sending a policeman with questions who had not yet returned. The inspector attributed the delays to the court officials' incompetence and ignorance of proper procedure. Khovansky proposed publicly punishing the court for deviating from legal procedure and noted that the secretary Zhuravkovich had already been dismissed for misconduct, calling for a reliable replacement so the case could not be further stalled.
