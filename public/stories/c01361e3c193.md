# Rabbi Binyaminson accepts the Zlobin rabbinate

*1927*

Binyaminson refused to enter a town divided by factional 'sides.' He first visited the elder Rabbi Wilda for his blessing, declaring he would not come if opposed. The elder rabbi agreed, the factions dissolved, and the Frierdiker Rebbe wrote praising the peace established (Sefer HaMaamarim 5687, supplement p. 269). Schusterman's father served as matchmaker for the arrangement.
