# R' Moshe ben David Yaffe immigrates from Shklov

*1820*

According to the 5599 census, R' Moshe ben David, then aged 30, had immigrated from Shklov to Eretz Yisrael in 5580. He was married with two sons at the time of the census and would later serve as an emissary for the Hebron Chabad community and subsequently for the chassidim of Tiberias.
