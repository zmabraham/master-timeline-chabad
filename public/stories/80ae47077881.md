# England severs diplomatic relations with Russia over espionage

*1927*

At the end of May 1927, England severed diplomatic relations with Russia after a police raid on the All-Russian Co-operative Society in the UK uncovered Soviet espionage activities. Conservative Prime Minister Stanley Baldwin presented the House of Commons with deciphered Soviet telegrams as proof. This rupture helped trigger Stalin's broader crackdown on perceived enemies inside Russia, including religious leaders.
