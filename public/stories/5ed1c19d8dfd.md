# R. Shlomo Segal sent as shadar from Eretz Yisrael

*1779*

Following R. Israel of Polotzk's earlier mission, R. Shlomo Segal was sent as a shadar from the chassidic settlement to the Diaspora in winter 5539. According to the analysis correcting A. Yaari's chronology, R. Shlomo Segal carried letters from R. Avraham of Kaliska to the Chassidim and returned to Eretz Yisrael by late summer 5539.
