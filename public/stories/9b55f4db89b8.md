# Anash in Vilna establish a Tomchei Tmimim branch at the Apatov shul

*1927*

The initiative is described in HaTamim issue 2, p. 133. It was driven by local Chabad askanim and would be staffed in time by figures including Eliyahu Weitz, Shneur Zalman Zeligson, Moshe Katzenelson and Chaim Shpreiregen. The Rebbe Rayatz responded with a letter of encouragement weeks after the project was launched.
