# Birth of Rebbetzin Chaya Mushka

*1901*

The Rebbetzin Chaya Mushka, future wife of the seventh Rebbe, was born in 5661 (1901). Her grandfather, the Rebbe Rashab, wrote a personal mazal tov letter on the occasion of her birth, which is preserved and displayed at the Lubavitch Library exhibition on family purity and Chabad life events.
