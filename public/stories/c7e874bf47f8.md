# R' Alchanan Dov Morozov appointed mashgiach for chassidut

*1908*

In the middle of winter 5668 (early 1908), R' Alchanan Dov Morozov was appointed mashgiach for chassidut studies in the Great Hall at Tomchei Tmimim. The appointment was announced through a letter from the Rebbe Rashab read by office manager R' Eliezer Kaplan. Morozov later served as secretary to the Rebbe Rayatz and was executed in Petersburg in 5698 (1938).
