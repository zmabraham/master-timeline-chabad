# Train ride from Vienna toward Steyr stalled in Soviet zone, Erev Shabbat Elul 5706

*1946*

Post-war Austria and Germany were divided into American, British, French, and Soviet occupation zones, and the Soviet zone authorities held the freight cars indefinitely. The travelers sat in darkness without food in cattle wagons, and the narrator gave R. Avraham Eliyahu Plotkin a piece of bread for kiddush. They were instructed to identify themselves as Greek nationals if questioned. The train finally rolled overnight and reached Steyr by Shabbat dawn, where the Joint and Vaad Hatzalah received them.
