# R. Shmuel Zalmanov born in Warsaw

*1903*

Shmuel Zalmanov, who would later become a senior figure in Tomchei Tmimim, editor of Hatamim, and head of Nichoach in New York, was born in Warsaw on 8 Tevet 5664. He first attended the yeshiva only for Chassidus studies when it opened in summer 5681, and entered as a full student in summer 5684.
