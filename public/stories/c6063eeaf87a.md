# Rebbe Rayatz arrives in Riga, Latvia after exile from Soviet Russia

*1927*

Two days after Simchat Torah, the Rebbe's train from Leningrad crossed into Latvia. At 7:00 a.m. on 25 Tishrei, 5688 the train pulled into Riga, where thousands gathered at the station and surrounding plaza. The Rebbe and his family slowly navigated the crowd to a local hotel, eventually moving to an apartment in an upscale Jewish neighborhood.
