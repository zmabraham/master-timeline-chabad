# Group photo of Tomchei Tmimim students taken in Vilna

*1940*

A series of group photographs of the Vilna Tomchei Tmimim students, mashgiach R. Eliyahu Moshe Lis, and administrators including R. Moshe Leib Rodstein, R. Shmuel Zalmanov and R. Yitzchak Dov Ber Ushpal were taken on 17 Adar I 5700. These photos are among the few surviving images of the yeshiva-in-exile in Vilna.
