# Purim farbrengen at Reb Elchanan Morozov in Leningrad

*1934*

On Purim 5694 (around March 1, 1934), Chassidim farbrenged at the home of Rabbi Elchanan Morozov in Leningrad. They later continued at the home of Rabbi Shmuel Nemotin, where a large farbrengen was held with long tables — Rabbi Chaikel Chaikin had just become engaged to his daughter. Rabbi Peretz Mochkin spoke, with prominent participants including Rabbi Baruch Shifrin, Rabbi Menashe Althaus, and Rabbi Michael Dvorkin (who was preparing to emigrate to Riga).
