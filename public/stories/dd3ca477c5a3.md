# Rebbe directs Kollel Chabad funds to support Vilna yeshiva

*1940*

Through coded language about a 'Ramban commentary,' the Rebbe arranged for monies collected in Lithuania for Kollel Chabad (Kupat Rabbi Meir Baal Hanes) to be given to R. Yehoshua Isaac Baruch to support the Vilna yeshiva. The Rebbe undertook to remit equivalent funds to Kollel Chabad in Eretz Yisroel.
