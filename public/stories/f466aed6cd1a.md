# R' Yosef Halevi appointed assistant gabbai in Hebron

*1830*

In 5590 (1830), after R' Chaim Yaakov Halevi ibn Yakar's 42 years of service as gabbai and pakid of the Hebron community, his son R' Yosef Halevi was appointed as his deputy and successor. The appointment was described as 'a sign of trust and honor' to the elder Halevi. R' Chaim Halevi was the first signatory on the 5590 inter-community agreement.
