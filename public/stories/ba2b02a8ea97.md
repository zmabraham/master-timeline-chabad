# Rebbe Rayatz departs Riga en route to the United States

*1940*

Together with his family and secretaries R. Chaim Liberman, R. Chaim Mordechai Aizik Hodakov and R. Nissan Mindel, the Rebbe began his journey to America. On 27 Adar I 5700 he wrote en route to the Vilna students, asking the senior students in particular to take responsibility for every aspect of the yeshiva and to conduct it in the spirit of Tomchei Tmimim as it had been in Otwock.
