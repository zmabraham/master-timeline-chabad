# R' Shmuel Schneiderman passes away in Philadelphia

*1955*

R' Schneiderman was among the small group of Tmimim who reached the United States during 5686–5688. He made his home in Philadelphia and remained there for the rest of his life, until his passing in 5715. His arrival had been part of the early wave that gradually rebuilt a Lubavitcher Tmimim presence in America during the years following the First World War.
