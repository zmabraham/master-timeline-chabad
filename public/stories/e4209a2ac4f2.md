# Author travels alone from Lipovets to study at the Kiev cheder katan

*1927*

Reaching the Podol address of R' Shlomo Telingator (already departed for Eretz Yisrael), he was guided to Morgenstern's yeshiva, which refused him as underage at 14. He was instead accepted into the small Lubavitch yeshiva ketana effectively run by R' Yaakov der Beker, with Rabbi Benyamin Gorodetzky overseeing it from the Chabad shul on Shchekavitskaya St. R' Yaakov Konotoper served as his maggid shiur, teaching Perek HaMafkid.
