# R. Yosef Hatzaddik of Posen passes away

*1801*

Born in Zolkov in 5486 (1726), R. Yosef Hatzaddik served as rabbi in Witkow, Skole, Yavorov and finally Posen, and was deeply praised by his father-in-law the Nodah BiYhudah. He died on 5 Adar 5561 (18 February 1801) and is buried in Posen; his book Zikhron She'erit Yosef was printed in Kolomea in 5642.
