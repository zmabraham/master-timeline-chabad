# Sheinin reports on 5690 tour of Vilna-region towns

*1930*

Sheinin recounted that during Shevat 5690 he visited multiple towns repeating chassidus and urging Tanya shiurim. In Haloboutchi, R. Yeshaya Refael recited chassidus each Shabbos; in Pliesy he prompted R. Yisrael to begin a daily Tanya shiur; in Disna he worked with R. Shemarya Yofeh to begin Tanya after his daily gemara; and in Vilna's Kirzner minyan he prompted R. Elye Weitz to add Tanya to his daily gemara shiur.
