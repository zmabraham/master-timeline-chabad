# The grand Zhlobin wedding unites the Berditchev and Liadi dynasties

*1807*

Around Parshat Naso in summer 5567, the celebrated 'Zhlobiner chasunah' was held in Zhlobin, drawing thousands of Chassidim from across Russia. The bride was Sarah, daughter of Rabbi Dovber (the future Mitteler Rebbe); the groom was a son of Rabbi Meir, son of Rabbi Levi Yitzchak of Berditchev. The match had been arranged by 5564 but was delayed by Rabbi Meir's death in 5566 and the subsequent mourning year.
