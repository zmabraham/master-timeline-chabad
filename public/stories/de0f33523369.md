# Rebbe Rashab travels to Chernigov for delegate selection

*1909*

The Rebbe Rashab journeyed to Chernigov, accompanied by his son (the future Rebbe Rayatz), to manage the campaign for delegate selection. Knowing his own nephew Yitzchak Schneersohn was a candidate but not sufficiently God-fearing, he insisted that in matters of holiness, family ties carry no weight. He met with each rabbi and delegate individually to hear their views before sharing his own.
