# Rebbe transfers yeshiva sections from Poland to Lithuania

*1939*

In a letter dated 14 Kislev 5700, the Frierdiker Rebbe reported that with great toil and expense he had transferred to Lithuania part of the Tomchei Temimim sections from Warsaw, Lodz, Kaloszyn, Chelm and elsewhere. This move was part of the Chabad effort to preserve the yeshiva network as the Nazi occupation of Poland tightened.
