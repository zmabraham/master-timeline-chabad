# Frierdiker Rebbe replies to Shlomo Zilberberg

*1946*

The Rebbe wrote that he was very pleased to hear that Hashem had saved Zilberberg and his brother through His great kindness, and requested a detailed account of what had happened to them and to the other surviving Anash. He blessed them to settle into a good family life with parnassah and fixed times for Torah study in nigleh and chassidut.
