# Tzemach Tzedek travels to Rogachev, Zhuravichi and Dribin

*1830*

Although the Tzemach Tzedek generally remained in Lyubavichi writing Chassidus, he periodically traveled to outlying towns to deliver maamarim publicly and to receive Chassidim in yechidus. This particular winter tour is reconstructed from headings on his maamarim, which routinely recorded the date and place of delivery. The journey is among the earliest documented in the registry of his discourses.
