# Tomchei Temimim branch moves from Bobinovich to Shchedrin

*1909*

The branch served as an intermediate level between the chadarim and the main Lubavitch yeshiva, with roughly sixty students in three classes. The town's householders agreed that about forty students would be supported by hosting meals ('teg'), while twenty would be supported wholly or partly by the Lubavitch yeshiva fund. Students traveled from Lubavitch to Shchedrin after Sukkot 5670.
