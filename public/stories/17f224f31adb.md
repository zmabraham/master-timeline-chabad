# Bar Mitzvah of the future Rayatz in Lubavitch

*1893*

In the summer of 5653 (1893), the Bar Mitzvah of the future Rebbe Yosef Yitzchak was celebrated in the large hall of the courtyard of the Rebbes in Lubavitch, the same hall used for the large communal gatherings of Chabad. The event is documented in Sefer HaMaamarim 5711.
