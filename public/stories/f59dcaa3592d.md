# Chabad Old City synagogue faces wartime closure

*1944*

With WWII straining the Jewish yishuv, the Rebbe Rayatz wrote on 10 Iyar 5704 that maintaining the Chabad synagogue in Jerusalem's Old City required 4–5 pounds per month to hire men for the minyan. He instructed Agudat Chasidei Chabad to take responsibility, allocating one pound monthly and securing matching contributions from Kolel Chabad, the Gemach, and Toras Emes. The Rebbe wrote separately to each institution.
