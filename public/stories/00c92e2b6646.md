# Keser Shem Tov first edition published in Zolkiew

*1794*

In 5554 (1794), the first edition of Keser Shem Tov, a collection of teachings of Rabbi Yisrael Baal Shem Tov, was published in Zolkiew. The Lubavitch Library preserves a copy of this first edition. The book is among the earliest printed sources for the Baal Shem Tov's teachings.
