# Rosenbaum moves from Berlin to Koenigsberg

*1893*

By 4 Parshas Tavo 5653 (1893) Rosenbaum was writing from Koenigsberg to R. Ze'ev Wolf and R. Chaim Gershon Kugel about the hardships of resettling in Berlin and then Koenigsberg. He recounts a long reply he received from R. Chaim Yaakov Widerewitz – chief rabbi of the Sephardic Congregation in America, who had emigrated in 5652 – warning that America was good for the strong but harsh for refined and delicate people. Identifying himself with the latter, Rosenbaum decided for the time being not to cross the ocean.
