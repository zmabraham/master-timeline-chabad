# Japanese government relocates yeshivah students from Kobe to Shanghai

*1941*

Rabbi Meir Ashkenazi, Shanghai's Ashkenazi chief rabbi and a Chabad Chasid, arranged for the Mir to use the Beth Aharon Synagogue. The Chabad students rented a separate location and continued their Polish-style integrated study of Talmud and Chasidut, isolated from teachers and from the Rebbe in New York.
