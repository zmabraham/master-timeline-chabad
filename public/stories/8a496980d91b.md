# Rebbe Rashab delivers maamar at Apatov synagogue on Lag B'Omer

*1909*

The Frierdiker Rebbe later recalled in a 5684 letter that the building was already being called 'Beis Knesses Lubavitch' when his father, the Rashab, said Chassidus there on Lag B'Omer. The visit cemented the synagogue's identity as the chassidic center of the city.
