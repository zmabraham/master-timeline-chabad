# Rabbi Yitzchak Elchanan Spektor grants smicha to Rafael of Amdur

*1887*

On 23 Tevet 5647, the famed posek and av beis din of Kovno, Rabbi Yitzchak Elchanan Spektor (author of Be'er Yitzchak), tested Rabbi Rafael of Amdur and found him fluent in Shas and capable of original Torah insights. He issued a full kabbalas smicha—yoreh yoreh, yadin yadin—stating that any community that accepts him as rav will derive pride and nachas from him.
