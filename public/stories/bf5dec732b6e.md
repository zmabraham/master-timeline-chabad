# Shmuel Zalmanov begins teaching Chassidus to younger students

*1926*

By summer 5686, Zalmanov was noted as excelling in both Nigleh and Chassidus, studying Likkutei Torah with one group in the morning and Hemshech Ayin-Beis with the Tmimim in the evening. He reviewed Chassidus well and spent extended time in prayer, foreshadowing his later leadership roles.
