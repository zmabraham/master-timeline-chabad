# Rebbe edits Kollel Chabad calendar for 5713

*1952*

Late in 5712 (1952), R. Avraham Chaim Naeh sent the Rebbe a proof of the wall calendar he was preparing for 5713. The Rebbe added and corrected dozens of details of Chabad practice, and from then on the published Kollel Chabad calendar reflected his revisions. The surviving Tishrei-to-Kislev sheet of the edited calendar is preserved in the library archive, with the glosses published in Kerem Chabad vol. 1.
