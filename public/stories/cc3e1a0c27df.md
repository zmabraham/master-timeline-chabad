# Eliezerov spends Shavuot with the Rashab in Lubavitch

*1905*

On Shavuot 5665 (June 9, 1905), Eliezerov was in Lubavitch, where the Rashab gave him letters of recommendation to the wealthy chassidim of Riga. The trip was tied to the purchase of Hatzer Romano in Hebron and other matters strengthening the Chabad settlement there. He then returned to Lubavitch to help organize the Vaad Chevroni in Tammuz.
