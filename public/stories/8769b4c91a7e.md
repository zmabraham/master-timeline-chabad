# Khovansky appeals the acquittal of the Mitteler Rebbe

*1827*

Prince Khovansky, Governor-General of Vitebsk, Mohilev, Smolensk and Kaluga, prepared a lengthy report to the Senate's Fifth Department arguing that the lower court's reasoning was unfounded and that the Mitteler Rebbe had in fact violated multiple provisions of the 1804 Jewish Constitution. While conceding that the imperial amnesties of 1814 and 1826 precluded actual punishment, he recommended barring the Rebbe from continuing as rabbi of the Chasidim and proposed broader restrictions on rabbinic authority in Russia. The appeal was filed in Vitebsk on May 15, 1827.
