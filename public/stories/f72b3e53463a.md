# Tomchei Tmimim Yeshiva founded in Vilna

*1927*

A branch of the Chabad Tomchei Tmimim yeshiva was established in Vilna, then under Polish rule, in the period prior to Adar 5688. The Rebbe Rayatz visited the yeshiva during his first trip to Lithuania, addressing the students at the Apatov shul where it was housed.
