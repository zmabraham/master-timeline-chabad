# Wedding of Leah to Yerachmiel Binyamin Klein

*1961*

The wedding was celebrated on 21 Adar 5721 (March 9, 1961) at Melrose-Chateau in Brownsville. Since Kollel Avreichim did not yet exist, the Rebbe instructed the chatan to specialize in the laws of mikvaot, which he learned with the Rav of Norolsk. A few months later the couple was sent on shlichus by Merkos L'Inyonei Chinuch to Australia.
