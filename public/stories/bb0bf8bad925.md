# R' Dovid Shifrin departs for America with Rebbe Rashab's notice

*1900*

Around 5660, before leaving for America, R' Dovid Shifrin of Pleshchenitsy (resident in Zembin) visited the Rebbe Rashab and said, 'Rebbe, I am going to America' — explaining that he had not come to ask permission, since his sons were already there and his ticket was bought, but that he did not want to be like a student who flees the cheder without telling his teacher. Shifrin became the central organizer of anash in America without title, office, or formal role, operating purely on personal bonds of chasidic brotherhood.
