# Rayatz's Rostov arrest by Yevsektzia

*1920*

In 1920, in Rostov, Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn was arrested after the Yevsektzia, the Jewish section of the Communist Party, leveled accusations against him. The Yevsektzia became a primary opponent of religious Jewish life in the early Soviet period, targeting Chabad activities. This marked the sixth of his seven arrests and his first under Communism.
