# Rebbe wires Riga to spend up to 3,000 lats on Pesach matzah and food for Warsaw

*1940*

Chefetz had reported obtaining a Soviet permit to ship 1,600 kilos of matzah and 1,600 kilos of other foodstuffs to Poland, and was already sending roughly 500 lats' worth to brethren in Russia. The Rebbe authorized expanding the operation, ultimately covering $3,000 personally plus $2,000 via Mizrach, with additional small Pesach shipments bringing the total to 6,000 lats.
