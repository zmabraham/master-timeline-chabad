# Maharashab arrives in Rostov-on-Don

*1915*

On Friday 28 Cheshvan 5676 (23 October 1915), Maharashab's passport was stamped in Rostov-on-Don, documenting the precise date the Rebbe and his household reached their new home in exile. The discovery of this passport stamp corrected earlier estimates and confirmed the family's testimony that the journey from Lubavitch to Rostov took less than two weeks.
