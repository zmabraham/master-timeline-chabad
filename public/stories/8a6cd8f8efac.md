# Rashag reports efforts to secure US and Canadian visas for Kobe students

*1941*

Rashag wrote on 12 Menachem Av 5701 (August 5, 1941) to Rabbi Rotstein, reporting that they were not sitting idly by but were working actively to secure American entry permits as well as Canadian visas for the students. He acknowledged the harsh new immigration restrictions and the worsening situation, awaiting new State Department forms.
