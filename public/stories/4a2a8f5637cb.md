# Failed attempt to print Sichas Hashavua in Moscow

*1991*

On 6 Tammuz 5751, R. Y.Y. Aharonov and the special-mission delegation in Moscow tried to arrange printing of the Russian Sichas Hashavua. After visiting Alagraphics, a dollar-only printer, and receiving an exorbitant $2,500 quote, Aharonov decided to print the sheets in Eretz Yisrael and ship them ready. The episode illustrates the extreme practical difficulties Chabad faced operating in post-Soviet Moscow.
