# Maharashab and family leave Lubavitch for Rostov

*1915*

On Sunday, 16 Cheshvan 5676 (October 1915), Maharashab left Lubavitch together with his entire family. Earlier accounts based on Chanoch L'Naar and the Rebbe Rayatz's letters had suggested they reached Rostov around mid-Kislev, but the dated passport stamps reveal a much shorter journey of less than two weeks.
