# Entire Lubavitch courtyard burns down

*1921*

The fire came one year after the passing of the Rashab in Rostov and three years after the yeshiva's departure for Kremenchug. With the courtyard gone and the Rebbe's family long departed, the town ceased to function as the movement's headquarters. The buildings of the great hall, the small hall, the residences, and the yeshiva facilities all perished.
