# Studies resume at Warsaw Tomchei Temimim with R' Alter as mashpia

*1928*

After Tishrei in Riga, R' Alter Simchowitz traveled to Warsaw to take up his new post. On Sunday, 30 Tishrei 5689, the students began studying chassidus with him at the appointed early hour. About thirty-five students filled the hall; their outward conduct was orderly but they urgently needed steady guidance and clearer instruction on the inner avodah expected of them.
