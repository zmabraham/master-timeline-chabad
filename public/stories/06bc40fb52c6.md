# R. Shraga Feivish Zalmanov arrives in Lubavitch to study

*1895*

Born in Lazovich near Klimovich, R. Shraga Feivish Zalmanov came to Lubavitch in 5655 to study. Tomchei Tmimim had not yet been founded, so he learned for two to three years among the 'yoshvim' under the mashpia R. Chanoch Hendel Kugel. He later married into the Horowitz family and settled in Warsaw.
