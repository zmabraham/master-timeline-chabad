# Rabbi Rabinovich (5783) Rabbi Rabinovich Rabbi Avraham Meir

*1980*

Rabbi Rabinovich (5783) Rabbi Rabinovich Rabbi Avraham Meir Rabinovich (born in the year 5740, 1980) is a dayan in the beit din for monetary matters affiliated with the beit horaah for halacha and law Rechovot, and a moreh tzedek in the Beit Horaah Kfar Chabad which he is one of the founders
