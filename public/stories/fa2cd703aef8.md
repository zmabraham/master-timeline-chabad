# Author moves to Crown Heights and becomes regular at 770

*1955*

Before Pesach 5715 (April 1955) the author relocated from East Flatbush to Crown Heights, becoming a fixed Shabbat mispallel at 770. The gabbai Reb Yochanan Gordon, who had been the regular baal korei for the Rebbe Rayatz, began calling him up each week to read from the Torah.
