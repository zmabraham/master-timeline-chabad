# Death of R' Yehuda Leib, great-grandson of the Maharal of Prague

*1705*

According to the Prague tombstone records compiled by R' Sinai Hock, R' Yehuda Leib ben R' Shmuel (grandson of R' Bezalel, the Maharal's only son) died in Tishrei 5466. This discovery overturned the long-held claim by R' Meir Pereles and others that R' Shmuel left only daughters, and helped establish the Alter Rebbe's descent from the Maharal through his great-great-grandfather R' Yehuda Leib of Posen.
