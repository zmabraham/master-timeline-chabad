# Mohilev General Prosecutor reports on Rebbe's case to Khovansky

*1826*

Smolaninov informed Khovansky that despite the demand for a quick legal resolution, the provincial government had taken no action. He summarized the latest developments: the lost interrogation responses, the second messenger who retrieved them on February 10, and the decision to gather sworn testimony from communities in Orsha, Kopust and Shklov. Smolaninov reported that he had personally ordered the district court inspector to ensure the required testimony was gathered promptly and that no further delay would be tolerated.
