# Belovezha Accords dissolve the Soviet Union

*1991*

The historic meeting produced a treaty establishing the CIS and was widely seen as the end of the USSR. Diary entries from Moscow record local and international anxiety about the implications, including for the unresolved Chabad library case. The Rebbe, asked about the new situation, replied that nothing needed to change — one must trust in G-d and increase the spread of Yiddishkeit.
