# Rebbe Rayatz travels to Warsaw to visit Tomchei Tmimim

*1928*

After preparations begun on the first night of Chanukah, the Rebbe Rayatz traveled to Warsaw on 28 Shevat 5688 and stayed approximately two weeks. The trip combined an inspection of Tomchei Tmimim with visits to Anash in Warsaw and the surrounding region. Detailed accounts were preserved by accompanying chassidim and later published in Sefer HaToldos and the Sefer HaMaamarim and Sichos of 5688.
