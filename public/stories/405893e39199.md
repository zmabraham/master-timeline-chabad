# Yeshiva fundraisers depart Lubavitch on night of 13 Adar

*1918*

With Tomchei Temimim collapsing financially, senior figures were dispatched to Russia's major Jewish centers. R' Shilem Kuratin went to Petersburg, R' Eliezer Kaplan to Vitebsk and Nevel, and the narrator with R' Leib Krasik were sent to Moscow. They read the megillah on the eve of 13 Adar and boarded the train that night, planning to daven and read megillah again in Moscow.
