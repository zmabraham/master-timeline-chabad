# Lamm letter to Rayatz on portrait sale

*1936*

In a German letter dated 19 August 1936, Amsterdam dealer Louis Lamm wrote to the Rebbe Rayatz about the sale of two oil portraits — R. Yosef Hatzaddik of Posen (obtained from Dr. Calvary of Posen in a barter for 60 Reichsmark in books) and R. Akiva Eiger (acquired from the Eiger family in 1913 for 25 Reichsmark) — along with 31 additional prints. Decades later this letter, found by R. Yitzchak Wilhelm during the archiving of the Rayatz's papers, finally identified the provenance of the portraits and many old calendars in the library.
