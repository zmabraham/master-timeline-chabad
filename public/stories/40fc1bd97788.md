# Rebbe launches Shmurah Matzah campaign on Shabbos Parshas HaChodesh

*1954*

At the Shabbos Parshas HaChodesh farbrengen of 5714 (1954), the Rebbe declared a request — 'lu yeyasher cheili' even a mitzvah — that all who can influence others should ensure they receive shmurah matzah for Pesach. The campaign grew over the years into a worldwide distribution reaching hundreds of thousands of Jews.
