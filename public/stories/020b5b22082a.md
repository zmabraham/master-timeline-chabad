# Passing of Rabbi Aryeh Leib Zivav, rav of Dvinsk

*1887*

Born around 5570-5571 and ordained by the Tzemach Tzedek at age eighteen, Rabbi Aryeh Leib Zivav served in the rabbinate of Babruysk, Smilovitch, Homel and finally Dvinsk-Dinaburg. He passed away on Monday, erev Rosh Chodesh Sivan 5647. Most of his responsa never reached our hands; only a small bundle written during his Homel years was preserved in manuscript.
