# Rashab tells Slonim not to leave Yafo for Hebron

*1912*

Following the founding of Yeshivas Toras Emes in Hebron, R' Shneur Zalman wished to lay down his Yafo burdens and return to Hebron. The Rebbe Rashab replied that he could not abandon Yafo after all the effort and toil he had invested to bring the community to its current state.
