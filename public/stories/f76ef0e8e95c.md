# Chassidim of Nikolayev outgrow single beis midrash

*1889*

By 5649, the Chabad community in Nikolayev had grown so large that a single beis midrash no longer sufficed. R' Asher Grossman began efforts to build an additional synagogue. On 8 Tevet 5649, the city's rabbi, Rabbi Avraham Dovid Lavut, wrote forbidding him from splitting off into a separate minyan now that the decision to build a new shul for the whole community had been made.
