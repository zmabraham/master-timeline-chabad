# Mitteler Rebbe and Tzemach Tzedek settle in Lubavitch

*1813*

After the disruptions of the Napoleonic war and the passing of the Alter Rebbe, the Mitteler Rebbe accepted the chassidim's invitation to return from Kremenchug to White Russia. The town of Lubavitch was chosen as the new center of the Chabad movement, and would remain its seat for over a century until 1915.
