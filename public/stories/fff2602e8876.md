# Ibrahim Pasha sacks Hebron - massacre and desecration

*1834*

On Monday 28 Tammuz 5594 the deputy commander arrived with his army and conquered Hebron. Despite Jewish hopes, the soldiers were given license to plunder. Homes were burned and ransacked for hidden treasures; oil was spilled and vessels shattered. Many women were violated and Torah scrolls were burned, torn, and spread on the ground with Torah scrolls rolled in blood. Two women, a child by the mikveh, and Sephardic sages were killed.
