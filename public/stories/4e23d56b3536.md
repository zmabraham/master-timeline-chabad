# Chabad Russian-translation Haggadah by Dovber Chatzkelevich

*1990*

In 5750 (1990), an 80-page octavo Haggadah with Russian translation by Dovber Chatzkelevich was published in New York. It served the rapidly expanding Russian-speaking Jewish community as the Iron Curtain began to fall. It joined the Chabad effort to provide Jewish liturgical texts in Russian for newly accessible Soviet Jews.
