# Jacobson receives letter about Rebbe Rayatz's planned visit to America

*1929*

In Shevat 5689 (early 1929), R. Yisrael Jacobson received a letter from the Rebbe Rayatz's secretary R. Yechezkel Feigin, informing him that the Rebbe was preparing to travel to the United States that summer on behalf of Russian Jewry. The letter sparked the first wave of preparations among Anash, who were astonished at the prospect but did not initially dare to formally invite the Rebbe themselves. Jacobson notes that it was the Rebbe's own self-sacrifice for Russian Jewry that ultimately drove him to undertake the difficult journey.
