# Defiant Purim farbrengen in Rostov under Bolshevik police visit

*1920*

Despite government bans on meetings and a 9 p.m. curfew, the Rashab encouraged singing and sent for additional liquor. When three police officers entered during a maamar, the Rashab declared 'We have to say chasidut, and they will be batul' and continued teaching about evil as a false reality. The officers gazed silently and left. The farbrengen lasted until morning light.
