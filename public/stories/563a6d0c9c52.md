# Rebbe Rashab resumes Hemshech Tarav on Shavuos 5674

*1914*

On Shavuos 5674 the Rebbe Rashab resumed delivering the Hemshech he had begun on Shavuos Tarav ("BeSha'a SheHikdimu"). The maamarim of summer 5674 dealt with the themes of tohu and tikkun, offering deep and luminous explanations. The series continued without interruption until Parshas Vayera 5676.
