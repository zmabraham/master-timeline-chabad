# Rebbe Rayatz writes Simpson about three donated manuscripts

*1937*

Replying to R. Eliyahu Simpson on 8 Elul 5697 (August 15, 1937), the Rebbe Rayatz identified the three manuscripts received as a portion of Sefer HaKavanot, a Siddur of the Arizal, and a handwritten machzor of the type used by Venetian notables on festivals. The Siddur HaArizal is cataloged in the Lubavitch manuscript collection as no. 1991 and the Venice machzor as no. 1996.
