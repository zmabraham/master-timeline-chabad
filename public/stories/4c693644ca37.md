# Hebron Chabad issues four honorary deeds to Montefiore

*1839*

The four documents were signed on 4 Tammuz by the leaders of the Hebron Chabad community and delivered to Montefiore that same day. They granted him and his wife places in the synagogue, appointed Sir Moses as patron and head of the Talmud Torah society, and appointed Lady Judith as patron of the Bikur Cholim and Hachnasat Kallah societies. The deeds bound the community to send accountings to her each year.
