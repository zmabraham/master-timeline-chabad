# Rabbi Menachem Kasher receives Rogatchover microfilms

*1956*

Rebbetzin Rachel Tzitran had photographed much of her father's writings on microfilm and sent them to family in America for safekeeping. In 5716 (1956), Rabbi Menachem Kasher received these manuscripts and began the work of editing them for the Tzofnat Paneach series on Talmud and Torah.
