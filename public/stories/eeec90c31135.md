# Great fire at the Alter Rebbe's home in Liadi

*1810*

In 5570 (1810), a great fire struck Rabbi Shneur Zalman of Liadi's home in Liadi. The original manuscript of the Tanya is believed to have been consumed in this fire, though a corrected manuscript of Iggeret HaTeshuvah in his hand survived in the Chabad library archives.
