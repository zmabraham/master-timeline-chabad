# The Rebbe acknowledges Slavita brothers' cane from R' Shmuel Aba Shapira

*1957*

The Rebbe's letter, printed in Igros Kodesh vol. 15 (#5452), thanked R' Shmuel Aba Shapira for sending 'a cane from a holy place.' The accompanying note explained the cane had belonged to R' Pinchas of Slavita, passed via his only son R' Heshel and his daughter-in-law Treina, who had carried it to the brothers' Moscow prison and broken it in the mud. A Polish prince repaired it with a new handle; the cane is catalogued in the library archive as item 5186.
