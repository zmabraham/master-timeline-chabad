# Theodore Wiener publishes supplements to Yaari's Haggadah bibliography

*1965*

In 5725 (1965), Theodore Wiener published bibliographical supplements to Avraham Yaari's Haggadah bibliography in Studies in Bibliography and Booklore, vol. VII (Cincinnati, 1965, p. 90). The supplements further refined the bibliographic record of printed Haggadot.
