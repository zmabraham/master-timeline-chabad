# Rashab's letter on the decline of Chassidus study in Eretz Yisrael

*1903*

The Rashab described how Chassidus study in Eretz Yisrael had reached its lowest ebb, with young Chabad avreichim studying in Perushim yeshivas instead. He expressed deep distress at the situation but rejoiced that a spirit of purification had stirred his followers there to establish Chabad yeshivas in Jerusalem and Hebron. This letter contextualizes the simultaneous push to found dedicated Chabad institutions in the Holy Land.
