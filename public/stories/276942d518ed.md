# Rosenbaum writes from Smolensk prison about his arrest

*1889*

In a letter dated 27.3.89 from his Smolensk cell, Rosenbaum describes the bitterness of being imprisoned in old age among violent criminals, his dignity stripped away. He explains that his only 'crime' was signing on behalf of a sole-son draftee, an act of mercy that had been twisted into an accusation of fraud. The letter offers a rare glimpse into his prison conditions and inner state during the tsarist incarceration.
