# R. Naeh sends Rayatz a carbon copy of his Sivan letter

*1922*

On 4 Marcheshvan 5683 (Oct/Nov 1922), R. Naeh fulfilled his Erev Sukkos promise and sent Rebbe Rayatz a copy of the long letter he had written on 18 Sivan, enclosing the carbon. This is illustrative of how slowly correspondence flowed between Eretz Yisrael and Soviet Russia in this period.
