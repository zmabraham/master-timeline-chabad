# Second denunciation against Vilna authorities filed on Shavuot

*1798*

On 6 Sivan 5558 (10 May 1798) — the first day of Shavuot — a second denunciation was filed, this one against the Vilna municipal authorities. The fact that it was composed on Shavuot and bore a distorted Hebrew signature suggests the actual author was not Jewish.
