# Document of sale of Hebron land to chassid R. Yechiel Michel of Birz

*1822*

Photographed and printed in the history of Chabad in Eretz Yisrael, the deed reflects the Mitteler Rebbe's broader initiative to establish a Chabad presence in Hebron. R. Yechiel Michel, a shochet from Birz, was among the supporters of the new settlement.
