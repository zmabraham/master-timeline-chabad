# Rashab seeks to print Choshen Mishpat responsa

*1894*

The Choshen Mishpat responsa had been blocked by Russian censors decades earlier. In 5654 the Rashab attempted to revive the project, corresponding with his mechutan about publication, but the effort did not come to fruition. Further attempts followed in 5660 and 5668, with the Rashab noting in the latter year that the volume was 'already arranged and copied, ready for printing' — yet still it did not appear.
