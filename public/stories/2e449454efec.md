# Benckendorff orders provincial investigations of Tzemach Tzedek

*1841*

Acting on Uvarov's 30 March request, Benckendorff dispatched orders to Governor-General Dyakov of Vitebsk-Mogilev and to General Freigang, commander of the Fourth District of the gendarmerie. They were instructed to discreetly investigate the character, conduct, opinions, contacts and influence of the Jew Mendel of Lyubavichi. The order set in motion the parallel investigations that would shape the Third Section's eventual report.
