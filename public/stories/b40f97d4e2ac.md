# US visas issued to the Rebbe and entourage in Riga

*1940*

After months of pressure from Rhoade, Cohen, and a coalition of congressmen and senators, the visas were granted. Sam Kramer informed Henry Butler the same day. The Rebbe's daughters Sheina and Chaya Mushka and their husbands were not included; the Schneersons later escaped via France and Spain, while the Horensteins were killed in Treblinka.
