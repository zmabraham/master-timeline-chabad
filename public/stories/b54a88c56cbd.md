# R' Alter Simchowitz sent to direct Tomchei Tmimim branch in Shchedrin

*1920*

The Rashab arranged for Simchowitz to travel from Lubavitch to Shchedrin with full instructions and orientation. The branch operated until 5678 when the yeshiva and its branches were relocated to Kremenchug, after which Simchowitz served there as supervisor of chassidus studies.
