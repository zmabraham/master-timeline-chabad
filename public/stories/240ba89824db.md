# R. Nissan Nemanov leads Chai Elul farbrengen in Pocking

*1946*

On Shabbat 18 Elul 5706 R. Nissan Nemanov held a joyous farbrengen in the Agudah synagogue at Pocking. The broken refugees in the camp could not understand the Lubavitchers' jubilation at having escaped Russia; one was overheard calling them 'a meshugene Agudah.' R. Nemanov soon arranged daily shiurim and rebuilt a yeshiva structure with melamdim.
