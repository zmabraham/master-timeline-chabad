# R. Baruch Mordechai Ettinga passes away in Jerusalem

*1852*

R. Baruch Mordechai had only enjoyed his long-awaited residence in Jerusalem for about a year before his passing. Letters from the Pekuamim sent in advance of Rosh Hashanah 5613 still presumed him living, and the funds dispatched arrived after his death. His matzevah, copied in Yerushalayim vol. 1, mistakenly led some later researchers to place his death in 5617.
