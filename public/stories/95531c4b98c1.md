# Bar mitzvah of Meir Greenberg sparks beard-removal campaign in Crown Heights

*1929*

The author urged the bar mitzvah boy's father to seek the Rebbe's blessing; the father had removed his beard with depilatory cream as was then standard practice. After the visit the Rebbe scolded the author that a beardless Jew was attending his shul, demanding to know how he could eat his bread under such circumstances. This conversation launched a sustained effort to convince Crown Heights congregants to stop removing their beards.
