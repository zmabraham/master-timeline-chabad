# Rabbi Chaim Kavash passes away in Samarkand

*1943*

Born in Vyetka in 5649, Chaim Kavash studied at Tomchei Tmimim in Lubavitch and in 5686 became rabbi of Yochavitch in the Polotsk district. As Soviet persecution of rabbis intensified he fled to Nevel; when the Germans invaded he escaped further east to Samarkand among Anash, dying there on 24 Iyar 5703. His estate included several heirloom vessels, including a spoon attributed to the Baal Shem Tov and a cup of R' Mordechai of Lechovitch.
