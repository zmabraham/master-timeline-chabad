# Rabbi Zirkus gives a class in the 'Ohr Menachem Mendel' kollel which he heads

*1967*

Rabbi Zirkus gives a class in the 'Ohr Menachem Mendel' kollel which he heads, farbrengs at the Lev institute. Rabbi Yosef Tzvi Zirkus (born 5727, 1967) is rabbi and mashpia of the 'Ohr Menachem Mendel' community in the Makor Baruch neighborhood in Jerusalem, among the rabbis of the senior Tomchei Tmimim Yeshiva
