# WWI begins; Russian Jews expelled from Eretz Yisrael

*1914*

The summer 5674 outbreak of WWI brought the Ottoman expulsion of Russian subjects from Eretz Yisrael. SILA, a Russian citizen, was forced to leave and used the opportunity for another emissary mission to Bukhara. He reportedly opened a yeshiva in Samarkand during his exile, where he is documented in Kislev 5677.
