# R' David Levanon, head of Kollel Chabad, passes away

*1946*

Levanon had served as co-signer on Kollel Chabad correspondence from 5688 through 5706, paired first with R' Yisrael Asher Liba and later with R' Shlomo Yehuda Leib Eliezrov. After his death on 4 Nissan, his administrative role was filled by R' Yitzchak Avigdor Ornstein, continuing the kollel's leadership structure.
