# Rayatz inquires from 770 about Lubavitch and the Ohel

*1949*

In 5709 (1949), in his final years from his residence at 770 Eastern Parkway in New York, the Rayatz sought information on the condition of the town of Lubavitch and the Ohel of his predecessors and explored whether any repair work could be undertaken. The political conditions of the time made it impossible for Jews to travel to Lubavitch for such purposes.
