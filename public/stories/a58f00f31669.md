# R' Levin moves from Hebron to Jerusalem

*1892*

Toward the end of 5652 (1892), R' Yehuda Leib Shaul Levin moved from Hebron to Jerusalem, ending five years of service as rav of Hebron's Chabad community. His departure is documented in the biographical work Zekher Aviad. The move closed the dual-rabbinate era he had shared with R' Shmuel Menachem Chaikin.
