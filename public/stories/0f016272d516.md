# First interrogation of the Mitteler Rebbe by the provincial commission

*1825*

On 7 October 1825 (7 Marcheshvan 5586), the commission established by the provincial governor delivered eleven written questions to the Mitteler Rebbe regarding a document — already confirmed to be in his own handwriting — that distributed 35,000 rubles among his relatives. The interrogators Colonel Loginov, Court Counselor Markov, and Major Morozov pressed him on the source of the funds, why he collected money from Chassidim beyond his official salary, the meaning of the 'shtar iska' (rabbinic workaround for interest-bearing loans), and the secrecy surrounding the document. They demanded answers without delay.
