# Morozov dismissed from the investigation against the Mitteler Rebbe

*1825*

About two weeks after assigning Loginov, Markov, and Morozov on 2 October 1825 to investigate the denunciation against the Mitteler Rebbe, Khovansky relieved Morozov of the assignment (document 18). The investigation thereafter continued under Colonel Loginov and Court Counselor Markov alone, who carried it forward to its conclusion.
