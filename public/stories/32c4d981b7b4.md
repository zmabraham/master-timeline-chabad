# Rebbe writes Chicago Anash on 15 Tammuz

*1946*

On 15 Tammuz 5706 (Igrot Kodesh vol. 9, letter 2928), the Frierdiker Rebbe wrote to Anash in Chicago that, thank G-d, his beloved Tmimim from Tomchei Tmimim in Shanghai were arriving safely in America at the end of that week. He asked them to organize a splendid reception in their city, conducted in proper order.
