# Mitteler Rebbe arranges distribution of maamad funds after the Alter Rebbe's passing

*1814*

In Tevet 5574 (December 1813–January 1814), one year after the passing of the Alter Rebbe on 24 Tevet 5573, the Mitteler Rebbe arranged the distribution of 35,000 rubles in maamad funds. The funds had been collected from chasidim to rebuild the household of the rebbe in Lubavitch after the original household in Liadi was destroyed by fire when the family fled Napoleon's invasion. The Mitteler Rebbe entrusted the distribution to Reb Pinchas Shick of Shklov, with Reb Shlomo Freidin and Reb Zalman Roizes Slonim of Shklov as oversight, allocating shares to his brothers, sisters, children, stepmother, and other relatives of the Alter Rebbe.
