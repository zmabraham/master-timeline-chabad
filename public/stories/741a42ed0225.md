# Rebbe's first post-stroke visit to the upstairs shul at 770

*1992*

In the weeks following his stroke on Purim 5752 the Rebbe gradually resumed public activities. The diary notes that on the Monday before Lag BaOmer the Rebbe was brought upstairs to visit the shul at 770 for the first time. The next day he would tour the library; both visits were framed as part of a slow return that those close to him hoped would lead to full recovery.
