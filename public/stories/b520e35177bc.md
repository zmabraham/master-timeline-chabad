# Riga library acquisition begins Rebbe's world-class collection

*1928*

The Rebbe envisioned a public library for research like the British Museum, requiring thousands of books, financing, a facility, and a catalog. The London paper HaOlam soon hailed the collection as a treasure of Jewish artifacts, citing one of the oldest printed siddurim from 1475 and 950 Haggadot. The Rebbe sought books from booksellers as far as Yemen, Australia, and Africa.
