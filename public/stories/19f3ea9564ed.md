# R' Shlomo Yosef Zevin inquires about identifying rescued sefarim in Eretz Yisrael

*1952*

In 5712, with quantities of sefarim arriving in Eretz Yisrael from postwar Europe, R' Shlomo Yosef Zevin asked the Rebbe (Ramash) whether the Rayatz library volumes could be identified. The Rebbe replied — after consulting R' Chaim Lieberman, who had served as the Rayatz's librarian — that there were no library marks, but some volumes bore an inscription identifying them as part of Shmuel Wiener's collection, which he would try to photograph and circulate.
