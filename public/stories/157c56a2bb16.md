# Mitteler Rebbe settles in Lubavitch as the new Chabad center

*1813*

Shortly after the Alter Rebbe's passing and the conclusion of the Napoleonic war in 5573 (1813), Rabbi Dovber, the second Rebbe of Chabad, established himself in the small White Russian town of Lubavitch. He built a residence and a large beit knesset there. For the next 102 years Lubavitch served as the central seat of the worldwide Chabad movement under successive Rebbes — and from this town the movement took its enduring name.
