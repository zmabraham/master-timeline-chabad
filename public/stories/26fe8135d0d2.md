# Rebbe Rayatz plans tour of Latvian towns

*1930*

In a letter dated 6 Tevet 5691 the Rebbe Rayatz revealed that the idea of personally visiting a number of Latvian communities had occurred to him, and that he was taking on the considerable burden of finding a source to cover the travel expenses. His stated intention was to learn first-hand what was happening in those places and to see whether direct encouragement might accomplish something.
