# Chelm community grants land for new yeshiva building

*1939*

By 5699 student numbers in the Chelm branch had outgrown its premises. Kornblit obtained a building site from the kehilla and a substantial pledge from Dr. Shmuel Deutschlander of Agudas Yisrael's Keren HaTorah. The outbreak of WWII cancelled all construction plans, and most Chelm Jews were later murdered.
