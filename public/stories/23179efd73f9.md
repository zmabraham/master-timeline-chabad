# Rebbe visits Warsaw yeshivah and conducts long Purim farbrengen

*1928*

The Rebbe met with Warsaw's leading rabbis and communal leaders, deepening awareness of the plight of Russian Jewry, and spent time in yechidut with many Chasidim. The highlight was a long Purim farbrengen with the yeshivah students that began at 3:30 p.m. and did not end until 2:00 a.m. The Warsaw yeshivah had grown to 250 students by that year.
