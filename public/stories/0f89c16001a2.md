# Completion of the Moshiach Sefer Torah

*1970*

On Friday, 10 Shevat 5730 (January 16, 1970), nearly 28 years after the project was launched by the Frierdiker Rebbe in 5702, the writing of the Sefer Torah to greet Moshiach was finally completed. The date coincided with the yahrzeit of the Rebbe Rayatz, whose initiative it had been. The siyum marked the conclusion of a worldwide undertaking in which chassidim from many lands had purchased letters.
