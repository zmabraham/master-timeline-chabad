# Rebbe organizes funds for Levitin's release

*1929*

Replying to a letter from the Rakishok chassidim on 24 Shevat 5689 (Igros Kodesh vol. 16, letter 6121), the Rebbe explained that Levitin's situation remained unclear. He told them the sum they had collected was inadequate and pressed them to raise a proper amount so it would be ready when an opportunity arose to bring him to them.
