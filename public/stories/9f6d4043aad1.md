# Rayatz works to repair the Lubavitch Ohel

*1924*

When the courtyard was destroyed, the Ohel covering the Rebbes' graves was also destroyed, the attached study hall was ruined, and non-Jews stole the stones of the surrounding fence. In 5684 (1924), the Rayatz worked to arrange repairs to the Ohel, as recorded in Igrot Kodesh vol. 1. The overall situation, however, continued to deteriorate.
