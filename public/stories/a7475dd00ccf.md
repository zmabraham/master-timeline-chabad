# Rosenbaum forbids wife from visiting him at Smolensk prison

*1889*

In a letter dated the week of Parshat Masei 5649 to the mashpia Reb Hendel, Reb Avraham Chaim explained why he had rejected his wife's plan to visit him in prison. He felt the trip would force abandonment of their home and store and would burden their son Zalman with running everything for a week without yielding any real benefit.
