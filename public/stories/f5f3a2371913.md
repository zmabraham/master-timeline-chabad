# Purim 5675 farbrengen at the Rebbe Rashab's home

*1915*

Purim 5675 began with a small gathering at the Rebbe's table where he discussed knowledge and free will, including whether gentiles possess true bechirah. As the night progressed the entire chassidic community joined and the Rebbe delivered a maamar. Maariv was davened at roughly 1:45 a.m., the latest the author had ever witnessed.
