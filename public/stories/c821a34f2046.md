# Rashab's Rostov home redeemed by Chabad

*1997*

After more than seven decades in foreign hands, in Marcheshvan 5758 the upper floor of the Rebbe's Rostov home was reclaimed through the purchase of several small apartments. The acquired rooms included most of the Rashab's living quarters: the 'zal' where he delivered chassidic discourses on Shabbos and yom tov and held yechidus, the dining room where he farbrenged, and the porch where the sukkah had stood.
