# Birth of Rebbetzin Sheina, daughter of Rebbe Rayatz

*1904*

The 5705 Motzaei Shabbat Shemot diary entry records the birth of the Rebbe Rayatz's daughter Sheina forty-one years earlier, on the night of Shabbat Kodesh Shemot 5664 (January 1904), at the twelfth hour of the night. The Rebbe spent three hours that night reconstructing every detail of her birth in his memory, then wept over not knowing where Sheina and her husband Menachem Mendel — son of his cousin Chaya Mushka — then were, and prayed for their safety among the rest of Israel.
