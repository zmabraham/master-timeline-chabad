# Lekach distribution before Yom Kippur 5723

*1962*

Levin waited in line to receive lekach from the Rebbe, who gave him a piece for his daughter as well. He also visited Rebbetzin Chana with his host's family — R' Berl Yunik's family — and received lekach and a blessing from her. She remembered Levin from Pocking, where he had stayed with Michoel Teitelbaum.
