# Bobruysk Chabad dynasty ends with passing of Rabbi Shmaryahu Noach

*1924*

After the 1869 passing of the third Rebbe Menachem Mendel, four of his sons established themselves as Rebbes in other Russian towns alongside Rabbi Shmuel in Lubavitch. Those dynasties eventually petered out. With Shmaryahu Noach's death, Chabad Chasidim from Kopust, Bobruysk, and elsewhere united behind the new Lubavitcher Rebbe.
