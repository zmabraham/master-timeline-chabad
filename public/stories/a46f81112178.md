# R' Yehuda Leib Shaul Levin appointed rav of Hebron Chabad

*1887*

In 5647 (1887), the elderly R' Shmuel Menachem Chaikin, then 85, sought a deputy. That year, R' Yehuda Leib Shaul Levin, formerly rabbi of Konstantinograd in Ukraine, made aliyah and settled in Hebron. After examining Levin's learning, Chaikin and the leading scholars of Hebron unanimously appointed him as rabbi, signing a formal kesav of acceptance.
