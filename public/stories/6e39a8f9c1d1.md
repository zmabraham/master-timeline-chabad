# Yeshivat Torat Emet reestablished in Jerusalem

*1922*

Kollel Chabad's leadership wrote to R. D.M. Rabinovitch of Boston that the Hebron Torat Emet yeshiva had ceased entirely and could not yet be reconstituted there. Rabbi Eliezerov together with two distinguished avreichim were establishing the yeshiva anew in Jerusalem according to the Rebbe Rashab's original plan. Donors were urged to support the renewed institution.
