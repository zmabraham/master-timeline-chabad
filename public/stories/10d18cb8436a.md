# Amsterdam Pekoa"M directs Hebron trustees to share funds with Jerusalem Chabad chassidim

*1865*

The Amsterdam Pekudei Eretz Yisrael had sent funds to Hebron on the understanding that they would be distributed to Kollel Chabad members across the land. The trustees confirmed that since the regular kollel income was already feeding Jerusalem chassidim, they had now allocated the new donation accordingly, drawing it from the Hebron portion. This exchange illustrates Hebron's role as the Kollel Chabad center distributing funds throughout the four holy cities.
