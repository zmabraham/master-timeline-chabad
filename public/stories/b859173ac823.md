# Birth of Rebbe Rashab in Lubavitch

*1860*

The Rashab was born to the Maharash and Rebbetzin Rivkah. He would later found the Tomchei Tmimim yeshiva in Lubavitch in 5657 and lead the movement through the upheavals of World War One. His birth in the courtyard further confirmed Lubavitch as the dynastic center.
