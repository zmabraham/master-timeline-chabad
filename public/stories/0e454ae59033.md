# Tomchei Tmimim classes move to renovated great hall

*1901*

The great hall, originally built only for summer use, lacked heating and had simple wooden walls that had deteriorated. Renovation began with installation of stoves, new foundations and flooring, and new windows, continuing until 5663. Eighteen large tables seating up to ten students each filled the hall, with a central bimah holding two tables for the mashgichim.
