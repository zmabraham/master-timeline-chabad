# R' Eber finishes examining Warsaw students and reports to Rayatz

*1931*

On 13 Iyar 5691, R' Yehuda Eber reported to the Rebbe that he had examined all the students currently in Warsaw and that about 20 would be suitable to transfer to Riga. He recorded his disagreement with the hanhalla's plan to close the lower preparatory class, arguing that new students typically join the younger classes and that chassidic families in the area especially wished their sons to learn at Tomchei Tmimim.
