# Famous Simchat Torah 'Eimasai Kaasi Mar' sicha and Stephen Wise visit

*1929*

Hakafos continued until dawn, and at the daytime meal the Rebbe was about to begin his sicha when Reform leader Rabbi Stephen Wise arrived to greet him. Yiddish journalists who were present struggled to capture the deep content; R. Chaim Lieberman, the Rebbe's secretary, stood behind him and transcribed the talk in pencil, later recopying it in ink. The sicha was published in a second edition in 5697 (1937) in three pamphlets.
