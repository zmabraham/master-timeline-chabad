# Founding of 'Achei Temimim' youth division in New York

*1940*

In the winter of 5701 (1940–41) the Rebbe Rayatz established Achei Temimim, the youth division of Tomchei Tmimim, in New York. He appointed R' Shneur Zalman Gurarie — newly settled in New York through his marriage into the Kopytznitz family — to head the new division. The story is recounted in Toldot Chabad in the United States, p. 180 onward.
