# Rebbe Rayatz writes urgent appeal to Aharon Teitelbaum

*1929*

The Rebbe described receiving a telegram from R. Lukshin reading 'no hope for the budget,' which devastated him. A second telegram said Teitelbaum 'gives hope for next week,' indicating the question would return to the table. He spelled out that Soviet institutions were 'flailing between life and death,' with R. Rosen having loaned 15,000 rubles.
