# Frierdiker Rebbe arrives in the United States

*1940*

After being rescued from Nazi-occupied Warsaw, the Frierdiker Rebbe reached the United States in the winter of 5700. His arrival mobilized the author, who immediately took on intensive new responsibilities: mashpia at central Tomchei Tmimim, chairman and director of Beis Rivkah and its New York branches, general gabbai of the Chevra Kadisha, rav of the Lubavitch community in New York, organizer of the Tomchei Tmimim branch in Newark, and founder and mashpia of the Hadar HaTorah yeshiva for newly observant students.
