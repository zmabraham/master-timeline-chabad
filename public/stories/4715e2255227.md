# Rebbe's letter to Shanghai students charges them with mission

*1946*

Upon their arrival from Shanghai, the sixth Rebbe sent a poignant letter laying out a new vision for his refugee students. He framed their long odyssey from Poland through China to America as a divine mission to bolster Jewish life, instructing them not to be sidetracked by American bounty but to look for the positive in others and the deficiencies in themselves.
