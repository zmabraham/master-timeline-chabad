# Yisrael Yehuda Levin wrote to Rebbe Rayatz requesting blessing for exit visa

*1934*

On Erev Shabbat Parshat Shemini, 28 Nissan 5694 (April 13, 1934), Yisrael Yehuda ben Ephraim Shmuel Levin wrote to the Rebbe Rayatz from Moscow. He asked for arousal of mercy from on high so that the Soviet officials granting exit permits would look favorably on his case and issue him travel documents. The letter was preserved in the archive of the Rebbe Rayatz's correspondence at the library of Agudas Chasidei Chabad.
