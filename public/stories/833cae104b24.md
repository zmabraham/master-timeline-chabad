# Rabbi Uri Kamishov presenting one of the books he translated into Russian

*1950*

Rabbi Uri Kamishov presenting one of the books he translated into Russian (5783). Rabbi Uri Kamishov (born 5711, 1950) is a Chabad chassid residing in Maale Adumim, who is engaged in translating Chassidus books into Russian for the 'Lechaim' publishing house, and in devotedly delivering classes
