# Rosenbaum sends 1,000 rubles of lottery winnings to Rebbe Rashab

*1906*

Reb Avraham Chaim won 5,000 rubles in a lottery and immediately sent 1,000 of them to the Rebbe Rashab. On 12 Tishrei 5667 (1 October 1906) the Rebbe wrote from Lubavitch confirming arrival of the money, enclosing a distribution list, and praying that Rosenbaum continue to merit livelihood and the ability to do tzedakah.
