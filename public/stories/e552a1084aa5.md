# Family crosses Soviet border from Lemberg to Poland on 15 Av 5706

*1946*

Because Yisrael Shimon's purchased passport lacked a photograph and Polish border crossing required one, the narrator forged a Samarkand police seal onto an affixed photo using ink-pencil tools acquired in Lemberg. Anash including R. Yehuda Leib Matzkin and R. Yaakov Yosef Raskin examined and approved the forgery. The train passed through the border station Medyka, and a Soviet officer accepted the doctored passport, allowing the families to reach Przemyśl on the Polish side.
