# Yeshiva and school students brought to Lenin Library awaiting Schneersohn books

*1991*

Word came that the Russian Federation foreign ministry had prepared papers for the USSR Culture Ministry to sign for releasing the library, and the boys were to be on hand before noon. Two busloads arrived at the library. Veronika gathered them into the waiting hall, where the author told them the story of the books and led them in saying L'Dovid Hashem Ori. The Culture Ministry then replied that they could not sign without a full council decision.
