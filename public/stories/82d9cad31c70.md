# Death of Rashbatz Sheftel

*1905*

R' Shmuel Betzalel (Rashbatz) Sheftel passed away on 15 Sivan 5665 (1905), after serving as chief mashpia at Tomchei Tmimim Lubavitch for five years. Following his death, R' Shmuel Gronem Esterman (Reshag) from Zembin was appointed in his place. The Zembin branch was simultaneously closed.
