# Babinovich Peace Court rules in favor of the Mitteler Rebbe

*1826*

On 20 July 1826, the peace court of Babinovich rendered its decision in the case opened against the Mitteler Rebbe on the basis of Shimcha Kissin's denunciation. The court concluded that contributions to the Rebbe were freely given and could not be considered illegal, dismissing the charges. The verdict was the first of several rulings exonerating the Rebbe.
