# Complete new edition of Tzemach Tzedek published

*1994*

Five years after the Rebbe's 5749 directive, the new comprehensive edition of the Tzemach Tzedek appeared. It consolidated 110 years of supplements, omissions, additions, and milu'im (including Choshen Mishpat) into a unified typeset series, finally resolving the longstanding difficulty of locating a given responsum scattered across multiple appendices.
