# Tomchei Tmimim Warsaw moves to Mlawska 7 / Sapuzinski 17

*1926*

After Pesach 5686 the rapidly growing yeshiva — with nearly three hundred students drawn from Poland and abroad — left Franciscan Street and moved into a far larger leased building at Mlawska 7 / Sapuzinski 17. The three-story former flour-mill complex, fitted out as kitchen, dining hall, five large classrooms, dormitory attic and a prayer hall, was renovated with a grant from Mr. Bernhard Cohen of the Joint Distribution Committee's reconstruction fund. The yeshiva remained at this address until autumn 5696 (1935), when it relocated to Otwock.
