# Rebbe announces Tomchei Tmimim students have received visas in Japan

*1941*

In a letter dated 4 Nisan 5701 (April 1, 1941), the Frierdiker Rebbe announced that his students who had escaped to Japan already held their visas and were only waiting for travel arrangements. He prayed that they would arrive safely and continue their Torah studies with fear of Heaven. This was part of the broader rescue effort to move Tomchei Tmimim students from Nazi-occupied Europe through Japan to America.
