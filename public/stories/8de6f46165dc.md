# Russian Jewish Statute restricting rabbinic fund collection

*1804*

The Russian Jewish Statute of 5565 (1804) regulated Jewish communal life and explicitly forbade rabbis from gathering money from their congregations beyond a fixed salary. This statute would later be invoked by opponents of Chabad and by Russian investigators questioning whether the Mitteler Rebbe's collection of ma'amad funds violated the law. The Senate ultimately ruled that the voluntary nature of the donations, combined with the Rebbe's non-official rabbinic status, placed him outside its prohibitions.
