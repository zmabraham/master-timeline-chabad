# Plague and quarantine begin in Tiberias

*1791*

The Chasidic letters from Tiberias describe a plague and quarantine that began in Adar 5551 and continued until the city gates were reopened in Tammuz. The community wrote of severe financial distress during the lockdown, when 'silver had no outlet.' The arrival of the shadar R' Meir of Bychov shortly after the gates reopened brought desperately needed relief.
