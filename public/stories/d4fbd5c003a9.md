# Rebbe tells Russian Chasidim to wait before fleeing

*1945*

Rabbi Shmuel Levitin in New York relayed a coded message from his son-in-law Binyamin Gorodetsky in Uzbekistan. The Rebbe's actual words being too dangerous to wire, Levitin sent a telegram saying simply, 'Grandfather says it's better to stay put,' urging caution until the window for safe Polish-passport border crossings became clearer.
