# Rashab declares mission of Tomchei Temimim as 'soldiers of King David'

*1900*

On Simchat Torah, the Rashab told the students they should 'divorce' themselves from worldly concerns and make spiritual development their priority. He charged them with disseminating chasidic teachings outward, recalling the Baal Shem Tov's vision of bringing redemption through spreading the wellsprings.
