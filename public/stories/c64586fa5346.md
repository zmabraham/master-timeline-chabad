# Shanghai students send petition to Rebbe Rayatz

*1945*

On 18 (Chai) Elul 5705, the students wrote to the Frierdiker Rebbe pleading from the depths of their hearts to be saved quickly from Shanghai — described as a city of an abyss with harsh, oppressive air that drained their strength and caused various illnesses. They emphasized that the city was unsuitable for yeshiva students and bnei Torah, and begged to be brought close to the Rebbe and his chassidim to resume orderly study and inner avodah.
