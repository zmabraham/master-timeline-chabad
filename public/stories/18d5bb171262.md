# R. Chaim Liberman writes to Mendel Rosenbaum for father's biography

*1940*

Acting on behalf of the Rayatz, secretary Chaim Liberman wrote to Mendel Rosenbaum, recently arrived in America, asking him to compose a full biography of his father R. Avraham Chaim Rosenbaum. The letter explained that former Lubavitch yeshiva students were producing collections devoted entirely to Chabad Chasidism and Chasidim of past generations.
