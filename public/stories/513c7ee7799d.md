# Author arrives in Lubavitch to begin yeshiva studies

*1907*

On Wednesday, the eve of Rosh Chodesh Marcheshvan 5668 (corresponding to early October 1907), the author and his father arrived in Lubavitch. They lodged with their relative R. Shlomo Chaim Kutain, a shochet who trained students in ritual slaughter, including the Rayatz. Roughly ten students at a time learned the craft under him.
