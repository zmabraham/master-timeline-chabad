# Globoke chassidim petition Rebbe to appoint Kuperstock

*1930*

On 14 Elul 5690 the chassidic community of Globoke convened and signed a letter to the Rebbe Rayatz, naming three delegates - R. Elyakum Reier, R. Baruch Shalom Tzepeliowitz, and R. Moshe Leib Rothstein - to formally request that R. Menachem Mendel Kuperstock be appointed as their rabbi. The letter expressed satisfaction with his earlier visit and was signed by 48 chassidim with the community's seals.
