# Rebbe instructs Levitin to pursue 300 visas for students stranded in Shanghai

*1941*

On 10 Kislev 5702 (November 30, 1941), the Rebbe wrote to Rabbi Shmuel Levitin, who had been sent to greet the students in Montreal. He noted that Rashag had forwarded the list of remaining Shanghai students to Levitin and Rabbi Yosef Albert, urging that no effort be spared to fill all 300 visas being sought. These additional visas were never granted, and the talmidim remained in Shanghai until the war's end.
