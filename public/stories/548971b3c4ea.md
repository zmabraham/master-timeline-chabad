# Yisrael Yehuda Levin completes memoir introduction

*1995*

Levin had begun writing his memories around 1960 and added to them periodically through the 1990s. His son Shalom Dovber and grandson Yosef Yitzchak Keller edited the manuscript for publication. The memoir covers his life from the 1919-1920 pogroms in Zhivotov through his first month with the Rebbe in Tishrei 5723.
