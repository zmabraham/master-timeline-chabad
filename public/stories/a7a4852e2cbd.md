# Rebbe calls for women and girls to light Shabbos candles

*1974*

From the latter part of 5734 (1974), the Rebbe began issuing a special public call that every Jewish woman and girl light Shabbos and Yom Tov candles, including unmarried girls from the age of chinuch. The campaign produced candlesticks, explanatory literature in many languages, and outreach materials distributed by Chabad centers worldwide. It became one of the Rebbe's signature mitzvah campaigns.
