# Rebbe Rayatz speaks on mesirus nefesh of Avraham Avinu vs. Rabbi Akiva

*1926*

Just after Simchas Torah 5687, R' Yechezkel Feigin wrote to the author conveying what the Frierdiker Rebbe had told him: Rabbi Akiva sought mesirus nefesh as an opportunity to fulfill the verse, whereas Avraham Avinu sought to publicize Hashem's existence in the world — accepting prison or being thrown into a furnace only as incidental consequences of that mission. The statement framed the Rebbe's own underground work in Soviet Russia and foreshadowed his arrest months later.
