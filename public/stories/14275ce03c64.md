# Pereshel facsimile of the Babad Amsterdam 1769 manuscript Haggadah

*1987*

In 5747 (1987), Tuvia Pereshel published in New York a facsimile of an illustrated manuscript Haggadah written in Amsterdam in 5529 (1769) by Yehoshua ben Mordechai Babad of Volochisk. The 47-page octavo edition included a bilingual English-Hebrew introduction by Pereshel. The 'Haggadat Babad' continued his annual facsimile project.
