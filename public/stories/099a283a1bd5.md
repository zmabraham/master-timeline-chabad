# Warsaw Haggadah with Sfat Emet and Tiferet Shlomo commentaries

*1925*

In 5685 (1925), a Haggadah featuring the 'Sfat Emet' commentary by Rebbe Yehuda Aryeh Leib Alter of Gur and the 'Tiferet Shlomo' by Rabbi Shlomo HaKohen, head of the Radomsk court, was issued in Warsaw by Yitzchak Meir Alter. The 83-leaf octavo edition was printed by Kupferstoch and Kramarsh. Leaves 2-4 apparently were never printed.
