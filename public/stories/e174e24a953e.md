# Frierdiker Rebbe writes to Yehoshua Heshel Reizman

*1946*

Replying to a letter from 8 Kislev, the Rebbe expressed joy that Reizman had been saved from the Nazis and pleasure at his efforts together with his fellow temimim to strengthen Torah, kosher chinuch, good middot and ahavat Yisrael. He urged them to persist despite all obstacles and obstructions in their country.
