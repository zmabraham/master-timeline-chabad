# Yehudah V'Yerushalayim published mentioning Aharon Lipkin's role

*1878*

In Yehudah V'Yerushalayim, Yoel Moshe Solomon recounted that money received from England by the General Vaad of Jerusalem's kollelim was distributed to each kollel separately. The Chabad portion was delivered to R. Aharon Lipkin, identified as their trustee. The account confirms Lipkin's continuing leadership role through the 1870s.
