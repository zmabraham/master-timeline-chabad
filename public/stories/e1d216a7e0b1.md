# R. Yehuda Eber arrives in Riga from Warsaw

*1940*

Rosh Yeshiva R. Yehuda Eber had failed to depart with the Rebbe in early Tevet. On 12 Adar Sheni 5700, R. Mordechai Chefetz reported he would arrive that Tuesday from Warsaw. On 21 Adar Sheni, Feigin confirmed Eber's safe arrival in Riga the previous Erev Shabbat.
