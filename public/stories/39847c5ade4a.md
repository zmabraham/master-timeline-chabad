# Rabbi Yoav Lamberg receives a winning certificate for his composition 'Hefsek b'Tefillah'

*1976*

Rabbi Yoav Lamberg receives a winning certificate for his composition 'Hefsek b'Tefillah' (5770). Rabbi Yoav Lamberg (born in the year 5736, 1976) is a member of the faculty of Beit Rivkah College Kfar Chabad, a Torah editor and author, and a member of the Chabad community in Petach Tikva
