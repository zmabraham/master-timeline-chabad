# Kol Yisrael article exposes Tel Aviv municipality's pressure on Chabad cheder

*1925*

After a donor offered to fund a building for the cheder, Malkov reported that the Tel Aviv municipality demanded either Hebrew-only instruction under municipal supervision, or a 40-pound permit fee. The municipality referred the matter to Ahad Ha'am, who refused to support the institution while it taught in Yiddish. Malkov framed this as religious persecution dressed in linguistic guise and a painful absurdity for Jewish life in Eretz Yisrael.
