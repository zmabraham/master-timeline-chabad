# Chabad delegation meets new Soviet Justice Minister Lushchenkov

*1990*

On 3 Tevet 5751 Veronika met briefly with the newly appointed Soviet Justice Minister Lushchenkov, who directed her to brief his deputies in detail and scheduled a full delegation meeting before Shabbat. Lushchenkov later told the delegation that Gubenko's refusal did not accord with Soviet law and pledged to back their effort, though formal action would await the conclusion of the Congress of People's Deputies. The change of minister was viewed as providential after weeks of stalemate at the Ministry of Culture.
