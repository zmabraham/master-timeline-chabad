# 770 Eastern Parkway purchased by Agudas Chassidei Chabad

*1940*

Rejecting Brownsville and suburban options, the Rebbe selected prestigious Crown Heights despite there being only three Chabad families in the neighborhood. The building, originally built as a doctor's home and clinic by architect Edwin Kline, had an elevator essential to the Rebbe's mobility. It would become the iconic '770,' replicated worldwide.
