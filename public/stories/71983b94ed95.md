# Fez Beit Din settlement over R' Yitzchak ibn Assoulin's estate

*1727*

Chaim, who had married R' Yitzchak's widow, demanded an accounting of the estate that R' Moshe had managed during years of famine, while R' Moshe claimed nothing remained. Mediators settled the case for sixteen ounces, all other claims were mutually waived, and a dwelling was preserved for the orphan Ayyush. R' Yehuda ibn Attar and Shmuel Gabbai presided and witnessed.
