# Rebbe Yosef Yitzchak arrives in Riga after escaping Warsaw siege

*1939*

Having been smuggled out of war-torn Warsaw, the Rebbe arrived in Riga on Sunday, 5 Tevet, and made relief for Polish Jewry his very first order of business. That same day he issued public appeals — one to general Jewry and one to anash worldwide — describing the catastrophic conditions and urging immediate fundraising through the Joint Distribution Committee. The arrival marked the start of a half-year base in Riga from which he would coordinate aid before sailing to America.
