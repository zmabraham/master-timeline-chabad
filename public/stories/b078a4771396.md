# Rayatz receives Massechet Chagigah at Machaneh Israel Siyum HaShas

*1945*

On Tuesday 24 Tevet 5705 the Rebbe Rayatz recorded that, at the previous night's chalukat haShas of Machaneh Israel, he had taken Massechet Chagigah as his portion. He prayed that God enlighten his eyes and heart in Torah and bless his work in all Torah and charitable institutions, despite the cold and head pains he was then suffering.
