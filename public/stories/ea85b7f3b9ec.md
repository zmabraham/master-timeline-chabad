# Bolsheviks overthrow Kerensky and ban Jewish religious parties

*1917*

Throughout 1917, in the freedom that followed the February Revolution, chassidim and other observant Jews had been organizing politically, urged on by the Rebbe Rashab, to win representation in the new communal structures. The planned all-Russian Jewish assembly never met. The Bolshevik overthrow of Kerensky's government ended these efforts entirely: the new regime invalidated the religious parties and disbanded the societies that had taken shape under the Provisional Government.
