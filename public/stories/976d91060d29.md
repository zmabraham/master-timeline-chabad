# Rayatz establishes Shulchan HaNesius over Kollel Chabad

*1930*

Having previously appointed R' Shlomo Yehuda Leib Eliezrov as his personal representative when he accepted the kollel's presidency, the Rayatz now created a Shulchan HaNesius to ensure direct supervision of the kollel's affairs. The committee included rabbis Eliezrov, Menachem Mendel Naeh, Chaim Kam, Yisrael Asher Liba, and Mordechai Reuven Rokeach. Its regulations were sent in a separate document no longer extant.
