# Rebbe writes to Hatamim about Achos Tmimim Riga

*1937*

The Rebbe named the three appointed shepherds - R. Avraham Eliyahu Asherov, R. Mordechai Chefetz, and R. Eliyahu Chaim Altheuse - and asked Hatamim to solicit each of their perspectives on the new women's organization, with Altheuse particularly asked to describe the matter at length.
