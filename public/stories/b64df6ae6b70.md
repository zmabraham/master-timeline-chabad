# Feigin writes to Axelrod about the custom of mother's name in pidyon

*1928*

On 6 Tevet 5689, secretary Yechezkel Feigin confirmed receipt of Axelrod's pidyon nefesh and his five-shekel check. He clarified that the established practice is to write the mother's name in a pidyon, citing both the Rebbe Rayatz's statement that the Rebbeim had always written the mother's name and a tradition from Rabbi Hillel of Paritch that in a pidyon one writes the mother's name while in a tzettel one writes the father's name.
