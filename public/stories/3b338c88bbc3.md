# Library opens 13th exhibition of rare incunabula on 20th yahrzeit

*2014*

On 3 Tammuz 5774 (July 1, 2014), marking the 20th yahrzeit of the Lubavitcher Rebbe, the Chabad Library opened its 13th exhibition — Ta'aruchat Ginzei Sefarim. The exhibition displays incunabula and rare books printed in the first 100 years of Hebrew printing (1475–1574), along with rare manuscripts, broadsides and posters. A guide booklet was prepared by Rabbi Shalom DovBer Levin and R. Moshe Heller.
