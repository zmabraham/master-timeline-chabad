# Mendelstam visits the Tzemach Tzedek to push new curriculum

*1848*

In the winter of 5608 (1848), Arye Leib Mendelstam, who had replaced Lilienthal at the Education Ministry, arrived in Lubavitch. He sought the Rebbe's approval for newly edited anthology textbooks meant for use in chadarim. The Tzemach Tzedek explained at length why these compilations should not be introduced into traditional Jewish elementary schools.
