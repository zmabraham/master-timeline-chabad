# Suitable building identified in Chicago for Yeshivat Achei Tmimim

*1944*

On the evening of 5 Marcheshvan 5705, the Rebbe Rayatz's son-in-law Rashag informed him that contacts in Chicago had reported finding a large, suitable building for Yeshivat Achei Tmimim Lubavitch and intended to purchase it the next day. The development came less than two weeks after the founding fundraising meeting on 28 Tishrei.
