# Pekuam complains about R' Yechiel Cohen's prolonged American mission

*1851*

On 25 Tammuz 5611 (July 24, 1851), the Pekuam wrote to Hebron's Chabad kollel that on several occasions they had received donations from New York with explicit instructions not to give a single penny to Hebron — because R' Yechiel Cohen was still touring America after a very long time. The letter is preserved in Pinkas 11 of the Pekuam records.
