# Birz kehillah and Chabad chassidim sign shochet agreement

*1819*

Following the Alter Rebbe's ruling requiring polished knives for shechita, chassidim across Lithuania sought their own shochtim. In Birz, where mitnagdic bans had targeted the practice, the two camps reached a written settlement allowing a chassidic shochet provided he submitted to the town rav and shared revenue. The agreement was signed by R. Yitzchak Isaac, mara d'asra of Birz, and the shamash R. David.
