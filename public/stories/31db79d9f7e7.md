# R. Elchanan Yakubovitz appointed second rabbi of Lubavitch

*1915*

When R. Elchanan b. Avraham Shimon Yakubovitz, former rosh mesivta of Telz, fled to Lubavitch during World War I, the householders of the town's two main shuls appointed him as a second rabbi on 23 Menachem Av 5675 (August 1915). The forty signatories agreed to a monthly salary of thirty rubles and authorized him to rule on routine questions in the Shliove and Lachluka street synagogues. The Rashab approved, and Yakubovitz also taught in the Tomchei Tmimim yeshiva.
