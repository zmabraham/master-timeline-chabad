# R' Avraham of Kalisk winter letter (5555)

*1795*

R' Avraham of Kalisk continued in winter 5555 with another letter to the Russian chassidim. He emphasized that for practical halachic guidance the chassidim had with them the great rabbi, beloved of God, the renowned holy one - the Alter Rebbe - whose honor was the honor of the Omnipresent.
