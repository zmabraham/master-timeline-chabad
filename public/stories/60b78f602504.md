# Polish Hasidim led by R' Yaakov Shimshon settle in Tiberias

*1794*

In 5554 (1794), several Hasidim from Poland led by R' Yaakov Shimshon of Shepetivka ascended to the Holy Land and settled in Tiberias. R' Avraham of Kalisk then turned to them and to the Hasidic communities in Volhynia and Poland, asking them to contribute proportionately to the Hasidic Yishuv. This aliyah eventually triggered demands for a separate Polish-Volhynian kollel in Tiberias.
