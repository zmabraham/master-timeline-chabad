# Calabrian etrog mix-up at Rebbe Rashab's home on Sukkos

*1917*

The Rebbe Rashab had several Calabrian etrogim that Sukkos, designating one for the chassidim to use for the blessing. When Pesach Wolf arrived mid-Chol Hamoed from Zhitomir, the attendant R' M.M. mistakenly brought him the Rebbe's own etrog. This sparked a subsequent halachic discussion about whether the blessing had been fulfilled or had to be repeated.
