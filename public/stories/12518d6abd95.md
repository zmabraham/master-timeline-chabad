# Farbrengen in Radom ghetto on Rosh Chodesh Elul

*1942*

When one of the participants groaned that nothing could be done if it was the Holy One's will that Jews suffer, R' Baruch Friedman rose and emotionally protested that it was forbidden to say such a thing. The gathering took place under intensifying persecution, with the yeshiva continuing its learning despite the deteriorating conditions in the ghetto.
