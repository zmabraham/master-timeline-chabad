# R' Avraham of Kalisk letter endorsing Alter Rebbe's leadership

*1790*

Continuing the practice begun by R' Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk before his passing, R' Avraham of Kalisk wrote regularly to Russian chassidim urging them to attach themselves to the Alter Rebbe. In this 5550 letter he described the Alter Rebbe as the great rabbi 'this Sinai' through whose light they would see eternal light and the treasury of life and peace.
