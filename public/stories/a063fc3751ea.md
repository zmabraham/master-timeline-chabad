# Rebbe Rashab and family leave Lubavitch for the south

*1915*

With the German army nearing Belarussia and Russian authorities seizing Jewish leaders as hostages near the front, the Rashab was forced to leave Lubavitch. Preparations had begun after Sukkot with wooden crates built for the seforim, and on Parshat Vayera he delivered a discourse on the levels of cholat ahavah. On 16 Cheshvan he departed; he stopped in the city of Ariel for several weeks before settling in Rostov-on-Don in the Cossack region.
