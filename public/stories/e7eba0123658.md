# Author studies Tanach and dikduk with R' Itshe Matlies in Zlobin

*1928*

By day he learned Gemara with Rabbi Benyaminson, and in the evenings R' Itshe taught him Yirmiyahu and dikduk. Toward the end of the term R' Itshe drilled him word-by-word through the opening chapters of Shemos, demanding on which letter the trop fell and why. Half a century later the author still retained the rules of mileil and milra he had learned then.
