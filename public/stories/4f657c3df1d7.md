# Rebbe Rayatz writes to Łódź students about study reports

*1930*

In a letter dated 19 Cheshvan 5691 (Igros Kodesh vol. 2, no. 498), the Rebbe Rayatz wrote to the talmidim of Tomchei Tmimim that he had taken great pleasure reading the lectures and study reports from Warsaw, Vilna and Łódź. The letter is the earliest extant reference to the Łódź branch in the Rebbe's correspondence. It documents the regular reporting system maintained between the branches and the Rebbe during his stay in Riga.
