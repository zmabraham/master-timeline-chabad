# Tzemach Tzedek's wedding in Parshat Toldot

*1803*

The wedding of the future Tzemach Tzedek to Rebbetzin Chaya Mushka took place during Parshat Toldot 5564 (Cheshvan, late 1803). This dating is used in the family chronicle to argue against an earlier proposed birth year of 5564 for their son Baruch Shalom, since a child born to them could only have arrived after the wedding.
