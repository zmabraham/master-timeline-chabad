# Death of Levin's son Ephraim Shmuel in Samarkand hospital

*1941*

On erev Shabbat 9 Kislev 5702, Levin's wife brought their two-and-a-quarter-year-old son Ephraim Shmuel to a Samarkand hospital, where he died — almost no one who entered the hospital in those days came out alive. The couple themselves carried his body several kilometers to the cemetery after Shabbat. Crushed, Levin prayed only that he not lose his faith and his bond with the Rebbe.
