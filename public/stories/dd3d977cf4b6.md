# Feigin writes of voided permits and new passport requirement

*1940*

In Tishrei 5701, R. Yechezkel Feigin wrote to the Frierdiker Rebbe that they had been instructed to submit fresh applications for Soviet passports, with a two-month wait for a Moscow decision. The American consul meanwhile would not renew expired visas without new authorizations from the US, including a new affidavit for their son Shmuel Leib.
