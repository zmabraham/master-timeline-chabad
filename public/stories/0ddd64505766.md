# R' Avraham of Kalisk letter (5551) praising Alter Rebbe

*1791*

R' Avraham of Kalisk continued his yearly campaign of letters endorsing the Alter Rebbe's leadership. In 5551 he wrote that the Alter Rebbe was beloved and treasured, a holy man of God, a wondrous counselor who would show chassidim the way of God, and that they should listen to him.
