# R' Menachem Tzvi Hirsch visits Lubavitch seeking the Tzemach Tzedek's help

*1835*

Struggling with poverty and poor health, R' Menachem Tzvi Hirsch sought guidance from the Tzemach Tzedek about finding a rabbinic position. The Rebbe lent him money to cover debts, blessed him to find a fitting position, and wrote a recommendation to R' Peretz Chen of Beshenkovichi.
