# R' Avraham Toledano conveys Fez mortgage to Rabbi Yaakov ibn Tzur (Yaavetz)

*1723*

R' Yaakov ibn Tzur served as rabbi of Meknes from circa 5478-5488 (1717/8-1727/8). Despite his seat in Meknes, in 5483 he acquired this Fez pawn deed, which had passed through many hands since 5476. Witnessed by Yehuda Halevi and David Shacrun in Fez.
