# R' Yehiel Tzvi Gurarie emigrates from Russia to Warsaw

*1925*

In 5685 (1924/25) R' Yehiel Tzvi Gurarie succeeded in escaping Soviet Russia and settling in Warsaw. He joined the three-man directing committee of the Warsaw Tomchei Tmimim, replacing R' Dov Moshe Shmotkin who had emigrated to the Holy Land. Together with R' Shneur Zalman Shmotkin and R' Shmuel Zalmanov's father, he bore the burden of running the yeshiva.
