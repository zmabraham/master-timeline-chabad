# Construction of large wooden synagogue begins in Lubavitch

*1814*

The synagogue measured roughly 21 by 13 meters with a vestibule open on two sides and a central bimah. According to investigator Korbitzky's 1826 court report, the building also housed Torah scrolls written by the Yanovich scribe, menorahs ordered from Petersburg, and a library of 611 books kept in four cabinets and available for lending.
