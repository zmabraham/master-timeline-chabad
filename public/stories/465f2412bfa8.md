# R. Yisrael Grossman writes to cousin documenting MaHaRIL descent

*1996*

The letter identifies three brothers — R. Schneur Zalman, R. Yisrael, and another — buried on the Mount of Olives, and traces the line back to MaHaRIL. It is a primary source for the family tree linking the Brimat/Saulson/Singer/Grossman families to the brother of the Baal HaTanya.
