# Polish refugees crash the Yom Kippur minyan in Samarkand

*1941*

After Stalin made peace with the Polish government-in-exile in 1941 he released his Polish-citizen prisoners from Siberia, and thousands drifted south into the warm Central Asian republics, including Samarkand. Wandering from courtyard to courtyard in search of a Kol Nidrei, they began pounding on R' Aizik's gate until it opened and the house filled. Frightened by the exposure, R' Aizik refused to host the following day, and the worshippers returned to the author's apartment to complete Yom Kippur.
