# R' Aharon Alexandrov brings Torah Or to press

*1837*

In 5597 (1837), Rabbi Aharon ben Moshe Chaim Alexandrov brought the sefer Torah Or to press. This volume of the Alter Rebbe's chassidic discourses was edited and arranged for publication by the Tzemach Tzedek, who attested on the title page that the Alter Rebbe himself had reviewed and corrected many of these maamarim during his lifetime in his own hand and language.
