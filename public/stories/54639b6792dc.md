# Rebbe's 70th birthday: Vaad 71 Mosdos established

*1972*

On 11 Nissan 5732 (1972), the Rebbe's 70th birthday, he declared that rather than slowing his pace at this age, activity must be intensified and expanded. He established the 'Vaad 71 Mosdos' — a project to found 71 new institutions during the coming 71st year of his life, fueling a major expansion of shlichus.
