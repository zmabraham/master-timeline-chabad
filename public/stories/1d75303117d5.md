# Passing of Rabbi Yehuda Leib of Yanovich, brother of the Alter Rebbe

*1826*

Rabbi Yehuda Leib, brother of the Alter Rebbe and rabbi of Yanovich, passed away in the winter of 5586 (1826). He was the author of Shearit Yehuda and had served as a senior halachic authority in Chabad. His passing is noted in the introductions to the Tzemach Tzedek's responsa.
