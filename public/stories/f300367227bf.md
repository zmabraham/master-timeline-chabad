# New EY fund distribution order established

*1849*

From 5609 the distribution formula was fixed: after expenses, funds were divided into 168 parts. The Sephardic kollels received 67/168. From the remaining 101/168, the HoD kollel took a third, and the remaining two-thirds was divided into 101 parts, of which Kollel Chabad in Hebron received 9/101.
