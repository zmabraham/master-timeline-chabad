# Rebbe asks U.S. government to keep Levitin in America

*1940*

After escaping Warsaw to Riga, the Rebbe wrote on 9 Adar I 5700 (Igros Kodesh vol. 14, letter 5261) to the Agudas Chabad leadership in New York. He asked them to lobby the Washington government to allow Levitin to remain in America and to bring his family to join him, since he was essential to the work of disseminating chassidus and organizing Chabad chassidim worldwide.
