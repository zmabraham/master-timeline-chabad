# Last pre-war European Haggadah printed in Munkacs

*1940*

In 5700 (1940), Rabbi Shaul Brach's son Shmuel published his father's Haggadah Tov Devarcha at the Grafia press in Munkacs. With the war engulfing Europe, it became one of the last Haggadot printed there before printing in Europe ground to a halt.
