# R. Aryeh Leib Sheinin sent to influence Vilna and Minsk towns

*1921*

Sheinin, one of the foremost Tomchei Tmimim students in Lubavitch, was sent by the Rebbe in the winter of 5681 as a mashpia in the towns of the Vilna and Minsk regions. On 17 Shevat 5681 the Rebbe wrote (Igros Kodesh vol. 16, letter 5723) singling him out as a model emissary urging communities to establish fixed Torah-study times in nigleh and chassidus.
