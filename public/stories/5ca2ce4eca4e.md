# Chevrei Adei-Ad organization launched

*1945*

On 22 Tevet 5705 (7 January 1945) the Rebbe Rayatz prepared the founding letters for the new institution Chevrei Adei-Ad: a general circular announcing its establishment, a supplementary letter to Rabbi Mordechai Mentlik regarding the administrative board, and individual letters to Rabbis Silver, Dushavitz and Telushkin inviting them to serve on its Torah council.
