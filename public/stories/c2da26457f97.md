# R' Dovber Lebertov warns memoirist to leave Moscow immediately

*1946*

Arriving in Moscow on a Thursday, the memoirist intended to spend Shabbos with relatives. When he told R' Dovber Kobeliaker of his plan, R' Dovber sharply commanded him to leave immediately, knowing how dangerous the city had become. The memoirist left the next day; R' Dovber was later repeatedly summoned by the GPU, ultimately arrested and exiled, dying in the wilds.
