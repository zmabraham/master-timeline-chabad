# Rabbi Zeitlin being interviewed by JEM

*1953*

Rabbi Zeitlin at the entrance to 770 with the group of girls he led. Rabbi Aharon Eliezer Zeitlin (5714, 1953 - 5776, 2015) was one of the Rebbe's shluchim to the Holy Land, one of the mashpi'im of Anash in Tzfat and chairman of the Chabad kindergarten network in Tzfat
