# Rashab appoints R' Eleazar Moshe Madiyevsky to head Hebron committee

*1904*

On 15 Shvat 5664 (February 1, 1904), the Rashab dispatched R' Sh.Y.L. Eliezerov to R' Eleazar Moshe Madiyevsky with a personal letter asking him to head a new Russia-based committee dedicated to improving conditions in Hebron. The discussions stretched on for a year and a half before the Vaad Chevroni was finally constituted in the summer of 1905.
