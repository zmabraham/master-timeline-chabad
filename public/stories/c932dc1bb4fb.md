# Shusterman family moves into Crown Heights home at 1717 President Street

*1955*

On Erev Purim 5715 (March 7, 1955), the author's family moved into their newly purchased Crown Heights home at 1717 President Street, beside the shul of Rabbi B.Z. Frankel with its mikveh. Earlier the author had asked the Rebbe about renting a Crown Heights apartment; the Rebbe replied that smaller people than him had managed to buy. The doubled 'tov' in the number 1717 was taken as a good sign.
