# Mitteler Rebbe founds Chabad settlement in Hebron

*1816*

Between 1816 and 1819, in response to deepening tensions that had developed since 1805 between the general chassidic community of Safed and Tiberias and the local Chabad chassidim, the Mitteler Rebbe founded a separate Chabad yishuv in Hebron. Until this settlement was established, the Mitteler Rebbe had counseled chassidim planning aliyah to settle in Jerusalem instead.
