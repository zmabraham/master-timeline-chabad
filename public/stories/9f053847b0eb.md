# Passing of the Tzemach Tzedek

*1866*

The Tzemach Tzedek's passing on 13 Nissan תרכ"ו shaped subsequent Chabad publishing history. When his sons brought a second edition of Likkutei Torah to press in the summer that followed, they declined to put their names on the title page out of mourning, leaving only R' Shmuel Betzalel Sheftil — the manager of the project — credited.
