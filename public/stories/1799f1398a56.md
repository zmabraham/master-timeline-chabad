# About 1,000 Chabad chassidim emigrate from Soviet Russia

*1946*

Over 5706–5707 (1946–1947) approximately a thousand Anash escaped Soviet Russia. After two years in displaced-persons camps in Germany and Austria and an extended stay in Paris, they began to settle permanently in various countries. A portion made aliyah to the Land of Israel on the instruction of the Frierdiker Rebbe.
