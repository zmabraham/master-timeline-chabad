# R' Shlomo Zalman HaKohen departs Tiberias on second shadar mission

*1785*

His signature on a Mi Sheberach written by the chassidim of Tiberias during winter 5545 places him in the city at that time. He left Tiberias at the end of that winter as shadar on his second mission to the diaspora, returning to the Land of Israel in the autumn of the following year.
