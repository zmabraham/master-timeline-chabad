# Rayatz letter outlines Hebron yeshivah purpose

*1911*

On 3 Kislev 5672 (November 24, 1911), the Rayatz sent a detailed letter to Rabbi Zalman Havlin and his Hebron students. It articulated the mission of Toras Emes: invigorating chasidic study in Eretz Yisrael and producing new Jewish leadership for the land, parallel to Tomchei Temimim's mission in Russia.
