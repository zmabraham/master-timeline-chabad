# Exhibition on Chabad in the World and Torah Giants

*1995*

Opening on Rosh Chodesh Sivan 5755 and continuing past the yahrzeit of the Rebbe on 3 Tammuz, this exhibition focused on the Rebbe, the Chabad Rebbes, and gedolei Yisrael, alongside the spread of Chabad activity around the world. It emphasized the leadership era of the Rebbe Rayatz and the early years of the Rebbe's nesius, when Chabad activity expanded globally. Items included photographs, letters, signs and artifacts from Chabad centers in Russia, Eretz Yisrael, Europe, America and beyond.
