# Alter Rebbe flees Liadi with family ahead of French advance

*1812*

In 1812, as Napoleon's army advanced into Belarus and neared Dubrovna, Rabbi Schneur Zalman of Liadi abandoned his spacious home, valuable library, and all possessions in Liadi, fleeing with his family and close followers — including his son DovBer — into the Russian interior out of loyalty to the Russian throne. The French subsequently looted and burned the family's property, inflicting a 60,000-ruble loss. The family wandered through Kursk province for about six months in severe destitution, a period that ended with the Alter Rebbe's passing in Haditch in Poltava province.
