# Rabbi Yisrael Jacobson meets with the Rebbe in Otwock about US relocation

*1939*

Five days before the German invasion of Poland, Rabbi Yisrael Jacobson met privately with the Sixth Rebbe in Otwock. The Rebbe announced his firm decision to move to the United States, citing the success of American students at the Polish yeshivah and Rabbi Shmuel Levitin's assessment that a Lubavitcher yeshivah could thrive in America. He told Jacobson, 'Arrange everything systematically. In about half a year, with G-d's assistance, we will come to America,' and signaled a turn inward toward teaching Chasidism and caring for Chasidim.
