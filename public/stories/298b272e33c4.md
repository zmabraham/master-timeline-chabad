# R' Yaakov 'Kuli' Slonim passes away in Hebron

*1856*

R' Yaakov Slonim, married to Menucha Rachel daughter of the Mitteler Rebbe, served as gabbai of the Hebron synagogue and chazzan on the High Holidays. He headed both the Chevra Kadisha and the Gemilut Chasadim society in Hebron. He passed away on 2 Tevet 5617, leaving three sons and three daughters.
