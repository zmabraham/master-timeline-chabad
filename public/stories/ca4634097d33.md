# R' Yochanan Gordon emigrates to New York

*1932*

In 5692, R' Yochanan Gordon left his hometown of Dokshitz with his family and emigrated to New York. He set himself up in his new country as a shochet u'bodek (ritual slaughterer and inspector), a trade he had presumably been trained in earlier. This move placed him among the growing community of Lubavitcher chassidim taking root in America.
