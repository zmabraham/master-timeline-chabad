# R. Menachem Mendel Rozenmutter appointed head of Łódź yeshiva

*1934*

In 5694 (1934), R. Menachem Mendel Rozenmutter, son-in-law of R. Dovber Moshe Shmotkin of Warsaw, was appointed rosh yeshiva of the Łódź branch. He refused to accept a salary, supporting himself from his candle factory; when the Rebbe heard this he was displeased, quoting 'a free doctor is worth nothing.' Rozenmutter was murdered in the Holocaust.
