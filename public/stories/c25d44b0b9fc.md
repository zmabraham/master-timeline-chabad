# Rebbe Rayatz born in Lubavitch

*1880*

The Rebbe Rayatz, Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn, was born on 12 Tammuz 5640 (1880) in Lubavitch. He would later become the sixth Rebbe of Chabad. His birthday of 12 Tammuz would also become significant as the date of his liberation from Soviet imprisonment decades later.
