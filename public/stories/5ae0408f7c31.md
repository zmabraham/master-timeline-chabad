# Chabad delegation hands Yeltsin appeal for Schneersohn library at Munich G7

*1992*

Having transferred from the Arabella to the Marriott — where Yeltsin and his government were staying — the delegation was on hand when Yeltsin arrived in the evening. Rabbis Cunin and Aharonov shook his hand and said 'Davai knigi Schneersohn!' (Give us the Schneersohn books!). Caught off guard, Yeltsin had no real answer; pressed by Aharonov 'Kogda?' (When?), he replied 'Skoro' (Soon). The next morning the delegation approached him again, and Rabbi Yitzchok Kogan gave him a Chitas as a segulah for success. The shliach Rabbi Diskin took members of the delegation to meet US official Spakoyne, who promised to push Helmut Kohl to raise the library with Yeltsin.
