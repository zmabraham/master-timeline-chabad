# Rosenbaum writes to R. Hendel from prison

*1889*

Writing on 20 Iyar 5649 (1889) from Smolensk, Rosenbaum reminisces with R. Hendel Kugel about his student days in Lubavitch alongside R. Chaim Yaakov Widerewitz, whom he had just learned was passing through nearby Smolensk en route to Vitebsk. In a separate letter dated 'Wednesday Parshas Masei 5649,' he rejoices that the shochet R. Reuven Liberman has established regular Chassidus learning in Plescenice and prays that the local chassidic community will flourish in his absence.
