# Rebbe Rashab's Purim Katan maamar in Rostov

*1916*

For Shabbat Purim Katan 5676 Rabbi Y.Y. of Kremenchug came to Rostov, and R. Tzvi Gurary, who had planned to leave Thursday night, stayed on to spend Shabbat with the Rebbe Rashab. The Rebbe delivered the maamar 'Ve'ahavta et Havayah Elokecha,' which was preserved in a separate written record.
