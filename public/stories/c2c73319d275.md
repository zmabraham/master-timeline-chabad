# Rabbi Rotter. Rabbi Betzalel Rotter Sol Rotter, born 28 Sivan

*1935*

Rabbi Rotter. Rabbi Betzalel Rotter Sol Rotter, born 28 Sivan 5695, 1935) is a chassidic shochet residing in Crown Heights, one of the elder chassidim in the neighborhood, from the first group of members of the community committee, gabbai of the 'Beis Binyomin' synagogue and owner of the 'Prim' store
