# Tomchei Tmimim opens a branch in Charol

*1919*

The author traveled to Charol ahead of the move to arrange housing for the students and confer with the local rabbinate. He stayed with R' Shmuel Gurary, the town's dayan, while R' Moshe Madayevsky served as rav. The move was driven by the worsening shortages and disorder around Kremenchug.
