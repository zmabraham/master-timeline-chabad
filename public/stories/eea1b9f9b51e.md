# New Vilna kahal elected with chassid R. Meir Refaels at head

*1799*

Two days after the imperial dissolution of the old kahal, fresh elections produced a kehillah board of eight men with the chassid R. Meir Refaels at its head. This was a dramatic reversal for the chassidim of Vilna, who had been the target of formal communal bans for over a decade.
