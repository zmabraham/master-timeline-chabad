# R' David Leib Yaffe of Hebron passes away in Jerusalem

*1909*

R' David Leib was the son of the emissary R' Moshe ben David Yaffe of Hebron and was born in Eretz Yisrael, listed as a child of three in the 5599 census and as age 31 in the 5626 census. He later moved to live in Jerusalem, where he passed away on 3 Cheshvan 5670, his death recorded in 'Avnei Zikaron' vol. 12.
