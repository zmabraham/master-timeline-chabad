# Last Chabad breakaway dynasty ends

*1923*

In 1923, Rabbi Shmaryahu Noach Schneersohn of Bobruysk passed away, marking the end of the last of the breakaway Chabad dynasties that had emerged after Rabbi Menachem Mendel's passing in 1866. At that point, all Chabad Chasidim accepted Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn as the sole Chabad Rebbe. This consolidation followed the establishment of Yeshivas Tomchei Temimim in Lubavitch, which had drawn students from across the various Chabad streams.
