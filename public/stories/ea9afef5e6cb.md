# Mittler Rebbe establishes the Chabad center in Lubavitch

*1813*

After his father's passing and the destruction of Liadi, the Mittler Rebbe spent half a year in Kremenchug before relocating to White Russia. He chose Lubavitch as his new seat, immediately at the beginning of his leadership. Lubavitch would remain the Chabad center for the next century, giving the movement its enduring name.
