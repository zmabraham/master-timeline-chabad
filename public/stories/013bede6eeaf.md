# Trial acquits Rabbi Zalman Neimark and Chassidim in Staradub

*1883*

Rabbi Zalman Neimark and a group of Chassidim from Staradub had been imprisoned and held over for trial for two years. The trial finally took place in the winter of 5643 (1883), ending with their full acquittal. Shortly after the verdict, Rabbi Neimark left Staradub and was soon invited to serve as rabbi of Vitebsk.
