# Rebbe distributes Kuntres Maamarim 5682 on Yud Shevat

*1992*

The diarist had arrived from Moscow hours earlier. When he passed in the distribution line the Rebbe added an extra booklet with the words 'far di sforim,' explicitly linking the publication to the still-unresolved Chabad library campaign in Russia. The same week the Rebbe gave him a second dollar 'for the books' at the Friday dollars distribution.
