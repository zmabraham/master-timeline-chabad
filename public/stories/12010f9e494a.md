# R. Meir Shlomo Yanovsky becomes Rabbi of Nikolayev

*1890*

In 5650 (1890), Rabbi Meir Shlomo Yanovsky succeeded his grandfather Rabbi Avraham David Lavut as Rabbi of Nikolayev, on Lavut's deathbed instruction. He held the position until his death on 23 Elul 5693 (1933), after which his son-in-law Rabbi Shmuel Schneersohn (uncle of the Rebbe) succeeded him.
