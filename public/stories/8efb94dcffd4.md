# Russian Ministry of Culture rules Schneersohn library should stay in Russia

*1991*

On Monday 28 Sivan 5751 (June 10, 1991), a multi-hour meeting at the Russian Ministry of Culture with Lenin Library director Gubinko and three lawyers produced a 16-page memorandum concluding the Schneersohn library collection should remain in Russia. Near the end of the meeting Gubinko fell ill; an ambulance was called but he refused hospitalization. Chernyaev later told Veronika by phone that the memo did not concern him and he still intended to conclude the matter favorably within days.
