# Tomchei Tmimim Warsaw moves to Franciszkanska 8

*1923*

The new site, a beit midrash of the Strikov chassidim, had two large halls plus a kitchen, dining area and dormitory rooms; a Jewish neighbor across the way cooked for the yeshiva. The yeshiva remained there through 5685, moving to Mlawska 7 in 5686.
