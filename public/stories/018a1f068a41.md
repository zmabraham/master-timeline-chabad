# Meeting with Russian Federation Foreign Ministry on Schneerson books

*1991*

On Wednesday 6 Adar at 10am, the delegation arrived to meet Foreign Minister Kozyrev, who maintains good relations with Gorbachev and intended to discuss the library matter with him. Sergei Reznikov received them and recounted how Gubenko had earlier called demanding their expulsion from Russia, a request the ministry refused. The visit took place amid sharp tensions between the USSR and Russian Federation governments, with Yeltsin publicly demanding Gorbachev's resignation.
