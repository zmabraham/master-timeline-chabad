# Tsar Paul I orders release of Alter Rebbe

*1798*

After interrogation and the Alter Rebbe's written replies in Hebrew were translated for Tsar Paul, the Tsar issued a decree on 19 Kislev 5559 finding nothing harmful to the state in the conduct of 'the Karliner sect' and ordering all of them released. The day became the festival of 19 Kislev, Chag HaGeulah, the founding holiday of Chabad.
