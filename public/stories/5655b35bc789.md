# Emissaries demand Alter Rebbe cancel old EY fund channels

*1803*

In winter 5563 emissaries arrived asking the Alter Rebbe to cancel all existing fundraising arrangements in Russia so they could circulate and create new arrangements through 'true friends' not called by the name Chabad. They claimed to act with R' Avraham of Kalisk's knowledge. The Alter Rebbe refused to avoid increasing strife but the dispute escalated.
