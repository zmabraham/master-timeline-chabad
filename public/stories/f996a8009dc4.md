# Tmimim begin chassidus shiurim that seed Achei Tmimim

*1931*

The three initially found the lessons difficult and came only to socialize, but after a few weeks of informal gatherings they returned to the formal Tanya study. They were later joined by Mordechai Altein, Yitzchak Matashin, Mordechai Fischer, and Zerach Gordon, laying the foundation for the Achei Tmimim organization.
