# Last news from anash in Warsaw reaches the Rebbe's secretariat

*1941*

Shmuel Shmotkin had received his visa but did not know what to do for his daughters over eighteen or his sons-in-law; Shneur Zalman Shmotkin reported that the packages were a literal lebens-frage and that R' Moshe Schneersohn had been moved from Lublin to Izbica; Leib Raskin thanked the Rebbe for parcels keeping his family alive. After this date contact with Warsaw effectively ceased.
