# Hebron Chabad letter to the Tzemach Tzedek

*1831*

The letter from Hebron Chabad leaders confirmed receipt of support funds sent by the third Lubavitcher Rebbe. R' Shimon Menashe Chaikin, who would later become Hebron's Chabad rav, signed last as the youngest of the signers and identified himself as 'son of R' Moshe shochet,' showing his trade was shechita while he was also active in community affairs.
