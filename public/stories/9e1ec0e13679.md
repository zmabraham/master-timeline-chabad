# Petersburg rabbinic commission convenes

*1843*

On 6 May 1843 (18 Iyar 5603), the rabbinic commission convened in Petersburg. The Tzemach Tzedek traveled there with R. Shmaria Paytelson as his translator and his son R. Yehuda Leib for medical assistance. The commission continued through August 1843 (Elul 5603), discussing Jewish educational reforms in great secrecy; the Rebbe later said the details could not be revealed.
