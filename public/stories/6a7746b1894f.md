# Likutim Yekarim — early Baal Shem Tov teachings printed in Lvov

*1792*

Likutim Yekarim, an early compilation of the Baal Shem Tov's teachings together with those of his disciples, was printed in Lvov in 5552 (1792). It appeared roughly twelve years after the first printed works of his disciple Rabbi Yaakov Yosef of Polonne.
