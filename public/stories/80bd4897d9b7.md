# R' Yaakov Boruch Krasik becomes mashgiach

*1911*

At the start of 5672 (autumn 1911), R' Yaakov Boruch Krasik, who had previously been mashgiach over nigleh in Shchedrin, was appointed mashgiach over nigleh at Tomchei Tmimim Lubavitch. He retained this position until the yeshiva moved to Kremenchug in 5678, where he continued until his death in 5681.
