# Wedding of Maharash's son Raza in the Small Hall

*1873*

Maharash had stipulated when building the Small Hall (Ulam HaKatan) on the site of the former beis midrash that he could use it for any purpose he wished. At the start of winter 5634 (1873), because the Large Hall was unheated, the wedding feast of his son Raza was held in the Small Hall with klezmer music, with Maharash citing that original stipulation.
