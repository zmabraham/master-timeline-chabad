# Maggid of Mezeritch passes away on 19 Kislev

*1772*

The Maggid of Mezeritch led the Chassidic movement after the Baal Shem Tov's passing. He died on 19 Kislev 5533 (1772). Over the next eight years his students compiled his teachings into Maggid Devarav L'Yaakov, first printed in Koretz in 1781.
