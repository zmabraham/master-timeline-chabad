# R' Yehuda Leib Levin's letter on his Hebron rabbinate

*1887*

On the 4th day of Selichot 5647 (September 1887), R' Yehuda Leib Shaul Levin wrote to his family from Hebron, describing the elderly R' Shmuel Menachem Chaikin and the process by which Levin had been examined and accepted as rabbi by the city's scholars. The letter is preserved in Sinai journal volume 87.
