# Rebbe Rashab writes long letter to JCA Paris committee

*1900*

The Rebbe described the spiritual ruination caused by Mefitzei Haskalah schools and argued that JCA's true mission—aid to suffering Russian Jews—called for establishing factories and employment in the Pale of Settlement, not secular schools for religious children. He proposed redirecting the one-million-franc allocation toward economic relief, and the letter became the foundation document of a years-long lobbying campaign.
