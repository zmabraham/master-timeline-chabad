# Rebbe Rashab delivers Shavuos maamar at Rebbetzin Rivka's home

*1908*

At Rebbetzin Rivka's dining room a long table was set for the Shavuos seudah, with the Rebbe Rashab at its head. Expecting a short, accessible maamar like the prior year's 'Keshe'alah Moshe LaMarom,' students crowded in, but this time the Rebbe delivered a deep maamar continuing the themes of the two days of Yom Tov. Older bachurim teased the younger ones about whether they had managed to follow it.
