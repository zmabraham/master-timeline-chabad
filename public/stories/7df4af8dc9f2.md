# Riga yeshivah opens then closes within months

*1932*

Following the success of the Warsaw visit, Riga Chasidim mobilized to establish their own yeshivah hoping it would anchor the Rebbe in Latvia. Students came from chasidic backgrounds via Lithuanian yeshivas, but the effort was short-lived. The Evening Post reported its closure in November, and Feigin later wrote that students were unable to secure military service deferments.
