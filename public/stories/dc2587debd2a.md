# Researcher Zalman Reisen visits Warsaw library

*1935*

Librarian Liberman reported to the Rebbe that Reisen was especially interested in the Yiddish and Ivri-Taytsh divisions and was shown manuscripts and broadsides. Reisen requested permission to publish some items in YIVO's anthologies, asked to borrow others, and inquired whether the library had a room where scholars could work — leaving the visit deeply impressed by holdings he had not expected to find.
