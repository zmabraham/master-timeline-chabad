# Thirty copies of Hachaltzu printed overnight for 50th anniversary

*1948*

On Chol HaMoed Sukkos close to Hoshana Rabbah, the Rebbe told the memoirist that the Frierdiker Rebbe wished a small printing of Hachaltzu to mark its 50th anniversary on Simchas Torah. With time short, the memoirist and Bochur Tzvi Gansburg worked through Hoshana Rabbah night — in place of Tikkun — typesetting and producing thirty stapled copies, which were handed to the Rebbe the next morning.
