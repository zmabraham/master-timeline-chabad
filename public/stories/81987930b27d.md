# R' Chaim Shmuel Shmerling moves to Jaffa to found Chabad settlement

*1866*

R' Chaim Shmuel Shmerling, son of R' Moshe and grandson of R' Shimon Shmerling, moved to Jaffa in 5626 and founded the Chabad community there. His move extended the Shmerling family's role as founders of Chabad settlements - from his grandfather establishing the Hebron community to his own founding of the Jaffa community.
