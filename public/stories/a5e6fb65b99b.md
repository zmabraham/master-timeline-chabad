# Yud-Beis Tammuz farbrengen marking entry into Rayatz's 70th year

*1949*

On 14 Tammuz 5709 at 6pm, a farbrengen was held in honor of the Chag HaGeulah and Rebbe Rayatz's entry into his 70th year. The Rebbe delivered an extended sicha — over an hour long — on avodah, the ways of Chassidus, and avodas hatefillah, and recorded that he would later arrange it in writing.
