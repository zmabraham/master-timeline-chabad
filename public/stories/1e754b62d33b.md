# Soviet committee clears name of Rabbi Levi Yitzchak Schneerson

*1989*

Rabbi Levi Yitzchak Schneerson, father of the Lubavitcher Rebbe, had been arrested in 1939 by Soviet authorities in Yekaterinoslav (Dnepropetrovsk). Fifty years later, on June 28, 1989, a special commission reviewed the more than one thousand documents in his case file and cleared his name. The exoneration opened the legal path for the archive to be transferred to the family of the accused.
