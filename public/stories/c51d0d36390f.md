# Gubenko vows on radio no Chabad book will be returned

*1991*

Following the Gos-Soviet referral, Gubenko gave a radio interview asserting the collection was needed by Jews in Russia and accusing the Chabad delegation of intimidation. Kol Yisrael reached Rabbi Aharonov for a live response, who explained that Gubenko was alone in the government in opposing the return. Pravda printed against the delegation while Nezavisimaya printed in favor, and Soviet TV ran several positive segments.
