# R. Eliezer Behlul composes Mareh Einayim in Meknes

*1712*

R. Eliezer Behlul of Meknes wrote his homiletical work Mareh Einayim in 1712, which remained in manuscript form. The book incorporated novel teachings he heard from the visiting emissaries R. Moshe Yisrael and R. Nissim Avraham Hakohen, who were then traveling through Morocco on Land of Israel missions.
