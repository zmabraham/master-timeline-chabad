# Hei Tevet celebrations at Moscow yeshiva and Machon Chaya Mushka

*1991*

Hei Tevet commemorates the 1987 US court ruling that the Chabad library belongs to the movement, and the night before the diarist's celebrations the Rebbe delivered a special sicha tying that victory to the books still awaiting redemption. The diarist spent the afternoon recounting the library saga to the bochurim and the evening doing the same in Russian and in Hebrew for the local and Israeli students at the women's institute.
