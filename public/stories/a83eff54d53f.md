# Rosh Chodesh Tammuz 5693 farbrengen at R. Zalman Moshe in Ilyinka

*1933*

Having arrived in Moscow at the end of Sivan to raise funds for the Kiev yeshiva, the author joined R. Dovber Gurevitch on Rosh Chodesh Tammuz for a major farbrengen in Ilyinka. R. Yaakov Gurkov of Kharkov attended as well. R. Zalman Moshe explained that the simcha of preparation for 12 Tammuz must begin already on Rosh Chodesh.
