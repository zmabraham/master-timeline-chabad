# Rayatz prepares chronological registry of Rashab's discourses

*1921*

This was the first systematic chronological list of the maamarim of a Rebbe organized by year of delivery. The Rayatz prepared it together with a general overview of the discourses of the Rashab's forty-year nesi'us. The methodology established here became the model for later registries of other Rebbeim's maamarim.
