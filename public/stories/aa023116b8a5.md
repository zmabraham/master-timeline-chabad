# Death of R' Shimon Menashe; R' DovBer Efrat appointed rabbi of Hebron

*1893*

Rabbi Shimon Menashe of Hebron died on 29 Sivan 1893 (כ"ט סיון תרנ"ג). R' DovBer Efrat, who had already arrived in Hebron from Moscow following the 1891 expulsion of Jews from that city, was appointed to succeed him as rabbi of the Ashkenazi community in Hebron.
