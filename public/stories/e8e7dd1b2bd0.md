# Hilula farbrengen for Rebbe Rashab with Basi LeGani maamar

*1926*

Following instructions from the Rayatz, Anash held a farbrengen on 2 Nissan at the Tzemach Tzedek synagogue at 184 Henry Street to mark the yahrzeit of his father, the Rebbe Rashab. As a guest, Jacobson was honored to deliver chassidus and repeated the maamar 'Basi LeGani,' which the Rayatz had sent from Russia specifically to be studied by Anash on the yahrzeit that year. The maamar was very well received, and Anash from Brownsville subsequently began coming to Jacobson's own shul for Seudah Shlishis.
