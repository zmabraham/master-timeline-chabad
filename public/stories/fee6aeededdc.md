# Publication of Rashab Maamarim Likut Vol. 2 in New York

*1993*

In 5753 (1993), 'Sefer HaMaamarim - Likut' Volume 2 of the Chabad Rashab Rebbe Shalom Dovber Schneersohn was published in New York. The 328-page octavo edition added to the publication of the fifth Chabad Rebbe's prolific Chassidic output. It maintained the Chabad publishing effort to release the discourses of the Rebbe Rashab in systematic order.
