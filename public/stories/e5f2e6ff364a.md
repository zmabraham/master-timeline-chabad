# Torat Emet opens Jerusalem branch under R' Tzvi Pesach Frank

*1914*

Following discussions about absorbing Jerusalem avreichim into the yeshiva, Torat Emet opened a Jerusalem branch in 5674 (1913–14). The Frierdiker Rebbe's letter of 3 Kislev 5674 to R' Shlomo Zalman Havlin asked for accounts of the new branch. The rosh metivta of the Jerusalem branch was R' Tzvi Pesach Frank, who would later become one of Jerusalem's leading rabbinic authorities.
