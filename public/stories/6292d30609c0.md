# Rebbe Rayatz recalls Rosenbaum's gift of the Tzemach Tzedek's tallis

*1935*

In a letter dated 12 Tishrei 5696 (1935), published in Igros Kodesh vol. 3, the Rebbe Rayatz tells R. Dovid Shifrin that 'many years ago' R. Avraham Chaim Rosenbaum had presented his father, the Rebbe Rashab, with the Tzemach Tzedek's tallis gadol. Rosenbaum had purchased the tallis with a generous sum from the meshulach R. Madil, recognized it physically as the one in which the Rebbe had prayed, and eventually sent it to Lubavitch rather than wear it himself.
