# Rebbe Rayatz reveals Simchat Torah 5691 directive to memorize 12 chapters of Tanya

*1930*

A letter from R' Yehuda Eber in Riga reached Vitebsk in the winter of 5691 reporting that on Simchat Torah the Rebbe Rayatz had revealed a 'secret': everyone must memorize the first 12 chapters of Tanya together with the introduction. The directive spread immediately through the underground network, and chassidim and bochurim across Russia began learning Tanya baal peh.
