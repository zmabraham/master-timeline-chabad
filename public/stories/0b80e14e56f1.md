# The Rebbe travels to Paris to bring his mother Rebbetzin Chana

*1947*

While in Paris in 5707 the Rebbe also received from Mrs. Chana Uzerman — the granddaughter of R' Menachem Mendel of Paris, son of the Maharash — a mosaic-style table built by the Maharash himself. The collapsible table had passed from the Maharash to his youngest son and then to Uzerman after R' Menachem Mendel's death during WWII. The Rebbe reassembled it in his 770 yechidus room, where it has remained ever since.
