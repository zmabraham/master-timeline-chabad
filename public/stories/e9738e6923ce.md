# R' Asher of Stolin's letter on R' Mordechai of Neskhiz's new role

*1797*

In winter 5557 (1797), R' Asher of Stolin wrote a letter to R' Baruch of Mezhibuzh describing how R' Mordechai of Neskhiz had received a letter from R' Avraham of Kalisk and the rav of Shepetivka about a new fundraising effort, and had begun involving himself for the first time. The letter also shows that the dispute over the proposed separate Volhynia-Poland kollel in Tiberias was still active a year after the Alter Rebbe's mediation attempt.
