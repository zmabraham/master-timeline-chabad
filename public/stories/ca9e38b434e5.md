# Tzemach Tzedek's sons write R' Aharon Lifshitz on the clothing decree

*1845*

Tsar Nicholas I's 1845 decree forced Jews to abandon traditional garb, with a transitional tax (korobka) levied on those still wearing long coats. Because the Tzemach Tzedek was under secret-police surveillance, his sons — the future rebbes of Kopust, Liadi, and Niezhin — wrote to R' Aharon Lifshitz, dayan of Belinitch, asking how the tax collection there with the Mohilev governor's office had been handled, hoping to learn from it for Lubavitch.
