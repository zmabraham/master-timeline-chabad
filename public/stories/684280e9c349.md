# Conflict in Shklov over Karliner takeover of main synagogue

*1822*

In 1822, Simcha Keysin found 'great confusion' in Shklov when chassidim took control of a room in the city's main synagogue. The mitnagdim prepared a complaint to the high government, but R. Pinchas Schick of Shklov, a senior chassidic leader, intervened and pledged to persuade Rabbi DovBer to ease pressure on the mitnagdim. As surety, R. Pinchas handed Keysin the Rebbe's handwritten document about Maamad fund distribution.
