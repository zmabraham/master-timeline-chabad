# Chernyaev signals Gorbachev plans to release Schneersohn library to Chabad

*1991*

On Monday 21 Sivan 5751 (June 3, 1991) in Moscow, Veronika reported to the Chabad delegation that Anatoly Chernyaev had said Gorbachev would come to Moscow on June 6 to settle the library question, with books to begin transfer from the Lenin Library on June 8. Chernyaev added that this time the books would be returned even without the consent of Lenin Library director Gubinko. The delegation also negotiated a three-month trial contract with Veronika to coordinate visas, synagogue recovery, and related advocacy.
