# Bobruisk Rebbe writes about Kollel donations after R. Dovid Aharon's passing

*1912*

In a letter dated 5 Menachem Av 5672 (July/Aug 1912), the Bobruisker Rebbe instructed the local administrators to publish in HaModia news of the passing of R. Dovid Aharon and direct that those sending money for etrogim send it under the names of Shlank and Slonim. The letter was preserved in several public circulars of the time.
