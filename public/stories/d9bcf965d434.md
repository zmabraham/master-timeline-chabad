# Pereshel-Weintraub facsimile of the Frankfurt 1710 Mateh Aharon Haggadah

*1982*

In 5742 (1982), Tuvia Pereshel and Munish Weintraub published in New York a facsimile of the 1710 Frankfurt printing of the Haggadah with the 'Mateh Aharon' commentary by Aharon Teomim. The 27-leaf octavo edition came with illustrations and a bilingual Hebrew-English introduction by the publishers. It maintained their tradition of preserving rare Haggadot.
