# Exhibition on the Alter Rebbe's 200th liberation anniversary

*1999*

In the winter of 5759 (1998–99), the Lubavitch Library presented an exhibition marking the 200th anniversary of the liberation of the Alter Rebbe, Rabbi Schneur Zalman of Liadi, from Czarist imprisonment on Yud Tes Kislev. Exhibits included items associated with the Alter Rebbe, such as a first edition of his Shulchan Aruch (Kapust 5574) and the first edition of Tanya (Slavita 5557), as well as a handwritten letter.
