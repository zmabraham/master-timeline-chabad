# Poltava community appoints Adermann as kosher meat overseer

*1908*

On Monday, 29 Marcheshvan 5669, the Poltava rav Rabbi Yaakov Mordechai Bezpolov together with the community's elected committee signed an agreement with Rabbi Shemaya Adermann, who undertook to uphold the full set of regulations they enacted to safeguard the kashrus of meat in the city. The document carries the rav's stamp and the signatures of community officers including Aharon HaKohen Glezer, Chaim Zissel Chernigover and others. A puzzling secondary date of 2/11/1903 is appended at the bottom of the signatures.
