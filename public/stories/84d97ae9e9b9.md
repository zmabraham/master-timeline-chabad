# Germany invades the USSR; the bride's family flees Shumyachi

*1941*

Gershon, the same brother who had been freed from Kursk, ultimately perished with the rest of his family, while the Tomchei Tmimim bochurim who had remained in NKVD captivity — including R' Yehoshua Raskin of London — survived their sentences and went on to rebuild families in Israel. The author writes that 'the ways of Hashem are hidden from us.' His wife Henya was the sole survivor of the entire Fradkin–Paz family.
