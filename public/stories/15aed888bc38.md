# R. Yekutiel Zusman 'Zushya' Paz dies in Moscow

*1953*

R. Yekutiel Zusman Paz, the legendary shamash of the Marina-Roshcha shul in Moscow, died on 2 Cheshvan 5714 (October 11, 1953) and was buried in Malachovka. Every night after maariv he would pretend to evict homeless mispalelim so the Gentile watchman Simon would lock up and leave, then quietly readmit them through a side door. During the war he reached Samarkand, where he served as shamash on Khodzhum Street and ran an unpaid cheder.
