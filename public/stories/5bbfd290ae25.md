# New York Haggadah 'Eshei Yisrael' on Modzitz teachings by Taub family

*1947*

In 5707 (1947), the Haggadah 'Eshei Yisrael' was published in New York, featuring two commentaries: 'Divrei Yisrael' by Rebbe Yisrael Taub of Modzitz and 'Yisa Berachah' by his son Rebbe Shaul Yedidya Eliezer Taub of Modzitz. Material from Rebbe Yechezkel of Kazmir and Rebbe Shmuel Eliyahu of Zvolin was also included. The 119-page octavo edition preserved the musical-Chassidic legacy of the Modzitz dynasty in postwar America.
