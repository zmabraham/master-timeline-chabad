# Rebbe Rayatz arrives in Riga from Soviet Russia

*1927*

The Rebbe Rayatz arrived in Riga with his family, having been appointed Chief Rabbi of the city, which had enabled his exit from Russia. His arrival expanded the existing Chabad community in Riga and made it the new hub of his work on behalf of Soviet Jewry. He first stayed at the Kommerz Hotel for about a month before securing apartments.
