# Keller completes registry of Alter Rebbe's maamarim

*1992*

Building on a 1981 preliminary list appended to Maamarei Admur HaZaken — HaKetzarim, R. Yosef Yitzchak Keller completed a comprehensive registry of the Alter Rebbe's maamarim, organized by year of delivery up through the Liadi period. The completed registry was published in the journal Kerem Chabad. The success of this project led directly into preparing a parallel registry for the Tzemach Tzedek.
