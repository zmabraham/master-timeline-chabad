# Pereshel facsimile of Aharon Schreiber Erlangen's 1751 Vienna Haggadah

*1978*

In 5738 (1978), Tuvia Pereshel published in New York a facsimile of a Haggadah manuscript written in Vienna in 5511 (1751) by Aharon Schreiber Erlangen. The 32-page octavo edition included Pereshel's bilingual English-Hebrew introduction. It was part of his ongoing facsimile-of-rare-Haggadot project with Munish Weintraub.
