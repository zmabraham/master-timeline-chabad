# Birth of daughter Leah outside Moscow during wartime

*1941*

With Luftwaffe air raids forcing Muscovites into makeshift shelters and many residents fleeing the city, the author's pregnant wife went to stay with her cousin Sterke in Yegoryevsk (116 km from Moscow), beyond the range of the bombers. There their first daughter Leah was born on the night of 12 Av 5701. The author later traveled to retrieve mother and 'new face' back to their home in Cherinya.
