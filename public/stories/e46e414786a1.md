# Berlin edition of Orchot Chaim part two

*1902*

In 5662 (1902), Rabbi M. Schlesinger published part two of Orchot Chaim in Berlin. In his introduction he explained the existence of two recensions of the work, noting that in the second edition — completed after the author reached Majorca and studied under Rabbi Shem Tov Falco — Rabbi Shem Tov is cited about fifteen times in part two alone.
