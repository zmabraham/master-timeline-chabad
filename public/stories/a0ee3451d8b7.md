# Birth of Rebbe Maharash (Rabbi Shmuel) in Lubavitch

*1834*

The Maharash is the only Chabad Rebbe to have been born, lived his entire life, and passed away in Lubavitch. He would assume Chabad leadership in 5626 following the passing of his father the Tzemach Tzedek. His birth in the town further entrenched Lubavitch as the dynastic seat of the movement.
