# Engagement contract of Rashab and Shterna Sara

*1865*

In 1865, Shterna Sara visited Lubavitch from her hometown of Ovruch as a young girl. Seeing his two grandchildren standing together in his home, Rabbi Menachem Mendel (the Tzemach Tzedek) declared, 'Bride and groom!' That year, while both children were still young, their parents wrote a tena'im, the formal engagement contract that would lead to their marriage a decade later.
