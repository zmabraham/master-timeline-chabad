# Avraham Chaim attends Yom Kippur services during Smolensk imprisonment

*1889*

On Yom Kippur of 5650 (October 5, 1889), Avraham Chaim was allowed out of prison to attend services at a Smolensk synagogue. He declined, however, to accept an aliyah to the Torah, citing that the congregants were Sabbath desecrators and Yom Kippur eaters and that several of the synagogue's Torah scrolls were halachically invalid. The account implies he prayed there regularly while imprisoned but withheld participation in public honors for these reasons.
