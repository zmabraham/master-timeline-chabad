# Chelm yeshiva opens with 40 students

*1936*

The yeshiva opened with about forty students, mostly local Chelm boys with some from surrounding towns. Avraham Goldman taught the lower class and Pesachya Meller the upper class. Local balebatim provided room and board for out-of-town students, while the Otwock central yeshiva sent a monthly subsidy for staff salaries.
