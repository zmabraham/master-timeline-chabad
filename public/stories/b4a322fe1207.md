# Mimeographed Haggadah produced in occupied Nice

*1941*

During the wartime spring of 5701 (1941), with France under Nazi occupation and printing impossible, the rabbinic association of Paris (then exiled in Nice) produced a Haggadah by hand and duplicated it on a mimeograph. The Lubavitcher Rebbe and Rebbetzin were among the Jews who had taken refuge in Nice that Passover.
