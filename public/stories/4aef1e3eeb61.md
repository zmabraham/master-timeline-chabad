# Rosenbaum writes from Smolensk prison requesting an etrog for Sukkot

*1888*

Writing from the Smolensk jail on 13 Elul 5648, Reb Avraham Chaim asked the rabbi of Pleschenitz to purchase a Yanover etrog of fine appearance, a complete lulav, and three properly meshulash hadasim. He requested they reach him immediately after Yom Kippur so he could fulfill the mitzvah of the Four Species inside the prison.
