# 19 Kislev 5673 farbrengen and maamar with Rebbe Rashab

*1912*

19 Kislev 5673 fell on a Friday, with the main celebration and meal held Motzei Shabbat in the great hall. The Rebbe Rashab came at about 10 PM and delivered the famous two-hour sicha on the four levels of tzaddik, yashar, tamim, and chassid, followed by the niggun for forty minutes and the 19 Kislev maamar from the Hemshech Tarav. He spoke of being an etzem unaffected by higher or lower, and said that leaving Lubavitch (for medical travel abroad) was "like separating from life" since his whole vitality was speaking Chassidus.
