# Mitteler Rebbe taken to Vitebsk for interrogation

*1825*

Governor Nikolai Khovansky ruled the provinces of Vitebsk, Mohilev, Smolensk and Kaluga. He ordered the police to bring the Rebbe immediately after the holiday. The case opened with the Rebbe verifying that the attached documents were in his hand, and his confidants Reb Zalman Roizes and Reb Shlomo Freides were summoned for parallel questioning.
