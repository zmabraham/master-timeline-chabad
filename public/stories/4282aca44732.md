# Luach Birchas Hanehenin first printed

*1795*

This work was printed several times together with Seder Netilas Yadayim Lese'udah and likely edited in the same period. It would later be supplanted by a revised version, 'Seder Birchas Hanehenin', integrated into the Alter Rebbe's Siddur of 5563. The revised edition departed from the Magen Avraham in several places based on study of the rishonim.
