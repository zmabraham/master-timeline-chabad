# Letter of Rabbi Yisrael Dov Twersky of Vilednik to Rabbi Aharon Lifshitz

*1844*

In a letter dated Tuesday of Parashat Vayishlach 5605, the Rebbe of Vilednik acknowledges Rabbi Aharon Lifshitz's letter about a marital question. He declines to rule and directs the questioner to 'the great rabbi of Lubavitch' — the Tzemach Tzedek — instead, illustrating how Ukrainian chasidic rebbes referred difficult halakhic cases to Lubavitch.
