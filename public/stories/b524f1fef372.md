# Fenton Haggadah illustrated and designed by Zvi Fenton in London

*1988*

In 5748 (1988), 'The Fenton Haggadah' was published in London, designed and illustrated by Zvi Fenton. The 64-page quarto edition was accompanied by a separate 26-page booklet containing an English translation. It served the English-speaking Jewish community of Britain.
