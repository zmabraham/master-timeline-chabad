# Senate confirms acquittal of the Mittler Rebbe

*1827*

On 28 November 1827 (21 Kislev 5588), the ruling Senate in St. Petersburg upheld the Mohilev higher court's acquittal of Rabbi DovBer, formally closing the criminal case that had been opened against him in 1825 following Simcha Keysin's denunciations. At the time of its ruling, the Senate was unaware that the Mittler Rebbe had passed away on 9 Kislev, just twelve days earlier.
