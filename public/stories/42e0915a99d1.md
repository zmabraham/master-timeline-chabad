# Death of R. Schneur Zalman b. Yaakov Mordechai (Singer/Grossman ancestor)

*1886*

Named for the Alter Rebbe, he was likely born around 1814, married young, and remained in Mogilev when his father and brothers made aliyah in 1832, joining them only many years later. His descendants include R. Yaakov Mordechai Singer (rabbi/shochet of Rishon LeTzion) and the Grossman family of Jerusalem.
