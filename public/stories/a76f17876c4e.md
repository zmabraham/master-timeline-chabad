# Derech Mitzvosecha and early Tzemach Tzedek chasidic works printed

*1911*

Some 45 years after the Tzemach Tzedek's passing, his chasidic writings began to appear in print. The works printed between 5671 and 5678 included Derech Mitzvosecha (on mitzvos), Derech Emunah (philosophical investigation), Yahel Ohr (on Tehillim), and the first volume of Or HaTorah. The majority of his chasidic corpus — the Or HaTorah series — would only see print in the seventh generation.
