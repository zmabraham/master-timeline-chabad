# Author's second trip to Rebbe Rayatz in Otwock with six students

*1939*

On the eve of World War II, the author led a group of six of his best students from Achei Tmimim to Europe, where the Frierdiker Rebbe then resided in Otwock, Poland and the Tomchei Tmimim yeshiva was located. The trip required complex arrangements, especially convincing parents who saw the plan as extreme and dangerous. During their first yechidus, prepared for over a long spiritual period, the Rebbe told the author that if he came to America it would be solely to function as Nasi of Chabad and spread the Alter Rebbe's teachings.
