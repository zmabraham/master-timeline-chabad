# Ireland offers thousands of tons of kosher meat to the Yishuv

*1947*

After the war, the Irish government proposed gifting thousands of tons of meat — kashered on site and canned — to the Jewish settlement in the Holy Land, an undertaking requiring shochtim, menakrim, supervising rabbis, and laborers. The Union of Refugee Rabbis in Paris, headed by the author's uncle R' Chaim Leib, judged the project beyond its capacity and referred it to R' Benyamin Gorodetzky's bureau, which obtained the Rebbe Rayatz's approval to take it on. Gorodetzky drew up a roster of avreichim who had studied shechita in Pocking, the author among them, though by then his U.S. visa was already in hand.
