# Warsaw Haggadah 'Tiferet Ish' by Alter Yisrael Shimon Perlov of Novominsk

*1933*

In 5693 (1933), the Haggadah 'Tiferet Ish' by Rebbe Alter Yisrael Shimon ben Yaakov Perlov of Novominsk was published in Warsaw by the Feder Brothers. The 96-plus-24-page octavo edition included a brief manuscript work by the author on Shir HaShirim and concluded with a commentary on Chad Gadya by his ancestor Rebbe Mordechai of Chernobyl. It represented the Novominsk Chassidic dynasty's interpretive tradition.
