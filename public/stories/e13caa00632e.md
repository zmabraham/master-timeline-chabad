# Rabbi Shmuel Schneersohn writes from Nikolaev on erev Yom Kippur

*1933*

Rabbi Shmuel Schneersohn, rabbi of Nikolaev and uncle of Zelda, wrote on erev Yom Kippur 5694 to his relative Rachel. He shared that his father-in-law had passed away one week before Rosh Hashanah of that year and offered New Year blessings for her and her daughter Zelda.
