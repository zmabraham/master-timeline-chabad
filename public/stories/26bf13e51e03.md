# Bush–Yeltsin summit: Bush raises Schneersohn library; Yeltsin silent

*1992*

Around 1 Tammuz 5752 the author noted that for the two weeks since the Bush–Yeltsin summit there had been no news from Washington on the library. Reports filtered back that Bush had raised the books issue with Yeltsin during the summit but Yeltsin had not responded — apparently because he wanted to release the collection but had not been authorized internally, and rather than admit that publicly chose to stay silent. Soon after, a fax from Devorah Rochel reported fresh movement on the library issue and urged immediate return to Moscow.
