# Facsimile of the Venice 1609 Italian-Hebrew Haggadah published in Jerusalem

*1974*

In 5734 (1974), a facsimile of the 1609 Venice Haggadah was issued in Jerusalem, vocalized and featuring an Italian translation rendered in Hebrew letters, with an introduction by Professor Bezalel Narkiss. The reproduction was made from a special copy printed on parchment in honor of Gershon Printz. The 70-page folio was titled 'Ma'aleh Bet Chorin'.
