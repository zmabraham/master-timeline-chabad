# R. Yosef Yozik Horowitz settles in Warsaw after Moscow expulsion

*1892*

R. Yosef Yozik Horowitz, son-in-law of R. Shneur Zalman Shmotkin, had originally lived in Dokshitz and then Moscow. When the Tsarist government expelled the Jews of Moscow in 5652-5653 (1892-1893), he resettled in Warsaw, becoming one of the earliest Chabad chassidim to establish himself there. He would later head Chabad activity in the city until his death.
