# Marriage of R. Yosef ben Yitzchak ibn Attar

*1734*

On 8 Tishri 5495 (~October 5, 1734), Yosef ben Yitzchak ibn Attar was married. The Ya'abetz's marriage register in Fez recorded the event. Like his brother Yom Tov who married three years earlier, Yosef was a grandnephew of the late Rabbi Yehuda ibn Attar through Yehuda's brother Yosef.
