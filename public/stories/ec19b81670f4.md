# Shluchim visit Gan Yisrael summer camp in Moscow

*1991*

Shluchim including R' Gurevich, R' Friedman, and R' Gerlik addressed the camp in Russian, while R' Moshe Kotlarsky spoke in English. R' Y. Meshulvin conveyed regards from the Rebbe and brought a video of the children's rally to show the campers. The child Moshe Pesach Finkel concluded by publicly thanking the Rebbe in fluent English for sending the shluchim.
