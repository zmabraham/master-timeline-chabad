# Author becomes director of Yeshivas Toras Emes in Boro Park

*1936*

He brought in R' Yosef Dov Castel as Rosh Mesivta of the upper division, R' Yissachar Frankel for the Gemara class, and R' Nachman Mandel as a teacher. The yeshiva's spiritual standard rose substantially and Brownsville parents sent their sons there. However, Anash failed to engage with the yeshiva's leadership, the finances deteriorated under fundraiser R' Baruch Karf, and the author eventually withdrew when salaries went unpaid.
