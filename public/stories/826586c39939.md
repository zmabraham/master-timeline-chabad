# Founding of Yeshivat Tzemach Tzedek in Jerusalem

*1920*

Following WWI, the managers of Kolel Chabad established Yeshivat Tzemach Tzedek in the Chabad synagogue in Jerusalem's Old City in 5680, with Yeshivat Midrash Shmuel founded subsequently. By this period most of the Chabad community had relocated to the New City and prayed primarily at the 'Ohel Yitzchak' synagogue. R' Avraham Zalman remained living inside the Old City synagogue throughout the war years.
