# Levin learns of Rayatz's passing en route to Bnei Brak

*1950*

Levin had been working at the matzah bakery in Pardes Katz under Rabbi Yaakov Landa's supervision during late winter 5710. On 11 Shevat, while traveling to Bnei Brak, he saw a newspaper at the central bus station with the news of the Rayatz's passing the day before. He arrived at work, asked someone to perform kriah for him, and sat on the floor in mourning.
