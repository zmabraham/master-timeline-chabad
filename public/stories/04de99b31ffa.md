# 'Kol Korei' published describing the new yeshiva building

*1927*

In Elul 5687 (August–September 1927) Tomchei Tmimim Warsaw issued a printed Kol Korei, describing for donors and the public the spacious accommodations at Mlawska 7 / Sapuzinski 15/17 — six classrooms, a large kitchen, prepared beds and orderly conduct in a dedicated three-story building. A photograph of the document later appeared in Beton Chabad issue 34.
