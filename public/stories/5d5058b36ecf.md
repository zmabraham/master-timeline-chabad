# Mohilev High Court acquits the Mitteler Rebbe

*1826*

Inquiries in Kapust, Orsha, Shklov and Mohilev had confirmed that chassidim had voluntarily invited the Rebbe to return from Kremenchug and pledged to support him. The court concluded that Ma'amad contributions were voluntary gifts to a non-salaried rabbi, not communal taxes, and so did not violate the 1805 Jewish charter. The verdict was sent to the prosecutor general and then to Governor Khovansky for confirmation.
