# R' Shneur Zalman Slonim dies in Yafo

*1936*

He had built the Chabad community in Yafo from the ground up: founding the women's mikveh, organizing shechita, building a Chabad shul in Neve Shalom, delivering weekly chassidus, and serving on the Tel Aviv Chief Rabbinate until his death. His 37-year tenure shaped Chabad presence in the city.
