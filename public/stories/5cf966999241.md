# House search and arrest threat force Rayatz to leave Rostov

*1924*

On 11 Nissan 5684, authorities conducted a search of the Rayatz's Rostov home and prepared to arrest him. The matter was resolved by an agreement that he would avoid imprisonment if he left Rostov for another city. The Rayatz and his family relocated to Leningrad shortly thereafter, and the Bratsky Pereulok house passed to local tenants, ending nearly a decade as the center of Chabad.
