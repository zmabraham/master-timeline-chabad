# Ancient Moroccan ketubah

*1659*

A second ancient ketubah in the Lubavitcher archive, discovered inside the binding of an old library book, is written on parchment in square and Sephardic Rashi script with decorative ornamentation. Dated 8 Cheshvan 5420 (with the place name partially damaged but appearing to be in Morocco), it records the wedding of a groom named Avraham to the maiden Esther bat Massoud ibn Attiyah.
