# Khovansky orders Mohilev prosecutor to oversee Rebbe's case

*1826*

Building on his earlier reports about the disorder at the Babinovich peace court, Khovansky directly instructed the provincial General Prosecutor to ensure the proposals issued to Mohilev province were carried out. The order emphasized swift execution of the Babinovich court's requests, and parallel cooperation from the lower court of Mohilev province, all to be done promptly and without delay.
