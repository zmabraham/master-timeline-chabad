# Agreement uniting Kollel Chabad with other Jerusalem kollels signed

*1872*

After the earlier 5628 (1868) understanding failed to specify each kollel's share, leaders of all Ashkenazi kollels in the Holy Land negotiated a detailed pact dividing donations from Russia and Siberia—four parts to Perushim and Volhynia and one-fifth to Chabad. The document established joint signatories on receipts, banned unilateral fundraising letters, and required all monies to pass through four designated rabbis. It was signed for Kollel Chabad by Shimon Menashe Chaikin, Levi Yitzchak Slonim, Shneur Zalman Pundiminsky, and Yehuda Leib Slonim, all based in Hebron.
