# Rebbe defends Mivtza Tefillin at Shabbat Bereishit 5728 farbrengen

*1967*

After receiving numerous queries and challenges to Mivtza Tefillin during the summer, the Rebbe devoted his Shabbat Bereishit 5728 farbrengen (Oct 28, 1967) to addressing them publicly. The edited sicha was printed as its own kuntres opening 'בהיות שהגיעו אלי כמה שאלות וטענות בנוגע לההתעוררות דמבצע תפלין' and was later reprinted in Likkutei Sichot vol. 6 p. 271ff.
