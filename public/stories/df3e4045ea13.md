# Arrest of Yeshiva Rosh R' Yitzchak Kopelman

*1946*

Once Polish-Jewish refugees emptied out of Samarkand in summer 1946, the atmosphere grew colder for those who remained. The famed Rosh Yeshiva R' Yitzchak Kopelman was arrested in broad daylight, and the memoirist's uncle R' Chaim Leib Zaks, rabbi of Uzda, was called for interrogation. R' Chaim Stein of Telshe, who had married into the Zaks family, fled by truck to Tashkent in the dead of night after being inquired about.
