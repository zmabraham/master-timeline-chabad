# Rabbi Borochov. Rabbi Yonatan Borochov (born 5742, 19

*1982*

Rabbi Borochov. Rabbi Yonatan Borochov (born 5742, 1982) is the rabbi of the Bukharian community of Rehovot and the 'Sha'arei Matityahu' synagogue, head of the 'Ateret Shalom' kollel, rabbi of 'Pisgot' College, and author of the halachic book series 'Ma'archei HaMishpat'
