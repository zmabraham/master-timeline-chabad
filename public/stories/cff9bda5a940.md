# Russian raid to seize Schneerson books from Lenin Library blocked by protest

*1992*

On Friday 10 Adar I, deputy PM Shakhin and Zaitinov convened a closed meeting with Veronika, Moscow Soviet representative Natalia, library leadership and police to organize an immediate extraction of the books to government offices on the Old Square. Director Philipov managed to delay the unit at shift change, and twenty minutes later news arrived that a hundred-strong protest had formed outside the library, halting the operation.
