# Lubavitch library packed and sent to storage in Moscow

*1915*

With the front of World War I advancing, the Rashab left Lubavitch carrying only his most-used books and the manuscript collection, sending the printed seforim to Moscow for storage. The crates were deposited at the warehouses of the wealthy chassid R. Zalkind Persitz. They would never return to family control: the warehouses were nationalized and the books transferred to state libraries, beginning the decades-long struggle to recover them.
