# Passing of R. Ephraim Shmuel Levin

*1933*

On 15 Shevat 5693 R. Ephraim Shmuel Levin passed away, leaving his wife Leah Beila a widow with three daughters and two sons. The author's brother Yosef and sister Sara Yehudit were studying in Kharkov to become a doctor and nurse, while the family pressed the author to abandon yeshiva study and find work. This bereavement and family pressure would later push the author to write to the Rebbe Rayatz for guidance and support.
