# Rabbi Shmuel becomes Rebbe Maharash

*1866*

In 1866, following the passing of his father Rabbi Menachem Mendel (the Tzemach Tzedek), Rabbi Shmuel became the fourth Lubavitcher Rebbe in Lubavitch. His brothers established rival Chabad courts in nearby towns: Rabbi Yehuda Leib in Kopust, Rabbi Chaim Schneur Zalman in Liadi, and Rabbi Yisrael Noach in Nizhyn. For the next half a century, Chabad Chasidim would be divided between these different Chabad Rebbes.
