# Leipzig facsimile of the Darmstadt Haggadah manuscript

*1927*

In 5687 (1927) and 5688 (1928), a facsimile of the Darmstadt Haggadah manuscript was issued in Leipzig in two volumes. The text-volume — featuring introduction and German commentary by Bruno Italianer — was 313 pages, while the facsimile-volume issued the following year reproduced 56 leaves of the original manuscript. Together they constituted one of the major early 20th-century scholarly publications of an illuminated medieval Haggadah.
