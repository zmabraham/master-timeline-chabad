# Faigin reports on Rebbe's Riga visit to Jacobson

*1933*

Faigin reported that the chassidic community in Riga had grown with newly arrived refugees from Russia, including R. Shlomo Tumarkin, Michael Dvorkin, and R. Rafael Cohen. They had established a shul named for the Rebbe with daily Gemara and Chassidus shiurim, plus chadarim for children and adults.
