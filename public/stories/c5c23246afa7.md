# Passing of Rabbi Avraham Eliyahu Axelrod in Baltimore

*1952*

On 4 Adar 5712 the famed chassid Rabbi Avraham Eliyahu Axelrod passed away in Baltimore. He had studied at Tomchei Temimim from 5666 to 5681, married and lived in Zembin from 5681 to 5684, and then emigrated to Baltimore where he served as a rabbi for the remaining decades of his life. His correspondence with the secretariat of Rebbe Rayatz preserves important historical material from the Rostov, Riga, and Otwock periods.
