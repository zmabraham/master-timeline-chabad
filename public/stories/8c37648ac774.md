# Romen Tomchei Tmimim shut down just as the author arrives

*1928*

The Yevsektsiya was systematically destroying the remaining small yeshivos by arresting or hounding the magidei shiur, since the young bochurim themselves could not be jailed. Romen, a major chassidic centre, still hosted a functioning Tomchei Tmimim, so the author travelled there with Rabbi Benyaminson, who was also exploring a rabbinic post in the town. They arrived to find the yeshiva already dispersed, and the author returned to Zlobin to continue learning with Rabbi Benyaminson through the summer.
