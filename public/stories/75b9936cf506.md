# Rabbis Cunin and Kogan fly to Madrid to confront Gubenko

*1991*

After Veronika Irina-Kogan reached Gubenko by phone in his Madrid hotel and Yitzchak Kogan continued the conversation with Lubimov, the delegation decided that Cunin and Kogan should fly out immediately to meet them face to face. They wrote to the Rebbe describing the plan as 'l'chatchila aribber' and received the reply 'Azkir al hatziyun.' In Madrid they laid tefillin with Lubimov, who tried to persuade Gubenko to authorize the warehouse to begin packing the books in honor of Yud-Aleph Nissan.
