# Facsimile of the first printed Haggadah from Guadalajara published in Jerusalem

*1961*

In 5721 (1961), a facsimile edition of the earliest printed Haggadah, originally produced around 1482 in Guadalajara (Wadi al-Hagara), was published in Jerusalem. The 12-leaf folio reproduction was made from the unique copy preserved in the National Library of Israel's Jewish National and University Library. It appeared as an appendix to the 'Bibliography of Passover Haggadot'.
