# Rabbi Kirshenbaum (left) with Rabbi Baruch Blizinski (5784)

*1981*

Rabbi Kirshenbaum (left) with Rabbi Baruch Blizinski (5784) - Rabbi Avraham Eliyahu Kirshenbaum (born 22 Adar I 5741, February 26, 1981) is a chassidic author and Torah editor, one of the prominent students of Rabbi Yoel Kahan and editor of commentaries
