# Soviet authorities impose a kehilla council on Homel

*1923*

As anti-religious decrees mounted — synagogues being seized through forced signature campaigns and mikvaot closed under hygienic pretexts — the government ordered the Jews of Homel to establish a community council. R' Shmuel Yitzchak Luria was elected chairman and worked closely with the younger tmimim, defying the protests of older communal figures. Through their efforts a new mikveh was built, and the Rebbe Rayatz sent Luria a long letter pledging support from his vast network of institutions despite carrying personal debts of tens of thousands of dollars.
