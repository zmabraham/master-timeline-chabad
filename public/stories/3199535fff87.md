# Passing of the Alter Rebbe

*1813*

The Alter Rebbe, Rabbi Schneur Zalman of Liadi, passed away on 24 Tevet 5573 (early January 1813 Gregorian) after fleeing Liadi during the Napoleonic war. His son, the Mitteler Rebbe, assumed leadership of Chabad and resettled the family in Lubavitch. A special ma'amad fund of 35,000 rubles was organized in the year following his passing to rebuild the rebbe's household.
