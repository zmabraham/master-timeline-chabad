# Tzemach Tzedek responsum delivered by his son Maharin

*1862*

From around 5620 onward, chassidim brought their questions through attendants who relayed them to the bedridden Tzemach Tzedek. He appointed his son R. Yisrael Noach (Maharin) to answer most halachic queries, occasionally forwarding harder ones to his father. Orach Chaim §85 (5622) is one of the late responsa explicitly mediated by Maharin.
