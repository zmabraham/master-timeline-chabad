# Rebbe Menachem Mendel Schneerson urges Hendel Deutsch to search Russian archives

*1976*

In 5736 (1976), R. Hendel Deutsch, a descendant of Chabad chassidim engaged in Russian historical research in Leningrad, visited New York. In a private audience, the Lubavitcher Rebbe proposed that he search for documents connected to Lubavitch in Soviet archives. Over the next decade Deutsch occasionally sent material to the Rebbe via his nephew R. Chaim Yitzchak Kugel.
