# Justinian Rambam printed in Venice (pirated edition)

*1550*

In 5310 (1550), as Bragadin began printing his edition of Rambam in Venice, the rival printer Justinian copied Maharam of Padua's glosses without authorization and rushed out his own edition at a lower price. The dispute became one of the most consequential printing controversies of the era and prompted the Rema's cherem against purchasing the pirated edition.
