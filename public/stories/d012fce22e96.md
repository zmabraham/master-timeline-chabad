# Sefer Ohr HaMeir presented to the Rebbe at his wedding

*1928*

At the Rebbe's wedding in Warsaw on 14 Kislev 5689, Rabbi Yehuda Meir Shapiro — founder of Yeshivas Chachmei Lublin and originator of the Daf Yomi — presented his responsa work Ohr HaMeir as a drasha gift. He inscribed it: 'A drasha gift to the choicest of vines, sons of holy supernal fathers, on the wedding day, 3 Vayishlach 5689, in Warsaw.' The Rebbe Rayatz directed his son-in-law to engage Rabbi Shapiro in Torah discussion at the wedding.
