# Rayatz writes Nimoitin that Riga study circle plan still not realized

*1930*

On 14 Kislev 5691, the Rebbe Rayatz wrote again to R' Yehuda Leib Nimoitin, noting that his wish for the good of the students and for raising the level of Torah knowledge had still not come to fruition. The plan to establish a Riga study circle was still encountering difficulties.
