# R' Chaim Chaikel of Amdura passes away

*1787*

In the winter of 5547 (1786/87) R' Chaim Chaikel of Amdura, one of the disciples of the Maggid of Mezritch active in Lithuania, passed away. His son and successor R' Shmuel, then twenty years old, began traveling to the Alter Rebbe to receive Torah from him.
