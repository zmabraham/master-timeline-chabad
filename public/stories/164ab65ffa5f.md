# Moshe Binyamin Kaplan flees Russia in Great Escape

*1946*

From the DP camp Kaplan wrote to the Rebbe in Brooklyn wondering what bond he could have, having never met him. The Rebbe replied that he remembered him very well and that the bond was through learning each other's Torah. Years later Rabbi Shalom Ber Levin found among the Rebbe's possessions a 1935 photo of Kaplan, taken when he was eighteen and sent secretly from Russia to Poland.
