# Baal Shem Tov passes away on Shavuot

*1760*

Rabbi Israel Baal Shem Tov founded the Chassidic movement and gathered a circle of disciples who carried his teachings forward. He passed away on Shavuot 5520 (1760). Over the next two generations his students collected the stories about him, eventually published as Shivchei HaBaal Shem Tov.
