# Yeshivat Magen Avot founded in Hebron

*1889*

Yeshivat Magen Avot, a Chabad yeshiva in Hebron, was established in 5649. It functioned in Hebron until Yeshivat Torat Emet was founded in 5672. The Rebbe Rashab took particular interest in Magen Avot and reorganized it in 5663 in an effort to deepen Chassidic learning in the Holy Land.
