# Crispo dedicates 'Shiyarei Knesset HaGedolah' to the Yaavetz

*1722*

On 25 Tammuz 5482 (July 1722), Rabbi Yom Tov Crispo — a Jerusalem emissary returning from Morocco — purchased the volume 'Shiyarei Knesset HaGedolah' in Alexandria and inscribed it as a gift to the Yaavetz. Earlier letters from Crispo (7 and 12 Marcheshvan 5482) record that he had also sent two other rare volumes, 'She'elot u'Teshuvot Bach' and 'Ginat Veradim,' at the Yaavetz's request. Several of these volumes are today held in the Library of Agudat Chasidei Chabad.
