# R' Yosef Goldstein joins Tomchei Tmimim Warsaw at age 14

*1922*

Goldstein had been studying at Yeshivat Toras Chaim when he and several other boys decided to transfer to Chabad. Because he was only 14 the yeshiva refused him at first, so he arranged private study with R' Avraham Ziskind and R' Moshe Yehuda Reichman until he was gradually absorbed into the yeshiva. Shmuel Zalmanov served as his mentor; the total enrollment at that point was 15 students.
