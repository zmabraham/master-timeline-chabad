# R. Levi Yitzchak Schneerson appointed Rabbi of Yekaterinoslav

*1909*

In 5669 (1908–1909), Rabbi Levi Yitzchak Schneerson, father of the Rebbe, was accepted as Rabbi of Yekaterinoslav. The Rebbe, born in Nikolayev, was seven years old at the time of the move. In subsequent years he would travel back to Nikolayev at the end of each summer to stay several weeks with his maternal grandparents.
