# Underground Malakhovka farbrengen reorganizes bochurim's nigleh learning

*1934*

In the summer of 5694 (1934) a chassidic farbrengen was held in the Malakhovka shul where it was decided that R. Refael Cohen of Nevel would begin teaching the bochurim nigleh. R. Pinchas Schreiber (Pinye Rakshiker) regularly taught them Hemshech 5666, while R. Elchonon Morozov occasionally taught a maamar. The group included future Lubavitcher figures like R. Shlomo Matusof, R. Meir Zarchi, R. Michoel Teitelbaum and R. Mendel Morozov, who later joined from Malakhovka.
