# Mohilev court confirms Lubavitch community's power of attorney

*1822*

The power of attorney from the Chasidic community of the Rebbe of Lubavitch, representing the brotherhood of Jewish residents of Mohilev, was presented and entered into the register of the Mohilev peace court as number 51. Signed by Mohilev residents including Litman Avramovich Makrievich, Leib Pevzner, Chaim Shimenovich Bichavski, and Shilem Movshovich Shainin, it was certified by the court with the government seal and witnessed by the mayor and councilmen.
