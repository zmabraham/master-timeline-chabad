# Tomchei Tmimim Warsaw relocates to Franciscan St. 8

*1922*

Between 5683 and 5685 (approx. 1922–1925) the yeshiva relocated within Warsaw to a roomier beit midrash at Franciscan Street 8. The move reflected steady growth in student numbers as the institution's reputation spread across Poland and neighboring countries. It remained at this address until the next, larger move in 5686.
