# OVIR apologizes to Chabad delegation for visa incident

*1990*

On 7 Tevet 5751 Veronika met with OVIR officials who apologized for the Friday hotel incident, blaming their ignorance of the delegation's diplomatic status. The same day R. Kogan met with Mossoviet officials about the Polyakov Synagogue and Chabad's building needs, hearing that a positive decision was pending final approval. The delegation also began coordinating to send the rescued books out through the upcoming Chernobyl children's charter flight to Israel.
