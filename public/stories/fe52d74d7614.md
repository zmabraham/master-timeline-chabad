# Israel Yehuda Leib Levin's family arrives in Samarkand

*1941*

After fleeing wartime Russia, the Levin family reached Samarkand in Kislev 5702 (late 1941). They found the city packed with newly released Polish deportees, swept by disease and starvation, with people collapsing dead in the streets daily. There was no place to settle and medicines were nearly impossible to obtain.
