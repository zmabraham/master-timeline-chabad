# The court ruling, Rabbi David Nachshon (center) with the Prime Minister of Israel

*1948*

Rabbi David Nachshon (center) with the Prime Minister of Israel Mr. Benjamin Netanyahu (left) by the Rebbe at the dollar distribution. Rabbi Yisrael David Nachshon (born on 2 Menachem Av 5708, 1948) is chairman of the Chabad mobile units for spreading Judaism in the Holy Land
