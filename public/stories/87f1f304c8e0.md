# Author crosses the Russian border, leaving the Soviet Union

*1946*

After the war an organised effort smuggled Lubavitcher chassidim out of the Soviet Union via Poland. The author crossed the border on 15 Av 5706 and reached America on 18 Adar Sheni 5708. He contrasts the eighteen-month exodus with the twelve-hour Moscow-to-New-York flights available to Soviet Jews leaving in 5752.
