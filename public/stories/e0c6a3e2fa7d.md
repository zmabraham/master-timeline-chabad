# Tomchei Tmimim Yeshiva founded in Warsaw

*1921*

The founders included R. Dovber Moshe Shmotkin, R. Shraga Feivish Zalmanov, and R. Shneur Zalman Shmotkin. The yeshiva opened at the Lubavitch beis midrash at Franciszkańska 30 after Pesach, with R. Baruch Friedman of Russia soon appointed as mashpia. The founding effectively transferred the center of the Tomchei Tmimim network from Soviet Russia to Poland.
