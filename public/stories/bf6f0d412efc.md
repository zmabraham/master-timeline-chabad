# Tomchei Temimim Warsaw opens 5692 school year with 120 students

*1931*

At the start of the 5692 school year, Tomchei Temimim Warsaw enrolled approximately 120 students. The faculty included R. Yehuda Eber as rosh yeshiva, R. David Teiblum as r"m, R. Baruch Friedman as mashpia, R. Shmuel Zalmanov as administrator, and R. Eliyahu Moshe Liss and R. Meir Glazman as Chassidus instructors.
