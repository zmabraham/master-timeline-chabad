# Tomchei Tmimim branch founded in Otwock

*1926*

Founded by R. Meir Blizinski shortly after his wedding, the Otwock branch opened after Pesach 5686 with three classes meeting at Beis Medrash Rindorf. It served as a feeder yeshiva for the central Tomchei Tmimim in Warsaw, but lasted only one year before closing in spring 5687 (1927) due to funding cuts.
