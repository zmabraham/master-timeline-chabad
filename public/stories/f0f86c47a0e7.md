# Montefiore census of Tzfat after the disasters

*1839*

On his visit to Tzfat at Erev Shavuot 5599, Montefiore registered the surviving Jewish residents after the earthquake and pogroms. R' Uri of Slonim, a Chabad chasid, was registered with the Perushim kollel since there was no Chabad kollel in Tzfat at the time. He signed a contemporaneous letter from the remaining Perushim community as 'Uri Chabad.'
