# Yaavetz elevated to dayan of Fez after Rabbi Vidal Tzarfati's death

*1704*

After Rabbi Vidal Tzarfati's passing in 5464 (1704), Rabbi Yaakov ibn Tzur was appointed to fill his seat as dayan on the Fez rabbinical court. He served alongside his teacher Rabbi Yehuda ibn Attar (the Maharibav) and Rabbi Shmuel Tzarfati, marking his ascension to the senior tier of Moroccan poskim. He would later author the influential responsa work Mishpat u'Tzedakah B'Yaakov.
