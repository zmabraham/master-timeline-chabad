# Kol Aryeh published in Jerusalem

*1866*

While serving as a Kollel Chabad emissary to the eastern lands, R' Shneur Zalman ben R' Menachem Mendel Mendelovitch obtained a major donation from the Sassoon family in Bombay for the Chabad synagogue in Jerusalem. He documented the trip in the introduction to his book Kol Aryeh, published in Jerusalem in 5626.
