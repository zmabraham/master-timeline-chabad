# Banderite raiders attempt to attack Eshelon No. 3 in western Ukraine

*1946*

Rumors said the train drivers cooperated with Banderite bandits, halting at forests so that the cars could be looted; Eshelon No. 2 had already been robbed in this way. R. Michoel Teitelbaum bolted the door from inside and beat off the attackers' fingers with empty bottles until dawn broke and the train moved on. The car had no windows because it was a freight and livestock wagon.
