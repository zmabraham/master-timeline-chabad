# Report on Shabbos Mevarchim Av and tamim Zelig Glass tour

*1940*

R. Yosef Genkin wrote that the tamim Zelig Glass had spent Shabbos Mevarchim Av in Vizun, delivering the maamar 'Ma Tovu' before three minyanim. The tamim Glass also spent four Shabbosos in Autian and visited the Amshinover Rebbe's shtibel in Kovno. R. Yehoshua Isaac Baruch's brother-in-law R. Moshe Yosef Gottlieb meanwhile delivered Chassidus in Kovno every Shabbos.
