# Exhibition of the Rebbeim's books

*2002*

During the year 5762 (2001–02), the library displayed first editions and other significant printings of works authored by the Chabad Rebbes. The exhibit included items such as the first edition of Maggid Devarav LeYaakov (Koretz 5541), Imrei Binah (Kapust 5581) and Tzemach Tzedek (Vilna 5631). It was the ninth themed exhibit since the hall opened in 5754.
