# Large public Chanukah menorah lighting in central Moscow

*1990*

During Chanukah 5751 Rabbi Moshe Chaim Levin and Veronika arranged a large public menorah on a Moscow square. After protests over a Manezh Square site near monasteries, the menorah was relocated near the Hammer Center on the riverfront. Busloads of children from the Chabad school, the Chernobyl children's camp, and the Sunday school attended with dancing, a play, and the twelve pesukim, and Soviet television gave the event wide coverage.
