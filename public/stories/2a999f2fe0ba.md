# Havlin and Rivkin travel to USA to fundraise for Torat Emet

*1928*

In Nisan 5688 (April 1928), R' Shlomo Zalman Havlin and R' Moshe Dovber Rivkin departed the Holy Land for the United States. They stopped first in Riga to consult with the Frierdiker Rebbe on the details of their mission. From then on Havlin spent extensive time in America raising funds for the yeshiva's deficit, while Rivkin remained in the US and joined Yeshivat Torah Vodaas as a rosh metivta.
