# R' Noach Moshe records debt for Likkutei Torah purchase in Lubavitch

*1858*

The new Likkutei Torah of the Tzemach Tzedek had become a prized acquisition for chasidic communities. R' Noach Moshe, while visiting Lubavitch, bought a copy on behalf of the Lubavitcher shul of Dokshitz, signing a debt note to be paid 'when the seller is in Dokshitz.' The shtar is a small witness to early Likkutei Torah distribution.
