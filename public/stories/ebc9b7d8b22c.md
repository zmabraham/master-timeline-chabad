# Engagement of Rayatz to Nechama Dina in Bolivke

*1896*

The Rashab and his wife had chosen Nechama Dina over wealthier candidates with large dowries, following the Rayatz's preference for marrying a relative. The tena'im was signed on 28 Sivan 5646 in Bolivke. The Rebbe Rashab undertook all wedding expenses since the bride's family had limited means, and the celebration lasted a week.
