# R' Avraham of Kalisk on the troubles from separating Volhynia and Russia

*1805*

In 5565 (1805), R' Avraham of Kalisk wrote that 'from the day the people of Volhynia were separated from the people of Russia, all the problems came out into the open, and great damage came from this.' Nevertheless, although collections in Europe remained organized separately by region, when the funds reached Eretz Yisrael they all passed through his hands. The letter is preserved in Yagdil Torah.
