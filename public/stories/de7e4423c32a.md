# R. Avraham David Wahrman begins rabbinate in Yazlovich

*1790*

Rabbi Avraham David of Buchach, a leading Galician halachist, started his rabbinic career in Yazlovich in 1790. He served there for twenty-four years, during which time he recorded daily every new Torah insight that came to him. Across his full fifty-year rabbinate this discipline produced tens of thousands of pages of chiddushim on the four parts of Shulchan Aruch, Tanach, and Talmud.
