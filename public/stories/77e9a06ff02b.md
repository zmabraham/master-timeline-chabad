# Frumkin describes plight of Hebron's Jews in Chabatzelet

*1876*

The editor of Chabatzelet, Yisrael Dov Frumkin, described how Hebron's Jews lived in conditions worse than Jews of other Palestinian cities. He depicted them as outnumbered by Turks of low moral standing, with weak local government and constant harassment — a Jew dared not even respond to insults lest a mob accuse him of insulting the religion and beat him severely.
