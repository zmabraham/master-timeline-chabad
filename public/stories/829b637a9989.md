# Rabbinical Conference in Korosten

*1926*

From 26 to 28 October 1926 (18-20 Cheshvan 5687), seventy-two Soviet-era rabbis convened in Korosten with about thirty additional unofficial participants — 101 men in all are numbered in the surviving group photograph. The official list of delegates includes communal rabbis from Malin, Korosten, Zhitomir, Ovruch, Berdichev and other towns, with guests from Kiev, Leningrad, Odessa, Vinnitsa and elsewhere; the photograph was first published by the author in 1989 in 'Toldot Chabad B'Russia HaSovietit.'
