# Rayatz's third arrest in Poalei Tzion conflict

*1906*

In 1906, Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn was arrested following a confrontation with Poalei Tzion, a Labor Zionist organization active in Lubavitch. The clash reflected the broader ideological battles between traditional Chabad Chasidism and emerging Jewish political movements in early twentieth-century Russia. This was one of multiple arrests he endured under czarist rule.
