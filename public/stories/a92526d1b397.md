# Rebbe Rashab elected delegate from two Russian provinces

*1909*

Departing from his usual practice, the Rebbe Rashab personally undertook campaign travel to the cities of Mogilev (whose province included Lubavitch) and Chernigov to ensure that pious rabbis were selected as delegates to the Petersburg conference. He stood as a candidate himself and was elected from both provinces, an event later recorded in Zikaron LiVnei Yisrael as 'the campaign' he undertook. He acknowledged the burden weighed heavily upon him but felt he could not refuse.
