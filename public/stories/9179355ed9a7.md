# Press conference on Schneersohn books with Shakhrai aide

*1992*

At a press conference held by Shakhrai, an aide to Yeltsin and head of the State Legal Office, Kogan and Pirtsova asked publicly about the justice of not returning the books despite a clear court ruling. Shakhrai said he knew of the matter though not closely, invited them to approach him after the press conference, and requested that they bring related documents the next day for review and possible help. Rabbi Cunin was meanwhile preparing a memorandum on the matter.
