# 19 Kislev farbrengen 5675 in Lubavitch

*1914*

The annual 19 Kislev celebration in 5675 took place in the great hall at Lubavitch. The Rebbe Rashab discussed matters unfolding in the World of Truth; R' Moshe of Zembin asked 'what will become of us?' and the Rebbe responded that one should hold tight to his gartel. Wartime scarcity forced the gathering to make do with unfamiliar liquors, and the Rebbe ended the farbrengen when his wife came to ask that they cease searching for more.
