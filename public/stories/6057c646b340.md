# Mordechai Perl delivers Ruzhin family letters to the Lubavitcher Rebbe

*1991*

In 5751 (1991), Mordechai Perl carried from Moscow to the Lubavitcher Rebbe a letter of Rabbi Yisrael of Ruzhin together with letters from his sons and grandsons — the rebbes of Chortkov, Sadigura, Husiatyn, and Mezhibuzh. Perl had inherited the letters from his father Yitzchak Dov Muterperel and grandfather Rabbi Asher Zelig Muterperel, rabbi of Michelfaliye.
