# Tanya reprinted in Shklov by the Alter Rebbe's sons with new sections

*1814*

This 5574 (1814) Shklov edition, prepared by the author's sons after his death, established the canonical five-part structure—Tanya, Shaar HaYichud VehaEmuna, Igeret HaTeshuva, Igeret HaKodesh, and Kuntres Acharon—that all subsequent Tanya editions would follow.
