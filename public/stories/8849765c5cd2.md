# Rebbe Rayyatz reaffirms Kollel Chabad is for Torah scholars only

*1922*

Echoing his father the Rashab's earlier letter, the Rayyatz declared that he could not extend his hand to anyone who wished to maintain the kollel for the sake of general yishuv-support. The kollel was created for those sitting in Torah, he insisted, and they were its essential beneficiaries. This continued a multi-decade debate over the kollel's purpose.
