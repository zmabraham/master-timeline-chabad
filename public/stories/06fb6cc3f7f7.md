# Rabbi Yechiel Michel Rapoport. Rabbi Yechiel Michel Rapoport (5674

*1914*

Rabbi Yechiel Michel Rapoport. Rabbi Yechiel Michel Rapoport (5674, 1914 – 2 Iyar 5743, 1983) was among the students of Tomchei Tmimim Yeshiva of Lubavitch, and for several years director of the Tomchei Tmimim branch in Kursk
