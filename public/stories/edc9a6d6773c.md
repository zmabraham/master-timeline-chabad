# R' Aharon of Tiveria recommends Alter Rebbe to Russian chassidim

*1783*

In 5543 (1783) R' Aharon HaGadol of Tiveria sent a letter to the chassidim of White Russia, advising them to attach themselves to R' Schneur Zalman of Horodok as 'unique in his generation among you in wisdom and chassidut.' This is the earliest documented signal of the Alter Rebbe's emerging role as exclusive leader of Russian chassidim.
