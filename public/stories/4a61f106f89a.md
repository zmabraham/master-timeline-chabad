# Chatzkevich leads Lithuanian delegation to the Rebbe

*1930*

In Kislev 5691 (late 1930), as recorded by the Rebbe's secretary R. Yechezkel Feigin (Sefer HaMaamarim Kuntreisim vol. 1, p. 153a), the chassidim of Lithuania sent a delegation to the Rebbe. R. Yisrael Noach Chatzkevich of Kupishok headed the group, which formally renewed the request that the Rebbe visit anash in Lithuania and fix a date for the trip.
