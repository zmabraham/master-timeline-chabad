# Roster of 78 students compiled at Warsaw yeshiva

*1925*

Toward the end of 5685, a list of 78 students of the Warsaw yeshiva was compiled and preserved in the archives. It included family details, abilities, and the diligence of each student, as well as the year each had been accepted, providing a record of the student body for the years 5682-5685. Of those listed, 54 were currently studying and 24 had already completed their studies.
