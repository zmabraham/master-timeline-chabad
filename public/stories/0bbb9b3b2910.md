# Levin family receives French visa and moves toward Paris

*1948*

After Pesach 5708 the long-delayed visa to France finally came through. Levin's in-laws R. Meir Gorkov and Fayge Rivka Gorkov, already in Paris, had pushed the paperwork while Mikhael Teitelbaum waited on a U.S. visa. The Levins relocated from Pocking to Bezancourt outside Paris.
