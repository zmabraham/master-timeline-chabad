# R' Berl Kornitzer captured by the Germans in Radom

*1942*

All efforts to free him failed, and his fate remained unknown. The yeshiva feared retribution because Kornitzer had been entrusted with gold coins received from the wealthy patron for the yeshiva's support, but news arrived that he had hidden the coins in his shoes and they were apparently not discovered. The incident shook the entire underground community in Radom.
