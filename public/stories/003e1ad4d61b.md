# Rebbe Rashab dispatches rabbis to Soviet Georgia

*1916*

The Georgian community was steeped in tradition but lacked rabbis and scholars. The Rashab sent Levitin, Slavin, Sasonkin, Perlow, and Zuber, among others. Decades later, when the underground emissary Simon Yakovshvili returned to Georgia, the region's Jewish life remained vibrant enough to alarm Yevesektzia leader Moshe Litvakov.
