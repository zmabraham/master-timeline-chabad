# Police raid on Tomchei Tmimim hall

*1902*

After Shavuot 5662 (1902), on a Friday afternoon, the district director from Mohilev province arrived at the yeshiva hall with the local police chief and secret police, after denunciations claimed over 200 students were studying illegally. Only about 30 students were present, and the director was impressed by their appearance, but ordered the hall sealed pending official permits. Within a day, after the menahel telegraphed Mohilev, the seal was removed.
