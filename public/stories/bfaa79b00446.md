# Rabbi Cunin instructed by the Rebbe to return to Los Angeles for Pesach

*1991*

After Jerry Weintraub wrote to the Rebbe that there was no point in Cunin remaining in Moscow over Pesach, the Rebbe replied that Weintraub had great merit for his efforts on the books and that the main thing was that the books arrive soon. The Rebbe then handed Cunin a separate note: 'In my opinion it is fitting that he celebrate the festival of Pesach with his family in Los Angeles.' Rabbi Leibel Groner relayed that no parallel directive had been given to the rest of the delegation.
