# Rebbe Rashab letter to R' Yeshaya Berlin about Mertzbacher collection

*1901*

In this letter (Igros #691), Rebbe Rashab described the pricing methodology based on the Rabinowitz catalog. He noted that Baron David Ginzburg would acquire such manuscripts at any cost without hesitation. The goal was to keep this Jewish treasure in observant hands rather than letting it pass to secular collectors.
