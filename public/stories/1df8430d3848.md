# Rebbe's letter urging publication of Rogatchover on Talmud

*1961*

On 1 Menachem Av 5721, the Rebbe wrote to Rabbi Kasher acknowledging Tzofnat Paneach on Exodus and urging that, if possible, he should publish a volume on a Talmudic tractate studied in yeshivot in time for the upcoming zman. The Rebbe noted that volumes on Talmud make a different first impression than volumes on Chumash.
