# Gorbachev declines to meet Chabad delegation

*1990*

On the first day of Chanukah 5751 (25 Kislev) the delegation was told that Culture Minister Gubenko had personally warned Gorbachev against returning the Chabad library. Gorbachev's office informed Chabad that the inter-ministerial disagreement left him unable to decide and a planned meeting — at which Chabad had hoped to present a special menorah — would not take place. Veronika immediately began preparing a legal brief and worked through the night with a partner attorney to build a court-ready dossier.
