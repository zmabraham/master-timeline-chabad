# R. Yerachmiel Binyaminson leads minyan to Rebbe Rayatz's tziyun on 15 Elul 5710 to plead for Ramash to accept nesius

*1950*

Binyaminson, an elder of the chassidic rabbinate, had personally pressed the Ramash to take the leadership, telling him 'the older generation is one thing, but the young chassidim need a Rebbe' — to which the Ramash replied 'I have never withheld myself from the young.' On 15 Elul — the founding date of Tomchei Temimim — Binyaminson assembled a minyan including the narrator and R. Yisrael Shimon Kalmenson and laid a written pidyon at the kever asking Heaven to move the Ramash to accept the nesius. Upon returning, he handed the pidyon to the Ramash, who, the narrator recalls, received it with quiet satisfaction — unlike a similar attempt by another askan some time earlier which had drawn only an indifferent reaction.
