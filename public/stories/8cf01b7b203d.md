# R' Shlomo of Karlin killed in Ludmir

*1792*

In 5552 (1792) R' Shlomo of Karlin was killed in Ludmir. In subsequent years two of his leading disciples — R' Mordechai of Lechovich and R' Asher son of R' Aharon HaGadol of Stolin — would return to settle in White Russia and Lithuania, laying foundations for the spread of general chassidut into the region after 19 Kislev 5559.
