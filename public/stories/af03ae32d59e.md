# After ceasing his work as a shochet, he devoted all his free time to Torah study

*1975*

After ceasing his work as a shochet, he devoted all his free time to Torah study, and during these years he twice held a magnificent siyum haShas celebration. He passed away in New York at age 79 on 17 Sivan 5735 (1975), and was buried in the Chabad section of the Montefiore Cemetery.
