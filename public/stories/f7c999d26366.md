# Rebbe Rashab leaves Orel for Rostov-on-Don

*1915*

On Tuesday, 25 Marcheshvan 5676, the Rebbe Rashab left Orel after eight days of cramped lodging. With R. David Fradkin's help, tickets were obtained on the express train running straight to Rostov-on-Don, and the family's luggage traveled with them. Fradkin himself accompanied them to see them off.
