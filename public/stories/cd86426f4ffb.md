# Future Rebbe writes to R. Eliyahu Simpson about acquiring books

*1936*

Writing on 21 Menachem Av 5696 (August 9, 1936), Rabbi Menachem Mendel Schneerson asked R. Eliyahu Simpson, a senior Tomim in America, to organize anash there to send books to the Rebbe Rayatz's library. He specifically requested old siddurim, chumashim, haggadot, machzorim, and any early Hebrew imprints from America.
