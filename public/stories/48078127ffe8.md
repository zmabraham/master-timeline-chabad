# Rebbe Rayatz blesses founding of Agudas Talmidei HaTmimim

*1934*

The Rebbe Rayatz blessed the proposal from former students to form a network for Tomchei Tmimim alumni. Headed by R' Schneur Zalman Gourary, the Agudah set up offices at Moranowski 7/9 in Warsaw — the same address as the Rebbe's beit midrash. Its aims included disseminating Chassidus and helping members secure rabbinic, shochet, and commercial positions.
