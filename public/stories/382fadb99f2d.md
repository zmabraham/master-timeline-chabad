# Jerusalem Anash hold elections, install three new Kollel administrators

*1912*

On 15 Tammuz 5672 (July 1912), 76 members of Anash gathered in Jerusalem and ran their own election for kollel administrators, choosing R. Shmuel Halevi Levin, R. Gershon Lapshitz, and R. Moshe Kazarnovsky. The move was undertaken without the consent of the Rebbe Rashab and ultimately led to a years-long split in Kollel Chabad.
