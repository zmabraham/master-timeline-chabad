# Rebbe Rashab's engagement signed on 10 Sivan

*1865*

On 10 Sivan 5625 (June 4, 1865), when Rebbe Rashab was four and a half years old, his tenaim were written. The Tzemach Tzedek personally added the opening line and a closing line of obligation, signing 'Menachem Mendel,' less than a year before his own passing.
