# Tomchei Tmimim funding collapses in Russia after 1905 pogroms

*1907*

Writing to R' Gershon Kapilowitz in summer 5667, Rosenbaum described Tomchei Tmimim, founded by the Rebbe Rashab in Lubavitch, with some 500 outstanding students supplied with all their needs. Until then Russian philanthropists had given tens of thousands of rubles per year, but the recent pogroms and disturbances had crippled their ability to give. American donors now had to step in with fixed annual pledges to keep the yeshivas functioning.
