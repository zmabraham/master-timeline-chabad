# Author moves to Moscow to learn the retouching trade

*1938*

In Zlobin it was impossible to daven with a minyan on the Yamim Noraim, so the author moved to Moscow, found temporary lodging in the Marina-Roshcha neighborhood near the shul, and began studying retouching. R' Zusya Paz, the chassidic shamash of the Marina-Roshcha synagogue who had known him from Surazh years earlier, welcomed him warmly. Hearing that a shidduch had been proposed for him, R' Zusya immediately said, 'Don't even think about a shidduch — I have one for you,' meaning his niece in Shumyachi.
