# Leah Beila Levin writes memoir-letter to Rebbe Rayatz

*1934*

The letter described her descent from Reb Avraham Yehuda of Linets and the Kosover-Berditchev rabbinic line, her childhood in Dashov, and her marriage to Ephraim Shmuel of Zhivotov. It detailed how their small shop and his teaching supported the family, and how she herself sometimes taught the children of his cheder. The letter is preserved in the Lubavitch library archive.
