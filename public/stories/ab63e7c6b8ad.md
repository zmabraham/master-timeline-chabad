Rabbi Avraham Gad Vankart Rabbi Avraham Gad Vankart (born 18 Shevat 5705, 1945) is a chassidic mashpia currently residing in the settlement of Bat Ayin, and gives regular shiurim to broad audiences, with most of his shiurim dealing with the books of general Chassidus
