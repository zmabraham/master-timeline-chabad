# Gorbachev decides to return the Chabad seforim

*1991*

On 8 Tammuz 5751 the delegation learned that Gorbachev, at 1 p.m. the previous day, had made a definitive decision to return the seforim of the Rebbeim. He aimed to complete the transfer before July 10 (28 Tammuz), ahead of the G7 meeting opening July 14. Veronika and Chernyaev's office were preparing the formal paperwork.
