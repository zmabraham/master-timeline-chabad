# Rashab buried in Rostov Jewish cemetery

*1920*

The casket was carried to the cemetery by yeshivah students and elders of anash, with the whole Jewish community of the city present. Standing at his father's fresh grave, the Rayatz publicly appointed three rabbis as a beit din and declared he was purchasing the plot conditionally, reserving the right to later reinter the Rashab in the ancestral burial ground in Lubavitch. The German invasion and the revolution had prevented the return to Lubavitch.
