# R. Yaakov ibn Tzur becomes rabbi of Meknes

*1718*

After serving as a judge in Fez, R. Yaakov ibn Tzur (the Ya'avetz), author of Mishpat U'Tzedaka B'Yaakov, was accepted as rabbi of Meknes in the summer of 1718. He served there until 1729, when he returned to Fez. Following the death of his teacher R. Yehuda ibn Atar, he served as head of the Fez rabbinical court until his death in 1753.
