# Mindel project to compile English booklet on Chabad communal work

*1944*

From Motzaei Shabbat Lech Lecha, 11 Marcheshvan 5705, the Rebbe Rayatz began quietly relating to his son-in-law Ramash (the future Rebbe) and to Nissan Mindel an account of the communal activity — especially economic — of the Mitteler Rebbe, the Tzemach Tzedek, the Maharash, and the Rashab through 5661. Ramash relayed the words to Mindel, who took notes; their stated aim was to compile and print a Chabad-leadership communal-activity booklet in English.
