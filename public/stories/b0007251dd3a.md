# 12 Tammuz 5693 farbrengen in Malachovka with new maamar from Rayatz

*1933*

An exceptional farbrengen brought together R. Zalman Moshe Yitzchaki, R. Shmuel Levitin, R. Shmuel Aba Dolitzky, and many younger Anash. The newly arrived maamar 'Nasata Lirei'echa Nes Lehisnoses' from the Frierdiker Rebbe was studied at length and Anash drank substantial mashke in celebration.
