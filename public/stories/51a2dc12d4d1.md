# Rebbe allocates $1200 between Dankra and Livenhof

*1940*

After arriving in America, the Frierdiker Rebbe managed to send $1200 to Mordechai Chefetz in Riga. On 1 Iyar 5700 he wrote (Igrot vol. 12, no. 4391) directing that R. Berchen receive 100 lats per month for the Dankra yeshiva and 50 lats per month, from Nissan onward, for the Livenhof branch.
