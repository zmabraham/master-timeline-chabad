# Montefiore census of Chevron

*1839*

On 4 Tammuz 5599 Montefiore registered the Jews of Chevron during his visit. The Chevron Chabad community optimistically registered R' Uri of Slonim as a resident, though he was still in Tzfat at the time and only intended to move. The inaccurate details in his Chevron entry (wrong children's names, ages, and aliyah year) reflect that the community recorded him in absentia.
