# Likkutei Torah reprint completed in Warsaw by Agudas HaTmimim of America

*1928*

One thousand copies were printed; five hundred were given as a gift to Tomchei Tmimim in Warsaw and five hundred were shipped to America, bound, and sold for five dollars the set. The title page credited Agudas HaTmimim of America (the alumni of Tomchei Tmimim Lubavitch) as sponsor, with the final volume listing every contributor. The printing significantly lifted the morale of anash in America.
