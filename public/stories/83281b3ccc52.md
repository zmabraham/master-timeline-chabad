# Lenin Library refuses to return Schneersohn collection

*1925*

The refusal letter listed several pretexts: the owners had failed to register the library with the Narkompros libraries department; absent registration it had been handed to the Rumyantsev Museum; librarian Shmuel Eisenstadt had opined the books were of special importance and should not be returned; the library's Jewish section was poor and would be enriched by the collection; and the books would otherwise serve only a narrow circle. The real obstacle was that officials could not locate the 1921 Narkompros authorization. Eisenstadt, a Zionist who later emigrated to Eretz Yisrael, was identified as the personal antagonist behind the refusal.
