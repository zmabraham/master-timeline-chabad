# Reception of Rabbi Aryeh Leib Kramer of Montreal Tomchei Tmimim

*1945*

On the evening of 22 Tevet 5705 the Rebbe Rayatz received Rabbi Aryeh Leib Kramer, principal of Yeshivat Tomchei Tmimim Lubavitch in Montreal and its branch ketanot yeshivot. Kramer reported in detail on the yeshivah's physical and spiritual state, and the Rebbe urged him to establish Beit Rivka, a school for girls aged 15–18 to be trained as teachers, alongside Beit Sarah for younger girls.
