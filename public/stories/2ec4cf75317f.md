# Earthquake in Hebron

*1834*

On Sunday 16 Iyar 5594 a great earthquake shook Hebron, collapsing several homes in the Jewish courtyard. The Jews fled their dwellings and stayed for a full week in the fields among armed Arabs - 'like sheep among wolves' - while aftershocks continued every hour. Lesser tremors continued for about four weeks afterward, leaving the community traumatized.
