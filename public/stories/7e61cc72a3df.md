# Tsarist secret committee opens review of Jewish laws

*1903*

On the eve of Sukkot 5664, the Rebbe Rashab wrote to R. Yeshaya Berlin urging him to use his contacts to influence the governors' replies before the October 1 deadline. The committee, chaired by Interior Minister Vyacheslav Plehve, convened in early January 1904 to weigh partial easings of the Pale alongside new restrictions.
