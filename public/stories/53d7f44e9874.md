# Rebbe Rashab flees Lubavitch for Rostov

*1915*

At the start of 5676 (autumn 1915), as World War I disrupted Lubavitch, the Rashab moved his household to Rostov. He could not carry his entire library, so most books were stored in a Moscow warehouse (where they remained for decades). He brought along the manuscript collection and about one hundred books, including a Jerusalem Talmud printed in Zhitomir with his handwritten glosses.
