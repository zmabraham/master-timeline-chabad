# Death of Rabbi Mordechai Twersky of Loyev

*1911*

Rabbi Mordechai of Loyev died in 5671, providing the latest possible date for his surviving letter of recommendation on behalf of a chasid seeking to settle in the Land of Israel. That letter, addressed to communities including Brahin, Layev, Mazyr, Rechitsa, and Bobruisk, also references the 'great rabbi of Lubavitch' (Maharash or Rashab) who interceded for the petitioner.
