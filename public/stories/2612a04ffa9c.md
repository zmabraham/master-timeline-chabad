# Rabbi Yochanan of Rachmistrivka endorses Rabbi Eliyahu Posek's appointment in Ravna

*1887*

Dated Sunday of Parashat Behaalotecha 5647, the letter expresses joy that the Ravna community accepted Rabbi Eliyahu Posek — author of Mor V'Ohalot — as rabbi without controversy. Rabbi Yochanan urges holdouts to add their signatures and instructs the community to pay both the rabbi and the shochet on time so the rabbi can study undisturbed.
