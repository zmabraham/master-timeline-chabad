# R' Yosef Yehuda Mashiov sent as Hebron Chabad emissary

*1843*

Around 5604 the Hebron Chabad community appointed R' Yosef Yehuda Leib Mashiov of Shklov as their emissary to collect previously gathered donations and to inform Diaspora Jewry of the suffering they had endured in those years. The mission also reflected his personal need, as his son had reached marriageable age. He served two years on this mission, returning in the summer of 5606.
