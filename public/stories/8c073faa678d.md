# JDC meets Chabad delegation on aid to refugees and the Vilnius yeshivah

*1940*

Citing US law forbidding organizations from financing immigration, the JDC declined the larger requests but agreed to a discretionary grant 'in view of Rabbi Schneersohn's leadership' and approved funding for Passover matzah for rabbis in Russia. The Rebbe would have to rely on his own resources to sustain the Vilnius yeshivah and rescue Chasidim still in Poland.
