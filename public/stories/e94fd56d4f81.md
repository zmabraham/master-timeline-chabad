# R. Moshe Pinchas Katz departs for United States

*1939*

On 7 Iyar 5699 (April 26, 1939), secretary Nissan Mindel wrote to R. Yosef Avraham Kagan in London that R. Moshe Pinchas Katz was departing that day for London on his way to America. R. Mentlik later confirmed on 23 Iyar (May 12) that he had received a telegram on Tuesday that his brother-in-law had arrived safely.
