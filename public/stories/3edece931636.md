# Contract signed to purchase Shmuel Wiener's library

*1924*

On 8 Cheshvan 5685 the Frierdiker Rebbe and bibliographer Shmuel Wiener executed a signed agreement transferring Wiener's massive bibliographic collection — including some 400 Haggadah editions, 140 Kinnos volumes, sixty Siddur recensions, machzorim, and Yiddish Tchines — to the Rebbe. The purchase price of $5,000 was to be paid in monthly installments of $50 over many years. About 4,000 unbound items came with the collection and would be bound after delivery.
