# Rabbi Avraham Mordechai Hirschberg in his youth Rabbi Dr. Avraham Mordechai Hirschberg

*1916*

Rabbi Avraham Mordechai Hirschberg in his youth Rabbi Dr. Avraham Mordechai Hirschberg (Avraham Mordechai Hirschberg, 5676 - 25 Elul 5745, 1916-1985) was a graduate of the Chachmei Lublin Yeshiva, a member of the spiritual staff of the yeshiva, rabbi of the central synagogue
