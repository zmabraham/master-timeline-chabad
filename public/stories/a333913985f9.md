# Rabbi Zalman Tuvia Abelsky installed as Rabbi of Kishinev

*1991*

On 15 Shevat, the formal installation of Rabbi Zalman Tuvia Abelsky as Rabbi of Kishinev took place in Moldova. The author had planned to travel with Rabbi Dovid Berel Lazar to attend the event, but Veronika Monosonik, who was to arrange their visas, refused to do so, so they remained in Moscow. The installation was part of a wider rebuilding of Chabad rabbinic infrastructure across the former Soviet Union during this period.
