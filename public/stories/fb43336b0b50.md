# Fire in Lubavitch destroys responsa manuscripts

*1857*

Compilers' notes at the end of the printed Tzemach Tzedek responsa state that 'our holy master wrote many responsa on this, and in the last fire, woe to us, the book was burned and only this responsum remained.' The Rebbe's notes, citing manuscripts datable to 5608–5616, place the fire between roughly 5616 and 5618. The disaster accounts for the loss of most of volumes 1–14.
