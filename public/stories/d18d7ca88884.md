# Second yechidus: pidyonos and instructions for the Rostov Ohel

*1925*

At 6pm Sunday yechidus the Rebbe summarized the Shabbos maamar and gave the author a bundle of pidyonos to place at the Ohel. He instructed that his own pidyon be read only once, only at the Ohel, never copied or shown to anyone, with no leniency. The Rebbe also sent the author to his mother, Rebbetzin Shterna Sarah, who gave him a pidyon and details about their former Rostov home at Bradsky Pereulok 44.
