# Author begins serving as ba'al koreh in Vitebsk

*1929*

Lodging in the same apartment building as rosh yeshiva R' Gavriel Kagan, with R' Tzemach (son of R' Yitzchak the masmid) serving as mashgiach, the author followed a schedule of Gemara until 8 PM and a half-hour of Tanya. Beginning with Parashat Bo and continuing through Yitro and the rest of the year, he reviewed each parsha after the evening seder and chanted it the next Shabbat at a nearby minyan, training himself into the role of ba'al koreh he would carry for life.
