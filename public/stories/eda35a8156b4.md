# Or HaGanuz printed with Tzemach Tzedek approbation

*1866*

Or HaGanuz appeared in 1866 with an unusual haskama from the Tzemach Tzedek and his sons. It is the only book to which the Tzemach Tzedek lent his name—he made the exception because the author's grandfather, Rabbi Yehuda Leib, had given an approbation to the Alter Rebbe's Tanya.
