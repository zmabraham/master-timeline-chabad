# Librarian writes David Sassoon requesting Ohel David

*1933*

The letter, signed only as 'director of the Lubavitch book and manuscript collection' without mentioning the Rebbe by name, reflected the library's new institutional identity. Liberman cited the precedent of many authors and publishers who had sent their works as gifts and thanked Sassoon in advance for joining them — one of dozens of such solicitation letters dispatched in this period.
