# Mandelbaum reports on 19 Kislev gatherings in Globoke

*1936*

Mandelbaum reported that during the Yud-Tes Kislev celebrations he had pushed for strengthened Ein Yaakov shiurim in every minyan, Mishnayos and Shulchan Aruch classes, his own daily gemara shiur in the Starasele minyan, and a resolution to learn Tanya on Friday nights.
