# Birth of Rebbe Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn in Lubavitch

*1880*

Rebbe Yosef Yitzchak (the Rayatz) was born on 12 Tammuz 5640 in Lubavitch, the town that gave Chabad its name. He was the only son of the Rebbe Rashab and would later succeed him as the sixth Rebbe of Chabad-Lubavitch. The date of his birth would later become doubly significant as the same date on which he was released from Soviet imprisonment in 1927.
