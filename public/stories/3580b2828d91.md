# Rebbe Rashab appeals to the JCA for industrial relief in the Pale

*1901*

In a long, anguished letter (Igrot Kodesh #102), the Rebbe portrayed families starving from the combined effects of Pale residency restrictions and the new state monopoly on liquor sales. He argued that funding industrial infrastructure inside the Pale would allow Jews to earn a living where they were trapped by law, rather than supporting the Society for the Diffusion of Enlightenment.
