# Kollel Chabad funds Tzfat Beit Midrash

*1873*

The Chabad Beit Midrash in Tzfat dated back to the pre-5583 Chabad settlement there. As the community redeveloped in 5633, Kollel Chabad in Jerusalem began allocating funds for its upkeep, recognizing the resurgent presence of Chabad in the holy city.
