# R. Issachar Ber Hurwitz appointed Rav of Lubavitch

*1832*

From 5592 to 5603 (1832-1843), R. Issachar Ber HaLevi Hurwitz held the rabbinate of Lubavitch. He maintained close halachic contact with the Tzemach Tzedek, frequently routing inquiries to him and binding the resulting responsa into volumes preserved in the library.
