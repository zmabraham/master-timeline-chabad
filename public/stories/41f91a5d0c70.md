# R' Yosef Blumenfeld appointed mashgiach in Warsaw yeshiva

*1924*

At the beginning of 5685, the Warsaw yeshiva received a new appointment of R"M and mashgiach with the Rebbe's consent: R' Yosef Blumenfeld of Dombrowa, who had studied for two years at Tomchei Tmimim in Lubavitch. He later served as R"M in the Radomsk Chassidic yeshivas 'Keter Torah,' then as rabbi in Krolewska Huta, and in winter 5695 immigrated to Eretz Yisrael where he served on the beis din in Tel Aviv.
