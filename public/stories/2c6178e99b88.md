# Acharon shel Pesach farbrengen at R' Tzvi Gurary in Kremenchug

*1918*

The narrator spent Pesach 5678 in Kremenchug as a guest of R' Nosson Gurary, whose home was open to all. On the last day of Pesach the local chassidim gathered at R' Tzvi Gurary's house for a farbrengen that extended until late at night, even as 10,000 dollars was being raised for Tomchei Temimim per the Rebbe Rashab's instruction.
