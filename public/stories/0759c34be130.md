# Rebbe sends second halachic reply to Rabbi Hutner on tefillin

*1968*

On 8 Shevat 5728 (Feb 7, 1968), the Rebbe responded a second time, opening by noting that Rabbi Hutner had not addressed his core proofs from tefilah and korban. He drew a sharp distinction between mistaken knowledge ('kasavur she'hu basar') as in the Shulchan Aruch HaRav on matzah, versus mere absence of knowledge, and added new chiddushim from the Alter Rebbe's pesakim on tefillin.
