# Tomchei Tmimim class photographs taken at Otwock

*1936*

At the beginning of 5697 (autumn 1936), class photographs were taken of every student of the yeshiva — both those in the main study hall and classes at Otwock and those in the preparatory program in Warsaw — along with every member of the yeshiva staff. The photo also included images of the yeshiva buildings in Otwock, which the yeshiva had entered at the beginning of 5696.
