# R' Avraham of Kalisk's emissaries seek to displace Alter Rebbe's emissaries

*1803*

In the winter of 5563 (1802/03) emissaries from R' Avraham of Kalisk in Eretz Yisrael came to the Alter Rebbe asking that the existing chassidic emissaries handling the Land of Israel maamadot be removed and replaced with new ones from among 'true lovers who are not called by the name Chabad.' The dispute marked the open split between Chabad and the general chassidic movement led by Kalisk.
