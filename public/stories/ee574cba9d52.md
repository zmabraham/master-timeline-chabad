# Rayatz responds to Yehoshua Isaac Baruch on Vilna talmidim and Lithuanian anash

*1940*

The mashpia R' Yehoshua Isaac Baruch was on the ground with the talmidim and corresponded directly with the Rayatz about their condition. The Rebbe's reply (Igrot Kodesh vol. 12, letter 4408) expresses satisfaction at the fullness of the information. Over the following year the Rayatz wrote frequently about the talmidim's situation and their eventual rescue.
