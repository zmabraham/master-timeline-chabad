# Tomchei Tmimim branches founded in Kaluszyn, Lenchna, Stopnica

*1938*

The 5698 expansion marked one of the last major growth waves before the war. Lenchna grew rapidly, reaching 75 students by the eve of war, while Kaluszyn would later be specifically mentioned by the Frierdiker Rebbe among the branches whose talmidim he rescued to Lithuania.
