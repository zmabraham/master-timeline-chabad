# Israeli Frumkin Commission established to investigate immigrant children's education

*1950*

Chaired by Gad Frumkin — son of a Chabad Chasid who had welcomed the Rebbe during his 1929 Palestine visit — the commission found that accusations of forcible removal of peyos and interference with religious studies were true, and that policies aimed to recruit children to the Histadrut rather than support religious parties.
