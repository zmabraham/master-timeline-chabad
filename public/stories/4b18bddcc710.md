# Wedding of R' Shlomo Kugel and Bracha Slava Rosenbaum

*1879*

R' Shlomo, son of R' Gershon Yehuda Leib Kugel, married Bracha Slava, daughter of R' Avraham Chaim Rosenbaum of Pleshchenitsy, in the week of Parshat Terumah 5639 (February 1879). R' Shmuel Dovber of Borisov, unable to attend in person because he was in Lubavitch, sent a mazal-tov letter on 25 Adar 5639.
