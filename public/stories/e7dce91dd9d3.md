# Letter to R' Hendel describing harsh new prison regime

*1890*

On Thursday of Parshat Tzav 5650, R' Avraham Chaim wrote to the mashpia R' Hendel describing in detail the new restrictions imposed by the changed prison administration. He worried especially about Passover, since the prohibition on private samovars made hot water for matzah impossible, and the prison ovens could not be koshered. He noted that only about two and a half months remained of his sentence.
