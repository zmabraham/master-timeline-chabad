# Israel Yehuda's family flees Kharkov amid German bombing after Shabbat Bereishit

*1941*

The night before departure a massive German bombardment partially destroyed their building; the families wrote a pidyon nefesh to the Rebbe during the raid. With the main Kharkov station destroyed and only the freight terminal operating, Israel Yehuda was nearly stranded with his belongings until two passing gentile laborers agreed to carry his bundles down the hill for thirty rubles. The train was bombed en route, but the group survived by remaining inside rather than running into the open.
