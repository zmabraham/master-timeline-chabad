# Mitteler Rebbe distributes ma'amad bais harav funds

*1814*

In Tevet 5574, one year after the Alter Rebbe's passing, the Mitteler Rebbe arranged the distribution of the ma'amad bais harav fund. He appointed R. Pinchas Schick of Shklov to administer the distribution, with R. Shlomo Freides and R. Zalman Reuzes of Shklov as overseers. Of the 35,000 rubles collected, two-thirds went to descendants of the Alter Rebbe and one-third was used by the Mitteler Rebbe to build his own home, a synagogue in Lubavitch, and to purchase seforim.
