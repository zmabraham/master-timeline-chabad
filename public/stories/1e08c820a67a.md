# Rebbe Rashab proposes Riga as home for Mertzbacher library

*1902*

Rebbe Rashab clarified his vision: the priority was that an important Jewish library exist in observant hands, even if located in Riga rather than Russia or Lubavitch (Igros #782). He proposed dedicating 2-3 rooms for books and one for study. He also approached R' Yaakov Horowitz of Riga as a possible co-funder.
