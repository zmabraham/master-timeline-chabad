# R. Z. Veliszher reminiscence published in Kfar Chabad magazine

*1983*

Gringlas's account preserves R. Zalman's recollection that as the Rashab uttered 'lo shamati,' he turned his head and confirmed: 'It is not written lo yadati but lo shamati.' The note resolves the textual puzzle that the Rashab had raised about R. Levi Yitzchok's letter.
