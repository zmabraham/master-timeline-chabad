# Rabbi Eliezer Silver's drasha in Łódź on Rosh Chodesh Tammuz

*1946*

Silver compared the chassidim's spiritual endurance under Soviet persecution to a flourishing tree in the desert whose water comes from a distant hidden spring — the Rebbe. He added that the Rebbe was likewise working with mesirus nefesh in the United States, and that he himself was strengthened by him. He then held a separate private meeting with the small Anash group in honor of the Rebbe, and afterward began advocating for Polish passports to Prague for them.
