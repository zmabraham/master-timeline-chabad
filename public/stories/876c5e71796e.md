# R' Yonah Cohen visits Tashkent fertility doctors with Levin's wife

*1946*

Childless after years of marriage, Levin and Michael Teitelbaum confided in their friend R' Yonah Cohen of Tashkent. Himself childless, R' Yonah declared he would do everything in his power for them: he hosted both wives at his Tashkent home for a month in winter 5706 and personally escorted them to leading specialists.
