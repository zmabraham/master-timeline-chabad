# Founding of Tomchei Tmimim yeshiva

*1897*

On 15 Elul 5657 (September 12, 1897), the Rebbe Rashab founded the Tomchei Tmimim yeshiva, with R' Chanoch Hendel overseeing the Lubavitch branch and R' Shmuel Gronem Esterman overseeing the Zembin branch in Minsk province. The yeshiva was established to strengthen young men in Torah study and fear of Heaven amid growing Haskalah and Zionist movements. It became the central Chabad yeshiva network.
