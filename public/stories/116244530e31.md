# Founding of Agudath Chassidei Chabad in America

*1924*

In 5684 (1924) Agudath Chassidei Chabad was established in the United States. It became the primary funder of Chabad's activities in Russia, Riga, and Poland, supporting the network of Tomchei Temimim yeshivos, the Rebbe Rayatz's household and secretariat, and the acquisition of books for the Lubavitch library.
