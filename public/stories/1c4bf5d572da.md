# R. Nathan Halevi of Polotzk urges Tzemach Tzedek to accept leadership

*1828*

On the eve of Rosh Chodesh Adar 5588 (February 1828) R. Nathan Halevi Horowitz of Polotzk sent a halachic she'eilah to Rabbi Menachem Mendel. He closed it with the unusual chassidic phrase 'prepared to obey him, desirous of his success,' and signed using his mother's name ('Nathan Halevi son of Chayena') as one would in a pidyon-nefesh to a Rebbe. The letter was thus both a halachic query and a coded plea that Tzemach Tzedek accept the leadership of Chabad.
