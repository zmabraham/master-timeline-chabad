# Rebbe launches Mivtza Tefillin during Six-Day War

*1967*

In Iyar 5727 (May 1967), in the lead-up to and aftermath of the Six-Day War, the Rebbe declared Mivtza Tefillin — a global initiative urging volunteers to help Jewish men, including many entirely unaffiliated, don tefillin. The campaign quickly became one of the Rebbe's signature mivtzaim, but also drew halachic and ideological questions from various rabbinic circles over the summer of 5727.
