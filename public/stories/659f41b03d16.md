# Sample Passover seder taught to yeshiva students

*1992*

Many of the 20 yeshiva students had only become observant within the past year and had never seen a seder. About 15 students attended a three-hour demonstration seder so they could learn to lead public seders. Together they were preparing seders in about 25 towns across Russia, relying mainly on Chabad shluchim in the towns and the yeshiva students themselves, with no help from American bochurim that year.
