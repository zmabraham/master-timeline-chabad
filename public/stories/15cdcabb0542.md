# Mogilev and Vitebsk Jews fund delegation to Petersburg

*1824*

In 1824, a regulation was formulated in the Lubavitch area by Jewish leaders of Mogilev and Vitebsk to fund a delegation traveling to Petersburg for shtadlanut on public affairs. Mogilev was assessed 1,030 rubles and Vitebsk 690 rubles for the emissaries' expenses. The takanah also affirmed that the chachamim, elders, heads of thousands and heads of hundreds named in the document were of equal authority, and the document later became evidence in the case against Rabbi DovBer of Lubavitch.
