# Mitteler Rebbe settles in the town of Lubavitch

*1813*

Following the passing of his father in early 5573, the Mitteler Rebbe took up the leadership of Chabad. At the end of that same year (1813) he settled in the small town of Lubavitch. There he constructed a home, a large beit knesset, and a library that became the new center of the movement.
