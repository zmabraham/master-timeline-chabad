# Rebbe Rayatz escapes Nazi-occupied Warsaw

*1940*

Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn had been in Warsaw when Nazi Germany invaded Poland at the start of 5700. After several months under occupation, a coordinated rescue effort succeeded in extracting him along with family members and secretaries in late winter 5700 (early 1940). They first traveled to Riga and from there continued onward to New York.
