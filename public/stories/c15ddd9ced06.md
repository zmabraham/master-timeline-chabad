# Exhibition on Chabad, Shlichus and the Mivtzaim

*1997*

In the summer of 5757 (1997), the Lubavitch Library presented an exhibition on Chabad's worldwide activity, the work of shluchim, and the Rebbe's mivtzaim—campaigns to spread observance of mitzvos such as tefillin, mezuzah, Shabbos candles, kashrus and the four species. The exhibit displayed candle-lighting sets, tzedakah boxes, menorahs, mivtza tanks' keys and other items sent to the Rebbe from Chabad centers around the world.
