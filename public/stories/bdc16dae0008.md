*1944*

Yitzchak After years of exile, Rabbi Levi Yitzchak moved to Alma Ata (Almaty), but due to the hardships of imprisonment and exile, he became ill there with a severe and terminal illness, from which he passed away on 20 Menachem Av 5704 (August 9, 1944), and was buried there
