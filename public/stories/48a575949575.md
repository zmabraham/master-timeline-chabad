# Veronika delivers press response on Chabad library to Tomarkin

*1991*

Through the second day of Chol HaMoed Pesach 5751, Veronika worked on a lengthy philosophical article on the return of the Schneerson books, with a section introducing the Chabad mission, plus a sharper rebuttal to the Domostroy attack. Tomarkin, an aide to Gorbachev, sent a driver to collect the material and undertook to place it in the press. The Rebbe had directed the delegation to take its cues from Veronika on whether and how to respond publicly.
