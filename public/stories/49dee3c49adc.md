# Third Chabad–Sephardic Hebron agreement at 500 grush

*1842*

By 5602 Sheikh Abdul Rahman had increased his protection demands and required separate payments from each kollel directly. The third agreement therefore reduced the Chabad contribution to general Hebron expenses from 900 to 500 grush per year, effective Tishrei 5603 onward. The text acknowledges that the Sephardim were the original Jewish settlers under whose communal umbrella the Chabad community had arrived in Hebron.
