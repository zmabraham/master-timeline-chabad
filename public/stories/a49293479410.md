# Roster of Tomchei Tmimim Warsaw students compiled

*1930*

The summer 5690 student list documents nearly sixty bochurim studying in the Warsaw yeshiva, with notes on their hometowns, ages, family backgrounds, chassidic affiliations, and academic progress through the yeshiva's classes. The list preserves a rare demographic snapshot of pre-war Polish Chabad youth, including students from Warsaw, Chelm, Lublin, Stopnica, Riga, Romania, and dozens of Polish towns. It serves as a key document for tracing the talmidim of Tomchei Tmimim before the Holocaust.
