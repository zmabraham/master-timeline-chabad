# R' Chaim Halevi begins 42-year tenure as gabbai of Hebron

*1788*

In 5548 (1788), R' Chaim Yaakov Halevi ibn Yakar began serving as gabbai and pakid (treasurer and officer) of the Hebron Sephardic community. He held the post for 42 years, handling all communal affairs until 5590 (1830). As a sign of trust and recognition, in 5590 his son R' Yosef Halevi was appointed as his deputy and eventual successor.
