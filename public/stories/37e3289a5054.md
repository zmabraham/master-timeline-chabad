# Rebbe Rashab writes from Würzburg accepting Tzemach Tzedek manuscript

*1907*

From Würzburg on 11 Elul 5667 the Rebbe Rashab acknowledged receipt of 100 marks Rosenbaum had sent on his behalf, and turned to a more precious offer. He told Rosenbaum that the Tzemach Tzedek's autograph was 'dearer to us than any wealth,' and asked him to send it by registered mail to Lubavitch, while books should go via Königsberg.
