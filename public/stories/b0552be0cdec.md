# First day Levin permitted to fully inspect Schneerson collection

*1999*

Mira Trifonenko, head of the Eastern books department, told Levin all 110 rare-museum books were confirmed Schneerson. The Polyakov collection was split into four parts: 110 rare museum books, 2,300 old books, 8,350 books from 17th–18th centuries at the Khimki depot, and 280 manuscripts known as the Schneerson archive. Levin began his planned categorization of marked, catalogued, and unmarked unlisted books.
