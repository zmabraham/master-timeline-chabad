# R' Yitzchak Yaakov accepted as additional rabbi in Romny

*1829*

Romny, a Chassidic town in the Poltava region near Haditch (burial place of the Alter Rebbe), already had R' Menachem Nachum as rabbi when R' Yitzchak Yaakov was added in 5589. He would later correspond with the Tzemach Tzedek on halachic matters.
