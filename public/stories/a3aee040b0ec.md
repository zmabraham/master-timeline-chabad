# Rebbe calls to 'conquer the world' with Torah on 15 Shvat

*1971*

In a sicha on 15 Shvat 5731 (1971), the Rebbe issued a call to 'conquer' the world through the study of Torah (Likkutei Sichos vol. 6 p. 312). This call became the foundation of Mivtza Torah and a series of subsequent Torah-related campaigns.
