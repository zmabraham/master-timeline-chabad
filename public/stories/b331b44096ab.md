# Rebbe thanks Dubin and Westerman for Dankra donation

*1938*

Six months after pushing for Lithuanian recruits, the Frierdiker Rebbe wrote to Eliyahu Chaim Althoiz of Riga on 3 Shevat 5698 thanking him for news of a donation from the philanthropist Westerman for the Tomchei Temimim mechina in Dankra. The gift had been secured through the efforts of the Latvian parliamentarian Mordechai Dubin.
