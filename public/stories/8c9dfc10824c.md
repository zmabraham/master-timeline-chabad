# Tziyun HaMetzuyenes published

*1993*

In Iyar 5753 (May 1993), R. Yosef Menachem Weiss published the book Tziyun HaMetzuyenes about the graves of the Nodah BiYhudah and his family in the Prague cemetery, with documents, photographs and biographies — including an entry on the Nodah BiYhudah's distinguished son-in-law R. Yosef Hatzaddik of Posen.
