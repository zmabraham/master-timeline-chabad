# Settlement check sent to Merkos over old partnership

*1992*

Citing Rambam's halachot of partners — that when partners separate each must swear about possible inadvertent overcharges — the author in 5752 (1992) wrote out a $500 check to Merkos L'Inyonei Chinuch. He delivered it to Rabbi Yehuda Krinsky, his liaison to Rabbi Chadakov, asking him to verbally mochel on behalf of Merkos any sums that may have remained with the partners contrary to din.
