# Agudas Chassidei Chabad established in the United States

*1924*

Led by Rabbi Yisrael Jacobson, the organization operated as a loose confederation of immigrant synagogues whose members were linked largely by sentimental attachment to Chabad rather than active chasidic practice. Though it had not matured into a major national body by 1939, it became the institutional vehicle through which the rescue of the Sixth Rebbe was organized.
