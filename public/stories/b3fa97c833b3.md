# Rotstein replies to Rodal with joy over Montreal arrival photographs

*1941*

On 13 Kislev 5702 (December 3, 1941), Rabbi Rotstein replied to talmid Yosef Rodal. He wrote that receiving the letter and two photographs had brought him great joy at seeing the students together with the gedolim, and hoped Rodal had also sent the photos to the Rebbe, anticipating the nachas this would cause the leader of the generation.
