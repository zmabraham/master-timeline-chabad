# Rosenbaum works as Torah journal editor in Berlin

*1893*

A surviving undated letter from this period describes Rosenbaum as 'arranging and preparing the words of the wise for print,' lodging in the home of an unnamed rabbinic publisher in Berlin. He used the post to correspond with R. Tzvi Tietz, author of Zikaron Yehuda on Eichah, about the wording of the bris milah blessing 'tziva yedidus,' explaining why he had cut a passage from Tietz's submitted essay.
