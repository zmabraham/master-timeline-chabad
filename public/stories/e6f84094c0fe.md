# Rebbe Rayatz refuses R' Eliyahu Weitz's resignation in Vilna

*1934*

R' Eliyahu Weitz, who bore the main burden of the Vilna yeshiva's leadership, wrote that he could no longer continue alone. The Rebbe Rayatz refused to accept his resignation, knowing it would mean the institution's destruction, and the same day wrote to Anash of Vilna chastising their cold attitude and pleading they actively support R' Weitz and the yeshiva.
