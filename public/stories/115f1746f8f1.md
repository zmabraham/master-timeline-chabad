# Rebbe Rashab's siyum-pidyon at zaide's tziyun

*1906*

During the Poalei Tzion crisis of 5666 (1906), the Rebbe Rashab wrote pidyonot at the tziyunim of his grandfather the Tzemach Tzedek and his father the Rebbe Maharash. He prayed for great mercy upon himself, his son Yosef Yitzchak, and the students of Tomchei Tmimim, who were being threatened with murder by Poalei Tzion if their financial and personnel demands were not met.
