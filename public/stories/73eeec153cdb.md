# Feigin describes Shabbos maamarim and Polish attendees

*1934*

Feigin wrote that every Shabbos the Rebbe delivered maamarim during Kabbalas Shabbos as in Lubavitch days, students gathered in the morning for chazara, and after mincha talmidim and anash assembled in the Rebbe's beis hamedrash. Many local Polish Jews drew close in order to hear Chassidus.
