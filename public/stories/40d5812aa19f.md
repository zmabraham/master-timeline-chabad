# 19 Kislev 5710 farbrengen with Rebbe Rayatz upstairs at 770

*1949*

Festival farbrengens were held on the second floor of 770, with the Rebbe Rayatz at the head of the table flanked by his sons-in-law and senior chassidim; the door was opened to admit guests but closed before start time due to the Rebbe's weakness. When R. Nissan Telushkin was admitted as an honored guest, additional chairs had to be brought near the Rebbe's seat. When fewer were produced than he had asked for — out of deference to crowding him — the Rebbe declared, 'Why so few? Yidden don't crowd me — I was raised on Jewish sweat!'
