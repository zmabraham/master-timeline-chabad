# Frierdiker Rebbe issues detailed organizational plan for Torat Emet

*1922*

On 10 Shvat 5682 (February 8, 1922), the Frierdiker Rebbe sent R' Shlomo Zalman Havlin a long and detailed letter laying out how to organize Yeshivat Torat Emet into three divisions, defining the student types, the rashei yeshiva to be hired, and the order of study. Havlin then recruited R' Moshe Aryeh Leib Shapira as a rosh metivta and R' Shimon Glitzenstein as secretary — both alumni of Torat Emet's original Hebron cohort — who would serve in these roles for decades.
