# Rebbe Rayatz vacations in Bulduri near Riga

*1928*

Worn out by imprisonment, exile, and intensive work, the Rebbe Rayatz had been advised to rest. He delayed it until midsummer, when he took a month-long stay in the seaside resort of Bulduri near Riga. By his own account he did nothing for his health there, continuing to work through correspondence each day.
