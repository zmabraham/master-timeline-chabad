# R' Yosef Yehuda Mashiov's Pesach letter from Shklov

*1846*

From Shklov on Erev Pesach 5606, the Hebron Chabad emissary R' Yosef Yehuda Mashiov wrote to Dr. Eliezer Halevi, secretary to Sir Moses Montefiore (letter published in Kerem Chabad vol. 2). He reported that he had already, in the previous Kislev, transmitted via Vilna to Sir Moses some 600 rubles (roughly 200 guineas) intended to reach Hebron via Damascus, and announced his plan to return home after the festival.
