# Rabbi Mendelsohn during the Lag BaOmer parade (5780)

*1987*

Rabbi Mendelsohn during the Lag BaOmer parade (5780). Rabbi Binyamin Mendelsohn (born 10 Tammuz 5747, 1987) is the gabbai and founder of the Chabad community in Ramah Gimmel-1 in Beit Shemesh, and the community institutions including a daycare, kindergartens, mikvah, and Tiferes Yisrael Yeshiva.
