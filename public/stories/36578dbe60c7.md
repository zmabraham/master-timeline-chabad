# Death of R' Avraham Chaim Rosenbaum in America

*1913*

Born around 5600, R' Avraham Chaim Rosenbaum had spent his youth at the court of the Tzemach Tzedek and later became a leading Chassid of the Maharash and the leader of the Pleshchenitsy Chassidic community. After emigrating and settling in America in 5658, he died there on 25 Kislev 5674 (24 December 1913), as recorded in the Rebbe's footnote in Sefer HaSichot Kayitz 5700.
