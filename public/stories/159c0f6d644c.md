# Illustrated Abarbanel Haggadah with Yiddish translation printed in Fürth

*1750*

Around 5510 (1750), an ornate folio Haggadah was printed in Fürth (Furth, Bavaria) containing the commentary of Don Isaac Abarbanel together with a Kabbalistic commentary. Yiddish translations of the liturgical poems appeared in Tzene-Rene script, and illustrations were placed below the Hebrew text and commentaries. The 16-leaf folio also exists in a variant dated 1757.
