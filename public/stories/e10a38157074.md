# Former librarian Goldenberg visits Levin in Brooklyn after leaving Russia

*1994*

Goldenberg revealed he had been ordered to separate marked books at the Khimki depot, selecting 3,500 of 8,000 books and transferring them to the Moscow library's restricted basement. He claimed most of the remaining 4,500 in Khimki also bore identifying marks but he was instructed not to be thorough. He confirmed 750 of the 2,000 books at the main library bore Schneerson marks, plus nearly 100 at the Muzey Knigi.
