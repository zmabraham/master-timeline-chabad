# Frierdiker Rebbe advises on Lod yeshiva and refugee student placement

*1949*

On 19 Adar 5709 (March 20, 1949), the Frierdiker Rebbe replied to R' Zushe Vilimovsky, the manager designated for the Lod yeshiva, that arriving Anash students should be placed in Tomchei Tmimim in Tel Aviv. He suggested that the Lod building be reserved so it could later serve as the site of the new Tomchei Tmimim branch when conditions were right.
