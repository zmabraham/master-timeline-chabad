# Yarn-trade livelihood begins after Purim 5702

*1942*

After Purim 5702 a stranger left yarn at Levin's home; that same day a Bukharian Jew offered to buy it at double the price. Reciting Tanya by heart to calm his fear of walking the streets without papers, Levin delivered seven kilos and earned 1,400 rubles in one transaction. From then on his livelihood eased, and he partnered with R' Pinchas Levitin in buying a wooden weaving machine.
