# Rebbe announces completion of Moshiach Torah scroll

*1970*

In the winter of 5730 (1970), the Rebbe announced the completion of the Sefer Torah for greeting Moshiach that the Frierdiker Rebbe had begun writing in 5702. The scroll was finished on Yud Shevat. Special pamphlets were printed publicizing the writing, and individuals purchased letters in the Torah.
