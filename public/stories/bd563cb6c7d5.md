# Senate confirms acquittal of the Mitteler Rebbe

*1827*

On 21 Kislev 5588 the Senate dismissed Khovansky's appeal and confirmed the Mogilev high court's verdict. This third and final stage of liberation came only days after the Rebbe's passing on 9 Kislev. The Mogilev court received notification on 7 Shvat 5588, and the question of trial expenses remained the subject of further correspondence into 1828.
