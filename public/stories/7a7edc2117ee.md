# R. Reitchik visits Globoke amid 1939 flight

*1939*

In Motzaei Shabbos Parshas Ki Sisa 5700, while Tomchei Tmimim students were fleeing Poland for Vilna, R. Shmuel Dovid Reitchik wrote to the Rebbe that he had traveled to Globoke on the eve of 14 Teves. He found Mandelbaum torn: he too wished to escape to Vilna but felt unable to abandon the town as its appointed rabbi.
