# Rosenbaum receives leave to visit chassidim for 19 Kislev farbrengen

*1889*

After R' Raza Schneerson's intervention with the Smolensk governor improved Rosenbaum's prison conditions, on the night of 19 Kislev 5650 the warden permitted him to leave the jail entirely. He spent the whole night at the chassidic gathering with anash, and the following day received leave to go into town to purchase oil for Chanukah lights.
