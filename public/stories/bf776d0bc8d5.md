# Tomchei Temimim yeshivah founded in Lubavitch

*1897*

Among the first students chosen was Elchonon Morozov, born 1877, who excelled to the point that the Rashab selected him as the personal study partner for his son the Rayatz. Tomchei Temimim was a revolutionary accomplishment close to the Rashab's heart — both the key to continuity of chasidic teachings and an incubator for new Jewish leadership. It would remain the last bastion of Jewish scholarship functioning in the Soviet Union.
