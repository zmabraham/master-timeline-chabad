# Rayatz writes R' Ashurov about appointing his successor in Drivin

*1929*

On 26 Tevet 5689, the Rebbe Rayatz wrote to R' Avraham Eliyahu Ashurov regarding the appointment of a rabbi and shochet in Drivin to replace him after his planned departure. The Rebbe urged him to make arrangements for the community before leaving for Riga.
