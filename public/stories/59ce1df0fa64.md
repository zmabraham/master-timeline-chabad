# Meeting at Gelman's home with theater director Smilansky

*1991*

Smilansky was surprised by Gubenko's hostile behavior, having known him as liberal and sympathetic before he became Culture Minister. He pledged to speak with everyone connected to the case in private and with force, and to approach Stepanov in Shevardnadze's office. Gelman added that Yakovlev had told him he was considering writing to Gorbachev about the books and raising the matter directly with Gubenko.
