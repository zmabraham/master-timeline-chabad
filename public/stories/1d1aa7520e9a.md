# Soviet army enters Vilna on Shavuot eve

*1940*

During the recitation of Tikkun Leil Shavuot, students heard the heavy chains of Soviet tanks moving on the main street opposite the yeshiva. The Red Army was occupying Lithuania, Latvia and Estonia. A heavy dread fell over the Jewish community, especially Torah students, but the Soviets initially refrained from closing the yeshivot and allowed the Joint to continue supporting Jewish refugees.
