# Rebbe blesses Yavneh society of Livenhof

*1931*

The Frierdiker Rebbe wrote on 20 Sivan 5691 (Igrot vol. 17, no. 6289) expressing joy at Pinchas Mordechai Teitz's report of the founding of Yavneh in Livenhof. He blessed its members for success in their Torah learning program and prayed they would grow in numbers and become a model among Jewish youth.
