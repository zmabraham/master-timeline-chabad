# Rebbe instructs Chaim Gutnick to remain in Australia

*1941*

Gutnick, the only Chabad follower among the Telz refugees, wrote to the Rebbe in Brooklyn asking him to arrange US admission. Instead the Rebbe charged him with a mission: 'Prepare the ground for the arrival of Jewish refugees so that it will be a place of Torah.' At a time when Allied victory was uncertain, Gutnick later called this 'the vision of a true Jewish leader.'
