# The Rebbe endorses the library vigil

*1991*

Rabbi Leibel Groner told the Rebbe about the delegation's continued presence inside the library director's office. The Rebbe answered 'a glaiche zach' (a fitting thing), adding that ultimately the world — including this place — must be conquered. When told about the ongoing vigil outside the library, he encouraged it and said they evidently understood they should stay there until the books were returned.
