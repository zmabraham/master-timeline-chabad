# Second edition of Likkutei Torah printed in Zhitomir

*1866*

The summer of תרכ"ו saw a reprinting of Likkutei Torah, this time managed by the famed mashpia R' Shmuel Betzalel Sheftil. Because it appeared only months after the Tzemach Tzedek's passing, the title page omitted the names of his sons who had financed it and named only the Rashbatz. The third edition (Vilna תרל"ח) later clarified that the second edition too had been funded for the benefit of the Tzemach Tzedek's family.
