# NKVD arrests R' Meir Gurkov in Kharkov on Shushan Purim

*1939*

After the informer David Itze appeared in Kharkov and lodged with R' Meir Gurkov, the family realized danger and Gurkov briefly hid at his son-in-law Israel Yehuda's apartment on the city's outskirts. On Shushan Purim 5699 the NKVD raided Gurkov's home, arresting him and three other Anash members under harsh conditions and interrogating their sons; the fear was so severe that family members could not look one another in the eye.
