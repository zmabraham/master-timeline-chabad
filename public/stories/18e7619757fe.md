# R. Avraham of Kaliska visits Tiberias for Rosh Chodesh Shvat

*1779*

After receiving repeated appeals from both Jerusalem and Tiberias, R. Avraham HaKohen of Kaliska traveled to Tiberias for Rosh Chodesh Shvat 5539. The trip was part of ongoing efforts to consolidate the chassidic presence in the holy cities and respond to community needs. This visit is documented in a letter that researcher Yaari originally misdated to 5538.
