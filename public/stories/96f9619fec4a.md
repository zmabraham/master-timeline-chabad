# Rebbe Rayatz arrives in New York

*1940*

On 9 Adar II 5700 (March 19, 1940), the Rebbe Rayatz arrived in New York with his household and secretariat, completing the rescue from Europe. His arrival formalized the long-planned transfer of Chabad's worldwide headquarters from Eastern Europe to the United States, where Agudath Chassidei Chabad had already been organized since 5684 (1924).
