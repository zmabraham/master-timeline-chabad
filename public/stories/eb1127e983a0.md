# Rebbe asks R' Yosef Goldstein to undertake a Krakow mission

*1931*

Building on his Warsaw conversations about strengthening Tomchei Temimim alumni scattered across Polish towns, the Rebbe noted that Goldstein lived not far from Krakow and might be able to visit the city periodically. He inquired whether Goldstein had already acted on their discussion and whether his schedule allowed him to take on a focused Krakow mission of several weeks.
