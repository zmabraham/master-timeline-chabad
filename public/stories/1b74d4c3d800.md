# Rebbe Rayatz blesses Tomchei Tmimim students for Yud-Tes Kislev

*1926*

In a letter dated 12 Kislev 5687, the Rebbe Rayatz blessed the students of Tomchei Tmimim in Warsaw, along with their mashpi'im, mashgichim, and administrators, in advance of Yud-Tes Kislev. He invoked the path of the Rebbeim who had given their souls to disseminate Chassidus, and prayed they would elevate themselves and strengthen the world's pillars through Torah and mitzvos.
