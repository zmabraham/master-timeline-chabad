# Gubenko writes birthday letter to the Lubavitcher Rebbe

*1991*

Meeting Cunin and Kogan in Madrid, Gubenko drafted a letter for Yud-Aleph Nissan describing the greatness of the Rebbe and the dedication of Chabad chassidim. Regarding the library he wrote that the books were important to the Jewish people and to Russia, that a committee was now working on the question, and expressed confidence that its decision by May 1 would satisfy everyone. The Chabad emissaries faxed copies to Moscow and to the Rebbe in New York.
