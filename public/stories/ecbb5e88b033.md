# Rayatz receives Tzemach Tzedek portrait from R.M.A. Stillerman

*1924*

On 4 Shevat 5684 (10 January 1924), R.M.A. Stillerman gave the Rebbe Rayatz a print of the Tzemach Tzedek's portrait. The print is one of the earliest preserved in the Chabad library and exhibits more of the Rebbe's chair, feet and bookcase than the well-known Metz lithograph, suggesting both derive from a still-earlier original.
