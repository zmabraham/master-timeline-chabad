# Louis Lamm receives the Dr. Baer estate of calendars

*1897*

Dr. Seligman Isaac Baer of Biebrich, the renowned masoretic scholar, died in 5657 (1897). Following his death, his bound estate of calendars came into the possession of Louis Lamm of Amsterdam. Part of the Chabad library's collection of ancient calendars — over a thousand items in some four hundred varieties — traces back to this Baer estate via Lamm.
