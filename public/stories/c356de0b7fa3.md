# Tomchei Tmimim Yeshiva founded in Hebron

*1912*

In 5672 (1911/12), the Rebbe Rashab founded the Torat Emet yeshiva in Hebron, sending a special mashpia and several outstanding students from Tomchei Tmimim Lubavitch to lead it. The yeshiva would later close during the deportations of World War I.
