# Mittler Rebbe's passing and Tzemach Tzedek's nesius

*1827*

Following the passing of his father-in-law the Mittler Rebbe on 9 Kislev 5588 (November 1827), R. Menachem Mendel Schneersohn assumed the leadership of Chabad chassidut. He served as Rebbe for 38 years, until his own passing on 13 Nisan 5626 (1866).
