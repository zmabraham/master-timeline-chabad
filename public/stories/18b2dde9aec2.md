# Rabbi Mordechai Menachem Mendel Horenstein murdered at Treblinka

*1942*

Survivor Mordechai Unrad, returning from his work assignment, found that Rabbi Horenstein had been removed from his barrack along with a group of other Jews and led to the crematoria. The Frierdiker Rebbe's nearly four-year rescue effort — visas, exit permits, intermediaries in Italy, Germany, Sweden, and the State Department — had been thwarted at every turn by the Germans' refusal to grant exit permits.
