# Rosh HaShanah under German bombing in Warsaw

*1939*

At 5 p.m. on 29 Elul, bombs struck the Muranowska Street building. Thrown across the courtyard by a blast, the Rebbe's family briefly feared a gas attack. With sunset approaching, a Jewish wagon driver risked arrest to transport them to 8 Gegrnitche Street. The Rebbe prayed Maariv without a minyan in barely a half hour, exhausted and unable to stand, yet reassuring everyone around him.
