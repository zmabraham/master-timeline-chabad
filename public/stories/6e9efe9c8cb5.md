# Rotstein and Zalmanov depart Riga for America

*1940*

On 19 Elul 5700, R. Mordechai Chefetz wrote to the Frierdiker Rebbe that he was going to escort R. Moshe Leib Rotstein and R. Shmuel Zalmanov, who were leaving Riga that day at 7 PM en route to America. The journey would take them through Russia and Japan.
