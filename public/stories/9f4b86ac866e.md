# Otwock branch closes after one year

*1927*

By spring 5687 the Relief organization had stopped supporting Polish yeshivos, claiming Polish Jewry could sustain them itself. Warsaw Tomchei Tmimim could only fund a reduced two-class operation in Otwock with lowered salaries; Blizinski withdrew, and no Tomchei Tmimim yeshiva existed in Otwock until the central yeshiva relocated there in 5696 (1936).
