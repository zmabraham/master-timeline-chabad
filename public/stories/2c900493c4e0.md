# Rebbe Rayatz approves new shipping route in three telegrams

*1940*

On 26 Tammuz / 1 August 1940, the Rebbe Rayatz dispatched three telegrams instructing that the library be redirected to Eydtkuhnen and on through Stettin to Stockholm, where it would be held until shipment to New York became feasible. R' Chefetz confirmed three days later that the cargo had been halted and rerouted as instructed.
