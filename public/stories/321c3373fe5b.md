# Paris Haggadah of the Rebbe with French translation

*1985*

In 5745 (1985), the Lubavitcher Rebbe's Haggadah with 'Likutei Ta'amim u'Minhagim' was issued in Paris with a French translation. The 191-page octavo edition (plus 173 French pages) served the growing French Chabad community. It marked a key expansion of the Rebbe's Haggadah into European languages.
