# Eliezerov reports cholera outbreak in Caucasus from Samarkand

*1904*

From Samarkand in Bukhara on 3 Tevet 5665 (December 11, 1904), Eliezerov wrote to his brother-in-law R' M.M. Naeh that this trip had been the hardest of his four missions combined. He had agreed to take on the Caucasus mission for the Sephardic community but cholera had broken out there, forcing him to plan a return route via Baku and Georgia if possible, otherwise straight back to Lubavitch as the Rashab had instructed.
