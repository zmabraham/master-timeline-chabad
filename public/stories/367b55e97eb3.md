# Rebbe Rashab leaves Lubavitch for the last time

*1915*

On Sunday, 16 Marcheshvan 5676, at 11:00 a.m., the Rebbe Rashab left Lubavitch as the German advance pressed eastward. Students of Tomchei Temimim, the staff, the town rav, townspeople, and even non-Jewish neighbors gathered in the courtyard to take leave of him. The stationmaster at Rudnya personally supervised the loading of the family's 97 puds of luggage onto the train.
