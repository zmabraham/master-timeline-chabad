# Joint Hebron sages letter printed in HaMagid

*1870*

In Tevet 1870 (טבת תר"ל), the leading rabbis of Hebron - both Sephardic and Chabad Ashkenazim - signed a joint appeal describing the community's plight. The letter was published as a supplement in HaMagid on 15 Adar I 1870, urging Jewish donors to contribute generously to support the suffering Holy Land communities.
