# Rebbe details ordeals in Russia in long letter from Riga

*1928*

He wrote, 'I fled the bad, but I have not yet found the good.' He continued, 'I was driven from my country and forced to separate from my beloved brothers and friends.' The Rebbe expressed his desire 'to live in our country of Russia, where people are putting their lives on the line for Torah and observance in the midst of deprivation.'
