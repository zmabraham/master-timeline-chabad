# Rebbe receives Kiryat Gat's key at Erev Rosh Hashana farbrengen

*1962*

On the night of Erev Rosh Hashana 5723 (September 18, 1962), Rabbi Yaakov Peles delivered Kiryat Gat's city key to the Rebbe during the welcome farbrengen for visiting guests in 770. The Rebbe responded with a sicha explaining the symbolism of a city key — that it opens and closes the city gates to protect it from outside threats (Toras Menachem Hisvaaduyos vol. 34, p. 324).
