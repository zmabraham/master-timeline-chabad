# R. Elchanan Yakubovitz emigrates to the Land of Israel

*1933*

In 5693 (1933) R. Elchanan Yakubovitz left Latvia for Eretz Yisrael, settling in Jerusalem. There he published the second volume of Even Shimon and remained until his death. He represents one of the few Lubavitch-era rabbinical figures whose career spanned both the late Russian period and the British Mandate yishuv.
