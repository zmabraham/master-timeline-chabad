# Rayatz departs Riga for tour of Polish yeshiva branches

*1931*

On 9 Tammuz 5691, the Rebbe Rayatz left Riga to visit the three branches of Tomchei Tmimim under his guidance in Poland: Warsaw, Vilna, and Lodz. He arrived in Warsaw on 8 Tammuz, intending to make personal visits to the yeshivas and meet privately with students.
