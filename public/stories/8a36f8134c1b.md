# Hebron Chabad community's letter to Montefiore on agricultural plan

*1839*

Sir Moses Montefiore traveled to Eretz Yisrael with a plan to lease land from Mohammed Ali for fifty years and settle Jews in agricultural villages. The Hebron Chabad community responded with an elaborately phrased letter dated 4 Tammuz endorsing the plan in principle but explaining that most of their members were elderly Torah scholars unsuited for farming, and that direct financial support would be a greater mitzvah for their community.
