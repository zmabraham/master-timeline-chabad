# Rabbi Anikster (5784). Rabbi Hillel Anikster (born in 5755, 1995) is a Chabad mashpia, Torah editor, head of the 'Tzemach Tzedek (Tzfat)' kollel, mashpia at the 'Ohel Menachem Mendel' yeshiva in Tzfat, and a halacha lecturer at the 'Beit Menachem Mendel Tzfat' yeshiva in Tzfat
