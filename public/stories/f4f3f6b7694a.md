# Rayatz writes Boston Rabbi Rabinowitz seeking Senator Borah's intervention

*1932*

Rayatz asks whether Rabbi Rabinowitz's son Asher can secure a letter from Senator Borah to Soviet Foreign Commissar Litvinov requesting return of the library according to Rayatz's existing inventory. At the time, the US had not yet recognized the Soviet government, and the senator's intervention did not produce the hoped-for result.
