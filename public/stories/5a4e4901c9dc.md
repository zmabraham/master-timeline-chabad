# Rebbe Rayatz becomes a citizen of the United States

*1949*

On Thursday 16 Adar 5709 (parashas Ki Sisa), Rebbe Rayatz became a citizen of the United States. The ceremony took place at his home with Judges Reifel and Kleinfeld officiating, witnessed by Rabbi Kazarnovsky, attorney Yekusiel Kramer, and Rabbi Chadakov; the proceedings were organized by his son-in-law the future Rebbe. The Rebbe expressed hope that the citizenship would bring health and success to him, his family, and his work in spreading Chassidus and strengthening Judaism.
