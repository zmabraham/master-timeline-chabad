# Rashag begins teaching Chassidus to Tomchei Tmimim students

*1939*

Rabbi Yisrael Yaakov Yeber, the rosh yeshiva, reported at the start of the year that the Rashag had recently begun speaking with the talmidim in Chassidus. The Rashag came to regard one of the senior students, Mendel Tenenboim, as outstanding among the talmidim.
