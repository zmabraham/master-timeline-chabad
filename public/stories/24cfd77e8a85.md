# Pesher allocates Siberian-city revenue 41% Chabad, 59% Kollel Reisin

*1908*

Per a longstanding pesher with the Vaad HaKlali, four of the five Siberian cities were treated as shared between Kollel Reisin and Kollel Chabad rather than belonging to Reisin exclusively. The Tammuz 5668 entry in the Kollel Chabad letter register pinpoints the division as Chabad 41% and Reisin 59%. The fifth city, Achinsk, is not mentioned in this allocation.
