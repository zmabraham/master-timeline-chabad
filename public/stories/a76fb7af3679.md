# Siyum HaRambam Tel Aviv 5769. Rabbi Gerlitzky shakes the hand

*1954*

Siyum HaRambam Tel Aviv 5769. Rabbi Gerlitzky shakes the hand of Tel Aviv mayor Mr. Ron Huldai (5778). Rabbi Yosef Shmuel Yehoshua Gerlitzky (born on 26 Cheshvan 5714, 1954) is the Rebbe's chief shliach to Tel Aviv, one of the Rebbe's shluchim
