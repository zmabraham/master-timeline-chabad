# Rosenbaum's halachic letter on Birkas HaLevana through a window

*1888*

Dated 13 Elul 5648 (1888) and addressed to a scholarly correspondent, the letter explores whether one may bless the new moon through window glass when it is impossible to go outside or open the window – for example in winter or when sewage is being removed nearby. Rosenbaum weighs the Dvar Shmuel's prohibition against the Magen Avraham's ruling permitting the havdalah candle blessing through glass, and proposes distinguishing between covering the viewer's eye and covering the object viewed. He also requests that the addressee acquire him a fine Genoese esrog and three matching hadas branches for Sukkos.
