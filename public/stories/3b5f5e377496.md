# Gentiles seal the Rashab's underground mikvah in Rostov

*1924*

Following the Rayatz's forced relocation in Nissan 5684, the new occupants of the Bratsky Pereulok house sealed up the ritual bath that the Rashab had constructed in the basement and laid the basement floor over it. The mikvah — a 'mikvah on mikvah' design the Rashab had personally specified in detailed letters from Slavyansk — would remain hidden for more than seventy years.
