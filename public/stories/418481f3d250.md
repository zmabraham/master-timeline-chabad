# Rebbe directs Rabbi Yakovshvili to study shechitah and work in Kutaisi

*1927*

The Rebbe wrote to Rabbi Simon Yakovshvili directing him to travel to Kutaisi in Soviet Georgia and study the art of shechitah. He told him to defer pursuing a shidduch and to consult with Avraham Levik Slavin and Schneur Zalman Altshuler on educational projects, requesting regular updates on all accomplishments.
