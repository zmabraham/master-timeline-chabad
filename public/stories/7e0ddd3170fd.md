# Mitzvah Tank name adopted by the Rebbe

*1974*

In 5734 (1974) the Rebbe adopted the name 'Tank HaMitzvos' (Mitzvah Tank) for the outreach vehicles used by shluchim to bring Yiddishkeit to every corner, with their operators called 'tankistim.' The Rebbe regularly handed out coins or bills for tzedakah through them to express appreciation for their work.
