# Gerer Rebbe escapes Poland to Trieste

*1940*

In a letter to attorney Butler on Erev Pesach 5700, Rashag noted that the Gerer Rebbe and 14 of his people had obtained German exit permits and reached Trieste roughly ten days earlier. He urged that the same channel be investigated for his brother-in-law Mordechai Menachem Mendel Horenstein, who was trapped with his wife Sheina in Otwock.
