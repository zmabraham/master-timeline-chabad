# Admor R' Shmuel of Slonim visits Rebbe Rashab

*1902*

R' Shmuel of Slonim and Rabbi Yosef Shlifer of Slonim had previously sent an emissary, R' Eliezer Yosef Klatzky, in late winter 5660 to engage the Rebbe Rashab. In Tevet 5662 R' Shmuel himself came to Lubavitch to plan a general organization called Machzikei HaDat that would unite multiple Chasidic and rabbinic factions in defense of traditional Judaism.
