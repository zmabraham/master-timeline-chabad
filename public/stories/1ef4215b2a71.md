# Mogilev high court acquits the Mitteler Rebbe (11 Kislev 5587)

*1826*

The second stage of liberation came when the Mogilev high court, after reviewing the Babinovichi peace court's findings from investigations in multiple towns, formally cleared the Rebbe of every accusation. The verdict closed the case at the provincial level, but it still required ratification by Governor Khovansky. He instead lodged an appeal to the Senate in St. Petersburg, prolonging the legal struggle by another year.
