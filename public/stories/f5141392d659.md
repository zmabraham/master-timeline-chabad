# Devorah Rachel Kogan writes Rebbe about opening Chabad library in Moscow

*1991*

The Rebbe responded that opening a new Chabad library in Russia could provoke opponents to argue the Schneerson books should stay in Russia rather than leave it. He framed the argument as so distorted yet predictable that Chabad should avoid raising any such ideas. This ruling guided Levin's opposition to subsequent wing proposals for years afterward.
