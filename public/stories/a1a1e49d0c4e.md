# Rebbe visits Rokiškis and addresses gathering of women

*1931*

Rabbi Feigin recorded that 'in the wake of the request, the Rebbe spoke to a gathering of women.' Historically, chasidic Rebbes only addressed men in public; the Rokiškis talk was a deliberate step toward empowering women in Chabad. The same year, the Rebbe lamented the lowered standards of Jewish scholarship in modern integrated schools.
