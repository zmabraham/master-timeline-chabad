# Stalin deports tens of thousands of Polish Jews to Siberia

*1939*

Many of the deportees perished from hunger, illness and cold. Two years later, when Hitler turned on the USSR, Stalin made peace with the Polish government-in-exile and released the surviving Polish citizens — who poured southward into the warm Central Asian republics, including Samarkand, where they would later collide with Lubavitcher refugees at clandestine Yom Kippur minyanim.
