# Mandelbaum sent to Krakow to teach chassidus

*1931*

In summer 5691 (1931), R. Yitzchak Mandelbaum, a Tomchei Tmimim student in Warsaw, was sent by the Rebbe to Krakow for a period to deliver chassidic discourses. He also attempted to open a branch of Tomchei Tmimim in his own town of Matzayov, though that effort did not materialize.
