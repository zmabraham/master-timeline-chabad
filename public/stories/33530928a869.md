# Jews of Dankra (Gostini) murdered by Nazis

*1941*

According to 'Hashmadat Yehudim B'Latvia' p. 198, on Erev Tisha B'Av 5701 the Jews of Dankra/Gostini were killed by the Nazis. The Frierdiker Rebbe, lacking firsthand information, later asked in coded language on 23 Adar II 5703 about Himmelstein of Dankra and R. Berchen ('Refael's son-in-law'), seeking news of their fate.
