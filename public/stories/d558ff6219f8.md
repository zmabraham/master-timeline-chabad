# Author begins studies at Polotsk Tomchei Tmimim yeshiva

*1928*

About thirty older bochurim were studying in Polotsk under rosh yeshiva R' Yechezkel Himmelstein (later murdered in Riga) and the celebrated mashgiach R' Shlomo Chaim Kesselman. The author and his fellow townsman Moshe Roxin — son of the Zlobin shochet R' Refoel Eliezer Roxin — were placed in lodgings ('eshel') and joined the shiur on the Bava Metzia mishna 'ha-zahav koneh et ha-kesef.' Two weeks in, the mashgiach told the author he could remain but that Moshe, weaker in learning, must transfer to Vitebsk; the author chose to accompany his friend.
