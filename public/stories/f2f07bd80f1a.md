# Rebbe Rashab writes letter against Zionism

*1899*

The letter, preserved as Igrot Kodesh letter 86, articulated the Rebbe's opposition to the political Zionist movement on religious and ideological grounds. It was written the same summer that he dispatched his emissary R' Eliezer Moshe Madievsky to the Machzikei HaDat office in Kovno to help unite traditional Jewish leadership against both Zionism and the Haskalah.
