# Frierdiker Rebbe writes on 25 Tammuz 5698 about Polish yeshivos ketanos

*1938*

He emphasized that no Polish city had organized Jewish chinuch outside of secular Tarbut schools and that small sums could fund roshei yeshiva for many new yeshivos ketanos. He cited the demonstrated success of existing Tomchei Tmimim preparatory branches as proof of concept.
