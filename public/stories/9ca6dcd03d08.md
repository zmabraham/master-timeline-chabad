# Frierdiker Rebbe writes letter endorsing Rabbi Eliezer Silver

*1946*

The letter reached the Anash refugees in Łódź a day or two before Rabbi Silver's visit and resolved their hesitation about how to receive a non-chassidic American rav. The Rebbe praised Silver's self-sacrifice in saving Jews and ranked him above factional politics. Receiving the letter after years of severed contact was experienced as the first overt miracle the refugees saw from the Rebbe after leaving Russia.
