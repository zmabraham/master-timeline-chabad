# Purim mass Megillah reading at Ezras Achim hall in Moscow

*1991*

After Mincha and the half-shekel offering at the Marina Roscha synagogue, the diarist traveled to a major Purim event in a large hall organized by Ezras Achim. He read the Megillah for around a thousand attendees and stayed about an additional hour. The program included separate adult and children's activities in adjoining halls.
