# Tzemach Tzedek defines the scope of Torah Or

*1812*

In a letter, the Tzemach Tzedek explained that Torah Or specifically draws from the Alter Rebbe's maamarim of this period because it was during these years that the Chabad explanations of his thought were elaborated in depth. The Mitteler Rebbe similarly dated the regular flow of public discourses as beginning around 1795/6.
