# Cantonist conscription law begins for Russian Jewry

*1827*

From 5587 (1827) onwards, the cruel cantonist law forced Jewish communities to supply fixed quotas of Jewish children — sometimes as many as 30 per thousand Jews — who were dispatched to the army for 25 years and completely cut off from their parents and Jewish life. When communities could not meet the quotas, khappers were sent to seize Jewish children from the streets amidst the cries of children and parents.
