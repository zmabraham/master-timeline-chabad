# Colel Chabad Jerusalem board pledges allegiance to Rebbe Rashab

*1920*

The decision marked a shift from the Kopust branch's sway over the organization back to the main Chabad-Lubavitch line. After the Rashab's passing, the board reaffirmed the pledge to the Rebbe Rayatz, though tensions over governance and finances persisted until the Rayatz visited in 1929.
