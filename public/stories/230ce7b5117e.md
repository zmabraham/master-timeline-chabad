# Frierdiker Rebbe directs memoirist to buy a Linotype machine

*1948*

The Frierdiker Rebbe knew the memoirist had begun printing the kuntresim and was paying others for typesetting. In yechidus he instructed him to purchase a Linotype machine. When the memoirist objected that the $3,000 price (an enormous sum at the time) was beyond him, the Rebbe answered, 'You don't need to pay it all — a bit on the mountain and a bit on the worry!'
