# Students cable Rebbe on Chol HaMoed Sukkot

*1945*

On the fourth day of Chol HaMoed Sukkot 5706, the students sent a telegram in reply to the Frierdiker Rebbe via R. Y. Zuber, explaining that the American consul in Shanghai was not yet handling visa matters. They asked whether all formalities could be prepared in advance before applications could be filed locally, hedging against further delays.
