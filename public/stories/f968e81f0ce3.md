# Yoreh Deah responsa of the Tzemach Tzedek printed

*1874*

The Yoreh Deah set appeared in 5634 as part of the ongoing publication of the Tzemach Tzedek's halachic corpus. After printing the second volume, three additional responsa on the laws of niddah and mikvaos were discovered and added as 'omissions' at the end of the third volume — an early instance of the pattern of supplements and addenda that would characterize the entire series.
