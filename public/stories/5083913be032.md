# Chabad delegation confronts Yeltsin and Kozyrev in Rome over books

*1991*

Italian Prime Minister Andreotti had already raised the books with Yeltsin to an unsatisfactory reply. At the businessmen's meeting Yeltsin took the Chabad letter without responding. In a recorded exchange Kozyrev told Aharonov he personally favored returning the books but the moment was too turbulent, while Petrov claimed the library deserved compensation for years of storage and falsely asserted the ruling required the collection to stay in Russia.
