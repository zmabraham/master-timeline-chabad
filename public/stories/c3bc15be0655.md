# Levitin appointed to lead rabbinical school in Nevel

*1924*

After roughly ten years in Kutaisi, Levitin was appointed by the Rebbe Rayatz in 5684 (1924) to establish and direct a beis midrash for rabbinical training in Nevel, attracting men in their 20s, 30s, and 40s to study laws of practical halacha.
