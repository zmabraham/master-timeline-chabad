# Chabad shlichus family relocates from hotel to Tikhvinskaya apartment

*1991*

Hotel Molodyozhnaya was over an hour and a half on foot from the AGUH shul, making it impractical for the Yamim Noraim. The new apartment sat about half an hour from the AGUH shul and a similar distance from Marina Roshcha. The author planned to spend Rosh Hashana and Yom Kippur at the AGUH shul.
