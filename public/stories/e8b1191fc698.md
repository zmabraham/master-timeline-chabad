# Two-hour meeting with Tolstikov on Schneersohn library access

*1991*

On 10 Iyar 5751 (April 24, 1991), the delegation met Mr. Tolstikov for some two hours. He stated the Schneersohn library issue was an ideological-political position held at the very top of the government — not by the deputy or minister of culture, nor by Prime Minister Pavlov, but by President Gorbachev himself. He compared it to Gorbachev's recent trip to Japan, where despite Russia needing 22 billion dollars and not needing the two small Kuril islands, no agreement was reached. Tolstikov stepped out to consult Babushkin and returned admitting he had been mistaken: the bar on the Chabad team helping librarians identify the Schneersohn collection had come from above, pending the commission's final ruling. He accepted a letter from the four-member delegation and promised to forward their requests to the Minister of Culture.
