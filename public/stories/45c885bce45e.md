# Birth of Meir Shalom Kugel during Rosenbaum's imprisonment

*1889*

R' Shlomo Kugel recorded the birth of his son Meir Shalom on Wednesday, 20 Sivan 5649 (19 June 1889). The birth came while the child's grandfather R' Avraham Chaim Rosenbaum was imprisoned in Smolensk; R' Hendel Kugel traveled to Pleshchenitsy for the brit, and R' Avraham Chaim sent congratulations from prison on Sunday Parshat Pinchas.
