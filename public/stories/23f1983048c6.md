# Rebbe Rashab writes about plans for new Hebron yeshiva

*1908*

In a letter from Cheshvan 5669 the Rebbe Rashab explained that although a Chabad yeshiva already existed in Hebron, it was 'small and not what we truly intend.' He envisioned a yeshiva that would attract God-fearing avreichim devoted to Torah study under proper supervision. This vision would be realized three years later with the founding of Yeshivat Torat Emet.
