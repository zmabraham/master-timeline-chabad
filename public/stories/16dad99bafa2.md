# Tomchei Temimim Moscow students seek Rebbe's approval for Lubavitch house

*1988*

At the start of 5749 (late 1988), yeshiva students from Tomchei Temimim in Moscow contacted the Rebbe's secretariat requesting approval to purchase a house in the town of Lubavitch. Within a few days, they received the Rebbe's response along with several hundred dollars as his participation in the purchase. The Rebbe wanted to be personally involved in establishing a Chabad presence in Lubavitch.
