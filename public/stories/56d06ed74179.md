# Toras Emes yeshivah established in Hebron

*1911*

Considered a branch of Tomchei Temimim in Lubavitch, the new yeshivah aimed to invigorate the study of chasidic teachings in Eretz Yisrael and create a new cadre of Jewish leaders there. Just as the Rashab hoped Tomchei Temimim graduates would set a new direction for Russian Jewry, he hoped Toras Emes graduates would do so in the Holy Land.
