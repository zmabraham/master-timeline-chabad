# Meir Hendel calls for Tomchei Temimim gathering in Radom

*1940*

Hendel, a student of Tomchei Temimim Otwock who had failed to cross the border into Vilna, returned to his home city of Radom. He called for senior students such as Yaakov Falkenfeld to gather on Yud-Tes Kislev to found a Radom branch of the yeshiva, but heavy snow and cold prevented anyone from reaching him at that time. The plan would later materialize as a clandestine yeshiva in the Radom ghetto.
