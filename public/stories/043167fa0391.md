# Death of R' Baruch, father of the Alter Rebbe

*1791*

After self-exile in his later years, R' Baruch settled in the town of Sulish (Sulish/Sulez) in Hungary, where he died on 8 Tishrei 5552 (September 16, 1791). His tombstone inscription records his name as 'Yisrael Baruch ben Avraham' — supporting the tradition reported in Eden Tzion that he originally bore the name Yisrael but dropped it after joining the Baal Shem Tov's circle out of deference to his teacher.
