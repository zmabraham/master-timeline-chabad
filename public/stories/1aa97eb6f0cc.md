# Hebron agreement: shdarus to Italy, Sephard, and Persia

*1830*

In 5590 (1830), the Sephardic and Chabad communities of Hebron signed a detailed agreement governing joint shdarus operations to Italy ('Frankia'), Persia ('Urmili and Arabistan'), and other lands. Italy's proceeds were to be divided 80% to Sephardim and 20% to Ashkenazim, while collections from Sephardic and Persian cities, fundraised by Ashkenazi shadarim, would be divided three-quarters to Ashkenazim and one-quarter to Sephardim. This agreement formalized cooperation alongside the Pekuam's centralized fundraising in Amsterdam.
