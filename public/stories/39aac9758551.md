# Rayatz writes to R. Michael Vilensky to commemorate his father

*1939*

The Rayatz wrote to R. Michael Vilensky requesting that he prepare a memorial article about his father, the Chasid R. Chaim Dover of Kremenchug, for the ninth volume of Hatamim then being edited. World War broke out shortly afterward, the issue never appeared, and Vilensky's reply never reached its destination.
