# District elections in Chernigov gubernia

*1909*

The week after the elections in the city of Chernigov, the remaining districts (uyezds) of the Chernigov gubernia held their elections. Attached to the Rebbe Rayatz's letter dated the night of 27 Cheshvan was the list of elected delegates, which included Rabbi Menachem Mendel Chen, son of the Rad"tz, chosen from his town of Nezhin. He would later play a major role in the conference.
