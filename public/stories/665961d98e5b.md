# Central yeshiva moves to Otwock

*1936*

In 5696 (1935-1936), the central Tomchei Tmimim yeshiva (with the older students) moved from Warsaw to Otwock, a resort town and known recuperative location near the Polish capital. Three lower classes remained in Warsaw, while the central yeshiva occupied several buildings in a healthful pine forest setting in Otwock.
