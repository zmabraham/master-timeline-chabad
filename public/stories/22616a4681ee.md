# Israel Yehuda dispatched to teach underground yeshiva in Krivoy Rog

*1937*

Following a clandestine meeting at R' Zalman Pruss's home in Moscow, R' Yonah Cohen organized the dispatching of older bochurim to teach groups of four boys per location, reportedly on the Rebbe's instruction. Israel Yehuda took over from Michoel Teitelbaum in Krivoy Rog, teaching Perek Shnayim Ochzin to boys including Leibke Mochkin, Yoel Duchman, Mendel Raskin, and Yehoshua Katzenelenbogen-Raskin at R' Yoel Itkin's home, which served the cause with extraordinary mesirat nefesh.
