# Reitchik departs Warsaw after parting blessing from the Rebbe

*1939*

R. Shmuel Dovid Reitchik later wrote a detailed report of his escape route. After parting blessings from the Rebbe Rayatz on the night of 18 Kislev, his group farbrenged on 19 Kislev in Praga, then traveled to Siedlce. After many obstacles they crossed the German-Soviet border on the night of 26 Kislev and reached Slonim by 29 Kislev.
