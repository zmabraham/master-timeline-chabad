# Rebbe directs library packing for Warsaw move

*1933*

In separate letters to R. Chaim Liberman and R. Moshe Leib Rothstein, the Rebbe specified wooden-box packing, censorship approvals, freight-company arrangements and even the labeling — directing that crates be numbered rather than marked 'Bibliotheca Lubavitch.' A subsequent letter on 22 Av to the Rebbetzin added detailed instructions about manuscript shipment and the yellow case of his father's writings.
