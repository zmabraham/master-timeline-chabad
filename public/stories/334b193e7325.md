# R' Eliezer Shimon Kaznovsky and family settle in Hebron

*1873*

R' Eliezer Shimon Kaznovsky, son of Sheterna Riza Slonim, made aliyah from abroad with his family in 5633 and settled in Hebron. His grandson R' Shlomo Yehuda Leib Eliezerov (known as Shila) came with him and would go on to become a leader of the Hebron community, its rabbi, and an administrator of Kollel Chabad from 5663 onward.
