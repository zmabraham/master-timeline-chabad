# Wedding of Moshe Aryeh Leib Ginzburg and Devorah Leah, daughter of Maharash

*1872*

In the summer of 5632 (1872), R' Moshe Aryeh Leib Ginzburg, son of the philanthropist Avraham Zalman Ginzburg of Vitebsk, married Devorah Leah, daughter of the Rebbe Maharash of Lubavitch. The match sealed a family connection between the Schneersohn dynasty and a leading Vitebsk merchant family.
