# Israel Yehuda relocates to Voronezh to study under R' Betzalel Wilshansky

*1935*

R' Yonah Cohen sent the older bochurim approaching marriage age to receive guidance from R' Betzalel Wilshansky in Voronezh. Israel Yehuda joined Michoel Teitelbaum, Shaul Prafoisker (Steinbach), and Shalom Wilenkin there to learn, while also tutoring R' Betzalel's twelve-year-old son Folka (later R' Refoel Wilshansky).
