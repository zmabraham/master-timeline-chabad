# R' Yehoshua Serero appointed cantor of Ibn Ravuach synagogue in Fes

*1722*

After the death of cantor R' Yaakov ibn Danan, the congregation had endorsed Serero's appointment, but a later writ claimed Ibn Danan had instructed that R' Yosef ibn Shikrun succeed him. The Fes beit din ruled the earlier congregational decision authoritative and barred any other cantor. The dispute was significant enough that the rabbis of Meknes - including R' Moshe Toledano, R' Yaakov ibn Tzur, and R' Moshe Berdugo - co-signed in support.
