# Rebbe Rayatz proposes dismissing R. David Teiblum to cut costs

*1932*

Facing the financial crunch, the Rebbe Rayatz proposed to the Warsaw administration that R. David Teiblum be released and his class be turned into a mechina taught by R. Shneur Zalman Gurary. The administration ultimately decided it was impossible to dismiss him mid-term and kept the arrangement unchanged.
