# Cheshvan 5683 census finds only 413 Jews remaining in Hebron

*1922*

Yisrael Kaplan reported in HaTor (issue 31) that a careful census in Cheshvan 5683 found just 413 Jews in Hebron — 306 Sephardim and 107 Ashkenazim. The Chabad kollel, once first in seniority and stature with 165 souls in 5678, had nearly ceased to exist; only nine remaining families received a meager distribution administered from Jerusalem.
