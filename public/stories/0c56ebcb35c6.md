# Rebbe Rayatz arrives in Jerusalem

*1929*

More than ten percent of Jerusalem's Jewish population turned out to welcome him. That same afternoon he made his way through the Old City on foot to the Western Wall, where 1,500 gathered and hundreds more watched from rooftops. 'I cried hot tears,' he wrote his family. 'Standing next to the Wall of the Beit HaMikdash, everything changes.'
