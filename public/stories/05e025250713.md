# Uvarov orders covert surveillance of the Tzemach Tzedek

*1841*

On 7 July 1841 (Rosh Chodesh Av 5601), Count Uvarov replied to Benckendorff that although Schneersohn had not committed unlawful acts and seemed harmless, secret surveillance should still be imposed on him. This was especially needed while the government implemented its new Jewish education plans. The two papers about Mendel were returned with the directive.
