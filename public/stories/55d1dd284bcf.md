# Zikhron Yerushalayim published in Jerusalem

*1876*

In 5636 R' Shneur Zalman Mendelovitch published Zikhron Yerushalayim in Jerusalem, which included a description of the Chabad Ashkenazi synagogue in Jerusalem. He recorded that the synagogue was founded with donations from Sir Abdullah Meir Sassoon and his brothers Eliyahu, Reuven, Avraham Shalom, and Sliman — sons of Sir David Sassoon — through the intermediation of the author and R' Zalman ben R' Nachum Yosef.
