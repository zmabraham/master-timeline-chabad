# Bar Mitzvah of the Frierdiker Rebbe in Lubavitch

*1893*

The celebration brought a large gathering of Chassidim to Lubavitch and used the same large hall that had hosted the 5652 Hornstein wedding. Four years later in 5657 the same hall would host the future Frierdiker Rebbe's wedding. This event marked the coming of age of Chabad's next generation of leadership.
