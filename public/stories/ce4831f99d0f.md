# Achos Tmimim chapter opened in Dvinsk

*1940*

R. Baruch Eliyahu Peletz reported to the Rebbe that the chapter had begun in Dvinsk (renamed Daugavpils). Six girls attended; some committed to come weekly and others said they needed to consult their parents. Mrs. Esther Pinkovich corresponded with the Riga central office for guidance on curriculum and method.
