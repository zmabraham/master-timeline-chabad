# Aliyah of R' Yisrael Yaffe the Kapust printer

*1823*

In 5583 (1823), R' Yisrael ben Yitzchak Yaffe of Kapust — a signatory of the 5556 Liozna takanot and one of the founders of the Hebron Yishuv — made aliyah to Eretz Yisrael. He soon served as a shadar before 5587 and held a yechidut with the Alter Rebbe documented in Igrot Kodesh. He passed away before Shvat 5589 (early 1829).
