# First Yud-Tes Kislev farbrengen in Rostov

*1915*

On 19 Kislev 5676 (Friday), chassidim arrived in waves at the Rebbe Rashab's new home in Rostov, with delegations from Kharkov, Homel, Minsk, Kremenchug, and elsewhere. On Thursday evening the Rebbe delivered the maamar 'Padah Beshalom Nafshi' lasting over an hour, and on Friday evening, despite displeasure that so many had come during wartime, he said the maamar 'Karov Havayah Lechol Korav.' The Shabbat meal continued for two hours of Torah and inspiration, and a closing Sunday meal stretched until 7:00 p.m.
