# Rebbe describes Rayatz entrusting manuscripts to him in 5737 sicha

*1976*

Published in the supplements to Hemshech BeSha'a SheHikdimu 5672 vol. 3 (1993 edition, p. 12), the sicha describes how, when his father-in-law had to leave his home, Rayatz sought someone he could trust to keep the manuscripts and return them intact — and apparently had no choice but to entrust them to him. This may correspond to the period in which the future Rebbe compiled the three-page manuscript inventory.
