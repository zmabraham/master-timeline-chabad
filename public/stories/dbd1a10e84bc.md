Rabbi Yitzchak David Grossman. Rabbi Yitzchak David Grossman (born on the 19th of Elul 5706, September 15, 1946) is the Rabbi of the city of Migdal HaEmek, head of the 'Migdal Ohr' institutions, and a member of the Council of the Chief Rabbinate of Israel. Rabbi Grossman is known publicly for his love of Israel. Rabbi
