# Cold Shabbat keeps delegation indoors in Moscow

*1991*

On Shabbat 19 Shevat 5751, while in Moscow pursuing the return of the Chabad library, the delegation remained indoors because of extreme cold of -30°C and inadequate winter clothing for the over-hour walk to synagogue. The Shabbat was used to plan the next moves, including sending Rabbis Koegan and Cunin to Leningrad to obtain a fresh letter from Ligachev.
