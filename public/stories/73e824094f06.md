# Rebbe Rayatz recalls Rosenbaum's Tzemach Tzedek gift in 5696 letter

*1935*

In a letter dated 12 Tishrei 5696 (Igrot Kodesh vol. 3 #803), the Rebbe Rayatz reminded R' David Schifrin that many years earlier the chassid Avraham Chaim Rosenbaum had presented the Rebbe Rashab with a handwritten power-of-attorney signed by the Tzemach Tzedek. The Rebbe Rashab had treasured the gift, and the memory was preserved in the Rebbe Rayatz's correspondence decades later.
