# Rebbe Rayatz writes about Avraham HaKohen's proposed Riga visit

*1929*

On 9 Adar II 5689 (February 1929) the Rebbe Rayatz wrote again to R' Avraham HaKohen, replying to a letter in which the philanthropist had proposed visiting Riga to continue their discussion of housing for the yeshiva. The Rebbe noted that such a visit would be most welcome and asked for the planned timing in detail. The letter is preserved in Igrot Kodesh vol. 16, letter 6153.
