# Farbrengen near Marseille on the eve of departure

*1949*

Levin and a group of Chassidim, including R' Shlomo Chaim, gathered by the sea near Marseille to board a ship to Eretz Yisrael. On Motzaei Shabbat Parshat Emor R' Peretz Mochkin joined them and led a farbrengen the night before they sailed.
