# Haggadah 'Kol Yehuda' on the teachings of Belz Rebbes printed in London

*1962*

In 5722 (1962), the Haggadah 'Kol Yehuda', interwoven with teachings from the Belz Rebbes by Rabbi Chaim Yehuda Segal Deutsch, was published in London. The 212-page octavo edition included notes by the author's son and added Passover discourses from Rebbe Aharon Rokeach of Belz. It contributed to the Belz Chassidic community's postwar publishing renaissance.
