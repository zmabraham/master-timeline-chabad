# First written account of the Tzemach Tzedek's portrait

*1889*

In the 5649 (1889) volume of HaAsif (p. 167), Alexander Zisskind Rabinowitz wrote that the portrait of R. Menachem Mendel of Lubavitch was widespread, having been made by a photographer who stole into his room against the Tzemach Tzedek's will — for Chassidic rebbes refused to leave images of themselves to posterity. This is the earliest published version of the famous tradition about the painting's origins.
