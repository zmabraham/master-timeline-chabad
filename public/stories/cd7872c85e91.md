# Underground Yud-Beis Tammuz farbrengen during the author's sheva brachos

*1940*

With public farbrengens impossible, R' Zusya the shamash quietly redirected anyone asking where to celebrate Yud-Beis Tammuz to the author's home, since the sheva brachos provided a cover for the NKVD. Chassidim arrived from the tram carrying a suitcase of mashke and farbeisen; the house and courtyard filled, and they danced 'Veharikosi lochem brocho' so vigorously that the floor began to creak. R' Yona Cohen broke up the gathering at dawn so that participants could leave individually — a neighbour across the lane worked for the NKVD.
