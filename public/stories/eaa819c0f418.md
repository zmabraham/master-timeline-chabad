# Investigator Korbitsky sent to inspect Lubavitch synagogue and library

*1825*

Acting on Governor Khovansky's order, the private investigator Korbitsky traveled to Lubavitch on October 19, 1825 — his second such mission. He measured and inventoried the wooden synagogue (built in 1814–1815 by the landowner Kruger for about 4,000–5,000 rubles), priced its furnishings at roughly 1,258 rubles, and catalogued 611 books in four cabinets in the Rebbe's home. Local Jews swore the building was financed by donations from the Rebbe's followers, but could not provide further details; Korbitsky recommended further investigation of Kruger and the scribe of Yanovich.
