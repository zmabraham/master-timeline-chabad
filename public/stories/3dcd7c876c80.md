# Rebbe Rayatz and Tomchei Temimim relocate to Otwock

*1935*

The Rebbe Rayatz moved his residence to the nearby town of Otwock, and the yeshiva followed into a three-story building at Slowackiego 1. Initially considered temporary (as letterhead reflected), the move soon became permanent, opening the 'golden era' of Tomchei Temimim in Otwock that lasted four years until the war.
