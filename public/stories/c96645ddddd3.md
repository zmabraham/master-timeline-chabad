# Rebbe corresponds with the Rogatchover on 22 Shevat

*1928*

On 22 Shevat 5688 (Feb 13, 1928), the future Lubavitcher Rebbe wrote a halachic letter to the Rogatchover Gaon. The Rogatchover replied on 24 Shevat, and the Rebbe wrote back again on 21 Adar of that year. The Rebbe later copied the exchange in his Reshimot, notebook 33.
