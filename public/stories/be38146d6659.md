# R' Yisrael Jacobson departs Riga for America

*1925*

Mid-Yud-Tes Kislev Farbrengen on 5686 (December 6, 1925) at the home of the shochet R' Mordechai Chefetz, R' Yisrael Jacobson received parting blessings from his Riga friends and departed. He had spent several months in the city after leaving Russia, joining its tmimim for farbrengens and study of chassidus.
