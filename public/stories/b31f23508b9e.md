# Goldstein flees Chmielnik after Nazi search

*1941*

On Purim 5701 (March 13, 1941), German agents came to seize Yosef Goldstein at his home in Chmielnik but did not find him, as he was praying in the beis midrash below. Realizing he had been informed upon — either over the yeshiva or his continued shechita work — he returned briefly, took only his tefillin (in which he had kept a 1933 letter from the Rebbe Rayatz), and fled the city. He credited that letter with miraculously saving him in the labor camps, escaping every Nazi search that followed.
