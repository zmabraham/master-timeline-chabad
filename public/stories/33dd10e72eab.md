# Chabad network smuggles 1,000 Chasidim out of the USSR on false passports

*1946*

In 1946, the underground Chabad network — which had endured through decades of Soviet persecution — orchestrated the escape of roughly a thousand Chasidim across the border to Poland on false passports. They and their children went on to provide leadership for the global Jewish renaissance and to inspire a new generation of postwar Chasidim.
