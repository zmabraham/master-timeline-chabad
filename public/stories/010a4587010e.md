# Rabbi Ziv Katzbi (5784)

*1980*

Rabbi Ziv Katzbi (5784) - Rabbi Zevulun (Ziv) Katzbi (born 5740, 1980) is one of the Rebbe's shluchim in Ramat HaSharon, and a sought-after chassidic lecturer who teaches at Midreshet Pnimiyut, at the Mimaal Mamash Center, and in other places, a member of Pardes - a platform for Jewish wisdom
