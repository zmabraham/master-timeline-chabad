# Balter arrives safely in Cape Town en route to Eretz Yisroel

*1941*

On 4 Tammuz 5701 (June 29, 1941), Balter arrived safely in Cape Town. He spent only a few days there but met members of Anash and visited the small Chabad shul. He had planned to review Chassidus on Shabbos there, but a ship for Eretz Yisroel departed before Shabbos, requiring him to continue his journey.
