# Passing of R. Yisrael Jacobson

*1975*

R. Yisrael Jacobson, author of these memoirs and a central figure in Chabad community life in America, passed away on 17 Sivan 5735 (1975). He had continued publishing installments of his memoirs in the quarterly Di Yiddishe Heim from 5724 until his death. His final years included activity in founding Agudas Chassidei Chabad, Agudas HaTmimim, and Achei Tmimim.
