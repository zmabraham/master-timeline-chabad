# Alter Rebbe freed from czarist prison; chasidic teaching expands

*1798*

The seventh Rebbe identified the 1798 release as the second transformational point in disseminating chasidic teachings. 'This began a new stage, when the teachings went chutzah, outside.' The 1897 founding of Tomchei Temimim and the later translation of maamarim into other languages built on the same trajectory.
