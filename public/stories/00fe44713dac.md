# R. Naeh notes long correspondence delays before Sukkos 5683

*1922*

Erev Sukkos 5683 (Oct 1922), R. Naeh wrote to Rebbe Rayatz that he had sent multiple letters that summer with no acknowledgment, and that he was puzzled by the Rebbe's failure to receive his 18 Sivan response. He promised to send a carbon copy of the letter after the holiday.
