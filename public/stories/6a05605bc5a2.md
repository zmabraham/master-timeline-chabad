# Birth of R' Shmuel Grossman in Nikolayev

*1890*

About a year after his sister Chaya Mushka's birth, R' Shmuel Grossman was born in Nikolayev in 5650 to R' Asher Grossman. On 4 Elul 5650, Rashab responded to R' Asher's question about hiring a wet nurse given his wife's weakened state after the births. R' Shmuel would later study in Tomchei Tmimim and serve as shochet.
