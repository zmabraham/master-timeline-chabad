# 'Sod Kedoshim' Haggadah of the Alter Rebbe and Aharon HaLevi of Strashelye

*1866*

In 5626 (1866), the Haggadah 'Sod Kedoshim' was published — possibly in Königsberg or Warsaw — featuring two commentaries: one by the Chabad Alter Rebbe Schneur Zalman of Liadi, and one by his disciple Rabbi Aharon HaLevi of Strashelye. The 49-leaf quarto edition transmitted core Chabad teachings on the Passover Seder. It became a foundational Chabad Haggadah commentary.
