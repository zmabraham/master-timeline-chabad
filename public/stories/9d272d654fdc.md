# Levin sends detailed Moscow library inspection plan by fax

*1999*

Mira quickly confirmed his visa was arranged for pickup at the embassy on December 3 and accepted his agenda and department list. Levin finalized his departure for motzei Shabbos Zos Chanukah and planned to start work the morning of 4 Tevet. He remained undecided whether to stay one or two weeks, depending on his access.
