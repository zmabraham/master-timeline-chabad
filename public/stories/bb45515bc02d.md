# Ewald reports 16 Hebron Jewish families have relocated to Jerusalem

*1847*

F.C. Ewald wrote on October 4, 1847 (published in London's Jewish Intelligence, January 1848) that since his previous report on the persecuted Jews of Hebron, 16 families had left and settled in Jerusalem, forming their own community of 53 souls. The letter is the earliest external attestation of the Hebron-to-Jerusalem migration of Chabad chassidim.
