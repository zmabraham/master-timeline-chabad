# Marriage of R. Levi Yitzchak Schneerson and Rebbetzin Chana

*1900*

In the summer of 5660 (1900), Rabbi Levi Yitzchak Schneerson, father of the Rebbe, was married to Rebbetzin Chana (Yanovsky). The date is documented in a letter of Rebbe Rashab (Igros Kodesh vol. 3, letter 656). It is suggested that during the years 5658–5660 he may have briefly studied at Lubavitch.
