# Passing of the Mittler Rebbe

*1827*

On 9 Kislev 5588 the Mittler Rebbe passed away. His son-in-law and nephew Rabbi Menachem Mendel Schneersohn, the Tzemach Tzedek, succeeded him as leader of Chabad. The Tzemach Tzedek continued sending funds to the Chabad community in Hebron, one of the three primary support channels for the Hebron chassidim alongside the Pekidim of Amsterdam and the joint Italy emissaries.
