# Rabbi Rozenshein - Rabbi Yitzchak Yaakov Rozenshein (born 5713

*1951*

Rabbi Rozenshein - Rabbi Yitzchak Yaakov Rozenshein (born 5713, 1951) is one of the Rebbe's shluchim to the Holy Land, serving as a Rosh Mesivta in Nigleh and a member of the educational staff of the Toras Emes Yeshiva Ketana. Biography edit edit source Born on the 3rd of Tammuz 5713
