# Kfar Chabad delegation brings new amud to the Rebbe on Erev Rosh Hashanah

*1962*

A chartered flight brought a group from Eretz Yisrael for the Tishrei holidays of 5723, and R' Chaim Schneur Zalman Lipsker carried the new amud with them. He and R' Dovber Teichman presented it at the special farbrengen held Or L'Erev Rosh Hashanah 5723, after which the Rebbe delivered a sicha explaining the inner meaning of the 'shtender' and recounted the Mitteler Rebbe's exchange with the Alter Rebbe about davening 'with the shtender.'
