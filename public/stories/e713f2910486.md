# Anash expand prayer arrangements in Malakhovka for Tishrei 5695

*1934*

By Tishrei 5695 (Fall 1934) the influx of Anash families and yeshiva bochurim to Malakhovka had grown so large that a separate prayer hall had to be rented for the High Holidays. The main shul served the general religious public while the new venue hosted only Anash and yeshiva bochurim, recreating the prayer atmosphere of pre-revolution Lubavitch. Sukkos and Simchas Torah were celebrated with extraordinary farbrengens.
