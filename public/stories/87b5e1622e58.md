# Rabbi Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk leads chassidim to Eretz Yisrael

*1777*

Rabbi Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk, a disciple of the Maggid of Mezritch, led a major group of chassidim on aliyah to Eretz Yisrael in 5537 (1777). They settled primarily in Tiberias, founding one of the earliest chassidic communities in the Holy Land.
