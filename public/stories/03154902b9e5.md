# Erev Rosh Hashanah encounter with Elye Chaim Althaus in Leningrad

*1924*

After Selichos and Shacharis, the author entered the Rebbe's apartment at Machovaya 12 and was shocked to find the prayer hall unprepared with no benches set up. When he asked R. Elye Chaim Althaus about it, Althaus repeated the question back to him, awakening the author to the fact that he himself bore responsibility for the Rebbe's house. The author said this moment 'made him ten years older.'
