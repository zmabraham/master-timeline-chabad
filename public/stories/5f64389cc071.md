# Rebbe writes 'Hilkhot Mendel' letter to Chaya Mushka

*1923*

A week after the Ramash returned to Rostov, the Rebbe wrote to Chaya Mushka, who remained in Kislovodsk, that he had been studying 'Hilkhot Mendel' (the subject of Mendel) for several hours each day. He noted that the Ramash had left with a pleasant impression of the Caucasus and would like another excursion in the mountains.
