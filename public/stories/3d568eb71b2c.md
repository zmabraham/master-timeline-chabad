# Rabbi Bentzion Kunin returns to Moscow with Rebbe's dollars

*1991*

On 24 Nisan 5751, Rabbi Bentzion Kunin returned to Moscow to rejoin the library mission. The day before, at the regular Sunday dollar distribution, the Rebbe had given him two dollars apiece for each of the three colleagues in Moscow and added a separate dollar 'for Lenin,' remarking that Lenin was said to be of Jewish descent. Kunin brought with him a tehillim soaked, he told the Rebbe, with tears shed in Moscow, wrapped in an image of the library where the books were being held captive.
