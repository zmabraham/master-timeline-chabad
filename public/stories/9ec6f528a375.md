# Hyman writes JDC assessment for American Jewish Yearbook

*1939*

'In less than a decade and a half,' Hyman wrote, 'the work of the Agro-Joint helped transform Russian Jewry from a downtrodden, almost helpless ghetto population into self-reliant and productive workers.' The piece did not reveal that close to two hundred staff had been killed, imprisoned, or deported and that all JDC assets in Russia had been seized.
