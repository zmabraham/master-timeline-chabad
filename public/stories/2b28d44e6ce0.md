# Chabad winter camp opens for Moscow children during school break

*1991*

On 17 Tevet R' Shalom Dovber Levin visited a Chabad winter camp where roughly 60 pupils from the Moscow Chabad school spent two weeks of school break living in a Jewish environment. The camp ran in a state school building empty for the holiday and was run by R' Moshe Chaim Levin and R' Zalman Korbsky. The visit also covered the new Aguch office obtained from the Mossoviet.
