# R. Avraham Chaim invites R. Hendel to visit him in Smolensk prison

*1889*

In the winter R. Avraham Chaim had discouraged R. Hendel from making the trip to Smolensk prison, both because of the cold and so the travel funds could be sent home to his family. On 20 Iyar 5649 he wrote that the time was now better for them both, asking R. Hendel to inform him before setting out. During that visit R. Hendel brought a gift for one of the guards, which led to a marked improvement in R. Avraham Chaim's treatment.
