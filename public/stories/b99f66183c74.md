# Maharayatz letter describes founding of 'Chevronim' vaad and new shadar

*1906*

The letter describes how the Chevronim vaad was tasked with expanding pushke placements 'in every place'. R. Meir Leib Horowitz had just been appointed as a new emissary to Russian cities, and R. Zalman Yitzchak Walshanik of Riga was named the dedicated gabbai for Eretz Yisrael funds. The decisions overrode an earlier proposal to abolish traveling shadarim in favor of mailing funds directly to Jerusalem, which Rebbe Rashab had opposed.
