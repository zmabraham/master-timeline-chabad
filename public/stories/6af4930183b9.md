# Author's sister Sasha Hinde emigrates to the United States

*1913*

In summer 5673 the author's sister, Mrs. Sasha Hinde, traveled to the United States. The father asked the Rebbe Rashab for permission for the author to come home from yeshiva to bid her farewell, but the Rebbe questioned the trip in the middle of zman. During the same yechidus the author received approval to begin laying Rabbeinu Tam tefillin upon turning 18 that Kislev.
