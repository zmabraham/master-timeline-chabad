# Tsarist search at Mitteler Rebbe's home documents 611-volume library

*1826*

In 5586 (1826) Tsarist authorities conducted a search of the Mitteler Rebbe's home in Lubavitch. Their detailed inventory recorded a personal library of 611 books arranged in four cabinets. This document provides the earliest concrete glimpse of the size of the Chabad library in its second generation.
