# Alter Rebbe reaffirms Liozna takanos at handover of Tanya for print

*1796*

On 27 Adar I 5556 (March 7, 1796), at the same time the Tanya was given to the printers, the Alter Rebbe re-confirmed his signature on the Liozna takanos and added new provisions, co-signed by leading chassidim including R' Yisrael ben Yitzchak of Kapust. The takanos restricted how often chassidim could come to him and limited entry to yechidut.
