# Yosef Rodal — first Shanghai student to marry — weds in Montreal

*1942*

Rodal had lost most of his family in the Holocaust. The Rebbe, knowing the bride was a Canadian who expected a diamond, gave Rodal money to buy one. A delegation of New York rabbis attended the wedding. Most of the Shanghai cohort would settle in Montreal, marry, and become teachers, rabbis, and dayanim.
