# Tanya circulates widely in handwritten copies among chassidim

*1792*

Around the year 5552 (1792), the kuntreisim that would later be printed as the Tanya were disseminated widely among Rabbi Schneur Zalman's chassidim. The work spread through many handwritten copies made by different scribes, leading to an accumulation of textual errors. This proliferation of corrupted copies eventually persuaded the Alter Rebbe to authorize a definitive printed edition of the work.
