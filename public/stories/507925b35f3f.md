# R. Yissachar Ber Horowitz studies with MaHaRiL of Yanovich

*1812*

After marrying into a Yanovich family, R. Yissachar Ber Horowitz settled there as a melamed and studied Torah with the Alter Rebbe's brother, the MaHaRiL of Yanovich, in 5572 (1812). Years later he would cite halachic teachings he heard from MaHaRiL during this period in his own responsa.
