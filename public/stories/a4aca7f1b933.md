# Malach's students leave Torah Vodaas to found Yeshivas Nesivos Olam

*1934*

Adopting distinctive external practices — long coats, large tzitzis, no ties, unfolded hat brims, and learning only from Slavita and Zhitomir Talmud editions — the Malach's students openly clashed with Torah Vodaas. They eventually left and founded Nesivos Olam, drawing major support from R' Ben Tzion Weberman (formerly a pillar of Torah Vodaas) and the wealthy R' Avraham Yaakov Katz.
