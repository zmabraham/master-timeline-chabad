# Alter Rebbe and R. Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk fail to meet the Vilna Gaon

*1772*

Hoping to defuse the opposition to chassidus by direct dialogue, the two leaders came to the Gaon's home but the door was closed against them twice. When they pressed further the Gaon departed Vilna entirely, staying away until they left. The Alter Rebbe recounted the failed attempt in his Iggeres 34.
