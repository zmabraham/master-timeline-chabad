# Merkos becomes one-third partner in Empire Press (Ezra) by directive of the Rebbe

*1953*

The Rebbe pressed the narrator's partner R. Tzvi on why Merkos was not yet a partner in the press; when the partners and R. Chadakov disagreed about whether Merkos should join — Chadakov insisting Merkos should not be in partnership at all — the Rebbe overruled all of them, ruling that the partners would draw a worker's salary and that Merkos would receive one-third of the profits when there were any. The print shop opened a 'Ezra–Merkos L'inyonei Chinuch' bank account signed by Chadakov or Shusterman. The narrator did not insist on a formal contract — a decision he later regretted on the advice of the Merkos bookkeeper Mr. Telushkin, son of R. Nissan Telushkin.
