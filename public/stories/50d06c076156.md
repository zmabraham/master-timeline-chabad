# Yechidus appoints author mashpia in Homel and emissary to Rostov

*1925*

In Thursday night yechidus the author first presented R. Yisrael Magidsohn's situation and then his own, and the Rebbe told him to begin preparations for the journey to America. The Rebbe also asked him to serve as mashpia in Homel for 40 rubles per month until his departure, regardless of his daytime occupation. Finally the Rebbe appointed him as emissary to travel to Rostov for the Rebbe Rashab's yahrzeit on 2 Nisan, which fell that year on Friday.
