# Rosenbaum released from Smolensk and flees Russia

*1892*

After his release, a sympathetic investigating magistrate quietly warned Rosenbaum that fresh charges were pending and hinted that he should escape abroad. On one of the days of Tishrei 5652/5653 (1891/2) he was tipped off that he would be re-arrested the day after Simchas Torah; he hosted a joyous farewell farbrengen for the Plescenice chassidim and vanished that night. He traveled first to Warsaw, where his brother lived, and then to Frankfurt, where he and his son ran a dairy shop for about a year.
