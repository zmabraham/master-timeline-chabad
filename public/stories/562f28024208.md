# Mitteler Rebbe requests stipend for R. Shmuel Segal's aliyah

*1823*

Around 1823, as R. Shmuel Segal prepared to leave Rogatchov for Hebron, the Mitteler Rebbe addressed a letter to the chassidim of Rogatchov, Bobruisk, Paritch, Zhlobin and their surrounding settlements. He requested they collectively pledge an annual stipend of thirty gold coins — ten from Rogatchov, five from Zhlobin, three from Stareshin, and ten from Bobruisk-Paritch — to enable Segal to devote himself to Torah and divine service in the Land of Israel.
