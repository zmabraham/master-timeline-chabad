# Alter Rebbe released from Petersburg by tsarist decree

*1801*

Following his second arrest and interrogation in Petersburg in 1801, Rabbi Schneur Zalman of Liadi (called Zalman Bruchavitch in Russian documents) was freed by imperial order. The civil governor of White Russia, Treviov, issued him a formal document on July 13, 1801, granting him full freedom to resume his religious leadership of the Chasidim throughout the region as he had done before.
