# R' Yehoshua Heshel Reizman passes away

*2009*

Born on 1 Rosh Chodesh Tammuz 5678 in Wodzislaw and raised in Suchedniow, R' Heshel Reizman studied at Tomchei Tmimim Warsaw from 5692, taught in its Mechina from spring 5697, and survived WWII. He passed away on 10 Tevet 5769. A short biography appears at the start of his sefer Iyunim B'Ta'aniyos.
