# Hebron Chabad settlement disrupted by World War I

*1914*

The Hebron Chabad community had been led for nearly twenty years by R' Dovber Efrat (until 5662) and then by R' Shiloh together with R' Mordechai Dovber Slonim and R' Binyamin Rivlin. When World War I began in 5674, the Chabad presence in Hebron came to a halt.
