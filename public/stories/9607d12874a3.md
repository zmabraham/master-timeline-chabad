# Mashpi'im assigned Chassidus shiurim with Malakhovka bochurim after Simchas Torah

*1934*

Following the Simchas Torah 5695 farbrengens (October 1934), a planning meeting of Anash activists assigned each mashpia to learn Chassidus with a small group of bochurim. The author and R. Shlomo Matusof were assigned to study with R. Nissan Nemenov, who traveled almost daily from Ilinka to teach them Hemshech 5664. Bochurim were also boarded with Anash families—the author lodged with R. Zalman Alpert (Zalman Kornitzer).
