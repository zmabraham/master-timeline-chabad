# First volume of Tzemach Tzedek's responsa printed

*1871*

Five years after the Tzemach Tzedek's passing, editors completed the first volume of his responsa, covering Even HaEzer. The series would take another thirteen years to complete and was given the name Tzemach Tzedek (gematria of Menachem Mendel). From the publication of these volumes onward, the Rebbe himself became known as 'the Tzemach Tzedek.'
