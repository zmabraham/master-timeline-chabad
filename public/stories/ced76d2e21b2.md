# Pereshel facsimile of the Livorno 1825 Ladino Haggadah

*1992*

In 5752 (1992), Tuvia Pereshel published in New York a facsimile of the 1825 Livorno Haggadah with Ladino translation. The 34-leaf octavo edition (including cover) came with an introduction by Pereshel. It marked his ongoing commitment to resurrecting rare printed Haggadot for new generations.
