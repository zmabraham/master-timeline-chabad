# Raza relocates to Vitebsk

*1893*

In the summer of 1893, Rabbi Zalman Aharon Schneersohn (the Raza) relocated from Lubavitch to Vitebsk, fifty miles away. The move possibly was meant to keep him out of the limelight, clearing the way for his younger brother the Rashab to emerge as Rebbe. The Raza had declared that 'My brother, the Rashab, was born a Rebbe and is a Rebbe' and may also have been motivated by financial difficulties. He continued to visit Lubavitch often and became a major influence on his nephew Yosef Yitzchak.
