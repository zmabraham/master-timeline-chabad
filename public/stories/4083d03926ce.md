# Rayatz letter to Havlin on entrepreneurial management approach

*1913*

Dated 3 Kislev 5674, the letter stated: 'Our approach is to appoint individuals and entrust them with the management, and they are responsible for the projects given over to them.' This entrepreneurial model would become emblematic of Chabad expansion in the decades to come, used by both the Rayatz and the seventh Rebbe.
