# R' Menachem Mendel Schneerson farbrengs publicly for first time, in Otwock

*1931*

On a night of Simchas Beis HaShoeiva during Sukkos 5692 in Otwock, the Rebbe Rayatz instructed his son-in-law R' Menachem Mendel Schneerson, then called the Ramash, to farbreng with the assembled community. Beginning at 8 PM and continuing until dawn, the Ramash opened on the mishna 'Hevei az kanamer' and astonished the entire crowd with the depth and brilliance of his Torah, drawing on Talmud, Midrash, Zohar, Kabbalah, and chassidic stories. From that moment Anash began to recognize his unique greatness in his own right.
