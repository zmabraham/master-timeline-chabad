# R' Alter Simchowitz leaves Warsaw yeshiva for Eretz Yisrael

*1924*

At the beginning of 5685 (autumn 1924), R' Alter Simchowitz fell ill with tuberculosis, attributed to his damp, dilapidated lodgings in Warsaw. Doctors advised that Jerusalem's air would be better for his health than Poland's humid climate. He left Poland to take up the position of mashpia at Yeshivas Toras Emes in Eretz Yisrael, and his departure significantly weakened the spiritual atmosphere of the Warsaw yeshiva.
