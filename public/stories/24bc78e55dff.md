# R. Yissachar Ber Horowitz appointed moreh tzedek of Lubavitch

*1833*

After leaving Bychov Chadash in 5593 (1833), R. Yissachar Ber Horowitz established his residence in Lubavitch as the town's moreh tzedek. He served there for a decade alongside the Tzemach Tzedek, engaging in extensive halachic correspondence with the Rebbe and serving as a conduit for questions from rabbis throughout the region. Many of these responsa have since been published in Kovetz Yagdil Torah.
