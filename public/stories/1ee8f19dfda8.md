# R' Schneur Zalman Gourary appointed maggid shiur of upper Mechina class

*1932*

R' Yehuda Eber reported to the Rebbe Rayatz on 7 Marcheshvan 5693 that R' Schneur Zalman Gourary would teach the upper Mechina class while R' Moshe Meirowitz continued the lower class. The two Mechina classes at the Agudas Chabad shul on Dzika 8 had grown to about 50 students.
