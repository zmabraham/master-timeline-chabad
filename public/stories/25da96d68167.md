# Rebbe Rashab letter on book spacing in library shelves

*1914*

On 15 Tevet 5674 (January 13, 1914), the Rebbe Rashab wrote to his son expressing satisfaction with the library organization. He instructed that books should not stand crowded and crammed together, as this damages them by preventing air circulation. While they need not be as spaciously arranged as Wiener had set them, they should not be packed tightly.
