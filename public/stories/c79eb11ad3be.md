# Letter to Rabbi Tzirelson of Ludmir on Eretz Yisrael fundraising

*1907*

On 20 Adar 5667, writing on the Rebbe Rayatz's stationery from Lubavitch, secretary Yaakov Kapil Zeliksohn sent a postcard to Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Tzirelson, rabbi of Ludmir (Vladimir-Volynsky). The letter urged Tzirelson to follow the model of the Kovel community in collecting Ramban funds for the Anash community in Hebron. At that time the future Rebbe Rayatz served as director of Tomchei Temimim and chairman of the Hebron Vaad.
