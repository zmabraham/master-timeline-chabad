# Spanish flu epidemic strikes Chabad community in Rostov

*1917*

The Spanish flu, accompanied by severe vomiting and diarrhea, swept through Rostov during Rosh Hashana 5678. The narrator himself was struck on the first night of the holiday. About forty people were unable to attend the Rebbe Rashab's Torah reading and shofar blowing, lying instead in the lower quarters of the building.
