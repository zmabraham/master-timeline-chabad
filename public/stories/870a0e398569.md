# Rebbe urges importing Lithuanian students to Dankra

*1937*

In a letter of 8 Elul 5697 (Igrot vol. 4, no. 940) the Frierdiker Rebbe told Refael Hakohen, head of the Chabad community in Riga, that the Dankra yeshiva was obligated to import students from Lithuania to inspire 'feelings of love for Torah' among Latvian youth. This was part of his strategy of seeding new branches with veteran Tomchei Temimim talmidim.
