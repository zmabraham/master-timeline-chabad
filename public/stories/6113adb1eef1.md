# Rebbe Rayatz sends maamar to Warsaw students from Russia

*1925*

On 25 Iyar 5685, the Rebbe Rayatz wrote from Russia to the students of Tomchei Tmimim in Warsaw, sending them the maamar 'Ein HaKadosh Baruch Hu Ba B'Truniya Im Briyosav' as a gift, with instructions to study it and know it by heart. He urged them to strengthen themselves in Torah study, both nigleh and Chassidus, and in avodah of prayer and refinement of character, promising Heaven's help in rising spiritually and materially.
