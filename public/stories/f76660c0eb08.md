# Rabbi Binyamin Sochaczek-Cohen (known as Benke; born in the year 5759

*1999*

Rabbi Binyamin Sochaczek-Cohen (known as Benke; born in the year 5759, 1999) is the Rebbe's emissary to the community of Alma in the north, a teacher at Talmud Torah Ohr Menachem, one of the regular writers in the 'HaTamim' magazine, and director of development of Kollel Tzemach Tzedek in Safed
