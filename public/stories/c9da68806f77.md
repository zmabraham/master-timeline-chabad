# Rebbe Rayatz proposes 40th anniversary HaTamim issue

*1937*

At the beginning of summer 5697 (1937), the future Rebbe (Ramash, the Rebbe Rayatz's son-in-law) wrote to the Rebbe Rayatz proposing that since summer marked the 40-year anniversary of Tomchei Tmimim's founding, the upcoming 12 Tammuz issue of HaTamim should be dedicated to the yeshiva's history with special material. He also urged that a list of all yeshiva students throughout the years be published in time for the jubilee.
