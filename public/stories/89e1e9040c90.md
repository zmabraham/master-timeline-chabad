# R' Chaim Liberman arrives in Riga

*1928*

R' Chaim Liberman arrived in Riga at 10 PM on 19 Iyar 5688 (9 May 1928), eight days after leaving Tambov. The Rayatz wrote that day that 'today one of my secretaries, Mr. Liberman, is arriving here,' describing how Mordechai Dubin had worked for some six months to obtain his exit permit after his arrest at the same time as the Rebbe.
