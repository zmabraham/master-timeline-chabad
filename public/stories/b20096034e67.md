# Group yechidus with Rebbe Rayatz on 27 Elul

*1946*

Upon arriving in New York, the students found the Frierdiker Rebbe in weakened health, and their yechidus was repeatedly postponed. As R. Shmuel Dovid Reitchik wrote, each day they were told they could enter 'tomorrow or the day after,' until finally on Monday before Rosh Hashanah — 27 Elul 5706 — the entire group entered together for a few minutes, during which the Rebbe spoke to them briefly.
