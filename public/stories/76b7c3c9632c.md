# Death of R' Yeshaya Halevi Horowitz of Beshenkovichi

*1864*

R' Yeshaya was a prominent chasid of the Tzemach Tzedek and the husband of Bila, niece of the Rebbe. His death left her widowed with five young orphans, leading to her eventual aliyah to Tzfat with her sons. The family became foundational to the renewed Chabad community in Tzfat.
