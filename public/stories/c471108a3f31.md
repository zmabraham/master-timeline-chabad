# R' Zev Wolf Bronstein dies in Spanish flu epidemic

*1917*

The epidemic claimed R' Zev Wolf Bronstein during the Ten Days of Repentance in 5678. He was the father of the well-known Bronstein brothers of Cherkassy — Chaim Ezra, Nosson, Mordechai, and Hershka — and later father-in-law of R' Shlomo Chaim Kesselman, who became a mashpia in Tomchei Temimim Lod.
