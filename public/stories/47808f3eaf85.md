# Kasher's Pesach Sheni letter on Tzofnat Paneach on Exodus

*1961*

On Pesach Sheni 5721, Rabbi Menachem Kasher wrote to the Rebbe that Tzofnat Paneach on Exodus was nearly finished and would be completed within a month. He also mentioned having submitted volumes on Leviticus and on Tractates Horayot, Eduyot and the minor tractates for printing in Israel.
