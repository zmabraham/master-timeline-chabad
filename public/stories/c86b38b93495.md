# Rayatz writes Henry Morgenthau requesting aid for Chabad aliyah colonies

*1949*

On 6 Nissan 5709, Rebbe Rayatz wrote to Mr. Henry Morgenthau requesting that £100,000 (Eretz Yisrael pounds) be allocated to establish colonies for 100 Chabad refugee families, with £2,000 needed per family. Half would come from local Israeli institutions on the guarantee of Zalman Shazar (Rubashov), and half from Agudas Chassidei Chabad. The letter was composed in Hebrew by the future Rebbe and translated to English by Mr. Mindel.
