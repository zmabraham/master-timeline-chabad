# R. Shmuel Segal makes aliyah from Rogatchov to Hebron

*1824*

In 1823-1824, R. Shmuel Segal left his fifteen-year post as moreh tzedek of Rogatchov and ascended to the Land of Israel. He settled in Hebron and became rabbi, maggid mesharim, and first appointed leader of the Chabad community established there by the Mitteler Rebbe. The Mitteler Rebbe wrote to chassidim of Rogatchov and surrounding towns requesting an annual stipend of thirty gold coins to support him.
