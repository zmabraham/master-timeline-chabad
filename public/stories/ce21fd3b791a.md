# Moscow arbitration court hears Chabad library case

*1991*

Veronika argued for Chabad while a Jewish attorney represented the library; the diary records that her arguments were clearly persuading the judges while the library lawyer grew flustered. Closing, she described the false imprisonment of Rabbi Levi Yitzchak Schneerson, asked the courtroom to rise in his memory, and argued that after the Previous Rebbe's exile and passing the moral duty was to return the books to his son. The judges announced a ruling would be given the following Tuesday.
