# Mohilev High Court relays Senate recommendations on rabbinic conduct

*1834*

Seven years after Khovansky submitted his original recommendations to the Senate, the Mohilev High Court's criminal division relayed a copy back to him. The document recommended dismissing the Mitteler Rebbe from his rabbinic post since he could not read or write Russian, Polish, or German as required by section 10 of the Jewish statute. It further proposed barring rabbis from organizing fundraising drives and from adjudicating civil disputes between Jews, restricting them to compensation set under provincial supervision.
