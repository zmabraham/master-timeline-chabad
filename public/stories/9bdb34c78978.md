# Chabad delegation greets US Secretary Baker at Russian Foreign Ministry

*1992*

During the Shluchim conference, the delegates drove to the Foreign Ministry and sang as Baker's motorcade entered. Rabbis Y. Kogan and Sh.B. Cunin then went to the Penta Hotel to meet Baker on his return and urged him to press for the return of the Schneerson books. Baker said he would raise it with Yeltsin once the Russian president was back in town.
