# Rebbe Rayatz writes to R' Chaim Ozer Grodzensky on Vilna funding

*1930*

The Rebbe Rayatz wrote a warm letter to Rabbi Chaim Ozer Grodzensky thanking him for his support of the Vilna Tomchei Tmimim and the Vaad HaYeshivot's monthly subsidy of 25 shekels. He requested that the subsidy be raised to 50 shekels monthly, expressing confidence that Rabbi Chaim Ozer would fulfill this request.
