# Vitebsk leaders ratify trustee list for expelled-Jews relief fund

*1824*

Three days after the main Lubavitch assembly, on Motzaei Shabbat into Sunday, 23 February 1824 (Adar I 5584), Vitebsk-area delegates signed a follow-up agreement in Vitebsk. They reaffirmed the 690-ruble share owed by their province, broke down the contribution by district, and replaced Binyamin Beinish Nachumowitz — who declined the trusteeship — with the prominent Chaim Simcha Ginzburg. The agreement was copied by Mendel the scribe and signed by representatives from Vitebsk and Polotsk.
