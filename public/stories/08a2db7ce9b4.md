# Assembly of Latvian rabbis convenes in Riga

*1932*

The Rebbe Rayatz urged R. Yaakov Landau to attend the gathering and arranged for him to come to Riga several days early to consult quietly with the organizers, Messrs. Chefetz and Chodakov. The Rebbe's secretary R. Yechezkel Feigin wrote on 26 Adar II to R. Israel Jacobson that the assembly produced concrete resolutions on strengthening Judaism in general and Shabbos observance in particular, and that the Rebbe himself opened the proceedings with a brief but substantive speech laying out a principal foundation for the discussions.
