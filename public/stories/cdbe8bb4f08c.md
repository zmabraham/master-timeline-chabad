# Kapust press closes mid-printing of Torah Ohr

*1837*

When the Russian government closed nearly all Jewish printing houses in early 5597, the Kapust press was among those shuttered. It had managed to print only the first part of Torah Ohr, the Alter Rebbe's chasidic discourses; the second volume's publication was delayed for over a decade. This loss shaped the trajectory of Chabad publishing for generations.
