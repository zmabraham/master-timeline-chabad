# Eliyahu Shusterman travels to Rebbe Rayatz in Leningrad

*1925*

For Shavuot 5685 (1925) — the 'matzaim' holiday — Eliyahu Shusterman traveled from Zhlobin to Leningrad to see the Frierdiker Rebbe. He intended to leave his post as shamash and relocate to Kharkov. The Rebbe did not agree and instructed him to continue in the shamash role at the Karmer Shul in Zhlobin.
