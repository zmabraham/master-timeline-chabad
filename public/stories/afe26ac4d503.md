# Kalligrapher Chaim ben Asher Anshil begins illuminated Haggadah work in Kitzee

*1741*

Between 5501 and 5542 (1741–1782), Chaim ben Asher Anshil worked as a calligrapher and illustrator in Kitzee, near Pressburg. During this period he wrote and decorated Haggadot, Birkat HaMazon booklets, and prayer texts that became prized manuscripts.
