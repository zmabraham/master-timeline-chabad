# Conference of Chabad rabbis convenes in Lubavitch at the Rashab's invitation

*1908*

In Cheshvan 5669, Rabbi Sholom DovBer of Lubavitch (the Rebbe Rashab) summoned a special conference of Chabad rabbis. Among the attendees was Rabbi Yaakov Mordechai Bezpolov, rav of Poltava, who remained at the gathering at least until 17 Cheshvan, as reflected in the published Igros Kodesh of the Rebbe Rashab.
