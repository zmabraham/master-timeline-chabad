# Tomchei Tmimim 'Toras Emes' yeshiva opens in Hebron

*1911*

At the start of 5672 a branch of Tomchei Tmimim, the Toras Emes yeshiva, opened in Hebron and operated for nearly three years. Studies began in the house donated by R' Shneur Zalman Berlin, which had previously housed the Magen Avos yeshiva, and were later moved to the Romano courtyard. Sleeping quarters for students and the mashpia's family were assigned in the large building purchased by the Rebbe Rashab in 5668.
