# Rebbe's response letter to Levin written in Brooklyn

*1944*

On 24 Nissan 5704, the Rebbe — through R' Moshe Gurary — answered Levin's smuggled letter from Brooklyn, blessing him with 'zera chaya vekayama' and good parnassah in both material and spiritual matters. The letter never reached Levin in Russia; only forty years later did he discover in the archive that the Rebbe had blessed him before his children were born. He saw in this the hidden providence behind his eventual escape from Russia.
