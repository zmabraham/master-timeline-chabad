# Delegation departs for G7 summit in London

*1991*

On 3 Menachem Av 5751, Veronika reported that Chernyaev had told her Gorbachev had pushed the seforim file back to Govinko and would not engage with it before the G7 summit. She insisted on traveling that night to London with R. Y.Y. Aharonov and R. Y. Kogan to escalate pressure during the summit. The Rebbe, informed by R. Groner, instructed: 'do everything as she says.'
