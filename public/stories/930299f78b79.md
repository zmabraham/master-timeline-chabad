# Tomchei Temimim branch reopens in Livenhof

*1939*

Approximately in 5699 the Frierdiker Rebbe founded a Tomchei Temimim branch in Livenhof, separate from R. Teitz's earlier yeshiva. Yeshaya Chanoch Berchen of Gostini directed both Latvian branches simultaneously, corresponding with the Rebbe about each.
