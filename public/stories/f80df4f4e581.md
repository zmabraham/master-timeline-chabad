# Robert Maxwell's last phone call with Rabbi Aharonov on Chabad library

*1991*

Maxwell, the British publisher, was working with Chabad activists to push Yeltsin and his circle to release the Schneerson library from the Russian State Library. Hours after the call he fell overboard near the Canary Islands and died. In that final conversation, conducted from the deck of his yacht, he had reaffirmed his desire to see the book-restoration effort completed.
