# Rebbe begins efforts to recover Chabad library from Moscow

*1980*

When the Rebbe Rashab fled Lubavitch in World War I, he placed the family library in storage in Moscow with the intention of retrieving it after the war. After the Soviet takeover, the books were nationalized and moved into the Russian State Library, where they remain. Beginning in 5741 (1980), the seventh Rebbe sent dedicated emissaries to Russia and pursued every avenue to secure their return, an effort that continues today.
