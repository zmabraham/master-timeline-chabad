# Memoirist begins typesetting at Modern Linotype on Eastern Parkway

*1948*

With no equipment of his own, the memoirist arranged through the Gertz brothers to work at R' Yechiel Shapira's Modern Linotype shop each evening from dusk until 10 p.m., paying Shapira for use of the machine. The Rebbe gave him manuscripts to typeset, including Ohr HaTorah on Shemos, Mafteach Ma'amarei Admur Maharashab, and the kuntresim for each chag and mo'ed.
