# Hatamim archive found after Rebbe's passing on 3 Tammuz 5754

*1994*

More than sixty years after the Rayatz first requested it, the file containing R. Michael Vilensky's answers about his father, together with the dossier on R. Avraham Chaim Rosenbaum, was found among the Rebbe's papers. The material was reviewed after the Rebbe's passing on 3 Tammuz 5754 and later published in Pardes Chabad and Toldos Avraham Chaim.
