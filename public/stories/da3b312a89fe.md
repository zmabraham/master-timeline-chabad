# Rebbe Maharid of Ladi writes Avraham Avli Person on business and family

*1863*

The future Admor Maharid of Ladi, author of Siddur Maharid, encouraged Person to keep faith despite slow earnings and to actively seek commissions. He recounted a visit from R' Moshe and his only son, who agreed to set up a shop, and asked Person to send him a commission. He also reported that the Tzemach Tzedek and his own father R' Chaim Shneur Zalman were both in good health.
