# Rebbe Yosef Yitzchak arrives in New York; addresses thousands at the port

*1940*

Stepping into the largest hall of the harbor, the Rebbe relayed regards from the Jews of Europe and called on American and Canadian Jewry to redouble their relief work and help refugees relocate. The very same day he issued a general letter (Igros V:1165) and within days established the 'Pidyon Shvuyim' fund to rescue chassidim and yeshiva students from occupied Europe.
