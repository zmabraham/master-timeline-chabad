# List of approximately fifty Jewish families in Lubavitch recorded

*1784*

This list, later published by Hendel Deutsch in his 1994 Russian booklet on Lubavitch, gives a snapshot of the town's Jewish community in the era of the Alter Rebbe's youth. It documents the modest scale of the settlement before Lubavitch became a movement headquarters. Around this period the future Alter Rebbe studied Torah in Lubavitch with Rabbi Yissachar Ber.
