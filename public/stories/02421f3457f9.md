# Sister-in-law travels to Smolensk to visit Rosenbaum in prison

*1889*

During Elul 5649, Rosenbaum's sister-in-law (the bride) traveled to Smolensk to see him. In replying to her, he addressed her fiancé Reb David, asking him to purchase a tallit katan of the highest quality from Kiryat Chana David before the new year. The request reflected both his ritual concerns and his confidence in his future relative's taste.
