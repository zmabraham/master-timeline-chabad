# Berditchev Siddur 'Seder Tefilah' with Maharid of Liadi's commentary

*1913*

In 5673 (1913), a Seder Tefilah following the Arizal nusach as composed by the Alter Rebbe was published in Berditchev, accompanied by a Chassidic 'Pirush HaMilot' commentary by Rebbe Yitzchak Dovber Schneersohn (Maharid of Liadi). The two-volume octavo edition came with 217 plus 231 leaves of teachings. It supplemented the Chabad liturgical canon with the Maharid's distinctive interpretation.
