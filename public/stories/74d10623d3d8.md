# Tzemach Tzedek again rejects Mendelstam's educational program

*1848*

On 24 Nissan 5608, the Tzemach Tzedek replied to Mendelstam's letter of Rosh Chodesh Nissan, received on 9 Nissan. He explained that after a month of deliberation, his view remained the same as what he had already conveyed on 13 Adar I and at the original commission. He pressed Mendelstam to accept his answer, written, he said, in truth and love.
