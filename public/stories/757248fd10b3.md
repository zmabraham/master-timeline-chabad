# Future Lubavitcher Rebbe arrives in America and begins communal work

*1941*

28 Sivan 5701 (June 23, 1941) marks the Rebbe's arrival in the United States after fleeing Nazi-occupied Europe. Upon arrival he was appointed by his father-in-law, the Previous Rebbe, to lead three of Chabad's central institutions: Machne Israel (the social services arm), Merkos L'Inyonei Chinuch (the educational arm), and the Kehot Publication Society. The anniversary was marked fifty years later, in 1991, by the Chabad delegation in Moscow with a yeshiva farbrengen.
