# Chabad delegation debates founding Igud Kehilot Chabad in Russia

*1991*

Five activists including Rabbi Berel Lazar, Rabbi M. Ch. Levin and Rabbi Z. Korobkin pressed the visiting delegation to formally register an Igud Kehilot Chabad before Pidrovsky's 'Vetzirna' body received exclusive recognition as the representative of Judaism in the USSR. The delegation declined to decide on the spot and asked for written pros and cons. The Rebbe later replied that the matter should be decided by experienced Chabad askanim.
