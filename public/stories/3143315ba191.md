# Visa #42 issued for R. Betzalel Katz

*1940*

R. Betzalel HaKohen Katz, called the elder of Lithuania's rabbis, had served the Rakishok community alongside R. Shmuel Levitin. In the summer of 5700 (1940), as part of the rescue effort, the Rebbe Rayatz arranged visa #42 of the first list of recipients for him to travel to the United States.
