# Fiery Purim farbrengen against the Yevesektzia

*1927*

The Rebbe screamed that the Yevesektzia did not frighten him and urged Chasidim to defy the regime to the point of self-sacrifice rather than send children to Communist schools. He told one Chasid that if confronted with the choice of a bonfire or yielding his child, he should accept the flames. Chasidim and informers alike sensed that retribution was imminent.
