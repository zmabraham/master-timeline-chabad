# Birth of Meir Shalom Kugel during grandfather's imprisonment

*1889*

Born on Wednesday Parshas Korach, Meir Shalom's birth was reported to his imprisoned grandfather R' Avraham Chaim by the mashpia R' Hendel Kugel, who traveled to Pleshchenitsy upon hearing the news. R' Avraham Chaim responded from prison on Parshas Pinchas with a blessing letter to his son-in-law and daughter, including detailed questions about the birth, the mohel, and the celebration.
