# Jerusalem Beis Din publishes 'Emet UMishpat Kasuv' in favor of Kollel officials

*1914*

The ruling, titled 'Emet UMishpat Kasuv', came after the two gabbaim had returned abroad without completing the formal din torah. It formalized the beis din's position that the Jerusalem officials were vindicated. Even so, accusations and counter-accusations between the two camps continued in printed pamphlets for years afterward.
