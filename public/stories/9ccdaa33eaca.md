# First post-war visit of Anash to Lubavitch; gravestones restored

*1966*

In 5726 (1966), for the first time since the war, members of Anash found an opportunity to come to Lubavitch. Rabbi Dovid Gurevitz traveled from distant Samarkand all the way to Lubavitch, where he re-erected the destroyed gravestones of the Tzemach Tzedek and the Rebbe Maharash. This visit marked the beginning of efforts to restore the holy site.
