# Chabad Portuguese-translation Haggadah by Shmuel Asher Bigon

*1992*

In 5752 (1992), Shmuel Asher Bigon's Portuguese translation of the Chabad Haggadah, with Zalman Kleiman's illustrations, was published in New York. The 84-page quarto edition served Brazilian and Portuguese-speaking Jewish communities. It continued the Chabad project of issuing the Haggadah in major world languages.
