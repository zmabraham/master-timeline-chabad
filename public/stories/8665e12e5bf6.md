# Confrontation between Tomchei Tmimim and Novardok yeshivas in Homel

*1922*

Novardok students had been founding small yeshivas across Soviet Russia, including one in Homel, but their unstructured curriculum and idiosyncratic mussar exercises — recording 'peulos' of humility such as asking shopkeepers for absurd goods — caused many students to transfer to the better-ordered Tomchei Tmimim. Two senior Novardok students, including Meir of Brisk, demanded that R' Yechezkel Feigin return their students or cease teaching. The author and Feigin physically escorted them out, and the matter was later argued with R' Avraham Yafen, then rosh yeshiva of Novardok in Homel.
