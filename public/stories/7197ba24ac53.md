# Itta Devorah Levin survives typhus at home in Samarkand

*1945*

In winter 5705 Itta Devorah Levin fell ill with brushnoi typhus; with Soviet hospitals nearly a death sentence, she was nursed at home and slowly recovered. Left weakened, she moved to her parents' home to convalesce, and the family changed apartments. The couple poured out prayers for healthy children, fulfilling 'horidi kanachal dimah.'
