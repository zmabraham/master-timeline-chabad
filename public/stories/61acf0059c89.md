# Sefer Lachmei Todah published in Offenbach

*1817*

The volume preserves the son's hesped describing the Baal Haflaah's more than thirty-three years of teaching Torah in Frankfurt. This precise phrasing allows historians to pin the Baal Haflaah's tenure to between summer 5531 and summer 5532. Lachmei Todah thus indirectly anchors the dating of the Alter Rebbe's editing of Hilchos Tzitzis and Pesach.
