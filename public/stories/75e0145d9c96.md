# Latest dated responsum in Mehadura Batra Orach Chaim manuscript

*1852*

Manuscript 1017 contains the Tzemach Tzedek's reorganized Orach Chaim responsa, drawing on lost volumes 2, 3, 6, 7, 8, 9, and 14 (the source volumes likely lost in the Lubavitch fire). Its latest internal date, 5612 (folio 276a), establishes the rough binding date.
