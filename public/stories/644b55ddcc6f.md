# Meeting with Robert Maxwell about the Chabad seforim

*1991*

On Motzaei Shabbos, 10 Tammuz 5751, Maxwell, Fisher and Jacobs hosted the delegation at Moscow's Hotel Oktyabrskaya. Fisher had spoken the day before with Zolotov, the Deputy Culture Minister, who confirmed only Gorbachev could now decide. Maxwell agreed Fisher would prepare the legal angle and Maxwell would press Primakov, Yakovlev and ultimately Gorbachev himself for the seforim's return.
