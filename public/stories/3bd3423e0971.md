# Rebbe directs book purchases from Purkersdorf sanitorium

*1935*

During the Rebbe's winter convalescence in Austria, he and his librarian conducted an active correspondence about acquisitions. Liberman's responses — including 7 Shevat 5695 — describe specific purchases of book lots for 25 to 200 zlotys, queries about exchanges of duplicates, and consultation on individual titles, evidence of the Rebbe's hands-on involvement in every significant decision.
