# Rebbe Rayatz makes brief stop in Warsaw en route from Ludomir

*1932*

On Tuesday 28 Menachem Av 5692 (August 31, 1932), the Rebbe Rayatz made a brief stop in Warsaw while traveling from Ludomir back to his home in Riga. It was one of two short visits to Warsaw in the years immediately preceding his 5693 relocation there.
