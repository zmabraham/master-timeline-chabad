# Walking stick of Berditchev and Slavita sent to the Rebbe

*1957*

In 1957 R. Shmuel Aba Shapira sent the Rebbe a walking stick that had belonged to the family—linked through marriage to Rabbi Levi Yitzchak of Berditchev and the Slavita brothers (R. Pinchas and R. Shmuel Aba). The stick had survived seizures by the Bolsheviks and Hitler; it broke en route to a Moscow prison and was repaired by a Polish prince who fashioned the current handle.
