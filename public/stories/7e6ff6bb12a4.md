# Cheder department opened in Tomchei Tmimim

*1903*

While initial discussions about opening a cheder began in summer 5662 with R' Menachem Mendel Diskin teaching eight boys, the cheder department was formally opened in 5663-5664. The Rebbe Rashab sent his brother Rashaz to Mohilev to obtain a permit assuring no government oversight of studies in the cheder. Within three years, the cheder grew into two shiur classes and three chadarim.
