# Alter Rebbe studies halacha with the Maggid in Rovno

*1772*

In his own writing the Alter Rebbe records that during summer 5532 (1772) he was in the home of the Maggid of Mezeritch in Rovno. They discussed a halachic question concerning shechita, and the Maggid concurred with his ruling. The episode reflects the close halachic training he received.
