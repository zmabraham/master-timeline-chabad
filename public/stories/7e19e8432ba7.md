# Reports on 2 and 13 Nissan hilula observances

*1940*

Even after the war had broken out in Europe but before the German conquest of Lithuania, R. Yehoshua Isaac Baruch continued his correspondence. He reported to the Rebbe Rayatz on hisva'aduyos held for the 2 Nissan (Rebbe Rashab) and 13 Nissan (Tzemach Tzedek) yahrzeits in Panidel, Kelm, Anishishok, Ponevezh, and Taurage, including the daily Tanya shiur of R. Yehoshua Asher Rotfeld's chaburah.
