# Tzemach Tzedek responsa to R. Avraham Sheines of Shklov

*1825*

According to Beis Rebbi (p. 120), R. Avraham son of R. Tzvi Sheines of Shklov brought before the Tzemach Tzedek a dispute among R. Aharon Halevi of Strashelye, R. Gershon of Shklov, R. Nechemiah of Dubrovna, the author of Beis Ephraim, and others. The Tzemach Tzedek's responses are recorded in Even HaEzer §§297–298, dated 5585.
