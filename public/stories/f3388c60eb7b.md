# Menachem Mendel Horenstein killed in Treblinka

*1942*

Mordechai Unrad, who had been in Mendel's barracks at Treblinka, confirmed the dates of death to the Ramash in August 1949. The couple had adopted their nephew Yaakov Yosef Lis, who also perished. The information was withheld from the Sixth Rebbe in his lifetime.
