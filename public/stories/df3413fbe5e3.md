# Publication of HaTamim journal halted by outbreak of WWII

*1939*

From the first issue in late summer 5695 through the eighth issue in winter 5699, the Rebbe Rayatz's son-in-law in Paris had continued to substantively edit HaTamim under cover of named Warsaw editors. The outbreak of war in autumn 5699 ended the journal's run; Agudas Talmidei HaTmimim had also been publishing Likkutei Dibburim, the Megillas Yud-Tes Kislev, and a Tehillim compilation under R' Schneur Zalman Gourary's direction.
