# Final photograph of students learning at Otwock

*1939*

On 10 Av 5699 (July 26, 1939), approximately one month before the outbreak of World War II, a photograph was taken at the Gelbfish pension showing yeshiva students learning sugyot together in the courtyard. R. Yosef Rodal sat at the center. The photograph was sent to R. Moshe Pinchas Katz, who had recently emigrated to America, and is likely the last image of Tomchei Tmimim students learning in Otwock.
