# Rabbi Moshe Axelrod leaves Zlobin for Suraz

*1927*

Axelrod had filled the position of his brother-in-law Rabbi Menachem Mendel Dubrawsky in Zlobin. The Frierdiker Rebbe wrote a letter of blessing upon his acceptance of the Suraz rabbinate, noting that 'from of old, men of name have sat there.' His departure opened the way for Rabbi Yerachmiel Binyaminson to come to Zlobin.
