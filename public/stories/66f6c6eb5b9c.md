# Yosef Goldstein flees from Chmielnik to Radom

*1941*

Once in the Radom ghetto, Goldstein was struck by the poverty and the sight of Jewish children and bochurim wandering without teachers or supervision. He concluded that a yeshiva was urgently needed, and together with Meir Hendel resolved to establish one. His arrival was a key catalyst for the underground Tomchei Temimim yeshiva in the Radom ghetto.
