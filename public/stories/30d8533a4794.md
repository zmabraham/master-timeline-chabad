# Chabad community purchases Beis Menachem synagogue outright

*1862*

After four years on a mortgage backed by Sassoon's donation, the community faced losing the property when the seller wanted his money back. A donor from Kherson contributed 150 lira sterling, additional gifts came in, and they took a permissible-interest loan to reach the full 600 lira sterling purchase price. The acquisition secured the synagogue for the Jerusalem Chabad community permanently.
