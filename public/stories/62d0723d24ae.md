# Latin translation of the Haggadah printed in Frankfurt

*1698*

In 5458 (1698), a Haggadah was published in Frankfurt featuring the Hebrew text alongside a parallel Latin translation. The Hebrew was printed without vowel points, and the edition included musical notation for the songs 'Ki Lo Naeh' and 'Adir Hu'. The 70-page work made the Seder text accessible to Latin-reading scholars of the era.
