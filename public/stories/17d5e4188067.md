# R' Yehuda Eber arrives in Warsaw to examine yeshiva students

*1931*

On Monday 2 Iyar 5691, R' Yehuda Eber arrived in Warsaw, sent by the Rebbe Rayatz from Riga to examine the students of Tomchei Tmimim Warsaw and select those suitable for transfer to a new Ulam being opened in Riga. He immediately began by testing the younger students.
