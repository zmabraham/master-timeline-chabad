# Manuscript of Rabbi Yechiel Halperin (author of Seder HaDoros), chiddushim

*1746*

Manuscript of Rabbi Yechiel Halperin (author of Seder HaDoros), chiddushim on tractates Zevachim-Menachos. Rabbi Yechiel Halperin 5420 (1660) - 5506 (1746) was a rabbi, kabbalist and researcher of the history of the Jewish people, who served in the rabbinate and as av beis din and as rosh yeshiva
