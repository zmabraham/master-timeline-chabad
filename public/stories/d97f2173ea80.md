# Ministry of Culture issues restrictive letter on Chabad library

*1991*

On Erev Shabbos Adar I 5751, the diarist retrieved from the Ministry of Culture a letter dated six weeks earlier stating that the delegation would only be allowed to photograph the books and discuss exchanges of duplicates — not receive them. He asked that the letter be dated the day of receipt to demonstrate that the Ministry had ignored Gorbachev's instructions to return the library.
