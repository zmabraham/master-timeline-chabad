# Yeshiva Toras Emes founded in Hebron

*1911*

The yeshiva Toras Emes was founded in Hebron in early 5672 (late 1911). It operated for some two and a half years until the outbreak of World War I forced the mashpia-director and students — Russian subjects in an enemy state — to return to Russia. The yeshiva would lie dormant for seven years until being refounded in Jerusalem.
