# Rebbe writes again to R' Alter pressing for expansion and student development

*1923*

The Rebbe noted he had received no updates since his Iyar letter and reiterated that students must repeat chassidus in public. He told Alter and the staff that the duty of rebuilding Tomchei Tmimim 'as it once was' rested on them, promising that a portion of the donations from overseas chassidim would be allocated to Warsaw's needs.
