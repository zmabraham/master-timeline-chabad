# Rabbi Uri Ben Shachar Rabbi Uri Ben Shachar 8 Av 5686 17 Elul

*2007*

Rabbi Uri Ben Shachar Rabbi Uri Ben Shachar 8 Av 5686 17 Elul 5767, 1926-2007) was a chief activist in the Ohalei Yosef Yitzchak network in the Holy Land, principal of the Chabad school in Jaffa, chairman of the religious council in Kfar Chabad, and head of the Tiferet Zkeinei kollel
