# Reports on 12-13 Tammuz Geulah celebrations

*1940*

R. Yehoshua Isaac Baruch transmitted reports from R. Leib Kamraz of Ushpol, R. Zev Razinkevitch of Birz (joined by bochurim from the Novardok yeshiva), R. Yosef Genkin of Vizun (who said the maamar 'Baruch HaGomel l'Chayavim Tovos'), and R. Itche Bangs of Taurage on the Chag HaGeulah farbrengens.
