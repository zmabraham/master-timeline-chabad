# Sh. D. Levin begins editing Igrot Kodesh series

*1980*

Starting in 5740 (1980), at the Lubavitcher Rebbe's directive, R' Shalom DovBer Levin began assembling and editing the Igrot Kodesh series of the Chabad Rebbes' letters. The historical research generated by this editorial work became the seed material for the Toldot Chabad volumes — including this one on Tsarist Russia.
