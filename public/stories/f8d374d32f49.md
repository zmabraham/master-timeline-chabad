# Hebrew-English Haggadah for children illustrated by Nachum Waldman and Boruch Gorkin

*1987*

Around 5747 (1987), a Haggadah following the Chabad Alter Rebbe's nusach was published in New York with English translation by Yaakov Emanuel Schochet and illustrations by Nachum Waldman and Boruch Gorkin. The 64-page octavo edition (also issued in a miniature format) targeted Chabad children. It contributed to the Rebbe's educational vision for Pesach observance.
