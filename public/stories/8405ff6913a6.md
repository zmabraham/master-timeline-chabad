# Rebbe declines proposal to name Aguch representative in Russia

*1991*

On 15 Tevet the delegation faxed R' Yehuda Leib Groner a proposal that Aguch appoint a representative in Russia to coordinate all Chabad institutions there. Groner replied that he had already raised the idea with R' Cunin and that the Rebbe had instructed not to intervene in such matters. The same day Veronika reported that Lichachev and Shenazara, a Gorbachev adviser, had pressed Gubinko to release the library.
