# Rabbi Baruch Ber Leibowitz appointed rosh yeshivah of Knesses Beis Yitzchak

*1904*

Rabbi Baruch Ber became one of the most influential Lithuanian rosh yeshivos of his era. He would later move the yeshivah to Vilna in 1921 and to Kamenitz in 1926, where it became known as the famed Kamenitz yeshivah.
