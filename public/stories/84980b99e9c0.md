# Baron Ginzburg intervenes to block Grodno assembly

*1903*

When press attacks and denunciations failed to block the Grodno assembly, Haskalah leaders dispatched Baron Naftali Hertz Ginzburg to lobby the Minister of the Interior directly. According to Ginzburg's report, he had turned the minister against the assembly by arguing that the invitees were rabbis from minor towns. Rebbe Rashab lamented in letters dated 3-8 Tevet that this exposed how far the power of those opposed to Orthodox interests had grown.
