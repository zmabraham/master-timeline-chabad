# Rebbe Rayatz publishes thanksgiving letter for recovery

*1944*

On 24 Kislev 5705 the Rebbe Rayatz, beginning to regain strength, composed a public letter (Igrot Kodesh vol. 8, letter 2,548) thanking God for His great kindness and Anash for their prayers and blessings, which he credited with raising him from three weeks of severe suffering. Even then, he was confined to bed by day and forbidden from returning to the yechidus room.
