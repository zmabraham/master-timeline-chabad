# Freigang orders gendarmerie officers to investigate Tzemach Tzedek

*1841*

Following Benckendorff's directive, Freigang relayed orders to Colonels Kutsinsky and Wojciech and Captain Yurkovsky. Wojciech of Mogilev would personally travel to Lyubavichi to interview the Rebbe under cover of investigating the local synagogue. The coordinated three-province inquiry generated the most detailed contemporary external descriptions of the Tzemach Tzedek's personality, household and influence.
