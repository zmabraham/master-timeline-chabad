# Khovansky appeals acquittal of Rabbi DovBer to the Senate in St. Petersburg

*1827*

On 15 May 1827 (Rosh Chodesh Sivan 5587), Governor Khovansky declined to confirm the Mohilev court's acquittal of Rabbi DovBer and instead lodged an appeal with the Senate in St. Petersburg. He also attached broader policy proposals affecting the status and rights of rabbis throughout Russia, which the Senate said it would consider separately.
