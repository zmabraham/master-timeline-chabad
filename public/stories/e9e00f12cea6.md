# Rebbe writes Achei Tmimim heads on 3 Tammuz

*1946*

On 3 Tammuz 5706 (Igrot Kodesh vol. 9, letter 2924), the Frierdiker Rebbe wrote to the heads of Achei Tmimim yeshivas in several American cities asking them to travel to the towns the Rashag would suggest and arrange welcome celebrations for the Tomchei Tmimim students arriving from Shanghai.
