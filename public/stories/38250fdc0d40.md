# Knesses Beis Yitzchak yeshivah founded in Slabodka

*1897*

A major controversy erupted in Slabodka between those who argued that study should focus exclusively on Talmud and those who supported teaching ethics through musar. Those opposed to musar opened Knesses Beis Yitzchak in 1897. In 1904 they hired Rabbi Baruch Ber Leibowitz as rosh yeshivah.
