# Hebron Sephardic and Ashkenazi communities agree on Montefiore split

*1906*

In 5666 (1906) the Sephardic and Ashkenazi communities of Hebron formalized how the Montefiore estate funds reaching the city would be divided between them. A receipt preserved in the SY"L Archive documents that on 22 Elul 5666 (September 12, 1906) one beneficiary received five pounds sterling as his share of the estate's fruits.
