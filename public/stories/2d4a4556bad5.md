# Chassidim sign debt note to the Tzemach Tzedek in Lubavitch

*1857*

The shtar may record a loan from the Tzemach Tzedek to two chassidim staying in Lubavitch, or possibly a ma'amad obligation pledged to the Rebbe. R' Yitzchak Chaim Dovber HaLevi Wilensky was among the great chassidim of the era, active in the courts of the Tzemach Tzedek, the Maharash, and the Rashab. He had arrived in Lubavitch shortly after his marriage around age 18-19.
