# R' Dovber Efrat takes over Hebron Chabad leadership

*1895*

Following the deaths of R' Shimon Menashe Chaikin, R' Yehuda Leib Slonim, and R' Levi Yitzchak Slonim, R' Dovber Efrat was appointed rabbi of Hebron Chabad and took over the kollel administration. He was joined by R' Mordechai Dovber Slonim and R' Binyamin Rivlin in leading the community.
