# Rebbe called for maftir on first day of Rosh Hashana 5723

*1962*

Because the first day of Rosh Hashana 5723 fell on Shabbat there was no shofar blowing. Levin pushed his way onto the bima next to baal koreh R' Mordechai Shusterman to hear the maftir from the Rebbe. The Rebbe read the haftarah of Chana with such inner weeping and sweetness that Levin had to cover his face with his tallit to muffle his sobs.
