# Rashag reports to Meisel on Passover matzah campaign progress

*1929*

The Rashag described the daily struggle with Russian officials, who repeatedly stalled and shifted authority between Riga, Moscow, and Berlin. He praised Chief Rabbi Hertz's major public announcement in London and the strong support from French Chief Rabbi Levi and Russian immigrant donors in Paris. Committees had been formed across Europe and as far as Shanghai and Harbin.
