# Rebbe calls to publicize teachings on yearning for redemption

*1985*

In a special sichah delivered on 7 MarCheshvan 5746 (October 1985), the Rebbe urged that the words of great Jewish leaders be publicized — namely, that the desire and anticipation for the redemption itself hastens its coming. This was part of the Rebbe's increasing emphasis on the imminence of Moshiach.
