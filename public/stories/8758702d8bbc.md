# R' Shmuel Chaim Kesselman reports new arrangement at Lod yeshiva

*1951*

On 8 Iyar 5711 R' Shmuel Chaim Kesselman wrote that as of Rosh Chodesh Iyar, the study schedule at Tomchei Tmimim at the Lod railway station was running well. The students transferred from Tel Aviv joined the older students already there plus the senior shiur, totalling about forty students all housed together in one large multi-room building suitable for study, meals, and lodging.
