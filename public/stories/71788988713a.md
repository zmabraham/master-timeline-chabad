# R' Avraham Chaim Rosenbaum arrested

*1888*

In 5648 (1888) R' Avraham Chaim Rosenbaum, leader of the Pleshchenitsy Chassidic community after R' Hendel Kugel moved to Lubavitch, was arrested. He remained imprisoned, including in Smolensk, until late 5650 (1890), writing several letters from prison to his friends and family during that period.
