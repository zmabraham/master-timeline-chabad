# Rebbe Rayatz passes away on 10 Shevat 5710

*1950*

Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn, the sixth Lubavitcher Rebbe, passed away on 10 Shevat 5710 (January 28, 1950). The pencil he used continuously in his final years was preserved by his secretary, Rabbi Nissan Mindel. His son-in-law, the Rebbe, wrote in a letter dated 20 Shevat 5710 that the loss had left Chassidim stunned but resolved to continue with even greater strength.
