# Eliyahu Segal purchases first portion of Lubavitch courtyard

*1996*

The purchase was the first step in reassembling the historic courtyard for use as a Chabad museum. Rabbi Nosson Gourary, the Rebbe's emissary to Buffalo, had been negotiating with the Russian government on the project since the opening of the Iron Curtain. The Rebbe had repeatedly urged him to bring the plan to fruition.
