# Cheder branch of Torat Emet founded in Jaffa

*1923*

In a letter dated Rosh Chodesh Marcheshvan 5684, the Rebbe Rayyatz responded to pleas from Chabad chassidim in Jaffa about their dire spiritual situation and instructed Havlin, director of Torat Emet in Jerusalem, to organize a children's cheder and yeshiva there under the Torat Emet name. R' David Patash, a prominent member of the Tel Aviv chareidi community who had recently arrived from Russia, helped lead the effort. The Rebbe also wrote directly to Patash a few weeks later seeking his help to support the new institution.
