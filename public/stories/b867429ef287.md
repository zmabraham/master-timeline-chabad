# Rebbe Rashab laments rabbinic inaction against Russian religious decline

*1901*

After repeated failed attempts to convene a rabbinical assembly in summer 5661, the Rebbe Rashab expressed bitter frustration in a letter to R. Eliezer Moshe Modayevsky. He reported informing R. Chaim Ozer Grodzinski of Vilna that the rabbis should no longer rely on him and must act independently, while he himself stood ready to travel for any assembly that would actually convene. Although R. Chaim Ozer was passionate about the cause, the Rebbe felt the man lacked the power to effect internal change.
