# Small Chabad community established in Riga

*1878*

The Riga Chabad community grew slowly until enhanced by Russian emigration in the early twentieth century. In the Jewish ghetto, one Chabad synagogue housed three adjacent sanctuaries for the varied Chabad sub-groups. But there was no yeshivah to bring a youthful spirit to the city, a far cry from Lubavitch's vibrant ethos.
