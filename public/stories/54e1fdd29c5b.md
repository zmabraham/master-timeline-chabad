# Maharash's first dated chassidic discourse manuscript

*1853*

Among the manuscripts inherited and produced by Maharash, the first dated hanacha (transcript) in his own handwriting is titled 'Beshalach, Motzaei Menucha, 5613.' These transcripts recorded discourses of his father, the Tzemach Tzedek, and would form a core part of the Chabad manuscript collection later catalogued by his descendants.
