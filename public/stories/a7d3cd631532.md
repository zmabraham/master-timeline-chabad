# Maharayatz to Kollel Chabad: a dedicated staffer for the kollel is essential

*1930*

After returning from his year-long trip to America, the Rebbe redoubled his involvement in kollel matters, sending shadarim and writing extensively to town gabbaim across Eastern Europe. His letter to the leadership argued that the cost of a dedicated staffer would itself be 'income, not expense', because of how much fundraising and administration it would unlock. The letter is part of an intense burst of administrative reorganization spanning 5690–5691.
