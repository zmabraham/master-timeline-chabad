# R. Chaikel Chaikin escorts Levin's group to Hof DP camp

*1946*

On Thursday Parshat Ki Seitzei, 9 Elul 5706, R. Chaikel Chaikin — already settled at Hof — drove out to fetch them. Hof was further from the border and considered safer. The group spent Shabbat Parshat Ki Seitzei (11 Elul) there before moving on.
