# Rebbe Rayatz instructs Jacobson to send Achei Tmimim students to Otwock

*1938*

The Rebbe Rayatz directed Jacobson 'to make efforts to send students from Achei Tmimim to the Tomchei Tmimim Yeshiva in Otwock,' adding that with God's help this would become the seed for establishing a yeshiva in their own country in the spirit of Tomchei Tmimim. This shaped subsequent travel plans for additional groups.
