# EY support sent via R' Ephraim Yitzchak Epstein

*1830*

The 5590 receipt records that the support funds for Eretz Yisrael were transmitted through R' Ephraim Yitzchak Epstein and his son-in-law R' Yosef Yehuda Leib ben R' Chaim of Shiof. According to the 5599 Montefiore census, both immigrated to Eretz Yisrael themselves in 5590 - two years after the Mitteler Rebbe's passing.
