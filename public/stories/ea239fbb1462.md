# Mohilev provincial report on continued investigations in Kapust, Orsha, and Mohilev

*1826*

The provincial government detailed how the Babinovich court had relied on a policeman who returned having lost the Rebbe's responses, then dispatched a second courier and ordered sworn testimony to be collected from the Jewish communities of Orsha, Kapust, and Shklov. The investigators sought to verify a claim that in 1813 the people of Kremenchug had invited Rabbi Schneur to succeed his father, promising to compensate his property losses from the French invasion and to support his entire family. Officials were fined for procedural lapses, and the courts were ordered to complete depositions and rule on the case as swiftly as possible.
