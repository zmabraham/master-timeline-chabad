# Sixth edition of Tanya printed in Shklov with revised Igeret HaTeshuva

*1806*

For this 5566 (1806) Shklov printing—the sixth edition within roughly a decade—the Alter Rebbe re-edited Igeret HaTeshuva and entrusted it to press together with the Tanya. The reworked Igeret HaTeshuva also appeared as a 20-folio standalone booklet in Shklov the same year.
