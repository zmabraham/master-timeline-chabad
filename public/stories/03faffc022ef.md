# Chabad delegation buys Moscow office near Kremlin

*1990*

During the special mission to Moscow the delegation acquired a 15-by-15-foot room in a historic building near Gorky Street and the Kremlin, paying a $300 down payment. They placed a set of Rambam in the office and started a library with the three volumes of Toldos Chabad, holding a siyum and hatchalah on the Rambam study. The Rebbe approved hanging a Beis Agudas Chassidei Chabad sign in Hebrew and Russian on the door and affixing a mezuzah.
