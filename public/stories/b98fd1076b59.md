# Marriage testimony for Mitteler Rebbe's daughter and R' Yaakov Yisrael of Cherkas

*1811*

On 7 Shevat 5571 (February 1, 1811), a 'zichron edut' testimony document was drawn up concerning the marriage of Rabbi Yaakov Yisrael of Cherkas to a daughter of Rabbi Dovber, the Mitteler Rebbe. The document records the name of the Mitteler Rebbe's wife as Shaina daughter of R' Yaakov, providing one of the few clear sources for identifying his father-in-law by name.
