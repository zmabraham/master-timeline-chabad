# R' Shlomo of Karlin becomes rabbi of Karlin

*1772*

The historical record establishes that R' Shlomo HaKohen of Karlin served as rabbi of Karlin starting in 5532 (1772) and continued there until shortly before 5544. He was a leading disciple of the Maggid of Mezritch and later a major chassidic leader in Lithuania.
