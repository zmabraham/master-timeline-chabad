# Rabbi Zalmanov — Rabbi Yisrael Yosef Zalmanov, 16 Menachem Av 5699

*2020*

Rabbi Zalmanov — Rabbi Yisrael Yosef Zalmanov (16 Menachem Av 5699, 1939 – 7 Menachem Av 5780, 2020) was among the dignitaries of the Chabad community in Petach Tikva and one of the senior Chabad chassidim in the Holy Land.
