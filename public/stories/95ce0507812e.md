# Rebbe Rayatz compiles inventory of his father's manuscripts

*1920*

On 19 Mar-Cheshvan 5681, during his year of mourning for his father, Rebbe Yosef Yitzchak compiled a detailed catalogue of every Chassidic maamar that remained in his father Rebbe Rashab's own handwriting. He also recorded the names of the chozrim who transmitted maamarim delivered orally, and summarized the collection of letters left behind. The list was later printed at the end of Kuntres Chanoch LaNaar.
