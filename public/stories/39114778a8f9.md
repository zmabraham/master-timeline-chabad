# R' Chaim Leib Gar's son R' Tzvi Gar born in Yakobshtadt

*1891*

R' Tzvi Gar was born in Yakobshtadt, Kurland — a stronghold of Chabad Chassidism — to his father R' Chaim Leib. He was educated at Tomchei Tmimim in Lubavitch and later served as a mashgiach in the Riga slaughterhouse, becoming the Rebbe Rayatz's contact person in Riga in the early years of his leadership.
