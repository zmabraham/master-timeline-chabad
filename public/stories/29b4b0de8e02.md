# Three-hour meeting at Soviet Foreign Ministry on Schneersohn books

*1991*

On 21 Adar 5751, Rabbis Kunin, Aharonov, Kogan and the diarist met Lebedev, Kruchkolov and Shmatov starting at 10am. Kunin handed over a numbered packet ranging from accreditation papers and letters from Senator Kennedy and Armand Hammer to letters from Soviet rabbis and Chabad leaders. Lebedev promised to study the material and try to give an answer the following Tuesday.
