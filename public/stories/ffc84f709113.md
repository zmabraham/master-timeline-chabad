# R. Baruch Shalom Cohen moves to Volomin and opens Tomchei Tmimim branch

*1912*

After living in Warsaw for over a decade, R. Baruch Shalom Cohen moved to the nearby town of Volomin in 5672 (1912). There he founded a branch of Yeshivat Tomchei Tmimim, extending the yeshiva network beyond Lubavitch itself into the Polish-Russian border region.
