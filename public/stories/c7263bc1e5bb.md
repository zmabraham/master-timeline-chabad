# Rebbe Rashab denounces Baron Ginzburg in 19 Kislev address

*1908*

In his 19 Kislev address the Rebbe denounced 'the architect of the movement in government circles,' Baron Hertz Ginzburg, and recounted that he had pleaded with him 'give me Yavneh'—asking that at least Lubavitch be spared a Mefitzei Haskalah school—and that the baron refused even this minimal concession.
