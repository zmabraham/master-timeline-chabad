# 'Lubavitch Collection' labeling of Polyakov books begins in Khimki

*2000*

On 8 Tammuz 5760, the emissary arrived in Moscow with 3,000 self-printed 'Lubavitch Collection' stickers, having distrusted the library's offer of blank labels. Working with librarians Oleg and Lena on the 10th floor in Khimki, he passed over about 2,500 books and identified 263 as bearing clear Lubavitch markings, which they immediately labeled.
