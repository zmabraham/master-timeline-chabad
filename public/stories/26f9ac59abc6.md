# Hidden Chanukah farbrengen at a tinsmith's house, Nun Hei of Chanukah 5674

*1913*

On the 5th night of Chanukah 5674 a secret farbrengen was held with R. Shmuel Gronem Esterman. Because doctors had forbidden him whiskey due to eye trouble, the gathering was kept hidden; the talmid Yitzchak Raskin had bought a menorah at a tinsmith's workshop near Chochlukeh Street and arranged to use the place. About thirty bochurim attended; R. Gronem taught them the niggun "Mipnei mah yarda haneshama" and spoke discreetly about shemiras habris.
