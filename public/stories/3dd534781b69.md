# Sheterna Riza and R' Shalom Kaznovsky make aliyah to Hebron

*1860*

Sheterna Riza, eldest daughter of Menucha Rachel and R' Yaakov Slonim, had married R' Shalom Kaznovsky abroad before her parents' aliyah. In 5620-5621, following the directive of the Tzemach Tzedek, the couple themselves made aliyah to Hebron. Their son R' Eliezer Shimon was already married at the time and remained abroad temporarily.
