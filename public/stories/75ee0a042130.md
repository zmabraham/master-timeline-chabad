# Death of Ephraim Shmuel Levin in Lipovets

*1933*

Ephraim Shmuel had taught Torah in Lipovets with great devotion, refusing to allow his daughters to attend Soviet schools even under threat of fines. His widow Leah Beila was sickly with heart disease and struggled to support the younger children Sarah, Rivka, and Freide. The eldest son Yosef was then nearing twenty-five and Yisrael Yehuda was about twenty-one.
