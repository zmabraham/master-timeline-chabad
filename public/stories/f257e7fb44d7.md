# Rebbe Rayatz rents two apartments in Riga

*1927*

After about a month at the Kommerz Hotel, the Rebbe Rayatz secured two small apartments on two floors. The lower floor housed his mother the Rebbetzin and contained the prayer room and part of his library. The upper floor was for the Rebbe and his daughters Chaya Mushka and Sheina, while his daughter Chana and son-in-law Rashag took a separate apartment.
