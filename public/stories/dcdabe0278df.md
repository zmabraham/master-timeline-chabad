# Rebbe Rashab's public letter in Iyar 5677

*1917*

In Iyar 5677 (May 1917), during the brief Kerensky government in revolutionary Russia, Rabbi Sholom Dovber Schneersohn (the Rebbe Rashab) issued a general public letter, later printed in his Igros Kodesh, vol. II, no. 469. The letter addressed his Chassidim during a turbulent period preceding the Bolshevik takeover.
