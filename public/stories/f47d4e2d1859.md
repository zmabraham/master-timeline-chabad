# R' Yehuda Leib Zalkind of Dinaburg passes away

*1857*

After roughly four decades as rabbi of Dinaburg, R' Yehuda Leib Zalkind passed away on 23 Kislev 5618 (December 1857). A disciple of the Alter Rebbe and contemporary of the Tzemach Tzedek, he had corresponded with the latter in halachic responsa from their youth.
