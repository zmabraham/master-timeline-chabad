# R. Shmuel ibn Danan sells future inheritance share to brother in Fez

*1697*

On 29 Shevat 5457 (1697) in Fez, R. Shmuel ibn Danan, son of the elder R. Saadia, sold to his brother Yitzchak whatever he would inherit from their still-living father, both the firstborn share and the regular share, in exchange for 500 ouquiyot. Because the inheritance was 'a thing not yet in the world,' a standard kinyan was legally insufficient, so the sale was reinforced by a severe oath that Shmuel would repay the sum in full if the sale ever lapsed. The shtar was signed by R. Yehuda ibn Atar and R. Avraham ibn Masud ibn Atia.
