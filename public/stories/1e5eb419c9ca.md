# R' Moshe Meisels passes away in Hebron

*1849*

R' Moshe Meisels, author of 'Shirat Moshe' on the 613 commandments, passed away in Hebron on 24 Menachem Av 1849. A longtime trustee of the Vilna community who later spied for Czar Alexander I against Napoleon and met Napoleon personally twice, he settled in Tzfat circa 1816 and moved to Hebron in 1823, where he became one of the leading administrators of the Chabad kollel.
