# Rabbi Yinon Giladi giving a Gemara shiur at the Chabad Yeshiva of Romema

*1981*

Rabbi Yinon Giladi giving a Gemara shiur at the Chabad Yeshiva of Romema. Rabbi Yinon Shalom Giladi (born 5741, 1981) is an expert in teaching Gemara to beginners, knowledgeable in matters of Torah and science and the integration between them, who delivers many shiurim across a range of Torah subjects
