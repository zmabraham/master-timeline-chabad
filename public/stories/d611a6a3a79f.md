# Memorial kuntres for Rebbe Rayatz published with dedication to Eliyahu Shusterman

*1951*

The kuntres that appeared for Rosh Hashana 5712 (autumn 1951) — published close to the conclusion of the author's shiva for his father — included a dedication for the elevation of the soul of Eliyahu ben Achiezer Shusterman. This was the first of several kuntresim he sponsored in his father's memory.
