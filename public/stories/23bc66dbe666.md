# Rebbe Rayatz writes to Elchanan Glaser about Polish quota

*1945*

On 26 Tishrei 5706 (Igrot Kodesh vol. 9, letter 2772), the Frierdiker Rebbe wrote in Yiddish to R. Elchanan Glaser explaining that his best students from the Warsaw and Vilna Tomchei Tmimim yeshivos were now stranded in Shanghai after missing the last ship due to visa formalities. He noted that with strong advocacy in Washington it might be possible to bring them under the Polish quota, or quotas of other countries that were nearly unused, and asked if Congressman Dickstein could be enlisted to take a serious interest in the matter.
