# R' Levin's letter on Hebron's Jewish court system

*1888*

On 22 Sivan 5648 (June 2, 1888), R' Yehuda Leib Shaul Levin wrote that din Torah cases in Hebron were numerous because the community did not use gentile courts, and even Jew-Muslim disputes were brought before the Jewish beit din. He explained that despite this, the rabbi received no fees for rulings, only the basic small salary. The letter is preserved in Sinai journal.
