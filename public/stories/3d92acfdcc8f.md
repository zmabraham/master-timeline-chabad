# Passing of R' Moshe Rosenblum in Rostov

*1920*

R' Moshe Rosenblum, a distinguished sofer and yerei shamayim, accompanied the Rashab's exile from Lubavitch in 5676. As they left the town he expressed hope to be reunited soon, and the Rebbe replied that he hoped it would be by Pesach. In the event Rosenblum remained in Rostov and died there on 2 Nisan 5680.
