# Arrest of Rebbe Rayatz

*1927*

During the imprisonment, Rayatz worried in his cell about whether the manuscripts in his home would be seized by the authorities. Simultaneously, the future Rebbe — then his son-in-law — quickly distributed the manuscripts among trusted chassidim to hide them, returning them after the liberation. The episode is recorded in Rayatz's prison diary in Likutei Dibburim vol. 4 p. 1246.
