# Government publishes Rambam excerpts under guise of Rebbe's approval

*1850*

In 5610 (1850), the Russian government in Petersburg printed condensed study booklets, 'Some Laws from the Yad Hachazaka of the Rambam,' for use in chadarim. The title page falsely implied the Rebbe's approval by referencing his responsa. This contradicted the Tzemach Tzedek's clear opposition to teaching such anthologies, which he considered useless to the children.
