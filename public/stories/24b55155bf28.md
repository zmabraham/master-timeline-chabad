# Poalei Tzion attack at yeshiva office

*1906*

On Motzaei Shabbat after Purim 5666 (1906), after a student named Dovber Davzik who had joined Poalei Tzion was expelled and his belongings confiscated, a group of about 40 men and women from Poalei Tzion came to the courtyard, shooting pistols at the office window. The bullet passed by a guest rabbi from Romanov who was leaning over the table — miraculously no one was hurt. The Rabbanit Rivka (mother of the Rebbe) heard the shots from her nearby home.
