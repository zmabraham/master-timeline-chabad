# Rebbe Rayatz secludes himself during mourning for his father

*1920*

After Rebbe Sholom DovBer passed away on 2 Nissan 5680, his son Rebbe Yosef Yitzchak entered a period of intense seclusion. As he later described in his letters, from the first day he felt as a man struck by thunder, finding no rest except in his thrice-daily prayers, and remaining closed in his room without speaking. This period of withdrawal and inner reckoning shaped his early nesius.
