# Rebbe Rayatz flees Schmotkin apartment as bombs fall

*1939*

Just before candle-lighting, a bomb struck the Schmotkin home and the building caught fire. Neighbors screamed and ran toward the gate, while chassidim surrounded the Rebbe to shield him. He sat on a chair murmuring with his lips while a chassid reviewed Mishnayos by heart, with the Rebbe correcting his errors. The family fled to another neighborhood for the High Holy Days.
