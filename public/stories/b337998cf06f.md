# Rosh Hashanah and Sukkot 5698 minyan in Krivoy Rog

*1937*

Fearing exposure at the main synagogue where a chazzan from Kremenchug officiated, the group organized their own minyan at R' Yoel Itkin's home. On Simchat Torah they secretly observed the synagogue hakafot and saw the visiting young chazzan from Dnepropetrovsk dancing to Chabad niggunim, later learning he was among Rabbi Levi Yitzchak Schneerson's students at the local technicum.
