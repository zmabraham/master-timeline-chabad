# Hebron Arab rebellion against Egyptian forces, Jews killed

*1834*

On Sunday 9 Iyar 5594 the Arabs of Hebron and surrounding villages rebelled against the Egyptian deputy commander stationed there. Several Chabad chassidim were killed in the attack, while about 400 others were trapped inside the citadel (kala) for weeks until starvation forced their surrender. The community endured constant threats of being burned alive and was saved only through massive ransom payments demanded by local officials.
