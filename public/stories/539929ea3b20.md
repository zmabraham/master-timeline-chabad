# First Hebron Chabad shadar journey rejected by Tzfat kollel

*1821*

In summer 1821 (קיץ תקפ"א), the head of the Tzfat Chassidic kollel, R' Gershon Margolies, opposed a shadar sent on behalf of the new Hebron Chabad community to the diaspora. He intercepted some of the propaganda letters intended to support the new Chabad settlement in Hebron.
