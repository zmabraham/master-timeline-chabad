# Birth of Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn

*1880*

On June 30, 1880 (12 Tamuz 5640), Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn was born in Lubavitch to Rabbi Shalom Dovber (the Rashab) and Rebbetzin Shterna Sara. His birth followed a vivid dream in which his mother was reassured by three previous Lubavitcher Rebbes that she would bear a son. At his circumcision, his grandfather Rabbi Shmuel served as sandek and prophetically declared that the child would become a Rebbe.
