# Munkacs Haggadah 'Tiferet Banim' by Tzvi Hirsch Shapira

*1914*

In 5674 (1914), the Haggadah with a Pardes-style commentary called 'Tiferet Banim' by Tzvi Hirsch Shapira, head of the Munkacs rabbinical court, was published in Munkacs by Kahn and Fried. The 82-leaf octavo edition represented the Munkacs Chassidic and rabbinical tradition. It contributed to the rich tradition of Carpathian Chassidic Haggadah commentaries.
