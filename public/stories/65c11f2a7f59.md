# Chatzkevich appointed rabbi of Kupishok

*1927*

At the start of 5688 (autumn 1927), R. Yisrael Noach Chatzkevich was accepted as rabbi of Kupishok. The Rebbe wrote two letters dated 15 Cheshvan 5688 (Igros Kodesh vol. 16, letters 5810-5811): one to Chatzkevich blessing his appointment and asking him to set up public shiurim in nigleh and chassidus, and one to the Kupishok chassidim congratulating them on selecting one of the foremost Tomchei Tmimim alumni.
