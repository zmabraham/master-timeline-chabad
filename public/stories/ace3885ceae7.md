# Rebbe Rayatz cites Warsaw activities as a distraction from expansion plans

*1928*

Writing to his uncle, the Rebbe Rayatz explained that if properly organized, Tomchei Tmimim of Warsaw could bring real light to the world. He had resolved during his Warsaw visit to take this work upon himself, but upon returning home he was overwhelmed by the burdens of running the yeshivos and chadarim in his own country. Earlier, on 2 Marcheshvan 5689, he had written to the yeshiva administration confirming in writing that the impression made by the institution was beyond description.
