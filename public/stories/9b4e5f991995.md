# Alter Rebbe of Chabad passes away on 24 Tevet

*1812*

The Alter Rebbe died on 24 Tevet 5573, during the flight from Napoleon's invasion of Russia. His grandson the future Tzemach Tzedek was 23 years old at the time. Almost all of the Tzemach Tzedek's surviving halachic writings date from after this date, making the rare manuscript in which he still refers to his grandfather as living an unusually early specimen.
