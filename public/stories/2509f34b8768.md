# Passing of Rebbe Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn (Rayatz)

*1950*

The Rayatz passed away on 10 Shevat 5710, a few days after writing to R. Michael Vilensky asking for letters that the Rebbe Rashab had sent to his father. His passing left ongoing biographical projects unfinished and ushered in the leadership of his son-in-law, the Rebbe Menachem Mendel Schneerson.
