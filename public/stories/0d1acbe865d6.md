# Rabbi Chaim Yitzchak Isaac Landau, Rabbi of Bnei Brak — born 6 Adar 5721

*1961*

Rabbi Chaim Yitzchak Isaac Landau, Rabbi of Bnei Brak. Born 6 Adar 5721. Place of activity: Bnei Brak. Rabbi Chaim Yitzchak Isaac Landau (born in 5721, 1961) is the rabbi and av beit din of Bnei Brak (together with Rabbi Shabach Tzvi Rosenblatt) and head of the kashrut certification system.
