# Polish repatriation office opens in Samarkand

*1946*

After the war, Poland's representation in Samarkand announced that Poles wishing to return to their homeland could register. By winter 5706 an office opened where anyone presenting some 'paperel' showing Polish birth could obtain (after a few months) a Polish passport allowing emigration to Poland — and from Poland onward, to the free world. Many Lubavitchers obtained such passports despite confusion over what the Frierdiker Rebbe wanted them to do.
