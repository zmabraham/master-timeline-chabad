# Alter Rebbe returns to Liozna after parting from R. Menachem Mendel

*1778*

After parting from R. Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk at Mohilov, the Alter Rebbe remained there for some time, where he reviewed the entire Talmud for the sixteenth time, standing day and night. Per the Rebbe Rayatz's notes in 'Avot HaChassidut,' he reached Liozna in mid-Shvat 5538. Other sources (Beis Rebbi) state he stayed in Mohilov a year and a half, returning home no earlier than 5539.
