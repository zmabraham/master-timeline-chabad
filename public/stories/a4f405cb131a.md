# Passing of the Maharshan of Bobruisk

*1923*

The Admor Shmaryahu Noach of Bobruisk, brother of the Maharshaz of Kopust, took his brother's place after the latter's passing in 5660 and continued to lead until his own death in 5683. He represented the Bobruisk-Kopust continuation of the Tzemach Tzedek's offspring dynasty alongside the main Lubavitcher line.
