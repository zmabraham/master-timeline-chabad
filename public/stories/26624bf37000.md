# Mohilev chief prosecutor confirms the acquittal verdict

*1827*

On 1 February 1827 (16 Shevat 5587), the chief prosecutor of Mohilev province endorsed the higher court's ruling acquitting Rabbi DovBer of the charges brought via Simcha Keysin's denunciations. The approved verdict was then sent on to Governor Khovansky in Vitebsk for his confirmation.
