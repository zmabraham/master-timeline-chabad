# Rayatz writes Zislin about discreet inquiry on the Kokorev crates

*1936*

The letter (4745) instructs Zislin to obtain information about the location of the boxes taken from the bailee R. Zalka Persitz — but without revealing who sent him on the errand. This represents one of the last surviving traces of the active 1930s effort to track the library, before contact was largely lost for fifty years.
