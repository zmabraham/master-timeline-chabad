# First letter from the Rebbe (Menachem Mendel) to Levin

*1950*

Before Rosh Hashana 5711 Levin sent a pan to be read at the Rayatz's ohel. The reply, dated 'between kesseh and asor' 5711, confirmed it was read on the ohel and in the heichal, noted that 'a tzaddik who has departed is present in this world more than during his lifetime,' and asked Levin to report on developments during Tishrei. This was the first of many letters Levin would receive from the next Rebbe.
