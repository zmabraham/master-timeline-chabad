# Rabbi Katz. Rabbi Katz delivering a general shiur at the yeshiva in Be'er Sheva

*1965*

Rabbi Katz delivering a general shiur at the yeshiva in Be'er Sheva, 5784. Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Katz (born 25 Tammuz 5725, 1965) is a veteran educator, a member of the Chabad community in Rehovot, and a Torah editor, who has served in several key roles in yeshivas
