# Rebbe departs Riga for Stockholm before crossing the Atlantic

*1940*

Shraga Feivel Zisman organized a human chain to allow the Rebbe's mother to be carried on a stretcher to the ambulance. The Rebbe asked Rabbi Mordechai Cheifetz to tell the crowd, 'The great ocean is not a barrier but a connector.' Discovering empty seats on the plane, he refused to depart until others were brought aboard, declaring, 'I cannot leave, I need to save others.'
