# Passing of R. Baruch, father of the Alter Rebbe

*1791*

Dating internal evidence in the Alter Rebbe's responsum on matza-baking practices relies on this yahrtzeit: since he still signs himself as 'son of my master and teacher R. Baruch,' the responsum predates Tishrei 5552, placing the change in matza-baking custom around 1772.
