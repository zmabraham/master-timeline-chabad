# Arrest of R. Moshe Rubinson and his students in Berditchev

*1937*

Under interrogation Rubinson disclosed that his correspondence with the Rebbe Rayatz was conducted through a longtime acquaintance named Kvash who lived in Poland. The Kvash connection between the underground Soviet talmidim and the Rebbe also surfaced in the 1935 interrogation file of R. Shammai Vigon in Rostov.
