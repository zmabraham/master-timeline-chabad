# Expansion of Chassidus study program for yeshiva students in Brooklyn

*1933*

Beginning with a Motzaei Shabbat Tanya class accompanied by stories of Lubavitch, the program grew to weekday classes on maamarim received from Europe and on Likutei Dibburim. The author also traveled twice weekly to the Division Ave Shtibel in Williamsburg to teach students. Participants included Avraham M.M. Barnetzki, Meir Greenberg, Menachem Mendel Feldman, and Yitzchak Kaladny.
