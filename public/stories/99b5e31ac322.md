# Rashab unveils plan for Yeshivas Tomchei Temimim

*1897*

Held the day of the Rayatz's wedding reception (15 Elul 5657), the Rashab announced his plan to 'open a yeshivah for our youth, with proper administration, so that they can dedicate themselves exclusively to Torah study.' Most attendees expressed serious reservations about the feasibility. The day of this meeting is considered the founding date of Yeshivas Tomchei Temimim.
