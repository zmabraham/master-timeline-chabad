# Tomchei Temimim Tomchei Tmimim founds Toras Emes yeshiva in Hebron

*1911*

After Shavuot 5671 (1911) it became known that the Rebbe Rashab was establishing a yeshiva in the Holy Land, choosing Hebron as its location. R. Shlomo Zalman Havlin traveled with five selected students—R. Yechezkel Feigin, R. Alter Simchovich, R. Hillel of Pochep, R. Yisrael Zalman of Yanovich, and R. Tzvi Katzav of Hadiach—along with three volunteers who joined them.
