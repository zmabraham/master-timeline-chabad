# Case transferred to Mogilev provincial government for trial

*1825*

Order No. 10265 forwarded the entire investigation, including Loginov and Markov's reports, to Mogilev's provincial government on 19 Kislev 5586. Khovansky concluded that the Rebbe collected 10,000-14,000 rubles annually for personal use and acted as a civil arbitrator, both in violation of Section 52 of the 1804 statute. Mogilev province routed the case to the Babinovichi peace court, which initiated further investigations in Kapust, Orsha, Shklov, and Mogilev.
