# Rayatz writes R' Gutnik in London asking aid for refugee rabbis in Riga

*1929*

On 21 Cheshvan 5690, the Rebbe Rayatz wrote to R' Mordechai Zev Gutnik, a rabbi in London, requesting help for two students of his stranded in Riga: R' Yaakov Yisrael Zuber and R' Avraham Eliyahu Ashurov. Both had hoped to emigrate to America but had been rejected by the American consul; the Rebbe asked Gutnik to secure entry permits and positions for them elsewhere.
