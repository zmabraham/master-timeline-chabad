# Rabbi Yosef Karo brings new Rambam edition to press in Venice

*1574*

In 5334 (1574), after more than twenty years in which no Rambam was printed in the wake of the Bragadin–Justinian controversy, Rabbi Yosef Karo (author of the Shulchan Aruch) brought a corrected edition of Rambam's Mishneh Torah to press in Venice. The edition included his commentary Kessef Mishneh, and several copies are preserved in the Chabad Library.
