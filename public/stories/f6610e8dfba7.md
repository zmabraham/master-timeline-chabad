# R' Yaakov Landa appointed rabbi of Kornitz

*1913*

At the end of 5673 (September 1913), R' Yaakov Landa was appointed to the rabbinate of Kornitz, filling the position of his late father R' Moshe Yehuda Leib. The appointment was formalized on 21 Elul 5673. He was forced to leave during World War I.
