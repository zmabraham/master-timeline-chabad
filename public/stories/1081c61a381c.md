# R' Yoel Smolyaner returns to Eretz Yisrael after delayed shlichut

*1791*

Sent abroad as the shadar for 5550, R' Yoel Smolyaner only returned to Eretz Yisrael on 10 Nissan 5551, well behind schedule. R' Avraham of Kalisk explicitly addressed 'the reason for the delay' in correspondence with the overseas overseers. His late arrival amid the Tiberias plague was contrasted with the more timely return of his successor R' Meir of Bychov.
