# Vaad convention votes to demand return of Schneerson library

*1991*

After Soviet officials kept insisting that Russian Jews wanted the Schneerson collection to remain in the USSR, the Chabad delegation went to the Vaad convention to refute that claim. Rabbi Kunin spoke before roughly a thousand Jewish delegates from across Russia, who responded with enthusiasm and voted overwhelmingly that the books must be returned to Chabad. A strongly worded letter from the Vaad demanding the return was drafted on the spot.
