# Chaim Liberman publishes list of 32 unknown Haggadot

*1938*

In 5698 (1938), Chaim Liberman — then director of the Rebbe's library in Warsaw — published in Kiryat Sefer (vol. 15, p. 258) a list of 32 editions of the Haggadah unknown from any other source. The Haggadot had been acquired by the Rebbe Rayatz between 5660 and 5698.
