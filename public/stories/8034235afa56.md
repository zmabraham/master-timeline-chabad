# Exhibit marking 200 years since Alter Rebbe's liberation

*1999*

On Tuesday, 19 Kislev 5759 (1998), 200 years had passed since the liberation of Rabbi Shneur Zalman of Liadi from his first imprisonment. The Chabad library mounted an exhibit running through 11 Nissan 5759 (1999), displaying first editions of the Alter Rebbe's works, original handwritten manuscripts, ancient copies, sacred artifacts, and portraits.
