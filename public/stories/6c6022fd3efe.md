# Rebbe Rayatz writes about Lubavitch ohel state

*1949*

On 4 Shevat 5709 (February 3, 1949), the Rebbe Rayatz wrote to R' Moshe Yitzchak Zuskewitz requesting detailed information about the ohelim of the Tzemach Tzedek and the Rebbe Maharash in Lubavitch. He asked whether they needed repairs, how much such repairs would cost, and through whom such work could be arranged. He requested that this be handled discreetly without mentioning his name.
