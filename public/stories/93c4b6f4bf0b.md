# Rebbe Rashab recounts history on 19 Kislev

*1914*

Speaking on the chag of redemption, Rebbe Rashab described how the Mitteler Rebbe's primary haNochos came from the post-Petersburg period rather than from Liozna. The transcript was preserved in Toras Shalom and provides a key source for the chronology of Chabad maamarim.
