# Alter Rebbe replies to summons from Mohilov rabbinate

*1787*

Between 5546–47 the heads of the Mohilov community summoned the Alter Rebbe to stand judgment before them and even appealed to the civil courts. In 5547 (1787) he responded with a lengthy letter rejecting the summons, refuting the charges, and protesting the harsh distancing decrees issued by some Shklov scholars against chassidim.
