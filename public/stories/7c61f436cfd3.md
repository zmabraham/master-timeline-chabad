# Five-man committee proposes formalizing Rayatz's Kollel Nesius

*1924*

On 12 Sivan 5684 (June 1924), the five-man committee wrote to the kollel administrators proposing that they formalize Rebbe Rayatz's Nesius through official government recognition, granting him authority to manage all kollel matters via his representative and to amend its bylaws. The administrators replied affirmatively, contingent on Rayatz's formal acceptance.
