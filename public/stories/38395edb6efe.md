# Mitteler Rebbe forwards Chernobyl agunah case to Tzemach Tzedek

*1823*

The Mitteler Rebbe, his father-in-law, directed the young Tzemach Tzedek along with his great-uncle Maharil to examine an agunah case (Even HaEzer §§347–348) referred to him by the Chernobyl beis din. In the resulting responsum the two write that 'it was our master's command... and although we know our small worth, that we have not reached the stature to take up the name and respond from afar, nevertheless one does not refuse a greater.'
