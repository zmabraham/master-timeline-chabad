# Rosh Yeshiva Eber introduces new written-exam method

*1933*

Eber gave the talmidim five questions with about two weeks to answer them; more than ten students responded with mostly accurate answers. The youngest, such as 14-year-old Eliezer Feldberg and Yaakov Falkenfeld, produced especially well-ordered and clear replies. The Rosh Yeshiva planned to pause briefly to avoid pulling students from their regular shiurim.
