# R' Avraham of Kalisk letter (5554) on Alter Rebbe's burdens

*1794*

In this letter (interpreted as 5554 because it elaborates on the Takanot Laznia of 5553), R' Avraham of Kalisk described the Alter Rebbe as the divine leader upon whom bundles of troubles from people hovered all day, who was approached by every troubled person and creditor seeking guidance in matters great and small.
