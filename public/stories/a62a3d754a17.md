# Rashag appeals for rescue funds from Chicago

*1940*

On 27 Elul 5700, the Frierdiker Rebbe's son-in-law the Rashag wrote to R. Shalom Posner, the shliach in Chicago. He reported that R. Yitzchak Dov Ushpal had already departed Japan for the US on June 26, while the Zalmanov and Rotstein families had reached Vladivostok needing funds and ship tickets for the next leg.
