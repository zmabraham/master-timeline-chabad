# Eight Tmimim approved to train as shochtim under R. Gordon

*1949*

On 23 Shevat 5709, Rebbe Rayatz recorded the names of eight Tomchei Tmimim students approved to study the craft of setting the shechita knife under shochet Rabbi Gordon at a cost of $25 per student, meeting daily at 4pm. The Rebbe arranged loans through his sons-in-law for the purchase of knives and sharpening stones, blessing the students that they should become skilled and disseminate proper chinuch in their future posts.
