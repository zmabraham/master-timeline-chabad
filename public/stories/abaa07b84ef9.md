# Rashab's final Purim farbrengen in Rostov

*1920*

At the 1920 Purim celebration in Rostov, the Rashab insisted on a full farbrengen despite the 9 p.m. curfew. When Bolshevik officers entered the room, the Rashab continued teaching a maamar on evil as a false reality. The officers stood silently and then departed, and the farbrengen continued until morning light.
