# Rebbe inquires about Feigin's contacts with Soviet relative

*1940*

On 27 Marcheshvan 5701, the Frierdiker Rebbe wrote to R. Yechezkel Feigin asking if he was in contact with 'sh'b R. Nissan' and what replies he had received. The cryptic references reflected the wartime need to mask the actual nature of Soviet rescue communications.
