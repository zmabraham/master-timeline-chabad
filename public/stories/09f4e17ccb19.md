# Secret covenant of ten in Moscow

*1924*

In the winter of 1924, with decrees against Torah students and Jewish schools intensifying, nine temimim gathered in Moscow and the Rebbe completed the quorum of ten. They swore an oath to defiantly fortify Judaism under the Soviet regime, even to the point of mesirat nefesh. The known members included Elchonon Dov Morozov, Bentzion Shemtov, Simon Yakovshvili, and Yerachmiel Binyaminson; classes were held in cold, windowless dungeons dug into the ground.
