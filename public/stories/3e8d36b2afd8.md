# SILA returns to Chevron from Bukhara

*1909*

Winter 5669 saw SILA in Lubavitch, and he reached his home in Chevron in Shevat 5669. The Rebbe Rashab welcomed him back with letters of 26 Shevat 5669 expressing relief at his safe arrival via Odessa and Jaffa. Rebbe Rayatz's 8 Adar 5669 letter reports that the family had been anxious during the journey's 20-day delay.
