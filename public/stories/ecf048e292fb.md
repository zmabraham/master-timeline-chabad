# Kogan meets Russian State Library deputies on Schneersohn collection

*1990*

On 13 Tevet 5751 Director Gubinko was unavailable, but his deputies and the adviser Yefimovich told R' Yosef Kogan the books would be returned and asked him to leave his letter with them. Yefimovich said a committee had been convened and a response was promised on Wednesday, November 2. Later Lichachev told Veronika that Gubinko had promised return but suggested waiting until after the Russian holiday.
