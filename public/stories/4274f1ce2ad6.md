# Japanese authorities establish Jewish ghetto in Shanghai's Hongkou district

*1943*

Food became scarce and the Chabad yeshivah students, already cut off from Vaad Hatzalah funds, were barely surviving on a bit of rice per day. Aryeh Leib Brailovsky, prompted by an enigmatic dream of the Rebbe pleading for his hungry children, raised funds locally to sustain them.
