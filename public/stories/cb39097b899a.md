# Liberation of Rebbe Rayatz and return of hidden manuscripts

*1927*

Duchman later wrote that he had received a large basket of manuscripts (ksvei-yad-kodesh) to hide and on Tuesday 12 Tammuz at 1:30 PM he brought them back. The future Rebbe — sitting in the western side of the great hall — told him as he handed them over: 'I will pay you for your effort with good news, that the Rebbe will arrive in time for Shabbos.' Years later in 5748, when asked whether some manuscripts may not have been returned, the Rebbe replied definitively: 'They were returned immediately!!'
