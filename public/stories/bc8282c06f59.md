# Alter Rebbe and Rabbi Levi Yitzchak study with the Maggid in Ravna

*1772*

In the summer of 5532, the Alter Rebbe and Rabbi Levi Yitzchak of Berditchev studied together at the Maggid of Mezeritch's home in Ravna. A halachic question about sirchot in the lung was brought before the Maggid, who ruled leniently and proved it from the Talmud. Nearly four decades later, when rabbis challenged Rabbi Levi Yitzchak's late ruling on the same question, the Alter Rebbe defended it by citing this very session with their teacher.
