# Rabbi Zalman Neimark becomes rabbi of Nevel

*1888*

After two years in Vitebsk, Rabbi Zalman Neimark accepted the position of rabbi in Nevel in White Russia. A responsum he wrote from Nevel on 9 Nisan 5649 references a fire 'last summer,' indicating he had already settled there by the start of summer 5648 (1888). He continued to serve as rabbi of Nevel until his death.
