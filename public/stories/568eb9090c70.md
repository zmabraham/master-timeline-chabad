# First interrogation of the Mitteler Rebbe

*1825*

On 7 Cheshvan 5586 the Rebbe answered the first round of questions from Khovansky's two appointed investigators. They focused on whether he had written and signed the 1813 financial document presented by Kissin, what portion of the funds went to relatives, and on what legal basis the money was collected. Two days later the Rebbe submitted written answers to their queries.
