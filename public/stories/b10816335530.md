# Alter Rebbe freed from Petersburg imprisonment on 19 Kislev

*1798*

Following denunciations by opponents of Chassidism, the Alter Rebbe was arrested and interrogated in Petersburg. On 19 Kislev 5559, while reading from Psalms the verse 'He redeemed my soul in peace,' he was released. He immediately wrote a letter announcing the redemption to Rabbi Levi Yitzchak of Berditchev, noting that the date doubled in meaning as the yahrzeit of their teacher, the Maggid of Mezeritch.
