# Author's tenaim signed on 16 Tammuz 5679 in Zhuravichi

*1919*

The father-in-law-to-be traveled to the author's parents in Zhuravichi for Shabbat 14 Tammuz, and the kishrei hatena'im were arranged after Shabbat. The kallah was not present, remaining in Gomel; a dowry of ten thousand rubles was committed, though the currency was already badly devalued by the upheaval.
