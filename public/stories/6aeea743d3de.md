# Arabic-script Haggadah translated by Hillel Farchi printed in Egypt

*1917*

In 5677 (1917), Hillel Farchi produced a Haggadah following Sephardic customs accompanied by an Arabic translation rendered in Arabic letters. Printed by Reuven Muskovitch in Egypt, the 42-page edition aimed to make the Seder accessible to Egyptian Jews more familiar with Arabic script than with Hebrew transliteration. The work reflected the linguistic realities of early 20th-century Egyptian Jewry.
