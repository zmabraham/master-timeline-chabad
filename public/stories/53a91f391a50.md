# R' Eliyahu Yosef Rivlin makes aliyah to Jerusalem

*1848*

The 5615 census records R' Eliyahu Yosef Rivlin as having immigrated in 5608 (1848). Rabbinic approbations to his Ohalei Yosef (Jerusalem 5626) note he 'pitched his tent of Torah in Jerusalem for some eighteen years' until his passing on 12 Tammuz 5625. He became one of the lead signatories on the founding documents of Jerusalem's Chabad community.
