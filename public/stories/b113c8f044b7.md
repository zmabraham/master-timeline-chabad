# Death of R' Nechemiah of Dubrovna, gabbai of Kollel Chabad

*1852*

R' Nechemiah of Dubrovna, author of Divrei Nechemiah, served as the general gabbai appointed by the Tzemach Tzedek to receive funds in the diaspora destined for the Chabad community in Eretz Yisrael. After his death on 15 Shevat 5612, the position passed to R' Zev of Retzitzeh and R' Avraham, rabbi of Drogichov. His death is recorded in Beis Rebbi.
