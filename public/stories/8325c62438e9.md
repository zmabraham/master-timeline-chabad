# Rebbe Rayatz writes Cramer about building purchase

*1937*

On 12 Cheshvan 5698 (October 17, 1937), the Rebbe Rayatz wrote to R. Chaim Shneur Zalman Cramer, chairman of Agudas Chassidei Chabad in the United States. He explained that for over a month he had decided that it was essential to purchase a building and courtyard for the Tomchei Tmimim yeshiva, and that thank G-d a suitable opportunity had arisen.
