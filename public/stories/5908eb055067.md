# R. Mentlik to receive visa

*1939*

On Erev Rosh Chodesh Elul 5699 (August 15, 1939), secretary R. Moshe Leib Rotshtein wrote to R. Yosef Avraham Kagan that R. Mordechai Mentlik would receive his visa in practice the next day. With the war breaking out only weeks later, this was among the final pieces of correspondence from Otwock's yeshiva administration.
