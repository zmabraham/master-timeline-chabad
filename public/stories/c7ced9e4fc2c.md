# Israel Yehuda's military exemption via glasses ruse on 11 Tishrei

*1935*

Facing conscription that endangered observant Jews, Israel Yehuda wrote to the Rebbe Rayatz and consulted senior Tmimim including R' Nissan Nemanov and R' Yaakov Maskalik. After receiving the Rebbe's directive to wear glasses, he obtained reading glasses at the Semashko clinic and on 11 Tishrei 5696 deliberately misread the eye chart, securing a complete exemption rather than the conditional one given to two other Anash bochurim that day.
