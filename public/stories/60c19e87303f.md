# Likkutei Torah with Rebbe Maharash's annotations published

*1884*

In 5644 (1884), an edition of Likkutei Torah on three parshiyos was printed in Vilna incorporating annotations of Rabbi Shmuel Schneersohn, the Rebbe Maharash. The Lubavitch Library preserves a copy of this edition. The work transmits Chassidic discourses of the Alter Rebbe with later commentary.
