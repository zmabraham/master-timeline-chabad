# Chabad delegation meets Israeli consul Levin on Schneersohn books

*1991*

Veronika had warned the delegation that Consul Levin spoke of providing only photocopies. In the 19 Adar meeting, Levin explained the photocopy proposal concerned the separate Ginzburg collection sought by the Israeli national library. He confirmed that everyone he spoke to assumed the Chabad books would be returned outright, urging the delegation to keep pressing firmly.
