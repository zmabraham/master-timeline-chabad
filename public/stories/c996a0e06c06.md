# Rebbe Rayatz visits Warsaw to review Tomchei Temimim yeshivah

*1931*

News of the visit electrified students in Warsaw, Vilnius, and Lodz. Thousands of Warsaw Jews welcomed the Rebbe at the yeshivah gates before Shabbat. He delivered maamarim, led a farbrengen marking his prison release, and held private yechidut sessions with each student over two days.
