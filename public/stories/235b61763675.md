# Alter Rebbe disputes with mitnagdim in Shklov

*1772*

After leaving Vilna the two chassidic leaders attempted a public disputation in Shklov. The local mitnagdim initially agreed to a peaceful debate but, unable to refute the chassidic arguments, broke their word and acted aggressively. The Alter Rebbe described the events in Iggeres 34 and Iggeres 51.
