# R' Baruch, father of the Alter Rebbe, joins Liozna Chevra Kadisha

*1746*

The Liozna communal register records that on 15 Kislev 5507 (December 8, 1746), 'the Torah scholar Moreinu Baruch ben Moreinu Avraham was accepted into the Chevra Kadisha.' This entry is one of the earliest documentary anchors for the Alter Rebbe's father and identifies his own father's name as Avraham, contradicting other traditions that named him Shneur Zalman.
