# Previous Rebbe declares 'Immediate Teshuvah, Immediate Redemption'

*1941*

In 5701 (1940–41), Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn, the sixth Lubavitcher Rebbe, declared the famous slogan 'L'alter L'teshuvah, L'alter L'geulah' (immediate repentance, immediate redemption) shortly after arriving in the United States. This call set the tone for ongoing Chabad efforts to hasten the redemption.
