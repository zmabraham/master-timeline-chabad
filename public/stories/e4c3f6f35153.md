# Rebbe begins hunger strike in Shpalerka prison demanding tefillin

*1927*

Following his arrival at Shpalerka, the Rebbe insisted on his right to pray with tefillin and was beaten and pushed down a ladder by a guard. His belt buckle gashed his stomach. When the prison warden told him to forget the 'foolishness,' the Rebbe began a hunger strike that lasted until Friday before sunset.
