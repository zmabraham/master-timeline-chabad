# Wedding of Feigel to Menachem Mendel Wolf

*1969*

The match was arranged by son-in-law Binyamin Klein, who proposed Feigel to the Wolfs while they were waiting for yechidus; in the yechidus the Rebbe himself raised the same suggestion. The wedding was on 15 Kislev 5730 (November 24, 1969) at the Menorah hall in Boro Park, and the couple later settled in Nachalat Har Chabad in Eretz Yisrael.
