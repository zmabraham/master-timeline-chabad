# Rebbe writes letter to anash and temimim in Russia (19 Kislev)

*1927*

Marking the Chabad festival of 19 Kislev, the Rebbe addressed his followers in the Soviet Union, recounting the Yevsektsia's persecution of his efforts to strengthen Torah. 'I was forced to leave Russia,' he wrote. 'My heart and soul are with the anash and temimim in our country.'
