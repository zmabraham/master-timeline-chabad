# Kollel administrators ask Rebbe Rayatz to formally accept Nesius

*1921*

On 10 Kislev 5682 (Dec 1921), administrators Menachem Mendel Naeh and Moshe Ornstein wrote to Rebbe Rayatz describing the post-war renewal of the kollel and the community's vote in Shvat 5681 to accept him as Nasi. They asked him to confirm and direct the next round of elections.
