# Alter Rebbe writes Seder Hachnasas Shabbos and Hilchasa Rabsa LeShabsa

*1797*

Manuscript 1064, written close to the Alter Rebbe's lifetime, lists 'Hilchasa Rabsa LeShabsa' under the year 5557. These laws did not appear in the first three editions of his Siddur (printed in Shklov and Kapust during his lifetime) but were later printed on separate folios. They form one of the latest precisely dated kuntreisim of the Alter Rebbe's middle period.
