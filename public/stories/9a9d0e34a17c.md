# Eber plans departure via Italy on May 28

*1940*

On 5 Iyar 5700, R. Mordechai Chefetz reported to the Frierdiker Rebbe that R. Yehuda Eber had received a transit permit through Italy and intended to depart on a ship leaving from there May 28. Two weeks later, however, the route was cut off when no ship was available.
