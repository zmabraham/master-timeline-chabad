# Second interrogation of Rabbi DovBer in Vitebsk

*1825*

On 15 October 1825 (15 Marcheshvan 5586), Rabbi DovBer was brought again before the state investigators in Vitebsk for a second interrogation. The session continued the inquiry into the collection of Maamad funds and the allegations made by Simcha Keysin, building the formal record that would later be reviewed by higher courts.
