# Mira Trifonenko confirms Levin's solo inspection visit to Moscow library

*1999*

Mira welcomed the proposal that Levin alone visit on Agudas Chabad funding, rather than convening the full commission. She asked him to indicate his preferred dates. Levin offered November 21–26 or December 12–17 and outlined a three-list inspection plan: marked Schneerson books, books in the 1914 Lubavitch catalogue, and unmarked unlisted books.
