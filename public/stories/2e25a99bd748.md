# Warsaw rabbis issue support letter for Tomchei Tmimim

*1937*

On 10 Kislev 5698 (November 14, 1937), members of the Council of Rabbis of Warsaw issued a letter of support praising Tomchei Tmimim as one of the most exalted Torah institutions in the world. They noted that they had personally tested many of the students, were amazed by their sharpness and erudition, and had granted them rabbinical ordination. The letter stated that seven branches of the yeshiva operated in Poland with nearly 700 students in total.
