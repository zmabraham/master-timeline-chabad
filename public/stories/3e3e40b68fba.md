# Rebbe's Haggadah with Likkutei Minhagim first edition

*1946*

In 5706 (1946), the Rebbe's Haggadah of Passover with Likkutei Minhagim u'Ta'amim was first published in New York. The work compiles Chabad customs and reasons accompanying the Pesach Haggadah. The Lubavitch Library preserves a copy of this first edition.
