# R' Aryeh Leib of Volochisk emigrates to the Holy Land

*1805*

In summer 5565 the elderly R' Aryeh Leib of Volochisk made aliyah, carrying with him funds entrusted by R' Levi Yitzchak of Berditchev for distribution to the poor in the Land of Israel. The Chozeh of Lublin's letter to R' Levi Yitzchak references this transmission. From this point a distinct Volhynian channel of support, separate from the Russian-Chabad collection, began operating openly.
