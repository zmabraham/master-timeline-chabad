# Tomchei Temimim opens; Yosef Yitzchak appointed director

*1897*

Shortly after his wedding, the Tomchei Temimim yeshiva was opened in Lubavitch under his father the Rebbe Rashab, and the future Rebbe Yosef Yitzchak was appointed to serve as its director. The yeshiva offices occupied the front of his new residence facing the courtyard. This appointment launched his lifelong leadership of Chabad's educational network.
