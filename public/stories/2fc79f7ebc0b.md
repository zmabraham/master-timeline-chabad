# Eshel Avraham second edition printed in Buchach

*1906*

A second edition of Eshel Avraham appeared in Buchach in 1906, twenty years after the Lvov first edition. Much later, a third edition drawn from the surviving manuscripts in the Chabad library was progressively printed in the Shulchan Aruch HaShalem of Machon Yerushalayim.
