# Rebbe Rayatz moves residence to Leningrad

*1924*

In 5684 (1923/24) the Rebbe Rayatz moved from his prior location to Leningrad. From there he maintained a steady correspondence with the Tomchei Tmimim administration in Warsaw on all matters of the yeshiva. The Warsaw committee — R' Shneur Zalman Shmotkin, R' Tzvi Gurarie and R' Shmuel Zalmanov's father — handled day-to-day affairs while reporting regularly to him.
