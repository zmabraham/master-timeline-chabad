# Rebbe's letter to Rabbi Yosef Wineberg on global mission

*1950*

The Rebbe's vision was articulated as: 'in every community and in every country, a student of Tomchei Temimim, a beacon of the Torah light in the ways of Chasidism.' Wineberg was assisting refugees with relocation, and this letter framed the broader strategic blueprint that the seventh Rebbe would expand into a global emissary network.
