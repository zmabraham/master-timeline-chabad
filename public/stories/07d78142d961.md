# Death of R' Yaakov Boruch Krasik

*1921*

R' Yaakov Boruch Krasik, who served as mashgiach over nigleh in Tomchei Tmimim throughout its years in Lubavitch and continued in Kremenchug after the move in 5678, passed away in Kremenchug in 5681 (1921). His brother R' Eliezer Krasik later wrote a memoir about him in Bitaon Chabad.
