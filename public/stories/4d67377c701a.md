# Rebbe notified of unconditional release from Kostroma exile (12 Tamuz)

*1927*

Sustained pressure from Madam Peshkova, telegrams to President Kalinin and Premier Rykov, and international protests forced Moscow to overturn the exile. On Tuesday July 12, the Kostroma GPU station was closed for a local holiday, but an officer told Althaus the release papers had arrived. The seventh Rebbe would later establish 12 Tamuz as an annual festival of liberation.
