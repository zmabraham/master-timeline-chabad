# Rebbe Rayatz writes 6 Tishrei letter on student visas

*1945*

In a letter dated 6 Tishrei 5706 (Igrot Kodesh vol. 9, letter 2766), sent alongside the telegram via R. Yaakov Yisrael Zuber of Stockholm, the Frierdiker Rebbe informed the students that a telegram had been sent to his contact about what they needed to do to obtain entry permits to the United States. He assured them that he was working on their behalf and prayed that G-d would fulfill their hearts' requests for good in both material and spiritual matters.
