# Rebbe freed from exile in Kostroma

*1927*

On Tuesday, July 12, 1927 (12 Tamuz), the Rebbe came to the GPU headquarters in Kostroma for his mandated weekly appearance. An officer told him papers had arrived freeing him. The international outcry combined with Madam Peshkova's lobbying had overcome the opposition of Leningrad GPU chief Stanislav Messing. The Rebbe marked the day by teaching a maamar on Psalms 118:7, 'G-d aided me'; 12 Tamuz has since been celebrated annually as a Chabad festival of liberation.
