# Rebbe Rashab replies from Marienbad acknowledging R. Avraham Chaim's will

*1908*

Receiving the copy of R. Avraham Chaim's will at his home, the Rebbe Rashab — then in Marienbad on the advice of Professor Berlin for his own health — responded on 23 Tammuz 5668. He commended the chassid for arranging his affairs as a G-d-fearing and discerning person should, and blessed him with longevity, parnassah, success, and the ability to engage in Torah and avodah in peace. The Rebbe also mentioned that he himself awaited a complete recovery among all of Israel.
