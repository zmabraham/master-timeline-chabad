# R' Avraham Chaim donates lottery winnings to the Rebbe Rashab

*1906*

On 12 Tishrei 5667 (October 1906), the Rebbe Rashab wrote to R' Avraham Chaim from Lubavitch confirming receipt of 1,000 rubles along with a record of how the money was distributed. The Rebbe blessed him with success and the means to continue doing tzedakah. A month later, on 18 Marcheshvan, the Rebbe advised him to invest the remaining funds in a house that would produce income.
