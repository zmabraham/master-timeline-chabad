# Rabbi Zev Wagner arrested at Lenin Library protest

*1992*

On the evening of 8 Adar I, Wagner's wife alerted the author, who appealed unsuccessfully to the Israeli and US embassies; the US declined because Wagner was an Israeli citizen. Rabbis Lazar, Lipsker, Aharonov and the author visited the police station and learned that charges would be filed the next day. Veronika urged a public outcry, viewing the arrest as pressure tied to the book negotiations.
