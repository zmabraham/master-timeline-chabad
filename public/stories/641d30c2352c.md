# Yeshiva students protest at Lenin Library

*1992*

Veronika requested that yeshiva students organize a demonstration at the library Monday morning. At 10:30 about 30 people gathered there, expecting a quiet protest. Almost immediately tensions with militia officers escalated into a mass scuffle in which a policeman was injured and bled. The shluchim then drove to government offices in the old square briefly before returning to the yeshiva to continue regular learning.
