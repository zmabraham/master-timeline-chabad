# Ottoman expulsion of Egyptian governor from the Land of Israel

*1840*

After the Ottoman authorities drove the Egyptian governor out of the Land of Israel in 5600, Sheikh Abdul Rahman resumed his rule over Hebron and its surroundings. He intensified his oppression of the Jews, imposing separate ransom payments on each of the two Jewish communities. The situation forced the diaspora kollel administrators to formally allocate him a share in the kollel's distribution list under the name 'the Black Rabbi.'
