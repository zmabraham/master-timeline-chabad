# Rabbeinu Yerucham's Toldot Adam ve-Chava printed in Constantinople

*1516*

In 5276 (1516), Rabbeinu Yerucham ben Meshullam's Toldot Adam ve-Chava was printed for the first time, in Constantinople. Rabbeinu Yerucham was a student of the Rosh from Provence, and his halachic compendium is among the foundational pre-Tur codes.
