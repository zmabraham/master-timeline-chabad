# R' Shmuel Dovber of Borisov writes RA"Ch on 'the fallen Sukkah of David'

*1882*

Dating from the period immediately after the Maharash's passing, a letter from R' Shmuel Dovber of Borisov to RA"Ch in Kislev 5643 hints at unsettled times among the Chassidim, alluding to the 'fallen Sukkah of David'. The letter signals that he has 'much, much to inform' RA"Ch about current events affecting Chabad.
