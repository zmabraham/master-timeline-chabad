# Rebbe dispatches Russia delegation for redemption of seforim

*1991*

In 5751 (1991), the Rebbe sent a special delegation to Russia to address the redemption of the Schneersohn family seforim, and appointed its four members as the leadership of Agudat Chabad in Russia (and later in the CIS). The delegation met with the Russian government, which then sent a recommendation to the Smolensk regional authorities to assist with Chabad's plans for the town of Lubavitch. Following the meeting, the chairman of the Council of Ministers wrote to Agudat Chabad proposing that a Chabad representative work directly with the Smolensk leadership on the project.
