# Anti-religious Chanukah parade mocks Avraham Hanoch's cheder in Lipovets

*1920*

Yevsektsiya operatives marched students and elders wearing red ties through the streets in an anti-Jewish demonstration, drawing cartoons of the father instructing his pupils and mocking the family cheder. The author's family experienced for the first time a threat aimed not only at their bodies but at their souls. The mother responded that being publicly linked with G-d and Torah was a great merit.
