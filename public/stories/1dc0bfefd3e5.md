# Detailed yeshiva report sent to the Rebbe Rayatz in Leningrad

*1926*

Immediately upon assuming his role as general supervisor, Zalmanov reported in detail on yeshiva conditions to the Rebbe Rayatz, who was then living in Leningrad. The report described three divisions plus the main hall, with approximately 170 students, listing teachers, study material, and pedagogical structure. A follow-up report was sent on Parshas Va'eira covering academic progress through Kesubos daf 71.
