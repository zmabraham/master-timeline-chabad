# Passing of R' Shalom Shachna, father of the Tzemach Tzedek

*1807*

Rabbi Shalom Shachna died in the summer of 5567 (1807), as established in the Igrot Kodesh of the Alter Rebbe. The dating became halachically relevant because the Tzemach Tzedek's son Baruch Shalom was born in 5566, before his namesake grandfather's death — a fact that complicates the Ashkenazi custom against naming a child for a living relative.
