# Siyum HaShas held at MetLife Stadium

*2019*

The celebration marked the completion of the daf-yomi cycle of Talmud study. Today Agudath Israel — founded in 1912 — is a major religious party with seats in the Israeli Knesset and a significant presence in the US and European countries. The MetLife event reflected the global scope of Talmud study in the postwar era.
