# Bar mitzvah of R' Shneur Zalman Gurarie in Nevel

*1924*

On Chanukah 5685 (December 1924) R' Shneur Zalman Gurarie reached bar mitzvah while studying at the Tomchei Tmimim yeshiva in Nevel. Shortly afterward he, his mother Esther, and his older brother R' Yechiel Tzvi left Soviet Russia and settled in Warsaw. There he enrolled at Tomchei Tmimim Warsaw and received personal study direction from the Rebbe Rayatz.
