# R. Shmuel HaLevi Levin serves as moreh tzedek of Lubavitch

*1867*

After R. Avraham haKohen's tenure, R. Shmuel b. Shlomo Tzvi HaLevi Levin briefly served as moreh tzedek of Lubavitch. A receipt he issued to a shochet on 15 Tammuz 5627 (July 1867) preserves his title. He was nicknamed 'the Rav of Lubavitch' for the rest of his life, though his actual tenure was short, ending when R. Avraham Zeligsohn was installed in the post.
