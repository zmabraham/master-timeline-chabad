# Tomchei Tmimim yeshiva opens in Vilna at Apatow Shul

*1927*

Following the Rebbe's blessing, the new yeshiva opened with a grand ceremony attended by congregants of both the Apatow and Kirzner Chabad shuls, along with the city's leading rabbis including the renowned Maggid of Vilna, Rabbi Menachem Kasovsky. Thirteen boys aged 14-15 had been gathered from surrounding shtetls; classes opened with Masechet Gittin under Rabbi Zeev Segal as first Rosh Yeshiva and R' Mordechai Leib Grossbein as mashgiach.
