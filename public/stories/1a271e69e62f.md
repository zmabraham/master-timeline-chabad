# Michael ben Shlomo of Nikolayev sends 1000 rubles to the Mitteler Rebbe

*1825*

On 18 Shevat 5585, Michael ben Shlomo of Nikolayev wrote to the Mitteler Rebbe (Admur HaEmtzaei) enclosing 1000 rubles in support, with a promise to send the additional sum by mail before Passover. The letter is one of many surviving documents showing the network of financial support flowing from chassidim across the Russian Empire to the Rebbe's court.
