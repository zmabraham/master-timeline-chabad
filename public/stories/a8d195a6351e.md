# Mrs. Devorah Chaikin, widow of Hebron Chabad rav, dies

*1913*

Mrs. Devorah—a widow with son Moshe Mordechai whom R' Shimon Menashe Chaikin married after his first wife Zissel died between 5626-5635—passed away on 13 Kislev 5674. Through her first marriage her son Moshe Mordechai was a descendant of R' Wolf of Tcharnostre buried in Tiberias, and later became son-in-law of R' Akiva Yosef Schlesinger.
