# Eshel Avraham first edition printed in Lvov

*1886*

The first edition of Eshel Avraham — chiddushim on Shulchan Aruch by R. Avraham David of Buchach — appeared in Lvov in 1886, forty-five years after the author's passing. The manuscripts had been preserved by his great-grandson R. Yisrael Aryeh Leib Wahrman, who copied them in unsystematic order.
