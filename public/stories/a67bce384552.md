# R' Avraham of Kalisk and remaining Chassidim move from Safed to Tiberias

*1783*

The pakid of Safed, hating that R' Avraham refused to eat from his shochet, expelled him forcefully, dragging many devoted Chassidim out with him. Some settled in the village of Pekiin, while R' Avraham himself moved to Tiberias where R' Menachem Mendel arranged a fine residence for him. The migration completed the relocation of the Chassidic community from Safed to Tiberias.
