# R' Eliyahu writes to Tzemach Tzedek about Romny rabbinate transition

*1835*

Dated 28 Nissan 5595, the letter recounts how six years of communal dispute in Romny led R' Menachem Nachum to accept a position in Chechersk. R' Eliyahu reluctantly agreed to temporarily serve as moreh tzedek in Romny until a permanent replacement could be found, and posed Pesach-related halachic questions to the Tzemach Tzedek.
