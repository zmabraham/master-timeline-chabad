# Pinkas of the Malbish Arumim clothing-the-poor society renewed in Lubavitch

*1859*

An earlier Malbish Arumim chevra in Lubavitch had collapsed after fire destroyed its pinkas, ending weekly donations that had clothed poor children, especially those attending cheder. On 18 Cheshvan 5620 (November 14, 1859), townsmen reorganized the society in a new pinkas, with the Tzemach Tzedek adding his haskama and his sons and grandsons — including R' Menachem (son of Maharash), R' Baruch Shalom, R' Yehuda Leib of Kopust, R' Chaim Shneur Zalman of Ladi and others — pledging annual contributions.
