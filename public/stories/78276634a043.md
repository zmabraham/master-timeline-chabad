# European shluchim kinus opens with trip to Lubavitch

*1991*

On Friday 15 Av 5751, four buses of more than 100 shluchim plus a busload of Minsk yeshiva students drove to Lubavitch, arriving around 4 p.m. R. Moshe Kotlarsky opened the program at the Ohel, the author spoke about the seforim, and the general pan was read by R. N. Sudak; Tehillim by R. S. Matusof; siyum by R. Y.Y. Matusof; dvar malchus by R. M. Garlick. They then davened Mincha in the courtyard, the author surveyed the history of the chatzer and its buildings, and they returned to Moscow at 4 a.m.
