# Yisrael Yehuda Levin mails photograph to Rebbe Rayatz from Moscow

*1935*

Hearing while in prison that the Rebbe Rayatz had asked chassidim to send him their photographs, the author resolved to comply once freed. On Friday 7 Iyar 5695 (May 10, 1935) he entered a Moscow photo studio, urging them to finish before Shabbos, and just before sunset received the prints and mailed them at the post office. His accompanying letter asked the Rebbe to arouse mercy for 'Yisrael Yehuda ben Leah Beila' that he merit to see the Rebbe face to face, and listed R. Chaim Ezra Bronstein as his return address.
