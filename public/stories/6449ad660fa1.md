# CNN segment on the Chabad library

*1991*

On 14 Tammuz 5751 (June 26, 1991), CNN aired its prepared piece on the seforim case, lasting about 3.5 minutes. The diary describes the coverage as objective and middle-of-the-road. Earlier that day the delegation was photographed in front of the library for a parallel article being prepared by Maxwell's reporter for a European newspaper.
