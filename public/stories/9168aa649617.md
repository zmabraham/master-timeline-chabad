# Pekuam letter to Hebron Chabad leaders praising unity

*1832*

On 18 Elul 5592 (September 14, 1832), the Pekuam Amsterdam committee wrote to the rabbis and administrators of the Chabad kollel in Hebron. They expressed great satisfaction with a recent letter announcing peace and unity between Chabad and the Sephardic pakid R' Chaim Yaakov Halevi ibn Yakar. The letter was published in Bezalel Landau's Kerem HaChasidut.
