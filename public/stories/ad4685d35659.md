# First Chabad charter flight from Eretz Yisrael to the Rebbe

*1962*

In Elul 5722 (August/September 1962), the first chartered flight of chassidim traveled from Eretz Yisrael to be with the Rebbe. They brought an amud (prayer-leader's stand) crafted at the trade school in Kfar Chabad. The amud was presented at a special farbrengen the Rebbe held in honor of the guests on the eve of Rosh Hashana 5723.
