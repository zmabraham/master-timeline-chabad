# Rebbe Rashab employs scribe R. Shmuel Rabinovich to copy manuscripts

*1891*

The Rebbe sent a list specifying which texts to copy fully and which to merely examine, requesting the completed copies. He also arranged that R. Yitzchak Noach the shochet would obtain certain Maamarim from R. Baruch Tumarkin for copying, sent ten rubles, and asked Rabinovich to advise on the total fee.
