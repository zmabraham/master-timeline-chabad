# Rabbi Eliezer Silver reception in Łódź; Frierdiker Rebbe's letter read aloud

*1946*

Held in the Agudath Israel secretariat at 66 Zachodnia Street, the reception included delegates from Agudah, Poalei Agudah, Mizrachi and Hapoel Hamizrachi. When R. Benyamin Gorodetzky rose with the Rebbe's letter, Rabbi Silver also stood out of respect, forcing the entire assembly to rise — a public win for the small Lubavitch contingent. At Mincha afterward Silver crossed the synagogue to greet Gorodetzky beside the Aron Kodesh, demonstratively honoring Chabad before the other delegates.
