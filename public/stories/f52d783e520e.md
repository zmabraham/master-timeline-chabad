# Rashab publishes diary entries after leaving Soviet Union

*1927*

In 1927, after leaving the Soviet Union following his arrest and death sentence, Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn began publishing the diary entries he had been compiling since his youth in Lubavitch. He had been writing down stories, chasidic lore, and customs heard from Chasidim and his father from a young age, encouraged by his teacher Reb Nissan. These publications became foundational sources for chasidic history and tradition.
