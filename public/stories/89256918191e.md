# Rayatz holds defiant Purim farbrengen in Leningrad

*1927*

Rabbi Zalman Duchman described the farbrengen as 'terrifying.' The Rayatz shouted, 'Who is the Yevsektzia? A rabbi's son? A butcher's son? I am not frightened by them!' He urged Chasidim to spread Judaism with self-sacrifice, telling one follower: 'Zalman, if they ever build a bonfire and confront you with the choice of yielding your child to their schools or casting yourself into the flames, let yourself be cast into the flames!'
