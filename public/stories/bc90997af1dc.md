# Stender presented to the Rebbe at erev Rosh Hashana farbrengen

*1962*

On the night of erev Rosh Hashana 5723 (1962), the Rebbe held a farbrengen for the Israeli Chassidim who came for the month of Tishrei. The new stender was presented, and the Rebbe spoke about its significance, recounting the Mitteler Rebbe's question to his father, the Alter Rebbe, on what he had prayed with on Rosh Hashana—answered with 'with the stender,' since 'all is G-dliness, He alone exists.'
