# Rayatz begins recovery from typhus on 19 Kislev

*1920*

After about three weeks of grave illness, the tide turned on 19 Kislev — the day marking Rabbi Schneur Zalman's 1798 release from prison and regarded as the New Year of Chabad Chasidism. Yeshivah students had been praying at the Rashab's ohel led by Reb Itche der Masmid, and a rabbinical court collected written pledges from students donating years of their lives, reportedly totaling thirty years.
