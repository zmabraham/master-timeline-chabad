# R. Sh.D.B. joins author's printing house as partner

*1953*

In Shevat 5713 (January 1953), after roughly eighteen months working without a partner, the author brought R. Sh.D.B. into the printing house. The author had invested $14,000; the new partner had promised $5,000 but produced only $1,500 in cash, which the author accepted because he wanted a working partner more than capital. The funds were transferred to R. Tzvi Gansburg for his share.
