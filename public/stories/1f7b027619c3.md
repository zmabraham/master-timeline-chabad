# Barnetzky and Levy arrive at Tomchei Tmimim in Otwock

*1938*

The two students sailed on the Queen Mary shortly after Sukkos 5699 and arrived in Otwock without prior notice. Rabbi Feigin wrote to Jacobson that the administration had been unprepared and noted that American students could not be sustained on the same austere terms as the local talmidim, requiring special attention both materially and spiritually.
