# Court issues investigation orders to Kapust, Orsha, Shklov and Mohilev

*1826*

On 12 February 1826 (17 Adar I 5586), the Babinovich peace court, dissatisfied with the evidence it could gather locally, sent instructions to courts in Kapust, Orsha, Shklov and the lower court of Mohilev. Each was ordered to interrogate the chassidim in its town about the collection and distribution of Maamad funds, expanding the geographic scope of the inquiry considerably.
