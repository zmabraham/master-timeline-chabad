# Stroganov confirms Tzemach Tzedek's great influence among Jews

*1841*

Stroganov reported he had no specific file on 'the Lyubavichi rabbi' but acknowledged that 'the Jewish rabbi Mendel of Lyubavichi who dwells in White Russia and belongs to the Chassidim sect, is truly respected by the Jews and has great influence on them, especially on the inhabitants of White Russia and Lithuania.' The confirmation gave official weight to the Maskilim's claims and intensified the case for direct surveillance.
