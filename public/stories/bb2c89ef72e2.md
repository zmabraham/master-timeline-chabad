# Rashag confirms students arrived in San Francisco and depart for Canada

*1941*

On 29 Tishrei 5702 (October 20, 1941), Rashag informed Rabbi Rotstein in Chicago that the students had arrived safely in San Francisco and would depart that evening for Canada via Chicago. He urged Rotstein to arrange to meet them on the train, since they would not be permitted to disembark in the city.
