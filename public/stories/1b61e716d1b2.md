# Library director appeals to Soviet Culture Minister to block search

*1988*

Diakonova's written objection prompted Nina Vlasova to suggest Hammer escalate directly to Culture Minister Vasily Zakharov. While waiting, the delegation drafted a formal memorandum with Mindel and Hammer's chief aide Jacobs, visited the Marina Roscha synagogue (where they immersed in the mikveh for the Alter Rebbe's yahrzeit), and reported the impasse back to the Rebbe's office in New York.
