# Frierdiker Rebbe departs Soviet Russia after Sukkos

*1927*

After the events of Tishrei 5688 in Leningrad, the Frierdiker Rebbe and his household departed Russia just after Sukkos. The exit had been arranged through R' Mordechai Dubin's leverage in Latvian parliament, with permission also granted to bring the Rebbe's manuscripts and library out of the country. American Chassidim received news of the journey only later, in stages.
