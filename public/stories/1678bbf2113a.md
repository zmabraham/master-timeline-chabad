# Rebbe writes to Zelda about her mother's tombstone inscription

*1965*

Following the passing of Rebbetzin Rachel Schneersohn in 5725, the Rebbe wrote on 9 Shevat 5725 to her daughter, the poet Zelda Mishkovsky, regarding the wording of the matzeva. The letter was later published in Igrot Kodesh vol. 23, letter 8934.
