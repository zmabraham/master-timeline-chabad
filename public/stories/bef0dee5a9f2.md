# Rebbe Rashab writes letter on JCA schools to R' Moshe Avigdor Chaikin

*1900*

The Rebbe described the spiritual damage of the Mefitzei Haskalah schools—including the newly opened school in Lubavitch funded with 5,000 rubles from JCA—and detailed his plan to address the JCA committee in Paris. He asked R' Chaikin to translate his letter into French with the care of one whose soul was engaged in the cause, and revealed that he had personally traveled to Petersburg two weeks earlier to strategize against the schools.
