# R. Avraham Chaim Rosenbaum settles in the United States

*1898*

Five to six years after leaving Russia, R. Avraham Chaim Rosenbaum arrived in the United States in 5658 (1898), where he established his residence. He was among the earlier Chabad chassidim to take up permanent residence in America, well before the major waves of Chabad emigration that followed the Russian Revolution. He would live out the last fifteen years of his life there.
