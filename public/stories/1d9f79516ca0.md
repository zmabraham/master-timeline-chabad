# Chabad delegation stages Shabbat sit-in at Lenin Library

*1991*

On the Shabbat before Rosh Hashana 5752 the delegation chose to remain inside the library when no answer came. The director offered them the conference room beside his office; police escorted students over 18 upstairs while younger boys walked to the Agudas Chassidei Chabad synagogue. They davened Kabbalas Shabbos, ate the Shabbos meal together, and slept on chairs and carpets as messages continued from Gubenko late into the night.
