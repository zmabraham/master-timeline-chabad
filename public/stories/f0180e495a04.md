# Mohammed Pasha conquers Hebron from Abdul Rahman

*1846*

After a long internal war between Abdul Rahman and his brothers, Mohammed Pasha, the Ottoman governor of Jerusalem, marched on Hebron in 5606 and captured it from the rebellious sheikh. During the conquest the city was given over to murder and plunder until the governor signaled a halt; the looters did not distinguish between Jews and gentiles. The Sephardi rabbis described how 'sons of Belial' stripped the entire city, while later accounts emphasize that during this second plunder lives were spared and the looting lasted only half a day.
