# Final labeling of 4,354 books as 'Lubavitch Collection' in Moscow

*2000*

On 17 Tammuz 5760, the labeling project was completed: 4,354 books in the public library bore 'Lubavitch Collection' stickers — 770 in the Khimki branch and the rest in the main building. The emissary estimated roughly 2,500 additional Lubavitch volumes remained mixed in the Polyakov collection without identifying marks, alongside 110 rare books in the museum and 280 manuscripts outside Trifonenko's jurisdiction.
