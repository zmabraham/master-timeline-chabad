# Chief prosecutor delegates oversight to Babinovich court inspector

*1826*

On 9 January 1826 (13 Shevat 5586), the chief prosecutor of Mohilev province handed over day-to-day supervision of the case against Rabbi DovBer to the inspector of the Babinovich district court, since the trial venue had shifted. This step set the stage for the actual investigative work in Babinovich to begin.
