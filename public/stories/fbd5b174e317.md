# Yeshivat Tomchei Tmimim founded in Warsaw

*1921*

Building on the established Chabad community of Warsaw, Yeshivat Tomchei Tmimim was founded there in 5681 (1921). At its head stood the three central Warsaw Chabad leaders: R. Shraga Feivish Zalmanov, R. Dovber Moshe Shmotkin, and R. Shneur Zalman Shmotkin. The yeshiva became a major hub of Chabad education in interwar Poland.
