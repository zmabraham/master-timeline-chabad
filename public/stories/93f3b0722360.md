# R. Baruch Mordechai Ettinga makes aliyah to Jerusalem

*1851*

Already in 5574-5 R. Baruch Mordechai had asked the Mitteler Rebbe about settling in Eretz Yisrael. Although he was instructed to remain in Bobruisk and continued there for another 35 years, in 5611 he finally made the move. By then the Chabad community in Jerusalem had grown, and he chose to settle there rather than in Hebron or Tzefas.
