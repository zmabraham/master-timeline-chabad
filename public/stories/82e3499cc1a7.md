# R' Alter Simchowitz arrives in Riga for the Days of Awe

*1928*

Following the Rebbe Rayatz's late-winter 5688 visit to Warsaw Tomchei Temimim, he resolved to overhaul the yeshiva's staffing. The chance arose when R' Alter Simchowitz of Jerusalem, who had served as mashpia in Warsaw from 5682-5685, traveled to Riga for the Days of Awe. On 18 Elul 5688, the Rebbe's secretary R' Yechezkel Feigin informed R' Israel Jacobson of the 'honored guest's' arrival, setting the stage for discussions to bring R' Alter back as chief mashpia.
