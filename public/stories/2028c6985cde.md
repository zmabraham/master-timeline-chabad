# Interrogation of R. Aharon of Strashelye in Vitebsk

*1825*

Aharon Movshovich HaLevi of Drizin, age 58, testified that he did not recognize a list of donations collected for the support of a rebbe. He stated he supported himself and his family on contributions of up to 3,000 rubles a year from Jews. In a follow-up session on October 28 he confirmed knowing some donors named in the ma'amad lists, and authenticated a letter sent to Simcha Kissin jointly with the Mitteler Rebbe. He was released around Rosh Chodesh Kislev, after less than two weeks in custody.
