# Rebbe begins sending yeshivah students as US emissaries

*1943*

The idea had originated in a 1938 letter from Otwock to Rabbi Dovid Rabinowitz, proposing that outstanding yeshivah students spend extended time in US communities on purely spiritual missions. The project did not materialize before the war but was realized in 1943 in America. The program continues today as a global Merkos Shlichus initiative.
