# Family Purity campaign launched on 15 Tammuz

*1975*

In a special sicha on 15 Tammuz 5735 (1975), the Rebbe directed the launching of a broad education and outreach campaign to strengthen family purity observance (Likkutei Sichos vol. 13 pp. 259-260). This established family purity as one of the ongoing mivtzaim of Chabad.
