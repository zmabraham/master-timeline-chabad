# Rabbi Eliyahu Mordechai (Eli) Cohen (born 19 Tammuz 5715,

*1955*

Rabbi Eliyahu Mordechai (Eli) Cohen (born 19 Tammuz 5715, July 9, 1955) is the CEO of the Crown Heights Community Council. Biography: Born to R. Gavriel Emanuel and Lyetta in Manchester, England. He became close to Chabad in his youth.
