# Rebbe writes Haffkine about strategizing against Yevsektzia

*1927*

The Rebbe explained that being just outside Russia's border allowed him to continue his efforts more freely for Russian Jewry. He told Haffkine he had 'not given up on Russia' and now sought to enlist his help in arranging a private meeting of Jewish leaders to find a way to 'extinguish the power of the Yevsektzia.' He emphasized the effort must be conducted with the utmost secrecy.
