# Fire destroys the houses of the Lubavitch courtyard

*1921*

At the beginning of Nissan 5681 (1921), one year after the passing of the Rebbe Rashab, a fire from heaven destroyed all the houses in the courtyard of the Rebbes in Lubavitch within half an hour. Whatever was stored in the cellars was looted and broken by non-Jews. The physical heart of Chabad's home town was effectively erased.
