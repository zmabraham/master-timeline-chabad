# R' Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk endorses Alter Rebbe and R' Yissachar Ber

*1784*

From Tiveria in the winter of 5544 (1784), R' Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk responded to complaints about livelihood and leadership reaching him from Russia. He instructed the chassidim to be content with the guidance of R' Yissachar Ber of Lubavitch and R' Schneur Zalman of Liozna rather than seeking new leadership from abroad.
