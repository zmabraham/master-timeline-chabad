# Alter Rebbe arrives in Piena after months of wartime wandering

*1812*

On Erev Shabbat parashat Vayishlach 5573 (December 1812), Rabbi Schneur Zalman reached the town of Piena, exhausted and elderly. The Tzemach Tzedek records that the harshness of the winter, the cold, and the constant displacement were far beyond what the aged Rebbe could bear physically. Yet he had refused under any circumstance to remain even one day under French rule, declaring he would not live under Napoleon's government.
