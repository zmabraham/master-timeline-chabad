# Rabbi Yossi Botman (right) together with Israel's ambassador to the UN

*1971*

Rabbi Yossi Botman (right) together with Israel's ambassador to the UN blessing on the Four Species on Sukkot. Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak (Yossi) Botman (born 5731, 1971) is one of the Rebbe's shluchim in Westchester County, New York, and director of Tzeirei Agudat Chabad of the United States
