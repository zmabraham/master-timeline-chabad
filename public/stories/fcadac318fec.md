# Gorbachev's office transfers Chabad library file back to Gubenko

*1991*

On 4 Adar 5751, Rabbis Cunin and Koegan made the rounds in Moscow. Gorbachev's office said it gives nothing in writing and clarified that, contrary to what was said before Shabbos, the office had merely transferred the file to Gubenko rather than issued an instruction to return the books; Tolstikov said he was no longer involved and only Yefimovich at the Ministry of Culture was now handling the matter.
