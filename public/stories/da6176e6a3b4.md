# Shaar HaMilu'im IV of Tzemach Tzedek printed

*1978*

By 5738 a further wave of newly surfaced material had accumulated, warranting another supplementary volume. From 5738 onward additional finds were published primarily in the Yagdil Torah journal printed in New York between 5737 and 5746, which became a major venue for ongoing Tzemach Tzedek scholarship.
