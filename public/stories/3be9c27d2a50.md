# Rebbe Rayatz writes son-in-law of survival of manuscripts and library

*1939*

On 8 Teves 5700 (December 20, 1939), shortly after reaching Riga, the Rebbe Rayatz wrote to his son-in-law (the future Rebbe), then in France, reporting that thank God the manuscripts had been preserved and so had the library. Uncertain whether they could yet be extracted from Otwock, he asked for a detailed list of which kisvei kodesh his son-in-law had photographed in prior years, including the small notebooks, letters, and the maamar 'Toldot HaChozrim' he feared had been burned.
