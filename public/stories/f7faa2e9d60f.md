# Rabbi Aryeh Leib Zivav appointed rabbi of Dvinsk-Dinaburg

*1865*

After serving as rav in Babruysk, Smilovitch and Homel, Rabbi Aryeh Leib Zivav was appointed rav of Dvinsk-Dinaburg around 5625, following Rabbi Yehuda Leib Batlan. He was a leading talmid of the Tzemach Tzedek and Rabbi Yitzchak Aizik of Homel and a recognized posek in his region, especially in the laws of writing gittin.
