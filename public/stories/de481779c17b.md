# Chabad delegation briefs Dennis Ross before Baker-Gorbachev summit in Moscow

*1991*

On 29 Adar 5751, at the Hammer Center in Moscow, the delegation met for thirty minutes with Dennis Ross, deputy to US Secretary of State James Baker, joined by Ambassador Collins and aide Downey. Rabbi Cunin recounted the long campaign to recover the Schneersohn library and presented nearly forty letters of support from US senators gathered in Washington. Ross conveyed that Baker had already raised the matter with Soviet Foreign Minister Bessmertnykh, whose hands were tied by Gubenko's refusal — setting the stage for Baker to press Gorbachev directly later that day.
