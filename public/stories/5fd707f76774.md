# Passing of the Tzemach Tzedek (13 Nissan)

*1866*

He was the first Chabad Rebbe to live in Lubavitch from start of leadership to his last day. His will forbade any structure of brick or wood over his grave, but his family built a roofless wall around it out of fear that gentiles would dig in search of buried gold. A study hall was later added on its southern side. His passing precipitated the succession crisis that would split Chabad into several branches.
