# Pell confirms to Rhoade that the Rebbe has been found alive

*1939*

A cable from the US Embassy in Berlin confirmed: 'He has been ill and is recovering' and that a German officer 'has been assigned to look after him.' Rhoade relayed the news to Sam Kramer and the American Chasidim, who rejoiced — though the road to safety remained long, dangerous, and dependent on still-elusive US visas.
