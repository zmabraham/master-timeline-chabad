# Rebbetzin Sheina Horenstein murdered at Treblinka

*1942*

A Kapo informed the survivor Mordechai Unrad on 3 Tishrei 5703 that Sheina had been led to the crematoria on the second day of Rosh Hashanah. The Frierdiker Rebbe, not yet knowing the details, continued to refer to his youngest daughter and her husband as still being in Otwock in correspondence into Adar II 5703.
