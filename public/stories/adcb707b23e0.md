# Rebbe Rayatz directs R' S. Z. Gurarie's course of study

*1926*

On 27 Nisan 5686 (April 1926) the Rebbe Rayatz, writing from Petersburg, instructed the teenage R' Shneur Zalman Gurarie that his principal study was to be Talmud with deep iyun. He asked the boy to inform him in detail which tractates he knew, including Tosafot, so that the studies could be properly arranged. The letter is preserved in Igrot Kodesh vol. 11, letter 3795.
