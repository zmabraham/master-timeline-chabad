# Prison administration changes and conditions harshen at Smolensk

*1890*

Toward the end of the winter of 5650, new guards took over the Smolensk prison and tightened the regime. Prisoners were forbidden to smoke, were limited to coarse prison-issue clothing and straw-stuffed pillows, lost access to private samovars, and were restricted to two meals' worth of food at a time. R' Avraham Chaim recorded these privations in his letters.
