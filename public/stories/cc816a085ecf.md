# Jacobson family arrives in America at Ellis Island

*1925*

After the stormy Atlantic crossing on the SS Paris, the ship docked on 5 Tevet. All the passengers, Jacobson and his family among them, were transferred to Ellis Island for immigration processing—closing out a journey from Homel that had taken roughly six months by way of Moscow, Riga, Kovno, Königsberg, Berlin, Paris, and Le Havre.
