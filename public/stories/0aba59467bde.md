# R. Eliyahu Moshe Lis writes Rebbe about wife's plan to flee Poland

*1940*

Lis, who served as mashgiach in the Vilna yeshiva, had left his wife Golda and daughters Rachel Miriam and Bluma behind in Biala. He worried that registering to cross into Soviet-held Lithuania could mean deportation to Siberia, as had happened to his older brother Chaim Meir. His family ultimately remained in Poland and perished in the Holocaust while he escaped via Shanghai to America.
