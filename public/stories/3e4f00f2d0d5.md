# Rebbe approves distribution of Chabad library booklet

*1991*

On motzaei Shabbos 24 Adar the booklet was brought before the Rebbe, who replied 'May it be in a good and auspicious time, and the moment and the matter — as the askanim advise.' The delegation decided to begin distributing it privately to individuals; if Secretary of State Baker's visit later that week failed to produce results, they would launch a full press campaign. R. Sholom Ber Kunin also wrote to the Rebbe about sending his sons to Washington to lobby senators.
