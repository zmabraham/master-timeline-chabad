# Letter from prison to Rabbi Yehuda Leib of Pleshchenitsy

*1889*

On the Sunday of Parshat Noach 5650, R' Avraham Chaim sent a parallel letter to Rabbi Yehuda Leib Maskil L'Eitan, rabbi of Pleshchenitsy. He repeated his wordplay on the year tarn as encoding mar-dror — bitterness turning to liberation — and described the 240 days remaining of his sentence as days that would be sweetened into glory upon his release.
