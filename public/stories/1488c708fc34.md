# Kin, and moved to live in Kfar Chabad after his marriage — served as melamed

*1987*

Kin, and moved to live in Kfar Chabad. After his marriage he served as melamed at the Oholei Torah Talmud Torah of Kfar Chabad. He previously served as a member of the 'Va'ad for the Integrity of the Nation,' and between the years 5744–5747 (1984–1987) took part in editing the bulletin "Shleimus" of the aforementioned va'ad.
