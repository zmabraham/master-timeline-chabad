# Threat to Stas and Ilya over Chabad library assistance

*1991*

On the night of Rosh Chodesh Adar 5751, Stas told the Chabad delegation in Moscow that Ilya had received a call around 1:00 a.m. warning that he and Stas would pay with their lives if they kept helping with the seforim. Stas linked the threat to a similar warning conveyed earlier by an Academy of Sciences contact to Rabbi Koegan, suggesting both calls came from the same source.
