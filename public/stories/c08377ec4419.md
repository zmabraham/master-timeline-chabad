# Simchat Torah celebration in Crown Heights

*1929*

Reform Rabbi Stephen S. Wise visited the Rebbe privately during the day, later telling his Manhattan students, 'It is worthwhile for you to visit the chakham, the wise leader of the Jewish people, in Brooklyn.' The celebration continued past sunset with editors, reform rabbis, yeshivah alumni, and Chasidim joining a long farbrengen and series of talks outlining a vision for American Jewry.
