# First volume of Shu"t Tzemach Tzedek printed in Vilna

*1871*

After the Tzemach Tzedek's passing, his manuscripts were entrusted to two Chabad rabbis, R' Chaim Yaakov Widrevitz and R' Shimon ben Dovid, to prepare the responsa and novellae for publication. They worked on the material for several years, and the first volume was finally printed in Vilna in 5631 (1871).
