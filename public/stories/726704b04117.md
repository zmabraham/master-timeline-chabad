# Founding of the small Tomchei Tmimim yeshiva in Suraz

*1927*

Schusterman's father brought seven twelve-year-old students from Zlobin, including the author, and arranged their lodging and meals. Three additional local students brought the total to ten. Rabbi Yeshaya Alewski, the shochet, served as magid shiur teaching Gemara Gittin. Axelrod insisted the boys read Torah publicly despite being below bar mitzvah age.
