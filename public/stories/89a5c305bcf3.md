# Eliyahu Shusterman born in Lohin, Volhynia

*1881*

Eliyahu Shusterman, the author's father, was born in the year 5641 (1880/1881) in the small Volhynian town of Lohin, near Korosten in the Berdichev region. He later studied in Vyetka near Homel, a town known as a seat of sharp Talmudic scholars. His Yiddish accent remained 'Litvish' from his upbringing in Belorussia, unlike his brother David who spoke in a 'Polish' Volhynian dialect.
