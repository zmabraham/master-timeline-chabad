# Rashab moves into rented Rostov apartment on Pushkinsky Street

*1915*

After two weeks in a hotel and a difficult search for housing, the Rashab moved his family into an apartment on Pushkinsky Street on erev Shabbos, 5 Kislev 5676. He described the rooms as good and high-ceilinged on a wide street, but complained in a Chanukah letter that the landlord was unpleasant and spoke improperly. The 19 Kislev farbrengen that year was held in this new apartment.
