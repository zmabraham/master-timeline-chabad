# Rebbe Rayatz on building purchase obstacle

*1938*

On 19 Tammuz 5698 (July 18, 1938), the Rebbe Rayatz wrote to R. Israel Jacobson that the obstacle to advancing the yeshiva building purchase was the lack of approved bylaws for Agudas Chabad from the US government and the Polish consul in New York. Only once these were approved could the building be purchased in Aguch's name.
