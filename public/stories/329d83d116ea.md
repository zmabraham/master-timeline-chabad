# Consul Finn halts Salama's persecution of Hebron Jews

*1854*

James Finn recorded in his diary: 'In Hebron I succeeded in ending the harassment which Salama, brother of Abd al-Rahman Amr, was inflicting on the Jews.' His interventions in Hebron during 1853-1856 were later documented in his book 'Stirring Times, or Records from Jerusalem Consular Chronicles' (London 1878), an important record of the persecutions endured by Hebron's Jewish community.
