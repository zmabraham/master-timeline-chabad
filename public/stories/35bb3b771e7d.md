# Tzemach Tzedek accepts leadership of Chabad

*1827*

He was nephew and son-in-law of his predecessor, and had lived in Lubavitch alongside him since 1813. For nearly four decades he led Chabad from Lubavitch, traveling each year to a different region of chassidic communities, and writing the more than one thousand responsa of the Tzemach Tzedek series.
