# Tzemach Tzedek moves into rebuilt home in Lyubavichi

*1859*

After spending time in the courtyard of the town's landowner following the fire, the Tzemach Tzedek and his sons returned to live in their newly rebuilt buildings in Lyubavichi at the beginning of 5620. Shortly afterwards, in Kislev of that same year, he became seriously ill. The episode is recorded in Beis Rebbi, part 3, chapter 6.
