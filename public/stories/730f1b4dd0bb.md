# R' Levin's final letter from Hebron

*1891*

On 17 Tammuz 5651 (June 23, 1891), R' Yehuda Leib Shaul Levin wrote his last preserved letter from Hebron, still signing 'rav and moreh in Hebron.' The following year he relocated to Jerusalem. His letters from Hebron are preserved in Sinai journal volume 87.
