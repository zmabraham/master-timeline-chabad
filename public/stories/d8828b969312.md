# R' Yaakov Himelfarb's father dies in Libau

*1891*

On 7 Iyar 5651 (May 1891) R' Yaakov Himelfarb's father died in Libau, leaving his eleven-year-old son to grow up in the community. The Himelfarb family — father, grandfather, and great-grandfather — were all Chabad chassidim who prayed in Nusach Ha'Ari.
