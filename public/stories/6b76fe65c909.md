# Senate acquits Mitteler Rebbe of all charges

*1827*

On 21 Kislev 5588, just twelve days after the Mitteler Rebbe's passing, the Supreme Senate in Petersburg upheld the Mohilev high court's acquittal and rejected Governor Khovansky's appeal. The ruling accepted that ma'amad donations were voluntary and that the Rebbe was not an official salaried rabbi, placing the practice outside the 1804 Jewish Statute's prohibitions. This decision legalized ma'amad as the financial foundation supporting Chabad rebbes for generations to come.
