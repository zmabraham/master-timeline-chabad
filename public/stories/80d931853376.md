# Reshadam writes Avraham Chaim Rosenbaum of Pleshchenitz about a halachic question

*1872*

Writing on motzaei Shabbat, 6 Tevet 5633 (December 1872), the Reshadam tells Rosenbaum that the Pesikta passage just received from the post and an oral ruling reported in Rosenbaum's name appear to contradict one another. He treats the printed source as 'halacha v'ein morin kein' because of the risk involved, and asks for a clear practical clarification before an awaited visitor arrives for the last days of Chanukah.
