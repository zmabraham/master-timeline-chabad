# R' Shimon Shmerling departs as first Chabad shadar from Hebron

*1821*

In summer 5581 R' Shimon Shmerling, originally of Shklov, departed Hebron as the first Chabad shadar sent from the Hebron community to the diaspora. He returned to Hebron after Pesach 5583. On his journey he registered communities such as Dubna for annual donations.
