# Chofetz Chaim's yeshiva relocates to Shumyachi during the revolution

*1917*

The upheavals of the Russian Revolution displaced great Torah institutions along with their host communities. R' Zusman Fradkin, the Shumyachi shochet and acting rav, drew close to the Chofetz Chaim during this period and received from him, even though R' Zusman himself had learned in Slobodka and was a Chabad chassid by inclination. R' Zusman was murdered with his sons by the Nazis on 29 Adar 5702.
