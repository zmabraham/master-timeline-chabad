# Tzemach Tzedek's testament to the Rebbe Maharash

*1866*

In the final year of his life, the Tzemach Tzedek addressed a testament to his son R. Shmuel, the future Rebbe Maharash, directing him to listen to chassidim, advise them, and review chassidic discourses before them. It appears as Igeret 78 in the Tzemach Tzedek's Igrot Kodesh and offers a sample of his handwriting in its final form.
