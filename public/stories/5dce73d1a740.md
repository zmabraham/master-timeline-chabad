# Cholera outbreak in the Vitebsk province

*1909*

In the summer of 5669 the dreaded cholera 'dever' spread through the Vitebsk province and surrounding regions. The Russian authorities ordered that only boiled water be drunk throughout the affected provinces. Large cauldrons of boiling water were kept in the yeshiva at all times, and even the drinking barrel in the zal was filled exclusively from boiled water.
