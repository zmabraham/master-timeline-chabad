# Professor Chavkin inspects Tomchei Tmimim Warsaw

*1927*

Professor Chavkin visited the yeshiva and recorded that 8 teachers aged 35-50 ran four classes plus a self-study fifth class for older students. He counted approximately 250 students aged 10 to 20, of whom 80 lived on-site and received meals; the rest stayed with families. He documented the committee leadership including Zalmanov, Shmotkin, Gurarie, Rothstein, Blum, and Rabbi Yosef Kalisch.
