# Rayatz's second arrest over secular school accusations

*1902*

In 1902, Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn faced his second arrest, brought about by accusations leveled by teachers at the secular school in Lubavitch. This arrest reflected ongoing tensions between traditional Chabad education and the secular educational system pushed by reformers. It was one of seven arrests he would experience over his lifetime under czarist and later Communist rule.
