# Rebbe Rayatz reaffirms R' Alter's mission at Toras Emes

*1929*

The Rebbe Rayatz prayed for R' Alter's strength to fulfill 'his task and his calling' as designated and blessed by his father, the Rebbe Rashab. He invoked the special kochos given to R' Alter for leading and sustaining Tomchei Temimim — but the plan to bring his family to Warsaw was now permanently set aside, and his role at Toras Emes was treated as fixed.
