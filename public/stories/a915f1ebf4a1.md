# Opening ceremony of Chabad school at Moscow's Archipov Great Synagogue

*1991*

After morning selichos the family attended the ceremony, where speakers included Korbsky, the author, R. Kogan, R. Lazar, Cherbinsky and Varovich. Yeshiva learning was meant to start the same morning at the AGUH shul, but the bus did not arrive and the students learned at Marina Roshcha instead.
