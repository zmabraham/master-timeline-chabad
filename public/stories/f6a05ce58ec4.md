# Rebbe Rayatz departs Leningrad with family

*1927*

After months of effort by Mordechai Dubin to secure exit papers, the Rebbe Rayatz departed Leningrad with his family on Thursday morning. Senior Chabad rabbis accompanied him by train to the border station at Pskov. From Pskov they wrote a farewell letter to Latvian Jewry on behalf of the Rebbe.
