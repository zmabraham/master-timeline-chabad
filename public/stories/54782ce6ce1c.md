# R' Alter Simchowitz comes to Lubavitch to study

*1904*

Born in Pochep around 5650, Simchowitz had first studied at the yeshiva of R' Tzvi Netta Gnessin in Pochep, whose graduates often progressed to Tomchei Tmimim. After arriving in Lubavitch he spent time in the preparatory program in Horodisht before joining the main yeshiva hall.
