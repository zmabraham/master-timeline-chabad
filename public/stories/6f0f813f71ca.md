# Rebbe Rashab selects R. Meir Leib Horowitz as kollel emissary

*1906*

The Rebbe Rashab held that emissary travel remained essential to invigorate R'Meir Baal HaNess collections, despite the move to postal collection. He explained in several Shvat 5666 letters that personal travel was needed to gather accumulated sums and to strengthen future giving by appointing local collectors. Horowitz was selected as 'shaliach mitzvah' for this great mitzvah.
