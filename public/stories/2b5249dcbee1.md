# Rebbetzin Chana Horenstein murdered at Treblinka

*1942*

According to the testimony of survivor Mordechai Unrad, who shared a barrack with Rabbi Horenstein at Treblinka, a Kapo brought a note from Sheina informing her husband that his mother had been led to the crematoria on 14 Elul 5702. The Horensteins had been trapped in Otwock since 1939 despite the Frierdiker Rebbe's prolonged efforts to extract them.
