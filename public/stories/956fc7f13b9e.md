# Alter Rebbe begins editing Hilchos Tzitzis and Pesach at the Maggid's court

*1771*

Selected by the Maggid of Mezritch from among his disciples to compose a new Shulchan Aruch with clear halachic rulings and their reasoning, the young Alter Rebbe began the project with Hilchos Tzitzis and Hilchos Pesach. According to the introduction by his sons, both sections were begun and finished while he sat at the Maggid's court. This marked the start of what would become a multi-decade halachic project.
