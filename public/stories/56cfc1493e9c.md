# Shloshim for traveling shliach R. Avraham Yitzchok Glick in Stamford Hill

*1992*

Stopping in London en route from Moscow to New York, the author found a packed shul at Stamford Hill assembled for the shloshim of R. Avraham Yitzchok Glick, a longtime traveling shliach. The elder Rabbi Padwa, Rabbi Ashkenazi, and other senior figures spoke in his memory. Spotting the author as he entered, the organizers seated him at the head table and asked him to say a few words. The evening concluded with maariv.
