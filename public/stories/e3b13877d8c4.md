# Rabbi DovBer submits request to Khovansky to be released home

*1825*

On 4 November 1825 (6 Kislev 5586), with his interrogations concluded, Rabbi DovBer of Lubavitch submitted a written request to Governor Khovansky asking to be released from Vitebsk and allowed to return home. The petition came after weeks of questioning, and the governor granted the release a few days later.
