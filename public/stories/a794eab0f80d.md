# Fez Beit Din confirms transfer of mortgage to R' Avraham Toledano

*1723*

Acting on behalf of the heirs of R' Yaakov Halevi, R' Yehuda ibn Attar and fellow dayanim conveyed the orphans' half of the rights in the mortgage to R' Avraham Toledano. The ruling cleared and emptied the orphans' lien on the pawn document.
