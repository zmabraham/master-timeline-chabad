# MaHaRiL switches choice of borer from R' Hillel to R' Aharon

*1855*

Months of correspondence with R' Hillel of Paritch failed to produce his acceptance as borer. On 4 Mar Cheshvan תרט"ז the MaHaRiL wrote definitively to R' Aharon of Belinitz that the decision had been made to switch back to him, promising to cover travel costs and a fitting honorarium, and stressing 'I have no one else fit for this matter.' The location of the din Torah was still undetermined, perhaps near Berditchev.
