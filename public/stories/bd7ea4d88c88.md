# Didan Notzach library trial begins in U.S. federal court

*1985*

In 5745 (1985) a grandson of the Rebbe Rayatz initiated proceedings claiming that a portion of the central Chabad library belonged to him personally. The case became known among Chasidim as the 'sefarim trial.' It was litigated through 5747 and centered on whether the Rebbeim had owned the books personally or as trustees of the movement.
