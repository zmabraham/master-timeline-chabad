# First printing of the Zohar in Mantua

*1558*

The Zohar of Rashbi was first printed in 5318-5320 (1558-1560). The Mantua edition was produced at the press of R. Meir b. Efraim of Padua and R. Yaakov b. Naftali of Gazzuolo, and it became the textual basis for most subsequent printings of the Zohar.
