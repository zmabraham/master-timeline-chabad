# Rebbe Rashab delivers maamar on yahrtzeit of Rebbetzin Rivkah in Rostov

*1916*

On Shabbat 10 Shevat 5676, parshat Beshalach, the Rebbe Rashab marked the yahrtzeit of his mother, the Rebbetzin Rivkah. He delivered the maamar beginning 'Vayehi Beshalach Pharaoh,' a brief notation of which appears in the Rayatz's short daily diary for that year.
