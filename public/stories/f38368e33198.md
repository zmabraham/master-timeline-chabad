# R' Chaim Eliezer Bichovsky enters the etrog trade for Kollel Chabad

*1891*

Bichovsky later recounted in a public reply that he had begun handling "the etrog business" of Kollel Chabad in 5651, the trade serving as a discreet front for the Eretz Yisrael fundraising operation. He partnered with R' Chaim Bagin, and the two ran the renamed Vaad Mesadrei HaReshimah until 5673, when leadership changed again.
