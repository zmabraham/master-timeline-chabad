# Khovansky assigns investigators to Kisin's denunciation against the Mitteler Rebbe

*1825*

On 2 October 1825 (document no. 4831), Khovansky formally tasked three officials with investigating the denunciation lodged in June 1825 by a Vitebsk Jew named Kisin against 'Berke Schneur,' the rebbe of the Chasidic sect, accusing him of extorting large sums from Jews loyal to the 'old faith.' Kisin had produced an 1813 Hebrew distribution document showing 35,000 rubles allegedly divided among the rebbe's relatives, and claimed the rebbe was violating the 9 December 1804 Jewish Statute (§52) forbidding rabbis from accumulating wealth. Khovansky ordered the investigators to verify the document's authenticity, examine the sources of the funds, and summon the rebbe for formal questioning.
