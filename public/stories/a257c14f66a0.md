# Rabbinical Association of CIS founded in Moscow

*1992*

Meeting on 23 Shevat as part of the Moscow Rabbis Conference, the assembled rabbis established a joint Rabbinical Association with Rabbi Berel Lazar of Moscow as chairman and Av Beis Din. Rabbi Azriel Chaikin of Brussels was appointed adviser on marital law and Rabbi Dan Yoel Levi of the US adviser on kashrus, with an executive board drawn from Moldova, Kazakhstan, Dnipropetrovsk, Kharkov, Ukraine, Zhitomir and Samarkand. The body also resolved to publish a Torah journal addressing rabbinic questions across the region.
