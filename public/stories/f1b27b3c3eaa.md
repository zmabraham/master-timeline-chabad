# Chabad community established in Jerusalem

*1847*

The founding of the Chabad presence in Jerusalem marked the beginning of an institutional infrastructure that would soon include trustees, census records, and ties to philanthropists abroad. By 5609 a formal census listed Rabbi Eliyahu Yosef Rivlin and Rabbi Uri Orenstein as appointed trustees. They would also sign the 5609 letter to Sir Moses Montefiore as the trustees of Kollel Chabad in Jerusalem.
