# Rashag and R. Benyamin Gorodetzky visit Pocking before Pesach

*1947*

Before Pesach 5707 R. Benyamin Gorodetzky and the Rashag came from the Rebbe to Pocking. The Rashag held a Shabbat farbrengen with anash; afterward R. Shlomo Matusof delivered a letter from the Rebbe rebuking Levin's preoccupations and urging him to immerse in study. They also brought freshly copied maamarim of the Rebbe from that year.
