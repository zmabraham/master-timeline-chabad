# HaTamim issue 7 published for 40th anniversary

*1937*

On 12 Tammuz 5697 (June 21, 1937), the 7th issue of HaTamim was published, dedicated to the 40-year jubilee of the founding of Tomchei Tmimim in 5657. The issue began a series of articles on the founding, purpose, development, and growth of the yeshiva, with promises of more letters and essays in future issues.
