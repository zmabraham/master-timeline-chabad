# Rabbi Hutner asks Rebbe to clarify objection to his first letter

*1968*

On 16 Tevet 5728 (Jan 17, 1968), Rabbi Hutner sent a very short third letter to the Rebbe, asking that he do him the kindness of pointing out the nekudat ha-hakpadah — the precise point of objection — to his original Mivtza Tefillin letter. This brief note appears to have prompted the Rebbe's lengthy detailed reply two days later.
