# Rebbe founds Agudas Nashim in Rakshik

*1931*

A pinkas was opened recording the founding members, with a handwritten introduction and blessing by the Rebbe at its front. The Rebbe wrote that 'every city and settlement where the women establish such an Agudas Nashim - whether by this name or another - whose aim is strengthening taharas hamishpacha, may they, their husbands and offspring be included in the blessing stated here.'
