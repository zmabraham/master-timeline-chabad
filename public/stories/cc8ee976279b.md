# Yehoshua Hutner, Rabbi Avraham Yehoshua Hutner, 17 Kislev 5671

*1910*

Yehoshua Hutner, Rabbi Avraham Yehoshua Hutner, 17 Kislev 5671, December 18, 1910 - 26 Nissan 5769, April 20, 2009) was the founder and director of the Yad HaRav Herzog institutes, director of the Talmudic Encyclopedia project, and the Complete Israeli Talmud Institute
