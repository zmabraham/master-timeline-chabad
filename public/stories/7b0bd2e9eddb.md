# Passing of R' Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk

*1788*

R' Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk, leader of the chassidic aliyah to Eretz Yisrael and mentor to the Alter Rebbe, passed away on Rosh Chodesh Iyar 5548 (May 7, 1788) in Tiveria. After his passing, R' Avraham of Kalisk continued to write to the Russian chassidim affirming that R' Schneur Zalman alone was their leader.
