# Baker-Yeltsin meeting on Schneersohn books

*1992*

Mr. Collins descended at the embassy to report that Baker had met Yeltsin to discuss the books. Yeltsin assured Baker the matter was already arranged and the books would soon be transferred to the Academy of Sciences for delivery to the Rebbe, adding that the requested photocopies had been made. Collins asked the Chabad delegation to wait patiently and avoid making noise while the process proceeded.
