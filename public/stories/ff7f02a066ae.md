# Flight transports 75 Chernobyl children to Eretz Yisrael

*1992*

Seventy of the children were from Kiev region and five from Moscow, accompanied by the author and his son Meir. After landing at Lod the plane was loaded with food supplies organized by Tzeirei Agudat Chabad in Eretz Yisrael for return to Kiev. About ten children from previous groups who needed to return came back on the same flight. The author left his son Meir to spend Passover with grandparents and the Hertzel family at Kfar Chabad.
