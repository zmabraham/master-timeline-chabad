# Tzli Esh Haggadah by Yehuda Aryeh of Modena printed in Amsterdam

*1765*

In 5525 (1765), an illustrated Haggadah was printed in Amsterdam featuring the 'Tzli Esh' commentary by Yehuda Aryeh of Modena, an abridgment of his earlier work 'Zevach Pesach'. The 32-leaf quarto edition included a complete Yiddish translation of the Haggadah in Tzene-Rene script. The work became a popular Amsterdam edition of the Italian-influenced Haggadah tradition.
