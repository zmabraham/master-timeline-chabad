# About 20 Otwock students reach Vilna on foot

*1939*

After the Ribbentrop-Molotov partition of Poland, Tomchei Tmimim students began making their way north on foot across two borders to reach Vilna, which had just been transferred from Soviet to Lithuanian control. Writing from Riga to R. Yisroel Jacobson on 10 Kislev 5700, R. Chaim Lieberman reported that some twenty bochurim had already arrived in Vilna.
