# R' Sh.Y.L. Eliezerov leaves Hebron for Europe and Russia

*1903*

On 26 Marcheshvan 5664 (November 15, 1903), Hebron Ashkenazi rabbi Shlomo Yehuda Leib Eliezerov departed his city to travel abroad on behalf of Hebron's institutions, especially the Magen Avot yeshiva. R' Yaakov Yosef Slonim's farewell address in HaChavatzelet spoke of a few months' absence, but the trip in fact lasted over two years. Eliezerov went first to Lubavitch to consult the Rashab on improving Hebron's material and spiritual state.
