# Belgrade facsimile of the Sarajevo Haggadah

*1985*

In 5745 (1985), Belgrade published a facsimile reproduction of the famous Sarajevo Haggadah, a Spanish manuscript from the second half of the 14th century preserved at the National Museum of Bosnia in Sarajevo. The 34-plus-104-leaf facsimile was accompanied by a Hebrew introduction by Shlomo Zucker (Tel Aviv 1986) and an English introduction by Hoger Werber (Belgrade 1985).
