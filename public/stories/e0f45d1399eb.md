# Direct postal collection replaces traveling shadarim

*1888*

Funds were no longer entrusted to traveling emissaries but were mailed by each community directly to the 'ba'al ha'adres' in Jerusalem. The reform aimed to reduce avoidable expense and risk of misappropriation associated with the older shadar model. R. Moshe Wittenberg was appointed as the first ba'al ha'adres to oversee the new system.
