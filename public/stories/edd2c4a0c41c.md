# Rayatz sends funds to repair the Lubavitch mikvah

*1921*

After the fire destroyed the courtyard and the mikvah, some members of Anash still living in Lubavitch relocated the synagogue into the stone house. At the start of 5682 (1921), the Rayatz sent them a sum of money to repair the mikvah, as documented in Igrot Kodesh vol. 14.
