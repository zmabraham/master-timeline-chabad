# Public letter confirms Slonim's Yafo appointment

*1900*

Some had questioned whether the Lublin Gaon had truly directed Slonim's move to Yafo. R' Yitzchak Shmerling responded with an open letter to HaTzvi, stating that he had spoken with the Gaon directly and heard from him that he had chosen R' Shneur Zalman to take the Yafo rabbinate.
