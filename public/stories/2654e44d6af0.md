# R' Shmuel Zalmanov reports Va'ad and Otwock visit to the Rebbe

*1928*

Zalmanov reported the Va'ad's plan to reduce the institution while replacing the nigleh supervisor with 'our man' R' Boruch Friedman, and to draw senior students into giving hashpa'a in chassidus. He recounted his Monday 8 Marcheshvan trip to Otwock, where Hornstein seemed willing in principle but had unresolved doubts and would only answer after writing the Rebbe himself. The yeshiva's financial state was 'awful and terrifying,' with creditors pressing and no income.
