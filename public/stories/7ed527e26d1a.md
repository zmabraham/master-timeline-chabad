# Marriage of R' Shneur Zalman Gurarie in New York

*1940*

In the winter of 5700 (1939–40) R' Shneur Zalman Gurarie was married in New York to the daughter of the Admur of Kopytznitz, R' Avraham Yehoshua Heschel. The wedding took place against the backdrop of the European war, with much of the Chabad world already in flight. His position in New York would soon allow him to assist the Rebbe Rayatz in establishing new institutions in America.
