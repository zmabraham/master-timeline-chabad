# Final accounting: 100 of 117 crates of library reached USA

*1944*

In an igeres dated 7 Shevat 5704, the Rebbe noted that not all of his sefarim were saved from Poland: of some 117 crates only 100 had arrived in the USA. R' Chaim Lieberman later told the author that the Germans inspected the first transport and found nothing objectionable, but by the second transport America was already at war; the storehouse holding the remaining books was eventually bombed.
