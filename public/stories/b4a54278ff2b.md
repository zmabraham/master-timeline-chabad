# Apatov chassidic shul founded in Vilna

*1850*

Apatov served as a Chassidic house of prayer in Vilna for decades before it was acquired by Chabad. Its existence within a city long associated with the misnagdim made it a logistical anchor for Chabad activity in Lithuania. The shul would later host both a major rabbinical conference and a branch of Tomchei Tmimim.
