# Rayatz founds Achei Tmimim yeshiva in Tel Aviv

*1938*

Continuing the pattern set by his predecessor, the Rayatz founded Achei Tmimim in Tel Aviv and later added a Tomchei Tmimim division. Both operated separately from Kollel Chabad's Jerusalem-based framework, shifting Chabad's institutional center of gravity in the Holy Land away from the older kollel structure.
