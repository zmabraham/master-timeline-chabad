# 100 crates of the Rebbe Rayatz's library arrive in New York

*1941*

On 11 Sivan 5701, the Rebbe Rayatz wrote that the first transport of the library had arrived in New York. Secretary R' Chaim Lieberman confirmed on 20 Sivan that about 100 crates of sefarim had been received that week, with roughly 50 more crates plus the manuscripts still in Warsaw.
