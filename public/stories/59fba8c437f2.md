# Soviet education minister Lunacharsky targets the Rebbe in NYT

*1928*

Lunacharsky said, 'We shall combat the relics of the Middle Ages, the Schneersohnovschina.' The Times explained the term as 'the religious activities of the Lubavitcher Rebbe, who is now in exile in Latvia.' According to Lunacharsky, the solution lay in 'the fusion of the Jews in the great melting pot of the peoples who inhabit the Soviet Union.'
