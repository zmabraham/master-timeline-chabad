# Rayatz letter to R' Eliyahu Simpson about library

*1926*

Writing to a leader of the Lubavitch community in New York, the Rayatz confirmed the books were lodged in the ex-church annex of what had been the Rumyantsev Museum—now the Lenin Library—and that the Yevsektsia had failed to take them. He laid out a strategy for lobbying the Soviet government from the United States to compel their return.
