# Likhachov dispatches letter to Gorbachev backing Schneersohn return

*1990*

By 9 Tevet 5751 the delegation learned that Likhachov had sent his letter to Gorbachev and Lushchenkov backing the return of the Schneersohn library, with plans to follow up with Gubenko the next day. The same evening they met with the head of a Moscow suburban district who offered land for a Maimoni Center — a Veronika-led medical and educational complex including schools. Authorities had renewed their inquiries about the delegation's status, and two letters arrived from the Russian Federation government supporting their visas.
