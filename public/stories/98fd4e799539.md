# Połaniec branch of Tomchei Tmimim founded

*1931*

At the start of 5692 (fall 1931), at the urging of the Rebbe Rayatz, a small yeshiva was opened in Połaniec as a branch of Tomchei Tmimim Warsaw. Beginning with about twenty-five students, the yeshiva grew to over sixty boys divided into five divisions, under the leadership of R. Moshe Aharon Morgel. He taught Gemara and some chassidus to the upper division, with public chassidus shiurim several times a week.
