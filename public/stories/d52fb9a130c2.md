# Yisrael Yehuda Levin arrested at R. Zalman Kornitzer's Malakhovka home

*1935*

Soon after Purim 5695, Soviet detectives raided R. Zalman Kornitzer's Malakhovka home in the night and arrested the author and Avraham Kievman, who were sleeping there unregistered within the forbidden 100-kilometer Moscow zone. They were jailed in Lyubertsy in a cell with thieves and murderers; the criminals plucked hairs from Kievman's red beard, breaking his spirit. After several days they were released on signing a pledge that a second offense would mean two years of exile.
