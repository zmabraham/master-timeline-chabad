# Birth of Sterna Sarah Levin in Pocking DP camp

*1947*

On 24 Elul 5707 Itta Devorah Levin gave birth to a daughter — Sterna Sarah — while still in Pocking awaiting visas. The yeshiva's cooks prepared a large and lengthy kiddush, with much mashke and a farbrengen lasting until evening. R. Avraham Mayor was so joyful he rolled along the benches.
