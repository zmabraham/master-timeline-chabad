# Chodakov reports growing Chassidus shiurim in Riga to Rebbe Rayatz

*1930*

Writing on 25 Nisan 5690, Chodakov informed the Rebbe Rayatz that batei midrash which had not heard a Chassidus discourse in living memory now hosted regular shiurim, and that a fresh shiur had just been started in an unexpected beis midrash in Riga. He singled out the contributions of refugee rabbis Israel Zuber, Avraham Eli Asherov, and Yehuda Eber, and especially R. Eli Chaim Althaus, whose enthusiasm and personal commitment to serve as mashpia in one shul made an entire shiur possible.
