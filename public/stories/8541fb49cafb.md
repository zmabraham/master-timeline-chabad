# Kollel Chabad center shifts from Hebron to Jerusalem

*1888*

Jerusalem-based Chabad figures began calling themselves 'heads of Kollel Chabad' and asked donors abroad to send funds directly to them rather than through the Hebron officials or the Rogachover Vaad's general gabbai R. Dan Tumarkin. The Hebron officials under R. Shimon Menashe Chaikin responded with a public kruz, supported by some 50 Hebron and 50 Jerusalem Chabad signatories. Although the Jerusalem activists did not get everything they wanted immediately, by 5651 the ba'al ha'address (the kollel's primary postal point of contact) was already based in Jerusalem, and from then on Jerusalem was effectively the kollel's center.
