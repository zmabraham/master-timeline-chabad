# Pogroms and earthquake devastate Tzfat

*1837*

In the disasters of 5594–5598 ('the two pillages and the earthquake'), Tzfat's Jewish population was repeatedly attacked and then struck by the Galilee earthquake of 1837. R' Uri of Slonim lost his father, three of his children, and all his property. The killing of his daughter Tzipa Leah is confirmed by the 'List of Tzfat Casualties' published years later.
