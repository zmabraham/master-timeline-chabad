# R' Avraham Chaim writes from prison about Parshas Lech Lecha maamar

*1889*

In early Kislev 5650 (late 1889), R' Hendel Kugel sent R' Avraham Chaim a summary of a maamar the Rebbe Rashab had delivered on Parshas Lech Lecha. On the eve of 19 Kislev, R' Avraham Chaim wrote back from his prison in Smolensk, expressing how precious the teaching was but lamenting that the summary alone was insufficient. He asked that R' Avraham the chozer record full maamarim and mail them to him several at a time.
