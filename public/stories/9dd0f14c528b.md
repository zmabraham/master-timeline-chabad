# Decision to found Achei Temimim yeshiva branch in Chelm

*1936*

After Passover 5696, students who had been home for the holiday from Tomchei Temimim Lubavitch in Otwock convened a farbrengen at the home of Moshe Berland. They decided to establish a yeshiva in Chelm as a branch of the Otwock yeshiva, to be named Yeshivas Achei Temimim Lubavitch. Yaakov Yitzchak Rosenbaum was elected chairman and Avraham Yitzchak Kornblit secretary.
