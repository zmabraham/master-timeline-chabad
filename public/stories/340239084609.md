# Rebbe instructs Chasidim to stay in Poland to free prisoners

*1947*

Berel Gurewicz, Berke Chein, and Tzipa Kozliner had been detained by local police in Lvov unaware of the larger escape network. The Rebbe coordinated a $7,000 bribe routed through a contact in Vilnius, written about in code as 'medicine' for those ill. The bribe led to improved conditions and reduced sentences for all three.
