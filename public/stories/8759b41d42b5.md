# Assassination of Russian Prime Minister Pyotr Stolypin

*1911*

Stolypin had been the target of the Rayatz's covert St. Petersburg mission to subvert his planned anti-Jewish decree. He had served Czar Nicholas II since 1906. Earlier in 1910, he had met disrespectfully with rabbinic leaders at the conclusion of the national rabbinic conference, blaming them for the Bund's anti-government activities.
