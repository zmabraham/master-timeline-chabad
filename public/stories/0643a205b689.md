# Rebbe leads 100-year hilula farbrengen

*1966*

On the eve of 13 Nisan 5726, the Rebbe held a dedicated farbrengen elucidating teachings of the Tzemach Tzedek and again repeating his maamar 'HaChodesh HaZeh Lachem,' said by the Tzemach Tzedek shortly before his own passing. At the close he again urged participation in Keren HaMeah, with joy.
