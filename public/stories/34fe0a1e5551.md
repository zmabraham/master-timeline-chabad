# Poalei Tzion raid the Rashab's summer home in Bolivke

*1906*

A few months after the yeshivah attack, Poalei Tzion intruders broke into the Rashab's Bolivke summer home with revolvers drawn, destroying furniture and ransacking the house in front of the Rayatz's young daughters and their grandmother. The Rashab was forced to hire security guards; his brother the Raza eventually negotiated a settlement from Vitebsk.
