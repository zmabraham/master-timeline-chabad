# Rebbe launches food-package operation to Soviet Jews

*1931*

Observant Jews who would not work on Shabbat were denied ration cards, and the Soviet famine of 1932-33 deepened the crisis. The Rebbe organized a global network of "relatives" sending packages from Europe, the US, and beyond. The Rashag coordinated, contacting Jewish leaders as far as Cape Town and Paris. The campaign operated covertly to avoid Soviet interference.
