# Russian arbitrage court orders Chabad collection sealed

*1991*

After Ruslan Khasbulatov sent the books matter to four Supreme Soviet committees, Veronika reached the arbitrage judge Grebenikov and obtained a court order to seal the Schneersohn collection. With the order in hand she went to the militia and into the library accompanied by police. A commission was set up to handle the transfer including Veronika, Rabbi Levin, Yakirson and Professor Chelnov.
