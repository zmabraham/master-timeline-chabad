# Levi Yitzchak of Berditchev family unites with Alter Rebbe family

*1807*

In Sivan 5567 R' Yekutiel Zusman, grandson of R' Levi Yitzchak of Berditchev, married a granddaughter of the Alter Rebbe. The two rebbes had been calling each other 'mechutan' (in-law) in earlier letters in anticipation of the match. R' Levi Yitzchak had stood with the Alter Rebbe through the dispute with R' Avraham of Kalisk, both regarding chassidic doctrine and regarding fundraising for the Land of Israel.
