# Stalin's purges target JDC Agro-Joint workers

*1937*

Many were shot, others died in prison or remained jailed for long periods. Among those targeted were forty-one German physicians who had come to serve the settlements — seventeen were shot or died in Soviet camps. JDC director Rosen protested unsuccessfully. The JDC remained publicly silent about the catastrophe, continuing instead to paint a positive picture of Soviet life.
