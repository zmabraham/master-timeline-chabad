# R' Yehuda Eber sends detailed report to Rebbe on Vilna students

*1930*

After his arrival as Rosh Yeshiva, R' Yehuda Eber individually examined every student and sent the Rebbe a detailed roster covering each student's abilities, diligence, and yiras shamayim. He noted that R' Yaakov Hochman, the mashpia, was dedicated to the students' conduct but had not been examining their progress; new procedures were instituted.
