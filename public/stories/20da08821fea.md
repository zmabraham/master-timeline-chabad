# R' Yeshayahu Berlin settles in Riga

*1878*

R' Yeshayahu Berlin, a wealthy benefactor and one of the main supporters of the Tomchei Tmimim yeshiva in Lubavitch as well as other activities of the Rebbe Rashab, established himself in Riga around 5638 (1878). His arrival anchored the growing Chabad community in the Latvian capital.
