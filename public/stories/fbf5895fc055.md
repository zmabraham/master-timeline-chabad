# Yevsektzia dismantled by Stalin

*1930*

Established in 1917 to fortify Bolshevik control over Jewish masses, the Yevsektzia had been the Rayatz's primary adversary throughout the 1920s. After 1930 many of its leaders, including Semyon Dimenstein and Moshe Litvakov, were killed or exiled in the Great Purge. After the Yevsektzia was disbanded, government actions against synagogues and religious leaders actually became harsher.
