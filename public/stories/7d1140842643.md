# R' Yitzchak Mandelbaum appointed rabbi of Chabad community in Glubokoye

*1935*

After the Maciów initiative failed to advance, the Rebbe Rayatz reassigned Mandelbaum in 1935, sending him to lead the Chabad kehillah in Glubokoye. This appointment redirected Mandelbaum's communal work from the abandoned Volhynian yeshiva project to congregational leadership.
