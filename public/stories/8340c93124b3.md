# Great fire destroys Tzemach Tzedek's courtyard in Lubavitch

*1857*

The Rebbe relocated to the estate of a local nobleman outside town while the courtyard was rebuilt on a more spacious scale. A letter from his son Rabbi Chaim Schneur Zalman (later the Rebbe of Liadi) survives, dated 8 Iyar, appealing to chassidim to contribute toward the rebuilding effort. The expanded compound included an enlarged synagogue and dedicated prayer halls within the Rebbe's own house.
