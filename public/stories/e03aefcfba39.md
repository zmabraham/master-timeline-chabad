# Rabbi Zeitlin is interviewed by JEM. Rabbi Zeitlin at the entrance to 770

*2015*

Rabbi Zeitlin is interviewed by JEM. Rabbi Zeitlin at the entrance to 770 with the group of girls he led. Rabbi Aharon Eliezer Zeitlin (5714, 1953 – 5776, 2015) was one of the Rebbe's shluchim to the Holy Land, one of the mashpi'im of Anash in Tzfat, and chairman of the Chabad kindergarten network in Tzfat
