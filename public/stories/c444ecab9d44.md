# Rebbe writes condolence letter to Zelda upon her husband's passing

*1970*

After the passing of Chaim Mishkovsky in 5730, the Rebbe wrote a letter of consolation to his widow Zelda on 21 Menachem Av of that year. In the letter the Rebbe expressed personal interest in every detail of how she would arrange her life going forward, noting that only a few remained of their family in this generation. The letter was published in Igrot Kodesh vol. 26, letter 9955.
