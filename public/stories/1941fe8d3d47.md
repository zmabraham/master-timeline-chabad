# Passing of Rebbe Rashab in Rostov

*1920*

After years of organizing his father's manuscripts during the civil war, the fifth Lubavitcher Rebbe — Sholom Dovber Schneersohn — passed away in the early hours of 2 Nissan 5680 (March 21, 1920) and was buried in Rostov-on-Don. His son and successor, the Rebbe Rayatz, assumed the leadership of Chabad.
