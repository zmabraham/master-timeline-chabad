# Rayatz releases his final maamar 'Basi Legani' for 10 Shevat

*1950*

The maamar, distributed for 10 Shevat 5710, would later become the foundational text for the seventh Rebbe's first maamar and for an annual teaching tradition. Every year on 10 Shevat, his successor expounded on a concept from this final maamar, building the seventh Rebbe's leadership directly on the foundation laid by his father-in-law.
