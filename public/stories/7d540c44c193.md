# Yechidus and departure of Chabad library delegation

*1988*

Speaking in Yiddish, the Rebbe described the trip as 'one unified shlichus' that should function 'like a single body,' gave them three multiples of chai dollars for charity in Russia, and told them to treat every doubt leniently. He concluded: 'I am not thanking you—the greatest thanks will come from the books themselves.'
