# Rebbe Rashab's Simchas Torah farbrengen on lengthy prayer

*1917*

The Shemini Atzeres and Simchas Torah farbrengens of 5678 in Rostov were extraordinary, all focused on the imperative of avodah and lengthy davening — the core mission of Lubavitch. Sichos from the farbrengen are preserved in transcripts and the printed Toras Shalom. The Rebbe also engaged Polish chassidic refugees of Ger and Alexander who were present, and the daytime farbrengen continued until near midnight with dancing through the house.
