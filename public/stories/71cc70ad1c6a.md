# Yochanan Gordon immigrates to America on Rebbe's promise

*1931*

Gordon had resisted joining his three brothers in America, fearing his children would abandon observance as theirs had. The Rebbe assured him the children would remain religious Jews and learn in his yeshivas. The pledge was fulfilled in 1940 when Gordon's son Shalom Ber became one of the first students at the Rebbe's Brooklyn yeshivah.
