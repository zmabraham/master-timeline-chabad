# Pereshel facsimile of the Sulzbach 1755 Abarbanel Haggadah

*1985*

In 5745 (1985), Tuvia Pereshel published in New York a facsimile of the 1755 Sulzbach Haggadah with Don Isaac Abarbanel's commentary and a Kabbalistic interpretation. The 14-leaf octavo edition included a Hebrew introduction by Pereshel. It contributed to his ongoing series of rare Haggadah facsimiles for the Passover holiday.
