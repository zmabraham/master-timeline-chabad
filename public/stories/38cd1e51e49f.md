# Rebbe Rashab campaigns for rabbinic exemption from conscription

*1916*

In March 1916 (Adar Sheni 5676), the Russian Interior Minister sent a circular to provincial governors ordering police to identify serving rabbis and forward the lists for conscription review. Recognizing the catastrophe that would befall Jewish religious life if rabbis and shochtim were drafted, especially during wartime displacement, the Rebbe Rashab traveled to Petrograd to consult with local rabbis and activists. A memorandum was prepared for the Interior and War Ministers; though Mr. Markon delayed signing it through Pesach, Baron Alexander Gunzburg and lawyer Sliozberg eventually submitted their own memorandum on rabbinic exemption to the Interior Minister.
