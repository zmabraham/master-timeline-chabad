# Warsaw yeshiva Va'ad meets to plan staff reorganization

*1928*

R' Alter Simchowitz, R' Shmuel Zalmanov, and the senior administration committee met to plan how to reduce the yeshiva in size without weakening essential study. They resolved to release three staff members—the nigleh supervisor R' Yehoshua Ber, the second-division rosh yeshiva R' Zvi Meir, and the mashpia RSh"L—and proposed installing R' Boruch Friedman as the new nigleh supervisor. They also decided that R' Shmuel Zalmanov would travel to Otwock to formally press R' Moshe Hornstein to accept the directorship.
