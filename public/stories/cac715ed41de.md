# Plehve rebukes Jewish notables in St. Petersburg

*1904*

Plehve rebuked the delegation over Jewish youth involvement in revolutionary movements, the alienating effect of Russian schooling on Jewish students, and the agitation of the foreign Hebrew press. He linked any easing of residency laws to internal Jewish reform and explicitly threatened a repeat of the 1903 Kishinev and Homel pogroms. The Rebbe Rashab, then in Paris, received the verbatim transcript with alarm and demanded active community response.
