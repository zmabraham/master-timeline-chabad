# Founding of Rabbinical Association at Moscow Shluchim Conference

*1992*

The week of 22–24 Shevat brought the first major gathering of Chabad shluchim, shluchos and rabbis serving in the former Soviet Union. A founding photograph of the rabbinical association captures Rabbis Gurevitch, Mushkovitz, Kaminetzky, Geisser, Levi, Levin, Chaikin, Avelsky, Berkahn and Krasik. The conference was organized in Moscow despite the parallel pressure of the unresolved library case.
