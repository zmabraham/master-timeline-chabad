# Rebbe Rashab letter on starting library catalog

*1913*

On Erev Rosh Chodesh Shevat 5673 (January 1913), the Rebbe Rashab wrote to his son expressing pleasure that the books had been organized and a catalog made. He instructed that a copy of the list should be sent to Shmuel Wiener, who had assisted the Rebbe in selecting valuable books for the library.
