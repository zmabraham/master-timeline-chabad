# Passing of Rabbi Meir Shlomo Yanovsky, Rabbi of Nikolayev

*1933*

On 23 Elul 5693 (Sept. 14, 1933), Rabbi Meir Shlomo Yanovsky, the Rebbe's maternal grandfather and longtime Rabbi of Nikolayev, passed away. He had served the community since 5650, succeeding his grandfather Rabbi Avraham David Lavut. After his passing, his son-in-law Rabbi Shmuel Schneersohn (uncle of the Rebbe) succeeded him in the rabbinate.
