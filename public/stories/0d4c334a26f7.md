# Yeltsin reneges, defers Schneersohn books to Gorbachev

*1990*

On 19 Kislev 5751, after Chabad arrived for the promised ceremonial handover, Yeltsin had press coverage canceled and told the delegation that returning the books was up to Gorbachev, not him. The reversal coincided with a long Yeltsin-Gorbachev meeting that day in which Deputy Prime Minister Ryzhkov was sidelined and Yeltsin and Gorbachev publicly closed ranks. Veronika assessed that Yeltsin had originally planned a publicity move against Gorbachev but switched to a unity stance once the leaders aligned.
