# Passing of the Rebbe Rashab in Rostov

*1920*

Two weeks after the Purim farbrengen, the Rashab fell ill on a Friday and was diagnosed with typhoid. On the following Shabbat night, after blessing each of his granddaughters and the Rayatz, he whispered the words 'bekhol levavkha uvekhol nafshekha' of Shema and his soul ascended, thrusting the Rayatz into the leadership of Chabad.
