# Death of Rabbi Menachem Nachum Twersky of Loyev

*1876*

Rabbi Menachem Nachum of Loyev, father of Rabbi Mordechai of Loyev, died in 5636. After his passing, his son Mordechai signed letters as 'son of the deceased Menachem Nachum,' a signature change that helps date the surviving correspondence of the Chernobyl-Rachmistrivka rabbinic family.
