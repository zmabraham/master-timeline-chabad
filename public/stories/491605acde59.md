# Kollel Chabad sends summary of Tzemach Tzedek yeshiva to Rebbe Rayatz

*1938*

The 19 Adar I 5698 protocol included a short overview of the yeshiva and a list of its students with their stipends. The report described the institution as fulfilling the Rebbe Rashab's wish to bind kollel scholars to Chassidus study and to memorialize donors through Mishnah study. The 22 students were avreichim from various Jerusalem kollelim spending two hours daily at Beit Menachem.
