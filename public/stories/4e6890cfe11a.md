# Tsar Paul I orders release of Alter Rebbe and Chabad chassidim

*1798*

Following interrogations and the translation of his answers into Russian, the Tsar concluded that the Karliner sect (as the chassidim were called in the official record) had done nothing to disturb public order. On 19 Kislev 5559 he signed the order of release. This date later became the Chassidic festival of 19 Kislev.
