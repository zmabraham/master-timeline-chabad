# Chabad of Moscow hosts mass Sukkot children's gathering

*1991*

During Chol Hamoed Sukkot a huge children's party filled the Agudas Chassidei Chabad synagogue in Marina Roscha. Korbsky spoke about the holiday, Rabbi Bentzion Shem Tov Kunin offered a blessing, Rabbi Kogan spoke about Simchas Torah, and the speaker announced that following the Rebbe's directive to open new yeshivos and schools especially in Russia, a Sunday school would now open at the synagogue. Raffles, food in the sukkah and singing left families eager to register.
