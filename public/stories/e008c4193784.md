# Chabad delegation meets Chief Rabbi Eliyahu at Hotel Rossiya in Moscow

*1991*

On the night of 25 Adar 5751, Rabbis Aharonov, Cunin, Kogan, and Levin met with newly arrived Chief Rabbi Mordechai Eliyahu at his Moscow hotel. They presented a decade of effort to recover the Schneersohn library from Soviet custody and discussed strategy for the days ahead. Rabbi Eliyahu pledged to raise the matter with senior Soviet religious-affairs officials and with the head of the Russian Orthodox Church.
