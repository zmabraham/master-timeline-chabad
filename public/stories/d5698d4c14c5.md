# Rashab corresponds with R. Dovid Yakobson on Lubavitch mikveh

*1907*

When a new mikveh was constructed in Lubavitch in 5667 (1907), the Rashab and the town's moreh tzedek R. Dovid b. Bentzion Yakobson exchanged a series of substantial halachic letters on the laws of mikvaot. These letters are preserved in Igrot Kodesh of the Rashab, vol. 1, nos. 167-171. R. Yakobson was the son-in-law of R. Avraham Zeligsohn and had inherited the post despite his relatively young age.
