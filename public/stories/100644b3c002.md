# Rabboseinu exhibition opens on 18 Elul

*1996*

The fourth exhibition of the Library — Taarukhas Rabboseinu — opened on 18 Elul 5756 (1996), the joint birthday of the Baal Shem Tov and the Alter Rebbe. It displayed first editions of the Rebbeim's works, manuscripts, sacred objects, and portraits, with dedicated cases for the Baal Shem Tov, Maggid of Mezeritch, and each of the Chabad Rebbeim.
