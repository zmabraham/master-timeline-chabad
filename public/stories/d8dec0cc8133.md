# Yom Kippur Mincha 5710: narrator leins for Ramash while Rebbe Rayatz takes maftir Yonah

*1949*

On yom tov a second minyan was held upstairs in 'the Alte Rebbetzin's room,' where the chazan stood and the leining took place, with the door to the Rebbe Rayatz's office open so he could hear the davening. Arriving from his East Flatbush shamash duties for Mincha, the narrator was asked by the Ramash to lein; a Torah scroll was placed on a small table in the corridor near the dining room. The Ramash himself received maftir Yonah at this minyan.
