# JDC report counts 15,000 children in secret schools

*1926*

The confidential report sent to the JDC in New York stated that some fifteen thousand children were being educated in a growing number of secret schools. In Moscow alone, three hundred children studied in groups of three, hiding in cellars and venturing to the woods in summer. Each teacher was paid thirty rubles a month, with another thirty rubles paid to a watchman keeping lookout for authorities.
