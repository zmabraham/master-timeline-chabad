# Asterisk footnote convention introduced in Kuntres 2 Nissan 5709

*1949*

After the lead pages of the 2 Nissan 5709 kuntres had already gone to the Manhattan press, R' Chadakov telephoned: the Frierdiker Rebbe had asked why no marker in the body of the ma'amar indicated the Ramash's marginal notes. The memoirist set new lines with asterisks, rushed them to the press at dawn, and replaced the old slugs. From that kuntres onward, asterisks marked the Ramash's marginal notes throughout the chassidus kuntreisim.
