# Veronica Irina confronts Soviet Culture Minister Gubenko over Schneersohn library

*1991*

On 28 Adar 5751, Veronica Irina met privately with Culture Minister Gubenko after refusing to negotiate in front of his recording device and aides. Gubenko admitted the Schneersohn collection had never been nationalized and conceded he was open to returning it, while saying preparations could take until May 1. He revealed that Rabbis Shayevich and Schneier had personally urged him not to rush the return, citing internal Jewish disagreement, and said only his own signed order would release the books for Gorbachev to authorize.
