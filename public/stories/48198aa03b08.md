# Passing of the Maharshaz of Kopust, author of Magen Avos

*1900*

The Admor Shlomo Zalman of Kopust, son of the Admor Maharil and grandson of the Tzemach Tzedek, lived until 5660. Known for his work Magen Avos, he had assumed his father's place in Kopust from 5627 onward and was the senior figure of the Kopust branch of Chabad.
