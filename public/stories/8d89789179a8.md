# Rebbe launches global Shluchim campaign

*1950*

Immediately upon accepting the leadership of Chabad in 5710 (1950), the Rebbe began sending shluchim to establish Chabad centers in every possible city and country. During the first decade of his leadership (5710–5720), shluchim were dispatched to North Africa, Italy, England, Brazil, Argentina, Australia, the Far East, and many cities in the Land of Israel and the United States.
