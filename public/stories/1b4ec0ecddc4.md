# Rebbe's encouraging response on Adar arrives in Moscow delegation

*1991*

On 18 Adar 5751, R. Yisroel Wilhelm relayed the Rebbe's reply to Stas's general report. The Rebbe pointedly highlighted 'Adar' in the date line and wrote that the continuing efforts should succeed in freeing the books. The delegation in Moscow took this as a major morale boost and intensified their lobbying with renewed vigor.
