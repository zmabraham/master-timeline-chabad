# Polish authorities revoke library export permit; threat to confiscate Jewish books

*1940*

On 13 Nissan, R' Mordechai Chefetz received word — and a confirming visit from the wife and son of R' Shmuel Zalmanov — that the German occupation authorities had revoked the library's export license and issued a general order that all sefarim in Poland would be confiscated. The disclosure put the entire Schneersohn library at imminent risk.
