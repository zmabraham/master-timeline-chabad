# Alter Rebbe begins composing the Shulchan Aruch HaRav

*1770*

The Maggid searched among his disciples for one who could compose a clear, reasoned compendium of halachic rulings and chose the young Schneur Zalman, refusing to relent until he agreed. Composition of the Shulchan Aruch HaRav began after 5530, becoming the authoritative halachic work of Chabad.
