# with his grandson Chaim, Rabbi Yoel Branchik, 12 Tevet 5634 - 19

*1874*

with his grandson Chaim, Rabbi Yoel Branchik, 12 Tevet 5634 - 19 Kislev 5702; 1874 - 1941) was one of the prominent Torah scholars and activists in Riga, established a branch of 'Tiferet Bachurim' in Riga, about whom the Rayatz expressed "a truly God-fearing man"
