# Passing of Rabbi Avraham Chaim Rosenbaum on first night of Chanukah

*1913*

In his final years Avraham Chaim suffered paralysis of his left arm, losing movement and sensation. When a visitor brought him a Torah Or siddur with the order of viduy for a dying person, he refused it, calling it a 'misnagdic viduy,' and instead recited Patach Eliyahu, the kabbalistic introduction said before Friday Mincha, from beginning to end with great sweetness. He passed away on 25 Kislev 5674 (December 24, 1913), the first night of Chanukah, his yahrzeit observed on that date.
