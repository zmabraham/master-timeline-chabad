# Chana Ariel by Rabbi Yitzchak Aizik of Homil published in Berditchev

*1912*

Chana Ariel is a major chassidic work by Rabbi Yitzchak Aizik of Homil, an outstanding disciple of the first three Chabad Rebbeim. The first edition appeared in Berditchev in 5672 (1912) and is exhibited at the Lubavitch Library.
