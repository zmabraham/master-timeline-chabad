# JTA publishes report from Riga on cause of Rebbe's arrest

*1927*

The JTA's Riga dispatch said that 'well-informed circles in Moscow' traced the arrest to the Lubavitcher Rebbe's resistance to the Leningrad conference set for October 21, 1927. The conference's initiators, it reported, decided to discredit the Rebbe before Soviet authorities to remove his opposition.
