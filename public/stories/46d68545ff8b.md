# General Procurator confirms Alter Rebbe's arrival in Petersburg

*1800*

On 11 Kislev 5561 (approximately November 28, 1800), General Procurator Obolininov sent a letter to Severin, governor of Belarus, confirming that Rabbi Schneur Zalman had been delivered to Petersburg. The procurator complained that no account books, documents, or correspondence had accompanied the prisoner, and ordered the governor to conduct a search and forward anything that could be found, including writings hidden with associates.
