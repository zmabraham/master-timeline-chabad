# Rabbi Avraham of Kalisk opposes the Tanya's approach

*1797*

Soon after the Tanya was printed in 5557, Rabbi Avraham of Kalisk—then leading Chassidim in Eretz Yisrael—came out publicly against its method. Disciples of the Maggid in Volhynia and Poland were drawn into the dispute, which continued for years and eventually produced a permanent split between general Chassidism and Chabad. Rabbi Levi Yitzchak of Berditchev took the Alter Rebbe's side, writing that the Tanya approach reflected 'the entire essence of the Maggid's own conduct.'
