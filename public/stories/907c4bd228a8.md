# Prison conditions sharply worsen at end of winter 5650 in Smolensk

*1890*

At the close of winter 5650 the prison administration was replaced and Rosenbaum wrote to R' Hendel describing harsher rules: only government-issued uniforms, straw pillows instead of feather, no private samovars, food rationed to two meals only, no chests or utensils beyond a single pot and spoon. He worried particularly about Pesach, when reliance on the common kettle would force compromise on chametz and warm food.
