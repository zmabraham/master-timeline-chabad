# Nazi conquest of Latvia; Riga Jews murdered

*1941*

Among those killed were R. Rafael Cohen, who ran into the burning shul to save the Sefer Torah and was locked inside; R. Eliyahu Chaim Altheuse, the leading figure of Riga anash; and R. Hillel Horwitz and his family. The decades of Chabad institutional growth in Riga came to an abrupt and devastating end.
