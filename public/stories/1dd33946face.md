# R' Yehuda Asad accepted as dayan in Sardhel

*1826*

In the year 5586, R' Yehuda Asad (later author of Yehuda Yaaleh) was received as dayan in the Hungarian community of Sardhel. His appointment came through the recommendations of R' Aharon Suditz-Bichler, the Chasam Sofer, and R' Mordechai Banet.
