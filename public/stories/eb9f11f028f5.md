# Future Rebbe submits family birth records for US visas

*1939*

Writing in French to Rabbi Israel Jacobson in New York, the future Rebbe Menachem Mendel Schneerson provided personal details to assist with US visa procurement: Chaya Mushka Schneersohn (b. Aug 13, 1901, Smolensk) and Menachem Mendel Schneersohn (b. Mar 1, 1895, Nikolaev, to Levik and Chana). The application was meant to bring the future Rebbe and Rebbetzin together with the Frierdiker Rebbe's family to America.
