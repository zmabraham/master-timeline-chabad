# Simcha Kisin denounces Rabbi DovBer of Lubavitch to authorities

*1825*

In July 1825, Simcha Kisin delivered a formal denunciation to Prince Nikolai Khovansky, Governor-General of Vitebsk, Mogilev, Smolensk and Kaluga, alleging that Rabbi DovBer of Lubavitch — the Mitteler Rebbe — was illegally raising money from his chassidim. This informer's report cited documents including a fund-distribution sheet and correspondence between the Rebbe and his associates. The denunciation triggered the formal criminal investigation that would lead to the Rebbe's arrest and eventual acquittal nearly two years later.
