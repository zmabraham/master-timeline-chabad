# Rabbi Zalmanov. Rabbi Yisrael Yosef Zalmanov (16 Menachem Av 5699, 1939 - 7 Menachem Av 5780, 2020)

*1939*

Rabbi Zalmanov. Rabbi Yisrael Yosef Zalmanov (16 Menachem Av 5699, 1939 - 7 Menachem Av 5780, 2020) was one of the dignitaries of the Chabad community in Petach Tikva and one of the elder Chabad Chassidim in the Holy Land
