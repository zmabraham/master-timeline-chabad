# Jacobson family boards SS Paris at Le Havre

*1925*

After about a week in Le Havre, the family embarked on Wednesday, the sixth Chanukah candle. The ship took on additional passengers via tender at an English coast before heading into open sea; Jacobson's wife was bedridden with seasickness from that stop onward, and elder daughter Chaya Sara also suffered, while a Friday-evening storm sent waves cresting over the ship's roof.
