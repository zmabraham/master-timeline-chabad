# Riga rescue hopes rise then fall in late summer 5700

*1940*

On 28 Menachem Av 5700, R. Mordechai Chefetz wrote to the Frierdiker Rebbe expressing hope that R. Yechezkel Feigin would soon receive his travel permit and that R. Yehuda Eber had already obtained his. By 4 Tishrei tash'a, however, both were still waiting in vain for salvation.
