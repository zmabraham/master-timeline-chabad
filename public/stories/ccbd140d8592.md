# Rebbe Rayatz commits monthly funding to Łódź yeshiva

*1931*

In a letter dated 7 Menachem Av 5691, the Rebbe Rayatz enclosed two checks of $25 each to the directorate of Tomchei Tmimim Łódź. The first was on account of his pledge toward yeshiva expenses, and the second toward the salary of R. Baruch Friedman, who served as the mashpia from 5689 to 5692. The Rebbe requested immediate confirmation of receipt.
