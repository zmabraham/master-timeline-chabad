# Sheina Schneersohn-Horenstein killed in Treblinka

*1942*

Sheina and her husband Menachem Mendel Horenstein had been unable to leave Poland because the Horensteins were Polish citizens denied entry to Latvia. Their fate remained unknown until after the war. The Ramash withheld the confirmed dates from the ailing Rebbe; the Seventh Rebbe later recited Kaddish annually on the second day of Rosh HaShanah for Sheina.
