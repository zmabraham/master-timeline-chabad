# Yaavetz returns from Meknes to Fez after eleven years

*1729*

In Adar Sheni 5489 (spring 1729), after some eleven years leading the Meknes community, Rabbi Yaakov ibn Tzur returned to Fez. The Fez community formally re-ratified its earlier oath of support just before his arrival. His marriage-records pinkas, which had recorded 'Meknesa' as the place of entry since 5480, switches back to 'Fez' from 5489 onward.
