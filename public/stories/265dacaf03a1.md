# Rebbe boards the SS Drottningholm in Gothenburg bound for America

*1940*

The Drottningholm was the only ocean liner still operating between neutral Sweden and the US. Mechanical problems detained it overnight at Bergen, Norway, with German submarines patrolling below in preparation for the invasion of Denmark a month later. German U-boats stopped the ship twice and British warships three times during the crossing, but each time the refugees were allowed to continue.
