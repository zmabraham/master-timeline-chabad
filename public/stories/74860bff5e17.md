# Rebbe summarizes Feigin and Eber's situation

*1941*

On 17 Tevet 5701, the Rebbe's secretariat wrote to the Gaon HaChasid R. Yerachmiel Binyaminson summarizing the situation: R. Yechezkel Feigin and Rosh Yeshiva R. Yehuda Eber remained in Riga, still unable to obtain exit permits. They prayed for divine mercy on all of them.
