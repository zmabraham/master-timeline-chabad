# Feigin reports HIAS will cover only 50%

*1940*

On Ta'anit Esther 5700, R. Yechezkel Feigin reported that Rosenbaum had received a telegram indicating HIAS would participate only at about 50% of the travel costs. With time running short, he was still uncertain how matters would resolve, and Rosenbaum was pushing him toward the April 13 Swedish ship despite the Pesach problem.
