# Simcha Kissin submits denunciations against Mitteler Rebbe

*1825*

Kissin's denunciation portrayed the Chassidic movement as exploiting youthful followers for money and seizing control of communal funds, slaughter, and printing. He attached a copy of the Mitteler Rebbe's 1814 Ma'amad distribution letter, attempting to prove that the Rebbe had amassed roughly 140,000 rubles. The Mitteler Rebbe was traveling abroad at the Carlsbad spa at the time and would be summoned upon his return.
