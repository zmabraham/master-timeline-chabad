# Rabbi Zaltzman speaks at the American Senate (5776). Rabbi Hillel Zaltzman

*1939*

Rabbi Zaltzman speaks at the American Senate (5776). Rabbi Hillel Zaltzman (born in 5699, 1939) is one of the activists of the Chamah organization in the Soviet Union, author of the book 'Samarkand Underground,' and chairman of the Chamah organization in the United States
