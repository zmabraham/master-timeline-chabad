# Aaron Dingol of The Day reports on Chabad refugee work in Paris

*1949*

On 9 Tevet 5709, Mr. Aaron Dingol, editor of the Yiddish daily Der Tog, visited Rebbe Rayatz and reported on his tour of post-war refugee camps. He described how most rabbis, shochtim and teachers there were affiliated with Lubavitch, and detailed the orderly Chabad institutions in Paris including Bais Rivka and Bais Sara schools serving Lubavitch refugees.
