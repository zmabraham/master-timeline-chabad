# Israel Aryeh Leib Schneersohn writes to his mother after her arrival in the US

*1947*

Following the dramatic rescue of Rebbetzin Chana Schneersohn from the Soviet Union and her arrival in the United States in summer 5707, her younger son Israel Aryeh Leib wrote her a warm letter on 4 Tammuz 5707. He expressed relief that her ordeal of waiting and travel had ended and asked after her health and her injured leg. He sent photographs of his daughter Dalia and looked forward to a reunion with his mother and brother.
