# Maharaitz's diary: outbreak of World War I observed at Zaalshe

*1914*

Beginning 13 Menachem Av 5674 (August 1914), while staying with his father at the Zaalshe summer retreat, Rebbe Yosef Yitzchak recorded daily entries about the outbreak of World War I. Maharashab followed the news closely through newspapers and visitors, expressed wonder at Germany's audacity to fight several nations at once, and refused to share his own opinions on the conflict.
