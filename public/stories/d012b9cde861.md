# Shainfinkel and Chagiz confirm to Rebbe Rashab that Kollel Chabad books are accurate

*1912*

Their certification, attached to Rashab's later circular, vouched that the figures aligned with the kollel's 'tagebuch' which was largely signed by recipients upon receipt of funds. Chagiz added his own confirmation that he too had reviewed the accounts and the ledgers and found everything correct. The document became a key piece of evidence in defending the Jerusalem officials against the gabbaim abroad.
