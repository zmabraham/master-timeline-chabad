# Library appeals ruling; arbitrage court orders rehearing of Chabad case

*1991*

An initial appeal under Culture Minister Gubenko's signature was rejected as improperly drafted, but new appeals of three and thirteen pages from the prosecutor general and from director Vyalik forced the court to respond. The chief judge and panel chose to convene a rehearing rather than answer by letter, and asked Chabad to supply evidence under Torah law that the collection belonged to the movement as a whole rather than personally to the Rebbe. When informed of the request, the Rebbe reportedly responded with displeasure: 'They need proofs again? This still isn't clear?'
