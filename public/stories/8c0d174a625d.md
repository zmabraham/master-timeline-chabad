# R. Avraham HaKohen of Kaliska writes from Tzfat about the new settlement

*1778*

R. Avraham HaKohen of Kaliska, one of the leaders of the new chassidic settlement, sent a letter from Tzfat at the end of Shvat 5538 to his followers in Smilian. He reported on their safe arrival, the establishment of their community in Tzfat, and sent the letter via R. Shlomo Segal as a trusted messenger.
