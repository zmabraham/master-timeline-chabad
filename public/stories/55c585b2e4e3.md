# R. Israel of Polotzk writes shadar letter from Iași

*1778*

While on his shadar mission back from Eretz Yisrael, R. Israel of Polotzk wrote a detailed letter from Iași on 9 Iyar 5538. He described the financial catastrophe caused by the many poor who joined the aliyah, the burden of supporting them through Constantinople, and pleaded for support of the new chassidic yishuv. The letter explicitly denied that he or R. Menachem Mendel had encouraged the poor to make aliyah, noting they had pressed themselves onto the group.
