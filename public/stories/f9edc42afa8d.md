# HaPardes advertisement solicits donations for Lubavitch library

*1933*

Dictated by the Rebbe himself and signed in the library's institutional name rather than his own, the notice appeared in the American journal HaPardes and listed the Riga address Pulko, Briero 10/5. It instructed rabbis, authors, publishers, and printers to send every imprint — books, broadsides, posters and notices alike — to the library, formalizing its public-institution character.
