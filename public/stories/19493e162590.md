# Bush raises seforim with Gorbachev at G7

*1991*

On 9 Av 5751 (the nidcheh), R. Y.Y. Aharonov reported by phone the delegation's London activity. Bush handed Gorbachev a letter prepared by Sandy on three subjects including the seforim, and Gorbachev replied that he knew of the matter and would arrange it shortly. The delegation also met Peter Hartman, an aide to Chancellor Kohl, after the Rebbe approved approaching the German delegation. Aharonov told Hartman that the whole saga began when the Frierdiker Rebbe fled Lubavitch as the Germans approached the region in 1915.
