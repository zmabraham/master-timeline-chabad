# Chabad delegation travels to Leningrad to lead public seders

*1991*

On the eve of Pesach 5751, two members of the special mission to recover the Schneerson library left Moscow by night train for Leningrad to lead public seders in the city's Great Synagogue. Three separate seders were held there, organized respectively by Shamir, the Jewish Agency, and the local Anash community, each drawing close to a hundred participants. Rabbi Levin led the seders while Rabbi Kogan translated and spoke; they also led prayers, Torah reading, and communal discussions before returning to Moscow.
