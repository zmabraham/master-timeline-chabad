# Rayatz announces plan to establish a Torah study circle in Riga

*1930*

On 2 Cheshvan 5691, soon after returning to Riga from America, the Rebbe Rayatz wrote to R' Yehuda Leib Nimoitin announcing his decision to establish a Torah study circle in Riga for outstanding students of Torah and yiras shamayim. He wished to gather ten select students who would devote themselves wholly to Torah study according to set conditions.
