# Rayatz sends future Rebbe on mission to Hans Gaslar in Berlin

*1932*

Rayatz writes that he can no longer postpone using personal connections and recommendations to recover the library. He provides detailed identification markers: about 5000 catalogued numbers (Shas, Rambam, Tur, Mikraos), each cross-referenced in the list he kept; bindings mostly in distinctive blue, green, or chocolate-colored cloth. He emphasizes the chillul Hashem of the holy books of the Rebbeim — including ones with their marginal notations — sitting in captivity, and the great mitzvah of rescuing them.
