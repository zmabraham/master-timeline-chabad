# HoD kollel begins receiving Pekoam share

*1837*

After many had begun immigrating from Holland and Germany to settle in Eretz Yisrael, from 5597 onward the Pekidim VeAmarkalim included them in the kollel distribution. The HoD share was carved out of the existing Ashkenazic portion, gradually evolving toward the formula codified in 5610.
