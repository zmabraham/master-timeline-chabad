# Court inspector Kilikovsky reports delays in Mitteler Rebbe case

*1826*

On 23 January 1826 (27 Shvat 5586), Inspector Kilikovsky of the Babinovich district court reported to Governor-General Khovansky that the case against Rabbi Berke Schneur of Lubavitch had stalled. The file had arrived at the peace court on 23 December 1825 and sat unexamined; only on 15 January 1826 did officials review it, opting on 16 January to send written questions via a constable to the rabbi and the Jewish communities — bypassing proper procedure, which required summoning the rabbi for direct questioning. Kilikovsky reported the irregularity and noted that the secretary Zhuravkovich had already been dismissed by the Mogilev provincial administration for misconduct.
