# Petersburg Rabbinical Conference of 1910

*1910*

After two years of intensive preparation, the official Rabbinical Conference (Ravvinskaya Komissiya) convened in Petersburg under the auspices of the Ministry of the Interior's religious affairs department. The conference was the culmination of years of effort by Rebbe Rashab and the Orthodox rabbinate, who had finally succeeded in ensuring that this iteration of the periodic government rabbinical commission would consist primarily of pious Orthodox rabbis from across the empire, including the Polish provinces. The conference proper lasted about a month, with follow-up work continuing through the end of 5670.
