# Rayatz responds to Agudat Chasidei Chabad first report

*1942*

On 6 Adar 5702 (Feb 1942), Rebbe Rayatz wrote letter 1719 acknowledging receipt of the Vaad's 8 Kislev report, the first since the founding letter of 19 Tammuz tashah. While crediting them for what was accomplished, he urged that more must be done to spread chassidus and strengthen Torah learning.
