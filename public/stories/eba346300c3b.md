# First issue of HaTamim journal printed in Warsaw

*1935*

On 20 Menachem Av 5695 the Rebbe Rayatz wrote that HaTamim was ready for press. Its formal editors were R' Yechezkel Feigin and R' Yehuda Eber, with R' Shmuel Zalmanov as general editor, but in practice the Rebbe Rayatz's son-in-law (later the Rebbe) in Paris substantially edited and annotated the material. R' Schneur Zalman Gourary oversaw publication on behalf of the Agudah.
