# Tzemach Tzedek's final illness begins in Kislev

*1859*

In Kislev 5620 the Tzemach Tzedek fell seriously ill and remained so for more than six consecutive years until his passing. During this period he could no longer hold full audience and answered questions submitted through his attendants on small notes, replying briefly in person or in writing. Manuscripts in his hand from these years are scarce and show a trembling script.
