# Letter to R. Chaim Ozer requesting 100 Palestine certificates

*1940*

Hearing that R. Chaim Ozer was about to receive a batch of British certificates through Rabbi Herzog's intervention, the Rebbe wrote requesting that the committee under R. Chaim Ozer's leadership reserve at least one hundred for Tomchei Tmimim talmidim and their rashei yeshiva. This was part of a parallel track to the American visa effort.
