# Haidamak pogroms reach Zhivotov

*1919*

Petliura and Denikin forces were fighting their civil war across Ukraine, and successive bands of pogromists — including those of Tyutyunyk and Sokolov — passed through the area. They imposed taxes on the Jewish community, conscripted men like Ephraim Shmuel to dig defensive trenches, and increasingly turned to murder. Local Ukrainian peasants joined the violence as the months wore on.
