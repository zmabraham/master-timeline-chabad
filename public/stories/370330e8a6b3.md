# Passing of the Mitteler Rebbe (9 Kislev)

*1827*

His passing came twelve days before the Senate in Petersburg confirmed his judicial acquittal. The proximity of his passing to Yud Kislev — what would have been the first major celebration of his redemption — meant that the day was never widely established as a festival during his lifetime, contributing to historical confusion about whether the release occurred in 5586 or 5587.
