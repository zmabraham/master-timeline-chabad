# 'Shliach Oseh Shliach' program launched on Shabbos Bereishis

*1984*

On Shabbos Bereishis 5745 (1984), the Rebbe announced the 'shliach oseh shliach' program — each shliach to appoint an additional shliach for his city — and pledged to fund two-thirds of the new shluchim's first-year expenses (Hisva'aduyos 5745 vol. 1 p. 461). This led to the founding of the annual Kinus Hashluchim near 770, held each year around Rosh Chodesh Kislev.
