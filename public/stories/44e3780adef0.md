# Polyakov Synagogue officially designated for return to Chabad

*1991*

The delegation, joined by R. M.M. Levin and R. Z. Koravsky, visited the synagogue building with Natalia from the Mossoviet. She told the current occupants on behalf of the municipality that the building, taken from the Jewish community in 1937, had been designated for return and they would need to vacate. The building required extensive repairs after a recent failure of its water and heating systems.
