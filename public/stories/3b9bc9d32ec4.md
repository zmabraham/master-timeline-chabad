# Alexander Bessmertnykh appointed Soviet Foreign Minister

*1991*

Bessmertnykh had previously served as Soviet ambassador to the United States, where he had once written a letter recommending that the Schneerson library be returned to Chabad. His appointment to head the Soviet foreign ministry was welcome news to the Chabad delegation in Moscow, who immediately received a fax copy of his earlier supportive letter. The delegation began maneuvering through American and Soviet diplomatic channels to secure a meeting with him about the library.
