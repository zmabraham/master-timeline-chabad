# Lag BaOmer parade through Moscow with cruise to river island

*1992*

Lag BaOmer 5752 in Moscow opened with a parade from the great synagogue to the riverbank, drawing several hundred children and adults marching with a band and placards. Participants then boarded two cruise ships out to an island on the river. The author returned to Marina Roshcha to daven, then drove with Rabbi Ariel Chaikin — back from inspecting the mikveh under construction in Kharkov — to catch the last leg of the boat trip to the island. They addressed the gathered children briefly before returning.
