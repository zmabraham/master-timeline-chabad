# Pogromist Sokolov extorts Zhivotov, kills ten

*1920*

When Sokolov's band approached Zhivotov, the town's elders went out to greet him with bread, water, and a Torah scroll, begging him to restrain his men. He agreed but said ten Jews would die regardless. Among the dead was the wife of the town's rabbi, Reb Chaim. The town considered itself spared compared to neighboring Tetiev.
