# Rebbe Rayatz writes Ramash about transferring funds

*1938*

On 23 Shevat 5698 (January 25, 1938), the Rebbe Rayatz wrote to his son-in-law (the future Rebbe, Ramash, in Paris) inquiring about the most economical way to transfer 10,000 zloty to his Warsaw account — whether by purchasing a bank check or direct transfer. Due to Polish currency restrictions, all support funds were routed to the Rebbe Rayatz through his son-in-law in Paris.
