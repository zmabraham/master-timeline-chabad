# Liberation of the Rebbe Rayatz on 12 Tammuz

*1927*

Arrested for his work sustaining Jewish life under Soviet repression, the Rebbe Rayatz was released on 12 Tammuz 5687. The date became a major commemorative occasion in Chabad, marked in 2000 by a library exhibition honoring his 120th birth-year and 73 years since his liberation.
