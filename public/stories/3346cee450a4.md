# Chabad periodical 'HaTamim' begins publication

*1935*

In 5695 (1934/35) the Igud Talmidei HaTemimim began issuing the periodical HaTamim, with R' Shneur Zalman Gurarie responsible for its publication. The journal collected discourses, articles and historical material from the world of Chabad and Tomchei Tmimim. It became a major outlet for the movement's printed output in pre-war Europe.
