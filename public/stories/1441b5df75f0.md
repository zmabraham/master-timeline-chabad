# R' Gershon Burkis gives the Rebbe new booklets of Machanaim

*1947*

R' Gershon Burkis gives the Rebbe new booklets of Machanaim. The publisher R' Gershon Burkis, with a set of "Sipurei Tzaddikim". Rabbi Gershon Burkis (born 5707, 1947) is the publisher of the 'Machanaim' series, which includes chassidic stories
