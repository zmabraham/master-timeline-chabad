# R. Yehuda ibn Atar orders Yaakov ben Atar to borrow at interest for Adhan orphans

*1708*

On 13 Sivan 5468 (1708) in Fez, with Yaakov ben Avraham ibn Atar having sworn poverty and unable to repay the widow Alaz and her sons Mordechai and Yosef on the debts to their late father R. Yitzchak ben Mordechai Adhan, R. Yehuda ibn Atar intervened. Moved by Yaakov's pursuit by many creditors and by the plight of Esther, a daughter who had reached marriageable age without dowry, R. Yehuda ordered Yaakov to borrow 50 ouquiyot from gentiles on credit and pay an additional 25 to the heirs within eighteen months. In return, the widow and orphans waived every claim against Yaakov, freed him from oaths and bans, and committed to indemnify him against any future claim.
