# Hebron Chabad community letter to Tzemach Tzedek on Hebron events

*1834*

Following the earthquake, the rebellion, and the brutal sack of Hebron, on 8 Elul 5594 the community leaders - R' Chaim Avraham (son of the Alter Rebbe) and R' Nachum (son of the Mitteler Rebbe) - wrote to the Tzemach Tzedek detailing the year's horrors. They begged him to publicize their suffering, noting that prices had tripled and they were borrowing food and money at interest to keep starving Jews alive.
