# Levin opens cheder for Anash children in Bezancourt

*1948*

On 18 Kislev 5709 Levin wrote to the Rebbe that he had now established himself fully as a melamed. In addition to his outreach work with Parisian families, he was teaching the small children of Anash families living in Bezancourt — laying the seeds of an Anash cheder for the postwar French community.
