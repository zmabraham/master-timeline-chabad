# Author leaves Chernigov for new Tomchei Tmimim branch in Kherson

*1916*

While studying under the Rad'tz, R' Dovid Tzvi Chen, in Chernigov, the author received instructions just before 19 Kislev 5677 directing him and four classmates to join a new Tomchei Tmimim branch opening in Kherson. The discipline among the talmidim was such that no one questioned the move — the author packed up immediately. R' Tzvi Gottlieb of Kovno served as rosh mesivta in Kherson; there was no mashpia yet. Local supporters, including R' Yisrael Tchertok and the Bezpalov brothers (sons of the rav of Poltava), housed and fed the students generously.
