# Reitchik sends detailed escape report and rescue updates to the Rebbe

*1940*

From Vilna, Reitchik catalogued his entire route to the Rebbe and reported on bochurim still stuck near borders in Oshmany, Lida and Sventzian, plus those rescued via Mendel Tenenbaum. He proposed sending an emissary back across the border to bring the remaining students and the chossid Chaim Meir Lis, and noted that the Vilna yeshiva had already drawn other bochurim attracted to Chassidus.
