# Sons of Tzemach Tzedek announce family unity pact

*1866*

After the Tzemach Tzedek's passing earlier that year, his sons issued a circular letter to chassidim describing their pact of peace and a weekly gathering in their father's birth-room. They explained that the surviving forty manuscript volumes — twenty in nigleh and twenty in chassidus — could not be divided, because the nigleh works were destined for printing. The Rebbe Maharash twice added a personal note saying he strongly wished all the writings to be printed and his father's wellsprings to spread outward, citing the Baal Shem Tov's famous letter.
