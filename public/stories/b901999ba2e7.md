# Tolodos Avraham Chaim biography published

*1998*

Issued in Brooklyn on 24 Teves 5758 (1998), the volume compiles the life story of the chossid R. Avraham Chaim Rosenbaum of Plescenice along with his correspondence with the Rebbe Rashab, R. Shmuel Dovber of Borisov, the mashpia R. Hendel Kugel and other senior chassidim. It also publishes his writings in halacha, Kabbalah, Chassidus, language and avodas Hashem, drawn from his archive in the Agudas Chassidei Chabad–Lubavitch library.
