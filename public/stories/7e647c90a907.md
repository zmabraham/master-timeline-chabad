# Death of Yetta Rosenbaum in New York

*1904*

In the course of 5664 Reb Avraham Chaim's wife Yetta lay ill for about four months before she died, and he expended over 1,000 dollars on her medical care. In the same year his diamond business lost nearly 2,000 dollars when merchandise from Germany failed to arrive, together amounting to roughly three-quarters of his savings in his old age.
