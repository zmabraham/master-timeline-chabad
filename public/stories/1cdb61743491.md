# Shearit Yehuda published in Vilna by MaharI"L's grandson

*1841*

In 5601 (1841), R' Ze'ev Wolf, grandson of MaharI"L (through his son R' Yeshaya), brought to press Shearit Yehuda — MaharI"L's collected rulings on melicha together with extensive responsa, glosses on his brother the Alter Rebbe's writings, and oral teachings transmitted in his name.
