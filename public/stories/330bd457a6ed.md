# Jewish Constitution of 1804 signed by the Tsar

*1804*

On December 9, 1804, the Tsar signed the comprehensive Jewish Constitution that governed Jewish life in the Empire for decades. Its provisions—including section 50 mandating triennial rabbinic elections, section 52 forbidding rabbis to collect funds beyond fixed salaries, and section 53 permitting separate congregations only under strict conditions—later became the legal basis for repeated investigations of the Chabad Rebbes.
