# Great fire in Liadi destroys Alter Rebbe's autograph Shulchan Aruch

*1810*

The fire consumed the Alter Rebbe's own handwritten Shulchan Aruch and other manuscripts that consequently never reached print. Among the lost works is the kuntres explaining all the details of the bill of sale for chametz, referenced in his general letter on the subject. The Tzemach Tzedek later searched for this teshuvah but could not locate it.
