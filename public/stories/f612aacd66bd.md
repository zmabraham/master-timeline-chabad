# Reb Itche der Masmid arrives in New York as Rebbe's emissary

*1933*

Celebrated for his diligence and piety, Reb Itche spent two years in the US teaching Chasidism and raising funds for the Rebbe's European work. The New York Chabad community borrowed money to gather $5,000 for his mission. He visited Boston and other cities, deeply impressing all who met him; the Rebbe later wanted to send him again but could not secure a visa.
