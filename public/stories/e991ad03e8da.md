# Purim children's campaign letter sent by Rebbe's secretariat

*1961*

At the beginning of Adar 5721 (1961), the Rebbe directed his secretariat to send a general letter to institutions, rabbis, yeshiva heads, teachers, and parents, urging organized efforts to ensure every child observes the mitzvos of Purim — especially mishloach manos and matanos l'evyonim — themselves (Igros Kodesh vol. 20 letter 7598).
