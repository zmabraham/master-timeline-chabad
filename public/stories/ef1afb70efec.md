# Khovansky transfers case to Mohilev provincial government

*1825*

Since Lubavitch lay in the Babinovichi district of Mohilev province, jurisdiction now passed there. The provincial authorities in turn sent the file to the Babinovichi peace court, which dispatched officers to Lubavitch and ordered parallel inquiries in Kapust, Orsha, Shklov and Mohilev to verify the Rebbe's account of how Ma'amad funds were collected.
