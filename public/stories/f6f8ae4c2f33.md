# Rebbe Rayatz directs registration of Otwock property under American Aguda

*1938*

Writing on 19 Tammuz 5698 (July 18, 1938), the Rebbe Rayatz personally pressed Rabbi Yisrael Jacobson to obtain governmental and Polish-consular ratification of Agudath Chabad's statute. He asked that the approved document arrive by the end of Menachem Av so the courtyard and building for Yeshivas Tomchei Temimim in Otwock could be purchased in time. The added clauses designated the Rebbe as Honorary President and recorded all real estate as Aguda property.
