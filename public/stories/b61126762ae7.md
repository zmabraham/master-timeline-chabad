# Frierdiker Rebbe spends 19 Kislev in Philadelphia

*1929*

On his cross-country American tour the Rebbe visited Philadelphia for Yud Tes Kislev. Among those drawn closer was the family of Michael Feder of Nevel, Vitebsk, who kashered their home. Their son Henry began putting on tefillin and briefly worked in the office handling the Rebbe's American visit.
