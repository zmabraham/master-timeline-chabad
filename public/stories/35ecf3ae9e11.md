# First record of Rogachov committee in Kollel Chabad ledger

*1873*

The 'Rogachov Vaad' served as the central committee receiving funds for Kollel Chabad in the diaspora. The earliest extant mention appears in a Kollel Chabad accounting ledger of 5633 noting 'sent from Rogachov.' The committee likely was established earlier, perhaps by the Tzemach Tzedek himself.
