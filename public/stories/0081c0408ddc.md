# Students send detailed letter on 23 Marcheshvan

*1945*

On the third day of parashat Chayei Sarah, 23 Marcheshvan 5706, the students wrote a fuller report to the Frierdiker Rebbe describing the telegram received via R. Y. Zuber on erev Yom Kippur instructing them to file visa applications. They expressed threefold joy: that this was the first news of his welfare in over three years, that it was the first telegram from him since their arrival in Shanghai, and most of all that he wished to bring them close to him in America.
