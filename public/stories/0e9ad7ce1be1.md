# Rebbe writes about R. Shmuel Abba Sneg

*1949*

In 5709 (1948/49), the Rebbe Rayatz wrote about R. Shmuel Abba Sneg to Israel's Chief Rabbi Herzog (Igros Kodesh vol. 10, letter 3499). The Rebbe noted Sneg's earlier role as chaplain in the Lithuanian army and his postwar role as av beis din for the Orthodox kehillos in the American zone of Germany, in connection with an offer he had received to serve as rabbi of Jaffa.
