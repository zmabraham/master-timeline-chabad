# Alter Rebbe yahrzeit seudah on 24 Tevet 5687 launches Likkutei Torah reprint

*1926*

On 24 Tevet 5687 the author convened a seudah at his home in honor of the Alter Rebbe's yahrzeit; after delivering a maamar, he spoke before bentching about the Alter Rebbe, Likkutei Torah, and the Warsaw yeshiva's offer. Donors pledged on the spot in amounts of $25 and $50, raising several hundred dollars and finally enabling the print run. Total printing costs eventually reached over $800.
