# Toldot Amudei HaChabad published in Königsberg

*1876*

In 5636 (1876), the writer Michael Levi Radkinson published Toldot Amudei HaChabad in Königsberg. The book gathered and reworked the existing biographical traditions of the Chabad Rebbes for a wider reading public. It served as a precursor to later, more rigorous works such as Beit Rebbi.
