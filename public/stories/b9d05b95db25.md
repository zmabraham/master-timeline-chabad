# Kletzk, to the 'Shulchan HaTorah' convention which was intended to resolve all the issues

*1795*

Kletzk, to the 'Shulchan HaTorah' convention which was intended to resolve all the halachic issues that had accumulated on their tables, where he met Rabbi Yosef Kalbo, who told him about the Alter Rebbe. In 5556 or 5557 (1795-1797) he returned to Vilna and served as rabbi for the Chassidic community in the city
