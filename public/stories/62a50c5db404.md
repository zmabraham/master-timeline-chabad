# Russian official Shvydkoy visits Levin to pitch loan-language compromise

*1994*

Levin refused firmly but gently to sign anything framed as conceding the books did not belong to Chabad. Shvydkoy described plans to allocate buildings at Chekhova 18 or Mokhovaya 6 for a special center housing the collection. Levin objected that participating in such a center would imply Chabad accepted the books were not theirs.
