# Telz Yeshiva reopens in Cleveland, Ohio

*1941*

Telz roshei yeshivah Katz and Bloch had traveled to the US in 1940 to explore relocation options, settling on Cleveland. The ten students who escaped Poland eastward and crossed to America through Australia became the founding cohort of the American Telz Yeshiva, which became a major center of American Orthodoxy.
