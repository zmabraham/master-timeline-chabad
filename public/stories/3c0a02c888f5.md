# Shabbos Chukas at the Kremlin memorial ceremony

*1991*

On Shabbos Chukas (10 Tammuz 5751), the delegation traveled to Marina Roshcha to be close to the Kremlin for a morning memorial ceremony. About 50 attended, including Mayor Popov, representatives of the Great Synagogue and the delegation, and clergy. The author had a long, emotional conversation with Rabbi Pinchas Goldschmidt about Chabad activity and the Chernobyl children's operation.
