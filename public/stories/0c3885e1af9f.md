# Warsaw branch of Yeshivas Tomchei Temimim opens

*1921*

The Rebbe called this 'days of light and days of joy.' He appointed an oversight board and instructed them to focus on ethics, G-d-fearing spirit, and character. By 1927 it had 157 students; by 1928, 250. It eventually relocated to Otwock and became the incubator for postwar Chabad rabbis.
