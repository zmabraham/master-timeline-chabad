# Rabbi Schnoor (first from left) alongside Rabbi Naftali Roth and Rabbi Binyamin

*1935*

Rabbi Schnoor (first from left) alongside Rabbi Naftali Roth and Rabbi Binyamin Zilberstrom. Rabbi Yaakov Schnoor 5695, 1935 – 3 Shevat 5786, January 21, 2026) was a Chabad activist in Jerusalem, founder and initiator of Kiryat Lubavitch in the Ramat Shlomo neighborhood of Jerusalem
