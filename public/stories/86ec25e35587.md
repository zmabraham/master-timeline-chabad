# MaharI"L co-signs psher ruling for Horodishtch community

*1796*

On 25 Tevet 5556 (January 5, 1796), MaharI"L of Yanovich signed a written compromise ruling for the community of Horodishtch, noting that 'all the above was done by law and by settlement, as my brother and master the Rabbi instructed me, with the consent of the entire community.' The document, preserved in the genizah, shows the Alter Rebbe routing communal halachic work through his trusted younger brother.
