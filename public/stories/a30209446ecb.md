# Lost manuscripts found and returned to Lubavitch

*1977*

The three crates of ancient Chabad manuscripts — Torah and correspondence of the Alter Rebbe and the five Chabad Rebbes who followed him — had been held by the American Embassy in Berlin before the US entered the war, then seized when the Germans confiscated embassy property. They were considered lost for decades, until summer 5737, when they were located and returned, as recorded in the 'Sifriyas Lubavitch' chapter 11.
