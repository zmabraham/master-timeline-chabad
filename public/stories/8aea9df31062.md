# War of 1812 destroys Rabbi Schneur's property in Lyady

*1812*

During Napoleon's 1812 invasion of Russia, the property of Rabbi Schneur Zalman of Liadi was destroyed by the enemy, with damages reaching 60,000 rubles according to his own account. He subsequently traveled from Kremenchug to Shklov collecting donations to cover these losses, and his associates continued the fundraising effort afterward. These collected funds, totaling 35,000 rubles, later became central evidence in the 1825 investigation against his son Rabbi DovBer.
