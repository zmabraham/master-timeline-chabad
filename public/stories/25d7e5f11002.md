# Levin hides from Red Army search in wall cavity

*1943*

During a summer 5703 sweep for army draft evaders, Levin hid in a wall cavity behind a curtain, reciting the maamar D"H Dirshu 5690 to himself. Soldiers broke the lock, searched the room, and one peered straight through the curtain crack but did not see him. A second search party was told 'we already checked here' and left — Levin and his wife celebrated their open miracle.
