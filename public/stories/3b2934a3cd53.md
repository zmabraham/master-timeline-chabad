# Underground Rosh Hashanah minyan in Kutaisi for 5692

*1931*

On Rosh Hashanah 5692 the students held a private minyan in the home of R. Yosef, brother of Chacham Yitzchak who had studied in Lubavitch. R. Eli Chaim the shochet led prayers with tears and dveikut. On motzaei Rosh Hashanah the maamar 'Hashem yechatu merivav' from 5689 was reviewed.
