# Tzemach Tzedek born in Liozna

*1789*

The third Chabad Rebbe, known as the Tzemach Tzedek after his magnum opus of responsa, was born on Erev Rosh Hashanah 5550. He was the grandson of the Alter Rebbe and would later become son-in-law of the Mitteler Rebbe. This birth date is used as the baseline for dating his earliest halachic activity.
