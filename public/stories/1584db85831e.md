# R' Shneur Zalman Slonim appointed rabbi in Yafo

*1900*

R' Shneur Zalman submitted to the Lublin Gaon's directive and moved to Yafo to serve the Chabad community. His first task was building a kosher mikveh for women in Neve Shalom, since the existing one was in dangerous and remote old Yafo. He also organized shechita, built a Chabad synagogue, and gave weekly chassidus discourses.
