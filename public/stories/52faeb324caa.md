# Tzemach Tzedek born on Erev Rosh Hashanah

*1789*

R. Menachem Mendel Schneersohn, later known as the Tzemach Tzedek, was born on Erev Rosh Hashanah 5550. On that very day his grandfather the Alter Rebbe began delivering the lengthy discourse 'Mashbiim Oto,' whose content forms the first three chapters of Tanya.
