# The philanthropist R' Yitzchak Mirilashvili, standing behind his father, the philanthropist

*1984*

The philanthropist R' Yitzchak Mirilashvili, standing behind his father, the philanthropist R' Michael Mirilashvili. Mr. Yitzchak Mirilashvili (born 5745, 1984) is a philanthropist close to and a great supporter of Chabad activities, who greatly assists the chareidi public in general and the Chassidus
