# Rebbe Rashab travels abroad for health reasons, early Kislev 5674

*1913*

Due to his weakened health, the Rebbe Rashab left for abroad around the week of Parshas Vayetzei, in the beginning of Kislev 5674, so that he was not in Lubavitch for 19 Kislev that year. Wealthy chassidim later traveled to be with him for Purim 5674. He did not return to Lubavitch until after Pesach 5674.
