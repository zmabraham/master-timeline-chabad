# Pereshel facsimile of Yosef of Leipnik's 1733 manuscript Haggadah

*1989*

In 5749 (1989), Tuvia Pereshel published in New York a facsimile of an illustrated manuscript Haggadah by Yosef ben David of Leipnik from 5493 (1733). The 48-page octavo edition included a bilingual Hebrew-English introduction by Pereshel. The work continued his annual series of facsimiles of rare Haggadot.
