# Wiener buys handwritten Tanya manuscript in Borisov

*1879*

In 5639 (1879), bibliophile Shmuel ben Yermiyahu Wiener acquired a handwritten Tanya manuscript in Borisov. The manuscript was missing its first page and conclusion, containing only up to chapter 37. Wiener's inscription on the inner cover documented the purchase. The manuscript was found in 1991 by Chabad emissaries in Leningrad's Asiatic Institute, in the Friedland collection.
