# Widow of R' Yaakov Halevi sells Fez mortgage to R' Avraham Toledano

*1723*

Acting with her late husband's interest, the widow used the kinyan-and-four-cubits-of-land form to convey the mortgage deed and all rights it carried. She swore she had not collected on it and would not transfer it again. Witnessed by Shmuel ibn Saadon and Mordechai Tzaba.
