# Fire destroys all buildings in Lubavitch courtyard

*1921*

In the early days of Nissan 5681 (April 1921), one year after the passing of the Rebbe Rashab and between his yahrzeit and that of the Tzemach Tzedek, fire destroyed all the buildings in the Rebbes' courtyard in Lubavitch. The fire consumed everything within half an hour, including the beis midrash with all its Torah scrolls, and spread to the market and many streets. When the Rebbe Rayatz visited in summer 5682, he was shocked by the desolate scene.
