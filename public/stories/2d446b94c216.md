# Arrest of Rabbi Schneur Zalman of Liadi

*1798*

In 1798, Rabbi Schneur Zalman of Liadi, the founder of Chabad, was arrested in czarist Russia for standing up for Judaism. This was the first in a series of imprisonments that Chabad Rebbes would endure under Russian oppression. The arrest became a foundational moment in Chabad history and a touchstone Yosef Yitzchak recalled while himself in prison.
