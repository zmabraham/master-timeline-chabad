# Zalmanov updates Rayatz on slow alumni response, plans newspaper notice

*1931*

On 6 Elul 5691, R' Shmuel Zalmanov updated the Rebbe Rayatz on the alumni project, noting that responses had so far been few. The hanhalla had nearly 300 alumni names on file, though not all addresses were precise, and planned to publish a notice in newspapers to reach more former students.
