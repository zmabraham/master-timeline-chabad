# Founding of Kfar Chabad vocational school

*1956*

In 5716, the Rebbe founded the Beis Sefer Lemelacha (vocational school) in Kfar Chabad, Israel. He appointed R' Shlomo Aharon Kazarnovsky as his personal emissary for the dedication ceremony, and Kazarnovsky subsequently served as director of the fund supporting the institution.
