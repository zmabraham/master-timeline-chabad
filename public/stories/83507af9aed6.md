# Nazi massacre of Lubavitch Jews

*1941*

After the Nazis conquered Lubavitch during World War II, around 5701 (1941), they found 630 Jews remaining—men, women, and children. They dug a mass grave and in a single day shot them all to death. By 5700 (1940), only about 120 Jewish families had remained in the town, as many had left when the courtyard and yeshiva closed.
