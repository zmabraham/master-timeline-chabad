# R' Shimon Glitzenstein enrolls at Yeshivat Torat Emet in Hebron

*1912*

At the end of summer 5672 (1912), R' Shimon Glitzenstein enrolled at Yeshivat Torat Emet in Hebron in its first year of operation. He replaced R' Alter Simchovitz, who had returned to Lubavitch over the course of that summer. Glitzenstein would later document the yeshiva's early years in writings published in Bitaon Chabad.
