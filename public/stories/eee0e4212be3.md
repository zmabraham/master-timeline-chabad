# for a person with certain medical issues, and the Rebbe said: 'If he needs a miracle - let him learn Rambam'

*1939*

for a person with certain medical issues, and the Rebbe said: 'If he needs a miracle - let him learn Rambam, for Rambam is the acronym of "Rabot Moftai b'Eretz Mitzrayim" (Many are My wonders in the land of Egypt)'
