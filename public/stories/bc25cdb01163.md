# Young Maharash buys Hebrew books from a Lubavitch bookseller

*1845*

According to a memoir of the Rebbe Rayatz, during the Tzemach Tzedek's monthly tests of his son and other cheder students, prizes were monetary, which Rabbi Shmuel saved together with a weekly ruble to buy seforim. In Elul 5605 (1845) a bookseller came to Lubavitch and the young Maharash requested 30 rubles from money his father held for him; his father gave him the sum plus 10 rubles as a gift, allowing him to buy a substantial collection of books.
