# R' Yehuda Eber's spring report on Warsaw yeshiva staff

*1931*

On 13 Iyar 5691 (30 April 1931) R' Yehuda Eber, sent to examine the Warsaw students, wrote a report to the Rebbe Rayatz evaluating each teacher and the chief mashgiach R' Yehoshua Berl. He praised R' David Teiblum's clear instruction, while observing that R' Yehoshua Berl, though a great scholar, was inclined to talmudic abstractions rather than the simple sense of the Gemara. The report set the stage for the Rebbe's later directive to reduce the Warsaw staff.
