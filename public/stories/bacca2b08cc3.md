# Levin obtains false Polish-refugee papers as Naftali Batt

*1943*

Summoned to a military medical exam, Levin slipped out before being seen and fled home without his papers. R' Waldman, a Polish Jew, supplied him with documents from a Polish refugee who had died in Siberia; when those wore out, Waldman brought new papers in the name Naftali Batt. Under that identity Levin lived hidden in Samarkand for several years, unable even to speak the Polish his papers claimed as his mother tongue.
