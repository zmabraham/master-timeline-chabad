# Rebbe Rayatz writes to father-in-law about R' Alter's mission

*1921*

The letter (Igrot Kodesh vol. 1, letter 95) reported that Alter remained in Minsk after Sukkot but was likely to travel on to R' Shraga Feivish, since the Warsaw founders had pleaded for an experienced mashpia and supervisor. The correspondence shows the Rebbe coordinating an international transfer of personnel under difficult conditions.
