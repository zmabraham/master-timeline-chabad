# Chabad students arrive in Kutaisi to establish underground yeshiva

*1931*

After traveling from Kiev to Baku and finding it unsuitable for study, the students continued to Kutaisi. They arrived shortly before Erev Tishah B'Av and were warmly welcomed by local Georgian Chabad chassidim, many of whom had previously studied at Tomchei Tmimim in Vitebsk, Nevel, or Lubavitch. The community helped fund their settlement so a yeshiva could be re-established in the city.
