# Rebbe describes acute fundraising difficulty in letter to R' Yehoshua Isaac Baruch

*1941*

The letter (Igros V:1337) noted that he had no fixed source of funds and that the travel costs already sent had required enormous effort to scrape together as gemach loans. He requested that Kovno and Riga representatives keep package costs as low as possible and send detailed accountings of past disbursements.
