# Baal Shem Tov ascends to heaven on Rosh HaShanah and meets Messiah

*1746*

According to the historic letter the Baal Shem Tov sent to his brother, using kabbalistic secrets his soul rose to heaven. He asked the Messiah, 'When will you come?' The answer: 'In the time when your teachings will become public and revealed in the world.' This principle later anchored the Chabad mission of spreading chasidic teachings to the world.
