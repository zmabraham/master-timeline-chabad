# Passing of R' Baruch, father of the Alter Rebbe

*1792*

Rabbi Baruch, father of Rabbi Schneur Zalman of Liadi (the Alter Rebbe), died in 5552 (1791/92). His name was carried by his great-grandson Baruch Shalom, eldest son of the Tzemach Tzedek, in line with the Ashkenazi practice of naming a child after a deceased relative.
