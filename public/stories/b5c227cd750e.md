# Rabbi Mordechai Cheifetz dispatched to US as Rebbe's emissary

*1938*

Cheifetz led chasidic gatherings lasting almost till dawn at New York yeshivas such as Torah Vodaath. Avraham Hecht recalled, "He could melt a stone. He melted me." Cheifetz inspired Hecht and five friends to travel to Otwock to study. Returning to Poland, Cheifetz spent ten days with the ailing Rebbe at Shavuot, reporting on each US Chasid by name.
