# Loginov and Markov submit summary report on Mitteler Rebbe case

*1825*

On November 10, 1825, the investigators Loginov and Markov submitted their final report to Governor-General Khovansky summarizing every interrogation conducted in Vitebsk, the search at Lubavitch by officer Korbitsky, and every document supplied by the informer Kisin or seized from the Rebbe. The report addressed the disputed distribution of 35,000 rubles, the Rebbe's role as arbiter, his correspondence about chassidic affairs, and the synagogue-building takanah. It was received by Khovansky's office the following day and forwarded to the Mogilev gubernia administration a week later.
