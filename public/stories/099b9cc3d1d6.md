# Kolel Chabad holds lottery for two Montefiore-fund beneficiaries

*1902*

On 19 Sivan 5662 (June 24, 1902), the trustees of Kolel Chabad notified the Vaad Klalli that a lottery — held before them and other notables — had selected R' David Gilel and R' Noach son of R' Chaim Ber to enjoy that year's distribution from Sir Moshe Montefiore's estate. Names of winners were sent to the London executors, who paid each recipient directly.
