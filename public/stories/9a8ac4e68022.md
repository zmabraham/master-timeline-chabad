# Secret farbrengen with R' Shlomo Zalman Havlin before draft

*1915*

When Shmuel Kadish Bertanovsky had to leave Tomchei Tmimim to present himself for conscription, the author and a few others arranged a covert gathering. Government liquor shops were shut by wartime decree, so Moshe Gurary paid two roubles for a single bottle of kummel. R' Havlin spoke of how Chabad chassidus brings the soul's innermost essence into revealed expression through the intellect and emotions, and the farbrengen lasted from late Thursday night until six in the morning.
