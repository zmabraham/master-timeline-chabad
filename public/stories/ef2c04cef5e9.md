*1953*

Rabbi Luria. Rabbi Chaim Nachum Yonatan Luria (born 16 Menachem Av 5713, 1953) is one of the members of the first group of emissaries to the Holy Land, currently residing in Kfar Chabad and managing one of the local etrog orchards
