# Rebbe travels to Ludmir with Rotstein as secretary

*1932*

On 23 Menachem Av 5692, the Frierdiker Rebbe set out from Druzgenik to visit chassidim in Ludmir. In his diary the Rebbe noted that 'the one traveling with me as secretary is the tamim R. Moshe Leib Rotstein,' formalizing the secretarial role.
