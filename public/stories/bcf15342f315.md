# Meeting of Anash leaders from Poland, Lithuania and Latvia at Rashag's home

*1930*

The future Rebbe (Ramash) participated in the meeting. The participants resolved to establish a permanent inter-country committee to support the Rebbe's beis chayeinu, to form an Agudat Chassidim across European countries, and formally to ask the Rebbe to settle in Poland. The minutes were later printed in a 2005 tshura by the Stambler family.
