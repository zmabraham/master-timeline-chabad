# Death of R' Avraham Mertzbacher, owner of major Munich book collection

*1885*

Mertzbacher had built his renowned bibliophile collection from 1866-1885 through Rabbi Raphael Nathan Rabinowitz, who used the books for his Dikdukei Sofrim project. After his death, his son Dr. Isaac Mertzbacher sought to sell the collection, which would later attract Rebbe Rashab's interest as a potential Jewish library acquisition.
