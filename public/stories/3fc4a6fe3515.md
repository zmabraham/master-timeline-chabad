# Chabad delegation tries to reach Yeltsin at the White House during coup

*1991*

With rumors that Yeltsin was gaining strength, the delegation made its way through thousands of demonstrators and police surrounding the Russian White House to one of Yeltsin's aides — the same official who had hosted them on 20 Kislev when Yeltsin promised to return the books at the first opportunity. The aide took the details to Yeltsin's office, but Yeltsin had just gone into an emergency session as the coup collapsed and could not receive the delegation.
