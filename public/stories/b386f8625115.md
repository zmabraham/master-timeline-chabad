# Frierdiker Rebbe transfers branches to Lithuania on 14 Tevet 5700

*1939*

The letter (Vol. 5, no. 1136) recounted great effort and expense to evacuate portions of the central yeshiva in Otwock and the branches in Warsaw, Lodz, Kaluszyn and Chelm to Lithuania. A large portion of students nonetheless remained trapped in Poland after the German invasion.
