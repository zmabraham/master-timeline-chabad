# Hilchos Talmud Torah first printed

*1794*

Unlike most of the other kuntreisim, which mainly summarize earlier poskim, Hilchos Talmud Torah contains many original rulings the Alter Rebbe derived from Talmudic sugyos in his Kuntres Acharon. Its first printing in 5554 provides one of the few firm dating anchors for the middle period of his halachic writing. The work was composed in the same era as the other Hilchos Hatzrichos compilations of 5545-5550.
