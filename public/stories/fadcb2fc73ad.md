# Berdichev underground yeshiva raided, 12 students and Rosh Yeshiva arrested

*1938*

Following the Berdichev arrests, the remaining students in Krivoy Rog fled to Kutaisi in Georgia. Around the same time the Kursk yeshiva was also shut down and its students dispersed; several young learners including R' Zalman Levin and R' Dovber Kiubman made their way to Kharkov, where Israel Yehuda and Michoel Teitelbaum sheltered and taught them.
