# HaChavatzelet announces R' DovBer Efrat's rabbinic appointment

*1893*

On 9 Tammuz 1893 (ט' תמוז תרנ"ג), the newspaper HaChavatzelet reported that R' DovBer Efrat, formerly one of the rabbis of Moscow, had been chosen as the new rabbi of the Ashkenazi community in Hebron. The notice described him as a 'distinguished guest' passing through Jerusalem.
