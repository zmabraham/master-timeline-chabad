# Manuscript volume sent to Chabad library for review

*1961*

R. Shmuel Ber Leikin sent a manuscript volume to the Chabad library asking what it contained. On 27 Nisan 5721 Lieberman replied that the volume held copies of chassidic discourses plus three original pages: two by the Tzemach Tzedek and one by the Rebbe Maharash — a letter to R. Chaim Yaakov Widrewitz. The original three pages later disappeared from the volume, and locating them became a decades-long search.
