# Maharayatz issues public letter endorsing Kollel Chabad

*1929*

He praised the kollel's leadership, administration, and orderly procedures, and described the breadth of its work in supporting Torah scholars and the yishuv of Eretz Yisrael. The letter was meant to encourage donors and to recommit the worldwide Chabad community to the kollel's mission. It accompanied his decision in principle to accept the formal nesius of the kollel.
