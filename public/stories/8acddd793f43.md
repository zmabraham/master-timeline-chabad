# Greenglass reports to Rebbe on students trapped in Poland

*1940*

Greenglass relayed letters from Yechezkel Shpiorvach of Kombin and Yitzchak Stulman of Bichawe, both of whom asked the Rebbe to arouse heavenly mercy on their behalf as they struggled to cross the closed Polish-Lithuanian border. His report was part of the steady stream of intelligence the Rebbe was receiving about scattered Tomchei Temimim students.
