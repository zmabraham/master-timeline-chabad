# Histalkus of Rashab in Rostov

*1920*

On 2 Nissan 5680, Rebbe Shalom DovBer Schneersohn passed away in Rostov-on-Don. His passing transferred leadership of Chabad to his only son, Rebbe Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn (Rayatz). At the time of his passing, Rashab held funds entrusted by R' Asher of Nikolayev for his unmarried daughter's dowry.
