# R. Yechiel Meir Blumenfeld murdered in Warsaw Ghetto

*1942*

Born in 1893, Blumenfeld had returned to Warsaw after wartime exile in Russia and helped establish Tomchei Tmimim there, while serving as a Mizrachi rav at Tachkemoni and publishing his sefer Kol HaMitzvah. His death on Yom Kippur 1942 was part of the Nazi destruction of the Warsaw Jewish community.
