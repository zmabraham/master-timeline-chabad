# Yitzchak Sharion arrives in Jerusalem

*1889*

Upon his arrival in Jerusalem in 5649, Sharion noted that Kollel Chabad had two trustees, the senior of whom hailed from Kherson in southern Russia and was known as 'the Khersoner.' Sharion's memoirs, published in Jerusalem in 1943, became a key source for the kollel's mid-19th-century history.
