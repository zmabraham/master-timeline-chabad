# Rebbe Rayatz directs Anash that he can no longer receive visitors

*1944*

On 21 Marcheshvan 5705 the Rebbe Rayatz wrote a general directive (printed in Igrot Kodesh vol. 8, letter 2,535) informing Chassidim that his doctors had ordered him to cease in-person audiences. He explained that he was suffering chest pain requiring full rest, yet noted that the demands of spreading Torah, fear of Heaven, and proper chinuch required tenfold more strength than before.
