# Latvia signs trade agreement with Soviet Union

*1927*

On June 2, 1927, Latvia and the Soviet Union signed a trade deal that was economically vital to Latvia but politically important to the diplomatically isolated Russians. Internal Latvian opposition meant the deal had to be ratified by the Saeima, where Chabad Chasid Mordechai Dubin held two seats. Dubin would later leverage his swing vote to pressure Russia into releasing the Rebbe.
