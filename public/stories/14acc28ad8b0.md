# Israel Yehuda visits R' Levi Yitzchak Schneerson in Dnepropetrovsk

*1937*

Traveling to Dnepropetrovsk to raise funds for the underground yeshiva, Israel Yehuda came to the home of Rabbi Levi Yitzchak Schneerson, the future Lubavitcher Rebbe's father. The Rebbetzin Chana directed him to the synagogue, where he found Rabbi Levi Yitzchak sitting and praying.
