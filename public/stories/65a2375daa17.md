# Tzemach Tzedek's Shu"t Even Ha'ezer Volume 1 printed in Vilna

*1871*

The volume bore the notation that it was brought to press by the Tzemach Tzedek's sons and grandsons, who received the income from sales. The publication was managed by R. Refael Mordechai Schneersohn and R. Meshulam Shilem Reich. After printing, copies were distributed among descendants for sale.
