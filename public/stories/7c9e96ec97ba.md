# Frierdiker Rebbe's earlier American visit on eve of Wall Street crash

*1929*

Recalling his earlier American visit during the 5699 yechidus, the Frierdiker Rebbe explained that his previous trip had been for the sake of general Jewish life and that he succeeded in that goal, with thanks to G-d. He raised several hundred thousand dollars to aid the Jews of Russia, on the very eve of the Wall Street stock market collapse, with his very presence stirring American Jewry to support his far-reaching activities. His deeper aim, however, had been spiritual: to awaken a feeling of teshuvah in the hearts of American Jewry.
