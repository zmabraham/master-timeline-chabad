# Levi Yitzchak and Chana Schneersohn write to Rachel Schneersohn after Rebbe's wedding

*1929*

Rabbi Levi Yitzchak Schneersohn and Rebbetzin Chana wrote from Dnepropetrovsk (Yekaterinoslav) in Adar I 5689, thanking Rachel and Zelda for their congratulations on the wedding of their son the Rebbe. Levi Yitzchak explained that they had been unable to attend the wedding for various reasons and described arranging financial support to be sent to Rachel through Rabbi Lakshin.
