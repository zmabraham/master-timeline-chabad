# R' Yisrael Jacobson arrives in Riga

*1925*

On Rosh Chodesh Menachem Av 5685 (July 22, 1925), R' Yisrael Jacobson reached Riga as part of his journey out of Soviet Russia toward the United States. He was held there awaiting an American entry visa, and during his stay delivered chassidic discourses in the Bazaar Berg synagogue.
