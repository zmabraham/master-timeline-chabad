# Library building adjacent to 770 purchased

*1967*

By 5727 (1967) the Rebbe's working library, built up since 1941 through emissaries around the world, had outgrown its space. The building next to 770 Eastern Parkway was purchased to house it. From then until 1985 two parallel libraries existed at the Chabad headquarters — the Rayatz's collection in 770 and the Rebbe's collection next door.
