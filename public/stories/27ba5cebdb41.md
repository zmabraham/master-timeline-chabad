# Rabbi Uri Ben Shachar. Rabbi Uri Ben Shachar, 8 Av 5686 - 17 E

*1926*

Rabbi Uri Ben Shachar. Rabbi Uri Ben Shachar, 8 Av 5686 - 17 Elul 5767, 1926-2007) was a chief activist in the Ohelei Yosef Yitzchak network in the Holy Land, principal of the Chabad school in Jaffa, chairman of the religious council in Kfar Chabad, and head of the Tiferet Zekenim kollel
