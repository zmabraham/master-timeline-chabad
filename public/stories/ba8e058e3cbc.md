# Alter Rebbe passes away on 24 Tevet

*1813*

The founder of Chabad Chassidus passed away on 24 Tevet 5573, ending the period during which his grandson the Tzemach Tzedek studied directly under him. After this date, the Alter Rebbe's brother Maharil of Yanovitch became the principal halachic respondent in the Chabad community, while the young Tzemach Tzedek continued his studies.
