# Birth of Rebbetzin Nechama Dina Schneersohn in Kishinev

*1881*

Nechama Dina was the daughter of Rabbi Avraham Schneersohn, a second cousin of the Rebbe Rashab. Her birth date corresponds to 17 Tishrei 5642. She would later marry Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn (the Rayatz) and become rebbetzin of the sixth Lubavitcher Rebbe.
