# R. Shalom DovBer Levin briefs Prof. Yirmiyahu Branover on the missing arrest file

*1992*

At the beginning of Tishrei 5753 (October 1992), Prof. Yirmiyahu Branover visited New York. R. Shalom DovBer Levin met with him and laid out his theory that the Khovansky-era arrest file of the Mitteler Rebbe could finally be located through the Shamir organization's newly authorized archive access in Russia. Levin then wrote a detailed memorandum identifying which provincial archives — particularly Minsk — should be searched.
