# Shulchan Aruch HaRav (Hilchot Pesach) printed in Shklov

*1814*

This volume bore an authentic Kapust rabbinical censor's stamp with the signature of Rabbi Nachum Yaffe of Kapust beside it. The genuine stamp would later help historians distinguish authentic Russian-Jewish printings from the wave of forgeries that followed the 1837 ban on Hebrew presses.
