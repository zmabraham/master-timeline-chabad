# Rabbi Aharonov flies to Israel for Maxwell's funeral on Rebbe's instruction

*1991*

With no direct Friday-morning flight available, the delegation flew via Europe hoping to reach the Holy Land before Shabbat or to continue from London after Shabbat. The three men had been among Maxwell's closest rabbinic contacts and the last to speak with him before he died, and were expected to take part in the Sunday funeral and possibly to deliver remarks.
