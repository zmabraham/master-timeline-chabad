# Rashab exempted from army draft

*1878*

In 1878, at the age of eighteen, Rabbi Shalom Dovber (the Rashab) was examined for the Russian army draft. The medical examiner discovered he had tuberculosis, leading to his exemption from military service. Professor Zacharov told the Rashab's younger brother Mendel that the Rashab had just two months to live, a prognosis that proved dramatically incorrect as he lived for another forty-two years.
