# Tomchei Tmimim Lubavitch Łódź founded

*1929*

According to the letterhead of the yeshiva, the Łódź branch of Tomchei Tmimim Lubavitch was founded on 17 Elul 5689 (Sept 22, 1929) at Paludniowa 25. R. Zalman Schneersohn served as chairman and his son R. Dovber Yitzchok as director. The yeshiva initially operated with two divisions and grew steadily under the leadership of Lubavitcher chassidim in Łódź.
