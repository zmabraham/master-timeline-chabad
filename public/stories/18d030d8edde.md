# Charity assessment in Rohatchov signed by Alter Rebbe

*1810*

In the winter of 5570 (1810) a tzedakah assessment for the town of Rohatchov was issued, bearing the signature of the Alter Rebbe. R' Shmuel, later the maggid of Hebron, appears on it as 'mo"tz' (moreh tzedek), reflecting his earlier rabbinic role in that town.
