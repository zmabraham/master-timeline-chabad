# Wedding of Sheina daughter of the Rebbe Rayatz

*1932*

On Tuesday 10 Sivan 5692, Sheina daughter of the Rebbe Rayatz married her cousin R' Menachem Mendel HaKohen Horenstein, son of R' Moshe HaKohen Horenstein who had been a son-in-law of the Rebbe Maharash. Their tnaim had been written several weeks earlier on Lag B'Omer of that year, in the Rebbe Rayatz's own hand. Sheina and her husband would later be murdered in the Holocaust, Hashem yikom damam.
