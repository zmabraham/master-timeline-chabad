# Author's birth in Zhuravichi

*1895*

Rabbi Yisrael Jacobson was born on 3 Kislev 5656 (corresponding to 1895) in the town of Zhuravichi to his father Reb Yaakov Leib. He descended from four generations of shochtim in Zhuravichi. The family traced its lineage to the Shlah HaKadosh through nine generations.
