# HaChavatzelet reports Montefiore estate distribution in Hebron

*1887*

After Moshe Montefiore's death at 101, his estate's trustees continued to send funds to the Holy Land. The 15 Av 5647 (1887) issue of HaChavatzelet described how Hebron's portion was split between the Sephardic and Ashkenazi communities by population, with a three-man Ashkenazi committee dividing the money at 40 piasters per soul.
