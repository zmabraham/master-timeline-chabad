# Rebbe's letter recommending move of Tel Aviv yeshiva to Lod

*1949*

Mashpia R' Shmuel Chaim Kesselman had suggested moving the yeshiva from noisy Tel Aviv to the quieter Lod campus near the railway station. On the seventh night of Chanukah the Ramash replied (Igrot Kodesh vol. 4, letter 842) strongly endorsing the move, citing the Rebbe Rayatz's praise of the calm environment of Lubavitch over big-city yeshivot, while recommending that one class remain in Tel Aviv as a feeder.
