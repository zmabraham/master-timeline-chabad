# Rebbe Rayatz departs Riga for Stockholm en route to America

*1940*

After two months in Riga coordinating the rescue of his yeshiva students from Poland and Soviet-held territory, the Rebbe Rayatz left for Stockholm at the end of Adar I 5700. From Stockholm he continued onward to the United States, where he would arrive that spring and re-establish the center of Chabad.
