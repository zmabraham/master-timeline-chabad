# Volume 13 of Tzemach Tzedek responsa bound

*1831*

Manuscript 1019 (volume 13) is a survivor of the early numbered series. It contains short summaries of responsa written in earlier volumes, citing them as chelek 1, 2, 3, 4, etc. Although undated internally, the responsa it digests can be matched to known dates, placing its compilation around 5590–5591.
