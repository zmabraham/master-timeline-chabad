# Lilienthal requests permission to write the Tzemach Tzedek officially

*1841*

On 17 March 1841 (7 Nissan 5601), six days after his initial letter, Lilienthal again wrote to the Education Ministry. He asked for permission to address Rabbi Menachem Mendel of Lubavitch in a formal capacity to learn his position on the proposed reforms. The official Dukst-Dushinsky replied that Lilienthal could write only as a private individual, not officially.
