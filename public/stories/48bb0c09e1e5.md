# R. Avraham Chaim writes a private will for his son Yaakov

*1911*

In ten clauses R. Avraham Chaim instructed Yaakov to bury him in his New York chevra's plot — warning him not to be swayed by the New Haven offer — and forbade any public hesped or newspaper notice. He detailed his prepared linen tachrichim from Germany, his Shabbos and weekday tallesim (including one inherited from the Tzemach Tzedek that he was afraid to wear in the next world), and asked that the bottom of the casket be broken so his body would touch the earth, per the Zohar in Parshas Vayechi and Terumah. He also charged Yaakov to forward 1,050 shekels of his estate as tzedakah to the Rebbe Rashab through his brother R. Z., to say kaddish, daven before the amud, and learn mishnayos throughout the year.
