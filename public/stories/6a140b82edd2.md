# First interrogation of Rabbi DovBer in Vitebsk

*1825*

On 7 October 1825 (7 Marcheshvan 5586), Rabbi DovBer of Lubavitch underwent his first official interrogation in Vitebsk in connection with Simcha Keysin's denunciations. The investigators questioned him about the collection and use of Maamad funds, the relations between chassidim and mitnagdim, and the authenticity of the documents Keysin had submitted as evidence.
