# Latvia signs trade agreement with the Soviet Union

*1927*

Though Latvia needed the deal more than the Soviets, the Bolsheviks wanted it as a step in peeling the Baltic states away from the West. Internal Latvian opposition meant the trade agreement had to be ratified by the Saeima. Mordechai Dubin's swing vote later became leverage to pressure Moscow into releasing the Rebbe and granting him an exit visa.
