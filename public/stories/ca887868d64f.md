# Rebbe writes to Dalhinov chassidim

*1929*

After the Dalhinov chassidim wrote to the Rebbe through their rabbi R. Gedalia Wilensky in late winter 5689, the Rebbe replied on 28 Nissan 5689 (Igros Kodesh vol. 16, letter 5186), blessing the community and their families with abundant life and material and spiritual blessing.
