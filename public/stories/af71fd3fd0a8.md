# Polish translation of the Haggadah by Fabius Strauch printed in Warsaw

*1881*

Around 5641 (1881), Fabius Strauch's translation of the Haggadah into Polish was issued in Warsaw. The 96-page volume made the Seder ritual accessible to Polish-speaking Jews who had become more integrated with the local language. It represented part of the broader 19th-century movement to render Jewish liturgical texts into local European languages.
