# Gershon Chanowitz and five students depart Vilna

*1941*

Writing to R. Moshe Leib Rotshtein in Chicago, Rashag also noted that Shmuel and Fruma Chanowitz cabled that they were leaving Vilna that week and needed $14 each for the leg from Vladivostok to Tokyo. The remaining students were expected to depart by the end of the month.
