# Rebbe Rayatz writes Raskin on dispatching mashpiim

*1924*

The Rebbe Rayatz outlined a plan to assign select chassidim as shadarim who would travel a regular circuit through Poland and Lithuania to strengthen Chassidic life. Among those named was R. Shimon Noach Nimoitin, who would be deployed wherever proved most useful. The letter is preserved as Igros Kodesh vol. 14, no. 5197.
