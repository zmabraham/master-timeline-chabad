# Krinsky tells Levin a Russia delegation is being assembled

*1987*

Rabbi Yehuda Krinsky, the Rebbe's secretary, told Rabbi Shalom DovBer Levin that within several weeks a delegation would travel to Moscow. It was to include Rabbi Baruch Shlomo Kunin (head shliach in California), Rabbi Nissan Mindel (the Rebbe's English-language secretary), and Rabbi Levin himself.
