# Alter Rebbe releases Tanya for copying and distribution

*1792*

In 5552 (1792) R' Schneur Zalman released the Likutei Amarim (Tanya) for copying and dissemination. In its 'Introduction of the Compiler' he explained that the work collected his answers to the questions chassidim brought him in private audience, since 'time no longer permits' him to address each one individually — directly linking the Tanya's publication to the Liozna takanos limiting yechidus.
