# Boys' division opened for Rostov children

*1909*

On 7 Tevet 5670 (December 1909), the Rebbe Rashab approved opening a new younger department in Lubavitch, primarily for a group of children from Rostov aged 11-12. This addressed Rostov chassidim's request that their children be educated in Lubavitch, with a special melamed assigned. The Rashab saw this as building Jewish education in Rostov as well.
