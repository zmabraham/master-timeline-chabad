# Rayatz letter mentioning Mohar'mtz Landau

*1935*

In a letter of 3 Sivan 5695 (June 1935), Rayatz addressed an elder relative as 'sh'ber bsari' (my flesh-relation), with the surname Landau noted at the top. Chabad historian Rabbi Levin conjectures this is Moshe Tzvi Landau, son of Dan Landau (a son-in-law of Rabbi Baruch Shalom), illustrating how scattered branches of the Tzemach Tzedek's descendants were tracked through Rayatz's correspondence.
