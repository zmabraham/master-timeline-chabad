# Rashab writes RA"Ch directing the organization of NY anash

*1902*

Responding to RA"Ch's report on the life of anash in New York, the Rebbe Rashab on 24 Adar I 5662 (Igrot Kodesh vol. 1, letter 114) acknowledged a $100 contribution and urged the immigrants to organize. He directed them to gather at least three times weekly — Thursday night, Shabbos, and motzaei Shabbos — for fixed study of Chassidus, and to make 'tikkunim' in the conduct of public prayer and mitzvah observance in their new setting.
