# Mission views original Tzemach Tzedek portrait at Ginzburg home

*1991*

On Isru Chag of Pesach 5751, Rabbi Levin visited the Moscow home of Yehuda Leib Ginzburg, a descendant of the Admor Maharshaz of Kopust and thus of the Tzemach Tzedek. On the wall hung a vivid color portrait of the Tzemach Tzedek that appeared at least a century old and clearly predated the standard image. Nearby stood a cabinet of bichlach manuscripts recognizable by their bindings as matching items in the Chabad library, and Levin proposed that the portrait too belonged in the Chabad library museum.
