# Senate rejects most of Khovansky's proposed reforms on Jewish status

*1834*

In the winter of 1834 (5594), after years of delay, the Senate responded to Governor Khovansky's policy proposals that had been attached to his 1827 appeal in the Mittler Rebbe's case. The Senate decided to reject most of his suggestions concerning the status and rights of rabbis and Jewish communities, accepting only a few minor points, while informing him that a comprehensive new statute on Jews was already being prepared.
