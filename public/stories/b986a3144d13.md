# R' Mordechai Banet writes recommendation for R' Yehuda Asad

*1826*

Dated 16 Sivan 5586, the letter from the elderly R' Mordechai Banet of Nikolsburg praises R' Yehuda Asad's diligence and mastery of halacha, urging R' Aharon to strengthen his standing in the community. The letter was part of the process that led to R' Asad's appointment as dayan.
