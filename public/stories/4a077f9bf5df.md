# Mayor Popov writes Rebbe; Gorbachev orders Schneersohn books returned

*1991*

On 22 Tevet R' Kogan brought back Mayor Popov's letter to the Rebbe, thanking him for help with Chernobyl children and Jewish education in Moscow and signaling the return of the Polyakov synagogue to Chabad. That evening Veronika called to report that Gorbachev's office had dispatched a government message to Gubinko ordering the Schneersohn library returned. The delegation broke into dance and l'chaim while R' Yehuda Leib Groner was still on the second telephone line.
