# R' Azriel Zelig Slonim sent as Kollel Chabad emissary to America

*1924*

Three administrators—R' Yisrael Asher Liba, R' Mordechai Kazarnovsky, and R' Moshe Ornstein—signed the agreement preserved in the kollel's letter ledger. The mission reflected the post-war effort to rebuild Kollel Chabad's finances after Russian funding had collapsed. Shadar missions to America became increasingly important during the interwar period.
