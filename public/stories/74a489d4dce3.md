# R. Meir Blizinski marries on 1 Nisan

*1926*

In a letter dated 13 Shevat 5737 to his grandson R. S. Heber, Blizinski recalled marrying on Rosh Chodesh Nisan 5686 at age nineteen. Soon after, he moved to Otwock and resolved to establish a yeshiva there, which became the first Tomchei Tmimim branch outside Warsaw.
