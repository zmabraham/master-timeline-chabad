# Tomchei Temimim shiur students relocated to Oryol

*1918*

As the financial and security situation in Lubavitch deteriorated under the new Bolshevik regime, the yeshiva's junior shiur students were sent to Oryol. There they were hosted by the shochet R' Yaakov Pantelyev and supported by the wealthy chassid Mr. Shmulevitch. Three older bochurim — Shmuel Chaim Shechtman of Zlobin, Abba of Bolhakov, and apparently R' Yisrael Zuber — accompanied them.
