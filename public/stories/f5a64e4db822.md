# Rebbe Rayatz leaves Riga for Warsaw to visit yeshivos

*1931*

As recounted in his letter of 5 Menachem Av 5691, the Rebbe Rayatz departed Riga on 9 Tammuz to travel to Warsaw and inspect the three yeshivos in Warsaw, Vilna and Łódź under his administration. During his time in Warsaw in mid-Tammuz, students from the Łódź branch traveled to meet him and were received in yechidus.
