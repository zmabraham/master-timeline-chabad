# Sasonkin writes from Rostov to Axelrod in Zembin

*1921*

On 13 Sivan 5681, writing from Rostov-on-Don, Rebbe Rayatz's secretary Nochum Shmaryahu Sasonkin wrote to the recently married Axelrod in Zembin. He acknowledged a letter from Axelrod's father in America and directed Axelrod to urge his father to mobilize donations to Tomchei Temimim through newspaper appeals. He listed contacts in Vienna, Iași (Romania), Warsaw, and Riga who could relay funds, noting that foreign currency would be worth two thousand times more in their home country.
