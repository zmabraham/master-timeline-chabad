# Adler-Rebbe Rayatz meeting on JDC support

*1929*

Adler, a traditional Jew educated in Orthodox schools, was sympathetic to the Rebbe's mission. In his internal JDC memo, Adler noted the difficulties of Russian Jewish life and was open to legally transferring funds. At a second meeting a month later, he assured the Rebbe the JDC would help significantly and secretly — a commitment that was ultimately not honored.
