# Levin family departs Samarkand on echelon no. 3

*1946*

After receiving their OVIR exit permits, the Levins were assigned to echelon no. 3, departing Samarkand on Monday 4 Sivan 5706 / June 3, 1946. They traveled with many Polish-refugee families rather than risk solo train travel, gambling on a chance — however slim — to escape Russia and reach the Rebbe.
