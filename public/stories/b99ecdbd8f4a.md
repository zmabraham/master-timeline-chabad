# Gabbaim of old Vilna beis midrash decide to oppress Chassidim

*1797*

On the second day of Chol HaMoed Sukkos 5558, one day before the Vilna Gaon's passing, the gabbaim of the Old Kloiz met to address what they viewed as the Chassidic deviation in prayer. They resolved to harass the 'Karliner' faction, deny them honors and aliyos, and force them to use the standard nusach without raising their voices. This formalized the institutional opposition in Vilna.
