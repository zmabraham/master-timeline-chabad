# Mantua Pragmatica sumptuary regulations renewed

*1722*

Mantua's community ordinances, known as the Pragmatica, restricted royal dress, jewelry, and dancing so that Jews would not stand out before the gentile population. On 29 Cheshvan 5483 (November 1722) the regulations were renewed for six years, from Rosh Chodesh Adar Sheni 5483 through Erev Rosh Chodesh Adar 5489, signed by Rabbi David Pinzi and the scribe R. Shimshon Moron.
