# Kiddush in Zhlobin marking first anniversary of Rayatz's liberation

*1928*

On 12 Tammuz 5688 (June 30, 1928), the first anniversary of the Frierdiker Rebbe's liberation, the Shusterman family hosted a kiddush in their Zhlobin home. The chassid R. Tzemach Horowitz, then serving as a mashpia in Zhlobin, participated. The author recalled the event still vividly decades later.
