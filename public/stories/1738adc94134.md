# Birth of R. Avraham Chaim Rosenbaum in Pleshenitz

*1840*

R. Avraham Chaim Rosenbaum was born around 5600 (1840) in Pleshenitz, Belarus. As a young married man he was already studying in the court of the Tzemach Tzedek, and later became a devoted chassid of the Rebbe Maharash. He earned a living as a businessman while maintaining an active Torah correspondence with leading rabbis of his generation.
