# Rebbe Rashab rebukes Rashaz Slonim for overpaying for a manuscript book

*1890*

After receiving a manuscript book sent via Rashaz's father, Rebbe Rashab expressed astonishment at the price paid (Igros #39). He warned that buying at any price would not only exhaust all means but also prevent assembling the truly essential manuscripts. He reaffirmed his core principle: manuscripts are the very life of his soul, yet acquisition must remain within limits.
