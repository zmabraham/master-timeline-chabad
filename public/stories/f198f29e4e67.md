# Rebbe Rayatz instructs R' Shimon Hauzman to prepare Hebron quarters

*1911*

In Tammuz 5671, as the decision was reached to send students from Lubavitch to Hebron, the Rebbe Rayatz wrote to R' Shimon Hauzman, who managed the Hebron building, asking him to prepare five rooms in the property — three on the upper floor for the mashpia and yeshiva office, and two on the lower floor for the visiting students. A parallel letter went to R' Yitzchak Chen, who had settled in Hebron that year.
