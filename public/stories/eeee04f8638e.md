# R. Yehuda Eber appointed director of Tomchei Temimim Warsaw

*1933*

After R. Yehuda Eber visited the Rebbe in Riga during Nisan 5693, the Rebbe gave him authority over the yeshiva's entire operation, including budget and personnel. The formal appointment letter was issued on 11 Iyar 5693, empowering him to manage all aspects of the institution and to correspond with donors.
