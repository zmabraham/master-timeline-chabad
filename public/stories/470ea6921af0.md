# R. Mordechai Shneur Zalman Schneersohn responds to R. Yosef Tumarkin

*1864*

R. Mordechai Shneur Zalman Schneersohn — son of the Rebbe of Avruch and grandson of the Tzemach Tzedek — served as a posek in Zhitomir. On 21 Adar I 5624 he replied to R. Yosef Tumarkin of Kremenchug on issues of hafrashas terumah and chalah, the copying costs of some manuscripts, and a question on the Ohr HaChaim to parshas Vayeira. He died young, and most of his writings have been lost.
