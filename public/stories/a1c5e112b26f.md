# Rosenbaum family arrives in New York on the SS Moravia

*1894*

Having finally decided to emigrate, the Rosenbaum family sailed from Hamburg and arrived at the port of New York on 11 December 1894 aboard the SS Moravia. The passenger manifest recorded Abraham (54), Yetta (53), Solomon (21) and Joseph (11). In New York he would settle, deal in diamonds and jewelry at 444 Grand Street, and gather chassidim around him.
