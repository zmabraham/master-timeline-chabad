# Yechidus where Rebbe revisits Hemshech 5666 printing

*1970*

At his Erev Rosh Chodesh Elul 5730 (August 31, 1970) birthday yechidus, the Rebbe asked, 'What is happening with the Samach-Vov?' The author explained that after the initial halt they had quietly continued, on the advice of the late Rabbi Mordechai Leib Rudshtein, to set another fifty galleys. The Rebbe exclaimed, 'Tavo alav berachah!'
