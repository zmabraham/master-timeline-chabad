# Rebbe issues centennial klalli letter

*1966*

The Rebbe's secretariat sent a klalli letter on 8 Nisan 5726 to 'Chabad organizations and institutions in every locality,' authorizing them as the Rebbe's shluchim to receive special hilula-day donations to a 'Keren HaMeah' for the printing of the Tzemach Tzedek's writings. The letter is published in Igrot Kodesh vol. 24, p. 113.
