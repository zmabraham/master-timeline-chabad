# The books in the Dvar Malchus set with elucidations is a series of books

*1991*

The books in the Dvar Malchus set with elucidations is a series of books containing the sichos of the Rebbe from the years 5751-5752 (1991-92) in a unique edition in which the source references in the margins of the sicha were elucidated, and detailed summaries of the flow of the sicha were printed
