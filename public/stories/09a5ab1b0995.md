# New York 'Birkat Avraham' Haggadah by Avraham Landa of Cichanow with grandson's commentary

*1950*

In 5710 (1950), the Haggadah 'Birkat Avraham' was published in New York by Yaakov Werdiger of Pardes Publishing, drawing on the 'Chesed L'Avraham' commentary by Rabbi Avraham Landa, head of the Cichanow rabbinical court, and 'Ateret Zekenim' by his grandson Rabbi Menachem Mendel Chaim Landa of Zawiercie. The 49-leaf octavo edition was a photo-offset of an earlier Piotrkow edition. It served Polish Chassidic survivors and their descendants in postwar America.
