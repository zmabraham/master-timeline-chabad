# Rayatz arrested by Cheka during morning prayers

*1920*

During the year of mourning for his father, the Rayatz was confronted by three armed officers, two of them Jewish Yevsektzia members, who demanded he remove his tallit and tefillin and come with them. At the police station, fifteen armed officers sat around a conference table to intimidate him. When one threatened with his gun, the Rayatz replied that 'this toy' impresses only those with no faith — 'we have one G-d and two worlds.'
