# Maggid Devarav LeYaakov first printed in Koretz

*1781*

Following the first chassidic book by Rabbi Yaakov Yosef of Polonne, the teachings of the Maggid of Mezritch were printed under the title Maggid Devarav LeYaakov in Koretz in 5541 (1781). It was the first publication of the Maggid's own teachings.
