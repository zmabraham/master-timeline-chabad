# Publication of Rashimat Maamarei DACh of the Tzemach Tzedek

*1994*

The work was based on a card catalog compiled beginning in 5741 (1981) by R. Avraham Boruch Yunik, drawing on dated discourse headings in Or HaTorah, R. Hillel of Paritch's hanachos, the Maharash's hanachos, and the Tzemach Tzedek's own manuscript volumes. R. Yosef Yitzchak Keller completed and prepared the work after finishing the Alter Rebbe registry. It overcame the unique difficulty that the Tzemach Tzedek's maamarim were preserved by topic rather than by year.
