# R' Moshe Katzenelson, Vilna yeshiva committee member, passes away

*1929*

R' Moshe Katzenelson was a member of the executive committee of the Vilna Tomchei Tmimim from its founding, devoted to the institution with complete faith. Originally from Polotzk, he served on the management board until his passing on 2 Nisan 5689.
