# First agreement between Hebron Sephardic and Chabad communities

*1825*

In 5585 (1825), the Sephardic and Chabad communities of Hebron concluded their first agreement, dividing Hebron's portion of the Pekuam Amsterdam funds between the two communities. The Pekuam committee did not accept their compromise, however, and additional negotiations were required regarding shdarus to other lands. These discussions ultimately produced the broader 5590 agreement.
