# Rabbinical conference convenes in Vilna in Iyar 5669

*1909*

The Rebbe Rashab was one of the conveners and chief figures of the gathering, whose details are recorded in Igrot Kodesh of the Rashab (vol. 4, letter 1029, with introduction p. 26). Beyond formal sessions, the visit brought the Rebbe Rashab into the newly acquired Apatov shul on Lag BaOmer, where he delivered a chassidic discourse.
