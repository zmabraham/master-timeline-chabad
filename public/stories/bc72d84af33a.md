# Levin travels to Moscow for Agudas Chabad conference around Yud Shevat

*1997*

Levin arrived Monday afternoon for the conference at Agudas Chabad's Moscow center. Aharonov also flew in Thursday afternoon. Conference attendees were invited to visit the library to view 16 Schneerson books prepared in a new wing, but Levin refused to formally request fuller access and left publicly to avoid legitimizing the new wing concept.
