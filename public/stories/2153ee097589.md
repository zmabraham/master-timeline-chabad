# Photograph of Rayatz with the Rebbe at Pirkersdorf

*1935*

On 25 Adar I 5695 (28 February 1935), at the Pirkersdorf convalescent resort where the Rayatz had gone for medical treatment, the Rayatz was photographed standing among the trees together with the Rebbe. Two further photographs from the same session show the Rayatz alone among the trees.
