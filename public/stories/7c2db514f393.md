# HaTamim reports Tomchei Temimim activity in Chelm

*1935*

Issue 1 of HaTamim (Tammuz 5695) describes a cluster of Tomchei Temimim alumni in Chelm running Torah classes and a Chassidus shiur for members of Tzeirei Agudas Yisrael. The chief organizer is identified as Yaakov Yitzchak Rosenbaum, a Warsaw Tomchei Temimim student. This reporting laid the groundwork for the formal Chelm branch founded the next year.
