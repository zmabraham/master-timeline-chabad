# Chiddushim on the Talmud of the Tzemach Tzedek printed

*1878*

Published roughly in the middle of the multi-decade Tzemach Tzedek printing project, the chiddushim on Shas included editorial cross-references to teshuvos prepared for the Choshen Mishpat volume — suggesting that volume was already ready for press, though Russian censors refused to permit its publication.
