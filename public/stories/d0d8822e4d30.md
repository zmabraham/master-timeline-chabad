# Rebbe Rayatz provides $500 'shlissel-geld' through Ramash for narrator's apartment, Nisan 5709

*1949*

Apartments in East Flatbush were so scarce that an under-the-table $500 key-fee was required. When the narrator asked the Ramash to consult the Rebbe Rayatz, the Rebbe answered that he must take the apartment but asked where such a sum would come from. The Ramash replied that he himself would supply the money, and the next day at 770 he counted out $500 for the narrator. The narrator repaid the full sum once his financial situation improved.
