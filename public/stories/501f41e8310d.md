# R' Yosef Kugel of Pleshchenitsy dies

*1876*

R' Yosef Kugel, brother of the mashpia R' Hendel Kugel, was one of the leading Chassidim of Pleshchenitsy. He died around Adar 5636, prompting R' Shmuel Dovber of Borisov to write to R' Avraham Chaim Rosenbaum on 26 Adar 5636 (22 March 1876) lamenting 'sudden news from Rebbi Z.R. of his camp of the passing of our beloved, God-fearing and trembling R' Y.'
