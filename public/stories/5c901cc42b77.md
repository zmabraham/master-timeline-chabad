# Founding Vaad of Agudat Chasidei Chabad reports first progress

*1941*

On 8 Kislev 5702 (Nov 1941), the Founding Vaad wrote to Rebbe Rayatz reporting that branches of Agudat Chasidei Chabad had been established in Tel Aviv and Jerusalem (as central branches), as well as in Haifa, Petach Tikvah, Bnei Brak, Ramat Gan, Rishon LeTzion, Chadera, Kfar Yechezkel and Migdal.
