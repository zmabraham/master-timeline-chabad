# Author's second visit to the Frierdiker Rebbe in Elul 5699

*1939*

The author delivered 213 pidyonot and regards from individuals and communal matters. The Rebbe explained that although people had approached him about moving to the United States, he had not held by the idea because the mode of his earlier American visit had been that of a public askan rather than reflecting his true purpose with chassidim and chassidus.
