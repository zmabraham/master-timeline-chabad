# Author's first yechidus with the Rebbe Rashab

*1907*

On Thursday, 2 Marcheshvan 5668, the author and his father were received in yechidus by the Rebbe Rashab — the author's first encounter with a Lubavitcher Rebbe. The Rebbe instructed that the boy be referred to the yeshiva administration. The next day he was examined by R. Yechiel Komisar and R. Yechezkel Himmelstein and admitted to R. Leib Babin's class.
