# Facsimile of the Kaufmann Haggadah published in Budapest

*1990*

Around 5750 (1990), the Hungarian Academy of Sciences Library's Oriental Collection sponsored the publication of a facsimile of the 14th-century illuminated 'Kaufmann Haggadah' in Budapest. Limited to 1,000 copies, the 60-page facsimile was accompanied by a separate English introductory booklet by Gabriel Sed-Rajna. The featured copy was number 21 of the print run.
