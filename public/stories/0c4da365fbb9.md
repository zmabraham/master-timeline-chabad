# Official meeting with Lenin Library archivists on Schneerson manuscripts

*1990*

On Tuesday, 7 Shevat 5750 (February 2, 1990), Rabbi Nissan Mindel, Rabbi Shlomo Cunin and Rabbi Berel Levine met with Medvedev, department head Dyragin and his deputy Midovich at the Lenin Library. Through pointed questioning they established that only part of the manuscript holdings came from the Ginzburg collection and that some 30 volumes of Chabad Chassidus were among the manuscripts. The meeting marked progress after a frustrating day of bureaucratic obstacles in arranging onward travel to Ukraine.
