# Mitteler Rebbe directs Chabad chassidim to settle in Hebron

*1821*

Having earlier settled in Tzfat between 5560 and 5565, Chabad chassidim were directed by the Mitteler Rebbe to move to Hebron during 5581-5583. Hebron became the principal Chabad center in the Holy Land for the next century. A handful remained in Tzfat until the 1837 earthquake.
