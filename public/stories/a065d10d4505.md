# Rabbi Avraham Aba Bruk - Rabbi Avraham Aba (Avush) Bruk, 3 Tev

*2023*

Rabbi Avraham Aba Bruk - Rabbi Avraham Aba (Avush) Bruk, 3 Tevet 5708, December 16, 1947 - 21 Shevat 5783, February 12, 2023) was a veteran educator and resident of Nachalat Har Chabad, whose roles included serving as director of the Kfar Chabad vocational school
