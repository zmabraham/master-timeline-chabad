# R' Chizkiyah Medini settles in Romano house

*1901*

R' Chizkiyah Medini, author of the Sdei Chemed, arrived in Hebron in 5661 and was housed by the Romano family in their stately mansion. He used the building, which contained a synagogue called the Istanbuli Beit Knesset, as his base for Torah study and teaching. There he completed his monumental Sdei Chemed before dying on 24 Kislev 5665. The 'Yeshivat Sdei Chemed' was later established in the same building in his memory.
