# Gorbachev's office declines audience with Chabad delegation

*1991*

Later on Rosh Chodesh Adar 5751, Malokoyedov's office called the Chabad delegation in Moscow with three points: thanks for the menorah, confirmation that authorization had been issued to the Ministry of Culture to return the books, and a refusal of the requested audience with Gorbachev citing his schedule. Rabbi Cunin was then summoned to the U.S. Consulate, where Mr. Collins relayed similar news from the Foreign Ministry and was about to meet with Tolstikov of the Ministry of Culture.
