# Birth of Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn in Lubavitch

*1880*

Born on 12 Tamuz 5640, he would grow up to succeed his father, the Rashab, as the sixth Lubavitcher Rebbe. His birthday, 12 Tamuz, later coincided with his release from Soviet imprisonment in 1927, making the date a celebrated chasidic holiday.
