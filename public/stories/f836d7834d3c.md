# Chabad delegation submits joint letter to Gorbachev on library return

*1991*

On 23 Shevat 5751, after meeting U.S. Consulate officials about U.S. visas and meeting with Stas and Ilya, the four delegation members signed a letter drafted at Stas's office to Gorbachev. They prepared three copies — one delivered by Rabbi Koegan to Gorbachev's office, one taken by Stas for Primakov, and one taken by Ilya for Raisa Gorbachev — while they awaited a positive reply.
