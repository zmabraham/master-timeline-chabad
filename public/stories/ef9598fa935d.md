# Rebbe writes from Riga seeking funds for student evacuation

*1939*

Once safe in Riga, the Rebbe Rayatz immediately mobilized international Chabad and the Joint. In letters of 14 Tevet he reported having moved part of the central yeshiva from Otwock and branches from Warsaw, Lodz, Kaluszyn and Chelm to Lithuania, and pleaded for $4,000 from the Joint to rescue the remainder still in mortal danger in Poland.
