# Royal decree on religious tolerance

*1905*

On 17 Tevet 5665 (1905), the Russian Tsar issued an eight-clause decree, the sixth clause of which directed administrative measures to remove obstacles to religious practice for various faiths. This decree raised hopes for the Rebbe Rashab that the Yavneh society he wished to establish to legalize Tomchei Tmimim would now be approved, though it ultimately was not.
