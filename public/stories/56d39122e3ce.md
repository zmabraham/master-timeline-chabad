# Anash of France petition the Rebbe to visit

*1953*

Anash in France, many of them refugees who had escaped the Iron Curtain, submitted a petition begging the Rebbe to travel abroad to visit them. They wrote that most chassidim of the past several decades knew the Rebbe only through hearsay and pleaded for a face-to-face encounter. The Rebbe noted in his own handwriting that the petition was received on 12 Tammuz 5713.
