# Wedding of the Rayatz in Lubavitch

*1897*

On 13 Elul 5657 (September 10, 1897), the Rayatz wed Nechama Dina in Lubavitch. The chuppah was held Friday afternoon in the courtyard of the main synagogue, with the Rayatz donning the customary Lubavitcher shtreimel. Chasidim streamed in from across Russia for the celebrations.
