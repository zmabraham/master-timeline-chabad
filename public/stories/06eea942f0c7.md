# Maharayatz urges Eliezerov to focus on youth and children of Kollel Chabad

*1932*

Beyond his focus on the elders and avreichim, the Rebbe wanted a dedicated vaad to periodically examine children studying in cheders and yeshivos, give prizes for good behavior and excellence in learning, and gently discipline those who needed it. For the older youth he proposed structured study sessions or printed materials to keep them connected to the kollel. The same theme is taken up in his 12 Cheshvan letter to the kollel's nesius committee.
