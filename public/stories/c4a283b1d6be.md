# Future Rebbe and Rebbetzin arrive in New York

*1941*

The Frierdiker Rebbe immediately dispatched letters and a cable to Chicago, Baltimore, and elsewhere announcing that 'my daughter and son-in-law have arrived safely.' The day later became one of the most significant in Chabad's modern calendar, marking the future seventh Rebbe's entry to America and the eventual revival of Chabad Lubavitch from 770 Eastern Parkway.
