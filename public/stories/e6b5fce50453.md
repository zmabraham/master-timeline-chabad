# Brothers R. Shmelke and R. Pinchas Horowitz praise Alter Rebbe's halachic writings

*1770*

The 'two golden pipes' — the brothers Horowitz — joined the Maggid's circle and examined two halachic works the young Alter Rebbe had composed. They praised him highly and urged him to 'be strong and complete the work,' showing that halachic study was part of the curriculum at Mezeritch alongside chassidic teaching.
