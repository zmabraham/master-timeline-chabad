# Knesses Beis Yitzchak hires Rabbi Baruch Ber Leibowitz

*1904*

In 1904, Knesses Beis Yitzchak in Slabodka hired Rabbi Baruch Ber Leibowitz to lead the yeshivah. He would later relocate the yeshivah to Vilna in 1921 and to Kamenitz in 1926, becoming one of the most influential Lithuanian roshei yeshivah of the era.
