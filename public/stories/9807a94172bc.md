# Kollel Chabad letter to Montefiore signed by Jerusalem trustees

*1849*

The 5609 census listed Rabbi Eliyahu Yosef Rivlin and Rabbi Uri Orenstein as the trustees of Kollel Chabad in Jerusalem. The same two trustees signed the kollel's letter to Sir Moses Montefiore that year. Their formal role marked early institutional recognition of the Chabad community within Jerusalem's Ashkenazi communal landscape.
