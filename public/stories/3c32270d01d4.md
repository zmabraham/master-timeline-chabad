# R' Avraham Chaim's grave located at Mount Zion cemetery

*1999*

After Tishrei 5760, R' Yisrael Mordechai HaLevi Kozaminsky discovered through a Brooklyn Charedi newspaper that R' Avraham Chaim's gravestone was at Mount Zion cemetery in Brooklyn rather than the Achuzas Olam plot in Staten Island where searchers had been looking. Upon visiting with R' Meir Simcha Abramovich, he confirmed the gravestone and corrected the recorded death year from 5674 to 5675.
