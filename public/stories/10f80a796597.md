# Chernobyl children's camp opens in Kiev

*1992*

Rabbi Yitzchak Kogan ran the camp with the author and his family, who traveled overnight by train from Moscow. The first day went well with 45 girls and 35 boys settled in two adjacent camps. Most lacked any traditional background but wanted to study at the religious school in Kfar Chabad. Rabbi Dov Ber Krasik in Kiev had organized the children and prepared the camp.
