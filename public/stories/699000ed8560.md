# Shadarut shaliach sets out to North Africa

*1857*

He carried a shadarut document signed by Chabad leaders in both Hebron and Jerusalem. His route went through Izmir, where he received a recommendation from R' Chaim Palagi in Marcheshvan, before continuing to Alexandria, Tunisia, Tripoli, Algeria, Oran, and Tlemcen — an unusual mission since these were traditionally Sephardic donor territories.
