Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Marzel. Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Marzel (born in the year 5738, 1978) is one of the shluchim of the Rebbe in Russia, head of the central kollel in the city of Moscow, and rabbi of the kashrus committee of Russia
