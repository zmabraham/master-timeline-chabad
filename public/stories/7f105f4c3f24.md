# Completion of first edit of Igrot Kodesh volume 20

*1992*

Back in New York between trips to Russia, the author spent two weeks editing Igrot Kodesh volume 20, finishing the first pass on Tuesday afternoon 29 Sivan 5752. He noted the absence of news from Washington in the wake of the recent Bush–Yeltsin summit. The next morning a fax arrived from Devorah Rochel in Moscow reporting renewed movement on the library and urging the delegation's immediate return.
