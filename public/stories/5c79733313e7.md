# Tomchei Tmimim Chmielnik 'Toras Emes' founded

*1929*

While visiting the Rebbe Rayatz in Riga during Tishrei 5690, Yosef Goldstein, a young shochet and former student of Tomchei Tmimim Warsaw, was instructed to establish a branch in Chmielnik called 'Toras Emes.' Together with Avraham Zonstein he gathered about fifteen students, and the yeshiva quickly grew. By the time of its report, three rashei yeshiva taught roughly seventy students across four divisions.
