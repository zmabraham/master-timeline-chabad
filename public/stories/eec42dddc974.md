# Rashab signs printing contract for fifth edition of Likkutei Torah

*1902*

The Rashab personally oversaw a new, carefully corrected fifth edition of Likkutei Torah. He signed the printing contract on 19 Adar I תרס"ב, with the printing plates remaining his property. He aimed to lower the price from 5-6 silver rubles per copy to roughly 2 rubles, so that even middle-class Jews could afford the set. The Rebbe and R' Shmuel Sofer (Rabinowitz) personally proofed the text.
