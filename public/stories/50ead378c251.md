# Rebbe asks R. Stein to push Freidin's election in Fastov

*1933*

With Kuperstock to be relocated, the Rebbe Rayatz wrote on 6 Iyar 5693 (Igros Kodesh vol. 11, letter 3888) asking R. Refael Stein to travel to Fastov and remind the chassidim of their duty to choose a chassidic rabbi from anash. He specifically endorsed R. Yehuda Leib Freidin for the role.
