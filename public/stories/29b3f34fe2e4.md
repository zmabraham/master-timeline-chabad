# R' Yehoshua Arsh becomes Rosh Yeshiva at Zal Hakatan

*1911*

R' Yehoshua Arsh, who had been head of the Dvinsk yeshiva endowed by the wealthy R' Moshe Weitenberg, was accepted in 5671 (1911) as Rosh Yeshiva of the Zal Hakatan ('small hall') of Tomchei Tmimim in Lubavitch. At the request of R' Dovid Volovich of Dvinsk, he brought his son Chaim Aba Veil along to study in Lubavitch.
