# Vilna yeshiva reports rapid growth to Rebbe Rayatz

*1927*

The Vilna directors reported that students were arriving daily from Disna, Dokshitz, Postov, Koretz and other towns, exceeding twenty in number. Rabbi Zeev Segal, formerly a Rosh Mesivta in Grodno, was appointed Rosh Yeshiva, and R' Mordechai Leib Grossbein was temporarily designated as mashpia in chassidus. Classes were divided by age into two levels studying Gemara with Tosafot.
