# R' Baruch of Mezhibuzh appoints leaders of Tiberias Chasidim

*1810*

Following the death of R' Avraham of Kalisk in 5570, the leadership of the general Chasidic community of Tiberias was reorganized by R' Baruch of Mezhibuzh. He asked the community to unify behind R' Tzvi Hirsch Halevi, with R' Meir of Bychov serving alongside him. The appointment letter, preserved in Yagdil Torah, asked the community to 'be one bound bundle in love, brotherhood, peace and friendship.'
