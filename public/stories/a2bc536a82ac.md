# Letter of Rabbi Menachem Nachum of Tolna for a blind chasid

*1894*

Dated Thursday of Parashat Chayei Sarah 5655 in Tolna, the letter introduces Tzvi Surkes — a man who had always supported himself by his own labor until suddenly going blind. The Tolna Rebbe asks Rabbi Orlozarov, a chasid of the Rebbe Rashab, to help Surkes both financially and in securing a consultation with a professor in Kharkov.
