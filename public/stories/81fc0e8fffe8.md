# Bnei Brak Haggadah 'Siach Avot' from Lechovitch-Koidanov dynasties

*1991*

In 5751 (1991), Yaakov Tzvi Meir Erlich compiled and the Siach Avot Institute published in Bnei Brak the 'Siach Avot' Haggadah, a collection of holy teachings from the Lechovitch and Koidanov Rebbes. The 236-page octavo edition added Seder laws and customs, Exodus material, and Passover and Sefirat HaOmer letters. It served the Belarussian Chassidic dynasty's contemporary followers.
