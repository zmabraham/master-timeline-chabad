# Rotstein and Zalmanov travel from Vladivostok to Japan

*1940*

On 4 Tishrei 5701, the Frierdiker Rebbe relayed in a letter that he had received a telegram that day from R. Moshe Leib Rotstein and R. Shmuel Zalmanov, reporting their safe arrival in Vladivostok and their continuing travel to Japan en route to America.
