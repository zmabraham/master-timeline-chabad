# Yakovshvili arrested in Moscow

*1937*

Yakovshvili was offered freedom in exchange for confirming the counterrevolutionary status of one hundred named Jews; he refused. He was flogged repeatedly and ultimately given a death sentence. Minutes before execution, an edict from fellow Georgian Lavrentiy Beria — head of Stalin's secret police — spared his life, though he served nearly a decade in the gulag.
