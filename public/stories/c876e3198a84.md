# Chabad Chassidim in Eretz Yisrael form a separate kollel

*1805*

Following years of disagreement between Rabbi Avraham of Kalisk and the Alter Rebbe over the Chabad approach to Chassidism, the rift carried into Eretz Yisrael. In 5565, Chassidim in Tzfat who followed the Alter Rebbe organized their own kollel, separate from the general Chassidic community led from Tiberias. The same year, Rabbi Levi Yitzchak of Berditchev sent dedicated funds for his own Chassidim in the land through Rabbi Aryeh Leib of Volochisk.
