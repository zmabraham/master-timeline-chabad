# Mitteler Rebbe writes coded letter on the state of Chassidut

*1816*

In 5576 (1816), early in his leadership, the Mitteler Rebbe sent a heavily coded letter to confidants — including Pinchas Shick of Shklov — about a group of Chassidim who circulated the claim that after the Alter Rebbe's passing there was no rabbi to fill his place and that Chassidut had ceased. The Rebbe acknowledged Pinchas's earlier 'prophecy' that this struggle would arise and pass, and observed that even as Chassidut was being denigrated, the practice of fundraising continued to spread. The letter was later seized from Pinchas Shick's estate and used by an informer as part of the case against the Rebbe.
