# Alter Rebbe writes Igeret HaTanchumin urging continued EY support

*1788*

After hearing that some chassidim wished to reduce their support of the Tiberias community because the original support had been directed at honoring the deceased tzaddik, the Alter Rebbe wrote his Igeret HaTanchumin (letter 11). He pleaded that not a single coin be withheld, emphasizing the supreme importance of supporting Jewish settlement in Eretz Yisrael, and praised R' Avraham HaKohen as the worthy successor.
