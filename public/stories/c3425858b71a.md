# Poalei Tzion confronts Rayatz with weapons over student Berel Davzik

*1906*

The Rayatz had telegrammed the parents of underage student Davzik, who had become active in Poalei Tzion. On Thursday night, group members marched in with weapons threatening the Rayatz, who responded fearlessly. Three days later they returned, shooting at the yeshivah and narrowly missing Rabbi Moshe Klatzkin of Romanov. Yeshivah students drove them off.
