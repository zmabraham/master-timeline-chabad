# R' Chaim Palagi endorses Chabad shaliach in Izmir

*1857*

On his way to Egypt and North Africa, R' Shaul stopped in Izmir to obtain endorsement from the renowned Sephardic posek R' Chaim Palagi. This recommendation was important because the shaliach was traveling to traditionally Sephardic communities on behalf of an Ashkenazic kollel.
