# Rebbe asks R' Yehuda Eber to map yeshivas of Polish Lithuania

*1931*

The Rebbe had originally written the prior winter asking Eber, head of Tomchei Temimim in Vilna, to gather this information. Now he renewed the request, observing that Vilna was a natural base from which such intelligence could be assembled. The data was to feed his broader planning for Tomchei Temimim's expansion across Polish Lithuania.
