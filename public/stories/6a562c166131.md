# Baal Shem Tov's soul ascends to Mashiach

*1746*

Rabbi Yisrael Baal Shem Tov wrote to his brother-in-law about a mystical ascent in which he asked the Messiah when he would come. The Messiah replied, 'When your wellsprings will spread to the outside' — a phrase that would later guide the Rebbe Rayatz's vision for spreading chasidic teachings to America.
