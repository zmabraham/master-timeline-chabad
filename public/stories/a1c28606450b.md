# Rebbe pushes Faigin on Agudas Nashim expansion

*1933*

The Rebbe expressed frustration: 'I already spoke with R. Mordechai Leib about this in Druzgenik - that they needed to establish Agudas Nshei Yisrael in every possible place... But to my regret all proposals for work and action remain only on paper as good intentions. Going forward, with G-d's help, this general matter must be rectified.'
