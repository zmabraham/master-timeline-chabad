# R' Menachem Tzvi Hirsch writes letter from Romny rabbinate

*1836*

Dated 7 Adar 5596, the letter recounts how R' Menachem Tzvi Hirsch travelled from Lubavitch through Beshenkovichi, Vitebsk (with R' Yitzchak Isaac), Mohilov (with R' Meir Berlin), and finally Homel where R' Yitzchak Isaac Epstein recommended him for the Romny position. He sought to repay the loan and asked for blessings for healing.
