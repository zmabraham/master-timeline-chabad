# In the United States. Her passing. Editing source. The Rebbe sheds a tear

*1964*

In the United States. Her passing. Editing source. The Rebbe sheds a tear at his mother's funeral. Grave of Rebbetzin Chana. The Rebbetzin passed away at the onset of Mincha on Shabbos, the 6th of Tishrei 5725 (September 12, 1964), and thousands of chassidim accompanied her coffin to its resting place
