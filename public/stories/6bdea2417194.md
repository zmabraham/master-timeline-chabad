# R' Oren Mizrachi (right) alongside the rabbi of the Chabad community in Elad, Rabbi

*1978*

R' Oren Mizrachi (right) alongside the rabbi of the Chabad community in Elad, Rabbi Schneur Zalman Yaroslavsky. R' Oren Moshe Mizrachi (born in the year 5739, 1978) is the chairman of the Chabad community council in Elad, CEO of Chabad institutions in the city, member of the city council on behalf of the 'Igud' faction
