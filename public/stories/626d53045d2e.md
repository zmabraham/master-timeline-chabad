# Rebbe blows shofar on second day of Rosh Hashana 5723

*1962*

Levin again ascended the bima and unexpectedly received the honor of gelilah. The Rebbe covered the bundled pidyonot with his tallit, wept, and blew only the first tekiah. After Mincha the Rebbe led the entire crowd to Tashlich along Eastern Parkway in remarkable order, with even non-Jewish onlookers watching with reverence. The maamar 'Yivchar Lanu Et Nachalateinu' was delivered at the farbrengen that followed.
