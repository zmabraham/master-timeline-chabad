# Sixth Lubavitcher Rebbe arrives in Riga, Latvia from Russia

*1927*

After enduring arrest and exile in Russia, the sixth Rebbe crossed into Latvia by train, arriving at 7:00 a.m. at Riga station. Thousands of Jews of every background filled the station and surrounding plaza to greet him. He and his family were eventually escorted to a hotel and later to an apartment in an upscale Jewish neighborhood.
