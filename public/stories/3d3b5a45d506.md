# Major Aliyah of Lubavitch refugees to Eretz Yisrael

*1949*

In 5709 (1949), a major portion of the chassidim who had fled the Soviet Union in 5706–5707 finally arrived in the Land of Israel after extensive wanderings in Europe. The yeshiva students from Tel Aviv moved to Lod, where several Anash families had settled, and reopened the yeshiva.
