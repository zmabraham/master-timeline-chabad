# R. Yitzchak Dov Oshpal smuggled across border into Vilna

*1939*

R. Yitzchak Dov Oshpal, head of the Vilna Tomchei Tmimim branch, had fled to his home in Kornitz when war erupted. In Tevet 5700, his student Yosef Kaminetsky came as a special emissary from Vilna with funds and a US visa held for him by R. Chaim Ozer Grodzenski. At 4 a.m. on 7 Tevet, after enormous danger, Oshpal crossed the border into Lithuania, noting the event in his Mishnayos.
