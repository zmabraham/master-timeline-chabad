# Tnaim of Sheina written in Rebbe Rayatz's own hand

*1932*

On Lag B'Omer 5692, several weeks before his daughter Sheina's wedding, the Rebbe Rayatz personally wrote the precise text of her tnaim — modeled on those of his daughter Chana — and handed it to the scribe to copy out fair. The original autograph version survives, along with the witnessed scribal copy signed by R' Yaakov Halevi Horowitz of Riga and the Rayatz's secretary R' Yechezkel Feigin (later murdered by the Soviets).
