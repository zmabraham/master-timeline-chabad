# Report on Shabbos Mevarchim Tammuz farbrengens

*1940*

R. Yehoshua Isaac Baruch reported the full Shabbos Mevarchim Tammuz seder in Kovno: tehillim said by the community, a Chassidus shiur, davening, and a farbrengen lasting until mincha. R. Yosef Genkin in Vizun wrote separately that they had also observed Shabbos Mevarchim with tehillim and a farbrengen, and intended to mark 12 Tammuz similarly.
