# Highly accurate Amsterdam Tanach printed

*1666*

This Amsterdam edition of the Tanach was edited by Jewish and Christian scholars and regarded as essentially error-free, with Latin glosses on certain words. Printing began in 5426 and concluded with approbations dated 5427. The Tzemach Tzedek later owned a copy, used it to recite the haftarah, and added marginal notes on textual readings, on minhag differences for which haftarah to read, and on triennial division points.
