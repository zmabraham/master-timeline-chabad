# Roza Melamed, last witness to Sixth Rebbe's Kostroma exile, dies in Beer Sheva

*2014*

The daughter of the Kostroma shochet Yerachmiel Kugel, Melamed recalled the Rebbe constantly studying Torah and asking the children daily how they were doing. He blessed her with long life and prayed she never know the suffering he had endured. She lived to fulfill the blessing, dying in Beer Sheva.
