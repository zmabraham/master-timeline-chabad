# Rashab departs Lubavitch for Rostov-on-Don

*1915*

On 16 Cheshvan 5676 (October 24, 1915), after 102 years of four Chabad Rebbes calling Lubavitch home, the Rashab and his family departed for Rostov-on-Don, seven hundred miles south of Moscow. The library was packed into 35 crates and stored in Moscow; the Rashab brought the valuable manuscripts with him.
