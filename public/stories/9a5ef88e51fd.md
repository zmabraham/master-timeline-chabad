# R. Naeh writes that Rebbe's 24 Tevet letter has not arrived

*1922*

On 7 Nissan 5682 (April 1922), R. Menachem Mendel Naeh wrote to Rebbe Rayatz expressing anxiety that the substantial letter of 24 Tevet had not arrived after such a long delay. He worried that the postal route had encountered some disaster and that the letter was lost.
