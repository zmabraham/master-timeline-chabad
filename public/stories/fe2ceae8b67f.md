# Society for Promotion of Enlightenment opens school in Lubavitch

*1899*

Society representative Mordechai Ben-Hillel HaKohen had investigated and recommended Lubavitch as the historic cradle of Chasidism where a Maskilic school could influence the entire Chasidic population. The school first opened in rented quarters with Gedalyahu Weinstein as principal. The Rebbe Rashab personally traveled to Petersburg to plead with Baron Ginzburg to spare Lubavitch this 'shame,' but the appeal failed.
