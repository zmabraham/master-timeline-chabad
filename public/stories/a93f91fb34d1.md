# Rebbe Rayatz responds about Achei Tmimim Tel Aviv leadership

*1941*

The Rebbe expressed great pleasure at the appointment of two committees—financial and spiritual—to assist director R. Eliezer Karasik. He acknowledged the institution's difficult material situation and committed to borrowing funds to send support, despite uncertainty about repayment. He sent personal greetings to each RaM, mashpia, teacher, and committee member by name.
