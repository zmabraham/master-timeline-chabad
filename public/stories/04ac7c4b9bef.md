# Rebbe sends detailed halachic reply to Rabbi Hutner on tefillin

*1968*

On Erev Shabbat 18 Tevet 5728 (Jan 19, 1968), the Rebbe wrote his major reply in the exchange, drafting it by hand directly on Rabbi Hutner's letter. He marshaled counter-proofs from tefilah (the Rambam's 'as if standing before the Shechinah'), korbanot, etrog and matzah, arguing that 'ein la-davar sof' if one demands knowledge of every halachic detail, and that general awareness of tefillin as a special mitzvah-object suffices. The handwritten draft was typed by the mazkirut and re-edited again in the Rebbe's own hand.
