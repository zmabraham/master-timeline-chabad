# Rebbe Rashab circular endorses Jerusalem etrog officials amid public controversy

*1913*

Amid public uproar over fresh accusations, Rashab told Anash that the Eretz Yisrael etrog officials were honest and that the auditors Shainfinkel and Chagiz had testified their accounts were entirely straight. The circular was distributed via mimeograph together with Shainfinkel's audit letter and signed by hand at the opening and close. It instructed senders to use the established 'Slonim Schlank' address for kessef hakodashim.
