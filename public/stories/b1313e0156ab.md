# Rebbe Rayatz writes to R' Avraham HaKohen on Chanukah

*1928*

On the fourth day of Chanukah 5689 (around 11 December 1928) the Rebbe Rayatz wrote to the Warsaw philanthropist R' Avraham HaKohen, gently alluding — without detail — to 'the great good things you wish to do.' The letter referred to Avraham HaKohen's earlier offer at the family wedding to fund a new yeshiva building. It is preserved in Igrot Kodesh vol. 16, letter 6004.
