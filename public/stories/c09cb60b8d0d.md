# Wiener library acquired in Kislev 5685

*1924*

Following negotiations begun the previous year, the Rebbe completed the purchase of Wiener's library in Kislev 5685 under terms of long-term monthly payments. The collection had been stored in Wiener's flooded basement and was rescued and dried in the upper floor of a factory rented by R. Eliyahu Chaim Althoz during the Soviet NEP era. The library would become the cornerstone of the future Lubavitch collection.
