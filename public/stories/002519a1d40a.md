# Massacre at Tetiev synagogue burning

*1920*

While fleeing Zhivotov through the fields, the Levin family watched a column of smoke rising from Tetiev in the distance. They later learned that pogromists had locked the Jews of Tetiev inside the synagogue and set it on fire. Tetiev was a deeply religious Chernobyl chassidic town of about 8,000 souls; fewer than 2,000 survived, most of them young orphans.
