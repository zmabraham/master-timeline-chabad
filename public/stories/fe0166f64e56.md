# Vaad HaKlali unites all Ashkenazi kollelim in Jerusalem

*1866*

Seeing that the Ashkenazi community had splintered into many particular kollelim, each looking after its own interests while the community as a whole declined, the rabbis and trustees of Jerusalem established a unified Vaad to oversee shared communal needs and represent the community to the Ottoman authorities. Both Perushim and Chassidim subdivisions participated, with one trustee from each kollel sitting on the Vaad.
