# Rebbe Rashab purchases books in Königswart

*1908*

On 10 Tammuz 5668 (July 9, 1908), the Rebbe Rashab wrote from Marienbad about visiting the small nearby town of Königswart with R' N. Gur-Aryeh and R' Z. Persitz. They found an old synagogue with an ancient Torah scroll, and from the caretaker he purchased several Prague-printed books including Chok L'Yisrael, Mesorat HaBrit HaKatan, Gevul Binyamin, and Horeh Gever.
