# Tel Aviv facsimile of the Altona 1738 manuscript Haggadah by Yosef ben David of Leipnik

*1987*

In 5747 (1987), a facsimile edition of a manuscript Haggadah from the Rosenthaliana Library, written in Altona in 5498 (1738) by Yosef ben David of Leipnik, was published in Tel Aviv. The volume included Don Isaac Abarbanel's commentary, a brief Kabbalistic commentary, Seder instructions in Yiddish and Ladino, and illustrations. A separate booklet contained an English description by Emile Schrijver translated into Hebrew by Shlomo Zucker, with a Haggadah translation by Aharon Asher Green.
