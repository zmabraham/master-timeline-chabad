# R. Shmuel Tzvi Lisson reports Lag B'Omer farbrengen in Ponevezh

*1940*

Lisson, who had studied at Tomchei Tmimim in Lubavitch from 5660 to 5669 and served as Ponevezh mashpia since 5669, wrote that the gathering was held at both Chabad minyanim in the city. The chassidim prayed for the speedy revelation of the wellsprings of Baal Shem Tov's teachings.
