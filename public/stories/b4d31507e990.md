# R' Moshe Mordechai Chaikin visits Hebron for father-in-law's yahrzeit

*1905*

R' Moshe Mordechai Chaikin, stepson and son-in-law of R' Shimon Menashe Chaikin, came each year from Jerusalem with his mother Devorah for the yahrzeit. His 27 Sivan 5665 letter to ShILA from Hebron mentioned the upcoming yahrzeit on the eve of 1 Tammuz. Moshe Mordechai later published R' Akiva Yosef Schlesinger's 'Brit Olam' with his own annotations in Jerusalem 5662-5663.
