# R. Baruch Mordechai Itinga dies in Jerusalem

*1852*

On 14 Elul 1852, R. Baruch Mordechai Itinga passed away in Jerusalem, just over a year after settling there. He had been a prominent Vilna chassid and son-in-law of R. Shmuel, av beit din of Vilna, and served as rabbi of Bobruisk for many years before his aliyah.
