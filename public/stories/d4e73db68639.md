# Beilis affair derails planned rabbinic conference

*1913*

The Russian government had been preparing for a follow-up rabbinic conference to be held in the winter of 5674 (1913-1914). However, the Beilis blood libel affair erupted and threw the Jewish world into turmoil. The matter dragged on through the end of 5674, when World War I broke out, and the second rabbinic conference never materialized.
