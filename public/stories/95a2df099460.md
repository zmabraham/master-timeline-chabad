# Rebbe Rayatz writes about house and library near yeshiva

*1938*

On 3 Shevat 5698 (January 5, 1938), the Rebbe Rayatz wrote to R. Israel Jacobson explaining the urgency of purchasing a residence for himself and the library, both for health reasons and for the inner workings of his activities. He stressed it must be adjacent to the yeshiva — courtyard next to courtyard — since he had moved from Riga to Poland only for the yeshiva's sake.
