# Russian White House backs Chabad property requests

*1990*

On Shabbat Miketz 5751 (4th Chanukah) the delegation met with four of Yeltsin's officials at the Russian government's White House. The officials agreed to write supportive letters on three Chabad initiatives — construction in Lubavitch town, the Poliakov Synagogue, and a Maimoni Center — addressed to the Smolensk city council and the Moscow city council under Deputy Mayor Muzykantsky. Copies were dispatched to each jurisdiction and to the delegation as confirmation.
