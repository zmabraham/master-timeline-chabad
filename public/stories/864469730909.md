# Chabad chassidim of Vilna purchase the Apatov synagogue

*1909*

Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak (later the Frierdiker Rebbe) wrote that the chassidim acquired the large brick building together with its sixty-year-old beis knesses, intending to install their own shochet as well. The building served as the heart of Chabad life in Vilna until the Holocaust.
