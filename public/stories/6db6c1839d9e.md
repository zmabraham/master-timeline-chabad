# Wedding officiated in Kharkov 54 years after parents' clandestine wedding

*1992*

With Rabbi Moshe Moshkovitz away in America, the author was asked to come to Kharkov to perform a Jewish wedding ceremony. Fifty-four years earlier in the winter of 5698, his own parents had married in Kharkov under entirely different conditions during the terrifying Stalin-Yezhov era—in secret, then renting a modest flat outside the city to avoid attention. Now their son openly performed a wedding ceremony in that same city with publicity and joy.
