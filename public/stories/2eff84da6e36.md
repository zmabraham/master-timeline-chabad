# Publication of Maamarei Admur HaEmtza'i (Mittler Rebbe) discourses in New York

*1985*

In 5745-5746 (1985-1986), 'Maamarei Admur HaEmtza'i' (the Mittler Rebbe Dovber Schneuri) on Sefer Vayikra was published in New York in two octavo volumes. Volume 1 ran 442 pages and Volume 2 contained pages 443-931, totaling almost 1000 pages. The work added critical primary-source Chabad material to the available canon.
