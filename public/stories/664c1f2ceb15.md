# Chafetz Chaim decides to leave Russia and return to Radin

*1921*

He wrote that the authorities imposed labor on the entire city on weekdays and Shabbat, including yeshiva students from the beis medrash whom he could not save. He concluded one must leave a place where God's name is publicly desecrated, even considering travel to Eretz Yisrael via Odessa, though he first returned to Radin.
