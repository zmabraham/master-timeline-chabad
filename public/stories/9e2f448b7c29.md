# Korosten conference of rabbis

*1926*

Led by Rabbi Zev Kipnis, the conference was government-sanctioned as a divide-and-conquer maneuver to weaken the Rebbe ahead of the Leningrad community conference. The Rebbe secretly sent the young Bentzion Shemtov to steer the agenda. When his telegram was read, the rabbis stood in respect; alarmed authorities later arrested Shemtov and Kipnis.
