# Rashab becomes owner of Beit Romano complex in Hebron

*1908*

Originally owned by an affluent Jew from Istanbul, the mansion was put up for sale by his children. The Rashab raised the necessary funds and navigated complicated legal steps over several years to acquire it. The Rayatz was intimately involved. The combined property became the base for the new yeshivah and gave the Rashab a strong foothold in Hebron.
