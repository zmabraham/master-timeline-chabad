# Lilienthal officially invites the Tzemach Tzedek to the Petersburg commission

*1843*

On 19 February 1843 (Rosh Chodesh Adar II 5603), Dr. Lilienthal formally notified Rabbi Menachem Mendel of Lubavitch of his invitation to the Petersburg commission on Jewish education. Three days later Lilienthal wrote to the Education Ministry recommending the Tzemach Tzedek as the most important rabbi among the Russian Jews. He argued that the Rebbe's agreement with government policy would carry binding weight for the Chassidim of Vitebsk, Mogilev, Chernigov, Kiev, Kherson, and Minsk.
