# Rashag writes to U.S. Embassy in Shanghai

*1946*

On 4 Shevat 5706, the Rashag wrote to the American Embassy in Shanghai per the advice of Mr. Eliot B. Coulter of the State Department, enclosing the required affidavits and a list of the names of the Tomchei Tmimim students stranded in Shanghai, China. He expressed hope that these documents would facilitate issuance of American visas so the immigration could take place as soon as possible.
