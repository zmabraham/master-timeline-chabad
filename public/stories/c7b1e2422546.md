# Pidyon HaBen of Rabbi Baruch Shalom with the Alter Rebbe's discourse

*1805*

The Alter Rebbe delivered a chassidic discourse at the pidyon haben feast for his grandson Baruch Shalom on Erev Chanukah 5566 (December 1805). The discourse is preserved in Maamarei Admur HaZaken 5566. Because Baruch Shalom was a 'safek bechor' (doubtful firstborn), the pidyon was performed from doubt — a notable halachic detail in the family record.
