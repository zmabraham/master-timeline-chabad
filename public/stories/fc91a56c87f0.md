# Aron Kodesh installed at Lubavitch Synagogue Agudat Chabad in Moscow

*1991*

After the building was secured, a mezuzah was affixed, a large Russian-language sign reading 'Beit Knesset Lubavitch Agudat Chabad' was hung, and Meir Stambler distributed flyers announcing the synagogue's return and the upcoming Shabbat tefillot. The diarist worked on setting up the aron until 4:30 PM before travelling back to camp for Shabbat, leaving the new shul ready for its first public Shabbat.
