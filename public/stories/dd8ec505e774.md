# Rebbe summarizes Persson's tour of European rabbis

*1901*

The Rebbe reported on visits to R' Chaim Ozer Grodzinski (whose letter to JCA was strong), R' Chaim Soloveitchik (whose letter was too harsh against committee members), and R' Eliyahu Chaim Meisel (where Persson stayed over two weeks). Persson was now traveling to Berlin and onward to Frankfurt, Karlsruhe, Halberstadt, Fulda, and Baden to gather support from German Orthodox rabbis before continuing to Paris.
