# Riga Anash plead with Rebbe Rayatz not to leave for Warsaw

*1931*

When word spread that the Rebbe Rayatz planned to relocate from Riga to Warsaw, the Riga community at first refused to believe it. Once the plan was confirmed, over a hundred Chassidim signed a letter on 12 Elul 5691 describing their broken hearts and pleading with the Rebbe to remain among them at least for the coming High Holidays rather than take up the larger discussion at such a sensitive moment in the calendar.
