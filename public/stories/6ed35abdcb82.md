# Shluchim fly from Moscow to Alma-Ata for kinus closing

*1991*

Upon arrival a celebration was held drawing about 200 local Jews, with speeches by R' Berel Lazar, R' Kotlarsky, R' Raskin, R' Yaffe and two local community leaders. R' A. Niselevich led the singing, the 'Lamplighters' video was shown with R' Y. Gisser explaining in Russian, and Yaakov Weisselbach of Kishinev translated the Yiddish speeches. The Rebbe had instructed during the prior Shabbat farbrengen that the shluchim daven both Shacharit and Mincha in Alma-Ata.
