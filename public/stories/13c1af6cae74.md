# Poalei Tzion raids Rashab's summer home in Bolivke

*1906*

Brandishing revolvers and exclaiming 'We'll show you!', the intruders frightened the Rayatz's young daughters and the Rebbetzin. They blocked the road to prevent police from arriving. The Rashab was forced to hire security guards. His brother the Raza, living in Vitebsk, eventually negotiated an agreement with Poalei Tzion.
