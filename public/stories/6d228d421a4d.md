# R. Avraham Chaim drafts his first will and sends it to the Rebbe Rashab

*1908*

Sensing the need to set his affairs in order, R. Avraham Chaim composed a will in 5668 and forwarded a copy to the Rebbe Rashab. The text of that first will has not survived, but the Rebbe's acknowledgement letter from Marienbad is preserved, praising him for arranging his matters as a G-d-fearing and prudent man should.
