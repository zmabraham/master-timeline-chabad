# Consul James Finn intervenes during Hebron extortion crisis

*1852*

Reports reached the British consulate that Abd al-Rahman intended to extort 40 purses (about 200 pounds) from Hebron's Jewish quarter that very morning. Finn traveled to Hebron where the European hospital doctor confirmed the news. Finn described the Jews' terror—pale faces and darting eyes, hanging on every word and movement. His presence temporarily restrained the persecutor, though the depredations resumed after Finn left.
