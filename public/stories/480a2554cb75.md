# Sixth Rebbe writes anguished letter to Jacob Klatzkin on Jewish leadership

*1943*

Responding to Klatzkin's call for public demonstrations with Torah scrolls, the Rebbe wrote that media 'sit behind a shade,' political leaders' ears are 'closed to our cries,' and that the time had come to call all Jews — irrespective of party — to fasting, prayer, and teshuvah. He compared the Jewish silence unfavorably to that of other faith communities.
