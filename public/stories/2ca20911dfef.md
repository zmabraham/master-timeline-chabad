# Russian court rules Schneerson collection belongs to Chabad

*1991*

The verdict rejected the State Library's claims, finding no evidence the collection had ever been nationalized or left without an owner. The court declined to adjudicate internal Chabad-community ownership questions but ordered the books moved to the new general Jewish library being established under Agudas Chasidei Chabad. Disputes over which volumes belonged to the collection would be settled jointly between library and Chabad representatives, with court-appointed experts as backstop.
