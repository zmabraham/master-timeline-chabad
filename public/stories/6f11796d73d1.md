# Rebbe writes qualified endorsement of agricultural colonies to JDC

*1925*

He wrote, 'It was gratifying to hear from Dr. Rosen about the establishment of the agricultural colonies,' but raised concerns about the 'lack of attention to religious institutions, mikveh, teachers, shochtim, rabbis, synagogues.' He encouraged the JDC to also help the thousands who could not work in agriculture. The letter was translated into English and read at the JDC board meeting.
