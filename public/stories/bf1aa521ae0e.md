# Luach Birkat HaNehenin reprinted in Zhalkva

*1801*

The 27-folio Zhalkva 5561 (1801) duodecimo carried the first recension of the Alter Rebbe's halachot of blessings. Multiple parallel printings in Russia-Poland and Korec followed in the early 1800s before the second recension superseded it in the 1803 siddur.
