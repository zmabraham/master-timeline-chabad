# Rabbi Yigal Hoshiar (5777)

*1963*

Rabbi Yigal Hoshiar (5777) Rabbi Yigal Hoshiar (born on the 12th of Kislev 5724, 1963) is one of the Chabad activists in Nof HaGalil, founder of the company Nitzotzot Shel Kedusha, director and producer of dozens of children's films, and previously served as a tankist in the Chabad Mitzvah Tanks in the Land of Israel
