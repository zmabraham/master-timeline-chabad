# Rebbe initiates study of Beit HaBechirah during Bein HaMetzarim

*1976*

In a special sichah delivered on Thursday night, the eve of Shabbat Mevarchim Menachem Av 5736 (1976), the Rebbe urged the study of topics related to the Beit HaBechirah during the days of Bein HaMetzarim. From that year onward, the Rebbe repeated this call at the start of the Three Weeks each year. Model replicas of the Beit HaMikdash were constructed to facilitate this study, one of which was given to the Rebbe.
