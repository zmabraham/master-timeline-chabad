# Rebbe Rayatz acknowledges Meir Gurkov's memoir

*1949*

The letter, published as Iggros Kodesh vol. 10, no. 3612, thanked Gurkov for the kuntres of memories he had submitted in response to the Rebbe's earlier instruction. The Rebbe stated that all senior temimim should similarly record what was worth remembering. Gurkov's memoir was later published as Sefer HaZichronot Divrei HaYamim.
