# Russian Chabad support funds arrive in Chevron

*1830*

Originally expected by Rosh Hashanah 5591, the emissaries were delayed and arrived in Chevron only in Kislev 5591. A 14 Kislev 5591 letter from the Chevron Chabad community thanked the Tzemach Tzedek for arranging the early dispatch despite the late delivery. The same letter records that 'the road went wrong and an hour of emergency caused them to be delayed.'
