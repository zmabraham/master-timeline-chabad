# Rebbe Rayatz writes coded letter from besieged Warsaw

*1939*

In a letter dated 15 Kislev 5700 (around November 27, 1939), Chaim Lieberman quoted the Rebbe Rayatz's first dispatch from besieged Warsaw. Because of heavy censorship the Rebbe wrote in German and in hints, stating he had no apartment and was crowded with his family at a friend's place with no room for the books Agudath Chabad had lent him for study — and would gladly see Agudath Chabad take them back, signaling the American organization to formally petition for the library's return.
