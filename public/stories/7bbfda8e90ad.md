# R' Avraham Chaim plans release from Smolensk prison on Parshat Korach

*1890*

In his Parshat Tzav letter, R' Avraham Chaim told R' Hendel that his planned day of departure from the Smolensk prison would be the Monday of Parshat Korach, in late spring 5650. He intended to travel directly to Lubavitch upon release, ending roughly a year of imprisonment.
