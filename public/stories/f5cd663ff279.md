# Sale of R' Yehuda Uziel's synagogue in Fes to R' Emanuel Mansano

*1709*

Following an existing mortgage, R' Yosef sold two-thirds of the synagogue (including Torah scrolls, ark, benches, and ritual items) to R' Emanuel Mansano for 680 ounces. R' Yaakov ibn Tzur, holding a one-sixth share, asserted neighbor's right and the parties compromised so that Mansano and Ibn Tzur each took one-third. The document was confirmed by the dayanim of Fes, including R' Yehuda ibn Attar.
