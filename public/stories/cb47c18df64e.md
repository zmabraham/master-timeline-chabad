# R. Moshe Leib Rotstein reports on Tomchei Tmimim on 18 Elul 5702

*1942*

The booklet 'Yeshivas Tomchei Tmimim Lubavitch' described the network as having operated in Lodz, Vilna, Chelm, Patzanow, Chmielnik, Globoke, Kaluszyn and other cities. By the time of publication most of these communities and their yeshivos had been destroyed.
