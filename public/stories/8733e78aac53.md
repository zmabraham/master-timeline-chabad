# Rabbi Hutner replies restating his halachic position to Rebbe

*1968*

On Yom Hamishi of Parshat Va'era 5728 (Jan 25, 1968), Rabbi Hutner wrote back, opening with thanks for the Rebbe's reply but maintaining his original position. He insisted his argument was ikar ha-din and not a mere chashash, brought further proofs from Responsa Rabbi Akiva Eiger §8, the Chavat Daat, and Tosafot Shabbat 11, and concluded that without awareness of the parshiyot the brachah would be in vain.
