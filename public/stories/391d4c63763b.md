# Stalin unleashes wave of executions after Voykov assassination

*1927*

In response to the Voykov assassination and the British diplomatic break, Stalin claimed a foreign monarchist plot and unleashed widespread repression. Party organizations affirmed the executions at hundreds of factory meetings, and the GPU received emergency authority to expedite cases. The Yevsektsia seized the moment to move against the Lubavitcher Rebbe, their most fiery religious adversary.
