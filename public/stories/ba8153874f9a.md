# Rebbe Rashab forced to flee Lubavitch due to WWI

*1915*

At the start of 5676 (1915), during World War I, as the German army approached the Lubavitch area, the Rebbe Rashab and his household were forced to flee. The decision to leave Lubavitch was made during Tishrei 5676, with the primary concern being how to transport the book collection. The Rebbe took the manuscript collection with him to Rostov but stored the printed books elsewhere.
