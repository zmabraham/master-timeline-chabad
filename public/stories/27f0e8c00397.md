# Rebbe blesses Sylvia Reinen for translating maamar into Yiddish

*1928*

In the letter to Alexander Sender Reinen, the Rebbe wrote, 'A special blessing to your wife for her efforts translating the discourse… It will be a great merit that those who don't understand another language are able to study it.' This marked the beginning of an effort to engage women with the intellectual depth of Chabad Chasidut.
