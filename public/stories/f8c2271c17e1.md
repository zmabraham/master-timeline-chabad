# Abd al-Rahman escapes Jerusalem prison and resumes ruling Hebron

*1852*

Captured by the Ottoman government, Abd al-Rahman was held briefly in chains in Jerusalem's seraglio before being permitted to walk freely with three guarantors. He escaped one night carrying his chains, jumping from the city wall, and reinstalled himself as mutasallim of Hebron. He immediately began imposing heavy fines on the population, especially the Jews.
