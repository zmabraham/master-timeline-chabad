# Lubavitch Library exhibition hall opens on 18 Elul

*1994*

The exhibition hall of the Library of Agudas Chasidei Chabad - Ohel Yosef Yitzchak - Lubavitch opened on 18 Elul 5754 (September 1994). Over the following years it hosted a sequence of major exhibitions on Chabad history, manuscripts, sacred objects, and the work of the seventh Rebbe.
