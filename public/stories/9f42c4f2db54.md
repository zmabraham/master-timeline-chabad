# Rebbe declares Mivtza Torah, Mezuzah, Tzedakah, Bayis Malei Sefarim, Neshek

*1973*

In the year 5734 (1973–74), the Lubavitcher Rebbe launched several mitzvah campaigns aimed at strengthening Jewish observance worldwide. These included Mivtza Torah, Mezuzah, Tzedakah, Bayis Malei Sefarim (Yavneh v'Chachameha), and Neshek (Shabbat candle lighting). The campaigns were a hallmark of the Rebbe's outreach efforts.
