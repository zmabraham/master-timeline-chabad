# Tzemach Tzedek visits Vilna and delivers Chassidic discourses

*1835*

In the summer of 5595 (1835), the Tzemach Tzedek visited Vilna. The visit was documented by Shmuel Yosef Fuenn, a maskil of that generation, who described the Rebbe delivering a Chassidic discourse with eyes closed and an expression of awe. Fuenn recorded that the Rebbe did not raise his hand, modulate his voice, or pause, as if a holy spirit spoke from his throat.
