# Some Chasidim move from Tzfat to Tiberias

*1781*

The branching of the Chasidic yishuv from Tzfat to Tiberias took place in 5541. This created the Tiberias-based Chasidic community that would be led for decades by R' Avraham of Kalisk and R' Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk. Both Galilean cities would remain centers of Chasidic settlement.
