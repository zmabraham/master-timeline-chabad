# Period of arrests and redemption of the Alter Rebbe begins

*1798*

The years 5559-5561 saw the Alter Rebbe's two imprisonments and miraculous releases at the hands of the Czarist government. From this period onward he undertook the third and final phase of editing his Shulchan Aruch, the mahadura basra in three layers. This work continued through the last fifteen years of his life.
