# Rayatz letter recounting Maharash's construction of the Large Hall

*1900*

In a letter dated to the beginning of Kislev 5661, the Rebbe Rayatz preserved family tradition about why Maharash, fearing the future neglect of his father's will, deliberately built the 30-by-30-cubit hall as a place of prayer and Torah on the site of the Tzemach Tzedek's home. This letter is among the primary sources for the early architectural history of the Lubavitch chatzer.
