# Mitteler Rebbe writes to R. Sh., R. Y., and R. M. about the investigations

*1826*

Around Adar–Nissan 5586 (spring 1826), in the midst of the Khovansky investigations, the Mitteler Rebbe wrote to three chassidim (R. Shmuel, R. Yosef Yitzchak, and R. Menachem Mendel) discussing the delicate political situation. He laid out a strategy whereby the rank-and-file chassidim themselves would petition the courts, attesting that chassidic and non-chassidic Jews lived in halachic unity, and pleaded with R. Tzvi of Polotsk not to separate himself from the chassidic community.
