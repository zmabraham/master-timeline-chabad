# Chabad delegation adopts organizational structure for work in Russia

*1991*

On Friday 18 Sivan 5751 (May 31, 1991), Agudas Chabad members in Moscow finalized resolutions to hire a Chabad manager working from a dedicated office, contract with Veronika for synagogue restoration and visa work, appoint a food distribution administrator, establish a Chabad beis din in Moscow recognized by Israel's chief rabbinate, recruit donors to fund operations, and designate R' Yosef Yitzchak Aharonov as coordinator reporting to the Agudas Chabad committee. The resolutions were sent to the Rebbe before Shabbat with a request for blessing.
