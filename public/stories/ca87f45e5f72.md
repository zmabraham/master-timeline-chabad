# Chabad activities exhibition opens on Lag BaOmer 5757

*1997*

On Lag BaOmer 5757 (1997) the fifth major Library exhibition opened — Taarukhas Chabad: HaPe'ilus, HaShlichus UMivtza'ei HaMitzvos. It presented the worldwide network of more than two thousand institutions run by the Rebbe's shluchim and the mivtzaim he founded over the half-century of his nesius, organized into thirteen themed display cases.
