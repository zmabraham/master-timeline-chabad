# Volume 1 of Tzemach Tzedek's responsa bound

*1819*

In his early years the Tzemach Tzedek bound his question-and-answer correspondence into numbered volumes (chelek 1, 2, 3, etc.). Volume 1, containing responsa from his earliest period, was bound around 5579. This volume, like most of the first fourteen, was later lost or destroyed in a fire in Lubavitch.
