# R' Yisrael Jacobson arrives in America at Ellis Island

*1925*

Arriving with his wife and two children, Jacobson was held at Ellis Island where his daughter Rachel Dvasha (age 1.5) was secretly coming down with measles that went undetected by the inspectors. After questioning about his finances (he had only one dollar) and rabbinic duties, the synagogue representatives Mr. Benjamin (president), Mr. Siegel (secretary), and his uncle Yosef Hillel came to vouch for him. The next Thursday morning they took the Third Avenue elevated train into Manhattan, passing through Wall Street which his uncle described as the richest place in the world.
