# Rebbe Rayatz arrives in New York

*1940*

In the late winter of 5700 (early 1940) the Rebbe Rayatz arrived in New York, having been rescued from Nazi-occupied Warsaw. From New York he began the rebuilding of Chabad institutions on American soil. Within months he would found new branches of Tomchei Tmimim and youth divisions to anchor the movement in the New World.
