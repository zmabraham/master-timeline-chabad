# Levin's first trip to the Rebbe for Tishrei 5723

*1962*

Levin had hoped for this meeting through years of underground religious work in the Soviet Union. He spent the entire month of Tishrei 5723 with the Rebbe, attending farbrengens, yechidus, and the Sukkos and Simchas Torah celebrations at 770. The memoir ends with the description of this Tishrei month.
