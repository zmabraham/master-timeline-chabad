# RMM of Vitebsk leads chassidic caravan from Russia to Eretz Yisrael

*1777*

In Adar 5537 (March 1777), R. Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk set out from Russia with two of the Maggid's disciples, R. Avraham of Kalisk and R. Yisrael of Polotsk, intending to lead a select group to Eretz Yisrael. Along the way more than 400 people joined them, gathering at Mohilov Podolsk on the Dniester to sail in several ships via Constantinople.
