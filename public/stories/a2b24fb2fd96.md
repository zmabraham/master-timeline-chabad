# National rabbinic conference convenes in St. Petersburg

*1910*

Traditional rabbis, crown rabbis, and community leaders gathered to seek consensus on community leadership and education. The Rashab was named as one of the conference organizers, with the Rayatz at his side throughout. Rabbi Chaim Ozer Grodzinski's compromise position on secular knowledge for rabbis was adopted by majority. The conference concluded with a disrespectful meeting with Prime Minister Stolypin.
