# R' David Gurevitch restores Lubavitch tombstones

*1966*

In 5726 (1966), the first opportunity arose for chassidim to visit Lubavitch. R' David Gurevitch traveled from distant Samarkand to Lubavitch and re-erected the destroyed tombstones of the Tzemach Tzedek and the Rebbe Maharash. Many of the cemetery's tombstones had previously been stolen by locals for use as building foundations.
