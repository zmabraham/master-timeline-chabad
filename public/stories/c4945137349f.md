# Rebbe insists books must accompany him out of Russia

*1929*

Writing in October 1929, R. Eliyahu Chaim Althoz recounted the Rebbe's earlier insistence — delivered while at the ohel in Rostov — that he would not depart if even one volume had to remain. Mordechai Dubin once again traveled to intercede and succeeded in extracting the export concession, a story the chassid R. Y. Jacobson similarly preserved in his memoir Zikaron Livnei Yisrael.
