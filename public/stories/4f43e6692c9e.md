# Rabbi Yosef Tzvi HaLevi Segal first from left, alongside Rabbi Shaya Yuzbitz and Rabbi Shlomo Aharon Henig

*1939*

Rabbi Yosef Tzvi HaLevi Segal first from left, alongside Rabbi Shaya Yuzbitz and Rabbi Shlomo Aharon Henig. The Gaon Rabbi Yosef Tzvi HaLevi Segal (born on 7 Cheshvan 5700, 1939) is the head of the Tzemach Tzedek Kollel in the Old City of Jerusalem, a member of the administration of Yeshiva Gedola
