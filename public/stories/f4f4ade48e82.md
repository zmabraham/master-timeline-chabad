# R' Shlomo Zalman HaKohen returns to Tiberias from three-year mission

*1792*

After three years abroad, R' Shlomo Zalman HaKohen reached Tiberias for the Shabbat between Yom Kippur and Sukkot 5553. In a letter to the chassidim in Russia he announced his return, writing that 'we have arrived in the holy city safely, all is peace.'
