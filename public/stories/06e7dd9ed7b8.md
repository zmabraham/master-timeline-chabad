# 12 Tammuz 5691 farbrengen with R' Binyamin of Minsk in Kiev's Dimievka

*1931*

Following the breakup of the Vitebsk yeshiva, the author and his chavrusa R' Michael Rapoport regrouped in Kiev for Shabbat 12 Tammuz before continuing on to Baku. The senior bochur R' Binyamin of Minsk, the mashpia of the Tomchei Tmimim yeshiva in Dimievka, led a warm farbrengen with the bochurim, providing a moment of spiritual encouragement amid the ongoing Soviet repression.
