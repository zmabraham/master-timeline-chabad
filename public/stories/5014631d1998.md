# Rebbe Rashab gives Mazal Tov gift on 19 Kislev 5680

*1919*

On 19 Kislev 5680, the festival commemorating the Alter Rebbe's liberation, Rebbe Sholom DovBer of Lubavitch presented a banknote bearing the inscription 'Mazal Tov, 19 Kislev 5680' to a chassid. The annotation in the margin was recorded by his secretary, Rabbi Elchonon Dov Marozov. This was one of the final such gifts of the Rebbe Rashab's life, given mere months before his passing.
