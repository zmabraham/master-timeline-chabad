# Soviet authorities expel the Polotsk Tomchei Tmimim yeshiva

*1929*

The clandestine Tomchei Tmimim yeshiva that had been functioning in Polotsk under R' Yechezkel Himmelstein and R' Shlomo Chaim Kesselman, with about thirty older students, was forcibly closed by Soviet authorities mid-winter. The author had already transferred to the Vitebsk branch a short time earlier, which he later recorded as providential. He completed the winter zman at Vitebsk under R' Gavriel Kagan without disturbance.
