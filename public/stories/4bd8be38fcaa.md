# R' Yehuda Eber reports on Vilna yeshiva class restructuring

*1931*

After visiting Warsaw and Lodz to examine students at the Rebbe Rayatz's request, R' Yehuda Eber returned to Vilna and immediately reorganized the classes. He reported that although the student count had decreased slightly from the winter, the qualitative level had improved. The preparatory class was disbanded, and not a single student was missing from his own advanced class or R' Zeev Segal's top class.
