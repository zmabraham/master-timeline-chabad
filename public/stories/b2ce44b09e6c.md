# Rayatz reflects on father's 1920 predictions in Moscow diary entry

*1927*

The Rayatz woke at 3 a.m. troubled by events in Moscow. After reflecting on the 1920 meeting with his father about the new regime and its leaders' fates, 'I went back to sleep, and my soul was relaxed.' These predictions endowed him with fortitude during the darkest times of Soviet persecution.
