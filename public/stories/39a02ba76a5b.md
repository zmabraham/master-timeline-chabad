# Rabbi Yehoshua Neuhauser - Rabbi Yehoshua Menachem HaLevi Neuhauser

*1980*

Rabbi Yehoshua Neuhauser - Rabbi Yehoshua Menachem HaLevi Neuhauser (born 5740, 1980) is a Ram in the Tomchei Tmimim Yeshiva of Or Yehuda and the Tomchei Tmimim Yeshiva of Netanya (gedolah), and a Torah author known for his extensive engagement with the Torah of the Rogatchover Gaon
