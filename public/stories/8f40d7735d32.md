# Rosenbaum writes from Königsberg about why he avoids emigrating to America

*1893*

Writing from Königsberg in Parshat Ki Tavo 5653 to R' Zev Wolf and R' Chaim Gershon Kugel, Reb Avraham Chaim quoted at length a letter he had received on 16 Iyar from R' Chaim Yaakov Widerewitz, rabbi of the Sephardic association in America. Widerewitz advised that any family with three or four strong working hands would find livelihood, but 'soft and delicate people' would suffer terribly. Knowing himself to be of the second type, Rosenbaum reluctantly resolved not to cross the ocean.
