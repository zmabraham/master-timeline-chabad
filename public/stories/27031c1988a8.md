# Avraham Chaim Rosenbaum begins imprisonment in Smolensk

*1888*

Avraham Chaim Rosenbaum, a Chabad chassid and scholar in halacha, kabbalah and Hebrew grammar, was sentenced to imprisonment in Smolensk. He arrived at the prison in the summer of 5648 (1888), where he was initially put to hard labor alongside violent criminals serving lifetime sentences of exile. Despite the harsh conditions, the authorities allowed him from the outset to bring ritual articles into his confinement.
