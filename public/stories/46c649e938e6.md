# At the chuppah (5784) - Rabbi Chaim Shlomo Diskin (born 5720)

*1959*

At the chuppah (5784). Rabbi Chaim Shlomo Diskin (born 5720, 1959) is the rabbi of the city of Kiryat Ata, and also serves as the Rebbe's chief shliach in the city, a member of the Chabad rabbinical court in the Holy Land, chairman of the alumni organization of the central Tomchei Tmimim Yeshiva.
