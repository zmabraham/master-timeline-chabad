# R' Leib of Sena emigrates to Eretz Yisrael

*1821*

The Montefiore census of 1839 records that R' Leib of Sena, known as Chitrik, emigrated to Eretz Yisrael in 1821 and settled in Hebron. He may have stopped in Istanbul on his journey, where he made acquaintances who later helped forward correspondence to Europe.
