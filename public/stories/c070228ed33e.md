# Levin's first flight to the Rebbe via charter from Lod

*1962*

After years of yearning, Levin joined a charter flight at the end of Elul 5722 for his first trip to the Rebbe. The plane departed Lod at noon, stopped two hours in Paris where Chassidim could see them only through a thick glass partition (only R' Raphael Wilshansky was allowed in), then continued to New York. The Rebbe was waiting at 770 and emerged from his room to daven Maariv when the group arrived.
