# Rabbi Yechezkel Abramsky visits Tomchei Temimim

*1936*

Abramsky, dayan of the London Beis Din, deliberately detoured to visit the yeshiva and found there about 200 fine bochurim diligently learning Torah and yiras Hashem, all sustained by the yeshivas treasury. A year later, on Lag BaOmer 5697, he publicized this visit in a letter of support urging Jews everywhere to become regular supporters of the yeshiva.
