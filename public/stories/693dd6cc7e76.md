# Mrs. Rashka Rivlin, granddaughter of Hebron's Chabad rav, dies at 92

*1935*

Rashka Rivlin, wife of R' David Tzvi Rivlin, was according to the introduction of 'Ohalei Yosef' on the Talmud (Jerusalem 1956) and a letter from M.S. Slonim, the granddaughter—not daughter—of Hebron's chief rabbi R' Shimon Menashe Chaikin. Born in 5603, she lived to 92 and died in Tevet 5695 in Jerusalem.
