# Civil registration of the marriage in Moscow

*1940*

Without a Moscow residence permit, the bride-to-be had to drift among relatives, and anyone caught without papers was deported from the city within 24 hours. Because the author held a permit for his apartment in Cherbinov, the couple went to the registry office on 1 April 1940 and registered her legally as his wife. Only later did the idea of an actual wedding crystallise in her mind.
