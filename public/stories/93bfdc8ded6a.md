# Rebbe Rayatz raises possibility of trip to America

*1929*

Citing advice from R. Chaim Ozer Grodzinski of Vilna and the Chofetz Chaim, the Rebbe wrote that he might have to make the journey to the United States to put the relief effort for Russian Jewry on a stable footing. The trip would be deferred until after the matzah operation, then take shape via the Holy Land starting Elul.
