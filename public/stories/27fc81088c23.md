# R. Feigin summarizes Rebbe Rayatz's chassidus discourses

*1938*

On 15 Shevat 5698 (January 17, 1938), secretary R. Yechezkel Feigin wrote to his brother-in-law R. Sholom Posner reporting that until 19 Kislev the Rebbe Rayatz had delivered chassidus on every Shabbat as usual, but only before a small group of about 20-30 yeshiva students who rotated each Shabbat. Feigin also noted that the Rebbe had spoken at the beginning of Kislev about the need to travel to a sanatorium to renew medical treatment.
