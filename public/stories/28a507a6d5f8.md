# Rabbi of Lipovets relocates to Kiev

*1925*

With the rav gone, a less stalwart replacement appeared in public debates with Yevsektsiya activists, and the position of religious Jewry in Lipovets weakened sharply. The Yevsektsiya summoned the new rabbinic representative to a public disputation in the town club, where he conceded much of the anti-religious argument. The author, then a youth, attended and was deeply shaken.
