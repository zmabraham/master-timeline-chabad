# Secretariat writes to Sweden on behalf of Heshel Reizman and wife

*1946*

Reizman had married Sheindel and was living in Czechy near Liptovský Mikuláš, with a Swedish visa already in hand and a passport expected soon. Since his wife had no visa of her own, the Rebbe's office — likely writing to Yaakov Yisrael Zuber — asked that an additional affidavit be sent on her behalf so the couple could travel together.
