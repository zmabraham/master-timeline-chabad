# Agreement between Kollel Chabad and other kollelim in Hebron

*1875*

In 5635 the Chabad community in Hebron reached an agreement with the other Ashkenazi kollelim there that all funds entering Kollel Chabad's coffers in the name of the Hebron Ashkenazi community would be shared equally with members of the other kollelim. Oversight of the fund remained exclusively with Kollel Chabad's Hebron managers, with the official name being 'Kollel Ashkenazim Chabad of Hebron.'
