# Rayatz founds Agudat Chabad of Eretz Yisrael in Tel Aviv

*1941*

Under the Rayatz's direct supervision, Agudat Chabad expanded into a new umbrella organization rivaling the older Kollel Chabad structure based in Jerusalem. This created tension over whether oversight of chassidus study programs in Jerusalem belonged to the Tel Aviv-based Agudat Chabad or to Kollel Chabad.
