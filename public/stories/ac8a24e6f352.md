# State Department investigators meet Chabad leaders in New York

*1940*

After Max Rhoade's relentless lobbying, the visa division finally accepted the principle that the Rebbe's entourage might be admitted as the leadership of a religious hierarchy rather than each requiring an individual congregational sponsor. The officials received a detailed briefing on Chabad's history, structure, finances, and US synagogue network — a precondition for issuing the long-delayed visas.
