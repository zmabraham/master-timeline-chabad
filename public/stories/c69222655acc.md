# Facsimile reproduction of the Soncino Haggadah published in Leipzig

*1923*

Around 5683 (1923), a facsimile reproduction of the Haggadah extracted from the c. 1485 Soncino Machzor was issued in Leipzig. Illustration plates were supplied by Albert Frisch, with the title and printing notes hand-lettered by Franziska Baruch. Two copies were produced, one on slightly larger paper.
