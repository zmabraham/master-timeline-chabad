# Rayatz writes Agudas HaTmimim in America accepting their offer to help recover books

*1926*

Members of Agudas HaTmimim in America had informed Rayatz they had organized a society called 'Beis Rebbi' as a memorial to past Rebbes, whose aim was to help retrieve the books that had been improperly seized in Moscow. Rayatz responds with encouragement, urging them to consult lawyers and well-connected figures of standing and predicting that their effort would succeed with G-d's help.
