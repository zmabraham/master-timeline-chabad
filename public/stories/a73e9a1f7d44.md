# Rebbe Rayatz visits Vilna in summer 5692

*1932*

R. Yitzchak Dov Ushpal recalled that during the Rebbe Rayatz's Vilna visit in summer 5692 (1932), after davening the Rebbe went to his hotel where senior Chabad rabbis assembled for seudah shlishis. Among those present were R. Shlomo Eliyahu Ushpal of Kornitz, R. Kuperstock of Fastov, R. Mendelovitz, R. Leib Sheinin of Dokshytsy, and the rabbis of Dalhinov and Radoshkovitz.
