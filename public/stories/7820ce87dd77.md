# First letter to Rebbe outlining trip questions

*1987*

Levin asked whether manuscripts brought from Leningrad by Prof. A.I. Katz indicated more items still lay there, whether to chase materials seized in 1927, and whether to extend the search to Rostov and Dnepropetrovsk. The Rebbe answered tersely—'Why shouldn't they search there?!'—and refused conditional limits.
