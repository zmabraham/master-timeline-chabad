# Sponsoring synagogue closes immediately after Jacobson's arrival

*1925*

The synagogue that had submitted the official request papers to bring Jacobson to America met in a rented storefront established by a small group of men. The Shabbos after his arrival he prayed there, but the very next day the synagogue closed permanently. This made clear that the shul had been founded specifically as a vehicle of divine providence to bring him to America.
