# R' Raza Schneerson travels to Smolensk to plead for Rosenbaum

*1890*

Acting on the late Rebbe Maharash's counsel from the previous years, in 5650 R' Raza Schneerson traveled to Smolensk and presented himself to local leaders and chassidim on Rosenbaum's behalf. Although he could not obtain Rosenbaum's release, his intervention with the governor freed him from hard labor, secured him a separate cell, and won him kindly treatment from the warden including a private stove and a chest of books.
