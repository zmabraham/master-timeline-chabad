# Judges hold press conference defending Schneersohn library ruling

*1991*

Russian and foreign press attended with simultaneous English translation, and the chief judge detailed how Gubenko had written letters to multiple officials and phoned him repeatedly trying to alter or block the ruling. Gribenikov said any informal appeal would not delay execution, that if the library refused to comply within thirty days an enforcement officer would be appointed, and that police could be used to compel cooperation. He distinguished sharply between Khasbulatov's policy referral and Gubenko's improper political pressure.
