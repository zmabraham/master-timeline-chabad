# Jacobson granted yechidus with Rebbe Rayatz in Moscow

*1925*

The Rebbe Rayatz visited Moscow while Jacobson was lodged in the Abramov family's apartment. The yechidus was held at the home of one of the Tmimim—either the shochet R. Chaim Zalman Gordon or R. Shimon Yaakobson—with Sender Rappaport, son-in-law of Shimon Yaakobson, managing entries and exits. The local circle of Anash and Tmimim included craftsmen and stocking-makers but also pnimi'usdike chassidim with deep grasp of Chassidus, gathering Shabbos mornings for two hours of Chassidus study followed by avoda-style davening.
