# Passing of Mitteler Rebbe (Rabbi Dovber)

*1827*

On 9 Kislev 5588 (late November 1827) the Mitteler Rebbe, Rabbi Dovber Schneuri, passed away in Niezhin. His son-in-law Rabbi Menachem Mendel (the future Tzemach Tzedek) was the natural successor, but for an extended period he refused to formally accept the leadership of Chabad. During this transitional time the chassidim of the movement were unsettled about who would lead them.
