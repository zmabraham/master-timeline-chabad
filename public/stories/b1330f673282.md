# Yeltsin refuses Gubenko's request to halt Schneersohn library return

*1991*

Reported on Leningrad television and seen by congregants in the Moscow synagogue, Gubenko reportedly left the meeting in anger declaring that Yeltsin was prepared to destroy Russia. The episode was part of a broader pattern of interference by Gubenko, including political maneuvering with Gorbachev's office, pressure on the library director, and later a decision to dismiss the director for cooperating with the court.
