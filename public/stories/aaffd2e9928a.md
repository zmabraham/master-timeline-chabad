# Riga Jewish community formally invites Rebbe as chief rabbi

*1927*

Mordechai Dubin, a Chabad chasid and member of the Latvian Saeima, presented the invitation to Russian officials, who initially rejected it. Simultaneously Dr. Oscar Cohn delivered an invitation from Frankfurt's Jewish community. Dubin then leveraged his swing vote on the unratified Latvia-Soviet trade agreement to extract the exit visa from Moscow.
