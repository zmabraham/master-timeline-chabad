# Portrait of Yehuda Leib Ben-Ze'ev. Yehuda Leib Ben-Ze'ev, 20 Av

*1811*

Portrait of Yehuda Leib Ben-Ze'ev. Yehuda Leib Ben-Ze'ev (20 Av 5524, August 18, 1764 – 23 Shevat 5571, February 17, 1811) was one of the founders of the first generation of the Haskalah movement, and authored books of commentary and explanation on topics of study
