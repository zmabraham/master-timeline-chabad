# Photograph of Rayatz with future Rebbe before wedding

*1929*

Among the library's photographs is one from 5689 (1929), evidently taken close to the Rebbe's wedding — perhaps when he was already a chassan. The future Rebbe stands while his future father-in-law, the Rayatz, sits and holds his hand, beside the dining-room table that was apparently in Riga. A companion image from the same session shows the Rayatz seated at the same table beside his wife Rebbetzin Nechama Dina, studying a book.
