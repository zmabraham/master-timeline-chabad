# Tzemach Tzedek requests travel permits for his assistants to Petersburg

*1843*

On 17 April 1843 (29 Nissan 5603), the Tzemach Tzedek wrote to Governor Diakov from Vitebsk. He requested that his travel pass be expanded to include Shmaria son of Mordechai Paytelson, needed to translate since he spoke only Yiddish, and his son Leib Schneersohn, needed to assist due to his weak health. He attached the passports and asked to be permitted to arrive in time for the commission's opening.
