# Chabad mass pilgrimage to Lubavitch on Maharash's birthday

*1991*

On 2 Iyar 5751 (April 16, 1991), the birthday of the Rebbe Maharash, two busloads of Moscow yeshiva students, the girls of Beis Chaya Mushka, anash and tmimim from Leningrad, and two shluchim from Cherson converged on Lubavitch — likely the largest gathering of anash there since the yeshiva left in the summer of 5678/1918. The trip, led by Rosh Yeshiva R' Uri Kamishov and shluchim Yaakov Goldschmidt and Shlomo Wilhelm, included visiting the Ohel of the Maharash and the Tzemach Tzedek, writing a klali pidyon nefesh for the Rebbe's success and the coming of Moshiach, and touring the burnt-out courtyard of the Rebbeim where a Soviet post office was being built. The travelers returned to Moscow at 2:30 a.m.
