# Death of R' Yaakov Zevulun m'Shyuf at age 42

*1868*

Born around 5586 and brought to Eretz Yisrael as a four-year-old in 5590, R' Yaakov Zevulun m'Shyuf died in 5628 at age 42. He was the eldest son of R' Yosef Yehuda Leib m'Shyuf, who had made aliyah from Shklov to Chevron with his father-in-law's family.
