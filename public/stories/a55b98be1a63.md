# Rabbi Meir Ashkenazi relocates from Shanghai to New York

*1948*

Once in New York, members of Vaad Hatzalah initiated a din Torah accusing him of having diverted Mir Yeshivah funds to Chabad students in Shanghai. Ashkenazi argued that taking a small percentage was justified given the mortal risk of collecting funds at a neutral embassy under Japanese occupation. The rabbinical court ruled in his favor.
