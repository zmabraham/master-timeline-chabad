# Tomchei Temimim Montreal dedicates former orphanage as new home

*1943*

Chief Rabbi Yehoshua Hirshorn called for the day to be marked as a Yom Tov for Montreal Jewry. By this point Bronfman, who had earlier tried to push the yeshivah to Toronto, had come to see its value. The yeshivah continued to expand, with the Rebbe corresponding regularly on curriculum and staffing.
