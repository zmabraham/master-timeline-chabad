# Ma'amad committee founded under R' Moshe Dovber Rivkin in USA

*1928*

R' Yechezkel Feigin had written from Riga that Havlin and Rivkin would be enlisted to revive ma'amad fundraising in America. Because the Rebbe's household in Riga was under genuine financial strain — only about $300 per month was reaching him — Agudas Chassidei Chabad created a special ma'amad department chaired by R' Rivkin, with the tomim R' Elchonon Fogelman as a board member. A circular was sent to Anash, and the idea that ma'amad was its own primary obligation — not merely a line in the Agudah budget — began to take hold.
