# Alter Rebbe settles in Liozna and begins leading White Russian Chassidim

*1783*

From 5543 (1783) onward, the Alter Rebbe based himself in Liozna and assumed leadership of the Chabad Chassidim across White Russia. From there, Chabad communities began to organize in Lithuania as well, especially in Vilna and Vidz. This marked the start of a structured Chabad presence in the region.
