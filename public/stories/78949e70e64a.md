# Chai Elul farbrengen at Telz led by Greenglass

*1940*

After mincha at the home of R. Tzvi Merkin, close to three minyanim of students gathered, including the son-in-law of Reb Elchanan Wasserman. Greenglass spoke about the chiddush of the Baal Shem Tov, sang Chabad niggunim including the niggun of the Alter Rebbe, and recited the maamar of Yom Tov Shel Rosh Hashana 5666.
