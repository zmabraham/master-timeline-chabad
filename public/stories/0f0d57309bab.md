# R' Shalom Mordechai Chaikin publishes pamphlet honoring R' Dan Tomarkin

*1888*

In 5648 R' Shalom Mordechai Chaikin published a proclamation describing how the Tzemach Tzedek and his sons had ordained R' Dan Tomarkin of Rogachov as gabbai of Kollel Chabad, praising his forty years of trustworthy service in distributing the funds that sustained the Chabad community in Eretz Yisrael. The text was later reprinted in Migdal Oz.
