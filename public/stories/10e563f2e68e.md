# Riga Jewish community invites Rebbe to be chief rabbi

*1927*

On August 21, 1927 (23 Menachem Av 5687), the Riga Jewish community formally invited the Rebbe to become its chief rabbi. Mordechai Dubin, head of the Riga community and a member of the Latvian Parliament, presented the letter to Russian officials. Combined with Dubin's swing vote on the pending Russia-Latvia trade treaty, the invitation became the key lever for obtaining the Rebbe's exit visa.
