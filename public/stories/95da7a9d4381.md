# February Revolution topples Tsar Nikolai II

*1917*

The Rebbe Rashab responded with three open letters: one congratulating the Provisional Government, one urging chassidim to purchase shares in the new state loan, and one calling on chassidim and observant Jews to organize for upcoming communal elections now that Jews enjoyed full civil rights. He quipped that 'our God-fearing brothers excel at sluggishness.' Religious and Torah-aligned political parties began forming across Russia in response.
