# End of WWII celebrated by Samarkand chassidim

*1945*

When news arrived on 5 Sivan 5705 that the war was over, Levin and Michael Teitelbaum sat together drinking l'chaim in great joy. Others mocked their celebration — they had profited from valid wartime travel papers — but Levin gave thanks that the bloodshed of Jews had ended and that he might now leave the house openly and resume contact with the Rebbe.
