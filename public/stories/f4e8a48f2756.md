# Birth of Rabbi Yaakov ibn Tzur (the Yaavetz) in Meknes

*1673*

Rabbi Yaakov ibn Tzur, later known as the Yaavetz and one of Morocco's preeminent halachic authorities, was born on Shabbat, 27 Iyar 5433 (May 1673), in Meknes. He was the son of Rabbi Reuven ibn Tzur. Strikingly, on the day of his circumcision (5 Sivan 5433) the renowned Land of Israel emissary Rabbi Elisha Ashkenazi passed away in Meknes, evoking the Talmudic image of one sun rising as another sets.
