# Russian parliament votes against returning the books

*1992*

Just a day after Yeltsin assured Baker the books would come back, the Russian parliament held a prolonged session and the majority voted against return; some legislators called for Yeltsin's resignation over his stance. Only about 150 of 500 deputies were in the plenum. Simultaneously the new court panel reversed the original ruling, holding that the books need not be returned. On TV that evening Yeltsin promised he would find a way to implement his plans.
