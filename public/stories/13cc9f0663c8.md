# Rayatz instructs Atkin to send the Kavash vessels via the Rebbe

*1947*

Having fled the Soviet Union, R' Meir Atkin offered Rebbe Rayatz the heirloom vessels he had purchased from the estate of R' Chaim Kavash — including a spoon attributed to the Baal Shem Tov and a cup of R' Mordechai of Lechovitch. Rayatz replied on 29 Iyar 5707 directing him to entrust them to R' Benjamin Gorodetsky for safekeeping. Atkin, however, ultimately handed them to the Ramash (the future Rebbe) in Paris on 25 Sivan, before the Rebbe's return to New York.
