# Students receive first telegram from Rebbe Rayatz erev Yom Kippur

*1945*

On erev Yom Kippur 5706, the students in Shanghai received the first telegram from the Frierdiker Rebbe via R. Yaakov Yisrael Zuber of Stockholm, instructing them to file visa applications and reporting his wish to bring them to the United States. After more than three years of no contact, this brought tremendous joy — a triple celebration — and the news was marked with farbrengens, niggunim, and dancing on motzaei Yom Kippur.
