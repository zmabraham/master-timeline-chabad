# Passing of the Tzemach Tzedek; Maharash assumes Chabad leadership

*1866*

After the Tzemach Tzedek's passing on 13 Nissan 5626 (1866), his younger son Rabbi Shmuel became the fourth Rebbe in Lubavitch. His brothers — also prominent rabbis — established branches of Chabad in towns such as Kopust, Liadi, Nizhin, and Ovruch. The succession defined the geographical structure of post-Tzemach-Tzedek Chabad in Tsarist Russia.
