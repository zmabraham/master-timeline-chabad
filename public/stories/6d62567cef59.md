# Report on attendance at Ohel Yitzchak beit medrash

*1903*

R' Yehuda Leib Menuchin, the administrator of Kollel Chabad in Jerusalem, wrote in mid-Tamuz 5663 that he had sent the shamash Shalom Yaakov to Ohel Yitzchak the previous Shabbat to attend R' Yaakov ben Tzvi Trisspolsky's class and report on activity there. The report counted five Chabad members and more than ten other Jews regularly present in the beit medrash. Attendance would later grow significantly as Jews moved from the Old City to the new neighborhoods.
