# Three tzaddikim issue decree forbidding loans from Eretz Yisrael fund

*1781*

Around 5541 (1781), the three tzaddikim entrusted with managing the Eretz Yisrael fund in Russia issued a binding decree (with nidui and shamta) forbidding anyone, including themselves, from borrowing even a single peruta from the money collected. The decree was prompted by losses sustained when administrators had used the money for other mitzvah purposes.
