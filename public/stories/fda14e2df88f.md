# Rebbe Rayatz stays in Otwock for Tishrei 5691

*1930*

During Tishrei 5691 (autumn 1930), Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn (the Rebbe Rayatz) stayed in the Polish town of Otwock, as reported by the local 'Otwocker Tzeitung.' The visit drew significant local attention. The Library preserves a copy of the newspaper edition reporting on his stay.
