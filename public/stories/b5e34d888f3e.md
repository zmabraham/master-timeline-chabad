# Rebbe Rayatz instructs Warsaw students to escape to Vilna

*1939*

After the Soviets handed Vilna over to independent Lithuania, the Rebbe Rayatz saw an opening to save his yeshiva. In mid-Kislev he received bochurim including R. Avraham Yitzchak Garfinkel for yechidus, blessed their escape, and pledged the funds needed to cross the German-Soviet and Soviet-Lithuanian borders. R. Shmaryahu Gourary handed each escapee 100-150 zlotys for travel and bribes.
