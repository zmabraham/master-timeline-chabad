# Rebbe Rashab writes blessing to mother from Slavyansk

*1917*

While residing in Slavyansk during the upheavals of World War I, Rebbe Sholom DovBer of Lubavitch wrote a brief handwritten response to a mother who had sought his blessing. He acknowledged receipt of her letter and prayed that G-d grant her son a good heart to walk in the good and upright way. The note is dated Monday of the week of Parshat Re'eh, 5677.
