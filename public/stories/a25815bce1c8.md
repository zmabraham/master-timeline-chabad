# Rebbe Rashab's passing on 2 Nissan 5680

*1920*

Rabbi Sholom Dovber Schneersohn, the fifth Lubavitcher Rebbe known as the Rebbe Rashab, passed away on 2 Nissan 5680 (March 21, 1920). His son, the Rebbe Rayatz, subsequently recorded a chart of all the Chabad Rebbeim's lifespans and resting places. He had marked the place where he had stopped learning in a sefer some ten days before his passing.
