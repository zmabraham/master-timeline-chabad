# Rebbe Rayatz organizes his father's manuscript archive

*1920*

Rebbe Rayatz catalogued 1,173 maamarei Chassidus surviving in Rebbe Rashab's own handwriting and documented an estimated 700-800 additional maamarim recorded only by the chozrim. He preserved hundreds of original letters and acquired letters from their recipients, eventually amassing some 2,000 letters of his father by 1927. Twenty-five volumes of these maamarim were eventually published as Sefer HaMaamarim 5643-5680.
