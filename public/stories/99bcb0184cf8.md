# Rebbe composes letter to Chasidim on train from Russia

*1927*

Heartbroken by the boisterous Leningrad send-off, the Rebbe composed a letter to the Chasidim while still on the train. He wrote that 'the essential connection between us cannot be broken, nor does the fact that distance stands between us break that bond.' The letter was dated 24 Tishrei, 5688.
