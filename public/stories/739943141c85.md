# R' Yisrael Jacobson founds students' minyan in New York

*1934*

R' Yisrael Jacobson established a minyan in New York in 5694 for the young men he was teaching. R' Yochanan Gordon played a central role: he learned Chassidus with the students before davening and served as the Torah reader. This represented one of the early efforts to transplant Lubavitcher chinuch and chassidic practice to American soil.
