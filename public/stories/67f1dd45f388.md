# Wedding of R' Menachem Tzvi b. R' Shmuel Dovber of Borisov in Lubavitch

*1871*

In Adar 5631 the wedding of R' Menachem Tzvi son of R' Shmuel Dovber (RShD"M) of Borisov was held in Lubavitch. RA"Ch sent a gift to the father of the groom in advance, and RShD"M acknowledged it in a letter that also mentioned an unspecified request that RA"Ch wished him to bring before the Maharash.
