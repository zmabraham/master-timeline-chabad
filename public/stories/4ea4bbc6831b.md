# Montreal students form Ezras Achim relief group

*1946*

As the Shanghai students' travel approached, the previously rescued Tomchei Tmimim students in Montreal organized 'Ezras Achim' to collect tzedakah for the needs of the students still in Shanghai waiting to come to America. The Frierdiker Rebbe replied on 24 Adar Rishon 5706 (Igrot Kodesh vol. 13, letter 4897) thanking them for arranging help for their brother-students of Tomchei Tmimim and blessing them to soon be reunited in a life of Torah.
