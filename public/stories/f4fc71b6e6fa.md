# Frierdiker Rebbe arrested by Soviet authorities in Leningrad

*1927*

News of the arrest reached America only with significant delay. American Chassidim were caught off-guard and largely unaware of the mortal danger — they had not lived under the Soviet terror firsthand. Attorney Mr. Kramer turned to Washington through Senator Borah, who carried influence with the U.S. government and across the Atlantic, to seek the Rebbe's release.
