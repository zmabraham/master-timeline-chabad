# Yaari's comprehensive Haggadah bibliography published in Jerusalem

*1960*

In 5721 (1960/61), Avraham Yaari published in Jerusalem his Bibliografia Shel Haggadot Pesach, listing 2,717 editions of the Haggadah from the dawn of printing through 5720. The bibliography drew in part on the descriptions of Haggadot in the Lubavitch library that had been sent to him by librarian Chaim Liberman.
