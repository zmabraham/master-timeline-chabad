# Maarechet HaElokut printed in Ferrara

*1558*

R. Peretz HaKohen of Spain (1305-1370), a Talmudic commentator and kabbalist, authored Maarechet HaElokut. R. Yehuda Chayyat, an exile of the Spanish expulsion who helped disseminate Kabbalah in Italy, wrote the commentary Minchat Yehuda upon it. The combined work was printed in Ferrara in 5318 (1558).
