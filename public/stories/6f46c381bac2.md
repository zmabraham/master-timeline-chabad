# Passing of R' Avraham Dovid Lavut, rabbi of Nikolayev

*1890*

On 18 Adar 5650, Rabbi Avraham Dovid Lavut, grandfather of the future Rebbe (Ramash), passed away. He left a written request that the chassidim of Nikolayev appoint his grandson Rabbi Meir Shlomo Yanovsky as his successor, and that the chassidim of Romanovka appoint his son-in-law R' Isser Yanovsky. The Nikolayev appointment was carried out; Romanovka instead appointed R' Shaymen Atkin.
