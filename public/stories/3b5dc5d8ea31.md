# Senate in Petersburg confirms acquittal of Mitteler Rebbe

*1827*

Khovansky had appealed in 1827, arguing that uncollected Ma'amad contributions were destabilizing and could divert tax revenue. The Senate sided with the Mohilev court, ruling Ma'amad legal as voluntary support for a non-salaried rabbi. The decision arrived twelve days after the Rebbe's passing and permanently legitimized Ma'amad as the financial basis of the Chabad dynasty.
