# Elul 5686 Agudas HaTmimim meeting commits funds to print Likkutei Torah

*1926*

After receiving word from Warsaw that Likkutei Torah could be reprinted there for roughly six hundred dollars, the author hosted a meeting of Agudas HaTmimim in Elul 5686. Members pledged sums proportionate to their means — some five dollars, some ten. The amount still fell short of even a down payment, and serious progress only came months later with the Tevet seudah.
