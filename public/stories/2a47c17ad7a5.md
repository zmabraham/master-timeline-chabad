# Branch opened in Horodishtch

*1901*

On 20 Iyar 5661 (1901), R' Shlomo Zalman Havlin arrived in Horodishtch to head a new branch of Tomchei Tmimim, with 20 students under his care. The Rashbatz had been sent ahead by the menahel to organize matters. The branch operated until 5671, when Havlin moved to Hebron to open the Toras Emes yeshiva.
