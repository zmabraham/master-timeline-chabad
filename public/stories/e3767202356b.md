# Eshelon train halted near Tambov, refugees fear deportation to Siberia

*1946*

Rumors spread that earlier eshelonim were still circling within Russia and that the passengers might be diverted to Siberia. After a long wait the train suddenly resumed moving, and by the next day they could see it was headed via Kursk toward Ukraine and Poland. A later remark attributed by the narrator to an American general framed Stalin's decision to release the Jews as a miracle.
