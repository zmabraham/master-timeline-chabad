# Soviet raid scatters Tomchei Tmimim central yeshiva in Nevel

*1929*

Nevel had been the heart of the Tomchei Tmimim network in Russia, housing R' Nissan Nemenov, R' Zalman Kornitzer, R' Yehuda Eber's shiur, and R' Shmuel Levitin's kollel for rabbinical students. When the GPU came to arrest everyone, the entire institution dispersed: the middle and part of the youngest class went to Leningrad with R' Nissan Nemenov, the senior class transferred to Vitebsk, and R' Yehuda Eber's shiur passed through Vitebsk on its way to Yekaterinoslav.
