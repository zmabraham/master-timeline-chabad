# Pesach visit to Peter and Paul Fortress, site of Alter Rebbe's imprisonment

*1992*

During Chol HaMoed Pesach 5752 the author crossed into the Peter and Paul Fortress on Petersburg's Neva island and toured its prison cells. Guides explained that the current prison post-dates the Alter Rebbe — they pointed to a sparse grove not far from the present building where the older prison had stood before being demolished when the current one was built. Afterwards he toured the nearby palace housing the Asian Institute, where Shimon Yakerson showed him rare books and incunabula from the institute's collection and heard updates on the campaign to recover the Schneersohn library.
