# Kehot publishes introduction analyzing the Mitteler Rebbe's petition

*1947*

In 5707 (1946-1947), Kehot Publication Society issued an edition featuring an introduction by the Rebbe examining the petition written to Vitebsk's General-Governor Khovansky. The introduction argued against attributing the petition to the Alter Rebbe, demonstrating from internal evidence and historical circumstances that it was authored by the Mitteler Rebbe during his Vitebsk arrest. The analysis became foundational to subsequent Chabad scholarship on the imprisonment and acquittal.
