# Chabad delegation expelled from Moscow hotel, shelters at Chernobyl camp

*1990*

On Friday 4 Tevet 5751, hotel staff abruptly demanded the delegation's passports for visa stamping while OVIR moved to nullify their recently extended visas. A parallel five-week visa had been issued by the Russian Federation Foreign Ministry, sidestepping the central Soviet authorities. Veronika urged the group to leave the hotel for safety, and they spent Shabbat with roughly seventy children at a Chernobyl recovery camp, davening, learning, and discovering descendants of the chossid R. Shmuel Dobruskin among the staff.
