# R' Yechezkel Himmelstein flees Polotsk to Vitebsk for Rosh Hashanah 5689

*1928*

The closure of the mikveh in Polotsk forced its rosh metivta R' Yechezkel Himmelstein to relocate to Vitebsk for the Yamim Noraim. He prayed together with the Tomchei Tmimim bochurim in the Zaranover shul, an early sign of how Soviet pressure was beginning to splinter the yeshiva network.
