# R' Eber writes Rayatz detailed final report on yeshiva students

*1931*

On Isru Chag Shavuot (7 Sivan) 5691, R' Yehuda Eber wrote in detail from Vilna to the Rebbe Rayatz, reporting that Warsaw had about 25 students in the Kibbutz, 27 in the older shiur, and 25 in the younger. He warned that two qualified teachers in nigleh and chassidus would urgently be needed if the planned student transfer to Riga did not proceed soon.
