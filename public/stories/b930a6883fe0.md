# Winter 1772: Alter Rebbe and R. Menachem Mendel attempt debate with Vilna Gaon

*1772*

Hoping to refute the slanders being told to the Vilna Gaon and forestall a herem against chassidim, the two emissaries went to his home twice and were turned away each time. When city leaders pressed the Gaon to debate, he left the city and only returned after they had departed.
