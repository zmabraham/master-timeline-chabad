# Expulsion of Jews from Moscow

*1891*

In 1891 (תרנ"א), the Czarist government issued the Moscow expulsion decree, forcing Jewish residents to leave the city. The two Chabad rabbis serving Moscow at the time, R' DovBer Efrat and R' Chaim Yaakov Widerwitz, both had to depart. Widerwitz emigrated to America, while Efrat made aliyah to Hebron where his grandmother and the Slonim family lived.
