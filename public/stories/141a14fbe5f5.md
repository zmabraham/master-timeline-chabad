# Meeting with Israeli Consul Aryeh Levin about Schneerson Library

*1991*

On Tuesday, 5 Adar 5751, Rabbi B.S. Kunin, R. Y.Y. Aharonov, and the diarist visited Israeli Consul Aryeh Levin to discuss the library's return. They informed him of the Ministry of Culture's falsehoods, Gorbachev's instruction to return the books, and Gubenko's refusal to cooperate. Levin promised to assist with President Herzog's letter and inquire about the best route to deliver it to Gorbachev.
