# R' Alter Simchowitz leaves Warsaw to return to Eretz Yisrael

*1924*

After roughly three years directing Tomchei Tmimim Warsaw's spiritual life, Simchowitz returned to Eretz Yisrael at the Rebbe's direction to take a mashpia role at Toras Emet. He would later be briefly recalled to Warsaw in 5689 before returning again to Jerusalem permanently.
