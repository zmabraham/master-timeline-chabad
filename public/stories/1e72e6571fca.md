# Chaim Liberman flees Warsaw to Riga

*1939*

With the outbreak of WWII, the Frierdiker Rebbe's secretary R. Chaim Liberman escaped Warsaw and reached Riga on 8 Tishrei 5700. From Riga he conducted rescue advocacy for the Rebbe and his family, remaining there until they too were rescued. He later traveled with the Rebbe and his household to the United States.
