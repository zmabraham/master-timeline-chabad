# Levin issues open letter rejecting Moscow Schneerson wing proposal

*1997*

Levin cited the Rebbe's 5751 ruling that opening a Chabad library in Russia would only fuel arguments against the books' return. He argued the 12,000-book collection was not particularly rare, with a market value under a quarter-million dollars, and held only sentimental value. He warned that sensational press coverage misled the Russians into thinking they had something marketable, undermining return efforts.
