# Shabbos Pinchas 5674 at the Zaalshe dacha

*1914*

On Shabbos Parshas Pinchas 5674 the author was again at Zaalshe, joined by R. Shilem Kuratin the mashgiach on Chassidus and guests including the maskil R. Pesach Frenkel and the tamim R. Shraga Feivish Zalmanov of Warsaw-Klimovich, son-in-law of R. Yosef Yuzik Horowitz of Warsaw.
