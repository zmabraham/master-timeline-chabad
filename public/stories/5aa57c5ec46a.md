# R' Shmuel Dovber of Borisov acknowledges RA"Ch's wedding gift

*1871*

On 26 Shevat 5631, in the run-up to his son's wedding in Lubavitch, R' Shmuel Dovber of Borisov wrote to RA"Ch thanking him for the gift and the blessing. He added that he would not be lax in raising RA"Ch's request before the Maharash, though the specific nature of the request is not preserved in the surviving correspondence.
