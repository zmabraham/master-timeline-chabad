# Passing of R. Avraham Chaim Rosenbaum in America

*1913*

R. Avraham Chaim Rosenbaum passed away on 25 Kislev 5674, the first night of Chanukah, corresponding to December 24, 1913. He died in the United States, where he had settled some fifteen years earlier. A chassid who had been close to both the Tzemach Tzedek and the Rebbe Maharash, his surviving Torah correspondence preserves valuable insights into the chassidic thought of late nineteenth-century Russia.
