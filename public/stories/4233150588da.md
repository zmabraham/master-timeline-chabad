# Rebbe Rayatz declines move to America, decides to remain in Europe

*1930*

After his US visit had raised hopes of relocation, the Rebbe wrote his American Chasidim explaining that European communal duties, the yeshivas, and weakness of health made a move premature. Quoting the talmudic principle of not demolishing one synagogue before building another, he implied that leaving Europe would weaken Chabad's work there. He left the door open to a future move with proper preparation.
