# Rabbi DovBer asked to authenticate documents attached to denunciation

*1825*

On 5 October 1825 (5 Marcheshvan 5586), investigators in Vitebsk asked Rabbi DovBer to confirm that the attachments to Simcha Keysin's denunciation were written in his own hand. The same day, two of his chassidim, R. Zalman Roizes of Slonim and R. Shlomo Freides of Shklov, were also brought before the investigators and interrogated as part of the gathering of evidence.
