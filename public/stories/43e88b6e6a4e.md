# R. Shmaryahu Yehuda Leib Medalia appointed rabbi of Vitebsk

*1908*

R. Shmaryahu Yehuda Leib Medalia took up the rabbinate of Vitebsk in Tishrei 5669, with his appointment documented in correspondence of the Rebbe Rashab. Within roughly a year and a half he would serve as Vitebsk's delegate to the 1910 St. Petersburg rabbinical conference, where he carried the city's anash petitions on chinuch and rabbinic credentials. Medalia would later become one of the most prominent Chabad rabbinic figures of the Soviet period.
