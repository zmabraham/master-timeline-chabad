# Pinchas Mordechai Teitz founds Yavneh society in Livenhof

*1931*

During Sivan 5691 Rabbi Teitz's son Pinchas Mordechai Teitz visited the Rebbe in Riga and described the new association 'Yavneh' he had formed to organize the Livenhof youth around study of Tanach, Kitzur Shulchan Aruch, Mishnayos and Aggadah, and to strengthen mitzvah observance.
