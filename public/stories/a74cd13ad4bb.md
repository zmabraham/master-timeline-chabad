# R' Meir of Bychov returns from his first shlichut in Av 5551

*1791*

Having departed in summer 5549, R' Meir returned to Eretz Yisrael in Menachem Av 5551 as the shadar 'of this year.' His on-time return, immediately following the lifting of the Tiberias plague quarantine in Tammuz, was greeted as a salvation by the Chasidic community. A general letter from the Tiberias Chasidim and a separate letter from R' Avraham of Kalisk both announced his arrival.
