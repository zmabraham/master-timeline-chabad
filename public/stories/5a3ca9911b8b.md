# Rebbe Rayatz visits Rakishok in Lithuania

*1931*

After the Lithuanian chassidim sent delegations led by Rabbi Yisrael Noach of Kupishok requesting a visit, the Rebbe traveled to Rakishok for nine days. He received hundreds for yechidus until late at night, addressed yeshiva students from Telz and Ponevezh, met with Yavneh school representatives and Tiferes Bachurim leaders, and delivered several maamarim including 'V'habriach Hatichon' and 'Atah Echad'.
