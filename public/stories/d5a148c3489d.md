# Rebbe delivers Simchat Torah talk demanding Jewish street outreach

*1930*

The Rebbe lamented that Latvia was 'Jewish only in appearance,' tasked Dubin's group with transforming Jewish education, and declared, 'You need to stand on the street corners and pull Jews into a synagogue to study.' He then sternly told those unwilling to put on tefillin to leave before hakafot — a directive later revealed to have foiled a Peretz Communist youth-club plot to disrupt the celebration.
