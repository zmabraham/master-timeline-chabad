# Rebbe offers R' Yitzchak Dov Ber Ushpal the Braslau yeshiva leadership

*1934*

After R' Shneur Aron of Braslau responded affirmatively, the Rebbe informed him that the candidate would be named the following week. The same day he wrote to Ushpal asking whether he could accept the appointment as rosh yeshiva and mechanech at the new Tomchei Temimim branch in Braslau. The Braslau branch never materialized; some months later Ushpal was instead sent to head the Vilna yeshiva.
