# Babinovitch branch opens

*1908*

In summer 5668 (1908), a Tomchei Tmimim branch opened in Babinovitch as an intermediate stage between the chadarim/shiurim and the Great Hall. R' Zev Wolf Halevi Lewitin oversaw nigleh and R' Shlomo Ben Dovid Risen oversaw chassidut. The Rebbe Rashab corresponded about it from Marienbad. The branch later relocated to Shchedrin.
