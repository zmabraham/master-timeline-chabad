# Feigin reports rescue logistics struggles in Riga

*1940*

On 12 Adar Rishon 5700, the secretary R. Yechezkel Feigin wrote to his brother-in-law R. Shalom Posner that his travel arrangements were stalled while he sought funding sources. He was in discussion with HIAS and hoped a solution would soon emerge, noting the Rebbe was planning to depart Riga around March 4.
