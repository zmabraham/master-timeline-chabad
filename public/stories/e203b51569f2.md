# Midrash Shmuel yeshiva opens in Old City Jerusalem

*1944*

The yeshiva was led by Rabbi Yitzchak Avigdor Ornstein as the Rayatz's representative, with Rabbis Alexander Yudasin and Yitzchak Naeh on the standing administration. Its stated mission was to anchor a Chabad center in the Old City for serious learners, teaching Shas, poskim and Chassidus in the path of Chabad's rebbeim.
