# Rebbe Rayatz leaves Otwock as war engulfs Poland

*1939*

As bombings intensified, the Rebbe departed Otwock for Warsaw with deep bitterness on his face. He blessed those gathered, telling them to accept the yoke of Heaven, and that God would protect them and the Rebbe wherever each would be. The original plan to reach Riga proved impossible; the family ended up at the Schmotkin apartment, Muranowska 32, Warsaw.
