# Tzemach Tzedek asks Alter Rebbe a Yevamot question

*1813*

In manuscript 1024 (folio 133a), later printed in Tzemach Tzedek Milu'im vol. 5 §59, the young Menachem Mendel records a question on tractate Yevamot that he asked 'before my grandfather and teacher the Gaon.' The note must therefore predate the Alter Rebbe's passing on 24 Tevet 5573, showing the Tzemach Tzedek was already engaged in halachic study during his grandfather's lifetime.
