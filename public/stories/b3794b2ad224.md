# Rebbe Rayatz thanks Rashaz Gurevitch for visit to Dr. Kahane on book list

*1946*

On 1 Tammuz 5706, the Rebbe acknowledged Rashag's report on Rashaz Gurevitch's visit to Rabbi Dr. David Kahane regarding the books found at the Warsaw Jewish community archive. The Rebbe urged Gurevitch to hurry the cataloging and shipment of the recovered volumes.
