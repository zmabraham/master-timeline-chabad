# R' Shlomo Zalman HaKohen returns to Tiberias with companions

*1785*

R' Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk writes that in mid-Cheshvan 5546 the emissary R' Shlomo Zalman HaKohen arrived safely back in Tiberias, accompanied by R' Tzvi Hirsch Besht of Vitebsk and R' Gershon Shochet. All three were in good health and brought the entirety of the trumah funds raised abroad.
