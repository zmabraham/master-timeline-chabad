# Alter Rebbe meets R. Pinchas of the Hafla'ah at Rovno

*1771*

The Alter Rebbe's later letter recalls a 'beloved acquaintance from days past in Mezeritch and Rovno' with R. Pinchas Horowitz. Since R. Pinchas left for the Frankfurt rabbinate by Tishrei 1771, the meeting at Rovno must have occurred no later than that date — establishing that the Maggid had already moved from Mezeritch to Rovno by then.
