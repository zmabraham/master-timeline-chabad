# R. Shmaryahu Mekler elected Vitebsk delegate to the Moscow conference

*1917*

In 5677, in the upheavals surrounding the Russian Revolution, the Vitebsk askan and gevir R. Shmaryahu Mekler was elected as the city's delegate to the rabbinical conference held in Moscow. He had earlier been among the lay leaders who backed the 1910 cheder-teachers' petition to the St. Petersburg conference, and his role in 1917 marks the continuity of anash communal organizing into the revolutionary period.
