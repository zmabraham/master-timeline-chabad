# Death of Zisil Ornstein, daughter of R' Yitzchak of Karlin

*1886*

Recorded in 'Avnei Zikaron' volume 12, Zisil's death is noted as 12 Adar II 5646. She was the daughter of R' Yitzchak the rabbi of Karlin and outlived her husband R' Uri by seven years. Together they had been part of the Slonim contingent that settled in the Chabad communities of Eretz Yisrael.
