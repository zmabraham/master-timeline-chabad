# Examinations at Tomchei Temimim attended by Rebbe Rayatz

*1910*

The memoirist and his brother-in-law Alter Beilin were tested by R. Raphael Cohen of Germanovitch and R. Avraham Eli Plotkin in Ketubot 74b. The exam took place Friday morning of erev Shabbat, and the Rebbe Rayatz, then director of Tomchei Temimim, entered during the testing. The author notes the awe his presence inspired and that they passed successfully.
