# Kfar Chabad Haggadah with additions on matzah campaign

*1977*

In 5737 (1977), Kfar Chabad published a Haggadah following the Alter Rebbe's nusach with added sections on chametz and matzah, the Mivtza Matzah campaign, and the 12 Pesukim (twelve verses). The 69-page duodecimo edition served the Israeli Chabad community. It exemplified the Rebbe's worldwide matzah campaign.
