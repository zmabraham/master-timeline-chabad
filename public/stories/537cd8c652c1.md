# R' Hillel of Paritch declines to serve as borer due to a vow

*1855*

The Tzemach Tzedek and his son MaHaRiL both wrote to the famed mashpia R' Hillel of Paritch (author of Pelach HaRimon) asking him to serve as their borer. Hillel replied that 31 years earlier he had taken a vow on da'as ha-makom never to act as dayan or borer, only as a deciding shalish — a safeguard he refused to lift even for the Rebbe's family. He had instituted this restriction because borerim sometimes act 'shelo k'das' by advocating only for the side that chose them.
