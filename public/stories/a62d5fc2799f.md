# Rebbe Rashab promises 'Reid' and Maamar that became Kuntres HaAvodah

*1909*

Overcome by heat and pressure of the crowd at the Simchat Torah 5670 farbrengen, the Rebbe Rashab stood and told the chassidim he would not release them until he gave them a 'reid' as Tomchei Temimim required, and a maamar. The discourse was eventually published in 5671 (1910/11) as Kuntres HaAvodah, though it was also called Kuntres 5669.
