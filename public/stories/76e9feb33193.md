# NYC Mayor Beame presents Rebbe city medallion shaped as a cup

*1977*

On 11 Nissan 5737 (March 30, 1977), New York City Mayor Abraham Beame presented the Rebbe with a medallion of the city, fashioned in the shape of a cup. The Rebbe himself wrote the date on an accompanying note, and the medallion and note are now displayed in the New York case of the library exhibition.
