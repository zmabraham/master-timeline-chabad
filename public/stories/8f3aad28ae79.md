# Lithuanian chassidim leaders released on 5th night of Chanukah

*1798*

Parallel to the arrest of the Alter Rebbe, twenty-two leading chassidim of Lithuania had been detained in early Cheshvan 5559 for interrogation. On 19 Kislev the Tsar ordered all of them released; the order reached Lithuania on 28 Kislev, and they were freed to their homes by the time of lighting the fifth Chanukah candle.
