# Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Gordon in an interview with JEM (5778). Rabbi Yosef

*1954*

Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Gordon in an interview with JEM (5778). Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Gordon (born 5714, 1954) is a shliach of the Rebbe in Australia, engaged in education at Yeshivas Ohelei Yosef Yitzchak - Lubavitch - Melbourne, and works on shlichus in Tasmania, Australia
