# Rebbe Rashab letter to son on Husiatin manuscript purchases

*1907*

The Rebbe identified specific manuscripts worth pursuing (Igros #961), suspecting Livnat HaSapir might be the autograph of R' Avraham Saba based on a marginal signature. Eighteen of the marked manuscripts were ultimately acquired and now hold catalog numbers 1972-1989 in the library. Each purchased volume bears Rebbe Rayatz's notation: 'This we bought.'
