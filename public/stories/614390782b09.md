# Father drafted to World War I army

*1917*

During the height of World War I, Ephraim Shmuel was taken to the front, forcing Leah Beila to run their small shop and continue the children's education on her own. She was unusually learned for a woman of her region, having attended cheder herself, and taught the children Hebrew reading, Tanach, grammar, and arithmetic. Ephraim Shmuel returned home in 1918 and reopened his cheder.
