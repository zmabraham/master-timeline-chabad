# Fiftieth wedding anniversary celebration

*1990*

On 9 Tammuz 5750 (July 2, 1990) the family marked fifty years since the author's 5700 wedding in Moscow with a small jubilee gathering at his daughter Leah's home. The author repeated themes from the maamar 'Kol Hamaarich Be'echad' that the Frierdiker Rebbe had said on 12 Tammuz 5700 in New York during the week of his sheva berachos, and made a siyum on Maseches Nedarim.
