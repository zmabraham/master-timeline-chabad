# Testimony of investment in mortgaged building in Fes

*1710*

R' Yitzchak rebuilt half of a shared wall in the mortgaged upper story, spending on lime, sand, bricks, debris removal, and workers' wages and meals. The owner R' Chaim Yaakov consented before the court to add this sum to the mortgage, so that on redemption the principal and addition would be paid together. The ruling was signed by R' Yehuda ibn Attar and other Fes dayanim.
