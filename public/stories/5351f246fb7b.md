# R. Levin receives first letter from Frierdiker Rebbe at Pocking

*1947*

After Levin and others demanded the camp leadership find a way to reestablish direct contact with the Rebbe, the Paris lishka began conveying letters. The Frierdiker Rebbe wrote to Levin on 23 Shevat 5707 with a 'baruchim haba'im' blessing for him and his wife and wishes for success in material and spiritual matters. Levin had waited for such a letter throughout the entire flight from Russia.
