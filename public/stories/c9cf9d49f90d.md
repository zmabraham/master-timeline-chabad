# Rebbe responds with joy to news of his students' arrival in Montreal

*1941*

On 7 Marcheshvan 5702 (October 28, 1941), the Frierdiker Rebbe replied to Rabbi Rotstein. He wrote that the news had revived him physically and was better for his weakened health than any medicine. He prayed that Hashem would help the students settle and succeed in their learning and avodah shebalev, and use the opportunity Providence had granted them to enliven Anash.
