# Rayatz writes to R. Shlomo Zalman Havlin about Kollel matters

*1922*

On 10 Shvat 5682 (Feb 1922), Rebbe Rayatz wrote letter 106 to R. Shlomo Zalman Havlin in Jerusalem, thanking him for regular updates and expressing hope that the kollel was being run faithfully along the lines his late father had outlined in tarav. He also referenced his 24 Tevet letter to Naeh, which Havlin had not yet seen.
