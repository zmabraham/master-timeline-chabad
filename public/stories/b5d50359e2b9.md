# Calabrian etrog identified by the Rebbe Rashab on Erev Sukkot 5679

*1918*

The etrog was sent by R' Shmuel Gurary and delivered by R' Dovber Ganzburg of Odessa amid wartime disruption of supply routes. Throughout Sukkot the Rebbe permitted every chassid to bless the etrog but allowed no one — not even his son the Rayatz — to perform na'anuim with it, and asked each person whether their hands were dry. He sat in the sukkah from 6 to 10 a.m. each day attending to the chassidim.
