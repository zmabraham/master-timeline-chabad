# Greenglas reports widening rescue correspondence to the Rebbe

*1940*

Following the Rebbe's directive, Greenglas wrote from Vilna to bochurim in Sidlowice, Suchedniow, Chmielnik, Pociejow, and Lodz, naming specific talmidim to bring along. He also reported on shtot rabbanim who had hosted and fed Tomchei Tmimim students in Otwock during the war's opening months, including Rabbi Yechiel Yeshaya Weinberg of Yendrzejov.
