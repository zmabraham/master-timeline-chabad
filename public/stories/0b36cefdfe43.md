# Montreal students thank Rotstein and Chicago Lubavitchers for reception

*1941*

On 16 Marcheshvan 5702 (November 6, 1941), the students wrote from Montreal to Rabbi Rotstein in Chicago thanking him for the warm reception, the $25 stipend they had received via Rabbi Rubinstein, and the provisions prepared for the remainder of their journey. They confirmed they had settled and were beginning to establish Yeshivas Tomchei Tmimim in Montreal per the Rebbe's special directive.
