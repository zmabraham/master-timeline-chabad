# Tsar Alexander I issues Jewish Statute regulating rabbinate

*1804*

The 1804 statute declared the rabbinate an office of honor rather than a paying position, forbidding rabbis from collecting funds beyond their fixed communal salary (Section 52). Section 53 allowed a faction to build its own synagogue when communal unity broke down, but limited each town to one official communal leader. Two decades later, the informer Shimcha Kissin invoked Section 52 to accuse Rabbi DovBer Schneuri of illegal fundraising.
