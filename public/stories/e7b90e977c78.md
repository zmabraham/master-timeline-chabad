# Toldot Yaakov Yosef — first printed chassidic book

*1780*

Toldot Yaakov Yosef by Rabbi Yaakov Yosef of Polonne, a disciple of the Baal Shem Tov, was printed in Koretz in 5540 (1780). It was the very first printed chassidic work — no chassidic book had been published during the lifetimes of either the Baal Shem Tov or the Maggid of Mezritch.
