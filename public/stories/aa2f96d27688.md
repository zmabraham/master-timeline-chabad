# Russian Revolution ends restriction on Jewish presses

*1917*

The Rebbe Rayatz noted that the Tzarist limitation of only two Jewish presses in Russia — in Vilna and Zhitomir — persisted from the 5597 decree until the 1917 revolution. This 80-year bottleneck was a major reason chasidic publishing remained sparse through the eras of the Maharash and Rashab. After the revolution Hebrew publishing in Russia briefly opened up before new Soviet restrictions emerged.
