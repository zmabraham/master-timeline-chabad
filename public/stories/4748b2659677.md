# Rosh Hashanah 5671 journey from Shchedrin to Lubavitch

*1910*

Eighteen students obtained permission to travel only after depositing five rubles each for return travel. Joined by chassidim from Bobruisk-area towns, they rented a dedicated train car from Zlobin to Orsha and spent the journey singing chassidic niggunim. R. Shaul Dov Zislin led the lively farbrengen on the train, drawing curious onlookers from other cars.
