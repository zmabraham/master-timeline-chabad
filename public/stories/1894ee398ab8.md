# Breakthrough meeting with Dmitry Likhachov on Schneersohn library

*1990*

On 8 Tevet 5751 the delegation met Dmitry Likhachov, the renowned Leningrad scholar overseeing the Russian Federation's rare cultural collections. Initially favoring a general principle of state custodianship, Likhachov was won over by Rabbi Shlomo Cunin and R. Kogan's exposition of the library's history and meaning. He attempted to call Gubenko on the spot and then drafted letters to Gorbachev and Justice Minister Lushchenkov recommending the books be returned. The night before, the delegation had also consulted journalist Viktor Louis, who counseled sustained pressure backed by the threat of litigation.
