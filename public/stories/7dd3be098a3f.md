# Violent opposition forces Kuperstock back to Fastov

*1930*

Kuperstock had traveled to Globoke for Shabbos Bereishis but encountered intense opposition. Youths jeered him in the Lubavitcher shul after Torah reading, and rowdies brandished their fists at him when he tried to speak in the Starasele shul. By nightfall a coalition demanded he leave the town that very night. He wrote to the Rebbe on 28 Tishrei 5691 describing the events, thankful that he had escaped with his life.
