# Plan to transfer Warsaw 'olam' students to Riga falls through

*1931*

In summer 5691 (1931) a plan was developed to move the advanced students of the Warsaw 'olam' to Riga, where the Rebbe Rayatz was then residing. R' Shneur Zalman Gurarie was among those selected for the move. When the plan ultimately failed, the Rebbe instead dispatched Gurarie to a number of towns in Volyn to attempt opening branches of Tomchei Tmimim there.
