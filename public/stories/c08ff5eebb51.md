# Khovansky transfers case to Mohilev province for trial

*1825*

On 17 November 1825 (19 Kislev 5586), Khovansky transferred the entire investigative file on Rabbi DovBer to the government of Mohilev province, since Lubavitch and Babinovich fell under that jurisdiction. He simultaneously assigned the chief prosecutor of Mohilev to supervise the trial that would follow.
