# Passing of Rabbi Avraham Zev Sharfstein

*1953*

Rabbi Avraham Zev Sharfstein, who had joined the management of the Shomrei Shabbos free-loan society in 5707 to fill R' Tzvi Hirsh Padnes's place, passed away on 21 Kislev 5714. After his death, R' Yochanan Gordon ran the gemach kupah by himself until his own final day on Erev Rosh Chodesh Elul 5729.
