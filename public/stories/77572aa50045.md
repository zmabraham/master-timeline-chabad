# Linov speaks at the Kinus Hashluchim event at Tomchei Tmimim Yeshiva Elad

*1968*

Linov speaks at the Kinus Hashluchim event at Tomchei Tmimim Yeshiva Elad (5783). Rabbi Belinov passes before the Rebbe during the kuntreisim distribution. Rabbi Avraham Asher Belinov (born 23 Kislev 5729, 1968) is the rosh yeshiva of Tomchei Tmimim Yeshiva Elad
