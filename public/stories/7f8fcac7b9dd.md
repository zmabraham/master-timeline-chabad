# Exhibition of Rabbeinu Nesieinu (2017)

*2017*

In the spring of 5777 (2017), the Lubavitch Library held its fifteenth themed exhibit, again focused on the Chabad Rebbes. The display included manuscripts in the handwriting of each successive Rebbe, personal items such as the Mitteler Rebbe's tefillin pouch and the Rebbe Rashab's passport, and selected first editions of their works.
