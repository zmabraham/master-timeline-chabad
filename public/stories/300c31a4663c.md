*1860*

— 80 Jewish families. In the year 5544 (1784) there were in Lubavitch about 50 Jewish families. In the year 5573 (1813) there were in Lubavitch about 110 Jewish families. In the year 5620 (1860): there were in Lubavitch 1516 souls, of which 978 were Jews
