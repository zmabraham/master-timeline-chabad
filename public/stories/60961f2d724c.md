# Rebbe Rayatz leaves Soviet Russia

*1927*

After years of persecution under the Communists, the Rebbe Rayatz departed Russia in late Tishrei 5688. Immediately upon his exit, Chabad chassidim in Warsaw appealed to him to settle in Poland, arguing it would allow them and the chassidim in the Polish-ruled regions of Lithuania (Vilna, Daksht, Dolhinov, Glubokie, etc.) to remain connected to their Rebbe.
