# Simchat Torah 5723 hakafot and 'Stav Ya Pitu' niggun

*1962*

After the late-night hakafot of Simchat Torah 5723, Chassidim waited as the Rebbe was set to distribute mashke. The Rebbe recounted the story behind the niggun 'Stav Ya Pitu' and began to sing it. Standing right beside the Rebbe, Levin watched as the Rebbe repeated the Russian-lyric melody many times until R' Yoel Kahn finally picked it up — since the wave of bochurim from Russia had not yet arrived.
