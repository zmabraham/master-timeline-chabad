# R. Yehuda Leib Freidin elected rabbi of Fastov

*1933*

R. Yehuda Eber, head of Tomchei Tmimim in Warsaw, wrote to the Rebbe on 12 Sivan 5693 reporting that Freidin had been elected rabbi in Fastov. The journal 'Shaarei Tziyon' (vol. 13, no. 6-12) also announced his appointment. The general kehilla's rabbi, R. Shmuel Dovid Walkin, initially opposed it and even summoned Freidin to a din torah in Vilna, but tensions eventually subsided.
