# Frierdiker Rebbe cables son-in-law about manuscripts

*1940*

The cable referred to the trunks of seforim and ksav-yad in the future Rebbe's possession, including his wedding gift books, photographic volumes of the Rebbe Rashab's maamarim, and his own Reshimos. Many of these manuscripts, preserved through the wartime ordeal, are today housed in the Chabad library.
