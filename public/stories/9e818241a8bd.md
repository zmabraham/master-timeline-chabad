# Denunciations sent against Grodno rabbinical assembly

*1903*

Opponents of the Orthodox rabbinical assembly escalated their resistance by sending official denunciations to Grodno authorities. Rebbe Rashab condemned the audacity, noting that beyond the threat to the assembly itself, the rabbis were being publicly disgraced before the gentiles. Despite this, the bureaucratic process for authorization continued; the governor had requested files on each registered participant and the permit was expected within roughly two weeks.
