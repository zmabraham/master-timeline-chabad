# R' Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk's letter on declining Volhynia support

*1782*

In 5542 (1782), R' Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk wrote a letter noting that financial support from Volhynia had dropped sharply, sending only fifty gold pieces. He explained that since beginning his service as a leader, he had moved his residence from Volhynia to Russia, which may have weakened the relationship. The letter is preserved in Likutei Amarim, letter 5.
