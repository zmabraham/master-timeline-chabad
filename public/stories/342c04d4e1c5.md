# Rebbe Rayatz sends detailed organizational instructions for Warsaw yeshiva

*1921*

The Rebbe instructed that the institution be called 'Merkezet Tomchei Tmimim Lubavitch in Warsaw' and named R. Dovber Moshe Shmotkin, R. Shraga Feivish Zalmanov, and R. Shneur Zalman Shmotkin as the core of a five-member governing committee. The letters specified standards of yiras shamayim, character development, and daily conduct expected of the Tmimim.
