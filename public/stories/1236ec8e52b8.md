# Rebbe writes R. Yehuda Eber on Łódź yeshiva's future

*1931*

Writing on 14 Menachem Av 5691, the Rebbe Rayatz urged R. Yehuda Eber to spend several days in Łódź to evaluate the situation. He clarified that the Łódź branch was intended to host shiurim only, with no large independent study hall — that role being reserved for Warsaw. The letter reflects the structural division the Rebbe envisioned among the Polish branches.
