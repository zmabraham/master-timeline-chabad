# Diragin threatens self-immolation with Chabad manuscripts

*1991*

Press reports on the afternoon of Erev Yom Kippur carried Diragin's threat to set himself and the manuscripts on fire rather than let them go back to Chabad. Police were sent to the library, though the follow-up was not known to the delegation. The threat was rebroadcast on Soviet television, and Rabbi Yitzchak Kogan publicly answered the library staff in a televised reply.
