# Shu"t HaRashba printed in Bologna

*1539*

In 5299 (1539), a volume of 'Teshuvot She'elot' by Rabbi Shlomo ibn Aderet (Rashba) containing 1,255 responsa was printed in Bologna. It became part 1 of the Rashba responsa series, joining the body of his thousands of rulings on questions sent to him from across the Jewish world.
