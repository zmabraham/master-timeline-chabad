# Synagogue land dispute with neighboring bank in Moscow

*1991*

For a month the bank next to the Marina Roscha synagogue had been encroaching on synagogue property to build extended parking, ignoring police, letters and protests. When workers arrived on Thursday with asphalt the yeshiva crowd dismantled the construction; police, summoned again, sided with Chabad and the bank was forced to restore the area and pay damages. The same day a slanderous newspaper article attacked the synagogue, apparently from tenants still occupying its upper floor.
