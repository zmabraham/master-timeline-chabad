# Rebbe's instruction confirmed to Rabbi Cunin

*1992*

After Veronika instructed the shluchim to occupy themselves only with Torah and prayer for a week while she met with government offices, Rabbi Cunin reported the meeting back. Leibel Gruner relayed that Cunin had said nothing was being done according to her instruction. The Rebbe responded in Yiddish: 'So it should be — that is what was agreed.'
