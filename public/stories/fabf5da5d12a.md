# Chelm hanhalah confirms receipt of Rebbe's monthly stipend

*1936*

On 22 Kislev 5697 the Chelm yeshiva managers Yitzchak Yaakov Rosenbaum and Avraham Yitzchak Kornblit wrote the Frierdiker Rebbe thanking him for the monthly support, noting that they had no other source to pay the maggidei shiurim for the last two weeks. They also reported on the 19 Kislev farbrengen and asked the Rebbe to confirm Tanya study with the second class.
