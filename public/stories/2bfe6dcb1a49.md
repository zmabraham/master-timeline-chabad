# R' Shlomo Yehuda Leib Eliezerov becomes rabbi of Hebron

*1903*

At the beginning of Iyar 1903 (תחילת אייר תרס"ג), R' Shlomo Yehuda Leib Eliezerov was accepted as the permanent rabbi of Hebron, succeeding R' DovBer Efrat who had moved to Jerusalem. On 25 Iyar, R' DovBer Efrat printed a notice in HaChavatzelet redirecting all donations to be sent to the new rabbi.
