# Governor Golitsyn endorses lifting Tzemach Tzedek's surveillance

*1847*

On 7 March 1847 (2 Nissan 5607), Prince Golitsyn replied from Vitebsk to Count Orlov. He reported that State Councillor Gamalin had confirmed Schneersohn's title as Honorary Citizen and that the six-year gendarmerie surveillance found his conduct exemplary. Golitsyn formally endorsed lifting the secret watch that had begun in 1841.
