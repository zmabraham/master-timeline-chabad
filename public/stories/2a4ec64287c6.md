# 52 US visas secured for Tomchei Tmimim students

*1940*

Writing to R. Yaakov Katz, the Rebbe announced the breakthrough that 52 entry permits for students and 47 for rabbis, their families and rashei yeshiva had been secured. The first list contained mostly senior Tomchei Tmimim talmidim from the Otwock branch and became the basis of the first rescue convoy through Russia and Japan.
