# Rebbe's 91st birthday celebrated with kos shel berachah

*1993*

On 11 Nissan 5753 (April 2, 1993), Rabbi Yitzchak Kaduri sent a 'cup of healing' inscribed with a blessing for the Lubavitcher Rebbe Menachem Mendel Schneerson on his 91st birthday. The cup carried the inscription invoking blessings for the Rebbe, son of Chana, and is preserved among the personal items associated with the Rebbe.
