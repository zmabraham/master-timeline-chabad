# Family homes in Lyadi reported burned in war's aftermath

*1813*

In the winter of 5573 (early 1813), shortly after the Alter Rebbe's passing, the Mitteler Rebbe wrote that he had heard from a trustworthy person that the family's houses in Lyadi had been consumed by a wartime fire. Any seforim or remnants of the Alter Rebbe's own manuscript writings that had remained in the Lyadi home were lost in this second fire — possibly including the last surviving fragments of his glosses on Shulchan Aruch.
