# Tikkunei Zohar printed in Kapust by Yehuda Yaffe

*1810*

This authentic Kapust edition of the Tikkunei Zohar carried a censor's approval dated 1810. Years later, a clandestine reprint forged a title page dated 5585 (1825) but copied this same 1810 censor approval, revealing the forgery. The Yaffe family press was a leading Hebrew printer in White Russia during the early nineteenth century.
