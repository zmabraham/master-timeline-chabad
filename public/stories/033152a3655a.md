# Yahrzeit visit to grave of R' Levi Yitzchak Schneersohn in Alma-Ata

*1991*

After mikveh and Shacharit at a local shul, the delegation went to the gravesite. R' Kotlarsky and the diarist spoke (the latter about the arrest file then being negotiated for return) and R' Kamenetzky spoke in Russian, after which R' Y.L. Raskin read the general Pan. Strong winds and a technical airport shutdown delayed the return flight to Moscow several hours, during which they davened Mincha as the Rebbe had directed and R' Kotlarsky formally closed the Third European Shluchim Kinus.
