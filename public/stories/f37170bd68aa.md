# Chabad activism on behalf of Rabbi Shlomo Maidanchik, Efrai

*1999*

Chabad activism on behalf of Rabbi Shlomo Maidanchik, Efraim Wolf, Yosef Blau, Berke Wolf. After his discharge from the IDF, he was appointed secretary of the Kfar Chabad vocational school, and in 5759 (1999) was elected to serve as deputy mayor of Lod and in charge of the welfare portfolio
