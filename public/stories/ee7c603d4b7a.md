# Chabad community of Jaffa announces construction of its synagogue

*1903*

On 24 Iyar 5663, leaders of Kollel Chabad recorded in the kollel pinkas that 'in Jaffa Anash are building a beis midrash, and it is already complete up to and including the dome; finishing touches remain.' This was the first synagogue dedicated to Chabad chassidim in Jaffa.
