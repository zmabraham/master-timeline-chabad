# Rashab delivers Simchat Torah warning about Kaiser Wilhelm

*1915*

The Rashab told the Chasidim: 'I know the kaiser well from the thirty years that I have been traveling outside Russia. He is an apikores (heretic), and under his rule I cannot dwell.' He recalled how during a public speech in Berlin, the kaiser had stared at them sharply and had police remove them, the Rashab interpreting it as Wilhelm already plotting war.
