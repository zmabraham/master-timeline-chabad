# Friday-night farbrengen with R. Yisroel Jacobson in Prague camp

*1946*

On Erev Shabbat Ekev, 19 Av 5706, bottles of mashke were brought to the camp from the city. At the Friday-night meal R. Yisroel Jacobson farbrenged with the group — Levin's first time experiencing a personal emissary of the Rebbe at a tisch. Jacobson, who otherwise ate only food brought from America, accepted chicken that Itta Devorah had koshered in Katowice once told her father was R. Meir Gorkov, and blessed her with offspring.
