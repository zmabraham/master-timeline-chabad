# An ancient goblet called the Goblet of the Four Rivers, given as a gift

*1993*

An ancient goblet called the Goblet of the Four Rivers, given as a gift and present by Rabbi Yitzchak Kaduri to the Rebbe in honor of his 91st birthday on the eve of 11 Nissan 5753, April 1, the evening of April 2, 1993 of their counting. Rabbi Yitzchak Kaduri, called "the Elder of the Kabbalists"
