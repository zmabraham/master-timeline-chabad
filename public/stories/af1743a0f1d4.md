# Arbitration agreement before the Mitteler Rebbe between Litoslavsky and Averba

*1814*

On 24 May 1814, landowner Ivan Litoslavsky and Yeshaya Berkovich Averba of Surazh district, Vitebsk province, signed an agreement in Lubavitch placing their 7,000-ruble debt dispute before the Mitteler Rebbe for binding arbitration. The agreement, witnessed by the Vilna merchant Shmaryahu Benyaminovich Berkin, declared that both parties would accept the rebbe's ruling without appeal. The document was later submitted as evidence to prove that the rebbe had violated Russian law by acting as arbitrator between a Jew and a non-Jew.
