# Paris assembly convened for Soviet Jewry aid

*1928*

The assembly drew Chief Rabbi Israel Levi, Chief Rabbi Eisenstadt, R. Moshe Lieber, Baron Pierre Ginzburg, S. Pollak, Mr. Unger of JCA, Mr. Bigart of the Alliance, Mr. Tedesco of the Paris Consistoire, Mr. Warnser as Baron Rothschild's secretary, and attorney Matieu Miller. The meeting consolidated French support for the Rebbe's work.
