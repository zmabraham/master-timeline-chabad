# R. Gershon Yehuda Leib Kugel passes away in Vilna

*1877*

His son R. Shlomo recorded in manuscript 584 that his father R. Gershon Yehuda Leib died on Motzaei Shabbos Parshas Nitzavim 5637 (1877) and was honored with burial in Vilna on 18 Elul, after midday. The yahrzeit is observed on 18 Elul. With his passing and the earlier move of the mashpia R. Hendel Kugel to Lubavitch, leadership of the Plescenice chassidim passed to R. Avraham Chaim Rosenbaum.
