# Death of R' Avraham Chaim Rosenbaum's wife in America

*1904*

R' Yehoshua Kastrel wrote a letter of condolence to R' Avraham Chaim in 5664 (1904), noting that he presumed Avraham Chaim was now living with his youngest son Yosef, who had married in America. Kastrel admitted he barely knew either the deceased wife or Yosef personally.
