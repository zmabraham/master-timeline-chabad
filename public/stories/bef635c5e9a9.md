# R' Asher Grossman signs introduction to Vilna Tanya

*1899*

His introduction details the painstaking emendation process — collating manuscripts held by descendants of the Tzemach Tzedek — and credits R' Moshe Weiler of Krislave for collecting numerous addenda to Iggeres HaKodesh. The introduction was attributed publicly only after Rebbe Rashab insisted R' Asher's name be added.
