# Vilna yeshiva confirms $100 stipend from Rebbe Rayatz

*1930*

Beyond communal donations and the Vaad HaYeshivot subsidy, the Rebbe Rayatz sent a monthly stipend from Riga to support the Vilna yeshiva. The administration's signed receipt for $100 covering Sivan was preserved, demonstrating the ongoing financial lifeline from the Rebbe's office.
