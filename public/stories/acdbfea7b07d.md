# Passing of Rabbi Aharon David Gordon in Shklov

*1851*

Rabbi Aharon David Gordon (Shklover), stepson of Rabbi Avraham Avli Posveler of Vilna, passed away in Shklov on 12 Av 5611 (August 9, 1851). He and his brother had organized about sixty of Rabbi Avraham Avli's responsa, intending to publish them as Be'er Avraham, though that edition never appeared. After his death the manuscripts passed to his son.
