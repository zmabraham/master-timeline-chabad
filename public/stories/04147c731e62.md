# R' Asher ben Shimshon of London donates 1000 grush for Hebron mikveh

*1847*

After lengthy debate about building a ritual bath in Hebron, the wealthy London philanthropist R' Asher ben Shimshon donated 1000 grush through the Amsterdam Pekidim and Amarkalim in 5607. Difficulties securing a suitable site led local administrators to request the funds be redirected, which the Pekuam refused without the donor's explicit consent.
