# Annual Tomchei Temimim celebration in New York

*1942*

On Erev Rosh Chodesh Tammuz 5702 (14 June 1942), the central yeshiva organization Tomchei Temimim held its annual celebration in New York. A photograph captures the Rayatz speaking while standing, with the Rebbe physically supporting him with both hands. Also present in the images are Dr. Leo Jung, the Rashag, R. Joseph B. Soloveitchik, R. Dovber Chaskind and others.
