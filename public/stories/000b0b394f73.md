# Sequel to 'MiBeis HaGenazim' published

*2018*

On 5 Teves 5778 (December 2017), Rabbi Shalom Dovber Levin, director of the Lubavitch Library, signed the preface to a new book continuing the work of the earlier album 'MiBeis HaGenazim,' which had accompanied the winter 5770 exhibition. The new volume collected the guide booklets the library had printed for each exhibition since 5754, together with summaries of those exhibits for which no booklet had been printed. Together the book documented 15 exhibitions across more than two decades.
