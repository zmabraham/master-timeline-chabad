# Rabbi Zusia Pruss (third from right) in the Six-Day War (5727

*1937*

Rabbi Zusia Pruss (third from right) in the Six-Day War (5727). Rabbi Zusia Pruss (Zusha Gross) (born in the year 5697, 1937) served in the past as principal of the 'Torah U'Melacha' high school in Tel Aviv and in subsequent years as inspector of the 'Or A' school network
