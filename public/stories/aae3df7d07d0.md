# Death of R' Uri Ornstein of Slonim

*1879*

R' Uri Ornstein, a Chabad chasid who had made aliyah from Slonim, served two terms as a Chevron Chabad emissary totaling twelve years. He died in Jerusalem on 17 Adar 5639. His wife Zisil, daughter of R' Yitzchak of Karlin, died seven years later on 12 Adar II 5646.
