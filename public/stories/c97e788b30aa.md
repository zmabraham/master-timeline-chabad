# Mogilev High Court acquits the Mitteler Rebbe on 11 Kislev 5587

*1826*

On November 29, 1826 — 11 Kislev 5587 — the First Department of the Mogilev High Court issued its verdict in favor of Rabbi DovBer (Berke Schneur) of Lubavitch, finding that the charges of unlawful collection of money from his chassidim could not stand. The decision was reached without opposition from the gubernia prosecutor general. This judgment, while not formally confirmed by higher authorities for several more months, secured the Rebbe's freedom from criminal jeopardy.
