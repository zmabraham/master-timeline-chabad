# Levin writes to Moscow librarian Goldenberg requesting Polyakov book list

*1993*

Thanking Goldenberg for helping his colleagues view books, Levin asked for the list of over 100 Polyakov books in the rare-books museum so he could compare them to the Schneerson catalogue. He framed this as a small first step before tackling the larger collections of 2,000 books in Moscow and 10,000 in the Khimki depot.
