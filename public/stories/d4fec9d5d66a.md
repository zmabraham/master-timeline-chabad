# Rebbe Rashab letter on destruction in Lubavitch

*1919*

On Rosh Chodesh Adar Sheni 5679 (1919), the Rebbe Rashab wrote about events in Lubavitch: the courtyard and houses had been confiscated and great destruction wrought. Book crates, cabinets, and furniture had been taken and much was broken. Valuable utensils worth great sums were left behind, and they had reportedly turned the brick house into a club out of spite.
