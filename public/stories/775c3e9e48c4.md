# R' Yehuda Eber reports Warsaw yeshiva mashpia shortage to Rebbe

*1931*

On 13 Iyar 5691, R' Yehuda Eber reported to the Rebbe Rayatz that the chief weakness at the Warsaw yeshiva, since R' Alter Simchowitz's return to Jerusalem, was the absence of capable senior staff. R' Zalman Shmotkin studied with the students and supervised them, but his other responsibilities prevented him from giving the role the full devotion it required.
