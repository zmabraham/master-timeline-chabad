# Wedding of Nachum Mordechai of Chortkov in Potik

*1862*

Rabbi David Moshe of Chortkov, son of the Ruzhiner Rebbe, sent a circular letter from Potik in 5622 announcing the wedding of his son Nachum Mordechai to his niece, daughter of his brother Rabbi Shalom Yosef of Sadigura. The wedding was scheduled for the Shabbat after Shavuot, and the letter — addressed to chasidim in Kalisz, Kotarid, Miyaskovke, Krati, Nemirov, and Krasna — requested contributions to defray its substantial costs.
