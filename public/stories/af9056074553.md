# Chicago Sentinel reports starving Lubavitch students

*1921*

The Sentinel reported that 'the yeshivah students who have evacuated Lubavitch for Rostov-on-Don are starving.' The financial pressures, famine, and disease prompted some students to leave the yeshivah and return home. Once one of the great centers of European Jewish scholarship, the yeshivah was whittled down to just forty students in Rostov.
