# Family departs Pocking DP camp on forged Prague passports, end of Iyar 5707

*1947*

The Paris lishka headed by R. Benyamin Gorodetzky supplied Pocking refugees with passports lacking the required Czech-German border stamps because the bearers had never actually been in Prague. Boarding the train at Munich, the family was discovered by a strict American military conductor who put them off at Karlsruhe, the last station before the French border. After hours of uncertainty, a station official ruled the documents valid and they continued on the next train to Paris, ending the Pocking-Germany chapter that had spanned late Elul 5706 through late Iyar 5707.
