# R' Yosef Morgenstern, Rebbe of Kotzk-Pilov, passes away

*1939*

On 14 Adar 5699 (5 March 1939) the Admur of Kotzk-Pilov, R' Yosef Morgenstern, passed away. He left no descendants, and his chassidim sought a successor among his close circle. Their choice fell on R' David Teiblum, then a maggid shiur at Tomchei Tmimim Warsaw.
