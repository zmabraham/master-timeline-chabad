# R' Mordechai of Lechovitch letter on new EY gabbaim

*1805*

R' Mordechai of Lechovitch wrote on 11 Kislev 5566 urging that whoever is for God should ensure their tzedakah is given to the appointed treasurers - R' Aharon of Ostravna and his colleague R' Yisrael. This formalized the new fund-collection structure established under R' Avraham of Kalisk's leadership outside the Alter Rebbe's network.
