# First Mesibos Shabbos children's parade

*1943*

While the Rebbe headed the Merkos L'Inyonei Chinuch before his nesius, he ran the Mesibos Shabbos campaign for children. In 5703 (1943) a public parade was organized through main streets, with children carrying signs about Torah and mitzvos after hearing the Rebbe speak. A special booklet with photos was published. The parade later became an annual Lag BaOmer event.
