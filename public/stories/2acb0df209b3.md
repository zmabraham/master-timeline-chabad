# 12 Tammuz farbrengen in Malakhovka marks year since author's arrival

*1934*

On 12 Tammuz 5694 (June 25, 1934)—the anniversary of the Rebbe Rayatz's liberation from Soviet imprisonment—the author returned from Moscow to Malakhovka, completing a full year since first arriving in summer 5693. A farbrengen was held in the Malakhovka shul. He resumed sleeping and learning there, joined by a growing number of Anash families and bochurim fleeing intensifying decrees throughout the country.
