# Pocking Shavuos memorial morphs into Lubavitch chinuch drive

*1947*

Acting on a rebuke from the Ramash that Pocking's Lubavitchers were sitting idle while DP children went untaught, Levin and Mikhael Teitelbaum independently summoned all 7,000 refugees on Shavuos 5707 to a 'memorial' at the Chabad shul. R. Avraham Eliyahu Plotkin and R. Yisrael of Nevel delivered fiery drashos linking Dovid HaMelech to the chinuch of children. After a stormy split between Mizrachi and Cherut factions, an agreement emerged: Lubavitcher melamdim would supplement the camp's secular schools with Torah hours, planting decades of warmth toward Yiddishkeit in Pocking-raised children.
