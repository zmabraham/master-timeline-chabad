# Rebbe declares 'a healing soon with song and praise'

*1992*

After two grave days when the Rebbe was at times unconscious, on Thursday evening he already stood for Maariv prayers with assistance, saying he would soon be able to do so alone. Rabbi Dov Ber Lazar reported that the Rebbe told Leibel Gruner: 'S'vet zayn a refuah b'karov b'rov shira u'b'zimra.' At the general gathering downstairs in the shul, the news brought joy and consolation to all assembled.
