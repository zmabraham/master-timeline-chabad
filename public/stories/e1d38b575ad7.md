# R. Zalman Butman sends coded report from Riga to Rebbe Rayatz on arrests

*1941*

On 1 Adar Aleph 5701 (27 February 1941), R. Yehuda Eber forwarded from newly Soviet-occupied Riga a letter that R. Shneur Zalman Butman had smuggled out of the USSR to the Rebbe Rayatz in America. The body of the letter, encoded in 'ksav stam' lettering and other cryptic devices to evade NKVD censorship, named anash arrested and executed by the Soviets in recent years, those who had been released, conditions of shechita and food in Moscow and Leningrad, and the impossibility of writing openly abroad. Eber, who had escaped Warsaw to Riga but was unable to obtain US visas, would be murdered together with the rest of Riga's Jews after the German conquest later that year.
