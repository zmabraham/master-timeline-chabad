# Yeshiva students travel to Rostov for Rosh Hashana with Rebbe Rashab

*1917*

For Rosh Hashana 5678 the entire student body of the main study hall traveled to Rostov to spend the High Holy Days with the Rebbe Rashab. The narrator stayed with his previous host R' Mordechai Gertzulin on Staraya Pochtovaya 122. This was part of an established pattern of Tomchei Temimim students gathering with the Rebbe for major holidays.
