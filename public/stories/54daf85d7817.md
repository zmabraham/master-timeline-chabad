# Eighty rabbis invited to Grodno religious assembly

*1903*

After years of failed attempts, real preparations finally materialized in 5664 to convene an assembly of Orthodox rabbis under government authorization in Grodno. R. Eliyahu Akiva Rabinovich of Poltava served as the official convener, and the proposal had the backing of R. Chaim Soloveitchik of Brisk. The Rebbe Rashab proposed a preliminary smaller gathering of senior rabbis to define an agenda before the main assembly.
