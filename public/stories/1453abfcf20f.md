# Future Rebbe Rayatz defers Hebron yeshiva affiliation due to Russian unrest

*1905*

In response to a Hebron request to add Magen Avot as a branch of Tomchei Tmimim, the Rayatz explained that the present year's 'great storms in our country, from within and from without'—a reference to the 1905 Russian Revolution upheavals—made expansion impossible. He expressed hope that with God's help fruitful approaches would emerge.
