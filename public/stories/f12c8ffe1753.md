# Power of attorney to litigate in Jerusalem

*1843*

Among the official acts of R' Shmuel Halevi as head of the Hebron Chabad community was a formal power of attorney from 5603 authorizing representation in legal proceedings in Jerusalem. The document is part of a series of signed instruments from his tenure as rabbi and maggid of Hebron. It reflects the community's regular need to handle disputes outside the city through proxies.
