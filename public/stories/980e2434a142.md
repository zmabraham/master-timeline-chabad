# Passing of R' Shalom Shachna, son-in-law of the Alter Rebbe

*1807*

From 1800 until his death, R' Shalom Shachna had occupied himself with printing the Talmud and Tur in Slavita on behalf of the Alter Rebbe, partnering with the Rebbe's brother R' Mordechai of Orsha. He likely also collaborated on printing the original Tanya in 1796–1797.
