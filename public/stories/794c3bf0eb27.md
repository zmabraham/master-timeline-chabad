# Alter Rebbe accompanies aliyah group as far as Mohilov on the Dniester

*1777*

Reaching Mohilov in Podolia near the Turkish border, the leaders agreed that the Alter Rebbe should not continue but remain in Russia to care for the spiritual and material needs of the chassidim. R. Mendel and his colleagues parted from him with great weeping. The Alter Rebbe remained in Mohilov for about a year and a half, where he completed his sixteenth review of the entire Talmud, standing day and night.
