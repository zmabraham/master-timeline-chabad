# Death of MaharI"L of Yanovich

*1826*

MaharI"L's death can be bracketed by two letters: a responsum the Tzemach Tzedek addressed to him in late summer 5585, and a letter from R' Yissachar Ber Horowitz dated 28 Adar I 5586 (March 1826) that already refers to him as 'the late.' He was buried in Yanovich, where local residents long revered his memory.
