# R' Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk announces emissary's safe return

*1781*

Writing from Tiberias in Nisan 5541, R' Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk reports that 'our beloved, wondrous rabbi R' Shlomo Zalman HaKohen, our emissary,' has returned safely with his person and finances intact. The letter does not specify when he had departed for the diaspora. Scholars conjecture, based on R' Shlomo Segal's 5539 journey, that R' Shlomo Zalman HaKohen left on his first shadar mission in 5540.
