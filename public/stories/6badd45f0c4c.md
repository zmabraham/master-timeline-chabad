# R' Shmuel Segal arrives in Holy Land from Rohatchov

*1824*

Per the 1839 census, R' Shmuel Halevi, then 64 years old, made aliyah from Rohatchov in 5584 (1824). Prior to his immigration he had served as a moreh tzedek (mo"tz) in Rohatchov and was a signatory on a charity assessment there in winter 5570 with the Alter Rebbe. After his arrival he became the leading rabbinic figure of the Chabad community in Hebron.
