# Ulmanis coup; Rabbi Hodakov appointed chief inspector of Jewish schools

*1934*

Prime Minister Kārlis Ulmanis disbanded political parties and parliament, but Dubin's personal bond with Ulmanis kept Agudath Israel functioning. Dubin arranged for Rabbi Chaim Mordechai Aizek Hodakov, the eighteen-year-old founding dean of Derech Eretz, to take over school inspection. Hodakov shifted historically secular Yiddishist schools toward Torah-based curriculum, replacing Sholem Aleichem with religious study and instituting prayer and yarmulkes.
