# Peace agreement ends Hebron rabbinic dispute

*1886*

After years of bitter dispute over the Hebron rabbinate, prompted by Markado Romano's conflict with R' Eliyahu Mani, the Jerusalem rabbis brokered a reconciliation. In Tammuz 5646 a peace agreement was signed, with both R' Eliyahu Mani and R' Rachamim Yosef Franko among the signatories. The accord established Mani as the Sephardi chief rabbi of Hebron and Franko (the Charif) as Av Beit Din.
