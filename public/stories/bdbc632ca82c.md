# Rabbi Yissachar Ber Halevi Horowitz appointed Rabbi of Lubavitch

*1832*

Horowitz served as town rabbi during much of the Tzemach Tzedek's leadership era. He was followed in the position by Rabbi Avraham son of Avigdor Hakohen. These local rabbinic appointments operated alongside the Chabad leadership presence in the town's main courtyard.
