# Hanhalla warns Rayatz of severe budget crisis at Tomchei Tmimim Warsaw

*1931*

On 22 Shevat 5691, the management of Tomchei Tmimim Warsaw wrote a desperate letter to the Rebbe Rayatz describing the yeshiva's dire financial condition. The Great Depression had cut income from America, and they considered shrinking the institution by reducing students and staff. They requested guidance and that the Rebbe telegraph New York for emergency support.
