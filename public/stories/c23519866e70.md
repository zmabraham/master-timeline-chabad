# Rabbi Zalman Aharon advocates for Avraham Chaim in Smolensk

*1889*

Acting on the Rebbe's instruction from the year 5650 (1889/1890), Rabbi Zalman Aharon Schneersohn (the Raza) traveled to Smolensk to advocate for his imprisoned relative. Although outright release was impossible, he persuaded the governor to exempt Avraham Chaim from hard labor and grant him a separate cell apart from the other prisoners. He also notified the leading members of the local community and Chabad chassidim so they could continue supporting Avraham Chaim, and his intervention paved the way for further leniencies.
