# R' Baruch ben Eliyahu prints Tanya in Shklov

*1806*

In 5566 (1806) the Shklov printer R' Baruch ben Eliyahu, working with his partner R' Yitzchak ben Shmuel, brought Tanya to press. Earlier authorities mistakenly identified this printer as a grandson of the Alter Rebbe (son of Freida); in fact he was the well-known printer who had previously worked in Shklov, Dubrovna and Ladi.
