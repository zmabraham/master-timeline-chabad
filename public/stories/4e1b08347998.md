# Rebbe Rayatz writes to Shemtov about memoir collection

*1949*

The letter, published as Iggros Kodesh vol. 10, no. 3365, directed Shemtov to correspond with Anash and Tomchei Tmimim alumni in Eretz Yisrael and elsewhere, gather their memoirs, and forward everything to the Rebbe. The Rebbe assigned a specific editor to organize the incoming material. This launched the project that produced multiple memoir volumes.
