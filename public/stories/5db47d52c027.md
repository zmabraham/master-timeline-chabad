# Hammer and Kunin meet Soviet Culture Minister Zakharov

*1988*

On 24 Tevet 5748—the yahrzeit of the founder of Chabad—the delegation finalized a supplemental memorandum laying out the documentary case that the Schneersohn collection had grown to roughly 7,000 titles by 1915. R' Kunin then joined Hammer for the high-level meeting with the Culture Minister, the escalation toward which the impasse had been pointing.
