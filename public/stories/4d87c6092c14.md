# Rayatz writes to son-in-law on Erev Rosh Chodesh Nissan

*1933*

The letter (vol. 15, no. 5533) recounts the warm reception by the young temimim, the recital of maamarim including 'V'Hayah Kaasher Yarim Moshe', the Purim seudah lasting past midnight, and the Shabbos meals among anash that ran far longer than usual.
