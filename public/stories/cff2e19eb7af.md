# Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak HaLevi Kirsch — photo from Rabbi Kirsch's yeshiva study years

*1961*

Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak HaLevi Kirsch. Photo from Rabbi Kirsch's yeshiva study years at the yeshiva in Kfar Chabad — second row, first from the right. Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak HaLevi Kirsch (born in 5721, 1961) is an emissary of the Rebbe to Tzur Hadassah and rabbi of the community.
