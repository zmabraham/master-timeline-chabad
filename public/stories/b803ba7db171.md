# GPO agents search author at Homel train station after Purim

*1925*

As the author boarded a train in Homel headed to Leningrad in the week after Purim, two GPO agents signaled him to follow them. They led him to their building next to the station and searched his clothing, body, and belongings. Among the searchers was a man who himself had smuggled goods for him from Minsk, but they found nothing and he managed to catch his scheduled train.
