# Ladino Haggadah with Hebrew commentary printed in Livorno

*1904*

In 5664 (1904), Shlomo Belforte & Co. printed a Haggadah in Livorno with Ladino translation and several illustrations. Maariv prayers were appended along with Shir HaShirim. The 42-leaf volume served the Ladino-speaking Mediterranean Sephardic community.
