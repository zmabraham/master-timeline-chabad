# Alter Rebbe writes to rabbis of Vilna on polished knives and peace

*1811*

The letter, printed as Iggeret 61 in volume one of his published correspondence, was addressed to the great rabbis of Vilna and asked them to deal mercifully with the chassidim under their authority. The Alter Rebbe acknowledged the rabbis' authority and urged that 'all the people will have peace.'
