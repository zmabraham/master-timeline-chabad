# Vilna mitnagdim alter Tzemach Tzedek's text on the Gra

*1884*

Piskei Dinim III, 69c originally read of an argument of the Vilna Gaon, 'this is no proof.' At the Vilna printing in 5644, soon after the passing of the Rebbe Maharash and during an interregnum in the Chabad leadership, type-setters substituted 'and I in my poverty have not merited to understand his holy words.' The Frierdiker Rebbe later recounted to the Rebbe that as soon as the forgery was discovered in Lubavitch, the offending pages were reprinted with the original wording, though some falsified copies could not be recalled.
