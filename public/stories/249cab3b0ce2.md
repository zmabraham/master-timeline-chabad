# Khovansky assigns investigators to lead the inquiry against Rabbi DovBer

*1825*

On 2 October 1825 (2 Marcheshvan 5586), Governor Khovansky handed responsibility for the inquiry to his special officials, Colonel Loginov and Court Counselor Markov, to gather evidence on the alleged illegal collection of funds by Rabbi DovBer. This marked the formal opening of the state's criminal investigation against the Mittler Rebbe.
