# R' Alter Simchowitz sent to found Yeshivat Toras Emet in Hebron

*1911*

The Rashab dispatched seven Tomchei Tmimim students to the Holy Land to seed a Chabad yeshiva in Hebron. Simchowitz returned to Lubavitch at the end of that same year and continued studying there until 5680, serving as a chozer who transmitted the maamarim of the Rashab.
