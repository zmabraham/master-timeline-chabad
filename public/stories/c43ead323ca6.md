# Additional positions: Chief shliach of the Rebbe to the Commonwealth of Independent States

*1964*

Additional positions: Chief shliach of the Rebbe to the Commonwealth of Independent States. Rabbi Lazar speaking. Rabbi Shlomo DovBer Pinchas Lazar (known in short as R' Berel, born on the 8th of Sivan 5724, May 19, 1964) is the chief shliach of the Rebbe to the state of Russia, and serves as its chief rabbi
