# SILA departs Lubavitch for Bukhara

*1907*

After spending Pesach and several weeks in Lubavitch consulting with the Rebbe Rashab, SILA left for Bukhara on 8 Iyar 5667. A letter from the Rebbe Rashab that day notes 'today our relative R' Shneur Zalman traveled to Bukhara via Moscow.' He was accompanied by a Samarkand resident who had been waiting for him in Moscow.
