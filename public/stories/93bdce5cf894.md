# Chassidut exhibit marking 300 years since the Baal Shem Tov's birth

*1998*

The Chabad library curated an exhibit of foundational chassidic works printed during the first jubilee following the Baal Shem Tov's passing (5520–5570). The exhibit opened toward Shavuot — the Baal Shem Tov's yahrzeit — and ran until 18 Elul 5758, marking 300 years since his birth on 18 Elul 5458 (1698).
