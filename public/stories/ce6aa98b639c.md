# Klausenberger Rebbe asks Rayatz how to relate to Israel's declaration

*1949*

On 17 Shevat 5709, the Klausenberger Rebbe approached Rebbe Rayatz on behalf of Agudas HaAdmorim with the question of how the Rebbes should relate to the recent declaration of the State of Israel. Rayatz replied that the matter was very serious and would have to be resolved by the senior Rebbes, and that he would give his opinion only after receiving their written, signed question.
