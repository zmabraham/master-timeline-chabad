# Petersburg Senate replies to Khovansky on rabbinic authority

*1834*

On 25 January 1834 (27 Shvat 5594), the Governing Senate in Petersburg responded to Governor-General Khovansky's three recommendations on rabbis. The first — that rabbinic pay require provincial approval — was deferred to the comprehensive Jewish statute then in deliberation at the State Council, citing an 1828 prohibition on new Jewish laws until the general code was complete. The second and third — barring extra donations to rabbis and barring them from civil adjudication — were not enacted as new law but issued as administrative directives to enforce Sections 49, 51, and 52 of the 1804 Jewish Constitution.
