# Zhuravichi delegation to Rebbe Rashab over land sold to non-Jews

*1913*

In summer 5673 the author's father came with R. Zev Brulin, secretary of the Zhuravichi kehilla, on a special mission to the Rebbe Rashab. The wealthy chassidim R. Shmuel Trainin of Petersburg and R. Yehoshua Rosenkin of Mogilev had bought the courtyard and land surrounding Zhuravichi from non-Jewish landowners and were now reselling it, leaving the town with no pasture for its cattle; the delegation asked the Rebbe to intervene on behalf of the town's poor.
