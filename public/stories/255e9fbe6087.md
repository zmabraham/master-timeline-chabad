# Chabad purchases 770 Eastern Parkway as movement headquarters

*1940*

At the end of summer 5700, the Chabad movement purchased the building at 770 Eastern Parkway in Brooklyn. From that point on, R' Shlomo Aharon Kazarnovsky was responsible for managing the building. He later led the expansion construction of the enlarged main synagogue in 5719-5720, built on what had previously been the courtyard.
