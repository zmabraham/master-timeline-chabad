# Slonim writes Shiloh on remaining in Yafo

*1904*

R' Shneur Zalman had initially expected to relocate to Jerusalem per the Rebbe's decision, but was told not to move without explicit instruction from the Rebbe himself. He recounted the hardships of his arrival in Yafo, observed that the community now had a clear path forward, and accepted that his lot was to remain in Yafo.
