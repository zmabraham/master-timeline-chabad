# Rebbe Rayatz travels to Berlin to meet Joint leadership

*1927*

On the same day he moved into his new apartment, the Rebbe traveled to Berlin to meet with major activists including R. Meir Hildesheimer, the Rogatchover Gaon, R. M.M. Zack of Riga, and Mordechai Dubin to discuss the budget for Torah institutions in Russia. This meeting led to the establishment of a special bureau under his direction for the relief work.
