# Wedding of Chaim Tolochinsky at Moscow Aguch shul

*1992*

The wedding was prepared by Rabbi Yitzchok Kogan, Rabbi Berel Lazar, and Rabbi Daniel Edri, with Rabbi Ariel Chaikin serving as mesader kiddushin. The author escorted the chossen to the Aguch shul and managed proceedings throughout the evening, returning home at 1 am. He records that this was the first time one of the top talmidim of the Moscow yeshiva and institute had married under the mission's auspices, calling the chuppah very beautiful and joyous.
