# Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Neuman (5771). Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak (Tzachi) Neuman

*1982*

Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Neuman (5771). Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak (Tzachi) Neuman (born on 13 Tammuz 5742, July 4, 1982) is the Rebbe's shliach in the settlement of Maccabim-Re'ut, founder and director of the 'Pardes - Forum for Jewish Wisdom' organization
