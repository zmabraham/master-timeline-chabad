# Rebbe launches Chanukah candle distribution on Shabbos Vayeshev

*1973*

At the Shabbos Parshas Vayeshev 5734 farbrengen (December 1973), the Rebbe directed intensified efforts that every Jewish home light Chanukah candles, with menorahs distributed free to those unable to afford them, and that soldiers in Eretz Yisrael also be helped to fulfill the mitzvah (Sichos Kodesh 5734 p. 196).
