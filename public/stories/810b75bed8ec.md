# Rashab gathers group of bachurim as 'yoshvim'

*1894*

In 5654 (1894), the Rebbe Rashab refused requests from Baron Ginzburg and Chovevei Tzion, and instead gathered a group of 16-17 bachurim, sons of chassidic balebatim, who came to Lubavitch as 'yoshvim' to study. This group became the foundation of Tomchei Tmimim, which the Rebbe saw as a vessel that would withstand both the Haskalah and Zionist movements.
