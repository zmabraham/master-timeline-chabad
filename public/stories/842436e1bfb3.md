# Rayatz writes R' Ashurov reassuring him over informants' libels

*1928*

On 24 Tishrei 5689, the Rebbe Rayatz wrote to R' Avraham Eliyahu Ashurov of Drivin, reassuring him not to worry about informers who had denounced him. He stated that truth shines and all libels are nullified, and that those who sit in judgment recognize the truth.
