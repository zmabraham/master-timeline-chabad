# Rosenbaum transferred to Smolensk prison

*1888*

Following the trial in Borisov, Rosenbaum was sentenced to two years in the 'rastantnaya rota' in Smolensk for signing a document on behalf of an only son seeking exemption from the army. The warden of the Borisov prison personally escorted him out and wept, recognizing that this prisoner was an unusually learned and refined man. Rosenbaum remained in Smolensk from the summer of 5648 (1888) until the summer of 5650 (1890).
