# Chassidic minyan in Vilna moves to R' Meir Refael's home

*1795*

Around 5555, the Vilna Chassidic minyan moved from the home of R' Eliezer ben Chaim to the home of R' Meir Refael's. The transfer was approved by Vilna kahal head R' Moshe ben Asher. This arrangement continued until persecutions intensified in 5557.
