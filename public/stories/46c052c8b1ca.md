# Prosecutor of Mogilev confirms receipt of Khovansky's order in the rebbe's case

*1825*

On 2 December 1825, Smolaninov, the General Prosecutor of Mogilev Province, sent report no. 2292 to Governor-General Khovansky in Vitebsk acknowledging receipt of Khovansky's 17 November order (no. 5850). The order had instructed him to supervise the provincial government's transfer of the investigation against Rabbi Dovber Schneuri to the appropriate court, and to ensure proper application of the law in handling the defendants. The acknowledgement was received in Vitebsk on 5 December 1825.
