# R. Avraham Chaim reports easing of prison conditions after gift to guard

*1889*

Fearing the censor's eye, R. Avraham Chaim switched mid-letter into Aramaic to recount his interaction with the prison official. He explained that he had appealed to the guard not to be forced like the other Jewish prisoners to bake and travel to the bathhouse on Shabbos, and the guard had given a half-promise to accommodate him. He noted wryly that those who appear friendly only while they enjoy a benefit do not stand with a man in his time of trouble.
