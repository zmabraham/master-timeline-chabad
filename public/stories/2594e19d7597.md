# Rebbe Rayatz urges Latvia Anash to expand public Chassidus study

*1929*

While visiting the United States the Rebbe Rayatz dispatched a general letter (Vol. II, Iggeret 464) to Anash and Chassidic families across Latvia, urging each one to weigh his present spiritual state against the standard of earlier Chassidim and to commit to fixed times of public Chassidus study. He announced the formation of a special committee for this purpose and called on rabbis, ritual slaughterers, and synagogue gabbaim throughout Latvia to join hands in bringing the threefold blessing tied to such study to every town.
