# Tzemach Tzedek refuses to travel to Minsk to meet Mendelstam

*1848*

On 13 Adar I 5608, the Tzemach Tzedek replied to Mendelstam's invitation to come to Minsk for further talks. He wrote that he could not travel under any circumstance and that nothing in the curriculum agreed at the 1843 commission should be diminished. He reiterated that the structure they had set for Torah study was an absolute necessity for the children.
