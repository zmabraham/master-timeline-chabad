# Fire breaks out in the small zal of Lubavitch yeshiva

*1908*

During the harsh winter of 5668 (early 1908), sparks were seen escaping the chimney of the heavily stoked stove in the small zal. Within a half hour the fire spread; with no modern firefighting equipment available, the students hauled water in large barrels from two wells in the courtyard and quenched the blaze. For some months afterward davening did not take place in the small zal, and the Rebbe Rashab began saying Chassidus in the large zal.
