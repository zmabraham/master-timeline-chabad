# Levin meets Igor and Boruch Gorin in Moscow's Marina Roshcha

*1993*

On 2 Shevat 5753 (24 January 1993), R. Shalom DovBer Levin met in Marina Roshcha, Moscow, with Igor, who was working for Shamir to locate Chabad-related documents, together with Boruch Gorin. Levin explained why the search should now concentrate on Minsk and handed over a written summary. A few months later Wagner joined the project.
