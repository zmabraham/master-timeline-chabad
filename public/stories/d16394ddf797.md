# Rebbe's interrogation-day response to Futerfas

*1947*

Futerfas later recounted that at his moment of crisis, he envisioned writing to the Rebbe and conjured the image of Simchat Torah 1927, the last time he saw him. On that same day his wife in England received a letter beginning 'Your telegram has been received,' dated 17 Iyar 5707. Only when reunited in 1963 did the couple piece together what had occurred.
