# Maamar 'HaYom Haras Olam' in Yiddish received for printing in America

*1928*

The shochet R' Menachem Mendel Kunin — described as an exceptionally generous supporter of Lubavitch — personally underwrote the printing of the ma'amar. Its appearance in America was felt as a stirring of renewed Lubavitcher spirit among Anash, signaling that the warmth of the chatzer was reviving on American soil even as the Russian community had been shattered.
