# Rebbe Rayatz announces Torah scroll to greet Moshiach

*1941*

On Simchat Torah 5702 (October 1941), the Frierdiker Rebbe, Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn, announced his decision to commission the writing of a Sefer Torah 'to greet Moshiach with.' After the holiday he publicized the initiative to chassidim across the world so that anyone who wished could take part in purchasing letters. This was launched amid the devastation of World War II as a call to spiritual mobilization and faith in the imminent redemption.
