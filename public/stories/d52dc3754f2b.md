# Akedah ceremony at Lubavitch Ohel

*1895*

On the Rayatz's fifteenth birthday in 1895, the Rashab brought him to the Ohel in Lubavitch and declared he was bringing his son to the akedah, drawing on the biblical binding of Yitzchak. Standing before the open ark, the Rashab transferred to his son the sacred task of working for the public good, recounted 140 years of Lubavitcher Rebbes' communal work, and inducted him as a Jewish leader. From that day, the Rayatz stood by his father's side as his personal secretary and confidant.
