# R. Morgel reports on Połaniec yeshiva to the Rebbe

*1936*

In his report of 7 Tevet 5697, R. Moshe Aharon Morgel informed the Rebbe Rayatz that the yeshiva had grown to approximately forty students divided into three divisions studying Bava Metzia, Bava Kamma, Yevamos and Shevuos with Rashi, Tosafos and select commentaries. After Maariv R. Yitzchok Pinchas, son of the tzaddik of Kinsk, taught a public Tanya chapter for laymen, youth and students of the third division.
