# Death of R' Michoel Bliner

*1911*

R' Michoel Bliner, known as 'Reb Michoel der Alter', served as an additional mashpia in Tomchei Tmimim from 5660 until his death on Motzaei Shabbat Toldot 5672 (November 1911). He was renowned for his extreme devotion and once shared with Ben-Zion Dinur how he had crossed the broken Dnieper ice three times to reach the Tzemach Tzedek before Pesach 5625.
