# Rabbi (Shalom) DovBer (Berl) Dovrobsky (born 5690,

*1930*

Rabbi (Shalom) DovBer (Berl) Dovrobsky (born 5690, 1930), is one of the elder Chabad chassidim in the world, a resident of Crown Heights, and one of the owners of the 'Lubavitch Matzah' matzah bakery in Crown Heights, where the matzot for the Rebbe and for the matzot he distributed were baked
