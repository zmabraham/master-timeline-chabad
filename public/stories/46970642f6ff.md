# Rashab summers in Bolivke with his son

*1895*

In the summer of 1895, the Rashab traveled with his wife to the resort town of Bolivke, forty-five miles from Lubavitch. While the official reason was his weakened health, the true motive was to spend time mentoring his son. For six years until 1901, the Rashab took the Rayatz on two-hour daily walks, studying Talmud and chasidic maamarim, sharing personal stories, and preparing him for future leadership.
