# Pekuam further correspondence about R' Yechiel Cohen

*1851*

On 28 Tishrei 5612 (October 23, 1851), the Pekuam wrote another letter to Hebron continuing to discuss R' Yechiel Cohen's prolonged American shadarus and its impact on New York donations. The letter is recorded in Pinkas 11 of the Pekuam records.
