# Passing of the Rebbe Rashab on 2 Nissan 5680

*1920*

The news reached the author in Gomel only on Erev Shabbat Parshat Shemini, when R' Akiva Trachtenberg told him at a cobbler's shop and he returned home weeping. That Shabbat the Gomel chassidim gathered at the Lubavitch shul for Mincha, where elders including R' Shlomo, R' Moshe and R' Shneur Zalman Tomarkin and R' Aharon Eliezer Zeitlin declared the Rebbe Rayatz as successor, danced together, and resolved to write a collective letter of hiskashrus.
