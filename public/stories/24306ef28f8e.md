# Hemshech 5666 published bound

*1970*

At the start of 5731 (fall 1970) Kehot received the bound copies of the long-delayed Hemshech 5666. The author distributed the bottle of wine the Rebbe had given him in 5725 to all the workers involved and gave each a bound copy. The Rebbe responded via Rabbi Chadakov, 'Their merit is very great; may it stand for them in this world and the next, I will mention them at the Tziyun.'
