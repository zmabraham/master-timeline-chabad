# England severs diplomatic relations with Russia over espionage

*1927*

At the end of May 1927, Conservative Prime Minister Stanley Baldwin presented Parliament with deciphered Soviet telegrams proving espionage activities by the All-Russian Co-operative Society. The break in diplomatic relations heightened Soviet paranoia. Together with the assassination of ambassador Voykov, it provided pretext for Stalin's wave of internal repression that swept up the Lubavitcher Rebbe.
