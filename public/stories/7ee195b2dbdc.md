# Aliyah of R' Mordechai of Shklov to Hebron

*1821*

R' Mordechai made aliyah from Shklov in 5581 (1821), as recorded in the 5599 census. His son R' Yisrael Yaffe joined him in Hebron in 5593 (1833). R' Mordechai signed the 5594 shadar document and the 5603 power-of-attorney for Jerusalem adjudication.
