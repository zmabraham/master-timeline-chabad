# Mitteler Rebbe organizes Ma'amad fund distribution

*1813*

Following his father's passing, the Mitteler Rebbe needed to rehabilitate the family after the destruction of the Liadi home. He entrusted the distribution to senior chassid Reb Pinchas Shick of Shklov, with Reb Shlomo Freides and Reb Zalman Roizes as overseers. Two-thirds went to relatives of the Alter Rebbe; one-third was used by the Mitteler Rebbe to build his home and synagogue in Lubavitch. This very distribution letter was later seized by the informer Simcha Kissin and used in the 1825 denunciation.
