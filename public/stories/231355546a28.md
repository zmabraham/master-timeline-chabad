# Rebbe's letter dated 29 Sivan 5707 reaches R. Levin

*1947*

Replying via the Paris office, the Frierdiker Rebbe wrote on 29 Sivan 5707 invoking blessings on the family members in Levin's earlier letter — his sister Frayde, her husband Asher Sosonkin, and their daughter Chava — who remained in the Soviet Union. The Rebbe blessed them with refuah, deliverance from hardship, and reunion with anash in the Rebbe's presence.
