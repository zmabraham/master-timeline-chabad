# Hebron Chabad community signs authorization for kollel emissaries

*1843*

The administrators and overseers of the Chabad community in Hebron, joined by the seven communal trustees, formally appointed R' Yosef Yehuda Mashiov and R' Moshe Shmerling as their representatives to negotiate and litigate with the Perushim kollel leadership in Jerusalem regarding shared funds from Hungary and Vienna. They were also empowered to settle outstanding claims with the community's own emissary R' Moshe ben David Yaffe.
