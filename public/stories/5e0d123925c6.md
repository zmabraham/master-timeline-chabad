# Meeting on Lubavitch rabbinic presence in Leningrad

*1991*

On 21 Tammuz 5751, the author met R. Netta Berchen, in Moscow on behalf of Shamir to supervise activity in Leningrad. After a lengthy conversation at the home of his relative, they agreed the single most important step for Leningrad was bringing a Chabad rav, and discussed R. Yerachmiel Bleinov as the candidate.
