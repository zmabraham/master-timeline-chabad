# Underground learning in Kiev's upper shiur during winter 5688

*1928*

Fear of arrest forced the small class to learn in hiding, sometimes in a large shul where the bitter cold made the boys' toes burn, and other times crammed onto a bed in the tiny room of R' Zalman Levin of Kursk. From then on R' Zalman Levin reviewed the shiur with the author, materially strengthening his Gemara study. The author writes that this period brought him to feel the meaning of 'Torah is upheld only by one who kills himself for it,' and that it eventually opened the door to Tomchei Temimim in Vitebsk.
