# R' Avraham Zaltzman arrives in Lubavitch

*1910*

R' Avraham Zaltzman recorded in a letter to his sons that he arrived in Lubavitch around Rosh Hashanah 5671 (1910). He progressed through the chadarim — studying with the Vietker melamed, then with Itzcheh, then with the Sener melamed, then with R' Yechiel, then with the Dvinsker R' Yehoshua Arsh, then with R' Shmuel Borisover — before being sent to study in Shchedrin.
