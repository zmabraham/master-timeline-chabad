# Poalei Tzion attack at Rebbe's summer dacha

*1906*

On the night of 7 Menachem Av 5666 (1906), six to eight Poalei Tzion members from Vitebsk came to the Rebbe Rashab's dacha with revolvers, destroyed the dining shed, broke tables, cabinets, and door glass. The Rebbetzin asked to take her terrified children outside but was forced back into the house at gunpoint. The attackers demanded 3,000 rubles. Eventually a settlement and ransom payment was reached during Elul.
