# Yeltsin signs order to return Schneersohn collection

*1990*

On 18 Kislev 5751 the Chabad delegation worked with Yeltsin's staff on memos and received an initial promise, then word, that the Russian Federation president had signed an order to give them the Schneersohn library. Yeltsin's office asked Chabad to organize broad press coverage and senatorial thank-you letters for a ceremonial transfer the next day on Yud-Tes Kislev.
