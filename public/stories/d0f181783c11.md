# Rebbe Rayatz visits Otwock on Shushan Purim

*1928*

During his first Warsaw visit, the Rebbe Rayatz traveled on Shushan Purim 5688 (March 7, 1928) to Otwock, the home of his uncle R. Moshe Hornstein, son-in-law of the Rebbe Maharash. Anash and select Tomchei Tmimim students joined him in Otwock for Shabbat Parshat Ki Tisa before he returned to Warsaw for further yechidut sessions.
