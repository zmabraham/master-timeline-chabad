# Author moves to Petersburg for Passover

*1992*

Yaakov Krovliak and Mendel Balkin met him at the train station. He inspected the Great Synagogue's matzah bakery — far from satisfactory — and Chief Rabbi Lewitas asked him to supervise the baking of 5 tons of shemurah flour. The author insisted Krovliak would supervise but he would not certify the matzot as fully proper. He gave a shiur on Passover halacha to the elders, visited a children's garden in the city, and discussed the rabbinic position offered to him by Lewitas.
