# Elul 5695 NKVD sweep of Malakhovka thwarted by R. Zalman Kornitzer

*1935*

At the start of Elul 5695 (late August 1935), NKVD investigators came to R. Zalman Kornitzer (Alpert) demanding 'Zalman Kornitzer'—which he denied, giving his legal name Alpert. While held at the village council he overheard officers reading a list of Anash to arrest: Shmuel Leib Paritzer (R. Shmuel Levitin), Shlomo Chaim Palatzker (Kesselman), Avraham Mayarer (Dreizin) and others. After being released he raced to warn each family on the list, saving many from arrest; investigators had tricked two captured boys into naming Anash, mistaking nicknames for surnames. R. Yonah Cohen then took it upon himself with mesirus nefesh to care for the bochurim and Anash children, receiving the Rebbe's blessing to be strong in this work.
