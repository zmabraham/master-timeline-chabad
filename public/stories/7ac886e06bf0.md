# R' Leib Chitrik begins service as Hebron Kolel Chabad administrator

*1827*

R' Leib Chitrik was among the earliest administrators of Hebron's Chabad community. From 1827 he signed emissary credentials and community letters on behalf of Kolel Chabad in Hebron, continuing in that role until 1844. The 1827 emissary document identifies him as 'Yehuda Leib son of Wolf, trustee of the holy city of Hebron.'
