# R' Eliezer Orlozarow passes away in Romny

*1901*

After nearly four decades serving as rabbi of Romny since 5623, R' Eliezer Orlozarow died in 5661. He was succeeded by R' Chaim Yeshaya Schneersohn (descendant of the Tzemach Tzedek through the Maharil of Kopust), with R' Akiva Yankilevsky (Orlozarow's son-in-law) appointed as additional moreh tzedek.
