# Tomchei Tmimim yeshiva relocates from Lubavitch to Kremenchug

*1918*

The final students dug deeply into the cellar of the Rebbe's residence to hide possessions before departing. They gathered for a bitter farbrengen marking their separation from Lubavitch, still hoping the move was temporary. With the Soviet anti-religious campaign that followed, the yeshiva eventually retreated underground across dozens of Russian towns.
