# Tzemach Tzedek's homes rebuilt after fire

*1859*

Following the fire that consumed the Tzemach Tzedek's home in Lyubavichi (which also destroyed many of his responsa written between 1848–1856), he had moved temporarily into the local landowner's courtyard. During the year 5619 the buildings for him and his sons were reconstructed. The family then prepared to return to their newly built homes at the start of the following Hebrew year.
