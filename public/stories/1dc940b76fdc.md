# R' Shneur Zalman Slonim arrives in Yafo

*1899*

His arrival was reported in the HaTzvi newspaper on 15 Kislev, noting that the new rabbi had been brought from Hebron at the request of chasidim and prominent householders. A reception followed on Shabbat with representatives of Bnei Brit, Achva, and Bnei Yisrael societies, lasting through the night.
