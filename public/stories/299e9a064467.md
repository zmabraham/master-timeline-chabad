# Rebbetzin's third Rebbe predecessor dies in Bobruysk

*1924*

After the 1869 passing of the third Rebbe Rabbi Menachem Mendel (Tzemach Tzedek), four of his sons established themselves as competing Rebbes in other towns while his youngest, Rabbi Shmuel, became Rebbe in Lubavitch. Those rival dynasties petered out without successors. With Rabbi Shmaryahu Noach's passing, the last of those branches ended and his Chasidim gravitated to the Rebbe Rayatz.
