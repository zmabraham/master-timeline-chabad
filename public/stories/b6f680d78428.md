# Altheuse writes to Rebbe about returning to Russia

*1940*

Altheuse - who had been the matchmaker for the Rebbe and Rebbetzin, accompanied the Rebbe into Kostroma exile in 5687, and remained the lifeblood of Riga anash after the Rebbe's departure - now considered returning to Russia to be with his son and household in Leningrad, despite worsening Soviet decrees. The plan never materialized.
