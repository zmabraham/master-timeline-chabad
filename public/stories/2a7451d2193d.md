# R' Avraham Dovid Pevzner becomes mashpia

*1913*

Approximately at the end of 5673 (1913), R' Avraham Dovid Pevzner was accepted as a mashpia at Tomchei Tmimim Lubavitch. R' Yisrael Jacobson described him as humble and soft-spoken, but at farbrengens he spoke with great warmth and tailored his words to each listener's spiritual state.
