# Provincial prosecutor reports collected testimony in Mitteler Rebbe case

*1826*

Prosecutor Smolaninov of Mohilev province informed Governor-General Khovansky that reports from the magistrate courts of Orsha and Kapust, as well as the lower court of Mohilev, had reached the Babinovich court, along with the depositions of Chassidim in Shklov needed for the case. He had sent a written demand urging Babinovich to rule promptly on the alleged unlawful actions of Rabbi Berke Schneur of Lubavitch, and informed the district court inspector accordingly.
