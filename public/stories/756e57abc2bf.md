# Rebbe Rayatz founds Bikur Cholim department of Machne Israel

*1944*

In summer 5704 the Rebbe Rayatz established a Bikur Cholim (visiting the sick) division within his Machne Israel organization. He appointed R' Yochanan Gordon as its chairman, leveraging Gordon's communal credibility and pastoral disposition. This was one of several institutional building blocks the Rebbe Rayatz put in place during his early American years.
