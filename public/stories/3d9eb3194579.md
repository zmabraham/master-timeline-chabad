# Memoirist travels to teach at Cherson yeshiva

*1929*

The memoirist boarded the train Sunday morning on Rosh Chodesh Cheshvan 5690 with fellow Zlobin native Dovber HaLevi Gurevitch, arriving in Cherson 36 hours later. The R'M R' Yosef Himelstein's wife met them outside in tears: her husband, the mashgiach, and all the students had been arrested. Too frightened to take them in, the residents sent the pair back, and a non-Jewish wagon driver sheltered them overnight before they returned to Kremenchug.
