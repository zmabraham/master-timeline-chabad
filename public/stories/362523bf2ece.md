# R. Feigin and Chaim Lieberman arrive in New York to prepare for Rebbe's visit

*1929*

In early Elul 5689, about a week before the Rebbe Rayatz himself was due to arrive, his secretary R. Yechezkel Feigin and Chaim Lieberman came to America to help organize the visit. They joined the ongoing debate over where the Rebbe would lodge, but no consensus emerged. By default the Rebbe was initially housed at R. Eliyahu Simpson's new apartment above a shul in Boro Park.
