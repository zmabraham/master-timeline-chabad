# Rebbe interrogated by GPU officers in Shpalerka prison

*1927*

The interrogators rested their hands on weapons laid on the table, accusing the Rebbe of counterrevolutionary activity, leading Soviet Jewry, influencing American bourgeois Jews through the JDC, and operating a network of religious institutions. The Rebbe rebuffed them, citing Soviet law that did not ban religious teaching. As the session ended, Lulov declared, 'In twenty-four hours, you will be shot.'
