# Rebbe Rayatz arrives in Warsaw and begins Tiku 5694 maamarim

*1933*

On Rosh Hashana 5694 the Rebbe Rayatz began delivering the long Hemshech 'Tiku' which concluded on Shabbos Parshas Toldos. Talmidim repeated the maamarim, and select students entered the Rebbe's room to chazer them before him. The Rebbe also wrote down the maamarim, which were then copied and studied by the talmidim.
