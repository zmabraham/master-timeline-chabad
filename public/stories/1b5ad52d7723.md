# Author arrives in Leningrad on 21 Adar to seek audience with the Rebbe

*1925*

The author arrived in Leningrad on Tuesday, 21 Adar. The Rebbe Rayatz was not present, having traveled to Moscow, and only returned home the following day, Wednesday. The author was traveling both as an emissary for R. Yisrael Magidsohn, who feared GPO surveillance after a search at his government workplace, and to receive the Rebbe's guidance on his own preparations to leave Russia.
