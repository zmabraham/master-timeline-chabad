# Shanghai refugee yeshiva students arrive in the United States

*1946*

Having fled Poland to Lithuania at the war's onset, the students were forcibly relocated through Russia and Japan to Shanghai, where they endured isolation and starvation. At the war's end they learned that almost all of their relatives had perished in the Holocaust. After a year waiting for American visas, they arrived in 1946 and became a key cadre for the sixth Rebbe's postwar vision.
