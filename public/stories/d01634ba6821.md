# Tsar Alexander signs Jewish Statute including section 53

*1804*

On 18 Tevet 5565 (9 December 1804), Tsar Alexander I signed the Russian Jewish Statute. Section 53 granted that wherever a community split such that one party did not want to share a synagogue with the other, either party could build its own synagogue and appoint its own rabbis — formally protecting the chassidim from interference by mitnagdim.
