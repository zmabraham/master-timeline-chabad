# Rabbi Gil Manor (born 5736, 1975) is one of the Rebbe's shluchim in Netivot

*1975*

Rabbi Gil Manor (born 5736, 1975) is one of the Rebbe's shluchim in Netivot, an entrepreneur and social activist who produces and delivers innovative programs to instill educational and Chassidic ideas in the light of the Rebbe's vision.
