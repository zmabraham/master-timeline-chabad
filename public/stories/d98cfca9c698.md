# Publication of Chukei Chevrat Binyan Yehuda VehaGalil

*1903*

In Jerusalem 5663 a small 18-page pamphlet entitled 'Sefer Chukei Chevrat Binyan Yehuda VehaGalil' was published, setting out the rules of the society that built shelters for the poor of Kollel Chabad in Jerusalem and Hebron. The booklet records R' Yitzchak ben Aharon's donations for the Ohel Yitzchak beit medrash in Meah Shearim and for four adjacent kollel houses, distributed to families by lottery every three years.
