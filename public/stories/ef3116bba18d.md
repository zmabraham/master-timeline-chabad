# Chabad enters Polyakov Synagogue in Moscow to reclaim it

*1991*

Hearing that the Mossoviet had decided to return Moscow's Polyakov Synagogue to Chabad but was hesitating to issue the decision in writing under Reform pressure, the delegation moved to take physical possession. On the morning of 28 Nisan 5751 they brought a Sefer Torah down from Leningrad, gathered with yeshiva students, prayed and studied in the building, and refused to leave when the resident company cut the electricity and called the police. Veronika negotiated through the day with the company, police, and Mossoviet officials, securing a meeting with deputy mayor Sevastianov for the following day to resolve the matter.
