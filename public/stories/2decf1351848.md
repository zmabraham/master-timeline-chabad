# Nine Tomchei Tmimim students arrive in Montreal to establish yeshiva

*1941*

On 2 Marcheshvan 5702 (October 23, 1941), the nine students reached Montreal. Rabbi Yisroel Jacobson came immediately, and a week later Rabbi Shmuel Levitin arrived from New York. The students wrote that the Rebbe had instructed them to establish Yeshivas Tomchei Tmimim in Montreal in the spirit of the original yeshiva in Lubavitch and Otwock, marking the founding of Lubavitch institutions in Canada.
