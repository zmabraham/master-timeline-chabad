# R' Elimelech Siminawski first travels to America

*1879*

R' Elimelech Siminawski traveled from Sosnitsa to America at the start of 5640, during the wave of departures triggered by Russian pogroms. He later recounted that the Rebbe Maharin of Nezhin, when asked whether one should flee to America, replied that if there were any place to escape from the Holy One he would flee there himself. His testimony helped frame the early Chabad community's ambivalent view of the chasidim who emigrated west.
