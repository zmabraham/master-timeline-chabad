# R' Yaakov Landa's rabbinical writ signed for Libau

*1928*

After repeated encouragement from the Rebbe Rayatz to accept the Libau rabbinate, R' Yaakov Landa's rabbinical writ was signed on 17 Tishrei 5689 (October 1, 1928) by some forty heads of the local Chassidic community. The writ formalized his position as rabbi of the Libau Chassidim.
