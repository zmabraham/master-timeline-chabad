# Mogilev court ratifies Lubavitch synagogue-building regulation

*1822*

On July 17, 1822, the chassidic community of Rabbi DovBer of Lubavitch formalized a takanah empowering specially trusted individuals to manage the construction of a synagogue, exempting them from any obligation to render accounts of income or expenses, both during construction and afterward. The document was written in Russian on three-ruble stamped paper and notarized as an official document before witnesses at the Mogilev peace court. This regulation later surfaced as evidence in the 1825-1827 investigation against the Mitteler Rebbe.
