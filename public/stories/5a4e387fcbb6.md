# Cyrus Adler argues JDC should not support Rebbe's matzah campaign

*1929*

Adler warned that the Yevsektzia might block deliveries, embarrass the Russians, or that the Soviets might not agree to allow shipments. Years later, Adler regretted the decision, writing in 1932, 'I am not disposed to take the attitude to completely surrender all Jewish religious practice in Russia to the Soviets.'
