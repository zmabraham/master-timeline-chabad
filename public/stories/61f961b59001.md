# R' Mordechai of Neskhiz issues general appeal for Eretz Yisrael fund

*1796*

In 5556 (1796), R' Mordechai of Neskhiz published a general appeal regarding the dire poverty of the Hasidic Yishuv in Eretz Yisrael, noting that he had previously stayed clear of fundraising. The Chozeh of Lublin joined the appeal, writing that Polish Jewry had until now lacked someone to organize collections as White Russia had. This launched a parallel Polish-Volhynian fundraising effort outside the Alter Rebbe's network.
