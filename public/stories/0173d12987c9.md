# Hebron Chabad sends shadar R' Yitzchak Ashkenazi to Babylonia

*1827*

In summer 1827 (קיץ תקפ"ז), the Chabad community of Hebron sent R' Yitzchak b"R Shlomo Ashkenazi to 'the cities of Babylonia and Basra and their surroundings.' Though the shadar was a Sephardic Jerusalem resident, he was dispatched by the Ashkenazi Chabad community, reflecting their isolation from traditional Russian funding streams.
