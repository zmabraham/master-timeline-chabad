# Tel Aviv cheder has closed; Havlin seeks to rent the building

*1935*

Citing the dire financial state of Torat Emet in Jerusalem, Havlin requested rabbinic approval to rent out the building at 16 Rav Kook Street in Tel Aviv, which had been donated by Mr. Friedman and registered in Havlin's name to circumvent registration restrictions. The rental did not actually take place, and the building was soon dedicated as a Chabad synagogue.
