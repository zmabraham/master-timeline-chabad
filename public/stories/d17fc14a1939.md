# R' Alter Simchowitz appointed mashgiach in Shchedrin

*1917*

On 16 Iyar 5677 (1917), the Rebbe Rashab wrote that R' Alter Simchowitz, who had come from Eretz Yisrael, should travel via Lubavitch to receive instructions from R' Shilem and from the office, before continuing to Shchedrin as mashgiach for chassidut. Simchowitz, son-in-law of R' Zalman Havlin, stayed in Shchedrin until 5679 when the yeshiva closed and moved to Kremenchug.
