# Murder of Riga's Jews by Nazis

*1941*

In Kislev 5702, the Nazis murdered the Jews of Riga, including the families of R. Yechezkel Feigin and R. Yehuda Eber, who had spent two years trying to escape to America. According to estimates from survivors of the killing, R. Eber and his family were among the thousands killed al kiddush Hashem.
