# Radal of Bichov replies to Lubavitch rabbi on get question

*1836*

R. Yissachar Ber HaLevi Horowitz, rabbi of Lubavitch, wrote to the Radal about a get question because the Tzemach Tzedek was away from home. The Radal replied briefly on Monday 11 Menachem-Av 5596, apologizing for the haste due to fast-day weakness and asking not to be troubled with further questions, given his resolution to refrain from issuing responsa for several years. He suggested the question be put again to the Tzemach Tzedek on his return.
