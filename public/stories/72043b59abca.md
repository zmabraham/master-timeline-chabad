# Family returns to Lubavitch after extended absence

*1890*

In 1890, after years of being absent from Lubavitch while seeking medical treatment in Russia and abroad, the Rashab and his wife finally returned home. The Rayatz later wrote that this was 'almost the first in my life that my father spent entirely at home,' as the greater part of previous years had been spent abroad. However, in December 1890, the Rashab fell ill with a high fever for about two months.
