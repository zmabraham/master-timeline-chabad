# Rabbi Eber sent to examine students in Warsaw and Vilna

*1930*

While stranded in Riga awaiting an American visa, Rabbi Yehuda Eber received word through Rabbi Yisrael Jacobson and Rabbi Y. Feigin that the Rebbe Rayatz wished him to travel immediately to Warsaw and Vilna to examine all the students of Tomchei Tmimim there. A telegraphic visa was arranged from Warsaw. The journey set in motion his future role as rosh yeshiva of the central yeshiva in Warsaw.
