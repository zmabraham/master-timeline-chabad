# Joint psak by Tzemach Tzedek and MaharI"L in Lubavitch

*1823*

A teshuva and psak written and co-signed in Lubavitch in Elul 5583 (August-September 1823) by MaharI"L and the Tzemach Tzedek, printed in Shearit Yehuda Even Haezer §5, documents their collaborative halachic work during the Mitteler Rebbe's tenure.
