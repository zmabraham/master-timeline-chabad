# Rabbi Aaron Rakeffet visits Riga on covert Mossad mission

*1985*

Rakeffet was surprised to find at the Riga synagogue a group of older Jews who had held on through Communism. After the service, he was told to wait until the government agents left, then 'they broke out vodka and cake, and we sat and had a farbrengen.' The Rebbe's six-year stay in Riga half a century earlier was still 'what kept them going all those years.'
