# Passing of R' Yaakov Shimshon of Shepetivka ends separate Volhynia kollel

*1801*

R' Yaakov Shimshon of Shepetivka passed away in 5561 (1801). From that point on, no separate Volhynia kollel existed in the Holy Land; funds again flowed through R' Avraham of Kalisk. Volhynian collections continued to be raised separately abroad, but distribution in Eretz Yisrael was reunified under a single Hasidic kollel.
