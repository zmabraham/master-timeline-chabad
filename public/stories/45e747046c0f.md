# Maharash builds the Large Hall on the Tzemach Tzedek's house site

*1872*

After the 1868 fire, Maharash addressed his father's halachic will by erecting in 5632 (1872) a large hall (30 cubits square) on the site of the Tzemach Tzedek's former house. The hall served for Yamim Noraim prayers and major chassidic gatherings, where in later years the Rebbe Rayatz's bar mitzvah and his wedding to Rebbetzin Nechama Dina would also be held.
