# Studies begin at Tomchei Temimim in Lubavitch

*1897*

Studies began on 18 Elul 5657 (September 15, 1897), the joint birthday of the Baal Shem Tov and the Alter Rebbe. The Rashab personally selected the first students from among the yoshvim and appointed Rabbi Shmuel Gronem as the yeshivah's first mashpia, with detailed curriculum instructions.
