# Pidrubsky meeting and Israeli consulate inauguration in Moscow

*1991*

On 25 Tevet R' Kogan, R' Cunin and R' Levin met Pidrubsky at the Arkhipova Choral Synagogue, who wrote a letter to Gubinko stating that anything tied to the Rebbe must be returned immediately. They then attended the inauguration of the Israeli consulate building, meeting U.S. deputy ambassador Collins, Moscow Mayor Popov and Israeli ambassador Arye Levin. The day closed with a farbrengen for Marina Roshcha talmidim at the Aguch office, filmed for video.
