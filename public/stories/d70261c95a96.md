# Jerusalem Beis Din Tzedek reaffirms trust in Kollel Chabad officials during WWI

*1919*

Critical letters had circulated in the Diaspora attacking the Jerusalem vaad under Rebbe Rashab's nesius, sowing discord and discouraging donors. The beis din declared that it knew the officials well as honest and trustworthy men, and that whenever funds arrived from America and England — Russian remittances having been cut off by the war — they were distributed to the poor of the kollel in Jerusalem, Hebron, Tzfat, Tiberias, Yafo and the moshavot. The pesak became a recurring touchstone in the kollel correspondence of 5681–5682.
