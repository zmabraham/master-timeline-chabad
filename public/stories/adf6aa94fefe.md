# About forty Tomchei Tmimim students gathered in Japan awaiting US travel

*1941*

In a letter dated 4 Iyar 5701 (May 1, 1941), the Frierdiker Rebbe wrote that roughly forty of his students were now in Japan, anticipating their journey to America. He noted that countless pleas were arriving from Lithuania and Latvia to be rescued in time, and his office maintained constant correspondence with those communities.
