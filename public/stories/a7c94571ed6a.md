# Alter Rebbe describes R. Yaakov of Smilian's two years as ma'amados gabbai

*1783*

In early summer 5543 (1783), the Alter Rebbe wrote in a letter (Yagdil Torah NY 51) that R. Yaakov of Smilian had served with great effort for two years as a trusted emissary collecting Eretz Yisrael funds. This implies that R. Yaakov was appointed in 5541, around the time the three tzaddikim formalized their joint leadership.
