# Hebron Chabad-Sephardic agreement signed

*1830*

Among the Chabad signatories were R' Moshe Meizlish, joined by R' Shmuel Segal, R' Yehuda Leib Sener, and others — without R' Tzvi Hirsh Lifshitz or R' Ephraim Yaffe of the original 5587 trio. The agreement allocated a quarter of all Chabad collections in Sephardic regions to the Hebron Sephardic community.
