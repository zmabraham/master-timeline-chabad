# Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Taub. Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Taub 5746 - 1986

*1986*

Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Taub. Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Taub (5746 - 1986) is the Rebbe's shliach, rabbi of the "Merkaz MMSh" beit midrash synagogue in the center of Netanya and head of a kollel for the study of the Torah of Redemption
