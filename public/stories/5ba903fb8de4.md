# Di Yisurim Fun Lubavitcher Rebben published in Riga

*1930*

Josselsohn, a religious Zionist who had studied in Jerusalem before returning to Riga to write for Frimorgen, captured the mood and climate of the turbulent era. The book was translated into English for the first time in volume 6 of Likkutei Dibburim (Kehot, 2012).
