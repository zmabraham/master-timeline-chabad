# Maharashab teaches Chassidus on Parshat Eikev

*1914*

On Shabbat Parshat Eikev 5674, Maharashab delivered a discourse beginning 'V'haya Eikev Tishmaun' as part of an ongoing series explaining the union of Or (light) and Keilim (vessels) — that the connection occurs specifically in the inner aspect of the vessels.
