# Tmimim raise emergency ma'amad funds after telegram from R' Feigin

*1929*

Roughly twelve Tmimim gathered at R' Rivkin's house, each pledging no less than $100 — except two who pledged $50, and R' Gershon Simpson who gave $150. The author himself took out a gmach loan he planned to repay $20 per week, though Providence enabled him to repay it sooner. One Tomim publicly demanded all give at least $100 yet ultimately contributed nothing himself and left angry with the author — an episode the author cites to illustrate how strained the Tmimim's finances were.
