# Corrected translation submitted in Mitteler Rebbe fundraising probe

*1828*

During the Rebbe's interrogation, investigators relied on a mistranslated Hebrew passage to claim he had collected roughly 140,000 rubles, of which only 35,000 had been distributed. The Rebbe insisted the translation was falsified and requested access to the original. A new accurate translation was prepared by the Rebbe's translator, the chasid Yehuda Yaffe of Kopust, who certified its accuracy alongside the Rebbe before Court Counselor Markov. The corrected text showed the disputed phrase referred to a portion of the total 35,000 rubles, not an additional sum.
