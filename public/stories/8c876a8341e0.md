# Vitebsk cheder teachers petition Petersburg conference against secular studies

*1910*

On 17 Adar Sheini 5670, fifteen melamdim and principals of cheders in Vitebsk signed a letter, led by R. Dov Nachman Horowitz, to the rabbis assembled in St. Petersburg. They warned from experience that exposing young children to Russian-language schoolbooks and gymnasium classes hollowed out their faith and led them to reject Chassidus and prayer. They proposed barring admission to Russian-language schools before age thirteen, requiring melamdim to teach mussar works approved by rabbis, and ensuring religious instruction in state schools be given only by Orthodox rabbis. The letter was conveyed to the conference through Vitebsk's rabbi, R. Shmaryahu Yehuda Leib Medalia.
