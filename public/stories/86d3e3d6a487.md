# Rebbe Rashab approves SILA's Bukhara shlichut

*1907*

The Rebbe Rashab responded on Rosh Chodesh Adar 5667 that 'the matter is necessary for him and cannot be postponed.' He also approved SILA's parallel travel to Lubavitch to discuss community affairs in person. The Rebbe blessed his journey with 'good and true reasons.'
