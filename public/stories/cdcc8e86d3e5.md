# Distribution of clothes at Moscow Chabad girls' summer camp

*1991*

Following the boys' camp distribution two days earlier, the author and his wife drove with seven crates to the girls' camp. He spoke about Chabad's broader work in Russia — bringing in kosher food, opening schools, and now leaving each girl Shabbos clothes as a keepsake of what they had learned about Shabbos and mitzvos. His wife organized the garments and helped the girls choose.
