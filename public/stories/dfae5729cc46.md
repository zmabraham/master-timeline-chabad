# Shabbos at the Ezras Achim children's camp

*1991*

On erev Shabbos 15 Tammuz 5751, the delegation traveled to the camp but, with sunset approaching while still several kilometers away, they left their belongings on the bus and walked. Arriving in the middle of the seudah, they sang with the campers and the author related the journey. Through Shabbos they spoke with the children, davened slowly together word-by-word, and the head counselor Shlomo Varovich explained tefillah and Torah reading.
