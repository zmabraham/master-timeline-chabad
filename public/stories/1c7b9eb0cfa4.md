# Rosenbaum works as proofreader in Berlin Torah publishing house

*1893*

In winter 5653 Reb Avraham Chaim worked in Berlin as the editor and proofreader of a Torah-scientific journal, lodging in the home of the rabbi who issued it. From there he wrote to Rabbi Tzvi Tietz, author of Zikhron Yehudah on Eichah, explaining why he had cut a passage from a submitted article on the wording of the bris-milah blessing 'tziva yedidut.'
