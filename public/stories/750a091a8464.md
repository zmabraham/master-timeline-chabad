# Soviet police arrest R. Yitzchak Kogan en route to Chernobyl camp

*1990*

In the early hours of Sunday 6 Tevet 5751 a car carrying R. Yitzchak Kogan, R. Yisroel Aronov, Kogan's eldest daughter, and Veronika's driver was stopped by Soviet police outside the children's camp. After a prolonged exchange the police held R. Kogan as a pledge and forced the others to return to the capital, ostensibly because the delegation's visas were restricted to Moscow. The incident was understood as retaliation by Gubenko's faction, and prompted Veronika to escalate the matter to the Russian Federation Foreign Ministry.
