# R' Levi Yitzchak of Berditchev expelled from Pinsk

*1785*

R' Levi Yitzchak had served as av beit din of Pinsk from 5535 to 5545. In the summer of 5545 (1785) the mitnagdim succeeded in expelling him from the city, and he relocated to Berditchev in Volhynia. His departure further consolidated the Alter Rebbe's position as sole chassidic leader in White Russia and Lithuania.
