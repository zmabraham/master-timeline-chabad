# Passing of the Mitteler Rebbe

*1827*

The Mitteler Rebbe's histalkus occurred on 9 Kislev 5588, just days before the Senate's final ratification of his acquittal. Because his passing fell one day before what would have been the inaugural Yud Kislev celebration, the festival was not established as widely as it might otherwise have been. Different traditions place the Rebbe's liberation in 5586, 5587, or 5588, reflecting the three distinct stages of his juridical exoneration.
