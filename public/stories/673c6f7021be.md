# Marriage of R. Shmuel ben Yosef ibn Attar

*1712*

On Friday, 26 Cheshvan 5473 (Nov 5, 1712), Shmuel ben Yosef ibn Attar — a nephew of Rabbi Yehuda ibn Attar — was married. The event was registered in Rabbi Yaakov ibn Tzur's marriage records. This entry reveals that Rabbi Yehuda had a previously unrecorded brother named Yosef who had three sons: Shmuel, Yaakov, and Yitzchak.
