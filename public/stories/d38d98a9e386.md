# Earliest extant dated Tzemach Tzedek responsum

*1819*

Internal evidence in the responsum (which refers to 'last year, 5578') places its composition in 5579. The original autograph has not survived; only a copy in manuscript 1041 with the Tzemach Tzedek's own glosses remains, with a header noting it was 'copied from volume 3 with annotations.' This is currently the oldest dated piece of his responsa literature in our hands.
