# Chabad elders convene to address succession

*1882*

On Shabbat, October 21, 1882 (8 Cheshvan 5643, Parashas Lekh Lekha), Chabad elders convened in the home of Rebbetzin Rivkah Schneersohn to address the future of the movement following Rabbi Shmuel's passing. Prominent rabbis including Rabbi Zalman Pinsker, Rabbi Dan Tumarkin, and Reb Chaim Yaakov Viderevitz attended. The two older sons, Rabbi Zalman Aharon and Rabbi Shalom Dovber, initially refused to sit with the rabbis, but eventually agreed to recite maamarim, tacitly stepping into their father's role.
