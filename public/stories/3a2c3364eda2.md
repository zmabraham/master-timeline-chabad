# Mitteler Rebbe released on Yud Kislev

*1825*

Though strictly only an interim release pending trial, this date became commemorated in Chabad as Yud Kislev, the festival of the Mitteler Rebbe's redemption. The eventual judicial acquittal would unfold in three further stages over the next two years; the Mitteler Rebbe himself never lived to see the final Senate confirmation.
