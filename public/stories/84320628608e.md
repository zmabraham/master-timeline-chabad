# R' Shmuel Grossman appointed director of Tomchei Tmimim in Wegscheid DP camp

*1947*

Following his emigration from Russia in 5707, R' Shmuel Grossman served for a period as director of the Tomchei Tmimim yeshiva that was established at the Wegscheid displaced persons camp in Austria. He later moved to Paris before eventually settling in London.
