# Rebbe inquires about the seforim case

*1991*

On the evening of 7 Tammuz 5751, R. Leibel Groner reported that the Rebbe had asked him about developments in the seforim case. That same night Veronika informed the Moscow delegation that Gorbachev had finally decided at 1 p.m. to return the Chabad library, hoping to do so before July 10 and the G7 summit. The delegation was instructed to keep the news confidential among themselves.
