# Secretary Feigin writes R. Yehuda Shmotkin about library acquisitions

*1936*

On the third day of Chol Hamoed Sukkos 5697 (October 1936), Rabbi Yechezkel Feigin wrote to R. Yehuda Shmotkin in Eretz Yisrael at the Rebbe Rayatz's request. Shmotkin was asked to approach authors and book-buyers to donate copies to the Rebbe's library at his Otwock address (Pruse 6), continuing the established custom of presenting books as gifts to the Lubavitch Bibliothek.
