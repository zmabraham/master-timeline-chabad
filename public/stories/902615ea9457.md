# Rebbe rebukes Wilshanski to pursue outreach to Australian youth

*1949*

Known for his scholarship from his Lubavitch yeshivah years, Wilshanski was learning to adapt as a public activist. The Rebbe exhorted him: 'Even those who apparently don't want to study, you should draw them closer by giving them reading material in their language. Don't get disillusioned in your efforts.'
