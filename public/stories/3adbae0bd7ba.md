# Rebbe Rashab founds Yeshivas Tomchei Temimim in Lubavitch

*1897*

Yehuda Geberer notes Chabad took the lead in this arena: 'The first who did this was the Rebbe Rashab with the opening of Yeshivas Tomchei Temimim in 1897.' With time, other chasidic groups followed Chabad's lead, establishing their own yeshivas. The seventh Rebbe identified the founding as a major stage in the dissemination of chasidic teachings.
