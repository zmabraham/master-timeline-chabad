# Rebbe Rashab moves into apartment on Pushkinskaya Street, Rostov

*1915*

On Friday, 5 Kislev 5676, the Rebbe Rashab moved his household into a rented apartment at 166 Pushkinskaya Street in Rostov. The previous tenant had only vacated at 2:00 p.m., furniture had not yet arrived, and the electric wiring was unfinished, yet the Rebbe insisted on moving in before Shabbat. A telephone (number 19-89) was installed in the following days as the family adjusted to urban Rostov life.
