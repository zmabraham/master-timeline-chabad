# R. Feigin writes Jacobson about renewed building plans

*1938*

On 9 Tammuz 5698 (July 8, 1938), secretary R. Yechezkel Feigin wrote to R. Israel Jacobson, director of Aguch in America, that after a long silence concerning the purchase of the yeshiva property — for various reasons the discussion had been suspended — by the Rebbe Rayatz's will, talks had been renewed to bring the purchase closer to fruition.
