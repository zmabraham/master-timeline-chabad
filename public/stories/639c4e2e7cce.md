# Meeting at US embassy in Moscow with deputy ambassador John Ordway

*2000*

On 9 Tammuz 5760, after a morning session with Mara Trifonenko, the emissary went to the US embassy for a two-hour meeting with deputy ambassador John Ordway and his aide Howard Solomon. He argued the Russian side had no real intention of returning books, using duplicate-checks and a committee to stall indefinitely. He proposed starting returns with the 500+ books the librarians had already verified, but was refused.
