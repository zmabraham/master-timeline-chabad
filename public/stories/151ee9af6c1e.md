# Wedding of Brachah Slava Rosenbaum to Shlomo Kugel

*1879*

The wedding took place during Parshas Terumah 5639 (early 1879). R' Avraham Chaim invited his close friend R' Shmuel Dovber of Borisov, who responded on 25 Adar with a mazal-tov letter explaining why he had been unable to attend. The match linked the Rosenbaum family with the Kugel family of Pleshchenitsy.
