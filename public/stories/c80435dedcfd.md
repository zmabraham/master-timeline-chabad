# R' Shilem Kuratin appointed mashgiach for chassidut

*1912*

In winter 5672 (early 1912), R' Shilem Kuratin from Disna was appointed mashgiach for chassidut studies at Tomchei Tmimim Lubavitch. He was known for his deep meditation in prayer and his ability to repeat the Rebbe Rashab's chassidic discourses verbatim. He continued in his role when the yeshiva moved to Kremenchug, until his passing in early Elul 5679.
