# 13 Nissan farbrengen at the Gurary shul in Kremenchug

*1918*

While stranded in Kremenchug arranging accommodations for the shiur students, the narrator attended a 13 Nissan farbrengen in the Gurary synagogue. R' Elchonon Dov Morozov was the principal speaker, with the meshulach R' Yechiel Halperin and others present. The gathering was part of the consolidation of the Tomchei Temimim community in its new wartime home.
