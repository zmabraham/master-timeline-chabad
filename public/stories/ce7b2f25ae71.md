# Chabad delegation petitions Lubyanka KGB for return of Schneersohn library

*1991*

While tanks were rolling through Moscow during the attempted hardline coup, Rabbis Aharonov, Kunin and Kogan walked into Lubyanka to request a meeting with the KGB chairman about the seized Schneersohn library. They were redirected to an adjacent building where two women received them, said they were aware of the books issue, and ultimately told them only to submit a formal written request — the KGB chairman was in fact one of the coup organizers.
