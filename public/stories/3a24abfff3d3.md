# Shabbos Chanukah 5706 farbrengen on emigration plans

*1945*

On Shabbos Chanukah 5706, Rabbis Nachum Sasonkin, Dov Chen, and other senior chassidim gathered at Levin's home for a farbrengen and challenged Levin and Michael Teitelbaum's plan to apply for exit papers. Teitelbaum, well into the mashke, retorted that those whose lives were comfortable in Russia could stay where they were — he and Levin, whose lives were bitter both materially and spiritually, would do everything in their power and trust in Hashem to help them.
