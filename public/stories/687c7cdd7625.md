# Rebbe Rayatz flies from Riga to Stockholm

*1940*

Jews of Riga, chassidim and non-chassidim alike, packed the aerodrome to bid farewell. Chief of Riga's community Mordechai Dubin accompanied the family. The elder Rebbetzin was carried aboard on a stretcher. The Rebbe blessed the Latvian government, its president, and the Jewish community, urging all to join Agudas Yisroel.
