# Rebbe Rashab travels to Petrograd to finalize rabbi exemption

*1916*

On the 4th of Iyar 5676, the first day of Parshat Behar, the Rebbe Rashab prepared to journey again to Petrograd. The purpose was to bring to completion the lobbying efforts with government ministers to exempt rabbis and shochtim from military service. He returned home for the festival of Shavuot.
