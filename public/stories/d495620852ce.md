# First visa application list for 47 anash families submitted to US government

*1940*

The petition asked for temporary visas to shelter anash in America until the war ended. It accompanied a separate list of 127 yeshiva-student visas already in process. A second batch of 62 visas covering roughly 200 more souls would follow in November, after which attorney Arthur Rabinowitz filed a consolidated brief through Rashag.
