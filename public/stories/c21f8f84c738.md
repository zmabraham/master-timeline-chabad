# Russian government order on Chabad academy, yeshiva and library

*1991*

The directive redirected the destination of the Schneerson books from a free-standing Jewish library to one housed inside the Chabad synagogue complex, alongside a new academy and yeshiva-university. The Rebbe, asked through Rabbi Groner whether to accept this arrangement, expressed sharp concern that under such a structure it would be unclear how the books could ever be brought to 770 if ownership formally passed to a Russian-Jewish library.
