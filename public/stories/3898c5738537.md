# R' Yitzchak Dov Oshpal reports 33 Vilna yeshiva students

*1936*

R' Oshpal submitted a formal report stating the yeshiva contained three classes: 15 in class A, 7 in class B, and 11 in class C. Of the 33 students, 7 were residents of Vilna while 26 came from other towns. A full roster of students with their ages was attached the following day.
