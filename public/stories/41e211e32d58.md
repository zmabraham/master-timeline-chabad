# Rebbe receives tefillin and food from home in Shpalerka

*1927*

On Friday June 17, 1927, after two nights and three days of imprisonment, a Jewish guard returned the Rebbe's confiscated tefillin and books. The Rebbe refused the prison's non-kosher meals and demanded food from home. Just before sunset, three loaves of uncut challah were delivered to his cell.
