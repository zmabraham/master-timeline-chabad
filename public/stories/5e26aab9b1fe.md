# Chabad chassidim begin migrating from Hebron to Jerusalem

*1847*

Suffering under pressure from local landlords and the harsh treatment of gentile neighbors, Chabad families began leaving Hebron for Jerusalem in 5607 (1847). They rented homes in the northern Old City along Harat al-Wad — which Ashkenazim soon nicknamed 'Chevronim Street' (Hebron Street) — laying the foundation for the Chabad community in Jerusalem.
