# RA"Ch writes from Smolensk prison about visiting Lubavitch after release

*1890*

On Thursday of Parshas Tzav 5650, as his release approached, RA"Ch wrote to R' Hendel that he intended to ask his elderly father in Pleshchenitz for permission, or advice, on whether to travel home via Lubavitch. He notes his father is so weak that he can no longer add greetings in his own hand to family letters and counts the hours until they meet again in life.
