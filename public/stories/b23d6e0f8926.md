# R. Mandelbaum reports overcrowded conditions at Łódź

*1931*

In his report of 8 Kislev 5692, R. Mandelbaum described the yeshiva's grave financial situation: income was minimal relative to expenses and the Schneersohn brothers were covering shortfalls from their own pockets. Nonetheless, he was pleased with the students' diligence in both nigleh and chassidus, and reported that the yeshiva had earned a reputation among the yeshivos of Łódź.
