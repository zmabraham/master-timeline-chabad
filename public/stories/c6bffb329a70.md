# Father-in-law R' Yehoshua Heshel Levin passes away

*1920*

He had long suffered from a debilitating leg ailment but could no longer travel to therapeutic spas because of the civil war. After the wedding his son brought him from Gomel to Belitsa, where he was bedridden until his death several weeks later. The author was then beginning to peddle small quantities of salt and tobacco between cities to support his family.
