# R' Yosef Blumenfeld immigrates to Eretz Yisrael

*1935*

After serving as rabbi in Krolewska Huta, R' Yosef Blumenfeld immigrated to Eretz Yisrael in the winter of 5695. There he served on the rabbinical court (beis din) in Tel Aviv and authored a commentary on the Kaftor VaFerach by Eshtori HaParchi.
