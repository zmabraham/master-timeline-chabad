# Bricha crossing from Poland into Czechoslovakia by night, late Av 5706

*1946*

The Bricha was an underground operation run by young Eretz Yisrael emissaries in coordination with the Joint and the American Vaad Hatzalah, smuggling Soviet refugees on foot across borders into Czechoslovakia, Austria, and Germany. The narrator carried two suitcases while his wife held the 1½-year-old Miriam and led five-year-old Leah by the hand. From the Czech DP camp they were transferred to Vienna and housed in the former Rothschild Hospital.
