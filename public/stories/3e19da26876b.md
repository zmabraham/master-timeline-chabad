# Tomchei Tmimim Vilna closes; talmidim scattered or rescued via Japan

*1941*

Across 5700–5701 the Rebbe Rayatz worked to obtain visas and travel papers for the Vilna talmidim, routing many through the Soviet Union to Japan, on to Shanghai, and ultimately to the United States and Canada. Some talmidim perished in the Shoah and others were swept into Nazi labor camps, surviving only by the end of the war. The branch that had taken root in the Apatov shul thirty-two years earlier closed with the broader destruction of Lithuanian Jewry.
