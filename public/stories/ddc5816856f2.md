# Standalone teshuva of the Alter Rebbe published in Kapust

*1815*

The 10-folio Kapust 5575 (1815) quarto presented one of the Alter Rebbe's responsa on its own, alongside other teshuvot that would be embedded into the Shulchan Aruch volumes. It would later be regathered into the Zhitomir 1847 responsa collection.
