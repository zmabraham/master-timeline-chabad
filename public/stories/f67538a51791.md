# R' Avraham Hershkop in the Rebbe's court. R' Avraham Yosef (Lemi) Hershkop

*1967*

R' Avraham Hershkop in the Rebbe's court. R' Avraham Yosef (Lemi) Hershkop (born 5727, 1967) is a Chabad activist, resident of Nachalat Har Chabad, among the founders of the local 'Ezrat Achim' organization, and among the initiators of the Keren Areivim program for Chabad chassidim in the Holy Land
