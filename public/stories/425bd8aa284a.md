# Death of R. Zev Wolf b. Yeshayahu, buried in Chabad section of Mount of Olives

*1882*

His name and date are recorded in 'Avnei Zikaron' chelek 12 (copied in 'Oholei Lyubavitch' vol. 3 p. 75 ff.). The entry establishes the presence of MaHaRIL's family descendants in nineteenth-century Eretz Yisrael and confirms their continued affiliation with the Chabad community.
