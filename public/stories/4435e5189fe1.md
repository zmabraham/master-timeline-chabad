# Chabad rabbi leaves Globoke

*1930*

In the winter of 5690 (1930), the rabbi of the Chabad community in Globoke left the town. The chassidic community (separate from the general kehilla, which had its own rabbi, R. Yosef HaLevi Katz) turned to the Rebbe Rayatz, then in America, asking him to send a Chabad rabbi to replace him.
