# Compromise agreement on Chernigov delegates

*1909*

On the morning of the assembly, Yitzchak Schneersohn came to the Rebbe Rashab's home immediately after prayers and proposed a compromise to avoid conflict: each side would get one delegate. The Rebbe's candidate Rabbi Mendel Chen of Nezhin would be elected, alongside one candidate from the opposing camp. The Rebbe, calculating the uncertainty of his less reliable supporters, accepted on the spot. Both delegates were subsequently elected from Chernigov gubernia.
