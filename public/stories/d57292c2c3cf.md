# RA"Ch describes year of financial and personal losses

*1905*

In a letter dated Rosh Chodesh Adar Sheni 5665, RA"Ch reflects on a punishing previous year: the death of his wife after a four-month illness on which he spent over a thousand dollars, and a separate loss of nearly two thousand dollars in his business when goods failed to arrive from Germany. Together, he writes, these consumed about three-quarters of all the savings he had accumulated in his later years.
