# Memoirist and father flee Zlobin to Zhitomir

*1938*

A woman who worked at the bread shop and was friendly with the NKVD secretary tipped the memoirist off that another round of arrests was planned that very night. Knowing his father and himself were on the list, the memoirist made hurried arrangements and that Friday night the two boarded a train for Zhitomir. From there the memoirist continued to Gomel, then to his brother Zev in Leningrad, until the danger passed.
