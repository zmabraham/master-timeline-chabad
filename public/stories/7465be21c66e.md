# Rebbe Rashab distributes banknotes on Sukkot 5680

*1919*

On Sukkot of 5680, Rebbe Sholom DovBer of Lubavitch distributed banknotes either as gifts or as a segulah (spiritual remedy). The dates were inscribed in the margins by his secretary, Rabbi Elchonon Dov Marozov. This was the Rebbe Rashab's final Sukkot before his passing on 2 Nissan 5680.
