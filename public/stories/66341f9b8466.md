# Agudat Chabad committee meeting on aid, arrest file, and library

*1991*

Decisions included moving food aid shipments from the Monosonik depot to the new synagogue, publicizing in Kfar Chabad magazine that the file documents proved R' Levi Yitzchak's arrest had been deliberately framed, and dispatching photographs of the documents for the article. Veronika reported that the synagogue building handover would be finalized the next day; on the library, however, she urged patience and waiting for the right opportunity rather than further public pressure.
