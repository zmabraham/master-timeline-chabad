# Rebbe Rayatz library collection arrives in New York

*1941*

When the Rebbe Rayatz was rescued from Warsaw in early 1940 his book collection remained behind in Nazi-occupied Poland. For a year and a half emissaries worked to move the collection through occupied Europe in stages. In late 5701 (1941) it finally arrived in New York and was housed on the ground floor of the newly acquired Chabad center at 770 Eastern Parkway.
