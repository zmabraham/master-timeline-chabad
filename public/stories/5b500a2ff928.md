# Yeshivas Zichron Yosef founded in Dankra

*1936*

Following the death of the Rogatchover Gaon Yosef Rosen in 5696, the town of Dankra (also called Gostini) in Latvia established a Chabad yeshiva named Zichron Yosef in his memory. The yeshiva was run by the town rabbi, Yeshaya Chanoch Berchen, son-in-law of Refael Hakohen of Riga.
