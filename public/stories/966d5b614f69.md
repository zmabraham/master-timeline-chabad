# Rashag-led delegation meets First Lady Eleanor Roosevelt at the White House

*1941*

Arranged by the Quaker-led American Friends Service Committee after Clarence Pickett's appeal to Mrs. Roosevelt, the delegation requested intervention with the Russian government. The effort failed: the Bobover Rebbe, who fled to Lvov, was beaten to death by German soldiers months later, and the Riga Chasidim were killed after the German invasion of Latvia.
