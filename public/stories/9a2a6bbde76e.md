# Alter Rebbe begins composing Shulchan Aruch with the Maggid

*1773*

The Maggid of Mezeritch repeatedly urged the young Shneur Zalman to compile a Shulchan Aruch. The project began during Shabbos Tachkemoni at the Maggid's court, where he completed the laws of Tzitzis and Pesach. The complete work would not be printed until decades after his passing.
