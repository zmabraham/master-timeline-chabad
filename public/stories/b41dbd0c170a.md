# R' Eliezer Moshe Madievsky sent to Machzikei HaDat in Kovno

*1899*

Madievsky was sent on behalf of the Rebbe Rashab to coordinate with the Machzikei HaDat office headed by R' Yaakov Lifshitz and R' Z. Landa, called by the secular press 'the Black Bureau.' The mission was to unite all 'God-fearing' Jews—rebbes, rabbis, and observant Maskilim—in a counter-offensive against the Zionist movement.
