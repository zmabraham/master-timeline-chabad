# Passing of R' Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk

*1788*

R' Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk, the foremost disciple of the Maggid of Mezritch who led the 1777 chassidic aliyah, passed away in summer 5548. After his passing, R' Avraham of Kalisk continued to guide the Holy Land community, while the Alter Rebbe assumed responsibility for the chassidim in Russia and their fundraising for Eretz Yisrael.
