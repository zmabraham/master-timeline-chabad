# Latvian authorities permit second Riga synagogue

*1878*

In 5638 (1878) the Latvian authorities permitted the construction of an additional synagogue building within the Riga Chassidic shul complex. From then on, three minyanim — all called 'Draisin' minyanim — operated under one roof: a mixed Chabad minyan, one exclusively for Lubavitch chassidim, and a larger one for Kapust chassidim.
