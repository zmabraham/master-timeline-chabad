# Hebron Vaad publishes public appeal for funds

*1890*

After community leaders begged them to continue, R' Binyamin Rivlin and R' Meir Tzvi Hershler published in Chabatzelet on 8 Tevet 5650 a public appeal describing the Vaad's emptied coffers, unpaid communal workers, and inability to pay the doctor and shochet, calling on diaspora Jewry to come to the aid of the Ashkenazi communities of Hebron.
