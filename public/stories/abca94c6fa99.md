# Kasher's letter thanking the Rebbe for Joint Distribution Committee support

*1961*

On 24 Sivan 5721, Rabbi Kasher wrote to the Rebbe with heartfelt thanks. JDC representatives had told Kasher that during their yechidut with the Rebbe, in the middle of discussing their own support for Chabad activities, the Rebbe had taken the opportunity to praise the Tzofnat Paneach project and urge them to support it.
