# Mittler Rebbe sends three foundational letters to Anash including Kuntres HaHispaalus

*1814*

Manuscript evidence in the Library identifies the date as 5574 for all three letters. The first served as introduction, the second became the famous Kuntres HaHispaalus on the levels of emotional response in avodah, and the third was later printed as Shaar HaYichud (Kuntres HaHisbonenus). They were published in reverse order across the following decades.
