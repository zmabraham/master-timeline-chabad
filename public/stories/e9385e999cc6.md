# Rebbe Rayatz arrives in New York harbor

*1940*

Delegations from Agudas HaAdmorim, Agudas HaRabbonim, major yeshivos, communal askanim, and charitable institutions welcomed him. In the harbor's largest hall, he conveyed greetings from European Jewry and urged American Jews to redouble efforts to aid those still trapped overseas. He stayed at the Greystone Hotel for nearly a month.
