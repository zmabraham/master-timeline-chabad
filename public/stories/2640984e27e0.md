# Rashab decides to send Tomchei Temimim students to expand Hebron yeshiva

*1911*

After more than a year of discussion, the Rashab convened the directors of Tomchei Temimim on 25 Iyar 5671 for over three hours to plan support for Hebron. He decided to dispatch outstanding avreichim, including a mashpia in Chassidus, to establish the Magen Avos yeshiva on a sound footing and to make it an open branch of Tomchei Temimim. The decision was reported in letters Shilo sent to Hebron on 9-10 Sivan.
