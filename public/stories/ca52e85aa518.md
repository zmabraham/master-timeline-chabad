# Alter Rebbe describes separate Chabad fund for Tzfat exiles

*1810*

In a 5570 letter the Alter Rebbe writes that he is sending separate support 'to revive the spirit of the lowly and crushed' Chabad community that had moved from Tiberias to Tzfat to escape harassment. Followers of R' Levi Yitzchak of Berditchev who had also fled to Tzfat stood with the Chabad chassidim. The Alter Rebbe coordinated with R' Levi Yitzchak's trusted messenger to transmit the funds.
