# Pledge of $8,000 to print Tzemach Tzedek

*1945*

On 19 Tevet 5705 (4 January 1945) the Rebbe Rayatz received Mr. Elchanan Glazer, whose father Mr. Yisrael Glazer was ill. He promised to give $8,000 — the estimated cost of printing the Tzemach Tzedek's works — toward that project. The Rebbe prayed that the pledge would quickly come to fruition.
