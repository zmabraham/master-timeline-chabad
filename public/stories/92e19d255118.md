# Levin escapes Soviet Union as Polish refugee 'Naftali Bat'

*1946*

Unable to renew his army exemption in Samarkand, Levin had gone underground using papers identifying him as a Polish refugee not yet drafted. He could not work openly under this name without Polish-language skills, so he worked under his wife's name. The same Polish papers eventually allowed both of them to leave Russia, with his wife re-registered as having remarried a Pole named Naftali Bat.
