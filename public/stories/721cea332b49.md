# Author returns to Lubavitch and revives Kupat Bachurim

*1917*

Train service had broken down, so the author traveled via Mohilev by steamboat to Orsha and onward by wagon. He found Lubavitch sharply diminished — the Rav's home in ruins, R' Zalman Fradkin visibly hungry when he came to the bochurim's dining hall, students rationed roughly two litras of bread per day. Shabbos challot were now baked small and of plain wheat flour. The author convened the older talmidim and refounded the Kupat Bachurim mutual aid society, writing to married Tomchei Tmimim alumni in scattered towns for help and securing supplemental support for the neediest students.
