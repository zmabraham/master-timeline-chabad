# Rebbe Rayatz released from Soviet imprisonment and exile

*1927*

After his arrest and exile in Soviet Russia in summer 1927 (5687), Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn was freed but remained under threat of further imprisonment. The danger compelled him to seek to leave Russia as quickly as possible. Latvian parliament deputy Mordechai Dubin worked through the summer to secure his exit visa by arranging his appointment as Chief Rabbi of Riga.
