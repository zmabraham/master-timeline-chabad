# Tomchei Tmimim's main class moves from Tel Aviv to Lod

*1951*

Following the Rebbe's endorsement, after Pesach 5711 the main shiur of the Tel Aviv yeshiva relocated to the existing Lod campus. Only the local 'Bnei Tmimim' children's cheder remained in Tel Aviv; once those boys finished cheder they moved on to study at Tomchei Tmimim in Lod.
