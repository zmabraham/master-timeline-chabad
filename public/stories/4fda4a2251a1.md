# Wedding of Sheina Schneerson and Mendel Horenstein in Landwarow

*1932*

Landwarow was chosen because of visa issues, sitting just over the border from Vilnius. Rabbi Baruch Ber Leibowitz of Kaminetz Yeshivah was also present and had a scholarly discussion with the Ramash. After the wedding, the couple moved to Paris, and a few months later Chaya Mushka and the Ramash joined them there.
