# New Italy/Spain shadarus compromise in Hebron

*1829*

On Rosh Chodesh Elul 1829 (ר"ח אלול תקפ"ט), a new compromise between the Chabad and Sephardic communities in Hebron came into effect regarding shadar missions to Italy and Spain. This compromise resolved disputes that had not been settled in the first 1825 agreement.
