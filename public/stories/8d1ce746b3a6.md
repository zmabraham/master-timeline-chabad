# Reception receptions in Seattle, San Francisco, Los Angeles

*1946*

Tomchei Tmimim representatives sent from New York by the Rashag — among them R. Yosef Weinberg and R. Moshe Hecht — met the students upon disembarkation. The group of about thirty was divided three ways: ten traveled to Seattle, Washington (welcomed at the train station by Rabbi Shapira and others), ten remained in San Francisco, and ten went to Los Angeles, where R. Moshe Hecht hosted a large reception in a packed Moshav Zekenim hall, the first such welcome for yeshiva war refugees in America.
