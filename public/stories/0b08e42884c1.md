# Rayatz farbrengs for hours on Purim after fast collapse

*1921*

Still recuperating from typhus, the Rebbe asked his doctor whether he could farbreng and say a maamar on Purim. The doctor allowed it but instructed him to limit the maamar to fifteen minutes. The next day the Rebbe spoke for two and a half hours and remained with the Chasidim for more than three additional hours.
