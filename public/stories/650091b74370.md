# Widow Alaz acknowledges Yaakov ibn Atar's debt of 400 ouquiyot

*1703*

On 18 Shevat 5463 (1703) in Fez, Alaz and her son Mordechai issued a mechilah to Yaakov ben Avraham ibn Atar, formally acknowledging that of orphan funds entrusted to him to be lent out on pledges in the customary manner, 400 ouquiyot remained outstanding. A debt note for that exact sum was executed the same day. Over the following years Yaakov repaid in installments noted on the back of the shtar, until only about 104 ouquiyot remained when he became impoverished.
