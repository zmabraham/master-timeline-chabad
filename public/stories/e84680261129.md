# Soviet Foreign Ministry calls Veronika with decision to return Chabad library

*1991*

In the early hours of 28 Shevat 5751, Veronika (Devorah Rochel) phoned the delegation to relay that Deputy Foreign Minister Ivanov had just informed her of the decision to return the Chabad library, with details to follow at 10 a.m. Shortly afterward Rabbi Leibel Groner relayed from New York that Secretary of State Baker had received a positive reply on the matter and that Mr. Shishlin reported a Friday government meeting that had decided in favor of return.
