# Rebbe launches international matzah campaign for Soviet Jewry

*1929*

The Rebbe enlisted Mordechai Dubin, the Rashag, and rabbis including Chaim Ozer Grodzinski, Yisrael Meir Kagan, and Yosef Chaim Sonnenfeld. Despite Soviet obstruction, 140,000 kilos of matzah reached Russian Jews, proving the Rebbe could marshal global support without the Jewish establishment.
