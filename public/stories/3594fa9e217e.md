# R' Chaim Liberman departs Tambov for Riga

*1928*

After roughly a year of imprisonment and exile, R' Chaim Liberman was released and obtained an exit permit—largely through the efforts of the Rayatz from Riga and the intervention of Latvian Sejm deputy Mordechai Dubin. A photograph in the library archive shows his last day in Tambov, marked '1 May 1928,' corresponding to 11 Iyar 5688. He stopped briefly in Moscow on the advice of fellow Chassidim R' Yaakov Maskalik and R' Shmuel Levitin not to delay his departure.
