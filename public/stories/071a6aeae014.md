# Rebbe arrives safely in Riga after escape from Nazi Europe

*1939*

The Rebbe was met by chasidic crowds at Vilnius and Kovno stations, where Eliyahu Vad of Dos Vort interviewed him about the state of the Jewish nation. At the Latvian border Bloch's escort departed. In New York Rabbi Jacobson did handsprings on the sidewalk in joy and hosted a farbrengen, then placed a transatlantic call to thank the Rebbe the next day.
