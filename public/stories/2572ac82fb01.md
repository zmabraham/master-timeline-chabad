# Reb Shraga Feivish Zalmanov arrives in Lubavitch

*1895*

In 5655 (1895), between Pesach and Shavuot, R' Shraga Feivish Zalmanov arrived in Lubavitch to study among the 'yoshvim'. He later recorded his memories of that period, describing how R' Chanoch Hendel would teach Tanya daily between Mincha and Maariv to the young men. The Rebbe Rayatz mentioned his arrival in Sefer HaSichot 5702.
