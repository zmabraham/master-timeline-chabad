# Haggadah 'BiMesila' anthology of Zidichov dynasty teachings published in New York

*1989*

In 5749 (1989), Moshe Aharon Moshkovitz published in New York the Haggadah 'BiMesila', a collection of Torah novellae from the Chassidic dynasties of Zidichov, Komarno, Sambor, and Rozdol, drawn from the writings of the Rebbes and their descendants. The 232-page octavo work gathered material from printed books and manuscripts. It served as a comprehensive anthology of these closely related Galician Chassidic courts.
