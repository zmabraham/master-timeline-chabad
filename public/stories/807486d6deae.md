# Megillat Esther with Akedat Yitzchak commentary printed in Constantinople

*1518*

In 5278 (1518), Shmuel Nachmias, son of David Nachmias, printed his final book — Megillat Esther with the commentary of Rabbi Yitzchak Arama (Akedat Yitzchak), one of the Spanish exiles. The Nachmias brothers had founded the first Hebrew press in Constantinople after the 1492 expulsion.
