# Sabbioneta prints Rif with Shiltei HaGiborim

*1554*

In 5314 (1554), with Talmud printing forbidden by papal decree, Tuviah Foa's Sabbioneta press shifted to printing the Rif. The edition was edited by Rabbi Yehoshua Boaz and included his newly composed Shiltei HaGiborim along with substantial marginal additions.
