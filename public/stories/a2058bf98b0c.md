# R' Avraham Chaim begins counting days to redemption from prison

*1889*

Immediately upon the entry of the year 5650 (autumn 1889), R' Avraham Chaim Rosin, held in the Smolensk fortress prison, began to count the days until his liberation. He expressed this hope in letters to acquaintances, calling the year tarn (תר"נ) a year of mattir asurim ('releaser of the bound'). The countdown framed his correspondence and poetry during the entire year of imprisonment.
