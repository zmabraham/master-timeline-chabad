# R' Shneur Zalman Shmotkin reports to R' Dovber Rivkin on Warsaw yeshiva

*1924*

On the 7th of Chanukah 5685, R' Shneur Zalman Shmotkin sent a letter to R' Dovber Rivkin in Eretz Yisrael, asking about R' Alter Simchowitz's arrival, his health, and the state of Yeshivas Toras Emes in both quantity and quality, in physical and spiritual matters. The same letter described the recent appointment of R' Yosef Blumenfeld as mashgiach in the Warsaw yeshiva.
