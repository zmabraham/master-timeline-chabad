# Tzemach Tzedek accepts Chabad leadership in Lubavitch

*1827*

The Tzemach Tzedek, both nephew and son-in-law of his predecessor, would lead the movement for nearly forty years from Lubavitch. In his early years he regularly toured Chassidic communities, having visited more than thirty towns by 5597. Lubavitch under his leadership became the cornerstone to which all Chabad communities turned.
