# Daughter Chaya Sara hospitalized with scarlet fever in Riga

*1925*

The American consul, on hearing of the contagion, warned that any entry to the consulate within three months would permanently void the visa. The municipal hospital had little warmth for Jewish refugees, so the parents could only visit at a window each day—including on Rosh Hashanah—bringing a hard-to-find orange and listening to their daughter beg to be taken home.
