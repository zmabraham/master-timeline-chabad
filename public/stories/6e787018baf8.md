# Hebron Tomchei Tmimim mashpia and students depart Lubavitch

*1911*

The Rashab told Havlin his sole focus in Hebron must be the yeshiva itself, apart from supervising Chabad properties. The Rayatz, as head of Tomchei Tmimim, formally appointed Havlin to the central vaad and as the local mashpia and representative, instructing him to commission an official institutional seal bearing the Tomchei Tmimim name.
