# Israel Yehuda's family arrives in Kzyl-Orda en route to Samarkand

*1941*

The journey took the family first to Saratov, where they sheltered with the distinguished R' Yaakov Yitzchak Karasik, who advised them that the evacuation already amounted to leaving the country since everyone was heading to Samarkand. Boarding another train, they reached Kzyl-Orda for Shabbat Mevarchim Kislev, finding bitter cold nights, hot days, and severe hunger.
