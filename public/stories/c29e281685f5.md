# Passing of the Alter Rebbe in Piena

*1813*

On Motzei Shabbat parashat Shemot, the beginning of 24 Tevet 5573 (around January 8, 1813), at approximately 11 o'clock at night, the Alter Rebbe passed away in the town of Piena. His grandson the Tzemach Tzedek described the event as the ark of holiness being taken captive — the light of Israel and the anointed of Hashem summoned to the heavenly academy. He died as a wartime refugee, having refused to remain under Napoleonic rule.
