# Investigators submit summary of investigation to Khovansky

*1825*

On 10 November 1825 (12 Kislev 5586), the investigators handling the case against Rabbi DovBer wrote a comprehensive review of the interrogations and evidence gathered, and turned the file over to Governor Khovansky. This summary became the basis for the subsequent transfer of the case to the Mohilev provincial authorities for formal judicial proceedings.
