# R' Yaakov Landau dispatched as covert envoy to refugee towns

*1916*

The Rebbe Rashab dispatched Reb Yaakov Landau of Kornitz (later rabbi of Bnei Brak) as a covert emissary to survey the religious state of the Jewish refugees. Landau departed shortly after Purim 5676 for a month-long journey and was forbidden to reveal his mission to anyone. He returned about a week before Pesach and delivered his report to the Rebbe on the night of bedikat chametz at Rostov, having returned for Shabbat HaGadol as the Rebbe had commanded.
