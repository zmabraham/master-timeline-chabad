# Radom underground yeshiva ceases to exist

*1942*

Yosef Goldstein later summarized that the yeshiva had operated from the beginning of 5701 until the end of 5702, after which all were destroyed and led as sheep to the slaughter together with the holy communities of Poland. R' Berl Kornitzer and R' Baruch Friedman were among those killed, along with most of the close to two hundred bochurim.
