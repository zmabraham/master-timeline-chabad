# Sixth Rebbe departs Russia after Simchat Torah in Leningrad

*1927*

Futerfas's last sight of the Rebbe was at the Simchat Torah gathering in Leningrad in 1927. When facing Soviet interrogation on 17 Iyar 5707, he conjured that image and mentally composed a letter requesting a blessing — to which, by mystical coincidence, the Rebbe in New York responded that very day.
