# Rebbe Rashab kuntres clarifies new pushkes are for entire kollel, not Hebron alone

*1906*

Some had interpreted the Chevronim vaad's expanded fundraising as specifically aiding Chabad's Hebron yishuv, prompting Rashab to publish a clarifying kuntres. He restated his earlier kruz that 'it never occurred to me that the new shoferos would be for Hebron alone.' The clarification reaffirmed that all new income belonged to the general kollel infrastructure.
