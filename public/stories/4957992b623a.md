# Yisrael Yehuda Levin returned from Leningrad to Moscow for Pesach

*1934*

On Erev Pesach 5694 (March 30, 1934), the author returned from Leningrad to Moscow and back to Malachovka. He had spent roughly a month in Leningrad, lodging with Rabbi Yitzchok Raskin and attending farbrengens and Shabbos seudos at the home of Rabbi Elchanan Morozov. Hosting unregistered Chassidim in Leningrad was literal mesirat nefesh given Soviet surveillance.
