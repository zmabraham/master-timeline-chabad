# Rabbi Yehuda ibn Attar born in Fez

*1655*

Yehuda ibn Attar was born in Fez (פאס) in the Hebrew year 5415 (1655). He would become the leading halachic authority of Moroccan Jewry, known by Moroccan Jews as 'Arbi Alkabir' (Our Great Rabbi). His family pedigree traced through generations of Fez scholars: his father R. Yaakov, grandfather R. Avraham, and great-grandfather R. Yaakov.
