# Rabbi Frishman; Rabbi Frishman passes before the Rebbe at the conclusion of yechidus

*2016*

Rabbi Frishman; Rabbi Frishman passes before the Rebbe at the conclusion of a general yechidus. Rabbi Yisrael Yitzchak Frishman (19 Adar 5718 – 9 Shevat 5776; March 1, 1958 – January 17, 2016) was a member of the directorate of Tzeirei Agudat Chabad in Jerusalem and one of the prominent askanim in the community
