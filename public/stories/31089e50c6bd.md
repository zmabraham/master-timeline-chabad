# Shemtovs sent to build Chabad in England

*1948*

Having survived three years in Siberia and decades of underground activism, Shemtov was sent on a new mission to the United Kingdom. He worked to build the Chabad community there before later moving to Israel, where he founded Machon Levi Yitzchak to publish the seventh Rebbe's talks in Hebrew. He passed away in 1975 at age seventy-two.
