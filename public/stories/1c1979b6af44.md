# Warsaw chassidim invite Rebbe Rayatz to settle in Poland

*1927*

After the Rebbe Rayatz left Soviet Russia and settled in Riga, the heads of Warsaw Chabad — R. Shraga Feivish Zalmanov, R. Shneur Zalman Shmotkin, and R. Yechiel Tzvi Gurary — wrote on 27 Tishrei 5688 inviting him to relocate to Poland or the Polish-ruled parts of Lithuania. They argued that the bulk of anash and the Tomchei Tmimim students were now concentrated in Poland, and that travel from Riga was very difficult. The Rebbe declined the move but agreed to make a visit.
