# Jacobson departs Riga for America on 19 Kislev

*1925*

Mid-seudah at the shochet R. Mordechai Chefetz's home, Jacobson received parting blessings from his friends and set out via Kovno, Königsberg, Berlin, Paris, and Le Havre. A planned reunion in Berlin with his friend Alter Beilin—who held his own American visa and would later become his brother-in-law—failed because Beilin's departure was on a different schedule and neither yet knew how to easily locate the other in a foreign city.
