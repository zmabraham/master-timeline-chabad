# Maharash builds large hall in Lubavitch courtyard

*1872*

The hall featured an unusually high ceiling and tall, wide windows on three sides. It was used for High Holiday prayers and large farbrengens whenever sizable crowds of Chassidim came to Lubavitch. It later hosted major family celebrations including the 5652 Hornstein wedding, the 5653 bar mitzvah, and the 5657 wedding of the Frierdiker Rebbe.
