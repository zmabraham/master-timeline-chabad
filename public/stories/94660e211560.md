# R' Yoel Smolyaner sent on shlichut to Russia

*1789*

In winter 5549 R' Yoel Smolyaner left the Holy Land as the official emissary to the Chasidic communities of Russia for the year 5550. A Tiberias letter from Rosh Chodesh Iyar 5549 already noted that his correspondence had reached Constantinople and that he was diverting via Trieste. His mission was unusually long and he did not return until Nissan 5551.
