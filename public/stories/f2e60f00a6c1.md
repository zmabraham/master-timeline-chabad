# R' Yehoshua of Horki passes away

*1842*

R' Yehoshua served as rabbi of Horki in the early years of the Tzemach Tzedek's leadership. His final psak was issued on Erev Shabbos in Nissan 5602, and the Tzemach Tzedek's responsa to him appear in Even HaEzer. He was succeeded by R' Yehoshua Eliyahu ben Yosef.
