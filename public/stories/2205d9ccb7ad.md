# Rebbe Rayatz cables New York demanding Joint funding for student rescue

*1940*

After Tropper of the European Joint deferred his earlier appeal, the Rebbe Rayatz wired R. Yisroel Jacobson and R. Shmuel Levitin in New York on 26 Shevat 5700, itemizing $5,000 for Pesach aid, $4,000 to rescue 200 yeshiva students, and $5,143 to bring six rabbis and their families to the US. He instructed them to meet Rosen urgently and recruit Kramer and Dr. Jung to lobby the Joint.
