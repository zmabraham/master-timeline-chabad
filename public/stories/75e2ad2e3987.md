# Birth of son Gershon in Paris during postwar refugee interlude

*1947*

Holding a U.S. visa but pregnant near term, the author's wife had consulted the Rebbe Rayatz on whether to sail before or after the birth, since trans-Atlantic travel was then by ship. The Rebbe approved their plan to emigrate. On the night entering Friday — erev Shabbat Vayishlach, 15 Kislev 5708 — their son Gershon was born in Paris among the postwar Chabad refugee community.
