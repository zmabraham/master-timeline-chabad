# R' Persson recalled from Paris for Pesach

*1901*

Persson had spent five weeks in Paris meeting with R' Tzadok Kahn, R' Shmuel Altmann, R' Shlomo Cohen, and JCA president M. Leven, struggling to overturn the Mefitzei Haskalah grant. The Rebbe instructed him to return home for the holiday and then travel back to Paris after Pesach for the JCA committee's late-April session, when the Rebbe himself would also travel to Paris.
