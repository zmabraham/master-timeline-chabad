# Publication of Rayatz's Kuntres Chag HaPesach 5708 in New York

*1948*

In 5708 (1948), Kuntres Chag HaPesach of the Chabad Rayatz Rebbe Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn was published in New York. The 29-page octavo edition contained the sixth Chabad Rebbe's discourses for the festival. It was issued during the Rebbe Rayatz's American years following his arrival in 1940.
