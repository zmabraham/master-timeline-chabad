# Alter Rebbe flees Liadi as Napoleon's army advances

*1812*

When Napoleon's army advanced into Russia in 1812, the Alter Rebbe and his family abandoned Liadi to escape the French. The entire Bais HaRav in Liadi was consumed by fire in the aftermath. This destruction left the family destitute and later prompted a special ma'amad collection organized to rebuild a home for the rebbe's family in Lubavitch.
