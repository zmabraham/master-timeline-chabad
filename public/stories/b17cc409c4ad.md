# Vilna rabbinical assembly convenes under Rebbe Rashab

*1909*

Rebbe Yosef Yitzchak later described it as 'the great Vilna assembly, the like of which had not been held in a hundred years.' Authorized by the Interior Ministry strictly for matters of religious strengthening, it nevertheless adopted a resolution to the ministry connecting religious decline to the economic catastrophe of the Pale of Settlement.
