# Aliyah of R. Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk to Eretz Yisrael

*1777*

Following years of persecution and the mounting conflict with the mitnagdim, R. Menachem Mendel emigrated with a chassidic group to Eretz Yisrael, eventually settling in Tzfat. He would continue to guide the Russian chassidic community by letter from there.
