# Shavuos kiddush in Brooklyn unites Lubavitcher community around Toras Emes

*1928*

Shavuos 5688 fell on Friday and Shabbos. The author hosted a kiddush with cholent at his home, inviting his own shul's mispallelim and several minyanim of Anash from Brownsville — then considered 'the Yerushalayim of America.' R' Shlomo Zalman Havlin's warmth captivated the crowd, and fundraising drives for Toras Emes were soon organized in several local shuls including the Lubavitcher shtibel on Watkins Street under Rav Tzvi Hirsch Dachowitz.
