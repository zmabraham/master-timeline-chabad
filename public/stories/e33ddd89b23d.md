# R' Yosef Yehuda Leib m'Shyuf and R' Ephraim Yitzchak Epstein make aliyah from Shklov

*1830*

In 5590, R' Ephraim Yitzchak Epstein traveled from Shklov to Chevron with his wife, son Zalman, daughter, son-in-law R' Yosef Yehuda Leib m'Shyuf, and their four-year-old son Yaakov Zevulun. Hearing of their journey, the Tzemach Tzedek entrusted them to deliver the Russian Chabad support funds to the Chevron community. They arrived in Kislev 5591 after road troubles delayed them past Rosh Hashanah.
