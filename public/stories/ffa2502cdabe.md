# Rebbe urges founding similar Achos Tmimim chapters elsewhere

*1937*

The Rebbe wrote that 'recently the daughters of the chassidim in Riga have organized to learn maamarim and hold farbrengens' and that 'it is correct and necessary' for chassidim in other locales to take this as an example, founding similar groups to 'revive the hearts of their daughters with the dew of life of the pleasant ways of Chassidus.'
