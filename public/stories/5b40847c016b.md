# Responsa attributed to Ramban printed in Venice

*1519*

In 5279 (1519), a volume titled 'Teshuvot leRabbeinu Moshe ben Nachman' containing 288 responsa was printed in Venice. The responsa were actually those of the Rashba but had been mistakenly attributed to Ramban; the volume is counted as part 8 of the Rashba responsa series.
