# Mogilev Chassidim sign agreement for new synagogue and mikveh

*1822*

On 17 July 1822 (11 Menachem Av 5582), members of the community identified as 'Chassidim of the Lubavitcher Rebbe' in Mogilev signed a binding agreement appointing Wolf Rashel, Shlomo Dankin, and Aizik Cohen, together with community elders Yosef Cohen and Yeshaya Mirkin, as trustees to oversee construction of a new synagogue on land purchased from Rasse Oleshevskaya. The trustees received full discretion over revenues, seat sales, and accounting, with no obligation to render reports. Any shortfall during construction would be advanced personally or borrowed, then repaid from future income of the adjoining bathhouse and mikveh, the synagogue's ground-floor rentals, and seat sales.
