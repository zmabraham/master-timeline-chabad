# Author and family arrive in Paris under the Joint and the Lubavitch Refugee Bureau

*1947*

After temporary housing at the Hotel La Chapelle, the family was moved to a sub-divided room in a former noble château in Sèvres, where more than ten refugee families lived together. During his stay in Paris the author received hagahah and kabbalah for shechting poultry, having begun sharpening knives back in Pocking, and shechted in the Pletzl market alongside R' Betzalel Wilshansky.
