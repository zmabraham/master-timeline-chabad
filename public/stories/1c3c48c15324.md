# Yiddish school network peaks at 1,100 schools

*1929*

The Yevsektzia championed Yiddish as the language of the 'Jewish proletariat' while abolishing Hebrew study and Zionist culture. By 1929 there were 1,100 such schools across the Soviet Union. Jewish religion, history, Hebrew, and tradition were excluded from the curriculum, and many religious parents sent children to non-Jewish schools to avoid the anti-religious propaganda.
