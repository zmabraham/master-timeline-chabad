# Cold passes; Rebbe Rayatz resumes yechidus on 26 Tevet

*1945*

On 26 Tevet 5705 the cold, nasal congestion and fever the Rebbe had been suffering finally passed. That evening he received Rabbi R. Sh. L. about the administration of Yeshivat Tomchei Tmimim Lubavitch in Montreal, and also Rabbi Kramer, the principal of that yeshivah.
