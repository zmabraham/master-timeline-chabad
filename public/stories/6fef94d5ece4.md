# Rashag departs Riga for Paris fundraising mission

*1928*

After Passover, the Rebbe Rayatz dispatched his son-in-law Rashag to England and France to organize a fundraising effort for Soviet Jewry suffering under anti-religious persecution. In Paris he was joined by R. Alexander Sender Reinin, R. Menachem Mendel Rotshtein of Warsaw, and Mr. Guggenheim of Switzerland.
