# Rebbe Rayatz instructs Anash to record Soviet-era memoirs

*1946*

The Rebbe specifically asked that participants record everything they could remember about underground religious work, the names of fellow Chassidim and students, and the places and times of their activities. Reb Bentzion Shemtov was charged with coordinating the collection. The instruction launched a tradition of Chabad memoir writing that produced major historical sources including Reb Meir Gurkov's Sefer HaZichronot.
