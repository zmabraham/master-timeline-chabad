# Yeshivas Tomchei Tmimim report lists over 700 students across branches

*1938*

The letter from the Yeshiva leadership in early Tishrei 5699 (October 2, 1938) summarized the scope of Tomchei Tmimim under the previous Rebbe (Rayatz). It reported that the institution had become world-renowned, producing thousands of Torah scholars, and that more than 700 students were currently being educated and supported through the central yeshiva and its many branches.
