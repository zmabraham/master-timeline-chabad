# R. Levi Yitzchok of Berditchev passes away

*1810*

Late in life R. Levi Yitzchok had permitted certain sirkhot via squeezing, generating controversy among Berditchev rabbis. After his passing the queries on his ruling were referred to the Alter Rebbe, who defended R. Levi Yitzchok's psak in several responsa.
