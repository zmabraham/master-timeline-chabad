# Rayatz delivers first maamar to Rivkin at end of shivah

*1920*

On the Shabbat that ended his week of mourning for his father, the Rayatz invited Berel Rivkin into the Rashab's study and recited a maamar his father had said on Purim, adding insights of his own. He asked Rivkin to keep it private, but news of the discourse soon spread among the Chasidim. It was his first step toward accepting the position of sixth Lubavitcher Rebbe.
