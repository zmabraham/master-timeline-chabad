# Rebbe Rayatz flees Otwock for Warsaw as WWII begins

*1939*

At the outbreak of World War II in late Elul 5699 (early September 1939), the Rebbe Rayatz left his home in Otwock and traveled to Warsaw, where he stayed in R. Shneur Zalman Shmotkin's house at Murnowska 32. During the German bombing of Warsaw, the building was hit and burned, destroying the Rebbe's clothing and belongings, and he relocated to the home of R. Yechiel Tzvi Gurary.
