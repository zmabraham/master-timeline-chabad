Rabbi Gerlitzky. Rabbi Avraham Yitzchak Baruch Gerlitzky (born in the year 5708, 1947) is one of the heads of Oholei Torah Yeshiva Crown Heights, and director of the editorial board of Kovetz He'arot U'Biurim Oholei Torah, and author of the books Yemot HaMashiach BaHalacha (3 volumes)
