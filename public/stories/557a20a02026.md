# Mechina relocates to Murnowska 32

*1936*

Over the years, R. Shneur Zalman Shmotkin had acquired a courtyard and building at Murnowska 32 in Warsaw, dedicating part of it as a Chabad shul. In 5696 (1936) the Mechina relocated there from Dzika 8. The same address would later host the Rebbe Rayatz himself during the first days of WWII.
