# Yisrael Yehuda Levin began service in Tomchei Tmimim Vitebsk

*1928*

After Pesach in 5688 (spring 1928), Yisrael Yehuda Levin began serving in the clandestine Tomchei Tmimim network in Vitebsk alongside the bachur Avraham Kvashe (later in Riga). He worked there for the entire stretch of that period before later moving on to similar work in Kiev. This was during the era of intense Soviet persecution of religious education.
