# Rebbe leads amud prayers in Crown Heights for Rebbetzin Rivka's yahrzeit

*1930*

Michael Feder traveled from Philadelphia and spent the entire Shabbat at the Rebbe's home to ease the test of Shabbat observance, davening near the Rebbe with hot tears streaming down. The Rebbetzin Rivka, wife of Rebbe Maharash, had passed away on Friday 10 Shevat 5674.
