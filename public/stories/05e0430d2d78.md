# Tena'im in the author's Cherbinov apartment

*1940*

The seasoned businessman father insisted the matter not be left hanging in mid-air, saying, 'Let the child have a name.' R' Zusya brought ten men from the shul — among them R' Sa'adia Liberov and his brother R' Chaim Chanye Paz of Klimovichi — to Cherbinov, and the tena'im were signed on the night before 4 Nisan 5700. The father then returned to Zlobin together with the author's sister Esther.
