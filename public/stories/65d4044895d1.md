# Maskilic denunciation of Tzemach Tzedek published in German-Jewish press

*1841*

The article, published in the German-Jewish weekly Israelitische Annalen issue 6, was sent from Vilna by Maskilim and named 'Mendel of Lyubavichi' as the leader of the Chassidim of Vitebsk and Mogilev. It claimed the Chabad movement was working to obstruct the government's educational plans and called for excluding Chassidim from communal leadership. The article was then submitted by Maskilim to Minister of Education Uvarov to provoke official action.
