# Beis din ruling forbids R' Meir Refael's from hosting a Chassidic minyan

*1798*

After R' Meir Refael's defended himself before the beis din, replying that he had found the truth among the Chassidim rather than within the kahal, the court issued its ruling on 21 Adar 5558. He was barred from hosting any minyan for the rest of his life, ordered to stop supporting the Chassidic rebbes, and required to pray only in the main shul or kloiz. He was also told to make public confession before the appointed officials.
