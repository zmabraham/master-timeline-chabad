# Shlomo Zalman Hecht and wife arrive in Otwock

*1938*

Rabbi Feigin reported to Rabbi Jacobson on 12 Tishrei 5699 that his daughter and son-in-law had arrived safely. Because they came right before Yom Tov, they initially stayed at a pension, but the Rebbe Rayatz advised them to find their own apartment for both material and spiritual reasons.
