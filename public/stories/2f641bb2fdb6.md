# R. Yehuda Eber reports to Rebbe on Łódź yeshiva

*1931*

After being dispatched in Iyar 5691 to examine students in Warsaw, Łódź and Vilna, R. Yehuda Eber reported from Warsaw on 22 Iyar (May 9, 1931). He found 52 students at Łódź studying in two divisions, described their daily schedule, and praised the dedication of the Schneersohn brothers — particularly Dovber — who funded the yeshiva from their own pockets. He observed that the city was fertile ground for growth but financial strain limited expansion.
