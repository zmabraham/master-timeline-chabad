# Rebbe sends emissary to arrange Levitin's Rakishok role

*1937*

Writing to Levitin on 29 Tammuz 5697 (Igros Kodesh vol. 4, letter 920), the Rebbe Rayatz said it was worthwhile to dispatch R. Mordechai, the shochet from Riga, as a special emissary with a letter to the chassidim in Rakishok, in order that Levitin's reinstallation be carried out properly and respectfully.
