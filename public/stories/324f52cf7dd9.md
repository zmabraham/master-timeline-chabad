# Rabbi Zaltzman speaking at the Moshiach rally in the square 5776. Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak

*1956*

Rabbi Zaltzman speaking at the Moshiach rally in the square 5776. Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Zaltzman on mivtza tefillin near the mivtzaim tank in Toronto. Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Zaltzman (born 5716, 1956) is the Rebbe's emissary and rabbi of the community of Russian immigrants in Toronto, Canada.
