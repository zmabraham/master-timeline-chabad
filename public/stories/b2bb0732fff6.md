# Levin's first yechidus with the Rebbe

*1962*

After a lifetime of hoping for this moment, Levin entered the Rebbe's room for his first yechidus in the early hours of 6 Tishrei 5723. He recited Shehecheyanu and presented a long written tzettel of questions. The Rebbe answered each one in depth, and when Levin failed to grasp a detail, the Rebbe patiently repeated his words verbatim.
