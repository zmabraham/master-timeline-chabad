# R' Chaim Shmuel Schmerling passes away in Jaffa

*1894*

After nearly three decades building communal life in Jaffa, R' Chaim Shmuel Schmerling passed away on 2 Adar 5654 (February 1894). His biography appears in the Schmerling pamphlet (Tidhar, Tel Aviv 5706) and in Toldos Mishpachas HaRav MiLadi p. 76.
