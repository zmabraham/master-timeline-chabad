# Rayatz letter to Rabbi Eliyahu Chaim Althoyz on recording chasidic history

*1935*

On 17 Marcheshvan 5696 (November 1935), Rayatz pressed the chasid R' Eliyahu Chaim Althoyz to set aside time to write down what he had heard from his father and uncle about their time in Lubavitch and what he remembered from Kremenchug, particularly about the elder chasidim known as 'the bears.' The letter is part of Rayatz's larger campaign to recruit chasidim to preserve oral chasidic history.
