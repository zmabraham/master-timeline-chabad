# Six American students depart for Otwock yeshivah with Rabbi Jacobson

*1939*

On 24 Av 5699, Jacobson left New York with six American students bound for Yeshivas Tomchei Temimim in Otwock. They arrived just seventeen days before the outbreak of World War II. The Rebbe had assured the parents 'you have nothing to fear,' and the boys' presence later became central evidence that a Chabad yeshivah could succeed in America.
