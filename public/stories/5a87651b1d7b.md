# Rayatz's St. Petersburg arrest over military exemption efforts

*1906*

In 1906, Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn was arrested in St. Petersburg after working to secure exemptions for rabbis from compulsory military service in the Russian army. His advocacy for protecting Jewish religious leaders from conscription was viewed as obstructionist by czarist authorities. This was one of his early imprisonments under the czarist regime.
