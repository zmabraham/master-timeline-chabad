# R' Meshulam Zalman Neumark passes away in Nevel

*1893*

After serving in Horki, Starodub, Vitebsk, and finally Nevel, R' Meshulam Zalman Neumark passed away on 28 Av 5653. His son R' Yechezkel Feivel succeeded him in the Nevel rabbinate, and his grandson R' Avraham Yaakov Neumark later published Eshel Avraham.
