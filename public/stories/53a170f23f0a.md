# Crispo writes from Fez to ibn Tzur on new Meknes post

*1718*

On 13 Av 1718, R. Yom Tov Crispo wrote from Fez to R. Yaakov ibn Tzur, who had just been appointed rabbi of Meknes. The letter expressed joy at the appointment and asked ibn Tzur to oversee the collection from a distance of the various pledges promised in Fez, Taza, Debdou, and other communities for the Jerusalem mission.
