# Rebbe Rashab rules yeshiva should remain in Lubavitch

*1918*

The Rebbe Rashab gave three reasons in yechidus: he believed the Germans would not capture Lubavitch; even if captured the area would not be fully controlled, allowing small group study; and even at worst the border would be porous enough to cross. He concluded that relocating Lubavitch itself was impossible — 'the light will be missing' — and a telegram was sent to halt the evacuation. By then it was too late and most students had already left.
