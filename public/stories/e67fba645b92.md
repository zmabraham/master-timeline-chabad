# Polish non-Jew brings Shu't Maharik with Alter Rebbe's glosses to America

*1986*

In the summer of 5746 (1986), a Polish non-Jew arrived in the United States carrying a copy of Shu't Maharik (Lvov 5558) with handwritten glosses by the Alter Rebbe in its margins. He claimed to have inherited the volume from his grandfather. The book was acquired by a Chabad chassid and returned to the Chabad library — joining the small group of surviving books bearing the Alter Rebbe's own hand to escape the Lyadi fires.
