# Rebbe Rashab prepares Vilna edition of Tanya

*1899*

Rebbe Rashab entrusted R' Asher, a chossid and shochet from Nikolayev, with proofreading the Tanya against early manuscripts and prints for the new Rom press edition. The Rebbe personally reviewed the proposed corrections and resolved unresolved questions, finalizing the text for the new edition.
