# Rebbe Rayatz's first visit to Tomchei Tmimim Warsaw

*1928*

The Rebbe Rayatz toured the entire yeshiva, beginning with the office where he examined account books and student records, then meeting individually with the rosh yeshivas, mashgichim, and mashpi'im. He proceeded through every classroom and the main study hall, telling the chief mashgiach 'fine personal property,' inspected the dormitories and dining hall, and recited Shehecheyanu. He later remarked that the yeshiva was 'an everlasting edifice — but in its inner dimension'.
