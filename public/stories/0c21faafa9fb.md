# Founding of Agudas Chassidei Chabad branches in New York neighborhoods

*1930*

The Rebbe summoned the chief Chabad askanim and met with them several times in his quarters to draft the agudah's constitution. Greater New York was divided into neighborhoods with appointed gabba'im and ledger books, and the Rebbe wrote a personal blessing in his own hand at the head of each branch's book. At the Rebbe's request Shmuel Hein was appointed secretary of the agudah.
