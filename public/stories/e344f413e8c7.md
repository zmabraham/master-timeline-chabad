# Maskilic press attacks planned Grodno rabbinical assembly

*1903*

When word of the planned Grodno assembly reached Haskalah circles, they launched a press campaign to discredit it, publishing protests in Ha-Melitz, Novosti, and other outlets. The Rebbe Rashab described the Hebrew papers as 'shouting and barking,' while R. Eliyahu Akiva Rabinovich answered the attacks in the journal Ha-Peles (Tevet 5664). The Maskilim also planted false reports that prominent rabbis like the Lodzer Rav Maizel and rabbis of Dvinsk and Plotsk were withdrawing from the assembly.
