# R. Baruch Mordechai Ettinga of Bobruisk receives haskamah on Likutei Torah

*1848*

R. Baruch Mordechai Ettinga served as rabbi of Bobruisk for some fifty years before making aliyah. His haskamah on Likutei Torah, dated 17 Sivan 5608, marks the last time he is documented outside of Eretz Yisrael. Shortly thereafter he prepared for his long-anticipated move to Jerusalem.
