# Tzvi Gansburg marries in Montreal and leaves printing partnership

*1952*

In spring 5712 (1952), the author's printing partner Tzvi (Hershel) Gansburg married the daughter of R. Yeshayahu Denberg of Shchedrin in Montreal, with the author's teacher Rabbi Binyaminsohn serving as shadchan. Hershel remained in Montreal, lured by a wealthy relative's unfulfilled promises, and left the printing house. The author was deeply unhappy, but the two remained loyal friends.
