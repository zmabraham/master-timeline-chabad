# Gertrude Zuckerkandel paints portraits of the Rebbe Rashab

*1935*

Four previously unpublished portraits of the Rebbe Rashab were found in a wall-cabinet of the Rayatz's yechidus room. Three are signed by Gertrude Zuckerkandel and dated 1935 (5695) — fifteen years after the Rashab's passing — leading to scholarly discussion as to whether they depict the Rashab from memory or are partly based on his son the Rayatz.
