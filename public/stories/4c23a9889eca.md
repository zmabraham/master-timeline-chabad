# R. Levin's group leaves Lodz for Katowice on Bricha escape route

*1946*

After finally receiving passports, Levin and a Chabad group left Lodz for Katowice on 10 Menachem Av 5706. Upon arrival they were asked not to appear on the streets with long beards. Mikhael Teitelbaum departed separately via Stettin, while Levin remained with the main group to travel by train toward Prague.
