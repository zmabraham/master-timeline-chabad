# Rosenbaum's 15 Shevat letter from prison anticipating freedom

*1890*

From Smolensk on 15 Shevat 5650, Reb Avraham Chaim told R' Yehuda Leib Maskil L'Eitan that he had no real news but that 'salvation draws nearer day by day.' He framed the long days behind bars with a Hebrew riddle: 'What was tomorrow and will be yesterday?'—answer, 'today'—expressing faith that he would yet see redemption.
