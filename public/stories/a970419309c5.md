# Pesach seudah at Tomchei Tmimim

*1913*

In a letter dated 23 Nissan 5673 (1913), the Rebbe Rayatz described the customary Acharon Shel Pesach seudah at Tomchei Tmimim. The Rebbe Rashab, the menahel, mashgichim, mashpiim, melamdim and office workers ate together with 139 students that year (excluding the younger shiur and cheder students). The meal continued until close to 10 PM the previous evening.
