# Death of Rabbi Nachum Dov Twersky of Ovrutch

*1894*

Rabbi Nachum Dov Twersky of Ovrutch, son of Rabbi Yitzchak Yosef of Ovrutch and brother-in-law of the Rebbe Rashab of Lubavitch, died in 5654. Surviving correspondence with chasidim such as Reb Yaakov Ayerov of Kovel includes a letter pleading on behalf of a man whose only son had been taken — likely conscripted into the Russian army.
