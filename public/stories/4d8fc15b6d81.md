# Aliyah of R' Yisrael Yaffe (the younger) of Kapust

*1820*

In 5580 (1820), R' Yisrael Yaffe — son of Alexander Sender Feivish Yaffe and one of the Kapust printers of the Alter Rebbe's Shulchan Aruch — made aliyah and settled in Hebron. The 5599 census recorded him as 64 years old, having come from Kapust in 5580. He is distinct from R' Yisrael ben Yitzchak Yaffe, also of Kapust, who arrived in 5583.
