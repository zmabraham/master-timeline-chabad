# Tel Aviv trustees attempt to dismantle Rogachov Vaad and Hebron leadership

*1888*

Calling themselves "heads of Kollel Chabad," the Jerusalem trustees circulated public notices proposing that every city send its donations directly through the post to Jerusalem, bypassing the overseas shadarim and the Hebron trustees. The Hebron community and many Jerusalemites protested fiercely in a counter-broadside, but the Jerusalem trustees succeeded on all three counts. From this point Hebron lost its place as the administrative center of the kollel.
