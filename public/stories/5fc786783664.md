# R. Avraham Chaim writes to R. Chaim Gershon Kogel from Smolensk prison

*1889*

Throughout his imprisonment in Smolensk, R. Avraham Chaim received consoling letters from friends across the Chabad world. On Rosh Chodesh Nissan 5649 he replied to R. Chaim Gershon Kogel, telling him that despite the many waters that had passed between them, the love expressed in his letter had not been extinguished. He observed that a prisoner's spirit cools from the heat of his oppressor when he sees that distant friends share in his sorrow.
