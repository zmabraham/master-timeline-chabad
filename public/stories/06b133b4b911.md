# Levin leaves to learn at Kiev yeshiva

*1927*

This trip marked the start of a decade of wandering through Soviet underground yeshivos. Levin would move between Vitebsk, Pereyaslav, Kiev, Baku, Kutaisi, Kulashi, Moscow, Chernigov, Malachovka, Leningrad, Voronezh, Krivoy Rog, and Kharkov as yeshivos were repeatedly shut down by the secret police. He lived without proper papers or with forged ones.
