# Shimcha Kissin files an informer's complaint against the Mitteler Rebbe

*1825*

In July 1825, the Jew Shimcha Kissin of Vitebsk submitted a petition to Governor-General Khovansky accusing the chassidic sect ("Karliners") and its leader Rabbi Berke Schneur (the Mitteler Rebbe) of pressuring traditional Jews and collecting large sums via emissaries for the Rebbe's personal benefit. He attached a Yiddish distribution document and two letters as evidence. The complaint launched the entire investigation that culminated in the Mitteler Rebbe's arrest.
