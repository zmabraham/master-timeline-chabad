# Petition over young rabbis' exemption status

*1916*

Reb Shmuel Michel Trainin reported through Reb Shmuel Gourary that the Director of the Religious Department had removed rabbis under age 25 from the exemption list. The Rebbe Rashab wrote that he could not understand why these rabbis should be excluded, since they had been certified by local police as serving rabbis. He argued that those who attained the rank of horaah at young ages were especially elevated, having dedicated all their days to Torah, and should not be excluded.
