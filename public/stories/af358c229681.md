# First day of Chabad yeshiva learning in Moscow (5752)

*1991*

Learning began at the Marina Roshcha shul while preparations were finalized at the AGUH building. The author delivered a general introductory shiur on Bava Basra to the two upper classes. Earlier discussions with Rabbi Berel Lazar and Uri Kamishov resulted in opening the yeshiva to thirteen- and fourteen-year-olds, who would learn secular studies in the morning at the Archipov shul school and join the yeshiva afterwards.
