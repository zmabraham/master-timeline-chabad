# R. Yehuda Eber writes about student Moshe Pinchas Katz

*1936*

On 29 Tammuz 5696 (July 19, 1936), Rosh Yeshiva R. Yehuda Eber wrote to R. Avraham Ziskind regarding their student Moshe Pinchas Katz, noting they were doing everything possible — and more than was within their means — to help him. The letter was part of efforts to arrange a shidduch with one of R. David Stockhamer's daughters and secure US entry papers.
