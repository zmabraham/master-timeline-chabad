# Russian decree restricting Hebrew printing presses

*1837*

The decree triggered a wave of clandestine printing inside Russia in which presses imitated the look of older Kapust and other authentic editions, often forging both title pages and the rabbinical censor's stamp. At least five such forgeries imitating the late Kapust press were printed after this decree, four of them works of Chabad Chassidut, suggesting the underground printer was himself a Chabad chassid.
