# Tefilot LeKol HaShanah with Chinuch HaDat introduction printed in Vilna

*1911*

In 5671 (1911), 'Tefilot LeKol HaShanah' was printed in Vilna following the Arizal nusach with laws drawn from the Alter Rebbe's Shulchan Aruch, marginal notes by 'Torah Or', reading marks, tagim and other details. It opened with an essay titled 'Chinuch HaDat V'HaLashon' (Religious and Linguistic Education). The 168-leaf octavo edition served the Chabad community of Lithuania before World War I.
