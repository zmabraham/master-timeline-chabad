# Pechersk Lavra library search reveals 1960 manuscript fire

*1990*

On Friday, 10 Shevat 5750 (February 5, 1990), the yahrzeit of the Frierdiker Rebbe, Rabbi Berel Levine's delegation visited three libraries at Kiev's Pechersk Lavra complex. A veteran librarian named Tsedenko revealed by telephone that book treasures had been stored in the Lavra's bell tower since 1951, transferred to the Vodavitsky monastery building around 1960, and that a fire that same year had destroyed part of the collection. Surviving books had reportedly been returned to the Academy of Sciences library, suggesting the lost Institute collection might still lie in unsorted storage there.
