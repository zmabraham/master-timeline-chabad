# Birth of the author's daughter Sarah on 19 Kislev 5681

*1920*

She arrived during the height of the post-civil-war famine, as Ukrainian refugees lay swollen with hunger in the streets and Jews in Gomel were reduced to eating bran, oil-seed residue, and potato peels. The rabbinate permitted kitniyos that Pesach because of the shortages, and the family relied on supplies the author hauled back and forth from the village of Zhuravichi.
