# Romm Vilna invoice for printing Likkutei Torah issued

*1904*

The detailed invoice from Romm's Vilna press itemized paper costs for 1,900 and 1,500 copies of the two volumes, printing fees, plate costs, binding (covers), and corrections. Against advance payments totaling 1,100 rubles in 1902-1903, a balance of 1,701.71 rubles remained owed as of the invoice date.
