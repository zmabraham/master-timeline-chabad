# Levin and Wagner plan a Shamir trip to Minsk archives

*1993*

On Erev Shabbat 20 Tammuz 5753 (9 July 1993), R. Shalom DovBer Levin met with Igor Wagner in Moscow to discuss the methodology of the document search. They agreed that immediately after Shabbat the team would travel to Minsk to look specifically for the file relating to the arrest and liberation of the Mitteler Rebbe.
