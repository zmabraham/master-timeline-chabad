# Twenty-five crown rabbis convene in Nikolaev

*1900*

Fear spread that the assembly would adopt resolutions hostile to traditional Judaism backed by the imprimatur of the Interior Ministry. The Slonimer Rebbe Shmuel Weinberg and R. Yosef Slifer urgently dispatched R. Eliezer Yosef Klatzky to the Rebbe Rashab, who endorsed the immediate organization of a counter-assembly of traditional rabbis and rebbes.
