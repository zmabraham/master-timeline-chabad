# R' Shneur Zalman Rosenbaum travels to America for Tomchei Tmimim

*1913*

Approximately in 5673, R' Shneur Zalman Rosenbaum traveled to America under the pretext of raising funds for Tomchei Tmimim. A letter later shown to the author from R' Yehoshua Kastrol in Trieste characterized the move as Rosenbaum's personal wish to reach America, for which he had persuaded the Rebbe Rashab to consent. The dating is anchored by the fact that the parallel Toras Emes yeshiva was founded just after Sukkos 5672.
