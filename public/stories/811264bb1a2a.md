# First volumes of the Alter Rebbe's Shulchan Aruch printed in Shklov-Kapust

*1814*

Roughly a year after the Alter Rebbe's passing, his sons brought the long-awaited Shulchan Aruch to press, drawing on student manuscripts to replace the holograph copies lost in the fires. The first volumes, including hilchot Pesach and the laws of niddah, appeared in 5574 (1814).
