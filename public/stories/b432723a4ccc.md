# 12 Tammuz 5692 farbrengen at the Liebov family home in Kutaisi

*1932*

By summer 5692 the Liebov family and additional Anash households had moved to Kutaisi, turning the city into a small Chabad settlement. The chag hageulah of 12 Tammuz was celebrated in the home of R. Yisrael Liebov's parents, a gathering that knit together the underground yeshiva students and the established families.
