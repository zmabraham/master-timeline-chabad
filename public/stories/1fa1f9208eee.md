# Rebbe writes to Vilna students about the yeshiva's transitional state

*1940*

Writing from Riga on 20 Tevet 5700, the Rebbe Rayatz acknowledged the temporary arrangements established for Tomchei Tmimim in Vilna and asked every student to understand that the yeshiva was in a state of transition and preparation. The letter was carried by R. Shmuel Zalmanov and R. Moshe Leib Rodstein, who were helping run the yeshiva-in-exile.
