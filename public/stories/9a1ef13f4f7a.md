# Outbreak of World War I and Rebbe Rashab's prophecy of a long war

*1914*

At the start of Elul 5674 several wealthy chassidim came to Zaalshe to ask the Rebbe Rashab's advice as World War I broke out. They assumed Russia would quickly be forced to seek peace against Germany and considered halting their business. The Rebbe told them to continue their commerce, saying the war would be long, in his words: "einer vet dem andern einshlingen un vet zikh mit im derverger" (one will swallow the other and choke on him together).
