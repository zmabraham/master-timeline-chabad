# Passing of Rabbi Levi Yitzchak of Berditchev

*1809*

On 25 Tishrei 5570, Rabbi Levi Yitzchak of Berditchev passed away after leading the Berditchev community since around 5545. The Alter Rebbe traveled to Berditchev for the consolation of mourners and remained in Volhynia through the winter of 5570. At the widow's request, he spent Pesach in Rabbi Levi Yitzchak's home and personally conducted the seder; during his stay there he was also asked to weigh in on one of Rabbi Levi Yitzchak's final halachic rulings on sirchot.
