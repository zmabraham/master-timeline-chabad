# Avraham Chaim writes from prison requesting etrog and lulav

*1888*

On 13 Elul 5648 (August 20, 1888), Avraham Chaim wrote from prison to Rabbi Yehuda Leib Maskil LeEitan asking him to acquire Sukkot articles on his behalf when purchasing his own. He requested a Yanover etrog, a lulav beautiful in appearance and complete in form, and three matched hadassim, with delivery to reach him immediately after Yom Kippur. The letter shows that prison authorities permitted him to receive religious articles within months of his arrival.
