# Chadakov writes Rayatz that refugee rabbis are benefiting Riga

*1930*

On 25 Nisan 5690, R' Chaim Mordechai Aizik Chadakov wrote to the Rebbe Rayatz that the Riga community was benefiting greatly from the rabbis who had come from the valley of weeping (Russia), namely R' Yisrael Zuber, R' Avraham Eliyahu Ashurov, and now also R' Yehuda Eber.
