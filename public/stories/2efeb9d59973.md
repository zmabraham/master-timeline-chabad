# Death of Shaina (Sania) Schusterman in Zlobin

*1927*

After Shaina's untimely death in summer 1927, the memoirist's mother no longer wished to remain in the family home. The father bought a larger house in the center of Zlobin and went into debt as a result, leaving the family in difficult straits when the memoirist later took ill with pleurisy.
