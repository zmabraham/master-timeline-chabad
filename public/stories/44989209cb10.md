# Maskilic counter-assembly convenes in Kovno

*1909*

Determined not to cede the field to the Orthodox rabbinate, the modernist faction and government-appointed rabbis organized a parallel assembly in Kovno called the 'Assembly of Jewish Public Activists.' They recorded all their proposals in a volume of roughly two hundred pages, submitted it to the Ministry's religious affairs department, later published a printed collection of the speeches and proposals, and established a standing bureau to prepare for the Petersburg Rabbinical Conference.
