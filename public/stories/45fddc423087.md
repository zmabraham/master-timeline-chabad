# Abramsky publishes letter of support for Tomchei Temimim

*1937*

Drawing on his personal visit to the yeshiva the previous year, Abramsky testified that seeing is not the same as hearing, and that he found 200 bochurim shokdim on Torah and yiras Hashem. He signed as 'dayan of the Beis Din of London and the country' and promised Hashems blessing in all good for those who became fixed supporters.
