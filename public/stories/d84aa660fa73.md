# Third edition of Likkutei Torah printed in Vilna

*1878*

The Vilna תרל"ח edition of Likkutei Torah included a printed statement that the haskamot of the great rabbis of the generation forbade reprinting without the permission of the Tzemach Tzedek's grandchildren, the sons of his son R' Menachem Mendel. The notice retroactively clarified that the prior תרכ"ו edition had also been undertaken for the Tzemach Tzedek's family.
