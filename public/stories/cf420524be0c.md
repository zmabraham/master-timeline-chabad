# Author secures Soviet exit visas for the Gurary family in Homel

*1925*

R. Shmarya Gurary asked the author whether he could arrange foreign-travel passports through Homel for the family of R. Tzvi Hirsch Gurary, offering 250 rubles (25 chervontses). After Nochum Gurelnik came to verify the possibility, R. Nosson Gurary brought the family to Homel, where they were registered in residents' household books. By winter's end the author had obtained exit permits for R. Tzvi's entire family, for R. Nosson's wife Esther, and for R. Shmarya Gurary.
