# The Rebbe arrives in New York

*1941*

After fleeing wartime Europe, the future seventh Lubavitcher Rebbe arrived in New York in the summer of 5701 (1941). His father-in-law, the Rebbe Rayatz, immediately appointed him to head Merkos L'Inyonei Chinuch, the educational arm of the movement. He soon began assembling a working library to support its activities.
