# Night march through forest into U.S.-occupied Germany

*1946*

On Monday Parshat Ki Seitzei, 6 Elul 5706, Bricha activists trucked the group to a stable beside a forest. After dark a guide led them into the woods; jeeps of U.S. Military Police fired into the trees on hearing rustling, forcing them to lie still. The guide briefly led them toward the Soviet zone before correcting course; they walked nearly all night.
