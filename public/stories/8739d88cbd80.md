# Notes on mezuzah on the right and Chanukah candle on the left

*1905*

Marked 'Chanukah 5666,' R' Avraham Chaim's note works through the seeming paradox that the mezuzah, identified with the lower sefirot vav-heh, is placed on the right, while the Chanukah lamp, identified with the higher chochmah-binah, is placed on the left. He resolves it via the doctrine that the recipient can outrank the giver in or chozer, citing the Gemara's eleven blessings for one who appeases a poor man and parallels in tefillin placement.
