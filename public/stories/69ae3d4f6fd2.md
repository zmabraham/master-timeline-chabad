# R. Binyamin Wolf Loew of Verbov writes to Maharia Asad on religious decline

*1843*

In a letter dated to the week of Parshas Nitzavim 5603, R. Binyamin Wolf Loew of Verbov poured out his anguish to R. Yehuda Asad over the spread of Reform and the loss of an older generation of rabbinic leaders who had stood in the breach. He confessed that the situation pained him daily and pressed Asad to share any practical strategy he might have for resisting the modernist breach-makers. The letter is the earliest of three surviving responses to Asad's outreach campaign for strengthening religious life in Hungary.
