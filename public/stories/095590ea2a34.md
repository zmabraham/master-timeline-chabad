# Marriage of Chaya Mushka Grossman to R' Yosef Shmuel Garfinkel

*1906*

In 5666, Chaya Mushka, daughter of R' Asher Grossman of Nikolayev, married R' Yosef Shmuel Garfinkel of Pahar, a student of Tomchei Tmimim in Lubavitch. On 6 Av 5666 Rashab wrote R' Asher congratulating him on the engagement. After their wedding, R' Yosef became shochet in Kislovodsk.
