# Rebbe Rashab letter to Rashaz Slonim acknowledging manuscript shipment

*1912*

After 25 years of correspondence with Rashaz Slonim about manuscript collection (Igros #344), Rebbe Rashab expressed his desire that Rashaz continue sending such precious materials regularly. The Birkat Chatanim manuscript was catalogued as #1194 and printed in Maamarei Admur Ha'emtza'i Droshei Chatuna Vol. 1, p. 146.
