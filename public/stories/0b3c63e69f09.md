# Rebbe sends R' Shneur Zalman Gurary to investigate Maciów yeshiva offer

*1931*

After receiving Mandelbaum's report, the Rebbe wrote both to Mandelbaum and to Gurary on the same day. He asked Gurary to view the buildings, send a detailed description, and act quietly so that no rival groups would seize the opportunity. The Rebbe hoped that once full details were in hand, arrangements could be made to open a yeshiva there.
