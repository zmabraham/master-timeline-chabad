# Senate responds to Khovansky on rabbinic appointments

*1834*

Seven years after the Rebbe's acquittal, the Senate addressed Khovansky's lingering proposals about regulating rabbinic appointments. The Mogilev high court forwarded copies of his recommendations to the Senate in February, and Khovansky relayed the response to the Mogilev provincial government. The exchange marks a final administrative coda to the policy program Kissin's denunciations had originally tried to advance.
