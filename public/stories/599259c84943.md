# Frierdiker Rebbe departs Riga for America

*1940*

With Latvia threatened by the war, the Frierdiker Rebbe departed Riga for Stockholm, where he would board a ship to New York. His daughter Chaya Mushka and her husband, the future Rebbe, remained behind in Paris, unable to obtain visas in time. The separation marked the start of more than a year of intense rescue efforts on the family's behalf.
