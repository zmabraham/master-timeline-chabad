# Tour of Moscow landmarks with European shluchim delegation

*1991*

The diarist served as guide, beginning at the library steps for a group photograph, then continuing through the Kremlin and Red Square. The route pointedly passed the Mossoviet, the Chabad office building, and the Polyakov Synagogue, after which buses brought the delegation to the Timiryazevskaya lecture hall. Word arrived that the Rebbe had spoken on Shabbat about the kinus shluchim visiting Lubavitch, Alma-Ata, and now the Soviet capital, instructing them to daven Mincha as well in Alma-Ata.
