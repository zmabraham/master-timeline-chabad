# Holodomor famine grips Ukraine while Kiev yeshiva learns 5693

*1933*

Although 5693 was a year of abundant harvest, Stalin's decrees caused mass starvation in Ukraine. Yeshiva students stood in bread lines for days, taking multiple places in the queue to feed the chevra. The young Jewish father-and-son bakers serving bread quietly risked their own safety by repeatedly handing out loaves to the bearded bochurim.
