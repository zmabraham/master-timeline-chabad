# Rebbe directs Rabbi Cheifetz to teach his daughter chasidic philosophy

*1935*

The Rebbe quoted his grandfather Rabbi Shmuel: 'amongst Chasidim there is no difference between a boy or a girl.' He lamented that this had been said fifty-five years earlier 'when the light of Chasidism was burning brightly,' adding that 'now this is an even greater imperative.' The directive catalyzed the formation of Achot Hatemimim, bringing teens and young women into serious study of chasidic texts.
