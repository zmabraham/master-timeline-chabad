# Tomchei Temimim center relocates from Lubavitch to Kremenchug

*1918*

After hiding the Rebbe's household vessels in a deep cellar, the last twelve bochurim and senior families departed Lubavitch via Rudnia toward Bryansk. From Klintsy they used peasant wagons through a neutral zone, paying off Russian soldiers and German border guards along the way. After bribery, beatings, and a tense night in no-man's land, they reached Gomel and continued to Kremenchug — formally ending the yeshiva's residence in Lubavitch.
