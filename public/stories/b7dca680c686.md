# Passing of maternal grandfather R. Avraham Nachum of Alkov

*1905*

R. Avraham Nachum, a well-off villager in Kanave near Zhuravichi who operated a water mill and a wool processing factory, passed away on 29 Tammuz 5665. He left behind his wife Rachel Dvasha, five sons, and one daughter (the author's mother). His will included his son-in-law Yaakov Leib among the heirs, and arrangements for dividing the estate followed.
