# Zhitomir edition of Shulchan Aruch published with sons' approbations

*1847*

When Russia's second Jewish press—the chassidic Zhitomir house—opened in 5607, the Tzemach Tzedek's circle entrusted it with a new Shulchan Aruch edition. The 1847–1848 Zhitomir printing added newly discovered material, a preface by R. Chaim Avraham, and approbations from the senior Chabad rabbis.
