# Rebbe Rashab denounces Kaiser Wilhelm at Simchas Torah farbrengen

*1915*

At the pre-hakafos farbrengen in his home on Simchas Torah 5676 the Rebbe Rashab spoke at length about Germany and Kaiser Wilhelm. He declared that he had known the Kaiser for thirty-one years, that he was Amalek whose end is destruction, and contrasted the Germans as 'apikorsim Amalekim' with the French and Africans. The farbrengen ran through the night and the hakafos that evening were not held.
