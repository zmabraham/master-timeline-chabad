# Rabbi Yissachar Ber Hurwitz appointed rabbi of Lubavitch

*1832*

He held the post throughout most of the Tzemach Tzedek's tenure, and was later succeeded by Rabbi Avraham son of Avigdor HaKohen. The town rabbinate was distinct from the Rebbe's role; the Tzemach Tzedek himself was never the salaried communal rabbi of Lubavitch.
