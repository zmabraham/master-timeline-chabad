# Rayatz renews request for library return from Leningrad

*1924*

In this petition (Beis HaGenazim ch. 63) Rayatz reminds officials of the Narkompros decision in his favor and explains that the obstacle to executing it — lack of housing for the books — has now been removed. He argues that the collection, by its sectarian content, would only interest a narrow circle of Jewish religious scholars, and asks for its return.
