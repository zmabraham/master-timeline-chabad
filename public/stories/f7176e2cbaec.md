# Erev Rosh Hashana 5723 farbrengen on Tzemach Tzedek's birthday

*1962*

On Thursday night, the eve of 29 Elul 5722 — the birthday of the Tzemach Tzedek — the Rebbe held a farbrengen at 770 with a microphone since it was the night before Rosh Hashana. The ezrat nashim was filled with women and the Rebbe addressed a sicha to them. He spoke about the mitzvah of hachnasat orchim being greater than receiving the Divine Presence, embracing the assembled guests.
