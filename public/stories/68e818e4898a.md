# Rabbi David Tzvi Hirsch Chen (the Radatz) passes away in Jerusalem

*1925*

Rabbi David Tzvi Hirsch Chen, known as the Radatz, served as rabbi of Chernigov before emigrating to the Land of Israel and settling in Jerusalem. Less than a year after his aliyah he passed away on 24 Kislev 5686. He was buried on the Mount of Olives, leaving behind his daughter Rachel and granddaughter Zelda.
