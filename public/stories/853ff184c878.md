# Fourth amud for the Rebbe built at Kfar Chabad trade school

*1962*

The fourth amud was built in 5722 at the Beis Hasefer Le'Mlacha trade school in Kfar Chabad, modeled on the wide and elegant third amud already in use at 770, with two small doors and a fold-out shelf. The new amud differed in having a thin dividing column between the two doors — a detail that mattered for Yom Kippur, when the Rebbe used to place his hat inside the amud during the Minchah-Maariv break.
