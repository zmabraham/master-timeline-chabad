# Rebbe Rayatz sends Torgsin dollars to Levin family in USSR

*1934*

Hiding in a corner of the Marina Roshcha shul yard, the author wrote a desperate letter to the Rebbe Rayatz describing his hunger, persecution, and his family's pressure to abandon yeshiva study. To his surprise he received a reply enclosing three dollars for himself and four for his mother, with a blessing that Hashem strengthen his siblings' resolve. The dollars arrived as Torgsin coupons—redeemable in the Soviet hard-currency stores—allowing him to buy rice, oranges and other unobtainable goods to send home.
