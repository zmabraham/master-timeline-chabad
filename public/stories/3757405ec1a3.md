# Drabitscher writes Rayatz from Moscow with possible book recovery channel

*1934*

Drabitscher reports that R. A. A. Axelrod of Baltimore asked him to use a senior contact at Mezhdunarodnaya Kniga to obtain the Schneersohn libraries, originally taken from the Kokorev warehouses and now in the Lenin Museum. After meeting them, Drabitscher was told they would consider it only if a foreign catalog were submitted and a substantial foreign currency payment offered.
