# Rabbi Yosef Avraham Pizem at the 'Yoma L'Rabbanan' conference of the institute Lema'an

*1962*

Rabbi Yosef Avraham Pizem at the 'Yoma L'Rabbanan' conference of the Lema'an Yilmedu institute. Rabbi Yosef Avraham Pizem (born 5722, 1962) is the rabbi of the Chabad community of Kiryat Shmuel, principal of Tzivos Menachem Talmud Torah in Kiryat Shmuel, and rosh yeshiva of Tomchei Tmimim Yeshiva
