# Kolel Chabad purchases building adjacent to Beit Menachem synagogues

*1907*

In Tammuz 5667 (July 1907), Kolel Chabad of Jerusalem issued a printed appeal explaining that the Beit Menachem synagogues — founded fifty years earlier by the Tzemach Tzedek opposite the Hurva ruin — were about to be sealed because the adjoining three-story courtyard with its shops was about to be sold to a Muslim buyer. One of the signatories donated 4,000 rubles personally and the community borrowed another 12,000 rubles to buy the courtyard and dedicate its income to needy Chabad scholars in perpetuity.
