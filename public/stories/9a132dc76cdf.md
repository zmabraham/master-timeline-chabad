# Rayatz begins composing his account of the move to Rostov

*1916*

Starting on 2 Adar I 5676 in Rostov, the Rayatz set down a meticulous record of the decision to leave Lubavitch, the journey, the first reception in Rostov, the apartment search, the inaugural Yud-Tes Kislev farbrengen in the new home, and the troubles with the landlord. The composition continued through Purim Katan of that same year.
