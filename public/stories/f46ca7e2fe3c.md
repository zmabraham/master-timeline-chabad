# Delegation pursues ownership order for Polyakov shul

*1991*

On 14 Tammuz 5751 Korovsky and M.C. Levin traveled to the privatization office of the Mossoviet to receive the order assigning the Polyakov synagogue to Agudas Chassidei Chabad. They were given paperwork to fill out at home, then redirected to obtain entrance approval from the building manager. When she denied knowing of any decision, the author faxed the formal decision document, but the manager continued to stonewall.
