# Soviet secret police file on Rabbi Levi Yitzchak Schneerson surfaces

*1991*

Talmid shliach Manes Barash phoned Moscow to report that a man with access to secret police archives had shown him a photograph of Rabbi Levi Yitzchak and the interrogation file from his arrest, claiming there were many more manuscripts to be retrieved. The Moscow delegation told him to fly in immediately with the contact and whatever documents he could obtain. The discovery opened a new front for recovering Soviet-era Chabad materials alongside the library campaign.
