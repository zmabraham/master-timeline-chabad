# Venice edition of Rambam printed

*1574*

In 5334 (1574), a Venice edition of the Rambam was printed. A surviving copy bearing the stamp of R' Avraham Klatzkin, the Chabad rabbi of Lozna, turned up in the Polyakov collection (originally the Schneersohn collection) at the Lenin Library in Moscow when Chabad emissaries searched it in 1991, helping confirm the collection's true origin.
