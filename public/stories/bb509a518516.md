# R' Aleksander Sender Yudasin appointed rabbi of Krivoy Rog

*1922*

After approximately fifteen years without clear succession following R' Isser Yanovsky's death, R' Aleksander Sender Yudasin was appointed rabbi of Krivoy Rog in early 5683. His rabbinical certificate is dated 14 Marcheshvan 5683. Yudasin was an alumnus of the Tomchei Tmimim yeshiva.
