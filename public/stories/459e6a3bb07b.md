# R' Shneur Zalman Gurary visits Maciów to inspect proposed yeshiva building

*1931*

Gurary described the structure in detail, sketching its layout since the town was too small for him to photograph or measure openly — a bearded bochur arriving in such a small town drew immediate attention. The local committee, prompted by Mandelbaum's letter forwarding the Rebbe's interest, had convened a meeting earlier that week and was actively considering the proposal. Gurary indicated that a formal letter from the committee to the Rebbe was forthcoming.
