# RA"Ch's wife dies after long illness in New York

*1904*

Writing on Rosh Chodesh Adar Sheni 5665, RA"Ch recounts that in the preceding year, 5664, his wife of his youth had died after lying on a sickbed for some four months, with over a thousand dollars spent on her care. Earlier that same year he had lost nearly two thousand dollars when goods from Germany failed to arrive, together amounting to roughly three-quarters of the savings he had built up in his old age.
