# Young Shneur Zalman accepted into Liozna Chevra Kadisha

*1749*

The Liozna ledger records that on 15 Kislev 5510 (December 5, 1749), 'the boy Shneur Zalman ben Moreinu Baruch was accepted into the Chevra Kadisha,' with his grandfather obligated to make annual donations on his behalf. This entry is a key biographical anchor for the future Alter Rebbe's earliest years in Liozna.
