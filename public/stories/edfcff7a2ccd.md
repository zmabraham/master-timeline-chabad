# Russian government approves Knesset Yisrael Orthodox organization

*1908*

After years of failed assemblies, the Charedi community achieved a major institutional milestone when the Russian government recognized Agudat Knesset Yisrael in winter 5668. R. Chaim Ozer Grodzinski of Vilna headed the organization, which became the legal framework that would later make a Vilna rabbinical assembly possible under its statute (paragraph 39). R. Eliezer Gordon of Telshe was deeply involved in the new body.
