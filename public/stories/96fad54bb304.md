# Abd al-Rahman taken in chains to Constantinople and sentenced to death

*1858*

After years of tormenting Hebron's Jewish community, Abd al-Rahman was finally seized in 5618. R' Moshe Risher recorded in 'Tevuot HaAretz' (5626/1866) that 'God in His mercy, eight years ago, seized him and took him to Constantinople in iron chains and there gave him the sentence of death.' The community treated his demise as deliverance from a sustained persecution.
