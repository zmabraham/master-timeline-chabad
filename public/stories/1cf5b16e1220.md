# Dvinsk anash commit to weekly chassidus study

*1936*

On 19 Kislev 5697 (December 3, 1936) a farbrengen was held in a Dvinsk hall, and the participants accepted upon themselves to learn chassidus together once a week. They signed a letter that day informing the Rebbe Rayatz, who responded with blessing on 29 Kislev. Organizer R' Baruch Eliyahu Peletz reported on 24 Tevet that 40-50 men, including older anash, were attending.
