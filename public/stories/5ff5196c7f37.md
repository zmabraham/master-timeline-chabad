# Rashab reports on stereotype plates in Rostov during WWI

*1918*

During the displacement of WWI the Rashab settled in Rostov-on-Don. From there, on 21 Av תרע"ח, he wrote to his son the future Rebbe Rayatz that the stereotype plates of Likkutei Torah and Tanya and the rest of the press equipment had survived intact — a fact that would later enable a sixth edition to be printed from those plates in תרפ"ח.
