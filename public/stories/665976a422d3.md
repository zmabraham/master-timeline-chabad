# Avraham Chaim leaves prison for Yud-Tes Kislev farbrengen

*1889*

By 19 Kislev 5650 (December 12, 1889) Avraham Chaim had so won over the Smolensk prison warden that he was released for the night to the local chassidim's meeting place, where he remained with fellow chassidim until morning. On the eve of the date he wrote that Rabbi Avraham Yitzchak Zarchi had visited him and that, with the warden's permission, he would travel into the city the next day to buy oil for the Chanukah candles. The leniency was a high point of the cumulative effect of Rabbi Zalman Aharon's advocacy earlier in the year.
