# Rebbe returns to Shmotkin's apartment two days before Yom Kippur

*1939*

As bombs continued falling, the Rebbe recited maftir Yonah with overflowing emotion. The closing service was hastened and the shofar sounded before nightfall to let worshippers leave before further bombing. After breaking the fast on soup, the Rebbe led the family in singing a chasidic melody while Warsaw was bombarded around them.
