# Passing of the Mitteler Rebbe

*1827*

The Mitteler Rebbe, Rabbi DovBer Schneuri, passed away on 9 Kislev 5588, just days before the Russian Senate's final verdict in his case. His passing came after a year of investigations stemming from Simcha Kisin's denunciation and his arrest in Vitebsk. The Senate's acquittal would arrive twelve days after his death.
