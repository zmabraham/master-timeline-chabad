# Rabbi Avishai Efargon (left) next to the director of Darkei Tmimim Yeshiva

*1974*

Rabbi Avishai Efargon (left) next to the director of Darkei Tmimim Yeshiva, Rabbi Ephraim Dagan (5782). Rabbi Avishai Eliyahu Ifargon (born 5734, 1974) is the head of Darkei Tmimim Yeshiva Tel Aviv, a military commentator who previously served in a classified IDF unit.
