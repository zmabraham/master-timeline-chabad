# Benckendorff reports findings on Tzemach Tzedek to Uvarov

*1841*

Having received the field reports through Dyakov and Freigang, Benckendorff forwarded the intelligence to Uvarov and requested a decision on next steps. The Third Section had now built a documentary file on the Tzemach Tzedek's character, family, conduct, and influence over the Jews of Vitebsk, Mogilev and Minsk. This concluded the surveillance phase and set the stage for the next move — pressuring the Rebbe to participate in the Rabbinical Conference.
