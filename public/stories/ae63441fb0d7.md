# Compromise on EY fund distribution: Chabad gets 9/101

*1829*

The Ashkenazic kollels' portion of Pekoam funds was divided into 101 equal parts, of which Kollel Chabad received 9 parts. This 5589 compromise remained the basis for Chabad's share even after the later HoD kollel was carved out in 5597, with Chabad still receiving 9/101 of the reduced Ashkenazic share.
