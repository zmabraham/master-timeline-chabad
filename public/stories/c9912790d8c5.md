# R. Nissan Nemanov organizes Anash vaad at Pocking

*1946*

On Wednesday Parshat Ki Tavo, 15 Elul 5706, R. Nissan Nemanov gathered the Lubavitchers in Pocking and, with general consensus, established a vaad to run their communal affairs. Mikhael Teitelbaum, reunited with Levin at Pocking, was included among the appointees. The community was filled with joy at having reconstituted itself in safety.
