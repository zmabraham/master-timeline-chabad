# Bomberg prints first complete Talmud in Venice

*1520*

Between 5280 and 5283 (1520–1523), Daniel Bomberg produced the first complete printed edition of the Babylonian Talmud at his Venice press, including Rashi and Tosafot on every page. This edition established the standard pagination of the Talmud, which has been retained by virtually every subsequent edition; standard citations of folio and side derive from it.
