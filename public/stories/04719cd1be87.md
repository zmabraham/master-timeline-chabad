# R' Baruch Mordechai Itigna immigrates to Eretz Yisrael

*1851*

After serving as moreh tzedek of Bobruisk until 5611, R' Baruch Mordechai Itigna immigrated to the Holy Land and settled in Jerusalem. His departure opened the way for R' Hillel of Paritch to be appointed as sole rabbi of Bobruisk, including over the misnagdic community.
