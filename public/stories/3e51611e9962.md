# Kollel Chabad accepts Rebbe Rayatz as Nasi after Rashab's passing

*1921*

In Shvat 5681 (Feb 1921), as the time for the kollel's annual elections arrived, the shocking news of the Rebbe Rashab's passing reached Jerusalem. Most members of the kollel gathered and signed a document accepting Rebbe Yosef Yitzchak (Rayatz) as their rebbe in spiritual guidance and as Nasi of the kollel, and asked the existing administrators to continue for another year.
