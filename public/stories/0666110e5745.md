# Rabbi Eliyahu Posek leaves Ravna to become rabbi of Alapolia

*1888*

Rabbi Eliyahu Posek's tenure in Ravna proved short-lived; in 5648 he moved to serve as rabbi in Alapolia. He was the author of Mor V'Ohalot and many other works, and Rabbi Yochanan of Rachmistrivka had publicly endorsed his earlier appointment in Ravna the previous year.
