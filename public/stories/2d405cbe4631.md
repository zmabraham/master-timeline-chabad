# Tzemach Tzedek's house in Lubavitch burns

*1868*

The home of the Tzemach Tzedek burned in 1868, two years after his death. The manuscripts of his Torah writings survived because they had been moved to and preserved in the home of his son the Rebbe Maharash. The fire is mentioned in Igros Kodesh of the Rebbe Maharash, letter 41.
