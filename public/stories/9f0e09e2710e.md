# R. Moshe Pinchas Katz marries Mindel Stockhamer

*1938*

On 15 Sivan 5698 (June 14, 1938), R. Moshe Pinchas Katz married Mindel Stockhamer at the building where the Rebbe Rayatz resided in Otwock — Prusa Street 6, as specified on the invitation. The Rosh Yeshiva, secretaries, and close friends of the groom participated in both the wedding and the sheva brachos.
