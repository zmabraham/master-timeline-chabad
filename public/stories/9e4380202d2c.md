# Letter on the Four Sons of the Haggadah from Pleshchenitsy

*1883*

Writing from Pleshchenitsy on 8 Marcheshvan 5644, R' Avraham Chaim addressed his prospective in-law R' Yehoshua Lipman. The letter unfolds a kabbalistic exposition of the Four Sons of the Passover Haggadah, mapping them onto the four worlds Atzilut–Beriah–Yetzirah–Asiyah and the four 'fierce ones' of Reish Lakish, and asks Lipman to clarify three differing orderings found in Pri Eitz Chaim and the siddur. He closes by inviting Lipman to bring the agreed wedding-gift sum in person.
