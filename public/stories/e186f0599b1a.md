# Tanya first printed

*1796*

In 5557, Rabbi Schneur Zalman's Tanya appeared in print for the first time, presenting his systematic Chabad approach to Chassidism. The same year, Rabbi Avraham of Kalisk launched a public critique of the Tanya's method, drawing in other Maggid disciples in Volhynia and Poland. The dispute lasted years and ultimately crystallized the separation between general Chassidism and the distinct Chabad movement.
