# Liberation of the Rayatz on Yud-Beis Tammuz

*1927*

After being arrested on 15 Sivan 5687 and exiled to Kostroma, the Rayatz was freed on 12-13 Tammuz 5687 (12-13 July 1927). The date became the great Chabad festival of liberation; later that fall, in the latter part of Tishrei 5688, he left Russia entirely and settled in Riga.
