# Mitteler Rebbe letter to Meisels and Itinga on aliyah plans

*1814*

Around 13 Iyar 1814, the Mitteler Rebbe wrote a counsel-letter to two prominent Vilna chassidim, R. Moshe Meisels (author of Shirat Moshe) and R. Baruch Mordechai Itinga (son-in-law of the Vilna av beit din), advising them against their plan to ascend to the Land of Israel. He warned that they would not enjoy long life there, citing reasons known to him. He added that if they nevertheless decided to settle, they should choose Jerusalem rather than Safed or Tiberias, and that the chassidic fund would allocate fifty gold coins annually for their support.
