# Death of R. Yehuda Leib Menuchin

*1907*

Menuchin had served as the kollel's bookkeeper in the early years of the 20th century until his role was transferred to R. Menachem Mendel Nae in late 5666. After his death, the active leadership of the Jerusalem kollel narrowed to Shlonk and Efrat as the address holders, with Yeshaya Ornstein as scribe and Nae as bookkeeper.
