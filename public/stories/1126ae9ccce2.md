# R' Yochanan Gordon studies at Tomchei Tmimim Lubavitch

*1909*

According to a self-recorded registration card, R' Yochanan Gordon learned in the Lubavitcher yeshiva Tomchei Tmimim from 5669 through 5677. This placed him at the central Chabad yeshiva during the final years of Rebbe Rashab's leadership and into the turbulent World War I period.
