# Haggadah 'Kedushat Levi' compilation from the Berditchever's works issued in Bnei Brak

*1990*

In 5750 (1990), Machon Nachalat Tzvi, edited by Shimon Hirshler, published in Bnei Brak the Haggadah 'Kedushat Levi', an anthology of Pardes-style commentaries on the Haggadah, Shir HaShirim, and the holiday, drawn from the writings of Rabbi Levi Yitzchak of Berditchev and his disciples and sons. The 252-page octavo edition also incorporated material from Yesod V'Shoresh HaAvodah. It made the Berditchever's teachings on Passover available in consolidated form.
