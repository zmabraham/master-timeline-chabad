# Rabbi Chaim Mordechai Aizik Hodakov born in Beshankovich

*1902*

Hodakov's family moved to Riga when he was two, where he studied under Rabbi Yoel Barantchik of the Novardok Musar movement. He became an ardent Chasid when the Rebbe arrived in 1928 and later founded the Derech Eretz school at eighteen.
