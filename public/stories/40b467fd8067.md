# Rebbe diagnosed with multiple sclerosis in Vienna

*1934*

The Ramash accompanied the Rebbe to Vienna where experts finally diagnosed the autoimmune disease, though they had no cure. Conflicting advice created confusion until they met Dr. Max Gerson, a German-Jewish refugee whose holistic dietary approach appealed to the Rebbe. The Rebbe moved into Gerson's Westend Sanatorium in Purkersdorf in December 1934.
