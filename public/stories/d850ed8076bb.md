# Shulsinger Brothers Haggadah with English translation by Abraham Regelson

*1950*

In 5710 (1950), the Shulsinger Brothers issued a second edition Haggadah in New York with English translation by Abraham Regelson printed in parallel columns. Sigmund Forst contributed color illustrations, Yehoshua Weisser provided musical notation, and Sh. B. Honig and Michal Yehoshua Shulsinger compiled a Yalkut for the Festival of Freedom appended in Hebrew and English. The 64-plus-80-page folio edition became a notable American Haggadah.
