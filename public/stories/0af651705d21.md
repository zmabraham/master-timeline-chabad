# Alter Rebbe sends his Siddur Nusach Ari to press in Shklov

*1803*

Tradition records that the Alter Rebbe examined some sixty existing siddurim before fixing his own nusach, and that on his deathbed he counted the formulation of this nusach as one of his three lifetime achievements. The siddur also incorporated his collected halachot and customs—the second recension of Birkat HaNehenin—and shaped Chabad davening to this day.
