# Decision to go to Lubavitch instead of Lida

*1907*

While waiting at the Molodechno train station to transfer to Lida, the author's father was approached first by a fundraiser for the Lida yeshiva who warned about lax standards among older students, and later by a venerable bearded man who denounced Rabbi Reines for sharing a stage with a partially uncovered woman and urged: 'If you want your son to remain an honest Jew, take him to Lubavitch.' After much inner turmoil, the boy chose Lubavitch and they boarded the train for Lubavitch instead.
