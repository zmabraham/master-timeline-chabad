# Founding of Kollel Chabad as a fourth separate kollel in Eretz Yisrael

*1827*

Building on the dedicated Chabad fund founded by the Alter Rebbe two decades earlier, Kollel Chabad emerged as an independent kollel structure in 5587. Over the following 40 years the four general kollelim subdivided into dozens of subsidiary kollelim corresponding to towns and regions of origin, until the 5626 merger created the Vaad HaKollel HaKlali.
