# Feigin writes Jacobson on Rayatz's reasoning for the Warsaw journey

*1931*

On 15 Menachem Av 5691, the Rebbe's secretary R' Yechezkel Feigin wrote R' Yisrael Jacobson describing how the Rebbe Rayatz had chosen, en route to Marienbad, to visit Poland and especially the yeshiva. The Rebbe had said the situation could no longer continue without a yeshiva nearby, deciding either to transfer part of the yeshiva to Riga or to relocate his own residence to Warsaw.
