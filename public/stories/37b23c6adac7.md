# Ancient ketubah from Crimea

*1680*

Among the more than fifty ancient ketubot in the Lubavitcher library archive is one from Krasov (Karasubazar) in Crimea, dated 21 Av 5440. The document records the union of Menachem ben Yosef Walid with Sara bat Gershon Ashkenazi; her dowry — including gold jewelry, silverware, garments listed in Tatar, copper vessels, and bedding — totaled 44,680 lakhans, to which the groom added 4,468 for a total of 49,148 lakhans in the coin of Murat Giray. The ketubah reached the Rebbe Rayatz in 5685 through the bibliographer R' Shmuel Wiener.
