# Spektor declines to issue an amulet, sends blessing for Yitzchak Yaffe's daughter

*1887*

Writing from Kovno on 20 Sivan 5647 in reply to a letter of 17 Sivan, Rabbi Spektor explains that he has never given amulets and that it is simply not his way. In their stead he extends a heartfelt blessing for the full recovery of Yitzchak Yaffe's daughter. He closes by noting that his weakened eyes make writing difficult.
