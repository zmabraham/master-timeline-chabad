# Rebbe Rashab and family leave Lubavitch for Rostov

*1915*

At 11 a.m. on Sunday, 16 Marcheshvan 5676 (24 October 1915), Rabbi Sholom DovBer Schneersohn left Lubavitch with his family, intending to return the following summer. He took the Rebbeim's manuscripts and a limited selection of books, while the bulk of the library — thousands of volumes in 35 crates — was deposited with the chassid R' Zalkind Persitz in Moscow. The hoped-for return never came, and a year-after-year exile began.
