# Rebbe writes anniversary letter calling 12 Tamuz a festival of liberation

*1928*

On June 3, 1928 (15 Sivan 5688), the Rebbe issued a public letter explaining that his liberation should be marked annually as a festival for those who love Torah and observe its commands, and indeed for every Jew, 'for the heart of every man of Israel is perfectly bound with G-d and His Torah.' This letter formalized the annual observance of 12 Tamuz across Jewish communities.
