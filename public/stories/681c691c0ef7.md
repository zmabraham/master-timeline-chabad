# Bobruisker Rebbe describes growing rift in Kollel Chabad

*1913*

In Adar 5673 (Feb/March 1913), the Bobruisker Rebbe published a circular describing how R. Gershon Lapshitz had proposed forming a separate kollel. He recounted that the old administrators threatened poor recipients with loss of stipends if they accepted money from the new ones, but in the end the breach widened. The letter was circulated under the title 'Copy of Letters of the Bobruisker Rebbe Regarding Etrog Trade'.
