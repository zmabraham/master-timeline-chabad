# Farbrengen on 19 Kislev decries Lubavitch maskil school

*1898*

On 19 Kislev 5659 (December 3, 1898), the Rashab spoke about a major farbrengen, lamenting that Baron Gunzburg had refused his plea to 'give me Yavneh' and spare Lubavitch from a maskil-funded school. The school had nonetheless opened with 130 students.
