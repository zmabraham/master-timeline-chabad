# R' Meir Heilperin letter on book selection for Rashab

*1912*

On 16 Elul 5672 (August 29, 1912), book dealer R' Meir Heilperin wrote requesting that R' M. Rabinowitz catalog all available books, both new and old half-quires, not just rare ones, so he could select what the Rebbe Rashab needed. The letter suggests Heilperin had already visited Lubavitch and examined the library during summer 5672.
