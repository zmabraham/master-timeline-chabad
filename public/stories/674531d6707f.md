Rabbi Yossi Nachshon. Rabbi Yossi (Yosef Sheva Or) Nachshon (born 5731, 1971) is the Rebbe's shliach in Ramat Mamre, Kiryat Arba, director of the Chabad Tank Hebron activities, and coordinator of the 'To Soldiers with Love' project which distributes free pairs of tefillin to IDF soldiers
