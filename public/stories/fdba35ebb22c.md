# Chol HaMoed Pesach 5673 Rayatz farbrengen in the Raza's rooms

*1913*

During Chol HaMoed Pesach 5673 the Rayatz farbrenged with the senior bochurim in the rooms of the RaZ"A (R. Zalman Aharon). He told how the Rashbatz had once made him pay six kopecks for whiskey and two for cake to hear a chassidishe vort, then delayed telling it: the parable of the wagon driver's apprentice who, after a fast on 17 Tammuz, said "today is not yesterday" — meaning each day must be better than the last.
