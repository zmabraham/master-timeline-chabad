# Alter Rebbe yahrzeit farbrengens held in Moscow amid Gubinko backlash

*1991*

On 24 Tevet 5751 Gubinko told Gorbachev that Arthur Schneier, Adolph Shayevitch and Edgar Bronfman opposed returning the library, prompting the delegation to spend the night chasing rebuttals via Bronfman's deputy Israel Singer at the Savoy hotel. Rosh Yeshiva R' Uri Kamishov hosted a farbrengen at Marina Roshcha that R' Levin then continued at the Aguch office until 11:30 pm, reviewing the Alter Rebbe's biography on his yahrzeit. The delegation returned to the apartment around 2 am.
