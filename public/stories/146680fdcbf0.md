# Renewed, and less than a month later on Shabbat Parshat Terumah of that year

*2000*

Renewed, and less than a month later on Shabbat Parshat Terumah of that year, the Rebbe expressed that this is 'his soul-felt request,' and within less than a year, on the eve of Rosh Hashanah 5745 the 2000th edition of the Tanya was already printed: recently a suggestion and soul-felt request was made
