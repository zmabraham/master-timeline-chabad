# Alter Rebbe begins revising Hilchos Netilas Yadayim for the Siddur

*1778*

His sons relate that after his sixteenth review of the Talmud the Alter Rebbe began emending and renewing his Shulchan Aruch, starting with Hilchos Netilas Yadayim. The revised version was later printed as the kuntres 'Luach Birchas Hanehenin' and 'Seder Netilas Yadayim Lese'udah' in his Siddur. This opened a roughly twenty-year middle period of writing thirty kuntreisei halachos, lasting through 5557.
