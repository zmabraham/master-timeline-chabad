# Soviet censors approve export of 59 boxes of books

*1927*

On 5 Menachem-Av 5687, representatives A. Feigelstein of the foreign-trade office and G. Alachbrov of the Leningrad customs office signed off on the inspection and official sealing of fifty-nine book boxes belonging to Y. Schneersohn. After initial Soviet refusal to permit export of the library, intercession by Dr. A. Cohen of Berlin and Latvian parliamentarian Mordechai Dubin secured Moscow's order to allow the books out, with the censor instructed to stamp every crate.
