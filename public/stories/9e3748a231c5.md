# Rabbi Yaakov Goldberg at an Inspirational Gathering on 'The Service of Prayer'

*1944*

Rabbi Yaakov Goldberg at an inspirational gathering on 'The Service of Prayer' Rabbi Yaakov Kapil Goldberg (born Menachem Av 5704, 1944) is the head of Yeshivas Kol Yaakov Yehuda - Hadar HaTorah for Ba'alei Teshuva in Crown Heights, and a maggid shiur at the Central Tomchei Tmimim Yeshiva
