# Ramash meets Chaya Mushka for first time in Kislovodsk

*1923*

The Rebbe Rayatz confided to Althaus that summer that he wished to marry off his daughter to the Ramash, and asked him to act as shadkhan. Althaus escorted the twenty-one-year-old groom on the 600-mile trip from Yekaterinoslav to Kislovodsk. The Ramash stayed two weeks before returning to Rostov with the Rebbe.
