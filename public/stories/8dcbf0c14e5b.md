# Sugihara issues Japanese transit visas to Mir and Tomchei Tmimim students

*1940*

Mir students from Holland first obtained Dutch visas to Curacao and then Japanese transit visas through Sugihara in Kovno. The Amshinover Rebbe, R. Shimon Shalom Kalish, urged the rest of the refugees to follow suit. After telegraphing the Rebbe Rayatz, the Tomchei Tmimim students did the same and obtained Japanese transit visas before the consulate closed when Lithuania was absorbed into the Soviet Union.
