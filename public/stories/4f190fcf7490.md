# R' Yaakov Shimshon distributes funds independently, sparking conflict

*1799*

In 5559 (1799), R' Yaakov Shimshon of Shepetivka brought the Polish-Volhynian funds to Tiberias and distributed them solely among his people, breaking from the previous arrangement under R' Avraham of Kalisk. The conflict between the two leaders intensified sharply. R' Nachman of Breslov attempted to mediate between them, as documented in Kovetz Beit Aharon V'Yisrael.
