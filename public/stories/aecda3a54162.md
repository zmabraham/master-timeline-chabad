# Captain Yisrael Yudkin as a soldier, Hatamim Captain Yisrael HaKohen Yudkin H

*2024*

Captain Yisrael Yudkin as a soldier, Hatamim Captain Yisrael HaKohen Yudkin Hy"d (5 Sivan 5761 - 14 Iyar 5784, 2024) served as a deputy company commander in the chareidi Netzach Yehuda battalion, and fell from Arab sniper fire, after fighting in his last months in the Gaza Strip
