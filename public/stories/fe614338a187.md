# Tzemach Tzedek defends R. Yissachar Ber against removal in Bychov Chadash

*1831*

In 5591 (1831) residents of Bychov Chadash attempted to install a new moreh tzedek in place of R. Yissachar Ber after they brought in a relative for the position. The Tzemach Tzedek responded with a stern ruling (Yoreh Deah 343) that custom across Israel held a moreh tzedek's appointment to be permanent and that it was absolutely forbidden to remove a qualified scholar without cause. Despite this, R. Yissachar Ber left for Lubavitch within two years.
