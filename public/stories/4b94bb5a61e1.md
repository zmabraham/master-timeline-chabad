# Rebbe blesses Chabad delegation at dollar distribution before returning to USSR

*1991*

The Rebbe (in Russian) told Veronika that what had been achieved was 'only a beginning' and praised Rabbi Shalom Ber Levin's deep familiarity with the books, saying she should now learn from him. He gave them additional dollars for tzedakah to be given in Russia and wished them 'good news, great success.'
