# R' Moshe Dovber Rivkin appointed spiritual director of Yeshiva Torah Vodaas

*1928*

R' Yeshaya Binush Tarshish approached the author with news that Torah Vodaas needed a new menahel ruchni following R' Shraga Feivel Mendlowitz's resignation from that position. The author and Rav Nissan Telushkin advocated for R' Rivkin, who at the time was floundering in an ill-defined ma'amad role. Rivkin was a natural fit — a scholar with yeshiva administrative experience — and his appointment came as Torah Vodaas stood on the threshold of major growth.
