# Rabbi Elchonon Morozov arrested

*1927*

Morozov was held with the worst political prisoners, creating an ominous atmosphere among the Chabad community. Before being taken, he secretly handed the list of underground yeshivas and schools to his eleven-year-old son, who hid it without the police noticing. In May 1927 he was indicted for helping Jews cross the border illegally and sentenced to three years exile in Krasnoyarsk.
