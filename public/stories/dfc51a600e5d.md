# Haggadah with commentary of Eliyahu ben Todros Broda printed in Prague

*1713*

In 5473 (1713), Shlomo Proops printed a Haggadah in Prague featuring a Yiddish translation by Eliyahu ben Todros Broda. The 16-leaf folio edition served the Yiddish-speaking Jewish community of Bohemia. The surviving copy in the library is missing its title page and possibly its opening section.
