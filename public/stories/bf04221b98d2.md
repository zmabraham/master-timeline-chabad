# Rebbe Rayatz delivers Rosh Hashanah maamar in Brownsville

*1929*

Years later Yehoshua Greenberg recalled that the shul was packed, with many gathered outdoors. 'The Rebbe's voice was booming,' he said, 'and even those outside heard him.' The maamar 'Zeh HaYom Techilat Maasekha' was later published.
