# Major General Aharon Yariv — 9 Tevet 5681 (December 20, 1920) – 26 Iyar 5754

*1997*

Major General Aharon Yariv (9 Tevet 5681, December 20, 1920 – 26 Iyar 5754, May 7, 1997) was a major general in the IDF, head of Aman, and a minister in the Israeli government, who received strategic guidance from the Rebbe. Biography section.
