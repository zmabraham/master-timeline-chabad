# Khovansky reports lost responses and orders fast case resolution

*1826*

Following the latest district court inspector's report, Khovansky relayed that the policeman sent with questions to the Rebbe had lost the responses on the road from Lubavitch to Babinovich. He proposed punishing those responsible at the Babinovich peace court for sending a policeman instead of bringing the Rebbe in for proper interrogation. Khovansky also instructed the Orsha and Kopust town councils and the Mohilev lower court to expedite the requested testimony, and reiterated his earlier call for a competent secretary to be appointed.
