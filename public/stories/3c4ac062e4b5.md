# Settlement between Moshe Vechiyon and Moshe ibn Assoulin in Fez

*1723*

Vechiyon was owed about 170 ounces; mediators settled the dispute and Shlomo ben Masoud ibn Botbol assumed a written obligation for the agreed sum. The parties mutually released all past money, oath, and gilgul-shevua claims, retaining only an outstanding loan with extended repayment until Sukkot eve. Witnessed by R' Yehuda ibn Attar and Yitzchak Maimran.
