# Letter to Rabbi Herzog on Palestine certificates for Polish yeshiva students

*1940*

With the British certificates being prepared for Polish yeshiva students, the Rebbe pressed Rabbi Herzog to reserve a known number for him personally to allocate to Tomchei Tmimim talmidim, some still in Poland and some already rescued. He also wrote the same day to R. Chanoch Hendel Havlin in Eretz Yisrael coordinating the request through R. Chaim Ozer.
