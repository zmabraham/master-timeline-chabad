# R. Yitzchok Aryeh Mandelbaum sent to head Łódź yeshiva

*1931*

Early in 5692 (autumn 1931), the Rebbe Rayatz dispatched R. Yitzchok Aryeh Mandelbaum to Łódź to head the yeshiva. He arrived on the night of Sunday of Parshas Noach and immediately set about examining and organizing students into divisions. Although officially appointed only as ram, the burden of administration also fell on his shoulders since the Schneersohn brothers were occupied with their business.
