# Rebbe declares duty to bring Moshiach in famed 27 Nissan sichah

*1991*

In a famous sichah delivered on 27 Nissan 5751 (April 11, 1991), the Rebbe declared that the obligation rests on every Jew to do everything possible to bring about the coming of Moshiach and to publicize the message of redemption on the broadest scale. The sichah marked a turning point in Chabad's intensified Moshiach campaign in its final years.
