# Chafetz Chaim writes about sales of completed Mishnah Berurah

*1907*

He sent his emissary R. Yitzchak ben Yisrael Yosef of Cizevo to distribute his books in towns near Warsaw, asking Jews to assist him. He specified prices for each volume: Mishnah Berurah parts 1, 2, 3, 5, 6 at 1.20 rubles each, part 4 at 90 kopeks bound, with 6.60 rubles for the complete set.
