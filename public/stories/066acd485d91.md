# Yeshiva reaches nearly 200 students in winter 5695

*1934*

The Rosh Yeshiva detailed enrollment: about 60 in the kibbutz, 30 in the great class, 29 with R. Dovid Teiblum, 23 with R. Zalman Gurary, and nearly 60 at the Zamenhof branch. He noted improvement in chazaras hamaamarim, with nearly all kibbutz students participating.
