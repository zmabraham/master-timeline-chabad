# Hebron arbitration ruling establishes joint Ashkenazi Vaad

*1887*

After disputes arose between Kollel Chabad in Hebron and the other Ashkenazi kollelim, both sides turned to the Jerusalem beis din under R' Shmuel Salant. The arbitrators Zalman Chaim Rivlin and Yoel Moshe Salomon, with R' Shmuel Salant's approval, ruled on 20 Tammuz 5647 that two Chabad representatives and one from the other kollelim would form a three-man 'Vaad of the Ashkenazi communities in Hebron' with shared seal and shared distribution of communal funds.
