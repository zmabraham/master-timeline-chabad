# Tzemach Tzedek begins responding to halachic questions

*1818*

While he had written occasional novellae earlier, the Tzemach Tzedek began responding to halachic queries with regularity around 5578, while his great-uncle Maharil of Yanovitch was still the primary respondent in the Chabad community. Responsum §349 in Yoreh Deah and others can be dated to this year, marking the beginning of his fifty-year career as a posek.
