# Society for Spreaders of Enlightenment founded in Russia

*1863*

Originally named 'Chevra Marbei Haskalah,' the society later changed its name to 'Chevra Mefitzei Haskalah.' It established a wide network of schools and Talmud-Torah institutions across the Pale of Settlement, employing Maskilic teachers and aiming to draw Jewish children toward secular learning and away from traditional observance. The Rebbe Rashab viewed it as a grave spiritual threat and organized sustained opposition against it.
