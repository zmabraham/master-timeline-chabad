# Agudas Chassidei Chabad of Poland, Lithuania, and Latvia founded in Riga

*1930*

Fearing the Rebbe Rayatz might relocate to the US, Chasidim who had flocked to Riga for the High Holidays met Saturday evening at the Rashag's home. The new organization aimed to unite the Chabad community, reinforce study of chasidic philosophy, and build a financial foundation enabling the Rebbe to relocate to Warsaw. They sent a letter to the Rebbe urging him to settle in Warsaw rather than America.
