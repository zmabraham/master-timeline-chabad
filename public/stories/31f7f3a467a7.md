# Rebbe visits Warsaw and dispatches students to scout Polish-Volhynian towns

*1931*

In mid-Tammuz 5691, while in Warsaw, the Rebbe Rayatz convened several senior students — including R' Shneur Zalman Gurary, R' Yosef Goldstein, R' Meir Glazman and R' Yitzchak Mandelbaum — and tasked them with surveying towns across Poland and the Volhynia region. Each was to assess where it would be feasible to establish new branches of the yeshiva. By mid-Av the students were reporting back, and the Rebbe responded with detailed instructions to each.
