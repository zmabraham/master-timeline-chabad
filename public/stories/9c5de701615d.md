# Fixed delegation work pattern for Moscow and camp

*1991*

On Motzaei Shabbos 16 Tammuz 5751, the family settled into a routine: the rebbetzin would oversee the camp kitchen during the day from a nearby inn, while the author would spend Shabbos at camp and weekdays in Moscow with the delegation. The diary notes the camp lacked proper kashrus oversight in the kitchen before her arrival. The author's sons began integrating with the campers despite the language barrier.
