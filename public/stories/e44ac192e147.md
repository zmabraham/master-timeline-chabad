# 1929 Hebron massacre destroys the Jewish community

*1929*

Just days after the Rebbe Rayatz's visit, the 1929 Hebron massacre fell upon the Jewish community. R' Moshe Goldsmid, the spiritual anchor of Chabad's tiny remnant, was among those slaughtered, hy"d. The surviving Jews abandoned Hebron, ending the Jewish settlement in the city.
