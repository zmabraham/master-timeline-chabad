# Leningrad conference announced for October

*1927*

Despite the Rebbe's opposition, LERO pressed ahead with plans for an October conference. The plan was secretly backed by the Yevsektzia, who saw it as a chance to create alternative leadership and divert JDC funds away from the Rebbe's network. With Moscow and Minsk withholding approval, the scheme faltered — making drastic action against the Rebbe a matter of time.
