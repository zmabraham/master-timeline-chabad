# All 28 matzah cars confirmed arrived in Soviet Russia

*1929*

After months of negotiations over export permits and customs rates — initially 5 kopecks per kilo, then a demand of 2 rubles per kilo before settling at half a ruble — 28 train cars of matzah were shipped from Riga and Berlin to 17 Soviet towns. Alongside the cars, 5,689 packages of matzah flour reached private addresses across 404 towns, all arriving by the eve of Passover 5689.
