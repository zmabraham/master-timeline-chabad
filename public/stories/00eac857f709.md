# Bobruisker Rebbe formally approves separate Kollel under Lapshitz-Kazarnovsky

*1913*

In Iyar 5673 (May 1913), the Bobruisker Rebbe issued a letter stating that a gathering of important Kollel members had concluded that a separate vaad must be established and that he had consented. He directed that any money sent in their names would reach the poor efficiently.
