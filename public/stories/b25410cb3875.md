# Interrogation of Rabbi Aharon Halevi of Staroshele

*1825*

From 21 to 28 October 1825 (21–28 Marcheshvan 5586), Rabbi Aharon Halevi of Staroshele, founder of the Strashelye chassidic court, was interrogated in Vitebsk by Russian state investigators. The questioning related to the parallel Maamad fundraising in his branch of Chabad and was part of the broader probe arising from Simcha Keysin's denunciations against Rabbi DovBer.
