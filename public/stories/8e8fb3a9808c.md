# Levin reports on Paris kiruv work to the Rebbe

*1948*

Levin wrote on 11 Elul 5708 that he was traveling daily from Bezancourt into Paris to teach the children of French Jewish 'patrons' who spoke only French. He had grown his roster from one student to eight, teaching kamatz alef and laying tefillin. He described coaxing reluctant students to don a hat and tefillin and distributing French-language Merkos pamphlets in the homes.
