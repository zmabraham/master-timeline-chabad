# R. Shlomo arrives in Tiberias from the Diaspora

*1779*

A letter from summer 5540 records that in Menachem Av 5539 an individual named R. Shlomo arrived in Tiberias from abroad, carrying a letter addressed to R. Yitzchak of Ciecierzec. This mention is significant for the chronology of early chassidic shadarim, as it may relate to the identity and movements of R. Shlomo Segal.
