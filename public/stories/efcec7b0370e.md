# Rebbe Rashab travels to Petersburg in Cheshvan 5671

*1910*

After the official protocol was prepared, Baron David Gunzburg signed it and prepared to submit it, but fell ill with his final illness. The question arose of who would deliver the protocol to the head of the Religious Affairs Department at the Interior Ministry, and several corrections were still required. The Rebbe Rashab traveled to Petersburg in Cheshvan 5671 to handle these matters; the protocol was apparently submitted at that time.
