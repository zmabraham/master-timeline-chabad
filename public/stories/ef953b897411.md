# Fire at Alter Rebbe's home destroys Shulchan Aruch manuscript

*1810*

In 5570 (1810), a fire broke out at the home of Rabbi Schneur Zalman of Liadi, the founder of Chabad. The blaze destroyed the autograph manuscript of his Shulchan Aruch HaRav, the legal code he had composed. It was the first of two devastating fires that struck his household in his final years.
