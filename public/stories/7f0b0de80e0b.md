# Chabad refugees settle at Waldstadt Pocking DP camp

*1946*

Following Shabbat at Hof the group moved to Waldstadt Pocking, a sprawling DP camp of about 7,000 Jews not far from Munich. Two blocks were assigned temporarily, with a permanent Lubavitcher corner — one room per family — promised within days. Pocking would become the main Chabad refugee hub in occupied Germany.
