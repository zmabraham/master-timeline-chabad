# R' Leib Baal HaYisurim of Hebron dies in Tzfat

*1836*

R' Leib, one of the founders of the Chabad settlement in Hebron alongside R' Shimon Shmerling, moved to Tzfat late in life and died there as an old man. According to 'Shaarei Yerushalayim,' he instructed before death that anyone in distress should prostrate on his grave for aid, a practice that continued for generations. His tombstone identifies him as 'R' Leib of Hebron, baal hayisurim, died 15 Cheshvan 5597.'
