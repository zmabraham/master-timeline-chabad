# R. Chaim of Volozhin visits Belarus and discusses polished shechitah knives

*1803*

Notables who were not chassidim heard from R. Chaim that the Vilna Gaon's ban on the chassidic-style polished shechitah knives had been issued as a 'distancing' measure against the chassidim, not as inherent halachic law. The Alter Rebbe cites this account in his 1811 epistle defending the practice.
