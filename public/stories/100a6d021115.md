# Inheritance farbrengen for the Horowitz heirs in Lubavitch

*1915*

Sons, sons-in-law and a grandson of the late R' Yosef Yozik Horowitz, who had held a monopoly on button sales, came to Lubavitch to divide his estate before the Rebbe Rashab. Once arrangements were finalized they hosted a farbrengen on Thursday night at the home of R' Michael Katzenelnbogen, attended by mashpi'im, melamdim and select students. R' Moshe of Zembin and the Rashbatz debated the relative spiritual stature of the Rebbe Rashab, with R' Yitzchak Magidson defusing tension through niggun.
