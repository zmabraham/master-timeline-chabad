# Beis Chabad house purchased in Lubavitch

*1988*

On 20 Marcheshvan 5749 (November 1988), the yeshiva students traveled to Lubavitch to find a house for sale. After a day of searching, they found the only house available for purchase, and on 21 Marcheshvan it was purchased in the name of student Nachum Tamarin. They added the inscription '770 - Beis Chabad' on the gate. The Rebbe subsequently called it 'Beis Schneersohn.'
