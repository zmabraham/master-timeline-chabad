# Solovki exile sentence reduced to three years in Kostroma

*1927*

On Thursday, after Madam Peshkova pressured the GPU again, the Rebbe was informed that instead of Solovki he would be exiled for three years to Kostroma. He saw the official document on the desk, with the original 'Death by firing squad' crossed out, replaced by 'Ten years in Solovki', and then by 'Three years in Kostroma.' Guards beat him repeatedly when he refused to stand to hear the sentence.
