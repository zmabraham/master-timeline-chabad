# Rebbe calls women and girls to light Shabbos candles

*1974*

In a special sicha on 24 Elul 5734 (1974), the Rebbe called for every Jewish woman and girl — including girls below marriageable age, from the age of education — to light Shabbos and Yom Tov candles. The campaign produced an enormous worldwide distribution of candlesticks and educational materials.
