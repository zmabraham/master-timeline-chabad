# Rayatz petitions Soviet scientific council for library return

*1925*

Rayatz describes how the library had been transferred from the former Kokorev warehouses to the museum about a year earlier and still sat in the original Lubavitch crates. He explains that since moving to Leningrad he now has both the space and the need to resume scholarly use of his collection. The appeal is structured around his earlier Narkompros authorization, which library administrators were claiming they could not locate.
