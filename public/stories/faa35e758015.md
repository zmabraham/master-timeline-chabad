# Earliest extant Naeh booklet calendar (5692)

*1931*

Before Kollel Chabad began publishing R. Naeh's calendar, he printed it himself as a booklet sent to overseas supporters. The library's earliest preserved copy is titled 'Luach for the year 5692 with the laws and customs of the year, by Rabbi Avraham Chaim Naeh, Jerusalem,' carrying his New Year greeting to donors.
