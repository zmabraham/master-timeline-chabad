# Golda Meir witnesses Chabad-organized Rosh Hashanah crowds in Moscow

*1949*

In 1949, newly appointed Ambassador Golda Meir attended High Holiday services at Moscow's Choral Synagogue. On the first day of Rosh HaShanah she found only a small group of elderly Jews, but on the second day thousands filled the streets to welcome her. Decades later she told Italian reporter Oriana Fallaci that 'it was Chabad that orchestrated the welcome of thousands.'
