# R' Shimon Menashe Chaikin, Hebron Chabad rav, dies at age 92

*1893*

Born in Shklov around 5562, R' Shimon Menashe Chaikin emigrated to Eretz Israel at age 18 in 5580 and moved from Tzfat to Hebron at the Mitteler Rebbe's directive. He served as Av Beit Din of Hebron's Chabad community for some fifty years. Hachavatzelet reported that 'all Hebron's inhabitants, Sephardic and Ashkenazi, streamed in reverence behind his bier and aroused bitter mourning over him.'
