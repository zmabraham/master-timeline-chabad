# Melaveh Malkah hosted for Stas and family in Moscow

*1991*

On Motzaei Shabbat 25 Shevat 5751 the delegation organized a Melaveh Malkah at their Moscow apartment for Stas and his entourage, including his mother — daughter-in-law of former USSR President Mikoyan — and the Levin sisters. From 9:00 p.m. to 12:30 a.m. they screened Chabad videos including on Chernobyl, sang Chabad songs in Russian, and the matriarch confided that she had spoken with Gorbachev at a cemetery visit, who said he intended to return the books but had been hindered by Gubenko.
