# Israel establishes Lishkat Hakesher to support Soviet Jewry

*1953*

Headed first by Shaul Avigur from 1953 to 1970, Lishkat Hakesher worked closely with Chabad's clandestine network in Russia. Its open activities ended after the Six-Day War when the Soviets cut ties with Israel, but it later became the global coordinator of the Soviet Jewry movement.
