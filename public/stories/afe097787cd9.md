# Government commission votes to return Chabad seforim

*1991*

On the afternoon of 13 Menachem Av 5751, Veronika told the delegation that a government commission had sat that day on several pending matters, with return of the seforim as the second item on the agenda. Eight representatives presented the case and all 100 members voted in favor. The resulting resolution, citing legal and political grounds for the return, was to be presented for Gorbachev's signature that Friday; Gorbachev himself had missed the session for another meeting.
