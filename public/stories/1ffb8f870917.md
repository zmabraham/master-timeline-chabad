# Didan Notzach — Chabad Library trial concludes in favor of Agudas Chassidei Chabad

*1987*

In 5747 (1987) the court issued its ruling: the Rebbeim had not held the library as personal property and the entire collection belonged to Agudas Chassidei Chabad. Following the verdict, the Rebbe launched a massive book-donation campaign and ordered the two parallel libraries — the Rayatz's and his own — to be merged into a single central library of Agudas Chassidei Chabad. The day of the verdict, 5 Tevet, entered the Chabad calendar as 'Didan Notzach.'
