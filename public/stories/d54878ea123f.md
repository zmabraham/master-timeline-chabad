# Rafael Cohen leaves Soviet Russia for Riga

*1933*

Cohen had served as rabbi in Osvet, Shchedrin, and Nevel before being forced to flee Nevel after the 5689 arrests. He served as ram in the Tomchei Temimim yeshivos in Vitebsk and Moscow before his emigration. The Rebbe wrote to welcome him to Riga, noting that 'here it is a land where the government does not interfere with the observance of Torah and mitzvos.'
