# Rebbe Rayatz urges Shmotkin to find a full-time fundraiser for Warsaw yeshiva

*1924*

The Rebbe (Igrot Kodesh vol. 1, letter 179) offered generous percentage compensation to anyone who would take on Warsaw's fundraising and growth as a full-time mission, arguing the institution could easily be five or six times larger than its current state. He warned that 'a thing that stands in place is truly going down.'
