# Mogilev community signs synagogue and mikvah trustee agreement

*1822*

On 11 Menachem-Av 5582, Chabad chassidim in Mogilev signed a formal trustee agreement governing their synagogue and mikvah. The agreement was deposited the same day at the Mogilev district peace court, giving it legal standing. The arrangement reflects the institutional organization of Chabad communal life that opponents like Kissin viewed as encroachment.
