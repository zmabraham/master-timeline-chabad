# Mitteler Rebbe's answers on Lubavitch synagogue construction

*1825*

In his written response to the committee's questions, the Rebbe specified that the large synagogue in Lubavitch had cost 6,000 rubles to build. Four Torah scrolls of the five books of Moses written on parchment cost 1,500 rubles, while crystal chandeliers, arks and other interior furnishings came to 500 rubles. Religious books reached roughly 3,000 rubles. The Rebbe stressed that since construction occurred over time, he could not state with certainty how much came from the collected funds versus later income.
