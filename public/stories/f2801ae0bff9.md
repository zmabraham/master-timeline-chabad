# Eretz Yisrael Anash ratify R. Menachem Mendel Naeh's appointment

*1912*

At the beginning of Marcheshvan 5673 (Oct/Nov 1912), the public of Anash from all parts of Eretz Yisrael affirmed the Rebbe Rashab's appointment of R. Menachem Mendel Naeh and signed letters confirming the new administrative arrangement. This was documented in the kuntres 'Tumas Yesharim'.
