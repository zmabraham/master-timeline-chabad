# R' Moshe Meizlish flees Vilna amid Mitnagdic persecution

*1803*

Once the Mitnagdim realized the Alter Rebbe had been freed, they turned their wrath on R' Moshe Meizlish, the former neeman of the Vilna kahal who had become a devoted disciple. He was stripped of his communal position and his livelihood, eventually being forced into wandering. The Alter Rebbe wrote a letter of introduction for him to the Baal HaHafla'ah in Germany. A pamphlet Meizlish printed in Kapust in 5572 dates his flight to the month of Ziv 5563.
