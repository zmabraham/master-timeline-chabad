# Rosenbaum permitted to build a sukkah in Smolensk prison courtyard

*1889*

Although Rosenbaum initially feared he would not even be able to eat a kezayit of bread in a sukkah and lamented his isolation from the community, the prison warden ultimately granted him permission to erect a sukkah in the prison yard for Sukkot 5650. The episode showed the warden's growing regard for him and was preserved in a letter by R' Shlomo Rosenbaum.
