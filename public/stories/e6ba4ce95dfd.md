# Rebbe's bris mazal-tov letter for Shalom DovBer Levin

*1948*

Following the bris of his newborn son, Levin received a letter dated 25 Tishrei 5709. In it the Frierdiker Rebbe wished him mazal tov on entering his son into the covenant of Avraham Avinu and blessed him to raise the child to Torah, chuppah and good deeds amid expansiveness in body and spirit.
