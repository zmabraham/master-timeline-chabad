# Rashag asks R' Yisroel Chasdan in Stockholm to expedite shipment to New York

*1941*

On 26 Shevat the Rashag wrote in English to R' Yisroel Chasdan, asking him to press the Schenker firm to release the Stockholm cargo and to arrange transit via Lisbon for the three crates of manuscripts being held under American Embassy custody in Berlin in the names of Rabbis Jacobson and Hecht.
