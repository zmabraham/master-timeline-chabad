# Rebbe arranges R' Moshe Hornstein's interim directorship of Warsaw yeshiva

*1928*

After traveling with R' Shraga Feivish Zalmanov, the Rebbe Rayatz had persuaded his uncle R' Moshe Hornstein to step in and put the yeshiva in order for about three months. He now asked Zalmanov to send his son Shmuel to Otwock to acquaint Hornstein with the operations, ledgers, and correspondence, and wrote Hornstein himself to confirm the plan. The arrangement does not appear in later sources, suggesting it was never carried out.
