# Bris milah for 16-year-old Zev from Zhitomir in Moscow

*1991*

Zev had fled his Zhitomir home about a month before the bris in order to learn in the Chabad yeshiva. His parents eventually consented to both his Torah study and his circumcision. The diarist served as sandek at the ceremony, held at the Moscow synagogue on 18 Adar 5751.
