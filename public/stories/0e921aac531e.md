# Hebron Ashkenazi and Sephardic communities sign formal agreement

*1830*

In 1830 the Sephardic and Ashkenazi communities of Hebron concluded a formal accord governing communal matters. R' Leib Chitrik, a senior trustee of Kolel Chabad, was one of the Ashkenazi signatories. The agreement was one of several inter-community arrangements that shaped Hebron's Jewish life in the 19th century.
