# Ksav Sofer writes to Maharia Asad about the Paks assembly's outcome

*1844*

Writing from Pressburg on 5 Cheshvan 5605, R. Avraham Shmuel Binyamin Sofer (Ksav Sofer) updated R. Yehuda Asad on developments since the Paks assembly. He confirmed that the magnates had withdrawn support for the reformers' seminary and the danger had passed, crediting community askanim who had worked tirelessly to defeat it. He recommended against convening a further public rabbinical council in Pressburg, urging that resistance proceed quietly until the controversy subsided.
