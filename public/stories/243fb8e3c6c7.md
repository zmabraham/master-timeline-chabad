# Latvian police raid four Chasidim running Russia aid operation

*1935*

Moshe and Shmaryahu Gurary, Dovber Chaskind, and Yosef Rosenblum had been working full-time from Riga shipping food packages and transferring funds to Jews in Russia. All four were jailed and released on bail. Forced to choose between prison or expulsion, the Rebbe advised emigration to British Mandate Palestine, where they continued the operation from Tel Aviv.
