# Rebbe deploys Chatzkevich across Lithuania and Latvia

*1923*

On 9 Elul 5683 the Rebbe wrote to Chatzkevich (Igros Kodesh vol. 14, letter 5178), formally charging him with founding chassidus-study groups in the provinces of Kovno, Vilna, and Dvinsk. The Rebbe also wrote to anash in those regions (letter 5181), asking them to support Chatzkevich and establish written and oral shiurim in every place anash lived.
