# Alter Rebbe receives release certificate

*1798*

On 20 Kislev 5559 (Nov 17, 1798 OS / Nov 28 NS) the Alter Rebbe received his written release certificate identifying him as 'Zalman ben Baruch' and authorizing his return to White Russia via Vitebsk. The same day, Prokuror General Pyotr Lopukhin formally notified the Belorussian governor Semyon Zhegulin of the imperial decision.
