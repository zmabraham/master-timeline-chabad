# Chabad yeshivah opens in Warsaw with twelve students

*1921*

The Rebbe referred to this time as 'days of light and days of joy,' even as the yeshivah in Rostov was being forcibly closed and the Chabad educational network in Russia went underground. He appointed a board of five members, instructing them to 'focus on discussions of ethical behavior, instilling a G-d-fearing spirit, and acquiring good character traits.' By 1928 the yeshivah had grown to 250 students.
