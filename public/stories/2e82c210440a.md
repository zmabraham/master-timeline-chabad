# Rebbe's letter to Rabbi Mordechai Savitsky on the Rogatchover

*1955*

On 17 Tevet 5715, the Rebbe wrote to Rabbi Mordechai Savitsky acknowledging receipt of his books Beit Mordechai vol. 2 and Mayim Adirim vol. 1. The Rebbe noted a special thread he saw in the books — Savitsky's frequent citation of the Rogatchover Gaon — and remarked on the wonder that scholars overlooked the wealth of insights in the Rogatchover's writings.
