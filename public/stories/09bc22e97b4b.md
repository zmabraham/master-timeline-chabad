# Passing of Rebbetzin Rivka, wife of Maharash

*1914*

Rebbetzin Rivka, daughter of Rabbi Aharon of Aleksandrov and wife of Maharash, died on 10 Shevat 5674 (1914) — a date later doubly significant in Chabad history. She was laid to rest near her husband Maharash adjacent to the Tzemach Tzedek's ohel in Lubavitch. A separate ohel in the cemetery housed graves of earlier rebbetzins of the Chabad dynasty.
