# Tomchei Tmimim talmidim send detailed report to Rebbe from Shanghai

*1941*

On Chai Elul 5701 (September 10, 1941), the student-leaders Yitzchok HaKohen Hendel, Yosef Weinberg, Yosef Menachem Mendel Tenenbaum, and Yosef Rodal wrote to the Rebbe. They described how Rabbi Meir Ashkenazi welcomed them warmly upon arrival before Shabbos Tetzei, hosted them for a farbrengen, and planned another for Chai Elul itself.
