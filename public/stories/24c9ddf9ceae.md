# Rebbe Rashab visits the Apatov shul in Vilna on Lag BaOmer 5669

*1909*

The visit is recorded in a letter of the Rebbe Rayatz (Igrot Kodesh vol. 1, letter 171). Coming during the Iyar 5669 rabbinical conference, it strengthened the standing of the newly acquired Chabad synagogue in Vilna. The discourse left a lasting impression on the local Chabad community.
