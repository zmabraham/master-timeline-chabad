# Rebbe Rayatz writes Boston rabbi about cramped library conditions

*1938*

Writing to Rabbi David Meir Rabinowitz of Boston on 18 Tammuz 5698 (July 17, 1938), the Rebbe Rayatz lamented that he and his books were 'in an exile of pressure and constraint' without adequate space. He reported owning approximately 13,000 printed volumes of holy books plus several thousand history and related works, only some of which stood on shelves while the rest had been locked in crates for years.
