# Shabbat Mevorchim Elul 5706 spent near Polish-Czech border before Bricha crossing

*1946*

The Anash group waited at this settlement near the Polish-Czech border for the Bricha to move them onward. At the Shabbat Mevorchim Elul farbrengen, one of the participants — likely R. Nissan Nemenov — observed that in Russia Yiddishkeit had been authentic precisely because Torah-learning and Shabbat brought five-year prison sentences, while in the West religious life would be cheapened by ease. On motzaei Shabbat they were told to prepare to cross the border that night.
