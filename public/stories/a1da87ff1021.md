# Reports reach Lubavitch of German atrocities at Kalisz

*1914*

Newspapers and private reports brought news of German army conduct in conquered territory, especially in the city of Kalisz where civilians, women and children had been slaughtered. Maharashab marveled at this, suspecting initially that the reports might be war propaganda to inflame hatred, but added a teaching that kings face stricter judgment in the heavenly court because rulers stand higher in matters of providence.
