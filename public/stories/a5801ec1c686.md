# Maskilim inform on Tzemach Tzedek; police search his home

*1841*

A special police officer came to Lubavitch to interrogate the Rebbe and search his home, and the Tzemach Tzedek fell ill from the shock. The incident triggered ongoing secret surveillance by the Russian secret police, which continued until the spring of 5607. The denunciation was part of a broader Haskalah-driven government effort to compel Jewish children into state-run secular education.
