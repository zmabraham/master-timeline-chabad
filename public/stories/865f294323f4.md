# Epidemic in Zhlobin disrupts yeshiva studies in Surazh

*1927*

When R. Moshe Axelrod moved to Surazh and established a yeshiva ketana there, Eliyahu Shusterman had taken his son's entire class — seven boys, all under bar-mitzvah age — and brought them to study and board in Surazh. At the end of summer 5687 (1927) a contagious disease spread through Zhlobin and the boys could not return; they remained in Zhlobin, where Rabbi Binyaminsohn taught them Bava Metzia and the elderly R. Yitzchak Matles taught them Tanach and Hebrew grammar in the evenings.
