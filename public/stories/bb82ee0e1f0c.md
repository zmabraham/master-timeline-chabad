# Rashab from Menton objects to merging Rayatz's books into his library

*1910*

While the Lubavitch books were being arranged in cabinets during the winter of 5670, Rayatz wished to fill out his father's collection from his own private holdings. Rashab, writing from Menton, replies that while he enjoys the completion, he is concerned that books be available in Rayatz's own room for casual learning. The episode marks the formal occasion when Rayatz instructed his bookbinder to emboss his initials 'yud-shin' in gold on the spines of his volumes to distinguish his collection.
