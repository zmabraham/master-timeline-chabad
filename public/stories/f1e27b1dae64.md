# Chassidus distribution campaign launched in Warsaw

*1932*

Under R. Yehuda Eber's direction in 5692, a young avreich named Moshe Chaim was sent into Warsaw shuls and yeshivot to teach Tanya and disseminate Chassidus. He established daily Tanya classes in the Bagne beis midrash (forty students), the Sochaczow Beis Avraham yeshiva (thirty boys for five zlotys a week), and the Franciscan 22 Chabad shul (four Ger chassidim). He also began saying maamarim every Shabbat in the Dzika shul.
