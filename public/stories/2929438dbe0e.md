# Alter Rebbe's Siddur first printed in Shklov

*1803*

The Siddur was the vehicle by which many of the Alter Rebbe's later halachic rulings reached print, often differing from his earlier Shulchan Aruch. The Tzemach Tzedek noted that 'the Siddur was written a long time after the Shulchan Aruch.' Two more editions appeared during the Alter Rebbe's lifetime in Kapust.
