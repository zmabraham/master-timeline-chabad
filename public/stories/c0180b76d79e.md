# Founding meeting for Yeshivat Achei Tmimim Lubavitch Chicago

*1944*

On 28 Tishrei 5705 (15 October 1944) Mr. Pinchas Rismann, Mr. and Mrs. Yechezkel Liesner, Mr. Palmer, Rabbi Rodstein, Rabbi Predmesky and the Rebbe's son-in-law Rashag met with the Rebbe Rayatz about establishing Yeshivat Achei Tmimim in Chicago. The Liesners pledged a $15,000 two-year loan, with Rismann and C. S. Palmer each adding $5,000, all earmarked to purchase a building for the new yeshivah.
