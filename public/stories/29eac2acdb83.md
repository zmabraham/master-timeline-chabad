# Library visitor publishes description in HaOlam London

*1928*

The visitor described a 440-year-old manuscript by R' Avraham Saba (author of Tzror HaMor), Ralbag's 1476 Torah commentary, and a Ramban printed in Lisbon in 1489. The published descriptions help corroborate which volumes were in the collection at that time, including the eight-volume Shemoneh Shearim autograph by R' Shmuel Vital.
