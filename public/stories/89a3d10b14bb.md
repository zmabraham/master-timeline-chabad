# Rabbi Frishman at the Geulah and Moshiach gathering 5784. Rabbi Chaim Alter

*1983*

Rabbi Frishman at the Geulah and Moshiach gathering 5784. Rabbi Chaim Alter Ben Tzion Bentzi Frishman (born on the 25th of Tevet 5743, 1983) is the director of the Chabad educational institutions in the city of Bat Yam, a member of the Association for the True and Complete Redemption, editor of Sichat HaGeulah
