# Babinovitsy peace court rules on Mitteler Rebbe's case

*1826*

After receiving the case in late 1825, the Babinovitsy peace court conducted additional inquiries and reached its decision on June 20, 1826. The case was then forwarded on August 3 to the First Department of the Mohilev Supreme Court for review—the body that would ultimately acquit the Rebbe.
