# Publication of 'At Our Rebbe's Seder Table' in English by Eliyahu Touger

*1995*

In 5755 (1995), Eliyahu Touger published 'At Our Rebbe's Seder Table' in English in New York. The 119-page octavo edition collected the Lubavitcher Rebbe's talks on the Haggadah translated into English. It made the Rebbe's Pesach teachings accessible to English-speaking Chabad followers worldwide.
