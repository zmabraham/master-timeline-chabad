# Birth of MaharI"L of Yanovich, brother of the Alter Rebbe

*1749*

MaharI"L of Yanovich, author of Shearit Yehuda and constant collaborator of his brother the Alter Rebbe, was born around 5509 (~1749). He would become the household manager of the Alter Rebbe's affairs, the chief transcriber of his discourses (later printed in Torah Or), and editor of his Shulchan Aruch.
