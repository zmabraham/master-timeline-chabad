# Passing of the Rebbe Rayatz on Yud Shevat

*1950*

On 10 Shevat 5710 (28 January 1950), the Rebbe Rayatz passed away in Brooklyn. The leadership of Chabad subsequently passed to his son-in-law, Rabbi Menachem Mendel Schneerson, who formally accepted the nesius a year later. Yud Shevat became one of the central dates on the Chabad calendar.
