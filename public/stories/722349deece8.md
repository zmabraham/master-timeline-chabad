# Pavlov currency reform cancels Soviet 50- and 100-ruble notes

*1991*

The Soviet government decreed that all banknotes of 50 rubles and higher were no longer legal tender, with citizens permitted to exchange up to 500 rubles at the bank and lose anything beyond that. The reform — equivalent to roughly two dollars per note at black-market rates — became the dominant topic of conversation across Moscow. The Chabad delegation noted it as part of the chaotic political and economic backdrop to their library campaign.
