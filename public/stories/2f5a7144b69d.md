# Passing of R' Shneur Zalman Gurarie in New York

*2003*

On the first day of Rosh Hashana 5764 (27 September 2003), Shabbat, R' Shneur Zalman Gurarie passed away in New York. He had served as a senior leader within the Chabad movement for decades — heading the Warsaw mechina in his youth, leading Achei Temimim in New York, and later serving on the Vaad HaMesader and the Vaad Hanhalat Agudat Chabad. His life spanned the destruction of pre-war European Chabad and its rebuilding in America.
