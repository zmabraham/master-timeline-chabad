# Rebbe Rayatz writes to RashZ Hablin about Yeshivat Torat Emet

*1921*

Anash in America had inquired about the signatories of the public appeal to rebuild Torat Emet and whether the Rebbe was still its director. The Rebbe Rayatz replied that the signatories were former students of the institution and that RashZ Hablin had been dispatched to Eretz Yisrael to take charge of Torat Emet. The letter clarified institutional authority during the transition.
