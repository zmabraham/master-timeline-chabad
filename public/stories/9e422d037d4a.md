# Chabad mass escape from Soviet Union on Polish passports

*1947*

Among those who escaped were Bentzion Shemtov, the Yakovshvili family (who changed their name to the more Polish-sounding Jacobson), and many activists from the underground network. After surviving the war in Tashkent and elsewhere, they masquerade as Polish refugees. Most were later resettled by the Rebbe to communities around the world.
