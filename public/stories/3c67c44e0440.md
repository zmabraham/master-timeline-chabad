# Vitebsk chassidic prisoners fast nine days through Pesach 5691

*1931*

All the imprisoned Vitebsk chassidim, including the daughter of R' Eliyahu Far who later became the wife of R' Michael Lipsker, refused to eat chametz in their cells during Pesach. For nine days they subsisted on a single piece of sugar daily rather than violate the holiday, even as the rest of the Vitebsk yeshiva infrastructure collapsed around them.
