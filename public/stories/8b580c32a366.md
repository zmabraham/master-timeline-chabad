# Rabbi DovBer travels abroad to Karlsbad for medical treatment

*1825*

In the summer of 1825 (Tammuz-Av 5585), Rabbi DovBer Schneuri traveled outside Russia to Karlsbad to seek treatment at its therapeutic springs. While he was away, Simcha Keysin's denunciations were being delivered to Governor Khovansky in Vitebsk, setting in motion the chain of events that would culminate in the Rebbe's arrest and trial later that year.
