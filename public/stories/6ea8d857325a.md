# Great fire destroys Alter Rebbe's house in Lyadi

*1810*

At the end of winter 5570 (early 1810), a small spark ignited a sudden blaze in the Alter Rebbe's large house in Lyadi while he was on a journey through the Volhynia provinces. Within minutes the entire roof and interior were engulfed, and nothing could be saved — not the costly seforim, household possessions, jewelry, nor pledged valuables that had been kept in the home. Most painful was the loss of the Rebbe's own manuscript glosses on the four sections of Shulchan Aruch.
