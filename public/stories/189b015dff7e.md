# Simchas Beis HaShoeivah, Crown Heights 5781 — singer Eli Marcus

*1987*

Simchas Beis HaShoeivah, Crown Heights 5781. Singer Eli Marcus (right) alongside his uncle Avraham Fried and his cousin Benny Friedman (Crown Heights, 5783). Eli Marcus (born 9 Tammuz 5747, 1987) is a Chabad chassidic singer who lives in Crown Heights.
