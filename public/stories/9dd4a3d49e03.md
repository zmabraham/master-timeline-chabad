# R. Naeh reports formation of rival Lapshitz-Kazarnovsky vaad

*1913*

On 13 Iyar 5673 (May 1913), R. Menachem Mendel Naeh wrote that on Sunday, Gershon Lapshitz and Moshe Kazarnovsky had gathered ten men at the home of R. Shmuel Levin and signed a paper agreeing to form their own vaad. This is the earliest dated documentation of the formal organizational split.
