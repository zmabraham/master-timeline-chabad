# Foreign Ministry meeting with Deputy Minister Petrovsky

*1990*

On 19 Kislev 5751 at 4:30 PM the delegation met with Deputy Foreign Minister Petrovsky and three aides. Petrovsky promised to speak with Gubenko and to seek 'other means' if no positive decision came. By night the case was being routed via Foreign Minister Shevardnadze, who was asked to deliver a Chabad file to Gorbachev.
