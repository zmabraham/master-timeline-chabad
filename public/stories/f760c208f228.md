# Rebbe's 70th birthday marked by greetings from Israeli leaders

*1972*

On 11 Nissan 5732 (25 April 1972), the Rebbe turned seventy, an occasion that drew formal written blessings from across Israeli society. Greeters included President Zalman Shazar, Rabbis Ovadia Yosef, Shlomo Goren and Moshe Zvi Neria, Foreign Minister Abba Eban, Minister Shimon Peres, Religious Affairs Minister Zerach Warhaftig, IDF Chief of Staff David Elazar, Generals Ariel Sharon and Rechavam Zeevi, Jerusalem Mayor Teddy Kollek, future President Ephraim Katzir, and poets Avraham Shlonsky and Uri Tzvi Greenberg. The collected blessings were assembled into 'Sefer HaBrachot LiShnat HaShivim' (The Book of Blessings for the Year of Seventy).
