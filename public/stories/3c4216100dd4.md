# Tomchei Tmimim branches founded in Patzanow, Riga, Kovel, Baranowicze

*1932*

While Patzanow became a stable long-term branch, the branches in Riga, Kovel and Baranowicze each lasted only one year. The 1932 expansion reflected the Frierdiker Rebbe's push to seed Chabad education across Poland, Lithuania and Latvia.
