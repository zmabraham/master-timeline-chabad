# Delegation meets Geleman and Rizov on Schneersohn library

*1991*

On the morning of 8 Iyar 5751 (April 22, 1991), the delegation, together with Mr. Geleman, met with Rizov in Moscow. Rizov related that two months earlier Yegorov had told him, in Gorbachev's name, that Gorbachev could not take responsibility for returning the books and wanted Gubinko and one other to decide. Rizov had proposed a special commission with two representatives each from Jewish communities, the Academy (including Likhachev), and the Ministry of Culture. He suggested approaching Gubinko directly during the parliamentary break that day.
