# Rebbe Rashab issues general letter on Eretz Yisrael donations and shadar Horowitz

*1906*

The Rebbe extolled the founding role of the Alter Rebbe in establishing the mitzvah of tzedakas Eretz Yisrael in Russia, and lamented that in recent years the practice had weakened in many towns. He instructed Anash to welcome Horowitz, install pushkes in homes lacking them, and follow detailed procedures spelled out in an accompanying letter from his son Maharayatz. A second circular soon clarified that the new pushkes were for the kollel as a whole and not specifically for Hebron.
