# 17th wedding anniversary of Maharaitz and Rebbetzin Nechama Dina

*1914*

On 13 Elul 5674, the 17th anniversary of his own wedding, Maharaitz recorded a prayer for length of days, nachas from his three daughters, and a son who would be a yarei shamayim, a chassid, a great scholar, and successful in all his affairs. At the time he had only daughters; the diary entry reflects his deep yearning for a male heir.
