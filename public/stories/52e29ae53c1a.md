# Rebbe gives dollar with blessing for long life on Erev Rosh Chodesh Elul

*1991*

The author marks his birthday on Erev Rosh Chodesh Elul 5751 by receiving dollars from the Rebbe, who blessed him with long and good years. The Rebbe then handed an additional dollar and added a blessing for success 'like the king who read the Torah before all the people,' with good timing and health.
