# R' Shaul Ashkenazi dies in Jerusalem

*1895*

Born in 5583 in Mohilev, R' Shaul made aliyah with his family in 5592. He taught Tanach and Gemara in Jerusalem according to the 5615 census, returned to Hebron in 5626 to join the kollel leadership, served on R' Shimon Menashe's beit din, and eventually returned to Jerusalem where he is buried. His descendants adopted the surname Shaulzon.
