# R. Zalman Levitin arrives in Kutaisi to head the underground yeshiva

*1932*

In mid-winter R. Zalman 'the Haditcher' Levitin joined the Kutaisi group and began guiding them in chassidus, teaching Shoresh Mitzvas HaTefillah, Haamanas Elokus, and chapters of Derech Mitzvosecha before moving them on to Hemshech 5659 with required memorization. He raised funds and ran the yeshiva with a firm hand, drawing additional bochurim including Moshe Rexin, Ze'ev Zalmanov, and Avraham Kievman.
