# Shterna Sara writes to family in Eretz Yisrael

*1896*

On 1 Elul 5656 (September 1, 1896), Rebbetzin Shterna Sara wrote to Rabbi Mordechai Dovber and Rabbi Schneur Zalman Schneersohn in Hebron, explaining that Nechama Dina was chosen for her piety despite the family forgoing significant dowry offers. She also asked them to pray for the family at the holy gravesites before Rosh HaShanah.
