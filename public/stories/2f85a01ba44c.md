# Author begins learning at Great Hall Lubavitch, 5672

*1911*

R. Shilem Koratin was appointed mashgiach for chassidus that winter, with R. Yaakov Baruch Krasik supervising nigleh and R. Shmuel Gronem Esterman serving as mashpia. They learned Derech Mitzvotecha until R. Gronem fell ill with eye trouble and had to travel for extended treatment, after which the students learned on their own from the Rebbe Rashab's maamarim.
