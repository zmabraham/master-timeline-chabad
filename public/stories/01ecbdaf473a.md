# Moscow Chabad Shluchim and Shluchot conferences open

*1992*

On 22 Shevat 5752, Chabad emissaries gathered in Moscow for parallel Shluchim and Shluchot conferences. The author opened the Shluchim session and then crossed to address the Shluchot. The gatherings ran alongside ongoing negotiations to recover the Schneerson library from the Russian State Library.
