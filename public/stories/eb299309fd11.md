# R. Shmuel Segal of Rogatchov listed in Alter Rebbe's charity assessment

*1810*

In the winter of 1810, R. Shmuel Segal was first recorded as 'moreh tzedek of the community of Rogatchov' in a charity assessment signed by the Alter Rebbe. He served as moreh tzedek in Rogatchov for some fifteen years before making aliyah to the Land of Israel in 1824.
