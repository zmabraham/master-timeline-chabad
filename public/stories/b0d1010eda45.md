# Rayatz orders Kollel Chabad to follow Agudat Chabad directives

*1944*

Disputes between Kollel Chabad and Agudat Chabad over jurisdiction—particularly over chassidus shiurim in Jerusalem—had been escalating between the two organizations. The Rayatz resolved the conflict by formally subordinating Kollel Chabad and Yeshivat Tzemach Tzedek's administrators to Agudat Chabad's directives. The ruling consolidated authority over Chabad activity in the Holy Land under the newer Tel Aviv-based body.
