# Gorbachev refers Chabad books case to the Gos-Soviet

*1991*

On the first day of Rosh Hashana Gorbachev called Gubenko to discuss the seforim. Gubenko proposed transferring the matter to the Gos-Soviet — the new umbrella body including Gorbachev, Yeltsin and the heads of the federations — and Gorbachev consented. Yeltsin reportedly took part in the session, and Veronika brought word to the Chabad delegation immediately.
