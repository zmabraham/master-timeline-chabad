# R' Avraham Chaim describes Pesach in Smolensk prison

*1889*

R' Avraham Chaim recounted that the previous Pesach in Smolensk prison, the local Chassid R' Zarchi brought him all his needs for the first day of Pesach and visited with his wife on Yom Tov bringing him beloved foods. Throughout that winter of 5649, he was also able to hire others to perform his mandatory prison labor.
