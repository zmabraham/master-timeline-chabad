# Rebbe Rayatz draws the Menorah of Chassidus

*1920*

During his year of mourning, Rebbe Yosef Yitzchak drew a seven-branched Menorah of Chassidus for himself, each branch dedicated to one of the founders of Chassidus and the Rebbeim of Chabad across the seven generations. Each Rebbe was inscribed with his name, years of leadership, yahrtzeit, and in some cases burial location. The drawing was framed by verses about the seven lamps illuminating Israel and the soul of man standing on four pillars: love, feeling, knowledge, and faith.
