# Refugee Chabad families from Russia resettle in Tel Aviv

*1935*

Between 5695 and 5697, dozens of Chabad families escaped the Soviet Union and arrived in the Land of Israel, most settling in Tel Aviv. They organized themselves and even established a yeshiva. The Rebbe Rayatz founded Agudas Chabad B'Eretz HaKodesh, headquartered in Tel Aviv, alongside the older Kollel Chabad in Jerusalem.
