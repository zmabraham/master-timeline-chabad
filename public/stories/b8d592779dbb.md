# R' Shneur Zalman Schneersohn considers Chicago rabbinate

*1924*

R' Shneur Zalman Schneersohn — son of the Retziter Rebbe and a senior great-grandson of the Alter Rebbe — received papers from Chicago inviting him to serve as rabbi and Chabad leader there, with an apartment and a gabbai already prepared. R' Chaim Eliezer Bichovsky urged him to accept, citing a tradition that chassidus would spread in the lower hemisphere before the coming of Mashiach; his brother R' Chaim Schneersohn of Bremen advised him instead to settle in Eretz Yisrael. The author, consulted on the matter, also recommended America, noting that the wave of emigrants from Russia in those years consisted largely of Torah-observant Jews seeking religious freedom.
