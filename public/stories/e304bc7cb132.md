# First Chassidic Haggadah 'Chelkat Binyamin' by Binyamin of Zalozitz printed in Lvov

*1794*

In 5554 (1794), 'Chelkat Binyamin', the first published Chassidic Haggadah commentary, was issued in Lvov by Rabbi Binyamin, maggid of Zalozitz, a leading disciple of Rabbi Yechiel Michel of Zlotchov. The 25-leaf quarto included a transcription of the Haggadah with the Kavanot of the Arizal. It marked a milestone in the printed dissemination of Chassidic teachings on the Passover liturgy.
