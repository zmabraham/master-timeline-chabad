# Levin prays at the Maharal's grave in Prague

*1946*

On Thursday Parshat Ekev, 18 Menachem Av 5706, the Chabad refugees went into Prague to daven at the Altneuschul. Afterward Levin and his wife Itta Devorah visited the adjacent graves of the Maharal of Prague and his righteous wife to beg for healthy offspring.
