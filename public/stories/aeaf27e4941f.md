# First edition of Alter Rebbe's Hilchos Pesach printed

*1814*

Printed years after the great Liadi fire and after the Alter Rebbe's own passing, this edition preserved the public-facing portions of his rulings on selling chametz. The accompanying kuntres explaining the details of the sale was not included; it had not been printed in the Siddur or the Shulchan Aruch and is presumed lost in the Liadi fire of 5570.
