# Chofetz Chaim passes through Homel en route to Poland

*1923*

When the Soviets permitted Polish nationals residing in Russia to return home, the Chofetz Chaim left the town of Snovsk, where he had been living with his students, and made his way toward Poland. En route he passed through Homel, staying as a guest in the home of R' Shmuel Yitzchak Luria. This was the only time the author met him, describing him as 'a very pious Jew.'
