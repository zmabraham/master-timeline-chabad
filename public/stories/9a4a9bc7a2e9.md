# Volume 14 of Tzemach Tzedek responsa bound

*1833*

Volume 14 contained a published responsum (Even HaEzer §48) explicitly written in 5593, and manuscripts 1050 and 1021 cite it repeatedly with notes such as 'copied here with corrections from chelek 14.' The volume itself, along with most of the volumes numbered 1–14, did not reach us — many believed lost in a fire in Lubavitch.
