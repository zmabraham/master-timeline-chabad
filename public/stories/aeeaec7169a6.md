# Passing of the Alter Rebbe in the village of Piena

*1813*

Forced to flee Liadi during Napoleon's 1812 campaign, the Alter Rebbe was carried from town to town and village to village for several months. His soul departed on 24 Tevet in the village of Piena. His passing set the stage for his son the Mittler Rebbe to relocate the Chabad center to Lubavitch later that year.
