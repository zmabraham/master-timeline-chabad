# Sighet Haggadah with 'Tzemach Hashem L'Tzvi' commentary by Tzvi Hirsh of Nadvorna

*1910*

In 5670 (1910), the Haggadah with the new commentary 'Tzemach Hashem L'Tzvi' by Tzvi Hirsh of Nadvorna was published in Sighet by Avraham Kaufman and Sons. The 39-leaf duodecimo edition included a 'Divrei Shalom' pamphlet by the author's father, Shalom Zelig. It served the Galician Nadvorna Chassidic community.
