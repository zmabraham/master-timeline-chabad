# MaHaRiL writes to R' Aaron of Belinitz seeking him as judge

*1855*

While his father the Tzemach Tzedek was still alive, the future MaHaRiL of Kopust faced a major din Torah and needed to choose an arbitrator. On the night of 15 Av תרט"ו he wrote to R' Aharon Lipshitz, moreh tzedek of Belinitz, declaring his settled intent to choose him. The letter is one of four surviving epistles documenting the affair, and represents the start of a months-long negotiation over the choice of arbitrator.
