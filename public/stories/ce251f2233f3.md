# Chabad chassidim in Vilna purchase the Apatov synagogue

*1909*

The purchase gave Vilna's Chabad community a permanent base and is documented in a letter of Rebbe Rayatz (Igrot Kodesh vol. 1, letter 25). The same building would later host the Vilna branch of Tomchei Tmimim. Its acquisition came just months before a major rabbinical conference convened in the city.
