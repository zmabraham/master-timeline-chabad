# First Library exhibition closes

*1994*

The first exhibition of the Lubavitch Library, which opened on Chai Elul 5754, closed on Erev Rosh Chodesh Kislev 5755 (early November 1994). It had displayed items linked to each of the Chabad Rebbes and to every sphere of Chabad activity. The exhibit established what would become a regular practice of mounting themed exhibitions every year or two.
