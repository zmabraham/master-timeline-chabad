# Rayatz announces R' Ashurov's appointment as Rosh Yeshiva in Warsaw

*1930*

On 19 Cheshvan 5691, in a general letter, the Rebbe Rayatz announced that he was sending R' Avraham Eliyahu Ashurov, described as a great Torah scholar of exceptional talent, to serve as Rosh Yeshiva in one of the divisions of Tomchei Tmimim in Warsaw. However, this plan was apparently never carried out, as Ashurov remained in Riga.
