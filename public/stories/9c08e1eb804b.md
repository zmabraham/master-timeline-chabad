# Simcha Kisin files denunciation against the Mitteler Rebbe

*1825*

In July 1825, the Jew Simcha Kisin of Vitebsk lodged a complaint with Governor-General Khovansky alleging that the Chasidim—whom he called 'Karliners'—were oppressing 'authentic Jews of the old faith,' and that their rebbe, Berke Shneur, was amassing wealth through dedicated emissaries. As evidence, Kisin produced an original Hebrew document of fund distribution along with a Russian translation and two letters from the Rebbe—the materials that launched the long investigation.
