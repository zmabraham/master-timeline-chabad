# R. Moshe Pinchas Katz passes away

*1986*

R. Moshe Pinchas Katz passed away in New York on Erev Rosh Chodesh Adar Sheni 5746 (March 11, 1986). He had served as a gabbai of the 770 synagogue and a member of the executive of Agudas Chassidei Chabad, Vaad HaMesader, Vaad LeHafatzas Chassidus, Vaad HaMaamad, and Vaad Kupas Bachurim.
