# Distribution of clothes at Moscow Chabad boys' summer camp

*1991*

Clothing shipments meant for the camp were stuck in Soviet customs for a month and only cleared the previous Wednesday. On Friday the author drove four large crates to the boys' camp, addressed the children on the laws of erev Shabbos, and distributed the garments together with his wife. The talk framed the gift as a reason to thank the Rebbe and his shluchim by committing to Shabbos, kashrus and Torah observance.
