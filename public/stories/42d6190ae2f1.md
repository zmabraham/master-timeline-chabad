# RMM of Vitebsk writes first letter from Eretz Yisrael

*1778*

In the winter of 5538 (1777-1778), R. Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk composed his first letter from the Holy Land. He described the safe arrival of the caravan in Tzfat despite the tragic shipwreck, marveled at the survival of about 30 souls who later joined him in Eretz Yisrael, and asked his disciples in Russia to pray for the new community.
