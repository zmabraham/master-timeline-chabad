# R. Yom Tov Crispo begins Jerusalem mission to North Africa

*1718*

R. Yom Tov Crispo was sent in 1718 on a Jerusalem emissary mission to North Africa, beginning with the towns of Morocco. The mission continued for four years until 1722, during which he traveled through Algeria, Tunisia, and Egypt. In Fez he was assisted by R. Yaakov ibn Tzur in raising support both locally and in surrounding towns.
