# Alter Rebbe's second arrest as collection catalyst

*1801*

The Rebbe Rayatz recorded that when the Alter Rebbe was taken to Petersburg in 5561, the future Tzemach Tzedek began assembling transcripts of his grandfather's teachings. By the time the Alter Rebbe returned in the summer of that year, the Tzemach Tzedek had over 1,100 bogen of transcripts. By the Alter Rebbe's passing in 5573, the collection exceeded 10,000 bogen — over a hundred large volumes — forming the foundation of the Chabad manuscript library.
