# Rayatz appointed director of Yeshivas Tomchei Temimim

*1898*

A group of Chasidim led by Rabbi Asher Grossman approached the Rayatz to take the role, citing his wisdom, motivation, and energy. After he demurred, they appealed to the Rashab, who endorsed the idea. The Rayatz immediately faced complex tasks of fundraising, hiring staff, and managing the yeshivah's growth.
