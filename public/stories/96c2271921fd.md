# R. Avraham Lisson flees Ponevezh as Germany invades

*1941*

The Frierdiker Rebbe had obtained American visas for R. Shmuel Tzvi Lisson at the end of 5700, but they did not reach him in time. When the Germans invaded the Soviet Union, the son escaped while his father and household perished in the Shoah, may Hashem avenge their blood.
