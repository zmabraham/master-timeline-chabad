# Reb Zalman Slonim testifies about the Mitteler Rebbe's maamad funds

*1825*

On 5 October 1825, Reb Zalman Slonim of Shklov, a 53-year-old chasid who had been appointed by the Mitteler Rebbe (together with Pinchas Shick and Shlomo Freidin) to oversee distribution of maamad funds, gave sworn testimony. He recounted how in spring 1813 the rebbe arrived in Shklov with his family, gathered prominent chasidim, and described his impoverishment after the French looting and burning of his Liadi properties. Slonim confirmed that contributions were freely given by chasidim according to ability, that the 35,000-ruble distribution document was indeed in the rebbe's handwriting, and that another rebbe, Aharon of Starosselye, also received voluntary support nearby.
