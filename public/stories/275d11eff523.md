# 19 Kislev farbrengen 5669 closed to younger students

*1908*

The Tomchei Tmimim administration barred cheder-level students from attending the 19 Kislev 5669 farbrengen with the Rebbe Rashab in the large zal. The Rebbe spoke with great bitterness against the founding of Russian-language 'shkoles' for Jewish children, condemning the initiative's patron Baron Horace Ginzburg, who had refused his plea to spare at least Lubavitch. The author later heard that the baron died around Purim that winter.
