# Rabbi Tal Dekel Rabbi Tal Dekel 5715, 1955 - 11 Sivan 5785

*2025*

Rabbi Tal Dekel Rabbi Tal Dekel 5715, 1955 - 11 Sivan 5785, June 8, 2025) was active in the Rebbe's mivtzaim in Haifa, Yokneam Illit, and Migdal HaEmek, and in his later days a shliach of the Rebbe at the Goldenkeier Dushi geriatric rehabilitation hospital in Nazareth. Biography
