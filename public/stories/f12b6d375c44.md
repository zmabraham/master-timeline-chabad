# R. Yisrael Eber proposes new Otwock branch on 12 Sivan 5693

*1933*

Eber wrote to the Frierdiker Rebbe describing a local Otwock yeshiva of about 35 students, previously funded by the late gvir R. Shlomo Goldberg, whose heirs would only give 400 zlotys monthly per his will. The local teachers proposed merging with Tomchei Tmimim provided the name retained a reference to Goldberg, but the plan ultimately did not materialize.
