# Tomchei Tmimim central yeshiva operates in Kharkov

*1924*

Following the dispersion of Tomchei Tmimim under Soviet pressure, the central yeshiva relocated to Kharkov by 5684. R' Yechezkel Feigin, who had earlier been called from Homel to lead the institution in Poltava, now served as mashgiach and manager of the Kharkov yeshiva, dispatching emissaries such as R' Shneur Gorelik and R' Yisrael Matmid to fundraise across the region.
