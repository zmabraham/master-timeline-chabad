# Rebbe Rayatz arrives in the United States

*1941*

From his arrival at the end of Sivan 5701 until his histalkus in the winter of 5710, Rebbe Rayatz used the second-floor room at 770 as his yechidus chamber, where he also delivered maamarim. After his passing the same room became the Rebbe's and Machneh Israel's office until the office layout was reorganized.
