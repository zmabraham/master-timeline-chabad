# Veronika summoned by KGB at Gorbachev's office

*1990*

On 17 Kislev 5751 Veronika was called in by the KGB section attached to Gorbachev's office and questioned about the Chabad mission and its members. She left the encounter satisfied and rejoined the delegation at the Lenin Library, where they were meeting with director Grigorov and manuscripts librarian Medvedev about the Schneersohn collection.
