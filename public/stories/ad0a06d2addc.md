# Rabbi Aryeh Leib of Volochisk carries Berditchever pidyon funds to Eretz Yisrael

*1805*

Rabbi Levi Yitzchak of Berditchev maintained a fund for his Chassidim in Eretz Yisrael, parallel to the Alter Rebbe's similar effort. In summer 5565, he sent that year's contribution with Rabbi Aryeh Leib of Volochisk, who was making his own journey to the land. The Chozeh of Lublin referenced the transfer in a letter to Rabbi Levi Yitzchak from that same season.
