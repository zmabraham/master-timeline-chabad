# Rosenbaum flees Russia after warning of renewed arrest

*1891*

On one of the days of Tishrei 5652-3, Reb Avraham Chaim was secretly told that fresh proceedings were being readied against him and that he must flee abroad. On Simchat Torah he hosted a great gathering for all the local chassidim, farbrenging joyously all day, and at nightfall vanished from town. Opponents who heard of his flight gave chase but failed to overtake him.
