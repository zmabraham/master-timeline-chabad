# R. Yerachmiel Binyaminson niftar on Rosh Chodesh Shevat 5715

*1955*

Binyaminson, a venerable Chabad rabbi who had previously brought a minyan to plead at the Rebbe Rayatz's kever for the Ramash to accept the nesius, came regularly from Montreal to New York and lodged with the narrator or with his sister. His passing on Rosh Chodesh Shevat 5715 was honored with burial adjacent to the Frierdiker Rebbe at the Old Montefiore section that would later become known as the Ohel.
