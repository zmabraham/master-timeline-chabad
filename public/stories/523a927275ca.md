# Rebbe visits Glubok and witnesses miraculous healing of Ida Haberman

*1934*

The local Yiddish paper Gluboker Lebn claimed the reception surpassed those given to the presidents of Czechoslovakia and Austria. Ida's father, not a Chasid, brought her to the train station fearing she was going blind. She regained her sight; later hidden during the Holocaust, she immigrated to the US. During the visit the Rebbe also navigated a local conflict over a rabbinic appointment.
