# Passing of Rebbe Rashab in Rostov

*1920*

Displaced by the war and unable to return to Lubavitch, the Rashab eventually settled in Rostov, where he passed away in 1920. His son and successor, the Rebbe Rayatz, would lead Chabad through Soviet repression. The Rashab's resting place in Rostov became an important pilgrimage site.
