# Rebbetzin Menucha Rochel immigrates to Hebron

*1845*

In 1845, Rebbetzin Menucha Rochel, daughter of the second Chabad Rebbe Rabbi Dovber, immigrated to Hebron. Her descendants maintained a close connection with the Rebbes in Lubavitch, traveling there regularly and anchoring the Chabad presence in Hebron for generations.
