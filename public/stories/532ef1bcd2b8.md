# Author's brother begins studying at Rabbi Morgenstern's yeshiva in Kiev

*1926*

The yeshiva was headed by Rabbi Morgenstern under the oversight of Kiev's chief rabbi, Rav Chaim Matasov, and admitted only older bachurim. Yosef boarded with R' Shlomo Telingator in Podol, a Boyan chassid and old friend of the author's father. Soon a Komsomol infiltrator with a fake beard arrived at the (Litvish) yeshiva to corrupt the students.
