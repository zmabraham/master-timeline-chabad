# Stas Vatulin visits the Rebbe in New York

*1991*

Y. Wilhelm picked Stas up around noon, showed him manuscripts and books from the Rebbe Rashab, the broadcasting room, and the synagogue, then brought him to the Rebbe. The Rebbe, told that Stas was working on the books issue, urged that the return happen 'as quickly as possible' and gave him an additional dollar for his mother who was also helping. Stas later prepared a five-page Russian memorandum for the Rebbe summarizing the situation.
