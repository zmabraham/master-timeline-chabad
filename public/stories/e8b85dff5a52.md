# R' Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk migrates from Safed to Tiberias

*1781*

Unable in 5540 to pay the annual fee demanded by the Sephardic pakid for Safed's communal needs, R' Menachem Mendel suffered repeated threats including imprisonment in iron chains. Though relief funds arrived through the emissary R' Shlomo Zalman HaKohen, the persistent torment caused him to fall ill for months and ultimately drove him to relocate to Tiberias. He found refuge in the house formerly occupied by R' Shlomo of Lvov, who had recently fled abroad.
