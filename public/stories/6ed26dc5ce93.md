# Rebbe Rashab letter on book duplicates and library organization

*1914*

In Tevet 5674 (January 1914), the Rebbe Rashab wrote multiple letters to his son the Rebbe Rayatz about library matters. He addressed which duplicate books could be sold to R' Meir Lubak on behalf of R' M.G., emphasizing that books inherited from his father, the Rebbe Maharash, should not be sold. He requested a list of duplicates with prices be sent to the buyer.
