# Lawyer Ibsen completes memorandum on rabbinic status

*1911*

The Rebbe Rashab had commissioned the Petersburg lawyer Ibsen to prepare a separate memorandum (doklad), apparently concerning rabbinic status, for submission to the Religious Affairs Department. The work dragged on until late Iyar 5671. Throughout the winter, the Rebbe repeatedly pressed Ibsen to finish, working through his son, Rabbi Shmuel Gourary, and Rabbi Menachem Mendel Chen. After completion, additional corrections were needed before submission to Chanan, head of the Religious Department.
