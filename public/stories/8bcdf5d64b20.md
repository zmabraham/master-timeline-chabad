# Rabbi Moshe Feinstein immigrates to United States

*1937*

Like dozens of other rabbis, Reb Moshe had stayed in Russia trying to preserve a core of observant Jews despite Yevsektzia oppression. He later became dean of Mesivta Tifereth Jerusalem and one of the greatest Jewish legal scholars of the twentieth century. He recalled discovering a family connection to Chabad — his great-grandmother Rochel had been a classmate of the Tzemach Tzedek in the Alter Rebbe's cheder.
