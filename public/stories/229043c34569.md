# Rebbe Rayatz departs Eretz Yisrael for the United States

*1929*

Fifteen busloads of Chasidim escorted the Rebbe from his Tel Aviv hotel to the Lod train station. 'The Rebbe was very emotional,' wrote Rabbi Shimon Glitzenstein, 'and gave his blessing to each and every person.' The Rebbe distributed a printed maamar so he could depart by sharing words of Torah.
