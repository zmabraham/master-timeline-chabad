# R' Shneur Zalman Gurarie is born in Kremenchug

*1911*

On the first night of Chanukah 5672 (approximately 16 December 1911) R' Shneur Zalman Gurarie was born in Kremenchug. He was the son of R' Natan Gurarie and the younger brother of R' Yechiel Tzvi. His early years coincided with the upheavals of revolution and civil war in Russia.
