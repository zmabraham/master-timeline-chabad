# R' Yehuda Eber tests Vitebsk Tomchei Tmimim students in Elul 5688

*1928*

During Elul review the rosh yeshiva R' Yehuda Eber came to Vitebsk to test the bochurim, with R' Avraham Mayorer and the mashgiach deciding in advance how to combine them into examination groups. The author was tested alongside Avrahmaleh Kvash, Meir Zarchi, Yosef Sushtarvitz, and Shimon Shtilerman, the youngest and most capable bochurim, and the group was earmarked to form a new advanced class for the coming winter.
