# R' Gershon Chen temporarily appointed mashgiach

*1906*

At the start of 5667 (autumn 1906), when the mashgiach R' Zev Wolf Lewitin fell ill, the student R' Gershon Chen was temporarily appointed in his place. A letter from the Rebbe Rayatz dated 5 Marcheshvan 5667 announced that Chen would supervise order in the Great Hall. When Lewitin recovered, he resumed his post.
