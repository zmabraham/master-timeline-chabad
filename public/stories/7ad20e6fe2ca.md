# Rebbe Rayatz writes 11+ letters from Riga in single day

*1927*

Working alone without secretaries, the Rebbe Rayatz wrote letters to close associates inside Russia and abroad, announcing his arrival, asking about their welfare, and requesting greetings be passed to various figures. Within the first month from Russia, he wrote at least 70 letters, mostly to heads of the Chasidic community in Russia, encouraging them in their difficult situation and directing the continuation of religious activity.
