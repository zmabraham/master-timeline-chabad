# Donation pledges to R. Aharon of Staroshele's Maamad fund

*1819*

On 12 June 1819 (Tammuz), Nissan Shlomovitch of Chertkov signed a pledge to give 50 silver rubles annually to Rabbi Aharon Halevi of Staroshele, the founder of the breakaway Strashelye branch of Chabad. He also contributed 25 rubles cash at that time. The records of these donations represent a fragment of Maamad-style fundraising for the parallel chassidic court that arose after the passing of the Alter Rebbe.
