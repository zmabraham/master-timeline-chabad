# Hungarian translation Haggadah printed in Vienna

*1886*

Around 5646 (1886), a Haggadah was issued in Vienna with a Hungarian translation displayed page-by-page opposite the Hebrew original. The volume included illustrations, with the laws printed in Yiddish using Tzene-Rene script and in Ladino in square letters. The 31-page edition catered to the multilingual Jewish communities of the Austro-Hungarian Empire.
