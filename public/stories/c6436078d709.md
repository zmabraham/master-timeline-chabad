# Disputes over Alter Rebbe's chassidic approach begin; his system called 'Chabad'

*1798*

Until 5558 the Alter Rebbe served as the sole acknowledged leader of the general chassidic community in Russia and Lithuania. That year, disputes emerged regarding his particular path in chassidus. Over the course of the controversy between 5558 and 5562, his approach became identified by the name 'Chabad'.
