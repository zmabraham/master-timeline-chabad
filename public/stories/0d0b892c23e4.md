# The Rebbe did not use the mikveh in recent years. He was accustomed to giving the Rebbe

*1977*

The Rebbe did not use the mikveh in recent years. He was accustomed to giving the Rebbe a special gift for his birthday on 11 Nissan, in addition to a donation in the amount of the Rebbe's years multiplied by a thousand dollars. In the year 5737 (1977), at the 11 Nissan farbrengen, he presented the Rebbe with a compilation of all the hanachos
