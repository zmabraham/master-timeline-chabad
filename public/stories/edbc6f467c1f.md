# Soviet decrees against religion intensify in the Homel region

*1922*

Following Passover, the Bolshevik regime escalated its persecution of religious institutions, issuing decrees aimed at closing yeshivas and chadarim. The chassidim, not yet familiar with Soviet tactics, were caught off guard as the authorities convened public meetings to attack religion and recruited informers — including former servants of rabbis — to act against the community.
