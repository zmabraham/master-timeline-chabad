# R' Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk's aliyah to Eretz Yisrael

*1777*

In 5537 (1777), R' Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk and R' Avraham of Kalisk led a group of Hasidim from White Russia and Lithuania to settle in Eretz Yisrael. The main flow of financial support came from those regions, though some assistance also arrived from Volhynia and Poland. This aliyah marked the foundation of the Hasidic Yishuv whose support eventually became the responsibility of the Alter Rebbe.
