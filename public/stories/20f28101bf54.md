# Passing of R' Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk

*1788*

On Rosh Chodesh Iyar 5548, the chassidic leader R' Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk passed away in Tiberias. His passing prompted some Russian chassidim to consider reducing or stopping their financial support of the chassidic settlement in Eretz Yisrael. Leadership of the Chabad chassidim in Tiberias transferred to R' Avraham of Kalisk.
