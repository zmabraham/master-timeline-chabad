# Chassidim in Russia begin sending photos to Rebbe Rayatz

*1933*

In the summer of 5693 (1933), several years after the Rebbe Rayatz was forced to leave Russia and settled in Riga, a custom arose among anash in Russia of sending personal photographs to the Rebbe. Persecution of Chabad in Russia was intensifying, and the Rebbe encouraged the practice in a general letter (Igros Kodesh II, no. 633), writing that seeing the families' faces brought him great joy.
