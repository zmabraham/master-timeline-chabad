# Wedding of Chaya Mushka Hornstein in Lubavitch

*1892*

This was among the major family celebrations held in the great hall the Maharash had erected in 5632. The large hall, with its high ceiling and wide windows, served as the venue for Chabad's most significant communal occasions when large crowds gathered in Lubavitch. The 5652 wedding was followed a year later by the bar mitzvah of the Frierdiker Rebbe in the same hall.
