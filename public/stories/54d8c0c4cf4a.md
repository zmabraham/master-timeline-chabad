# Rabbi Yosef Goldstein opens yeshivah in Chmielnik

*1931*

Goldstein, twenty-four, was startled when the Rebbe placed a hand on his shoulder and assigned him the mission. With friends including Rabbi Mottel Bryski, he founded a yeshivah that grew to over seventy students and spawned an elementary program. Chaim Meir Bukiet enrolled there at age eleven, transferring later to Warsaw and Otwock.
