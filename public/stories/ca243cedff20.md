# R' Shlomo of Lvov, author of Mercavat HaMishneh, dies in Salonika

*1781*

R' Shlomo had settled in 5539 in Tiberias's finest house on the strength of a letter from Istanbul recommending him for honor and a worthy dwelling. He was eventually expelled, wandering through Sha'farem, Acre, and other locations, suffering bodily and financial harm at each stop. He finally fled abroad to Salonika where he died, his vacated Tiberias house becoming the residence of R' Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk.
