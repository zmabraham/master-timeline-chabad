# R' Mordechai Dubin secures Rebbe Rayatz's exit visa via Latvian parliament

*1927*

Newly independent Latvia operated as a democratic parliament whose vote on Soviet recognition was finely split. R' Mordechai Dubin, an Agudas Yisroel delegate with influence over several non-Jewish parliamentarians, traveled to Russia and promised that if the Rebbe were permitted to leave, he would help deliver Latvian recognition. After the Rebbe insisted at the Ohel in Rostov that he would not leave without his ksavim and seforim, Dubin returned and secured that concession as well.
