# R. Chaim Avraham Bichowsky begins serving Kollel Chabad

*1891*

Bichowsky wrote that when he entered the kollel's service in 5651, the ba'al ha'address was already R. Moshe Wienberg in Jerusalem. After Wienberg's death the post passed jointly to R. Manus Werevoleanski and R. Mordechai Schlank, and when Manus died, R. DovBer Efrat was appointed alongside Schlank. His chronology established that Jerusalem had already been the kollel's central address before 5651.
