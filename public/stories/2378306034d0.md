# Rabbi Frishman. Rabbi Frishman passing before the Rebbe at the conclusion of a yechidus

*1958*

Rabbi Frishman. Rabbi Frishman passing before the Rebbe at the conclusion of a general yechidus. Rabbi Yisrael Yitzchak Frishman, 19 Adar 5718 – 9 Shevat 5776; March 1, 1958 – January 17, 2016) was a member of the directorate of Tzeirei Agudas Chabad in Jerusalem and one of the prominent activists in the community
