# Rebbe travels to Warsaw and on to Marienbad

*1933*

Doctors insisted the ailing Rebbe travel to the Marienbad spa. With great difficulty Lubavitch chassidim raised the funds for the trip. R. Yechezkel Feigin wrote that the Rebbe left Riga on 10 Tammuz, visited the yeshiva in Warsaw on 12 Tammuz, and arrived in Marienbad on 13 Tammuz to begin medical care during the deepest months of the economic depression.
