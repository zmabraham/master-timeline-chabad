# Wedding of Chaim Kavash officiated by the Rebbe Rashab in Rostov

*1918*

The Rebbe Rashab stood at the entrance of the Shaalash while the chuppah was held in the courtyard. For the bracha of 'Asher Bara' he honored a religious German army officer who was lodging in the Rebbe's home. Witnesses noted that during the blessings the Rebbe held his hands raised, fingers spread above his eyebrows.
