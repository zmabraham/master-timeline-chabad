# Rebbe Rayatz arrives in Riga after escaping Warsaw

*1939*

Following his dramatic rescue from Warsaw, the Rebbe Rayatz reached Riga on 5 Tevet 5700. From there he immediately began encouraging the Tomchei Tmimim students who had also fled to Vilna, urging them to maintain their studies. His arrival in Riga marked the start of a transitional period before his eventual emigration to the United States.
