# First Chabad boys' summer camp Gan Yisroel founded

*1956*

In summer 5716 (1956) the Rebbe founded the first boys' summer camp and named it Gan Yisroel. The Rebbe personally visited the camps from 5716 through 5720 (1956-1960), and at a Shavuos 5716 talk explained the unique educational influence camps exert on children.
