# Rabbi Avraham Eliyahu Axelrod emigrates to Baltimore

*1924*

In 5684 Rabbi Avraham Eliyahu Axelrod, a noted Tomchei Temimim alumnus who had been living in Zembin since 5681, emigrated from Soviet Russia to America and settled in Baltimore. He would serve as a rabbi there for nearly three decades. His departure was later praised by Rebbe Rayatz's secretary Marozov as an escape from the spiritual darkness of Soviet life.
