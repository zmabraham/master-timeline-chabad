# Simchat Torah farbrengen with Riga Tmimim

*1925*

The gathering included R. Mordechai Chefetz Hy"d, his brother R. Michoel Leib Hy"d, R. Tzvi Gor (son-in-law of the chazan R. M.M. Volosov), R. Yaakov Horowitz of Liepaja Hy"d, and R. Mordechai Dubin. The Riga Tmimim had also received the Rebbe Rayatz's Rosh Hashanah 5686 maamarim, which Jacobson learned with the local chavurah during his five-month stay.
