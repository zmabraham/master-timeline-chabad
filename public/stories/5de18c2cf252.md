# Simchat Torah 1930 — Rebbe's stern address foils Peretz disruption

*1930*

On this Simchat Torah, the Rebbe also founded Agudas Chassidei Chabad of Poland, Lithuania, and Latvia, criticized Latvian Jewry as 'Jewish only in appearance,' and called on Chasidim to 'stand on the street corners and pull Jews into a synagogue to study' — the seed of public outreach that would later define Chabad.
