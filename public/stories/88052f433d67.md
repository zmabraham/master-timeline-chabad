# Marriage of R. Yehuda ben Oved ibn Attar, grandson of Rabbi Yehuda

*1755*

On Wednesday, 24 Cheshvan 5516 (~November 8, 1755), Yehuda ben Oved ibn Attar was married. He was a grandson and namesake of the renowned Rabbi Yehuda ibn Attar of Fez, who had died over two decades earlier. The marriage was recorded in the Ya'abetz's register, which referred to his late grandfather as 'the great rabbi, the wondrous of the Sanhedrin, elder of judgments.'
