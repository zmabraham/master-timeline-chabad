# Albany Haggadah 'Kuntres Marbeh L'Saper' on personalities in the Haggadah

*1996*

In 5756 (1996), Yisrael Rubin, the Chabad shaliach in Albany, New York, published 'Kuntres Marbeh L'Saper' (The Albany Haggadah), containing notes and studies on the personalities mentioned in the Haggadah. The 110-plus-10-page octavo edition exemplified the personalized scholarly Haggadah pamphlets produced by Chabad emissaries. It served the Albany Chabad community.
