# Yeltsin aide Shakhin orders Lenin Library to release Schneerson books

*1992*

On 24 Shevat, lawyer Veronika reported that Philipov had received an unambiguous order to return the books, with deputy PM Burbulis warning Shakhin that the dispute could derail Yeltsin's upcoming US trip. Officers were posted at the manuscripts department, which was sealed pending return of the Schneersohn manuscripts. The arrangement, however, kept the books at Chabad's Moscow shul rather than allowing transfer to the Rebbe in New York.
