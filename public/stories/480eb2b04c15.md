# Frierdiker Rebbe leads Rosh Hashana prayers at Anshei Lubavitch shul, Brownsville

*1929*

Because the Rebbe's Crown Heights apartment was too small for the anticipated crowd, anash chose the large Anshei Lubavitch shul in Brownsville for his Yamim Noraim prayers. Rosh Hashana night prayers stretched many hours with weeping that listeners would never forget. The Rebbe stayed in the home of Dr. Akin on Stone Avenue, secured through community askan Aaron Siminovsky.
