# Kollel Chabad signs agreement with Tzemach Tzedek yeshiva managers

*1922*

On 25 Sivan an agreement clarified that the gathering of scholars studying Chassidus daily at Beit Menachem—founded by the kollel in 5680—would remain the kollel's institution and property. It would continue to be called Yeshivat Tzemach Tzedek as printed in the yearly calendar. All internal and external affairs would be conducted under the kollel managers' directives.
