# Rebbe Rayatz arrives in the United States for the second time

*1940*

Having been extricated from Nazi-occupied Warsaw, the Rebbe Rayatz landed in America on 9 Adar Sheni 5700, beginning the era in which Lubavitch's center moved permanently to the United States. A small remaining stock of the 1928 Likkutei Torah reprint was still available on his arrival. Unlike his 1929 visit, this arrival marked permanent settlement and the relocation of the movement's leadership.
