# Plague strikes Safed, Tiberias and surrounding regions

*1786*

The plague first ravaged Safed and surrounding areas, sending many Chassidim fleeing to Tiberias where R' Menachem Mendel arranged their needs. When the outbreak finally reached Tiberias from Purim through several days into Iyar, R' Menachem Mendel locked himself with over a minyan of followers inside his new courtyard rather than fleeing the city. Other Tiberias residents followed his example, sealing themselves into their homes; the surrounding villages including Pekiin were spared entirely.
