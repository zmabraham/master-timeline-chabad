# Narrator returns to Lubavitch from Kremenchug around Rosh Chodesh Iyar

*1918*

Around Rosh Chodesh Iyar 5678 the narrator left Kremenchug for Lubavitch with 10,000 rubles hidden in his stockings. At Orsha he had to walk roughly three miles between the German and Russian customs posts in the dark, assisting an elderly Jewish woman who could not keep pace. He arrived in Lubavitch via Krasna in the morning.
