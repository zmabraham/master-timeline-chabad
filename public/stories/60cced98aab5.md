# Frierdiker Rebbe writes from Stockholm before sailing

*1940*

Awaiting his transatlantic crossing, the Rebbe Rayatz used Russian to evade wartime censors. He confirmed that $1,000 had been allocated for travel preparations and asked whether Mushka and Mendel could purchase tickets themselves or required them to be forwarded by Jacobson. Coded references and intermediaries became central to the rescue correspondence.
