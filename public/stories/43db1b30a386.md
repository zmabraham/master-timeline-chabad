# Montefiore 1875 census lists the three Brimat brothers in Hebron

*1875*

By 1875 the patriarch R. Yaakov Mordechai had already passed, and only his three Hebron-resident sons were enumerated, each under the BeRiMaT (ben R. Mordechai Twer) surname they had adopted. The census documents the consolidation of the family identity in the Old Yishuv.
