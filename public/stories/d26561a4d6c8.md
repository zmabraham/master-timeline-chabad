# Passing of MaHaRiL of Kopust

*1866*

The MaHaRiL of Kopust, eldest son of the Tzemach Tzedek and founder of the Kopust line, passed away on 3 Mar Cheshvan תרכ"ז, about two and a half months after his last letter to the Rashbatz concerning the Likkutei Torah second edition. He had served as Rebbe briefly following his father's passing in Nissan of that year.
