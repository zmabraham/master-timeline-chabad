# Arrest of R' Avraham Sverdlov in Leningrad

*1938*

Sverdlov had been one of the directors of the underground Tomchei Tmimim branch in Odessa, an unusually broad talent — a master of nigleh and chassidus, a brilliant darshan, a first-rate ba'al koreh. He was arrested in Leningrad in 1938; no trace of him was found thereafter, Hashem yikom damo.
