# Publication of Sefer HaToldot Admur Maharash with maamar registry

*1947*

The Rebbe Rayatz expanded the registry methodology he had developed earlier for his father's maamarim, applying it to the maamarim of his grandfather, the Rebbe Maharash. The work was printed as a booklet in 1947 as part of Sefer HaToldot Admur Maharash. It would later be supplemented in 1981 with more than two hundred additional maamarim discovered in manuscript.
