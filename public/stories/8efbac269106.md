# Mondshine searches Touro library for missing Maharash letter

*1988*

Researcher R. Yehoshua Mondshine traced Leikin's library to Touro College in New York and visited there in 5748 looking for the lost manuscript pages, including the Maharash letter to R. Chaim Yaakov Widrewitz. After being passed between librarians, clerks, and managers, he left empty-handed. The search continued unresolved for over a decade more.
