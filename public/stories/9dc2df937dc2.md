# Rabbi Dovid Meir Rabinowitz immigrates to United States

*1896*

Rabinowitz pioneered traditional Judaism and Jewish education in Boston. In 1927, his daughter received a two-word telegram from the Rebbe's family — 'Tatte arestirt' — and he dispatched his sons Asher and Peretz to Washington, where they enlisted Justice Louis Brandeis to lobby on the Rebbe's behalf.
