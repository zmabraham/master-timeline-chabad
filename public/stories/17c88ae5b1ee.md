# Tzemach Tzedek still refusing to accept pidyonot

*1829*

On 8 Tishrei 5590 (October 1829) R. Nathan Halevi again signed a responsum to Tzemach Tzedek with his mother's name. Tzemach Tzedek struck the word 'Chayena' from the autograph, refusing to receive the letter as a pidyon-nefesh. A few weeks later he again deleted the honorifics 'light of our eyes' and 'his servant' from another of Nathan's letters, evidence that he had not yet fully assumed the role of Rebbe.
