# Rebbe Maharash begins nesius in Lubavitch

*1866*

After the passing of the Tzemach Tzedek in 5626 (1866), his son Rabbi Shmuel became the new Rebbe of Chabad. He continued to reside and lead from Lubavitch until his own passing in 5643 (1882). During this period R. Avraham Zeligsohn served as the town's moreh tzedek.
