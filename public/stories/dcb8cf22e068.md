# Rashag returns to New York from Eretz Yisrael via Paris

*1949*

On 7 Tammuz 5709 at 5pm, Rebbe Rayatz's son-in-law Rabbi Shmaryahu Gurary (Rashag) arrived by airplane from Paris on his return from Eretz Yisrael. He extolled the spiritual state of anash in general and the refugees in particular, expressing astonishment at their devotion to Chassidus and to avodas hatefillah.
