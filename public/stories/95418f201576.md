# Rebbe announces 100-year hilula initiatives

*1965*

At the farbrengen of Rosh Chodesh Elul 5725, the Rebbe announced that, in connection with the centennial of the Tzemach Tzedek's hilula, every chassid should contribute 100 coins toward printing his works. He reiterated the call at the Shabbat Nitzavim (28 Elul) farbrengen and added a directive to study the first three chapters of Tanya on Erev Rosh Hashanah, the Tzemach Tzedek's birthday.
