# Rebbe Rayatz blesses R' Alter Simchowitz with a refuah shleima

*1928*

The Rebbe Rayatz answered R' Alter's letter with a blessing that Hashem grant him a refuah shleima and a calm, strong mind to teach Torah and avodah she'b'lev. He invoked the inner authority his father, the Rebbe Rashab, had given them, that the students should ascend to the level of halacha through 'the form overpowering the matter'.
