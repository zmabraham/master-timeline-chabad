# Alter Asher Rivlin: Alter Asher Anshel Rivlin-Daragov, 29

*1930*

Alter Asher Rivlin: Alter Asher Anshel Rivlin-Daragov, 29 Elul 5620, 1860 - 24 Cheshvan 5691, November 14, 1930) was a well-known Hebron merchant and banker, a Chabad chassid, of the Chabad Rivlin family in Hebron, an activist and representative of the Jews in the council
