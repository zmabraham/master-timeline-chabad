# First 19 Kislev farbrengen at the Kiev yeshiva ketana

*1927*

R' Binyamin Gorodetzky asked the author to collect money from the boys of the underground yeshiva ketana in Kiev to organize a 19 Kislev gathering. The resulting farbrengen was the author's first participation in a Chag HaGeulah celebration, held quietly as Soviet surveillance of the yeshiva was beginning to tighten and synagogues were being closed across Russia.
