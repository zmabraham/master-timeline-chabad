# Rebbe Rayatz urges students to flee Poland for the Vilna yeshiva

*1940*

Still based in Riga before his journey to New York, the Rebbe Rayatz sent a letter (Igrot Kodesh vol. 5, no. 1153) to one of the Tomchei Temimim students. He instructed each student to write to his peers in Poland — including those at the Chelm branch and other smaller yeshivot — to rouse them to come, to seek out missing students, and to push past every obstacle to reach the yeshiva.
