# Rebbe Rashab accepts Rosenbaum's first will from Marienbad

*1908*

Reb Avraham Chaim drafted a will in 5668 and sent a copy to the Rebbe Rashab. On 23 Tammuz 5668 the Rebbe replied from Marienbad commending him for arranging his affairs—'as a God-fearing and sensible man should do'—and blessing him with long life, livelihood, peace of mind for Torah and avodah, and good health.
