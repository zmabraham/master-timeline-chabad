# Lubavitch convenes preparatory assembly of Chabad rabbis

*1908*

As preparations for the Vilna assembly intensified, the Rebbe Rashab gathered Chabad rabbis and community activists in Lubavitch itself for a strategic conference. The gathering established a standing committee to handle the preparatory work for the Vilna assembly. The Chabad assembly also decided to seek the Gerrer Rebbe's approval to send two Chabad representatives, R. Menachem Mendel Chein of Nezhin and R. Shmaryahu Leib Medalia of Vitebsk, to participate in the upcoming assembly of Polish rabbis.
