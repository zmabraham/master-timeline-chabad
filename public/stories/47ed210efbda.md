# Mitteler Rebbe's second interrogation on synagogue and book costs

*1825*

The committee of investigators — Colonel Loginov, Court Counselor Markov, and Major Morozov — pressed the Rebbe to specify how much of the collected funds went to building the large Lubavitch synagogue and acquiring sacred books. Distrusting his answers, the governor would soon dispatch a special investigator to Lubavitch to verify the responses on the ground. The Rebbe's written replies were given the same day, with the on-site verification mission departing four days later.
