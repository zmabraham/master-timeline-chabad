# Semicha granted to Tomchei Tmimim Warsaw students by Warsaw rabbinic luminaries

*1930*

A landmark celebration marked the awarding of semicha to the first group of Tomchei Tmimim bochurim in Warsaw by major poskim including Rabbi Menachem Zemba, Rabbi Shlomo Dovid Kahane, Rabbi Chaim Leib Yudkovsky, Rabbi Yehoshua Plate, and Rabbi Yitzchak Meir Kanal. Surviving semicha documents from the estate of Rabbi Moshe Pinchas Katz, dated Adar 5690, were issued by Rabbi Shlomo Dovid Kahane, Rabbi Yitzchak Meir Kanal, Rabbi Sholom Shachna Cherniak of Maciejów, and Rabbi Yechezkel Michelson. The achievement demonstrated the high Talmudic level the yeshiva had reached in exile.
