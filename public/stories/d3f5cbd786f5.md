# World War II begins; Rebbe Rayatz directs American talmidim to Warsaw and Riga

*1939*

On Friday morning the students heard the roar of aircraft and bombings begin. The Rebbe Rayatz called them in to yechidus, told them to travel calmly to Warsaw and then on to Riga 'to my Chassidim,' where Rabbi Mordechai Chefetz would arrange their studies in nigleh and Chassidus until the storm passed. Meir Greenberg was given a private mission to communicate with Mordechai Dubin in Riga regarding a Latvian visa for the Rebbe.
