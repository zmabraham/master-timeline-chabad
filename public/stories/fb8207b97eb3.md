# Tzemach Tzedek tours Ukraine; visits graves of Alter Rebbe and Mitteler Rebbe

*1836*

This extensive summer journey took the Tzemach Tzedek through Bayov, Shklov, Zhlobin, Bobruisk, Hlusk, Paritch, Chernigov, Nezhin (to prostrate at the grave of his father-in-law the Mitteler Rebbe), Priluk, Kremenchug, Chernobyl, Brahin, Haditch (to prostrate at the grave of his grandfather the Alter Rebbe), Panitch, and Hornostaypil (for the wedding of his son R. Yosef Yitzchak). The trip is documented through the dated headings of maamarim he delivered in each town. It is one of the most extensive recorded journeys of his nesi'us.
