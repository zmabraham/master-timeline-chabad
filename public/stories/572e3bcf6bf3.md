# Baker to deliver 100-senator letter demanding return of Schneersohn library

*1992*

Rabbi Shlomo Cunin assembled signatures from 100 US senators demanding that Yeltsin honor his commitment to Baker to return the Schneersohn library. Baker was to deliver the letter and signatures to Foreign Minister Kozyrev, who had come to Washington to prepare the Bush–Yeltsin summit. The author judged Yeltsin unlikely to ignore it, since he was hoping for major US aid and could not afford bitterness from senators and the administration. The pressure was compounded by separate leverage over the Smolensk archive — three million documents whose return to Russia President Bush had paused pending release of the Schneersohn collection.
