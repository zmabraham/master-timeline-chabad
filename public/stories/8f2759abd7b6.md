# Haggadah with Likutei Minhagim u'Taamim published

*1946*

Kehot issued the Rebbe's Haggadah with Likutei Minhagim u'Taamim in 1946. It collected Chabad customs and reasons for the seder, including the note that 'in the Rebbe's house the matzot are arranged on a cloth, not a plate—except the Rebbe himself, who arranges them on a silver tray.'
