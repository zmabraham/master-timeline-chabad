# Rosenbaum writes private will for son Yaakov, designating burial in New York

*1911*

On 2 Iyar 5671 (April 30, 1911) Reb Avraham Chaim wrote a personal will to his son Yaakov. He insisted on burial in the New York chevra plot, refusing offers from New Haven where R' David Schifrin lived. He also reminded his son that 1,050 dollars from the estate were earmarked as tzedakah for the Rebbe, to be sent through his brother R' Zalman.
