# Rebbe's secretariat writes to Shvadron about library effort

*1929*

Efforts to recover the Chabad library held by Soviet authorities continued throughout the late 1920s. In 1929, the Frierdiker Rebbe's secretariat sent a letter to Shvadron stating that 'hope appears for the good' regarding the library. The letter is documented in the Rebbe Rayatz's published correspondence (vol. 1, p. 538).
