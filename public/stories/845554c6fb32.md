# Permit secured for Vilna rabbinical assembly via Ministry of Interior

*1908*

When Dr. Krebs, the 'Learned Jew' at the Ministry of the Interior, circulated preparatory questions for the upcoming Petersburg Rabbinical Conference, the Rebbe Rashab seized the opportunity. Through the wealthy Petersburg askan R. Shmuel Michel Treinin, a petition signed by Yitzchak Liubinsky was submitted requesting permission for twenty-five named Orthodox rabbis to meet in Vilna to consult on the upcoming conference's religious questions. The director of the relevant department signed the permit, ending years of failed attempts to obtain authorization.
