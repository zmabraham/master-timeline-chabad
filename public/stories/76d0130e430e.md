# Hebron Chabad community letters and shadarut documents

*1834*

During 5594 (1834) R' Shmuel Halevi and his son R' Menachem Aryeh signed multiple official Hebron Chabad documents, including correspondence to the Tzemach Tzedek and credentials for emissaries (shadarim) sent to raise funds abroad. These documents form the earliest preserved record of the community's institutional activity, reflecting the burdens of supporting the Hebron yishuv under Sheikh Abdul Rahman's exactions.
