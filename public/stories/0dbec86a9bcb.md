# Gorbachev and Lukyanov approve return of Schneersohn library

*1990*

Motzei Shabbos Vayechi 12 Tevet 5751 brought the long-sought message: Gorbachev and Lukyanov had signed off on returning the books, and the file had been forwarded to Culture Minister Gubenko for implementation. Lukyanov's commission concluded the books should be returned without a public government ceremony. Likhachov warned obstacles from Gubenko's office were still likely, gave Veronika his private Leningrad number, and instructed her to call immediately if problems arose.
