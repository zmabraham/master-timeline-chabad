# Rayatz relocates from Rostov to Leningrad

*1924*

The apartment had belonged to a baron from Kass, the governor of Bessarabia, who was banished from Leningrad in June 1924 and signed the dwelling and much of its furniture over to the Rebbe. Without an official permit for prayer assemblies, Chasidim gathered in a large room similar to the Lubavitch hall. The city's chief rabbi was the respected David Tevel Katzenellenbogen.
