# Major diplomatic push for return of Schneersohn library

*1992*

After a long lull the books issue exploded into activity. Rabbi Yosef Yitzchok Aharonov flew to New York and, through Mr. Fishman, arranged a meeting between himself, Rabbi Shlomo Cunin, and Vorontsov — Yeltsin's deputy, in NY for the UN. Yeltsin called culture minister Sidorov for a report; Robert Strauss was to meet Yeltsin on the library that day; Aubrey at the US embassy summoned Mr. Weissman for a briefing; and Veronika was scheduled to meet Prime Minister Gaidar. Veronika also received a copy of a letter from Mr. Philipov to the Rebbe stating that beyond the 2,000 books previously identified, the library had now found Schneersohn-collection markings on another 700 volumes, with months of identification work still ahead.
