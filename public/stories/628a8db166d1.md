# Historic Vilna rabbinical assembly convenes

*1909*

After nearly a decade of failed attempts, the official Vilna rabbinical assembly finally took place over ten days in Iyar 5669, with the Gerrer Rebbe (R. Avraham Mordechai Alter) joining for part of the proceedings. Twenty of the twenty-five authorized rabbis attended. The assembly resolved to dispatch traveling rabbis and preachers to towns across Russia to strengthen religious observance, organize Torah study circles, and establish local educational committees (Vaadei Chinuch) of pious learned men to supervise schooling. The Rayatz, designated by the Rebbe Rashab to handle all operational details, prepared the formal protocol that was submitted via the governor to the Ministry of Interior's religious affairs department.
