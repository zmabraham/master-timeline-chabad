# Older bochurim assigned to learn Chassidus with younger students at Tomchei Tmimim

*1912*

At the start of 5673, after the mashpia R. Shilem Kuratin suspected that several students were not committing themselves to learning Chassidus, a new arrangement was instituted in Tomchei Tmimim. A few of the senior bochurim were paired with younger students to study Chassidus together. The author was assigned to R. Nota Pinski.
