# Rashag appeals to Latvian envoy in Washington

*1940*

On June 23, 1940 / 17 Sivan 5700, the Rashag wrote to Alfred Bilmanis at the Latvian embassy in Washington following a phone conversation. He listed the Hornstein, Eber, and Feigin families with their dates of birth, noted they held US visas, and asked Bilmanis to request Finland's envoy to instruct his Riga embassy to facilitate transit through Petsamo.
