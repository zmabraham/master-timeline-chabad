# Library book transfer halted by Shakhin's intervention

*1992*

When the plan was set to transfer the Schneersohn library books to Veronika's academy, Shakhin abruptly intervened, telling Zhitinov to halt because he was about to fly to Petersburg and Yeltsin and Burbulis would return the next day. At the subsequent meeting Zhitinov hid Shakhin's decision and sought pretexts to delay. Under attack, Veronika canceled the academy transfer plan, leaving Zhitinov to argue the books must be returned to the Rebbe.
