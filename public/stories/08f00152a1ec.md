# Author begins studying with Rav Baruch Rabinowitz in Zhuravichi

*1906*

In the winter of 5667 (late 1906 / early 1907), at approximately age 11, the author's father placed him to study with Rav Baruch Rabinowitz, the Ashkenazi rabbi of Zhuravichi. They learned Tractate Bava Basra together, and the rabbi found favor in him. However, lacking a study partner, the need to travel to a yeshiva became clear.
