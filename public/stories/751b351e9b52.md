# Final Tomchei Temimim students leave Lubavitch

*1918*

Although the Rashab left Lubavitch in late 1915, the Tomchei Temimim yeshiva students remained behind to learn for another two and a half years. Before the final group departed at the end of summer 5678 (1918), they organized the belongings left behind and buried valuables deep in the cellar of the Rebbe's house for safekeeping. The move ended Lubavitch's centuries-long role as the seat of Chabad.
