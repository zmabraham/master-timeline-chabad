# Alter Rebbe's second arrest and transfer to St. Petersburg

*1800*

At the beginning of 5561 (autumn 1800) a fresh denunciation against the Alter Rebbe was filed and he was once more brought to St. Petersburg for questioning. In his testimony there he cited Tsar Paul's earlier release decree and Beklashov's order tolerating the chassidim as proof of the lawfulness of the movement.
