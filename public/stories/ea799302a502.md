# Jacobson delivers maamar at bris of Chaim Eliezer Horowitz in Moscow

*1925*

During his Moscow stay he was honored to chazer Chassidus at the bris. Accustomed in Homel to chazering in Yiddish so that the balebatim could understand, he did the same in Moscow—drawing critique from the Moscow Tmimim, who considered the audience refined enough for Chassidus in Loshon HaKodesh and remarked that he was 'translating' Chassidus.
