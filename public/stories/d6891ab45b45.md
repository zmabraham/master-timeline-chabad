# Rebbe writes British Chief Rabbi Hertz pleading for Russian Jewry

*1928*

Following the Rashag's earlier diplomatic visit to London, the Rebbe wrote, 'It is not a time for silence or to hold back on action. We must save Russian Jewry.' He told Hertz he knew 'of twenty thousand children in the USSR who wish to study Torah and are unable to – not because of the persecution, but rather the lack of money.'
