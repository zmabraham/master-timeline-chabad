# First Chevron Chabad letter to Montefiore signed by R' Yosef Yehuda Leib

*1839*

Six letters were sent by the leaders of the Chevron Chabad community to Montefiore during summer 5599, the first dated 28 Sivan 5599. R' Yosef Yehuda Leib son of R' Chaim signed all of them. Notably, he already signed using the phrase 'son of the late' two days before the actual death of R' Chaim the Jerusalem shochet, demonstrating that the Jerusalem shochet was not actually his father.
