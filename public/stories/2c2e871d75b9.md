# Tomchei Tmimim Warsaw operates at Franciscan St. 30

*1921*

In the years 5681–5682 (approximately 1920–1922) the Warsaw branch of Tomchei Tmimim was housed in the synagogue at Franciscan Street 30. The yeshiva was still in its early formative period in Poland and used existing study halls before later expansions. This location preceded its move to a more spacious house of study on the same street.
