# R. Mordechai Mentlik marries Gittel Stockhamer

*1938*

On Isru Chag Shavuos 5698 (7 Sivan, June 6, 1938), R. Mordechai Mentlik married Gittel Stockhamer, one of the daughters of R. David Stockhamer of Newark. The Rosh Yeshiva and the Rebbe Rayatz's secretaries — R. Yechezkel Feigin, R. Moshe Leib Rotshtein, and board member R. Shraga Feivish Zalmanov — participated in the wedding and the sheva brachos.
