# Imperial Russian library founded with legal-deposit law

*1818*

In 5578 (1818), the major Russian imperial library was founded in St. Petersburg, later renamed Saltykov-Shchedrin. From the date of its founding, Russian law required every published book in the empire to be deposited at the library. This made it a near-complete repository of Hebrew printings, including early Chabad publications that emissaries searched there in 1991.
