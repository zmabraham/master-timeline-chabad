# Rebbe Rayatz's train stops in Vilna en route to Latvia

*1927*

As the Rebbe Rayatz traveled from Russia to Latvia, his train passed through Vilna. The yeshiva's bochurim and administration eagerly awaited him at the station. The Rebbe stepped out onto the carriage steps and conversed with the Rosh Yeshiva and mashpia, but the brief stop did not permit longer interaction before the train continued to Riga.
