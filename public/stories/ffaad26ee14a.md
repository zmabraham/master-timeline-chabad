# Vilna publication of 'Maamar Yetziat Mitzrayim' by Yitzchak Aizik of Homil

*1877*

In 5637 (1877), 'Maamar Yetziat Mitzrayim' — a Chassidic Chabad commentary by Rabbi Yitzchak Aizik HaLevi Epstein of Homil — was published in Vilna. The 29-leaf quarto edition expounded the inner meaning of the Exodus narrative according to Chabad Chassidic teaching. It served as a key text for Chabad students of the early Russian period.
