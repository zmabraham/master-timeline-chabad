# R. Avraham Chaim hears 'Tze'enah uRe'enah' discourse from Rebbe Maharash in Lubavitch

*1879*

R. Avraham Chaim Rosenbaum traveled to Lubavitch for Shavuot 5639, during the lifetime of the Rebbe Maharash. He heard the discourse 'Tze'enah uRe'enah' on the source of Kerias Shema in the Giving of the Torah, and in yechidus asked the Rebbe how this fit with the standard Kabbalistic mapping of positive and negative mitzvos to the divine name. He preserved the Rebbe's brief answer in writing for future study.
