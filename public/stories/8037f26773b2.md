# Rebbe writes that he will not settle permanently in America

*1930*

Despite the vaad's efforts to bring him to settle in America, the Rebbe wrote that this was not his intention. He later explained to the author in Elul 5699 that the manner of his American visit had been that of a public askan, an area in which he had skill and success but which was not his true work — his work was chassidim and chassidus.
