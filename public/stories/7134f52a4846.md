# Chabad refugees board freight train out of Samarkand (Eshelon No. 3)

*1946*

After waiting on the tracks most of the day, the refugees were finally permitted to board the freight train in the late afternoon. For the narrator it was the first time leaving Samarkand, where fear of the Soviet authorities had kept him indoors for years. The departure marked the start of the post-war 'Eshelonim' escape from the USSR using forged Polish papers.
