# Hebron Chabad beit din bans modern schools

*1873*

Following the 5631 ruling by Jerusalem's rabbis against the new haskalah-influenced modern schools, the Hebron Chabad rav R' Shimon Menashe Chaikin signed a parallel prohibition with his beit din of R' Chaim Yaakov and R' Shaul. Hungarian Chassidim of Hebron including R' Alexander Zushe Hoizman, his son R' Shimon, and R' Aryeh Leib also added their signatures. The ban appears in 'Maaseh Avot' (Jerusalem 1901).
