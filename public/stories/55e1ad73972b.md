# Judges' letter to Lenin Library director orders immediate compliance

*1991*

Rabbi Kogan had visited the judges to report that the library was not cooperating, that the enforcement office would not act without a separate court order, and that the library was distributing leaflets attacking the ruling. The judges wrote a formal letter to director Vyalik declaring he had a personal duty to carry out the ruling immediately, independent of any enforcement proceedings. Rabbi Kogan hand-delivered the letter to the director's secretary and obtained her signed acknowledgment of receipt.
