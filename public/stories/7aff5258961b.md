# Rebbe Rayatz formally appoints R' Alter chief mashpia of Warsaw yeshiva

*1928*

On 2 Marcheshvan 5689, the Rebbe Rayatz sent a letter to the Warsaw Va'ad declaring that he was placing on R' Alter Simchowitz the 'sacred yoke' of overseeing all hashpa'a and hadracha at the yeshiva. In a parallel letter that day to R' Alter, he charged him with ordering the studies, awakening the students' inner point of avodah she'b'lev through chassidic teaching, and producing fruit in middos and conduct.
