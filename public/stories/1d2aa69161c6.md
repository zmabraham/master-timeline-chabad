# R' Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk completes his house and synagogue in Tiberias

*1786*

After years of struggle with the Sephardic pakid who initially refused permission for a separate Ashkenazi prayer space, R' Menachem Mendel built a permanent three-story house with an integrated synagogue. The Sephardic pakid who had tormented him died suddenly after attempting to extort funds, and the new pakid was peaceful. The synagogue served as a center of prayer and Torah study for the Chassidic community in Tiberias.
