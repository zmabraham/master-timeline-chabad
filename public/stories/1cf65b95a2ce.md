# Tzemach Tzedek's first edition published in Vilna

*1871*

In 5631 (1871), the first edition of the responsa work 'Tzemach Tzedek' by Rabbi Menachem Mendel Schneersohn was published in Vilna. The book gathered the halachic responsa of the third Chabad Rebbe. A copy of this first edition is preserved in the Lubavitch Library.
