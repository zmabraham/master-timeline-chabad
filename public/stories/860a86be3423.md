# Father released from army, reopens cheder in Zhivotov

*1918*

After his release following the fall of the Tsar, Ephraim Shmuel resumed teaching Torah to the youth of Zhivotov. The political climate had changed dramatically: Russia was now a republic, parties were forming, and secular movements were drawing young Jews away from religion. The first public political address in the synagogue by a young woman shocked the elders and ignited communal conflict.
