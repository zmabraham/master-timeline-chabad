# Passing of Rabbi Shmuel, the Rebbe Maharash

*1882*

In 1882, three days after Yom Kippur, Rabbi Shmuel Schneersohn, the Rebbe Maharash and fourth Lubavitcher Rebbe, passed away in Lubavitch at the age of forty-eight. On the eve of Rosh HaShanah that year, he had cryptically told his wife that she would rise up thirty-two steps physically while he would rise spiritually, alluding to his impending passing. He had been Rebbe for a little more than sixteen years, leaving his youngest daughter just four years old.
