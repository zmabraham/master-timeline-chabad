# Fez community begins effort to bring the Yaavetz back from Meknes

*1726*

During Hanukkah 5487 (December 1726), some thirty to forty leading members of the Fez community traveled to Meknes and committed under oath to support Rabbi Yaakov ibn Tzur if he would return to Fez. The agreement, which detailed financial terms and pledged unconditional obedience to his rulings, was eventually ratified in Adar Sheni 5489 once the move actually took place. The signed parchment survives in the Ibn Tzur archive at the Agudat Chasidei Chabad library.
