# R. Dovber Garfinkel (Kornitzer) appointed mashgiach in Otwock

*1936*

Renowned for knowing Shas by heart and for unusual clarity of explanation, Kornitzer transformed the yeshiva atmosphere from the moment he arrived. He instituted Erev Shabbos chazara of maamarim in the forest surrounding the yeshiva, where chaburos of students sat at fixed tables among tall trees to repeat Chassidus before Kabbalas Shabbos.
