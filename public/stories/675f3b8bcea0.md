# Alter Rebbe sends 800 rubles via R' Aryeh Leib to fund Vilna Chassidim

*1796*

Around 5556, R' Aryeh Leib ben Yehoshua Zelig of Vilna drew close to Chassidus and traveled three times to the Alter Rebbe in Liozna. The Rebbe entrusted him with a letter and 800 ruble assignats to be delivered to R' Nachum ben Yitzchak, treasurer of the Vilna Chassidic kehilla. Around the same time R' Meir Refael's received two letters from the Rebbe.
