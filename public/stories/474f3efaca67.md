# Rebbe Rayatz besieged in Warsaw during German invasion

*1939*

As WWII engulfed Europe, funding from abroad to Yeshivat Achei Tmimim in Tel Aviv nearly ceased. The Rebbe Rayatz himself spent much of the winter caught in the siege of Warsaw until divine assistance enabled his escape from the persecutors to Riga and onward to America. The crisis severely disrupted communication and support for Chabad institutions in Eretz Yisrael.
