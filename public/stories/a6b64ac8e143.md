# R' Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk's final letter on Alter Rebbe's leadership

*1788*

In his final letter from Tiveria, dated 5548 (1788), R' Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk wrote: 'We have anointed him to be moreh tzedek in their land, so that the congregation of Hashem should not be like sheep without a shepherd… who like him is teacher and rav in their country.' This sealed the Alter Rebbe's standing as exclusive leader.
