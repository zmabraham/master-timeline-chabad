# Officer sent to Lubavitch with written questions for Rabbi DovBer

*1826*

On 16 January 1826 (20 Shevat 5586), the Babinovich peace court dispatched a police officer to Lubavitch carrying a list of written questions for Rabbi DovBer. The Rebbe's written answers would have been used to fill out the formal court record without bringing him back to Babinovich in person.
