# Death of Rabbi Reuven ibn Tzur, son of the Yaavetz, in Salé

*1742*

On 3 Av 5502 (summer 1742), Rabbi Reuven ibn Tzur — son of the Yaavetz and a young scholar of about twenty-five — passed away in Salé during Chol HaMoed. Rabbi Eliyahu Tzarfati delivered a eulogy for him on 21 Av, preserved in the work 'Na'ar Bocheh.' He was buried in Alcazar (Ksar), and his death left Rabbi Refael Oved as the Yaavetz's only surviving son.
