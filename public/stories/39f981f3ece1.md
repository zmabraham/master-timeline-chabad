# R' Chaim Meir Lis appointed mashpia in Warsaw yeshiva

*1924*

After R' Alter Simchowitz left for Eretz Yisrael, R' Chaim Meir Lis was appointed as one of the mashpi'im in the Warsaw yeshiva. He initially refused the position, wanting to support himself through his own labor, but accepted after receiving a second handwritten letter from the Rebbe explaining the role of tmimim as 'lanterns of light.' He served as mashpia until after Purim 5687 (1927).
