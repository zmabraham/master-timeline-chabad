# Levins submit OVIR exit applications via Polish identity

*1946*

Hearing that Polish refugees could repatriate and possibly continue on to American-occupied Germany, Levin and Michael Teitelbaum applied to OVIR in mid-winter 5706. To register his wife under the Polish name Naftali Batt, Levin tore numbered pages from her passport that recorded her maiden name Gurkov; the clerk did not notice and stamped her marriage to 'Batt.' A subsequent midnight visit from NKVD-linked questioners about the application terrified them but did not stop them.
