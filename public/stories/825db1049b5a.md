# R. Mordechai Mentlik arrives in United States

*1939*

R. Mordechai Mentlik arrived in the United States in Tishrei 5700 (September-October 1939), having received his US visa at the beginning of Elul 5699 just before the outbreak of war. He later served as the senior rosh yeshiva of Tomchei Tmimim in New York and was a member of the Aguch executive.
