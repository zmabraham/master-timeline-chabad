# Rebbe orders weekly contact with European talmidim

*1940*

From New York, on 15 Kislev 5701, the Frierdiker Rebbe wrote to Moshe Pinchas Katz (Igrot vol. 5, no. 1310) instructing him to maintain weekly correspondence with Tomchei Temimim students still in Vilna, Riga and Poland, keeping them informed and gathering reports on their material and spiritual situations.
