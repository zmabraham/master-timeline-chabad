# Lubavitchers regroup in Paris and found Brunoy yeshiva and Beis Rivka

*1948*

In 5708 (1948), the bulk of the Lubavitcher refugees from the DP camps gathered in Paris and then dispersed across France. They immediately founded the Tomchei Tmimim yeshiva in Brunoy and the Beis Rivka girls' school in Yerres, both of which have continued to grow until today.
