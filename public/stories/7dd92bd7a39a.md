# Rechovot Water - Additional Roles: Member of the Spiritual Committee of Kfar Chabad

*1959*

Rechovot water, additional roles, member of the spiritual committee of Kfar Chabad. Rabbi Chaim Aharon in the video program of the 'Lachluchis Geulasis' organization. Rabbi Chaim Aharon (born 14 Adar I 5719, 1959) is the rosh yeshiva of Tomchei Tmimim Yeshiva Rechovot and a member of the committee.
