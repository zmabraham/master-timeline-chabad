# Rebbe Rashab instructs Havlin to revive Toras Emes at first opportunity

*1920*

Before his passing, the Rebbe Rashab spoke with R' Shlomo Zalman Havlin on Purim 5680 in Rostov, instructing him that as soon as travel became possible he must return to the Land of Israel — without regard to obstacles — to resume directing Yeshiva Toras Emes as before. The instruction is preserved in a 5684 letter of the Rebbe Rayatz (Igros Kodesh vol. 1, no. 134).
