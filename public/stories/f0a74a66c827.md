# Death of R. Avraham Mertzbacher of Munich

*1885*

Mertzbacher had assembled his important collection between 1866 and 1885, including 43 incunabula printed before 1500. After his death, his son Dr. Yitzchak Eizik Mertzbacher inherited the library and later sought to sell it, prompting interest from the Rebbe Rashab.
