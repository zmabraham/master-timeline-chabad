# Rashab considers opening Tomchei Temimim branch in Eretz Yisrael

*1910*

Jerusalem's historically religious character was being transformed by non-religious Zionist immigrants, while Hebron remained a spiritual oasis. To bolster the impoverished Chabad community there, the Rayatz established Chevronim, a special fund to help Hebron's Jews. The community leaders asked the Rashab to purchase Beit Romano.
