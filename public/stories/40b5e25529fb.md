# Rebbetzin Rivka gives Maharash's leather portfolio to grandson Rayatz

*1888*

Rebbe Rayatz inscribed a note documenting that the leather portfolio used for holding papers, from the estate of his great-grandfather the Maharash, was given to him as a gift by his grandmother Rebbetzin Rivka in the winter of 5648. The portfolio is preserved with his inscription identifying its provenance.
