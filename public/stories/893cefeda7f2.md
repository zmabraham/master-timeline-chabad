# Rabbi Shalom Shlomo Schneersohn passes away in Jerusalem

*1926*

Rabbi Shalom Shlomo Schneersohn was the brother of Rabbi Levi Yitzchak Schneersohn and served as rabbi of Shiroka before fleeing to Chernigov during the Russian Revolution. He later made aliyah with his father-in-law the Radatz but died on 7 Shevat 5686 in Jerusalem, less than a year after arriving. He was buried on the Mount of Olives, survived by his wife Rachel and daughter Zelda.
