# R. Avraham Chaim Rosenbaum writes Yud-Tes Kislev Torah letter from Smolensk

*1889*

On the morning of Yud-Tes Kislev 5650 (December 12, 1889), R. Avraham Chaim Rosenbaum wrote from Smolensk to his colleague R. Chanoch Hendel. The letter discussed a maamar said on Parshas Lech Lecha concerning the concept of 'goy gadol' and a new creation through good deeds. He raised questions about the conflicting interpretations of the Yerushalmi, the Zohar, Targum Yonatan, and Rashi on the verse 'Hashem yotzei mimekomo,' asking that his doubts be brought before the Rebbe (Maharash) for clarification.
