# Death of R' Shlomo Aharon Kazarnovsky

*1982*

On 7 Marcheshvan 5743, R' Shlomo Aharon Kazarnovsky passed away. His tombstone records his energetic work for Chabad institutions in Eretz Yisrael, his role as one of the planters of the Chabad orchard in America, his closeness to the Rebbeim, his decades of rabbinical service, and his outreach work, particularly with youth, up until his final day.
