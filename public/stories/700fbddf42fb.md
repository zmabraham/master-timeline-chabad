# Pekuam queries Hebron Chabad on R' Yechiel Cohen's American mission

*1847*

On 15 Elul 5607 (August 27, 1847), the Pekuam Amsterdam committee wrote to the heads of Hebron's Chabad kollel inquiring about R' Yechiel Cohen's shadarus to America. The philanthropist R' Asher b'R' Shimshon of London had told them Cohen claimed to represent both Hebron kollelim. The Pekuam wanted to know exactly how much had been collected and what share Chabad had received.
