# Chabad Library begins annual themed exhibitions

*1994*

In 5754 (1993–1994), the Agudas Chasidei Chabad – Ohel Yosef Yitzchak – Lubavitch Library at 770 Eastern Parkway began its tradition of mounting themed exhibitions of rare items from its archives. Over the years more than ten exhibitions have been organized, each dedicated to a different theme such as rare manuscripts, Chabad Rebbeim, Haggadot, or the bicentennial of the Alter Rebbe's liberation.
