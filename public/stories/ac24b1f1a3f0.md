# R' Shmuel Grossman becomes shochet in London

*1948*

In 5708, R' Shmuel Grossman moved from Paris to London after receiving a visa through his brother-in-law R' Asher Abramson. On 10 Nissan 5708 he wrote Rayatz seeking approval, which Rayatz granted. On 3 Av 5708, the future Rebbe wrote to him expressing satisfaction at hearing he had not only settled successfully but was also engaging in outreach work to strengthen Chassidus and Torah observance.
