# R. Sholom Noach Berzovsky appointed RaM at Achei Tmimim Tel Aviv

*1940*

When external funding nearly collapsed and the yeshiva nonetheless flourished with growing enrollment, the administration restructured staff. R. Shaul Bruk was named mashpia in addition to his roles as RaM and supervisor, while R. Berzovsky was newly appointed for the senior class. R. Nissan Eber remained with the younger class, allowing the yeshiva to continue functioning that year.
