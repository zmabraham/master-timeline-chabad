# Korosten rabbinical conference in Ukraine

*1926*

The Rebbe was skeptical the conference would be productive but believed the noble intentions of organizer Rabbi Zev Kipnis would prevent harm. He dispatched Bentzion Shemtov to ensure an agenda strengthening Jewish education. When the Rebbe's telegram was read aloud, all the rabbis stood in respect — but the esteem displayed alarmed the Communist authorities, who had hoped the conference would divide Russian Jewry.
