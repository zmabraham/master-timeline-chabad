# Rebbe affirms all hidden manuscripts were returned after Rayatz's release

*1988*

On the special shlichus to Russia in 5748 — documented in 'Yoman HaShlichus HaMeyuchedes' — the question was put to the Rebbe whether perhaps not all the manuscripts hidden during the 5687 arrest had been recovered, and whether it might be worth searching for them. The Rebbe replied: 'They were returned immediately!!' — closing the question on the manuscript dispersal during the 12 Tammuz episode.
