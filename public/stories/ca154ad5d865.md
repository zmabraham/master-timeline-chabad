# Meeting with US embassy representative Mary Dietrich on the books

*1999*

On 7 Tevet 5760, the emissary met with US embassy representative Mary Dietrich and Russian State Library Oriental Books department head Mara Trifonenko for over two hours. Trifonenko detailed a four-stage plan involving a committee, duplicate-checks, and a new building before any books could be returned. The emissary argued the plan was unrealistic and would take decades, but provided a list of 100 books for the librarians to verify.
