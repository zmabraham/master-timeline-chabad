# Rabbanit Chana passes away on 6 Tishrei

*1964*

After leaving Russia, Rabbanit Chana continued reciting her husband's Tehillim daily until her passing in New York on 6 Tishrei 5725 (Sept 12, 1964). She had recorded on its flyleaf the story of how the Tehillim had been brought to her late husband in Soviet exile.
