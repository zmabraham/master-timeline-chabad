# Mitteler Rebbe accepts leadership and a 35,000-ruble fund

*1813*

In 1813 the Jews of White Russia summoned Berke Shneur—Rabbi Dovber, the Mitteler Rebbe—to take his father's place as their rebbe. They promised to compensate the family for losses sustained when the enemy destroyed their assets and to provide dignified support through voluntary donations. He gathered 35,000 rubles, distributed among relatives, personal needs, and synagogue construction—an act later cited as evidence in the government's investigation of him.
