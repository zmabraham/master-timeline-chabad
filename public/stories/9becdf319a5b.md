# Arrest of R. M.Z. Neimark and 80 Jews of Starodub

*1881*

Acting under the guidance of the Rebbe Maharash, R. Mushulam Zalman Neimark of Starodub had worked to secure exemptions for Jewish children from service in the Russian army. After the maskil Dov Lazarov denounced him, he and eighty Starodub Jews were arrested. The yeshiva in Starodub was shut by the police and even the Rebbe Maharash himself was nearly indicted, with Chasidim interrogated about their travels to Lubavitch.
