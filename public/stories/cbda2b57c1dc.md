# Rayatz writes letter recalling lifelong passion for collecting books

*1930*

In a letter dated Rosh Chodesh Iyar 5690 (Iggeres 3837), the Rebbe Rayatz reflects on himself, noting that there are those who learn from books, those who read books, and those who collect them — and that he has been a collector since his earliest years. This statement frames a lifelong project of acquiring and preserving Jewish books that paralleled his father Rebbe Rashab's library work in Lubavitch.
