# Passing of Rebbetzin Chana Schneerson

*1964*

Rebbetzin Chana Schneerson, mother of the Lubavitcher Rebbe, kept the Tehillim that had belonged to her husband Rabbi Levi Yitzchak in his Kazakhstan exile. She recited Tehillim from it until her passing in New York on 6 Tishrei 5725 (September 1964), having brought it with her when she left Russia.
