# Author visits Jerusalem and consults genealogist Shmuel Gur

*1986*

During a summer 5746 (1986) visit to Jerusalem, R' Sh. D. Levin consulted the genealogist R' Shmuel Gur on the disputed question of whether R' Shmuel ben Bezalel (grandson of the Maharal) had any sons. Gur pointed him to a Prague tombstone inscription that resolved the question and validated the Rebbe Rayatz's family tradition.
