# Rebbe references searching Rayatz's general index in Riga

*1969*

In Sichos Kodesh 5730 p. 719, recorded during the consolation visit for Rashag (Rabbi Shemaryahu Gurary), the Rebbe says 'I looked for this when I was in Riga, in the index of my father-in-law.' The reference confirms the general index was already a working scholarly tool while Rayatz lived in Latvia.
