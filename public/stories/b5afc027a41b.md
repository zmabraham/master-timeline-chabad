# Italian facsimile of the 15th-century Yoel ben Shimon Haggadah from the British Library

*1985*

In 5745 (1985), a facsimile of a 15th-century Haggadah manuscript was published in Italy. The original — held at the British Library — was written and illustrated by Yoel ben Shimon Feibush HaAshkenazi, with a commentary drawn from the school of Rabbi Eleazar of Worms. The 49-plus-40 leaf folio included an introduction, notes, and French transcription by David Goldstein.
