# Declaration signed that Poliakov synagogue belongs to Agudas Chabad

*1991*

The Mossoviet had summoned Korabelsky to sign a renovation and office-transfer agreement for the Poliakov shul. Because the formal status of the synagogue between Agudas Chabad and the local community had never been settled, the diarist met both rabbis and required them to sign a declaration affirming that Agudas Chabad alone would determine how the synagogue was run.
