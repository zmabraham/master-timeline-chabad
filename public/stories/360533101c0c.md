# Rebbe Rashab founds religious aid committee in Petrograd

*1916*

The interior gubernias to which refugees had been displaced were outside the former Pale of Settlement and lacked synagogues, chadarim, mikvaot, and other religious institutions. The Rebbe Rashab traveled to Petrograd immediately after Purim 5676 to address this, organizing a special committee through an assembly of rabbis and activists. The committee, headed by Reb Shmuel Michel Trainin, worked to establish chadarim and Talmud Torahs, mikvaot, and to supply tefillin, tzitzit, and siddurim in the refugee locations.
