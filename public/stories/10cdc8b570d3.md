# Passing of Rabbi Levi Yitzchak Schneerson in Alma-Ata

*1944*

The Rebbe's father, Rabbi Levi Yitzchak Schneerson, passed away on 20 Menachem Av 5704 (August 1944) in Alma-Ata, capital of Kazakhstan. He had been allowed, after great efforts of devoted friends working at risk to their lives, to move from the village of Chili to Alma-Ata, where conditions were better both materially and spiritually because a Jewish community existed there.
