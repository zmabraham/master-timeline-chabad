# Rabbi Avraham Abba Bruk, Rabbi Avraham Abba (Avush) Bruk, 3 Teves

*1947*

Rabbi Avraham Abba Bruk, Rabbi Avraham Abba (Avush) Bruk, 3 Teves 5708, December 16, 1947 - 21 Shevat 5783, February 12, 2023) was a veteran educator, a resident of Nachalat Har Chabad, whose roles included serving as the director of the vocational school of Kfar Chabad
