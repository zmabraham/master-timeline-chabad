# Wife of R' Elchanan Dov Morozov dies in childbirth

*1915*

In the winter of 5675 the wife of R' Elchanan Dov Morozov, sister of the noted tamim R' Avraham Dovid of Klimavichi, died in childbirth. The Rebbe Rashab attributed her death to the dictum 'there are those who perish without judgment,' since adequate medical care was unavailable in the small town at the moment of need.
