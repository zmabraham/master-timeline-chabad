# Romano courtyard construction completed

*1878*

After years of planning and building, Markado Romano's grand mansion in Hebron was completed in 5638. The completion immediately sparked a controversy when Romano had a dispute with one of the builders, who brought the matter before R' Eliyahu Mani, Sephardi rabbi of Hebron. When Mani ruled against Romano, Romano accused him of favoritism, igniting a years-long communal conflict that drew in the entire Holy Land Jewish community.
