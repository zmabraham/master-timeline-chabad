# Mitteler Rebbe sends circular letter about the Lyadi fire

*1810*

In Nisan-Iyar 5570 (April-May 1810), shortly after the great fire in Lyadi, the Mitteler Rebbe sent a circular letter to the wider community of chassidim describing the loss. He underscored particularly the destruction of his father's manuscripts of glosses on the four sections of Shulchan Aruch, alongside books, household goods, and pledged valuables that had been kept in the house. He warned chassidim of the terrible blow this would be to his elderly father when he returned.
