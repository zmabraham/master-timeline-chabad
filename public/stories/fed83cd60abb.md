# Eliezerov returns to Hebron after over two years abroad

*1906*

HaChavatzelet of 9 Nisan 5666 (April 3, 1906) reported that Rabbi Shlomo Leib Eliezerov, Ashkenazi av beit din of Hebron, had arrived in Jerusalem on Rosh Chodesh Nisan returning from his trip to Russia, and continued to Hebron on 3 Nisan. Soon after, the Sephardic community issued him a formal release certifying his fourth mission had been the most profitable yet.
