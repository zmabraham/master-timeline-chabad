# Rebbe Rashab affirms Slonim-Shlank as Kollel Chabad address

*1911*

On 26 Cheshvan 5672 (Nov 1911), the Rebbe Rashab wrote in letter 349 affirming that the official mailing address for Kollel Chabad in Jerusalem should be 'Slonim Shlank' – the names of administrators R. Mordechai Dovber Slonim and R. Mordechai Shlank. This was meant to maintain the existing administrative structure of the kollel.
