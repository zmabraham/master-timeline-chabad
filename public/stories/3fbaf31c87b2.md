# Visit to the Malachovka synagogue outside Moscow

*1991*

On Sunday 30 Nissan 5751 (April 14, 1991), with all government offices closed, the writer drove about an hour from central Moscow to visit the Malachovka synagogue, accompanied by shluchim Yaakov Goldschmidt and Shlomo Wilhelm and bochur Yitzchak Reitman in a car provided by R' A. Shentsis of Marina Roscha. The synagogue had been renovated since his father studied there sixty years earlier. They then visited the nearby cemetery with thousands of graves, recognizing only one — that of Rabbi M.L. Ginzburg, son-in-law of the Rebbe Maharash.
