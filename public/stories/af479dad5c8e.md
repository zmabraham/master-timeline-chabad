# Yeshivah relocates from Kremenchuk to Rostov

*1920*

During the war the yeshivah had been transferred from Lubavitch to Kremenchuk, but with key Jewish supporters fleeing the city, students were forced to ration bread to every other day. The Rayatz declared, 'I want Tomchei Temimim to be next to me,' and within two weeks roughly eighty boys arrived, many ill and in torn clothing. Rabbi Shmuel Gurary borrowed funds and sold personal property to keep the yeshivah afloat.
