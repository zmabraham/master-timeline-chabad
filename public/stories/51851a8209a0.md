# Rosenbaum expelled from Germany, moves to Amsterdam

*1893*

After roughly a year running a dairy shop in Frankfurt with his son, a police official informed Rosenbaum that he had no right to reside in Germany and ordered him out. He moved to Amsterdam, where he partnered in business with the chossid R. Abba Person of Koenigsberg, who had likewise been forced to leave Russia. Unable to find sustainable income there, he would soon be pushed, against his preference, to consider emigration to America.
