# Lublin Haggadah 'Sefer HaZmanim' by Yaakov Leiner of Izbica

*1903*

In 5663 (1903), Yaakov ben Mordechai Yosef Leiner of Izbica published 'Sefer HaZmanim' in Lublin, printed by Moshe Schneidmesser and the widow Nechama Hershenhorn. The first part contained essays and homilies on the month of Nissan and its laws, an explanation of the Haggadah according to the Arizal's text, and material on dew, Sefirat HaOmer, the entire Passover holiday, Iyyar, and Pesach Sheni. The 55-leaf quarto reflected the teachings of the Izbica Chassidic dynasty.
