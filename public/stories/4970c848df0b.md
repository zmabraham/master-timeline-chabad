# Rabbi Yosef Dov Soloveitchik appointed rosh yeshivah of Yeshivah University

*1941*

The YU board was divided, but the Sixth Rebbe wrote a letter calling Soloveitchik a 'gaon' whose appointment would 'restore the school's former glory.' He also personally lobbied board member Abraham Mazer. Soloveitchik's son Haym later said the Rebbe was 'the deciding factor' in the appointment.
