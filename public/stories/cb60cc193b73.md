# Yevesektzia dismantled; Soviet state takes over anti-religious campaign

*1930*

Until 1930 the Rebbe and the Committee of Rabbis had often played government officials against Yevesektzia zealots. With its closure, government restraint ended and persecution became state policy. Stalin's later purges devoured Yevesektzia leaders themselves: Dimenstein was killed in 1938, Litvakov executed in 1937, and Frumkin died in a Kazakhstan prison camp after her 1938 arrest.
