# Great fire of Lubavitch destroys Tzemach Tzedek manuscripts

*1857*

The fire devastated Lubavitch including the Tzemach Tzedek's residence and the homes of chasidim who held his manuscripts on loan. Twenty volumes were lost at the home of Rabbi Avraham, the local moreh tzedek, including the missing siman additions to the Alter Rebbe's Shulchan Aruch. The Rebbe lamented he no longer had the strength in his old age to rewrite them. Sources variously date the fire to 5616, 5617, or 5618.
