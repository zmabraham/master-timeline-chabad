# Simcha Kisin denounces the Mitteler Rebbe

*1826*

Simcha Kisin — son of R' Lipman Kisin, parnas of Vitebsk and cousin of the Alter Rebbe's wife Sterna — turned informant against the Mitteler Rebbe in 5586 (1826). The denunciation led to the well-known arrest and release recorded in Maasar U'Geulat Admur Ha'emtza'i, despite the family's earlier closeness to the Rebbe's household.
