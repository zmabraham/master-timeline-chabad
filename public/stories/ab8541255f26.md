# Sefer Avodas HaLevi by Rabbi Aharon HaLevi of Staroshelye published

*1842*

Rabbi Aharon HaLevi of Staroshelye was a leading disciple of the Alter Rebbe whose writings became foundational texts of Chabad chassidus. This first edition of Avodas HaLevi was printed in Lvov in 5602 (1842) and is now displayed in the Library's exhibition of works by the great Chabad chassidim.
