# R' Alter Simchowitz reports yeshiva conditions to the Rebbe

*1928*

On Monday 8 Marcheshvan R' Alter had visited Otwock to relay the Rebbe's proposal to R' Moshe Hornstein, but Hornstein gave only a tentative response pending the Rebbe's letter. Writing on 9 Marcheshvan, R' Alter described being overwhelmed by the disorder—the financial straits, the gap in nigleh supervision, and the absence of mature students to whom hashpa'a could yet be delegated—and stressed that nothing could be set right until a chief director was installed.
