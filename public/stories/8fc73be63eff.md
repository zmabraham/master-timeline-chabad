# Case returned to Mohilev higher court with Babinovich recommendation

*1826*

On 3 August 1826 (12 Av 5586), with the local investigation complete, the file was sent back to the higher court of Mohilev province, accompanied by the recommendation of the Babinovich peace court. The higher court would now deliberate on a final, binding verdict in the case against Rabbi DovBer.
