# Publication of Maamarei Admor HaZaken (collected discourses of the Alter Rebbe) in New York

*1983*

In 5743 (1983), 'Maamarei Admor HaZaken' was published in New York, containing the Chabad Alter Rebbe Schneur Zalman of Liadi's Chassidic discourses on the Torah portions and festivals. The Bereishit-Shemot volume ran 504 pages and the Vayikra-Devarim volume ran 1086 pages. The work made the Alter Rebbe's wealth of Chassidic teachings systematically available.
