# American talmidim study in Riga under Rabbi Itche Masmid

*1939*

During Chol HaMoed Sukkos the group decided to remain in Riga as long as possible to fulfill their original purpose of intensive study. Rabbi Yitzchak the Masmid taught them Likkutei Torah, another chossid taught them Tanya in the evenings, and Rabbi Mordechai Dubin paid for their lodging.
