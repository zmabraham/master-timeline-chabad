# Ukrainian pogroms of 5679–5680 kill roughly 300,000 Jews

*1919*

Rival armies competing for control of Ukraine after the German withdrawal openly permitted their soldiers to attack Jewish communities. Kremenchug suffered an incursion by Petliura's troops for about a week, with house searches and several casualties; chassidim hid in cellars. Later the Makhnovist forces, who had killed R' Menachem Mendel of Bobruisk in Yekaterinoslav, besieged Kremenchug from across the Dnieper at Kraukov until Red Army artillery drove them back across the bridge.
