# 10 Shevat 5751 farbrengen marks 41st yahrzeit of Rebbe Rayatz in Moscow

*1991*

On the eve of 10 Shevat, a gathering opened at 7 p.m. in honor of the yahrzeit of Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn and the Rebbe's assumption of leadership in 5711 (1951). The author addressed the talmidim about the 1950–1951 period and the acceptance of the nesius, with Rabbi Moshe Nudelman translating into Russian. Rabbi Dovid Berel Lazar and Rabbi Moshe Chaim Levin also spoke, and the assembly listened to a recording of the Rebbe's original Yud Shevat 5711 farbrengen.
