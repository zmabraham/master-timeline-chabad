# Montefiore's second visit to the Holy Land begins

*1839*

Montefiore's second trip to the Holy Land lasted about two months, from the end of Iyar through the end of Tammuz 5599. The party first toured Tzfat, Tiberias, and surrounding villages. They then traveled to Jerusalem, where due to a plague and quarantine they pitched their tents on the Mount of Olives opposite the Temple Mount.
