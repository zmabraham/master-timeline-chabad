# R. Avraham Chaim Rosenbaum leaves Russia

*1892*

After decades of life as a Chabad chassid and businessman in the Russian Empire, R. Avraham Chaim Rosenbaum left Russia in the years 5652-5653. The departure came in the early years of the Rebbe Rashab's leadership, as significant numbers of Russian Jews were beginning to seek refuge in the West. He would spend several years in transit before reaching his final destination.
