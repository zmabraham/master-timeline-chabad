# Rebbe receives pidyonot on Erev Rosh Hashana 5723

*1962*

Early Friday morning of Erev Rosh Hashana 5723, the Rebbe led Selichot and Shacharit before a large crowd of Tishrei guests. He performed hatarat nedarim before a minyan of elder Chassidim, then received personal pidyonot at his hand. The Vaad HaMesader and elder Anash then brought a collective pan, with each signer giving a dollar. The Rebbe then traveled to the Ohel to read and tear the pidyonot.
