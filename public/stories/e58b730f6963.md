# Reb Aharon of Staroselye interrogated in Vitebsk

*1825*

On 19 Cheshvan a special officer had been dispatched to summon Reb Aharon, a leading disciple of the Alter Rebbe who led his own circle in Staroselye. His interrogation extended the inquiry beyond the Mitteler Rebbe's immediate circle, signaling that Khovansky was probing the Chassidic movement more broadly.
