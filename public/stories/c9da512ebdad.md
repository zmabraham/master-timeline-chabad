# Ch - Rabbi Yitzchak Chai Ladiov - one of the members of the Jewish underground in the community

*1940*

Ch - Rabbi Yitzchak Chai Ladiov - one of the members of the Jewish underground in the Chabad community in Samarkand, and after his aliyah to the Land was an activist of the Bucharian community and resident of the Chabad community in Lod. 5701 (05/10/1940) - Rabbi Nachum Kaplan, mashpia in the Chabad community
