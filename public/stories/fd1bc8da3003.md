# New harsh labor rules imposed on Smolensk prisoners

*1889*

R' Avraham Chaim wrote to R' Hendel Kugel on 20 Iyar 5649 about a new harsh regime in the Smolensk prison: every prisoner had to rise at the bell at 4 a.m. and stand in formation while the warden selected work assignments — a wait of two or three hours during the best part of the day. R' Avraham Chaim himself had been assigned for five days to fence the property of the prison administration.
