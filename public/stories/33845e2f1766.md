# Hebrew-English Chabad Haggadah illustrated by Zalman Kleiman

*1992*

In 5752 (1992), a Haggadah following the Chabad Alter Rebbe's nusach was published in New York with English translation by Yaakov Emanuel Schochet and illustrations by Zalman Kleiman. The 81-page quarto edition also appeared in a miniature format. Kleiman's distinctive illustrations became iconic in Chabad children's Haggadah publishing.
