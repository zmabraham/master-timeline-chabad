# Rashag named president of Tomchei Tmimim executive board

*1938*

After a brief earlier involvement in 5691, the Rashag returned to active leadership of Tomchei Tmimim in 5699. Letters from this year, written on the Yeshiva's stationery, identify him as president of the executive board. By Adar 5699 he was taking part in all of the Yeshiva's operations, with all expenditures passing through him.
