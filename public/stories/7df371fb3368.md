# Passing of R' Yosef Yehuda Shmerling in Jerusalem

*1895*

R' Yosef Yehuda Shmerling, born around 5581 at the time of his family's immigration to Eretz Yisrael, was known as 'Yosef Yehuda the firstborn.' He signed the 5604 letter to the Pekidim VeAmarkalim as 'trustee of the kollel.' He passed away in Jerusalem on 2 Elul 5655, continuing the Shmerling family's central role in the Chabad settlement of the Holy Land.
