# Seven Lubavitch books loaned to Rabbi Cunin via US government

*1994*

In 5754 (1994), Rabbi B.S. Cunin signed for seven books from the Schneersohn collection as a two-month loan, the transaction mediated through the US government. He was told his signature was a mere formality and the books would not need to be returned; they were given to the Rebbe and placed sealed in his study. The Moscow Library subsequently filed a claim at the International Court of Justice in The Hague.
