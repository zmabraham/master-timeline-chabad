# R' Shimon Zelichover joins Yeshivat Chochmei Lublin as spiritual supervisor

*1932*

Engel had taught the senior shiur at Tomchei Tmimim Warsaw at least through the end of 5687 before being recruited by R' Meir Shapiro for Chochmei Lublin. He remained in Lublin until shortly after Shapiro's passing.
