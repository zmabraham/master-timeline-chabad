# Tzemach Tzedek additions printed in Vilna Shulchan Aruch

*1905*

Between 5645 and 5665, additional responsa of the Tzemach Tzedek covering all four sections of Shulchan Aruch came to light. Rather than issue a separate supplement, the editors of the 5665 Vilna edition of Shulchan Aruch HaRav incorporated them as an appendix to that printing — making the new Vilna edition an essential reference for halachic researchers.
