# Rebbe Rayatz founds Agudat Chasidei Chabad in Eretz Yisrael

*1941*

On 19 Tammuz 5701 (July 1941), during WWII, Rebbe Rayatz issued the founding letter (Igrot Kodesh vol. V, 1482) establishing Agudat Chasidei Chabad in Eretz Yisrael. He called for branches in every city to host public chassidus classes and gatherings, and appointed R. Shaul Dov Zislin, R. Moshe Gurary and R. Chaim Yosef Rosenblum as the founding committee.
