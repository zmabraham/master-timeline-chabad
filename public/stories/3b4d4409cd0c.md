# Rebbe Rayatz writes about move to Poland for sake of yeshiva

*1933*

After years of failed plans to bring Tomchei Temimim closer to him (Riga in 1931, Otwock in 1931), the Rebbe Rayatz finally decided to move from Riga to Warsaw. In this letter he wrote of his 'innermost yearning' to dwell amid the tent and surroundings of Torah, identifying proximity to the yeshiva as the central motivation for the move.
