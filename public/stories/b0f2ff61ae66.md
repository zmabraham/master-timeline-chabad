# Tzemach Tzedek becomes gravely ill

*1859*

Beis Rebbi records that the Tzemach Tzedek 'became very ill in the month of Kislev 5620 and was ill for more than six straight years.' During this period he transferred most halachic answering to his son R. Yisrael Noach (Maharin), who would present harder cases back to his father.
