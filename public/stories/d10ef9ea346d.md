# WWII breaks out; German bombs fall on Otwock during yeshiva founding-day gathering

*1939*

Rabbi Dov had led the Tomchei Tmimim bochurim in a yeshiva-anniversary farbrengen through the night of 17 Elul. At dawn, walking back from the mikveh half an hour away, the students heard explosions they first mistook for Polish drills. By morning it was clear that Germany had invaded Poland, bombs had hit Jewish neighborhoods, and the Second World War had begun.
