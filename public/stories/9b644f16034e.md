# Rabbi Levi Yitzchak Schneerson released from Soviet exile

*1944*

Rabbi Levi Yitzchak Schneerson was released from Soviet exile in 5704 (1944). A photograph taken after his release so changed his appearance that the Rebbe, on first seeing it, wrote in disbelief 'AAZ"L?' — 'is this truly my late father?' During his exile his wife Rebbetzin Chana joined him and produced ink from local plants so he could write his Torah glosses.
