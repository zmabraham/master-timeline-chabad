# Reprint of Hachaltzu for 127th birthday of the Rebbe Rashab

*1987*

For the 127th birthday of the Rebbe Rashab, the Hachaltzu kuntres was reprinted in a special edition. During its distribution to chassidim, the memoirist had time only to tell the Rebbe, 'This is also the first kuntres I printed,' before R' Leibel Groner urged him along to avoid holding up the long line.
