# Shaglov publishes index of the Rebbe's maamarim, sichos and letters

*1980*

The volume, titled 'Maftech Maamarim Sichos uMichtavim,' continued the line of chronological registries begun by the Rayatz for earlier Rebbeim. By the early 1980s registries had been compiled for all the Rebbeim except the Tzemach Tzedek, whose maamarim posed special chronological difficulties because most were preserved undated in volumes organized by Tanach and Chazal rather than by year.
