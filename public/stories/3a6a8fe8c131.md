# R' Chaim Yeshaya Schneersohn passes away in Romny

*1927*

R' Chaim Yeshaya, a great-grandson of the Tzemach Tzedek through the Maharil of Kopust, had served Romny since 5661. His passing left the Romny rabbinate vacant during the harsh Soviet years, eventually leading to R' Yani Gurarie being offered the position.
