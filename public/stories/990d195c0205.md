# Greenglass reports to Rebbe on Telz mission

*1940*

Greenglass described placing the young orphan at the Telz yeshiva with the help of R. Tzvi Merkin and Yehuda Leib Mintz, the Chai Elul farbrengen he had led, and his shiur in Tanya chapter 29 with local Hassidic-lineage students. He also reported on a new student, Yehuda Leib Mintz, with whom he had begun studying maamarim.
