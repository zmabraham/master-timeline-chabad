# Tomchei Tmimim fundraising crisis after 1905 Russian pogroms

*1907*

In a summer 5667 (1907) letter to R' Gershon Kopilovitz, R' Avraham Chaim explained that some 500 yeshiva students studied at Tomchei Tmimim in Lubavitch under the direct supervision of the Rebbe Rashab. With Russian pogroms devastating the donor base, the yeshiva faced terrible shortages, and R' Avraham Chaim called on American supporters to commit to regular annual pledges.
