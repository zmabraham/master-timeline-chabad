# Vilna kahal declares cherem against the Chassidim on Hoshana Rabba

*1797*

One day after the Vilna Gaon's burial, the Vilna kahal proclaimed a sweeping cherem on the Chassidim of the city. The decree, read in every shul amid shofar blasts and extinguished candles, declared Chassidim excommunicated, denied them communal standing, and barred their bread and wine. A committee of five empowered as 'rodef' was authorized to act against them, marking the legal start of the Vilna persecution.
