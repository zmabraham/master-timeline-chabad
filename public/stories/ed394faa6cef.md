# Publication of 'Toldos Avraham Chaim' book

*1997*

At the start of 5758, the book 'Toldos Avraham Chaim' was published, including on pages 62-64 the detailed will of R' Avraham Chaim. The will requested burial in Achuzas Olam cemetery in New York. Following publication, various searches were undertaken to locate the original Chabad cemetery plot in New York.
