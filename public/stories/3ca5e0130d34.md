# Passing of the Alter Rebbe

*1812*

On 24 Tevet 5573, the Alter Rebbe Rabbi Schneur Zalman of Liadi passed away while displaced by Napoleon's advance into Russia. He was succeeded by his son, Rabbi Dovber, the Mitteler Rebbe, who continued his father's joint efforts with the Berditchever line—including the parallel program of support for Chassidim in Eretz Yisrael, now coordinated with Rabbi Yisrael of Pikov, Rabbi Levi Yitzchak's son and successor.
