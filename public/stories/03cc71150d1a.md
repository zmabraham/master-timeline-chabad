# R' Avraham Chaim Rosenbaum released from prison

*1890*

After his arrest in 5648, R' Avraham Chaim Rosenbaum remained imprisoned until the end of 5650 (1890). During this period he wrote numerous letters, including one on Thursday Parshat Tzav 5650 in which he discussed plans to travel via Lubavitch upon release, and another describing his elderly father counting the moments until they would meet again.
