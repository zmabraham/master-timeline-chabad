# Rebbe's postcard from prison reaches family

*1927*

On Shabbat, June 25, 1927, a postcard the Rebbe had written from Shpalerka was delivered to his family in Leningrad. Despite repeated beatings and excruciating pain, the message was upbeat. He asked his family to send him sefarim, including Likkutei Torah and a Chumash Bemidbar.
