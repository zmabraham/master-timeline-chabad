# Founding of Chevrat Trumat HaKodesh in USA

*1833*

In early 5593 (1833), the Chevrat Trumat HaKodesh society was established in the United States, modeled on the Amsterdam Pekuam. Like its European counterpart, it sought to consolidate American Eretz Yisrael fundraising and discourage independent shadarim, withholding allocations from communities that hosted unauthorized envoys. The organization later interacted closely with the Pekuam and the Hebron Chabad kollel.
