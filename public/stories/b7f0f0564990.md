# R. Israel of Polotzk sent as shadar to raise funds in the Diaspora

*1777*

Within weeks of the group's arrival in Tzfat, the dire poverty of the settlers and the many poor who had attached themselves to the aliyah forced R. Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk to send R. Israel of Polotzk back to the Diaspora as a shadar. In a letter, R. Menachem Mendel wrote that if they received 10,000 monthly it still would not suffice. R. Israel left Eretz Yisrael in Marcheshvan 5538 and traveled through Constantinople to raise funds for the new chassidic settlement.
