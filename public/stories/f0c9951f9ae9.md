# Tzava'at HaRivash printed in Zhovkva

*1795*

Tzava'at HaRivash, a collection of practices and teachings attributed to the Baal Shem Tov, was printed in Zhovkva in 5555 (1795). This work would later become a flashpoint of controversy when Avigdor of Pinsk cited it in his denunciations against the Alter Rebbe.
