# Tomchei Tmimim yeshiva opens in Homel

*1921*

After receiving the Rebbe Rayatz's approval, the Homel chassidim engaged R' Yechezkel Feigin — who had recently moved from Kremenchug to his mother-in-law's home — to serve as rosh yeshiva, with his salary paid in flour because of the famine. The yeshiva opened on Chanukah in the Babushkin shul, the only synagogue willing to host the students out of fear they would break the windows and benches. For its first three weeks the only student was Hillel Sklyar, son of R' Avraham Yaakov Sklyar of Zlobin.
