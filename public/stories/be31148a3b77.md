# R' Yosef Eliezer Friedman appointed rabbi of Hadiach

*1894*

In 5654, Rabbi Yosef Eliezer Friedman became rabbi of Hadiach, the city associated with the burial place of the Alter Rebbe. The post had been considered for R' Isser Yanovsky two years earlier, when Rashab wrote on 15 Adar 5652 endorsing such an appointment, but that arrangement never came to fruition.
