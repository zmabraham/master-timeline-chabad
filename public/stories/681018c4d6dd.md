# Rebbe Rayatz blesses students on 8 Adar Sheni

*1946*

On 8 Adar Sheni 5706 (Igrot Kodesh vol. 13, letter 4901), the Frierdiker Rebbe replied to the Shanghai students' telegram that they were expected to receive the U.S. visas. He blessed them to receive them with ease and success in material and spiritual matters, that G-d would arrange a means of travel, and that they would arrive and become settled in a complete order of Torah study and avodah she-balev, and engage in spreading Torah and yiras Shamayim.
