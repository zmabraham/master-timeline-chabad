# Max Lilienthal opens first German-style Jewish secular school in Riga

*1839*

At the time, there were fewer than a thousand Jews in Riga. According to Jewish Museum director Ilja Lensky, the community wanted a secular Jewish school, possibly under Russian government pressure for a less religious education system. From that beachhead Lilienthal attempted to transform Jewish education throughout Russia, and by 1927 secular-oriented Jewish schools dominated Riga.
