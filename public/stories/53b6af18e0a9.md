# R. Shmuel Zalmanov resigns from yeshiva administration in Warsaw

*1932*

Having taken on private business obligations, R. Shmuel Zalmanov informed the Rebbe at the start of Marcheshvan 5693 that he could no longer manage yeshiva administration. From Rosh Chodesh Kislev he ceased most involvement, and the burden fell on R. Yehuda Eber until a new manager could be appointed.
