# Aliyah of R' Yisrael ben Mordechai from Shklov to Hebron

*1833*

In 5593 (1833), R' Yisrael ben Mordechai, age 25 and married with a son and daughter, made aliyah from Shklov to Hebron. His father R' Mordechai had preceded him by two years, arriving in 5581. This R' Yisrael Yaffe of Shklov is the third Hebron-based figure of that name in the period.
