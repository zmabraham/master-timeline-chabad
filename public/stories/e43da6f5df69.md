# R' Avraham Avli Persson dispatched to lobby JCA

*1900*

The Rebbe Rashab chose R' Avraham Avli Persson of Konigsberg to lead the JCA lobbying effort. Persson was to visit Vilna's R' Chaim Ozer Grodzinski, Brisk's R' Chaim Soloveitchik, and Lodz's R' Eliyahu Chaim Meisel to obtain their letters to JCA, then continue to Frankfurt, Karlsruhe, Halberstadt, Fulda, Baden, and finally Paris itself.
