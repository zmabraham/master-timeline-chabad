Rabbi Chaim Yaakov Delfin. Rabbi Chaim Yaakov Delfin (born in 5721, 1961) is a popular lecturer and author, a Chassidic mentor in the Jewish community in Borough Park, and founder of the Museum of Jewish Culture in Jerusalem.
