# Toras Emes yeshivah reconstituted in Jerusalem

*1922*

Before his passing in 1920, the Rashab had instructed Havlin to return to Eretz Yisrael as soon as possible. The yeshivah, originally founded in Hebron in 1911 but disrupted by World War I, was now re-established in Jerusalem. Today it remains a flourishing center of Jewish scholarship.
