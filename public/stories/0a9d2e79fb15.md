# R' Shraga Feivel Mendlowitz turns against Chabad influence at Torah Vodaas

*1933*

After introducing Kuzari, Chovos Halevavos, Noam Elimelech and Tanya into Torah Vodaas, Mendlowitz objected to the author 'turning students into Lubavitcher chassidim.' He led a group of students, joined by several baalei batim, to R' Chaim Avraham Dov Levin — the Malach — who served as rav in the Nusach Ha'ari shul in the Bronx. R' Moshe David Rivkin was later sent to ask the author to stop teaching Chassidus to the students.
