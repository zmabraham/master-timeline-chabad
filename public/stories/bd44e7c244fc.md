# Rebbe Rayatz issues general letter pledging not to rest until students saved

*1940*

Writing his klali letter in Nisan 5700 (Igros Kodesh vol. 5, letter 1181), the Rebbe Rayatz described in Yiddish the terror of being trapped under bombs in Warsaw with hundreds of his Tomchei Tmimim students. He declared his life-mission upon being rescued: to do everything in his power to save every dear talmid chacham still left behind in Poland.
