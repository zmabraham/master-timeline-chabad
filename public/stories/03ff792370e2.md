# The shluchim couple Markowitz of Tirat Carmel won the lifetime achievement award

*1982*

The shluchim couple Markowitz of Tirat Carmel won the lifetime achievement award. As written on the municipality's website: The Markowitz couple arrived in Tirat Carmel on the shlichus of the Rebbe of Lubavitch in 5742 - 1982, and since then have made it their home and the center of their activities
