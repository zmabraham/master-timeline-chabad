# Ibrahim Pasha becomes governor of Eretz Yisrael

*1833*

Following the successful conquest, from 5593 until 5600 Ibrahim Pasha served as the general governor of the territory conquered by Egypt. His rule brought significant changes to the Jewish communities of Eretz Yisrael, though it would later be marred by the violent suppression of local rebellions including the events of 5594 in Hebron.
