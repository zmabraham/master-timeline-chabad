# Rebbe Rashab relocates from Lubavitch with his family

*1915*

On Sunday Parshas Chayei Sara 5676, the day after delivering the closing maamar of his great Hemshech, the Rebbe Rashab departed Lubavitch with his entire family. The town of Lubavitch had been the Rebbes' seat since the Mitteler Rebbe; the departure marked the end of the Lubavitch era as the front lines of WWI advanced.
