# Rashab announces affordable Likkutei Torah project

*1902*

In a letter dated 6 Iyar תרס"ב the Rashab explained the motivation behind the fifth edition: existing copies cost chassidim 5-6 silver rubles with great difficulty in obtaining them, while the new edition would be priced around 2 rubles and well-proofed. The project reflected his broader concern with making Chabad chassidic texts widely available.
