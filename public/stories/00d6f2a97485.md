# Rebbe Rayatz returns to study after six-week heart illness

*1944*

On 7 Tevet 5705 (23 December 1944), after about six weeks of heart illness, the Rebbe Rayatz returned to his study for the first time, reading books although still feeling weak. He prayed for strength to resume spreading Torah with fear of Heaven, strengthening institutions, kosher education, guiding Anash, and editing his memoir manuscripts.
