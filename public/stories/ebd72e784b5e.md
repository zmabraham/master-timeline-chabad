# Sephardic Chief Rabbi appeals to Tzemach Tzedek's sons for kollel support

*1867*

Following the Tzemach Tzedek's passing, Hebron and Jerusalem Chabad communities feared the loss of their crucial support. Sephardic Chief Rabbi Chaim David Chazan dispatched R' Yehuda Leib Slonim to Lubavitch with his letter urging the rebbe's sons to assume their father's role. He warned that without their oversight Chabad communities would become 'like sheep without a shepherd' and praised how splendid the kollelot had been until then.
