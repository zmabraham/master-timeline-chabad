# R. Yechiel Meir Blumenfeld appointed rav at Tachkemoni in Warsaw

*1923*

Blumenfeld's family had been expelled from Poland to Russia during World War I, where they encountered Chabad and became sympathizers. After returning to Warsaw he helped establish Tomchei Tmimim and gave shiurim there in Maseches Shabbos before his Tachkemoni appointment.
