# Death of Rabbi Refael Oved ibn Tzur, son of the Yaavetz

*1769*

On Friday afternoon, 19 Iyar 5529 (May 1769), Rabbi Refael Oved ibn Tzur passed away in Fez. He had inherited his father's rabbinic mantle, served as a senior dayan, and continued the family's marriage-registration practice. His own son Rabbi Shlomo Shmuel ibn Tzur recorded the entry in the family pinkas, citing his grandfather the Yaavetz's note that Refael Oved had been born on 3 Shevat 5466.
