# First meeting of Vaad Magen UMekhaseh held in Riga

*1927*

The committee included Rabbi Yosef Rosen (the Rogotchover Gaon), Rabbi Meir Hildesheimer of Berlin, and Rabbi Mendel Zak of Riga. It later gained the backing of Rabbis Yisrael Meir Kagan and Chaim Ozer Grodzinski, and organized worldwide fasts and prayers for Soviet Jewry.
