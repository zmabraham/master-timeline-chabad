# Rayatz writes to R. Zalman Yitzchak Volshanik of Riga about Kollel

*1924*

On 3 Adar Sheni 5684 (March 1924), Rebbe Rayatz wrote letter 191 to R. Zalman Yitzchak Volshanik of Riga. He explained that he had purposely refrained from replying earlier, overwhelmed by his struggle to sustain Torah scholars under Soviet conditions, and emphasized that the kollel must focus on supporting scholars rather than on the schism between the two kollels.
