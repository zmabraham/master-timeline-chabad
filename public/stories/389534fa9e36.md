# GPU arrests Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn in Leningrad

*1927*

Minutes past midnight on June 15, 1927 (14 Sivan 5687), GPU officers Nachmanson and Lulov, both from Chabad families, led a contingent of armed men into the sixth Rebbe's Leningrad apartment. After a tense search lasting an hour and a half during which the Rebbe insisted on first reciting grace and warned the officers they would regret the arrest, he was taken at around 2:20 a.m. to the notorious Shpalerka prison. By taking a wrong corridor inside the prison, the Rebbe miraculously avoided being grouped with twelve other clergy who were summarily shot that night.
