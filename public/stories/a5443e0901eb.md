# R' Moshe Dovber Rivkin appointed senior teacher at Torat Emet

*1924*

In summer 5684 (1924) Torat Emet expanded and needed an additional rosh metivta. Just then R' Moshe Dovber Rivkin made aliyah and settled in Jerusalem. The Frierdiker Rebbe wrote to him on 2 Elul approving his joining the yeshiva, and to Havlin on Erev Rosh Chodesh Marcheshvan 5685 instructing that distinguished students be placed under his guidance. He served in that role for about three years before relocating to America.
