# MaharI"L approbates Kapust edition of the Siddur

*1822*

The 5582 (1822) Kapust edition of the Alter Rebbe's siddur, produced by the printer R' Yisrael Yaffe, carried MaharI"L's published haskama. The approbation was one of MaharI"L's continuing roles as guardian of his late brother's textual legacy.
