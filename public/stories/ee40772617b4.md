# Chabad summer camp ends, campers return to Moscow

*1991*

The camp's closing program had included a writing exercise where every child sent a letter to the Rebbe, and an evening celebration combining brisos, pidyonei haben and bar mitzvos for children who had passed those milestones during the session. The staff had planned to fly by helicopter directly to Lubavitch after the buses left, but rainy weather grounded the helicopter and they returned to Moscow instead.
