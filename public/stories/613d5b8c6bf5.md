# Author's family arrives in Moscow for summer camp

*1991*

On Thursday evening 13 Tammuz 5751, the author's wife and children arrived from the U.S. together with 28 young men and women coming to staff the Ezras Achim summer camps throughout Russia. They also brought roughly 150 packages of food and supplies for the camps. With no help available, the delegation unloaded everything from the truck themselves at the Molodyozhnaya Hotel.
