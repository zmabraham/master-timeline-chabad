# Latest dated responsum in Mehadura Batra Even HaEzer manuscript

*1843*

In later years the Tzemach Tzedek began reorganizing his responsa according to the sections of the Shulchan Aruch, possibly to prepare them for print. Manuscript 1050 — labeled in his own hand 'Responsa Even HaEzer, mehadura batra, chelek 2' — was bound around the time of its latest dated responsum, 5603.
