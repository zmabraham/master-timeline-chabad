# R' Yisroel Chasdan secures place for library on freighter from Stockholm

*1941*

On Erev Rosh Chodesh Adar, R' Chasdan replied to the Rashag describing his joy at securing the shipment. He had explained to the Schenker director the long history of the books — through 'Edom' and beyond — and obtained agreement to load 12 tons of sefarim onto a freighter departing Stockholm the next week.
