# Kehot reissues the Tzemach Tzedek portrait

*1953*

In 5713 (1953), Kehot Publication Society reissued the Tzemach Tzedek's portrait. The plate was intentionally reversed so the right side of the coat would lie over the left (as Jewish custom dictates) and so that the book held in his hand would open from the right like a Hebrew text — corrections to the original gentile artist's depiction discussed in Chabad tradition.
