# Liberation of the Alter Rebbe — Yud-Tes Kislev

*1798*

On 19 Kislev 5559 (16 November 1798), Tsar Paul I ordered the release of Rabbi Shneur Zalman of Liadi from imprisonment in Petersburg. The day became known as Yud-Tes Kislev, celebrated annually as the 'Rosh Hashanah of Chassidut' marking the vindication of chassidic teachings.
