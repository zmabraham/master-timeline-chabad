# Vienna Haggadah 'Maadanei Melech' by Asher Horowitz of Dzikov

*1934*

In 5694 (1934), the Haggadah 'Maadanei Melech' by the Admor Asher ben Meir Horowitz of Dzikov, who was then residing in Vienna, was published by the Appel Brothers in Vienna. The 80-page octavo edition served the Dzikov-Ropshitz Chassidic community and disciples of the Horowitz dynasty. It became one of the notable Hungarian-Galician Chassidic Haggadot of the interwar period.
