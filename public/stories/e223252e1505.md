# Russian FM tells delegation Gorbachev will resolve Schneersohn books matter

*1991*

On Tuesday 22 Sivan 5751 (June 4, 1991), the foreign minister of the Russian Federation told Veronika by phone that Gorbachev had replied to the US embassy in Washington that he had decided to conclude the Schneersohn books matter positively. The delegation continued parallel work on recovering the Polyakov synagogue in Moscow and preparing the Chernobyl children's camp for the planned airlift to Israel. David Kogan arrived from Leningrad to help run the camp.
