# Meeting with Italian ambassador in Moscow

*1991*

On Wednesday 13 Menachem Av 5751, the Italian shluchim arrived for the kinus and went to meet Italy's ambassador in Moscow, asking the author to join. They first met his deputy, fully briefed on the seforim, then the ambassador himself, who likewise knew the file but lacked any record of Andreotti–Gorbachev exchanges from London. The ambassador requested community paperwork, but Veronika preferred to wait, expecting good news that evening.
