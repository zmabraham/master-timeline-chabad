# Shterna Sara writes cousins about Rayatz's bride and wedding delay

*1896*

Writing on 1 Elul 5646 to Rabbi Mordechai Dovber and Rabbi Schneur Zalman Schneersohn in Hebron, Shterna Sara praised Nechama Dina as 'charming and very observant' and explained that the family had given up major financial benefits to select the most proper young woman. She requested they pray for the family at the holy places before Rosh HaShanah.
