# Rebbe Rayatz sends $90 Tammuz subsidy to Vilna yeshiva

*1930*

The Rebbe Rayatz wrote to the Vilna yeshiva committee enclosing a check for $90 - the standard monthly $100 stipend, with $10 deducted for the gemach (free-loan fund). This documented one example of the systematic monthly support the Rebbe provided from Riga.
