# Gubenko delivers formal rejection of Chabad library petition

*1990*

On Zos Chanukah (2 Tevet 5751) the Chabad delegation in Moscow received Gubenko's formal rejection of their request to repatriate the Lubavitcher Rebbes' library held by the Lenin Library. The letter argued the books had become national property when nationalized in 1918–1919 and offered at most to provide copies after further consultation with jurists and scholars. The delegation immediately escalated through American senators, an Inyurkollegia legal opinion contradicting Gubenko, and outreach to Russian Federation officials, while continuing their hunger strike.
