# Alter Rebbe travels to the Maggid in Rovno in summer 5532

*1772*

After the Vilna and Shklov debates, the two leaders went up to their teacher in Rovno. The Alter Rebbe remained with the Maggid in Rovno, and later in Anipoli, until the Maggid's passing. He continued working on Hilchos Orach Chaim during this period and presented halachic novellae to the Maggid for approval.
