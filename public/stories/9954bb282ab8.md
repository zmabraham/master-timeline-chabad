# R' Aharon the Great returns to Eretz Yisrael

*1788*

R' Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk's last letter, written in Nisan 1788, reports that 'the ship arrived, thank G-d, with the aforementioned shadar and his companions... they came in peace in the middle of Adar I.' This confirmed R' Aharon the Great's return to Eretz Yisrael after his shadar mission.
