# Rebbe speaks again about Chabad books at Shabbat Vayechi farbrengen

*1991*

The Rebbe noted that even after the nations had ruled and promised the books' return, they had not yet been moved. He hinted at a gentile and several Jews actively blocking the transfer — 'woe to them' — and tied the delay to the geopolitical upheaval and the relocation of capitals across the former USSR.
