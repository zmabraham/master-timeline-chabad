# R' Yechiel Cohen sent as joint shadar to USA from Hebron

*1847*

In 5607 (1847), R' Yechiel Cohen was sent as a shadar to the United States, carrying credentials from both the Sephardic and Chabad communities of Hebron. He stopped in London on the way and met the philanthropist R' Asher b'R' Shimshon. The Pekuam later sought clarification from the Chabad kollel about the trip's fundraising results.
