# Komsomolskaya Pravda issues press release on Schneerson library

*1991*

On 26 Nisan 5751, the editor of Komsomolskaya Pravda issued a press release on the Schneerson library, with the text read on Soviet radio at 10:40 that morning. The statement declared that the Chabad library, which had belonged to the Rebbe Rayatz, had been located after decades, that academics agreed it should be returned, and that despite the Chabad delegation's four-month presence in Moscow, Minister Gubenko refused to meet them while the books deteriorated uncatalogued. A copy was faxed to the Rebbe in New York.
