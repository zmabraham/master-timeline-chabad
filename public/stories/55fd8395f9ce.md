# Founding of Mesivta R' Chaim Berlin after Chabad-led plan is blocked

*1935*

Parents from R' Chaim Berlin's yeshiva had approached the author to start a high school division combining limudei kodesh with secular studies, and R' Yitzchak Shmidman of Yeshivas Toras Chaim had pledged support. Brownsville and East New York rabbanim, fearing Lubavitch influence, secretly approached the young R' Yitzchak Hutner and the Stone Ave Talmud Torah baalei batim. They opened what became known as Mesivta R' Chaim Berlin with Hutner as Rosh Mesivta.
