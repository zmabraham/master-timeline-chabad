# Birth of Rebbe Maharash in Lubavitch

*1834*

Rabbi Shmuel was the youngest son of the Tzemach Tzedek and would become the only Chabad Rebbe born, living his entire life, and passing away in Lubavitch. His birth on 2 Iyar 5594 (1834) coincides with the day Chasidim later mark as a special date connected to him. He would later succeed his father as the fourth Rebbe of the Chabad-Lubavitch dynasty.
