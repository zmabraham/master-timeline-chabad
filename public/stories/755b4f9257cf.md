# Rebbe Rayatz departs Riga for Warsaw visit

*1928*

After the Warsaw Chabad community invited the Rebbe to settle there following his exit from Russia, he replied that this was impossible but he wished to visit. He set out from Riga in the morning, stopping in Dvinsk, Vilna, and other towns en route. The trip combined visits to yeshivas and Chasidim with meetings on Soviet Jewry relief.
