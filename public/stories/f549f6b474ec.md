# Rebbe Rashab opens letter campaign to youth

*1897*

In 5657, prior to founding Tomchei Tmimim, the Rebbe Rashab turned to youth and parents in a general letter (Igrot Kodesh vol. 1, letter 68) regarding their conduct and studies. He also waged battle against schools being established by the Society for the Diffusion of Enlightenment and by the ICA. This concern for the next generation motivated the founding of the yeshiva.
