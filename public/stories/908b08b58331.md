# Yevsektzia raid foiled at Moscow hotel

*1927*

The Rebbe had traveled to Moscow under tight secrecy to meet JDC representatives. After being smuggled between hotels, he met with Mark Semyonovitch Bashkov, an OGPU executive board member he had encountered on the train. When Yevsektzia officer Krotov burst in with armed men, Bashkov produced his credentials — and the would-be arresters 'stood like blocks of wood, as if thunderstruck' and fled.
