# Marriage of Rivka bat Oved ibn Attar to Shmuel ben Nachmosh

*1712*

On Friday, 17 Kislev 5473 (Dec 6, 1712), Rivka bat Oved — a granddaughter of Rabbi Yehuda ibn Attar through his son Oved — was married to Shmuel ben Nachmosh of the Mayara family in Fez. The marriage was registered in the records of Rabbi Yaakov ibn Tzur (the Ya'abetz).
