# Rabbi Gedaliah Hertz. The Gaon and Chassid Rabbi Gedaliah Hertz (Tevet 5674 – 6 Shevat 5738, January 14, 1978) was a graduate of the Tomchei Tmimim Yeshiva of Warsaw, the first rosh yeshiva of Chiddushei HaRim Yeshiva, and one of the influential figures in Ger Chassidus in Israel
