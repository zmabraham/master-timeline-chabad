# Alter Rebbe writes letter to chassidim of Vilna defending the movement

*1797*

The letter (Iggeret 34) is a key source for the early conflict, describing how the Vilna Gaon twice closed his door on R. Menachem Mendel and the Alter Rebbe and left town to avoid public debate. It frames the chassidic response to renewed persecution near the end of the century.
