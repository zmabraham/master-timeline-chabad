# Hebron Chabad letter on famine and war

*1846*

On 13 Shevat 1846 (י"ג שבט תר"ו), Chabad leaders in Hebron wrote a desperate appeal describing a heavy famine in the Holy Land. War between local rulers had been ongoing for nearly a year, preventing the import of grain, leaving women and children begging for bread. The letter was later reproduced by the Pekidim u'Amarkalim in their fundraising circulars.
