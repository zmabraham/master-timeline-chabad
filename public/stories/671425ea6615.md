# Fez synagogue formally installs Yaavetz as permanent rabbi

*1697*

In 5457 (1697) the congregation of the Yaavetz's Fez synagogue ratified a sweeping rabbinic appointment installing him as their permanent leader, with the position to pass to his descendants 'until the coming of Messiah.' The signed contract — which also turned over three Torah scrolls and the entire synagogue endowment to his discretion — is preserved in the Ibn Tzur archive at the Library of Agudat Chasidei Chabad.
