# GPU arrests Rebbe Rayatz at his Leningrad apartment

*1927*

Officers Mikhail Nachmanson and Grigory Lulov, both from Chabad families, led a contingent of armed men in a search lasting an hour and a half. The Rebbe recited grace slowly to allow family members to conceal incriminating documents. Despite his daughters' pleas and his mother Shterna Sara's tearful outburst, he was taken at 2:20 a.m. to the notorious Shpalerka prison.
