# R' DovBer Efrat issues shochet certification in Hebron

*1891*

On 13 Kislev 1891 (י"ג כסלו תרנ"ב), R' DovBer Efrat gave a relative a shochet u'bodek certification. The document opens with his statement that the Almighty had granted him to come to the Holy City and notes the duration of his residence there, confirming his arrival shortly before.
