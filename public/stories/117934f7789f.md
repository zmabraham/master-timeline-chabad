# Passing of R' Tzvi Hirsh Padnes, treasurer of Shomrei Shabbos

*1945*

R' Tzvi Hirsh Padnes, who served as treasurer (gizbor) of the Chabad Shomrei Shabbos gemach in New York, passed away on 11 Shevat 5705 and was buried on 12 Shevat. R' Yochanan Gordon wrote a touching biographical letter about him to the Rebbe Rayatz, describing how Padnes had been orphaned young in Russia, served four years in the Russian army at Libau, fled the Russo-Japanese War to America, and spent his evenings emptying Rabbi Meir Baal HaNess pushkas for Chabad and visiting the sick. The Rebbe Rayatz responded with letters of consolation (Igros Kodesh vol. 8, nos. 2630-2631).
