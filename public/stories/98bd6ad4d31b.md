# R. Shneur Zalman of Lublin, author of Toras Chesed, passes away

*1902*

R. Shneur Zalman of Lublin lived in a house in Jerusalem purchased for him by R. Yeshaya Berlin and dedicated to Kollel Chabad. His passing on the night of 5 Nisan 5662 prompted the Jerusalem trustees to propose using his house as a new Chabad yeshiva. This proposal was the catalyst for the multi-year discussion that culminated in establishing Chabad yeshivas in Eretz Yisrael.
