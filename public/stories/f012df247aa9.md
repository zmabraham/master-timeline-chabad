# Rebbe responds to Berchen on Latvian yeshivot

*1940*

Replying on 5 Tammuz 5700 (Igrot vol. 12, no. 4426), the Frierdiker Rebbe expressed great satisfaction at hearing news of the talmidim at Dankra and Livani. He asked Berchen to convey his brachos to the students for health and success in Torah, and to their teachers and supporters.
