# Second great fire destroys the Lubavitch courtyard

*1868*

Two years after Maharash took up leadership, in summer 5628 (1868), the Chabad courtyard in Lubavitch suffered another major fire, destroying the houses and many of the Tzemach Tzedek's seforim and manuscripts. This created a legal-halachic dilemma because the Tzemach Tzedek's will had stipulated that none of his descendants live in his house — only Torah study or a beis midrash should occupy the site.
