# Soviet justice minister tells Chabad mission he cannot help on library

*1991*

On 3 Chol HaMoed Pesach 5751, the Chabad mission tried again to obtain a written statement from Justice Minister Lushchikov, who had privately supported the legality of returning the library. Reached by phone, the minister told Rabbi Kogan that he recognized the justice of Chabad's claim but that the issue had been taken over by senior political figures and was beyond his ability to influence. The delegation pivoted toward a press campaign instead.
