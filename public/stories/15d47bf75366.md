# R. Feigin writes Tel Aviv Chabad about building purchase

*1937*

On 28 Tishrei 5698 (October 3, 1937), secretary R. Yechezkel Feigin wrote to the heads of the Chabad community in Tel Aviv urging quick action on the building purchase. He explained that the Rebbe Rayatz had departed from his usual practice and personally assigned set sums to each chassid and student as founding members of the campaign.
