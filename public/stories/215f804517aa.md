# Passing of Rebbetzin Rivka, wife of the Maharash, 10 Shevat 5674

*1914*

Rebbetzin Rivka, wife of the Rebbe Maharash, passed away on Friday morning, 10 Shevat 5674. She had caught a cold earlier in the week, which developed into pneumonia; Dr. Lieberman of Vitebsk was summoned, and on Thursday night R. Nachum Glass traveled to Vitebsk to bring oxygen, but she died that Friday morning. The Rebbe Rashab was in Menton at the time and the Rayatz led the levaya, choosing a plot opposite the ohel of the Tzemach Tzedek and the Maharash, conditional on the Rebbe Rashab's approval.
