# Alter Rebbe becomes sole leader of Russian-Lithuanian Chassidim

*1782*

About a year after the three-leader appointment letter, Rabbi Yisrael of Polotsk passed away around 5543, and Rabbi Yissachar Ber of Lubavitch deferred to the Alter Rebbe. Rabbis Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk and Avraham of Kalisk, writing from Eretz Yisrael, then designated him sole leader of Russian and Lithuanian Chassidim. In the same year he settled in his hometown Liozna as maggid-meisharim, where he would lead Chabad until 5561.
