# R' Eber sends Rayatz first report on Warsaw student examinations

*1931*

On 10 Iyar 5691, R' Yehuda Eber reported to the Rebbe Rayatz on his progress examining students at Tomchei Tmimim Warsaw. He had finished testing the younger students in the shiurim taught by R' Naftali and R' Dovid, and had begun examining the older students of the 'Kibbutz' (advanced division), enclosing a detailed list.
