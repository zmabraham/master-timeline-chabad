# Rebbe announces Soviet recognition of Agudas Chassidei Chabad

*1990*

On Shabbat Vayishlach 5751 the Lubavitcher Rebbe declared at a farbrengen that the Soviet government had officially recognized Agudas Chassidei Chabad as a separate entity, provided it with a building, and committed to return the seforim, manuscripts, and correspondence of the Chabad rebbes. He compared the return to retrieving a pledge and urged that pikuach nefesh of pidyon shvuyim justified non-Jewish officials working on the matter even on Shabbat. The announcement framed the Moscow mission's pursuit of the Schneersohn collection.
