# R' Tzvi Hirsh Lehrin notifies Hebron Chabad of Mindel's London oil endowment

*1832*

R' Tzvi Hirsh Lehrin, who headed the Pekidim and Amarkalim in Amsterdam, informed the Hebron Chabad leadership of a new fund obtained through his London trip that summer. Mrs. Mindel daughter of Yehudah of London consecrated 25 pounds as keren kayemet whose interest would purchase oil for lamps in the community's synagogue and beit midrash. The arrangement was made through R' Asher ben Shimshon, a regular benefactor of Hebron Chabad.
