# Rayatz welcomes Levin's arrival in Eretz Yisrael

*1949*

Levin wrote to the Rebbe Rayatz on 16 Av 5709 reporting that he had reached Eretz Yisrael and settled in Kfar Sefaria, where he worked irrigating orchards. The Rebbe's reply, dated 27 Menachem Av, blessed the family with material and spiritual sustenance. The signature notably emphasized the letter yud, a usage Rayatz adopted from Shevat 5709 onward.
