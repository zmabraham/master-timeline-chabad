# Tomchei Tmimim children's parade from 770 courtyard

*1945*

Rebbe Rayatz's network of Tomchei Tmimim chadarim in America held a children's gathering in the 770 courtyard, where the synagogue was later built. On 9 Iyar 5705 a parade was organized for them, departing from the courtyard. Surviving photographs from that period show R' Yitzchak Dovber Ushpal, R' Shneur Zalman Gourary, and R' Shmuel Isaac Popack with the children at the Eastern Parkway entrance.
