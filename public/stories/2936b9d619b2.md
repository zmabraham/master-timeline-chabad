# Vilna chassidim freed on fifth night of Chanukah

*1798*

Although the Alter Rebbe had been freed on 20 Kislev, his chassidim from Vilna only reached their homes a week later. Because Chanukah candle-lighting in that region precedes 3 pm, the release after lighting was attributed to the fifth night, and that date entered chassidic memory as the day of the Vilna chassidim's geulah.
