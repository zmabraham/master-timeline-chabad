# First $2,000 wartime aid transfer via the Mizrach channel from Riga

*1940*

Because direct money transfers from the US to occupied Europe were illegal, the Rebbe ran a hidden parallel-payment scheme: Mizrach gave dollars in Riga to R' Mordechai Chefetz, and the Rebbe matched the sum from New York to a beneficiary Mizrach designated. The arrangement was concealed in coded correspondence using terms like 'books,' 'Ben Kalman,' and 'RIVK,' and ultimately moved $5,500 through this single channel.
