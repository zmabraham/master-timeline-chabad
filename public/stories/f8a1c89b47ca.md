Rabbi Silverberg – Rabbi Eliyahu Nasan HaKohen Silverberg (born in 5733, 1973), is the rosh yeshiva of Mesivta Lubavitch Chicago (yeshiva ketana), and author of the 'Likut Biurim' series of books on the tractates of Shas studied in yeshivas
