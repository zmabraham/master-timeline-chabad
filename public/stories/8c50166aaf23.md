# Persian translation Haggadah 'Chukat HaPesach' for Bukharian Jews printed in Jerusalem

*1904*

In 5664 (1904), 'Chukat HaPesach' was printed in Jerusalem by Shmuel HaLevi Zuckerman, featuring readings for the entire month of Nissan, the Korban Pesach with an extract from Shimshon of Ostropoli, and a Persian translation of the Haggadah by Shimon Hakham. The 68-page work also included tales and songs related to Pesach intended to benefit the Bukharian Jewish community. A separately titled Persian translation of Shir HaShirim was appended at the end.
