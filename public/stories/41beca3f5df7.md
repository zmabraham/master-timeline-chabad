# Rebbe endorses Kuperstock as Globoke's Chabad rabbi

*1930*

In his handwritten letter of 8 Tishrei 5691 (published in Igros Kodesh vol. 17, letter 275), the Rebbe encouraged the Globoke chassidim who were upholding R. Menachem Mendel Kuperstock's appointment and blessed them with health and abundant livelihood. The letter survives only in part.
