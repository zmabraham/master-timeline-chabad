# Rabbi Yehuda ibn Attar adjudicates dilapidated property dispute

*1726*

On 25 Tammuz 5486 (July 25, 1726), Rabbi Yehuda ibn Attar wrote from Fez to the rabbis and leaders of Meknes regarding the case of Shlomo Ag'iani and Yosef Abuzaglu over the property known as 'Dar Mawlana' purchased from the royal court. A previous letter from him in the same period addressed an upper-story building in the 'Dar Bunfad' courtyard that had become structurally dangerous and required immediate sale or repair authorization.
