# Ruling Senate exonerates the Mitteler Rebbe of all charges

*1827*

On 28 November 1827, the Ruling Senate decreed that Kissin's denunciation had no legal basis and that Rabbi Berke Schneur was not guilty of any criminal offense, ordering him fully cleared. Khovansky's accompanying proposals regarding Jewish rabbis were referred to the First Chamber of the Senate for separate review. The Senate's decree was communicated to the Mogilev provincial government, formally ending the legal proceedings.
