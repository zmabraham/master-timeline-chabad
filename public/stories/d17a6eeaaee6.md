# R. Yechezkel Feigin summarizes branch finances on 18 Shevat 5695

*1935*

Feigin explained that the Warsaw kibbutz and top two classes ran on their own income, while four lower Warsaw classes plus the Vilna, Lodz and Patzanow branches were funded by the Rebbe's accounts. In times of severe shortage, R. Yehuda Eber would appeal directly to the Rebbe to prevent closures.
