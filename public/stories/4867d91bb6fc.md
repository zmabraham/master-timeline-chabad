# Dubna community settles annual Hebron pledge with emissary

*1823*

The Dubna kahal pinkas records that on Sunday 12 Av 5583 an emissary arrived from Hebron carrying a register of communal pledges, showing Dubna obligated to send 200 zehuvim annually. The community settled all accumulated dues, exempting future leaders from the obligation, and the emissary signed the pinkas. It is unclear whether the messenger was R' Shimon Shmerling, R' Leib of Belz, or another shadar 'connected to Hebron.'
