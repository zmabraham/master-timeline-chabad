# Rebbe Rayatz's first visit to the United States

*1929*

At the close of 5689 the Rebbe Rayatz arrived in America for his first visit, traveling among Jewish communities including St. Louis, where he received $600 from R' Shaul Dovber Zislin's brother toward Zislin's rescue from Russia. After this visit obtaining Soviet exit permits became markedly harder, and the author's rescue work in subsequent years yielded fewer successes. The Rebbe's response to one rescue request was that supernatural intervention would be required to bring the family out.
