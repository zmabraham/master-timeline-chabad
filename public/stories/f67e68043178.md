# Reburial of the Rebbe Rashab in Rostov

*1940*

Rabbi Levi Yitzchak Schneerson gave detailed instructions to a group of ten Chasidim, who fasted and performed the transfer late at night despite the danger of police discovery. Twenty years after the Rashab's passing, his body was found whole and intact, shocking the participants. Shmuel Gurary was also reinterred next to him.
