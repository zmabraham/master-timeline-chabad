# Rebbe writes coded inquiry to Dubin about fate of Riga anash

*1943*

Phrased to evade Soviet censors, the letter inquired after Chatche Feigin, Mottel the shochet Chefetz, Avraham Eli Asheroff, Elya Chaim Althaus, Mendel Zak, Avigdor Volshanik and others — all of whom, unknown to the Rebbe at that point, had already perished in the Riga massacres.
