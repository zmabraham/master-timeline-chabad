# Marriage of Shi'acha bat Yehuda ibn Attar to Shem Tov ben Avraham

*1711*

On Friday, 17 Cheshvan 5472 (Nov 7, 1711), Shi'acha bat Yehuda ibn Attar married Shem Tov, son of her uncle Avraham ibn Attar, in Fez. The wedding was recorded in the marriage register of Rabbi Yaakov ibn Tzur (the Ya'abetz). The match kept the ibn Attar family lineage close, with Shi'acha marrying her first cousin.
