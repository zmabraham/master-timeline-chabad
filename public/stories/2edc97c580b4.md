# Responsum 353 with conditional psak formula

*1832*

The Tzemach Tzedek wrote in §353 (5592) that 'because of the gravity of the matter, I do not want anyone to rely on me alone, but only with the concurrence of two famous poskim or at least one famous moreh hora'ah, and if [the case develops further], then... it appears to me to permit her, if three great poskim concur.' The responsum is a paradigmatic illustration of his halachic humility, the dominant feature of his style.
