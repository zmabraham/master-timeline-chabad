# Yeshivas Tomchei Temimim founded by Rebbe Rashab

*1897*

Historian Yehuda Geberer notes this was Chabad's leading response to 'the great crisis' — declining chasidic numbers in the interwar years. The yeshivah balanced talmudic study with chasidic philosophy, creating the model later expanded by the Rayatz across Poland and Lithuania.
