# Joint telegram requesting American ship berths

*1940*

On 30 May 1940 / 22 Iyar 5700, R. Yechezkel Feigin and R. Yehuda Eber jointly cabled the Frierdiker Rebbe requesting his help to book berths on an American vessel. They asked the shipping company to wire Cook's Riga office authorization to sell them tickets, and sought guidance on whether to try the Italian sailing if it materialized.
