# Chafetz Chaim transfers yeshivah back to Poland

*1921*

Having transferred the yeshivah to Russia in 1915 at the start of World War I, the Chafetz Chaim moved it back to Poland in 1921. Stopping in Minsk, he told the local rabbi Nissan Telushkin that he had no strength left to battle the malicious government and 'the evil renegades' forcing yeshivah students to desecrate Shabbat. Later in life he reportedly regretted leaving.
