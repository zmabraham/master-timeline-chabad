# Riga's Chabad community murdered by the Nazis

*1941*

Months earlier the Soviet conquest of Latvia had already cut off the package-shipment lifeline from Riga to Poland. With the German occupation that followed Operation Barbarossa, the Chabad community of Riga that had hosted the Rebbe in early 1940 was annihilated; the specific fates would only be confirmed gradually through later correspondence.
