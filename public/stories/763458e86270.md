# Montefiore prays at Chabad synagogue in Hebron

*1839*

During the Shabbat of Montefiore's visit, his party remained in their tents in the field. For the prayer services they went into the synagogues in the Jewish courtyard, and at Mincha Montefiore prayed in the Chabad synagogue. The community would later memorialize this event in a formal deed transferring his prayer spot in the synagogue to him as an eternal possession.
