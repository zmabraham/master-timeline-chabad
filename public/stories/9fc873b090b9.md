# The poet Zelda Mishkovsky-Schneersohn passes away

*1984*

Zelda Schneersohn Mishkovsky, the renowned Hebrew poet and only child of Rabbi Shalom Shlomo Schneersohn and Rachel Chen, passed away on 27 Nissan 5744. Childless, her literary estate including correspondence from the Rebbe and his father Rabbi Levi Yitzchak was scattered after her death. Years later, photocopies of these family letters were transmitted to the Chabad library.
