# R' Yani Gurarie sentenced to five months hard labor by Soviet authorities

*1928*

Following an earlier 5687 prosecution that was commuted by amnesty, the Yevsektsiya brought a fresh protokol against R' Gurarie in Teves 5688 and staged a public show trial (pokazatelni sud) on Pesach. Despite intense efforts, he was sentenced to five months of forced labor, after which the yeshiva closed.
