# Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn freed from Soviet imprisonment

*1927*

The author, then learning at the Kiev cheder katan, observed Rabbi Gorodetzky displaying a photograph of the Rebbe Rayatz to local balebatim and telling them it was a mitzvah to gaze upon him. He did not yet grasp the full significance, but the timing — soon after Yud-Beis Tammuz — places it just after the Rebbe's release from Soviet prison. The episode marks his earliest known exposure to the Frierdiker Rebbe.
