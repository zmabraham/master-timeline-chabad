# Six American students arrive at Tomchei Tmimim in Otwock

*1939*

Met in Paris by the Rebbe's son-in-law (the future Rebbe), the group continued by train through Europe, arriving at the Yeshiva on Rosh Chodesh Elul. Rabbi Shmuel Dovid Reitchik was the first to engage them in learning, and they were tested in both nigleh and Chassidus by Rabbi Berel Kornitzer.
