# Frierdiker Rebbe reports US visas approved in Riga

*1940*

The Frierdiker Rebbe informed his son-in-law in Paris that, thank G-d, all were alive and well, and that American visas had been arranged for the entire family. He proposed planning a joint journey to the United States. In practice, the Paris visas had only been promised, not issued, and the actual struggle to obtain them would continue for over a year.
