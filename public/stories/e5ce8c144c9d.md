# Eshelon No. 3 crosses into Poland at Przemyśl

*1946*

Soviet officials forced an unfavorable ruble-to-złoty rate at the border but the narrator and R. Michoel refused to risk delay and exchanged everything they had. Border guards merely called out names from a list without searching the cars, allowing undocumented young women hidden in the wheat fields to slip aboard. Once in Przemyśl Polish merchants paid three złoty per ruble, and the refugees realized they were finally out of Russia.
