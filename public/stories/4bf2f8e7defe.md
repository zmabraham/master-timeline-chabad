# Rabbi Taller (right) together with Rabbi Refael Tzvi Hartman (5782)

*1982*

Rabbi Taller (right) together with Rabbi Refael Tzvi Hartman (5782). Rabbi Yaakov Elimelech (Meilech) Taller (born 5742, 1982) is a public activist, secretary of the Chabad community committee in the Ramah Dalet neighborhood of Beit Shemesh, founder and gabbai of the local Chabad synagogue
