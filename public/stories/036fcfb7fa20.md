# First post-war Haggadah printed in Föhrenwald DP camp

*1945*

In 5705/5706 (1945–1946), the first Haggadah in liberated Europe was printed in Föhrenwald as a photo-offset reproduction of a Warsaw edition. Produced by the YPA publishing house, it was intended for the She'erit HaPleita displaced-person camps in Germany and was supplemented with illustrations taken from other Haggadot.
