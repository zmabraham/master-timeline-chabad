# Rashab's prophetic Purim-eve conversation with the Rayatz

*1920*

On 8 Adar 5680 (February 27, 1920), the Rashab summoned the Rayatz into his candlelit study. He foretold at least twenty-two years of dark clouds for Russian Jewry, predicted the eventual fall of the Soviet regime and specific fates for Lenin, Trotsky, and Stalin, and charged his son with self-sacrifice to defend Torah and Judaism.
