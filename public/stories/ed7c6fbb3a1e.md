# Bichowsky faction founds breakaway 'Kollel Chabad HaMeyuchad' in Jerusalem

*1914*

When the unity proposal required retaining the existing Jerusalem managers, members of the Bichowsky group refused, citing past suffering at the hands of those managers. The next day they published a kruz announcing the founding of 'Kollel Chabad HaMeyuchad' as a separate body, and stating that the din torah against the previous managers would be conducted only once R. Chaim Bagin — who was ill — arrived. The split between the two kollelim from this point became final.
