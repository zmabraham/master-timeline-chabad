# R' Havlin arrives in Jerusalem to refound Toras Emes

*1922*

After a circuitous four-month journey from Rostov through Tiflis, Batum and Constantinople, R' Shlomo Zalman Havlin arrived in Jerusalem on erev Shabbos 6 Tevet 5682. R' Menachem Mendel Naeh recorded his arrival in a 15 Tevet letter to the Rebbe. Toras Emes was reestablished in Jerusalem, where it continues to operate.
