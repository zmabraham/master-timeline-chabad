# Three students dispatched from Kutaisi to Kulashi for 19 Kislev 5693

*1932*

As the GPU crackdown intensified, the Haditcher dispersed students to lower-profile locations. The three were warmly received in Kulashi and learned in a tiny shul lit by a single kerosene lamp. Avraham Kievman was an extreme masmid who balanced his oil lamp by the clock so he could rise at 5 a.m. and continue until past midnight.
