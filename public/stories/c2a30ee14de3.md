# Remaining Kobe students write farewell letter to Montreal-bound talmidim

*1941*

On 3 Marcheshvan 5702 (October 24, 1941), the students who remained behind wrote to those headed for Montreal. They lamented that the rapid departure had not allowed time for proper farewells or to discuss yeshiva matters, but trusted it was hashgachah pratis that they had not parted for long, and reported that Erev Yom Kippur and Yom Kippur passed with their accustomed order.
