# Pereshel reproduction of the Fürth 1741 Haggadah

*1976*

In 5736 (1976), Tuvia Pereshel published in New York a facsimile reproduction of the 1741 Fürth Haggadah, which included a Yiddish translation. The 16-leaf octavo edition came with bilingual English and Hebrew introductions by Pereshel. It continued his annual project of resurrecting rare printed Haggadot.
