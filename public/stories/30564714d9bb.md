# Death of R. Dov Ber Fradkin

*1905*

Fradkin had been added to the roster of kollel trustees as an honorary member, signing letters of the kollel out of respect for the institution's prestige. His role brought distinction without operational responsibilities, as he was not directly engaged in the kollel's affairs. His death further consolidated the active leadership around Shlonk, Efrat, Menuchin, and Ornstein.
