# Mitteler Rebbe's letter about distribution of Maamad funds

*1814*

Following the passing of the Alter Rebbe, his son Rabbi DovBer documented the allocation of donor funds collected for the rebbe's household (the maamad). The letter assigned half-yearly living expenses and gifts to relatives. This document, smuggled to the informer Pinchas Schick of Shklov in 1822, became the central exhibit Shimcha Kissin used against the Rebbe a decade later.
