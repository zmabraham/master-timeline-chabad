# Rebbe released from Shpalerka and exiled to Kostroma

*1927*

On Sunday, July 3, 1927 (3 Tamuz 5687), the Rebbe left Shpalerka prison after 18 days, 11 hours, and 15 minutes of imprisonment. He had refused to leave on Thursday because the train would have forced him to desecrate Shabbat; Madam Peshkova's intercession through Premier Rykov and GPU chief Menzhinsky won him a delay until Sunday. After meeting Chasidim at home, an honor-guard of Leningrad Jews lined his path to the train, where he gave a defiant platform speech declaring that 'only our bodies were sent into exile.'
