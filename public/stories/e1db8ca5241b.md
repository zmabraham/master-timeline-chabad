# The Rebbe during the saying of the sicha. The famous sicha is a sicha delivered

*1991*

The Rebbe during the saying of the sicha. The famous sicha is a sicha delivered by the Rebbe on 28 Nissan 5751 (April 12, 1991), in which he expressed his sorrow over the apathy regarding bringing Moshiach, and placed upon the listeners the responsibility to act for his coming: "The only thing that still can"
