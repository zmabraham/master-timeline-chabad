# Mrs. Bila immigrates to Tzfat with orphaned sons

*1870*

After her husband R' Yeshaya Halevi Horowitz of Beshenkovichi died in 5624, Bila returned to her uncle the Tzemach Tzedek's home with her five young orphans. The Tzemach Tzedek corresponded with R' Aharon of Karlin and R' Aharon of Chernobyl, and together they agreed to support her settlement in the Holy Land. She then made aliyah to Tzfat with her sons, joining the renewed Chabad community there.
