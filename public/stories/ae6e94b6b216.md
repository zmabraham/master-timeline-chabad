# Rabbi Avraham Yitzchak Prudenstetsky at the inauguration of the Chabad House of Ganei Yochanan

*1983*

Rabbi Avraham Yitzchak Prudenstetsky at the inauguration of the Chabad House of Ganei Yochanan (5783). Rabbi Avraham Yitzchak Prudenstetsky (born in the year 5743, 1983) is the director of the Chabad House in the settlement of Ganei Yochanan
