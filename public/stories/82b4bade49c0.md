# Rebbe Rashab travels to Petrograd during February Revolution

*1917*

Having promised in mid-Tevet to travel within two weeks, the Rebbe arrived in Petrograd in Shevat and stayed until after Purim. During this period the February Revolution broke out, the Tsarist regime fell, and the democratic Provisional Government was established. No letters of his from Shevat-Adar 5677 survived.
