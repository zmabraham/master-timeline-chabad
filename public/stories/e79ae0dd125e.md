# Rebbe declares Mivtza Kashrut and Taharat HaMishpacha

*1974*

Continuing his series of mitzvah campaigns, in the year 5735 (1974–75) the Rebbe declared Mivtza Kashrut and Mivtza Taharat HaMishpacha. These campaigns aimed to strengthen observance of dietary laws and family purity among world Jewry.
