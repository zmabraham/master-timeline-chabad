# Hecht couple rents apartment near Rebbe's secretary in Otwock

*1938*

Following the Rebbe Rayatz's advice, the young couple moved out of the pension and rented an apartment in the courtyard where the Rebbe's secretary and the Rebbetzin also lived. The arrangement was deemed beneficial both for the wife, who would not be alone while her husband studied, and for the young couple's domestic guidance.
