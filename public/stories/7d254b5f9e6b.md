# R' Yehuda Leib Slonim, Kollel Chabad administrator, dies

*1893*

R' Yehuda Leib Slonim, who together with his brothers R' Levi Yitzchak and R' Mordechai Dovber managed Kollel Chabad's affairs in Hebron, traveled repeatedly to Lubavitch to coordinate funds and policy with successive rebbes. His passing on 27 Tammuz 5653, weeks after Hebron's chief rav, marked the end of an era for the community.
