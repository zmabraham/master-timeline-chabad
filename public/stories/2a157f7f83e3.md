# R. Lis writes from Shanghai pleading for news of wife and children

*1941*

Having reached Shanghai with the rest of the Tomchei Temimim refugees, Lis appealed to the Rebbe to help locate his family and send whatever aid was possible, since he himself could do nothing from East Asia. The Rebbe responded (Igrot Kodesh vol. 6, no. 1604) with consolation and a prayer for good news, but Golda and the daughters never escaped — they perished in the Holocaust while Lis survived alone and continued on to America.
