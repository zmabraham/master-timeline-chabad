# Mitteler Rebbe sends R' Shmerling to establish Hebron settlement

*1822*

According to tradition recorded in family histories, in Tishrei 5583 the Mitteler Rebbe sent R' Shimon Shmerling to found the Chabad settlement in Hebron. His son R' Moshe was engaged to Chana Devorah, and the Rebbe instructed him to travel via her town, not dismount the wagon, ask whether she would join them to Eretz Yisrael, arrange the wedding immediately, and bring her along - which he did.
