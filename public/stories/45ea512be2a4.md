# Sheina Paz dies in Moscow

*1961*

Sheina bat Mordechai, the wife of the Marina-Roshcha shamash R. Yekutiel Zusman 'Zushya' Paz, died on 28 Tammuz 5721 (July 12, 1961). She was buried beside her husband in Malachovka outside Moscow. Their daughter, who lived in a village near Moscow, had supplied chalav yisrael to the shul daily from her cow.
