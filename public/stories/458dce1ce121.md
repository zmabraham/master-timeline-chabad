# Yeshivat Torat Emet founded in Hebron with seven Tomchei Tmimim students

*1911*

Seven select students of Tomchei Tmimim — among them Alter Simchovitz, Hillel Pevzner, Tzvi Hirsch Katzav, Yechezkel Feigin, Menachem Krasik, Avraham Eli Asherov, and Yisrael Zalman Asnas — were dispatched in 1911 to found Yeshivat Torat Emet in Hebron. Nine additional students from Hebron and Jerusalem joined them. R' Shimon Glitzenstein later recalled enrolling in the yeshiva's first year, at the end of summer 5672.
