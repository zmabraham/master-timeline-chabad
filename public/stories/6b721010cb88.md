# Birth of Rabbi Menachem Mendel (Tzemach Tzedek) in Liadi

*1789*

Born on the eve of Rosh Hashanah 5550, the future Tzemach Tzedek would later become both nephew and son-in-law of the Mittler Rebbe. He would assume Chabad leadership in 1827 and guide the movement from Lubavitch for nearly four decades. His birth in Liadi predates the relocation of the Chabad center to Lubavitch.
