# First attempted Czech–German border crossing repulsed by U.S. MPs

*1946*

After Shabbat in Asch the Chabad group was driven by Bricha guides to the U.S.-zone border on Sunday Parshat Shoftim, 28 Av 5706. The original guards had been replaced and U.S. Military Police forced them — including pregnant women such as R. Pinchas Schreiber's daughter — back onto the truck. They returned to Asch and soon moved to a former Wehrmacht camp closer to the frontier to wait for another attempt.
