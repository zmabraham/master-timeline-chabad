# Rebbe Rashab travels to Mohilev for delegate elections

*1909*

The Rebbe Rashab journeyed to Mohilev to lead what was termed in local parlance a 'campaign' for delegate elections. The wealthy Reb Yehoshua Rozinkin built a new brick home for him with all new furnishings and even new silk bed coverings. Dignitaries of the city greeted the Rebbe at the train station with a carriage drawn by horses, as was customary for nobility.
