# R. Geleshrtein writes about new student Preger

*1938*

On 4 Iyar 5698 (May 5, 1938), R. Avraham Mordechai Gelershtein of Brisk wrote to yeshiva secretary R. Yosef Avraham Kagan urging acceptance of the student Menachem Nachum Preger of Brisk, describing him as an outstanding talmid with fine character and talent. Preger was accepted, studied in Otwock until the war's outbreak, later escaped to Vilna and then Japan and Shanghai, and ultimately reached New York.
