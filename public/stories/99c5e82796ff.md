# Rebbe Rayyatz urges restoring Tel Aviv cheder as Torat Emet branch

*1927*

Responding to a report about the diminished state of the Tel Aviv cheder, the Rebbe expressed sorrow that financial shortfall had shrunk the institution. He emphasized that the original plan was for Torat Emet to plant branches in every city in Eretz Yisrael, and asked the new local manager R' Uri to formally re-establish the Tel Aviv cheder as a branch of Torat Emet under Jerusalem's direction.
