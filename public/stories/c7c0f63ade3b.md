# Founding of Achos Tmimim in New York

*1938*

In 5698 the Chabad women's organization Achos Tmimim was founded in New York. R' Yochanan Gordon learned Chassidus with the members and led farbrengens for them. The Rebbe Rayatz appointed a va'ad of three to oversee the group: Rabbi Yisrael Jacobson, Rabbi Shlomo Aharon Kazarnovsky, and R' Yochanan Gordon.
