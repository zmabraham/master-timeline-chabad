# Mechina preparatory division founded at Tomchei Tmimim yeshiva in Warsaw

*1931*

After deliberations through summer 5691, the Rebbe Rayatz decided in Tishrei 5692 to keep the central Tomchei Tmimim yeshiva in Warsaw and add a Mechina branch for younger students. Classes opened on Sunday of Parshat Vayera with 10–11 boys, supplementing the existing two shiurim for ages 14–17 and the senior Olam for self-learners. Initial enrollment was limited because the new program was announced late.
