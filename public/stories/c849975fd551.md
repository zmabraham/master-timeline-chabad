# Rebbe writes Rizik recounting departure from Russia

*1927*

The Rebbe described thousands filling the Leningrad station, 'full of emotion,' surging toward the train. He wrote of passing 'eighteen hours with anguish in the heart, pain in the soul, the darkness of the night, the ringing of the wheels of the train only adding to the sense of sadness.' His spirits lifted when Latvian Chasidim boarded the train an hour from Riga and sang the joyous melody from his Lubavitch chuppah three decades earlier.
