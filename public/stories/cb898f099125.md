# Girls' letter to the Rebbe on Yud-Tes Kislev gathering

*1936*

The letter, photographed in Di Yiddishe Heim no. 88 (Tammuz 5742), opens 'We the daughters of Chabad chassidim celebrated this year as well the Chag HaGeulah Yud-Tes Kislev, as in the past year.' It shows that organized women's farbrengens preceded the formal naming of Achos Tmimim.
