# Identification of 845 new Lubavitch books in Moscow library

*2000*

On 16 Tammuz 5760, the emissary completed sorting through the 1,500 unidentified volumes in the main building, identifying an additional 74 Lubavitch books and bringing his two-visit total to about 845. The team also began affixing 'Lubavitch Collection' stickers to the 3,000 books originally identified by library staff in 5752, completing 825 of them that day.
