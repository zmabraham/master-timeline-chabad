# Rebbe released for home visit and exiled to Kostroma (3 Tamuz)

*1927*

Continued lobbying by Madam Peshkova overturned the Solovki decree, and the sentence was reduced to three years in Kostroma. The Rebbe refused to leave on Thursday because the journey would violate Shabbat; he was kept until Sunday morning July 3. At Leningrad station thousands of Jews formed an honor guard as he gave a defiant speech declaring that only Jewish bodies, not souls, are subject to foreign rule.
