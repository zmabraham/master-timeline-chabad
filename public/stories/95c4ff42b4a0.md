# Students cable visa news on erev Shabbat Pekudei

*1946*

On erev Shabbat Parashat Pekudei, 5 Adar Sheni 5706, the Shanghai students cabled the Frierdiker Rebbe in Yiddish that UNRRA was reporting good prospects of obtaining the visas soon, but expressing doubt whether all 29 of them would be able to travel together. The number reflected the 27 students plus the wives of the two who were married.
