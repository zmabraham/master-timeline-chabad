# Rabbi Eliyahu Ki Tov (Mokotovsky). Rabbi Avraham Eliyahu Ki Tov

*1912*

Rabbi Eliyahu Ki Tov (Mokotovsky). Rabbi Avraham Eliyahu Ki Tov (formerly Mokotovsky, 4 Nissan 5672 – 6 Adar I 5736, 1912-1976) was a public activist who was among the people of PAGI (which split from PAI), editor of the journal "HaKol," an educator
