# R' Eliezerov writes blessing letter to new Hebron rabbi

*1893*

On 15 Av 1893 (ט"ו רחמים תרנ"ג), while serving as a shadar abroad, R' Shlomo Yehuda Leib Eliezerov (Shil"a) wrote a heartfelt blessing letter to R' DovBer Efrat congratulating him on his appointment as rabbi of Hebron. The letter wished him strength to lead with justice and uprightness.
