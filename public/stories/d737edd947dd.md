# Publication of Shivchei HaRav, early biography of the Alter Rebbe

*1864*

In 5624 (1864) the small biographical work Shivchei HaRav appeared in Lvov. It collected and edited oral traditions about the life of the Alter Rebbe that Chasidim had long preserved in private manuscripts. It became one of the foundational printed sources for the Alter Rebbe's biography.
