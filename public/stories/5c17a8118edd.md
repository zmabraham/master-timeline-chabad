# Rabbi Levi Yitzchak Schneerson begins exile glosses

*1939*

After being exiled by the Soviet government as punishment for spreading religion, Rabbi Levi Yitzchak Schneerson—Yekaterinoslav rabbi and father of the Rebbe—wrote Kabbalistic glosses on the few books he had with him from 1939 to 1944. He also sent his son letters and kuntreisim on Chassidut and Kabbalah from exile.
