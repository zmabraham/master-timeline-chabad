# Publication of the corrected Tanya edition (Vilna 5660)

*1900*

In 5660, Rashab published a corrected edition of the Tanya in Vilna. The proofreading was performed by R' Asher Grossman, shochet of Nikolayev, whom Rashab called 'the chief compiler' in letters of that year. R' Asher's signed 'Modaa Rabba' notice has appeared in every subsequent edition of the Tanya.
