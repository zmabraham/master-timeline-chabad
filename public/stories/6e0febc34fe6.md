# R' Meir Pereles completes Megillat Yuchasin in Prague

*1727*

Megillat Yuchasin, a key source on the descendants of the Maharal of Prague, was written by R' Meir Pereles in 5487 (he states 'until today, which is the year 5487'). The work omitted the male line through R' Yehuda Leib ben R' Shmuel, an omission that later researchers had to correct using Prague gravestone evidence.
