# Rebbe's sicha of Parshat Miketz on naming customs

*1982*

In a sicha delivered on Parshat Miketz 5743 (December 1982), printed in Hisvaaduyos vol. 43, the Rebbe Menachem Mendel addressed the differing customs between Sefardim and Ashkenazim regarding naming a child after a living grandparent. A follow-up question about whether this related to his own grandfather Baruch Schneur being named in his great-grandfather Baruch Shalom's lifetime was sent to him; he replied that he had not heard a tradition on the matter.
