# Latvian branches consolidate in Riga

*1940*

By Tishrei 5701 conditions in Dankra had become unbearable and the yeshiva relocated to Riga, where it joined the Livenhof branch and the Riga branch reopened in 5699. R. Berchen wrote the Rebbe on 6 Tishrei 5701 in coded language about his sons (the students) having left him for Riga to continue their studies, hinting at the move under Soviet censorship.
