# Sasson donation enables acquisition of the Beis Menachem synagogue

*1858*

Lacking a synagogue, the Chabad community in Jerusalem received 100 lira sterling from David Sassoon of India as a key-money donation to take the courtyard under a four-year mortgage. The courtyard contained a synagogue, women's section, mikveh, and other facilities. A commemorative inscription was installed on 20 Sivan 5618 honoring Sassoon's generosity.
