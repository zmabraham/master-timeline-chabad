# Rayatz nearly arrested at Moscow hotel

*1927*

Traveling secretly to meet JDC representatives, the Rebbe was spotted on the train by Mark Semyonovitch Bashkov, a senior OGPU member of Belorussian-chasidic descent. A friendly hotel manager warned the Rebbe at midnight and relocated him. Days later, Yevesektzia officer Krotov burst into the new room to arrest him, but Bashkov's OGPU credentials drove the agents off.
