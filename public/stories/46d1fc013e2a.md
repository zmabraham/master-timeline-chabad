# Rebbe suffers stroke at the Ohel

*1992*

While visiting the Ohel the Rebbe collapsed; Rabbi Krinsky entered, found him, and immediately called an ambulance and doctors. The Rebbe instructed that he be taken specifically to 770, where doctors treated him continuously. When his speech became unclear, doctors tried showing him siddur letters, but the Rebbe pushed the siddur away and worked to speak; speech returned slowly along with hand strength, suggesting the episode was mild and passing.
