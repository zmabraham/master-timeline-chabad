# Alter Rebbe urges refugees to farm

*1756*

In 1756, at the age of eleven, the future Alter Rebbe, Rabbi Schneur Zalman, urged refugees arriving in Russia from Prague to become farmers as a means of earning a stable livelihood. This early act of communal service initiated the 140-year tradition of communal work by the Lubavitcher Rebbes that the Rashab would later recount to his son at the Ohel. The episode established a lifelong pattern of practical leadership concerned with Jewish material welfare.
