# Warsaw leadership absorbs Otwock yeshiva as Tomchei Tmimim branch

*1926*

R. Feivel Zalmanov, R. Zalman Shmuktin and R. Tzvi Gurary HY"D came to Otwock on a Shabbos in Elul 5686, informed the teachers the yeshiva was now Tomchei Tmimim Otwock, and planned to bring in around twenty out-of-town students. R. Chaim Meir Liss was sent as mashpia while Blizinski remained gashmi administrator handling salaries, lodging and meals.
