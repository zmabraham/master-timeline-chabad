# Jerusalem Haggadah 'Hallel Nirtzeh' and 'Chasdei David' by Greenwald brothers

*1989*

In 5749 (1989), a Haggadah featuring two commentaries by the brothers Moshe Greenwald ('Hallel Nirtzeh') and Eliezer David Greenwald ('Chasdei David') was published in Jerusalem by Kollel Arugat HaBosem. The 177-page edition included drashot for Shabbat HaGadol and explanations of Sefirat HaOmer and Ketzirat HaOmer alongside laws of bedikat and biur chametz. It was published in honor of the wedding of Eliezer Tzvi and Leah Toiba Safrin.
