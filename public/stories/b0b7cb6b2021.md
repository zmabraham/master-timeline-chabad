# First Agudas Chabad meeting in Soviet Union convenes in Moscow

*1991*

On 16 Sivan 5751 (May 29, 1991), the inaugural meeting of Agudas Chabad in the Soviet Union took place in Moscow. R' Yitzchak Kogan opened with words of Torah on Parshat Beha'aloscha, and R' Shlomo Cunin read a response from the Lubavitcher Rebbe. The agenda covered the Schneersohn library campaign, the Polyakov synagogue, future organizational structure, and the Chernobyl children rescue.
