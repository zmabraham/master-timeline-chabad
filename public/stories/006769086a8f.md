# First railcar with 100 crates of Schneersohn library leaves Stettin for Stockholm

*1940*

On 16 Menachem Av, Blum confirmed that the first transport — 100 cases — was on its way from Stettin to Stockholm. A second railcar with 17 additional cases remained in Warsaw pending further payment. Blum sent repeated reminders through autumn 1940, but the second shipment never left Poland.
