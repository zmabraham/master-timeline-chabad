# Jerusalem Haggadah 'Aryeh D'Bei Ilai' of Shinava-Stropkov Chassidut

*1956*

In 5716 (1956), a pamphlet entitled 'Aryeh D'Bei Ilai' was published in Jerusalem by the Shinava-Stropkov Chassidic council, containing essential matters drawn from the 'Divrei Yechezkel Shraga' Haggadah commentary, along with responsa and letters from holy Geonim. The surviving copy contains only 32 pages of the original. It served the Shinava-Stropkov community after the Holocaust.
