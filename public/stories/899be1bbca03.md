# Chassidic aliyah to Eretz Yisrael led by R. Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk

*1777*

In the summer of 5537 (1777), R. Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk led a major group of Chassidim to settle in Eretz Yisrael. The Alter Rebbe accompanied them as far as Mohilov in Podolia, where the Chassidim pressed him to remain in Russia to lead their community. The parting was marked by great weeping, and the Alter Rebbe returned to Russia while the group continued on.
