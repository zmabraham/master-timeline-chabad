# Engagement of Maharashab to Sterna Sarah finalized by the Tzemach Tzedek

*1865*

In 5625 (1865), the Tzemach Tzedek finalized and signed the tenaim between his grandson Sholom DovBer (Maharashab), then aged four and a half, and Sterna Sarah, daughter of his son Yosef Yitzchak, Rebbe of Ovruch. The agreement stipulated that the wedding would take place ten years later in 5635 (1875).
