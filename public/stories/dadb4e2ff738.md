# Sir Moses Montefiore transfers 600 rubles to Hebron Chabad

*1845*

During Kislev 5606, the Hebron Chabad emissary R' Yosef Yehuda Mashiov forwarded 600 rubles, equivalent to about 200 guineas, from Shklov via Vilna to Sir Moses Montefiore. The funds were to be transmitted to Damascus and from there to the Chabad community in Hebron, demonstrating the cross-continental philanthropic network supporting the kollel.
