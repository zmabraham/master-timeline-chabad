# Marienbad summer conference of Chassidic Rebbes

*1908*

Outside the reach of Russian government scrutiny, leading Orthodox figures including Rebbe Rashab and the Gerrer Rebbe (R. Avraham Mordechai Alter) met in the Bohemian spa town of Marienbad. They discussed the preparations for the upcoming Vilna assembly and the Petersburg Rabbinical Conference. The Gerrer Rebbe asked Rashab to clarify the situation in Petersburg through the special emissary being sent to the capital.
