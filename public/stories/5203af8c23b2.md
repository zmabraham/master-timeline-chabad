# Last Tomchei Temimim students leave; municipality seizes the Lubavitch courtyard

*1918*

At the end of 5678 (1918), the last students of the Tomchei Temimim yeshiva left Lubavitch. The local municipality then seized the courtyard of the Rebbes: the great hall became the municipal theater, the Rashab's house became the courthouse, the Rebbe Maharash's rooms housed the municipal leadership committee, the Rayatz's rooms became the municipal officials' building, and the stone house was turned into the city school.
