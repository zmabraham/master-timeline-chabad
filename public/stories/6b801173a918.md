# Rabbi Avraham Chaim Nae returns to Jerusalem to rebuild Yeshivat Torat Emet

*1921*

Rabbi Avraham Chaim Nae joined SiLA and R. Moshe Aryeh Leib Shapira to issue a public appeal to revive the yeshiva originally established by the Rebbe Rashab in Hebron. Rabbi Shlomo Yehuda Leib Eliezerov, who had returned from his wartime exile, was enlisted to lead the yeshiva based on the Rebbe Rashab's mandate. The appeal stressed completing what they had begun in the holy work of rebuilding Torat Emet.
