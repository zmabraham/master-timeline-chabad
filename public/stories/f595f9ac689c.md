# Maharashab speaks on yeshiva reform and balancing Torah with prayer

*1914*

On 28 Menachem Av 5674, Maharashab walked with his son and discussed the Tomchei Temimim yeshiva, including the dormitory conditions and the need for students to study Nigleh. He explained that although many bochurim who came to him wished to devote themselves more to prayer, he typically refused: both Torah and prayer were essential, and the leader needed wisdom to keep the matter pure of intent.
