# Death of R. Yisrael Chanovich, shochet of Globoke

*1924*

R. Yisrael Chanovich, who served as the shochet of Globoke, passed away on 19 Marcheshvan 5685, one year after his daughter Kayla's marriage to R. Moshe Leib Rotstein. He left behind sons Chaim, Shmuel Avraham, and Gershon, and several daughters.
