# Winter 1772: Shklov debate between Alter Rebbe and R. Avraham of Kalisk

*1772*

The mitnagdim of Shklov broke prior assurances and confronted the chassidic leaders with force. Unable to answer the chassidic arguments, they appealed back to the Vilna Gaon, who then leaned toward declaring chassidim as 'moridin' — kindling the wider campaign against the movement.
