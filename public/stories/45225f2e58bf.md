# Secretary Chaim Lieberman escapes Warsaw to Riga

*1939*

Leaving 'naked and destitute,' Lieberman had no time to gather belongings. From Riga he began the intensive three-month effort to obtain exit permits and US entry visas for the Rebbe Rayatz and his family. The same day, the Rebbe and his family returned to their original Muranowska 32 apartment.
