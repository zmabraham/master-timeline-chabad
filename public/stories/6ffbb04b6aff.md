# Thirty minyanim of Temimim gather for Rosh Hashanah 5685 with the Rebbe Rayatz

*1924*

Around thirty minyanim of Temimim, the overwhelming majority students of Tomchei Temimim, came to Leningrad to be with the Rebbe Rayatz for Rosh Hashanah. Even R. Leib Bagad, a balebatish figure from Lubavitch generally distant from chassidus, prayed there and embraced the visiting Temimim like brothers. The warmth and bond among the visitors was deeply felt throughout the holiday.
