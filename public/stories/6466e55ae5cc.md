# Shavuos farbrengen with yeshiva students sparks public scandal

*1933*

At the gathering R' Yochanan Gordon and his brother R' Yitzchak Yaakov Gordon participated; the boys, seated on the floor, were given mashke two or three times. News quickly spread that 'R' Yisroel Jacobson had gotten the boys drunk,' causing an uproar throughout Brownsville and the Williamsburg yeshiva. Parents from non-Chabad backgrounds began visiting the author's home to understand his influence on their sons.
