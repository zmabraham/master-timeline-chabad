# Rebbe Rashab writes to R. Avraham Dovid Lavut requesting manuscript

*1887*

The Rebbe wrote to the noted Chabad gaon Lavut — author of Kav Naki and Shaar HaKollel — expressing joy at receiving a page of holy autograph manuscript via Lavut's grandson R. M. A. Sh. He further requested that the grandson send the autograph responsa of R. Y. L. (Maharil) for the same reasons of preservation.
