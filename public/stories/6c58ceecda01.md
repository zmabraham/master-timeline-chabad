# R. Moshe Meisels makes aliyah to Eretz Yisrael

*1816*

Around 1816, R. Moshe Meisels, author of Shirat Moshe and former trustee of the Vilna kehilla, ascended to the Land of Israel. Although the Mitteler Rebbe had earlier advised against this move, in 1816-1817 he instructed several of his chassidim to make aliyah. When the Mitteler Rebbe later founded the Chabad community in Hebron, Meisels became one of its leading members and signed most communal documents between 1827 and 1844.
