# R. Shainfinkel reports finding Kollel Chabad accounts in order to Bobruisker Rebbe

*1912*

Following accusations of embezzlement against the Jerusalem officials, Rebbe Rashab of Lubavitch and Rebbe Shemarya Noach of Bobruisk had asked the wealthy laymen R. Shalom Simcha Shainfinkel and R. Gershon Lifshitz to inspect the kollel's books. Shainfinkel reported that he had visited the kollel office repeatedly, examined the cashbox and ledgers, and found administration 'in proper and upright order' with no grounds to doubt the officials' integrity. Lifshitz participated only on condition that this not be construed as accepting any formal appointment.
