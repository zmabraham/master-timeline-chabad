# Rebbe Rayatz writes to students on 7 Shevat

*1946*

On 7 Shevat 5706 (Igrot Kodesh vol. 9, letter 2839), the Frierdiker Rebbe wrote to his beloved students, thanking G-d for the great kindness shown to them in the merit of his holy forebears — especially his father, the Rebbe Rashab, who gave his soul for the building of Tomchei Tmimim. He expressed inexpressible joy at the news of their rescue and strong hope that they would soon arrive in America to spread Torah and yiras Shamayim from a settled material and spiritual situation.
