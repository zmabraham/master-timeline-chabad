# Rebbe Rayatz stops in Warsaw en route to Marienbad

*1933*

The Rebbe Rayatz left Riga on 10 Tamuz 5693 and arrived in Warsaw on 12 Tamuz (July 6, 1933) for a two-day stop that included a visit to the yeshiva. He continued onward to Marienbad on 13 Tamuz. This was the last of his short visits before relocating to Warsaw permanently later that year.
