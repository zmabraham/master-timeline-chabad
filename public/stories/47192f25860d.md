# R. Pinchas Horowitz arrives in Frankfurt-am-Main to take his rabbinical seat

*1772*

After being elected by the Frankfurt community in spring 1771 and traveling via Brod, the Baal Haflaah's journey from Volhynia stretched from Cheshvan to Tevet of 5532. Frankfurt's communal records date his arrival to 26 Tevet 5532. He would serve as Frankfurt's rabbi for more than thirty-three years until his passing in 5565.
