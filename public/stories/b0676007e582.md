# HaKriah VeHaKedushah monthly magazine launched

*1940*

Published from late 1940 through the war's end, the magazine carried the Rebbe's call of 'L'alter leteshuvah, l'alter legeulah' — immediate repentance prompts redemption. A small English section was added in April 1941. Its message was that prayer and repentance would catalyze divine deliverance.
