# Khovansky appeals the acquittal to the Senate in St. Petersburg

*1827*

Refusing to accept the Mogilev verdict, Khovansky on Rosh Chodesh Sivan 5587 lodged a formal appeal with the Senate. He simultaneously instructed the Mogilev court to forward the case file to Petersburg. Khovansky's hope was to have the Rebbe brought to the capital for renewed proceedings, but the appeal would ultimately fail.
