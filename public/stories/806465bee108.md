# Rayatz begins recovery from typhus

*1920*

Chasidim led by Reb Itche der Masmid prayed repeatedly at the Ohel of the Rashab, and Goldin telegrammed Chasidim in Lubavitch, Nizhyn, and Hadyach to pray at the graves of earlier Rebbes. The improvement coincided with 19 Kislev, the chasidic festival marking the Alter Rebbe's release from czarist prison in 1798.
