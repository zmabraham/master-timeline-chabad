# R' Menachem Aryeh Segal replaces R' Avigdor in kollel

*1851*

The administrators reported that R' Avigdor had withdrawn due to old age and wished him renewed vigor. They welcomed his replacement R' Menachem Aryeh — son of the late R' Shmuel Segal — citing 'the threefold cord that is not quickly broken.'
