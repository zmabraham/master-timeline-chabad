# Chabad shluchim wave to President Bush outside Moscow embassy

*1991*

Following R' Yosef Aronov's proposal at the kinus, shluchim halted their bus convoy en route to the airport for Alma-Ata and positioned themselves at the embassy corner. After more than an hour of waiting, Bush slowed his motorcade and waved at length as he passed. Bush carried a letter to Gorbachev about the Schneersohn library and raised the issue at their meeting, though no answer had yet come back.
